# Feature map

Every screen, how to reach it from the dashboard, and what tests it. A test
(`internal/ui/features_test.go`) fails if a dashboard key is missing here.

| Key | Screen | What it does | Code | Tests |
|-----|--------|--------------|------|-------|
| — | Title 西行 | The opening screen: Continue (the journey played last), New journey (named; your words and reviews come with you), Load journey (list with progress, purse and when played; d twice deletes). Each journey is a save file in `wiki/saves/` holding its story, purse, gear, bonds, quests and collection; study progress (word schedules, the day's paid words, bounties, streak shields in `wiki/study.yaml`) is shared by all. An old `wiki/journey.yaml` is adopted as the first save. esc on the dashboard returns here | `internal/ui/title`, `internal/wiki/saves.go`, `internal/wiki/study.go` | screen snapshots (first launch, saves), new / continue / load / delete confirm, journeys keep their own game but share study, old journey adopted |
| — | Road 西行路 | The journey's hub: the region's tile map, walked with the arrow keys (24 regions; the header says which of the 24 it is, and a strip under the map lists every town on the road in order: requests done for those reached, ？？？ and the tribulation that reaches the rest, and where the pilgrim stands among them in `content/regions.yaml`, drawn from a fixed seed per region by `journey.BuildMap`). Towns (H) are entered by walking in; each tribulation (!) bars the road until its battle is won, the region's last is the demon's lair (&); the doors (< >) lead to the neighbouring regions. Sealed tribulations say what they need. Each step onto wild ground has a 12% chance of meeting one of the region's clans (minions most, captains rarely) quizzing the coming stage's words; the road is safe. HP carries on the road as wounds (shown as a bar); every fresh right answer anywhere heals 2, a town inn (r) heals all for 10 铜钱; losing a road battle wakes the pilgrim at the last town passed with half HP. Where the pilgrim stands is saved with the journey. the sidebar keeps a log of hunts under way and where to report. c opens camp; battles return here | `internal/ui/roam`, `internal/journey/regionmap.go` | first-region snapshot, every map walkable and gated, a tribulation bars the road until won, doors and saved place, walking into a town, wild encounters and a safe road, wounds carried in and a fall that wakes you in town, healing by study and at the inn, road wins count toward hunts and sparring doesn't |
| — | Progress file | The learner's progress lives in one SQLite file, `wiki/progress.db` (ignored by git, pure-Go driver): each journey's save, study bookkeeping, settings and the forge cache as rows of `docs` under their old YAML names; review schedules (with reps and lapses) and a log of every answer in tables of their own. | — | Shadowing 跟读 | In an episode, s: hear a line of its own audio (tab again), press space and say it back; ffmpeg records about the line's length from the default microphone and whisper.cpp (`whisper-cli`, the largest ggml model in ~/.local/share/whisper, or TUILAOSHI_WHISPER_MODEL) transcribes it on this Mac, steered to simplified Mandarin. The line is shown with the characters heard in green and the rest underlined in red, a percentage, what was heard and the English; digits match Chinese numerals and a dropped 儿 isn't held against you. n another line; leaving says the best match. | — | Claude use and a daily cap | Every call to Claude goes through one place (`claude.Client.send`), which asks a meter first and records each call's tokens in the progress file's `usage` table by day and model. | — | Crash log | When a screen panics, Bubble Tea restores the terminal and writes the stack to stderr; everything on stderr is also appended to `wiki/crash.log` (ignored by git), and the game says where the details are and that progress up to the last answer is saved | `cmd/mandarin/crash.go` | stderr kept in the log, no log when nothing is written |
| — | Tone colours 声调色 | Pinyin everywhere is drawn a syllable at a time in its tone's colour: first red, second orange, third green, fourth blue, neutral grey. | — | Buying in Chinese 量词 | At the store, b (斋饭) and x (sandals for the party) ask for it in Chinese with a blank for the measure word, 老板，我要五___草鞋。, and four to choose from. | — | Hear it (ctrl+r) | ctrl+r reads aloud the Chinese on screen where there was no voice before: a trail event or crossing (and its result once chosen), a town's notice, alms scene (with the thanks once asked well) or description, a festival's story or question, the diary's prompt or today's entry. | — | The first hour | A new learner facing a big backlog (500+ due, no level check yet) is pointed to the level check first (camp's Start here, a). | — | Review rules | A miss keeps a quarter of the interval (at least a day) instead of starting over; marking a word unknown while reading starts it over, due tomorrow. Intervals stop at 365 days, and from 20 days spread by up to 5% either way (the same for a card on a day), so a big day's reviews don't come due together. | — | FSRS scheduling | Settings' Scheduler chooses classic (the fixed multipliers above, the default) or fsrs: a compact FSRS-4.5 memory model with the published default weights, aiming for 90% recall. Each card keeps a stability and a difficulty (two columns on `schedules`, added in place to older progress files), updated on every review under either scheduler; a card new to the model starts from its first rating, or from its classic interval taken as its stability. FSRS intervals keep the 365-day cap and the spread. My progress replays the review log through both and says how many words each would have due in the next 7 days | `internal/wiki/fsrs.go`, `internal/wiki/srs.go`, `internal/ui/stats/ahead.go` | Good, Good, Again, Good against worked values, the setting switch, an old progress file upgraded, the replayed comparison |

Shared pieces:

- Saving progress: `wiki.Store.RecordReview` is the only path that writes review schedules; session files go through `internal/wiki/sessions.go`. Tests in `internal/wiki`.
- Speech: `ui.SpeakCmd` (Fish Audio with `FISH_AUDIO_API_KEY`, otherwise macOS `say -v Tingting`); `ctrl+r` replays in drill and review; failures show as "Audio failed".
- Pinyin input: `internal/ui/input`; syllable validation tests.

Recordings (`make recordings`, needs `brew install vhs`; tapes in `docs/tapes`, played on a copy of the wiki):
[battle](recordings/battle.gif), [journey](recordings/journey.gif).

Journey content (tribulations, demons, bosses, companions, lore, dialogue, vocabulary themes, story scenes, dialogue episodes, demon and companion abilities) is data in `internal/journey/content/*.yaml`, embedded in the binary; `internal/journey/content_test.go` validates it as a whole, so adding a demon or lore passage is a YAML-only change.

Launching: `make launcher` puts a self-contained TuiLaoShi.app on the Desktop (the tutor built for Apple silicon and Intel, a wiki snapshot, and the API keys from `~/.config/tuilaoshi/env`) and a `tuilaoshi` command in /opt/homebrew/bin. On this Mac the app runs from the repo on the real wiki; on another Mac sharing the Desktop it runs on its own, keeping that Mac's progress in `~/Library/Application Support/TuiLaoShi`.
