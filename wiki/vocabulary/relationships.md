## Example Sentences

- 在中国，过年的时候单身的年轻人最怕亲戚问"有没有对象"。(Zài Zhōngguó, guònián de shíhou dānshēn de niánqīngrén zuì pà qīnqi wèn "yǒu méiyǒu duìxiàng".) — In China, single young people dread relatives asking "Do you have a partner?" during the New Year.
- 他们认识了半年就结婚了，朋友们都觉得太快了。(Tāmen rènshile bàn nián jiù jiéhūn le, péngyoumen dōu juéde tài kuài le.) — They got married after knowing each other for only six months; all their friends thought it was too fast.

## Usage Notes

对象 (duìxiàng) is a common colloquial term for "partner/significant other" and is what relatives typically use when asking about your relationship status. 相亲 refers to arranged meetings between potential partners, often set up by parents or matchmakers — this remains common in Chinese culture. 恋爱 is a verb-object compound, so you say 谈恋爱 (tán liàn'ài, "to be in a relationship") rather than just 恋爱 alone as a verb.
