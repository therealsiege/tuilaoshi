# Body & Health 身体健康

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 头 | tóu | head | 我头疼。 |
| 手 | shǒu | hand | 请洗手。 |
| 脚 | jiǎo | foot | 我的脚很疼。 |
| 眼睛 | yǎnjing | eye | 她的眼睛很大。 |
| 医院 | yīyuàn | hospital | 他去医院看病了。 |

| 身体 | shēntǐ | body; health | 你身体怎么样？ |

## Example Sentences

- 我不舒服，要去医院看医生。(Wǒ bù shūfu, yào qù yīyuàn kàn yīshēng.) — I don't feel well, I need to go to the hospital to see a doctor.
- 身体健康最重要。(Shēntǐ jiànkāng zuì zhòngyào.) — Good health is the most important thing.

## Usage Notes

看病 (kàn bìng) means "to see a doctor" from the patient's perspective, while 看医生 (kàn yīshēng) also works but is less idiomatic. In Chinese, 身体 can mean both the physical body and one's health in general. The greeting 你身体怎么样？ asks about someone's health, not their body literally. Chinese medicine (中医, zhōngyī) and Western medicine (西医, xīyī) coexist in China, and many people use both. Common body-part compounds include 头疼 (tóuténg, headache), 肚子疼 (dùzi téng, stomachache), and 嗓子疼 (sǎngzi téng, sore throat).
