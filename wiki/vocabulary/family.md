# Family 家人

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 爸爸 | bàba | dad | 爸爸在看电视。 |
| 妈妈 | māma | mom | 妈妈做的菜很好吃。 |
| 哥哥 | gēge | older brother | 我哥哥比我高。 |
| 姐姐 | jiějie | older sister | 姐姐在北京工作。 |
| 弟弟 | dìdi | younger brother | 弟弟今年十岁。 |

## Example Sentences

- 我家有五口人。(Wǒ jiā yǒu wǔ kǒu rén.) — There are five people in my family.
- 我爸爸妈妈住在上海。(Wǒ bàba māma zhù zài Shànghǎi.) — My mom and dad live in Shanghai.

## Usage Notes

Chinese distinguishes between older and younger siblings: 哥哥/姐姐 for older, 弟弟/妹妹 for younger. There is no single word for "brother" or "sister" without specifying relative age. Similarly, paternal and maternal grandparents have different terms — 爷爷/奶奶 are paternal, while 外公 (wàigōng) / 外婆 (wàipó) are maternal. The measure word for family members is 个 (gè), but when counting how many people are in a family, use 口 (kǒu).
