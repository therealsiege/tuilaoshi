# Nature 自然

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 山 | shān | mountain | 那座山很高。 |
| 河 | hé | river | 河里的水很清。 |
| 海 | hǎi | sea, ocean | 我想去看海。 |
| 湖 | hú | lake | 湖边的风景很美。 |
| 树 | shù | tree | 这棵树很大。 |
| 花 | huā | flower | 花园里有很多花。 |
| 草 | cǎo | grass | 草地上有小朋友在玩。 |
| 动物 | dòngwù | animal | 孩子们喜欢看动物。 |
| 太阳 | tàiyáng | sun | 太阳出来了。 |
| 月亮 | yuèliang | moon | 今天晚上的月亮很圆。 |

## Example Sentences

- 山上有很多树和花。(Shān shàng yǒu hěn duō shù hé huā.) — There are many trees and flowers on the mountain.
- 我们去湖边走走吧，那儿风景很美。(Wǒmen qù hú biān zǒuzou ba, nàr fēngjǐng hěn měi.) — Let's go for a walk by the lake, the scenery there is beautiful.
- 太阳下山了，月亮出来了。(Tàiyáng xiàshān le, yuèliang chūlái le.) — The sun has set and the moon has come out.

## Usage Notes

Nature words each take specific measure words: 座 (zuò) for mountains (一座山), 条 (tiáo) for rivers (一条河), 棵 (kē) for trees (一棵树), 朵 (duǒ) for flowers (一朵花), and 只 (zhī) for most animals (一只动物). Note that 月亮 (yuèliang) has a neutral tone on the second syllable. The word 海 appears in many compound words: 海边 (hǎibiān, seaside), 海鲜 (hǎixiān, seafood), 上海 (Shànghǎi, literally "on the sea"). Chinese has a rich vocabulary connecting nature to culture and poetry. For example, 山水 (shānshǔi, mountains and water) refers to landscape painting, and 风景 (fēngjǐng, wind + scene) means "scenery."
