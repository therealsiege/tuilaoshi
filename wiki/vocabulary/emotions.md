# Emotions 情感

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 高兴 | gāoxìng | happy, glad | 我很高兴认识你。 |
| 难过 | nánguò | sad, upset | 听到这个消息，她很难过。 |
| 生气 | shēngqì | angry | 老师生气了。 |
| 害怕 | hàipà | afraid, scared | 小孩子害怕黑暗。 |
| 紧张 | jǐnzhāng | nervous, tense | 考试前我很紧张。 |
| 开心 | kāixīn | happy, joyful | 今天玩得很开心。 |
| 伤心 | shāngxīn | sad, heartbroken | 她伤心地哭了。 |
| 着急 | zháojí | anxious, worried | 别着急，慢慢来。 |
| 无聊 | wúliáo | bored, boring | 这个电影太无聊了。 |
| 满意 | mǎnyì | satisfied | 老板对他的工作很满意。 |

## Example Sentences

- 他考试考得很好，所以很高兴。(Tā kǎoshì kǎo de hěn hǎo, suǒyǐ hěn gāoxìng.) — He did well on the exam, so he is very happy.
- 你别生气了，他不是故意的。(Nǐ bié shēngqì le, tā bú shì gùyì de.) — Don't be angry, he didn't do it on purpose.
- 她一个人在家，觉得很无聊。(Tā yí ge rén zài jiā, juéde hěn wúliáo.) — She is home alone and feels very bored.

## Usage Notes

Most emotion words in Chinese function as adjectives (or stative verbs) and can be used directly with 很 (hěn) as an intensifier: 我很高兴 (I am happy). Note the difference between 高兴 and 开心 — both mean "happy," but 开心 emphasizes the feeling of joy and fun, while 高兴 is more general gladness. 伤心 and 难过 both mean "sad," but 伤心 implies deeper emotional pain (heartbroken), whereas 难过 is a more general feeling of being upset. 着急 specifically describes the restless anxiety of worrying about something urgent, often used with 别 (don't) to tell someone to calm down.
