# Clothing 衣服

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 衣服 | yīfu | clothes | 这件衣服很漂亮。 |
| 裤子 | kùzi | pants, trousers | 我想买一条裤子。 |
| 裙子 | qúnzi | skirt, dress | 她穿了一条红裙子。 |
| 鞋子 | xiézi | shoes | 这双鞋子很舒服。 |
| 帽子 | màozi | hat, cap | 外面冷，戴上帽子吧。 |
| 外套 | wàitào | coat, jacket | 今天要穿外套。 |
| 衬衫 | chènshān | shirt, blouse | 他穿了一件白衬衫。 |

| 戴 | dài | to wear (accessories) | 他戴了一顶帽子。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你今天穿的衣服真好看。(Nǐ jīntiān chuān de yīfu zhēn hǎokàn.) — The clothes you're wearing today look really nice.
- 天气冷了，你应该穿外套。(Tiānqì lěng le, nǐ yīnggāi chuān wàitào.) — The weather is cold, you should wear a coat.
- 这条裙子太贵了，我不想买。(Zhè tiáo qúnzi tài guì le, wǒ bù xiǎng mǎi.) — This skirt is too expensive, I don't want to buy it.

## Usage Notes

Chinese distinguishes between 穿 (chuān) and 戴 (dài) for "to wear." Use 穿 for clothing that goes on the body (shirts, pants, skirts, shoes, socks) and 戴 for accessories worn on the head, hands, or face (hats, glasses, gloves, watches, jewelry). Clothing items use specific measure words: 件 (jiàn) for upper-body garments (一件衣服, 一件衬衫), 条 (tiáo) for long items like pants and skirts (一条裤子, 一条裙子), 双 (shuāng) for pairs like shoes and socks (一双鞋子, 一双袜子), and 顶 (dǐng) for hats (一顶帽子).
