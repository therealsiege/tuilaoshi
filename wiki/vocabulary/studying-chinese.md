# Studying Chinese 学中文

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 练习 | liànxí | practice, exercise | 你要多练习说中文。(Nǐ yào duō liànxí shuō Zhōngwén.) |
| 复习 | fùxí | review (previously learned material) | 考试以前要好好复习。(Kǎoshì yǐqián yào hǎohāo fùxí.) |
| 预习 | yùxí | preview, prepare (upcoming material) | 请大家预习第八课。(Qǐng dàjiā yùxí dì bā kè.) |
| 发音 | fāyīn | pronunciation | 你的发音很标准。(Nǐ de fāyīn hěn biāozhǔn.) |
| 声调 | shēngdiào | tone (in Chinese) | 中文有四个声调。(Zhōngwén yǒu sì ge shēngdiào.) |
| 语法 | yǔfǎ | grammar | 中文语法不太难。(Zhōngwén yǔfǎ bú tài nán.) |
| 生词 | shēngcí | new vocabulary word | 这一课有二十个生词。(Zhè yí kè yǒu èrshí ge shēngcí.) |
| 课文 | kèwén | lesson text | 请打开书，看课文。(Qǐng dǎkāi shū, kàn kèwén.) |
| 听写 | tīngxiě | dictation | 明天有听写，别忘了。(Míngtiān yǒu tīngxiě, bié wàng le.) |
| 作文 | zuòwén | essay, composition | 这篇作文写得很好。(Zhè piān zuòwén xiě de hěn hǎo.) |

## Example Sentences

- 学中文最难的是声调和发音。(Xué Zhōngwén zuì nán de shì shēngdiào hé fāyīn.) — The hardest part of learning Chinese is tones and pronunciation.
- 老师说我们要预习课文，复习生词。(Lǎoshī shuō wǒmen yào yùxí kèwén, fùxí shēngcí.) — The teacher said we need to preview the lesson text and review the vocabulary.
- 每天练习写汉字，你的作文会越来越好。(Měitiān liànxí xiě Hànzì, nǐ de zuòwén huì yuèláiyuè hǎo.) — If you practice writing characters every day, your essays will get better and better.

## Usage Notes

练习, 复习, and 预习 all share the component 习 (xí, to study/practice). 练习 = drill/practice, 复习 = review (复 = again), 预习 = preview/prepare (预 = in advance). These three words form a natural study cycle: 预习 before class, pay attention during class, 复习 and 练习 after class. 听写 is literally "listen-write" — a standard classroom exercise where the teacher reads words aloud and students write them. 课文 (lesson text) and 生词 (new words) are the two main components of a textbook lesson.
