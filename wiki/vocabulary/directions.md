# Directions 方向

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 前面 | qiánmiàn | in front, ahead | 学校在前面。 |
| 后面 | hòumiàn | behind, in back | 他坐在我后面。 |
| 左边 | zuǒbiān | left side | 银行在左边。 |
| 右边 | yòubiān | right side | 超市在右边。 |
| 上面 | shàngmiàn | above, on top | 书在桌子上面。 |
| 下面 | xiàmiàn | below, underneath | 猫在桌子下面。 |
| 旁边 | pángbiān | beside, next to | 他坐在我旁边。 |
| 对面 | duìmiàn | opposite, across | 医院在学校对面。 |
| 里面 | lǐmiàn | inside | 房间里面有人。 |
| 外面 | wàimiàn | outside | 外面在下雨。 |

## Example Sentences

- 银行在超市的左边，邮局在右边。(Yínháng zài chāoshì de zuǒbiān, yóujú zài yòubiān.) — The bank is to the left of the supermarket, the post office is to the right.
- 你的前面是图书馆，后面是公园。(Nǐ de qiánmiàn shì túshūguǎn, hòumiàn shì gōngyuán.) — In front of you is the library, behind you is the park.
- 请在外面等一下，里面有人。(Qǐng zài wàimiàn děng yíxià, lǐmiàn yǒu rén.) — Please wait outside for a moment, someone is inside.

## Usage Notes

Direction words in Chinese can end in 面 (miàn) or 边 (biān), and in most cases these are interchangeable: 前面/前边, 后面/后边, 上面/上边. The 面 forms tend to be slightly more formal. When used with 在 (zài) to describe location, the pattern is: A 在 B 的 direction — 猫在桌子的下面 (The cat is under the table). The 的 can often be dropped in casual speech. Note that 对面 only takes 面, not 边. Chinese also uses cardinal directions (东 dōng east, 南 nán south, 西 xī west, 北 běi north) more frequently than English does in everyday conversation, especially when giving street directions.
