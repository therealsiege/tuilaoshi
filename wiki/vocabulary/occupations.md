# Occupations 职业

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 医生 | yīshēng | doctor | 她是一位医生。 |
| 老师 | lǎoshī | teacher | 王老师教我们中文。 |
| 学生 | xuéshēng | student | 我是大学生。 |
| 工人 | gōngrén | worker | 工人们在工厂工作。 |
| 司机 | sījī | driver | 司机开车很小心。 |
| 服务员 | fúwùyuán | waiter/waitress | 服务员，买单！ |
| 经理 | jīnglǐ | manager | 他是公司的经理。 |
| 警察 | jǐngchá | police officer | 警察在路口指挥交通。 |
| 护士 | hùshi | nurse | 护士照顾病人。 |
| 律师 | lǜshī | lawyer | 她想当律师。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你做什么工作？我是老师。(Nǐ zuò shénme gōngzuò? Wǒ shì lǎoshī.) — What do you do for work? I am a teacher.
- 他的爸爸是医生，妈妈是护士。(Tā de bàba shì yīshēng, māma shì hùshi.) — His father is a doctor and his mother is a nurse.
- 我长大以后想当律师。(Wǒ zhǎngdà yǐhòu xiǎng dāng lǜshī.) — When I grow up, I want to be a lawyer.

## Usage Notes

To state someone's occupation, Chinese uses 是 (shì): 她是医生 (She is a doctor). No article is needed before the occupation noun. To ask about occupation, the common patterns are 你做什么工作？ (What work do you do?) or 你是做什么的？ (What do you do?). To express wanting to become something, use 当 (dāng, to serve as): 我想当老师 (I want to be a teacher). Note that 护士 is pronounced hùshi with a neutral tone on the second syllable, not hùshì. The suffix 员 (yuán) appears in many job titles and means "member/person": 服务员 (service person), 演员 (yǎnyuán, actor), 运动员 (yùndòngyuán, athlete).
