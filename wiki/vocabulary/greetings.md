# Greetings 问候

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 你好 | nǐ hǎo | hello | 你好，我是小明。 |
| 再见 | zài jiàn | goodbye | 明天再见！ |
| 谢谢 | xiè xie | thank you | 谢谢你的帮助。 |
| 不客气 | bú kè qi | you're welcome | 不客气！ |
| 对不起 | duì bu qǐ | sorry | 对不起，我迟到了。 |
| 没关系 | méi guānxi | it doesn't matter | 没关系，别担心。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你好！你叫什么名字？(Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) — Hello! What's your name?
- 谢谢你！再见！(Xiè xie nǐ! Zài jiàn!) — Thank you! Goodbye!

## Usage Notes

你好 is formal. Among friends, people often use 嗨 (hāi) or 哈喽 (hālóu) borrowed from English.
