## Example Sentences

- 你的电话号码是多少？(Nǐ de diànhuà hàomǎ shì duōshao?) — What is your phone number?
- 一共三百二十五块钱。(Yígòng sān bǎi èrshí wǔ kuài qián.) — It is 325 yuan in total.

## Usage Notes

二 and 两 (liǎng) both mean "two" but are used differently. 两 is used before measure words (两个人, two people), while 二 is used in numbers, math, and phone numbers. Chinese uses 万 (ten thousand) as a unit — there is no single word for "ten thousand" in the same way. So 100,000 is 十万 (ten ten-thousands). The ordinal prefix 第 (dì) turns any number into an ordinal: 第二 (second), 第三 (third). The number 四 (four) sounds like 死 (sǐ, death), so it is considered unlucky; 八 (eight) sounds like 发 (fā, prosperity) and is considered very lucky.
