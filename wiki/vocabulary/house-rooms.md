# House & Rooms 房子

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 房间 | fángjiān | room | 这个房间很大。 |
| 客厅 | kètīng | living room | 我们在客厅看电视。 |
| 卧室 | wòshì | bedroom | 卧室里有一张大床。 |
| 厨房 | chúfáng | kitchen | 妈妈在厨房做饭。 |
| 卫生间 | wèishēngjiān | bathroom | 卫生间在哪儿？ |
| 门 | mén | door | 请关门。 |
| 窗户 | chuānghù | window | 请把窗户打开。 |
| 桌子 | zhuōzi | table, desk | 书在桌子上。 |
| 椅子 | yǐzi | chair | 请坐这把椅子。 |
| 床 | chuáng | bed | 他躺在床上看书。 |

## Example Sentences

- 我的房间不大，但是很干净。(Wǒ de fángjiān bú dà, dànshì hěn gānjìng.) — My room is not big, but it is very clean.
- 厨房里有一张桌子和四把椅子。(Chúfáng lǐ yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi hé sì bǎ yǐzi.) — There is a table and four chairs in the kitchen.
- 请把门关上，外面太冷了。(Qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng, wàimiàn tài lěng le.) — Please close the door, it's too cold outside.

## Usage Notes

Furniture and room items each take specific measure words: 张 (zhāng) for flat surfaces like tables and beds (一张桌子, 一张床), 把 (bǎ) for things with handles like chairs (一把椅子), 扇 (shàn) for doors and windows (一扇门, 一扇窗户), and 间 (jiān) for rooms (一间房间, 一间卧室). Note that 房间 is the general word for "room," while specific rooms use different terms. 卫生间 is the polite term for bathroom; in casual speech you may also hear 厕所 (cèsuǒ), which is more direct.
