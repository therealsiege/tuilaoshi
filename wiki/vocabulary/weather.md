# Weather 天气

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 天气 | tiānqì | weather | 今天天气怎么样？ |
| 热 | rè | hot | 夏天很热。 |
| 冷 | lěng | cold | 冬天北京很冷。 |
| 下雨 | xià yǔ | to rain | 外面在下雨。 |
| 下雪 | xià xuě | to snow | 冬天常常下雪。 |
| 风 | fēng | wind | 今天的风很大。 |
| 晴天 | qíngtiān | sunny day | 明天是晴天。 |
| 阴天 | yīntiān | cloudy day | 今天是阴天，可能会下雨。 |
| 温度 | wēndù | temperature | 今天的温度是二十五度。 |

## Example Sentences

- 明天天气怎么样？(Míngtiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?) — What will the weather be like tomorrow?
- 下雨了，你带伞了吗？(Xià yǔ le, nǐ dài sǎn le ma?) — It's raining, did you bring an umbrella?

## Usage Notes

下 (xià, to fall/descend) is used as a verb with both 雨 (rain) and 雪 (snow): literally "falling rain" and "falling snow." Temperature in China uses Celsius (摄氏度, shèshìdù). When describing wind strength, Chinese uses 大 (dà, big) for strong wind and 小 (xiǎo, small) for light wind, rather than "strong" and "weak." A common greeting in daily conversation is 今天天气怎么样？ though Chinese people also frequently use 吃了吗？(Have you eaten?) as a casual greeting.
