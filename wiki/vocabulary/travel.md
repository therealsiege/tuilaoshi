# Travel 旅行

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 飞机 | fēijī | airplane | 我坐飞机去北京。 |
| 火车 | huǒchē | train | 从上海到南京坐火车两个小时。 |
| 出租车 | chūzūchē | taxi | 我们打出租车去机场吧。 |
| 公共汽车 | gōnggòng qìchē | bus | 公共汽车站在前面。 |

## Example Sentences

- 请问，去机场坐几路公共汽车？(Qǐngwèn, qù jīchǎng zuò jǐ lù gōnggòng qìchē?) — Excuse me, which bus goes to the airport?
- 我的护照和签证都准备好了。(Wǒ de hùzhào hé qiānzhèng dōu zhǔnbèi hǎo le.) — My passport and visa are both ready.

## Usage Notes

The verb for taking transportation in Chinese depends on the vehicle: 坐 (zuò, to sit) is used for planes, trains, buses, and taxis (坐飞机, 坐火车). However, for taxis specifically, 打车 (dǎ chē) or 打的 (dǎ dī) are very common colloquial expressions meaning "to take a taxi." 酒店 and 宾馆 (bīnguǎn) both mean "hotel," with 酒店 being more commonly used for modern hotels. In everyday speech, 公共汽车 is often shortened to 公交车 (gōngjiāochē) or simply 公交 (gōngjiāo).
