# Sports & Hobbies 运动爱好

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 跑步 | pǎobù | run, jogging | 我每天早上跑步。 |
| 游泳 | yóuyǒng | swim | 夏天我喜欢游泳。 |
| 打篮球 | dǎ lánqiú | play basketball | 他喜欢打篮球。 |
| 踢足球 | tī zúqiú | play soccer | 我们周末踢足球吧。 |
| 唱歌 | chànggē | sing | 她唱歌唱得很好。 |
| 跳舞 | tiàowǔ | dance | 他们在跳舞。 |
| 画画 | huàhuà | draw, paint | 妹妹喜欢画画。 |
| 弹钢琴 | tán gāngqín | play piano | 她会弹钢琴。 |
| 看电影 | kàn diànyǐng | watch a movie | 我们一起去看电影吧。 |
| 听音乐 | tīng yīnyuè | listen to music | 我喜欢听音乐。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你有什么爱好？我喜欢跑步和游泳。(Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào? Wǒ xǐhuan pǎobù hé yóuyǒng.) — What are your hobbies? I like running and swimming.
- 她从小就学弹钢琴。(Tā cóngxiǎo jiù xué tán gāngqín.) — She has been learning to play the piano since she was little.
- 周末我们去看电影，好不好？(Zhōumò wǒmen qù kàn diànyǐng, hǎo bu hǎo?) — Let's go watch a movie this weekend, okay?

## Usage Notes

Chinese uses different verbs for "play" depending on the sport or instrument. 打 (dǎ, to hit/strike) is used for sports involving hands or rackets: 打篮球, 打网球 (tennis), 打乒乓球 (table tennis). 踢 (tī, to kick) is used for sports involving feet: 踢足球. For musical instruments, 弹 (tán, to pluck/play) is for keyboard and string instruments (弹钢琴, 弹吉他), while 拉 (lā, to pull) is for bowed instruments (拉小提琴, violin) and 吹 (chuī, to blow) is for wind instruments (吹笛子, flute). Many hobby words like 唱歌, 跳舞, and 画画 are verb-object compounds, so modifiers insert between them: 唱了一首歌 (sang a song).
