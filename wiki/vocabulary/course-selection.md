# Course Selection 选课

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 选课 | xuǎnkè | to select courses | 下学期的选课下周开始。 |
| 学分 | xuéfēn | credit (academic) | 毕业需要修够一百六十个学分。 |

## Example Sentences

- 选课的时候要注意，热门课很快就选满了。(Xuǎnkè de shíhou yào zhùyì, rèmén kè hěn kuài jiù xuǎnmǎn le.) — Be careful when selecting courses; popular classes fill up fast.
- 期末考试占总成绩的百分之四十，平时作业也很重要。(Qīmò kǎoshì zhàn zǒng chéngjì de bǎifēn zhī sìshí, píngshí zuòyè yě hěn zhòngyào.) — The final exam is 40% of the total grade, but regular homework is also important.

## Usage Notes

必修 and 选修 are often used with 课 (course): 必修课 (required course), 选修课 (elective course). 学分 in China typically follows a system where each course is worth 2-4 credits. 论文 refers specifically to academic papers; for shorter essays or compositions, use 作文 (zuòwén).
