# Shopping 购物

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 买 | mǎi | to buy | 我想买一本书。 |
| 卖 | mài | to sell | 这家店卖水果。 |
| 钱 | qián | money | 你带钱了吗？ |
| 多少钱 | duōshao qián | how much money | 这个多少钱？ |
| 便宜 | piányi | cheap; inexpensive | 这件衣服很便宜。 |
| 贵 | guì | expensive | 这个太贵了。 |
| 块 | kuài | yuan (colloquial) | 这本书二十块钱。 |
| 商店 | shāngdiàn | shop; store | 商店几点关门？ |
| 超市 | chāoshì | supermarket | 我去超市买东西。 |

## Example Sentences

- 这个多少钱？太贵了，能便宜一点吗？(Zhège duōshao qián? Tài guì le, néng piányi yīdiǎn ma?) — How much is this? Too expensive, can you make it a bit cheaper?
- 我在超市买了很多水果。(Wǒ zài chāoshì mǎi le hěn duō shuǐguǒ.) — I bought a lot of fruit at the supermarket.

## Usage Notes

买 (mǎi, to buy, third tone) and 卖 (mài, to sell, fourth tone) are a classic tone-pair that learners must distinguish carefully. 块 is the colloquial measure word for the Chinese yuan (元, yuán is the formal written form). In conversation, prices are stated as number + 块: 五块 (five yuan). Bargaining (讨价还价, tǎojià huánjià) is common at street markets but not at fixed-price stores or supermarkets. The phrase 买东西 (mǎi dōngxi) means "to go shopping" or "to buy things."
