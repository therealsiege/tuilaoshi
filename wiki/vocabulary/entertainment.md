## Example Sentences

- 这部电视剧一共五十集，我花了两个星期才看完。(Zhè bù diànshìjù yígòng wǔshí jí, wǒ huāle liǎng ge xīngqī cái kànwán.) — This TV series has 50 episodes; it took me two weeks to finish watching it.
- 博物馆的展览很精彩，我推荐你去看看。(Bówùguǎn de zhǎnlǎn hěn jīngcǎi, wǒ tuījiàn nǐ qù kànkan.) — The museum exhibition is wonderful; I recommend you go check it out.

## Usage Notes

粉丝 is a loanword from English "fans," using characters that happen to also mean "glass noodles" (粉丝 fěnsī). Context always makes the meaning clear. 火 (huǒ, "fire") is colloquially used to mean "popular/hot" when describing entertainment: 这部剧很火 (this show is really popular). Chinese TV series (电视剧) tend to be much longer than Western ones, often 40-60 episodes per season.
