## Example Sentences

- 大学宿舍一般住四个人，每个人都有自己的书桌。(Dàxué sùshè yìbān zhù sì ge rén, měi ge rén dōu yǒu zìjǐ de shūzhuō.) — University dorms usually house four people, and each person has their own desk.
- 我室友每天晚上十二点才睡觉，我很不习惯。(Wǒ shìyǒu měitiān wǎnshang shí'èr diǎn cái shuìjiào, wǒ hěn bù xíguàn.) — My roommate doesn't go to sleep until midnight every night; I'm really not used to it.

## Usage Notes

习惯 can function as both a verb ("to be used to") and a noun ("habit"). As a verb: 我习惯了这里的生活。As a noun: 这是一个好习惯。适应 emphasizes the process of adjusting to something new, while 习惯 emphasizes the end state of being accustomed.
