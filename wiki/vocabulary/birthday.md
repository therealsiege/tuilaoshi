# Birthday 生日

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 生日 | shēngrì | birthday | 今天是我的生日。(Jīntiān shì wǒ de shēngrì.) |
| 蛋糕 | dàngāo | cake | 这个生日蛋糕很好吃。(Zhège shēngrì dàngāo hěn hǎochī.) |
| 蜡烛 | làzhú | candle | 蛋糕上有十八根蜡烛。(Dàngāo shàng yǒu shíbā gēn làzhú.) |
| 礼物 | lǐwù | gift, present | 你的生日礼物是什么？(Nǐ de shēngrì lǐwù shì shénme?) |
| 聚会 | jùhuì | party, gathering | 我们办一个生日聚会吧。(Wǒmen bàn yí ge shēngrì jùhuì ba.) |
| 祝贺 | zhùhè | congratulate | 祝贺你！(Zhùhè nǐ!) |
| 快乐 | kuàilè | happy, joyful | 祝你生日快乐！(Zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!) |
| 岁 | suì | years old (age) | 你今年多大岁数？(Nǐ jīnnián duō dà suìshu?) |
| 请客 | qǐngkè | treat (pay for someone) | 今天我生日，我请客。(Jīntiān wǒ shēngrì, wǒ qǐngkè.) |
| 惊喜 | jīngxǐ | surprise | 朋友们给了我一个惊喜。(Péngyoumen gěi le wǒ yí ge jīngxǐ.) |

## Example Sentences

- 祝你生日快乐！这是给你的礼物。(Zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè! Zhè shì gěi nǐ de lǐwù.) — Happy birthday! This is a gift for you.
- 我们给她办了一个惊喜聚会。(Wǒmen gěi tā bàn le yí ge jīngxǐ jùhuì.) — We threw her a surprise party.
- 在中国，过生日的人要请客吃饭。(Zài Zhōngguó, guò shēngrì de rén yào qǐngkè chīfàn.) — In China, the birthday person treats everyone to a meal.

## Usage Notes

An important cultural difference: in China the birthday person often 请客 (treats others to a meal), rather than others paying for the birthday person as is common in Western cultures. 祝 (zhù) is a verb meaning "to wish" and appears in many blessings: 祝你生日快乐 (happy birthday), 祝你新年快乐 (happy new year), 祝你身体健康 (wishing you good health). 岁 is the measure word for age — 我二十岁 (I'm 20 years old). For young children, you ask 你几岁了？ For adults, 你多大了？ is more common.
