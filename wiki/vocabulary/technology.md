# Technology 科技

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 手机 | shǒujī | cell phone | 我的手机没电了。 |
| 电脑 | diànnǎo | computer | 我用电脑工作。 |
| 上网 | shàngwǎng | go online | 你每天上网几个小时？ |
| 发短信 | fā duǎnxìn | send a text message | 我给他发了一条短信。 |
| 打电话 | dǎ diànhuà | make a phone call | 我给你打电话。 |
| 电子邮件 | diànzǐ yóujiàn | email | 请给我发一封电子邮件。 |
| 密码 | mìmǎ | password | 我忘了密码。 |
| 网站 | wǎngzhàn | website | 这个网站很有用。 |
| 下载 | xiàzài | download | 我要下载一个软件。 |
| 搜索 | sōusuǒ | search | 你可以在网上搜索。 |

## Example Sentences

- 我的手机没电了，你能借我用一下你的电脑吗？(Wǒ de shǒujī méi diàn le, nǐ néng jiè wǒ yòng yíxià nǐ de diànnǎo ma?) — My phone is out of battery, can you lend me your computer?
- 他每天上网看新闻，然后给朋友发短信。(Tā měitiān shàngwǎng kàn xīnwén, ránhòu gěi péngyou fā duǎnxìn.) — He goes online every day to read the news, then texts his friends.
- 我忘了密码，没办法下载那个软件。(Wǒ wàng le mìmǎ, méi bànfǎ xiàzài nàge ruǎnjiàn.) — I forgot my password, so I can't download that software.

## Usage Notes

Many technology terms in Chinese are descriptive compounds: 手机 (hand + machine), 电脑 (electric + brain), 电子邮件 (electronic + mail). 电子邮件 is the full formal term for email; in casual speech people often shorten it to 邮件 (yóujiàn) or use the English loanword 伊妹儿 (yīmèir). 打电话 and 发短信 are verb-object compounds, so modifiers go between the verb and object: 打了一个电话 (made a phone call), 发了一条短信 (sent a text). The measure word for text messages and news items is 条 (tiáo), for emails it is 封 (fēng), and for phone calls it is 个 (gè). 下载 (download) pairs with 上传 (shàngchuán, upload).
