# Food & Drink 饮食

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 米饭 | mǐfàn | cooked rice | 我每天都吃米饭。 |
| 面条 | miàntiáo | noodles | 这碗面条很好吃。 |
| 水 | shuǐ | water | 请给我一杯水。 |
| 茶 | chá | tea | 你想喝茶吗？ |
| 咖啡 | kāfēi | coffee | 我早上喜欢喝咖啡。 |
| 菜 | cài | vegetable; dish | 今天的菜很新鲜。 |
| 肉 | ròu | meat | 我不太喜欢吃肉。 |
| 水果 | shuǐguǒ | fruit | 多吃水果对身体好。 |
| 鸡蛋 | jīdàn | egg | 早饭我吃了两个鸡蛋。 |
| 面包 | miànbāo | bread | 这个面包是刚烤的。 |
| 牛奶 | niúnǎi | milk | 孩子们每天喝牛奶。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你想吃米饭还是面条？(Nǐ xiǎng chī mǐfàn háishi miàntiáo?) — Do you want to eat rice or noodles?
- 我要一杯茶，不要咖啡。(Wǒ yào yī bēi chá, bú yào kāfēi.) — I want a cup of tea, not coffee.

## Usage Notes

菜 has a double meaning: it can mean "vegetable" as an ingredient or "dish" as in a prepared item on a menu. When ordering at a restaurant, 点菜 (diǎn cài) means "to order dishes." The measure word for drinks is 杯 (bēi, cup/glass) or 瓶 (píng, bottle). Rice as a grain or uncooked is 大米 (dàmǐ), while 米饭 specifically refers to cooked rice ready to eat.
