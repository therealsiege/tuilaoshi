# Airport 机场

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 飞机 | fēijī | airplane | 我坐飞机去北京。(Wǒ zuò fēijī qù Běijīng.) |
| 机场 | jīchǎng | airport | 机场离市中心很远。(Jīchǎng lí shì zhōngxīn hěn yuǎn.) |
| 登机牌 | dēngjīpái | boarding pass | 请出示你的登机牌。(Qǐng chūshì nǐ de dēngjīpái.) |
| 行李 | xíngli | luggage, baggage | 你的行李太重了。(Nǐ de xíngli tài zhòng le.) |
| 护照 | hùzhào | passport | 出国要带护照。(Chūguó yào dài hùzhào.) |
| 签证 | qiānzhèng | visa | 去中国需要办签证。(Qù Zhōngguó xūyào bàn qiānzhèng.) |
| 安检 | ānjiǎn | security check | 过安检的时候不能带水。(Guò ānjiǎn de shíhou bù néng dài shuǐ.) |
| 起飞 | qǐfēi | take off (of a plane) | 飞机几点起飞？(Fēijī jǐ diǎn qǐfēi?) |
| 降落 | jiàngluò | land (of a plane) | 飞机已经安全降落了。(Fēijī yǐjīng ānquán jiàngluò le.) |
| 航班 | hángbān | flight (scheduled) | 我的航班是下午三点的。(Wǒ de hángbān shì xiàwǔ sān diǎn de.) |
| 转机 | zhuǎnjī | transfer flights, layover | 这个航班要在上海转机。(Zhège hángbān yào zài Shànghǎi zhuǎnjī.) |

## Example Sentences

- 请问，去机场怎么走？我的航班下午两点起飞。(Qǐngwèn, qù jīchǎng zěnme zǒu? Wǒ de hángbān xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn qǐfēi.) — Excuse me, how do I get to the airport? My flight takes off at 2 PM.
- 过安检以前，请把护照和登机牌准备好。(Guò ānjiǎn yǐqián, qǐng bǎ hùzhào hé dēngjīpái zhǔnbèi hǎo.) — Before going through security, please have your passport and boarding pass ready.
- 我不喜欢转机，太麻烦了，我要直飞的航班。(Wǒ bù xǐhuan zhuǎnjī, tài máfan le, wǒ yào zhífēi de hángbān.) — I don't like layovers, they're too much trouble. I want a direct flight.

## Usage Notes

飞机 literally means "fly machine." 机场 means "machine field/yard" — 机 here is short for 飞机. Many airport words use 机: 登机 (board a plane), 转机 (transfer planes), 登机牌 (boarding pass), 登机口 (boarding gate). 坐飞机 (sit airplane) is the standard way to say "take a flight" — Chinese uses 坐 (sit) for most vehicles: 坐火车, 坐公共汽车, 坐地铁. 办签证 uses 办 (handle/process), the verb for dealing with official paperwork and procedures.
