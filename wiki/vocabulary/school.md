# School 学校

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 学校 | xuéxiào | school | 我的学校很大。 |
| 教室 | jiàoshì | classroom | 教室里有三十个学生。 |
| 课 | kè | class; lesson | 今天有四节课。 |
| 考试 | kǎoshì | exam | 明天有一个考试。 |
| 作业 | zuòyè | homework | 我还没做完作业。 |
| 图书馆 | túshūguǎn | library | 我喜欢在图书馆学习。 |
| 老师 | lǎoshī | teacher | 王老师教我们中文。 |
| 同学 | tóngxué | classmate | 我和同学一起吃午饭。 |
| 学生 | xuéshēng | student | 这个学校有两千个学生。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你在哪个学校上学？(Nǐ zài nǎge xuéxiào shàng xué?) — Which school do you attend?
- 老师说明天不用交作业。(Lǎoshī shuō míngtiān bú yòng jiāo zuòyè.) — The teacher said we don't need to turn in homework tomorrow.

## Usage Notes

老师 is also used as a respectful form of address, not just a job title. You can call someone 王老师 (Teacher Wang) even outside a classroom setting as a sign of respect. The measure word for classes/lessons is 节 (jié): 一节课 (one class period). 同学 literally means "same study" and refers to classmates, but it is also used broadly to address students in a group, similar to how a teacher might say "class" in English. Chinese students typically say 上课 (shàng kè, attend class) and 下课 (xià kè, finish class).
