# Online Shopping 网上购物

|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 网购 | wǎnggòu | online shopping | 现在年轻人都喜欢网购。 |

## Example Sentences

- 双十一的时候，大家都在网上抢购打折商品。(Shuāng shíyī de shíhou, dàjiā dōu zài wǎng shàng qiǎnggòu dǎzhé shāngpǐn.) — During Singles' Day, everyone rushes to buy discounted products online.
- 这个快递三天了还没到，我打电话问问。(Zhège kuàidì sān tiān le hái méi dào, wǒ dǎ diànhuà wènwen.) — This package hasn't arrived after three days; I'll call and ask.

## Usage Notes

打折 literally means "hit/strike a discount." The discount is expressed as a fraction of 10: 打八折 means 80% of the original price (a 20% discount). 优惠 is a broader term for any type of favorable pricing or promotion. 网购 is a contracted form of 网上购物 and is very common in spoken Chinese.
