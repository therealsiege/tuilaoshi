# Chinese Geography 中国地理

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 省 | shěng | province | 中国一共有二十三个省。 |
| 城市 | chéngshì | city | 上海是中国最大的城市之一。 |
| 首都 | shǒudū | capital (city) | 北京是中国的首都。 |
| 北京 | Běijīng | Beijing | 我去过北京两次，参观了故宫。 |

## Example Sentences

- 中国地理非常丰富，东边有大海，西边有高山和沙漠。(Zhōngguó dìlǐ fēicháng fēngfù, dōngbian yǒu dàhǎi, xībian yǒu gāoshān hé shāmò.) — China's geography is very diverse, with the ocean to the east and high mountains and deserts to the west.
- 很多外国人来中国旅游的时候，第一站就是北京，因为那里有长城和故宫。(Hěn duō wàiguó rén lái Zhōngguó lǚyóu de shíhou, dì yī zhàn jiùshì Běijīng, yīnwèi nàli yǒu Chángchéng hé Gùgōng.) — When many foreigners visit China, their first stop is Beijing because of the Great Wall and the Forbidden City.

## Usage Notes

不到长城非好汉 (bú dào Chángchéng fēi hǎohàn) is a famous saying meaning "One who has not reached the Great Wall is not a true hero" — used to express that you must persevere to achieve a goal. 长江 and 黄河 are called 母亲河 (mǔqīn hé, "mother rivers") of Chinese civilization. Note that 长 in 长城 and 长江 is cháng (second tone, "long"), not zhǎng.
