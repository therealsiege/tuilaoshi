# Appointments 约时间

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 约 | yuē | to make an appointment, arrange | 我们约个时间见面吧。(Wǒmen yuē ge shíjiān jiànmiàn ba.) |
| 见面 | jiànmiàn | meet, see each other | 很高兴见到你！(Hěn gāoxìng jiàndào nǐ!) |
| 方便 | fāngbiàn | convenient | 你什么时候方便？(Nǐ shénme shíhou fāngbiàn?) |
| 有空 | yǒu kòng | have free time | 你明天有空吗？(Nǐ míngtiān yǒu kòng ma?) |
| 时候 | shíhou | time, moment | 你什么时候来？(Nǐ shénme shíhou lái?) |
| 迟到 | chídào | be late, arrive late | 对不起，我迟到了。(Duìbuqǐ, wǒ chídào le.) |
| 准时 | zhǔnshí | on time, punctual | 他每次都很准时。(Tā měi cì dōu hěn zhǔnshí.) |
| 改天 | gǎitiān | another day, some other time | 今天没空，改天吧。(Jīntiān méi kòng, gǎitiān ba.) |
| 取消 | qǔxiāo | cancel | 明天的会议取消了。(Míngtiān de huìyì qǔxiāo le.) |
| 安排 | ānpái | arrange, schedule | 我来安排时间。(Wǒ lái ānpái shíjiān.) |

## Example Sentences

- 你明天有空吗？我们约个时间喝咖啡吧。(Nǐ míngtiān yǒu kòng ma? Wǒmen yuē ge shíjiān hē kāfēi ba.) — Are you free tomorrow? Let's set a time to get coffee.
- 不好意思，我今天不方便，改天可以吗？(Bù hǎoyìsi, wǒ jīntiān bù fāngbiàn, gǎitiān kěyǐ ma?) — Sorry, today isn't convenient for me. Can we do another day?
- 请准时到，不要迟到。(Qǐng zhǔnshí dào, bú yào chídào.) — Please arrive on time, don't be late.

## Usage Notes

约 can function as both a verb (to arrange/invite) and part of compound words like 约会 (date) and 约定 (agreement). 有空 and 方便 are both used to ask about someone's availability, but 方便 is slightly more formal and can also imply logistical convenience (location, situation), not just free time. 改天 is a polite way to postpone without committing to a specific new date — it softens a refusal. 见面 is a verb-object compound, so modifiers go between: 见过面 (have met before), 见了一次面 (met once).
