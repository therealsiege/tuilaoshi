# Colors 颜色

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 红 | hóng | red | 她穿了一件红色的裙子。 |
| 蓝 | lán | blue | 今天的天很蓝。 |
| 绿 | lǜ | green | 春天树叶都变绿了。 |
| 黄 | huáng | yellow | 秋天的叶子是黄色的。 |
| 白 | bái | white | 他喜欢穿白衬衫。 |

## Example Sentences

- 你最喜欢什么颜色？(Nǐ zuì xǐhuan shénme yánsè?) — What is your favorite color?
- 红色在中国代表好运。(Hóng sè zài Zhōngguó dàibiǎo hǎo yùn.) — Red represents good luck in China.

## Usage Notes

Colors can function as adjectives directly (红花, red flower) or with the suffix 色 (sè) to form the noun form of the color (红色, the color red). In casual speech, the single-character form is common as a modifier, while 色 is added when talking about colors as concepts. Red (红) is the most auspicious color in Chinese culture, associated with luck, happiness, and celebration. White (白) and black (黑) are associated with mourning and funerals. Yellow (黄) was historically the imperial color, reserved for the emperor.
