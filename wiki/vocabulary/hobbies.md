# Hobbies 爱好

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 爱好 | àihào | hobby, interest | 你有什么爱好？(Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào?) |
| 打球 | dǎqiú | play ball (sports) | 我周末常常去打球。(Wǒ zhōumò chángcháng qù dǎqiú.) |
| 游泳 | yóuyǒng | swim | 夏天游泳很舒服。(Xiàtiān yóuyǒng hěn shūfu.) |
| 唱歌 | chànggē | sing | 他很喜欢唱歌。(Tā hěn xǐhuan chànggē.) |
| 跳舞 | tiàowǔ | dance | 她每天晚上去跳舞。(Tā měitiān wǎnshang qù tiàowǔ.) |
| 画画 | huàhuà | draw, paint | 我小时候很喜欢画画。(Wǒ xiǎoshíhou hěn xǐhuan huàhuà.) |
| 弹钢琴 | tán gāngqín | play piano | 妹妹弹钢琴弹得很好。(Mèimei tán gāngqín tán de hěn hǎo.) |
| 下棋 | xiàqí | play chess | 爷爷每天下午下棋。(Yéye měitiān xiàwǔ xiàqí.) |
| 看电影 | kàn diànyǐng | watch a movie | 你想不想去看电影？(Nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng qù kàn diànyǐng?) |
| 听音乐 | tīng yīnyuè | listen to music | 我做作业的时候喜欢听音乐。(Wǒ zuò zuòyè de shíhou xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè.) |
| 运动 | yùndòng | exercise, sports | 多运动对身体好。(Duō yùndòng duì shēntǐ hǎo.) |

## Example Sentences

- 你的爱好是什么？我喜欢下棋和听音乐。(Nǐ de àihào shì shénme? Wǒ xǐhuan xiàqí hé tīng yīnyuè.) — What are your hobbies? I like playing chess and listening to music.
- 我们下课以后去打球吧！(Wǒmen xiàkè yǐhòu qù dǎqiú ba!) — Let's go play ball after class!
- 他有很多爱好，比如游泳、画画和弹钢琴。(Tā yǒu hěn duō àihào, bǐrú yóuyǒng, huàhuà hé tán gāngqín.) — He has many hobbies, such as swimming, painting, and playing piano.

## Usage Notes

This page focuses on IC1 Lesson 4 hobby vocabulary. For additional sports and activity verbs with detailed verb-pairing notes (打 vs 踢 vs 弹 vs 拉), see [[sports-hobbies]]. 下棋 (xiàqí) literally means "go down chess" — 下 here means "to play" specifically for board games. 运动 can function as both a noun (exercise/sports) and a verb (to exercise).
