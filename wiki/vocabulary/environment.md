## Example Sentences

- 为了保护环境，很多城市开始实行垃圾分类。(Wèile bǎohù huánjìng, hěn duō chéngshì kāishǐ shíxíng lājī fēnlèi.) — To protect the environment, many cities have begun implementing garbage sorting.
- 如果每个人都能节约一点资源，减少一点浪费，环境就会好很多。(Rúguǒ měi ge rén dōu néng jiéyuē yìdiǎn zīyuán, jiǎnshǎo yìdiǎn làngfèi, huánjìng jiù huì hǎo hěn duō.) — If everyone could conserve a few resources and reduce a little waste, the environment would improve a lot.

## Usage Notes

垃圾 is pronounced lājī (not lèsè, which is a regional pronunciation used in some southern dialects). 垃圾分类 (lājī fēnlèi, "garbage sorting") became a major topic in China starting around 2019 when Shanghai introduced mandatory sorting rules. 节约 and 节省 (jiéshěng) are similar, but 节约 emphasizes conservation as a virtue, while 节省 focuses more on saving money or resources practically.
