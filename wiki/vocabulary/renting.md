# Renting 租房

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 租 | zū | rent | 我想租一套公寓。(Wǒ xiǎng zū yí tào gōngyù.) |
| 房子 | fángzi | house | 这套房子很大。(Zhè tào fángzi hěn dà.) |
| 公寓 | gōngyù | apartment | 学校旁边有很多公寓。(Xuéxiào pángbiān yǒu hěn duō gōngyù.) |
| 卧室 | wòshì | bedroom | 这套公寓有两个卧室。(Zhè tào gōngyù yǒu liǎng ge wòshì.) |
| 客厅 | kètīng | living room | 客厅很亮，也很大。(Kètīng hěn liàng, yě hěn dà.) |
| 厨房 | chúfáng | kitchen | 厨房里有冰箱和炉子。(Chúfáng lǐ yǒu bīngxiāng hé lúzi.) |
| 卫生间 | wèishēngjiān | bathroom, restroom | 卫生间在卧室旁边。(Wèishēngjiān zài wòshì pángbiān.) |
| 房租 | fángzū | rent (payment) | 房租一个月多少钱？(Fángzū yí ge yuè duōshao qián?) |
| 押金 | yājīn | deposit | 要付两个月的押金。(Yào fù liǎng ge yuè de yājīn.) |

| 房东 | fángdōng | landlord | 房东人很好。(Fángdōng rén hěn hǎo.) |

## Example Sentences

- 我想租一套两室一厅的公寓，房租不要太贵。(Wǒ xiǎng zū yí tào liǎng shì yì tīng de gōngyù, fángzū bú yào tài guì.) — I want to rent a two-bedroom, one-living-room apartment; the rent shouldn't be too expensive.
- 签合同以前，先问房东押金是多少。(Qiān hétong yǐqián, xiān wèn fángdōng yājīn shì duōshao.) — Before signing the lease, first ask the landlord how much the deposit is.
- 搬家很麻烦，东西太多了。(Bānjiā hěn máfan, dōngxi tài duō le.) — Moving is such a hassle; there's too much stuff.

## Usage Notes

Chinese apartment listings use a shorthand format: 两室一厅 (liǎng shì yì tīng) means two bedrooms and one living room, 一室一厅 means one bedroom and one living room. 租 can mean both "to rent from someone" and "to rent to someone" — context clarifies the direction. The measure word for houses and apartments is 套 (tào, a set/suite): 一套房子, 一套公寓. 房东 literally means "house-east" — historically the host sat facing east, the position of honor.
