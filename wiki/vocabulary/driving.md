## Example Sentences

- 在中国考驾照要先学理论，再练车，最后路考。(Zài Zhōngguó kǎo jiàzhào yào xiān xué lǐlùn, zài liànchē, zuìhòu lùkǎo.) — In China, getting a driver's license requires first studying theory, then practicing driving, and finally a road test.
- 下班的时候路上堵车堵得厉害，我花了两个小时才到家。(Xiàbān de shíhou lù shàng dǔchē dǔ de lìhai, wǒ huāle liǎng ge xiǎoshí cái dào jiā.) — Traffic was terrible during the evening commute; it took me two hours to get home.

## Usage Notes

加油 has a dual meaning: literally "to add fuel/gas" and figuratively "come on! / keep it up!" as encouragement. 堵车 uses 堵 (to block/clog) — the image is of vehicles being jammed together. In China, 高速公路 are toll roads (收费公路), and drivers pay at toll booths (收费站).
