# Dating 约会

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 约会 | yuēhuì | date, appointment | 他们今天晚上有个约会。(Tāmen jīntiān wǎnshang yǒu ge yuēhuì.) |
| 喜欢 | xǐhuan | like | 我很喜欢跟你在一起。(Wǒ hěn xǐhuan gēn nǐ zài yìqǐ.) |
| 爱 | ài | love | 我爱你。(Wǒ ài nǐ.) |
| 帅 | shuài | handsome | 她觉得那个男生很帅。(Tā juéde nàge nánshēng hěn shuài.) |
| 漂亮 | piàoliang | pretty, beautiful | 你今天穿得很漂亮。(Nǐ jīntiān chuān de hěn piàoliang.) |
| 温柔 | wēnróu | gentle, tender | 她是一个很温柔的人。(Tā shì yí ge hěn wēnróu de rén.) |
| 浪漫 | làngmàn | romantic | 他准备了一个浪漫的晚餐。(Tā zhǔnbèi le yí ge làngmàn de wǎncān.) |
| 电影院 | diànyǐngyuàn | movie theater | 我们去电影院看电影吧。(Wǒmen qù diànyǐngyuàn kàn diànyǐng ba.) |
| 散步 | sànbù | take a walk, stroll | 我们去公园散步吧。(Wǒmen qù gōngyuán sànbù ba.) |
| 牵手 | qiānshǒu | hold hands | 他们牵着手散步。(Tāmen qiānzhe shǒu sànbù.) |

## Example Sentences

- 你想跟我去看电影吗？算是一个约会。(Nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ qù kàn diànyǐng ma? Suàn shì yí ge yuēhuì.) — Do you want to go see a movie with me? Consider it a date.
- 他很帅，也很温柔，很多女生都喜欢他。(Tā hěn shuài, yě hěn wēnróu, hěn duō nǚshēng dōu xǐhuan tā.) — He's handsome and gentle; many girls like him.
- 晚饭以后我们去河边散散步吧。(Wǎnfàn yǐhòu wǒmen qù hébiān sànsanbù ba.) — Let's take a walk by the river after dinner.

## Usage Notes

喜欢 (like) and 爱 (love) differ in intensity, similar to English. In Chinese, saying 我爱你 is considered very serious and is not used as casually as "I love you" in some Western contexts. 帅 is used for men and 漂亮 primarily for women, though 漂亮 can also describe things (漂亮的花, beautiful flowers). 散步 is a verb-object compound — the reduplicated form 散散步 softens it into a casual suggestion. 牵手 literally means "pull hand" — 牵着手 uses the progressive aspect marker 着 to describe the ongoing action of holding hands.
