# Growing Up 长大

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 小时候 | xiǎoshíhou | when one was young | 小时候我最喜欢吃冰淇淋。 |
| 长大 | zhǎngdà | to grow up | 我在一个小城市长大。 |
| 记得 | jìde | to remember | 你还记得小学的老师吗？ |

## Example Sentences

- 小时候放暑假的时候，我常常去爷爷奶奶家玩。(Xiǎoshíhou fàng shǔjià de shíhou, wǒ chángcháng qù yéye nǎinai jiā wán.) — When I was young, during summer vacation I often went to my grandparents' house to play.
- 他从小学到高中都在同一个城市上的学，大学才去了北京。(Tā cóng xiǎoxué dào gāozhōng dōu zài tóng yí ge chéngshì shàng de xué, dàxué cái qùle Běijīng.) — He went to school in the same city from elementary through high school, and only went to Beijing for college.

## Usage Notes

长大 uses the tone zhǎng (third tone), meaning "to grow," not cháng (second tone, meaning "long"). 小时候 is a time expression meaning "when [one] was little" and is placed at the beginning of a sentence or clause. The Chinese school system follows 6+3+3: six years of 小学, three years of 初中 (together called 义务教育, compulsory education), and three years of 高中.
