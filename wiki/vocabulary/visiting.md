# Visiting 做客

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 请进 | qǐng jìn | please come in | 请进，请进！(Qǐng jìn, qǐng jìn!) |
| 坐 | zuò | sit, have a seat | 请坐，别站着。(Qǐng zuò, bié zhànzhe.) |
| 喝茶 | hē chá | drink tea | 来，喝茶吧。(Lái, hē chá ba.) |
| 客气 | kèqi | polite, courteous | 你太客气了！(Nǐ tài kèqi le!) |
| 礼物 | lǐwù | gift, present | 这是送给你的礼物。(Zhè shì sòng gěi nǐ de lǐwù.) |
| 水果 | shuǐguǒ | fruit | 我带了一些水果来。(Wǒ dài le yìxiē shuǐguǒ lái.) |
| 欢迎 | huānyíng | welcome | 欢迎你来我家！(Huānyíng nǐ lái wǒ jiā!) |
| 打扰 | dǎrǎo | disturb, bother | 不好意思，打扰你了。(Bù hǎoyìsi, dǎrǎo nǐ le.) |
| 做客 | zuòkè | be a guest, visit | 今天去朋友家做客。(Jīntiān qù péngyou jiā zuòkè.) |
| 招待 | zhāodài | host, entertain | 谢谢你的招待！(Xièxie nǐ de zhāodài!) |

## Example Sentences

- 欢迎欢迎！请进，请坐！(Huānyíng huānyíng! Qǐng jìn, qǐng zuò!) — Welcome! Please come in, please have a seat!
- 你太客气了，还带了礼物。(Nǐ tài kèqi le, hái dài le lǐwù.) — You're too polite, you even brought a gift.
- 去别人家做客的时候应该带一点水果。(Qù biéren jiā zuòkè de shíhou yīnggāi dài yìdiǎn shuǐguǒ.) — When visiting someone's home you should bring some fruit.

## Usage Notes

In Chinese culture, bringing a small gift (水果, tea, snacks) when visiting someone's home is customary. The host will typically say 你太客气了 (you're too polite) when receiving gifts. The phrase 别客气 (bié kèqi, don't be polite) or 不用客气 (bú yòng kèqi) is the host's way of saying "make yourself at home." 做客 is a verb-object compound meaning to be someone's guest — it emphasizes the guest's role, while 招待 emphasizes the host's role.
