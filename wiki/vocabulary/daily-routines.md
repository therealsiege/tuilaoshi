# Daily Routines 日常生活

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning | Example |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 起床 | qǐchuáng | get up | 我每天六点起床。 |
| 刷牙 | shuā yá | brush teeth | 早上起床先刷牙。 |
| 洗脸 | xǐ liǎn | wash face | 刷牙以后洗脸。 |
| 吃早饭 | chī zǎofàn | eat breakfast | 你今天吃早饭了吗？ |
| 上班 | shàng bān | go to work | 他八点上班。 |
| 下班 | xià bān | get off work | 她五点下班。 |
| 睡觉 | shuìjiào | sleep, go to bed | 我十一点睡觉。 |
| 洗澡 | xǐzǎo | take a bath/shower | 我每天晚上洗澡。 |
| 做饭 | zuò fàn | cook | 妈妈在做饭。 |
| 休息 | xiūxi | rest | 你累了就休息一下。 |

## Example Sentences

- 我每天早上七点起床，然后刷牙洗脸。(Wǒ měitiān zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng, ránhòu shuā yá xǐ liǎn.) — I get up at 7 every morning, then brush my teeth and wash my face.
- 他下班以后喜欢在家做饭。(Tā xià bān yǐhòu xǐhuan zài jiā zuò fàn.) — After getting off work, he likes to cook at home.
- 周末的时候我喜欢多休息。(Zhōumò de shíhou wǒ xǐhuan duō xiūxi.) — On weekends I like to rest more.

## Usage Notes

Many daily routine words in Chinese are verb-object compounds (离合词, líhécí). For example, 睡觉 is composed of 睡 (sleep) + 觉 (a nap/sleep), and 洗澡 is 洗 (wash) + 澡 (bath). This matters because aspect particles like 了 or duration complements insert between the verb and object: 我睡了一个小时的觉 (I slept for one hour), not 我睡觉了一个小时. Similarly, 上班 and 下班 use 上/下 as directional complements with 班 (shift/work), so "going to work" does not require an additional verb of motion.
