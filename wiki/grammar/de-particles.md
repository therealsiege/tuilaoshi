# 的/地/得 Particles

- **Pattern**: Modifier + 的 + Noun / Adverb + 地 + Verb / Verb + 得 + Complement
- **Structure**: 的 (noun modifier), 地 (adverb marker), 得 (complement marker)

## Explanation

Chinese has three structural particles all pronounced "de" but written with different characters. Each one connects a different type of modifier to a different part of speech:

**的 (de)** — Links a modifier to a noun. Think of it as "'s" or a relative clause marker. The modifier can be an adjective, a noun (possession), a verb phrase, or a clause.

**地 (de)** — Links an adverb or adverbial phrase to a verb. It marks HOW an action is performed. It appears between the descriptive word and the verb.

**得 (de)** — Links a verb or adjective to a complement that describes the degree or result. It appears AFTER the verb and introduces a description of how well, how much, or to what extent.

A helpful mnemonic: 的 points right to a noun, 地 points right to a verb, 得 points left back to a verb (describing its result).

## Example Sentences

### 的 (noun modifier)
- 我的书 (wǒ de shū) — my book
- 漂亮的花 (piàoliang de huā) — beautiful flowers
- 他写的文章很好。(Tā xiě de wénzhāng hěn hǎo.) — The article he wrote is very good.

### 地 (adverb marker)
- 她高兴地笑了。(Tā gāoxìng de xiào le.) — She laughed happily.
- 他认真地学习。(Tā rènzhēn de xuéxí.) — He studies seriously.
- 请慢慢地走。(Qǐng mànmàn de zǒu.) — Please walk slowly.

### 得 (complement marker)
- 他跑得很快。(Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.) — He runs very fast.
- 她说中文说得很好。(Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.) — She speaks Chinese very well.
- 你写得不错。(Nǐ xiě de búcuò.) — You write quite well.

## Common Errors

- Mixing up 的 and 得: ✗ 他跑的很快。 ✓ 他跑得很快。(Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.) — After a verb for degree/result, use 得.
- Mixing up 的 and 地: ✗ 她高兴的笑了。 ✓ 她高兴地笑了。(Tā gāoxìng de xiào le.) — Before a verb for manner, use 地.
- Omitting 得 in complement constructions: ✗ 他跑很快。 ✓ 他跑得很快。
