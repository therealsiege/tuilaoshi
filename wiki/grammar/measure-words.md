# Common Measure Words 量词

- **Pattern**: Number + Measure Word + Noun
- **Structure**: Num + MW + N

## Explanation

Chinese requires a measure word (量词 liàngcí, also called a classifier) between a number or demonstrative (这/那) and a noun. English does this sometimes ("a piece of paper," "a cup of tea"), but Chinese does it for everything. Each noun has a specific measure word based on the shape, type, or category of the object. When in doubt, 个 (gè) is the most general-purpose measure word, but using the correct one sounds more natural and precise.

### Common Measure Words

| Measure Word | Pinyin | Used For | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 个 | gè | general / people / objects | 一个人 (yí gè rén) — one person |
| 本 | běn | books, volumes | 两本书 (liǎng běn shū) — two books |
| 杯 | bēi | cups, glasses of liquid | 一杯咖啡 (yì bēi kāfēi) — a cup of coffee |
| 只 | zhī | small animals | 三只猫 (sān zhī māo) — three cats |
| 张 | zhāng | flat objects (paper, tables, tickets) | 一张纸 (yì zhāng zhǐ) — a piece of paper |
| 件 | jiàn | clothing, matters, luggage | 一件衣服 (yí jiàn yīfu) — a piece of clothing |
| 条 | tiáo | long/thin objects (roads, fish, pants) | 一条路 (yì tiáo lù) — a road |
| 位 | wèi | people (polite/formal) | 一位老师 (yí wèi lǎoshī) — a teacher (respectful) |

## Example Sentences

- 我要两杯水。(Wǒ yào liǎng bēi shuǐ.) — I want two glasses of water.
- 桌子上有三本书。(Zhuōzi shàng yǒu sān běn shū.) — There are three books on the table.
- 她有一只小狗。(Tā yǒu yì zhī xiǎo gǒu.) — She has a puppy.
- 请给我一张票。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yì zhāng piào.) — Please give me a ticket.
- 那位先生是我爸爸。(Nà wèi xiānshēng shì wǒ bàba.) — That gentleman is my father.

## Common Errors

- Omitting the measure word: ✗ 一书 ✓ 一本书 (yì běn shū)
- Using 个 for everything: ✗ 一个书 ✓ 一本书 — 个 works as a fallback, but specific measure words are preferred.
- Confusing 只 and 条 for animals: ✗ 一条猫 ✓ 一只猫 (yì zhī māo) — 只 for small animals, 条 for long ones like fish or snakes.
