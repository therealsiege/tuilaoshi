# 不但...而且 (Not Only...But Also — Progressive Compound Sentences)

- **Pattern**: 不但 + A, 而且 + B
- **Structure**: 不但 (bùdàn) introduces the first (lesser) point; 而且 (érqiě) introduces the second (greater or additional) point

## Explanation

不但...而且 expresses a progressive or additive relationship: "not only A, but also B." The second clause introduced by 而且 adds information that goes further than the first — it represents a stronger claim, a higher degree, or an additional fact that reinforces the first.

When both clauses share the same subject, the subject comes before 不但. When the two clauses have different subjects, 不但 comes before its own clause's subject, and the second subject appears after 而且. This subject-placement rule mirrors 虽然...但是.

而且 can sometimes be replaced by 还 (hái, "also, in addition") in more casual speech, especially when the second clause simply adds information rather than escalating.

## Example Sentences

- 他不但会说中文，而且会说日文。(Tā bùdàn huì shuō Zhōngwén, érqiě huì shuō Rìwén.) — He can speak not only Chinese but also Japanese.
- 这个地方不但漂亮，而且很安静。(Zhège dìfang bùdàn piàoliang, érqiě hěn ānjìng.) — This place is not only beautiful but also very quiet.
- 不但我喜欢这部电影，而且我朋友也喜欢。(Bùdàn wǒ xǐhuan zhè bù diànyǐng, érqiě wǒ péngyou yě xǐhuan.) — Not only do I like this movie, but my friend likes it too.
- 她不但学习好，而且体育也很好。(Tā bùdàn xuéxí hǎo, érqiě tǐyù yě hěn hǎo.) — She is not only good at studying but also good at sports.

## Negation

Negation goes inside the individual clauses:
- 他不但不生气，而且还笑了。(Tā bùdàn bù shēngqì, érqiě hái xiào le.) — Not only was he not angry, he even laughed.
- 她不但没迟到，而且还早到了十分钟。(Tā bùdàn méi chídào, érqiě hái zǎo dào le shí fēnzhōng.) — Not only was she not late, she even arrived ten minutes early.

## Common Errors

- Reversing the escalation (putting the stronger point first): ✗ 他不但会说五种语言，而且会说中文。 ✓ 他不但会说中文，而且会说五种语言。(Tā bùdàn huì shuō Zhōngwén, érqiě huì shuō wǔ zhǒng yǔyán.) — He not only speaks Chinese but speaks five languages.
- Wrong subject placement with different subjects: ✗ 他不但喜欢，而且我也喜欢。 ✓ 不但他喜欢，而且我也喜欢。(Bùdàn tā xǐhuan, érqiě wǒ yě xǐhuan.) — Not only does he like it, but I like it too.
