# 是...的 Construction (Emphasizing When/Where/How of Past Actions)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 是 + Time/Place/Manner + Verb + 的
- **Structure**: 是 (shì) and 的 (de) frame the element being emphasized; the action is already known to have happened

## Explanation

The 是...的 construction is used when a past action is already established as fact, and the speaker wants to highlight a specific detail about it — typically WHEN it happened, WHERE it happened, HOW it happened, or WHO did it. The action itself is not new information; the emphasized detail is.

是 is placed before the detail being stressed, and 的 comes at the end of the sentence (or before the object in some structures). 是 can sometimes be omitted in casual speech, but 的 is always kept.

When there is an object, 的 can appear either after the verb (before the object) or at the very end of the sentence. Both positions are acceptable:
- 我是在北京学的中文。 (的 before object)
- 我是在北京学中文的。 (的 at end)

## Example Sentences

- 你是什么时候来的？(Nǐ shì shénme shíhou lái de?) — When did you come?
- 我是昨天到的。(Wǒ shì zuótiān dào de.) — I arrived yesterday.
- 他是坐飞机来的。(Tā shì zuò fēijī lái de.) — He came by plane.
- 我是在大学学的中文。(Wǒ shì zài dàxué xué de Zhōngwén.) — I studied Chinese at university. (Emphasizing where.)
- 这本书是我朋友送的。(Zhè běn shū shì wǒ péngyou sòng de.) — This book was given by my friend. (Emphasizing who.)

## Negation

Negate 是 with 不是 to deny the emphasized detail (not the action itself):
- 我不是昨天来的，是前天来的。(Wǒ bù shì zuótiān lái de, shì qiántiān lái de.) — I didn't come yesterday; I came the day before.
- 他不是坐飞机来的，是坐火车来的。(Tā bù shì zuò fēijī lái de, shì zuò huǒchē lái de.) — He didn't come by plane; he came by train.

## Common Errors

- Using 是...的 for actions that haven't happened yet (it's for past events only): ✗ 我是明天去的。 ✓ 我是昨天去的。(Wǒ shì zuótiān qù de.) — I went yesterday.
- Confusing 是...的 with the simple past 了 (了 reports a new action; 是...的 highlights a detail of a known action): ✗ 你是来了吗？ ✓ 你是什么时候来的？(Nǐ shì shénme shíhou lái de?) — When did you come?
- Omitting 的: ✗ 我是在北京学中文。 ✓ 我是在北京学的中文。(Wǒ shì zài Běijīng xué de Zhōngwén.) — I studied Chinese in Beijing.
