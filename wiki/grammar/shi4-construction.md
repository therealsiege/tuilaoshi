# 是 Construction (Copula)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 是 + Noun/Noun Phrase
- **Structure**: A 是 B

## Explanation

是 (shì) is the Chinese copula verb, similar to English "is/am/are." It links a subject to a noun or noun phrase. Unlike English, 是 is NOT used with adjectives — use 很 instead.

## Example Sentences

- 我是学生。(Wǒ shì xuéshēng.) — I am a student.
- 她是老师。(Tā shì lǎoshī.) — She is a teacher.
- 这是我的书。(Zhè shì wǒ de shū.) — This is my book.

## Negation

Negate with 不 (bù) before 是:
- 我不是美国人。(Wǒ bú shì Měiguó rén.) — I am not American.

## Common Errors

- Using 是 with adjectives: ✗ 他是高。 ✓ 他很高。(Tā hěn gāo.) — He is tall.
- Omitting 是 between nouns: ✗ 我学生。 ✓ 我是学生。
