# 会/能/可以 Modal Verbs

- **Pattern**: Subject + Modal Verb + Verb
- **Structure**: S + 会/能/可以 + V

## Explanation

Chinese has three main modal verbs that all translate to English "can," but each has distinct meanings:

**会 (huì)** — Learned ability or skill; also used for future likelihood. "Can" in the sense of knowing how to do something through learning. Also means "will" for predictions.
- 我会说中文。— I can (know how to) speak Chinese.
- 明天会下雨。— It will rain tomorrow.

**能 (néng)** — Innate ability, physical capacity, or circumstantial permission. "Can" in the sense of having the physical or situational ability to do something.
- 他能跑很快。— He can run fast (physical ability).
- 你今天能来吗？— Can you come today? (Are you able to?)

**可以 (kěyǐ)** — Permission or possibility. "Can" in the sense of being allowed to, or something being acceptable.
- 这里可以停车吗？— Can I park here? (Am I allowed to?)
- 你可以走了。— You may go now.

### Quick Comparison

| Modal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 会 | learned skill / will | 她会游泳。(She can swim — she learned.) |
| 能 | physical ability / circumstantial | 他受伤了，不能走路。(He's injured, can't walk.) |
| 可以 | permission / allowed | 可以进来吗？(May I come in?) |

## Example Sentences

- 你会做中国菜吗？(Nǐ huì zuò Zhōngguó cài ma?) — Can you cook Chinese food? (Do you know how?)
- 我今天不能去，我生病了。(Wǒ jīntiān bù néng qù, wǒ shēngbìng le.) — I can't go today, I'm sick.
- 这里不可以抽烟。(Zhèlǐ bù kěyǐ chōuyān.) — You can't smoke here. (Not allowed.)
- 她会弹钢琴。(Tā huì tán gāngqín.) — She can play the piano. (Learned skill.)

## Negation

All three are negated with 不:
- 我不会开车。(Wǒ bú huì kāi chē.) — I can't drive. (Never learned.)
- 他不能喝酒。(Tā bù néng hē jiǔ.) — He can't drink alcohol. (Not able to / not supposed to.)
- 你不可以在这里拍照。(Nǐ bù kěyǐ zài zhèlǐ pāizhào.) — You can't take photos here. (Not allowed.)

## Common Errors

- Using 会 for permission: ✗ 这里会停车吗？ ✓ 这里可以停车吗？(Zhèlǐ kěyǐ tíng chē ma?)
- Using 可以 for learned skills: ✗ 她可以说法语。(Implies permission.) ✓ 她会说法语。(Tā huì shuō Fǎyǔ.) — She can speak French.
- Using 能 for learned skills: ✗ 你能说中文吗？(Asks about physical/situational ability.) ✓ 你会说中文吗？(Nǐ huì shuō Zhōngwén ma?) — Can you speak Chinese? (Do you know how?)
