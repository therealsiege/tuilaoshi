# 越来越 (More and More — Progressive Change)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 越来越 + Adjective/Verb
- **Structure**: 越来越 (yuèláiyuè) modifies an adjective or verb to express increasing degree over time

## Explanation

越来越 expresses that something is progressively increasing in degree — "more and more." It is placed directly before an adjective or a psychological/stative verb. The construction emphasizes a trend or gradual change rather than a static comparison.

Do not confuse 越来越 with the related pattern 越 A 越 B (yuè A yuè B), which means "the more A, the more B" and links two changing qualities. 越来越 focuses on a single quality intensifying over time.

## Example Sentences

- 天气越来越冷了。(Tiānqì yuèláiyuè lěng le.) — The weather is getting colder and colder.
- 他的中文越来越好了。(Tā de Zhōngwén yuèláiyuè hǎo le.) — His Chinese is getting better and better.
- 城市里的人越来越多。(Chéngshì lǐ de rén yuèláiyuè duō.) — There are more and more people in the city.
- 我越来越喜欢这个地方。(Wǒ yuèláiyuè xǐhuan zhège dìfang.) — I like this place more and more.

## Negation

越来越 is not directly negated. To express a decreasing trend, use 越来越 with an adjective that has the opposite meaning, or use 越来越不:
- 他越来越不想去了。(Tā yuèláiyuè bù xiǎng qù le.) — He wants to go less and less.
- 她越来越不开心了。(Tā yuèláiyuè bù kāixīn le.) — She is becoming less and less happy.

## Common Errors

- Adding 很 before the adjective (越来越 already expresses degree): ✗ 天气越来越很冷。 ✓ 天气越来越冷了。(Tiānqì yuèláiyuè lěng le.) — The weather is getting colder and colder.
- Confusing 越来越 with 越...越 (different structure): ✗ 越来越吃越胖。 ✓ 越吃越胖。(Yuè chī yuè pàng.) — The more you eat, the fatter you get.
