# 连...都/也 (Even... — Emphasis Construction)

- **Pattern**: 连 + NP + 都/也 + VP
- **Structure**: 连 (lián) highlights the noun phrase as extreme or surprising; 都 (dōu) or 也 (yě) reinforces the emphasis before the verb

## Explanation

连...都/也 is used to emphasize that something surprising or extreme is the case — "even X does/is Y." The noun phrase after 连 represents the most unlikely or extreme example, and the verb phrase states what is true even for that extreme case. The implication is: if even this extreme example applies, then everything else certainly does too.

都 and 也 are largely interchangeable in this construction, though 都 is slightly more common and carries a stronger sense of totality ("all, even"), while 也 leans toward "also, even so."

连 must be paired with 都 or 也 — using 连 alone without a corresponding adverb sounds incomplete.

## Example Sentences

- 他连自己的名字都不会写。(Tā lián zìjǐ de míngzi dōu bù huì xiě.) — He can't even write his own name.
- 这道题连老师都不会做。(Zhè dào tí lián lǎoshī dōu bù huì zuò.) — Even the teacher can't solve this problem.
- 她忙得连饭都没吃。(Tā máng de lián fàn dōu méi chī.) — She was so busy she didn't even eat.
- 连小孩子也知道这个道理。(Lián xiǎo háizi yě zhīdào zhège dàoli.) — Even children understand this principle.

## Negation

Negation appears in the VP after 都/也:
- 他连一个字也不认识。(Tā lián yí gè zì yě bù rènshi.) — He doesn't recognize even a single character.
- 我连一分钟都没有休息。(Wǒ lián yì fēnzhōng dōu méiyǒu xiūxi.) — I didn't rest for even one minute.

## Common Errors

- Omitting 都 or 也 (连 requires a paired adverb): ✗ 他连饭没吃。 ✓ 他连饭都没吃。(Tā lián fàn dōu méi chī.) — He didn't even eat.
- Placing the emphasized NP after 都/也 instead of after 连: ✗ 都连他不知道。 ✓ 连他都不知道。(Lián tā dōu bù zhīdào.) — Even he doesn't know.
