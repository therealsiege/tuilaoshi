# 在 + Verb (Progressive Aspect)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 在 + Verb (+ 呢)
- **Structure**: S + 在 + V (+ 呢)

## Explanation

在 (zài) before a verb indicates that an action is currently in progress, similar to the English "-ing" form. The sentence-final particle 呢 (ne) is often added for a softer, more conversational tone but is not required. You may also encounter 正在 (zhèngzài) for emphasis, meaning "right in the middle of doing."

The three progressive markers can be combined: 正在 + V + 呢 is the fullest form, but any subset works — 在 + V, V + 呢, 正在 + V, or 正 + V + 呢.

## Example Sentences

- 他在看书。(Tā zài kàn shū.) — He is reading.
- 我在吃饭呢。(Wǒ zài chī fàn ne.) — I'm eating (right now).
- 她正在写作业。(Tā zhèngzài xiě zuòyè.) — She is (right now) doing homework.
- 他们在打篮球呢。(Tāmen zài dǎ lánqiú ne.) — They are playing basketball.

## Negation

Negate with 没(有) and drop 在:
- 他没有看书。(Tā méiyǒu kàn shū.) — He is not reading. / He wasn't reading.

To express "not currently doing" more explicitly, use 没在:
- 他没在看书。(Tā méi zài kàn shū.) — He isn't reading (right now).

## Common Errors

- Using 不 for progressive negation: ✗ 他不在看书。(This means "He doesn't read here.") ✓ 他没在看书。(Tā méi zài kàn shū.)
- Putting 在 after the verb: ✗ 他看在书。 ✓ 他在看书。(Tā zài kàn shū.)
