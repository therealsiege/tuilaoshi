# 一边...一边 (While Doing A, Also Doing B — Simultaneous Actions)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 一边 + Verb A, 一边 + Verb B
- **Structure**: 一边 (yìbiān)...一边 (yìbiān) frames two actions happening at the same time

## Explanation

一边...一边 describes two actions performed simultaneously by the same subject. Both actions are ongoing and concurrent — neither is background to the other. The construction is similar to English "while doing X, (also) doing Y" but with a stronger sense of equal weight between the two activities.

The two verbs should represent actions that can realistically be done at the same time. Each 一边 is followed by a verb phrase (not a bare adjective). The subject appears once before the first 一边.

For background actions where one is clearly secondary, Chinese often uses the progressive 在 (zài) instead. 一边...一边 implies both actions are deliberate and equally foregrounded.

## Example Sentences

- 他一边吃饭，一边看电视。(Tā yìbiān chīfàn, yìbiān kàn diànshì.) — He eats while watching TV.
- 她一边走路，一边听音乐。(Tā yìbiān zǒulù, yìbiān tīng yīnyuè.) — She listens to music while walking.
- 我们一边喝咖啡，一边聊天。(Wǒmen yìbiān hē kāfēi, yìbiān liáotiān.) — We chat while drinking coffee.
- 孩子们一边唱歌，一边跳舞。(Háizimen yìbiān chànggē, yìbiān tiàowǔ.) — The children sing and dance at the same time.

## Negation

Negate the verb within each 一边 clause using 不 or 没:
- 他没有一边吃饭一边看电视。(Tā méiyǒu yìbiān chīfàn yìbiān kàn diànshì.) — He didn't eat and watch TV at the same time.
- 不要一边走路一边看手机。(Bùyào yìbiān zǒulù yìbiān kàn shǒujī.) — Don't walk and look at your phone at the same time.

## Common Errors

- Using 一边 with actions that can't be simultaneous: ✗ 他一边睡觉，一边跑步。 ✓ 他一边跑步，一边听音乐。(Tā yìbiān pǎobù, yìbiān tīng yīnyuè.) — He listens to music while running.
- Using only one 一边: ✗ 她一边吃饭看书。 ✓ 她一边吃饭，一边看书。(Tā yìbiān chīfàn, yìbiān kàn shū.) — She reads while eating.
