# Complement of Degree 得

- **Pattern**: Verb + 得 + Adjective/Complement
- **Structure**: S + V + 得 + Adj / S + V + O + V + 得 + Adj

## Explanation

The complement of degree (程度补语, chéngdù bǔyǔ) uses 得 (de) after a verb to describe how well, how badly, or to what degree an action is performed. It answers the question "how?" about the verb. This structure is introduced in IC1 Lesson 10.

得 links the verb to an evaluative description:
- 她说得很好。(Tā shuō de hěn hǎo.) — She speaks very well.
- 他跑得很快。(Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.) — He runs very fast.

When the verb takes an object, the verb must be repeated so that 得 appears directly after the verb, not after the object:
- 她说中文说得很好。(Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.) — She speaks Chinese very well.
- Alternatively, front the object: 中文她说得很好。(Zhōngwén tā shuō de hěn hǎo.)

**Do not confuse the three de particles**: 得 (complement of degree) vs 的 (attributive/possessive) vs 地 (adverbial). Compare:
- 她说得很好。(complement: how she speaks)
- 她的中文很好。(attributive: her Chinese)
- 她高兴地说。(adverbial: she happily said)

## Example Sentences

- 你写得很漂亮。(Nǐ xiě de hěn piàoliang.) — You write beautifully.
- 他吃得太多了。(Tā chī de tài duō le.) — He ate too much.
- 老师教得很清楚。(Lǎoshī jiāo de hěn qīngchǔ.) — The teacher teaches very clearly.
- 她唱歌唱得非常好听。(Tā chàng gē chàng de fēicháng hǎotīng.) — She sings extremely well.
- 他起得很早。(Tā qǐ de hěn zǎo.) — He gets up very early.

## Negation

Place 不 before the adjective/complement, after 得:
- 她说得不好。(Tā shuō de bù hǎo.) — She doesn't speak well.
- 他写得不清楚。(Tā xiě de bù qīngchǔ.) — He doesn't write clearly.
- 我睡得不好。(Wǒ shuì de bù hǎo.) — I didn't sleep well.

## Common Errors

- Placing 得 after the object instead of the verb: ✗ 她说中文得很好。 ✓ 她说中文说得很好。(Tā shuō Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.)
- Confusing 得 with 的 or 地: ✗ 她说的很好。 ✓ 她说得很好。(Tā shuō de hěn hǎo.) — She speaks very well.
- Negating before 得 instead of after: ✗ 她不说得好。 ✓ 她说得不好。(Tā shuō de bù hǎo.) — She doesn't speak well.
