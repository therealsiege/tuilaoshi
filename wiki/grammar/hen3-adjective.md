# 很 + Adjective (Predicate Adjectives)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 很 + Adjective
- **Structure**: A 很 Adj

## Explanation

In Chinese, adjectives function as predicates on their own and do NOT use 是 (shì). Instead, 很 (hěn) serves as the default, neutral link between a subject and an adjective. When 很 is used in this default way, it does not carry its literal meaning of "very" — it simply fills a structural role. To actually emphasize degree, stress 很 or use stronger adverbs like 非常 (fēicháng) or 特别 (tèbié).

**Tone note**: 很 is third tone (hěn). When followed by another third-tone syllable, it undergoes tone sandhi and is pronounced as second tone (hén). For example, 很好 is pronounced hén hǎo.

## Example Sentences

- 今天很冷。(Jīntiān hěn lěng.) — It's cold today.
- 这本书很有意思。(Zhè běn shū hěn yǒu yìsi.) — This book is interesting.
- 她很漂亮。(Tā hěn piàoliang.) — She is pretty.
- 中文很难。(Zhōngwén hěn nán.) — Chinese is hard.

## Negation

Negate with 不 (bù) before the adjective. Drop 很 when negating:
- 今天不冷。(Jīntiān bù lěng.) — It's not cold today.
- 这本书不难。(Zhè běn shū bù nán.) — This book is not hard.

## Common Errors

- Using 是 with adjectives: ✗ 她是漂亮。 ✓ 她很漂亮。(Tā hěn piàoliang.) — She is pretty.
- Omitting 很 in neutral statements: ✗ 今天冷。 ✓ 今天很冷。(Jīntiān hěn lěng.) — It's cold today. (Without 很, the bare adjective implies contrast or comparison.)
