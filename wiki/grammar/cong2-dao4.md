# 从...到 (From...To)

- **Pattern**: 从 + Start Point + 到 + End Point
- **Structure**: 从 A 到 B

## Explanation

从 (cóng) means "from" and 到 (dào) means "to/until." Together, 从...到 expresses a range in time, place, or abstract scope. The pattern works like English "from A to B." 从 can also appear on its own to mark a starting point, and 到 can appear alone to mark a destination or endpoint.

When indicating direction of travel or origin, 从 replaces the English preposition "from": 我从北京来 (I come from Beijing).

## Example Sentences

- 从我家到学校要二十分钟。(Cóng wǒ jiā dào xuéxiào yào èrshí fēnzhōng.) — From my home to the school takes twenty minutes.
- 我从星期一到星期五都上班。(Wǒ cóng xīngqī yī dào xīngqī wǔ dōu shàngbān.) — I work from Monday to Friday.
- 从早上到晚上他都在学习。(Cóng zǎoshang dào wǎnshang tā dōu zài xuéxí.) — He studies from morning to night.
- 我从中国来。(Wǒ cóng Zhōngguó lái.) — I come from China.

## Negation

Negate the main verb of the sentence, not 从 or 到 themselves:
- 我从星期一到星期五都不上班。(Wǒ cóng xīngqī yī dào xīngqī wǔ dōu bù shàngbān.) — I don't work from Monday to Friday.

## Common Errors

- Wrong word order with 从: ✗ 我来从中国。 ✓ 我从中国来。(Wǒ cóng Zhōngguó lái.) — Prepositional phrases go before the verb.
- Omitting 从 or 到 in a range: ✗ 我星期一星期五上班。 ✓ 我从星期一到星期五上班。
- Using 从 for the English sense of "since" with duration: ✗ 我从三年学中文。 ✓ 我学了三年中文。(Wǒ xué le sān nián Zhōngwén.) — For duration, use 了 instead.
