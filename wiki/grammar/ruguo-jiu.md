# 如果...就 (If...Then — Conditional Sentences)

- **Pattern**: 如果 + Condition, (Subject) 就 + Result
- **Structure**: 如果 (rúguǒ) marks the condition; 就 (jiù) marks the consequence

## Explanation

如果...就 is the primary conditional construction in Chinese, corresponding to English "if...then." 如果 introduces the hypothetical condition, and 就 (an adverb meaning "then, right away") appears before the verb in the result clause to signal the logical consequence. 就 must come after the subject and before the verb — it cannot start a clause.

In casual speech, 如果 is sometimes shortened to 要是 (yàoshi) or 假如 (jiǎrú) in more formal registers. The 就 in the result clause is frequently kept even when 如果 is dropped, because 就 alone can imply conditionality.

## Example Sentences

- 如果明天下雨，我们就不去了。(Rúguǒ míngtiān xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù bù qù le.) — If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
- 如果你有时间，就来我家吧。(Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu shíjiān, jiù lái wǒ jiā ba.) — If you have time, come to my place.
- 如果你不懂，就问老师。(Rúguǒ nǐ bù dǒng, jiù wèn lǎoshī.) — If you don't understand, ask the teacher.
- 如果我早点儿起床，就不会迟到了。(Rúguǒ wǒ zǎo diǎnr qǐchuáng, jiù bù huì chídào le.) — If I had gotten up earlier, I wouldn't have been late.

## Negation

Negation appears within either clause:
- 如果你不想去，就别去了。(Rúguǒ nǐ bù xiǎng qù, jiù bié qù le.) — If you don't want to go, then don't go.
- 如果没有问题，我们就开始吧。(Rúguǒ méiyǒu wèntí, wǒmen jiù kāishǐ ba.) — If there are no questions, let's begin.

## Common Errors

- Placing 就 at the start of the result clause (English "then" word order): ✗ 如果下雨，就我们不去。 ✓ 如果下雨，我们就不去。(Rúguǒ xiàyǔ, wǒmen jiù bù qù.) — If it rains, we won't go.
- Omitting 就 entirely (the sentence works but loses the tight conditional link): ✗ 如果你有空，来我家。 ✓ 如果你有空，就来我家吧。(Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu kòng, jiù lái wǒ jiā ba.) — If you're free, come to my place.
