# 因为...所以 (Because...Therefore — Causal Sentences)

- **Pattern**: 因为 + Cause, 所以 + Result
- **Structure**: 因为 (yīnwèi) introduces the reason; 所以 (suǒyǐ) introduces the consequence

## Explanation

因为...所以 is the standard cause-and-effect construction in Chinese. Like 虽然...但是, Chinese typically uses BOTH connectors together in a single sentence, whereas English normally uses only one ("because" or "so," not both). The 因为 clause always states the cause, and the 所以 clause states the result.

In casual speech, one of the two connectors may be dropped — either 因为 or 所以 — but using both is always correct and clearer. When answering a 为什么 (wèishénme, "why") question, you can start directly with 因为 without needing 所以.

## Example Sentences

- 因为今天下雨，所以我没出去。(Yīnwèi jīntiān xiàyǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ méi chūqù.) — Because it rained today, I didn't go out.
- 因为他很努力，所以考试考得很好。(Yīnwèi tā hěn nǔlì, suǒyǐ kǎoshì kǎo de hěn hǎo.) — Because he worked hard, he did well on the exam.
- 因为路上堵车，所以我迟到了。(Yīnwèi lùshàng dǔchē, suǒyǐ wǒ chídào le.) — Because of the traffic jam, I was late.
- 她因为生病了，所以没来上课。(Tā yīnwèi shēngbìng le, suǒyǐ méi lái shàngkè.) — She didn't come to class because she was sick.

## Negation

Negation goes inside the individual clauses as needed:
- 因为他不舒服，所以没去上班。(Yīnwèi tā bù shūfu, suǒyǐ méi qù shàngbān.) — Because he wasn't feeling well, he didn't go to work.
- 因为没有时间，所以我不能去。(Yīnwèi méiyǒu shíjiān, suǒyǐ wǒ bù néng qù.) — Because there's no time, I can't go.

## Common Errors

- Using both "because" and "so" logic but dropping the Chinese connectors inconsistently: ✗ 因为他累了，他不想去。 ✓ 因为他累了，所以他不想去。(Yīnwèi tā lèi le, suǒyǐ tā bù xiǎng qù.) — Because he was tired, he didn't want to go.
- Reversing the clause order: ✗ 所以我迟到了，因为堵车。 ✓ 因为堵车，所以我迟到了。(Yīnwèi dǔchē, suǒyǐ wǒ chídào le.) — I was late because of traffic.
