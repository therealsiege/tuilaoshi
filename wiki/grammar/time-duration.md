# Time Duration Expressions

- **Pattern**: Subject + Verb + 了 + Duration + (Object)
- **Structure**: S + V + 了 + Duration (+ O) / S + V + O + V + 了 + Duration

## Explanation

Time duration in Chinese indicates how long an action lasts. The duration phrase is placed after the verb, not before it (unlike English "I studied for two years"). This is a key structural difference covered in IC1 Lesson 7.

When the verb has an object, two main strategies avoid placing the duration between the verb and its object:

1. **Repeat the verb**: 我学中文学了两年。(Wǒ xué Zhōngwén xué le liǎng nián.) — I studied Chinese for two years.
2. **Duration before object** (with 的): 我学了两年(的)中文。(Wǒ xué le liǎng nián de Zhōngwén.) — I studied two years of Chinese.

**Time-when vs time-duration**: Time-when (昨天, 三点, 星期一) goes before the verb. Time-duration (两个小时, 三年) goes after the verb. This distinction is critical:
- 我昨天学中文。(time-when, before verb) — I studied Chinese yesterday.
- 我学了两个小时中文。(time-duration, after verb) — I studied Chinese for two hours.

The particle 了 (le) marks the completion or realization of the duration. For ongoing actions, 了 appears at the end of the sentence to indicate "up to now": 我学了三年中文了。(I've been studying Chinese for three years now.)

## Example Sentences

- 我学了两年中文。(Wǒ xué le liǎng nián Zhōngwén.) — I studied Chinese for two years.
- 他等了半个小时。(Tā děng le bàn ge xiǎoshí.) — He waited for half an hour.
- 她睡了十个小时。(Tā shuì le shí ge xiǎoshí.) — She slept for ten hours.
- 我们走了二十分钟。(Wǒmen zǒu le èrshí fēnzhōng.) — We walked for twenty minutes.
- 他在中国住了三年了。(Tā zài Zhōngguó zhù le sān nián le.) — He has lived in China for three years (and still does).

## Negation

Negate with 没(有). Drop 了 when negating:
- 我没学很长时间。(Wǒ méi xué hěn cháng shíjiān.) — I didn't study for a long time.
- 他没等多久。(Tā méi děng duōjiǔ.) — He didn't wait long.

To say "only" a short duration, use 才 or 只:
- 我才学了两个月中文。(Wǒ cái xué le liǎng ge yuè Zhōngwén.) — I've only studied Chinese for two months.

## Common Errors

- Placing duration before the verb (English transfer): ✗ 我两年学了中文。 ✓ 我学了两年中文。(Wǒ xué le liǎng nián Zhōngwén.)
- Confusing time-when and time-duration positions: ✗ 我学了昨天中文。 ✓ 我昨天学了中文。(Wǒ zuótiān xué le Zhōngwén.) — I studied Chinese yesterday.
- Forgetting 了 with duration: ✗ 我学两年中文。 ✓ 我学了两年中文。(Wǒ xué le liǎng nián Zhōngwén.) — I studied Chinese for two years.
