# 把 Construction (Disposal)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + Complement
- **Structure**: S + 把 + O + V + Complement/Result

## Explanation

The 把 (bǎ) construction is used when the subject does something to the object that changes it in some way — moving it, transforming it, or disposing of it. The object (what is acted upon) is pulled in front of the verb, and the verb must have some additional element after it (a complement, 了, a directional word, or a result). A bare verb after 把 is grammatically incomplete.

Key conditions for using 把:
1. The object must be specific and definite (not "a book" but "the book" or "that book").
2. The verb must express an action that affects or changes the object.
3. The verb must have something after it — a result complement, 了, a directional complement, etc.

## Example Sentences

- 请把门关上。(Qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng.) — Please close the door.
- 他把作业做完了。(Tā bǎ zuòyè zuò wán le.) — He finished his homework.
- 她把那本书放在桌子上了。(Tā bǎ nà běn shū fàng zài zhuōzi shàng le.) — She put that book on the table.
- 我把咖啡喝了。(Wǒ bǎ kāfēi hē le.) — I drank the coffee (all of it).

## Negation

Place 不 or 没 before 把:
- 我没把作业做完。(Wǒ méi bǎ zuòyè zuò wán.) — I didn't finish the homework.
- 你不要把书弄丢了。(Nǐ búyào bǎ shū nòng diū le.) — Don't lose the book.

## Common Errors

- Using 把 with a bare verb: ✗ 他把门关。 ✓ 他把门关上。(Tā bǎ mén guān shàng.) — The verb needs a complement.
- Using 把 with an indefinite object: ✗ 我把一本书看了。 ✓ 我把那本书看了。(Wǒ bǎ nà běn shū kàn le.) — The object must be specific.
- Placing 把 after the verb: ✗ 他关把门上。 ✓ 他把门关上。
