# Advanced Comparison Patterns

- **Pattern**: A 跟 B 一样 Adj / A 不如 B / A 有 B 那么 Adj / 越...越...
- **Structure**: See individual patterns below

## Explanation

Beyond the basic 比 (bǐ) comparison, Chinese has several patterns for expressing equality, inferiority, and correlative comparisons. These are covered in IC2 Lesson 5 and build on the basic 比 pattern.

**1. 跟...一样 (gēn...yīyàng)** — "the same as" (equality):
- A 跟 B 一样 + Adj: 他跟我一样高。(He is as tall as me.)
- Negative: A 跟 B 不一样: 他跟我不一样高。(He is not as tall as me.)

**2. 不如 (bùrú)** — "not as good as" / "inferior to" (inferiority):
- A 不如 B: 坐公共汽车不如坐地铁方便。(Taking the bus is not as convenient as taking the subway.)
- 不如 is more literary/formal than 没有...那么.

**3. 有/没有 + Noun + 那么 (yǒu/méiyǒu...nàme)** — "as...as" or "not as...as":
- A 有 B 那么 Adj: 他有你那么高吗？(Is he as tall as you?)
- A 没有 B 那么 Adj: 今天没有昨天那么冷。(Today is not as cold as yesterday.)
- This is the most common way to express "not as...as" in spoken Chinese.

**4. 越...越 (yuè...yuè)** — "the more...the more" (correlative comparison):
- 越来越 + Adj: 天气越来越冷了。(The weather is getting colder and colder.)
- 越 A 越 B: 他越说越快。(The more he talks, the faster he gets.)
- Subject + 越 V1 越 V2: 我越学越喜欢。(The more I study it, the more I like it.)

## Example Sentences

- 他跟她一样聪明。(Tā gēn tā yīyàng cōngmíng.) — He is as smart as she is.
- 坐飞机不如坐火车舒服。(Zuò fēijī bùrú zuò huǒchē shūfu.) — Flying is not as comfortable as taking the train.
- 弟弟没有哥哥那么高。(Dìdi méiyǒu gēge nàme gāo.) — The younger brother is not as tall as the older brother.
- 中文越学越有意思。(Zhōngwén yuè xué yuè yǒu yìsi.) — The more you study Chinese, the more interesting it gets.
- 她的成绩跟我的一样好。(Tā de chéngjì gēn wǒ de yīyàng hǎo.) — Her grades are as good as mine.

## Negation

Each pattern has its own negation:
- 跟...一样 negated with 不: 他跟我不一样高。(Tā gēn wǒ bù yīyàng gāo.) — He is not as tall as me.
- 不如 is itself a negative comparison (A is inferior to B).
- 有...那么 negated with 没: 今天没有昨天那么热。(Jīntiān méiyǒu zuótiān nàme rè.) — Today is not as hot as yesterday.
- 越...越 is not typically negated directly; instead negate the verb: 越想越不明白。(Yuè xiǎng yuè bù míngbái.) — The more I think, the less I understand.

## Common Errors

- Using 比 in equality comparisons: ✗ 他比我一样高。 ✓ 他跟我一样高。(Tā gēn wǒ yīyàng gāo.) — He is as tall as me.
- Adding 很 after 比 or 一样: ✗ 他比我很高。 ✓ 他比我高。/ ✗ 他跟我一样很高。 ✓ 他跟我一样高。
- Confusing word order in 没有...那么: ✗ 今天没有那么昨天冷。 ✓ 今天没有昨天那么冷。(Jīntiān méiyǒu zuótiān nàme lěng.)
- Using 越来越 with a past time: ✗ 去年天气越来越冷了。 ✓ 天气越来越冷了。(Tiānqì yuè lái yuè lěng le.) — The weather is getting colder and colder. (越来越 implies an ongoing/current trend.)
