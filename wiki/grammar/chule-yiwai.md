# 除了...以外 (Besides / Except — Scope Construction)

- **Pattern**: 除了 + X + 以外, 都/还 + VP
- **Structure**: 除了 (chúle) introduces the exception or addition; 以外 (yǐwài) closes the phrase; 都 (dōu) signals exclusion ("except X, all others"); 还 (hái) signals inclusion ("besides X, also")

## Explanation

除了...以外 has two distinct meanings depending on whether the result clause uses 都 or 还:

1. **Exclusion (都)**: "Except for X, everyone/everything else..." — X is excluded from the group.
2. **Inclusion (还)**: "Besides X, also..." — X is included, and something additional is added.

以外 is often omitted in casual speech, leaving just 除了 + X. The key to understanding the meaning is the adverb in the main clause: 都 means exclusion, 还/也 means inclusion.

This is a common source of confusion because the same frame (除了...) can mean opposite things depending on what follows.

## Example Sentences

**Exclusion (except):**
- 除了他以外，大家都去了。(Chúle tā yǐwài, dàjiā dōu qù le.) — Except for him, everyone went.
- 除了星期天以外，我每天都上班。(Chúle xīngqītiān yǐwài, wǒ měitiān dōu shàngbān.) — Except for Sunday, I work every day.
- 除了数学，其他科目我都喜欢。(Chúle shùxué, qítā kēmù wǒ dōu xǐhuan.) — I like all subjects except math.

**Inclusion (besides):**
- 除了中文以外，他还会说法语。(Chúle Zhōngwén yǐwài, tā hái huì shuō Fǎyǔ.) — Besides Chinese, he also speaks French.
- 除了游泳，她还喜欢跑步。(Chúle yóuyǒng, tā hái xǐhuan pǎobù.) — Besides swimming, she also likes running.

## Negation

Negation appears in the main clause:
- 除了这件事，我什么都不知道。(Chúle zhè jiàn shì, wǒ shénme dōu bù zhīdào.) — Except for this matter, I don't know anything.
- 除了周末，他都没有时间。(Chúle zhōumò, tā dōu méiyǒu shíjiān.) — Except for the weekend, he doesn't have any time.

## Common Errors

- Mixing up 都 and 还 (changes meaning entirely): ✗ 除了他以外，大家还去了。(This means "besides him, everyone also went" — different meaning.) ✓ 除了他以外，大家都去了。(Chúle tā yǐwài, dàjiā dōu qù le.) — Except for him, everyone went.
- Omitting both 都 and 还 (leaves meaning ambiguous): ✗ 除了英语，他会说法语。 ✓ 除了英语以外，他还会说法语。(Chúle Yīngyǔ yǐwài, tā hái huì shuō Fǎyǔ.) — Besides English, he also speaks French.
