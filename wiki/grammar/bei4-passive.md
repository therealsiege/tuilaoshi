# 被 Passive Construction (Passive Voice)

- **Pattern**: Subject + 被 + (Agent) + Verb + Complement
- **Structure**: 被 (bèi) marks the passive; the agent (doer) is optional; the verb usually requires a complement or result

## Explanation

The 被 construction is the primary way to express passive voice in Chinese. Unlike English, where passive is stylistically neutral, the 被 passive in Chinese traditionally carries a negative connotation — it is used when the subject is adversely affected by the action (something bad happened to them). In modern Mandarin, especially in formal writing and news language, 被 is increasingly used in neutral or positive contexts, but the negative flavor remains dominant in everyday speech.

The verb in a 被 sentence cannot stand alone — it almost always needs a complement (result complement, directional complement, 了, etc.) to indicate that the action has been completed or has produced a result. A bare verb after 被 sounds incomplete.

The agent (the doer of the action) may be omitted when unknown or unimportant.

## Example Sentences

- 我的手机被偷了。(Wǒ de shǒujī bèi tōu le.) — My phone was stolen.
- 他被老师批评了。(Tā bèi lǎoshī pīpíng le.) — He was criticized by the teacher.
- 那本书被借走了。(Nà běn shū bèi jiè zǒu le.) — That book was borrowed (taken away).
- 蛋糕被孩子们吃完了。(Dàngāo bèi háizimen chī wán le.) — The cake was eaten up by the children.
- 这个消息被很多人知道了。(Zhège xiāoxi bèi hěn duō rén zhīdào le.) — This news became known by many people.

## Negation

Place 没(有) or 不 before 被:
- 他没有被录取。(Tā méiyǒu bèi lùqǔ.) — He wasn't admitted.
- 这件事不会被忘记。(Zhè jiàn shì bù huì bèi wàngjì.) — This matter won't be forgotten.

## Common Errors

- Using a bare verb without a complement after 被: ✗ 我的自行车被偷。 ✓ 我的自行车被偷了。(Wǒ de zìxíngchē bèi tōu le.) — My bike was stolen.
- Using 被 for self-initiated actions (no passive meaning): ✗ 我被去了北京。 ✓ 我去了北京。(Wǒ qù le Běijīng.) — I went to Beijing.
- Forgetting the negative connotation in casual speech: ✗ 他被表扬了 (acceptable in modern usage but traditionally unexpected — 被 implies adversity).
