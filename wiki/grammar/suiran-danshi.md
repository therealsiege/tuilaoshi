# 虽然...但是 (Although...But — Concessive Sentences)

- **Pattern**: 虽然 + Clause A, 但是 + Clause B
- **Structure**: 虽然 (suīrán) states a conceded fact; 但是 (dànshì) introduces the contrasting main point

## Explanation

虽然...但是 is the standard concessive construction in Chinese, equivalent to "although...but" in English. Unlike English, Chinese uses BOTH the concessive marker and the adversative marker in the same sentence — dropping 但是 sounds incomplete to a Chinese ear. The clause with 但是 carries the speaker's main point.

虽然 can appear before or after the subject of Clause A. When the two clauses share the same subject, the subject typically comes before 虽然. When the subjects differ, 虽然 comes before its own clause's subject. 但是 can be shortened to 但 (dàn) or replaced by 可是 (kěshì) in less formal speech.

## Example Sentences

- 虽然他很忙，但是他每天都运动。(Suīrán tā hěn máng, dànshì tā měitiān dōu yùndòng.) — Although he is busy, he exercises every day.
- 这个菜虽然贵，但是很好吃。(Zhège cài suīrán guì, dànshì hěn hǎochī.) — Although this dish is expensive, it's delicious.
- 虽然外面下雨了，但是我们还是去了公园。(Suīrán wàimiàn xiàyǔ le, dànshì wǒmen háishi qù le gōngyuán.) — Although it was raining outside, we still went to the park.
- 她虽然年纪小，但是很懂事。(Tā suīrán niánjì xiǎo, dànshì hěn dǒngshì.) — Although she is young, she is very mature.

## Negation

Negation is applied within the individual clauses, not to the frame itself:
- 虽然他不高，但是篮球打得很好。(Suīrán tā bù gāo, dànshì lánqiú dǎ de hěn hǎo.) — Although he's not tall, he plays basketball very well.
- 虽然天气不好，但是我们没有取消活动。(Suīrán tiānqì bù hǎo, dànshì wǒmen méiyǒu qǔxiāo huódòng.) — Although the weather was bad, we didn't cancel the event.

## Common Errors

- Dropping 但是 (English transfer — "although" alone feels complete in English): ✗ 虽然他很累，他还是来了。 ✓ 虽然他很累，但是他还是来了。(Suīrán tā hěn lèi, dànshì tā háishi lái le.) — Although he was tired, he still came.
- Using 虽然 in the wrong clause: ✗ 他很忙，虽然他每天运动。 ✓ 虽然他很忙，但是他每天运动。(Suīrán tā hěn máng, dànshì tā měitiān yùndòng.) — Although he's busy, he exercises every day.
