# Location and Direction Words 里/上/下/前/后/旁边

- **Pattern**: 在 + Place + Location Word
- **Structure**: S + 在 + Place + 里/上/下/前/后/旁边 (+ V)

## Explanation

Chinese uses a system of location words (方位词, fāngwèicí) that attach after a noun to specify spatial relationships. Unlike English prepositions that come before the noun ("in the room"), Chinese location words come after: 房间里 (fángjiān lǐ, "room-inside"). This is covered in IC1 Lesson 8.

The core location words are:

| Word | Pinyin | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| 里 | lǐ | inside |
| 上 | shàng | on top, above |
| 下 | xià | below, under |
| 前 | qián | in front |
| 后 | hòu | behind |
| 旁边 | pángbiān | beside, next to |
| 外 | wài | outside |
| 中间 | zhōngjiān | in the middle |
| 对面 | duìmiàn | opposite, across from |
| 左边 | zuǒbiān | left side |
| 右边 | yòubiān | right side |

These combine with 在 (zài) to express location: 在桌子上 (on the table), 在学校里 (inside the school), 在银行旁边 (next to the bank).

For direction of movement, use 往 (wǎng) or 向 (xiàng) + direction: 往左走 (wǎng zuǒ zǒu, walk to the left), 往前走 (wǎng qián zǒu, walk forward).

## Example Sentences

- 书在桌子上。(Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.) — The book is on the table.
- 猫在椅子下面。(Māo zài yǐzi xiàmiàn.) — The cat is under the chair.
- 银行在学校旁边。(Yínháng zài xuéxiào pángbiān.) — The bank is next to the school.
- 请往前走。(Qǐng wǎng qián zǒu.) — Please walk forward.
- 图书馆在书店对面。(Túshūguǎn zài shūdiàn duìmiàn.) — The library is across from the bookstore.

## Negation

Negate with 不在 (bú zài):
- 书不在桌子上。(Shū bú zài zhuōzi shàng.) — The book is not on the table.
- 他不在教室里。(Tā bú zài jiàoshì lǐ.) — He is not in the classroom.

## Common Errors

- Omitting the location word after a place noun: ✗ 猫在桌子。 ✓ 猫在桌子上。(Māo zài zhuōzi shàng.) — The cat is on the table.
- Using English preposition order: ✗ 在里房间。 ✓ 在房间里。(Zài fángjiān lǐ.) — Inside the room.
- Confusing 里 with 上 for surfaces vs enclosures: ✗ 书在桌子里。 ✓ 书在桌子上。(Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.) — The book is on the table. (Use 里 for enclosed spaces, 上 for surfaces.)
