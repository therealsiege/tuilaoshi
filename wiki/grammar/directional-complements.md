# Directional Complements 上来/下去/进来/出去/回来/过去

- **Pattern**: Verb + Directional Complement
- **Structure**: V + 来/去 (simple) / V + Direction + 来/去 (compound)

## Explanation

Directional complements (趋向补语, qūxiàng bǔyǔ) attach to verbs to indicate the direction of movement. They are introduced across IC1 Lessons 14-15 and are essential for describing physical movement and, later, figurative/abstract meanings.

**Simple directional complements** use 来 (lái, toward speaker) or 去 (qù, away from speaker):
- 上来 (shàng lái) — come up
- 下去 (xià qù) — go down

**Compound directional complements** combine a direction word with 来 or 去:

| Direction | + 来 (toward) | + 去 (away) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| 上 (up) | 上来 shàng lái | 上去 shàng qù |
| 下 (down) | 下来 xià lái | 下去 xià qù |
| 进 (in) | 进来 jìn lái | 进去 jìn qù |
| 出 (out) | 出来 chū lái | 出去 chū qù |
| 回 (back) | 回来 huí lái | 回去 huí qù |
| 过 (across) | 过来 guò lái | 过去 guò qù |
| 起 (up/rise) | 起来 qǐ lái | — |

When a place word is involved, it inserts between the direction and 来/去:
- 走进教室来。(Zǒu jìn jiàoshì lái.) — Walk into the classroom (toward speaker).

**Figurative/extended uses** are common at higher levels:
- 想起来 (xiǎng qǐlái) — to recall, remember
- 看出来 (kàn chūlái) — to discern, figure out
- 冷下来 (lěng xiàlái) — to cool down
- 说下去 (shuō xiàqù) — to continue speaking

## Example Sentences

- 请进来！(Qǐng jìn lái!) — Please come in!
- 他走出去了。(Tā zǒu chūqù le.) — He walked out.
- 她跑上楼来了。(Tā pǎo shàng lóu lái le.) — She ran upstairs (toward speaker).
- 把书拿回来。(Bǎ shū ná huílái.) — Bring the book back.
- 我想起来了！(Wǒ xiǎng qǐlái le!) — I remembered!

## Negation

Negate with 没(有) before the verb:
- 他没走出去。(Tā méi zǒu chūqù.) — He didn't walk out.
- 她没跑上来。(Tā méi pǎo shànglái.) — She didn't run up.

For inability, use the potential form with 不:
- 我走不上去。(Wǒ zǒu bú shàngqù.) — I can't walk up (there).
- 他跑不回来。(Tā pǎo bù huílái.) — He can't run back.

## Common Errors

- Confusing 来 and 去 perspective: ✗ 请进去！(said to someone approaching you) ✓ 请进来！(Qǐng jìn lái!) — Please come in! (来 = toward speaker)
- Incorrect placement of location: ✗ 她跑上来楼了。 ✓ 她跑上楼来了。(Tā pǎo shàng lóu lái le.) — She ran upstairs. (Location goes between direction and 来/去.)
- Forgetting figurative meanings: 起来 does not always mean "stand up" — it often signals inception or realization (想起来 = recall, 笑起来 = burst out laughing).
