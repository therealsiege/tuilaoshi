# 吗 and 呢 Question Particles

- **Pattern**: Statement + 吗？ / Noun/Pronoun + 呢？
- **Structure**: S + V + O + 吗？ / Topic + 呢？

## Explanation

Chinese forms yes/no questions by appending the particle 吗 (ma) to a declarative statement. The word order does not change — you simply add 吗 at the end. This is one of the first structures learned in Mandarin and appears in Integrated Chinese Lesson 1.

The particle 呢 (ne) has several question functions. Most commonly it creates follow-up or "bounce-back" questions meaning "and what about...?" after a topic has been established. It can also form tag questions when appended to a statement, softly seeking confirmation. 呢 appears in IC1 Lesson 2.

Neither 吗 nor 呢 carries lexical tone in connected speech — both are neutral tone (轻声, qīngshēng).

**Key distinction**: 吗 asks a brand-new yes/no question. 呢 presupposes shared context and asks about a new topic within it.

## Example Sentences

- 你是学生吗？(Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?) — Are you a student?
- 你好吗？(Nǐ hǎo ma?) — How are you? (literally: Are you well?)
- 他是中国人吗？(Tā shì Zhōngguó rén ma?) — Is he Chinese?
- 我很好，你呢？(Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?) — I'm fine, and you?
- 我喜欢咖啡，你呢？(Wǒ xǐhuān kāfēi, nǐ ne?) — I like coffee, how about you?

## Negation

吗 questions are negated in the answer, not in the question itself:
- Q: 你是学生吗？(Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?) — Are you a student?
- A: 不是，我不是学生。(Bú shì, wǒ bú shì xuéshēng.) — No, I'm not a student.

Do not combine 吗 with negative statements to ask questions — use affirmative-negative (A-not-A) form instead:
- 你是不是学生？(Nǐ shì bu shì xuéshēng?) — Are you a student (or not)?

## Common Errors

- Adding 吗 to a question that already has a question word: ✗ 你去哪里吗？ ✓ 你去哪里？(Nǐ qù nǎlǐ?) — Where are you going?
- Using 呢 without established context: ✗ 呢？(out of the blue) ✓ 我去图书馆，你呢？(Wǒ qù túshūguǎn, nǐ ne?) — I'm going to the library, and you?
- Combining 吗 with A-not-A form: ✗ 你是不是学生吗？ ✓ 你是不是学生？(Nǐ shì bu shì xuéshēng?)
