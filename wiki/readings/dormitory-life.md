# 宿舍生活 Dormitory Life

## Text

我叫张伟，今年大二。去年九月我第一次住大学宿舍，刚开始的时候真的不太习惯。我们宿舍住四个人，我的室友来自不同的地方。我旁边床的室友叫小刘，他是四川人，特别爱吃辣的。他每天都带辣椒酱回宿舍，整个房间都是辣味。我是上海人，以前从来不吃辣，闻到那个味道就受不了。

宿舍的作息时间让我很不适应。学校规定晚上十一点熄灯，可是我在家的时候常常十二点以后才睡觉。而且洗澡要去公共浴室，每天晚上排队的人特别多，有时候要等半个小时。虽然这些事情让我很不舒服，但是我慢慢地习惯了。

到了期末考试的时候，大家都很紧张。我们经常一边吃泡面，一边复习功课，有时候学到凌晨两三点。小刘的数学很好，他帮我讲题，我帮他练英语口语。我们互相帮助，最后考试成绩都不错。

现在一年过去了，小刘已经成了我最好的朋友。我也能吃辣了，虽然还是没有他那么能吃，但是四川火锅我已经听懂了怎么点菜。下学期我们打算继续住在一起。宿舍生活虽然有很多不方便的地方，但是交到了这么好的朋友，我觉得很值得。

## Translation

My name is Zhang Wei, and I'm a sophomore this year. Last September was my first time living in a university dormitory, and at the beginning I really wasn't used to it. Four people live in our dorm room, and my roommates come from different places. The roommate in the bed next to mine is called Xiao Liu. He's from Sichuan and absolutely loves spicy food. He brings chili sauce back to the dorm every day, and the whole room smells spicy. I'm from Shanghai and never used to eat spicy food at all -- just smelling it was unbearable.

The dorm schedule was hard for me to adjust to. The school requires lights out at eleven at night, but when I was at home I usually didn't go to sleep until after midnight. Also, you have to go to the communal bathroom to shower, and every night the line is really long -- sometimes you have to wait half an hour. Although these things made me very uncomfortable, I gradually got used to them.

When finals came around, everyone was nervous. We would often eat instant noodles while reviewing our lessons, sometimes studying until two or three in the morning. Xiao Liu is great at math -- he helped me work through problems, and I helped him practice spoken English. We helped each other, and in the end we all did well on exams.

Now a year has passed, and Xiao Liu has become my best friend. I can eat spicy food too now. Although I still can't handle as much as he can, I've already learned how to order at a Sichuan hotpot restaurant. Next semester we plan to keep living together. Even though dorm life has many inconveniences, making such a good friend has been completely worth it.

## Grammar Notes

1. **虽然...但是 (although...but)**: This paired conjunction appears multiple times: 虽然这些事情让我很不舒服，但是我慢慢地习惯了 and 宿舍生活虽然有很多不方便的地方，但是交到了这么好的朋友. Note that 虽然 can appear before or after the subject. See [[grammar/suiran-danshi]].

2. **一边...一边 (doing X while doing Y)**: The pattern 一边吃泡面，一边复习功课 describes two actions happening simultaneously. Both actions must be performed by the same subject. See [[grammar/yibian-yibian]].

3. **Resultative complements**: Several verbs carry result complements showing the outcome of an action: 习惯了 (got used to it), 听懂了 (understood by listening), 受不了 (can't endure), 交到了 (succeeded in making [friends]). The complement tells you whether the action succeeded or what state it produced. See [[grammar/de-particles]].
