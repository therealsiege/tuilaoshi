# 周末计划 Weekend Plans

## Text

小王：小李，这个周末你有什么计划？

小李：我还没想好呢。你呢？

小王：我想星期六上午去打羽毛球，你想不想一起去？

小李：好啊！我很久没运动了。在哪儿打？

小王：学校旁边的体育馆。我们九点半见面，怎么样？

小李：没问题。打完球以后我们去吃午饭吧？学校对面新开了一家四川餐厅，听说很好吃。

小王：太好了，我最喜欢吃辣的！下午你有空吗？我还想去看一个电影。

小李：什么电影？

小王：一个新的中国电影，下午两点的。我已经买好票了。

小李：真的吗？那太好了！星期天呢？

小王：星期天我要在家休息，还要准备下周一的考试。

小李：我也是。那我们星期六见！

小王：好，不见不散！

## Pinyin

Xiǎo Wáng: Xiǎo Lǐ, zhège zhōumò nǐ yǒu shénme jìhuà?

Xiǎo Lǐ: Wǒ hái méi xiǎng hǎo ne. Nǐ ne?

Xiǎo Wáng: Wǒ xiǎng xīngqīliù shàngwǔ qù dǎ yǔmáoqiú, nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng yìqǐ qù?

Xiǎo Lǐ: Hǎo a! Wǒ hěn jiǔ méi yùndòng le. Zài nǎr dǎ?

Xiǎo Wáng: Xuéxiào pángbiān de tǐyùguǎn. Wǒmen jiǔ diǎn bàn jiànmiàn, zěnmeyàng?

Xiǎo Lǐ: Méi wèntí. Dǎ wán qiú yǐhòu wǒmen qù chī wǔfàn ba? Xuéxiào duìmiàn xīn kāi le yì jiā Sìchuān cāntīng, tīngshuō hěn hǎochī.

Xiǎo Wáng: Tài hǎo le, wǒ zuì xǐhuān chī là de! Xiàwǔ nǐ yǒu kòng ma? Wǒ hái xiǎng qù kàn yí gè diànyǐng.

Xiǎo Lǐ: Shénme diànyǐng?

Xiǎo Wáng: Yí gè xīn de Zhōngguó diànyǐng, xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn de. Wǒ yǐjīng mǎi hǎo piào le.

Xiǎo Lǐ: Zhēn de ma? Nà tài hǎo le! Xīngqītiān ne?

Xiǎo Wáng: Xīngqītiān wǒ yào zài jiā xiūxi, hái yào zhǔnbèi xià zhōuyī de kǎoshì.

Xiǎo Lǐ: Wǒ yě shì. Nà wǒmen xīngqīliù jiàn!

Xiǎo Wáng: Hǎo, bú jiàn bú sàn!

## Translation

Xiao Wang: Xiao Li, do you have any plans this weekend?

Xiao Li: I haven't decided yet. How about you?

Xiao Wang: I want to play badminton Saturday morning. Do you want to come along?

Xiao Li: Sure! I haven't exercised in a long time. Where are we playing?

Xiao Wang: The gym next to the school. Let's meet at 9:30, how about that?

Xiao Li: No problem. After we finish playing, shall we go have lunch? A new Sichuan restaurant just opened across from the school. I hear it's really good.

Xiao Wang: Great, I love spicy food! Are you free in the afternoon? I also want to go see a movie.

Xiao Li: What movie?

Xiao Wang: A new Chinese movie, the 2:00 pm showing. I've already bought the tickets.

Xiao Li: Really? That's awesome! How about Sunday?

Xiao Wang: Sunday I'm going to rest at home and prepare for the exam on Monday.

Xiao Li: Me too. Then let's meet Saturday!

Xiao Wang: Deal, be there or be square!

## Grammar Notes

1. **想 as a modal verb**: 我想去打羽毛球 uses 想 (xiǎng) to express a desire or intention, meaning "want to." The affirmative-negative question form 想不想 is used to ask "do you want to or not?" See [[grammar/modal-verbs]].

2. **了 indicating completion or change of state**: 我已经买好票了 uses 了 to mark a completed action, while 新开了一家餐厅 signals a new situation. 我很久没运动了 uses 没...了 to express that something has not happened for a long time. See [[grammar/le5-completion]].

3. **Time expressions as adverbials**: Time words like 星期六上午, 九点半, and 下午两点 are placed before the verb in Chinese, following the pattern Subject + Time + Verb. This reflects the broader principle that modifiers precede what they modify.

## Comprehension Questions

**1. 小王和小李星期六上午打算做什么？**

他们打算去学校旁边的体育馆打羽毛球。

**2. 他们中午打算去哪儿吃饭？为什么？**

他们打算去学校对面新开的四川餐厅吃饭，因为听说那里很好吃。

**3. 他们星期天要做什么？**

他们星期天都要在家休息，还要准备下周一的考试。
