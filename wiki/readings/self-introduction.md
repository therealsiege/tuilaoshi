# 自我介绍 Self Introduction

## Text

大家好！我叫李明，今年二十一岁。我是中国人，来自北京。现在我在大学学习英语，我觉得英语很有意思，但是有时候也很难。我家有四口人：爸爸、妈妈、姐姐和我。我爸爸是医生，妈妈是老师。姐姐已经工作了，她在一家公司上班。我喜欢看书和听音乐，周末的时候我常常和朋友一起打篮球。我也喜欢吃中国菜，特别是北京烤鸭。很高兴认识你们！

## Pinyin

Dàjiā hǎo! Wǒ jiào Lǐ Míng, jīnnián èrshíyī suì. Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén, lái zì Běijīng. Xiànzài wǒ zài dàxué xuéxí Yīngyǔ, wǒ juéde Yīngyǔ hěn yǒu yìsi, dànshì yǒu shíhou yě hěn nán. Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén: bàba, māma, jiějie hé wǒ. Wǒ bàba shì yīshēng, māma shì lǎoshī. Jiějie yǐjīng gōngzuò le, tā zài yì jiā gōngsī shàngbān. Wǒ xǐhuān kàn shū hé tīng yīnyuè, zhōumò de shíhou wǒ chángcháng hé péngyou yìqǐ dǎ lánqiú. Wǒ yě xǐhuān chī Zhōngguó cài, tèbié shì Běijīng kǎoyā. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐmen!

## Translation

Hello everyone! My name is Li Ming, and I am twenty-one years old this year. I am Chinese, from Beijing. Right now I am studying English at university. I think English is very interesting, but sometimes it is also very hard. There are four people in my family: my dad, my mom, my older sister, and me. My dad is a doctor, and my mom is a teacher. My sister is already working -- she works at a company. I like reading books and listening to music. On weekends I often play basketball with friends. I also like eating Chinese food, especially Peking duck. Nice to meet you all!

## Grammar Notes

1. **是 + Noun (copula sentences)**: The text uses 我是中国人 and 我爸爸是医生 to identify nationality and profession. See [[grammar/shi4-construction]].

2. **很 + Adjective (predicate adjectives)**: Patterns like 很有意思 and 很难 use 很 to link the subject to an adjective, serving as a neutral connector rather than meaning "very." See [[grammar/hen3-adjective]].

3. **Measure words**: 四口人 uses the measure word 口 (kǒu), specific to family members. 一家公司 uses 家 (jiā) for businesses and establishments. See [[grammar/measure-words]].
