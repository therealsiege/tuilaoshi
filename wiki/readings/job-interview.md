# 面试 Job Interview

## Text

上个月我在网上看到一家互联网公司的招聘广告，觉得那个职位很适合我，就把简历发了过去。没想到第二天就收到了面试通知，让我下周一上午十点去他们公司。

为了准备面试，我花了整整一个周末。我先把简历重新修改了一遍，又在网上查了很多关于那家公司的资料。面试前一天晚上，我把最正式的衬衫和西裤找了出来，熨好挂在衣架上。可是那天晚上我太紧张了，翻来覆去睡不着，到了两点多才迷迷糊糊地睡着了。

面试那天早上，我提前一个小时出门，坐地铁去公司。在地铁上我一直看手机上的面试技巧，心里不断地练习自我介绍。快到站的时候，我突然发现领带被我落在家里了，心里一下子更紧张了。

到了公司以后，前台的小姐让我在会议室等一下。面试官是一位四十多岁的女经理，她态度很友好。她先问了我的教育背景和工作经验，然后问我觉得自己最大的优点是什么。我说我不但有很强的学习能力，而且做事情非常认真负责。她点了点头，又问了几个专业问题。面试大概进行了四十分钟，比我想象的要顺利。

三天以后，我被通知去参加第二轮面试。我高兴极了！虽然还不知道最后的结果，但是至少第一步已经成功了。

## Translation

Last month I saw a job posting from an internet company online. I felt the position was a good fit for me, so I sent my resume over. To my surprise, I received an interview notification the very next day, telling me to come to their office at ten in the morning the following Monday.

To prepare for the interview, I spent an entire weekend. First I revised my resume from start to finish, then I looked up a lot of information about the company online. The night before the interview, I dug out my most formal dress shirt and slacks, ironed them, and hung them on a hanger. But that night I was too nervous -- I tossed and turned and couldn't fall asleep, finally dozing off sometime after two.

The morning of the interview, I left an hour early and took the subway to the company. On the subway I kept reading interview tips on my phone and constantly rehearsed my self-introduction in my head. Just before reaching my stop, I suddenly realized I had left my tie at home, which made me even more nervous.

After arriving at the company, the receptionist asked me to wait in the conference room. The interviewer was a female manager in her forties, and her manner was very friendly. She first asked about my educational background and work experience, then asked what I thought my greatest strength was. I said that not only do I have strong learning ability, but I am also very conscientious and responsible in my work. She nodded and asked a few more technical questions. The interview lasted about forty minutes and went more smoothly than I had imagined.

Three days later, I was notified to attend a second round of interviews. I was overjoyed! Although I don't yet know the final result, at least the first step was a success.

## Grammar Notes

1. **把 construction**: The 把 structure is used throughout to show deliberate disposal or handling of objects: 把简历发了过去 (sent the resume over), 把简历重新修改了一遍 (revised the resume), 把衬衫和西裤找了出来 (dug out the shirt and pants). The pattern is Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + Complement, emphasizing what was done to the object. See [[grammar/ba3-construction]].

2. **被 passive**: Two instances show the passive voice: 领带被我落在家里了 (the tie was left at home by me) and 我被通知去参加第二轮面试 (I was notified to attend a second round). 被 marks the receiver of the action as the subject. See [[grammar/bei4-passive]].

3. **不但...而且 (not only...but also)**: The sentence 我不但有很强的学习能力，而且做事情非常认真负责 uses this conjunction to add emphasis by listing two positive qualities. The second clause adds to and strengthens the first. See [[grammar/budan-erqie]].
