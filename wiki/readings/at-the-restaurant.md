# 在餐厅 At the Restaurant

## Text

服务员：两位，里面请坐。这是菜单，请先看看。

小张：谢谢。小陈，你想吃什么？

小陈：我先看看。这家餐厅的菜看起来都不错。你来过这里吗？

小张：来过一次。上次我点了宫保鸡丁，味道特别好。

小陈：那我也要一个宫保鸡丁。你再点别的吧。

小张：好，再来一个麻婆豆腐和一个西红柿炒鸡蛋。你觉得够不够？

小陈：再要一个青菜汤吧，今天有点儿冷，喝点儿热汤舒服。

小张：好主意。服务员，我们点菜。

服务员：好的，请说。

小张：一个宫保鸡丁、一个麻婆豆腐、一个西红柿炒鸡蛋，再来一个青菜汤。

服务员：好的。请问你们要什么饮料？

小陈：来两瓶啤酒吧。

服务员：不好意思，啤酒卖完了。可乐和果汁还有。

小陈：那就来两杯果汁吧。

服务员：好的，请稍等。

（过了二十分钟）

服务员：你们的菜齐了，请慢用。

小张：这个宫保鸡丁跟上次一样好吃！

小陈：麻婆豆腐太辣了！但是真的很好吃，我吃得停不下来。

小张：哈哈，下次我们再来！

小陈：一定！服务员，买单。

服务员：一共一百二十八块。

小张：我来付吧，今天我请客。

小陈：那下次我请你！

## Translation

Waiter: Table for two, please come in and have a seat. Here is the menu. Please take a look first.

Xiao Zhang: Thanks. Xiao Chen, what would you like to eat?

Xiao Chen: Let me take a look. The dishes at this restaurant all look pretty good. Have you been here before?

Xiao Zhang: Once. Last time I ordered Kung Pao Chicken and it tasted really good.

Xiao Chen: Then I'll have the Kung Pao Chicken too. You order some other things.

Xiao Zhang: Okay, let's also get a Mapo Tofu and a Tomato and Egg Stir-fry. Do you think that's enough?

Xiao Chen: Let's also get a vegetable soup. It's a bit cold today, and some hot soup would feel nice.

Xiao Zhang: Good idea. Waiter, we'd like to order.

Waiter: Sure, go ahead.

Xiao Zhang: One Kung Pao Chicken, one Mapo Tofu, one Tomato and Egg Stir-fry, and one vegetable soup.

Waiter: Okay. What would you like to drink?

Xiao Chen: Two bottles of beer, please.

Waiter: Sorry, the beer is sold out. We still have cola and juice.

Xiao Chen: Then two glasses of juice.

Waiter: All right, one moment please.

(Twenty minutes later)

Waiter: All your dishes are here. Enjoy your meal.

Xiao Zhang: This Kung Pao Chicken is just as good as last time!

Xiao Chen: The Mapo Tofu is so spicy! But it really is delicious -- I can't stop eating it.

Xiao Zhang: Ha, let's come back next time!

Xiao Chen: Definitely! Waiter, the check please.

Waiter: The total is 128 yuan.

Xiao Zhang: I'll pay. My treat today.

Xiao Chen: Then next time it's on me!

## Grammar Notes

1. **过 for past experience**: 你来过这里吗 and 来过一次 use the experiential aspect marker 过 (guo) to indicate that something has happened at least once before. The response pattern with 一次 (once) specifies how many times. See [[grammar/guo4-experience]].

2. **太...了 for excess**: 太辣了 uses the pattern 太 + Adjective + 了 to express that something is excessively or overwhelmingly a certain quality. Despite the literal meaning of "too (much)," this pattern can sometimes express enthusiastic approval (太好了). See [[grammar/hen3-adjective]] for comparison with neutral 很.

3. **来 as a colloquial "to get/bring"**: Phrases like 再来一个麻婆豆腐 and 来两瓶啤酒 use 来 (lái) in its informal restaurant sense meaning "bring us" or "let's have." This is standard ordering language in Chinese restaurants.

## Comprehension Questions

**1. 小张以前来过这家餐厅吗？他上次吃了什么？**

来过一次。他上次点了宫保鸡丁，觉得味道特别好。

**2. 他们为什么没有喝啤酒？**

因为啤酒卖完了，所以他们点了两杯果汁。

**3. 谁付了钱？他们怎么说的？**

小张付的钱，他说今天他请客。小陈说下次他请小张。
