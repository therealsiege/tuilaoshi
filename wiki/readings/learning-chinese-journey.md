# 学中文的故事 My Chinese Learning Journey

## Text

我叫大卫，是美国人。我学中文已经学了三年了。回想起来，这三年的经历就像坐过山车一样，有高兴的时候，也有想放弃的时候。

刚开始学中文的时候，最难的是声调。英语没有声调，所以我怎么也说不好四个声调。老师说"买"和"卖"不一样，可是我说得一模一样，同学们都笑了。写汉字也让我很头疼。从小我只写二十六个字母，突然要记住几千个汉字，每个字都有那么多笔画。我每天花两个小时练字，手都酸了，可是第二天又忘了一半。

学了一年以后，有一天我一个人去中国城吃饭。我用中文跟服务员点了菜，她居然听懂了！她还夸我中文说得不错。那是我第一次觉得学中文真的有用，从那以后我学习的动力大多了。

大三的时候，我去北京留学了一个学期。从美国到中国，从课本到真实生活，一切都不一样了。刚到北京的时候，出租车司机说话说得特别快，我一句也听不懂。可是住了两个月以后，我不但能听懂日常对话了，还能跟房东聊天聊半个小时。我每天从早到晚都在用中文，进步比在美国快得多。

现在我已经回到美国了，但是我坚持每天看中文新闻，跟中国朋友打视频电话。学中文不只是学一门语言，它让我看世界的方式完全不同了。以前我觉得世界上只有一种思考方式，学了中文以后才发现，从不同的语言可以看到不同的世界。这三年的路虽然走得很辛苦，但是每一步都值得。

## Translation

My name is David, and I'm American. I've been studying Chinese for three years now. Looking back, these three years have been like a roller coaster -- there were happy times, and times when I wanted to give up.

When I first started learning Chinese, the hardest part was the tones. English doesn't have tones, so no matter what I did, I couldn't get the four tones right. The teacher said "mǎi" (buy) and "mài" (sell) are different, but I pronounced them exactly the same, and the whole class laughed. Writing characters also gave me a headache. I'd only ever written twenty-six letters since I was little, and suddenly I had to memorize thousands of characters, each with so many strokes. I spent two hours every day practicing characters until my hand was sore, but by the next day I'd forgotten half of them.

After studying for a year, one day I went to Chinatown to eat by myself. I ordered food in Chinese with the server, and she actually understood me! She even complimented me, saying my Chinese was pretty good. That was the first time I felt learning Chinese was truly useful, and from then on my motivation was much stronger.

In my junior year, I went to Beijing to study abroad for a semester. From America to China, from textbooks to real life, everything was different. When I first arrived in Beijing, taxi drivers spoke so fast that I couldn't understand a single sentence. But after living there for two months, not only could I understand everyday conversation, I could even chat with my landlord for half an hour. Every day from morning to night I was using Chinese, and my progress was much faster than in America.

Now I'm back in the United States, but I persist in watching Chinese news every day and video-calling my Chinese friends. Learning Chinese isn't just learning a language -- it has completely changed the way I see the world. I used to think there was only one way to think, but after learning Chinese I discovered that different languages let you see different worlds. Although the road these three years has been tough, every step has been worth it.

## Grammar Notes

1. **Complement of degree (说得/走得)**: The text uses verb + 得 to describe how actions are performed: 说得一模一样 (pronounced them exactly the same), 说话说得特别快 (speaks extremely fast), 中文说得不错 (speaks Chinese pretty well), 走得很辛苦 (walked with difficulty). When the verb has an object, the verb is repeated: 说话说得快. See [[grammar/de-particles]].

2. **从...到 (from...to)**: This pattern marks ranges across space, time, or abstract concepts: 从美国到中国 (from America to China), 从课本到真实生活 (from textbooks to real life), 从早到晚 (from morning to night), 从不同的语言可以看到不同的世界 (from different languages one can see different worlds). See [[grammar/cong2-dao4]].

3. **Time duration with 了**: Expressing how long an action has been going on uses the pattern Verb + 了 + duration: 学了三年了 (have been studying for three years), 住了两个月 (lived there for two months), 花两个小时 (spend two hours). The double 了 in 学了三年了 indicates the action is still ongoing. See [[grammar/le5-completion]].
