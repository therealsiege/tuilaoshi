# 我的学校 My School

## Text

我的学校叫北方大学，在北京的西边。学校很大，里面有很多教学楼和一个很漂亮的花园。我们学校有三十个教室，每个教室里都有电脑和投影仪。教室很干净，也很明亮。

学校的图书馆在教学楼的后面，一共有五层。图书馆里有很多中文书和英文书。我每天下课以后都去图书馆看书，那里很安静，学习的时候不会被打扰。

我们的老师都很好。我的中文老师姓王，她教书教了十五年了。王老师说话很清楚，上课的时候总是很有耐心。同学们都很喜欢她。

我每天早上八点上课，下午四点半下课。上午有四节课，下午有两节课。中午十二点到一点半是午休时间，我们在学校的食堂吃午饭。食堂的饭菜很便宜，味道也不错。

我很喜欢我的学校。

## Translation

My school is called Northern University, located on the west side of Beijing. The school is very large. Inside there are many academic buildings and a beautiful garden. Our school has thirty classrooms, and every classroom has computers and a projector. The classrooms are very clean and bright.

The school library is behind the academic buildings and has five floors in total. The library has many Chinese books and English books. Every day after class I go to the library to read. It is very quiet there, and you won't be disturbed while studying.

Our teachers are all very good. My Chinese teacher's surname is Wang. She has been teaching for fifteen years. Teacher Wang speaks very clearly and is always very patient in class. All the classmates really like her.

I start class at 8:00 every morning and finish at 4:30 in the afternoon. There are four class periods in the morning and two in the afternoon. Noon from 12:00 to 1:30 is rest time -- we eat lunch in the school cafeteria. The food in the cafeteria is very cheap, and the taste is pretty good too.

I really like my school.

## Grammar Notes

1. **在 for location**: Phrases like 在北京的西边, 在教学楼的后面, and 在学校的食堂 use 在 (zài) to indicate where something is located. The pattern is Subject + 在 + Place. See [[grammar/zai4-progressive]] for the progressive aspect of 在, though here it functions as a location marker.

2. **很 + Adjective for description**: The text uses this pattern extensively: 很大, 很漂亮, 很干净, 很明亮, 很安静, 很便宜. These are neutral descriptive statements where 很 fills a structural role. See [[grammar/hen3-adjective]].

3. **Measure words throughout**: The text uses several measure words: 个 for classrooms (三十个教室), 层 for floors (五层), 节 for class periods (四节课). Each noun requires its specific classifier. See [[grammar/measure-words]].

## Comprehension Questions

**1. 学校的图书馆在哪儿？有几层？**

图书馆在教学楼的后面，一共有五层。

**2. 王老师教了多少年书？同学们觉得她怎么样？**

王老师教了十五年书。同学们都很喜欢她，因为她说话很清楚，上课很有耐心。

**3. 学生们每天上午和下午分别有几节课？中午做什么？**

上午有四节课，下午有两节课。中午十二点到一点半是午休时间，他们在食堂吃午饭。
