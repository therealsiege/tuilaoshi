# 中国节日 Chinese Holidays

## Text

中国有很多传统节日，其中最重要的是春节。春节是中国的农历新年，一般在一月底或者二月初。因为春节是全家团圆的日子，所以不管多远，人们都要回家过年。每年春节前，中国会出现世界上最大的人口流动，大家把这叫做"春运"。

春节前几天，每家每户都忙着准备过年。人们打扫房子，因为他们觉得这样可以把旧的一年的坏运气扫走。虽然现在很多年轻人喜欢在网上买年货，但是很多老人还是喜欢亲自去市场挑选。大人们一边准备年夜饭，一边跟家人聊天，厨房里充满了欢笑声。

除夕那天晚上，全家人围坐在一起吃年夜饭。北方人一般吃饺子，因为饺子的形状像古代的金元宝，代表着新的一年会发财。南方人的年夜饭菜式更丰富，鱼是必不可少的，因为"鱼"和"余"的发音一样，意思是年年有余。吃完饭以后，一家人一边看春节联欢晚会，一边包饺子，等待新年的到来。

大年初一早上，孩子们穿上新衣服，给长辈拜年。长辈们会给孩子们红包，红包里面装着压岁钱。虽然现在很多人用手机发红包，但是传统的纸质红包还是更有过年的气氛。

春节期间，到处都能看到红色的灯笼和对联。人们一边放鞭炮，一边互相说"新年快乐"和"恭喜发财"。虽然有些城市因为安全原因已经禁止放鞭炮了，但是春节的热闹气氛一点都没有减少。

## Translation

China has many traditional holidays, and the most important one is the Spring Festival. The Spring Festival is the Chinese Lunar New Year, usually falling at the end of January or the beginning of February. Because the Spring Festival is a time for family reunion, no matter how far away they are, people all want to go home for the New Year. Every year before Spring Festival, China sees the world's largest population movement, which people call "Chunyun" (the Spring Festival travel rush).

A few days before Spring Festival, every household is busy preparing for the New Year. People clean their houses because they believe this can sweep away the bad luck of the old year. Although many young people nowadays like to buy New Year goods online, many older people still prefer to go to the market and select things in person. Adults prepare the New Year's Eve dinner while chatting with family, and the kitchen fills with the sound of laughter.

On the evening of New Year's Eve, the whole family sits together to eat the New Year's Eve dinner. Northerners generally eat dumplings, because the shape of dumplings resembles ancient gold ingots, symbolizing wealth in the new year. Southerners have a more diverse New Year's Eve dinner, and fish is essential, because the word for "fish" (yu) sounds the same as the word for "surplus" (yu), meaning prosperity year after year. After the meal, the family watches the Spring Festival Gala while wrapping dumplings, waiting for the arrival of the new year.

On the morning of New Year's Day, children put on new clothes and pay New Year's visits to their elders. Elders give children red envelopes containing lucky money. Although many people now send red envelopes by phone, traditional paper red envelopes still feel more festive.

During the Spring Festival, red lanterns and spring couplets can be seen everywhere. People set off firecrackers while saying "Happy New Year" and "Wishing you prosperity" to each other. Although some cities have already banned firecrackers for safety reasons, the lively atmosphere of Spring Festival has not diminished in the slightest.

## Grammar Notes

1. **因为...所以 (because...therefore)**: The text uses this paired conjunction several times: 因为春节是全家团圆的日子，所以不管多远，人们都要回家过年. Chinese formally marks both the cause and the effect, unlike English which typically uses only "because." Either half can be omitted in casual speech, but the full pair appears in written Chinese. See [[grammar/cong2-dao4]] for another paired-conjunction structure.

2. **虽然...但是 (although...but)**: Used three times in the text, for example: 虽然现在很多年轻人喜欢在网上买年货，但是很多老人还是喜欢亲自去市场挑选. Chinese requires both "although" and "but" together, which feels redundant in English. The 还是 (still) often reinforces the contrast in the second clause.

3. **一边...一边 (doing X while doing Y)**: Appears three times: 一边准备年夜饭，一边跟家人聊天 describes two simultaneous actions. Both actions must be ongoing and performed by the same subject. This pattern emphasizes that both activities happen at the same time. See [[grammar/zai4-progressive]] for another way to express ongoing actions.

## Comprehension Questions

**1. 为什么每年春节前中国会出现很大的人口流动？**

因为春节是全家团圆的日子，不管多远，人们都要回家过年，所以会出现世界上最大的人口流动，这叫做"春运"。

**2. 北方人和南方人的年夜饭有什么不同？**

北方人一般吃饺子，因为饺子的形状像金元宝，代表新年会发财。南方人的菜式更丰富，鱼是必不可少的，因为"鱼"和"余"发音一样，意思是年年有余。

**3. 传统的春节和现代的春节有哪些变化？**
