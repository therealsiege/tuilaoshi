# 网购还是逛街 Online Shopping or In-Store Shopping

## Text

现在越来越多的人喜欢网购。打开手机，什么东西都能买到，不用出门，不用排队，坐在家里等快递就行了。可是也有很多人觉得，逛街的乐趣是网购代替不了的。那么，网购和逛街到底哪个更好呢？

说到方便，网购确实不如逛街麻烦。除了不用出门以外，网购还可以随时比较不同商店的价格，很容易就能找到最便宜的。而且很多网店经常打折，价格比实体店便宜不少。现在快递也越来越快了，上午下单，下午就能送到，有的甚至两个小时就到了。

虽然网购有这么多好处，但是逛街也有网购比不上的优点。买衣服就是一个很好的例子。在网上看到的衣服，颜色、大小跟实际拿到手的常常不一样。你在网上看的是红色，收到的可能是橙色；你买的是M号，穿上以后发现太紧了。可是在商店里，你可以亲自试穿，看看合不合适，摸摸布料的质量怎么样。退换货也是一个问题。网购退货虽然免费，但是要重新包装、寄回去，等退款，很花时间。在商店里退换就简单多了。

除了实用的方面以外，逛街还有一个很重要的作用，那就是社交。周末和朋友一起去商场，一边逛一边聊天，累了就找个咖啡店坐下来休息。这种快乐，难道坐在家里点手机能得到吗？网购虽然效率高，但是少了这份人与人之间的温暖。

我觉得网购和逛街不是非此即彼的关系。日常用品、电子产品这些不需要试的东西，网购又快又方便；衣服、鞋子这些需要试穿的，还是去店里比较好。最理想的方式，大概就是把两者结合起来，既享受网购的便利，也不放弃逛街的乐趣。

## Translation

Nowadays more and more people enjoy online shopping. Open your phone and you can buy anything -- no need to go out, no need to stand in line, just sit at home and wait for the delivery. But many people also feel that the pleasure of in-store shopping is something online shopping simply cannot replace. So which is really better, online shopping or going to the stores?

When it comes to convenience, online shopping is certainly less hassle than going out. Besides not having to leave the house, you can compare prices across different stores at any time and easily find the cheapest option. Moreover, many online shops frequently have sales, with prices considerably lower than brick-and-mortar stores. Delivery is getting faster and faster too -- order in the morning and it can arrive by afternoon, sometimes even within two hours.

Although online shopping has so many advantages, in-store shopping has merits that online shopping can't match. Buying clothes is a perfect example. Clothes you see online often look different from what you actually receive in terms of color and size. What looks red on screen might turn out to be orange; you buy a medium, but after putting it on you discover it's too tight. But in a store, you can try things on in person, see whether they fit, and feel the quality of the fabric. Returns and exchanges are another issue. Although online returns are free, you have to repackage the item, ship it back, and wait for the refund -- it takes a lot of time. Returning things in a store is much simpler.

Beyond the practical aspects, going to the stores has another very important function: socializing. Going to the mall with friends on the weekend, browsing and chatting at the same time, sitting down in a coffee shop to rest when you're tired -- can you really get that kind of happiness by sitting at home tapping your phone? Online shopping may be efficient, but it lacks that warmth of human connection.

I think online shopping and in-store shopping are not an either-or choice. For everyday items and electronics -- things you don't need to try -- online shopping is fast and convenient. For clothes and shoes -- things you need to try on -- it's still better to go to a store. The ideal approach is probably to combine the two, enjoying the convenience of online shopping while not giving up the pleasure of browsing in person.

## Grammar Notes

1. **虽然...但是 and 除了...以外**: This passage layers two key paired conjunctions. 虽然网购有这么多好处，但是逛街也有网购比不上的优点 concedes a point before introducing a counterargument. 除了不用出门以外，网购还可以随时比较价格 means "besides not needing to go out, you can also compare prices." 除了...以外 adds items to an already-stated point when followed by 还/也. See [[grammar/suiran-danshi]] and [[grammar/chule-yiwai]].

2. **不如 (not as good as)**: The sentence 网购确实不如逛街麻烦 uses 不如 for a downward comparison meaning "online shopping is truly not as troublesome as going to the stores" (i.e., it's less of a hassle). A 不如 B + adjective means A doesn't reach B's level on that quality. See [[grammar/bi3-comparison]].

3. **Rhetorical question with 难道...吗**: The sentence 难道坐在家里点手机能得到吗？ uses 难道 to form a rhetorical question: "Can you really get that by sitting at home tapping your phone?" The expected answer is clearly "no." This structure adds emphasis and persuasive force to the argument.
