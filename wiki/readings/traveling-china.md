# 中国旅行 Traveling in China

## Text

寒假快到了，我和好朋友小美打算一起去旅行。我们讨论了很长时间，最后选了两个地方：西安和桂林。我想去西安，因为我对中国历史很感兴趣；小美想去桂林，因为她听说桂林的山水特别美。

我告诉小美，西安比桂林的历史景点多。在西安可以看兵马俑，还可以去古城墙上骑自行车。西安的小吃也很有名，羊肉泡馍跟北京烤鸭一样有名。小美说她知道西安很好，但是桂林的风景跟画一样美丽。坐船游漓江的时候，两边都是绿色的山，水也是清清的，简直像仙境一样。

我们比较了一下价格。从上海到西安的火车票比到桂林的便宜一点，但是西安的旅馆没有桂林的便宜。我们算来算去，两个地方的花费差不多。最后小美说："要不我们两个都去吧！先去西安，再去桂林。"我觉得这个主意太好了！

我们在网上买了高铁票。现在中国的高铁越来越方便了，从上海到西安只要六个小时。到了西安以后，我们看了兵马俑，那些两千多年前的士兵像真人一样站在那里，太震撼了！第三天我们坐火车去了桂林，漓江的风景果然名不虚传。这次旅行让我们都觉得，中国真的太大、太美了。

## Pinyin

Wǒ gàosu Xiǎo Měi, Xī'ān bǐ Guìlín de lìshǐ jǐngdiǎn duō. Zài Xī'ān kěyǐ kàn Bīngmǎyǒng, hái kěyǐ qù gǔ chéngqiáng shàng qí zìxíngchē. Xī'ān de xiǎochī yě hěn yǒumíng, yángròu pàomó gēn Běijīng kǎoyā yíyàng yǒumíng. Xiǎo Měi shuō tā zhīdào Xī'ān hěn hǎo, dànshì Guìlín de fēngjǐng gēn huà yíyàng měilì. Zuò chuán yóu Lí Jiāng de shíhou, liǎng biān dōu shì lǜsè de shān, shuǐ yě shì qīngqīng de, jiǎnzhí xiàng xiānjìng yíyàng.

Wǒmen bǐjiào le yíxià jiàgé. Cóng Shànghǎi dào Xī'ān de huǒchē piào bǐ dào Guìlín de piányi yìdiǎn, dànshì Xī'ān de lǚguǎn méiyǒu Guìlín de piányi. Wǒmen suàn lái suàn qù, liǎng ge dìfang de huāfèi chàbuduō. Zuìhòu Xiǎo Měi shuō: "Yào bù wǒmen liǎng ge dōu qù ba! Xiān qù Xī'ān, zài qù Guìlín." Wǒ juéde zhège zhǔyi tài hǎo le!

Wǒmen zài wǎng shàng mǎi le gāotiě piào. Xiànzài Zhōngguó de gāotiě yuè lái yuè fāngbiàn le, cóng Shànghǎi dào Xī'ān zhǐ yào liù ge xiǎoshí. Dào le Xī'ān yǐhòu, wǒmen kàn le Bīngmǎyǒng, nàxiē liǎng qiān duō nián qián de shìbīng xiàng zhēnrén yíyàng zhàn zài nàlǐ, tài zhènhàn le! Dì sān tiān wǒmen zuò huǒchē qù le Guìlín, Lí Jiāng de fēngjǐng guǒrán míngbùxūchuán. Zhè cì lǚxíng ràng wǒmen dōu juéde, Zhōngguó zhēn de tài dà, tài měi le.

## Translation

Winter break was approaching, and my good friend Xiao Mei and I were planning to travel together. We discussed it for a long time and narrowed it down to two places: Xi'an and Guilin. I wanted to go to Xi'an because I'm very interested in Chinese history; Xiao Mei wanted to go to Guilin because she'd heard the scenery there is especially beautiful.

I told Xiao Mei that Xi'an has more historical sites than Guilin. In Xi'an you can see the Terracotta Warriors, and you can also ride a bicycle on top of the ancient city wall. Xi'an's street food is also famous -- lamb pao mo is as well-known as Peking duck. Xiao Mei said she knew Xi'an was great, but Guilin's scenery is as beautiful as a painting. When you take a boat down the Li River, there are green mountains on both sides, and the water is crystal clear -- it's practically like a fairyland.

We compared the prices. Train tickets from Shanghai to Xi'an are a little cheaper than to Guilin, but hotels in Xi'an aren't as cheap as in Guilin. After calculating back and forth, the total cost for both places was about the same. Finally Xiao Mei said, "How about we go to both! Xi'an first, then Guilin." I thought that was a fantastic idea!

We bought high-speed rail tickets online. China's high-speed rail is getting more and more convenient nowadays -- it only takes six hours from Shanghai to Xi'an. After arriving in Xi'an, we saw the Terracotta Warriors. Those soldiers from over two thousand years ago stand there looking like real people -- it was absolutely stunning! On the third day we took a train to Guilin, and the Li River scenery truly lived up to its reputation. This trip made us both feel that China is truly vast and beautiful.

## Grammar Notes

1. **比 comparison**: The text uses 比 to make direct comparisons: 西安比桂林的历史景点多 (Xi'an has more historical sites than Guilin) and 火车票比到桂林的便宜一点 (train tickets are a little cheaper than to Guilin). The basic pattern is A 比 B + adjective (+ specific degree). See [[grammar/bi3-comparison]].

2. **跟...一样 (the same as)**: This pattern draws equivalences: 羊肉泡馍跟北京烤鸭一样有名 (lamb pao mo is as famous as Peking duck) and 风景跟画一样美丽 (the scenery is as beautiful as a painting). The structure is A 跟 B 一样 (+ adjective). See [[grammar/bi3-comparison]].

3. **越来越 (more and more)**: The phrase 中国的高铁越来越方便了 (China's high-speed rail is getting more and more convenient) uses 越来越 + adjective to express a trend of increasing change over time. See [[grammar/yue-lai-yue]].

## Comprehension Questions

**1. 为什么两个人一开始选不出去哪里旅行？**

因为他们想去的地方不一样。一个想去西安看历史景点，一个想去桂林看美丽的风景。

**2. 两个地方的旅行花费有什么不同？**

从上海到西安的火车票比到桂林的便宜一点，但是西安的旅馆没有桂林的便宜。算来算去两个地方的花费差不多。

**3. 他们最后是怎么决定的？去了以后感觉怎么样？**

他们决定两个地方都去，先去西安再去桂林。去了以后觉得兵马俑很震撼，漓江的风景名不虚传，中国又大又美。
