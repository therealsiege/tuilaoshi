# 谲 Deceitful, cunning, strange

- **Character**: 谲
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: deceitful, cunning, strange
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 诡谲 (guǐ jué) — crafty, cunning
- 谲诈 (jué zhà) — crafty and deceitful

## Example Sentences

- 他的手段十分诡谲。(Tā de shǒu duàn shí fēn guǐ jué.) — His methods are very cunning.

## Notes

Literary term for deceptiveness or strange cunning
