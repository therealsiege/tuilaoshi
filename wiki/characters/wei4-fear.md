# 畏 To Fear

- **Character**: 畏
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: to fear, to dread
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 畏惧 (wèi jù) — to fear
- 敬畏 (jìng wèi) — to revere

## Example Sentences

- 我们要敬畏自然。(Wǒ men yào jìng wèi zì rán.) — We should revere nature.

## Notes

畏 shows a tiger 虎 (top portion) over field 田, representing the fear farmers had of tigers.
