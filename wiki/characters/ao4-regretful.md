# 懊 Regretful

- **Character**: 懊
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: regretful, remorseful
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 懊悔 (ào huǐ) — to regret
- 懊恼 (ào nǎo) — annoyed

## Example Sentences

- 他懊悔不已。(Tā ào huǐ bù yǐ.) — He regrets it deeply.

## Notes

懊 has heart 忄 with 奥 (profound), heart deeply troubled by regret.
