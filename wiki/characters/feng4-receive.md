# 奉 To Receive Respectfully

- **Character**: 奉
- **Pinyin**: fèng
- **Meaning**: to receive respectfully, to offer
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奉献 (fèng xiàn) — to devote, to dedicate
- 信奉 (xìn fèng) — to believe in

## Example Sentences

- 他把一生都奉献给了教育事业。(Tā bǎ yī shēng dōu fèng xiàn gěi le jiào yù shì yè.) — He devoted his whole life to education.

## Notes

奉 shows two hands 手 offering something upward, representing respectful service or dedication.
