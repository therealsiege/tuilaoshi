# 壶 Pot, kettle, jug

- **Character**: 壶
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: pot, kettle, jug
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 茶壶 (chá hú) — teapot
- 水壶 (shuǐ hú) — kettle

## Example Sentences

- 请给我倒杯茶壶里的茶。(Qǐng gěi wǒ dào bēi chá hú lǐ de chá.) — Please pour me a cup of tea from the teapot.

## Notes

Vessel with spout for liquids
