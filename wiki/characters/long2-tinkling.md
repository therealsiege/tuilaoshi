# 珑 Tinkling of jade, exquisite

- **Character**: 珑
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: tinkling of jade, exquisite
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 玲珑 (líng lóng) — exquisite, delicate
- 八面玲珑 (bā miàn líng lóng) — smooth and slick

## Example Sentences

- 这件玲珑的工艺品令人赞叹。(Zhè jiàn líng lóng de gōng yì pǐn lìng rén zàn tàn.) — This exquisite handicraft is admirable.

## Notes

Describes delicate craftsmanship or clever social skills.
