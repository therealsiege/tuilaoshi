# 爆 To Explode

- **Character**: 爆
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: to explode, to burst
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 爆炸 (bào zhà) — to explode
- 爆发 (bào fā) — to erupt

## Example Sentences

- 火山爆发了。(Huǒ shān bào fā le.) — The volcano erupted.

## Notes

爆 combines fire 火 and 暴 (violent), representing violent explosion or bursting.
