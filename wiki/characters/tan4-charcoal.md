# 炭 Charcoal

- **Character**: 炭
- **Pinyin**: tàn
- **Meaning**: charcoal, carbon
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 雪中送炭 (xuě zhōng sòng tàn) — help in time of need
- 木炭 (mù tàn) — charcoal

## Example Sentences

- 朋友在困难时雪中送炭。(Péngyǒu zài kùnnan shí xuě zhōng sòng tàn.) — A friend sends charcoal in the snow during hard times.

## Notes

Used in 雪中送炭: providing charcoal during a snowstorm; timely assistance when most needed.
