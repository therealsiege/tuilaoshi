# 搓 To Rub

- **Character**: 搓
- **Pinyin**: cuō
- **Meaning**: to rub, to scrub with hands
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 搓手 (cuō shǒu) — to rub hands
- 搓澡 (cuō zǎo) — to scrub in bath

## Example Sentences

- 他搓着手取暖。(Tā cuō zhe shǒu qǔ nuǎn.) — He rubbed his hands to keep warm.

## Notes

搓 hand 扌 plus 差 (difference), rubbing motion.
