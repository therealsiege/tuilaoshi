# 恐 To fear, dread, perhaps

- **Character**: 恐
- **Pinyin**: kǒng
- **Meaning**: to fear, dread, perhaps
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恐怖 (kǒng bù) — terror, horrible
- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear, dread

## Example Sentences

- 他对黑暗感到恐惧。(Tā duì hēi'àn gǎndào kǒngjù.) — He feels fear of the dark.

## Notes

Core character for fear and dread, also used as 'perhaps' in formal contexts.
