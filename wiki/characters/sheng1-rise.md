# 升 To Rise

- **Character**: 升
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: to rise, to hoist, liter, measure word
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 上升 (shàng shēng) — to rise
- 升级 (shēng jí) — to upgrade

## Example Sentences

- 太阳从东方升起。(Tài yáng cóng dōng fāng shēng qǐ.) — The sun rises from the east.

## Notes

升 means to rise or ascend. Also measure word for liters. Used in上升 (rising), 升旗 (raise flag).
