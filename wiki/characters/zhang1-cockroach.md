# 蟑 Cockroach

- **Character**: 蟑
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: cockroach
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 蟑螂 (zhāng láng) — cockroach
- 小蟑螂 (xiǎo zhāng láng) — small cockroach

## Example Sentences

- 我最怕蟑螂。(Wǒ zuì pà zhāngláng.) — I'm most afraid of cockroaches.

## Notes

Always used with 螂 as 蟑螂. Common household pest. Insect radical.
