# 轴 Axis, axle, shaft

- **Character**: 轴
- **Pinyin**: zhóu
- **Meaning**: axis, axle, shaft
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 轴心 (zhóu xīn) — axis, core
- 中轴 (zhōng zhóu) — central axis

## Example Sentences

- 地球绕着地轴自转。(Dì qiú rào zhe dì zhóu zì zhuàn.) — The Earth rotates around its axis.

## Notes

Technical term for axis or shaft. Used in geometry (坐标轴), mechanics, and anatomy.
