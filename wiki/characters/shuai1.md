# 衰 Decline, weak

- **Character**: 衰
- **Pinyin**: shuāi
- **Meaning**: decline, weak
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 衰退 (shuāi tuì) — decline, recession
- 衰落 (shuāi luò) — decline, wane

## Example Sentences

- 经济出现衰退迹象。(Jīng jì chū xiàn shuāi tuì jì xiàng.) — The economy shows signs of recession.

## Notes

衰败 means decline/decay. Opposite of 兴/旺.
