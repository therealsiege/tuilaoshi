# 窗 Window

- **Character**: 窗
- **Pinyin**: chuāng
- **Meaning**: window
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 窗户 (chuāng hu) — window
- 窗口 (chuāng kǒu) — window, aperture

## Example Sentences

- 阳光从窗户洒进房间。(Yáng guāng cóng chuāng hu sǎ jìn fáng jiān.) — Sunlight streams through the window into the room.

## Notes

Basic architectural element; also used metaphorically.
