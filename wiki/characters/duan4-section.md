# 段 Section

- **Character**: 段
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: section, paragraph, segment
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 一段 (yī duàn) — a section
- 段落 (duàn luò) — paragraph

## Example Sentences

- 这段路很难走。(Zhè duàn lù hěn nán zǒu.) — This stretch of road is difficult to walk.

## Notes

段 is a measure word for sections of road, time, text, or stories.
