# 淙 Gurgling sound of water

- **Character**: 淙
- **Pinyin**: cóng
- **Meaning**: gurgling sound of water
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淙淙 (cóng cóng) — gurgling
- 泉水淙淙 (quán shuǐ cóng cóng) — spring water gurgling

## Example Sentences

- 泉水淙淙流淌。(Quán shuǐ cóng cóng liú tǎng.) — Spring water gurgles along.

## Notes

Onomatopoeia for flowing water
