# 旋 To Revolve

- **Character**: 旋
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: to revolve, to spin
- **Radical**: 方 (direction)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 旋转 (xuán zhuǎn) — to rotate
- 螺旋 (luó xuán) — spiral

## Example Sentences

- 地球围绕太阳旋转。(Dì qiú wéi rào tài yáng xuán zhuǎn.) — The Earth revolves around the Sun.

## Notes

旋 combines 方 (direction) and 疋 (foot), representing circular movement in all directions.
