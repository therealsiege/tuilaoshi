# 蜕 To Molt

- **Character**: 蜕
- **Pinyin**: tuì
- **Meaning**: to molt, to shed skin
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蜕变 (tuì biàn) — to transform
- 蜕皮 (tuì pí) — to shed skin

## Example Sentences

- 蛇每年蜕一次皮。(Shé měi nián tuì yí cì pí.) — Snakes shed their skin once a year.

## Notes

蜕 has insect 虫 with 兑 (to exchange), casting off old skin.
