# 挂 To Hang

- **Character**: 挂
- **Pinyin**: guà
- **Meaning**: to hang, to put up, to register
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挂号 (guà hào) — to register
- 挂念 (guà niàn) — to worry about

## Example Sentences

- 请在医院挂号。(Qǐng zài yī yuàn guà hào.) — Please register at the hospital.

## Notes

挂 simplified from 掛. Hand 扌 hanging something up.
