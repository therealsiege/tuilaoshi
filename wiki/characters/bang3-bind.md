# 绑 To Bind

- **Character**: 绑
- **Pinyin**: bǎng
- **Meaning**: to bind, to tie, to truss up
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 捆绑 (kǔn bǎng) — to tie up
- 绑架 (bǎng jià) — to kidnap

## Example Sentences

- 强盗把他绑起来了。(Qiáng dào bǎ tā bǎng qǐ lái le.) — The robbers tied him up.

## Notes

绑 means to bind or tie tightly. Common in绑架 (kidnapping) and捆绑 (tying up securely).
