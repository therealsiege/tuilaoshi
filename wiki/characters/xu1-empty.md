# 虚 Empty, void, false

- **Character**: 虚
- **Pinyin**: xū
- **Meaning**: empty, void, false
- **Radical**: 虍 (tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 虚无 (xū wú) — void, nothingness
- 虚假 (xū jiǎ) — false, fake

## Example Sentences

- 这些都是虚假的承诺。(Zhè xiē dōu shì xū jiǎ de chéng nuò.) — These are all false promises.

## Notes

Important in Taoist and Buddhist philosophy about emptiness
