# 石 Stone

- **Character**: 石
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: stone, rock
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 石头 (shí tou) — stone, rock
- 宝石 (bǎo shí) — gemstone

## Example Sentences

- 路上有很多石头。(Lù shàng yǒu hěn duō shí tou.) — There are many stones on the road.

## Notes

石 means stone or rock. Radical for stone-related characters. Used in石头 (stone), 化石 (fossil).
