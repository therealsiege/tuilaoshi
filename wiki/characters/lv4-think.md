# 虑 To think over, consider, worry, 慮 (traditional)

- **Character**: 虑
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: to think over, consider, worry, 慮 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 虍 (tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 考虑 (kǎo lǜ) — to consider, think over
- 忧虑 (yōu lǜ) — to worry, anxiety

## Example Sentences

- 我会认真考虑你的建议。(Wǒ huì rènzhēn kǎolǜ nǐ de jiànyì.) — I will seriously consider your suggestion.

## Notes

Can mean thoughtful consideration or worried contemplation.
