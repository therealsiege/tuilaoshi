# 捆 To tie, to bundle up

- **Character**: 捆
- **Pinyin**: kǔn
- **Meaning**: to tie, to bundle up
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捆绑 (kǔn bǎng) — to tie up, to bind
- 一捆 (yī kǔn) — a bundle

## Example Sentences

- 请把这些书捆起来。(Qǐng bǎ zhèxiē shū kǔn qǐlái.) — Please bundle up these books.

## Notes

Emphasizes tying things together firmly.
