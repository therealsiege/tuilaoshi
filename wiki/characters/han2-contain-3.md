# 含 To contain, hold in mouth

- **Character**: 含
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: to contain, hold in mouth
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 包含 (bāo hán) — to contain, include
- 含义 (hán yì) — meaning, implication

## Example Sentences

- 这句话含有讽刺意味。(Zhè jù huà hányǒu fěngcì yìwèi.) — This sentence contains sarcasm.

## Notes

Can mean physical containment in mouth or abstract inclusion.
