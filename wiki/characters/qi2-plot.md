# 畦 Plot, field, furrow

- **Character**: 畦
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: plot, field, furrow
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 田畦 (tián qí) — field plot
- 花畦 (huā qí) — flower bed

## Example Sentences

- 菜园被分成了几个小畦。(Cài yuán bèi fēn chéng le jǐ gè xiǎo qí.) — The vegetable garden was divided into several small plots.

## Notes

Rectangular plots of farmland separated by ridges. This organization facilitates irrigation and crop management.
