# 侣 Companion, associate, partner

- **Character**: 侣
- **Pinyin**: lǚ
- **Meaning**: companion, associate, partner
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 伴侣 (bàn lǚ) — companion, partner
- 情侣 (qíng lǚ) — lovers, couple

## Example Sentences

- 他们是一对情侣。(Tāmen shì yī duì qínglǚ.) — They are a couple.

## Notes

Often used for romantic partners or close companions.
