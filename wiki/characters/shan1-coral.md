# 珊 Coral

- **Character**: 珊
- **Pinyin**: shān
- **Meaning**: coral
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 珊瑚 (shān hú) — coral
- 珊瑚礁 (shān hú jiāo) — coral reef

## Example Sentences

- 珊瑚礁保护海洋生态。(Shān hú jiāo bǎo hù hǎi yáng shēng tài.) — Coral reefs protect marine ecosystems.

## Notes

珊 uses jade radical 玉, always paired with 瑚 to mean coral, valued as jewelry material.
