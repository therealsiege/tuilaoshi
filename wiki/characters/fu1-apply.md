# 敷 Apply, spread on, sufficient

- **Character**: 敷
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: apply, spread on, sufficient
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 敷衍 (fū yǎn) — to do sth. perfunctorily
- 外敷 (wài fū) — to apply externally

## Example Sentences

- 医生让我在伤口上外敷药膏。(Yī shēng ràng wǒ zài shāng kǒu shàng wài fū yào gāo.) — The doctor told me to apply ointment to the wound externally.

## Notes

Means to apply or spread. 敷衍 means to be perfunctory. 入不敷出 means expenses exceed income.
