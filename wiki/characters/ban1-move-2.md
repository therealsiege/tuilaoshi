# 搬 Move

- **Character**: 搬
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: move, carry
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 搬家 (bān jiā) — move house
- 搬运 (bānyùn) — transport

## Example Sentences

- 我们下个月要搬家。(Wǒmen xià gè yuè yào bān jiā.) — We're moving house next month.

## Notes

Moving objects or residence. Hand radical. 搬家 is very common life event. Essential practical vocabulary.
