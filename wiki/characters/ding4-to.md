# 订 To订阅

- **Character**: 订
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: to订阅, to revise, to订purchase
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 订阅 (dìng yuè) — to订阅
- 预订 (yù dìng) — to reserve

## Example Sentences

- 我订了一本杂志。(Wǒ dìng le yī běn zá zhì.) — I订阅d to a magazine.

## Notes

订 is simplified from 訂. Used for订阅s,订购s, and agreements.
