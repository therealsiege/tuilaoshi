# 膏 Ointment

- **Character**: 膏
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: ointment, paste
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 药膏 (yào gāo) — ointment, salve
- 软膏 (ruǎn gāo) — ointment
- 牙膏 (yá gāo) — toothpaste

## Example Sentences

- 在伤口上涂药膏。(Zài shāng kǒu shang tú yào gāo.) — Apply ointment to the wound.

## Notes

Topical paste or cream; external application.
