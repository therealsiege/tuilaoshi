# 盈 Full

- **Character**: 盈
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: full, surplus, profit
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 盈利 (yíng lì) — profit
- 充盈 (chōng yíng) — abundant

## Example Sentences

- 公司今年盈利了。(Gōng sī jīn nián yíng lì le.) — The company made a profit this year.

## Notes

盈 shows a vessel 皿 filled to overflowing, representing surplus or abundance.
