# 绢 Thin tough silk

- **Character**: 绢
- **Pinyin**: juàn
- **Meaning**: thin tough silk
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 绢本 (juàn běn) — painting on silk
- 绢花 (juàn huā) — silk flower

## Example Sentences

- 这幅画是绢本的。(Zhè fú huà shì juàn běn de.) — This painting is on silk.

## Notes

Thin, tough silk fabric used for painting and calligraphy.
