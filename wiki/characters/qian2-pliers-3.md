# 钳 Pliers, clamp

- **Character**: 钳
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: pliers, clamp
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钳子 (qián zi) — pliers
- 火钳 (huǒ qián) — tongs

## Example Sentences

- 用钳子夹住零件。(Yòng qián zi jiā zhù líng jiàn.) — Use pliers to grip the part.

## Notes

Used in 钳子 (pliers), tools and robotics
