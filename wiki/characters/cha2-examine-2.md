# 察 To Examine

- **Character**: 察
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: to examine, inspect, observe
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 观察 (guān chá) — to observe
- 考察 (kǎo chá) — to investigate

## Example Sentences

- 我们要仔细观察情况。(Wǒ men yào zǐ xì guān chá qíng kuàng.) — We need to carefully observe the situation.

## Notes

察 shows detailed examination under a roof, suggesting careful inspection.
