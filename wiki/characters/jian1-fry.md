# 煎 To fry in shallow oil, to decoct

- **Character**: 煎
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: to fry in shallow oil, to decoct
- **Radical**: 灬(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 煎蛋 (jiān dàn) — fried egg
- 煎药 (jiān yào) — to decoct herbal medicine

## Example Sentences

- 早餐我吃了煎蛋和面包。(Zǎocān wǒ chī le jiān dàn hé miànbāo.) — For breakfast I ate fried eggs and bread.

## Notes

Pan-frying with less oil than deep-frying; also for herbal medicine.
