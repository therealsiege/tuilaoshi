# 积 To Accumulate

- **Character**: 积
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to accumulate, to pile up, product (math)
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 积极 (jī jí) — active, positive
- 积累 (jī lěi) — to accumulate

## Example Sentences

- 我们要积极参加活动。(Wǒ men yào jī jí cān jiā huó dòng.) — We should actively participate in activities.

## Notes

积 shows grain piling up, representing accumulation. 积极 (active/positive) is very common.
