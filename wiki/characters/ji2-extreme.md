# 极 Extreme

- **Character**: 极
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: extreme, pole, very, extremely
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 积极 (jī jí) — active
- 太极 (tài jí) — taiji

## Example Sentences

- 这极其重要。(Zhè jí qí zhòng yào.) — This is extremely important.

## Notes

极 represents the roof beam (extreme point), meaning pole or extreme. Used as intensifier meaning 'very' or 'extremely'.
