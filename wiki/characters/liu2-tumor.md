# 瘤 Tumor

- **Character**: 瘤
- **Pinyin**: liú
- **Meaning**: tumor, lump
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 肿瘤 (zhǒng liú) — tumor
- 瘤子 (liú zi) — lump

## Example Sentences

- 这是良性肿瘤。(Zhè shì liáng xìng zhǒng liú.) — This is a benign tumor.

## Notes

瘤 has sickness 疒 with 留 (to stay), abnormal growth that stays.
