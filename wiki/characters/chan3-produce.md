# 产 To Produce

- **Character**: 产
- **Pinyin**: chǎn
- **Meaning**: to produce, product, property
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 产品 (chǎn pǐn) — product
- 生产 (shēng chǎn) — to produce

## Example Sentences

- 这个产品很受欢迎。(Zhè ge chǎn pǐn hěn shòu huān yíng.) — This product is very popular.

## Notes

产 is simplified from 產. 财产 = property, 房产 = real estate.
