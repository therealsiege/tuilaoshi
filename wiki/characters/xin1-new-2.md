# 新 New, fresh

- **Character**: 新
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: new, fresh
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 更新 (gēng xīn) — update
- 创新 (chuàng xīn) — innovation

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个创新的解决方案。(Zhè shì yī gè chuàng xīn de jiě jué fāng àn.) — This is an innovative solution.

## Notes

Used in 更新 (update), 创新 (innovation), 最新 (latest)
