# 砍 To Chop

- **Character**: 砍
- **Pinyin**: kǎn
- **Meaning**: to chop, to cut
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砍伐 (kǎn fá) — to fell trees
- 砍价 (kǎn jià) — to bargain

## Example Sentences

- 工人在砍树。(Gōng rén zài kǎn shù.) — Workers are cutting down trees.

## Notes

砍 combines stone 石 and 欠 (lack), representing chopping with stone tool.
