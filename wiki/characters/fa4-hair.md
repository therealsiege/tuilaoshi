# 发 Hair

- **Character**: 发
- **Pinyin**: fà
- **Meaning**: hair; to send out (fā)
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 头发 (tóu fa) — hair (on head)
- 理发 (lǐ fà) — to cut hair

## Example Sentences

- 她的头发很长。(Tā de tóu fa hěn cháng.) — Her hair is very long.

## Notes

发 simplified from 髮 (hair) and 發 (send). 头发 = hair on head. 理发 = haircut.
