# 南 South

- **Character**: 南
- **Pinyin**: nán
- **Meaning**: south
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 南方 (nán fāng) — the South
- 南京 (nán jīng) — Nanjing

## Example Sentences

- 他来自南方。(Tā lái zì nán fāng.) — He is from the South.

## Notes

南 pairs with 北 (north). Ancient Chinese maps often placed south at the top.
