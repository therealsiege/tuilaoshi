# 拆 To Take Apart

- **Character**: 拆
- **Pinyin**: chāi
- **Meaning**: to take apart, demolish
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拆除 (chāi chú) — to demolish, remove
- 拆开 (chāi kāi) — to take apart

## Example Sentences

- 这栋楼要拆了。(Zhè dòng lóu yào chāi le.) — This building is going to be demolished.

## Notes

拆 combines hand 扌 with 斥 (to split), representing taking things apart by hand.
