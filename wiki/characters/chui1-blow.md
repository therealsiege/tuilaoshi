# 吹 To Blow

- **Character**: 吹
- **Pinyin**: chuī
- **Meaning**: to blow, to play (wind instrument)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吹风 (chuī fēng) — wind blows
- 吹牛 (chuī niú) — to brag

## Example Sentences

- 他会吹笛子。(Tā huì chuī dí zi.) — He can play the flute.

## Notes

吹 combines mouth 口 and 欠 (to lack/yawn), representing blowing air from mouth.
