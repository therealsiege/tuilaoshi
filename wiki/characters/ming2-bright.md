# 明 Bright, clear, Ming Dynasty

- **Character**: 明
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: bright, clear, Ming Dynasty
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 明白 (míng bai) — to understand, clear
- 光明 (guāng míng) — bright, promising

## Example Sentences

- 我明白你的意思。(Wǒ míngbai nǐ de yìsi.) — I understand what you mean.

## Notes

Brightness, clarity of understanding, or Ming Dynasty.
