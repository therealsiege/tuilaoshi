# 予 To give, to grant

- **Character**: 予
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: to give, to grant
- **Radical**: 亅(hook)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 给予 (jǐ yǔ) — to give, to grant
- 授予 (shòu yǔ) — to award, to confer

## Example Sentences

- 政府给予他荣誉称号。(Zhèngfǔ jǐyǔ tā róngyù chēnghào.) — The government granted him an honorary title.

## Notes

Formal term for giving or granting, often used in writing.
