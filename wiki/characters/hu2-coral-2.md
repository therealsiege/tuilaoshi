# 瑚 Coral

- **Character**: 瑚
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: coral
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 珊瑚 (shān hú) — coral
- 珊瑚虫 (shān hú chóng) — coral polyp

## Example Sentences

- 珊瑚礁是海洋生态系统的重要组成部分。(Shānhú jiāo shì hǎiyáng shēngtài xìtǒng de zhòngyào zǔchéng bùfen.) — Coral reefs are an important part of marine ecosystems.

## Notes

Always used with 珊 as 珊瑚. Jade radical for precious material.
