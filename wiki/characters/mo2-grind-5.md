# 磨 To Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: to grind, to rub, to wear down, mill
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 折磨 (zhé mó) — to torment
- 磨练 (mó liàn) — to temper, to steel

## Example Sentences

- 她在磨刀。(Tā zài mó dāo.) — She is sharpening the knife.

## Notes

磨 can be mó (to grind, to sharpen) or mò (mill, millstone). As mó, used for grinding and sharpening.
