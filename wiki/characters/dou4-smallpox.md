# 痘 Smallpox, pox, pimple

- **Character**: 痘
- **Pinyin**: dòu
- **Meaning**: smallpox, pox, pimple
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 水痘 (shuǐ dòu) — chickenpox
- 天花 (tiān huā) — smallpox

## Example Sentences

- 青春痘困扰着很多年轻人。(Qīngchūn dòu kùnrǎo zhe hěnduō niánqīng rén.) — Acne troubles many young people.

## Notes

Pox diseases or pimples. Historically, smallpox (天花/痘) was a major cause of death before vaccination.
