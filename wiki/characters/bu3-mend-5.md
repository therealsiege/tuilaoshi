# 补 Mend, supplement, make up

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: mend, supplement, make up
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 补充 (bǔchōng) — supplement, replenish
- 补贴 (bǔtiē) — subsidy

## Example Sentences

- 需要补充说明一下。(Xūyào bǔchōng shuōmíng yīxià.) — Need to add a supplementary explanation.

## Notes

Common for supplementing, mending, or making up deficiencies.
