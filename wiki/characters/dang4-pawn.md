# 当 Pawn, serve as

- **Character**: 当
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: pawn, serve as
- **Radical**: 彐 (snout)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 典当 (diǎn dàng) — pawn
- 当铺 (dàng pù) — pawn shop

## Example Sentences

- 当铺可以换现金。(Dàng pù kě yǐ huàn xiàn jīn.) — Pawn shops can exchange for cash.

## Notes

Different pronunciation from 当 (dāng = serve as). 抵当 means mortgage.
