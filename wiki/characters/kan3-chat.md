# 侃 Chat, upright

- **Character**: 侃
- **Pinyin**: kǎn
- **Meaning**: chat, upright
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 侃侃而谈 (kǎnkǎn ér tán) — speak with confidence
- 侃大山 (kǎn dàshān) — chat, talk idly

## Example Sentences

- 他们侃侃而谈。(Tāmen kǎnkǎn ér tán.) — They spoke with confidence.

## Notes

Common in northern dialects for chatting; literary usage for upright speech.
