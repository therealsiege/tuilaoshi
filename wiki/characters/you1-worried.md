# 忧 Worried

- **Character**: 忧
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: worried, concerned
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忧虑 (yōulǜ) — worried
- 担忧 (dānyōu) — worry about

## Example Sentences

- 她很担忧孩子的健康。(Tā hěn dānyōu háizi de jiànkāng.) — She's very worried about her child's health.

## Notes

Deep concern. Heart radical. More formal than 愁. 担忧 is common compound. Literary flavor.
