# 帆 Sail, canvas

- **Character**: 帆
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: sail, canvas
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 帆船 (fān chuán) — sailboat
- 扬帆 (yáng fān) — to set sail

## Example Sentences

- 帆船在海面上扬帆起航。(Fān chuán zài hǎi miàn shàng yáng fān qǐ háng.) — The sailboat set sail on the sea.

## Notes

Means sail. 一帆风顺 is an idiom meaning smooth sailing. 帆布 means canvas.
