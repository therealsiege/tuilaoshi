# 东 East

- **Character**: 东
- **Pinyin**: dōng
- **Meaning**: east
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 东西 (dōng xī) — thing, stuff
- 东方 (dōng fāng) — the East

## Example Sentences

- 我想买些东西。(Wǒ xiǎng mǎi xiē dōng xī.) — I want to buy some things.

## Notes

东 is simplified from 東. 东西 literally means 'east-west' but colloquially means 'thing/stuff.'
