# 划 Plan, delimit

- **Character**: 划
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: plan, delimit
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 计划 (jì huà) — plan, project
- 规划 (guī huà) — plan, layout

## Example Sentences

- 需要制定营销计划。(Xū yào zhì dìng yíng xiāo jì huà.) — Need to formulate a marketing plan.

## Notes

策划 means plan/devise. 企划 means planning.
