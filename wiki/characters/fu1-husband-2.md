# 夫 Husband

- **Character**: 夫
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: husband, man, worker
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 丈夫 (zhàng fū) — husband
- 夫人 (fū rén) — Mrs., wife

## Example Sentences

- 她的丈夫很好。(Tā de zhàng fū hěn hǎo.) — Her husband is very nice.

## Notes

夫 shows a person (大) with a hairpin — an adult man. 功夫 = kung fu, skill.
