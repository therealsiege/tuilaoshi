# 掠 To Plunder

- **Character**: 掠
- **Pinyin**: lüè
- **Meaning**: to plunder, sweep past
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掠夺 (lüè duó) — to plunder, rob
- 掠过 (lüè guò) — to sweep past

## Example Sentences

- 一只鸟从头顶掠过。(Yī zhī niǎo cóng tóudǐng lüèguò.) — A bird swept past overhead.

## Notes

掠 combines hand 扌 with 京 (capital), suggesting seizing or plundering.
