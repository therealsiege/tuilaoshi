# 暴 Violent, cruel, sudden

- **Character**: 暴
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: violent, cruel, sudden
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 暴力 (bào lì) — violence
- 暴露 (bào lù) — expose, reveal

## Example Sentences

- 反对一切暴力行为。(Fǎn duì yī qiè bào lì xíng wéi.) — Oppose all violent behavior.

## Notes

Violence, cruelty, or sudden occurrence
