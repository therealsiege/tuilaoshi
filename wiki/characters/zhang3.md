# 掌 Palm

- **Character**: 掌
- **Pinyin**: zhǎng
- **Meaning**: palm, control
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 手掌 (shǒuzhǎng) — palm
- 掌握 (zhǎngwò) — master, grasp

## Example Sentences

- 他的手掌很大。(Tā de shǒuzhǎng hěn dà.) — His palms are very large.

## Notes

Means both palm and control/mastery. 掌握 is a key word meaning to master or grasp knowledge.
