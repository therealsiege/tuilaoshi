# 铜 Copper

- **Character**: 铜
- **Pinyin**: tóng
- **Meaning**: copper, bronze
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铜锣 (tóng luó) — bronze gong
- 青铜 (qīng tóng) — bronze

## Example Sentences

- 铜锣是用铜制成的。(Tóng luó shì yòng tóng zhì chéng de.) — Bronze gongs are made of copper.

## Notes

铜 means copper or bronze; many traditional instruments use bronze.
