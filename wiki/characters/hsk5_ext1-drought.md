# 旱 Drought, Dry Land, 干旱 Drought

- **Character**: 旱
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: drought, dry land, 干旱 drought
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 干旱 (gān hàn) — drought
- 旱灾 (hàn zāi) — drought disaster
- 抗旱 (kàng hàn) — combat drought

## Example Sentences

- 今年雨水少，发生了严重的干旱。(Jīnnián yǔshuǐ shǎo, fāshēng le yánzhòng de gānhàn.) — There was little rain this year, causing a serious drought.

## Notes

Weather and agricultural term for drought conditions. 干旱 is major agricultural disaster discussed in news and climate contexts.
