# 么 Question Particle

- **Character**: 么
- **Pinyin**: me
- **Meaning**: interrogative particle
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 什么 (shén me) — what
- 怎么 (zěn me) — how

## Example Sentences

- 怎么了？(Zěn me le?) — What happened?

## Notes

么 is a neutral-tone suffix appearing in question words: 什么, 怎么, 那么, 这么.
