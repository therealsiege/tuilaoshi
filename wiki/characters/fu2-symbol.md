# 符 Symbol

- **Character**: 符
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: symbol, mark, to符 符
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 符号 (fú hào) — symbol, mark
- 音符 (yīn fú) — musical note

## Example Sentences

- 这个符号代表什么？(Zhè ge fú hào dài biǎo shén me?) — What does this symbol represent?

## Notes

符 with bamboo radical originally meant tally stick, now for all kinds of symbols and marks.
