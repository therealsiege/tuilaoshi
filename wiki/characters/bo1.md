# 播 To sow, to broadcast, to spread

- **Character**: 播
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to sow, to broadcast, to spread
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 播放 (bō fàng) — to broadcast, to play
- 播种 (bō zhǒng) — to sow seeds

## Example Sentences

- 电视台正在播放新闻。(Diànshìtái zhèngzài bōfàng xīnwén.) — The TV station is broadcasting the news.

## Notes

Used for sowing seeds or broadcasting media.
