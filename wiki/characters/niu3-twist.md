# 扭 To Twist

- **Character**: 扭
- **Pinyin**: niǔ
- **Meaning**: to twist, to turn
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 扭伤 (niǔ shāng) — to sprain
- 扭转 (niǔ zhuǎn) — to reverse

## Example Sentences

- 他扭伤了脚踝。(Tā niǔ shāng le jiǎo huái.) — He sprained his ankle.

## Notes

扭 combines hand 扌 and 丑 (ugly), representing twisting or contorting movements.
