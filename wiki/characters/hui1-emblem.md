# 徽 Emblem, badge

- **Character**: 徽
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: emblem, badge
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 徽章 (huī zhāng) — badge, emblem
- 国徽 (guó huī) — national emblem

## Example Sentences

- 员工佩戴公司徽章。(Yuán gōng pèi dài gōng sī huī zhāng.) — Employees wear company badges.

## Notes

徽标 means logo/emblem. 校徽 is school badge.
