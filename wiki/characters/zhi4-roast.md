# 炙 Roast, broil, cauterize

- **Character**: 炙
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: roast, broil, cauterize
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 炙热 (zhì rè) — scorching hot
- 脍炙人口 (kuài zhì rén kǒu) — widely praised

## Example Sentences

- 他的作品脍炙人口。(Tā de zuò pǐn kuài zhì rén kǒu.) — His works are widely praised.

## Notes

Used in idioms about popularity and in TCM
