# 歌 Song

- **Character**: 歌
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: song, to sing
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 唱歌 (chàng gē) — to sing
- 歌手 (gē shǒu) — singer

## Example Sentences

- 她唱了一首歌。(Tā chàng le yī shǒu gē.) — She sang a song.

## Notes

歌 means song; combines 哥 (elder brother, phonetic) with 欠 (to lack breath).
