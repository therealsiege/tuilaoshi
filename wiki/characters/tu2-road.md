# 途 Road

- **Character**: 途
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: road, way, journey
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 半途而废 (bàn tú ér fèi) — give up halfway
- 途径 (tú jìng) — way, approach

## Example Sentences

- 做事不能半途而废。(Zuò shì bù néng bàn tú ér fèi.) — You shouldn't give up halfway.

## Notes

Used in 半途而废: the road or journey that is abandoned midway.
