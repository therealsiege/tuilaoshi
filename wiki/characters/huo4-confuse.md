# 惑 To Confuse

- **Character**: 惑
- **Pinyin**: huò
- **Meaning**: to confuse, to mislead
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 迷惑 (mí huò) — to confuse
- 疑惑 (yí huò) — doubt

## Example Sentences

- 他的话让我很疑惑。(Tā de huà ràng wǒ hěn yí huò.) — His words confused me.

## Notes

惑 combines 或 (perhaps) and 心 (heart), uncertain heart.
