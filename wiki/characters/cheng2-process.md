# 程 Process

- **Character**: 程
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: process, journey, program
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 程序 (chéng xù) — program, procedure
- 课程 (kè chéng) — course, curriculum

## Example Sentences

- 这个程序很复杂。(Zhè ge chéng xù hěn fù zá.) — This program is very complex.

## Notes

程 originally meant measuring distance, now extensively used in computing and process terminology.
