# 气 Air

- **Character**: 气
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: air, gas, spirit, angry
- **Radical**: 气 (steam)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 天气 (tiān qì) — weather
- 生气 (shēng qì) — angry

## Example Sentences

- 别生气了。(Bié shēng qì le.) — Don't be angry.

## Notes

气 is simplified from 氣. It represents vapor or breath rising. Central concept in Chinese philosophy and medicine.
