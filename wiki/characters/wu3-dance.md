# 舞 Dance

- **Character**: 舞
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: dance, to dance, to wield
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — to dance
- 舞台 (wǔ tái) — stage

## Example Sentences

- 她在舞台上表演。(Tā zài wǔ tái shàng biǎo yǎn.) — She performed on stage.

## Notes

舞 means dance or stage. 舞蹈 = dance (art form). 舞台 = stage.
