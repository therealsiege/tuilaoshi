# 频 Frequency, frequently

- **Character**: 频
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: frequency, frequently
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 频率 (pín lǜ) — frequency
- 频繁 (pín fán) — frequent

## Example Sentences

- 这种现象发生的频率很高。(Zhè zhǒng xiàn xiàng fā shēng de pín lǜ hěn gāo.) — This phenomenon occurs with high frequency.

Used in physics and statistics for frequency
