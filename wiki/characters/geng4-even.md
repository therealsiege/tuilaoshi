# 更 Even More

- **Character**: 更
- **Pinyin**: gèng
- **Meaning**: even more, still more
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 更好 (gèng hǎo) — even better
- 更加 (gèng jiā) — even more

## Example Sentences

- 今天比昨天更冷。(Jīn tiān bǐ zuó tiān gèng lěng.) — Today is even colder than yesterday.

## Notes

更 (gèng) means 'even more' in comparisons. Also read gēng meaning 'to change' or 'watch of the night.'
