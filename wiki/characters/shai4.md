# 晒 Sun

- **Character**: 晒
- **Pinyin**: shài
- **Meaning**: sun, dry in sun
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晒衣服 (shài yīfu) — hang clothes to dry
- 晒太阳 (shài tàiyáng) — sunbathe

## Example Sentences

- 她在院子里晒衣服。(Tā zài yuànzi lǐ shài yīfu.) — She's hanging clothes to dry in the yard.

## Notes

Common in daily life. 晒太阳 means to bask in the sun. The sun radical indicates sun-related action.
