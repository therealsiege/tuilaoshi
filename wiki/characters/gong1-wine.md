# 觥 Wine vessel, goblet

- **Character**: 觥
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: wine vessel, goblet
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 觥筹 (gōng chóu) — wine cups and chips
- 觥筹交错 (gōng chóu jiāo cuò) — lively drinking party

## Example Sentences

- 宴会上觥筹交错，热闹非凡。(Yàn huì shàng gōng chóu jiāo cuò, rè nào fēi fán.) — At the banquet, wine cups were passed around, very lively.

## Notes

Ancient wine vessel; used in idioms about drinking.
