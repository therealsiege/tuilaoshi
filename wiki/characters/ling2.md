# 灵 Spirit

- **Character**: 灵
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: spirit, clever
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灵魂 (líng hún) — soul
- 灵活 (líng huó) — flexible, agile

## Example Sentences

- 她的思维很灵活。(Tā de sī wéi hěn líng huó.) — Her thinking is very flexible.

## Notes

灵 originally showed rain falling on ritual objects, meaning spiritual efficacy. Now means spirit or clever.
