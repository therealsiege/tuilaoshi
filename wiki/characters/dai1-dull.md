# 呆 Dull, stupid, stay

- **Character**: 呆
- **Pinyin**: dāi
- **Meaning**: dull, stupid, stay
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 呆板 (dāi bǎn) — stiff, rigid
- 发呆 (fā dāi) — to stare blankly, daydream

## Example Sentences

- 他呆呆地看着窗外。(Tā dāi dāi de kàn zhe chuāng wài.) — He stared blankly out the window.

## Notes

Describes dullness or stupidity. 呆子 means fool. 发呆 means to be in a daze.
