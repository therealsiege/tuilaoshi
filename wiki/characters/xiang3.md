# 想 To Think/Want/Miss

- **Character**: 想
- **Pinyin**: xiǎng
- **Meaning**: to think, to want, to miss
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 想法 (xiǎngfǎ) — idea, way of thinking
- 想念 (xiǎngniàn) — to miss (someone)
- 思想 (sīxiǎng) — thought, ideology
- 理想 (lǐxiǎng) — ideal, dream
- 想象 (xiǎngxiàng) — to imagine, imagination

## Example Sentences

- 我想去中国。(Wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó.) — I want to go to China.
- 你在想什么？(Nǐ zài xiǎng shénme?) — What are you thinking about?
- 我很想家。(Wǒ hěn xiǎng jiā.) — I really miss home.

## Notes

想 is composed of 相 (xiāng, mutual/appearance) on top providing the sound, and 心 (xīn, heart) on the bottom providing the meaning — thoughts and desires originate from the heart-mind in Chinese philosophy. This character carries three distinct but related senses: cognitive ("to think"), volitional ("to want to"), and emotional ("to miss"). Context determines which meaning applies: 想 + verb = "want to"; 想 + noun (person/place) = "miss."
