# 珍 Precious, treasure, rare

- **Character**: 珍
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: precious, treasure, rare
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 珍贵 (zhēn guì) — precious, valuable
- 珍珠 (zhēn zhū) — pearl

## Example Sentences

- 博物馆收藏了许多珍贵的文物。(Bó wù guǎn shōu cáng le xǔ duō zhēn guì de wén wù.) — The museum houses many precious cultural relics.

## Notes
