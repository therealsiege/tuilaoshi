# 瞰 To overlook, survey from above

- **Character**: 瞰
- **Pinyin**: kàn
- **Meaning**: to overlook, survey from above
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 俯瞰 (fǔ kàn) — to overlook, bird's eye view
- 鸟瞰 (niǎo kàn) — bird's eye view

## Example Sentences

- 从山顶可以俯瞰整个城市。(Cóng shāndǐng kěyǐ fǔkàn zhěnggè chéngshì.) — From the mountain top, you can overlook the entire city.

## Notes

Primarily used in the compound 俯瞰 for viewing from a high vantage point.
