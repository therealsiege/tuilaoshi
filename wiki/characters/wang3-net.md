# 网 Net, network, 網 (traditional)

- **Character**: 网
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: net, network, 網 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 网 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 网络 (wǎng luò) — network, internet
- 视网膜 (shì wǎng mó) — retina

## Example Sentences

- 视网膜是眼睛的重要部分。(Shìwǎngmó shì yǎnjing de zhòngyào bùfen.) — The retina is an important part of the eye.

## Notes

Net or network, including anatomical retina.
