# 行 Go, walk, do, profession

- **Character**: 行
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: go, walk, do,行业
- **Radical**: 行 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执行 (zhíxíng) — execute, carry out
- 银行 (yínháng) — bank

## Example Sentences

- 立即执行命令。(Lìjí zhíxíng mìnglìng.) — Execute the order immediately.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: xíng (go, do) and háng (row, profession).
