# 顿 Pause, lag

- **Character**: 顿
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: pause, lag
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 卡顿 (kǎ dùn) — lag, freeze
- 停顿 (tíng dùn) — pause

## Example Sentences

- 游戏有点卡顿。(Yóu xì yǒu diǎn kǎ dùn.) — The game is a bit laggy.

## Notes

Used in 卡顿 (lag), performance issues terminology
