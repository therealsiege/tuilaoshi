# 送 Send, deliver, give

- **Character**: 送
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: send, deliver, give
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 送给 (sònggěi) — give to
- 押送 (yāsòng) — send under escort

## Example Sentences

- 我送你回家。(Wǒ sòng nǐ huíjiā.) — I'll see you home.

## Notes

Very common for sending and giving.
