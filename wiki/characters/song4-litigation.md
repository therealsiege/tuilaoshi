# 讼 Litigation, lawsuit

- **Character**: 讼
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: litigation, lawsuit
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 诉讼 (sùsòng) — lawsuit, litigation
- 讼事 (sòngshì) — lawsuit

## Example Sentences

- 诉讼程序很复杂。(Sùsòng chéngxù hěn fùzá.) — Litigation procedures are very complex.

## Notes

Formal legal term for litigation.
