# 觉 — Feel / Sleep

- **Character**: 觉
- **Pinyin**: jué (feel/sense) / jiào (sleep)
- **Meaning**: to feel, to sense; to sleep (jiào)
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 觉得 (juéde) — to feel, to think
- 感觉 (gǎnjué) — feeling, sensation
- 睡觉 (shuìjiào) — to sleep
- 发觉 (fājué) — to discover, to realize
- 自觉 (zìjué) — self-aware, conscious
- 午觉 (wǔjiào) — afternoon nap

## Example Sentences

- 我觉得今天很冷。(Wǒ juéde jīntiān hěn lěng.) — I feel it's very cold today.
- 你昨天晚上睡觉睡得好吗？(Nǐ zuótiān wǎnshang shuìjiào shuì de hǎo ma?) — Did you sleep well last night?
- 她突然发觉自己忘了带钥匙。(Tā tūrán fājué zìjǐ wàng le dài yàoshi.) — She suddenly realized she forgot to bring her keys.

## Notes

觉 is a multi-reading character: pronounced jué when meaning "to feel, perceive, become aware" and jiào when meaning "sleep, nap." The character contains 见 (see) as its radical, connecting to the idea of perception and awareness. The upper component 学 (learn) minus its bottom part suggests that awareness comes through a kind of inner seeing. 觉得 is one of the most common ways to express opinions and feelings in daily conversation.
