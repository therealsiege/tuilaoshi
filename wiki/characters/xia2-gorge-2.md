# 峡 Gorge

- **Character**: 峡
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: gorge, strait
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 海峡 (hǎi xiá) — strait
- 峡谷 (xiá gǔ) — canyon

## Example Sentences

- 台湾海峡很宽。(Tái wān hǎi xiá hěn kuān.) — The Taiwan Strait is very wide.

## Notes

峡 simplified from 峽. Mountain 山 with narrow gap, gorge.
