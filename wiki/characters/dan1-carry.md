# 担 To Carry

- **Character**: 担
- **Pinyin**: dān
- **Meaning**: to carry, to undertake; to worry (dān)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 担心 (dān xīn) — to worry
- 负担 (fù dān) — burden

## Example Sentences

- 别担心。(Bié dān xīn.) — Don't worry.

## Notes

担心 means to worry. As dàn it means a carrying pole or a unit of weight.
