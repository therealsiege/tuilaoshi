# 殊 Different

- **Character**: 殊
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: different, special, outstanding
- **Radical**: 歹 (bad)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 特殊 (tè shū) — special
- 殊途同归 (shū tú tóng guī) — different routes to same destination

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个特殊的情况。(Zhè shì yī gè tè shū de qíng kuàng.) — This is a special situation.

## Notes

殊 combines 歹 (death/bad) and 朱 (vermillion), representing something exceptional or standing out.
