# 脾 Spleen

- **Character**: 脾
- **Pinyin**: pí
- **Meaning**: spleen
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 脾脏 (pí zàng) — spleen
- 脾气 (pí qi) — temper, temperament

## Example Sentences

- 脾脏参与免疫功能。(Pí zàng cān yù miǎn yì gōng néng.) — The spleen participates in immune function.
