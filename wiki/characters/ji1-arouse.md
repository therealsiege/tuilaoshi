# 激 Intense

- **Character**: 激
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to arouse, intense, radical
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 激动 (jī dòng) — excited
- 激烈 (jī liè) — intense

## Example Sentences

- 比赛很激烈。(Bǐ sài hěn jī liè.) — The competition is very intense.

## Notes

激 shows water 氵 striking rocks, representing turbulent water hitting rocks, hence arousing intense feelings or radical actions.
