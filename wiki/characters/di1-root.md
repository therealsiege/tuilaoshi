# 氐 Root

- **Character**: 氐
- **Pinyin**: dī
- **Meaning**: Root constellation, foundation
- **Radical**: 氏(clan)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 氐宿 (dī xiù) — Root constellation
- 氐族 (dī zú) — Di ethnic group

## Example Sentences

- 氐宿是东方青龙七宿之一。(Dī xiù shì dōngfāng qīnglóng qī xiù zhī yī.) — Root is one of the seven mansions of the Eastern Azure Dragon.

## Notes

氐 (dī) refers to the Root constellation in the 28 lunar mansions. Also refers to the ancient Di people.
