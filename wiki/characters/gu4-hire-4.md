# 雇 Hire, employ

- **Character**: 雇
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: hire, employ
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 雇佣 (gù yōng) — employ, hire
- 雇主 (gù zhǔ) — employer

## Example Sentences

- 雇主必须遵守劳动法。(Gù zhǔ bì xū zūn shǒu láo dòng fǎ.) — Employers must comply with labor laws.

## Notes

Common in employment law. 雇员 means employee.
