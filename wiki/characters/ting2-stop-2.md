# 停 Stop

- **Character**: 停
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: to stop, halt
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 停赛 (tíng sài) — to suspend from competition
- 停止 (tíng zhǐ) — to stop

## Example Sentences

- 他因为犯规被停赛两场。(Tā yīnwèi fànguī bèi tíngsài liǎng chǎng.) — He was suspended for two games for fouling.

## Notes

Stopping; suspensions and halts
