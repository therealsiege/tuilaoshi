# 瑰 Magnificent, precious stone

- **Character**: 瑰
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: magnificent, precious stone
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 瑰宝 (guī bǎo) — treasure, gem
- 瑰丽 (guī lì) — magnificent, gorgeous

## Example Sentences

- 这座古城是建筑艺术的瑰宝。(Zhè zuò gǔ chéng shì jiàn zhù yì shù de guī bǎo.) — This ancient city is a treasure of architectural art.

## Notes

Often used to describe magnificent cultural treasures.
