# 漏 To leak, to divulge, to omit

- **Character**: 漏
- **Pinyin**: lòu
- **Meaning**: to leak, to divulge, to omit
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漏水 (lòu shuǐ) — to leak water
- 泄漏 (xiè lòu) — to leak, to divulge

## Example Sentences

- 屋顶漏雨了。(Wūdǐng lòu yǔ le.) — The roof is leaking rain.

## Notes

Can mean physical leaking or revealing secrets.
