# 友 Friend

- **Character**: 友
- **Pinyin**: yǒu
- **Meaning**: friend
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 朋友 (péng yǒu) — friend
- 友好 (yǒu hǎo) — friendly

## Example Sentences

- 我们是好朋友。(Wǒ men shì hǎo péng yǒu.) — We are good friends.

## Notes

友 shows two hands reaching together, symbolizing friendship and cooperation.
