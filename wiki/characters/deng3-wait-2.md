# 等 To Wait

- **Character**: 等
- **Pinyin**: děng
- **Meaning**: to wait, rank, equal, etc.
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 等待 (děng dài) — to wait
- 等等 (děng děng) — et cetera

## Example Sentences

- 请等一下。(Qǐng děng yī xià.) — Please wait a moment.

## Notes

等 means to wait. 等级 = grade, rank. 等于 = to equal.
