# 陷 Sink, fall into, trap

- **Character**: 陷
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: sink, fall into, trap
- **Radical**: 阜 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 塌陷 (tā xiàn) — cave in, subside
- 下陷 (xià xiàn) — sink, subside

## Example Sentences

- 地面塌陷形成了大坑。(Dì miàn tā xiàn xíng chéng le dà kēng.) — The ground subsidence formed a large pit.
