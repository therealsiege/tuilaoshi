# 篇 Article

- **Character**: 篇
- **Pinyin**: piān
- **Meaning**: measure word for articles, essays, sheets
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 文章 (wén zhāng) — article, essay
- 长篇 (cháng piān) — long (novel/article)

## Example Sentences

- 我写了一篇文章。(Wǒ xiě le yī piān wén zhāng.) — I wrote an article.

## Notes

篇 is the measure word for written pieces. The bamboo radical reflects ancient writing on bamboo slips.
