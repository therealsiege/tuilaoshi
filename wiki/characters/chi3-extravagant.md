# 侈 Extravagant, wasteful

- **Character**: 侈
- **Pinyin**: chǐ
- **Meaning**: extravagant, wasteful
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奢侈 (shēchǐ) — extravagant, luxurious
- 侈谈 (chǐtán) — talk extravagantly

## Example Sentences

- 不要侈谈理想而不行动。(Bùyào chǐtán lǐxiǎng ér bù xíngdòng.) — Don't just talk extravagantly about ideals without action.

## Notes

Literary term for extravagance or excess, often paired with 奢.
