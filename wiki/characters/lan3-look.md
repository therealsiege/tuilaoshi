# 览 To look at, view, 覽 (traditional)

- **Character**: 览
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: to look at, view, 覽 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浏览 (liú lǎn) — to browse, skim through
- 游览 (yóu lǎn) — to tour, go sightseeing

## Example Sentences

- 我浏览了一下网页。(Wǒ liúlǎnle yīxià wǎngyè.) — I browsed the webpage.

## Notes

Common in compounds related to viewing, touring, or browsing.
