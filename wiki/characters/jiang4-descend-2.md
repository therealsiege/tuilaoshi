# 降 Descend

- **Character**: 降
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: to descend, drop
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 下降 (xià jiàng) — to descend, drop
- 降落 (jiàng luò) — to land, descend

## Example Sentences

- 滑雪运动员快速下降。(Huáxuě yùndòngyuán kuàisù xiàjiàng.) — The skier descends rapidly.

## Notes

Descending; downhill movement
