# 垛 Battlement

- **Character**: 垛
- **Pinyin**: duǒ
- **Meaning**: battlements, target, stack
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 垛口 (duǒ kǒu) — battlement
- 草垛 (cǎo duǒ) — haystack

## Example Sentences

- 城墙上有垛口。(Chéng qiáng shàng yǒu duǒ kǒu.) — There are battlements on the city wall.

## Notes

垛 has earth 土 with 朵 (flower), defensive notches in wall.
