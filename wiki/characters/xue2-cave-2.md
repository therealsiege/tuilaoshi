# 穴 Cave, hole, den

- **Character**: 穴
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: cave, hole, den
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 洞穴 (dòng xué) — cave, cavern
- 穴居 (xué jū) — cave dwelling

## Example Sentences

- 兔子住在地下的洞穴里。(Tù zi zhù zài dì xià de dòng xué lǐ.) — Rabbits live in underground burrows.

## Notes

Holes and caves where animals live
