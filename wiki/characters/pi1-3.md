# 批 Approve, wholesale, batch

- **Character**: 批
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: 批准, wholesale, batch
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 批准 (pī zhǔn) — approve, ratify
- 批发 (pī fā) — wholesale

## Example Sentences

- 我们从批发市场进货。(Wǒ men cóng pī fā shì chǎng jìn huò.) — We purchase goods from the wholesale market.

## Notes

Dual meaning: approval process and wholesale trade.
