# 减 Reduce, subtract, decrease

- **Character**: 减
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: reduce, subtract, decrease
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 减少 (jiǎnshǎo) — reduce, decrease
- 减刑 (jiǎnxíng) — reduce sentence

## Example Sentences

- 他因表现良好获得减刑。(Tā yīn biǎoxiàn liánghǎo huòdé jiǎnxíng.) — He got his sentence reduced for good behavior.

## Notes

Common for reduction and subtraction.
