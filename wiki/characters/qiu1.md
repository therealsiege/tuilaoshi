# 秋 Autumn

- **Character**: 秋
- **Pinyin**: qiū
- **Meaning**: autumn, fall, harvest time
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 秋天 (qiū tiān) — autumn, fall
- 中秋节 (zhōng qiū jié) — Mid-Autumn Festival

## Example Sentences

- 秋天树叶变黄了。(Qiū tiān shù yè biàn huáng le.) — The leaves turn yellow in autumn.

## Notes

秋 combines grain (禾) and fire (火), representing the harvest season when fields are burned after harvest.
