# 坊 Workshop, lane

- **Character**: 坊
- **Pinyin**: fāng
- **Meaning**: workshop, lane
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 作坊 (zuō fang) — workshop
- 街坊 (jiē fāng) — neighborhood

## Example Sentences

- 这里有很多手工作坊。(Zhè lǐ yǒu hěn duō shǒu gōng zuō fang.) — There are many handicraft workshops here.

## Notes

酒坊 is brewery. 染坊 is dyeing workshop. Traditional term.
