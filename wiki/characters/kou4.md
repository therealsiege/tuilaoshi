# 扣 To button up, to detain, to deduct

- **Character**: 扣
- **Pinyin**: kòu
- **Meaning**: to button up, to detain, to deduct
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扣子 (kòu zi) — button
- 扣除 (kòu chú) — to deduct

## Example Sentences

- 请把衬衫的扣子扣好。(Qǐng bǎ chènshān de kòuzi kòu hǎo.) — Please button up your shirt.

## Notes

Multiple meanings including fastening and deducting.
