# 耳 Ear

- **Character**: 耳
- **Pinyin**: ěr
- **Meaning**: ear
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 耳朵 (ěr duo) — ear
- 耳机 (ěr jī) — earphones

## Example Sentences

- 我的耳朵疼。(Wǒ de ěr duo téng.) — My ear hurts.

## Notes

耳 pictograph of ear. 耳朵 is complete word. 耳机 = headphones/earphones.
