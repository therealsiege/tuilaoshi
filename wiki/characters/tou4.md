# 透 To Penetrate

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: to penetrate, thorough
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 透明 (tòu míng) — transparent
- 看透 (kàn tòu) — to see through

## Example Sentences

- 这个玻璃很透明。(Zhège bōli hěn tòumíng.) — This glass is very transparent.

## Notes

透 combines 秀 (show) with 辶 (walk), representing something showing through or penetrating.
