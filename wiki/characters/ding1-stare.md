# 盯 To stare, to gaze, to fix eyes on

- **Character**: 盯
- **Pinyin**: dīng
- **Meaning**: to stare, to gaze, to fix eyes on
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 盯着 (dīng zhe) — to stare at
- 紧盯 (jǐn dīng) — to watch closely

## Example Sentences

- 他一直盯着屏幕看。(Tā yīzhí dīngzhe píngmù kàn.) — He kept staring at the screen.

## Notes

Emphasizes fixed, intense gazing.
