# 媚 To Flatter

- **Character**: 媚
- **Pinyin**: mèi
- **Meaning**: to flatter, charming, obsequious
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 妩媚 (wǔ mèi) — charming, enchanting
- 谄媚 (chǎn mèi) — to flatter

## Example Sentences

- 她的笑容很妩媚。(Tā de xiào róng hěn wǔ mèi.) — Her smile is very enchanting.

## Notes

媚 shows woman 女 and 眉 (eyebrow), representing feminine charm or flattery.
