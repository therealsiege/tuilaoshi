# 庭 Courtyard, hall, family

- **Character**: 庭
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: courtyard, hall, family
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 庭院 (tíng yuàn) — courtyard
- 法庭 (fǎ tíng) — court of law

## Example Sentences

- 四合院的庭院里种着许多花草。(Sì hé yuàn de tíng yuàn lǐ zhòng zhe xǔ duō huā cǎo.) — The courtyard of the siheyuan is planted with many flowers and plants.

## Notes

Central element of traditional Chinese residential architecture.
