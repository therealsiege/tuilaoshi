# 昴 Pleiades

- **Character**: 昴
- **Pinyin**: mǎo
- **Meaning**: Pleiades star cluster
- **Radical**: 日(sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 昴宿 (mǎo xiù) — Pleiades constellation
- 昴星团 (mǎo xīng tuán) — Pleiades star cluster

## Example Sentences

- 昴宿星团在中国被称为七姐妹星团。(Mǎo xiù xīngtuán zài Zhōngguó bèi chēng wéi qī jiěmèi xīngtuán.) — The Pleiades is called the Seven Sisters cluster in China.

## Notes

昴 refers to the Pleiades star cluster, visible to the naked eye. Part of the 28 lunar mansions.
