# 汹 Turbulent, noisy

- **Character**: 汹
- **Pinyin**: xiōng
- **Meaning**: turbulent, noisy
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 汹涌 (xiōngyǒng) — turbulent, surging
- 汹涌澎湃 (xiōngyǒng péngpài) — surging tumultuously

## Example Sentences

- 河水汹涌而下。(Héshuǐ xiōngyǒng ér xià.) — The river rushes down tumultuously.

## Notes

Describes violent or turbulent water movements.
