# 籍 Record, register, nationality

- **Character**: 籍
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: record, register, nationality
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 书籍 (shū jí) — books
- 户籍 (hù jí) — household register

## Example Sentences

- 他的户籍在北京。(Tā de hù jí zài běi jīng.) — His household registration is in Beijing.

## Notes

Important for official records and nationality status
