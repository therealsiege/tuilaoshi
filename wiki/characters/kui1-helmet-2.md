# 盔 Helmet

- **Character**: 盔
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: helmet, armor
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 头盔 (tóu kuī) — helmet
- 钢盔 (gāng kuī) — steel helmet

## Example Sentences

- 骑车时一定要戴头盔。(Qí chē shí yīdìng yào dài tóukuī.) — You must wear a helmet when cycling.

## Notes

Protective helmet; safety gear
