# 补 To Mend

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: to mend, to supplement, to make up for
- **Radical**: 衤(clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 补充 (bǔ chōng) — to supplement
- 弥补 (mí bǔ) — to make up for

## Example Sentences

- 我需要补充一下。(Wǒ xū yào bǔ chōng yī xià.) — I need to add something.

## Notes

补 originally meant to mend clothing (衤radical). Extended to supplement or make up for.
