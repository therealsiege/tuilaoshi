# 惭 Ashamed

- **Character**: 惭
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: ashamed
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed
- 羞惭 (xiū cán) — shamefaced

## Example Sentences

- 他感到很惭愧。(Tā gǎn dào hěn cán kuì.) — He feels very ashamed.

## Notes

惭 simplified from 慚. Heart 忄 with 斩 (to cut), heart cut with shame.
