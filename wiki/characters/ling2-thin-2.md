# 绫 Thin silk

- **Character**: 绫
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: thin silk, damask
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绫罗 (líng luó) — fine silks
- 绫绸 (líng chóu) — silk fabrics

## Example Sentences

- 古代贵族喜欢穿绫罗绸缎。(Gǔdài guìzú xǐhuan chuān líng luó chóu duàn.) — Ancient nobility liked to wear fine silks and satins.

## Notes

绫 is a type of thin, patterned silk fabric. Often appears in classical literature describing luxury textiles.
