# 赦 Pardon, forgive, amnesty

- **Character**: 赦
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: pardon, forgive, amnesty
- **Radical**: 赤 (red)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 赦免 (shè miǎn) — to pardon, amnesty
- 大赦 (dà shè) — general pardon

## Example Sentences

- 总统赦免了部分罪犯。(Zǒng tǒng shè miǎn le bù fèn zuì fàn.) — The president pardoned some criminals.

## Notes

Legal term for pardon or amnesty. 特赦 means special pardon. 赦罪 means to forgive sins.
