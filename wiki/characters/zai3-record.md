# 载 Record, carry

- **Character**: 载
- **Pinyin**: zǎi
- **Meaning**: record, carry
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 刊载 (kān zǎi) — publish, carry
- 记载 (jì zǎi) — record, put down in writing

## Example Sentences

- 杂志刊载了这篇文章。(Zá zhì kān zǎi le zhè piān wén zhāng.) — The magazine published this article.

## Notes

Different from 载 (zài = carry/load). 转载 means reprint.
