# 鹤 Crane

- **Character**: 鹤
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: crane (bird)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 白鹤 (bái hè) — white crane
- 仙鹤 (xiān hè) — red-crowned crane

## Example Sentences

- 鹤是长寿的象征。(Hè shì cháng shòu de xiàng zhēng.) — Cranes symbolize longevity.

## Notes

鹤 simplified from 鶴. Bird radical 鸟 representing the elegant long-legged bird.
