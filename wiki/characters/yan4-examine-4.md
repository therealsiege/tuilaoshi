# 验 Test

- **Character**: 验
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: examine, test
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 检验 (jiǎn yàn) — to test, inspect
- 化验 (huà yàn) — laboratory test
- 试验 (shì yàn) — experiment, test

## Example Sentences

- 血液化验结果正常。(Xiě yè huà yàn jié guǒ zhèng cháng.) — The blood test results are normal.

## Notes

Laboratory testing; diagnostic confirmation.
