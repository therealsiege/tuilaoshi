# 呈 To present, to appear, to show

- **Character**: 呈
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to present, to appear, to show
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 呈现 (chéng xiàn) — to present, to appear
- 呈报 (chéng bào) — to present a report

## Example Sentences

- 天空呈现出美丽的颜色。(Tiānkōng chéngxiàn chū měilì de yánsè.) — The sky presents beautiful colors.

## Notes

Used for presenting or displaying something.
