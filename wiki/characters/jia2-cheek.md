# 颊 Cheek

- **Character**: 颊
- **Pinyin**: jiá
- **Meaning**: cheek
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 脸颊 (liǎn jiá) — cheek
- 两颊 (liǎng jiá) — both cheeks

## Example Sentences

- 她的脸颊红红的。(Tā de liǎn jiá hóng hóng de.) — Her cheeks are rosy.

## Notes

颊 simplified from 頰. Head 页 with 夹 (to squeeze between), sides of the face.
