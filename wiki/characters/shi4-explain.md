# 释 Explain, release, Buddhism

- **Character**: 释
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: explain, release, Buddhism
- **Radical**: 釆 (distinguish)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 解释 (jiě shì) — explain
- 释放 (shì fàng) — release

## Example Sentences

- 请你解释一下这个问题。(Qǐng nǐ jiě shì yī xià zhè gè wèn tí.) — Please explain this problem.

## Notes

Also refers to Shakyamuni Buddha (释迦牟尼)
