# 蔓 Vine, creeper

- **Character**: 蔓
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: vine, creeper
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蔓延 (màn yán) — to spread, to extend
- 瓜蔓 (guā wàn) — melon vine

## Example Sentences

- 火势在蔓延。(Huǒshì zài mànyán.) — The fire is spreading.

## Notes

Can mean vine or spreading. Two pronunciations: màn and wàn. Grass radical.
