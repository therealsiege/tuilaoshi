# 拙 Clumsy

- **Character**: 拙
- **Pinyin**: zhuō
- **Meaning**: clumsy, awkward
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 笨拙 (bèn zhuō) — clumsy
- 拙劣 (zhuō liè) — clumsy, inferior

## Example Sentences

- 他的动作很笨拙。(Tā de dòng zuò hěn bèn zhuō.) — His movements are very clumsy.

## Notes

拙 shows hand 扌 and 出 (to go out), representing unskilled or awkward manual work.
