# 悔 Regret

- **Character**: 悔
- **Pinyin**: huǐ
- **Meaning**: regret
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 后悔 (hòuhuǐ) — regret
- 悔恨 (huǐhèn) — remorse

## Example Sentences

- 他后悔没有好好学习。(Tā hòuhuǐ méiyǒu hǎohǎo xuéxí.) — He regrets not studying hard.

## Notes

Common emotion. Heart radical. 后悔 is very frequently used. Important for expressing past mistakes.
