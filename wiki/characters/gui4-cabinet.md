# 柜 Cabinet

- **Character**: 柜
- **Pinyin**: guì
- **Meaning**: cabinet, cupboard
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 柜子 (guì zi) — cabinet
- 衣柜 (yī guì) — wardrobe

## Example Sentences

- 衣柜里有很多衣服。(Yī guì lǐ yǒu hěn duō yī fú.) — There are many clothes in the wardrobe.

## Notes

柜 simplified from 櫃. Wood radical 木 indicates wooden storage furniture for keeping items.
