# 去 To Go

- **Character**: 去
- **Pinyin**: qù
- **Meaning**: to go, to leave
- **Radical**: 厶 (private)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 去年 (qùnián) — last year
- 出去 (chūqù) — to go out
- 过去 (guòqù) — the past; to go over
- 回去 (huíqù) — to go back
- 下去 (xiàqù) — to go down, to continue

## Example Sentences

- 我去学校。(Wǒ qù xuéxiào.) — I go to school.
- 你去过中国吗？(Nǐ qùguò Zhōngguó ma?) — Have you been to China?
- 我们一起去吧！(Wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba!) — Let's go together!

## Notes

去 is one of the most fundamental directional verbs in Chinese. In ancient script it depicted a person leaving a dwelling. As a directional complement it indicates motion away from the speaker — the opposite of 来 (lái, to come). The pair 来/去 forms the basis for many compound directional expressions like 上来/上去, 下来/下去, 出来/出去.
