# 羊 Sheep

- **Character**: 羊
- **Pinyin**: yáng
- **Meaning**: sheep, goat
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 羊肉 (yáng ròu) — mutton, lamb
- 山羊 (shān yáng) — goat

## Example Sentences

- 羊肉串很好吃。(Yáng ròu chuàn hěn hǎo chī.) — Lamb skewers are delicious.

## Notes

羊 pictograph of sheep's head with horns. Common in northwestern Chinese cuisine.
