# 敏 Quick, nimble, sensitive

- **Character**: 敏
- **Pinyin**: mǐn
- **Meaning**: quick, nimble, sensitive
- **Radical**: 攵 (rap)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 敏捷 (mǐnjié) — agile, nimble
- 灵敏 (língmǐn) — sensitive, keen

## Example Sentences

- 他反应很敏捷。(Tā fǎnyìng hěn mǐnjié.) — His reaction is very quick.

## Notes

Common formal term for quickness or sensitivity.
