# 悟 To Realize

- **Character**: 悟
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: to realize, to comprehend
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 领悟 (lǐng wù) — to comprehend
- 觉悟 (jué wù) — to awaken to

## Example Sentences

- 他终于领悟了。(Tā zhōng yú lǐng wù le.) — He finally comprehended.

## Notes

悟 has heart 忄 with 吾 (I/me), insight from within one's heart.
