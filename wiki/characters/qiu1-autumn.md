# 秋 Autumn

- **Character**: 秋
- **Pinyin**: qiū
- **Meaning**: autumn, fall, harvest
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 秋天 (qiū tiān) — autumn, fall
- 中秋节 (zhōng qiū jié) — Mid-Autumn Festival

## Example Sentences

- 秋天天气很好。(Qiū tiān tiān qì hěn hǎo.) — The weather is nice in autumn.

## Notes

秋 combines grain 禾 and fire 火 - harvest time when grain is dried. 中秋节 = Moon Festival.
