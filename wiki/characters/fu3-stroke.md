# 抚 To stroke, comfort, foster, 撫 (traditional)

- **Character**: 抚
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: to stroke, comfort, foster, 撫 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抚养 (fǔ yǎng) — to foster, raise
- 抚摸 (fǔ mō) — to stroke, caress

## Example Sentences

- 她轻轻抚摸孩子的头。(Tā qīngqīng fǔmō háizi de tóu.) — She gently stroked the child's head.

## Notes

Can mean physical stroking or nurturing care of children.
