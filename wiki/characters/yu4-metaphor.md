# 喻 Metaphor

- **Character**: 喻
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: metaphor, to understand
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 比喻 (bǐ yù) — metaphor
- 譬喻 (pì yù) — analogy

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个生动的比喻。(Zhè shì yí gè shēng dòng de bǐ yù.) — This is a vivid metaphor.

## Notes

喻 has mouth 口 with 俞 (to heal), using words to enlighten.
