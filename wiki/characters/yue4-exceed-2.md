# 越 Exceed

- **Character**: 越
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: to exceed, cross over
- **Radical**: 走 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 跨越 (kuà yuè) — to stride across
- 越野 (yuè yě) — cross-country

## Example Sentences

- 越野跑很有挑战性。(Yuèyě pǎo hěn yǒu tiǎozhànxìng.) — Cross-country running is very challenging.

## Notes

Crossing and exceeding; cross-country sports
