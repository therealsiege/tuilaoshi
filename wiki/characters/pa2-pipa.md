# 琶 Pipa

- **Character**: 琶
- **Pinyin**: pá
- **Meaning**: pipa (part of)
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琵琶 (pí pá) — pipa
- 琶音 (pá yīn) — arpeggio

## Example Sentences

- 琵琶是中国传统乐器。(Pí pá shì zhōng guó chuán tǒng yuè qì.) — The pipa is a traditional Chinese instrument.

## Notes

琶 only appears in 琵琶 and musical terms like 琶音 (arpeggio).
