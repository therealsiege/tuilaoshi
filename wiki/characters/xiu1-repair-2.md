# 修 To Repair

- **Character**: 修
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: to repair, to修改, to study
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — to modify
- 修理 (xiū lǐ) — to repair

## Example Sentences

- 我需要修改这篇文章。(Wǒ xū yào xiū gǎi zhè piān wén zhāng.) — I need to modify this article.

## Notes

修 represents fixing, improving, and cultivating oneself or things.
