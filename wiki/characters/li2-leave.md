# 离 To Leave

- **Character**: 离
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: to leave, from, distance
- **Radical**: 离 (leave)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 离开 (lí kāi) — to leave
- 距离 (jù lí) — distance

## Example Sentences

- 学校离我家很近。(Xué xiào lí wǒ jiā hěn jìn.) — The school is close to my home.

## Notes

离 is used to express distance: A离B很近/远 (A is close to/far from B). 离开 = to leave.
