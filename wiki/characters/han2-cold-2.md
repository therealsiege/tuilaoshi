# 寒 Cold

- **Character**: 寒
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: cold, chill
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 寒冷 (hán lěng) — cold
- 发寒 (fā hán) — to feel chills
- 风寒 (fēng hán) — wind-cold (TCM)

## Example Sentences

- 感冒时会发寒发热。(Gǎn mào shí huì fā hán fā rè.) — When you have a cold, you'll have chills and fever.

## Notes

Cold sensation; fever accompaniment.
