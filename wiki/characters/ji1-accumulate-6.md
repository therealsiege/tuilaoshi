# 积 Accumulate

- **Character**: 积
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to accumulate, to store up
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 厚积薄发 (hòu jī bó fā) — build up before breaking through
- 积累 (jī lěi) — to accumulate

## Example Sentences

- 积少成多。(Jī shǎo chéng duō.) — Many a little makes a lot.

## Notes

In 厚积薄发: to accumulate deeply; uses the grain radical suggesting storing harvests.
