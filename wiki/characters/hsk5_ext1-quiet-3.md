# 谧 Quiet, Peaceful, 宁谧 Tranquil

- **Character**: 谧
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: quiet, peaceful, 宁谧 tranquil
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 宁谧 (níng mì) — tranquil, peaceful
- 静谧 (jìng mì) — quiet and peaceful
- 安谧 (ān mì) — peaceful

## Example Sentences

- 夜晚的村庄宁谧安详。(Yèwǎn de cūnzhuāng níngmì ānxiáng.) — The village at night is tranquil and peaceful.

## Notes

Literary term for deep peace and tranquility. More formal and poetic than 安静. Often used in written descriptions.
