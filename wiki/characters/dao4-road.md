# 道 Road

- **Character**: 道
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: road, way, path, measure word
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 道路 (dào lù) — road
- 隧道 (suì dào) — tunnel

## Example Sentences

- 这条道路很宽。(Zhè tiáo dào lù hěn kuān.) — This road is very wide.

## Notes

道 has walk 辶 with 首 (head), path one follows.
