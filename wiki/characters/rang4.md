# 让 — Let / Allow

- **Character**: 让
- **Pinyin**: ràng
- **Meaning**: to let, to allow; to yield, to make (someone do something)
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 让步 (ràngbù) — to concede, to make a concession
- 让开 (ràngkāi) — to get out of the way
- 让座 (ràngzuò) — to offer one's seat
- 转让 (zhuǎnràng) — to transfer (rights, property)
- 谦让 (qiānràng) — to modestly decline, to be polite and yielding

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈让我先做作业。(Māma ràng wǒ xiān zuò zuòyè.) — Mom told me to do homework first.
- 请让一下。(Qǐng ràng yīxià.) — Please make way. / Excuse me.
- 别让他等太久。(Bié ràng tā děng tài jiǔ.) — Don't make him wait too long.

## Notes

让 (simplified from 讓) carries the speech radical 讠, reflecting that "letting" or "allowing" is often conveyed through words. Grammatically, 让 is a key causative verb in Mandarin, used in the pattern 让 + person + verb to mean "let/make someone do something." It also functions as a passive marker in some constructions, similar to 被 (bèi), though more colloquial: 我的手机让他拿走了 (My phone was taken by him). In Integrated Chinese, 让 is essential for expressing permission and requests.
