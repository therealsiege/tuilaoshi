# 妖 Demon

- **Character**: 妖
- **Pinyin**: yāo
- **Meaning**: demon, bewitching
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 妖怪 (yāo guài) — monster, demon
- 妖精 (yāo jīng) — spirit, demon

## Example Sentences

- 传说中有很多妖怪。(Chuán shuō zhōng yǒu hěn duō yāo guài.) — There are many demons in legends.

## Notes

妖 uses woman radical 女, suggesting bewitching or seductive supernatural beings. Can describe eerie beauty.
