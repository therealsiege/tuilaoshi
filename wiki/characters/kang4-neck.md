# 亢 Neck

- **Character**: 亢
- **Pinyin**: kàng
- **Meaning**: neck constellation, high
- **Radical**: 亠(lid)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 亢宿 (kàng xiù) — Neck constellation
- 亢奋 (kàng fèn) — excited, agitated

## Example Sentences

- 亢宿是东方七宿的第二宿。(Kàng xiù shì dōngfāng qī xiù de dì èr xiù.) — Neck is the second mansion of the Eastern Seven Mansions.

## Notes

亢 (kàng) refers to the Neck constellation in the 28 mansions. Also means high, proud, or excessive.
