# 迂 Circuitous, pedantic

- **Character**: 迂
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: circuitous, pedantic
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 迂回 (yū huí) — circuitous, roundabout
- 迂腐 (yū fǔ) — pedantic

## Example Sentences

- 他做事太迂腐了。(Tā zuò shì tài yū fǔ le.) — He is too pedantic in doing things.

## Notes

Roundabout paths or old-fashioned thinking
