# 烈 Intense

- **Character**: 烈
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: intense, fierce, strong
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 激烈 (jī liè) — intense
- 强烈 (qiáng liè) — strong

## Example Sentences

- 阳光很强烈。(Yáng guāng hěn qiáng liè.) — The sunlight is very strong.

## Notes

烈 shows fire 火 and 列 (to arrange), representing fierce intense burning flames.
