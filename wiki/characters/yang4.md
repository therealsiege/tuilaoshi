# 漾 Ripple, overflow, toss

- **Character**: 漾
- **Pinyin**: yàng
- **Meaning**: ripple, overflow, toss
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 荡漾 (dàng yàng) — ripple, sway
- 漾起 (yàng qǐ) — ripple up

## Example Sentences

- 湖面荡漾着微波。(Hú miàn dàng yàng zhe wēi bō.) — The lake surface ripples with small waves.

## Notes

Gentle water movement
