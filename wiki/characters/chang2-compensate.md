# 偿 To Compensate

- **Character**: 偿
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: to compensate, to repay
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 赔偿 (péi cháng) — to compensate
- 补偿 (bǔ cháng) — to make up for

## Example Sentences

- 保险公司赔偿了损失。(Bǎo xiǎn gōng sī péi cháng le sǔn shī.) — The insurance company compensated the losses.

## Notes

偿 means to repay or compensate. 偿还 = to repay (debt).
