# 涌 Surge, gush, emerge

- **Character**: 涌
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: surge, gush, emerge
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涌现 (yǒng xiàn) — emerge in large numbers
- 泉涌 (quán yǒng) — gush like a spring

## Example Sentences

- 人群涌向广场。(Rén qún yǒng xiàng guǎng chǎng.) — The crowd surged toward the square.

## Notes

Surging movement of water or crowds
