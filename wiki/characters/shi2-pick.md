# 拾 To Pick Up

- **Character**: 拾
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: to pick up, to collect, ten
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拾起 (shí qǐ) — to pick up
- 收拾 (shōu shi) — to tidy up

## Example Sentences

- 请把垃圾拾起来。(Qǐng bǎ lā jī shí qǐ lái.) — Please pick up the trash.

## Notes

拾 hand 扌 plus 合 (to join), gathering together.
