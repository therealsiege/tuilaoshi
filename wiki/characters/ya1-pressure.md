# 压 Pressure

- **Character**: 压
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: pressure, to press, to压制
- **Radical**: 厂 (factory)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 压力 (yā lì) — pressure
- 压制 (yā zhì) — to压制

## Example Sentences

- 他工作压力很大。(Tā gōng zuò yā lì hěn dà.) — He has a lot of work pressure.

## Notes

压 is simplified from 壓. Represents physical or psychological pressure.
