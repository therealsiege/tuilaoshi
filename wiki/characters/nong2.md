# 浓 Thick

- **Character**: 浓
- **Pinyin**: nóng
- **Meaning**: thick, dense, strong (flavor)
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浓厚 (nóng hòu) — dense, thick
- 浓度 (nóng dù) — concentration

## Example Sentences

- 这里有浓厚的文化氛围。(Zhè lǐ yǒu nóng hòu de wén huà fēn wéi.) — There's a strong cultural atmosphere here.

## Notes

浓 simplified from 濃. Water radical 氵 representing concentrated or dense liquid.
