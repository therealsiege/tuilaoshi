# 乓 Pong

- **Character**: 乓
- **Pinyin**: pāng
- **Meaning**: pong (sound)
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 乒乓球 (pīng pāng qiú) — table tennis, ping pong
- 乒乓 (pīng pāng) — ping pong (sound)

## Example Sentences

- 乒乓球是奥运会项目。(Pīngpāng qiú shì Àoyùnhuì xiàngmù.) — Table tennis is an Olympic sport.

## Notes

Complements 乒 in table tennis terminology; represents the bouncing sound
