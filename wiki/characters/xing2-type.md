# 型 Model

- **Character**: 型
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: type, model, pattern
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 类型 (lèi xíng) — type, category
- 典型 (diǎn xíng) — typical

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么类型的电脑？(Zhè shì shén me lèi xíng de diàn nǎo?) — What type of computer is this?

## Notes

型 originally meant mold for casting, now means type or model. Common in technical and classification contexts.
