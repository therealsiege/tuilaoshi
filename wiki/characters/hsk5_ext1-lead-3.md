# 领 Lead, Collar, 领导 Lead

- **Character**: 领
- **Pinyin**: lǐng
- **Meaning**: lead, collar, 领导 lead
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 领导 (lǐng dǎo) — leader, lead
- 领域 (lǐng yù) — field, domain
- 本领 (běn lǐng) — skill, ability

## Example Sentences

- 她领导团队完成了项目。(Tā lǐngdǎo tuánduì wánchéng le xiàngmù.) — She led the team to complete the project.

## Notes

Key character for leadership and receiving things. 领导 (leader/lead) is one of the most common workplace terms.
