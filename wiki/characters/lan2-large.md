# 澜 Large waves, billows

- **Character**: 澜
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: large waves, billows
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 波澜 (bō lán) — billows, waves
- 狂澜 (kuáng lán) — raging billows

## Example Sentences

- 历史的波澜壮阔。(Lì shǐ de bō lán zhuàng kuò.) — The magnificent waves of history.

## Notes

Large waves, often used metaphorically
