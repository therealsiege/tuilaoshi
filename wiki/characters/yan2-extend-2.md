# 延 To Extend

- **Character**: 延
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: to extend, to延长, to delay
- **Radical**: 廴 (move)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 延长 (yán cháng) — to延长
- 延迟 (yán chí) — to delay

## Example Sentences

- 会议延长了一个小时。(Huì yì yán cháng le yī ge xiǎo shí.) — The meeting was extended by one hour.

## Notes

延 shows movement stretching out, representing extension or延长.
