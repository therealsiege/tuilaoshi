# 笑 To Laugh

- **Character**: 笑
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: to laugh, to smile
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 笑话 (xiào huà) — joke
- 微笑 (wēi xiào) — to smile

## Example Sentences

- 她在笑什么？(Tā zài xiào shén me?) — What is she laughing at?

## Notes

笑 combines bamboo 竹 and 夭 (bent/young). Laughter bends you like bamboo in wind.
