# 畅 Smooth, unimpeded, 暢 (traditional)

- **Character**: 畅
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: smooth, unimpeded, 暢 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 畅通 (chàng tōng) — unobstructed, smooth
- 流畅 (liú chàng) — fluent, smooth

## Example Sentences

- 交通很畅通。(Jiāotōng hěn chàngtōng.) — The traffic is flowing smoothly.

## Notes

Smooth flow without obstruction.
