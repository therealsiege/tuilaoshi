# 隅 Corner, nook

- **Character**: 隅
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: corner, nook
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 一隅 (yī yú) — a corner, one part
- 海隅 (hǎi yú) — coastal area

## Example Sentences

- 这只是问题的一隅。(Zhè zhǐ shì wèn tí de yī yú.) — This is just one aspect of the problem.

## Notes

Often used in literary contexts for corner or corner region.
