# 亏 Lose, deficit, treat unfairly

- **Character**: 亏
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: lose, deficit, treat unfairly
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 亏损 (kuīsǔn) — loss, deficit
- 吃亏 (chīkuī) — suffer a loss

## Example Sentences

- 这家公司去年亏损严重。(Zhè jiā gōngsī qùnián kuīsǔn yánzhòng.) — This company suffered serious losses last year.

## Notes

Opposite of 盈; indicates financial loss or being treated unfairly.
