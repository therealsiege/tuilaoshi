# 拘 To Arrest

- **Character**: 拘
- **Pinyin**: jū
- **Meaning**: to arrest, to restrain
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拘留 (jū liú) — to detain
- 拘束 (jū shù) — to restrain

## Example Sentences

- 他被拘留了。(Tā bèi jū liú le.) — He was detained.

## Notes

拘 combines hand 扌 and 句 (sentence/phrase), grasping someone.
