# 栽 To plant, to grow, to fall

- **Character**: 栽
- **Pinyin**: zāi
- **Meaning**: to plant, to grow, to fall
- **Radical**: 木(wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 栽培 (zāi péi) — to cultivate, to grow
- 栽树 (zāi shù) — to plant trees

## Example Sentences

- 春天是栽树的好季节。(Chūntiān shì zāi shù de hǎo jìjié.) — Spring is a good season for planting trees.

## Notes

Primary meaning is to plant; can also mean to fall.
