# 好 Good

- **Character**: 好
- **Pinyin**: hǎo
- **Meaning**: good, well, fine
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 好吃 (hǎo chī) — delicious
- 好看 (hǎo kàn) — good-looking

## Example Sentences

- 你好！(Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!

## Notes

好 combines 女 (woman) and 子 (child), suggesting 'a woman with a child is good.'
