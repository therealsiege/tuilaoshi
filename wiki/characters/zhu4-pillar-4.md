# 柱 Pillar

- **Character**: 柱
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: pillar, column, post
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柱子 (zhù zi) — pillar
- 支柱 (zhī zhù) — pillar, mainstay

## Example Sentences

- 这根柱子支撑着屋顶。(Zhè gēn zhù zi zhī chēng zhe wū dǐng.) — This pillar supports the roof.

## Notes

柱 has wood 木 with 主 (master), main supporting post.
