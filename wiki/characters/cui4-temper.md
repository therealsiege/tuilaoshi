# 淬 To Temper

- **Character**: 淬
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: to temper, to quench
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淬火 (cuì huǒ) — to quench (metal)
- 淬炼 (cuì liàn) — to temper

## Example Sentences

- 这把剑经过淬火处理。(Zhè bǎ jiàn jīng guò cuì huǒ chǔ lǐ.) — This sword has been quenched.

## Notes

淬 uses water radical 氵, referring to plunging hot metal into water to harden it. Metaphor for tempering.
