# 珍 Precious

- **Character**: 珍
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: precious, treasure
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 珍珠 (zhēn zhū) — pearl
- 珍贵 (zhēn guì) — precious

## Example Sentences

- 她戴着一串珍珠项链。(Tā dài zhe yì chuàn zhēn zhū xiàng liàn.) — She's wearing a string of pearls.

## Notes

珍 has jade 玉 with 㐱 (to examine), valuable gemstone.
