# 道 Path

- **Character**: 道
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: path, way
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 跑道 (pǎo dào) — runway, track
- 泳道 (yǒng dào) — swimming lane

## Example Sentences

- 运动员在第三跑道。(Yùndòngyuán zài dì sān pǎodào.) — The athlete is in lane three.

## Notes

Path and lane; tracks and lanes
