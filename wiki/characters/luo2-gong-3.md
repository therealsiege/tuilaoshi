# 锣 Gong

- **Character**: 锣
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: gong
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锣鼓 (luó gǔ) — gongs and drums
- 敲锣 (qiāo luó) — to strike a gong

## Example Sentences

- 锣鼓喧天，热闹非凡。(Luó gǔ xuān tiān, rè nào fēi fán.) — Gongs and drums resounded, extremely lively.

## Notes

锣 is essential in traditional Chinese opera and celebrations.
