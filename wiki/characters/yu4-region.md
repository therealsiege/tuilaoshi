# 域 Region, area, domain

- **Character**: 域
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: region, area, domain
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 区域 (qū yù) — region, area
- 领域 (lǐng yù) — field, domain

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个新的研究领域。(Zhè shì yī gè xīn de yán jiū lǐng yù.) — This is a new research field.

## Notes

Used for both physical territories and abstract domains
