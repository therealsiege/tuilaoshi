# 磅 Pound (weight), majestic

- **Character**: 磅
- **Pinyin**: páng
- **Meaning**: pound (weight), majestic
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 磅礴 (pángbó) — majestic, boundless
- 气势磅礴 (qìshì pángbó) — majestic, imposing

## Example Sentences

- 山势磅礴壮观。(Shānshì pángbó zhuàngguān.) — The mountains are majestic and magnificent.

## Notes

Used for the weight unit pound and in the literary compound 磅礴.
