# 媒 Matchmaker, medium, media

- **Character**: 媒
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: matchmaker, medium, media
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 媒体 (méi tǐ) — media
- 传媒 (chuán méi) — mass media

## Example Sentences

- 社交媒体改变了人们的沟通方式。(Shè jiāo méi tǐ gǎi biàn le rén men de gōu tōng fāng shì.) — Social media has changed the way people communicate.

## Notes

Originally 'matchmaker', now widely used for media and communication channels. Essential in modern vocabulary.
