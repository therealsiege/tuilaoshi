# 担 Carry, undertake

- **Character**: 担
- **Pinyin**: dān
- **Meaning**: carry, undertake
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 担保 (dān bǎo) — guarantee, vouch for
- 承担 (chéng dān) — bear, undertake

## Example Sentences

- 谁来承担这个责任?(Shéi lái chéng dān zhè ge zé rèn?) — Who will bear this responsibility?

## Notes

担保人 is guarantor. 担保贷款 is guaranteed loan.
