# 驱 To Drive

- **Character**: 驱
- **Pinyin**: qū
- **Meaning**: to drive, to expel
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 驱逐 (qū zhú) — to expel, to deport
- 驱动 (qū dòng) — to drive, to power

## Example Sentences

- 政府驱逐了非法移民。(Zhèng fǔ qū zhú le fēi fǎ yí mín.) — The government deported illegal immigrants.

## Notes

驱 uses horse radical 马, originally meaning to drive horses. Extended to driving away or powering.
