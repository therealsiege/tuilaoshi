# 民 People

- **Character**: 民
- **Pinyin**: mín
- **Meaning**: people, citizen, folk
- **Radical**: 氏 (clan)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 人民 (rén mín) — the people
- 农民 (nóng mín) — farmer

## Example Sentences

- 人民生活水平提高了。(Rén mín shēng huó shuǐ píng tí gāo le.) — The people's living standards have improved.

## Notes

民 originally depicted an eye being pierced, meaning slave; now means common people or citizens.
