# 隔 Isolate

- **Character**: 隔
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: separate, isolate
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 隔离 (gé lí) — to isolate, quarantine
- 隔开 (gé kāi) — to separate
- 间隔 (jiàn gé) — interval, space

## Example Sentences

- 传染病患者需要隔离。(Chuán rǎn bìng huàn zhě xū yào gé lí.) — Infectious disease patients need to be isolated.

## Notes

Separation; infection control measure.
