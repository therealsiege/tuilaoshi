# 两 Two

- **Character**: 两
- **Pinyin**: liǎng
- **Meaning**: two (with measure words), both
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 两个 (liǎng gè) — two (of something)
- 两边 (liǎng biān) — both sides

## Example Sentences

- 我有两个孩子。(Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè hái zi.) — I have two children.

## Notes

两 is used before measure words (两个人) while 二 is used for counting and numbers (第二, 二十). 两 means 'both.'
