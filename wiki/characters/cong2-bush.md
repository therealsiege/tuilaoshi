# 丛 Cluster

- **Character**: 丛
- **Pinyin**: cóng
- **Meaning**: bush, cluster, thicket
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 草丛 (cǎo cóng) — grass, thicket
- 花丛 (huā cóng) — flower bed

## Example Sentences

- 小鸟躲在草丛里。(Xiǎo niǎo duǒ zài cǎo cóng lǐ.) — The little bird hid in the grass.

## Notes

丛 simplified from 叢. Two 人 (people) together, cluster of plants.
