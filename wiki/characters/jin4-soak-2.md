# 浸 To Soak

- **Character**: 浸
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to soak, to steep, to immerse
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浸泡 (jìn pào) — to soak, to steep
- 沉浸 (chén jìn) — to immerse

## Example Sentences

- 把衣服浸在水里。(Bǎ yī fu jìn zài shuǐ lǐ.) — Soak the clothes in water.

## Notes

浸 means to soak or immerse in liquid. Used for soaking clothes, steeping tea, and immersion.
