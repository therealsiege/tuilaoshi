# 拜 Worship, Visit, 拜访 Visit

- **Character**: 拜
- **Pinyin**: bài
- **Meaning**: worship, visit, 拜访 visit
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拜访 (bài fǎng) — visit, call on
- 崇拜 (chóng bài) — worship, adore
- 拜年 (bài nián) — pay New Year call

## Example Sentences

- 春节期间要拜年。(Chūnjié qījiān yào bàinián.) — During Spring Festival, one should pay New Year calls.

## Notes

Versatile verb for worship, respect, and formal visits. 拜年 (New Year greetings) is essential Spring Festival custom.
