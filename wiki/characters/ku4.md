# 酷 Cruel, severe, cool

- **Character**: 酷
- **Pinyin**: kù
- **Meaning**: cruel, severe, cool
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 酷刑 (kù xíng) — torture
- 残酷 (cán kù) — cruel, brutal

## Example Sentences

- 这是残酷的现实。(Zhè shì cán kù de xiàn shí.) — This is the cruel reality.

## Notes

Extreme severity or modern slang for cool
