# 傲 Proud

- **Character**: 傲
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: proud, arrogant
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 骄傲 (jiāo ào) — proud; arrogant
- 傲慢 (ào màn) — arrogant

## Example Sentences

- 我为你感到骄傲。(Wǒ wèi nǐ gǎn dào jiāo ào.) — I'm proud of you.

## Notes

骄傲 can be positive (proud) or negative (arrogant) depending on context.
