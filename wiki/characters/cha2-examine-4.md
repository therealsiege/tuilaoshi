# 查 Examine, investigate, check

- **Character**: 查
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: examine, investigate, check
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 调查 (diàochá) — investigate, survey
- 检查 (jiǎnchá) — inspect, check

## Example Sentences

- 需要调查清楚事实。(Xūyào diàochá qīngchu shìshí.) — Need to investigate and clarify the facts.

## Notes

Very common for checking and investigating.
