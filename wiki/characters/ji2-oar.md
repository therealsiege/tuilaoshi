# 楫 Oar, paddle

- **Character**: 楫
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: oar, paddle
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 舟楫 (zhōu jí) — boat and oars
- 楫木 (jí mù) — oar

## Example Sentences

- 他挥动着楫桨前进。(Tā huī dòng zhe jí jiǎng qián jìn.) — He moved forward wielding the oar.

## Notes

Literary term; 桨 is the common modern word.
