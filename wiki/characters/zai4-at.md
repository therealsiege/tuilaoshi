# 在 At

- **Character**: 在
- **Pinyin**: zài
- **Meaning**: at, in, to be at
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 现在 (xiàn zài) — now
- 存在 (cún zài) — to exist

## Example Sentences

- 他在家。(Tā zài jiā.) — He is at home.

## Notes

在 can function as a verb (to be at), a preposition (at/in), or part of the progressive aspect (在 + verb = doing).
