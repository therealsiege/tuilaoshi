# 价 Price

- **Character**: 价
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: price, value
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 价格 (jià gé) — price
- 评价 (píng jià) — to evaluate

## Example Sentences

- 这个东西的价格很便宜。(Zhè ge dōng xi de jià gé hěn pián yi.) — The price of this thing is very cheap.

## Notes

价 represents the value or price of something. Essential for商ial商 discussions.
