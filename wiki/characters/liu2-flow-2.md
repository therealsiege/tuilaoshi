# 流 To Flow

- **Character**: 流
- **Pinyin**: liú
- **Meaning**: to flow, current
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 电流 (diàn liú) — electric current
- 交流 (jiāo liú) — to exchange, AC

## Example Sentences

- 电流太强了。(Diàn liú tài qiáng le.) — The current is too strong.

## Notes

流 for flowing water extends to electrical current and data streams.
