# 培 To cultivate, to earth up, to train

- **Character**: 培
- **Pinyin**: péi
- **Meaning**: to cultivate, to earth up, to培养
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 培养 (péi yǎng) — to cultivate, to train
- 培训 (péi xùn) — to train, training

## Example Sentences

- 学校培养了很多人才。(Xuéxiào péiyǎng le hěn duō réncái.) — The school has cultivated many talented people.

## Notes

Often used for cultivating talents or training.
