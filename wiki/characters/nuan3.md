# 暖 Warm

- **Character**: 暖
- **Pinyin**: nuǎn
- **Meaning**: warm
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 温暖 (wēn nuǎn) — warm
- 暖和 (nuǎn huo) — warm, nice and warm

## Example Sentences

- 房间很暖和。(Fáng jiān hěn nuǎn huo.) — The room is very warm.

## Notes

暖 with sun radical 日. 温暖 and 暖和 both mean warm, pleasant temperature.
