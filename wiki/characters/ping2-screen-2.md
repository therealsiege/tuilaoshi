# 屏 Screen, to shield, to hold breath

- **Character**: 屏
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: screen, to shield, to hold breath
- **Radical**: 尸(corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen (TV, computer)
- 屏障 (píng zhàng) — barrier, shield

## Example Sentences

- 电脑屏幕坏了。(Diànnǎo píngmù huài le.) — The computer screen is broken.

## Notes

Pronounced píng for screen; bǐng for to hold back/shield.
