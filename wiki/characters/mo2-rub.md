# 摩 Rub

- **Character**: 摩
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: rub, friction
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 按摩 (àn mó) — massage
- 摩擦 (mó cā) — friction, to rub
- 观摩 (guān mó) — to observe and learn

## Example Sentences

- 用精油按摩效果更好。(Yòng jīng yóu àn mó xiào guǒ gèng hǎo.) — Massage with essential oils works better.

## Notes

Rubbing motion; massage component.
