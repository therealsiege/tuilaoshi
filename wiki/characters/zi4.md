# 字 Character

- **Character**: 字
- **Pinyin**: zì
- **Meaning**: character, word, letter
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 汉字 (hàn zì) — Chinese character
- 名字 (míng zì) — name

## Example Sentences

- 这个字怎么写？(Zhè ge zì zěn me xiě?) — How do you write this character?

## Notes

字 shows a child (子) under a roof (宀) — nurturing/educating children, hence 'writing/character.'
