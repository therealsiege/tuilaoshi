# 瘫 Paralyzed

- **Character**: 瘫
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: paralyzed, collapsed
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘫痪 (tān huàn) — paralysis
- 瘫倒 (tān dǎo) — to collapse

## Example Sentences

- 他累得瘫倒在沙发上。(Tā lèi de tāndǎo zài shāfā shàng.) — He collapsed on the sofa from exhaustion.

## Notes

瘫 combines sickness 疒 with 难 (difficult), representing the difficulty of paralysis or complete exhaustion.
