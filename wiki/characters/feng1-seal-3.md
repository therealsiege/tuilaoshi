# 封 To Seal

- **Character**: 封
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: to seal, to封闭, envelope
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 封闭 (fēng bì) — to seal off
- 信封 (xìn fēng) — envelope

## Example Sentences

- 请把信封起来。(Qǐng bǎ xìn fēng qǐ lái.) — Please seal the letter.

## Notes

封 originally depicted a tree planted on a mound to mark territory; extended to sealing.
