# 疾 Disease, rapid, hate

- **Character**: 疾
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: disease, rapid, hate
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 疾病 (jí bìng) — disease, illness
- 疾苦 (jí kǔ) — hardship, suffering

## Example Sentences

- 预防胜于治疗，要重视疾病预防。(Yùfáng shèng yú zhìliáo, yào zhòngshì jíbìng yùfáng.) — Prevention is better than cure; disease prevention should be emphasized.

## Notes

Disease or illness. Also means fast or swift, as in 疾驰 (gallop). Implies sudden or acute conditions.
