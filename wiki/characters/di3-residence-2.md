# 邸 Residence, mansion

- **Character**: 邸
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: residence, mansion
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 府邸 (fǔ dǐ) — mansion, official residence
- 官邸 (guān dǐ) — official residence

## Example Sentences

- 总统官邸在市中心。(Zǒng tǒng guān dǐ zài shì zhōng xīn.) — The presidential residence is in the city center.

## Notes

Formal term for large residence. 私邸 means private residence.
