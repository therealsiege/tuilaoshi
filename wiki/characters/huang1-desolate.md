# 荒 Desolate, waste, absurd

- **Character**: 荒
- **Pinyin**: huāng
- **Meaning**: desolate, waste, absurd
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荒地 (huāng dì) — wasteland
- 荒芜 (huāng wú) — waste, uncultivated

## Example Sentences

- 这片荒地已经多年未耕种。(Zhè piàn huāng dì yǐ jīng duō nián wèi gēng zhòng.) — This wasteland has not been cultivated for many years.

## Notes

Uncultivated and abandoned land
