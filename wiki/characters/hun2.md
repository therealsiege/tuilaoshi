# 魂 Soul

- **Character**: 魂
- **Pinyin**: hún
- **Meaning**: soul, spirit
- **Radical**: 鬼 (ghost)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 灵魂 (líng hún) — soul, spirit
- 魂魄 (hún pò) — soul

## Example Sentences

- 音乐是人类的灵魂。(Yīn yuè shì rén lèi de líng hún.) — Music is the soul of humanity.

## Notes

魂 represents the heavenly soul that ascends after death. Paired with 魄 for complete soul.
