# 栏 Railing, fence, column

- **Character**: 栏
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: railing, fence, column (in newspaper)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing, balustrade
- 专栏 (zhuān lán) — special column

## Example Sentences

- 桥上的石栏杆雕刻着精美的图案。(Qiáo shàng de shí lán gān diāo kè zhe jīng měi de tú àn.) — The stone railings on the bridge are carved with exquisite patterns.

## Notes

Also used for newspaper/magazine columns.
