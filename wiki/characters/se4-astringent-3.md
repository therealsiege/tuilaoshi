# 涩 Astringent, difficult

- **Character**: 涩
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: astringent, difficult
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 艰涩 (jiānsè) — difficult, obscure
- 晦涩 (huìsè) — obscure, abstruse

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章晦涩难懂。(Zhè piān wénzhāng huìsè nándǒng.) — This article is obscure and difficult to understand.

## Notes

Describes astringent taste or difficulty in understanding.
