# 夹 Clamp

- **Character**: 夹
- **Pinyin**: jiā/jiá
- **Meaning**: clamp, pinch
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 夹板 (jiā bǎn) — splint
- 夹子 (jiā zi) — clip, clamp
- 夹杂 (jiā zá) — to mix, intermingle

## Example Sentences

- 用夹板固定骨折的手臂。(Yòng jiā bǎn gù dìng gǔ zhé de shǒu bì.) — Use a splint to immobilize the fractured arm.

## Notes

Clamping or splinting; orthopedic stabilization.
