# 非 Not

- **Character**: 非
- **Pinyin**: fēi
- **Meaning**: not, wrong, non-
- **Radical**: 非 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 口是心非 (kǒu shì xīn fēi) — say one thing, mean another
- 非常 (fēi cháng) — extremely

## Example Sentences

- 她总是口是心非。(Tā zǒng shì kǒu shì xīn fēi.) — She always says one thing but means another.

## Notes

Used in 口是心非: the heart's truth differs from spoken words.
