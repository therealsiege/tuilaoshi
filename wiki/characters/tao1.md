# 涛 Large Waves

- **Character**: 涛
- **Pinyin**: tāo
- **Meaning**: large waves, billows
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 波涛 (bō tāo) — great waves
- 怒涛 (nù tāo) — raging waves

## Example Sentences

- 海上波涛汹涌。(Hǎi shàng bō tāo xiōng yǒng.) — The sea has surging waves.

## Notes

涛 specifically refers to large ocean waves. Often used in poetic or descriptive contexts.
