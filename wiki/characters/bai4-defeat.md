# 败 Defeat, fail, spoil

- **Character**: 败
- **Pinyin**: bài
- **Meaning**: defeat, fail, spoil
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 失败 (shībài) — fail, failure
- 败诉 (bàisù) — lose a lawsuit

## Example Sentences

- 原告败诉了。(Yuángào bàisù le.) — The plaintiff lost the lawsuit.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: defeat, failure, spoiling.
