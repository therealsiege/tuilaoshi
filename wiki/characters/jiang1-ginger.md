# 姜 Ginger

- **Character**: 姜
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: ginger
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 生姜 (shēng jiāng) — fresh ginger
- 姜片 (jiāng piàn) — ginger slices

## Example Sentences

- 这个汤里有姜的味道。(Zhège tāng lǐ yǒu jiāng de wèidào.) — This soup has a ginger flavor.

## Notes

Key ingredient for removing fishy smell. Also a surname.
