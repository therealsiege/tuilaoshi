# 拨 拨款, allocate, dial

- **Character**: 拨
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: 拨款, allocate, dial
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拨款 (bō kuǎn) — allocate funds
- 调拨 (diào bō) — allocate, transfer

## Example Sentences

- 政府拨款支持项目。(Zhèng fǔ bō kuǎn zhī chí xiàng mù.) — The government allocates funds to support the project.

## Notes

拨打 means dial (phone). 分拨 means distribute.
