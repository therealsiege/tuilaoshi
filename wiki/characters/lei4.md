# 累 Tired

- **Character**: 累
- **Pinyin**: lèi
- **Meaning**: tired, to tire, to accumulate (lěi)
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 疲累 (pí lèi) — tired
- 劳累 (láo lèi) — fatigued

## Example Sentences

- 我今天很累。(Wǒ jīn tiān hěn lèi.) — I'm very tired today.

## Notes

累 shows threads 糸 piled up, representing accumulated burden causing fatigue. As lěi it means to accumulate.
