# 订 订购, subscribe, fix

- **Character**: 订
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: 订购,订阅, fix
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 订购 (dìng gòu) — 订购
- 签订 (qiān dìng) — sign (contract)

## Example Sentences

- 我们已经签订了合同。(Wǒ men yǐ jīng qiān dìng le hé tong.) — We have already signed the contract.

## Notes

订单 is order. 预订 means book/reserve.
