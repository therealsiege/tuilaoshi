# 举 To Lift

- **Character**: 举
- **Pinyin**: jǔ
- **Meaning**: to lift, to raise, to cite, to举办
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 举手 (jǔ shǒu) — to raise one's hand
- 举办 (jǔ bàn) — to hold (event)

## Example Sentences

- 请举手发言。(Qǐng jǔ shǒu fā yán.) — Please raise your hand to speak.

## Notes

举 means to lift or raise, often with effort. Also used for举办 (to hold/organize events) and 举例 (to give examples).
