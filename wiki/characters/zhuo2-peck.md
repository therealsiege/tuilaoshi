# 啄 To Peck

- **Character**: 啄
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: to peck
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 啄食 (zhuó shí) — to peck at food

## Example Sentences

- 鸡在啄米。(Jī zài zhuó mǐ.) — The chicken is pecking at rice.

## Notes

啄 combines mouth 口 and 豖 (pig), representing the pecking action of birds.
