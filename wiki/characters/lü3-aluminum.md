# 铝 Aluminum

- **Character**: 铝
- **Pinyin**: lǚ
- **Meaning**: aluminum
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铝合金 (lǚ hé jīn) — aluminum alloy
- 铝箔 (lǚ bó) — aluminum foil

## Example Sentences

- 铝是一种轻便耐用的金属。(Lǚ shì yī zhǒng qīngbiàn nàiyòng de jīnshǔ.) — Aluminum is a lightweight and durable metal.

## Notes

铝 is aluminum, widely used in construction, packaging, and transportation due to its light weight.
