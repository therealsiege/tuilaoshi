# 奉 To奉

- **Character**: 奉
- **Pinyin**: fèng
- **Meaning**: to奉, receive respectfully
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奉献 (fèng xiàn) — to dedicate
- 信奉 (xìn fèng) — to believe in

## Example Sentences

- 我们要奉献自己的力量。(Wǒmen yào fèngxiàn zìjǐ de lìliang.) — We should dedicate our strength.

## Notes

奉 shows hands 廾 offering something above 𠬞, representing respectful presentation or service.
