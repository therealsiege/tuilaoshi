# 钝 Blunt, dull, stupid

- **Character**: 钝
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: blunt, dull, stupid
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 迟钝 (chí dùn) — slow, dull
- 钝角 (dùn jiǎo) — obtuse angle

## Example Sentences

- 这把刀太钝了,需要磨一磨。(Zhè bǎ dāo tài dùn le, xū yào mó yi mó.) — This knife is too blunt, it needs sharpening.

## Notes

Opposite of 锐 (sharp). Can describe physical bluntness or mental dullness.
