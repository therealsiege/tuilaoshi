# 璧 Jade Disc

- **Character**: 璧
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: jade disc, perfection
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 完璧归赵 (wán bì guī zhào) — return sth intact
- 珠联璧合 (zhū lián bì hé) — perfect match

## Example Sentences

- 他们是珠联璧合的一对。(Tā men shì zhū lián bì hé de yī duì.) — They are a perfect match.

## Notes

璧 refers to a flat jade disc with a hole, symbol of perfection in ancient China. Used in idioms.
