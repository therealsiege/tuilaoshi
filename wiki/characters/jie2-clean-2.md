# 洁 Clean, pure, 潔 (traditional)

- **Character**: 洁
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: clean, pure, 潔 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 清洁 (qīng jié) — clean, sanitary
- 纯洁 (chún jié) — pure, chaste

## Example Sentences

- 保持环境清洁很重要。(Bǎochí huánjìng qīngjié hěn zhòngyào.) — Maintaining environmental cleanliness is important.

## Notes

Cleanliness and purity, both physical and moral.
