# 茎 Stem

- **Character**: 茎
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: stem, stalk
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 花茎 (huā jīng) — flower stem
- 茎叶 (jīng yè) — stems and leaves

## Example Sentences

- 植物的茎支撑着叶子。(Zhí wù de jīng zhī chēng zhe yè zi.) — The plant's stem supports the leaves.

## Notes

茎 simplified from 莖. Grass 艹 with 巠 phonetic, main stalk.
