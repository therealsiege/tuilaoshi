# 协 To Cooperate

- **Character**: 协
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: to cooperate, harmonious
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 协会 (xié huì) — association
- 协作 (xié zuò) — to collaborate

## Example Sentences

- 双方达成协议。(Shuāng fāng dá chéng xié yì.) — Both sides reached an agreement.

## Notes

协 simplified from 協. Multiple forces 办 working together.
