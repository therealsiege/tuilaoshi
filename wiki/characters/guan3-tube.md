# 管 Tube

- **Character**: 管
- **Pinyin**: guǎn
- **Meaning**: tube, wind instrument, to manage
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 管乐 (guǎn yuè) — wind music
- 管理 (guǎn lǐ) — to manage

## Example Sentences

- 管乐队在演奏。(Guǎn yuè duì zài yǎn zòu.) — The wind ensemble is performing.

## Notes

管 has bamboo 竹 with 官 (official), bamboo tube, wind instrument.
