# 具 Tool, device

- **Character**: 具
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: tool, device
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 模具 (mó jù) — mold, die
- 工具 (gōng jù) — tool

## Example Sentences

- 模具设计很重要。(Mó jù shè jì hěn zhòng yào.) — Mold design is important.

## Notes

Used in 模具 (mold), 工具 (tool), 夹具 (fixture)
