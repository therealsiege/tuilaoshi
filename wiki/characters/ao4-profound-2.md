# 奥 Profound

- **Character**: 奥
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: profound, mysterious
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 奥妙 (ào miào) — subtlety
- 深奥 (shēn ào) — profound

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题很深奥。(Zhè gè wèn tí hěn shēn ào.) — This question is very profound.

## Notes

奥 simplified from 奧. Represents inner recesses, hence profound.
