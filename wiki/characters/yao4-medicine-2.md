# 药 Medicine

- **Character**: 药
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: medicine, drug
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 吃药 (chī yào) — to take medicine
- 药物 (yào wù) — drug, medication
- 中药 (zhōng yào) — Chinese medicine

## Example Sentences

- 你按时吃药了吗？(Nǐ àn shí chī yào le ma?) — Did you take your medicine on time?

## Notes

General term for medication; fundamental healthcare term.
