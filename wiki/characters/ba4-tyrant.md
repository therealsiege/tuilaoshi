# 霸 Tyrant

- **Character**: 霸
- **Pinyin**: bà
- **Meaning**: tyrant, hegemon
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 霸权 (bà quán) — hegemony
- 恶霸 (è bà) — local tyrant

## Example Sentences

- 他总是霸占最好的位置。(Tā zǒngshì bàzhàn zuì hǎo de wèizhì.) — He always monopolizes the best spot.

## Notes

霸 originally meant 'feudal lord' in ancient China. Now means hegemon or to dominate by force.
