# 穹 Vault, dome, sky

- **Character**: 穹
- **Pinyin**: qióng
- **Meaning**: vault, dome, sky
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 穹顶 (qióng dǐng) — dome, vault
- 苍穹 (cāng qióng) — the firmament, sky

## Example Sentences

- 教堂的穹顶上有精美的壁画。(Jiào táng de qióng dǐng shàng yǒu jīng měi de bì huà.) — There are exquisite murals on the church's dome.

## Notes

Literary term for dome or vault of heaven. 苍穹 is a poetic word for sky.
