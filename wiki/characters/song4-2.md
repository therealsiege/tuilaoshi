# 送 送达, give, see off

- **Character**: 送
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: 送达, give, see off
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 送货 (sòng huò) — 送达
- 邮送 (yóu sòng) — mail, send by post

## Example Sentences

- 快递员正在送货。(Kuài dì yuán zhèng zài sòng huò.) — The courier is delivering goods.

## Notes

免费送货 means free delivery. 送货上门 means home delivery.
