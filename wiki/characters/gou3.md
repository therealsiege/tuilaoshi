# 狗 Dog

- **Character**: 狗
- **Pinyin**: gǒu
- **Meaning**: dog
- **Radical**: 犭(animal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 小狗 (xiǎo gǒu) — puppy
- 热狗 (rè gǒu) — hot dog

## Example Sentences

- 那只狗很可爱。(Nà zhī gǒu hěn kě ài.) — That dog is very cute.

## Notes

狗 uses the animal radical 犭. The measure word for dogs is 只 (zhī) or 条 (tiáo).
