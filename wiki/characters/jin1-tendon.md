# 筋 Muscle

- **Character**: 筋
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: tendon, muscle
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 筋骨 (jīn gǔ) — muscles and bones
- 抽筋 (chōu jīn) — muscle cramp
- 脑筋 (nǎo jīn) — brains, mind

## Example Sentences

- 我腿抽筋了。(Wǒ tuǐ chōu jīn le.) — My leg cramped up.

## Notes

Refers to tendons and muscles; common in traditional medicine.
