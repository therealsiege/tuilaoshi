# 孕 Pregnant, conceive

- **Character**: 孕
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: pregnant, conceive
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 怀孕 (huáiyùn) — be pregnant
- 孕育 (yùnyù) — breed, give birth to

## Example Sentences

- 她怀孕三个月了。(Tā huáiyùn sān gè yuè le.) — She is three months pregnant.

## Notes

Common term for pregnancy and conception, literal or figurative.
