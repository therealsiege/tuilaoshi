# 帘 Curtain

- **Character**: 帘
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: curtain, screen
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 窗帘 (chuāng lián) — window curtain
- 门帘 (mén lián) — door curtain

## Example Sentences

- 她拉上了窗帘。(Tā lā shàng le chuāng lián.) — She drew the curtains.

## Notes

帘 simplified from 簾. Cloth 巾 covering, hanging screen.
