# 涛 Large waves, billows

- **Character**: 涛
- **Pinyin**: tāo
- **Meaning**: large waves, billows
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 波涛 (bō tāo) — great waves
- 涛声 (tāo shēng) — sound of waves

## Example Sentences

- 波涛汹涌的大海。(Bō tāo xiōng yǒng de dà hǎi.) — The surging sea.

## Notes

Large ocean waves, often poetic
