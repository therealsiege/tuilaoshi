# 伏 To lie down, ambush, subdue

- **Character**: 伏
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: to lie down, ambush, subdue
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 埋伏 (mái fú) — to ambush
- 起伏 (qǐ fú) — ups and downs

## Example Sentences

- 士兵们埋伏在树林里。(Shìbīngmen máifú zài shùlín lǐ.) — The soldiers ambushed in the woods.

## Notes

To lie prone, hide in ambush, or subdue.
