# 撕 To tear, to rip

- **Character**: 撕
- **Pinyin**: sī
- **Meaning**: to tear, to rip
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撕开 (sī kāi) — to tear open
- 手撕 (shǒu sī) — hand-torn

## Example Sentences

- 手撕包菜是一道家常菜。(Shǒu sī bāocài shì yī dào jiācháng cài.) — Hand-torn cabbage is a homestyle dish.

## Notes

手撕 is a cooking technique. Hand radical indicates manual action.
