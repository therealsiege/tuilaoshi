# 眨 To blink, to wink

- **Character**: 眨
- **Pinyin**: zhǎ
- **Meaning**: to blink, to wink
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 眨眼 (zhǎ yǎn) — to blink, to wink
- 一眨眼 (yī zhǎ yǎn) — in the blink of an eye

## Example Sentences

- 一眨眼的工夫他就不见了。(Yī zhǎ yǎn de gōngfu tā jiù bùjiàn le.) — He disappeared in the blink of an eye.

## Notes

Common for blinking or indicating quick passage of time.
