# 碧 Jade green, emerald

- **Character**: 碧
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: jade green, emerald
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 碧绿 (bì lǜ) — jade green
- 碧空 (bì kōng) — azure sky

## Example Sentences

- 湖水碧绿清澈。(Hú shuǐ bì lǜ qīng chè.) — The lake water is jade green and clear.

## Notes

Poetic term for deep green or blue-green.
