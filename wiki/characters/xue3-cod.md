# 鳕 Cod

- **Character**: 鳕
- **Pinyin**: xuě
- **Meaning**: cod
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 鳕鱼 (xuě yú) — cod
- 银鳕鱼 (yín xuě yú) — silver cod

## Example Sentences

- 鳕鱼肉质细嫩。(Xuěyú ròuzhì xìnèn.) — Cod has tender meat.

## Notes

Cold water fish, popular in Western and Asian cuisine. Fish radical.
