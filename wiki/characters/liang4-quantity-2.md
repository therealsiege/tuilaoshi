# 量 Quantity, measure, capacity

- **Character**: 量
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: quantity, measure, capacity
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 数量 (shù liàng) — quantity, amount
- 重量 (zhòng liàng) — weight

## Example Sentences

- 测量这个物体的重量。(Cè liàng zhè ge wù tǐ de zhòng liàng.) — Measure the weight of this object.

Measurable amount
