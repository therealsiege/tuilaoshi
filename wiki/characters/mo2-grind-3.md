# 磨 To grind, to polish, to wear down

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: to grind, to polish, to wear down
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 磨练 (mó liàn) — to temper, to steel
- 折磨 (zhé mó) — to torment, to torture

## Example Sentences

- 困难磨练了他的意志。(Kùnnan móliàn le tā de yìzhì.) — Difficulties tempered his will.

## Notes

Pronounced mó for grinding; mò for millstone.
