# 拨 Allocate, dial, move

- **Character**: 拨
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: allocate, dial, move
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拨款 (bōkuǎn) — allocate funds
- 调拨 (diàobō) — allocate, transfer

## Example Sentences

- 政府拨款支持这个项目。(Zhèngfǔ bōkuǎn zhīchí zhège xiàngmù.) — The government allocated funds to support this project.

## Notes

Government and finance term for fund allocation.
