# 埚 Crucible

- **Character**: 埚
- **Pinyin**: guō
- **Meaning**: crucible
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 坩埚 (gān guō) — crucible
- 熔埚 (róng guō) — melting pot

## Example Sentences

- 实验室里有许多坩埚。(Shí yàn shì lǐ yǒu xǔ duō gān guō.) — There are many crucibles in the laboratory.

## Notes

Always used in the compound 坩埚.
