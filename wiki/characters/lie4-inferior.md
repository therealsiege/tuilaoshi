# 劣 Inferior

- **Character**: 劣
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: inferior, bad, of poor quality
- **Radical**: 力 (strength)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 恶劣 (è liè) — vile, nasty
- 劣质 (liè zhì) — poor quality

## Example Sentences

- 天气恶劣。(Tiān qì è liè.) — The weather is terrible.

## Notes

劣 shows 少 (few/little) and strength 力, representing insufficient or inferior quality.
