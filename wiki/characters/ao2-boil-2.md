# 熬 Simmer

- **Character**: 熬
- **Pinyin**: áo
- **Meaning**: boil, simmer
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熬药 (áo yào) — to decoct medicine
- 煎熬 (jiān áo) — to suffer, torment
- 熬夜 (áo yè) — to stay up late

## Example Sentences

- 把中药熬两个小时。(Bǎ zhōng yào áo liǎng ge xiǎo shí.) — Simmer the Chinese medicine for two hours.

## Notes

Long simmering; extracts medicinal compounds.
