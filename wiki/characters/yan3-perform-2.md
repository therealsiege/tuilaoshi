# 演 To Perform

- **Character**: 演
- **Pinyin**: yǎn
- **Meaning**: to perform, to演act
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 表演 (biǎo yǎn) — to perform
- 演出 (yǎn chū) — performance

## Example Sentences

- 他在电影中演主角。(Tā zài diàn yǐng zhōng yǎn zhǔ jué.) — He plays the lead role in the film.

## Notes

演 combines water 氵 with 寅, originally meaning flowing extensively, extended to演演unfolding演a演performance.
