# 倍 Times

- **Character**: 倍
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: times, -fold
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 两倍 (liǎng bèi) — double
- 加倍 (jiā bèi) — to double

## Example Sentences

- 这是去年的两倍。(Zhè shì qù nián de liǎng bèi.) — This is double last year's.

## Notes

倍 expresses multiples: 三倍 = three times, 十倍 = ten times.
