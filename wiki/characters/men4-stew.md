# 焖 To stew, to braise

- **Character**: 焖
- **Pinyin**: mèn
- **Meaning**: to stew, to braise
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 红烧 (hóng shāo) — to braise in soy sauce
- 焖饭 (mèn fàn) — to steam rice

## Example Sentences

- 她在做红烧肉。(Tā zài zuò hóngshāo ròu.) — She is making braised pork.

## Notes

Cooking method involving simmering in covered pot.
