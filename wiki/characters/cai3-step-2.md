# 踩 Step on

- **Character**: 踩
- **Pinyin**: cǎi
- **Meaning**: step on, trample
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 踩到 (cǎi dào) — step on
- 踩脚 (cǎi jiǎo) — step on foot

## Example Sentences

- 小心别踩到水坑。(Xiǎoxīn bié cǎi dào shuǐkēng.) — Be careful not to step in the puddle.

## Notes

Stepping or trampling action. Foot radical. Common warning verb. Can be accidental or deliberate.
