# 改 To Change

- **Character**: 改
- **Pinyin**: gǎi
- **Meaning**: to change, to alter, to correct
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 改变 (gǎi biàn) — to change
- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — to modify

## Example Sentences

- 你能改一下吗？(Nǐ néng gǎi yī xià ma?) — Can you change it?

## Notes

改 means to change or correct. 改善 = improve, 改革 = reform.
