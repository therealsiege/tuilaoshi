# 躲 Hide

- **Character**: 躲
- **Pinyin**: duǒ
- **Meaning**: to hide, avoid
- **Radical**: 身 (body)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 躲避 (duǒ bì) — to avoid, evade
- 闪躲 (shǎn duǒ) — to dodge, evade

## Example Sentences

- 防守时要学会躲避攻击。(Fángshǒu shí yào xuéhuì duǒbì gōngjī.) — When defending, you must learn to avoid attacks.

## Notes

Avoiding and evading; defensive movement
