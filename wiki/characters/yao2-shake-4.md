# 摇 To Shake

- **Character**: 摇
- **Pinyin**: yáo
- **Meaning**: to shake, to rock, to wave
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摇头 (yáo tóu) — to shake head
- 动摇 (dòng yáo) — to waver

## Example Sentences

- 他摇头表示不同意。(Tā yáo tóu biǎo shì bù tóng yì.) — He shook his head to indicate disagreement.

## Notes

摇 simplified from 搖. Hand 扌 rocking motion.
