# 阶 Steps

- **Character**: 阶
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: stairs, steps, rank
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 楼梯 (lóu tī) — stairs
- 台阶 (tái jiē) — steps

## Example Sentences

- 他走下台阶。(Tā zǒu xià tái jiē.) — He walks down the steps.

## Notes

阶 simplified from 階. Mound 阝 plus steps, graded levels.
