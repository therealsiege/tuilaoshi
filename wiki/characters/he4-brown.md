# 褐 Brown, coarse cloth

- **Character**: 褐
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: brown, coarse cloth
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 褐色 (hè sè) — brown
- 短褐 (duǎn hè) — short coarse coat

## Example Sentences

- 他穿着一件褐色的外套。(Tā chuān zhe yī jiàn hè sè de wài tào.) — He wore a brown coat.

## Notes

Standard term for brown color.
