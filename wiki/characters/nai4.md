# 耐 Endurance

- **Character**: 耐
- **Pinyin**: nài
- **Meaning**: endurance, withstand
- **Radical**: 而 (and)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 耐力 (nài lì) — endurance, stamina
- 忍耐 (rěn nài) — to endure

## Example Sentences

- 马拉松需要很强的耐力。(Mǎlāsōng xūyào hěn qiáng de nàilì.) — Marathon requires strong endurance.

## Notes

Endurance and stamina; essential for long-distance sports
