# 会 Meeting

- **Character**: 会
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: meeting, association, can
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 会议 (huì yì) — meeting
- 社会 (shè huì) — society

## Example Sentences

- 明天有一个重要会议。(Míng tiān yǒu yí gè zhòng yào huì yì.) — There is an important meeting tomorrow.

## Notes

会 simplified from 會. People gathering under a roof.
