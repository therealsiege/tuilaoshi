# 烂 Rotten

- **Character**: 烂
- **Pinyin**: làn
- **Meaning**: rotten, soft, messy
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 腐烂 (fǔ làn) — to rot, to decay
- 烂掉 (làn diào) — to rot away

## Example Sentences

- 水果腐烂了。(Shuǐ guǒ fǔ làn le.) — The fruit has rotted.

## Notes

烂 simplified from 爛. Fire radical 火 representing breaking down through heat or decay.
