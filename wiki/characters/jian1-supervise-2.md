# 监 Supervise, monitor

- **Character**: 监
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: supervise, monitor
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 监督 (jiān dū) — supervise, oversee
- 监管 (jiān guǎn) — regulate, supervise

## Example Sentences

- 政府监管金融市场。(Zhèng fǔ jiān guǎn jīn róng shì chǎng.) — The government regulates the financial market.

## Notes

Essential for compliance and governance. 监事 means supervisor in corporate structure.
