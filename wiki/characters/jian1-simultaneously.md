# 兼 Simultaneously

- **Character**: 兼
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: simultaneously, to hold two posts
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 兼职 (jiān zhí) — part-time job
- 兼顾 (jiān gù) — to attend to simultaneously

## Example Sentences

- 他兼职做翻译。(Tā jiān zhí zuò fān yì.) — He works part-time as a translator.

## Notes

兼 shows a hand holding two stalks of grain, representing doing two things at once.
