# 鞭 Whip

- **Character**: 鞭
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: whip, to whip
- **Radical**: 革 (leather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 鞭炮 (biān pào) — firecracker
- 鞭策 (biān cè) — to spur on

## Example Sentences

- 过年放鞭炮。(Guò nián fàng biān pào.) — Set off firecrackers for New Year.

## Notes

鞭炮 = firecrackers, an essential part of Chinese New Year celebrations.
