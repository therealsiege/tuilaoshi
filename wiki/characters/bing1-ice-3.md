# 冰 Ice

- **Character**: 冰
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: ice
- **Radical**: 冰 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冰块 (bīngkuài) — ice cube
- 冰箱 (bīngxiāng) — refrigerator

## Example Sentences

- 河面上结冰了。(Hé miàn shàng jié bīng le.) — The river surface has frozen over.

## Notes

Very common character. 冰箱 (refrigerator) is essential vocabulary. The character itself is a radical.
