# 核 Core, examine, nucleus

- **Character**: 核
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: 核心, examine,核
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 核实 (hé shí) — verify, check
- 审核 (shěn hé) — examine, audit

## Example Sentences

- 财务部门正在审核账目。(Cái wù bù mén zhèng zài shěn hé zhàng mù.) — The finance department is auditing the accounts.

## Notes

Critical for compliance. 核算 means accounting/calculation.
