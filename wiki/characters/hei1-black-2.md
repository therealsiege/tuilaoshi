# 黑 Black

- **Character**: 黑
- **Pinyin**: hēi
- **Meaning**: black, dark
- **Radical**: 黑 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 黑色 (hēi sè) — black color
- 黑板 (hēi bǎn) — blackboard

## Example Sentences

- 天黑了。(Tiān hēi le.) — It's gotten dark.

## Notes

黑 shows person with soot/ash from fire. Opposite of 白 (white). 黑板 = blackboard.
