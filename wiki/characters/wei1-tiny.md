# 微 Tiny

- **Character**: 微
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: tiny, micro, slight
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 微小 (wēi xiǎo) — tiny, minute
- 微笑 (wēi xiào) — to smile

## Example Sentences

- 她脸上露出了微笑。(Tā liǎn shàng lùchū le wēixiào.) — A smile appeared on her face.

## Notes

微 shows a person walking in small steps, representing something minute or slight.
