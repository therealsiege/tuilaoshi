# 盾 Shield

- **Character**: 盾
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: shield
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 盾牌 (dùn pái) — shield
- 矛盾 (máo dùn) — contradiction

## Example Sentences

- 这两件事很矛盾。(Zhè liǎng jiàn shì hěn máo dùn.) — These two things are contradictory.

## Notes

盾 means shield. 矛盾 (contradiction) comes from a story about an unstoppable spear (矛) and an impenetrable shield (盾).
