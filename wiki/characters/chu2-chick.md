# 雏 Chick, fledgling, young

- **Character**: 雏
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: chick, fledgling, young
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 雏鸟 (chú niǎo) — fledgling
- 雏形 (chú xíng) — embryonic form

## Example Sentences

- 这个计划还处于雏形阶段。(Zhè gè jì huà hái chǔ yú chú xíng jiē duàn.) — This plan is still in its embryonic stage.

## Notes

Metaphorically used for early stages of development
