# 证 证明, certificate

- **Character**: 证
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: 证明, certificate
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 证明 (zhèng míng) — 证明, certificate
- 证书 (zhèng shū) — certificate, credentials

## Example Sentences

- 请出示工作证。(Qǐng chū shì gōng zuò zhèng.) — Please show your work ID.

## Notes

Many types: 营业执照 (business license), 资格证 (qualification certificate).
