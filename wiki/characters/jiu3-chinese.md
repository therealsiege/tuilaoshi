# 韭 Chinese chive

- **Character**: 韭
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: Chinese chive
- **Radical**: 韭 (leek)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 韭菜 (jiǔ cài) — Chinese chives, garlic chives
- 韭黄 (jiǔ huáng) — yellow chives

## Example Sentences

- 韭菜鸡蛋饺子很好吃。(Jiǔcài jīdàn jiǎozi hěn hǎochī.) — Chive and egg dumplings are delicious.

## Notes

Popular dumpling filling. This character is itself a radical.
