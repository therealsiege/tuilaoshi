# 情 Feeling

- **Character**: 情
- **Pinyin**: qíng
- **Meaning**: feeling, emotion, situation
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 事情 (shì qíng) — matter, thing
- 感情 (gǎn qíng) — feelings, emotion

## Example Sentences

- 怎么回事情？(Zěn me huí shì qíng?) — What's going on?

## Notes

情 uses the heart radical because emotions come from the heart. 情况 means 'situation,' 爱情 means 'romantic love.'
