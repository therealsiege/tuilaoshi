# 泳 Swim

- **Character**: 泳
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: swim
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 游泳 (yóu yǒng) — to swim, swimming
- 泳池 (yǒng chí) — swimming pool

## Example Sentences

- 夏天我每天去游泳。(Xiàtiān wǒ měi tiān qù yóuyǒng.) — I go swimming every day in summer.

## Notes

Always related to swimming; common in pool and swimming terminology
