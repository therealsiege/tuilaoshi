# 涂 To Smear

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: to smear, to apply, to scribble
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear, to daub
- 涂料 (tú liào) — coating, paint

## Example Sentences

- 在墙上涂油漆。(Zài qiáng shàng tú yóu qī.) — Apply paint on the wall.

## Notes

涂 simplified from 塗. Water radical 氵 representing applying liquid or coating.
