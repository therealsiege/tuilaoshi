# 鼻 Nose

- **Character**: 鼻
- **Pinyin**: bí
- **Meaning**: nose
- **Radical**: 鼻 (nose)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 鼻子 (bí zi) — nose
- 鼻炎 (bí yán) — rhinitis
- 鼻腔 (bí qiāng) — nasal cavity

## Example Sentences

- 我鼻子不通气。(Wǒ bí zi bù tōng qì.) — My nose is blocked.

## Notes

Organ of smell and breathing; frequently affected by allergies.
