# 输 Transfuse

- **Character**: 输
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: transport, transfuse
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 输液 (shū yè) — IV drip, infusion
- 输血 (shū xiě) — blood transfusion
- 运输 (yùn shū) — to transport

## Example Sentences

- 病人需要输液补充水分。(Bìng rén xū yào shū yè bǔ chōng shuǐ fèn.) — The patient needs IV fluids to rehydrate.

## Notes

Intravenous delivery; fluids or blood.
