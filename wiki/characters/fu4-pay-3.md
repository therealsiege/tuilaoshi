# 付 Pay, hand over

- **Character**: 付
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: pay, hand over
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 支付 (zhī fù) — pay
- 付款 (fù kuǎn) — pay

## Example Sentences

- 请用现金支付。(Qǐng yòng xiàn jīn zhī fù.) — Please pay in cash.

## Notes

对付 means deal with. 付出 means pay/invest.
