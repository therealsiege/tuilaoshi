# 截 To Cut Off

- **Character**: 截
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: to cut off, to截intercept, section
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 截止 (jié zhǐ) — deadline
- 截断 (jié duàn) — to sever

## Example Sentences

- 报名截止日期是明天。(Bào míng jié zhǐ rì qī shì míng tiān.) — The registration deadline is tomorrow.

## Notes

截 combines halberd 戈 and 隹 (bird), representing cutting off or intercepting.
