# 视 To look at, regard, 視 (traditional)

- **Character**: 视
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to look at, regard, 視 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 视力 (shì lì) — vision, eyesight
- 重视 (zhòng shì) — to attach importance to

## Example Sentences

- 他视金钱如粪土。(Tā shì jīnqián rú fèntǔ.) — He regards money as dirt.

## Notes

Versatile character for vision and how one views or regards things.
