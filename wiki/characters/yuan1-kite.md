# 鸢 Kite (bird), kite (toy)

- **Character**: 鸢
- **Pinyin**: yuān
- **Meaning**: kite (bird), kite (toy)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 风筝 (fēng zheng) — kite
- 鸢飞鱼跃 (yuān fēi yú yuè) — kites fly and fish leap

## Example Sentences

- 鸢飞鱼跃，道在其中。(Yuān fēi yú yuè, dào zài qí zhōng.) — Kites fly and fish leap, the Way is within.

## Notes

Classical reference to the harmony of nature
