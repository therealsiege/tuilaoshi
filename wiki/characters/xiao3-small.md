# 小 Small

- **Character**: 小
- **Pinyin**: xiǎo
- **Meaning**: small, little, young
- **Radical**: 小 (small)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 小时 (xiǎo shí) — hour
- 小心 (xiǎo xīn) — careful

## Example Sentences

- 这只猫很小。(Zhè zhī māo hěn xiǎo.) — This cat is very small.

## Notes

小 depicts something being divided into small pieces. Opposite of 大.
