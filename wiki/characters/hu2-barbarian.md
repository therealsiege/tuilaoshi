# 胡 Barbarian

- **Character**: 胡
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: barbarian, recklessly
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胡琴 (hú qín) — huqin (fiddle)
- 二胡 (èr hú) — erhu

## Example Sentences

- 二胡是中国传统乐器。(Èr hú shì zhōng guó chuán tǒng yuè qì.) — The erhu is a traditional Chinese instrument.

## Notes

胡 originally referred to northern peoples; appears in many instrument names.
