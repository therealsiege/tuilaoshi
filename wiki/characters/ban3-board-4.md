# 板 Board, plate

- **Character**: 板
- **Pinyin**: bǎn
- **Meaning**: board, plate
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 主板 (zhǔ bǎn) — motherboard
- 键盘 (jiàn pán) — keyboard

## Example Sentences

- 主板需要更换。(Zhǔ bǎn xū yào gēng huàn.) — The motherboard needs to be replaced.

## Notes

Used in 主板 (motherboard), 电路板 (circuit board), 键盘 (keyboard)
