# 缝 Suture

- **Character**: 缝
- **Pinyin**: féng/fèng
- **Meaning**: sew, suture
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缝合 (féng hé) — to suture, sew up
- 缝针 (féng zhēn) — to stitch (a wound)
- 裂缝 (liè fèng) — crack, fissure

## Example Sentences

- 伤口需要缝合。(Shāng kǒu xū yào féng hé.) — The wound needs to be sutured.

## Notes

Stitching wounds; closes incisions or lacerations.
