# 肺 Lung

- **Character**: 肺
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: lung
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肺部 (fèi bù) — lungs
- 肺炎 (fèi yán) — pneumonia

## Example Sentences

- 肺负责呼吸和气体交换。(Fèi fù zé hū xī hé qì tǐ jiāo huàn.) — The lungs are responsible for breathing and gas exchange.
