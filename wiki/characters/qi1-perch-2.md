# 栖 To Perch

- **Character**: 栖
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: to perch, to dwell
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 栖息 (qī xī) — to perch, to rest
- 两栖 (liǎng qī) — amphibious

## Example Sentences

- 鸟儿在树上栖息。(Niǎo ér zài shù shàng qī xī.) — Birds perch in the trees.

## Notes

栖 has wood 木 with 西 (west), birds resting on branches.
