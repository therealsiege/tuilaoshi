# 赔 Compensate, lose money

- **Character**: 赔
- **Pinyin**: péi
- **Meaning**: compensate, lose money
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赔偿 (péi cháng) — compensate
- 赔款 (péi kuǎn) — reparations, indemnity

## Example Sentences

- 损坏物品要赔偿。(Sǔn huài wù pǐn yào péi cháng.) — Must compensate for damaged items.

## Notes

赔钱 means lose money. 赔本 means sell at a loss.
