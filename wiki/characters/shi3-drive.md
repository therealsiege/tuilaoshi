# 驶 To Drive

- **Character**: 驶
- **Pinyin**: shǐ
- **Meaning**: to drive, to sail
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 驾驶 (jià shǐ) — to drive
- 行驶 (xíng shǐ) — to travel (vehicle)

## Example Sentences

- 汽车慢慢驶向车站。(Qì chē màn màn shǐ xiàng chē zhàn.) — The car slowly drove toward the station.

## Notes

驶 simplified from 駛. Horse radical 马 with 史 (history/official), representing controlled movement of vehicles.
