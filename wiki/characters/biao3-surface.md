# 表 Surface

- **Character**: 表
- **Pinyin**: biǎo
- **Meaning**: surface, form, watch, to express
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 手表 (shǒu biǎo) — wristwatch
- 表示 (biǎo shì) — to express

## Example Sentences

- 你的手表很漂亮。(Nǐ de shǒu biǎo hěn piào liàng.) — Your watch is very pretty.

## Notes

表 means outer surface or form. 表达 and 表示 both mean to express.
