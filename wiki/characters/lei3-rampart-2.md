# 垒 Rampart

- **Character**: 垒
- **Pinyin**: lěi
- **Meaning**: rampart, to pile up
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 堡垒 (bǎo lěi) — fortress
- 垒球 (lěi qiú) — softball

## Example Sentences

- 他们攻破了敌人的堡垒。(Tā men gōng pò le dí rén de bǎo lěi.) — They broke through the enemy's fortress.

## Notes

垒 simplified from 壘. Earth 土 stacked for defensive walls.
