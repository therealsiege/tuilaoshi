# 物 Thing

- **Character**: 物
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: thing, object, matter
- **Radical**: 牛 (ox)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 动物 (dòng wù) — animal
- 礼物 (lǐ wù) — gift

## Example Sentences

- 这个礼物是给你的。(Zhè ge lǐ wù shì gěi nǐ de.) — This gift is for you.

## Notes

物 means thing or matter. 物理 = physics, 植物 = plant, 人物 = character/figure.
