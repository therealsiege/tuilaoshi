# 彼 That

- **Character**: 彼
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: that, the other
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 彼此 (bǐ cǐ) — each other
- 彼岸 (bǐ àn) — the other shore

## Example Sentences

- 彼此彼此。(Bǐ cǐ bǐ cǐ.) — Likewise. / Same to you.

## Notes

彼 is literary for 'that one.' 彼此 = each other, mutual. 知己知彼 = know yourself, know your enemy.
