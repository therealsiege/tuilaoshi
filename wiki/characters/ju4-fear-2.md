# 惧 To fear, be afraid, 懼 (traditional)

- **Character**: 惧
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: to fear, be afraid, 懼 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 畏惧 (wèi jù) — to fear, be afraid of
- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear, dread

## Example Sentences

- 我们不应该畏惧困难。(Wǒmen bù yīnggāi wèijù kùnnan.) — We should not fear difficulties.

## Notes

Often used in compounds with other fear-related characters.
