# 炖 To stew, to simmer

- **Character**: 炖
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: to stew, to simmer
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 炖汤 (dùn tāng) — to stew soup
- 炖肉 (dùn ròu) — to stew meat

## Example Sentences

- 奶奶在炖鸡汤。(Nǎinai zài dùn jī tāng.) — Grandma is stewing chicken soup.

## Notes

Slow cooking method for soups and stews.
