# 懈 To slacken, lazy

- **Character**: 懈
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to slacken, lazy
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 懈怠 (xiè dài) — to slack off, neglect
- 松懈 (sōng xiè) — to relax, slacken

## Example Sentences

- 学习不能松懈。(Xuéxí bù néng sōngxiè.) — You cannot slack off in your studies.

## Notes

Describes slackening effort or becoming lazy in one's work.
