# 除 Remove, except

- **Character**: 除
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: remove, except
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 除非 (chú fēi) — unless
- 消除 (xiāo chú) — eliminate, remove

## Example Sentences

- 解除合同需要双方同意。(Jiě chú hé tong xū yào shuāng fāng tóng yì.) — Contract termination requires mutual consent.

## Notes

开除 means dismiss/expel. 排除 means exclude/eliminate.
