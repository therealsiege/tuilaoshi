# 茄 Eggplant

- **Character**: 茄
- **Pinyin**: qié
- **Meaning**: eggplant
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 茄子 (qié zi) — eggplant
- 番茄 (fān qié) — tomato

## Example Sentences

- 鱼香茄子是我最喜欢的菜。(Yúxiāng qiézi shì wǒ zuì xǐhuan de cài.) — Fish-flavored eggplant is my favorite dish.

## Notes

茄子 is eggplant, 番茄 is tomato. Both popular vegetables.
