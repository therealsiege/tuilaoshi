# 泻 Flow swiftly, pour, diarrhea

- **Character**: 泻
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: flow swiftly, pour, diarrhea
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 倾泻 (qīng xiè) — pour down
- 腹泻 (fù xiè) — diarrhea

## Example Sentences

- 阳光倾泻而下。(Yáng guāng qīng xiè ér xià.) — Sunlight pours down.

## Notes

Rapid flow of water or light
