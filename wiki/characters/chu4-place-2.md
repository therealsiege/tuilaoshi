# 处 Place

- **Character**: 处
- **Pinyin**: chù
- **Meaning**: place, department, to deal with
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 到处 (dào chù) — everywhere
- 好处 (hǎo chù) — benefit

## Example Sentences

- 你到处都能看到这种花。(Nǐ dào chù dōu néng kàn dào zhè zhǒng huā.) — You can see this flower everywhere.

## Notes

处 is simplified from 處. Also read as chǔ meaning 'to handle' or 'to punish'.
