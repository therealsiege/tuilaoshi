# 树 Tree

- **Character**: 树
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: tree
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 树木 (shù mù) — trees
- 树叶 (shù yè) — leaf

## Example Sentences

- 公园里有很多树。(Gōng yuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō shù.) — There are many trees in the park.

## Notes

树 is simplified from 樹. The measure word for trees is 棵 (kē).
