# 评 Comment, assess, evaluate

- **Character**: 评
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: comment, assess, evaluate
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 评价 (píngjià) — evaluate, assess
- 评标 (píngbiāo) — evaluate bids

## Example Sentences

- 专家组正在评标。(Zhuānjiāzǔ zhèngzài píngbiāo.) — The expert panel is evaluating the bids.

## Notes

Used for commenting, evaluating, and assessing.
