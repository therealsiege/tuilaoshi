# 利 Profit

- **Character**: 利
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: profit, benefit, sharp, advantageous
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 利益 (lì yì) — benefit
- 胜利 (shèng lì) — victory

## Example Sentences

- 这对我们很有利。(Zhè duì wǒ men hěn yǒu lì.) — This is very advantageous for us.

## Notes

利 shows grain + knife, representing sharp cutting (efficient harvesting), hence profit and advantage.
