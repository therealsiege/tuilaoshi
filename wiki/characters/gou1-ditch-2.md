# 沟 Ditch

- **Character**: 沟
- **Pinyin**: gōu
- **Meaning**: ditch, gully, groove
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沟通 (gōu tōng) — to communicate
- 水沟 (shuǐ gōu) — ditch

## Example Sentences

- 他们之间缺乏沟通。(Tā men zhī jiān quē fá gōu tōng.) — There is a lack of communication between them.

## Notes

沟 simplified from 溝. Water 氵 channel, drainage ditch.
