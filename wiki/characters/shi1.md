# 师 Teacher/Master

- **Character**: 师
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: teacher, master, expert; army (classical)
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 老师 (lǎoshī) — teacher
- 师傅 (shīfu) — master, skilled worker
- 律师 (lǜshī) — lawyer
- 医师 (yīshī) — doctor, physician
- 工程师 (gōngchéngshī) — engineer
- 厨师 (chúshī) — chef, cook

## Example Sentences

- 王老师教我们中文。(Wáng lǎoshī jiāo wǒmen Zhōngwén.) — Teacher Wang teaches us Chinese.
- 他是一位有名的律师。(Tā shì yí wèi yǒumíng de lǜshī.) — He is a famous lawyer.
- 师傅，请问去火车站怎么走？(Shīfu, qǐngwèn qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu?) — Sir, may I ask how to get to the train station?

## Notes

师 is the simplified form of 師. It originally referred to a military unit (an army of 2,500 soldiers), then extended to mean "leader" and "expert." Its modern use focuses on teachers and skilled professionals. The suffix -师 appears in many profession names. 师傅 (shīfu) is used as a respectful form of address for taxi drivers, workers, and skilled tradespeople.
