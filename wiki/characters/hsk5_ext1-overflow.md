# 漫 Overflow, All Over, 漫画 Comic

- **Character**: 漫
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: overflow, all over, 漫画 comic
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漫画 (màn huà) — comic, cartoon
- 浪漫 (làng màn) — romantic
- 漫长 (màn cháng) — very long, endless

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个漫长的过程。(Zhè shì yī gè màncháng de guòchéng.) — This is a lengthy process.

## Notes

Means overflow/spread everywhere. 浪漫 (romantic), 漫画 (comics), 漫长 (very long) are common compounds with different nuances.
