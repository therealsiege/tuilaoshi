# 描 To Trace

- **Character**: 描
- **Pinyin**: miáo
- **Meaning**: to trace, to depict
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 描写 (miáo xiě) — to describe
- 描绘 (miáo huì) — to depict

## Example Sentences

- 作者详细描写了风景。(Zuò zhě xiáng xì miáo xiě le fēng jǐng.) — The author described the scenery in detail.

## Notes

描 combines hand 扌 and 苗 (seedling), representing tracing outlines with hand.
