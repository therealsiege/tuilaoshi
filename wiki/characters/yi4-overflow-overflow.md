# 溢 To Overflow

- **Character**: 溢
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to overflow, to brim over
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 溢出 (yì chū) — to overflow
- 洋溢 (yáng yì) — brimming with

## Example Sentences

- 水从杯子里溢出来了。(Shuǐ cóng bēi zi lǐ yì chū lái le.) — Water overflowed from the cup.

## Notes

溢 has water 氵 plus 益 (benefit/increase), water rising over the brim.
