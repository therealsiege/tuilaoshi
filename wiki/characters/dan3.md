# 掸 To dust, flick off

- **Character**: 掸
- **Pinyin**: dǎn
- **Meaning**: to dust, flick off
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掸子 (dǎn zi) — duster
- 掸灰 (dǎn huī) — to dust

## Example Sentences

- 她用掸子掸去家具上的灰尘。(Tā yòng dǎn zi dǎn qù jiā jù shàng de huī chén.) — She used a duster to remove dust from the furniture.

## Notes

Specifically for dusting or flicking off light particles. 掸子 is a feather duster.
