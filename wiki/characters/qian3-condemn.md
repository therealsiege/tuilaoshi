# 谴 To Condemn

- **Character**: 谴
- **Pinyin**: qiǎn
- **Meaning**: to condemn, to denounce
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 谴责 (qiǎn zé) — to condemn, to denounce
- 遭谴 (zāo qiǎn) — to be condemned

## Example Sentences

- 国际社会强烈谴责这一暴行。(Guó jì shè huì qiáng liè qiǎn zé zhè yī bào xíng.) — The international community strongly condemned this atrocity.

## Notes

谴 is formal language for official condemnation or denunciation, often used in diplomatic contexts.
