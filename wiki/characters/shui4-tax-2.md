# 税 Tax

- **Character**: 税
- **Pinyin**: shuì
- **Meaning**: tax, duty
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 税收 (shuì shōu) — tax revenue
- 纳税 (nà shuì) — to pay taxes

## Example Sentences

- 每个公民都要纳税。(Měi gè gōng mín dōu yào nà shuì.) — Every citizen must pay taxes.

## Notes

税 combines grain 禾 and 兑 (exchange), originally grain tribute.
