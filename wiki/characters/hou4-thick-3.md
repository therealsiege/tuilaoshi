# 厚 Thick, deep, kind

- **Character**: 厚
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: thick, deep, kind
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 厚度 (hòudù) — thickness
- 忠厚 (zhōnghòu) — honest and tolerant

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很厚。(Zhè běn shū hěn hòu.) — This book is very thick.

## Notes

Common character for thickness or kindness/depth of character.
