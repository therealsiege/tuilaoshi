# 漏 To Leak

- **Character**: 漏
- **Pinyin**: lòu
- **Meaning**: to leak, to omit, funnel
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漏水 (lòu shuǐ) — to leak water
- 遗漏 (yí lòu) — to omit

## Example Sentences

- 水管漏水了。(Shuǐ guǎn lòu shuǐ le.) — The water pipe is leaking.

## Notes

漏 has water 氵 with 雨 (rain), water escaping through gaps.
