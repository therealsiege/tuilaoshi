# 芽 Sprout, bud

- **Character**: 芽
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: sprout, bud
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 发芽 (fā yá) — to sprout, to germinate
- 豆芽 (dòu yá) — bean sprout

## Example Sentences

- 种子开始发芽了。(Zhǒngzi kāishǐ fāyá le.) — The seeds have started to sprout.

## Notes

豆芽 (bean sprouts) are common vegetable. Grass radical for plant.
