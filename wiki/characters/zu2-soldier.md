# 卒 Soldier

- **Character**: 卒
- **Pinyin**: zú
- **Meaning**: soldier, pawn, to die
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 士卒 (shì zú) — soldiers
- 卒业 (zú yè) — to graduate

## Example Sentences

- 古代士卒生活很艰苦。(Gǔ dài shì zú shēng huó hěn jiān kǔ.) — Ancient soldiers lived very hard lives.

## Notes

卒 shows a garment with marks, representing a common soldier or foot soldier.
