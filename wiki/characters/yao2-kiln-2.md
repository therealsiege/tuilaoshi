# 窑 Kiln

- **Character**: 窑
- **Pinyin**: yáo
- **Meaning**: kiln
- **Radical**: 穴(cave)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 瓷窑 (cí yáo) — porcelain kiln
- 窑炉 (yáo lú) — kiln furnace

## Example Sentences

- 陶工把陶器放进窑里烧制。(Táogōng bǎ táoqì fàng jìn yáo lǐ shāozhì.) — The potter put the pottery into the kiln to fire.

## Notes

窑 is a kiln for firing pottery, bricks, or other materials. The cave radical suggests its enclosed structure.
