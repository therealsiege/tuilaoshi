# 贷 Lend, loan, borrow

- **Character**: 贷
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: lend, loan, borrow
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贷款 (dàikuǎn) — loan, credit
- 借贷 (jièdài) — borrow and lend

## Example Sentences

- 他向银行申请了贷款。(Tā xiàng yínháng shēnqǐng le dàikuǎn.) — He applied for a loan from the bank.

## Notes

Essential banking term for loans and lending.
