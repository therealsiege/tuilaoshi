# 例 Example

- **Character**: 例
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: example, case, precedent
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 例子 (lì zi) — example
- 病例 (bìng lì) — medical case

## Example Sentences

- 请举个例子。(Qǐng jǔ ge lì zi.) — Please give an example.

## Notes

例 combines person and cutting, suggesting established patterns or precedents.
