# 掐 To Nip

- **Character**: 掐
- **Pinyin**: qiā
- **Meaning**: to pinch, to nip off
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掐断 (qiā duàn) — to pinch off
- 掐脖子 (qiā bó zi) — to strangle

## Example Sentences

- 掐掉枯萎的花。(Qiā diào kū wěi de huā.) — Pinch off the withered flowers.

## Notes

掐 hand 扌 plus 甲 (first), pinching with fingernails.
