# 放 Release, let go, place

- **Character**: 放
- **Pinyin**: fàng
- **Meaning**: release, let go, place
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 释放 (shì fàng) — to release
- 放弃 (fàng qì) — to abandon

## Example Sentences

- 他决定放弃这个机会。(Tā jué dìng fàng qì zhè ge jī huì.) — He decided to give up this opportunity.

## Notes

Versatile character. 放心 means to be at ease. 解放 means liberation. 开放 means to open.
