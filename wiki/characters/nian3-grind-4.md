# 碾 Crush

- **Character**: 碾
- **Pinyin**: niǎn
- **Meaning**: grind, crush
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 碾碎 (niǎn suì) — to crush, grind
- 碾压 (niǎn yā) — to roll over, crush
- 石碾 (shí niǎn) — stone mill

## Example Sentences

- 用碾子碾碎药材。(Yòng niǎn zi niǎn suì yào cái.) — Use a grinder to crush the medicinal materials.

## Notes

Crushing action; powder preparation.
