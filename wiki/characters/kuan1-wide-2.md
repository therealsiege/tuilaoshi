# 宽 Wide, broad, lenient, 寬 (traditional)

- **Character**: 宽
- **Pinyin**: kuān
- **Meaning**: wide, broad, lenient, 寬 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽容 (kuān róng) — tolerant, lenient
- 宽广 (kuān guǎng) — wide, broad

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该宽容待人。(Wǒmen yīnggāi kuānróng dài rén.) — We should treat people with tolerance.

## Notes

Physical width or metaphorical tolerance and leniency.
