# 降 To descend, to fall, to drop, to lower

- **Character**: 降
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: to descend, to fall, to drop, to降低
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 降低 (jiàng dī) — to reduce, to lower
- 降落 (jiàng luò) — to descend, to land

## Example Sentences

- 气温降低了。(Qìwēn jiàngdī le.) — The temperature has dropped.

## Notes

Pronounced jiàng for descending; xiáng for surrender.
