# 搽 To apply, rub on

- **Character**: 搽
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: to apply, rub on
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 搽粉 (chá fěn) — to powder (one's face)
- 搽药 (chá yào) — to apply medicine

## Example Sentences

- 她每天早上都要搽护肤霜。(Tā měi tiān zǎo shang dōu yào chá hù fū shuāng.) — She applies skin cream every morning.

## Notes

Specifically for applying cosmetics or medicine to skin. Similar to 涂 but more specific.
