# 瓢 Ladle, dipper, gourd

- **Character**: 瓢
- **Pinyin**: piáo
- **Meaning**: ladle, dipper, gourd
- **Radical**: 瓜 (melon)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 水瓢 (shuǐ piáo) — water ladle
- 瓢泼大雨 (piáo pō dà yǔ) — pouring rain

## Example Sentences

- 外面下着瓢泼大雨。(Wài miàn xià zhe piáo pō dà yǔ.) — It's pouring rain outside.

## Notes

Gourd ladle for scooping water
