# 党 Party, 政党 Political Party

- **Character**: 党
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: party, 政党 political party
- **Radical**: 党 (party)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 政党 (zhèng dǎng) — political party
- 共产党 (Gòngchǎn Dǎng) — Communist Party
- 党员 (dǎng yuán) — party member

## Example Sentences

- 他是一名党员。(Tā shì yī míng dǎngyuán.) — He is a party member.

## Notes

Fundamental character in political contexts. Used for political parties and party organizations.
