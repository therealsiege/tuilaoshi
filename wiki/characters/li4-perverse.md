# 戾 Perverse, violent, cruel

- **Character**: 戾
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: perverse, violent, cruel
- **Radical**: 户 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 戾气 (lì qì) — violent temper
- 乖戾 (guāi lì) — perverse, disagreeable

## Example Sentences

- 他性格乖戾，难以相处。(Tā xìng gé guāi lì, nán yǐ xiāng chǔ.) — He has a perverse temperament and is hard to get along with.

## Notes

Violent or perverse character
