# 叙 Narrate, chat, put in order

- **Character**: 叙
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: narrate, chat, put in order
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 叙述 (xùshù) — narrate, describe
- 叙旧 (xùjiù) — reminisce, talk about old times

## Example Sentences

- 老朋友见面总要叙叙旧。(Lǎo péngyou jiànmiàn zǒng yào xùxù jiù.) — Old friends always reminisce when they meet.

## Notes

Can mean both formal narration and informal chatting about the past.
