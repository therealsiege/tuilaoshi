# 钻 Diamond

- **Character**: 钻
- **Pinyin**: zuàn
- **Meaning**: diamond, to drill, to bore
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钻石 (zuàn shí) — diamond
- 钻研 (zuān yán) — to study intensively

## Example Sentences

- 她有一枚钻石戒指。(Tā yǒu yī méi zuàn shí jiè zhi.) — She has a diamond ring.

## Notes

钻 can be zuàn (diamond, to drill) or zuān (to bore into). As zuàn, used for diamonds and drilling.
