# 圃 Garden Plot

- **Character**: 圃
- **Pinyin**: pǔ
- **Meaning**: garden plot, nursery
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 苗圃 (miáo pǔ) — nursery (plants)
- 花圃 (huā pǔ) — flower bed

## Example Sentences

- 苗圃里培育树苗。(Miáo pǔ lǐ péi yù shù miáo.) — Tree seedlings are cultivated in the nursery.

## Notes

圃 with enclosure radical for specialized growing plots, agricultural spaces.
