# 椎 Vertebra, spine

- **Character**: 椎
- **Pinyin**: zhuī
- **Meaning**: vertebra, spine
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 脊椎 (jǐ zhuī) — spine, vertebra
- 椎骨 (zhuī gǔ) — vertebra

## Example Sentences

- 他的脊椎受伤了。(Tā de jǐzhuī shòushāng le.) — His spine was injured.

## Notes

Medical term for vertebrae, often used in compound 脊椎.
