# 服 Take

- **Character**: 服
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: take (medicine)
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 服用 (fú yòng) — to take (medicine)
- 服药 (fú yào) — to take medicine
- 内服 (nèi fú) — to take orally

## Example Sentences

- 饭后服用此药。(Fàn hòu fú yòng cǐ yào.) — Take this medicine after meals.

## Notes

Oral medication intake; standard medical verb.
