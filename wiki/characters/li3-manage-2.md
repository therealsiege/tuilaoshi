# 理 Care

- **Character**: 理
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: manage, care for
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 护理 (hù lǐ) — nursing care
- 料理 (liào lǐ) — to manage, take care of
- 理疗 (lǐ liáo) — physiotherapy

## Example Sentences

- 她在学习护理专业。(Tā zài xué xí hù lǐ zhuān yè.) — She is studying nursing.

## Notes

Management and care; appears in many healthcare compounds.
