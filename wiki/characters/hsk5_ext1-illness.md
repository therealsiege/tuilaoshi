# 疾 Illness, Swift, 疾病 Disease

- **Character**: 疾
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: illness, swift, 疾病 disease
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 疾病 (jí bìng) — disease, illness
- 疾速 (jí sù) — at top speed
- 残疾 (cán jí) — disabled

## Example Sentences

- 预防疾病很重要。(Yùfáng jíbìng hěn zhòngyào.) — Preventing disease is very important.

## Notes

Formal medical term for illness and disease. Also has secondary meaning of swiftness. 疾病 is standard medical terminology.
