# 壳 Shell, husk

- **Character**: 壳
- **Pinyin**: ké
- **Meaning**: shell, husk
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 贝壳 (bèi ké) — seashell
- 蛋壳 (dàn ké) — eggshell

## Example Sentences

- 这个贝壳很漂亮。(Zhège bèiké hěn piàoliang.) — This seashell is very beautiful.

## Notes

Also pronounced qiào. Refers to hard outer covering.
