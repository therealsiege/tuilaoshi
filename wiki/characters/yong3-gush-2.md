# 涌 To Gush

- **Character**: 涌
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: to gush, to surge, to well up
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涌现 (yǒng xiàn) — to emerge
- 涌出 (yǒng chū) — to gush out

## Example Sentences

- 泉水从地下涌出。(Quán shuǐ cóng dì xià yǒng chū.) — Spring water gushes from the ground.

## Notes

涌 simplified from 湧. Water 氵 surging upward powerfully.
