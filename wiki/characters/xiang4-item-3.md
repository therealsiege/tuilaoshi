# 项 Item

- **Character**: 项
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: item, neck, clause
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 项目 (xiàng mù) — project
- 事项 (shì xiàng) — matter

## Example Sentences

- 这个项目很重要。(Zhè ge xiàng mù hěn zhòng yào.) — This project is very important.

## Notes

项 simplified from 項. Originally neck/nape, extended to items/categories.
