# 束 To tie, to bind, bundle, bunch

- **Character**: 束
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: to tie, to bind, bundle, bunch
- **Radical**: 木(wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 束缚 (shù fù) — to bind, to restrict
- 一束花 (yī shù huā) — a bouquet of flowers

## Example Sentences

- 他买了一束玫瑰花。(Tā mǎi le yī shù méiguī huā.) — He bought a bouquet of roses.

## Notes

Can be a verb (to bind) or measure word for bundled items.
