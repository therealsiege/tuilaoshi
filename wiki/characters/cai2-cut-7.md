# 裁 Cut, judge, decide

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: cut, judge, decide
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 仲裁 (zhòngcái) — arbitration
- 裁决 (cáijué) — adjudicate, rule

## Example Sentences

- 法院作出裁决。(Fǎyuàn zuòchū cáijué.) — The court made a ruling.

## Notes

Legal term for judgment and decision; also means to cut (cloth).
