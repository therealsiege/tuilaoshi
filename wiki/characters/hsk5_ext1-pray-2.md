# 祈 Pray, Entreat, 祈祷 Pray

- **Character**: 祈
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: pray, entreat, 祈祷 pray
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 祈祷 (qí dǎo) — pray
- 祈求 (qí qiú) — pray for, entreat
- 祈盼 (qí pàn) — hope for

## Example Sentences

- 我们祈求平安。(Wǒmen qíqiú píng'ān.) — We pray for peace and safety.

## Notes

Formal verb for earnest prayer or requesting. Often paired with 祷 in 祈祷, or used with 求 in 祈求 for specific requests.
