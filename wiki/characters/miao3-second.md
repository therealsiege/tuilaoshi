# 秒 Second

- **Character**: 秒
- **Pinyin**: miǎo
- **Meaning**: second (time)
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 秒钟 (miǎo zhōng) — second (time)
- 分秒 (fēn miǎo) — minute and second

## Example Sentences

- 等我一秒钟。(Děng wǒ yī miǎo zhōng.) — Wait for me one second.

## Notes

秒 is the time unit for second. 秒表 = stopwatch.
