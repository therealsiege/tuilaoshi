# 红 Red

- **Character**: 红
- **Pinyin**: hóng
- **Meaning**: red
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 红色 (hóng sè) — red color
- 红包 (hóng bāo) — red envelope (gift money)

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着红色的裙子。(Tā chuān zhe hóng sè de qún zi.) — She's wearing a red skirt.

## Notes

红 represents good luck and celebration in Chinese culture. 红包 given during holidays/weddings.
