# 折 To Break

- **Character**: 折
- **Pinyin**: zhé
- **Meaning**: to break, to snap, to fold
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 折断 (zhé duàn) — to snap
- 打折 (dǎ zhé) — discount

## Example Sentences

- 树枝被风折断了。(Shù zhī bèi fēng zhé duàn le.) — The branch was broken by the wind.

## Notes

折 combines hand 扌 and 斤 (axe), representing breaking or bending.
