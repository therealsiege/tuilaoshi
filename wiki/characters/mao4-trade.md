# 贸 Trade, commerce, rashly

- **Character**: 贸
- **Pinyin**: mào
- **Meaning**: trade, commerce, rashly
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贸易 (mào yì) — trade, commerce
- 外贸 (wài mào) — foreign trade

## Example Sentences

- 两国的贸易往来日益频繁。(Liǎng guó de mào yì wǎng lái rì yì pín fán.) — Trade exchanges between the two countries are increasingly frequent.

## Notes

Essential commerce term. 贸然 means rashly or hastily. 经贸 means economy and trade.
