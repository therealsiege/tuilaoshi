# 寓 Reside, apartment

- **Character**: 寓
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: reside, apartment
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 公寓 (gōng yù) — apartment
- 寓所 (yù suǒ) — residence

## Example Sentences

- 他住在高层公寓里。(Tā zhù zài gāo céng gōng yù lǐ.) — He lives in a high-rise apartment.

## Notes

单身公寓 is studio apartment. 寓意 means implied meaning.
