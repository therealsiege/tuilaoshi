# 谣 Ballad, folk song, rumor

- **Character**: 谣
- **Pinyin**: yáo
- **Meaning**: ballad, folk song, rumor
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 民谣 (mín yáo) — folk song, ballad
- 谣言 (yáo yán) — rumor

## Example Sentences

- 中国各地都有独特的民谣文化。(Zhōngguó gè dì dōu yǒu dútè de mínyáo wénhuà.) — Every region in China has its unique folk song culture.

## Notes

Originally referred to folk songs and ballads. Now also commonly used for rumors or unfounded talk.
