# 粘 To Stick

- **Character**: 粘
- **Pinyin**: zhān
- **Meaning**: to stick, to paste, sticky
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粘贴 (zhān tiē) — to paste
- 粘性 (zhān xìng) — stickiness

## Example Sentences

- 粘贴这个标签。(Zhān tiē zhè ge biāo qiān.) — Paste this label.

## Notes

粘 as zhān means to stick; as nián means sticky. With rice radical suggesting sticky rice.
