# 儿 Child

- **Character**: 儿
- **Pinyin**: ér
- **Meaning**: child, son, suffix
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 女儿 (nǚ ér) — daughter
- 儿子 (ér zi) — son

## Example Sentences

- 他有一个儿子。(Tā yǒu yī gè ér zi.) — He has a son.

## Notes

儿 as a suffix creates the 儿化 (érhuà) Beijing accent: 花儿 (huār, flower), 这儿 (zhèr, here).
