# 融 Melt, fuse, finance

- **Character**: 融
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: melt, fuse, finance
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 金融 (jīn róng) — finance, banking
- 融合 (róng hé) — to fuse, blend

## Example Sentences

- 他在金融行业工作。(Tā zài jīn róng háng yè gōng zuò.) — He works in the finance industry.

## Notes

Essential in finance contexts. 融资 means financing. 融洽 means harmonious. 融化 means to melt.
