# 传 Transmit, transfer

- **Character**: 传
- **Pinyin**: chuán
- **Meaning**: transmit, transfer
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 传输 (chuán shū) — transmit, transfer
- 传送 (chuán sòng) — transmit

## Example Sentences

- 数据传输速度很快。(Shù jù chuán shū sù dù hěn kuài.) — Data transmission speed is fast.

## Notes

Essential for 传输 (transmit), 传送 (transfer), 传播 (propagate), data transfer
