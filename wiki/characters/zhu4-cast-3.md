# 铸 To Cast

- **Character**: 铸
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to cast (metal), to forge
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铸造 (zhù zào) — to cast, to forge
- 铸成 (zhù chéng) — to cast into

## Example Sentences

- 这座雕像是铸铜制成的。(Zhè zuò diāo xiàng shì zhù tóng zhì chéng de.) — This statue is cast in bronze.

## Notes

铸 simplified from 鑄. Metal radical 钅 representing pouring molten metal into molds.
