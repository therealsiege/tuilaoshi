# 令 Order, Command, 命令 Order

- **Character**: 令
- **Pinyin**: lìng
- **Meaning**: order, command, 命令 order
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 命令 (mìng lìng) — order, command
- 法令 (fǎ lìng) — law, decree
- 口令 (kǒu lìng) — password, command

## Example Sentences

- 上级下达了新的命令。(Shàngjí xiàdá le xīn de mìnglìng.) — The superiors issued new orders.

## Notes

Used in formal and military contexts for orders and commands. Also appears in 令人 (causing people to...) constructions.
