# 承 Bear, carry

- **Character**: 承
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: bear, carry
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 轴承 (zhóu chéng) — bearing
- 承载 (chéng zài) — carry, bear

## Example Sentences

- 这个轴承已经磨损了。(Zhè gè zhóu chéng yǐ jīng mó sǔn le.) — This bearing is worn out.

## Notes

Used in 轴承 (bearing), 承载 (load bearing), mechanical components
