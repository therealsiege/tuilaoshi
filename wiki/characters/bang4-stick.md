# 棒 Stick

- **Character**: 棒
- **Pinyin**: bàng
- **Meaning**: stick, club, great
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 棒球 (bàng qiú) — baseball
- 棒子 (bàng zi) — stick, club

## Example Sentences

- 他的棒球技术很棒。(Tā de bàngqiú jìshù hěn bàng.) — His baseball skills are great.

## Notes

Means stick/bat; used in baseball; also means excellent as adjective
