# 唢 Suona

- **Character**: 唢
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: suona (part of)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 唢呐 (suǒ nà) — suona (horn)
- 唢声 (suǒ shēng) — suona sound

## Example Sentences

- 唢呐声音洪亮。(Suǒ nà shēng yīn hóng liàng.) — The suona has a loud sound.

## Notes

唢 only appears in 唢呐, a loud double-reed horn.
