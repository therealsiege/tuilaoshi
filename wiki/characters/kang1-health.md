# 康 Health

- **Character**: 康
- **Pinyin**: kāng
- **Meaning**: health, well-being
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 健康 (jiàn kāng) — healthy, health
- 康复 (kāng fù) — to recover, rehabilitation
- 小康 (xiǎo kāng) — moderately prosperous

## Example Sentences

- 他正在康复中。(Tā zhèng zài kāng fù zhōng.) — He is recovering.

## Notes

State of health and wellness; recovery focus.
