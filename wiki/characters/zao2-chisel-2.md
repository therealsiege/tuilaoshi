# 凿 To Chisel

- **Character**: 凿
- **Pinyin**: záo
- **Meaning**: to chisel, to bore
- **Radical**: 凵 (receptacle)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 开凿 (kāi záo) — to cut (a canal)
- 凿壁偷光 (záo bì tōu guāng) — to bore a hole in the wall for light

## Example Sentences

- 古人开凿了运河。(Gǔ rén kāi záo le yùn hé.) — The ancients cut canals.

## Notes

凿 simplified from 鑿. Represents cutting/boring through material.
