# 凶 Fierce, terrible, ominous

- **Character**: 凶
- **Pinyin**: xiōng
- **Meaning**: fierce, terrible, ominous
- **Radical**: 凵 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 凶手 (xiōng shǒu) — murderer
- 凶猛 (xiōng měng) — fierce, ferocious

## Example Sentences

- 老虎很凶猛。(Lǎo hǔ hěn xiōng měng.) — Tigers are very fierce.

## Notes

Ferocity and inauspiciousness
