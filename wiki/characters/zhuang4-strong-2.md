# 壮 Strong, magnificent

- **Character**: 壮
- **Pinyin**: zhuàng
- **Meaning**: strong, magnificent
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 雄壮 (xióngzhuàng) — majestic, imposing
- 壮观 (zhuàngguān) — magnificent, spectacular

## Example Sentences

- 瀑布景色壮观。(Pùbù jǐngsè zhuàngguān.) — The waterfall scenery is spectacular.

## Notes

Describes physical strength or magnificent, imposing qualities.
