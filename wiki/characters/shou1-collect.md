# 收 Collect, receive

- **Character**: 收
- **Pinyin**: shōu
- **Meaning**: collect, receive
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 回收 (huí shōu) — recycle
- 收集 (shōu jí) — collect

## Example Sentences

- 回收利用很环保。(Huí shōu lì yòng hěn huán bǎo.) — Recycling is environmentally friendly.

## Notes

Used in 回收 (recycle), 收集 (collect), 接收 (receive)
