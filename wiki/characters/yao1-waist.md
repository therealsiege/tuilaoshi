# 腰 Waist

- **Character**: 腰
- **Pinyin**: yāo
- **Meaning**: waist, lower back
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腰带 (yāo dài) — belt
- 弯腰 (wān yāo) — to bend over

## Example Sentences

- 我的腰疼。(Wǒ de yāo téng.) — My lower back hurts.

## Notes

腰 refers to both the waist and lower back area. 腰包 (waist pouch) metaphorically means one's wallet or finances.
