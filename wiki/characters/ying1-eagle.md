# 鹰 Eagle

- **Character**: 鹰
- **Pinyin**: yīng
- **Meaning**: eagle, hawk
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 老鹰 (lǎo yīng) — eagle
- 雄鹰 (xióng yīng) — male eagle

## Example Sentences

- 老鹰在天空盘旋。(Lǎo yīng zài tiān kōng pán xuán.) — The eagle circles in the sky.

## Notes

鹰 simplified from 鷹. Bird radical 鸟 representing the raptor bird.
