# 倦 Tired

- **Character**: 倦
- **Pinyin**: juàn
- **Meaning**: tired, weary
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 疲倦 (píjuàn) — tired, weary
- 厌倦 (yànjuàn) — be tired of

## Example Sentences

- 工作一天后感到很疲倦。(Gōngzuò yī tiān hòu gǎndào hěn píjuàn.) — Feel very tired after working all day.

## Notes

Physical and mental tiredness. Person radical. 疲倦 describes exhaustion. Common in daily contexts.
