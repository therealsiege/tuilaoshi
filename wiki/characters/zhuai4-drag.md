# 拽 To Drag

- **Character**: 拽
- **Pinyin**: zhuài
- **Meaning**: to drag, to pull, to show off
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拽住 (zhuài zhù) — to hold onto
- 拉拽 (lā zhuài) — to drag

## Example Sentences

- 他拽着我的胳膊。(Tā zhuài zhe wǒ de gē bo.) — He pulled my arm.

## Notes

拽 hand 扌 plus 曳 (to drag), forceful pulling.
