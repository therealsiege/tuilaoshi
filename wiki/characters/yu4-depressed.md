# 郁 Depressed

- **Character**: 郁
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: depressed, dense, fragrant
- **Radical**: 阝(city)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抑郁 (yì yù) — depression
- 郁闷 (yù mèn) — gloomy

## Example Sentences

- 他患有抑郁症。(Tā huàn yǒu yì yù zhèng.) — He suffers from depression.

## Notes

郁 simplified from 鬱. Originally meant dense vegetation, extended to oppressed mood.
