# 杯 Cup

- **Character**: 杯
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: cup, glass
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 杯子 (bēizi) — cup
- 茶杯 (chábēi) — tea cup

## Example Sentences

- 桌上有两个杯子。(Zhuō shàng yǒu liǎng gè bēizi.) — There are two cups on the table.

## Notes

Very common word. Also used as measure word: 一杯水 (a cup of water). Essential daily vocabulary.
