# 逢 To Meet

- **Character**: 逢
- **Pinyin**: féng
- **Meaning**: to meet, to come across
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 相逢 (xiāng féng) — to meet (esp. by chance)
- 每逢 (měi féng) — whenever, every time

## Example Sentences

- 每逢佳节倍思亲。(Měi féng jiā jié bèi sī qīn.) — During festivals, one misses family even more.

## Notes

逢 shows movement 辶 with 夆 (meet head-on), representing meeting or encountering.
