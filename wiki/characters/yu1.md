# 迂 Circuitous, pedantic

- **Character**: 迂
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: circuitous, pedantic
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 迂回 (yū huí) — roundabout, circuitous
- 迂腐 (yū fǔ) — pedantic, old-fashioned

## Example Sentences

- 他的想法有些迂腐。(Tā de xiǎng fǎ yǒu xiē yū fǔ.) — His ideas are somewhat pedantic.

## Notes

Describes roundabout routes or old-fashioned thinking. Often used negatively.
