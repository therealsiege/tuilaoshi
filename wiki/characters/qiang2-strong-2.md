# 强 Strong

- **Character**: 强
- **Pinyin**: qiáng
- **Meaning**: strong, powerful
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 强壮 (qiáng zhuàng) — strong, sturdy
- 增强 (zēng qiáng) — to strengthen

## Example Sentences

- 他的身体很强壮。(Tā de shēntǐ hěn qiángzhuàng.) — His body is very strong.

## Notes

Strength and power; essential for describing athletic ability
