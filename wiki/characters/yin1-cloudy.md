# 阴 Cloudy

- **Character**: 阴
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: cloudy, overcast, shade, yin
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阴天 (yīn tiān) — cloudy day
- 阴阳 (yīn yáng) — yin and yang

## Example Sentences

- 今天是阴天。(Jīn tiān shì yīn tiān.) — Today is cloudy.

## Notes

阴 simplified from 陰. Represents shaded side of hill. Opposite of 阳 (yang, sunny).
