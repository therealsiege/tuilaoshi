# 测 To Measure

- **Character**: 测
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: to measure, to test, to survey
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 测试 (cè shì) — test
- 测量 (cè liáng) — to measure

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要做一个测试。(Wǒ men xū yào zuò yī ge cè shì.) — We need to do a test.

## Notes

测 means to measure or test. 预测 = predict, 侦测 = detect.
