# 万 Ten Thousand

- **Character**: 万
- **Pinyin**: wàn
- **Meaning**: ten thousand, myriad
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 一万 (yī wàn) — ten thousand
- 万一 (wàn yī) — just in case

## Example Sentences

- 长城有一万多里。(Cháng chéng yǒu yī wàn duō lǐ.) — The Great Wall is over ten thousand li.

## Notes

Chinese counting goes by ten-thousands (万), not thousands. 一亿 = 100 million = 一万万. This differs from English.
