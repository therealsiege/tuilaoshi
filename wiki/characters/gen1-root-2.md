# 根 Root

- **Character**: 根
- **Pinyin**: gēn
- **Meaning**: root, base
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 树根 (shù gēn) — tree root
- 根本 (gēn běn) — fundamental

## Example Sentences

- 问题的根源在这里。(Wèn tí de gēn yuán zài zhè lǐ.) — The root of the problem is here.

## Notes

根 has wood 木 with 艮 (limit), underground foundation of plant.
