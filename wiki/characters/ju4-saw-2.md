# 锯 Saw

- **Character**: 锯
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: saw
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锯子 (jù zi) — saw
- 电锯 (diàn jù) — electric saw

## Example Sentences

- 木匠用锯子锯木板。(Mùjiàng yòng jùzi jù mùbǎn.) — The carpenter sawed the wooden plank with a saw.

## Notes

锯 is a saw, used for cutting wood or metal. Can be both noun and verb.
