# 悲 Sad

- **Character**: 悲
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: sad, tragic
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 悲伤 (bēi shāng) — sad
- 悲观 (bēi guān) — pessimistic

## Example Sentences

- 听到这个消息，她很悲伤。(Tīng dào zhè gè xiāo xī, tā hěn bēi shāng.) — Hearing this news, she was very sad.

## Notes

悲 shows非 (not/negative) over heart 心, representing the heart in a negative sorrowful state.
