# 二 Two

- **Character**: 二
- **Pinyin**: èr
- **Meaning**: two, second
- **Radical**: 二 (two)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 第二 (dì èr) — second
- 二月 (èr yuè) — February

## Example Sentences

- 他排第二。(Tā pái dì èr.) — He ranked second.

## Notes

二 is used for ordinal numbers, counting, and phone numbers. Before measure words, use 两 instead.
