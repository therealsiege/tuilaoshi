# 端 To Hold Level

- **Character**: 端
- **Pinyin**: duān
- **Meaning**: to hold level,端端端端端端端端端端端端端, proper
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 端正 (duān zhèng) — upright
- 端起 (duān qǐ) — to pick up (with hands)

## Example Sentences

- 她端着一杯茶。(Tā duān zhe yì bēi chá.) — She holds a cup of tea.

## Notes

端 combines standing立立and山 (mountain), representing holding something level and proper.
