# 移 Move

- **Character**: 移
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: to move, to shift, to change
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 愚公移山 (yú gōng yí shān) — perseverance conquers all
- 转移 (zhuǎn yí) — to transfer

## Example Sentences

- 请把桌子移到那边。(Qǐng bǎ zhuōzi yí dào nà biān.) — Please move the table over there.

## Notes

In 愚公移山: to move (mountains); determination overcomes obstacles.
