# 词 Word

- **Character**: 词
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: word, term, part of speech
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 词语 (cí yǔ) — word, term
- 生词 (shēng cí) — new word

## Example Sentences

- 这个词我不认识。(Zhè gè cí wǒ bù rèn shi.) — I don't know this word.

## Notes

词 refers to words or vocabulary. In linguistics, 词类 means 'part of speech'. Also refers to classical poetry form (ci).
