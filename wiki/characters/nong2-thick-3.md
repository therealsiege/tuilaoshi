# 浓 Thick

- **Character**: 浓
- **Pinyin**: nóng
- **Meaning**: thick, dense, strong (flavor)
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浓厚 (nóng hòu) — dense, strong
- 浓度 (nóng dù) — concentration

## Example Sentences

- 这里的雾很浓。(Zhè lǐ de wù hěn nóng.) — The fog here is very thick.

## Notes

浓 simplified from 濃. Water 氵 concentrated, thick liquid.
