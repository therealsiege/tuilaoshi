# 档 Grade

- **Character**: 档
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: grade,档, file, stall
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 档案 (dàng àn) — 档,档
- 高档 (gāo dàng) — high-grade

## Example Sentences

- 这是一家高档餐厅。(Zhè shì yī jiā gāo dàng cān tīng.) — This is a high-end restaurant.

## Notes

档 simplified from 檔. Wood radical 木 originally referred to wooden档 frames, extended to classification levels.
