# 垫 Pad

- **Character**: 垫
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: pad, cushion, to pad
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 垫子 (diàn zi) — cushion, mat
- 鼠标垫 (shǔ biāo diàn) — mouse pad

## Example Sentences

- 我需要一个鼠标垫。(Wǒ xū yào yí ge shǔ biāo diàn.) — I need a mouse pad.

## Notes

垫 with earth radical suggests foundation/padding, used for pads and cushions.
