# 月 Moon

- **Character**: 月
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: moon, month
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 一月 (yī yuè) — January
- 月亮 (yuè liàng) — moon

## Example Sentences

- 今天几月几号？(Jīn tiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?) — What is today's date?

## Notes

月 is a pictograph of a crescent moon. It doubles as a radical meaning 'flesh/body' in medical characters like 肝 (liver).
