# 冰 Ice

- **Character**: 冰
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: ice, cold
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冰冷 (bīng lěng) — ice cold
- 冰雪 (bīng xuě) — ice and snow

## Example Sentences

- 冬天湖面结冰了。(Dōng tiān hú miàn jié bīng le.) — The lake froze over in winter.

## Notes

冰 represents frozen water. 结冰 = to freeze, 冰山 = iceberg.
