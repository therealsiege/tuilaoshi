# 给 To Give

- **Character**: 给
- **Pinyin**: gěi
- **Meaning**: to give, for, to
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 送给 (sòng gěi) — to give as gift
- 给予 (jǐ yǔ) — to give, to grant

## Example Sentences

- 我给你一本书。(Wǒ gěi nǐ yī běn shū.) — I'll give you a book.

## Notes

给 functions as verb (to give) and preposition (for/to). Essential in dative constructions.
