# 好 Good

- **Character**: 好
- **Pinyin**: hǎo
- **Meaning**: good, well, fine
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 好吃 (hǎo chī) — delicious
- 好看 (hǎo kàn) — good-looking
- 好人 (hǎo rén) — good person
- 你好 (nǐ hǎo) — hello

## Example Sentences

- 你好！(Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!
- 这个很好吃。(Zhè ge hěn hǎo chī.) — This is delicious.
- 今天天气很好。(Jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.) — The weather is good today.

## Notes

好 is composed of 女 (woman) and 子 (child). The idea of a woman with a child representing "good" reflects traditional Chinese values about family.
