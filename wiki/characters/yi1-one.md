# 一 One

- **Character**: 一
- **Pinyin**: yī
- **Meaning**: one
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 1

## Compounds

- 一起 (yī qǐ) — together
- 一些 (yī xiē) — some

## Example Sentences

- 我有一本书。(Wǒ yǒu yī běn shū.) — I have one book.

## Notes

一 changes tone based on the following character: yí before 4th tone, yì before 1st/2nd/3rd tones.
