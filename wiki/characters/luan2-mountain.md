# 峦 Mountain range, peak

- **Character**: 峦
- **Pinyin**: luán
- **Meaning**: mountain range, peak
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 山峦 (shān luán) — mountain range
- 峰峦 (fēng luán) — peaks and ridges

## Example Sentences

- 远处的山峦若隐若现。(Yuǎn chù de shān luán ruò yǐn ruò xiàn.) — The distant mountain ranges appear and disappear.

## Notes

Often used in literary descriptions of mountain scenery.
