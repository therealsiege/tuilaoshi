# 绕 To Wind

- **Character**: 绕
- **Pinyin**: rào
- **Meaning**: to wind, to circle
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 围绕 (wéi rào) — to revolve around
- 绕行 (rào xíng) — to detour

## Example Sentences

- 讨论围绕这个话题展开。(Tǎo lùn wéi rào zhè gè huà tí zhǎn kāi.) — The discussion revolves around this topic.

## Notes

绕 simplified from 繞. Silk radical 纟 representing winding thread around something.
