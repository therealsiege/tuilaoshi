# 末 End, powder

- **Character**: 末
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: end, powder
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 周末 (zhōu mò) — weekend
- 粉末 (fěn mò) — powder

## Example Sentences

- 周末你有什么计划?(Zhōumò nǐ yǒu shénme jìhuà?) — What plans do you have for the weekend?

## Notes

Can mean end or fine powder. Wood radical suggests sawdust origin.
