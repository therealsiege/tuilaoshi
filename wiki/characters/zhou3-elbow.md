# 肘 Elbow

- **Character**: 肘
- **Pinyin**: zhǒu
- **Meaning**: elbow
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 胳膊肘 (gē bo zhǒu) — elbow
- 掣肘 (chè zhǒu) — hamper, hinder

## Example Sentences

- 我的胳膊肘受伤了。(Wǒ de gē bo zhǒu shòu shāng le.) — My elbow is injured.

## Notes

Body part, also used metaphorically for interference
