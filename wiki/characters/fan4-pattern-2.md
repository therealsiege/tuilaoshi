# 范 Pattern, model, scope

- **Character**: 范
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: pattern, model, scope
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 范围 (fànwéi) — scope, range
- 模范 (mófàn) — model, example

## Example Sentences

- 这超出了我的工作范围。(Zhè chāochū le wǒ de gōngzuò fànwéi.) — This exceeds my scope of work.

## Notes

Used for patterns, models, and defining boundaries or scope.
