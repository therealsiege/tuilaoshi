# 插 To Insert

- **Character**: 插
- **Pinyin**: chā
- **Meaning**: to insert, to stick in, to plug in
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 插入 (chā rù) — to insert
- 插座 (chā zuò) — socket, outlet

## Example Sentences

- 请把插头插入插座。(Qǐng bǎ chā tóu chā rù chā zuò.) — Please plug the plug into the socket.

## Notes

插 means to stick in or insert. 插嘴 = to interrupt (a conversation).
