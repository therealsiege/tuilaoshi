# 免 Exempt, avoid, dismiss

- **Character**: 免
- **Pinyin**: miǎn
- **Meaning**: exempt, avoid, dismiss
- **Radical**: 儿 (son)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 免费 (miǎn fèi) — free of charge
- 赦免 (shè miǎn) — to pardon

## Example Sentences

- 这项服务免费提供。(Zhè xiàng fú wù miǎn fèi tí gōng.) — This service is provided free of charge.

## Notes

Means to exempt or avoid. 避免 means to avoid. 免疫 means immune. 罢免 means to recall/dismiss.
