# 勃 Sudden, vigorous, prosperous

- **Character**: 勃
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: sudden, vigorous, prosperous
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 蓬勃 (péng bó) — vigorous, flourishing
- 勃勃 (bó bó) — thriving, vigorous

## Example Sentences

- 年轻人充满勃勃生机。(Nián qīng rén chōng mǎn bó bó shēng jī.) — Young people are full of vitality.

## Notes

Indicates sudden vigor or energy. 勃然大怒 means to fly into a rage. 生机勃勃 means full of vitality.
