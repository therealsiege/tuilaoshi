# 怡 Happy, pleased, harmony

- **Character**: 怡
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: happy, pleased, harmony
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 怡然 (yí rán) — happy, pleased
- 心旷神怡 (xīn kuàng shén yí) — feel carefree and happy

## Example Sentences

- 美景令人心旷神怡。(Měi jǐng lìng rén xīn kuàng shén yí.) — The beautiful scenery makes one feel carefree and happy.

## Notes

Joy and mental pleasure
