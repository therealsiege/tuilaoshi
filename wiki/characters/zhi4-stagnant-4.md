# 滞 Stagnant, sluggish

- **Character**: 滞
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: stagnant, sluggish
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 停滞 (tíng zhì) — stagnate
- 滞销 (zhì xiāo) — unsalable, slow-selling

## Example Sentences

- 产品滞销。(Chǎn pǐn zhì xiāo.) — Products are selling slowly.

## Notes

滞后 means lag behind. 呆滞 means stagnant/dull.
