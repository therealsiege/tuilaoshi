# 颈 Neck, 颈椎 Cervical Vertebra

- **Character**: 颈
- **Pinyin**: jǐng
- **Meaning**: neck, 颈椎 cervical vertebra
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 颈椎 (jǐng zhuī) — cervical vertebra
- 脖颈 (bó jǐng) — neck
- 瓶颈 (píng jǐng) — bottleneck

## Example Sentences

- 长时间用电脑容易导致颈椎问题。(Cháng shíjiān yòng diànnǎo róngyì dǎozhì jǐngzhuī wèntí.) — Long-term computer use easily leads to cervical spine problems.

## Notes

Formal/medical term for neck. Also used figuratively in 瓶颈 (bottleneck). 颈椎 refers to cervical spine health issues.
