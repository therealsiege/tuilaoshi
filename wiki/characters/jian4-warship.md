# 舰 Warship, naval vessel

- **Character**: 舰
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: warship, naval vessel
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 军舰 (jūn jiàn) — warship
- 舰队 (jiàn duì) — fleet

## Example Sentences

- 航空母舰是海军的重要力量。(Háng kōng mǔ jiàn shì hǎi jūn de zhòng yào lì liàng.) — Aircraft carriers are an important naval force.

## Notes

Specifically for warships and large naval vessels. 舰长 means captain of a warship.
