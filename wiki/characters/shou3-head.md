# 首 Head

- **Character**: 首
- **Pinyin**: shǒu
- **Meaning**: head, first, measure word for songs/poems
- **Radical**: 首 (head)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 首都 (shǒu dū) — capital city
- 一首歌 (yī shǒu gē) — one song

## Example Sentences

- 我会唱很多首歌。(Wǒ huì chàng hěn duō shǒu gē.) — I can sing many songs.

## Notes

首 originally depicted a head with hair. As a measure word, it's specifically for songs and poems.
