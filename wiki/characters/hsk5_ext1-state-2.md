# 邦 State, Country, 联邦 Federation

- **Character**: 邦
- **Pinyin**: bāng
- **Meaning**: state, country, 联邦 federation
- **Radical**: ⻏ (city)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 联邦 (lián bāng) — federation
- 邦交 (bāng jiāo) — diplomatic relations
- 友邦 (yǒu bāng) — friendly nation

## Example Sentences

- 两国建立了邦交关系。(Liǎng guó jiànlì le bāngjiāo guānxì.) — The two countries established diplomatic relations.

## Notes

Formal term used in political and diplomatic contexts. 联邦 (federation) is used for federal systems of government.
