# 遥 Distant, remote, far

- **Character**: 遥
- **Pinyin**: yáo
- **Meaning**: distant, remote, far
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 遥远 (yáo yuǎn) — distant, remote
- 遥控 (yáo kòng) — remote control

## Example Sentences

- 那是一个遥远的国家。(Nà shì yī gè yáoyuǎn de guójiā.) — That is a distant country.

## Notes

Emphasizes great distance in space or time.
