# 咸 Salty

- **Character**: 咸
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: salty
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 咸菜 (xián cài) — pickled vegetables
- 咸鱼 (xián yú) — salted fish

## Example Sentences

- 太咸了。(Tài xián le.) — It's too salty.

## Notes

咸 with mouth radical 口. One of five basic tastes. 咸鱼 = preserved salted fish.
