# 赚 To earn, to make a profit

- **Character**: 赚
- **Pinyin**: zhuàn
- **Meaning**: to earn, to make a profit
- **Radical**: 贝(shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赚钱 (zhuàn qián) — to make money, to earn
- 盈利 (yíng lì) — to profit, to gain

## Example Sentences

- 这个生意很赚钱。(Zhège shēngyi hěn zhuàn qián.) — This business is very profitable.

## Notes

Emphasizes profit or gain from business.
