# 得 Structural Particle

- **Character**: 得
- **Pinyin**: de
- **Meaning**: structural particle; to get (dé)
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 觉得 (jué de) — to feel, to think
- 得到 (dé dào) — to obtain

## Example Sentences

- 他跑得很快。(Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.) — He runs very fast.

## Notes

得 has three readings: de (complement marker: 跑得快), dé (to get: 得到), děi (must: 得去). The complement marker is by far the most common.
