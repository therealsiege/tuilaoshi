# 井 Well

- **Character**: 井
- **Pinyin**: jǐng
- **Meaning**: Well constellation, well
- **Radical**: 二(two)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 井宿 (jǐng xiù) — Well constellation
- 水井 (shuǐ jǐng) — water well

## Example Sentences

- 井宿是南方朱雀七宿的第一宿。(Jǐng xiù shì nánfāng zhūquè qī xiù de dì yī xiù.) — Well is the first mansion of the Southern Vermillion Bird.

## Notes

井 means well. In astronomy, the Well constellation, shaped like the opening of a well.
