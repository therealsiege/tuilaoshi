# 杯 Cup

- **Character**: 杯
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: cup, glass, measure word for drinks
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 杯子 (bēi zi) — cup
- 干杯 (gān bēi) — cheers, bottoms up

## Example Sentences

- 请给我一杯水。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yī bēi shuǐ.) — Please give me a glass of water.

## Notes

杯 is the measure word for drinks: 一杯咖啡 (a cup of coffee). 干杯 = cheers.
