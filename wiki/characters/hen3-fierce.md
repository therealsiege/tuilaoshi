# 狠 Fierce, ruthless, resolute

- **Character**: 狠
- **Pinyin**: hěn
- **Meaning**: fierce, ruthless, resolute
- **Radical**: 犭 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 狠心 (hěn xīn) — heartless, ruthless
- 凶狠 (xiōng hěn) — fierce, ferocious

## Example Sentences

- 他下狠心离开了家乡。(Tā xià hěn xīn lí kāi le jiā xiāng.) — He resolutely left his hometown.

## Notes

Ruthlessness or determination
