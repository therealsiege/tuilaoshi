# 仓 Warehouse

- **Character**: 仓
- **Pinyin**: cāng
- **Meaning**: warehouse, storehouse, cabin
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 仓库 (cāng kù) — warehouse
- 粮仓 (liáng cāng) — granary

## Example Sentences

- 货物存放在仓库里。(Huò wù cún fàng zài cāng kù lǐ.) — The goods are stored in the warehouse.

## Notes

仓 is simplified from 倉. 仓库 = warehouse, 仓促 = hasty.
