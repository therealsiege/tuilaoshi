# 瘫 Paralyzed, 癱 (traditional)

- **Character**: 瘫
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: paralyzed, 癱 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘫痪 (tān huàn) — paralyzed
- 半瘫 (bàn tān) — hemiplegia

## Example Sentences

- 他因车祸而瘫痪了。(Tā yīn chēhuò ér tānhuàn le.) — He was paralyzed due to a car accident.

## Notes

Medical term for paralysis, often used in compound 瘫痪.
