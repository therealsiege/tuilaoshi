# 纸 Paper

- **Character**: 纸
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: paper
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 报纸 (bào zhǐ) — newspaper
- 纸张 (zhǐ zhāng) — paper

## Example Sentences

- 给我一张纸。(Gěi wǒ yī zhāng zhǐ.) — Give me a piece of paper.

## Notes

纸 means paper. Used in报纸 (newspaper), 白纸 (white paper), 纸巾 (tissue).
