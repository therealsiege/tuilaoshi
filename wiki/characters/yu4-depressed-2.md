# 郁 Depressed

- **Character**: 郁
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: depressed, gloomy
- **Radical**: 阝 (city)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抑郁 (yì yù) — depression, depressed
- 郁闷 (yù mèn) — gloomy, depressed
- 忧郁 (yōu yù) — melancholy

## Example Sentences

- 他被诊断为抑郁症。(Tā bèi zhěn duàn wéi yì yù zhèng.) — He was diagnosed with depression.

## Notes

Low mood state; major mental health concern.
