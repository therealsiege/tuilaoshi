# 输 To Lose

- **Character**: 输
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: to lose, to transport
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 输入 (shū rù) — to input
- 运输 (yùn shū) — to transport

## Example Sentences

- 他们输了比赛。(Tā men shū le bǐ sài.) — They lost the match.

## Notes

输 shows vehicle 车 and 俞 (consent), originally meaning to transport, later extended to 'lose' as in transferring victory to another.
