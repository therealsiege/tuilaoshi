# 品 Product

- **Character**: 品
- **Pinyin**: pǐn
- **Meaning**: product, grade, to taste
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 作品 (zuò pǐn) — work (of art)
- 小品 (xiǎo pǐn) — sketch (comedy)

## Example Sentences

- 春晚的小品很搞笑。(Chūn wǎn de xiǎo pǐn hěn gǎo xiào.) — The comedy sketches on the Spring Gala are very funny.

## Notes

品 means product or grade; 小品 are short comedy sketches.
