# 裂 Split, crack, break

- **Character**: 裂
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: split, crack, break
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裂缝 (liè fèng) — crack, crevice
- 分裂 (fēn liè) — split, divide

## Example Sentences

- 墙上出现了裂缝。(Qiáng shàng chū xiàn le liè fèng.) — Cracks appeared on the wall.
