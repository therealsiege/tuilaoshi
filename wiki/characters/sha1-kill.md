# 杀 Kill

- **Character**: 杀
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: kill
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 杀菌 (shā jūn) — to sterilize, kill bacteria
- 杀虫 (shā chóng) — to kill insects
- 自杀 (zì shā) — suicide

## Example Sentences

- 高温可以杀菌。(Gāo wēn kě yǐ shā jūn.) — High temperature can kill bacteria.

## Notes

Killing action; sterilization method.
