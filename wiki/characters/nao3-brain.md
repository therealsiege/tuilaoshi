# 脑 Brain

- **Character**: 脑
- **Pinyin**: nǎo
- **Meaning**: brain
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 大脑 (dà nǎo) — cerebrum, brain
- 脑袋 (nǎo dai) — head
- 头脑 (tóu nǎo) — brains, mind

## Example Sentences

- 他的大脑受伤了。(Tā de dà nǎo shòu shāng le.) — His brain was injured.

## Notes

Key organ for cognition; used in compounds related to mental functions and neurological conditions.
