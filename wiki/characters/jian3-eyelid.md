# 睑 Eyelid

- **Character**: 睑
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: eyelid
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 眼睑 (yǎn jiǎn) — eyelid
- 睑炎 (jiǎn yán) — blepharitis
- 上睑 (shàng jiǎn) — upper eyelid

## Example Sentences

- 他眼睑红肿。(Tā yǎn jiǎn hóng zhǒng.) — His eyelids are red and swollen.

## Notes

Protects eyes; common site of inflammation and infection.
