# 塌 Collapse, cave in, sink

- **Character**: 塌
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: collapse, cave in, sink
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 倒塌 (dǎo tā) — collapse, fall down
- 塌陷 (tā xiàn) — cave in, subside

## Example Sentences

- 老房子年久失修,终于倒塌了。(Lǎo fáng zi nián jiǔ shī xiū, zhōng yú dǎo tā le.) — The old house, long in disrepair, finally collapsed.

## Notes

Structural failure and collapse
