# 厅 Hall

- **Character**: 厅
- **Pinyin**: tīng
- **Meaning**: hall, office
- **Radical**: 厂 (factory)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 客厅 (kè tīng) — living room
- 餐厅 (cān tīng) — dining hall

## Example Sentences

- 客厅很宽敞。(Kè tīng hěn kuān chǎng.) — The living room is very spacious.

## Notes

厅 with factory/cliff radical for large hall/room, official or public spaces.
