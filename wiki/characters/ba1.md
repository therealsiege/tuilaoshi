# 八 Eight

- **Character**: 八
- **Pinyin**: bā
- **Meaning**: eight
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 八月 (bā yuè) — August
- 八十 (bā shí) — eighty

## Example Sentences

- 八是一个幸运数字。(Bā shì yī gè xìng yùn shù zì.) — Eight is a lucky number.

## Notes

八 is the luckiest number in Chinese culture because it sounds like 发 (fā, to prosper). The Beijing Olympics opened on 8/8/08.
