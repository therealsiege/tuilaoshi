# 疑 To Doubt

- **Character**: 疑
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: to doubt, to suspect
- **Radical**: 疋 (bolt)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 怀疑 (huái yí) — to doubt, to suspect
- 疑问 (yí wèn) — question, doubt

## Example Sentences

- 我怀疑他说的话。(Wǒ huái yí tā shuō de huà.) — I doubt what he said.

## Notes

疑 shows person with staff 矢 looking at tracks 疋, representing examining with doubt or suspicion.
