# 朦 Hazy

- **Character**: 朦
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: dim, hazy, obscure
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 朦胧 (méng lóng) — hazy, dim
- 朦朦胧胧 (méng méng lóng lóng) — vaguely

## Example Sentences

- 月光朦胧。(Yuè guāng méng lóng.) — The moonlight is hazy.

## Notes

朦 combines moon 月 and 蒙 (to cover), moonlight obscured.
