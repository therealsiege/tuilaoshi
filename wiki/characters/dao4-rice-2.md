# 稻 Rice, paddy

- **Character**: 稻
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: rice, paddy
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 水稻 (shuǐ dào) — rice, paddy
- 稻谷 (dào gǔ) — unhusked rice

## Example Sentences

- 南方的主要农作物是水稻。(Nánfāng de zhǔyào nóngzuòwù shì shuǐdào.) — The main crop in the south is rice.

## Notes

Rice plant and crop. Fundamental staple in southern and central China, requiring flooded paddies for cultivation.
