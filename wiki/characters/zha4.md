# 炸 To Explode

- **Character**: 炸
- **Pinyin**: zhà
- **Meaning**: to explode, to fry
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 爆炸 (bào zhà) — to explode
- 油炸 (yóu zhá) — to deep-fry

## Example Sentences

- 这道菜是油炸的。(Zhè dào cài shì yóu zhá de.) — This dish is deep-fried.

## Notes

炸 combines fire 火 and 乍 (sudden), representing sudden explosion or deep-frying.
