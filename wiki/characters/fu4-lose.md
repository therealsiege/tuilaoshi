# 负 Lose

- **Character**: 负
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to lose, bear, carry
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 负责 (fù zé) — to be responsible for
- 胜负 (shèng fù) — victory or defeat

## Example Sentences

- 这场比赛他们三胜两负。(Zhè chǎng bǐsài tāmen sān shèng liǎng fù.) — In this competition they won three and lost two.

## Notes

Formal losing term; also means negative, to bear burden
