# 防 Defend

- **Character**: 防
- **Pinyin**: fáng
- **Meaning**: to defend, prevent
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 防守 (fáng shǒu) — to defend, defense
- 预防 (yù fáng) — to prevent

## Example Sentences

- 他们的防守很严密。(Tāmen de fángshǒu hěn yánmì.) — Their defense is very tight.

## Notes

Defense in sports; also means prevent, guard against
