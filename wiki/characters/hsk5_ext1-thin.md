# 细 Thin, Fine, 仔细 Careful

- **Character**: 细
- **Pinyin**: xì
- **Meaning**: thin, fine, 仔细 careful
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 仔细 (zǐ xì) — careful, attentive
- 细节 (xì jié) — detail
- 详细 (xiáng xì) — detailed

## Example Sentences

- 请注意细节问题。(Qǐng zhùyì xìjié wèntí.) — Please pay attention to details.

## Notes

Important character for describing precision and care. 细节 (detail) and 仔细 (careful) are very common in both spoken and written Chinese.
