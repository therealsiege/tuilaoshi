# 纳 Nano, accept

- **Character**: 纳
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: nano, accept
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纳米 (nà mǐ) — nanometer
- 纳入 (nà rù) — include

## Example Sentences

- 纳米技术很先进。(Nà mǐ jì shù hěn xiān jìn.) — Nanotechnology is advanced.

## Notes

Used in 纳米 (nano), 纳秒 (nanosecond), nanoscale
