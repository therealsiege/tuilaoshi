# 瘾 Addiction, craving, 癮 (traditional)

- **Character**: 瘾
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: addiction, craving, 癮 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 上瘾 (shàng yǐn) — to become addicted
- 烟瘾 (yān yǐn) — tobacco addiction

## Example Sentences

- 他抽烟上瘾了。(Tā chōuyān shàngyǐn le.) — He became addicted to smoking.

## Notes

Addiction or strong craving for something.
