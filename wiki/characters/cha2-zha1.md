# 查 Check

- **Character**: 查
- **Pinyin**: chá/zhā
- **Meaning**: examine, check
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — to examine, inspect
- 筛查 (shāi chá) — screening, to screen
- 调查 (diào chá) — to investigate

## Example Sentences

- 医生给他做全面检查。(Yī shēng gěi tā zuò quán miàn jiǎn chá.) — The doctor gave him a comprehensive examination.

## Notes

Investigation or examination; diagnostic step.
