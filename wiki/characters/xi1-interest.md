# 息 Interest, rest

- **Character**: 息
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: interest, rest
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 利息 (lì xī) — interest
- 息税 (xī shuì) — interest and tax

## Example Sentences

- 银行利息很低。(Yín háng lì xī hěn dī.) — Bank interest rates are very low.

## Notes

年息 is annual interest. 本息 means principal plus interest.
