# 飘 To Flutter

- **Character**: 飘
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: to float in air, to flutter
- **Radical**: 风 (wind)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 飘动 (piāo dòng) — to flutter
- 飘扬 (piāo yáng) — to wave

## Example Sentences

- 国旗在空中飘扬。(Guó qí zài kōng zhōng piāo yáng.) — The national flag is waving in the air.

## Notes

飘 simplified from 飄. Wind 风 carrying things aloft.
