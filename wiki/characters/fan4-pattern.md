# 范 范

- **Character**: 范
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: pattern, model, range, scope
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 范围 (fàn wéi) — scope, range
- 示范 (shì fàn) — to demonstrate

## Example Sentences

- 这超出了我的范围。(Zhè chāo chū le wǒ de fàn wéi.) — This exceeds my范.

## Notes

范 represents a pattern or model to follow. Common in范围 (scope) and示范 (demonstration).
