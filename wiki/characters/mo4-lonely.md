# 寞 Lonely

- **Character**: 寞
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: lonely, silent, deserted
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 寂寞 (jì mò) — lonely
- 落寞 (luò mò) — lonely, dejected

## Example Sentences

- 她感到很寂寞。(Tā gǎn dào hěn jì mò.) — She feels very lonely.

## Notes

寞 has roof 宀 with 莫 (nothing/don't), emptiness under the roof.
