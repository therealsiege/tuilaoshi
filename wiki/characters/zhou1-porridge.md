# 粥 Porridge, congee

- **Character**: 粥
- **Pinyin**: zhōu
- **Meaning**: porridge, congee
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喝粥 (hē zhōu) — to eat porridge
- 小米粥 (xiǎo mǐ zhōu) — millet porridge

## Example Sentences

- 早上我喝了一碗粥。(Zǎoshang wǒ hēle yī wǎn zhōu.) — I had a bowl of porridge this morning.

## Notes

Common Chinese breakfast food. Rice radical shows it's made from grains.
