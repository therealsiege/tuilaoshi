# 息 Rest

- **Character**: 息
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: rest, cease
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 休息 (xiū xi) — to rest
- 安息 (ān xī) — to rest in peace
- 气息 (qì xī) — breath

## Example Sentences

- 医生建议他多休息。(Yī shēng jiàn yì tā duō xiū xi.) — The doctor advised him to rest more.

## Notes

Rest and cessation; essential for recovery.
