# 着 Ongoing State

- **Character**: 着
- **Pinyin**: zhe
- **Meaning**: continuous state particle
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 看着 (kàn zhe) — looking at
- 穿着 (chuān zhe) — wearing

## Example Sentences

- 门开着。(Mén kāi zhe.) — The door is open (in a state of being open).

## Notes

着 (zhe) indicates a continuing state or action. Also read as zháo (touching) and zhuó (to apply).
