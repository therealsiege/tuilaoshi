# 投 Throw, invest, submit

- **Character**: 投
- **Pinyin**: tóu
- **Meaning**: throw, invest, submit
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 投资 (tóu zī) — to invest
- 投票 (tóu piào) — to vote

## Example Sentences

- 他投资了一家科技公司。(Tā tóu zī le yī jiā kē jì gōng sī.) — He invested in a tech company.

## Notes

Versatile character. 投降 means to surrender. 投入 means to put in. 投诉 means to complain.
