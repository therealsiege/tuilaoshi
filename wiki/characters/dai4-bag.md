# 袋 Bag

- **Character**: 袋
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: bag, sack, pocket
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 袋子 (dài zi) — bag
- 口袋 (kǒu dài) — pocket

## Example Sentences

- 给我一个袋子。(Gěi wǒ yī gè dài zi.) — Give me a bag.

## Notes

袋 uses clothing radical because bags/pockets are part of clothing. 口袋 = pocket.
