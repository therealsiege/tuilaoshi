# 厅 Hall

- **Character**: 厅
- **Pinyin**: tīng
- **Meaning**: hall, office, department
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 客厅 (kè tīng) — living room
- 大厅 (dà tīng) — hall

## Example Sentences

- 客人在客厅等候。(Kè rén zài kè tīng děng hòu.) — The guests wait in the living room.

## Notes

厅 simplified from 廳. Shelter 厂 with space, reception hall.
