# 瓮 Earthen jar, urn

- **Character**: 瓮
- **Pinyin**: wèng
- **Meaning**: earthen jar, urn
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 酒瓮 (jiǔ wèng) — wine jar
- 瓮中捉鳖 (wèng zhōng zhuō biē) — catch a turtle in a jar

## Example Sentences

- 瓮中捉鳖，手到擒来。(Wèng zhōng zhuō biē, shǒu dào qín lái.) — It's as easy as catching a turtle in a jar.

## Notes

Large earthenware vessel
