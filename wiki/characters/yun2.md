# 云 Cloud

- **Character**: 云
- **Pinyin**: yún
- **Meaning**: cloud
- **Radical**: 二 (two)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 云朵 (yún duǒ) — cloud
- 白云 (bái yún) — white cloud

## Example Sentences

- 天上有很多白云。(Tiān shàng yǒu hěn duō bái yún.) — There are many white clouds in the sky.

## Notes

云 simplified from 雲, originally a pictograph of swirling clouds. Also appears in 云南 (Yunnan province, 'south of the clouds').
