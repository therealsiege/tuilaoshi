# 化 Change, transform, -ize

- **Character**: 化
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: change, transform, -ize
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 进化 (jìn huà) — evolution, evolve
- 变化 (biàn huà) — change, variation

## Example Sentences

- 物种通过自然选择不断进化。(Wù zhǒng tōng guò zì rán xuǎn zé bù duàn jìn huà.) — Species continuously evolve through natural selection.
