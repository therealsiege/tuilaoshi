# 翔 To Soar

- **Character**: 翔
- **Pinyin**: xiáng
- **Meaning**: to soar, to circle in the air
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 飞翔 (fēi xiáng) — to fly, to soar
- 翱翔 (áo xiáng) — to soar, to hover

## Example Sentences

- 老鹰在天空中翱翔。(Lǎo yīng zài tiān kōng zhōng áo xiáng.) — The eagle soars in the sky.

## Notes

翔 combines羊 (sheep) and feather 羽, representing graceful soaring flight.
