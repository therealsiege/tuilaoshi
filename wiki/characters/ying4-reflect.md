# 映 To Reflect

- **Character**: 映
- **Pinyin**: yìng
- **Meaning**: to reflect, to shine
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 反映 (fǎn yìng) — to reflect
- 放映 (fàng yìng) — to show (film)

## Example Sentences

- 阳光映在水面上。(Yáng guāng yìng zài shuǐ miàn shàng.) — Sunlight reflects on the water surface.

## Notes

映 combines sun 日 and 央 (center), representing light shining and reflecting.
