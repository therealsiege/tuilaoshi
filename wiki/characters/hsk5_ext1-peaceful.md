# 宁 Peaceful, Rather, 安宁 Peaceful

- **Character**: 宁
- **Pinyin**: níng
- **Meaning**: peaceful, rather, 安宁 peaceful
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 安宁 (ān níng) — peaceful, tranquil
- 宁静 (níng jìng) — quiet, tranquil
- 宁可 (nìng kě) — would rather

## Example Sentences

- 我宁可走路也不坐车。(Wǒ nìngkě zǒulù yě bù zuò chē.) — I would rather walk than take a vehicle.

## Notes

As níng: peaceful (安宁). As nìng: would rather (宁可). 宁静 (tranquil) is common descriptive term.
