# 窗 Window

- **Character**: 窗
- **Pinyin**: chuāng
- **Meaning**: window
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 窗户 (chuāng hu) — window
- 窗口 (chuāng kǒu) — window, counter

## Example Sentences

- 请开窗。(Qǐng kāi chuāng.) — Please open the window.

## Notes

窗 uses cave radical 穴. 窗户 is complete word for window in buildings.
