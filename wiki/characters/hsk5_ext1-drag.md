# 拖 Drag, Delay, 拖延 Delay

- **Character**: 拖
- **Pinyin**: tuō
- **Meaning**: drag, delay, 拖延 delay
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拖延 (tuō yán) — delay, procrastinate
- 拖拉 (tuō lā) — drag, procrastinate
- 拖鞋 (tuō xié) — slippers

## Example Sentences

- 不要拖延时间。(Bùyào tuōyán shíjiān.) — Don't delay time.

## Notes

Versatile verb for dragging physically (拖地 mop floor) or delaying (拖延 procrastinate). 拖鞋 (slippers) from dragging feet.
