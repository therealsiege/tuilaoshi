# 让 让步, allow, yield

- **Character**: 让
- **Pinyin**: ràng
- **Meaning**: 让步, allow, yield
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 让步 (ràng bù) — 让步, make concessions
- 转让 (zhuǎn ràng) — transfer, assign

## Example Sentences

- 价格上我们可以让步。(Jià gé shàng wǒ men kě yǐ ràng bù.) — We can make concessions on price.

## Notes

出让 means transfer/sell. 谦让 means modest/yield.
