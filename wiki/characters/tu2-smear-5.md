# 涂 Smear

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: smear, apply, coat
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂料 (tú liào) — coating, paint
- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — smear, daub

## Example Sentences

- 工人在墙上涂了一层白色涂料。(Gōngrén zài qiáng shàng tú le yī céng báisè túliào.) — The worker applied a layer of white paint to the wall.

## Notes

涂 means to smear, apply, or coat with a substance. Common in painting and surface treatment.
