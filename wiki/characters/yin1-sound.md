# 音 Sound

- **Character**: 音
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: sound, tone, music
- **Radical**: 音 (sound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 声音 (shēng yīn) — sound, voice
- 音乐 (yīn yuè) — music

## Example Sentences

- 他的声音很好听。(Tā de shēng yīn hěn hǎo tīng.) — His voice sounds very pleasant.

## Notes

音 means sound or tone; fundamental character in music terminology.
