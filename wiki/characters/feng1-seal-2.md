# 封 To Seal

- **Character**: 封
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: to seal, to close, envelope, measure word
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 信封 (xìn fēng) — envelope
- 封闭 (fēng bì) — to seal, to close

## Example Sentences

- 请把这封信寄出去。(Qǐng bǎ zhè fēng xìn jì chū qù.) — Please send this letter.

## Notes

封 means to seal or close. Also measure word for letters (一封信). Used in封锁 (blockade).
