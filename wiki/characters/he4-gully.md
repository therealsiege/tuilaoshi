# 壑 Gully, ravine, valley

- **Character**: 壑
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: gully, ravine, valley
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 沟壑 (gōu hè) — gully, ravine
- 千沟万壑 (qiān gōu wàn hè) — innumerable ravines

## Example Sentences

- 黄土高原上沟壑纵横。(Huáng tǔ gāo yuán shàng gōu hè zòng héng.) — The loess plateau is crisscrossed with ravines.

## Notes

Literary term for ravines and gullies. 欲壑难填 means insatiable greed.
