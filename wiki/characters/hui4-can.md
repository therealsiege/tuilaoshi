# 会 Can

- **Character**: 会
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: can, will, to meet
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 开会 (kāi huì) — to have a meeting
- 社会 (shè huì) — society

## Example Sentences

- 你会说中文吗？(Nǐ huì shuō zhōng wén ma?) — Can you speak Chinese?

## Notes

会 indicates learned ability (as opposed to 能 which is innate ability or permission).
