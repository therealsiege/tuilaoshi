# 赖 Rely

- **Character**: 赖
- **Pinyin**: lài
- **Meaning**: rely on, depend
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 依赖 (yī lài) — to depend on, dependence
- 信赖 (xìn lài) — to trust
- 赖以 (lài yǐ) — to rely on

## Example Sentences

- 避免对安眠药产生依赖。(Bì miǎn duì ān mián yào chǎn shēng yī lài.) — Avoid developing dependence on sleeping pills.

## Notes

Reliance; addiction component.
