# 降 To Descend

- **Character**: 降
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: to descend, to lower, to drop
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 降低 (jiàng dī) — to reduce, to lower
- 下降 (xià jiàng) — to descend, to drop

## Example Sentences

- 温度降低了。(Wēn dù jiàng dī le.) — The temperature has dropped.

## Notes

降 can also be read as xiáng meaning 'to surrender'. As jiàng it's the opposite of 升 (to rise).
