# 密 Dense

- **Character**: 密
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: dense, close, secret
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 秘密 (mì mì) — secret
- 密切 (mì qiè) — close, intimate

## Example Sentences

- 我们关系很密切。(Wǒ men guān xì hěn mì qiè.) — We have a very close relationship.

## Notes

密 shows roof 宀 with 山 (mountain) and 虫 (insects), representing things packed closely together.
