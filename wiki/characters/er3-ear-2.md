# 耳 Ear

- **Character**: 耳
- **Pinyin**: ěr
- **Meaning**: ear
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 耳朵 (ěr duo) — ear
- 耳鼻喉 (ěr bí hóu) — ear, nose and throat (ENT)
- 中耳炎 (zhōng ěr yán) — otitis media

## Example Sentences

- 他耳朵听不清楚。(Tā ěr duo tīng bu qīng chu.) — He can't hear clearly with his ears.

## Notes

Organ of hearing and balance; ENT specialty focus.
