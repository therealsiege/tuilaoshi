# 锦 Brocade

- **Character**: 锦
- **Pinyin**: jǐn
- **Meaning**: brocade, bright, splendid
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锦上添花 (jǐn shàng tiān huā) — make good things better
- 锦绣 (jǐn xiù) — beautiful, splendid

## Example Sentences

- 这个设计锦上添花。(Zhège shèjì jǐn shàng tiān huā.) — This design is the icing on the cake.

## Notes

Used in 锦上添花: adding flowers to already beautiful brocade; opposite of 雪中送炭.
