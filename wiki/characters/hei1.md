# 黑 Black

- **Character**: 黑
- **Pinyin**: hēi
- **Meaning**: black, dark
- **Radical**: 黑 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 黑色 (hēi sè) — black (color)
- 黑夜 (hēi yè) — dark night

## Example Sentences

- 天黑了。(Tiān hēi le.) — It's gotten dark.

## Notes

黑 originally depicted a person with soot on their face, darkened by fire. Colloquially can mean 'shady, illegal.'
