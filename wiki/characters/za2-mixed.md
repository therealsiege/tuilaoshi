# 杂 Mixed, miscellaneous

- **Character**: 杂
- **Pinyin**: zá
- **Meaning**: mixed, miscellaneous
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 杂乱 (záluàn) — messy, disorderly
- 复杂 (fùzá) — complex, complicated

## Example Sentences

- 房间很杂乱。(Fángjiān hěn záluàn.) — The room is very messy.

## Notes

Common character for mixed, miscellaneous, or disorderly.
