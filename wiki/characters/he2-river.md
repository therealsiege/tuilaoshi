# 河 River

- **Character**: 河
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: river
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 河流 (hé liú) — river
- 黄河 (huáng hé) — Yellow River

## Example Sentences

- 黄河是中国的母亲河。(Huáng hé shì zhōng guó de mǔ qīn hé.) — The Yellow River is China's mother river.

## Notes

河 refers to rivers. The two great rivers of China are 黄河 (Yellow River) and 长江 (Yangtze River, literally 'Long River').
