# 耘 Weed, hoe

- **Character**: 耘
- **Pinyin**: yún
- **Meaning**: weed, hoe
- **Radical**: 耒 (plow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耕耘 (gēng yún) — plow and weed, cultivate
- 耘田 (yún tián) — weed the fields

## Example Sentences

- 一分耕耘，一分收获。(Yī fēn gēng yún, yī fēn shōuhuò.) — You reap what you sow (literally: one part cultivation, one part harvest).

## Notes

Weeding and cultivating crops. Part of the farming idiom emphasizing that effort leads to results.
