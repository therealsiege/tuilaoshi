# 挡 Block

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, stop
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to block, obstruct
- 挡球 (dǎng qiú) — to block a ball

## Example Sentences

- 后卫挡住了对方的射门。(Hòuwèi dǎng zhù le duìfāng de shèmén.) — The defender blocked the opponent's shot.

## Notes

Blocking and stopping; defensive action
