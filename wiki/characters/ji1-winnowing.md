# 箕 Winnow

- **Character**: 箕
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: winnowing basket, constellation
- **Radical**: 竹(bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 箕宿 (jī xiù) — Winnowing Basket constellation
- 簸箕 (bò ji) — dustpan

## Example Sentences

- 箕宿是东方七宿的最后一宿。(Jī xiù shì dōngfāng qī xiù de zuìhòu yī xiù.) — Winnowing Basket is the last of the seven Eastern mansions.

## Notes

箕 refers to a winnowing basket or dustpan. In astronomy, the Winnowing Basket constellation.
