# 麦 Wheat, barley, oats

- **Character**: 麦
- **Pinyin**: mài
- **Meaning**: wheat, barley, oats
- **Radical**: 麦 (wheat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 小麦 (xiǎo mài) — wheat
- 大麦 (dà mài) — barley

## Example Sentences

- 小麦是北方的主要粮食作物。(Xiǎo mài shì běi fāng de zhǔ yào liáng shi zuò wù.) — Wheat is the main food crop in the north.

## Notes

Staple grain crop especially in northern China
