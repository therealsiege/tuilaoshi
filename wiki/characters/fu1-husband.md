# 夫 Husband, man, manual worker

- **Character**: 夫
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: husband, man, manual worker
- **Radical**: 大(big)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 丈夫 (zhàng fu) — husband
- 夫妇 (fū fù) — married couple

## Example Sentences

- 她的丈夫是医生。(Tā de zhàngfu shì yīshēng.) — Her husband is a doctor.

## Notes

Common in compounds related to husband or manhood.
