# 怒 Anger

- **Character**: 怒
- **Pinyin**: nù
- **Meaning**: anger, fury, to bloom vigorously
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 心花怒放 (xīn huā nù fàng) — be overjoyed
- 愤怒 (fèn nù) — fury, anger

## Example Sentences

- 听到好消息，她心花怒放。(Tīng dào hǎo xiāoxi, tā xīn huā nù fàng.) — Hearing the good news, she was overjoyed.

## Notes

In 心花怒放, 怒 means vigorously (not angrily); flowers of the heart bloom intensely.
