# 匣 Box

- **Character**: 匣
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: box, case
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 匣子 (xiá zi) — small box
- 暗匣 (àn xiá) — dark box

## Example Sentences

- 她打开了一个小匣子。(Tā dǎ kāi le yí gè xiǎo xiá zi.) — She opened a small box.

## Notes

匣 has box 匚 enclosing 甲 (first/armor), a container.
