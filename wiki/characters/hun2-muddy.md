# 浑 Muddy, simple, whole

- **Character**: 浑
- **Pinyin**: hún
- **Meaning**: muddy, simple, whole
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浑浊 (hún zhuó) — muddy, turbid
- 浑身 (hún shēn) — all over (the body)

## Example Sentences

- 他浑身是汗。(Tā hún shēn shì hàn.) — He was covered in sweat.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: muddy, whole, simple. 浑然一体 means to form an organic whole.
