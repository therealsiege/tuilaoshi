# 女 Woman

- **Character**: 女
- **Pinyin**: nǚ
- **Meaning**: female, woman
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 女人 (nǚ rén) — woman
- 女儿 (nǚ ér) — daughter

## Example Sentences

- 她是一个好女孩。(Tā shì yī gè hǎo nǚ hái.) — She is a good girl.

## Notes

女 is a pictograph of a woman kneeling. As a radical it appears in many characters: 妈, 姐, 妹, 好.
