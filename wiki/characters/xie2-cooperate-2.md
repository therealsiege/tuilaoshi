# 协 To Cooperate

- **Character**: 协
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: to cooperate, to协助,協調
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 协会 (xié huì) — association
- 协议 (xié yì) — agreement

## Example Sentences

- 双方达成了协议。(Shuāng fāng dá chéng le xié yì.) — Both parties reached an agreement.

## Notes

协 is simplified from 協. Represents cooperation and joint effort.
