# 养 To Raise

- **Character**: 养
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: to raise, to keep, to cultivate
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 培养 (péi yǎng) — to cultivate
- 养成 (yǎng chéng) — to form (habit)

## Example Sentences

- 她养了一只猫。(Tā yǎng le yì zhī māo.) — She keeps a cat.

## Notes

养 simplified from 養. Shows sheep 羊 under roof, representing providing care and nourishment.
