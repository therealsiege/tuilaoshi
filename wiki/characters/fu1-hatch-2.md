# 孵 To Hatch

- **Character**: 孵
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: to hatch, to incubate
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 孵化 (fū huà) — to hatch
- 孵卵 (fū luǎn) — to incubate eggs

## Example Sentences

- 母鸡在孵小鸡。(Mǔ jī zài fū xiǎo jī.) — The hen is hatching chicks.

## Notes

孵 has child 子 with 卵 (egg) and 孚 (to trust), warming eggs to hatch.
