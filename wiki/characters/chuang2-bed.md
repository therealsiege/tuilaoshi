# 床 Bed

- **Character**: 床
- **Pinyin**: chuáng
- **Meaning**: bed
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 上床 (shàng chuáng) — to go to bed
- 床单 (chuáng dān) — bedsheet

## Example Sentences

- 我要上床睡觉了。(Wǒ yào shàng chuáng shuì jiào le.) — I'm going to bed.

## Notes

床 with shelter radical 广. 上床 means to get into bed, 起床 means to get out of bed.
