# 灸 Moxibustion

- **Character**: 灸
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: moxibustion
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 针灸 (zhēn jiǔ) — acupuncture and moxibustion
- 艾灸 (ài jiǔ) — moxibustion

## Example Sentences

- 针灸是中医的重要疗法。(Zhēnjiǔ shì zhōngyī de zhòngyào liáofǎ.) — Acupuncture and moxibustion are important therapies in Chinese medicine.

## Notes

Burning moxa (mugwort) near acupoints to stimulate healing. Often combined with acupuncture as 针灸.
