# 总 Total, general, always

- **Character**: 总
- **Pinyin**: zǒng
- **Meaning**: total, general, always
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 总是 (zǒngshì) — always
- 总理 (zǒnglǐ) — premier, prime minister

## Example Sentences

- 国务院总理主持会议。(Guówùyuàn zǒnglǐ zhǔchí huìyì.) — The State Council Premier presided over the meeting.

## Notes

Very common for totals, generals, and always.
