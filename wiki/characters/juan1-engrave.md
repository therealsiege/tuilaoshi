# 镌 Engrave, carve

- **Character**: 镌
- **Pinyin**: juān
- **Meaning**: engrave, carve
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 镌刻 (juān kè) — engrave, carve
- 镌写 (juān xiě) — inscribe

## Example Sentences

- 石碑上镌刻着先烈的名字。(Shí bēi shàng juān kè zhe xiān liè de míng zi.) — The martyrs' names are engraved on the stone tablet.

## Notes

Formal term for engraving, often on stone or metal.
