# 稚 Young

- **Character**: 稚
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: young, childish
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 幼稚 (yòu zhì) — childish, immature
- 稚嫩 (zhì nèn) — young and tender

## Example Sentences

- 他的想法很稚嫩。(Tā de xiǎngfǎ hěn zhìnèn.) — His ideas are very immature.

## Notes

稚 combines grain 禾 with 隹 (short-tailed bird), representing young grain or young creatures.
