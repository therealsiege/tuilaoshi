# 纳 Pay, accept

- **Character**: 纳
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: pay, accept
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纳税 (nà shuì) — pay taxes
- 接纳 (jiē nà) — accept, admit

## Example Sentences

- 所有企业都要依法纳税。(Suǒ yǒu qǐ yè dōu yào yī fǎ nà shuì.) — All enterprises must pay taxes according to law.

## Notes

纳税人 is taxpayer. 纳入 means bring into/incorporate.
