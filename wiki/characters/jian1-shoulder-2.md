# 肩 Shoulder

- **Character**: 肩
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: shoulder
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肩膀 (jiānbǎng) — shoulder
- 肩负 (jiānfù) — shoulder (responsibility)

## Example Sentences

- 他的肩膀很宽。(Tā de jiānbǎng hěn kuān.) — His shoulders are very broad.

## Notes

Often used as 肩膀. Important for describing body position and carrying things.
