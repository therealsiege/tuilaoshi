# 后 After

- **Character**: 后
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: after, behind, later
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 以后 (yǐ hòu) — after, later
- 后面 (hòu miàn) — behind

## Example Sentences

- 以后再说吧。(Yǐ hòu zài shuō ba.) — Let's talk about it later.

## Notes

后 is simplified from 後. It means both spatial 'behind' and temporal 'after.'
