# 豪 Heroic, grand, wealthy

- **Character**: 豪
- **Pinyin**: háo
- **Meaning**: heroic, grand, wealthy
- **Radical**: 豕 (pig)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 豪华 (háohuá) — luxurious, sumptuous
- 豪爽 (háoshuǎng) — forthright, generous

## Example Sentences

- 他性格豪爽。(Tā xìnggé háoshuǎng.) — He is forthright and generous.

## Notes

Common for heroism, luxury, or generous character.
