# 澄 Clear

- **Character**: 澄
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: clear, transparent, to clarify
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 澄清 (chéng qīng) — to clarify
- 澄澈 (chéng chè) — limpid

## Example Sentences

- 他澄清了事实。(Tā chéng qīng le shì shí.) — He clarified the facts.

## Notes

澄 has water 氵 with 登 (to ascend), settling to clarity.
