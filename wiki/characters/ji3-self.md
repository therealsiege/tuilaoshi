# 己 Self

- **Character**: 己
- **Pinyin**: jǐ
- **Meaning**: self, oneself
- **Radical**: 己 (self)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 知己知彼 (zhī jǐ zhī bǐ) — know yourself and your enemy
- 自己 (zì jǐ) — oneself

## Example Sentences

- 知己知彼，百战百胜。(Zhī jǐ zhī bǐ, bǎi zhàn bǎi shèng.) — Know yourself and your enemy, and you'll win every battle.

## Notes

Used in 知己知彼: know yourself; from Sun Tzu's Art of War.
