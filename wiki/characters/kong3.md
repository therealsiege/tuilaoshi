# 恐 To Fear

- **Character**: 恐
- **Pinyin**: kǒng
- **Meaning**: to fear, afraid, terror
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恐怕 (kǒng pà) — I'm afraid that, perhaps
- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear

## Example Sentences

- 恐怕要下雨了。(Kǒng pà yào xià yǔ le.) — I'm afraid it's going to rain.

## Notes

恐 shows工 (work) over heart 心, representing心 oppressed by labor or threat, hence fear.
