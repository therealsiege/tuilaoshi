# 刷 Brush, swipe, print

- **Character**: 刷
- **Pinyin**: shuā
- **Meaning**: brush, swipe, print
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 印刷 (yìn shuā) — print
- 刷新 (shuā xīn) — refresh

## Example Sentences

- 请刷新页面。(Qǐng shuā xīn yè miàn.) — Please refresh the page.

## Notes

Used in 刷新 (refresh), 印刷 (print), 刷卡 (swipe card)
