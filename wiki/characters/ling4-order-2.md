# 令 Order

- **Character**: 令
- **Pinyin**: lìng
- **Meaning**: to order, to command, decree
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 命令 (mìng lìng) — order
- 法令 (fǎ lìng) — decree

## Example Sentences

- 上级下达了新命令。(Shàng jí xià dá le xīn mìng lìng.) — The superiors issued a new order.

## Notes

令 person kneeling under roof, receiving commands.
