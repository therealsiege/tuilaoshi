# 幽 Secluded, quiet, deep

- **Character**: 幽
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: secluded, quiet, deep
- **Radical**: 幺 (tiny)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 幽静 (yōu jìng) — quiet and secluded
- 幽雅 (yōu yǎ) — serene and elegant

## Example Sentences

- 竹林深处环境幽静。(Zhú lín shēn chù huán jìng yōu jìng.) — Deep in the bamboo forest, the environment is quiet and secluded.

## Notes

Describes secluded, quiet, or subtle beauty.
