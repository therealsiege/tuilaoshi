# 门 Door

- **Character**: 门
- **Pinyin**: mén
- **Meaning**: door, gate
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 开门 (kāi mén) — to open the door
- 关门 (guān mén) — to close the door

## Example Sentences

- 请关门。(Qǐng guān mén.) — Please close the door.

## Notes

门 is simplified from 門, a pictograph of a double door. As a radical it appears in 间, 问, 闻, 闭.
