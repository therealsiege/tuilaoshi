# 珀 Amber

- **Character**: 珀
- **Pinyin**: pò
- **Meaning**: amber
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 琥珀 (hǔ pò) — amber
- 蜜珀 (mì pò) — honey amber

## Example Sentences

- 琥珀中保存了古代昆虫。(Hǔ pò zhōng bǎo cún le gǔ dài kūn chóng.) — Ancient insects are preserved in amber.

## Notes

珀 has jade 玉 with 白 (white), fossilized tree resin.
