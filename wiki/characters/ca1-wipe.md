# 擦 To wipe, rub, scrape

- **Character**: 擦
- **Pinyin**: cā
- **Meaning**: to wipe, rub, scrape
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 擦拭 (cā shì) — to wipe clean
- 摩擦 (mó cā) — friction

## Example Sentences

- 请把黑板擦干净。(Qǐng bǎ hēi bǎn cā gān jìng.) — Please wipe the blackboard clean.

## Notes

Common verb for wiping or rubbing. 擦边球 means to push the limits. 擦肩而过 means to brush past.
