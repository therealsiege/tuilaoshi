# 阶 Step

- **Character**: 阶
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: step, stage, rank
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阶段 (jiē duàn) — stage, phase
- 楼梯 (lóu tī) — stairs

## Example Sentences

- 这是重要的阶段。(Zhè shì zhòng yào de jiē duàn.) — This is an important stage.

## Notes

阶 with mound radical for step/stair, extended to阶 stages and ranks.
