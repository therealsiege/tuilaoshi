# 底 Bottom

- **Character**: 底
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: bottom, base, background
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 到底 (dào dǐ) — after all, on earth
- 彻底 (chè dǐ) — thorough

## Example Sentences

- 你到底想要什么？(Nǐ dào dǐ xiǎng yào shén me?) — What on earth do you want?

## Notes

到底 means 'after all/exactly' and emphasizes a question. 底下 = under/below.
