# 堡 Fort

- **Character**: 堡
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: fort, fortress, castle
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 城堡 (chéng bǎo) — castle
- 堡垒 (bǎo lěi) — fortress

## Example Sentences

- 这座城堡有上百年历史。(Zhè zuò chéng bǎo yǒu shàng bǎi nián lì shǐ.) — This castle has hundreds of years of history.

## Notes

堡 has earth 土 with 保 (to protect), fortified earthwork structure.
