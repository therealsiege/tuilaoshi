# 债 Debt, bond, owe

- **Character**: 债
- **Pinyin**: zhài
- **Meaning**: debt, bond, owe
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 债务 (zhài wù) — debt, liability
- 国债 (guó zhài) — government bond

## Example Sentences

- 公司需要偿还债务。(Gōng sī xū yào cháng huán zhài wù.) — The company needs to repay its debts.

## Notes

Fundamental finance term. 债券 means bond. 债主 means creditor. 负债 means to be in debt.
