# 坍 To Collapse

- **Character**: 坍
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to坍fall in
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 坍塌 (tān tā) — to collapse
- 坍方 (tān fāng) — landslide

## Example Sentences

- 大坝坍塌了。(Dà bà tān tā le.) — The dam collapsed.

## Notes

坍 combines earth 土 and 旦 (dawn), representing earth or structure collapsing.
