# 高 Tall/High

- **Character**: 高
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: tall, high
- **Radical**: 高 (tall)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 高兴 (gāoxìng) — happy, glad
- 提高 (tígāo) — to improve, to raise
- 高中 (gāozhōng) — high school
- 高级 (gāojí) — advanced, high-level
- 高速 (gāosù) — high-speed

## Example Sentences

- 他很高。(Tā hěn gāo.) — He is very tall.
- 我很高兴认识你。(Wǒ hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.) — I'm very happy to meet you.
- 那座山很高。(Nà zuò shān hěn gāo.) — That mountain is very high.

## Notes

高 is a pictographic character depicting a tall building or watchtower — the top portion represents the roof and the lower section the structure beneath. It is itself a radical (radical 189). The extremely common compound 高兴 (gāoxìng, happy) literally suggests "high spirits." 高 describes both physical height (for people and objects) and abstract levels (高级 advanced, 高等 higher-level). Its antonym is 矮 (ǎi, short in stature) for people, or 低 (dī, low) for abstract uses.
