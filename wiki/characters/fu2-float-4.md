# 浮 Float, superficial

- **Character**: 浮
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: float, superficial
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浮动 (fú dòng) — fluctuate
- 上浮 (shàng fú) — float up, rise

## Example Sentences

- 汇率浮动很大。(Huì lǜ fú dòng hěn dà.) — Exchange rates fluctuate greatly.

## Notes

浮动工资 is floating wage. 浮躁 means impetuous.
