# 依 To Depend On

- **Character**: 依
- **Pinyin**: yī
- **Meaning**: to depend on, according to
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 依靠 (yī kào) — to rely on
- 依然 (yī rán) — still, as before

## Example Sentences

- 她依然在等待。(Tā yī rán zài děng dài.) — She is still waiting.

## Notes

依 shows person 亻 and 衣 (clothing), representing leaning on something like clothing wraps the body.
