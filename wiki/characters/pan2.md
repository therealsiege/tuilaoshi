# 盘 Plate

- **Character**: 盘
- **Pinyin**: pán
- **Meaning**: plate, dish, tray
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盘子 (pán zi) — plate, dish
- 一盘 (yī pán) — one plate

## Example Sentences

- 盘子里有苹果。(Pán zi lǐ yǒu píng guǒ.) — There are apples on the plate.

## Notes

盘 simplified from 盤, uses dish radical 皿. Also measure word for plated food.
