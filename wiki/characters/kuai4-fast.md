# 快 Fast

- **Character**: 快
- **Pinyin**: kuài
- **Meaning**: fast, quick, soon
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 快乐 (kuài lè) — happy
- 快要 (kuài yào) — about to

## Example Sentences

- 快一点！(Kuài yī diǎn!) — Hurry up!

## Notes

快 means both 'fast' and 'happy' (as in 快乐). 快要 means 'about to.' Opposite of 慢 (slow).
