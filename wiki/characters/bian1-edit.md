# 编 Edit, compile, weave

- **Character**: 编
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: edit, compile, weave
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 编辑 (biān jí) — to edit, editor
- 编写 (biān xiě) — to compile, write

## Example Sentences

- 她在出版社担任编辑工作。(Tā zài chū bǎn shè dān rèn biān jí gōng zuò.) — She works as an editor at the publishing house.

## Notes

Originally 'to weave', extended to organizing and editing text. Common in publishing industry.
