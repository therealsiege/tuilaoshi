# 杂 Mixed

- **Character**: 杂
- **Pinyin**: zá
- **Meaning**: mixed, miscellaneous
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 杂技 (zá jì) — acrobatics
- 复杂 (fù zá) — complex

## Example Sentences

- 杂技表演很精彩。(Zá jì biǎo yǎn hěn jīng cǎi.) — The acrobatic performance is very spectacular.

## Notes

杂 means mixed or miscellaneous; 杂技 are variety acts and acrobatics.
