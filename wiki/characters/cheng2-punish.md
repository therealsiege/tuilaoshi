# 惩 Punish, discipline, warn

- **Character**: 惩
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: punish, discipline, warn
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惩罚 (chéng fá) — to punish
- 惩处 (chéng chǔ) — to punish, penalize

## Example Sentences

- 公司严惩违反规定的员工。(Gōng sī yán chéng wéi fǎn guī dìng de yuán gōng.) — The company severely punishes employees who violate regulations.

## Notes

Formal term for punishment. 惩戒 means to discipline. 严惩 means to punish severely.
