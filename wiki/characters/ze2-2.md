# 则 则, principle, then

- **Character**: 则
- **Pinyin**: zé
- **Meaning**: 则, principle, then
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 规则 (guī zé) — 则, regulation
- 原则 (yuán zé) — principle

## Example Sentences

- 公司有严格的规章制度。(Gōng sī yǒu yán gé de guī zhāng zhì dù.) — The company has strict rules and regulations.

## Notes

法则 means law/principle. 守则 means code of conduct.
