# 瓦 Tile, watt

- **Character**: 瓦
- **Pinyin**: wǎ
- **Meaning**: tile, watt
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 瓦片 (wǎ piàn) — tile, shingle
- 砖瓦 (zhuān wǎ) — bricks and tiles

## Example Sentences

- 屋顶铺着青灰色的瓦片。(Wū dǐng pū zhe qīng huī sè de wǎ piàn.) — The roof is covered with gray-green tiles.

## Notes

Traditional roofing material; also unit for electrical power.
