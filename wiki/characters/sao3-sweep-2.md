# 扫 To Sweep

- **Character**: 扫
- **Pinyin**: sǎo
- **Meaning**: to sweep, to clear away
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扫地 (sǎo dì) — to sweep floor
- 打扫 (dǎ sǎo) — to clean

## Example Sentences

- 她每天都扫地。(Tā měi tiān dōu sǎo dì.) — She sweeps the floor every day.

## Notes

扫 simplified from 掃. Hand 扌 sweeping motion.
