# 慕 To admire, to yearn for

- **Character**: 慕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: to admire, to yearn for
- **Radical**: 心(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 羡慕 (xiàn mù) — to envy, to admire
- 爱慕 (ài mù) — to adore, to admire

## Example Sentences

- 我很羡慕他的才华。(Wǒ hěn xiànmù tā de cáihuá.) — I really admire his talent.

## Notes

Expresses admiration or longing for something.
