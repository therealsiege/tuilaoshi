# 翻 Flip

- **Character**: 翻
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: to flip, turn over
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 翻滚 (fān gǔn) — to roll, tumble
- 翻身 (fān shēn) — to turn over

## Example Sentences

- 体操运动员做了个后空翻。(Tǐcāo yùndòngyuán zuò le gè hòukōngfān.) — The gymnast did a backflip.

## Notes

Flipping and tumbling; key for gymnastics
