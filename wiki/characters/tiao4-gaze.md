# 眺 To Gaze

- **Character**: 眺
- **Pinyin**: tiào
- **Meaning**: to gaze into the distance
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 眺望 (tiào wàng) — to gaze into the distance
- 远眺 (yuǎn tiào) — to gaze far

## Example Sentences

- 他站在山上眺望远方。(Tā zhàn zài shān shàng tiào wàng yuǎn fāng.) — He stood on the mountain gazing into the distance.

## Notes

眺 has eye 目 with 兆 (omen/trillion), looking far ahead.
