# 牵 To lead along, to pull, to involve

- **Character**: 牵
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: to lead along, to pull, to involve
- **Radical**: 牛(cow)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 牵手 (qiān shǒu) — to hold hands
- 牵挂 (qiān guà) — to worry about, to be concerned

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈牵着孩子的手过马路。(Māma qiānzhe háizi de shǒu guò mǎlù.) — The mother held the child's hand crossing the street.

## Notes

Can mean physical leading or emotional connection.
