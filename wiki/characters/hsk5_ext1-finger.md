# 指 Finger, Point, 指导 Guide

- **Character**: 指
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: finger, point, 指导 guide
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 指导 (zhǐ dǎo) — guide, instruct
- 手指 (shǒu zhǐ) — finger
- 指出 (zhǐ chū) — point out

## Example Sentences

- 老师指导学生做实验。(Lǎoshī zhǐdǎo xuéshēng zuò shíyàn.) — The teacher guides students in doing experiments.

## Notes

Versatile character meaning both the noun 'finger' and verbs like 'point' or 'guide'. 指导 (guidance) is essential in educational contexts.
