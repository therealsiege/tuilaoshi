# 潮 Tide, current, damp

- **Character**: 潮
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: tide, current, damp
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潮汐 (cháo xī) — tide
- 涨潮 (zhǎng cháo) — high tide

## Example Sentences

- 潮汐是由月球引力引起的。(Cháo xī shì yóu yuè qiú yǐn lì yǐn qǐ de.) — Tides are caused by the moon's gravitational pull.
