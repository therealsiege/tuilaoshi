# 忐 Timorous

- **Character**: 忐
- **Pinyin**: tǎn
- **Meaning**: timorous, nervous
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忐忑 (tǎn tè) — perturbed, mentally disturbed

## Example Sentences

- 我心里忐忑不安。(Wǒ xīn lǐ tǎn tè bù ān.) — I feel uneasy in my heart.

## Notes

忐 shows up 上 over heart 心, part of the pair忐忑 describing anxious心 going up and down.
