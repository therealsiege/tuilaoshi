# 痴 Foolish, infatuated, crazy about

- **Character**: 痴
- **Pinyin**: chī
- **Meaning**: foolish, infatuated, crazy about
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痴迷 (chī mí) — infatuated, obsessed
- 白痴 (bái chī) — idiot, imbecile

## Example Sentences

- 他痴迷于音乐。(Tā chīmí yú yīnyuè.) — He is obsessed with music.

## Notes

Foolishness or passionate infatuation/obsession.
