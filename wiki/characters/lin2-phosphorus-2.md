# 磷 Phosphorus, shimmer

- **Character**: 磷
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: phosphorus, shimmer
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 磷火 (lín huǒ) — phosphorescent light
- 磷光 (lín guāng) — phosphorescence

## Example Sentences

- 坟地里偶尔会出现磷火。(Fén dì lǐ ǒu ěr huì chū xiàn lín huǒ.) — Phosphorescent lights occasionally appear in graveyards.
