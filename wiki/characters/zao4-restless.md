# 躁 Restless

- **Character**: 躁
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: restless, impetuous
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 急躁 (jízào) — impatient
- 烦躁 (fánzào) — irritable

## Example Sentences

- 他性格很急躁。(Tā xìnggé hěn jízào.) — He has a very impatient personality.

## Notes

Restless emotion. Foot radical suggests inability to stay still. 急躁 and 烦躁 describe temperament.
