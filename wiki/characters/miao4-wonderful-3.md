# 妙 Wonderful

- **Character**: 妙
- **Pinyin**: miào
- **Meaning**: wonderful, subtle
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 奥妙 (ào miào) — subtlety
- 玄妙 (xuán miào) — mysterious

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗意境深妙。(Zhè shǒu shī yì jìng shēn miào.) — This poem has profound and subtle imagery.

## Notes

妙 has woman 女 with 少 (few), young and delicate, hence subtle.
