# 塑 Plastic

- **Character**: 塑
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: to mold, plastic
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 塑料 (sù liào) — plastic
- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture

## Example Sentences

- 塑料袋对环境有害。(Sù liào dài duì huán jìng yǒu hài.) — Plastic bags are harmful to the environment.

## Notes

塑 means to mold or plastic. Used in塑料 (plastic material), 雕塑 (sculpture).
