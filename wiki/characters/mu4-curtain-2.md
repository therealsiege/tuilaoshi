# 幕 Curtain

- **Character**: 幕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: curtain, screen, act
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen
- 开幕 (kāi mù) — opening ceremony

## Example Sentences

- 开幕式很精彩。(Kāi mù shì hěn jīng cǎi.) — The opening ceremony was wonderful.

## Notes

幕 originally meant curtain, now part of 屏幕 (screen) and theater/ceremony terminology.
