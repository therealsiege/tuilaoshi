# 罗 Gauze

- **Character**: 罗
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: gauze, net
- **Radical**: 罒(net)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 纱罗 (shā luó) — gauze fabric
- 罗纹 (luó wén) — ribbed pattern

## Example Sentences

- 这种罗织品很透气。(Zhè zhǒng luó zhīpǐn hěn tòuqì.) — This gauze fabric is very breathable.

## Notes

罗 refers to a light, open-weave fabric. The net radical 罒 suggests its mesh-like structure.
