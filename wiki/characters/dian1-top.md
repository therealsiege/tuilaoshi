# 颠 Top

- **Character**: 颠
- **Pinyin**: diān
- **Meaning**: top, to overturn, to jolt
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 颠倒 (diān dǎo) — to reverse, to invert
- 颠簸 (diān bǒ) — to bump, to jolt

## Example Sentences

- 路很颠簸。(Lù hěn diān bǒ.) — The road is very bumpy.

## Notes

颠 simplified from 顛. Page/head radical 页, representing the top or jolting motion.
