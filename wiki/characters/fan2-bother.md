# 烦 To bother, annoyed, 煩 (traditional)

- **Character**: 烦
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: to bother, annoyed, 煩 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烦恼 (fán nǎo) — worried, vexed
- 麻烦 (má fan) — trouble, troublesome

## Example Sentences

- 别烦我，我在工作。(Bié fán wǒ, wǒ zài gōngzuò.) — Don't bother me, I'm working.

## Notes

Common character for annoyance, bother, and vexation.
