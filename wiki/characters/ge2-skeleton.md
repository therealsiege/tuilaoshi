# 骼 Skeleton, framework

- **Character**: 骼
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: skeleton, framework
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 骨骼 (gǔ gé) — skeleton, skeletal
- 体骼 (tǐ gé) — physique

## Example Sentences

- 人体骨骼有206块骨头。(Réntǐ gǔgé yǒu èr bǎi líng liù kuài gǔtou.) — The human skeleton has 206 bones.

## Notes

Used in compound 骨骼 for the skeletal system.
