# 兑 Exchange, convert, cash

- **Character**: 兑
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: exchange, convert, cash
- **Radical**: 儿 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 兑换 (duìhuàn) — exchange, convert
- 兑现 (duìxiàn) — cash, honor (a promise)

## Example Sentences

- 我需要兑换一些美元。(Wǒ xūyào duìhuàn yīxiē měiyuán.) — I need to exchange some US dollars.

## Notes

Common in banking for currency exchange and cashing checks.
