# 浪 Wave

- **Character**: 浪
- **Pinyin**: làng
- **Meaning**: wave
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 冲浪 (chōng làng) — to surf
- 波浪 (bō làng) — waves

## Example Sentences

- 他喜欢在海上冲浪。(Tā xǐhuan zài hǎi shàng chōnglàng.) — He likes surfing on the ocean.
