# 耀 To Shine

- **Character**: 耀
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: to shine, to dazzle, glorious
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 耀眼 (yào yǎn) — dazzling
- 闪耀 (shǎn yào) — to shine

## Example Sentences

- 钻石闪耀着光芒。(Zuàn shí shǎn yào zhe guāng máng.) — The diamond shines brilliantly.

## Notes

耀 has 光 (light) components, brilliant radiant light.
