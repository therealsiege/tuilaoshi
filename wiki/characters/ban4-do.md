# 办 To Handle

- **Character**: 办
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to do, to manage, to handle
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 办公 (bàn gōng) — to work (office)
- 办法 (bàn fǎ) — method, way

## Example Sentences

- 没办法。(Méi bàn fǎ.) — There's no way. / Can't be helped.

## Notes

办 means to handle or manage. 办公室 = office, 举办 = to host.
