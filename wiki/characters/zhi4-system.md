# 制 System

- **Character**: 制
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: system, to制造, to control
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 制度 (zhì dù) — system, institution
- 控制 (kòng zhì) — to control

## Example Sentences

- 我们要建立新制度。(Wǒ men yào jiàn lì xīn zhì dù.) — We need to establish a new system.

## Notes

制 represents制造, controlling, and establishing systems.
