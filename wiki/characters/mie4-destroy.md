# 灭 To Extinguish

- **Character**: 灭
- **Pinyin**: miè
- **Meaning**: to destroy, to extinguish, to go out
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 熄灭 (xī miè) — to extinguish
- 灭火 (miè huǒ) — to fight a fire

## Example Sentences

- 消防员在灭火。(Xiāo fáng yuán zài miè huǒ.) — The firefighters are putting out the fire.

## Notes

灭 means to extinguish or destroy. Used for extinguishing fires, destroying things, extinction (灭绝).
