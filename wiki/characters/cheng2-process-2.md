# 程 Process, procedure

- **Character**: 程
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: process, procedure
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 程序 (chéng xù) — program, procedure
- 流程 (liú chéng) — process, workflow

## Example Sentences

- 我正在学习编程序。(Wǒ zhèng zài xué xí biān chéng xù.) — I am learning to program.

## Notes

Key character for 程序 (program), 编程 (programming), 进程 (process)
