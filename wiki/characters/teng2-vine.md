# 藤 Vine

- **Character**: 藤
- **Pinyin**: téng
- **Meaning**: vine, rattan
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 藤蔓 (téng màn) — vine
- 紫藤 (zǐ téng) — wisteria

## Example Sentences

- 墙上爬满了藤蔓。(Qiáng shàng pá mǎn le téng màn.) — The wall is covered with vines.

## Notes

藤 shows grass 艹 with騰 (to gallop), representing climbing vines.
