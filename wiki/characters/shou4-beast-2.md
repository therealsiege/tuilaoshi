# 兽 Beast

- **Character**: 兽
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: beast, animal
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 野兽 (yě shòu) — wild beast
- 兽医 (shòu yī) — veterinarian

## Example Sentences

- 森林里有很多野兽。(Sēn lín lǐ yǒu hěn duō yě shòu.) — There are many wild beasts in the forest.

## Notes

兽 simplified from 獸. Represents four-legged animals.
