# 筷 Chopstick

- **Character**: 筷
- **Pinyin**: kuài
- **Meaning**: chopstick
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 筷子 (kuài zi) — chopsticks
- 竹筷 (zhú kuài) — bamboo chopsticks

## Example Sentences

- 你会用筷子吗？(Nǐ huì yòng kuài zi ma?) — Can you use chopsticks?

## Notes

筷 with bamboo radical 竹. Chopsticks traditionally made from bamboo. Always used in pairs.
