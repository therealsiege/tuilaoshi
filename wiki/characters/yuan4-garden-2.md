# 苑 Garden

- **Character**: 苑
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: garden, park, academy
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 花苑 (huā yuàn) — flower garden
- 学苑 (xué yuàn) — academy

## Example Sentences

- 皇家园苑很大。(Huáng jiā yuán yuàn hěn dà.) — The royal garden is very large.

## Notes

苑 with grass radical for enclosed garden, formal parks and academies.
