# 塞 Border

- **Character**: 塞
- **Pinyin**: sài
- **Meaning**: border, frontier, to block
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 塞翁失马 (sài wēng shī mǎ) — a blessing in disguise
- 边塞 (biān sài) — border region

## Example Sentences

- 塞翁失马，焉知非福。(Sài wēng shī mǎ, yān zhī fēi fú.) — The old man lost his horse — who knows if it's not a blessing?

## Notes

Used in 塞翁失马: the border in northern China; also read as sāi (to stuff) or sè (to block).
