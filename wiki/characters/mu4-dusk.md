# 暮 Dusk

- **Character**: 暮
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: dusk, evening, twilight
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 暮色 (mù sè) — twilight
- 朝朝暮暮 (zhāo zhāo mù mù) — day and night

## Example Sentences

- 暮色渐渐降临。(Mù sè jiàn jiàn jiàng lín.) — Twilight gradually descends.

## Notes

暮 has sun 日 setting among 莫 (not/dusk), evening time.
