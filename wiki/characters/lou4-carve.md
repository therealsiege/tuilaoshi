# 镂 Carve, engrave

- **Character**: 镂
- **Pinyin**: lòu
- **Meaning**: carve, engrave
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 镂空 (lòu kōng) — openwork, fretwork
- 雕镂 (diāo lòu) — carve, engrave

## Example Sentences

- 这扇门上有精美的镂空图案。(Zhè shàn mén shàng yǒu jīng měi de lòu kōng tú àn.) — This door has exquisite openwork patterns.

## Notes

Specialized term for carved openwork designs.
