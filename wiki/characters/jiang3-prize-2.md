# 奖 Prize, reward, encourage

- **Character**: 奖
- **Pinyin**: jiǎng
- **Meaning**: prize, reward, encourage
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 奖励 (jiǎnglì) — reward, award
- 奖金 (jiǎngjīn) — bonus, prize money

## Example Sentences

- 他获得了年终奖金。(Tā huòdé le niánzhōng jiǎngjīn.) — He received a year-end bonus.

## Notes

Common term for rewards, prizes, and bonuses.
