# 扎 To tie, to bind, to prick, to pierce

- **Character**: 扎
- **Pinyin**: zā
- **Meaning**: to tie, to bind, to prick, to pierce
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 扎实 (zhā shi) — solid, sturdy, substantial
- 扎根 (zhā gēn) — to take root

## Example Sentences

- 她把头发扎成马尾。(Tā bǎ tóufa zā chéng mǎwěi.) — She tied her hair into a ponytail.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations with different meanings; zā for binding.
