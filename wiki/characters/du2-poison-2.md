# 毒 Poison

- **Character**: 毒
- **Pinyin**: dú
- **Meaning**: poison, toxic
- **Radical**: 毋 (do not)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 消毒 (xiāo dú) — to disinfect
- 中毒 (zhòng dú) — poisoning
- 病毒 (bìng dú) — virus

## Example Sentences

- 酒精可以消毒伤口。(Jiǔ jīng kě yǐ xiāo dú shāng kǒu.) — Alcohol can disinfect wounds.

## Notes

Toxic substances; infection or poisoning.
