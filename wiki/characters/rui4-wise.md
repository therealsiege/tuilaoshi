# 睿 Wise, sagacious

- **Character**: 睿
- **Pinyin**: ruì
- **Meaning**: wise, sagacious
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 睿智 (ruìzhì) — wise, sagacious
- 睿见 (ruìjiàn) — wise judgment

## Example Sentences

- 他是睿智的领导。(Tā shì ruìzhì de lǐngdǎo.) — He is a wise leader.

## Notes

Formal literary term for wisdom or sagacity.
