# 平 Flat

- **Character**: 平
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: flat, level, even
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 平衡 (píng héng) — balance
- 水平 (shuǐ píng) — level, standard

## Example Sentences

- 体操运动员在平衡木上表演。(Tǐcāo yùndòngyuán zài pínghéngmù shàng biǎoyǎn.) — Gymnasts perform on the balance beam.

## Notes

Balance and levelness; equilibrium
