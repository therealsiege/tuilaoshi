# 项 Item

- **Character**: 项
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: item, clause, neck, back of neck
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 项目 (xiàng mù) — project, item
- 事项 (shì xiàng) — matter, item

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个重要项目。(Zhè shì yī ge zhòng yào xiàng mù.) — This is an important project.

## Notes

项 is simplified from 項. Originally meant neck, extended to items and categories.
