# 迷 Confused

- **Character**: 迷
- **Pinyin**: mí
- **Meaning**: to be confused, to be fascinated
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 迷路 (mí lù) — to get lost
- 着迷 (zháo mí) — fascinated

## Example Sentences

- 我在森林里迷路了。(Wǒ zài sēn lín lǐ mí lù le.) — I got lost in the forest.

## Notes

迷 walk 辶 plus 米 (rice grains), wandering among countless grains.
