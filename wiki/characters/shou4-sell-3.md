# 售 Sell

- **Character**: 售
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: sell
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 出售 (chū shòu) — sell, offer for sale
- 销售 (xiāo shòu) — sell, market

## Example Sentences

- 这款产品销售很好。(Zhè kuǎn chǎn pǐn xiāo shòu hěn hǎo.) — This product sells very well.

## Notes

售货员 is sales clerk. 售后服务 is after-sales service.
