# 标 Mark

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, javelin
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 标枪 (biāo qiāng) — javelin
- 目标 (mù biāo) — goal, target

## Example Sentences

- 标枪是古老的田径项目。(Biāoqiāng shì gǔlǎo de tiánjìng xiàngmù.) — Javelin is an ancient track and field event.

## Notes

Javelin; also means mark or standard
