# 密 Dense, Secret, 秘密 Secret

- **Character**: 密
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: dense, secret, 秘密 secret
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 秘密 (mì mì) — secret
- 密切 (mì qiè) — close, intimate
- 密度 (mì dù) — density

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个秘密。(Zhè shì yī gè mìmì.) — This is a secret.

## Notes

Dual meaning: dense/close (密切 close relationship, 密度 density) and secret (秘密). Common in both physical and abstract contexts.
