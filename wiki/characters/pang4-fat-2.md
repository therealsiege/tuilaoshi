# 胖 Fat

- **Character**: 胖
- **Pinyin**: pàng
- **Meaning**: fat, plump
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胖子 (pàng zi) — fat person
- 发胖 (fā pàng) — to gain weight

## Example Sentences

- 我最近胖了。(Wǒ zuì jìn pàng le.) — I've gotten fatter recently.

## Notes

胖 uses the meat radical 月. Opposite of 瘦 (thin). In Chinese culture, calling a baby 胖 is a compliment.
