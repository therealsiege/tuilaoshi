# 岬 Cape, promontory

- **Character**: 岬
- **Pinyin**: jiǎ
- **Meaning**: cape, promontory
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 海岬 (hǎi jiǎ) — cape, headland
- 岬角 (jiǎ jiǎo) — promontory

## Example Sentences

- 灯塔建在海岬上。(Dēng tǎ jiàn zài hǎi jiǎ shàng.) — The lighthouse is built on the cape.

## Notes

Geographic term for land projecting into water.
