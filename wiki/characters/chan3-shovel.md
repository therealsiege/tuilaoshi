# 铲 Shovel, spade, eradicate

- **Character**: 铲
- **Pinyin**: chǎn
- **Meaning**: shovel, spade, eradicate
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铲子 (chǎn zi) — shovel
- 铲除 (chǎn chú) — eradicate, root out

## Example Sentences

- 要铲除一切邪恶势力。(Yào chǎn chú yī qiè xié è shì lì.) — We must eradicate all evil forces.

## Notes

Tool for scooping or metaphor for elimination
