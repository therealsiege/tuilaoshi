# 折 Break

- **Character**: 折
- **Pinyin**: zhé
- **Meaning**: to break, to fold, setback
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 百折不挠 (bǎi zhé bù náo) — indomitable
- 折扣 (zhé kòu) — discount

## Example Sentences

- 他百折不挠地追求梦想。(Tā bǎi zhé bù náo de zhuīqiú mèngxiǎng.) — He pursued his dream indomitably.

## Notes

In 百折不挠: setbacks or adversities; also means to fold or discount.
