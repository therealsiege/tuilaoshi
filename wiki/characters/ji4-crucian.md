# 鲫 Crucian carp

- **Character**: 鲫
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: crucian carp
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鲫鱼 (jì yú) — crucian carp
- 鲫鱼汤 (jì yú tāng) — crucian carp soup

## Example Sentences

- 鲫鱼汤很有营养。(Jìyú tāng hěn yǒu yíngyǎng.) — Crucian carp soup is very nutritious.

## Notes

Popular freshwater fish in Chinese cuisine. Fish radical.
