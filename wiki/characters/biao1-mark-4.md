# 标 Mark

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, standard
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 标准 (biāo zhǔn) — standard
- 目标 (mù biāo) — goal, target

## Example Sentences

- 这是国际标准。(Zhè shì guó jì biāo zhǔn.) — This is the international standard.

## Notes

标 originally meant tree top as a marker, now for all kinds of marks and standards.
