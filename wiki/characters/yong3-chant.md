# 咏 To chant, to recite, to sing

- **Character**: 咏
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: to chant, to recite, to sing
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 歌咏 (gē yǒng) — to sing, to chant
- 咏叹 (yǒng tàn) — aria, to chant admiringly

## Example Sentences

- 诗人咏叹自然之美。(Shīrén yǒngtàn zìrán zhī měi.) — The poet sings of the beauty of nature.

## Notes

Literary term for singing or chanting, especially poetry.
