# 原 Original, primary, source

- **Character**: 原
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: original, primary, source
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 原子 (yuán zǐ) — atom
- 原理 (yuán lǐ) — principle, theory

## Example Sentences

- 我们要了解事物的原理。(Wǒ men yào liǎo jiě shì wù de yuán lǐ.) — We need to understand the principles of things.
