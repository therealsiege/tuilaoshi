# 皮 Skin, leather, peel

- **Character**: 皮
- **Pinyin**: pí
- **Meaning**: skin, leather, peel
- **Radical**: 皮 (skin)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 树皮 (shù pí) — tree bark
- 皮肤 (pí fū) — skin

## Example Sentences

- 树皮保护树木不受损伤。(Shù pí bǎo hù shù mù bù shòu sǔn shāng.) — Tree bark protects trees from damage.

## Notes

Outer covering of plants and animals
