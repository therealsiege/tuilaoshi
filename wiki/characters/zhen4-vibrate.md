# 振 Vibrate, oscillate

- **Character**: 振
- **Pinyin**: zhèn
- **Meaning**: vibrate, oscillate
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 振动 (zhèn dòng) — vibration
- 振荡 (zhèn dàng) — oscillate

## Example Sentences

- 晶体振荡器很精确。(Jīng tǐ zhèn dàng qì hěn jīng què.) — Crystal oscillators are very precise.

## Notes

Used in 振动 (vibration), 振荡器 (oscillator), electronics
