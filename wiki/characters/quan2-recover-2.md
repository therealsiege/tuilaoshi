# 痊 To recover, heal

- **Character**: 痊
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: to recover, heal
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 痊愈 (quán yù) — to recover fully
- 痊可 (quán kě) — to recover

## Example Sentences

- 病人很快就痊愈了。(Bìngrén hěn kuài jiù quányù le.) — The patient recovered quickly.

## Notes

Complete recovery from illness, used in compound 痊愈.
