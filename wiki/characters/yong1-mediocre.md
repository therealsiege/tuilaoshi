# 庸 Mediocre, ordinary, to employ

- **Character**: 庸
- **Pinyin**: yōng
- **Meaning**: mediocre, ordinary, to employ
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 平庸 (píng yōng) — mediocre, ordinary
- 庸俗 (yōng sú) — vulgar, philistine

## Example Sentences

- 他不甘心过平庸的生活。(Tā bù gān xīn guò píng yōng de shēng huó.) — He is unwilling to live a mediocre life.

## Notes

Usually describes mediocrity or ordinariness. 庸人自扰 means to worry unnecessarily.
