# 评 To Comment

- **Character**: 评
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: to comment, to评价, to judge
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 评价 (píng jià) — to evaluate
- 批评 (pī píng) — to criticize

## Example Sentences

- 老师评价了我的作业。(Lǎo shī píng jià le wǒ de zuò yè.) — The teacher evaluated my homework.

## Notes

评 is simplified from 評. The speech radical indicates verbal assessment.
