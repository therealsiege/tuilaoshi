# 融 Melt, blend, fuse

- **Character**: 融
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: melt, blend, fuse
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 融化 (róng huà) — melt, thaw
- 融合 (róng hé) — fuse, merge

## Example Sentences

- 冰雪在阳光下融化。(Bīng xuě zài yáng guāng xià róng huà.) — Ice and snow melt in the sunlight.

## Notes

Solid to liquid transition
