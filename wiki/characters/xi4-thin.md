# 细 Thin

- **Character**: 细
- **Pinyin**: xì
- **Meaning**: thin, fine, detailed
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 仔细 (zǐ xì) — careful, attentive
- 细节 (xì jié) — detail

## Example Sentences

- 请仔细听。(Qǐng zǐ xì tīng.) — Please listen carefully.

## Notes

细 with silk radical for fine thread. Opposite of 粗. 仔细 = careful (fine attention).
