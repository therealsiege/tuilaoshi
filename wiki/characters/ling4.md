# 另 Another

- **Character**: 另
- **Pinyin**: lìng
- **Meaning**: other, another, separate
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 另外 (lìng wài) — in addition, another
- 另一个 (lìng yī gè) — another one

## Example Sentences

- 另外还有什么问题？(Lìng wài hái yǒu shén me wèn tí?) — Are there any other questions?

## Notes

另 means separate/different. 另外 means 'in addition' or 'another.' 另一方面 = on the other hand.
