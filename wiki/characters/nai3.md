# 奶 Grandmother

- **Character**: 奶
- **Pinyin**: nǎi
- **Meaning**: grandmother, milk, breast
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 奶奶 (nǎi nai) — grandmother (paternal)
- 牛奶 (niú nǎi) — milk

## Example Sentences

- 奶奶很爱我。(Nǎi nai hěn ài wǒ.) — Grandma loves me very much.

## Notes

奶 means grandmother (paternal) and milk. 奶奶 = grandma, 牛奶 = cow's milk.
