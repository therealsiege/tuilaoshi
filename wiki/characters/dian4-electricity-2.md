# 电 Electricity, electric

- **Character**: 电
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: electricity, electric
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 电脑 (diàn nǎo) — computer
- 电力 (diàn lì) — electric power

## Example Sentences

- 电源已连接。(Diàn yuán yǐ lián jiē.) — Power is connected.

## Notes

Fundamental for 电脑 (computer), 电力 (power), 电子 (electronic)
