# 刻 Quarter Hour

- **Character**: 刻
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: quarter (hour), to carve, moment
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 立刻 (lì kè) — immediately
- 刻苦 (kè kǔ) — hardworking

## Example Sentences

- 现在三点一刻。(Xiàn zài sān diǎn yī kè.) — It's 3:15 now.

## Notes

一刻 = quarter hour (15 minutes). 立刻 = immediately. 深刻 = profound.
