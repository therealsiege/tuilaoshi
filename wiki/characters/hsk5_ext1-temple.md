# 庙 Temple, 寺庙 Temple

- **Character**: 庙
- **Pinyin**: miào
- **Meaning**: temple, 寺庙 temple
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 寺庙 (sì miào) — temple
- 庙宇 (miào yǔ) — temple
- 太庙 (tài miào) — imperial ancestral temple

## Example Sentences

- 这座寺庙有一千多年的历史。(Zhè zuò sìmiào yǒu yī qiān duō nián de lìshǐ.) — This temple has over a thousand years of history.

## Notes

Common term for religious temples and shrines. 寺庙 is general term while specific types include 佛庙 (Buddhist) and 道观 (Taoist).
