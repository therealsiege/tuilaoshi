# 刷 Brush, paint

- **Character**: 刷
- **Pinyin**: shuā
- **Meaning**: brush, paint
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刷漆 (shuā qī) — paint, brush paint
- 粉刷 (fěn shuā) — whitewash, paint

## Example Sentences

- 墙壁需要重新粉刷。(Qiáng bì xū yào chóng xīn fěn shuā.) — The walls need to be repainted.

## Notes

刷新 means refresh/update. Also used for credit card swiping.
