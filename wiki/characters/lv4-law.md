# 律 Law

- **Character**: 律
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: law, rhythm, pitch
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 法律 (fǎ lǜ) — law
- 节律 (jié lǜ) — rhythm

## Example Sentences

- 音乐的节律很美。(Yīn yuè de jié lǜ hěn měi.) — The rhythm of the music is beautiful.

## Notes

律 has step 彳 with 聿 (brush), regulated pattern, law or musical pitch.
