# 望 Inspect

- **Character**: 望
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: look, hope
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 望诊 (wàng zhěn) — inspection (TCM diagnosis)
- 希望 (xī wàng) — hope
- 观望 (guān wàng) — to wait and see

## Example Sentences

- 中医诊断包括望闻问切。(Zhōng yī zhěn duàn bāo kuò wàng wén wèn qiē.) — TCM diagnosis includes inspection, listening, inquiry, and palpation.

## Notes

Visual inspection; first TCM diagnostic method.
