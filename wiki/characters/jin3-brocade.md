# 锦 Brocade, bright and beautiful

- **Character**: 锦
- **Pinyin**: jǐn
- **Meaning**: brocade, bright and beautiful
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锦缎 (jǐn duàn) — brocade
- 锦绣 (jǐn xiù) — beautiful, splendid

## Example Sentences

- 大好河山如锦绣般美丽。(Dà hǎo hé shān rú jǐn xiù bān měi lì.) — The great rivers and mountains are beautiful as brocade.

## Notes

Luxury silk fabric; also means splendid.
