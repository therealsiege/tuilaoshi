# 增 To Increase

- **Character**: 增
- **Pinyin**: zēng
- **Meaning**: to increase, to add
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 增加 (zēng jiā) — to increase
- 增长 (zēng zhǎng) — to grow, to increase

## Example Sentences

- 人口增加了。(Rén kǒu zēng jiā le.) — The population has increased.

## Notes

增 is the formal word for increase, opposite of 减 (decrease). Often used in formal or written contexts.
