# 焊 Weld

- **Character**: 焊
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: weld, solder
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 焊接 (hàn jiē) — weld, welding
- 焊工 (hàn gōng) — welder

## Example Sentences

- 工人正在焊接钢管。(Gōngrén zhèngzài hànjiē gāngguǎn.) — Workers are welding steel pipes.

## Notes

焊 refers to welding or soldering metals together using heat. Essential skill in metal fabrication.
