# 断 To break, decide, judge, 斷 (traditional)

- **Character**: 断
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: to break, decide, judge, 斷 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 诊断 (zhěn duàn) — to diagnose
- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge, determine

## Example Sentences

- 医生很快就做出了诊断。(Yīshēng hěn kuài jiù zuò chūle zhěnduàn.) — The doctor quickly made a diagnosis.

## Notes

To break/sever or to make judgments and decisions.
