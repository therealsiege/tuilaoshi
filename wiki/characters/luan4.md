# 乱 Chaotic

- **Character**: 乱
- **Pinyin**: luàn
- **Meaning**: chaotic, messy, disorder
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 混乱 (hùn luàn) — chaos, confusion
- 乱七八糟 (luàn qī bā zāo) — in a mess

## Example Sentences

- 房间乱七八糟的。(Fáng jiān luàn qī bā zāo de.) — The room is in a complete mess.

## Notes

乱 simplified from 亂. Depicts tangled threads, representing disorder and confusion.
