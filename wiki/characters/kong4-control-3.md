# 控 Control

- **Character**: 控
- **Pinyin**: kòng
- **Meaning**: to control
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 控球 (kòng qiú) — ball control
- 控制 (kòng zhì) — to control

## Example Sentences

- 控球后卫负责组织进攻。(Kòngqiú hòuwèi fùzé zǔzhī jìngōng.) — The point guard is responsible for organizing the offense.

Ball control and possession management
