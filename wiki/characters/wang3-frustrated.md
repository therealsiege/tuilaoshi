# 惘 Perplexed

- **Character**: 惘
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: frustrated, perplexed
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惘然 (wǎng rán) — frustrated, lost
- 迷惘 (mí wǎng) — perplexed

## Example Sentences

- 他一脸迷惘的表情。(Tā yì liǎn mí wǎng de biǎo qíng.) — He has a perplexed expression.

## Notes

惘 has heart 忄 with 罔 (none), heart feeling lost or confused.
