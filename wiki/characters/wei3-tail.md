# 尾 Tail

- **Character**: 尾
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: tail, end
- **Radical**: 尸(corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 尾巴 (wěi ba) — tail
- 尾宿 (wěi xiù) — Tail constellation

## Example Sentences

- 尾宿代表青龙的尾巴。(Wěi xiù dàibiǎo qīnglóng de wěiba.) — Tail constellation represents the Azure Dragon's tail.

## Notes

尾 means tail or end. In astronomy, the Tail constellation, representing the dragon's tail in the 28 mansions.
