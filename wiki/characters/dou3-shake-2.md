# 抖 To Shake

- **Character**: 抖
- **Pinyin**: dǒu
- **Meaning**: to shake, to tremble
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to tremble
- 抖动 (dǒu dòng) — to shake

## Example Sentences

- 他抖了抖衣服上的灰尘。(Tā dǒu le dǒu yī fú shàng de huī chén.) — He shook the dust off his clothes.

## Notes

抖 combines hand 扌 and 斗 (dipper), representing shaking or trembling motion.
