# 常 Often

- **Character**: 常
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: often, frequent, ordinary
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 经常 (jīng cháng) — often
- 平常 (píng cháng) — ordinary

## Example Sentences

- 我经常去那家餐厅。(Wǒ jīng cháng qù nà jiā cān tīng.) — I often go to that restaurant.

## Notes

常 means both 'often/frequent' and 'ordinary/common.' 非常 (fēi cháng) means 'extremely' — literally 'not ordinary.'
