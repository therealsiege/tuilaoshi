# 政 Politics

- **Character**: 政
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: politics, government, political
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 政府 (zhèng fǔ) — government
- 政治 (zhèng zhì) — politics

## Example Sentences

- 政府发布了新政策。(Zhèng fǔ fā bù le xīn zhèng cè.) — The government issued a new policy.

## Notes

政 combines 正 (correct) and 攵 (action), suggesting corrective governance.
