# 歉 To apologize, deficient

- **Character**: 歉
- **Pinyin**: qiàn
- **Meaning**: to apologize, deficient
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 抱歉 (bào qiàn) — sorry, to apologize
- 歉意 (qiàn yì) — apology, regret

## Example Sentences

- 对不起，我很抱歉。(Duìbuqǐ, wǒ hěn bàoqiàn.) — I'm sorry, I apologize.

## Notes

Apology or feeling of insufficiency/deficiency.
