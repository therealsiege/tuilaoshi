# 沐 To wash, bathe, receive

- **Character**: 沐
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: to wash, bathe, receive
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沐浴 (mù yù) — to bathe
- 如沐春风 (rú mù chūn fēng) — like being bathed in spring breeze

## Example Sentences

- 阳光沐浴着大地。(Yángguāng mùyùzhe dàdì.) — Sunlight bathes the earth.

## Notes

To wash/bathe or receive (grace, teaching, etc.).
