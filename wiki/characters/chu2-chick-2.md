# 雏 Chick

- **Character**: 雏
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: chick, fledgling
- **Radical**: 隹 (short-tailed bird)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 雏鸟 (chú niǎo) — chick, fledgling
- 雏形 (chú xíng) — embryonic form

## Example Sentences

- 小雏鸟刚学会飞。(Xiǎo chú niǎo gāng xué huì fēi.) — The little chick just learned to fly.

## Notes

雏 simplified from 雛. Bird 隹 with 刍 (grass), young bird.
