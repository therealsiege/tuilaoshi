# 击 Strike

- **Character**: 击
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to hit, strike, attack
- **Radical**: 凵 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 击球 (jī qiú) — to hit a ball
- 拳击 (quán jī) — boxing

## Example Sentences

- 他击中了目标。(Tā jī zhòng le mùbiāo.) — He hit the target.

## Notes

Hitting, striking in sports and combat; used broadly across sports
