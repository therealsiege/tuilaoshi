# 段 Section

- **Character**: 段
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: section, segment, paragraph, period
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 一段 (yī duàn) — a section, a period
- 手段 (shǒu duàn) — method, means

## Example Sentences

- 我在中国住了一段时间。(Wǒ zài zhōng guó zhù le yī duàn shí jiān.) — I lived in China for a period of time.

## Notes

段 indicates divisions or sections of time, space, or text. Essential for discussing portions of things.
