# 促 促进, urge, urgent

- **Character**: 促
- **Pinyin**: cù
- **Meaning**: 促进, urge, urgent
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 促进 (cù jìn) — 促进, boost
- 促销 (cù xiāo) — promote sales

## Example Sentences

- 商店正在搞促销活动。(Shāng diàn zhèng zài gǎo cù xiāo huó dòng.) — The store is having a sales promotion.

## Notes

促成 means facilitate. 敦促 means urge.
