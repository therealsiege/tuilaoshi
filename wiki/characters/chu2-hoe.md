# 锄 Hoe, weed

- **Character**: 锄
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: hoe, weed
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锄头 (chú tou) — hoe
- 锄草 (chú cǎo) — weed, hoe

## Example Sentences

- 锄禾日当午，汗滴禾下土。(Chú hé rì dāng wǔ, hàn dī hé xià tǔ.) — Hoeing grain at noon, sweat drips to the soil below.

## Notes

Famous line from Tang poetry about farming
