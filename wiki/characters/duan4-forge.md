# 锻 To Forge

- **Character**: 锻
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: to forge, to temper
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — to exercise, to temper
- 锻造 (duàn zào) — to forge

## Example Sentences

- 每天锻炼身体很重要。(Měi tiān duàn liàn shēn tǐ hěn zhòng yào.) — Daily physical exercise is very important.

## Notes

锻 refers to forging metal by hammering. Extended metaphorically to mean tempering or exercising.
