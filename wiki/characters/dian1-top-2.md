# 颠 Top

- **Character**: 颠
- **Pinyin**: diān
- **Meaning**: top, summit, to颠overturn
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 颠倒 (diān dǎo) — to reverse
- 颠覆 (diān fù) — to subvert

## Example Sentences

- 他把顺序颠倒了。(Tā bǎ shùn xù diān dǎo le.) — He reversed the order.

## Notes

颠 simplified from 顛. Head radical 页 representing the top or overturning.
