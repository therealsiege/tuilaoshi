# 欠 To owe, to lack, to yawn

- **Character**: 欠
- **Pinyin**: qiàn
- **Meaning**: to owe, to lack, to yawn
- **Radical**: 欠(owe)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 欠债 (qiàn zhài) — to owe a debt
- 欠缺 (qiàn quē) — to lack, deficiency

## Example Sentences

- 他欠我一百块钱。(Tā qiàn wǒ yībǎi kuài qián.) — He owes me one hundred yuan.

## Notes

Primary meaning is to owe money or be in debt.
