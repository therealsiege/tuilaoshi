# 穴 Cave, hole, acupoint

- **Character**: 穴
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: cave, hole, acupoint
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 穴位 (xué wèi) — acupoint
- 洞穴 (dòng xué) — cave, cavern

## Example Sentences

- 针灸需要准确找到穴位。(Zhēn jiǔ xū yào zhǔn què zhǎo dào xué wèi.) — Acupuncture requires accurately locating acupoints.

## Notes

Radical itself, means hole or cave. Important in traditional Chinese medicine for acupoints.
