# 锁 Lock

- **Character**: 锁
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: lock, to lock
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锁门 (suǒ mén) — to lock the door
- 钥匙 (yào shi) — key

## Example Sentences

- 别忘了锁门。(Bié wàng le suǒ mén.) — Don't forget to lock the door.

## Notes

锁 uses metal radical 钅, showing its nature as a metal device for securing things.
