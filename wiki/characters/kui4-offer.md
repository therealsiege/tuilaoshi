# 馈 Offer food, present

- **Character**: 馈
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: offer food, present
- **Radical**: 食 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 馈赠 (kuìzèng) — present as a gift
- 反馈 (fǎnkuì) — feedback

## Example Sentences

- 他馈赠了一份珍贵的礼物。(Tā kuìzèng le yī fèn zhēnguì de lǐwù.) — He presented a precious gift.

## Notes

Formal term for giving gifts or food, also used in technical term 'feedback'.
