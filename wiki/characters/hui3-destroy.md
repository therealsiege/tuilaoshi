# 毁 To Destroy

- **Character**: 毁
- **Pinyin**: huǐ
- **Meaning**: to destroy, ruin
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摧毁 (cuī huǐ) — to destroy
- 毁坏 (huǐ huài) — to damage, destroy

## Example Sentences

- 火灾毁坏了整栋楼。(Huǒzāi huǐhuài le zhěng dòng lóu.) — The fire destroyed the entire building.

## Notes

毁 combines 臼 (mortar) and weapon 殳, suggesting destruction through crushing and striking.
