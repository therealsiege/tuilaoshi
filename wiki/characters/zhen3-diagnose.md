# 诊 Diagnose

- **Character**: 诊
- **Pinyin**: zhěn
- **Meaning**: diagnose, examine
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诊断 (zhěn duàn) — diagnosis
- 门诊 (mén zhěn) — outpatient service
- 诊所 (zhěn suǒ) — clinic

## Example Sentences

- 医生诊断他得了流感。(Yī shēng zhěn duàn tā dé le liú gǎn.) — The doctor diagnosed him with the flu.

## Notes

Core medical activity; identifies disease.
