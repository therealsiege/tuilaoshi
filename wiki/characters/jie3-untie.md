# 解 To untie, solve, explain

- **Character**: 解
- **Pinyin**: jiě
- **Meaning**: to untie, solve, explain
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 理解 (lǐ jiě) — to understand
- 解决 (jiě jué) — to solve, resolve

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题很难解决。(Zhège wèntí hěn nán jiějué.) — This problem is difficult to solve.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: jiě (untie/solve), jiè (transport), xiè (lax).
