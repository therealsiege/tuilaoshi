# 虾 Shrimp, prawn

- **Character**: 虾
- **Pinyin**: xiā
- **Meaning**: shrimp, prawn
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 大虾 (dà xiā) — prawn
- 虾仁 (xiā rén) — shrimp meat

## Example Sentences

- 我点了一盘虾仁炒饭。(Wǒ diǎnle yī pán xiārén chǎofàn.) — I ordered a plate of shrimp fried rice.

## Notes

Popular seafood. Insect radical for arthropod.
