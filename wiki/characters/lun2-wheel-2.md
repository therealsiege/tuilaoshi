# 轮 Wheel

- **Character**: 轮
- **Pinyin**: lún
- **Meaning**: wheel, to take turns
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 轮流 (lún liú) — to take turns
- 车轮 (chē lún) — wheel

## Example Sentences

- 我们轮流值班。(Wǒ men lún liú zhí bān.) — We take turns on duty.

## Notes

轮 simplified from 輪. Vehicle 车 wheel rotating in cycles.
