# 跃 To jump, to leap

- **Character**: 跃
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: to jump, to leap
- **Radical**: 足(foot)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 跳跃 (tiào yuè) — to jump, to leap
- 飞跃 (fēi yuè) — to leap, leap forward

## Example Sentences

- 运动员纵身一跃跳过障碍。(Yùndòngyuán zòngshēn yī yuè tiàoguò zhàng'ài.) — The athlete leaped over the obstacle.

## Notes

Emphasizes dynamic jumping or leaping forward.
