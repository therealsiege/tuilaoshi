# 彰 Manifest, obvious

- **Character**: 彰
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: manifest, obvious
- **Radical**: 彡 (hair)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 彰显 (zhāngxiǎn) — manifest, display
- 表彰 (biǎozhāng) — commend, honor

## Example Sentences

- 这次活动彰显了团队精神。(Zhè cì huódòng zhāngxiǎn le tuánduì jīngshén.) — This activity manifested team spirit.

## Notes

Formal term for making evident or commending.
