# 旨 Purpose, imperial decree

- **Character**: 旨
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: purpose, imperial decree
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 宗旨 (zōng zhǐ) — purpose, aim
- 主旨 (zhǔ zhǐ) — gist, main idea

## Example Sentences

- 这个组织的宗旨是帮助穷人。(Zhège zǔzhī de zōngzhǐ shì bāngzhù qióngrén.) — The purpose of this organization is to help the poor.

## Notes

Formal term for purpose or main point, also historical for imperial edicts.
