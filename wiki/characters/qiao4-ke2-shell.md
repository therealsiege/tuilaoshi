# 壳 Shell, crust, husk

- **Character**: 壳
- **Pinyin**: qiào/ké
- **Meaning**: shell, crust, husk
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 地壳 (dì qiào) — earth's crust
- 外壳 (wài ké) — outer shell, casing

## Example Sentences

- 地壳是地球最外层的固体部分。(Dì qiào shì dì qiú zuì wài céng de gù tǐ bù fen.) — The Earth's crust is the outermost solid part of the planet.

## Notes

Used in geology for Earth's crust
