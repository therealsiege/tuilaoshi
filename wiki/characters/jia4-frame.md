# 架 Frame

- **Character**: 架
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: frame, shelf, to support
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 书架 (shū jià) — bookshelf
- 框架 (kuàng jià) — framework

## Example Sentences

- 书架上放满了书。(Shū jià shàng fàng mǎn le shū.) — The bookshelf is full of books.

## Notes

架 shows two pieces of wood 木 joined together with 加 (add), representing a wooden support structure.
