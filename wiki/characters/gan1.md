# 干 Dry

- **Character**: 干
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: dry; to do (gàn)
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 干净 (gān jìng) — clean
- 干燥 (gān zào) — dry, arid

## Example Sentences

- 衣服干了。(Yī fu gān le.) — The clothes are dry.

## Notes

干 means dry (gān) or to do (gàn). 干净 = clean. Opposite of 湿 (wet).
