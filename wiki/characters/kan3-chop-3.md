# 砍 Chop

- **Character**: 砍
- **Pinyin**: kǎn
- **Meaning**: to chop, hack
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 劈砍 (pī kǎn) — to chop
- 砍刀 (kǎn dāo) — chopping knife

## Example Sentences

- 武术中有砍的动作。(Wǔshù zhōng yǒu kǎn de dòngzuò.) — There are chopping movements in martial arts.

## Notes

Chopping strikes; martial arts technique
