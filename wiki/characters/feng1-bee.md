# 蜂 Bee, wasp

- **Character**: 蜂
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: bee, wasp
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蜜蜂 (mì fēng) — honeybee
- 蜂蜜 (fēng mì) — honey

## Example Sentences

- 蜜蜂在花丛中采蜜。(Mìfēng zài huācóng zhōng cǎi mì.) — Bees are collecting nectar among the flowers.

## Notes

Important for pollination and honey production. Insect radical.
