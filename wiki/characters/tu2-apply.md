# 涂 To Apply

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: to apply, to smear
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂料 (tú liào) — coating, paint
- 糊涂 (hú tú) — confused

## Example Sentences

- 涂上一层保护涂料。(Tú shàng yī céng bǎo hù tú liào.) — Apply a layer of protective coating.

## Notes

涂 with water radical for applying liquids, essential for painting and coating.
