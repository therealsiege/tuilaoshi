# 错 Wrong, error, interlocked

- **Character**: 错
- **Pinyin**: cuò
- **Meaning**: wrong, error, interlocked
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 错误 (cuò wù) — mistake, error
- 交错 (jiāo cuò) — to crisscross

## Example Sentences

- 我犯了一个严重的错误。(Wǒ fàn le yī ge yán zhòng de cuò wù.) — I made a serious mistake.

## Notes

Common character for mistakes and errors. Also means interlocked or staggered (错开).
