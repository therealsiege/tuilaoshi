# 息 Rest, cease, interest (finance)

- **Character**: 息
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: rest, cease, interest (finance)
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 利息 (lìxī) — interest (on loan)
- 休息 (xiūxi) — rest, take a break

## Example Sentences

- 银行的利息很低。(Yínháng de lìxī hěn dī.) — The bank's interest rate is very low.

## Notes

Dual meaning: rest/cease and financial interest.
