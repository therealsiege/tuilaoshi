# 礁 Reef, rock

- **Character**: 礁
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: reef, rock
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 珊瑚礁 (shān hú jiāo) — coral reef
- 暗礁 (àn jiāo) — hidden reef

## Example Sentences

- 船只要小心暗礁。(Chuán zhī yào xiǎo xīn àn jiāo.) — Ships must be careful of hidden reefs.

## Notes
