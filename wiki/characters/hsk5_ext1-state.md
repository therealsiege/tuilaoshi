# 述 State, Narrate, 叙述 Narrate

- **Character**: 述
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: state, narrate, 叙述 narrate
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 叙述 (xù shù) — narrate, recount
- 陈述 (chén shù) — state, declare
- 综述 (zōng shù) — summary, review

## Example Sentences

- 请陈述事实经过。(Qǐng chénshù shìshí jīngguò.) — Please state the facts of what happened.

## Notes

Common in formal and academic writing. Used to describe the act of narrating or stating information clearly and systematically.
