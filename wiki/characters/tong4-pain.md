# 痛 Pain

- **Character**: 痛
- **Pinyin**: tòng
- **Meaning**: pain, ache, painful
- **Radical**: 疒(sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 痛苦 (tòng kǔ) — pain, suffering
- 头痛 (tóu tòng) — headache

## Example Sentences

- 我肚子很痛。(Wǒ dù zi hěn tòng.) — My stomach hurts a lot.

## Notes

痛 uses sickness radical 疒 with 甬 (passage), representing pain that penetrates through the body.
