# 进 To Enter

- **Character**: 进
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to enter, to advance
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 进来 (jìn lái) — to come in
- 进步 (jìn bù) — progress

## Example Sentences

- 请进！(Qǐng jìn!) — Please come in!

## Notes

进 is simplified from 進. It uses 辶 (walking) and 井 as phonetic. Opposite of 出.
