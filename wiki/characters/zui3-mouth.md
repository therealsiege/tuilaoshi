# 嘴 Mouth

- **Character**: 嘴
- **Pinyin**: zuǐ
- **Meaning**: mouth
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 嘴巴 (zuǐ ba) — mouth
- 张嘴 (zhāng zuǐ) — to open mouth

## Example Sentences

- 闭上嘴巴。(Bì shàng zuǐ ba.) — Close your mouth.

## Notes

嘴 colloquial word for mouth. 嘴巴 adds suffix. 口 is more formal/written.
