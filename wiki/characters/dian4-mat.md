# 垫 Mat

- **Character**: 垫
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: mat, cushion, pad
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 垫子 (diàn zi) — mat, cushion
- 坐垫 (zuò diàn) — seat cushion

## Example Sentences

- 体操运动员在垫子上练习。(Tǐcāo yùndòngyuán zài diànzi shàng liànxí.) — Gymnasts practice on mats.

## Notes

Exercise mats and cushions; safety equipment
