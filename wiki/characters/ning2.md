# 凝 Congeal, condense, freeze

- **Character**: 凝
- **Pinyin**: níng
- **Meaning**: congeal, condense, freeze
- **Radical**: 冰 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 凝固 (níng gù) — solidify, congeal
- 凝结 (níng jié) — condense, coagulate

## Example Sentences

- 水在零度以下会凝固成冰。(Shuǐ zài líng dù yǐ xià huì níng gù chéng bīng.) — Water solidifies into ice below zero degrees.

## Notes

Liquid to solid transition
