# 仇 Enemy

- **Character**: 仇
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: enemy, hatred, revenge
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 仇恨 (chóu hèn) — hatred, enmity
- 报仇 (bào chóu) — to revenge

## Example Sentences

- 他们之间有深仇大恨。(Tāmen zhī jiān yǒu shēn chóu dà hèn.) — There is deep hatred between them.

## Notes

仇 can also be read as qiú (partner, spouse). The chóu reading meaning 'enemy' is more common.
