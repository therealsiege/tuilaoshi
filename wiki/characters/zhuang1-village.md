# 庄 Village, solemn, grave

- **Character**: 庄
- **Pinyin**: zhuāng
- **Meaning**: village, solemn, grave
- **Radical**: 广(shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 庄重 (zhuāng zhòng) — solemn, grave
- 庄严 (zhuāng yán) — solemn, dignified

## Example Sentences

- 葬礼气氛庄重。(Zànglǐ qìfēn zhuāngzhòng.) — The funeral atmosphere was solemn.

## Notes

Can mean village or describe solemn/serious demeanor.
