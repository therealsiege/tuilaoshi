# 初 Beginning

- **Character**: 初
- **Pinyin**: chū
- **Meaning**: beginning, initial, first
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 最初 (zuì chū) — initially
- 初级 (chū jí) — elementary

## Example Sentences

- 我是初学者。(Wǒ shì chū xué zhě.) — I'm a beginner.

## Notes

初 means beginning or first. 初恋 = first love. 当初 = originally.
