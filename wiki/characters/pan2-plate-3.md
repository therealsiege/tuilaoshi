# 盘 Plate

- **Character**: 盘
- **Pinyin**: pán
- **Meaning**: plate, tray, to coil
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 键盘 (jiàn pán) — keyboard
- 硬盘 (yìng pán) — hard disk

## Example Sentences

- 电脑硬盘坏了。(Diàn nǎo yìng pán huài le.) — The computer hard disk is broken.

## Notes

盘 simplified from 盤. Dish 皿 with items arranged, extended to disk.
