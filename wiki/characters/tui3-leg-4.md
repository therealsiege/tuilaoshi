# 腿 Leg

- **Character**: 腿
- **Pinyin**: tuǐ
- **Meaning**: leg
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 踢腿 (tī tuǐ) — to kick (leg)
- 大腿 (dà tuǐ) — thigh

## Example Sentences
