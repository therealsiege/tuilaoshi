# 钟 Clock

- **Character**: 钟
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: clock, bell, o'clock
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 分钟 (fēn zhōng) — minute
- 钟头 (zhōng tóu) — hour

## Example Sentences

- 等一分钟。(Děng yī fēn zhōng.) — Wait a minute.

## Notes

钟 is simplified from 鐘/鍾. It uses the metal radical 钅because clocks and bells are made of metal.
