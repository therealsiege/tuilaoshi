# 零 Zero, fragmentary, retail

- **Character**: 零
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: zero, fragmentary, retail
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 零售 (líng shòu) — retail
- 零件 (líng jiàn) — spare part, component

## Example Sentences

- 这家店主要做零售生意。(Zhè jiā diàn zhǔ yào zuò líng shòu shēng yi.) — This store mainly does retail business.

## Notes

零 contrasts with 批 (wholesale). 零花钱 means pocket money. 零星 means scattered or sporadic.
