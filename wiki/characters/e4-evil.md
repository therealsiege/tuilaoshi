# 恶 Evil, bad, to hate, 惡 (traditional)

- **Character**: 恶
- **Pinyin**: è
- **Meaning**: evil, bad, to hate, 惡 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恶劣 (è liè) — vile, nasty
- 厌恶 (yàn wù) — to detest, loathe

## Example Sentences

- 天气恶劣，航班取消了。(Tiānqì èliè, hángbān qǔxiāo le.) — Due to bad weather, the flight was cancelled.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: è (evil/bad), wù (to hate), ě (nausea).
