# 咏 To Chant

- **Character**: 咏
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: to chant, to sing, to praise
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 歌咏 (gē yǒng) — to sing
- 咏叹 (yǒng tàn) — to sing with admiration

## Example Sentences

- 诗人咏叹自然之美。(Shī rén yǒng tàn zì rán zhī měi.) — The poet sang in praise of nature's beauty.

## Notes

咏 emphasizes lyrical or poetic singing, often praising something.
