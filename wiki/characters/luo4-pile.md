# 摞 To pile up, stack, pile

- **Character**: 摞
- **Pinyin**: luò
- **Meaning**: to pile up, stack, pile
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 一摞 (yī luò) — a pile, a stack
- 摞起来 (luò qǐlái) — to stack up

## Example Sentences

- 桌上有一摞书。(Zhuō shàng yǒu yī luò shū.) — There is a stack of books on the table.

## Notes

Measure word and verb for stacking or piling.
