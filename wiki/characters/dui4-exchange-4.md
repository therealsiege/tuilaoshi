# 兑 Exchange, convert

- **Character**: 兑
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: exchange, convert
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 兑换 (duì huàn) — exchange, convert
- 兑现 (duì xiàn) — cash, honor (check)

## Example Sentences

- 在哪儿可以兑换外币?(Zài nǎr kě yǐ duì huàn wài bì?) — Where can I exchange foreign currency?

## Notes

兑付 means honor payment. 兑换率 is exchange rate.
