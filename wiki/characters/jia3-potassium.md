# 钾 Potassium

- **Character**: 钾
- **Pinyin**: jiǎ
- **Meaning**: potassium
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钾肥 (jiǎ féi) — potassium fertilizer
- 补钾 (bǔ jiǎ) — potassium supplement

## Example Sentences

- 香蕉含有大量的钾。(Xiāng jiāo hán yǒu dà liàng de jiǎ.) — Bananas contain a lot of potassium.

## Notes

Essential mineral for muscle and nerve function
