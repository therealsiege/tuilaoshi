# 害 To Harm

- **Character**: 害
- **Pinyin**: hài
- **Meaning**: to harm, to kill, evil
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 伤害 (shāng hài) — to harm
- 害怕 (hài pà) — to be afraid

## Example Sentences

- 吸烟有害健康。(Xī yān yǒu hài jiàn kāng.) — Smoking is harmful to health.

## Notes

害 shows a roof 宀 with mouth 口 attacking 丰, representing harmful influence entering a household.
