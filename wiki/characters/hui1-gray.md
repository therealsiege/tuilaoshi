# 灰 Gray

- **Character**: 灰
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: gray, ash, dust
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 灰色 (huī sè) — gray color
- 灰尘 (huī chén) — dust

## Example Sentences

- 他穿着灰色的外套。(Tā chuān zhe huī sè de wài tào.) — He's wearing a gray coat.

## Notes

灰 combines fire (火) producing ash/dust. Also means discouraged or disheartened (灰心 to lose heart).
