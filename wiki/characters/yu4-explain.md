# 喻 To Explain

- **Character**: 喻
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to explain, metaphor
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 比喻 (bǐ yù) — metaphor, to compare
- 家喻户晓 (jiā yù hù xiǎo) — known to all

## Example Sentences

- 老师用比喻来解释这个概念。(Lǎo shī yòng bǐ yù lái jiě shì zhè ge gài niàn.) — The teacher used a metaphor to explain this concept.

## Notes

喻 uses mouth radical 口, meaning to explain or make understood through comparison or metaphor.
