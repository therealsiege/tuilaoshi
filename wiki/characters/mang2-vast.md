# 茫 Vast, boundless, uncertain

- **Character**: 茫
- **Pinyin**: máng
- **Meaning**: vast, boundless, uncertain
- **Radical**: 艹(grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 茫茫 (máng máng) — vast and hazy
- 迷茫 (mí máng) — confused, perplexed

## Example Sentences

- 他对未来感到迷茫。(Tā duì wèilái gǎndào mímáng.) — He feels confused about the future.

## Notes

Can mean vast/boundless or confused/uncertain.
