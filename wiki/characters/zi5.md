# 子 Son

- **Character**: 子
- **Pinyin**: zi
- **Meaning**: son, child, suffix
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 孩子 (hái zi) — child
- 坠子 (zhuì zi) — ballad singing

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的儿子。(Tā shì wǒ de ér zi.) — He is my son.

## Notes

子 means son or child; often used as a noun suffix.
