# 旺 Prosperous, vigorous, flourishing

- **Character**: 旺
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: prosperous, vigorous, flourishing
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 兴旺 (xīng wàng) — prosperous
- 旺盛 (wàng shèng) — vigorous, exuberant

## Example Sentences

- 他的生意很旺。(Tā de shēng yi hěn wàng.) — His business is very prosperous.

## Notes

Indicates vigorous prosperity. 旺季 means peak season. 人气旺 means very popular.
