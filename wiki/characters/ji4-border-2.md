# 际 Border

- **Character**: 际
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: border, occasion, between
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 国际 (guó jì) — international
- 实际 (shí jì) — actual, reality

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个国际会议。(Zhè shì yī gè guó jì huì yì.) — This is an international conference.

## Notes

际 simplified from 際. Shows mound/boundary 阝 representing borders or limits between things.
