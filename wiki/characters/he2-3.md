# 核 Nucleus

- **Character**: 核
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: 核心, nucleus, to check
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 核心 (hé xīn) — core, nucleus
- 核查 (hé chá) — to verify

## Example Sentences

- 这是问题的核心。(Zhè shì wèn tí de hé xīn.) — This is the core of the problem.

## Notes

核 originally meant fruit pit/kernel, extended to核心 and verification.
