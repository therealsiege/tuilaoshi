# 截 Intercept

- **Character**: 截
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: to intercept, cut off
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 截球 (jié qiú) — to intercept (ball)
- 拦截 (lán jié) — to intercept

## Example Sentences

- 他截下了传球。(Tā jié xià le chuánqiú.) — He intercepted the pass.

## Notes

Interception; stealing possession
