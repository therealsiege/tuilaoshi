# 梅 Plum

- **Character**: 梅
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: plum, plum blossom
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 望梅止渴 (wàng méi zhǐ kě) — console with false hope
- 梅花 (méi huā) — plum blossom

## Example Sentences

- 望梅止渴解决不了实际问题。(Wàng méi zhǐ kě jiějué bù liǎo shíjì wèntí.) — False hope can't solve real problems.

## Notes

Used in 望梅止渴: Cao Cao told his thirsty troops there were plum trees ahead; plum blossom is China's national flower.
