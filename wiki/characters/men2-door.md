# 门 Door

- **Character**: 门
- **Pinyin**: mén
- **Meaning**: door, gate, entrance
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 开门 (kāi mén) — to open door
- 关门 (guān mén) — to close door

## Example Sentences

- 请关门。(Qǐng guān mén.) — Please close the door.

## Notes

门 simplified from 門, pictograph of double door. Used as radical in many characters.
