# 脏 Dirty

- **Character**: 脏
- **Pinyin**: zāng
- **Meaning**: dirty, filthy (also zàng: internal organ)
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 肮脏 (āng zāng) — dirty, filthy
- 心脏 (xīn zàng) — heart (organ)

## Example Sentences

- 衣服脏了要洗。(Yī fú zāng le yào xǐ.) — Dirty clothes need to be washed.

## Notes

脏 simplified from 髒/臟. As zāng means dirty, as zàng refers to internal organs (viscera).
