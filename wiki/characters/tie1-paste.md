# 贴 Paste, stick, subsidy

- **Character**: 贴
- **Pinyin**: tiē
- **Meaning**: paste, stick, subsidy
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 津贴 (jīn tiē) — allowance, subsidy
- 补贴 (bǔ tiē) — subsidy, allowance

## Example Sentences

- 政府发放住房补贴。(Zhèng fǔ fā fàng zhù fáng bǔ tiē.) — The government issues housing subsidies.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: to paste/stick or subsidy. 贴近 means to be close to. 体贴 means considerate.
