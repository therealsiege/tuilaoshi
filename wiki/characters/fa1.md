# 发 Send, issue, develop

- **Character**: 发
- **Pinyin**: fā
- **Meaning**: send, issue, develop
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 发送 (fā sòng) — to send, dispatch
- 发展 (fā zhǎn) — to develop

## Example Sentences

- 公司发展迅速。(Gōng sī fā zhǎn xùn sù.) — The company is developing rapidly.

## Notes

Extremely versatile character. 发财 means to get rich. 批发 means wholesale. 发票 means invoice.
