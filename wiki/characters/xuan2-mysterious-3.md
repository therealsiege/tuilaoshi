# 玄 Mysterious

- **Character**: 玄
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: mysterious, profound
- **Radical**: 玄 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 玄妙 (xuán miào) — mysterious
- 玄学 (xuán xué) — metaphysics

## Example Sentences

- 这个理论很玄妙。(Zhè gè lǐ lùn hěn xuán miào.) — This theory is very mysterious.

## Notes

玄 represents deep, dark color, extended to profound mystery.
