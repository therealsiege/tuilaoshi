# 捕 To Catch

- **Character**: 捕
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: to catch, to seize
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 逮捕 (dài bǔ) — to arrest
- 捕捉 (bǔ zhuō) — to catch

## Example Sentences

- 猫在捕捉老鼠。(Māo zài bǔ zhuō lǎo shǔ.) — The cat is catching mice.

## Notes

捕 uses hand radical 扌, indicating active pursuit and capture. Used for animals, criminals, or abstract concepts.
