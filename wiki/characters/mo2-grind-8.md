# 磨 To Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: to grind, to polish, to wear down
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 磨练 (mó liàn) — to temper
- 折磨 (zhé mó) — to torment

## Example Sentences

- 他用石头磨刀。(Tā yòng shí tou mó dāo.) — He sharpens the knife with a stone.

## Notes

磨 has stone 石 at bottom, 麻 (hemp) on top, grinding hemp on stone.
