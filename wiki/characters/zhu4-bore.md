# 蛀 To Bore

- **Character**: 蛀
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to bore, to eat into (insects)
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 蛀虫 (zhù chóng) — moth, wood borer

## Example Sentences

- 木头被蛀虫吃了。(Mù tou bèi zhù chóng chī le.) — The wood was eaten by borers.

## Notes

蛀 combines insect 虫 and主 (host), representing insects boring into wood or grain.
