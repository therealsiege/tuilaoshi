# 志 Will, mark

- **Character**: 志
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: will, mark
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 标志 (biāo zhì) — mark, sign
- 杂志 (zá zhì) — magazine

## Example Sentences

- 公司标志设计得很好。(Gōng sī biāo zhì shè jì de hěn hǎo.) — The company logo is well designed.

## Notes

志向 means aspiration. 意志 means will/willpower.
