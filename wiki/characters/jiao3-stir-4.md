# 搅 To stir, to mix

- **Character**: 搅
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: to stir, to mix
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir, to mix
- 搅动 (jiǎo dòng) — to stir, to agitate

## Example Sentences

- 做蛋糕要把鸡蛋搅拌均匀。(Zuò dàngāo yào bǎ jīdàn jiǎobàn jūnyún.) — When making cake, you need to mix the eggs evenly.

## Notes

Essential cooking action. Hand radical indicates manual action.
