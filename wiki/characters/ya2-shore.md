# 涯 Shore, bank, boundary

- **Character**: 涯
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: shore, bank, boundary
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 天涯 (tiān yá) — edge of the sky, remotest place
- 海涯 (hǎi yá) — seashore

## Example Sentences

- 他们走遍了天涯海角。(Tā men zǒu biàn le tiān yá hǎi jiǎo.) — They traveled to the ends of the earth.

## Notes

Edge or boundary of water
