# 苑 Garden, park, institution

- **Character**: 苑
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: garden, park, institution
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 花苑 (huā yuàn) — flower garden
- 文苑 (wén yuàn) — literary circles

## Example Sentences

- 皇家园林中有精美的花苑。(Huáng jiā yuán lín zhōng yǒu jīng měi de huā yuàn.) — There are exquisite flower gardens in the imperial gardens.

## Notes

Often used for imperial gardens or cultural institutions.
