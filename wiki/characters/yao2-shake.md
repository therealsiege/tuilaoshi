# 摇 To Shake

- **Character**: 摇
- **Pinyin**: yáo
- **Meaning**: to shake, to rock, to wave
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摇头 (yáo tóu) — to shake one's head
- 摇动 (yáo dòng) — to shake, to sway

## Example Sentences

- 他摇了摇头。(Tā yáo le yáo tóu.) — He shook his head.

## Notes

摇 simplified from 搖. Hand radical 扌 representing shaking or rocking motion.
