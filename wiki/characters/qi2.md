# 奇 Strange

- **Character**: 奇
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: strange, odd, wonderful
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奇怪 (qí guài) — strange, odd
- 好奇 (hào qí) — curious

## Example Sentences

- 这件事很奇怪。(Zhè jiàn shì hěn qí guài.) — This matter is very strange.

## Notes

奇 shows big 大 and uneven 可, representing something unusual or remarkable.
