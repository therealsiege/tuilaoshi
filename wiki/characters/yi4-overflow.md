# 溢 To overflow, to brim over

- **Character**: 溢
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to overflow, to brim over
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 洋溢 (yáng yì) — to be permeated with, to brim with
- 溢出 (yì chū) — to overflow, to spill over

## Example Sentences

- 她的脸上洋溢着幸福的笑容。(Tā de liǎn shàng yángyì zhe xìngfú de xiàoróng.) — Her face was beaming with a happy smile.

## Notes

Often used figuratively for emotions or atmosphere overflowing.
