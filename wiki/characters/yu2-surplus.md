# 余 Surplus, remainder

- **Character**: 余
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: surplus, remainder
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 余额 (yú é) — balance, remainder
- 结余 (jié yú) — surplus, balance

## Example Sentences

- 账户余额不足。(Zhàng hù yú é bù zú.) — Account balance insufficient.

## Notes

余款 means remaining sum. 富余 means have a surplus.
