# 郡 Prefecture, commandery

- **Character**: 郡
- **Pinyin**: jùn
- **Meaning**: prefecture, commandery
- **Radical**: 阝 (city)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 郡县 (jùn xiàn) — prefectures and counties
- 郡守 (jùn shǒu) — prefectural governor

## Example Sentences

- 秦朝建立了郡县制。(Qín cháo jiànlì le jùn xiàn zhì.) — The Qin Dynasty established the prefecture-county system.

## Notes

Administrative division in ancient China, larger than a county. The Qin Dynasty's prefecture system centralized imperial control.
