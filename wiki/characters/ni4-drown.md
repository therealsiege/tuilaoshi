# 溺 To drown, indulge

- **Character**: 溺
- **Pinyin**: nì
- **Meaning**: to drown, indulge
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 溺爱 (nì ài) — to spoil, dote on
- 溺水 (nì shuǐ) — to drown

## Example Sentences

- 父母不应该溺爱孩子。(Fùmǔ bù yīnggāi nì'ài háizi.) — Parents should not spoil their children.

## Notes

To drown or indulge excessively.
