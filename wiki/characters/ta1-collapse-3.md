# 塌 To Collapse

- **Character**: 塌
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to cave in
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 倒塌 (dǎo tā) — to collapse
- 塌方 (tā fāng) — cave-in

## Example Sentences

- 房屋倒塌了。(Fáng wū dǎo tā le.) — The house collapsed.

## Notes

塌 combines earth 土 and 㚔 (phonetic), representing ground or structure sinking.
