# 抹 To smear, to wipe

- **Character**: 抹
- **Pinyin**: mǒ
- **Meaning**: to smear, to wipe
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear, to apply
- 抹布 (mā bù) — cleaning cloth

## Example Sentences

- 她在脸上抹了护肤霜。(Tā zài liǎn shàng mǒle hùfū shuāng.) — She applied face cream on her face.

## Notes

Three pronunciations: mǒ (smear), mā (wipe), mò (erase). Hand radical.
