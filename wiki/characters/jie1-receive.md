# 接 To Receive

- **Character**: 接
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: to receive, to pick up, to connect
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 接受 (jiē shòu) — to accept
- 直接 (zhí jiē) — direct

## Example Sentences

- 我去机场接你。(Wǒ qù jī chǎng jiē nǐ.) — I'll pick you up at the airport.

## Notes

接 means to receive, connect, or pick up (a person). 接电话 = answer the phone.
