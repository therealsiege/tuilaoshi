# 巅 Summit, peak, top

- **Character**: 巅
- **Pinyin**: diān
- **Meaning**: summit, peak, top
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 巅峰 (diān fēng) — summit, peak
- 山巅 (shān diān) — mountaintop

## Example Sentences

- 他的事业达到了巅峰。(Tā de shì yè dá dào le diān fēng.) — His career reached its peak.

## Notes

Often used metaphorically for the highest point of achievement.
