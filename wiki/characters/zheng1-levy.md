# 征 To Levy

- **Character**: 征
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: to levy, expedition
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 征服 (zhēng fú) — to conquer
- 象征 (xiàng zhēng) — to symbolize

## Example Sentences

- 这座山很难征服。(Zhè zuò shān hěn nán zhēngfú.) — This mountain is hard to conquer.

## Notes

征 combines walking 彳 with correct 正, suggesting a punitive expedition or征.
