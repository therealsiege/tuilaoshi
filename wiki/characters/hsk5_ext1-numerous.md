# 繁 Numerous, Complex, 繁荣 Prosperous

- **Character**: 繁
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: numerous, complex, 繁荣 prosperous
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 繁荣 (fán róng) — prosperous, flourishing
- 繁忙 (fán máng) — busy
- 频繁 (pín fán) — frequent

## Example Sentences

- 这个城市经济繁荣。(Zhège chéngshì jīngjì fánróng.) — This city's economy is prosperous.

## Notes

Versatile character meaning numerous, complex, or prosperous. 繁体字 (traditional characters) vs 简体字 (simplified characters).
