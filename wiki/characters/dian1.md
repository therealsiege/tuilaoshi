# 颠 Top

- **Character**: 颠
- **Pinyin**: diān
- **Meaning**: top, summit, to topple
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 颠倒 (diān dǎo) — to reverse, to invert
- 颠覆 (diān fù) — to overturn, to subvert

## Example Sentences

- 他颠覆了传统观念。(Tā diān fù le chuán tǒng guān niàn.) — He overturned traditional concepts.

## Notes

颠 uses the head radical 页, suggesting something relating to the top or summit. Often appears in compounds meaning to overturn or upset.
