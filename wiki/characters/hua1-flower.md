# 花 Flower

- **Character**: 花
- **Pinyin**: huā
- **Meaning**: flower, to spend
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 花园 (huā yuán) — garden
- 花钱 (huā qián) — to spend money

## Example Sentences

- 这朵花很漂亮。(Zhè duǒ huā hěn piào liang.) — This flower is very beautiful.

## Notes

花 means both 'flower' (noun) and 'to spend' (verb). The connection may be that flowers are ephemeral, like spent money.
