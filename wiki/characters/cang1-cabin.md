# 舱 Cabin

- **Character**: 舱
- **Pinyin**: cāng
- **Meaning**: cabin, hold (of ship/aircraft)
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 船舱 (chuán cāng) — ship cabin
- 机舱 (jī cāng) — aircraft cabin

## Example Sentences

- 请回到机舱就座。(Qǐng huí dào jī cāng jiù zuò.) — Please return to the cabin and take your seat.

## Notes

舱 means cabin or compartment. Uses the boat radical 舟.
