# 渗 To Seep

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: to seep, to ooze, to permeate
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate
- 渗漏 (shèn lòu) — to leak

## Example Sentences

- 水渗进墙里了。(Shuǐ shèn jìn qiáng lǐ le.) — Water seeped into the wall.

## Notes

渗 means to seep or permeate. Used for liquids seeping through surfaces or ideas permeating minds.
