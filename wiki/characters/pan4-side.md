# 畔 Side, bank, edge

- **Character**: 畔
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: side, bank, edge
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 湖畔 (hú pàn) — lakeside
- 河畔 (hé pàn) — riverside

## Example Sentences

- 他们在湖畔散步。(Tā men zài hú pàn sàn bù.) — They are strolling by the lakeside.

## Notes

Literary term for the edge of water bodies.
