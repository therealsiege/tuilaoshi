# 迟 Late

- **Character**: 迟
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: late, slow, delayed
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 迟到 (chí dào) — to be late
- 延迟 (yán chí) — to delay

## Example Sentences

- 对不起，我迟到了。(Duì bu qǐ, wǒ chí dào le.) — Sorry, I'm late.

## Notes

迟 simplified from 遲. Shows the walk radical 辶, indicating slow delayed movement.
