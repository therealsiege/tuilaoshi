# 懈 Slacken

- **Character**: 懈
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to slacken, lax, slack
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 坚持不懈 (jiān chí bù xiè) — persevere relentlessly
- 松懈 (sōng xiè) — to relax vigilance

## Example Sentences

- 我们不能有丝毫的松懈。(Wǒmen bù néng yǒu sīháo de sōngxiè.) — We cannot have the slightest lapse.

## Notes

Used in 坚持不懈: not slackening; the negation 不懈 means unwavering.
