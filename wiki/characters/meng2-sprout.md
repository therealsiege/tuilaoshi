# 萌 Sprout, bud, germinate

- **Character**: 萌
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: sprout, bud, germinate
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 萌芽 (méng yá) — sprout, germinate
- 萌发 (méng fā) — sprout, germinate

## Example Sentences

- 种子在温暖的环境中萌发。(Zhǒng zi zài wēn nuǎn de huán jìng zhōng méng fā.) — Seeds germinate in a warm environment.
