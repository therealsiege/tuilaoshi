# 恬 Calm, peaceful, quiet

- **Character**: 恬
- **Pinyin**: tián
- **Meaning**: calm, peaceful, quiet
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恬静 (tián jìng) — quiet and peaceful
- 恬淡 (tián dàn) — quiet and indifferent

## Example Sentences

- 她的性格很恬静。(Tā de xìng gé hěn tián jìng.) — Her personality is very peaceful.

## Notes

Tranquil and undisturbed state of mind
