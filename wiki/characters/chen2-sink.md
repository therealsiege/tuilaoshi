# 沉 Sink, submerge, heavy

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: sink, submerge, heavy
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沉积 (chén jī) — sedimentation, deposit
- 下沉 (xià chén) — sink, descend

## Example Sentences

- 沉积岩是由沉积物形成的。(Chén jī yán shì yóu chén jī wù xíng chéng de.) — Sedimentary rock is formed from sediment.

## Notes

Key term in geology for sedimentation
