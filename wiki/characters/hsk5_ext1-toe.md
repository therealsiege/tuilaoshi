# 趾 Toe, 脚趾 Toe

- **Character**: 趾
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: toe, 脚趾 toe
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 脚趾 (jiǎo zhǐ) — toe
- 趾甲 (zhǐ jiǎ) — toenail
- 大脚趾 (dà jiǎo zhǐ) — big toe

## Example Sentences

- 他的大脚趾受伤了。(Tā de dà jiǎozhǐ shòushāng le.) — His big toe is injured.

## Notes

Formal term for toe. 脚趾 is standard usage. Parallel structure with 手指 (finger) and 脚趾 (toe).
