# 斗 To Fight

- **Character**: 斗
- **Pinyin**: dòu
- **Meaning**: to fight, to struggle
- **Radical**: 斗 (dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 斗争 (dòu zhēng) — struggle
- 战斗 (zhàn dòu) — to fight

## Example Sentences

- 我们要跟困难斗争。(Wǒ men yào gēn kùn nán dòu zhēng.) — We must struggle against difficulties.

## Notes

斗 as dòu means fight/struggle. As dǒu it's a measure word for grain. Originally depicted two people fighting.
