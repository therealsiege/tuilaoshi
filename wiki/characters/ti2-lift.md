# 提 To Lift

- **Character**: 提
- **Pinyin**: tí
- **Meaning**: to lift, to raise, to mention
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 提高 (tí gāo) — to improve, to raise
- 提前 (tí qián) — to advance, to move up

## Example Sentences

- 我想提高我的中文水平。(Wǒ xiǎng tí gāo wǒ de zhōng wén shuǐ píng.) — I want to improve my Chinese level.

## Notes

提 physically means to lift/carry, and metaphorically to raise (standards, questions, etc.). Essential in academic contexts.
