# 惯 Accustomed

- **Character**: 惯
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: accustomed to, habit
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 习惯 (xí guàn) — habit; to be used to
- 惯例 (guàn lì) — convention

## Example Sentences

- 你习惯了吗？(Nǐ xí guàn le ma?) — Have you gotten used to it?

## Notes

习惯 works as both noun (habit) and verb (to be accustomed to).
