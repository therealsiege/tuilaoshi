# 花 Flower

- **Character**: 花
- **Pinyin**: huā
- **Meaning**: flower, to spend
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 花鼓 (huā gǔ) — flower drum (opera)
- 鲜花 (xiān huā) — fresh flower

## Example Sentences

- 花鼓戏是湖南的地方戏。(Huā gǔ xì shì hú nán de dì fāng xì.) — Flower drum opera is a local opera from Hunan.

## Notes

花 means flower; 花鼓戏 is a lively folk opera with drum and gong.
