# 洞 Cave, hole, penetrate

- **Character**: 洞
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: cave, hole, penetrate
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 洞穴 (dòng xué) — cave
- 山洞 (shān dòng) — mountain cave

## Example Sentences

- 探险队进入了一个神秘的山洞。(Tàn xiǎn duì jìn rù le yī ge shén mì de shān dòng.) — The expedition team entered a mysterious mountain cave.

## Notes

Common character for caves and holes. 洞察 means to see clearly or perceive. 窟窿 vs 洞穴 both mean cave.
