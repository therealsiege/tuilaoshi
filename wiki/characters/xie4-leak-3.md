# 泄 To Leak

- **Character**: 泄
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to leak, to release
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泄漏 (xiè lòu) — to leak
- 发泄 (fā xiè) — to vent

## Example Sentences

- 秘密被泄漏了。(Mì mì bèi xiè lòu le.) — The secret was leaked.

## Notes

泄 has water radical 氵 representing liquid leaking out.
