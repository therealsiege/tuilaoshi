# 播 Broadcast, sow

- **Character**: 播
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: broadcast, sow
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 播放 (bō fàng) — broadcast, play
- 广播 (guǎng bō) — broadcast

## Example Sentences

- 电视台播放广告。(Diàn shì tái bō fàng guǎng gào.) — The TV station broadcasts advertisements.

## Notes

直播 means live broadcast. 转播 means relay broadcast.
