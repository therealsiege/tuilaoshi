# 分 Divide

- **Character**: 分
- **Pinyin**: fēn
- **Meaning**: to divide, part, minute, cent
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 入木三分 (rù mù sān fēn) — penetrating insight
- 分别 (fēn bié) — to part, respectively

## Example Sentences

- 分析问题要入木三分。(Fēnxī wèntí yào rù mù sān fēn.) — Analyzing problems requires penetrating insight.

## Notes

In 入木三分, 分 is a unit of depth (about 3mm); also read fèn for portion/role.
