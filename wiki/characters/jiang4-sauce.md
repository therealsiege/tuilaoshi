# 酱 Sauce, paste, 醬 (traditional)

- **Character**: 酱
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: sauce, paste, 醬 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 酱油 (jiàng yóu) — soy sauce
- 果酱 (guǒ jiàng) — jam

## Example Sentences

- 做菜要放酱油。(Zuò cài yào fàng jiàngyóu.) — You need to add soy sauce when cooking.

## Notes

Sauce or paste used as condiment or flavoring.
