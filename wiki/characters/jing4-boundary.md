# 境 Boundary

- **Character**: 境
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: boundary, border, situation, territory
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 环境 (huán jìng) — environment
- 边境 (biān jìng) — border

## Example Sentences

- 这里的环境很好。(Zhè lǐ de huán jìng hěn hǎo.) — The environment here is very good.

## Notes

境 combines土 (earth) with境 (sound), representing territory or situation. Essential for discussing environments and borders.
