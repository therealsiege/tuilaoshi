# 少 Few

- **Character**: 少
- **Pinyin**: shǎo
- **Meaning**: few, little, less
- **Radical**: 小 (small)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 多少 (duō shǎo) — how many
- 至少 (zhì shǎo) — at least

## Example Sentences

- 他的钱很少。(Tā de qián hěn shǎo.) — He has very little money.

## Notes

少 can also be read as shào meaning 'young' as in 少年 (shào nián, youth).
