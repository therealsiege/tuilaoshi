# 远 Far

- **Character**: 远
- **Pinyin**: yuǎn
- **Meaning**: far, distant
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 跳远 (tiào yuǎn) — long jump
- 远足 (yuǎn zú) — hiking

## Example Sentences

- 他的跳远成绩很好。(Tā de tiàoyuǎn chéngjì hěn hǎo.) — His long jump results are very good.

## Notes

Distance; long jump and far distances
