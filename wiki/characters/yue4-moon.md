# 月 Moon, month

- **Character**: 月
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: moon, month
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 月球 (yuè qiú) — the moon
- 月亮 (yuè liang) — moon

## Example Sentences

- 月球绕地球旋转。(Yuè qiú rào dì qiú xuán zhuǎn.) — The moon revolves around the Earth.

## Notes

Natural satellite of Earth, also means month
