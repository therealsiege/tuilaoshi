# 醇 Pure, mellow, rich

- **Character**: 醇
- **Pinyin**: chún
- **Meaning**: pure, mellow, rich
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 醇厚 (chún hòu) — mellow, rich
- 纯醇 (chún chún) — pure, genuine

## Example Sentences

- 这酒口感醇厚，回味悠长。(Zhè jiǔ kǒu gǎn chún hòu, huí wèi yōu cháng.) — This wine has a mellow taste with a long aftertaste.

## Notes

Describes mellow taste of wine or pure character.
