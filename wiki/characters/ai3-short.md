# 矮 Short

- **Character**: 矮
- **Pinyin**: ǎi
- **Meaning**: short (height), low
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 矮小 (ǎi xiǎo) — short and small
- 高矮 (gāo ǎi) — height

## Example Sentences

- 这个桌子太矮了。(Zhè ge zhuō zi tài ǎi le.) — This table is too low.

## Notes

矮 opposite of 高 (tall/high). Used for height of people and objects.
