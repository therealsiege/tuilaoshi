# 连 To Connect

- **Character**: 连
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: to connect, even, company
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 连接 (lián jiē) — to connect
- 连续 (lián xù) — continuous

## Example Sentences

- 他连续工作了十个小时。(Tā lián xù gōng zuò le shí ge xiǎo shí.) — He worked continuously for ten hours.

## Notes

连 is simplified from 連. Shows continuous movement or connection.
