# 系 To tie, to fasten, to button up

- **Character**: 系
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to tie, to fasten, to button up
- **Radical**: 糸(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 系鞋带 (jì xiédài) — to tie shoelaces
- 联系 (lián xì) — to contact, connection

## Example Sentences

- 上车后请系好安全带。(Shàngchē hòu qǐng jì hǎo ānquándài.) — Please fasten your seatbelt after boarding.

## Notes

Pronounced jì when meaning to tie; xì for system or department.
