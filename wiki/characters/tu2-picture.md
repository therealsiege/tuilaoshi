# 图 Picture

- **Character**: 图
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: picture, drawing, map, diagram
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 地图 (dì tú) — map
- 图书馆 (tú shū guǎn) — library

## Example Sentences

- 你有地图吗？(Nǐ yǒu dì tú ma?) — Do you have a map?

## Notes

图 encompasses all visual representations. 图书馆 (library) literally means 'picture-book-house'.
