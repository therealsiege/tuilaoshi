# 道 Way

- **Character**: 道
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: way, path, Taoism
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 道路 (dào lù) — road
- 道情 (dào qíng) — Taoist ballad

## Example Sentences

- 道情是道教的说唱形式。(Dào qíng shì dào jiào de shuō chàng xíng shì.) — Daoqing is a Taoist narrative singing form.

## Notes

道 means way or path; 道情 is religious ballad singing.
