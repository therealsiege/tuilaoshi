# 碎 Broken

- **Character**: 碎
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: to break into pieces, broken
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 粉碎 (fěn suì) — to smash to pieces
- 破碎 (pò suì) — broken, fragmentary

## Example Sentences

- 玻璃杯摔碎了。(Bō li bēi shuāi suì le.) — The glass cup shattered.

## Notes

碎 uses stone radical 石, indicating something hard breaking into fragments.
