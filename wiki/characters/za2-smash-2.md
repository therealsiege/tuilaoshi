# 砸 To Smash

- **Character**: 砸
- **Pinyin**: zá
- **Meaning**: to smash, to pound
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 砸碎 (zá suì) — to smash to pieces
- 砸开 (zá kāi) — to break open

## Example Sentences

- 小心别砸到手。(Xiǎo xīn bié zá dào shǒu.) — Be careful not to smash your hand.

## Notes

砸 combines stone 石 and 匝 (to encircle), representing striking forcefully with stone.
