# 晕 Dizzy

- **Character**: 晕
- **Pinyin**: yūn
- **Meaning**: dizzy, to faint, halo
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 头晕 (tóu yūn) — dizzy
- 晕倒 (yūn dǎo) — to faint

## Example Sentences

- 他感到头晕。(Tā gǎn dào tóu yūn.) — He feels dizzy.

## Notes

晕 simplified from 暈. Sun 日 with 军 (army), halo around sun, dizziness.
