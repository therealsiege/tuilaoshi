# 刚 Hard, firm, strong

- **Character**: 刚
- **Pinyin**: gāng
- **Meaning**: hard, firm, strong
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刚强 (gāng qiáng) — firm and strong
- 刚正 (gāng zhèng) — upright and outspoken

## Example Sentences

- 他性格刚强，从不向困难低头。(Tā xìnggé gāngqiáng, cóng bù xiàng kùnnan dītóu.) — He has a strong character and never yields to difficulties.

## Notes

Represents strength and firmness, both physical and moral. Often contrasted with 柔 (soft, gentle).
