# 遍 Time (Occurrence)

- **Character**: 遍
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: time (occurrence), all over
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 一遍 (yī biàn) — one time through
- 普遍 (pǔ biàn) — universal

## Example Sentences

- 请再说一遍。(Qǐng zài shuō yī biàn.) — Please say it one more time.

## Notes

遍 counts complete run-throughs: 读一遍 (read through once). Different from 次 which counts occurrences.
