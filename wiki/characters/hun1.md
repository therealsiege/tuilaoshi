# 昏 Dusk

- **Character**: 昏
- **Pinyin**: hūn
- **Meaning**: dusk, dim, confused
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 昏暗 (hūn àn) — dim
- 黄昏 (huáng hūn) — dusk

## Example Sentences

- 黄昏时分天色渐暗。(Huáng hūn shí fēn tiān sè jiàn àn.) — The sky gradually darkens at dusk.

## Notes

昏 has sun 日 with 氏 (clan), sun low and dim.
