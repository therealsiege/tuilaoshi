# 审 To Examine

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: to examine, to review, to try
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěn chá) — to examine
- 审理 (shěn lǐ) — to try (a case)

## Example Sentences

- 法院正在审理这个案件。(Fǎ yuàn zhèng zài shěn lǐ zhè gè àn jiàn.) — The court is trying this case.

## Notes

审 simplified from 審. Shows roof 宀 over careful examination, representing careful indoor deliberation.
