# 偏 Bias, deviate

- **Character**: 偏
- **Pinyin**: piān
- **Meaning**: bias, deviate
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偏差 (piān chā) — deviation, bias
- 偏见 (piān jiàn) — bias, prejudice

## Example Sentences

- 模型有偏差。(Mó xíng yǒu piān chā.) — The model has bias.

## Notes

Used in 偏差 (bias), 偏置 (bias term), machine learning terminology
