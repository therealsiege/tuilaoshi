# 悟 To Comprehend

- **Character**: 悟
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: to awaken, to comprehend
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 领悟 (lǐng wù) — to comprehend
- 觉悟 (jué wù) — enlightenment

## Example Sentences

- 他终于领悟了真谛。(Tā zhōng yú lǐng wù le zhēn dì.) — He finally comprehended the truth.

## Notes

悟 has heart 忄 with 吾 (I), my heart understanding.
