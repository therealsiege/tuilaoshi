# 蟋 Cricket

- **Character**: 蟋
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: cricket
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 蟋蟀 (xī shuài) — cricket
- 斗蟋蟀 (dòu xī shuài) — cricket fighting

## Example Sentences

- 秋天可以听到蟋蟀叫。(Qiū tiān kě yǐ tīng dào xī shuài jiào.) — You can hear crickets chirping in autumn.

## Notes

蟋 has insect 虫 with 悉 (to know), the familiar chirping insect.
