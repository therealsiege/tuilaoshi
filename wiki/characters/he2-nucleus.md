# 核 Nucleus, core, kernel

- **Character**: 核
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: nucleus, core, kernel
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 核心 (hé xīn) — core, nucleus
- 原子核 (yuán zǐ hé) — atomic nucleus

## Example Sentences

- 这是问题的核心所在。(Zhè shì wèn tí de hé xīn suǒ zài.) — This is the core of the problem.

## Notes

Used in nuclear physics and core concepts
