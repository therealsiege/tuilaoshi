# 惜 Cherish

- **Character**: 惜
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: cherish, pity
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 可惜 (kěxī) — it's a pity
- 珍惜 (zhēnxī) — cherish

## Example Sentences

- 我们要珍惜时间。(Wǒmen yào zhēnxī shíjiān.) — We should cherish time.

## Notes

Positive emotion. Heart radical. 可惜 and 珍惜 are very common. Important life philosophy vocabulary.
