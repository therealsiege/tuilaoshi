# 到 To Arrive/To

- **Character**: 到
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to arrive, to reach; to (a place/time)
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 到达 (dàodá) — to arrive, to reach
- 到处 (dàochù) — everywhere
- 得到 (dédào) — to obtain, to get
- 看到 (kàndào) — to see
- 找到 (zhǎodào) — to find
- 迟到 (chídào) — to arrive late

## Example Sentences

- 我到了。(Wǒ dào le.) — I've arrived.
- 从北京到上海要多长时间？(Cóng Běijīng dào Shànghǎi yào duō cháng shíjiān?) — How long does it take from Beijing to Shanghai?
- 他每天迟到。(Tā měitiān chídào.) — He is late every day.

## Notes

到 combines 至 (zhì, to arrive) on the left with the knife radical 刂 on the right. As a main verb it means "to arrive." As a result complement after other verbs, it indicates successful completion of the action: 看到 (managed to see), 找到 (managed to find), 听到 (managed to hear). As a preposition it marks endpoints in space or time, often paired with 从 (cóng, from) in the pattern 从...到... ("from...to...").
