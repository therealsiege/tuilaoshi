# 锄 Hoe, to hoe, to weed

- **Character**: 锄
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: hoe, to hoe, to weed
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锄头 (chú tou) — hoe
- 锄草 (chú cǎo) — to hoe, to weed

## Example Sentences

- 他拿着锄头去锄草。(Tā názhe chútou qù chú cǎo.) — He took a hoe to go weeding.

## Notes

Both a tool (hoe) and the action of using it.
