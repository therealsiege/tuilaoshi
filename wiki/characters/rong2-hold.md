# 容 To Hold

- **Character**: 容
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: to hold, appearance, to allow
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 容易 (róng yì) — easy
- 笑容 (xiào róng) — smiling expression

## Example Sentences

- 这个容易学。(Zhè ge róng yì xué.) — This is easy to learn.

## Notes

容 shows house 宀 holding valley 谷. 容易 (easy) opposite of 难 (difficult).
