# 角 Horn, angle, corner

- **Character**: 角
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: horn, angle, corner
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 角度 (jiǎo dù) — angle, perspective
- 角膜 (jiǎo mó) — cornea

## Example Sentences

- 从不同角度看问题。(Cóng bùtóng jiǎodù kàn wèntí.) — Look at the problem from different angles.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: jiǎo (angle/horn), jué (role/actor).
