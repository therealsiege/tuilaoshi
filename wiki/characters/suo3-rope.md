# 索 Rope

- **Character**: 索
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: rope, to search
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 索道 (suǒ dào) — cable car, ropeway
- 钢索 (gāng suǒ) — steel cable

## Example Sentences

- 我们坐索道上山。(Wǒmen zuò suǒdào shàng shān.) — We take the cable car up the mountain.

## Notes

Rope and cable; cable cars and climbing ropes
