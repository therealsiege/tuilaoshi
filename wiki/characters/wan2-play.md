# 玩 To Play

- **Character**: 玩
- **Pinyin**: wán
- **Meaning**: to play, to have fun
- **Radical**: 王 (king/jade)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 好玩 (hǎo wán) — fun, interesting
- 玩具 (wán jù) — toy

## Example Sentences

- 我们出去玩吧。(Wǒ men chū qù wán ba.) — Let's go out and have fun.

## Notes

玩 uses the jade radical 王 because playing with jade objects was an ancient pastime. 好玩 means 'fun.'
