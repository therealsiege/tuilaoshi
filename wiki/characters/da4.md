# 大 Big

- **Character**: 大
- **Pinyin**: dà
- **Meaning**: big, large, great
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 大学 (dà xué) — university
- 大家 (dà jiā) — everyone
- 大人 (dà rén) — adult
- 很大 (hěn dà) — very big

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间很大。(Zhè ge fángjiān hěn dà.) — This room is very big.
- 他是大学生。(Tā shì dàxuéshēng.) — He is a university student.

## Notes

大 is a pictograph of a person standing with arms and legs spread wide, representing the concept of "big."
