# 锤 Hammer

- **Character**: 锤
- **Pinyin**: chúi
- **Meaning**: hammer
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锤子 (chúi zi) — hammer
- 铁锤 (tiě chúi) — iron hammer

## Example Sentences

- 铁匠挥舞着大锤打铁。(Tiějiàng huīwǔ zhe dà chúi dǎ tiě.) — The blacksmith swung the sledgehammer to strike the iron.

## Notes

锤 refers to a hammer, an essential tool in metalworking. Can also be a verb meaning to hammer.
