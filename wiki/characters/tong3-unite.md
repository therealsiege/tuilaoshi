# 统 System

- **Character**: 统
- **Pinyin**: tǒng
- **Meaning**: to unite, system, lineage
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 系统 (xì tǒng) — system
- 传统 (chuán tǒng) — tradition

## Example Sentences

- 这个系统很复杂。(Zhè ge xì tǒng hěn fù zá.) — This system is very complex.

## Notes

统 is simplified from 統. The silk radical suggests continuous threads or lineage.
