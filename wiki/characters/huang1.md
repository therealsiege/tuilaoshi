# 慌 Flustered

- **Character**: 慌
- **Pinyin**: huāng
- **Meaning**: flustered, confused, panicked
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 慌张 (huāng zhāng) — flustered, hurried
- 惊慌 (jīng huāng) — panic

## Example Sentences

- 别慌张，慢慢来。(Bié huāng zhāng, màn màn lái.) — Don't panic, take it slow.

## Notes

慌 shows heart 忄 and 荒 (waste/wild), representing心 in a confused or panicked state.
