# 谕 Instruct, edict

- **Character**: 谕
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: instruct, edict
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 谕旨 (yùzhǐ) — imperial instruction
- 晓谕 (xiǎoyù) — instruct, make known

## Example Sentences

- 官府晓谕百姓遵守新规。(Guānfǔ xiǎoyù bǎixìng zūnshǒu xīn guī.) — The authorities instructed the people to follow the new regulations.

## Notes

Formal term for official instructions or pronouncements.
