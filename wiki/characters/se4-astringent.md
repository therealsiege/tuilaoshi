# 涩 Astringent, rough, difficult

- **Character**: 涩
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: astringent, rough, difficult
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 苦涩 (kǔ sè) — bitter, anguished
- 晦涩 (huì sè) — obscure, abstruse

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章很晦涩难懂。(Zhè piān wén zhāng hěn huì sè nán dǒng.) — This article is very obscure and hard to understand.

## Notes

Rough texture or difficult comprehension
