# 约 约, 约约, 约约约

- **Character**: 约
- **Pinyin**: yuē
- **Meaning**: 约,约约,约约约
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 约会 (yuē huì) — appointment, date
- 大约 (dà yuē) — approximately

## Example Sentences

- 我们约好明天见面。(Wǒ men yuē hǎo míng tiān jiàn miàn.) — We agreed to meet tomorrow.
