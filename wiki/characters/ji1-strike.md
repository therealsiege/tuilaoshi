# 击 To Strike

- **Character**: 击
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to strike, to hit, to attack
- **Radical**: 凵 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 打击 (dǎ jī) — to strike, to hit
- 攻击 (gōng jī) — to attack

## Example Sentences

- 他受到了沉重的打击。(Tā shòu dào le chén zhòng de dǎ jī.) — He suffered a heavy blow.

## Notes

击 simplified from 擊. Represents striking or hitting forcefully.
