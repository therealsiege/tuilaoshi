# 查 查看, examine, investigate

- **Character**: 查
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: 查看, examine, investigate
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — inspect, examine
- 查账 (chá zhàng) — audit accounts

## Example Sentences

- 审计师来查账了。(Shěn jì shī lái chá zhàng le.) — The auditor came to audit the accounts.

## Notes

调查 means investigate. 查询 means inquire/query.
