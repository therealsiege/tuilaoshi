# 标 Mark

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, standard, to mark
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 标准 (biāo zhǔn) — standard
- 目标 (mù biāo) — goal, target

## Example Sentences

- 我们的目标是什么？(Wǒ men de mù biāo shì shén me?) — What is our goal?

## Notes

标 originally meant tip of a tree, representing something prominent or marking/标anding out. Used for marks and standards.
