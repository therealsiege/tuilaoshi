# 让 To allow, to let, to yield, to give way

- **Character**: 让
- **Pinyin**: ràng
- **Meaning**: to allow, to let, to yield, to give way
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 让步 (ràng bù) — to concede, to give in
- 谦让 (qiān ràng) — to modestly decline, to be humble

## Example Sentences

- 请让我来帮你。(Qǐng ràng wǒ lái bāng nǐ.) — Please let me help you.

## Notes

Very common verb for allowing or yielding to others.
