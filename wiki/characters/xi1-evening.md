# 汐 Evening tide, ebb tide

- **Character**: 汐
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: evening tide, ebb tide
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 潮汐 (cháo xī) — tide
- 朝汐 (cháo xī) — morning and evening tides

## Example Sentences

- 潮汐现象影响海洋生态。(Cháo xī xiàn xiàng yǐng xiǎng hǎi yáng shēng tài.) — Tidal phenomena affect marine ecology.

## Notes

Evening or ebb tide
