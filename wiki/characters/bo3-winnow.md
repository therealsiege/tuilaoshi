# 簸 To Winnow

- **Character**: 簸
- **Pinyin**: bǒ
- **Meaning**: to winnow, to toss
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 颠簸 (diān bǒ) — bumpy
- 簸箕 (bò ji) — dustpan

## Example Sentences

- 路很颠簸。(Lù hěn diān bǒ.) — The road is very bumpy.

## Notes

簸 combines bamboo 竹 radical, originally referring to winnowing grain with bamboo basket.
