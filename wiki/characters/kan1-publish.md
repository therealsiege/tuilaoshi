# 刊 Publish, periodical

- **Character**: 刊
- **Pinyin**: kān
- **Meaning**: publish, periodical
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 刊登 (kān dēng) — publish, carry
- 期刊 (qī kān) — periodical, journal

## Example Sentences

- 报纸刊登了广告。(Bào zhǐ kān dēng le guǎng gào.) — The newspaper published the advertisement.

## Notes

刊物 means publication. 月刊 is monthly magazine.
