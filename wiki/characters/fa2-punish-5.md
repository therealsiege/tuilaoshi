# 罚 Punish, fine

- **Character**: 罚
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: punish, fine
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 罚款 (fá kuǎn) — fine, penalty
- 处罚 (chǔ fá) — punish, penalize

## Example Sentences

- 违约要支付罚款。(Wéi yuē yào zhī fù fá kuǎn.) — Breach of contract requires paying a fine.

## Notes

罚金 is monetary penalty. 罚息 is penalty interest.
