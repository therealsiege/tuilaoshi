# 亿 Hundred Million, 亿万 Millions Upon Millions

- **Character**: 亿
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: hundred million, 亿万 millions upon millions
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 亿万 (yì wàn) — millions upon millions
- 数亿 (shù yì) — hundreds of millions
- 几亿 (jǐ yì) — several hundred million

## Example Sentences

- 中国有十几亿人口。(Zhōngguó yǒu shí jǐ yì rénkǒu.) — China has over a billion people.

## Notes

Essential large number unit (100,000,000). Critical for understanding Chinese population, economic, and statistical data.
