# 登 Publish, ascend

- **Character**: 登
- **Pinyin**: dēng
- **Meaning**: publish, ascend
- **Radical**: 癶 (footsteps)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 登记 (dēng jì) — register, check in
- 刊登 (kān dēng) — publish

## Example Sentences

- 广告登在头版。(Guǎng gào dēng zài tóu bǎn.) — The ad was published on the front page.

## Notes

登录 means log in. 登陆 means land/launch.
