# 币 Currency, coin

- **Character**: 币
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: currency, coin
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 货币 (huò bì) — currency, money
- 人民币 (rén mín bì) — RMB, Chinese yuan

## Example Sentences

- 请用人民币支付。(Qǐng yòng rén mín bì zhī fù.) — Please pay in RMB.

## Notes

外币 is foreign currency. 硬币 is coin.
