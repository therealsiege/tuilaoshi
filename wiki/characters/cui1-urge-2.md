# 催 Urge, hasten

- **Character**: 催
- **Pinyin**: cuī
- **Meaning**: urge, hasten
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 催促 (cuī cù) — urge, press
- 催款 (cuī kuǎn) — press for payment

## Example Sentences

- 银行开始催款了。(Yín háng kāi shǐ cuī kuǎn le.) — The bank started pressing for payment.

## Notes

催收 means debt collection. 催债 means press for debt repayment.
