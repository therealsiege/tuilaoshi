# 阐 Explain, expound

- **Character**: 阐
- **Pinyin**: chǎn
- **Meaning**: explain, expound
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 阐述 (chǎnshù) — elaborate, expound
- 阐明 (chǎnmíng) — clarify, elucidate

## Example Sentences

- 教授详细阐述了他的理论。(Jiàoshòu xiángxì chǎnshù le tā de lǐlùn.) — The professor elaborated on his theory in detail.

## Notes

Formal term for systematic explanation or exposition.
