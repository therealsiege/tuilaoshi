# 菌 Fungus

- **Character**: 菌
- **Pinyin**: jūn
- **Meaning**: mushroom, fungus, bacteria
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 细菌 (xì jūn) — bacteria
- 蘑菇 (mó gu) — mushroom

## Example Sentences

- 这种菌可以食用。(Zhè zhǒng jūn kě yǐ shí yòng.) — This type of fungus is edible.

## Notes

菌 has grass 艹 with 囷 (round granary), fungal organisms.
