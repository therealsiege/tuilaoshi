# 车 Car

- **Character**: 车
- **Pinyin**: chē
- **Meaning**: vehicle, car, wheel
- **Radical**: 车 (car)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 汽车 (qì chē) — car
- 火车 (huǒ chē) — train

## Example Sentences

- 我坐车去学校。(Wǒ zuò chē qù xué xiào.) — I take the car to school.

## Notes

车 means vehicle. 自行车 = bicycle. 出租车 = taxi.
