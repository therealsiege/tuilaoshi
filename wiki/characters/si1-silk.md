# 丝 Silk

- **Character**: 丝
- **Pinyin**: sī
- **Meaning**: silk, thread, trace
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 丝绸 (sī chóu) — silk
- 一丝 (yī sī) — a trace of

## Example Sentences

- 丝绸之路很有名。(Sī chóu zhī lù hěn yǒu míng.) — The Silk Road is very famous.

## Notes

丝 means silk or thread. Radical for silk-related characters (纟). Used in丝绸 (silk fabric).
