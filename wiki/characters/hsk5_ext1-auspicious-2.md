# 瑞 Auspicious, 祥瑞 Auspicious Sign

- **Character**: 瑞
- **Pinyin**: ruì
- **Meaning**: auspicious, 祥瑞 auspicious sign
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 祥瑞 (xiáng ruì) — auspicious sign
- 瑞雪 (ruì xuě) — timely snow
- 瑞士 (Ruì shì) — Switzerland

## Example Sentences

- 瑞雪兆丰年。(Ruì xuě zhào fēng nián.) — Timely snow indicates a prosperous year.

## Notes

Auspicious and fortunate. 瑞雪兆丰年 is traditional saying. Also in 瑞士 (Switzerland) - transliteration.
