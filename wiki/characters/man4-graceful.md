# 曼 Graceful, prolonged

- **Character**: 曼
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: graceful, prolonged
- **Radical**: 曰 (say)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 曼妙 (màn miào) — graceful, wonderful
- 曼延 (màn yán) — extend, spread

## Example Sentences

- 她的舞姿曼妙动人。(Tā de wǔ zī màn miào dòng rén.) — Her dancing posture is gracefully enchanting.

## Notes

Often used to describe graceful movements or long extension.
