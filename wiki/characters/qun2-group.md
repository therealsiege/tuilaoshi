# 群 Group, flock, herd

- **Character**: 群
- **Pinyin**: qún
- **Meaning**: group, flock, herd
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 群体 (qún tǐ) — group, colony
- 兽群 (shòu qún) — herd of animals

## Example Sentences
