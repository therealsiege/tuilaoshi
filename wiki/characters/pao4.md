# 泡 Bubble

- **Character**: 泡
- **Pinyin**: pào
- **Meaning**: bubble, to soak
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 浸泡 (jìn pào) — to soak
- 气泡 (qì pào) — bubble

## Example Sentences

- 茶叶要泡五分钟。(Chá yè yào pào wǔ fēn zhōng.) — The tea leaves need to steep for five minutes.

## Notes

泡 as pào means bubble or to soak. As pāo it means something puffy or swollen.
