# 豆 Bean, pea

- **Character**: 豆
- **Pinyin**: dòu
- **Meaning**: bean, pea
- **Radical**: 豆 (bean)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 豆腐 (dòu fu) — tofu
- 黄豆 (huáng dòu) — soybean

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢吃红豆汤。(Wǒ xǐhuan chī hóngdòu tāng.) — I like to eat red bean soup.

## Notes

This character is itself a radical. Beans are central to Chinese cuisine.
