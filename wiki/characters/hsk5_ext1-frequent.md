# 频 Frequent, 频繁 Frequent

- **Character**: 频
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: frequent, 频繁 frequent
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 频繁 (pín fán) — frequent
- 频率 (pín lǜ) — frequency
- 频道 (pín dào) — channel (TV)

## Example Sentences

- 他频繁出差。(Tā pínfán chūchāi.) — He frequently goes on business trips.

## Notes

Key term for frequency in both everyday (频繁 frequent) and technical (频率 frequency) contexts. 频道 means TV/radio channel.
