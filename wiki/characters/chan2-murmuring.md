# 潺 Murmuring of flowing water

- **Character**: 潺
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: murmuring of flowing water
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潺潺 (chán chán) — murmuring
- 流水潺潺 (liú shuǐ chán chán) — murmuring water

## Example Sentences

- 溪水潺潺，鸟语花香。(Xī shuǐ chán chán, niǎo yǔ huā xiāng.) — Streams murmur, birds sing and flowers bloom.

## Notes

Gentle sound of flowing water
