# 钱 Money

- **Character**: 钱
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: money
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钱包 (qián bāo) — wallet
- 赚钱 (zhuàn qián) — make money

## Example Sentences

- 做生意就是为了赚钱。(Zuò shēng yì jiù shì wèi le zhuàn qián.) — Doing business is to make money.

## Notes

Most common word for money. 价钱 means price.
