# 僵 Stiff, rigid, deadlocked

- **Character**: 僵
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: stiff, rigid, deadlocked
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 僵硬 (jiāng yìng) — stiff, rigid
- 僵局 (jiāng jú) — deadlock, impasse

## Example Sentences

- 谈判陷入了僵局。(Tán pàn xiàn rù le jiāng jú.) — The negotiations reached a deadlock.

## Notes

Indicates stiffness or deadlock. 僵尸 means zombie. 僵持 means to be in a stalemate.
