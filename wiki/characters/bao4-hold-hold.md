# 抱 To Hold

- **Character**: 抱
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: to hold, to hug, to embrace
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拥抱 (yōng bào) — to embrace
- 抱歉 (bào qiàn) — sorry

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈抱着孩子。(Māmā bào zhe háizi.) — Mom is holding the child.

## Notes

抱 means to hold in arms. 抱歉 is a formal way to say 'sorry.'
