# 赐 Bestow, grant

- **Character**: 赐
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: bestow, grant
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赏赐 (shǎngcì) — bestow, grant as reward
- 赐予 (cìyǔ) — bestow, grant

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝赏赐功臣。(Huángdì shǎngcì gōngchén.) — The emperor rewarded his meritorious officials.

## Notes

Implies giving from superior to inferior, often used in classical contexts.
