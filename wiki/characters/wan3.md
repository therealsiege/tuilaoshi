# 晚 Evening

- **Character**: 晚
- **Pinyin**: wǎn
- **Meaning**: evening, late
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 晚上 (wǎn shàng) — evening
- 晚饭 (wǎn fàn) — dinner

## Example Sentences

- 晚安！(Wǎn ān!) — Good night!

## Notes

晚 uses 日 (sun) suggesting the end of the day. 太晚了 means 'it's too late.' Opposite of 早.
