# 剪 Scissors, to cut, to clip

- **Character**: 剪
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: scissors, to cut, to clip
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 剪刀 (jiǎn dāo) — scissors
- 剪纸 (jiǎn zhǐ) — paper cutting

## Example Sentences

- 请用剪刀剪这张纸。(Qǐng yòng jiǎndāo jiǎn zhè zhāng zhǐ.) — Please cut this paper with scissors.

## Notes

Used for cutting with scissors or trimming.
