# 卡 Card, stuck

- **Character**: 卡
- **Pinyin**: kǎ
- **Meaning**: card, stuck
- **Radical**: 卜 (divination)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 显卡 (xiǎn kǎ) — graphics card
- 网卡 (wǎng kǎ) — network card

## Example Sentences

- 我的显卡很强大。(Wǒ de xiǎn kǎ hěn qiáng dà.) — My graphics card is very powerful.

## Notes

Used in 显卡 (graphics card), 网卡 (network card), also means 'stuck/lag'
