# 代 Generation

- **Character**: 代
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: to replace, generation, era, dynasty
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 代替 (dài tì) — to replace
- 时代 (shí dài) — era, age

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个新的时代。(Zhè shì yī ge xīn de shí dài.) — This is a new era.

## Notes

代 means generation or to replace. Used in 现代 (modern times), 古代 (ancient times), 代表 (to represent).
