# 棍 Stick

- **Character**: 棍
- **Pinyin**: gùn
- **Meaning**: stick, club, staff
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 木棍 (mù gùn) — wooden stick
- 棍棒 (gùn bàng) — club, cudgel

## Example Sentences

- 武术表演中他耍棍很精彩。(Wǔshù biǎoyǎn zhōng tā shuǎ gùn hěn jīngcǎi.) — His staff performance in the martial arts show was spectacular.

## Notes

Staff and stick weapons in martial arts
