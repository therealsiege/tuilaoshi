# 溶 Dissolve

- **Character**: 溶
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: dissolve
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 溶解 (róng jiě) — to dissolve
- 溶化 (róng huà) — to dissolve, melt
- 溶液 (róng yè) — solution

## Example Sentences

- 将药片溶解在水中服用。(Jiāng yào piàn róng jiě zài shuǐ zhōng fú yòng.) — Dissolve the tablet in water before taking.

## Notes

Dissolution process; preparation method.
