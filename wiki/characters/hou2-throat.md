# 喉 Throat

- **Character**: 喉
- **Pinyin**: hóu
- **Meaning**: throat, larynx
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喉咙 (hóu long) — throat
- 喉炎 (hóu yán) — laryngitis
- 咽喉 (yān hóu) — pharynx and larynx

## Example Sentences

- 我喉咙痛，说不出话。(Wǒ hóu long tòng, shuō bu chū huà.) — My throat hurts, I can't speak.

## Notes

Contains vocal cords; common in respiratory illness descriptions.
