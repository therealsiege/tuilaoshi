# 眯 To squint, to narrow eyes

- **Character**: 眯
- **Pinyin**: mī
- **Meaning**: to squint, to narrow eyes
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 眯眼 (mī yǎn) — to squint
- 眯着眼 (mī zhe yǎn) — with eyes squinted

## Example Sentences

- 太阳太刺眼，他眯起了眼睛。(Tàiyáng tài cìyǎn, tā mī qǐ le yǎnjing.) — The sun was too dazzling, so he squinted.

## Notes

Describes narrowing or closing eyes partially.
