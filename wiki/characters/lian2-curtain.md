# 帘 Curtain, screen

- **Character**: 帘
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: curtain, screen
- **Radical**: 巾(towel)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 窗帘 (chuāng lián) — curtain, drapes
- 门帘 (mén lián) — door curtain

## Example Sentences

- 请拉上窗帘。(Qǐng lā shang chuānglián.) — Please close the curtains.

## Notes

Refers to fabric curtains or screens.
