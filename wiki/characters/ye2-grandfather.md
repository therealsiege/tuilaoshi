# 爷 Grandfather

- **Character**: 爷
- **Pinyin**: yé
- **Meaning**: grandfather, old man
- **Radical**: 父 (father)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 爷爷 (yé ye) — grandfather (paternal)
- 老爷 (lǎo yé) — lord, master

## Example Sentences

- 爷爷今年八十岁。(Yé ye jīn nián bā shí suì.) — Grandpa is eighty years old this year.

## Notes

爷 specifically paternal grandfather. Reduplicates to 爷爷. Can address elderly men.
