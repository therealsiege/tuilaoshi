# 操 Exercise

- **Character**: 操
- **Pinyin**: cāo
- **Meaning**: to drill, exercise
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 体操 (tǐ cāo) — gymnastics
- 做操 (zuò cāo) — to do exercises

## Example Sentences

- 学生们每天做早操。(Xuéshēngmen měi tiān zuò zǎocāo.) — Students do morning exercises every day.

## Notes

Exercises and drills; calisthenics and gymnastics
