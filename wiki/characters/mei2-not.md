# 没 Not Have

- **Character**: 没
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: not, have not
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 没有 (méi yǒu) — don't have, not yet
- 没关系 (méi guān xì) — it doesn't matter

## Example Sentences

- 我没去过中国。(Wǒ méi qù guò zhōng guó.) — I haven't been to China.

## Notes

没 negates 有 and past actions. Use 不 for present habits and future intentions, 没 for past/completion.
