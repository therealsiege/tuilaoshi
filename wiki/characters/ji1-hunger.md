# 饥 Hunger

- **Character**: 饥
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: hunger, famine
- **Radical**: 饣 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 画饼充饥 (huà bǐng chōng jī) — feed on illusions
- 饥饿 (jī è) — hunger, starvation

## Example Sentences

- 饥饿是最好的调味品。(Jī è shì zuì hǎo de tiáowèipǐn.) — Hunger is the best seasoning.

## Notes

Used in 画饼充饥: the hunger that a painted cake cannot satisfy.
