# 黍 Millet, sorghum

- **Character**: 黍
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: millet, sorghum
- **Radical**: 黍 (millet)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 黍稷 (shǔ jì) — millet and sorghum
- 黄黍 (huáng shǔ) — yellow millet

## Example Sentences

- 古代以黍稷为主要粮食。(Gǔ dài yǐ shǔ jì wéi zhǔ yào liáng shi.) — In ancient times, millet and sorghum were main food grains.

## Notes

Important grain in ancient Chinese agriculture
