# 再 Again

- **Character**: 再
- **Pinyin**: zài
- **Meaning**: again, once more
- **Radical**: 冂 (wide)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 再见 (zài jiàn) — goodbye
- 再说 (zài shuō) — to discuss later

## Example Sentences

- 请再说一遍。(Qǐng zài shuō yī biàn.) — Please say it again.

## Notes

再 refers to future repetition ('will do again'), while 又 refers to past repetition ('did again').
