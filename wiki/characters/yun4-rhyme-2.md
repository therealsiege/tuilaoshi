# 韵 Rhyme, charm, 韻 (traditional)

- **Character**: 韵
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: rhyme, charm, 韻 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 音 (sound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 韵律 (yùn lǜ) — rhythm, cadence
- 押韵 (yā yùn) — to rhyme

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗很押韵。(Zhè shǒu shī hěn yāyùn.) — This poem rhymes well.

## Notes

Important in poetry and music for rhyme and rhythmic beauty.
