# 偏 Inclined, partial, deviate

- **Character**: 偏
- **Pinyin**: piān
- **Meaning**: inclined, partial, deviate
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偏见 (piān jiàn) — prejudice, bias
- 偏差 (piān chā) — deviation, error

## Example Sentences

- 我们不应该对任何人有偏见。(Wǒ men bù yīng gāi duì rèn hé rén yǒu piān jiàn.) — We should not have prejudice against anyone.

## Notes

Indicates deviation from center or fairness. 偏偏 means deliberately contrary or just happens to.
