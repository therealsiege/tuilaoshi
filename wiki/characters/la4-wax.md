# 蜡 Wax

- **Character**: 蜡
- **Pinyin**: là
- **Meaning**: wax, candle
- **Radical**: 虫(insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蜡烛 (là zhú) — candle
- 蜂蜡 (fēng là) — beeswax

## Example Sentences

- 失蜡法是一种古老的铸造技术。(Shīlà fǎ shì yī zhǒng gǔlǎo de zhùzào jìshù.) — The lost-wax method is an ancient casting technique.

## Notes

蜡 means wax. The insect radical refers to beeswax. Used in casting, candle-making, and polishing.
