# 港 Harbor

- **Character**: 港
- **Pinyin**: gǎng
- **Meaning**: harbor, port
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 港口 (gǎng kǒu) — port
- 香港 (Xiāng gǎng) — Hong Kong

## Example Sentences

- 船停在港口。(Chuán tíng zài gǎng kǒu.) — The ship docks at the port.

## Notes

港 has water 氵 with 巷 (alley), waterway into land.
