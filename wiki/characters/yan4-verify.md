# 验 Verify, test

- **Character**: 验
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: verify, test
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 检验 (jiǎn yàn) — test, inspect
- 验证 (yàn zhèng) — verify

## Example Sentences

- 需要验证账目的准确性。(Xū yào yàn zhèng zhàng mù de zhǔn què xìng.) — Need to verify the accuracy of the accounts.

## Notes

体验 means experience. 试验 means experiment/test.
