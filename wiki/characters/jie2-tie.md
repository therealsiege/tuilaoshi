# 结 To Tie

- **Character**: 结
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: to tie, to conclude, result
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 结果 (jié guǒ) — result
- 结束 (jié shù) — to end

## Example Sentences

- 结果怎么样？(Jié guǒ zěn me yàng?) — What was the result?

## Notes

结 as jié: to conclude (结束, 结果). As jiē: to bear fruit (结果子).
