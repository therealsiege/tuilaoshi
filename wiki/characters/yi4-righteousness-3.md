# 义 Righteousness

- **Character**: 义
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: righteousness, justice, meaning
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 见义勇为 (jiàn yì yǒng wéi) — act bravely for justice
- 正义 (zhèng yì) — justice

## Example Sentences

- 见义勇为是值得尊敬的。(Jiàn yì yǒng wéi shì zhíde zūnjìng de.) — Acting bravely for justice is worthy of respect.

## Notes

Core Confucian virtue in 见义勇为: when you see righteousness, act courageously.
