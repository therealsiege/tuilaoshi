# 椒 Pepper, chili

- **Character**: 椒
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: pepper, chili
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 辣椒 (là jiāo) — chili pepper
- 花椒 (huā jiāo) — Sichuan pepper

## Example Sentences

- 这个菜放了很多辣椒。(Zhège cài fàngle hěn duō làjiāo.) — This dish has a lot of chili peppers in it.

## Notes

Essential spice in Chinese cooking. Wood radical from pepper tree origin.
