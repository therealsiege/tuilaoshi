# 栋 Ridge Beam

- **Character**: 栋
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: ridge beam, building (measure word)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栋梁 (dòng liáng) — pillar of state
- 一栋楼 (yì dòng lóu) — a building

## Example Sentences

- 这里有三栋大楼。(Zhè lǐ yǒu sān dòng dà lóu.) — There are three large buildings here.

## Notes

栋 simplified from 棟. Wood 木 with 东 (east), main roof beam.
