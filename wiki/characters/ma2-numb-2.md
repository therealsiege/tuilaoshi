# 麻 Numb

- **Character**: 麻
- **Pinyin**: má
- **Meaning**: numb, tingling
- **Radical**: 麻 (hemp)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 麻木 (má mù) — numb, insensitive
- 麻醉 (má zuì) — anesthesia
- 麻痹 (má bì) — paralysis, numbness

## Example Sentences

- 我腿麻了。(Wǒ tuǐ má le.) — My leg is numb.

## Notes

Indicates loss of sensation; can signal nerve issues.
