# 绑 To tie, to bind

- **Character**: 绑
- **Pinyin**: bǎng
- **Meaning**: to tie, to bind
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绑架 (bǎng jià) — to kidnap
- 捆绑 (kǔn bǎng) — to tie up, to bind

## Example Sentences

- 他们把犯人的手绑在背后。(Tāmen bǎ fànrén de shǒu bǎng zài bèihòu.) — They tied the criminal's hands behind his back.

## Notes

Often implies forceful or restrictive binding.
