# 见 To See

- **Character**: 见
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: to see, to meet
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 看见 (kàn jiàn) — to see
- 再见 (zài jiàn) — goodbye

## Example Sentences

- 再见！(Zài jiàn!) — Goodbye!

## Notes

见 is simplified from 見. As a result complement it means the action achieved perception: 看见 (saw), 听见 (heard).
