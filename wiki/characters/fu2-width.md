# 幅 Width, measure word

- **Character**: 幅
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: width, measure word
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 幅度 (fú dù) — range, extent
- 横幅 (héng fú) — banner

## Example Sentences

- 店门口挂着横幅广告。(Diàn mén kǒu guà zhe héng fú guǎng gào.) — A banner advertisement hangs at the shop entrance.

## Notes

大幅 means large-scale. Measure word for paintings, flags.
