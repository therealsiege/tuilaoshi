# 根 Root, base, stem

- **Character**: 根
- **Pinyin**: gēn
- **Meaning**: root, base, stem
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 根部 (gēn bù) — root, base
- 树根 (shù gēn) — tree root

## Example Sentences

- 根部吸收土壤中的水分和养分。(Gēn bù xī shōu tǔ rǎng zhōng de shuǐ fèn hé yǎng fèn.) — Roots absorb water and nutrients from the soil.
