# 挡 To Block

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, to ward off
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to block
- 挡住 (dǎng zhù) — to block

## Example Sentences

- 他挡住了攻击。(Tā dǎng zhù le gōng jī.) — He blocked the attack.

## Notes

挡 simplified from 擋. Hand 扌 with 当 (to face), blocking with hand.
