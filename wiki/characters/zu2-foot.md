# 足 Foot

- **Character**: 足
- **Pinyin**: zú
- **Meaning**: foot, sufficient
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 足球 (zú qiú) — soccer, football
- 足够 (zú gòu) — enough, sufficient

## Example Sentences

- 足球是世界上最流行的运动。(Zúqiú shì shìjiè shàng zuì liúxíng de yùndòng.) — Soccer is the most popular sport in the world.

## Notes

Primary meaning is foot; used in football/soccer and sufficiency contexts
