# 忍 To endure, to tolerate, to forbear

- **Character**: 忍
- **Pinyin**: rěn
- **Meaning**: to endure, to tolerate, to forbear
- **Radical**: 心(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忍受 (rěn shòu) — to endure, to bear
- 忍耐 (rěn nài) — to exercise patience, to restrain oneself

## Example Sentences

- 他忍受不了这种痛苦。(Tā rěnshòu bù liǎo zhè zhǒng tòngkǔ.) — He cannot endure this kind of pain.

## Notes

Emphasizes enduring hardship or controlling emotions.
