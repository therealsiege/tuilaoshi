# 炒 To stir-fry, to sauté, to speculate

- **Character**: 炒
- **Pinyin**: chǎo
- **Meaning**: to stir-fry, to sauté, to speculate
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 炒菜 (chǎo cài) — to stir-fry vegetables
- 炒饭 (chǎo fàn) — fried rice

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈在厨房炒菜。(Māma zài chúfáng chǎo cài.) — Mom is stir-frying vegetables in the kitchen.

## Notes

Most common Chinese cooking method; also means to speculate financially.
