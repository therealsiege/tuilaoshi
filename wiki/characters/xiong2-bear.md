# 熊 Bear

- **Character**: 熊
- **Pinyin**: xióng
- **Meaning**: bear
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熊猫 (xióng māo) — panda
- 北极熊 (běi jí xióng) — polar bear

## Example Sentences

- 熊猫是中国的国宝。(Xióng māo shì zhōng guó de guó bǎo.) — The panda is China's national treasure.

## Notes

熊 shows a bear shape with fire 灬 below, representing the powerful animal.
