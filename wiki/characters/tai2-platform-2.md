# 台 Platform

- **Character**: 台
- **Pinyin**: tái
- **Meaning**: platform, stage, terrace
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 舞台 (wǔ tái) — stage
- 阳台 (yáng tái) — balcony

## Example Sentences

- 她站在舞台上唱歌。(Tā zhàn zài wǔ tái shàng chàng gē.) — She stands on the stage singing.

## Notes

台 simplified from 臺. Originally depicted a raised platform.
