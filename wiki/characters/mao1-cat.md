# 猫 Cat

- **Character**: 猫
- **Pinyin**: māo
- **Meaning**: cat
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 小猫 (xiǎo māo) — kitten
- 猫咪 (māo mī) — kitty

## Example Sentences

- 我养了一只猫。(Wǒ yǎng le yī zhī māo.) — I have a cat.

## Notes

猫 uses dog/animal radical 犭. The phonetic 苗 gives the sound māo (onomatopoeia).
