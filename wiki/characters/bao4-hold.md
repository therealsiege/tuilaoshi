# 抱 To hold, to carry in arms, to hug, to embrace

- **Character**: 抱
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: to hold, to carry in arms, to hug, to embrace
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拥抱 (yōng bào) — to embrace, hug
- 抱歉 (bào qiàn) — sorry, to feel apologetic

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈抱着孩子走进房间。(Māma bàozhe háizi zǒujìn fángjiān.) — The mother carried the child into the room.

## Notes

Used for holding someone or something in one's arms.
