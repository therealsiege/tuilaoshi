# 岔 Fork, branch off, diverge

- **Character**: 岔
- **Pinyin**: chà
- **Meaning**: fork, branch off, diverge
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 岔路 (chà lù) — fork in the road
- 岔子 (chà zi) — mishap, trouble

## Example Sentences

- 前面有个岔路口,我们往左走。(Qián miàn yǒu ge chà lù kǒu, wǒ men wǎng zuǒ zǒu.) — There's a fork ahead, we'll go left.

## Notes

Indicates forking or branching. 出岔子 means something went wrong. 岔开话题 means to digress.
