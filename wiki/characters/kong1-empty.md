# 空 Empty

- **Character**: 空
- **Pinyin**: kōng
- **Meaning**: empty, sky, air; free time (kòng)
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 天空 (tiān kōng) — sky
- 有空 (yǒu kòng) — to have free time

## Example Sentences

- 你明天有空吗？(Nǐ míng tiān yǒu kòng ma?) — Are you free tomorrow?

## Notes

空 as kōng: empty, sky (天空, 空气). As kòng: free time (有空, 空闲).
