# 笃 Sincere, thick, deep

- **Character**: 笃
- **Pinyin**: dǔ
- **Meaning**: sincere, thick, deep
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 笃信 (dǔxìn) — firmly believe
- 深笃 (shēndǔ) — deep, profound

## Example Sentences

- 他笃信佛教。(Tā dǔxìn fójiào.) — He firmly believes in Buddhism.

## Notes

Formal literary term for deep sincerity or firm belief.
