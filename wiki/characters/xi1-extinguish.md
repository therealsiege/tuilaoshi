# 熄 To Extinguish

- **Character**: 熄
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: to extinguish, to go out (of fire)
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熄火 (xī huǒ) — to put out a fire
- 熄灭 (xī miè) — to extinguish

## Example Sentences

- 火熄灭了。(Huǒ xī miè le.) — The fire went out.

## Notes

熄 means to extinguish or go out. Used for putting out fires, turning off engines (熄火).
