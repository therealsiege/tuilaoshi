# 辱 To humiliate, to insult, to disgrace

- **Character**: 辱
- **Pinyin**: rǔ
- **Meaning**: to humiliate, to insult, to disgrace
- **Radical**: 辰(morning)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 侮辱 (wǔ rǔ) — to insult, to humiliate
- 羞辱 (xiū rǔ) — to humiliate, to shame

## Example Sentences

- 这种行为是对他的侮辱。(Zhè zhǒng xíngwéi shì duì tā de wǔrǔ.) — This behavior is an insult to him.

## Notes

Strong negative term for causing shame or disgrace.
