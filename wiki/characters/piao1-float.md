# 飘 To Float

- **Character**: 飘
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: to float, to drift
- **Radical**: 风 (wind)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 飘扬 (piāo yáng) — to flutter
- 飘浮 (piāo fú) — to float

## Example Sentences

- 国旗在风中飘扬。(Guó qí zài fēng zhōng piāo yáng.) — The national flag flutters in the wind.

## Notes

飘 simplified from 飄. Wind radical 风 representing light floating movement.
