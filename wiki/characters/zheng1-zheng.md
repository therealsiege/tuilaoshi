# 筝 Zheng

- **Character**: 筝
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: zheng (Chinese zither)
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 古筝 (gǔ zhēng) — guzheng
- 风筝 (fēng zheng) — kite

## Example Sentences

- 她会弹古筝。(Tā huì tán gǔ zhēng.) — She can play the guzheng.

## Notes

筝 has bamboo 竹 with 争 (to contend), plucked string instrument.
