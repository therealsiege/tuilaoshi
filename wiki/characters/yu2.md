# 鱼 Fish

- **Character**: 鱼
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: fish
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 鱼肉 (yú ròu) — fish meat
- 金鱼 (jīn yú) — goldfish

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢吃鱼。(Wǒ xǐ huān chī yú.) — I like to eat fish.

## Notes

鱼 is a pictograph of a fish. It sounds like 余 (yú, surplus), so fish symbolizes abundance at Chinese New Year.
