# 画 To Draw

- **Character**: 画
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to draw, to paint, painting
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 画画 (huà huà) — to draw
- 绘画 (huì huà) — painting

## Example Sentences

- 他画了一幅山水画。(Tā huà le yì fú shān shuǐ huà.) — He painted a landscape.

## Notes

画 simplified from 畫. Shows a hand holding brush within boundaries, representing painting.
