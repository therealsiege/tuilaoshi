# 段 Segment, section

- **Character**: 段
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: segment, section
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 频段 (pín duàn) — frequency band
- 片段 (piàn duàn) — fragment

## Example Sentences

- 这个频段被占用了。(Zhè gè pín duàn bèi zhàn yòng le.) — This frequency band is occupied.

## Notes

Used in 频段 (frequency band), 片段 (segment), 手段 (means)
