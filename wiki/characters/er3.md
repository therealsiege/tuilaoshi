# 耳 Ear

- **Character**: 耳
- **Pinyin**: ěr
- **Meaning**: ear
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 耳朵 (ěr duo) — ear
- 耳环 (ěr huán) — earring

## Example Sentences

- 我的耳朵疼。(Wǒ de ěr duo téng.) — My ears hurt.

## Notes

耳 is a pictograph of an ear. It's both a character and a radical. In modern speech, usually appears as 耳朵.
