# 宅 Residence

- **Character**: 宅
- **Pinyin**: zhái
- **Meaning**: residence, house
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 住宅 (zhù zhái) — residence
- 宅基地 (zhái jī dì) — homestead

## Example Sentences

- 这是私人住宅。(Zhè shì sī rén zhù zhái.) — This is a private residence.

## Notes

宅 with roof radical for residence, formal term for dwelling places.
