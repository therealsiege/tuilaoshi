# 漆 Paint

- **Character**: 漆
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: paint, lacquer, varnish
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 油漆 (yóu qī) — paint
- 漆黑 (qī hēi) — pitch-black

## Example Sentences

- 墙上刷了新油漆。(Qiáng shàng shuā le xīn yóu qī.) — The wall was painted with new paint.

## Notes

漆 means paint or lacquer. Used in油漆 (paint), 漆黑 (pitch-black), traditional lacquerware.
