# 术 Surgery

- **Character**: 术
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: technique, surgery
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 手术 (shǒu shù) — surgery, operation
- 技术 (jì shù) — technology, technique
- 美术 (měi shù) — fine arts

## Example Sentences

- 他明天要做手术。(Tā míng tiān yào zuò shǒu shù.) — He's having surgery tomorrow.

## Notes

Method or procedure; commonly surgery in medical contexts.
