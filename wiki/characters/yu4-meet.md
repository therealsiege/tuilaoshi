# 遇 To Meet

- **Character**: 遇
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to meet, to encounter
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 遇到 (yù dào) — to encounter
- 相遇 (xiāng yù) — to meet

## Example Sentences

- 我在路上遇到了麻烦。(Wǒ zài lù shàng yù dào le má fán.) — I encountered trouble on the road.

## Notes

遇 combines movement 辶 and 禺 (corner), representing meeting someone while traveling.
