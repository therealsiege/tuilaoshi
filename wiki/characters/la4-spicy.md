# 辣 Spicy

- **Character**: 辣
- **Pinyin**: là
- **Meaning**: spicy, hot (food)
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 辣椒 (là jiāo) — chili pepper
- 麻辣 (má là) — spicy and numbing

## Example Sentences

- 四川菜很辣。(Sì chuān cài hěn là.) — Sichuan food is very spicy.

## Notes

辣 with bitter radical 辛. 辣椒 = chili pepper. 麻辣 = numbing-spicy (Sichuan style).
