# 苍 Dark blue, gray, old

- **Character**: 苍
- **Pinyin**: cāng
- **Meaning**: dark blue, gray, old
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 苍天 (cāng tiān) — heaven, sky
- 苍白 (cāng bái) — pale

## Example Sentences

- 苍天在上，我绝不说谎。(Cāng tiān zài shàng, wǒ jué bù shuō huǎng.) — Heaven above, I absolutely won't lie.

## Notes

Often refers to heaven or sky in classical Chinese
