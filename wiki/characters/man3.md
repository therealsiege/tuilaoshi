# 满 Full

- **Character**: 满
- **Pinyin**: mǎn
- **Meaning**: full, filled, satisfied
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 满意 (mǎn yì) — satisfied
- 充满 (chōng mǎn) — full of, filled with

## Example Sentences

- 房间里满是人。(Fáng jiān lǐ mǎn shì rén.) — The room is full of people.

## Notes

满 indicates fullness or completeness. Also used for age: 他满18岁了 (he's turned 18).
