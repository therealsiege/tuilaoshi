# 骚 Coquettish, literary elegance

- **Character**: 骚
- **Pinyin**: sāo
- **Meaning**: coquettish, literary elegance
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 离骚 (Lí Sāo) — famous poem by Qu Yuan
- 骚动 (sāo dòng) — disturbance, commotion

## Example Sentences

- 《离骚》开创了中国浪漫主义诗歌的先河。('Lí Sāo' kāichuàng le Zhōngguó làngmàn zhǔyì shīgē de xiānhé.) — 'Li Sao' pioneered romantic poetry in China.

## Notes

Most famously used in 离骚 (Encountering Sorrow), Qu Yuan's masterpiece. Refers to a lyrical style in classical Chinese poetry.
