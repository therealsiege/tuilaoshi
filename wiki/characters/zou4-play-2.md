# 奏 To Play

- **Character**: 奏
- **Pinyin**: zòu
- **Meaning**: to play (music), to present
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 演奏 (yǎn zòu) — to perform (music)
- 伴奏 (bàn zòu) — to accompany

## Example Sentences

- 乐队正在演奏交响乐。(Yuè duì zhèng zài yǎn zòu jiāo xiǎng yuè.) — The orchestra is performing a symphony.

## Notes

奏 specifically refers to playing music or presenting to the emperor.
