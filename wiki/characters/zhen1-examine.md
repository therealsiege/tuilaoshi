# 甄 Examine, discern, select

- **Character**: 甄
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: examine, discern, select
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 甄别 (zhēnbié) — distinguish, screen
- 甄选 (zhēnxuǎn) — select, choose

## Example Sentences

- 需要甄别真伪。(Xūyào zhēnbié zhēnwěi.) — Need to distinguish between true and false.

## Notes

Formal term for discriminating examination or selection.
