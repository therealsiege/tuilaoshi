# 梅 Plum

- **Character**: 梅
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: plum, plum blossom
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 梅花 (méi huā) — plum blossom
- 杨梅 (yáng méi) — bayberry

## Example Sentences

- 梅花在冬天开放。(Méi huā zài dōng tiān kāi fàng.) — Plum blossoms bloom in winter.

## Notes

梅 shows wood 木 with每 (every), representing the plum tree that blooms reliably.
