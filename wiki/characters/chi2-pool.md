# 池 Pool

- **Character**: 池
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: pool, battery
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 电池 (diàn chí) — battery
- 游泳池 (yóu yǒng chí) — swimming pool

## Example Sentences

- 电池没电了。(Diàn chí méi diàn le.) — The battery is dead.

## Notes

池 originally meant pond/pool, now commonly used for batteries (electrical reservoir).
