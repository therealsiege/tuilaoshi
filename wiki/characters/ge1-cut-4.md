# 割 Cut, reap, cede

- **Character**: 割
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: cut, reap, cede
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 割让 (gēràng) — cede (territory)
- 分割 (fēngē) — divide, partition

## Example Sentences

- 历史上曾割让领土。(Lìshǐ shàng céng gēràng lǐngtǔ.) — Territory was ceded in history.

## Notes

Used for cutting, reaping, and ceding territory.
