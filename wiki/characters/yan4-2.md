# 砚 砚砚

- **Character**: 砚
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: 砚砚
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砚台 (yàn tái) — 砚砚
- 笔墨纸砚 (bǐ mò zhǐ yàn) — 砚砚砚砚砚

## Example Sentences

- 笔墨纸砚是文房四宝。(Bǐ mò zhǐ yàn shì wén fáng sì bǎo.) — Brush, ink, paper, and inkstone are the four treasures of the study.

## Notes

One of the four treasures of the study in Chinese calligraphy.
