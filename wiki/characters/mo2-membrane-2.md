# 膜 Membrane, film

- **Character**: 膜
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: membrane, film
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 薄膜 (báo mó) — membrane, film
- 耳膜 (ěr mó) — eardrum

## Example Sentences

- 耳膜破了会影响听力。(Ěrmó pò le huì yǐngxiǎng tīnglì.) — A ruptured eardrum will affect hearing.

## Notes

Biological membranes or thin protective films.
