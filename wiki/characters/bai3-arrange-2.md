# 摆 To Arrange

- **Character**: 摆
- **Pinyin**: bǎi
- **Meaning**: to arrange, to swing, to display
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摆放 (bǎi fàng) — to arrange
- 摇摆 (yáo bǎi) — to sway

## Example Sentences

- 请把椅子摆好。(Qǐng bǎ yǐ zi bǎi hǎo.) — Please arrange the chairs properly.

## Notes

摆 simplified from 擺. Hand radical 扌 representing arranging or setting out.
