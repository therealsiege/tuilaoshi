# 诵 To recite, to chant

- **Character**: 诵
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: to recite, to chant
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 朗诵 (lǎng sòng) — to read aloud with expression
- 背诵 (bèi sòng) — to recite from memory

## Example Sentences

- 学生们在朗诵课文。(Xuéshēngmen zài lǎngsòng kèwén.) — The students are reciting the text aloud.

## Notes

Emphasizes reciting aloud, often from memory.
