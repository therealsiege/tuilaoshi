# 亿 Hundred Million

- **Character**: 亿
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: hundred million
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 一亿 (yī yì) — one hundred million
- 十亿 (shí yì) — one billion

## Example Sentences

- 中国有十四亿人口。(Zhōng guó yǒu shí sì yì rén kǒu.) — China has 1.4 billion people.

## Notes

亿 is hundred million. Used for very large numbers. 亿万 = millions upon millions.
