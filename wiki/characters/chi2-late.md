# 迟 Late, slow, delay

- **Character**: 迟
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: late, slow, delay
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 迟到 (chí dào) — be late
- 迟钝 (chí dùn) — slow, dull

## Example Sentences

- 他的反应有些迟钝。(Tā de fǎn yìng yǒu xiē chí dùn.) — His reaction is somewhat slow.

## Notes

Lateness and slowness in time or response
