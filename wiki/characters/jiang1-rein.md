# 缰 Reins

- **Character**: 缰
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: reins, bridle
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 缰绳 (jiāng shéng) — reins
- 脱缰 (tuō jiāng) — unbridled

## Example Sentences

- 他拉紧了缰绳。(Tā lā jǐn le jiāng shéng.) — He tightened the reins.

## Notes

缰 simplified from 韁. Silk 纟 with 畺 (boundary), rope controlling a horse.
