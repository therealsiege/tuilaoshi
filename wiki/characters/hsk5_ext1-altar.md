# 坛 Altar, Platform, 坛子 Jar

- **Character**: 坛
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: altar, platform, 坛子 jar
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 祭坛 (jì tán) — altar
- 天坛 (Tiān tán) — Temple of Heaven
- 论坛 (lùn tán) — forum

## Example Sentences

- 天坛是明清皇帝祭天的地方。(Tiāntán shì Míng Qīng huángdì jì tiān de dìfāng.) — The Temple of Heaven is where Ming and Qing emperors made sacrifices to heaven.

## Notes

Term for altars and ceremonial platforms. 天坛 is famous Beijing landmark. Modern usage includes 论坛 (forum/discussion platform).
