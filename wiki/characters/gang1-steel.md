# 钢 Steel

- **Character**: 钢
- **Pinyin**: gāng
- **Meaning**: steel
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钢铁 (gāng tiě) — steel
- 钢琴 (gāng qín) — piano

## Example Sentences

- 她会弹钢琴。(Tā huì tán gāng qín.) — She can play the piano.

## Notes

钢 means steel, a more refined and stronger metal than iron. 钢琴 (piano) uses this character, perhaps reflecting its steel strings.
