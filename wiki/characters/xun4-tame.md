# 驯 Tame, docile

- **Character**: 驯
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: tame, docile
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 驯服 (xùnfú) — tame, domesticate
- 驯养 (xùnyǎng) — domesticate, raise

## Example Sentences

- 他们驯养了许多动物。(Tāmen xùnyǎng le xǔduō dòngwù.) — They domesticated many animals.

## Notes

Refers to taming wild animals or people becoming docile.
