# 峰 Peak

- **Character**: 峰
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: peak, summit
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 高峰 (gāo fēng) — peak, summit
- 山峰 (shān fēng) — mountain peak

## Example Sentences

- 这座山有三个高峰。(Zhè zuò shān yǒu sān gè gāofēng.) — This mountain has three peaks.

## Notes

峰 combines mountain 山 with 夆 (to meet), representing the pointed meeting point at a mountain's summit.
