# 润 Moist

- **Character**: 润
- **Pinyin**: rùn
- **Meaning**: moist, smooth, to moisten, profit
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 利润 (lì rùn) — profit
- 湿润 (shī rùn) — moist

## Example Sentences

- 公司今年的利润很高。(Gōng sī jīn niān de lì rùn hěn gāo.) — The company's profit this year is very high.

## Notes

润 originally meant moist/smooth, extended to profit (making things flow smoothly). Used in润月 (intercalary month).
