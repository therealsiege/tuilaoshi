# 巧 Skillful

- **Character**: 巧
- **Pinyin**: qiǎo
- **Meaning**: skillful, coincidental, ingenious
- **Radical**: 工 (work)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 巧妙 (qiǎo miào) — ingenious, clever
- 技巧 (jì qiǎo) — skill, technique

## Example Sentences

- 这个设计很巧妙。(Zhè gè shè jì hěn qiǎo miào.) — This design is very ingenious.

## Notes

巧 shows work 工 with a bent piece, representing skillful craftsmanship.
