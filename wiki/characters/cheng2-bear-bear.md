# 承 To Bear

- **Character**: 承
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to bear, to undertake, to inherit
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 承担 (chéng dān) — to undertake
- 承受 (chéng shòu) — to bear

## Example Sentences

- 他承担了这个责任。(Tā chéng dān le zhè ge zé rèn.) — He undertook this responsibility.

## Notes

承 shows hands 手 holding up from below, to bear weight.
