# 摊 Stall, booth

- **Character**: 摊
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: stall, booth
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摊位 (tān wèi) — booth, stall
- 地摊 (dì tān) — street stall

## Example Sentences

- 夜市有很多小吃摊。(Yè shì yǒu hěn duō xiǎo chī tān.) — The night market has many food stalls.

## Notes

摆摊 means set up a stall. 摊贩 means street vendor.
