# 壁 Wall

- **Character**: 壁
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: wall, constellation
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 壁宿 (bì xiù) — Wall constellation
- 墙壁 (qiáng bì) — wall

## Example Sentences

- 壁宿是北方玄武七宿之一。(Bì xiù shì běifāng xuánwǔ qī xiù zhī yī.) — Wall is one of the seven mansions of the Northern Black Tortoise.

## Notes

壁 means wall. In astronomy, the Wall constellation, representing a wall between two palace areas.
