# 装 Pack, install, decorate

- **Character**: 装
- **Pinyin**: zhuāng
- **Meaning**: pack, install, decorate
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 装修 (zhuāng xiū) — decorate, renovate
- 安装 (ān zhuāng) — install

## Example Sentences

- 新房正在装修。(Xīn fáng zhèng zài zhuāng xiū.) — The new house is being renovated.

## Notes

装潢 means interior decoration. 精装修 means finely decorated.
