# 制 To Manufacture

- **Character**: 制
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: to manufacture, system, to control
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 制度 (zhì dù) — system, institution
- 控制 (kòng zhì) — to control

## Example Sentences

- 这个制度很完善。(Zhè ge zhì dù hěn wán shàn.) — This system is very comprehensive.

## Notes

制 shows cutting/shaping with a knife, representing manufacturing or establishing systems and controls.
