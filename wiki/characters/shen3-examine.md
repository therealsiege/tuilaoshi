# 审 Examine, review, inspect

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: examine,審 review, inspect
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěn chá) — to examine, review
- 审批 (shěn pī) — to examine and approve

## Example Sentences

- 这个项目需要经过严格的审查。(Zhè ge xiàng mù xū yào jīng guò yán gé de shěn chá.) — This project needs to undergo strict examination.

## Notes

Used in legal, administrative, and editorial contexts. 审理 means to try a case in court.
