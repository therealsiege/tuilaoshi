# 栏 Fence

- **Character**: 栏
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: fence, hurdle
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 跨栏 (kuà lán) — hurdles
- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing, fence

## Example Sentences

- 他打破了110米跨栏纪录。(Tā dǎpò le yībǎi yīshí mǐ kuàlán jìlù.) — He broke the 110-meter hurdles record.

## Notes

Hurdles and fences in track and field
