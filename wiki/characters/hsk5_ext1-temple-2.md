# 寺 Temple, Buddhist Monastery, 寺庙 Temple

- **Character**: 寺
- **Pinyin**: sì
- **Meaning**: temple, Buddhist monastery, 寺庙 temple
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 寺庙 (sì miào) — temple
- 寺院 (sì yuàn) — monastery
- 佛寺 (fó sì) — Buddhist temple

## Example Sentences

- 少林寺是著名的佛教寺院。(Shǎolín Sì shì zhùmíng de fójiào sìyuàn.) — Shaolin Temple is a famous Buddhist monastery.

## Notes

Specifically for Buddhist temples, though can be used generally. Famous examples include 少林寺, 白马寺, and 大雁塔寺.
