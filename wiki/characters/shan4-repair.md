# 缮 Repair, mend, transcribe

- **Character**: 缮
- **Pinyin**: shàn
- **Meaning**: repair, mend, transcribe
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 修缮 (xiū shàn) — repair, renovate
- 缮写 (shàn xiě) — transcribe

## Example Sentences

- 这座古建筑需要修缮。(Zhè zuò gǔ jiàn zhù xū yào xiū shàn.) — This ancient building needs repair.
