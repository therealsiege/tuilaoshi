# 霞 Rosy clouds, sunset glow

- **Character**: 霞
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: rosy clouds, sunset glow
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 晚霞 (wǎn xiá) — sunset glow
- 朝霞 (zhāo xiá) — morning glow

## Example Sentences

- 天边的晚霞非常美丽。(Tiān biān de wǎn xiá fēi cháng měi lì.) — The sunset glow on the horizon is very beautiful.

## Notes

Colorful clouds at sunrise/sunset
