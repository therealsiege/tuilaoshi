# 拟 To Plan

- **Character**: 拟
- **Pinyin**: nǐ
- **Meaning**: to plan, to draft, to imitate
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 模拟 (mó nǐ) — to simulate
- 拟定 (nǐ dìng) — to draft, to draw up

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要拟定一个计划。(Wǒ men xū yào nǐ dìng yī gè jì huà.) — We need to draft a plan.

## Notes

拟 simplified from 擬. Hand radical 扌 representing preparing or imitating something.
