# 因 Cause, reason, factor

- **Character**: 因
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: cause, reason, factor
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 基因 (jī yīn) — gene
- 原因 (yuán yīn) — reason, cause

## Example Sentences

- 科学家正在研究基因疗法。(Kē xué jiā zhèng zài yán jiū jī yīn liáo fǎ.) — Scientists are researching gene therapy.

## Notes

Used in genetics for genes and causation
