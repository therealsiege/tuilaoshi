# 训 Train, instruct

- **Character**: 训
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: train, instruct
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 训练 (xùn liàn) — train, training
- 培训 (péi xùn) — training

## Example Sentences

- 模型需要大量训练数据。(Mó xíng xū yào dà liàng xùn liàn shù jù.) — The model needs a large amount of training data.

## Notes

Used in 训练 (training), 培训 (training), machine learning terminology
