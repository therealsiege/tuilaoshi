# 闸 Sluice, gate, brake

- **Character**: 闸
- **Pinyin**: zhá
- **Meaning**: sluice, gate, brake
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 闸门 (zhá mén) — sluice gate, floodgate
- 刹车 (shā chē) — to brake

## Example Sentences

- 水闸控制着河水的流量。(Shuǐ zhá kòng zhì zhe hé shuǐ de liú liàng.) — The sluice gate controls the river's flow rate.

## Notes

Used for gates controlling flow (water, traffic). 电闸 means circuit breaker.
