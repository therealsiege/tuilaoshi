# 交 Hand over, deliver, intersect

- **Character**: 交
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: hand over,交, intersect
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 交付 (jiāofù) — hand over, deliver
- 交易 (jiāoyì) — transaction, deal

## Example Sentences

- 明天交付货物。(Míngtiān jiāofù huòwù.) — Deliver the goods tomorrow.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: hand over, deliver, intersect, make friends.
