# 拧 To Twist

- **Character**: 拧
- **Pinyin**: níng
- **Meaning**: to twist, to wring
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拧毛巾 (níng máo jīn) — to wring a towel
- 拧开 (níng kāi) — to unscrew

## Example Sentences

- 拧干毛巾。(Níng gān máo jīn.) — Wring out the towel.

## Notes

拧 simplified from 擰. Hand 扌 twisting motion.
