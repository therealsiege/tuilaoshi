# 单 Single

- **Character**: 单
- **Pinyin**: dān
- **Meaning**: single, odd, bill
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 单位 (dān wèi) — unit
- 菜单 (cài dān) — menu

## Example Sentences

- 点击菜单栏。(Diǎn jī cài dān lán.) — Click the menu bar.

## Notes

单 as dān means single/simple; as shàn is a surname. Essential in menu and list terminology.
