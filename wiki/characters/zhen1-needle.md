# 针 Needle, acupuncture, injection

- **Character**: 针
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: needle, acupuncture, injection
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 针头 (zhēn tóu) — needle tip
- 打针 (dǎ zhēn) — give injection

## Example Sentences

- 医生给病人打了一针。(Yī shēng gěi bìng rén dǎ le yī zhēn.) — The doctor gave the patient an injection.

## Notes

Used in medical contexts and sewing
