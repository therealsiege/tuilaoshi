# 移 Transplant

- **Character**: 移
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: move, shift
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 移植 (yí zhí) — to transplant
- 转移 (zhuǎn yí) — to transfer, metastasize
- 移动 (yí dòng) — to move

## Example Sentences

- 癌细胞已经转移到肝脏。(Ái xì bāo yǐ jīng zhuǎn yí dào gān zàng.) — Cancer cells have metastasized to the liver.

## Notes

Transfer or movement; transplant or metastasis.
