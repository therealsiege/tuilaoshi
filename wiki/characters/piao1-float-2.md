# 飘 To Float

- **Character**: 飘
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: to float, to drift, to flutter
- **Radical**: 风 (wind)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 飘扬 (piāo yáng) — to flutter
- 飘动 (piāo dòng) — to float

## Example Sentences

- 树叶在空中飘。(Shù yè zài kōng zhōng piāo.) — Leaves are floating in the air.

## Notes

飘 means to float or drift in air. Used for things floating on wind like leaves, flags, or fragrance.
