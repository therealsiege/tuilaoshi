# 翰 Writing Brush

- **Character**: 翰
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: writing brush, writing
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 翰林 (hàn lín) — Hanlin Academy
- 翰墨 (hàn mò) — brush and ink

## Example Sentences

- 他是一位翰林学士。(Tā shì yí wèi hàn lín xué shì.) — He is a Hanlin scholar.

## Notes

翰 has feather 羽 with 倝 phonetic, a writing brush made of feathers.
