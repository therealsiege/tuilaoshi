# 空 Empty

- **Character**: 空
- **Pinyin**: kōng
- **Meaning**: empty, air, sky
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 空中 (kōng zhōng) — in the air, midair
- 腾空 (téng kōng) — to soar, jump in air

## Example Sentences

- 他在空中完成了动作。(Tā zài kōngzhōng wánchéng le dòngzuò.) — He completed the move in midair.

## Notes

Air and sky; used for aerial sports movements
