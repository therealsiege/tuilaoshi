# 阔 Wide, broad, wealthy

- **Character**: 阔
- **Pinyin**: kuò
- **Meaning**: wide, broad, wealthy
- **Radical**: 门(gate)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 宽阔 (kuān kuò) — wide, broad
- 阔气 (kuò qi) — extravagant, luxurious

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很宽阔。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn kuānkuò.) — This road is very wide.

## Notes

Can mean physically wide or financially wealthy.
