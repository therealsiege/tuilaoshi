# 痹 Paralysis, numbness, 痺 (traditional)

- **Character**: 痹
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: paralysis, numbness, 痺 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 麻痹 (má bì) — paralysis, numb
- 风湿性关节炎 (fēng shī xìng guān jié yán) — rheumatoid arthritis

## Example Sentences

- 不能麻痹大意。(Bù néng mábì dàyì.) — We cannot be complacent.

## Notes

Medical numbness/paralysis, also metaphorical complacency.
