# 誉 Reputation

- **Character**: 誉
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: reputation, fame, praise
- **Radical**: 言 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 名誉 (míng yù) — reputation, fame
- 信誉 (xìn yù) — credit, reputation

## Example Sentences

- 他的名誉很好。(Tā de míng yù hěn hǎo.) — His reputation is very good.

## Notes

誉 is simplified from 譽. Represents spoken praise and reputation.
