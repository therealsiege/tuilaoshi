# 扣 Smash

- **Character**: 扣
- **Pinyin**: kòu
- **Meaning**: to smash, button
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扣球 (kòu qiú) — to spike (volleyball)
- 扣篮 (kòu lán) — to dunk (basketball)

## Example Sentences

- 她扣球很有力。(Tā kòuqiú hěn yǒulì.) — Her spike is very powerful.

## Notes

Spiking in volleyball, dunking in basketball; powerful downward strike
