# 弓 Bow

- **Character**: 弓
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: bow (weapon)
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow
- 拉弓 (lā gōng) — to draw a bow

## Example Sentences
