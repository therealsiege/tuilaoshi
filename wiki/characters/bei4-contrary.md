# 悖 Contrary, perverse, violate

- **Character**: 悖
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: contrary, perverse, violate
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 悖论 (bèi lùn) — paradox
- 违悖 (wéi bèi) — to violate, go against

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个有趣的悖论。(Zhè shì yī ge yǒu qù de bèi lùn.) — This is an interesting paradox.

## Notes

Indicates contradiction or going against something. 悖理 means contrary to reason.
