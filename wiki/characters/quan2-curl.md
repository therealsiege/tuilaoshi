# 蜷 To Curl Up

- **Character**: 蜷
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: to curl up
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蜷缩 (quán suō) — to curl up
- 蜷曲 (quán qū) — to curl

## Example Sentences

- 猫蜷缩在沙发上。(Māo quán suō zài shā fā shàng.) — The cat curled up on the sofa.

## Notes

蜷 combines insect 虫 and 卷 (roll), representing curling up like an insect.
