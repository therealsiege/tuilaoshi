# 割 To cut, to reap, to sever

- **Character**: 割
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: to cut, to reap, to割断
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 割草 (gē cǎo) — to mow grass
- 分割 (fēn gē) — to divide, to partition

## Example Sentences

- 农民在割稻子。(Nóngmín zài gē dàozi.) — The farmer is harvesting rice.

## Notes

Used for cutting, reaping, or severing.
