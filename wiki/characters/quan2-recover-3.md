# 痊 To Recover

- **Character**: 痊
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: to recover from illness
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 痊愈 (quán yù) — to fully recover
- 痊可 (quán kě) — to heal

## Example Sentences

- 他的病已经痊愈了。(Tā de bìng yǐ jīng quán yù le.) — His illness has fully recovered.

## Notes

痊 has sickness 疒 with 全 (complete), completely recovered from illness.
