# 偶 Even

- **Character**: 偶
- **Pinyin**: ǒu
- **Meaning**: even number, accidental, spouse
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偶然 (ǒu rán) — accidental
- 配偶 (pèi ǒu) — spouse

## Example Sentences

- 这次相遇纯属偶然。(Zhè cì xiāng yù chún shǔ ǒu rán.) — This encounter was purely accidental.

## Notes

偶 means even (numbers), accidental, or paired; in 对偶 refers to parallelism.
