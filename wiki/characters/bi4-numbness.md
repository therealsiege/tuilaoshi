# 痹 Numbness

- **Character**: 痹
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: numbness, paralysis
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 麻痹 (má bì) — paralysis, numbness
- 风湿痹痛 (fēng shī bì tòng) — rheumatic pain

## Example Sentences

- 风湿导致关节痹痛。(Fēng shī dǎo zhì guān jié bì tòng.) — Rheumatism causes joint pain and numbness.

## Notes

Blockage syndrome; TCM pain concept.
