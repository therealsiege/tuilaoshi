# 雁 Wild Goose

- **Character**: 雁
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: wild goose
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大雁 (dà yàn) — wild goose
- 雁群 (yàn qún) — flock of geese

## Example Sentences

- 秋天大雁南飞。(Qiū tiān dà yàn nán fēi.) — Wild geese fly south in autumn.

## Notes

雁 combines person 人 and bird 隹, representing the migratory wild goose.
