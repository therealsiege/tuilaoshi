# 星 Star

- **Character**: 星
- **Pinyin**: xīng
- **Meaning**: Star constellation, star
- **Radical**: 日(sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 星宿 (xīng xiù) — Star constellation
- 星星 (xīng xing) — star

## Example Sentences

- 星宿是南方朱雀的心脏部位。(Xīng xiù shì nánfāng zhūquè de xīnzàng bùwèi.) — Star constellation is the heart position of the Southern Vermillion Bird.

## Notes

星 means star. In the 28 mansions system, Star is one of the southern mansions near Hydra.
