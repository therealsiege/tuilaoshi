# 隔 To separate, partition, next to

- **Character**: 隔
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: to separate, partition, next to
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 隔壁 (gé bì) — next door
- 隔阂 (gé hé) — estrangement

## Example Sentences

- 隔壁住着一家人。(Gébì zhùzhe yī jiā rén.) — A family lives next door.

## Notes

To separate or be separated by a partition/distance.
