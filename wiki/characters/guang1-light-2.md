# 光 Light, optical

- **Character**: 光
- **Pinyin**: guāng
- **Meaning**: light, optical
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 激光 (jī guāng) — laser
- 光纤 (guāng xiān) — optical fiber

## Example Sentences

- 光纤传输速度很快。(Guāng xiān chuán shū sù dù hěn kuài.) — Optical fiber transmission is very fast.

## Notes

Used in 激光 (laser), 光纤 (optical fiber), 光盘 (CD)
