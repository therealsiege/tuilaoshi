# 灵 Spirit

- **Character**: 灵
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: spirit, nimble, effective
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灵活 (líng huó) — flexible, nimble
- 精灵 (jīng líng) — spirit, fairy

## Example Sentences

- 她的思维很灵活。(Tā de sī wéi hěn líng huó.) — Her thinking is very flexible.

## Notes

灵 simplified from 靈. Originally showed rain falling to produce life, representing spiritual essence or nimbleness.
