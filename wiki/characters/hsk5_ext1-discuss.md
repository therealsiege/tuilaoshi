# 论 Discuss, Theory, 理论 Theory

- **Character**: 论
- **Pinyin**: lùn
- **Meaning**: discuss, theory, 理论 theory
- **Radical**: 讨 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 理论 (lǐ lùn) — theory
- 讨论 (tǎo lùn) — discuss
- 论文 (lùn wén) — thesis, dissertation

## Example Sentences

- 这个理论很有争议。(Zhège lǐlùn hěn yǒu zhēngyì.) — This theory is very controversial.

## Notes

Essential character in academic contexts. 论文 (thesis), 理论 (theory), and 讨论 (discuss) are fundamental academic terms.
