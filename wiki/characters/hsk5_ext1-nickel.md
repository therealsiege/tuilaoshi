# 镍 Nickel, 镍币 Nickel Coin

- **Character**: 镍
- **Pinyin**: niè
- **Meaning**: nickel, 镍币 nickel coin
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 镍币 (niè bì) — nickel coin
- 镍合金 (niè hé jīn) — nickel alloy
- 镍铬 (niè gè) — nickel-chromium

## Example Sentences

- 不锈钢含有镍和铬。(Bùxiùgāng hányǒu niè hé gè.) — Stainless steel contains nickel and chromium.

## Notes

Industrial metal nickel. Important component in stainless steel and other alloys. Used in coins and batteries.
