# 富 Rich, wealthy

- **Character**: 富
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: rich, wealthy
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 富有 (fù yǒu) — rich, wealthy
- 财富 (cái fù) — wealth

## Example Sentences

- 这个地区很富有。(Zhè ge dì qū hěn fù yǒu.) — This area is very wealthy.

## Notes

富人 means rich person. 富裕 means prosperous/well-off.
