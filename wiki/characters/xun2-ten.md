# 旬 Decade

- **Character**: 旬
- **Pinyin**: xún
- **Meaning**: ten days, decade
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 上旬 (shàng xún) — first ten days of month
- 中旬 (zhōng xún) — middle ten days

## Example Sentences

- 六月上旬我去北京。(Liù yuè shàng xún wǒ qù běi jīng.) — I'm going to Beijing in early June.

## Notes

旬 means ten-day period. 下旬 = last ten days. Three 旬 make a month.
