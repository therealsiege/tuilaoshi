# 缔 Conclude, tie, join

- **Character**: 缔
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: conclude, tie, join
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缔约 (dì yuē) — to conclude a treaty
- 缔结 (dì jié) — to conclude, establish

## Example Sentences

- 两国缔结了友好条约。(Liǎng guó dì jié le yǒu hǎo tiáo yuē.) — The two countries concluded a friendship treaty.

## Notes

Formal term used in legal and diplomatic contexts for establishing treaties or agreements. 缔造 means to create or establish.
