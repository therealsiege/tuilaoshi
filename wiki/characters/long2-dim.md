# 胧 Dim

- **Character**: 胧
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: dim moonlight, hazy
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 朦胧 (méng lóng) — hazy
- 胧月 (lóng yuè) — hazy moon

## Example Sentences

- 窗外是朦胧的夜色。(Chuāng wài shì méng lóng de yè sè.) — Outside the window is a hazy night scene.

## Notes

胧 simplified from 朧. Moon 月 plus 龙 (dragon), dim moonlight.
