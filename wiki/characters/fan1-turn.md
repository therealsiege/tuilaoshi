# 翻 To Turn Over

- **Character**: 翻
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: to turn over, to translate, to climb over
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 翻译 (fān yì) — to translate
- 翻开 (fān kāi) — to open (a book)

## Example Sentences

- 请翻到第五页。(Qǐng fān dào dì wǔ yè.) — Please turn to page five.

## Notes

翻 means to flip/turn. 翻译 = translate, 翻身 = turn over (in bed), 翻山 = climb a mountain.
