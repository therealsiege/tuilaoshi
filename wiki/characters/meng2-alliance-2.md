# 盟 Alliance

- **Character**: 盟
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: alliance, oath, to swear
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 联盟 (lián méng) — alliance, union
- 盟友 (méng yǒu) — ally

## Example Sentences

- 两国结成了联盟。(Liǎng guó jié chéng le lián méng.) — The two countries formed an alliance.

## Notes

盟 shows blood oath over a vessel, representing solemn alliances.
