# 冒 To Emit

- **Character**: 冒
- **Pinyin**: mào
- **Meaning**: to emit, to give off, to risk, to pretend
- **Radical**: 冂 (upside down box)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 冒险 (mào xiǎn) — to take risks
- 感冒 (gǎn mào) — cold, flu

## Example Sentences

- 烟囱在冒烟。(Yān cōng zài mào yān.) — The chimney is emitting smoke.

## Notes

冒 means to emit or give off. Also means to risk (冒险) and appears in感冒 (common cold).
