# 看 Guard, watch, look after

- **Character**: 看
- **Pinyin**: kān
- **Meaning**: guard, watch, look after
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 看守 (kānshǒu) — guard, watch
- 看管 (kānguǎn) — take care of, guard

## Example Sentences

- 看守所关押嫌疑人。(Kānshǒusuǒ guānyā xiányírén.) — The detention center holds suspects.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: kàn (look, see) and kān (guard, watch).
