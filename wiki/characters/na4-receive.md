# 纳 Receive, accept, pay

- **Character**: 纳
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: receive, accept,納納
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 容纳 (róng nà) — to contain
- 纳税 (nà shuì) — to pay tax

## Example Sentences

- 公民有义务纳税。(Gōng mín yǒu yì wù nà shuì.) — Citizens have an obligation to pay taxes.

## Notes

Means to receive or accept. 接纳 means to accept. 采纳 means to adopt (suggestion). 归纳 means to summarize.
