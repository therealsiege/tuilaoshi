# 惜 To cherish, to pity, to spare

- **Character**: 惜
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: to cherish, to pity, to spare
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 珍惜 (zhēn xī) — to treasure, to cherish
- 可惜 (kě xī) — it's a pity, unfortunately

## Example Sentences

- 我们要珍惜时间。(Wǒmen yào zhēnxī shíjiān.) — We should cherish time.

## Notes

Expresses valuing or feeling regret about something.
