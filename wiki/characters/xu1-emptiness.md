# 虚 Emptiness

- **Character**: 虚
- **Pinyin**: xū
- **Meaning**: Emptiness constellation, empty
- **Radical**: 虍(tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 虚宿 (xū xiù) — Emptiness constellation
- 虚空 (xū kōng) — void

## Example Sentences

- 虚宿象征着空虚和危险。(Xū xiù xiàngzhēng zhe kōngxū hé wēixiǎn.) — Emptiness constellation symbolizes void and danger.

## Notes

虚 means empty or void. In astronomy, the Emptiness constellation, suggesting a dangerous empty area.
