# 腌 To Pickle

- **Character**: 腌
- **Pinyin**: yān
- **Meaning**: to pickle, to marinate
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 腌制 (yān zhì) — to pickle
- 腌菜 (yān cài) — pickled vegetables

## Example Sentences

- 她腌了一些黄瓜。(Tā yān le yì xiē huáng guā.) — She pickled some cucumbers.

## Notes

腌 has flesh/meat 月 radical, relating to preserving food.
