# 我 I/Me

- **Character**: 我
- **Pinyin**: wǒ
- **Meaning**: I, me
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 我们 (wǒmen) — we, us
- 我的 (wǒ de) — my, mine
- 自我 (zìwǒ) — self, ego
- 忘我 (wàngwǒ) — selfless, self-forgetting

## Example Sentences

- 我是学生。(Wǒ shì xuéshēng.) — I am a student.
- 我很喜欢中国菜。(Wǒ hěn xǐhuān Zhōngguó cài.) — I really like Chinese food.
- 请告诉我。(Qǐng gàosù wǒ.) — Please tell me.

## Notes

我 originally depicted a hand holding a weapon (戈 halberd), suggesting "the one who holds the weapon" — i.e., oneself. The left side resembles 手 (hand) in an older form. It is one of the most frequently used characters in Chinese.
