# 镶 Inlay, mount, edge

- **Character**: 镶
- **Pinyin**: xiāng
- **Meaning**: inlay, mount, edge
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 镶嵌 (xiāng qiàn) — inlay, set
- 镶边 (xiāng biān) — edge, border

## Example Sentences

- 这幅画镶了一个金色的框。(Zhè fú huà xiāng le yī gè jīn sè de kuàng.) — This painting is mounted in a golden frame.

## Notes

Commonly used for mounting gems or decorative borders.
