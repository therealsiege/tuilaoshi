# 挑 To Carry

- **Character**: 挑
- **Pinyin**: tiāo
- **Meaning**: to carry on shoulder pole, to choose
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挑选 (tiāo xuǎn) — to select
- 挑战 (tiǎo zhàn) — to challenge

## Example Sentences

- 他挑着担子上山。(Tā tiāo zhe dàn zi shàng shān.) — He carries loads with pole up the mountain.

## Notes

挑 combines hand 扌 and 兆 (omen), can mean to carry (tiāo) or to pick (tiǎo).
