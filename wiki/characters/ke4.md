# 刻 To Carve

- **Character**: 刻
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: to carve, quarter (hour), harsh
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — to carve
- 刻苦 (kè kǔ) — hardworking

## Example Sentences

- 他学习很刻苦。(Tā xué xí hěn kè kǔ.) — He studies very diligently.

## Notes

刻 shows knife 刂 and 亥 (12th earthly branch), originally carving notches to mark time, now means carve or moment.
