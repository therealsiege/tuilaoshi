# 隔 To Separate

- **Character**: 隔
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: to separate, to partition
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 隔壁 (gé bì) — next door
- 间隔 (jiān gé) — interval

## Example Sentences

- 隔壁住着一个外国人。(Gé bì zhù zhe yī gè wài guó rén.) — A foreigner lives next door.

## Notes

隔 combines mound 阝 and 鬲 (tripod cauldron), originally depicting a physical barrier separating spaces.
