# 奖 Reward, prize

- **Character**: 奖
- **Pinyin**: jiǎng
- **Meaning**: reward, prize
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 奖金 (jiǎng jīn) — bonus, award
- 奖励 (jiǎng lì) — reward, encourage

## Example Sentences

- 公司给优秀员工发奖金。(Gōng sī gěi yōu xiù yuán gōng fā jiǎng jīn.) — The company gives bonuses to excellent employees.

## Notes

年终奖 is year-end bonus. 奖惩 means reward and punishment.
