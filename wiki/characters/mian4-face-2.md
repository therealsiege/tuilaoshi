# 面 Face

- **Character**: 面
- **Pinyin**: miàn
- **Meaning**: face, surface, side
- **Radical**: 面 (face)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 表面 (biǎo miàn) — surface
- 面积 (miàn jī) — area

## Example Sentences

- 桌面很干净。(Zhuō miàn hěn gān jìng.) — The table surface is very clean.

## Notes

面 means face/surface/noodles depending on context, extremely versatile character.
