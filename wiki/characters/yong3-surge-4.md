# 涌 To Surge

- **Character**: 涌
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: to surge, to gush
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涌现 (yǒng xiàn) — to emerge
- 汹涌 (xiōng yǒng) — turbulent

## Example Sentences

- 人群涌向出口。(Rén qún yǒng xiàng chū kǒu.) — The crowd surged toward the exit.

## Notes

涌 combines water 氵 and 甬 (path), water rushing through.
