# 耕 To plow, to till

- **Character**: 耕
- **Pinyin**: gēng
- **Meaning**: to plow, to till
- **Radical**: 耒(plow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耕地 (gēng dì) — to plow land, arable land
- 耕种 (gēng zhòng) — to plow and plant

## Example Sentences

- 农民在春天耕地。(Nóngmín zài chūntiān gēng dì.) — Farmers plow the land in spring.

## Notes

Traditional agricultural term for plowing.
