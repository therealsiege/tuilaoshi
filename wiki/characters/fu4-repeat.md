# 复 To Repeat

- **Character**: 复
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to repeat, to recover, complex
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 复习 (fù xí) — to review
- 复杂 (fù zá) — complicated

## Example Sentences

- 考试前要复习。(Kǎo shì qián yào fù xí.) — You need to review before the exam.

## Notes

复 is simplified from 復/複. 复习 = review, 复杂 = complicated, 恢复 = recover.
