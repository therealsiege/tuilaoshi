# 受 Receive, accept, suffer

- **Character**: 受
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: receive, accept, suffer
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 接受 (jiēshòu) — accept
- 受理 (shòulǐ) — accept and handle (case)

## Example Sentences

- 法院受理了这个案件。(Fǎyuàn shòulǐ le zhège ànjiàn.) — The court accepted this case.

## Notes

Very common for receiving and accepting.
