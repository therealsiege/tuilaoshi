# 磨 Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó/mò
- **Meaning**: grind, polish
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 研磨 (yán mó) — to grind
- 磨碎 (mó suì) — to grind to pieces
- 折磨 (zhé mó) — to torture

## Example Sentences

- 把药片磨成粉末吞服。(Bǎ yào piàn mó chéng fěn mò tūn fú.) — Grind the tablet into powder and swallow.

## Notes

Grinding or wearing; powder preparation.
