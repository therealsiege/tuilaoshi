# 拂 To brush away, whisk, go against

- **Character**: 拂
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: to brush away, whisk, go against
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拂晓 (fú xiǎo) — daybreak, dawn
- 拂逆 (fú nì) — to go against, disobey

## Example Sentences

- 拂晓时分,部队开始行动。(Fú xiǎo shí fēn, bù duì kāi shǐ xíng dòng.) — At dawn, the troops began to move.

## Notes

Means to brush or whisk. Also means to go against. 拂袖而去 means to leave in a huff.
