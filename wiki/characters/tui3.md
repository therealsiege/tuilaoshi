# 腿 Leg

- **Character**: 腿
- **Pinyin**: tuǐ
- **Meaning**: leg
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 大腿 (dà tuǐ) — thigh
- 小腿 (xiǎo tuǐ) — calf, lower leg

## Example Sentences

- 我的腿很累。(Wǒ de tuǐ hěn lèi.) — My legs are tired.

## Notes

腿 uses the meat/flesh radical 月. Can also refer to furniture legs (桌子腿 table leg).
