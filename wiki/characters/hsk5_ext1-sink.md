# 陷 Sink, Trap, 陷入 Fall Into

- **Character**: 陷
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: sink, trap, 陷入 fall into
- **Radical**: ⻏ (city)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陷入 (xiàn rù) — fall into, sink into
- 陷阱 (xiàn jǐng) — trap, pitfall
- 缺陷 (quē xiàn) — defect, flaw

## Example Sentences

- 他陷入了困境。(Tā xiànrù le kùnjìng.) — He fell into a difficult situation.

## Notes

Describes falling into traps or difficult situations, both literally and figuratively. 陷入 is common for entering negative states.
