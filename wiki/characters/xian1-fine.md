# 纤 Fine, delicate, slender

- **Character**: 纤
- **Pinyin**: xiān
- **Meaning**: fine, delicate, slender
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 纤细 (xiān xì) — slender, fine
- 纤弱 (xiān ruò) — delicate, frail

## Example Sentences

- 她的手指纤细修长。(Tā de shǒu zhǐ xiān xì xiū cháng.) — Her fingers are slender and long.

## Notes

Also pronounced qiàn in 纤夫 (tracker, boat tower).
