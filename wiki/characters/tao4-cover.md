# 套 To cover, to encase, set, suit

- **Character**: 套
- **Pinyin**: tào
- **Meaning**: to cover, to encase, set, suit
- **Radical**: 大(big)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 套装 (tào zhuāng) — suit (of clothes)
- 全套 (quán tào) — complete set

## Example Sentences

- 他买了一套西装。(Tā mǎi le yī tào xīzhuāng.) — He bought a suit.

## Notes

Can mean to cover/put on or refer to a set of items.
