# 弃 To abandon, discard, 棄 (traditional)

- **Character**: 弃
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: to abandon, discard, 棄 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 放弃 (fàng qì) — to give up, abandon
- 抛弃 (pāo qì) — to abandon, forsake

## Example Sentences

- 他放弃了这个机会。(Tā fàngqì le zhège jīhuì.) — He gave up this opportunity.

## Notes

Strong action of abandoning or discarding something/someone.
