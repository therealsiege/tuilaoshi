# 挂 To Hang

- **Character**: 挂
- **Pinyin**: guà
- **Meaning**: to hang, to register, to worry
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挂号 (guà hào) — to register (hospital)
- 牵挂 (qiān guà) — to worry about

## Example Sentences

- 墙上挂着一幅画。(Qiáng shàng guà zhe yī fú huà.) — A painting hangs on the wall.

## Notes

挂 means to hang or suspend. 挂电话 = to hang up the phone.
