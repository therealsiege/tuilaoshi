# 削 To Peel

- **Character**: 削
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: to peel, to pare, to cut
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 削皮 (xiāo pí) — to peel
- 削减 (xuē jiǎn) — to cut down

## Example Sentences

- 她在削苹果皮。(Tā zài xiāo píng guǒ pí.) — She is peeling an apple.

## Notes

削 肖 (to resemble) plus knife 刂, shaving thin layers.
