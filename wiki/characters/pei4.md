# 配 To配tch

- **Character**: 配
- **Pinyin**: pèi
- **Meaning**: to match, to fit, to配配, to deserve
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 配合 (pèi hé) — to cooperate
- 分配 (fēn pèi) — to allocate

## Example Sentences

- 我们要配合工作。(Wǒ men yào pèi hé gōng zuò.) — We need to cooperate in our work.

## Notes

配 means to match or coordinate. Common in配合 (cooperation), 分配 (allocation).
