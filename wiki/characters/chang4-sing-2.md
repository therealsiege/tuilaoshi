# 唱 To Sing

- **Character**: 唱
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: to sing
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 唱歌 (chàng gē) — to sing
- 歌唱 (gē chàng) — to sing

## Example Sentences

- 她唱得很好听。(Tā chàng de hěn hǎo tīng.) — She sings very beautifully.

## Notes

唱 combines mouth 口 and 昌 (prosperous), representing melodious vocal expression.
