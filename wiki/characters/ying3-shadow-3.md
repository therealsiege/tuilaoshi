# 影 Shadow

- **Character**: 影
- **Pinyin**: yǐng
- **Meaning**: shadow, image, film
- **Radical**: 彡 (bristle)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 电影 (diàn yǐng) — movie
- 影响 (yǐng xiǎng) — influence

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢看电影。(Wǒ xǐ huān kàn diàn yǐng.) — I like to watch movies.

## Notes

影 means shadow or image; 电影 literally means 'electric shadow'.
