# 薪 Salary, wages, firewood

- **Character**: 薪
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: salary, wages, firewood
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 薪水 (xīn shuǐ) — salary
- 薪资 (xīn zī) — salary, pay

## Example Sentences

- 他的薪水很高。(Tā de xīn shuǐ hěn gāo.) — His salary is very high.

## Notes

Originally firewood, now means salary. 年薪 means annual salary. 加薪 means salary increase.
