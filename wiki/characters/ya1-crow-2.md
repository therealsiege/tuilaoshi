# 鸦 Crow

- **Character**: 鸦
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: crow
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 乌鸦 (wū yā) — crow
- 鸦雀无声 (yā què wú shēng) — silence, not a sound

## Example Sentences

- 乌鸦在树上叫。(Wūyā zài shù shàng jiào.) — The crow is cawing in the tree.

## Notes

Often associated with bad omens in Chinese culture. Bird radical indicates type.
