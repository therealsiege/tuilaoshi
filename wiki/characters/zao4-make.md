# 造 To Make

- **Character**: 造
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: to make, to manufacture, to build
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 制造 (zhì zào) — to manufacture
- 创造 (chuàng zào) — to create

## Example Sentences

- 这家工厂制造汽车。(Zhè jiā gōng chǎng zhì zào qì chē.) — This factory manufactures cars.

## Notes

造 means to make or manufacture. Common in制造 (manufacturing), 创造 (creation).
