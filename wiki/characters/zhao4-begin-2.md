# 肇 Begin, originate, cause

- **Character**: 肇
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: begin, originate, cause
- **Radical**: 聿 (brush)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 肇始 (zhàoshǐ) — begin, originate
- 肇事 (zhàoshì) — cause an accident

## Example Sentences

- 此事肇始于去年。(Cǐ shì zhàoshǐ yú qùnián.) — This matter originated last year.

## Notes

Formal literary term for beginnings or causing events.
