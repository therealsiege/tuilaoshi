# 朴 Simple, plain, sincere

- **Character**: 朴
- **Pinyin**: pǔ
- **Meaning**: simple, plain, sincere
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 朴素 (pǔsù) — simple, plain
- 淳朴 (chúnpǔ) — honest and simple

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着很朴素。(Tā chuānzhuó hěn pǔsù.) — She dresses very simply.

## Notes

Common term for simplicity, plainness, or sincerity.
