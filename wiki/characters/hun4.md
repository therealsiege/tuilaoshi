# 混 To Mix

- **Character**: 混
- **Pinyin**: hùn
- **Meaning**: to mix, to confuse, muddy
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 混合 (hùn hé) — to mix, to blend
- 混乱 (hùn luàn) — chaos, confusion

## Example Sentences

- 不要把这两个概念混淆。(Bù yào bǎ zhè liǎng gè gài niàn hùn xiáo.) — Don't confuse these two concepts.

## Notes

混 shows water 氵 and 昆 (many descendants), representing waters mixing together.
