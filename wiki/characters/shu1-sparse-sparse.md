# 疏 Sparse

- **Character**: 疏
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: sparse, to dredge, distant
- **Radical**: 疋 (bolt of cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 疏忽 (shū hū) — to neglect
- 疏远 (shū yuǎn) — to become estranged

## Example Sentences

- 他们的关系越来越疏远。(Tā men de guān xì yuè lái yuè shū yuǎn.) — Their relationship is becoming more distant.

## Notes

疏 originally meant to dredge waterways; extended to sparse, distant.
