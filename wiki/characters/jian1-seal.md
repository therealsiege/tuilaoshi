# 缄 Seal, keep silent

- **Character**: 缄
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: seal, keep silent
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缄默 (jiānmò) — keep silent
- 缄口 (jiānkǒu) — keep one's mouth shut

## Example Sentences

- 他对此事缄口不言。(Tā duì cǐ shì jiānkǒu bù yán.) — He kept silent about this matter.

## Notes

Literary term for sealing or maintaining silence.
