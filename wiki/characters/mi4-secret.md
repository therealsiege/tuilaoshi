# 秘 Secret, mysterious

- **Character**: 秘
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: secret, mysterious
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 秘密 (mì mì) — secret
- 神秘 (shén mì) — mysterious

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个秘密,不能告诉别人。(Zhè shì yī ge mì mì, bù néng gào su bié rén.) — This is a secret, can't tell others.

## Notes

Common character for secrets and mystery. 秘书 means secretary. 秘诀 means secret formula.
