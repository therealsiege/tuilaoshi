# 厚 Thick, deep, generous

- **Character**: 厚
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: thick, deep, generous
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 深厚 (shēn hòu) — deep, profound
- 厚重 (hòu zhòng) — thick and heavy

## Example Sentences

- 他们有深厚的友谊。(Tā men yǒu shēn hòu de yǒu yì.) — They have a deep friendship.

## Notes

Means thick or generous. 厚道 means kind and honest. 得天独厚 means exceptionally favored.
