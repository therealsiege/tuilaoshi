# 针 Needle, injection

- **Character**: 针
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: needle, injection
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 针灸 (zhēn jiǔ) — acupuncture
- 打针 (dǎ zhēn) — give an injection

## Example Sentences

- 针灸治疗已经有几千年的历史。(Zhēnjiǔ zhìliáo yǐjīng yǒu jǐ qiān nián de lìshǐ.) — Acupuncture treatment has a history of several thousand years.

## Notes

Fine needle used in acupuncture or for injections. Acupuncture needles are inserted at specific points to regulate qi flow.
