# 瘟 Plague

- **Character**: 瘟
- **Pinyin**: wēn
- **Meaning**: plague, pestilence
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘟疫 (wēn yì) — plague
- 瘟神 (wēn shén) — god of plague

## Example Sentences

- 历史上发生过多次瘟疫。(Lì shǐ shàng fā shēng guò duō cì wēn yì.) — Many plagues have occurred in history.

## Notes

瘟 has sickness 疒 with 温 (warm) phonetic, epidemic disease.
