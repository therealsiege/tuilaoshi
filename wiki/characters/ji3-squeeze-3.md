# 挤 To Squeeze

- **Character**: 挤
- **Pinyin**: jǐ
- **Meaning**: to squeeze, to crowd
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拥挤 (yōng jǐ) — crowded
- 挤压 (jǐ yā) — to squeeze

## Example Sentences

- 地铁里很拥挤。(Dì tiě lǐ hěn yōng jǐ.) — The subway is very crowded.

## Notes

挤 simplified from 擠. Hand 扌 pressing together.
