# 妄 Absurd

- **Character**: 妄
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: absurd, rash, false
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 妄想 (wàng xiǎng) — delusion
- 狂妄 (kuáng wàng) — arrogant

## Example Sentences

- 这种想法太妄想了。(Zhè zhǒng xiǎng fǎ tài wàng xiǎng le.) — This idea is too delusional.

## Notes

妄 combines 亡 (to lose/die) and 女 (woman), lost to reality.
