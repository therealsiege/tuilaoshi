# 倒 To Fall

- **Character**: 倒
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: to fall, to collapse, to pour
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 摔倒 (shuāi dǎo) — to fall down
- 倒塌 (dǎo tā) — to collapse

## Example Sentences

- 他在冰上摔倒了。(Tā zài bīng shàng shuāi dǎo le.) — He fell on the ice.

## Notes

倒 person 亻 plus 到 (to arrive), person falling down.
