# 老 Old/Prefix

- **Character**: 老
- **Pinyin**: lǎo
- **Meaning**: old, aged; always; prefix (familiar/respectful)
- **Radical**: 老 (old)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 老师 (lǎoshī) — teacher
- 老板 (lǎobǎn) — boss
- 老婆 (lǎopo) — wife (colloquial)
- 老公 (lǎogōng) — husband (colloquial)
- 老人 (lǎorén) — elderly person
- 老外 (lǎowài) — foreigner (informal)

## Example Sentences

- 我的老师很好。(Wǒ de lǎoshī hěn hǎo.) — My teacher is very good.
- 这个城市很老。(Zhè ge chéngshì hěn lǎo.) — This city is very old.
- 他老是迟到。(Tā lǎo shì chídào.) — He is always late.

## Notes

老 depicts a person with long hair leaning on a cane, representing old age. As a prefix, it creates familiar or affectionate terms: 老王 (Lǎo Wáng, "Old Wang" -- a friendly way to address someone surnamed Wang). In Chinese culture, 老 carries respect rather than negativity -- age implies wisdom and experience. The adverbial use meaning "always" is colloquial: 你老说这个 (nǐ lǎo shuō zhè ge, "you always say this").
