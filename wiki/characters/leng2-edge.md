# 棱 Edge, corner, angle

- **Character**: 棱
- **Pinyin**: léng
- **Meaning**: edge, corner, angle
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 棱角 (léng jiǎo) — edge and corner
- 棱镜 (léng jìng) — prism

## Example Sentences

- 正方体有十二条棱。(Zhèng fāng tǐ yǒu shí èr tiáo léng.) — A cube has twelve edges.
