# 痢 Dysentery

- **Character**: 痢
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: dysentery
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 痢疾 (lì ji) — dysentery
- 赤痢 (chì lì) — bloody dysentery

## Example Sentences

- 痢疾导致严重腹泻。(Lì ji dǎo zhì yán zhòng fù xiè.) — Dysentery causes severe diarrhea.

## Notes

Intestinal infection; serious GI illness.
