# 垫 Cushion

- **Character**: 垫
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: cushion, pad, to pad
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 垫子 (diàn zi) — cushion
- 坐垫 (zuò diàn) — seat cushion

## Example Sentences

- 椅子上放着一个坐垫。(Yǐ zi shàng fàng zhe yí gè zuò diàn.) — There is a cushion on the chair.

## Notes

垫 simplified from 墊. Earth 土 beneath, something to elevate.
