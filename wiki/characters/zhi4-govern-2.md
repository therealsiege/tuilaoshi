# 治 Govern, treat (medically), control

- **Character**: 治
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: govern,治疗, control
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 治理 (zhìlǐ) — govern, manage
- 治疗 (zhìliáo) — treat (medically)

## Example Sentences

- 政府治理环境污染。(Zhèngfǔ zhìlǐ huánjìng wūrǎn.) — The government manages environmental pollution.

## Notes

Used for governance and medical treatment.
