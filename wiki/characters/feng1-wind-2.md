# 风 Wind

- **Character**: 风
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: wind
- **Radical**: 風 (wind)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 大风 (dàfēng) — strong wind
- 台风 (táifēng) — typhoon

## Example Sentences

- 今天风很大。(Jīntiān fēng hěn dà.) — The wind is very strong today.

## Notes

Essential weather vocabulary. 台风 (typhoon) is important for coastal regions. Simplified from 風.
