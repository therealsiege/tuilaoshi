# 鸠 Turtledove, pigeon

- **Character**: 鸠
- **Pinyin**: jiū
- **Meaning**: turtledove, pigeon
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 斑鸠 (bān jiū) — turtledove
- 鸠占鹊巢 (jiū zhàn què cháo) — the dove occupying the magpie's nest

## Example Sentences

- 树上有一只斑鸠。(Shù shàng yǒu yī zhī bānjiū.) — There is a turtledove in the tree.

## Notes

Bird radical indicates bird type. Used in the idiom 鸠占鹊巢 (usurper).
