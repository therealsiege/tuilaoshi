# 换 To Exchange

- **Character**: 换
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: to exchange, to change
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 交换 (jiāo huàn) — to exchange
- 兑换 (duì huàn) — to convert

## Example Sentences

- 我想换一个新手机。(Wǒ xiǎng huàn yī gè xīn shǒu jī.) — I want to change to a new phone.

## Notes

换 shows hand 扌 and 奂 (spread), representing exchanging or replacing items.
