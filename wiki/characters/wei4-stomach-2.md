# 胃 Stomach

- **Character**: 胃
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: stomach, Stomach constellation
- **Radical**: 月(meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胃宿 (wèi xiù) — Stomach constellation
- 胃口 (wèi kǒu) — appetite

## Example Sentences

- 胃宿是西方白虎七宿之一。(Wèi xiù shì xīfāng báihǔ qī xiù zhī yī.) — Stomach is one of the seven mansions of the Western White Tiger.

## Notes

胃 means stomach. In astronomy, the Stomach constellation, one of the 28 lunar mansions.
