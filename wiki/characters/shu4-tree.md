# 树 Tree

- **Character**: 树
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: tree
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 树林 (shù lín) — woods, grove
- 果树 (guǒ shù) — fruit tree

## Example Sentences

- 这棵树很高。(Zhè kē shù hěn gāo.) — This tree is very tall.

## Notes

树 simplified from 樹, combines wood 木 with 寸 (hand measurement), suggesting a plant tall enough to be measured. 种树 means to plant trees.
