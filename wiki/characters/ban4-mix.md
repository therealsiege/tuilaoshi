# 拌 To mix, to toss (salad)

- **Character**: 拌
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to mix, to toss (salad)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir, to agitate
- 凉拌 (liáng bàn) — cold and dressed with sauce

## Example Sentences

- 这是凉拌黄瓜。(Zhè shì liángbàn huángguā.) — This is cold cucumber salad.

## Notes

Often used in cooking for mixing or tossing food.
