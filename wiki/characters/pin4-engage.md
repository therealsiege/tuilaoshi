# 聘 Engage, hire, invite

- **Character**: 聘
- **Pinyin**: pìn
- **Meaning**: engage, hire, invite
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 聘请 (pìn qǐng) — to engage, hire
- 招聘 (zhāo pìn) — to recruit

## Example Sentences

- 公司招聘新员工。(Gōng sī zhāo pìn xīn yuán gōng.) — The company is recruiting new employees.

## Notes

Formal term for hiring. 聘用 means to employ. 解聘 means to dismiss. 聘书 means letter of appointment.
