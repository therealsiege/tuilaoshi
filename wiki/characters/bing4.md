# 并 And

- **Character**: 并
- **Pinyin**: bìng
- **Meaning**: and, furthermore, to combine
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 并且 (bìng qiě) — and, moreover
- 合并 (hé bìng) — to merge

## Example Sentences

- 我并不同意这个观点。(Wǒ bìng bù tóng yì zhè gè guān diǎn.) — I don't agree with this viewpoint (emphatic).

## Notes

并 simplified from 並. Shows two people standing side by side, representing combination or simultaneity.
