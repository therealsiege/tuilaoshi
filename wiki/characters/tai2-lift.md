# 抬 To Lift

- **Character**: 抬
- **Pinyin**: tái
- **Meaning**: to lift, to raise, to carry together
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抬起 (tái qǐ) — to lift up
- 抬头 (tái tóu) — to raise one's head

## Example Sentences

- 请抬头看黑板。(Qǐng tái tóu kàn hēi bǎn.) — Please look up at the blackboard.

## Notes

抬 often implies two or more people lifting together, as it contains two hand radicals. 抬头 (raise head) vs 低头 (lower head).
