# 阻 To Block

- **Character**: 阻
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to block, resistance
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 电阻 (diàn zǔ) — resistance (electrical)
- 阻止 (zǔ zhǐ) — to prevent

## Example Sentences

- 这个电阻值不对。(Zhè ge diàn zǔ zhí bú duì.) — This resistance value is wrong.

## Notes

阻 with mound radical suggests obstruction, perfect for electrical resistance.
