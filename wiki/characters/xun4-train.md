# 训 To Train

- **Character**: 训
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: to train, to teach
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 训练 (xùn liàn) — to train
- 培训 (péi xùn) — to train

## Example Sentences

- 他每天训练两小时。(Tā měi tiān xùn liàn liǎng xiǎo shí.) — He trains two hours every day.

## Notes

训 simplified from 訓. Speech radical 讠 with 川 (river), representing instruction flowing like water.
