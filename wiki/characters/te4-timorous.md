# 忑 Timorous

- **Character**: 忑
- **Pinyin**: tè
- **Meaning**: timorous, nervous
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忐忑 (tǎn tè) — anxious, apprehensive

## Example Sentences

- 他忐忑不安地等待结果。(Tā tǎn tè bù ān de děng dài jié guǒ.) — He waited anxiously for the result.

## Notes

忑 shows down 下 over heart 心, paired with忐 to describe心 fluctuating with anxiety.
