# 艘 Measure word for ships

- **Character**: 艘
- **Pinyin**: sōu
- **Meaning**: measure word for ships
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 一艘船 (yī sōu chuán) — one ship
- 几艘 (jǐ sōu) — several (ships)

## Example Sentences

- 港口停泊着十艘货船。(Gǎng kǒu tíng bó zhe shí sōu huò chuán.) — Ten cargo ships are moored at the port.

## Notes

Specific measure word for ships and boats of all sizes.
