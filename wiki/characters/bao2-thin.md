# 薄 Thin

- **Character**: 薄
- **Pinyin**: báo
- **Meaning**: thin, flimsy; slight (bò)
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 薄弱 (bó ruò) — weak
- 薄片 (báo piàn) — thin slice

## Example Sentences

- 这张纸很薄。(Zhè zhāng zhǐ hěn báo.) — This paper is very thin.

## Notes

薄 is read báo for physical thinness and bó in more literary/abstract uses.
