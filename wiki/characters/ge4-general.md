# 个 General Measure Word

- **Character**: 个
- **Pinyin**: gè
- **Meaning**: general measure word
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 一个 (yī gè) — one (of something)
- 个人 (gè rén) — individual

## Example Sentences

- 我要三个苹果。(Wǒ yào sān gè píngguǒ.) — I want three apples.

## Notes

个 is the most common measure word in Chinese, used as a default when no specific measure word is known.
