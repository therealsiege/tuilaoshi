# 盘 Plate

- **Character**: 盘
- **Pinyin**: pán
- **Meaning**: plate, game, dribble
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盘球 (pán qiú) — to dribble (ball)
- 盘带 (pán dài) — to dribble

## Example Sentences

- 他盘球技术很好。(Tā pánqiú jìshù hěn hǎo.) — His dribbling skills are very good.

## Notes

Dribbling in football; also means plate and coil
