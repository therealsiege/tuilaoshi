# 撑 Support

- **Character**: 撑
- **Pinyin**: chēng
- **Meaning**: to support, prop up
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 支撑 (zhī chēng) — to support, prop up
- 撑竿跳 (chēng gān tiào) — pole vault

## Example Sentences

- 他做俯卧撑锻炼身体。(Tā zuò fǔwòchēng duànliàn shēntǐ.) — He does push-ups to exercise.

## Notes

Supporting with hands; push-ups and pole vault
