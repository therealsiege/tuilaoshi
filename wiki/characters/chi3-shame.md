# 耻 Shame, disgrace

- **Character**: 耻
- **Pinyin**: chǐ
- **Meaning**: shame, disgrace
- **Radical**: 耳(ear)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 羞耻 (xiū chǐ) — shame, disgrace
- 耻辱 (chǐ rǔ) — shame, humiliation

## Example Sentences

- 他认为说谎是可耻的。(Tā rènwéi shuōhuǎng shì kěchǐ de.) — He thinks lying is shameful.

## Notes

Refers to the feeling or state of shame.
