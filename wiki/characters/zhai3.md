# 窄 Narrow

- **Character**: 窄
- **Pinyin**: zhǎi
- **Meaning**: narrow, tight
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 狭窄 (xiá zhǎi) — narrow
- 窄小 (zhǎi xiǎo) — narrow and small

## Example Sentences

- 这条小巷很窄。(Zhè tiáo xiǎo xiàng hěn zhǎi.) — This alley is very narrow.

## Notes

窄 shows cave 穴 with 乍 (suddenly), representing a cramped or confined space.
