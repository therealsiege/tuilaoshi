# 避 To Avoid

- **Character**: 避
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to avoid, to evade
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 躲避 (duǒ bì) — to evade
- 避免 (bì miǎn) — to avoid

## Example Sentences

- 他躲避了攻击。(Tā duǒ bì le gōng jī.) — He evaded the attack.

## Notes

避 has walk 辶 with 辟 (to open), walking away to avoid.
