# 范 Pattern, Model, 范围 Scope

- **Character**: 范
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: pattern, model, 范围 scope
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 范围 (fàn wéi) — scope, range
- 模范 (mó fàn) — model, example
- 示范 (shì fàn) — demonstrate

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题超出了我的研究范围。(Zhège wèntí chāochū le wǒ de yánjiū fànwéi.) — This problem exceeds my research scope.

## Notes

Common in academic and formal contexts. 范围 (scope/range) is essential for defining boundaries and limits.
