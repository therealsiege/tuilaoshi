# 坡 Slope

- **Character**: 坡
- **Pinyin**: pō
- **Meaning**: slope, hillside
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 斜坡 (xié pō) — slope, incline
- 上坡 (shàng pō) — uphill

## Example Sentences

- 滑雪道的坡度很陡。(Huáxuědào de pōdù hěn dǒu.) — The ski slope is very steep.

## Notes

Slopes and inclines; terrain for skiing
