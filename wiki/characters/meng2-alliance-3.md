# 盟 Alliance

- **Character**: 盟
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: alliance, oath
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 联盟 (lián méng) — alliance
- 同盟 (tóng méng) — alliance

## Example Sentences

- 两国结成军事同盟。(Liǎng guó jié chéng jūn shì tóng méng.) — The two countries formed a military alliance.

## Notes

盟 combines 明 (bright) and 皿 (vessel), oath sworn over ritual vessel.
