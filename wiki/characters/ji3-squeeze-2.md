# 挤 To Squeeze

- **Character**: 挤
- **Pinyin**: jǐ
- **Meaning**: to squeeze, to press, to crowd
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拥挤 (yōng jǐ) — crowded
- 排挤 (pái jǐ) — to push aside

## Example Sentences

- 地铁里很挤。(Dì tiě lǐ hěn jǐ.) — The subway is very crowded.

## Notes

挤 describes squeezing or pressing together. Very common for describing crowded conditions.
