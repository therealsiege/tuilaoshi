# 篇 Article

- **Character**: 篇
- **Pinyin**: piān
- **Meaning**: piece (of writing), article
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 一篇文章 (yī piān wén zhāng) — an article
- 篇幅 (piān fú) — length (of writing)

## Example Sentences

- 我写了一篇作文。(Wǒ xiě le yī piān zuò wén.) — I wrote an essay.

## Notes

篇 is measure word for articles or essays. 长篇 = long (article).
