# 统 统一, unite, whole

- **Character**: 统
- **Pinyin**: tǒng
- **Meaning**: 统一, unite, whole
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 统计 (tǒng jì) — statistics
- 系统 (xì tǒng) — system

## Example Sentences

- 财务部统计本月收入。(Cái wù bù tǒng jì běn yuè shōu rù.) — The finance department tallies this month's income.

## Notes

统一 means unify. 传统 means tradition.
