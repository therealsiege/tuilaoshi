# 琵 琵琶 (pipa, first character)

- **Character**: 琵
- **Pinyin**: pí
- **Meaning**: 琵琶 (pipa, first character)
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琵琶 (pí pá) — pipa, Chinese lute
- 琵琶行 (Pí pá xíng) — Song of the Pipa

## Example Sentences

- 她弹奏的琵琶曲非常动听。(Tā tán zòu de pí pá qǔ fēi cháng dòng tīng.) — The pipa music she plays is very pleasant to hear.

## Notes

Always used in combination as 琵琶.
