# 救 Save

- **Character**: 救
- **Pinyin**: jiù
- **Meaning**: to save, rescue
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 救生 (jiù shēng) — lifesaving
- 救援 (jiù yuán) — to rescue

## Example Sentences

- 救生员及时救起了溺水者。(Jiùshēngyuán jíshí jiù qǐ le nìshuǐzhě.) — The lifeguard rescued the drowning person in time.

## Notes

Rescuing; lifesaving and emergency response
