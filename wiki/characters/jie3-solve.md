# 解 To Solve

- **Character**: 解
- **Pinyin**: jiě
- **Meaning**: to solve, to understand, to untie
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 了解 (liǎo jiě) — to understand
- 解决 (jiě jué) — to solve

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题已经解决了。(Zhè ge wèn tí yǐ jīng jiě jué le.) — This problem has been solved.

## Notes

解 originally meant to cut apart an ox with a knife. Extended to 'solve' and 'understand.'
