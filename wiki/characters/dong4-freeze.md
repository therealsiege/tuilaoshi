# 冻 Freeze

- **Character**: 冻
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: freeze, frostbite
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 冻伤 (dòng shāng) — frostbite
- 冰冻 (bīng dòng) — frozen
- 冻僵 (dòng jiāng) — frozen stiff

## Example Sentences

- 在雪地里太久会冻伤。(Zài xuě dì lǐ tài jiǔ huì dòng shāng.) — Staying in the snow too long can cause frostbite.

## Notes

Cold injury; damages tissue through freezing.
