# 害 To Harm

- **Character**: 害
- **Pinyin**: hài
- **Meaning**: to harm, harmful, to cause
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 害怕 (hài pà) — to be afraid
- 厉害 (lì hài) — formidable, severe

## Example Sentences

- 你太厉害了！(Nǐ tài lì hài le!) — You're so awesome!

## Notes

害 means harm. 厉害 colloquially means 'awesome/impressive' but literally means 'formidable.'
