# 频 Frequent

- **Character**: 频
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: frequent
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 频繁 (pín fán) — frequent
- 尿频 (niào pín) — frequent urination
- 频率 (pín lǜ) — frequency

## Example Sentences

- 夜间尿频影响睡眠。(Yè jiān niào pín yǐng xiǎng shuì mián.) — Frequent urination at night affects sleep.

## Notes

Repetition rate; describes symptom frequency.
