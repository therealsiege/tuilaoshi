# 落 To Fall

- **Character**: 落
- **Pinyin**: lào
- **Meaning**: to fall, to lag behind
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 落后 (luò hòu) — to lag behind
- 莲花落 (lián huā lào) — lotus-fall

## Example Sentences

- 莲花落是北方的说唱。(Lián huā lào shì běi fāng de shuō chàng.) — Lianhua lao is a northern narrative performance.

## Notes

落 (lào) in 莲花落; also read as luò meaning to fall.
