# 窥 To Peep

- **Character**: 窥
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: to peep, to spy on
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 窥探 (kuī tàn) — to spy on
- 窥视 (kuī shì) — to peek at

## Example Sentences

- 他从门缝窥视外面。(Tā cóng mén fèng kuī shì wài miàn.) — He peeked outside through the door crack.

## Notes

窥 simplified from 窺. Cave 穴 plus 规 (rule/compass), looking through an opening.
