# 宽 Wide

- **Character**: 宽
- **Pinyin**: kuān
- **Meaning**: wide, broad
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 带宽 (dài kuān) — bandwidth
- 宽度 (kuān dù) — width

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很宽。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn kuān.) — This road is very wide.

## Notes

宽 for width/broadness is essential in bandwidth and measurement contexts.
