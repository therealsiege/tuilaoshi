# 式 Style

- **Character**: 式
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: style, type, form, ceremony
- **Radical**: 弋 (shoot)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 形式 (xíng shì) — form
- 方式 (fāng shì) — way, manner

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢这种方式。(Wǒ xǐ huan zhè zhǒng fāng shì.) — I like this way/method.
