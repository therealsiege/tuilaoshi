# 剂 Dose

- **Character**: 剂
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: dose, preparation
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 剂量 (jì liàng) — dosage
- 药剂 (yào jì) — pharmaceutical preparation
- 针剂 (zhēn jì) — injection

## Example Sentences

- 请严格按照剂量服用。(Qǐng yán gé àn zhào jì liàng fú yòng.) — Please take strictly according to the dosage.

## Notes

Dosage form or amount; pharmaceutical term.
