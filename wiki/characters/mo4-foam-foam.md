# 沫 Foam

- **Character**: 沫
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: foam, froth, suds
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泡沫 (pào mò) — foam
- 唾沫 (tuò mò) — saliva

## Example Sentences

- 他吹出了很多泡沫。(Tā chuī chū le hěn duō pào mò.) — He blew many bubbles.

## Notes

沫 has water 氵 with 末 (end/fine), fine water droplets forming foam.
