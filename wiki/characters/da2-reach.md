# 达 Reach, attain, understand

- **Character**: 达
- **Pinyin**: dá
- **Meaning**: reach, attain, understand
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 达到 (dádào) — reach, attain
- 通达 (tōngdá) — understand, accessible

## Example Sentences

- 我们达到了目标。(Wǒmen dádào le mùbiāo.) — We reached our goal.

## Notes

Common character for reaching, attaining, or understanding.
