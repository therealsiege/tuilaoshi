# 舍 To give up, to abandon, to be willing to part with

- **Character**: 舍
- **Pinyin**: shě
- **Meaning**: to give up, to abandon, to舍得
- **Radical**: 舌(tongue)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 舍得 (shě de) — to be willing to part with
- 施舍 (shī shě) — to give alms, to charity

## Example Sentences

- 他舍不得离开家乡。(Tā shěbude líkāi jiāxiāng.) — He is reluctant to leave his hometown.

## Notes

Pronounced shě for giving up; shè for house/dwelling.
