# 诧 Surprised

- **Character**: 诧
- **Pinyin**: chà
- **Meaning**: to be surprised, to wonder
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诧异 (chà yì) — surprised
- 惊诧 (jīng chà) — astonished

## Example Sentences

- 她感到非常诧异。(Tā gǎn dào fēi cháng chà yì.) — She felt very surprised.

## Notes

诧 means surprised or astonished. More literary than 惊讶.
