# 鹉 Parrot

- **Character**: 鹉
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: parrot (in 鹦鹉)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 鹦鹉 (yīng wǔ) — parrot

## Example Sentences

- 这只鹦鹉很聪明。(Zhè zhī yīng wǔ hěn cōng míng.) — This parrot is very smart.

## Notes

鹉 simplified from 鵡. Bird 鸟 with 武 (martial), used only in 鹦鹉.
