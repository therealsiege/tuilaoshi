# 京 Capital

- **Character**: 京
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: capital, Beijing
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 北京 (běi jīng) — Beijing
- 京剧 (jīng jù) — Beijing opera

## Example Sentences

- 京剧是中国的国粹。(Jīng jù shì zhōng guó de guó cuì.) — Beijing opera is China's national treasure.

## Notes

京 means capital; 京剧 is Beijing opera, China's most famous opera style.
