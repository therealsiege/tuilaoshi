# 逮 To Arrest

- **Character**: 逮
- **Pinyin**: dǎi
- **Meaning**: to arrest, to catch
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 逮捕 (dài bǔ) — to arrest
- 逮住 (dǎi zhù) — to catch

## Example Sentences

- 警方逮捕了罪犯。(Jǐng fāng dài bǔ le zuì fàn.) — The police arrested the criminal.

## Notes

逮 as dǎi means to catch; as dài it's used in formal compounds like 逮捕 (arrest).
