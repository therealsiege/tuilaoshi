# 酥 Crispy, flaky

- **Character**: 酥
- **Pinyin**: sū
- **Meaning**: crispy, flaky
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 酥脆 (sū cuì) — crispy
- 酥饼 (sū bǐng) — crisp biscuit

## Example Sentences

- 这个饼干很酥很好吃。(Zhège bǐnggān hěn sū hěn hǎochī.) — This cookie is very crispy and delicious.

## Notes

Describes ideal texture for baked goods and fried foods.
