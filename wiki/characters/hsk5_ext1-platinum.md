# 铂 Platinum, 铂金 Platinum

- **Character**: 铂
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: platinum, 铂金 platinum
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 铂金 (bó jīn) — platinum
- 白金 (bái jīn) — platinum (colloquial)
- 铂族 (bó zú) — platinum group

## Example Sentences

- 铂金戒指价格昂贵。(Bójīn jièzhǐ jiàgé ángguì.) — Platinum rings are expensive.

## Notes

Precious metal platinum. 铂金 is standard term, while 白金 (white gold) is also used colloquially for platinum.
