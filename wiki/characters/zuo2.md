# 昨 Yesterday

- **Character**: 昨
- **Pinyin**: zuó
- **Meaning**: yesterday, past
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 昨天 (zuó tiān) — yesterday
- 昨晚 (zuó wǎn) — last night

## Example Sentences

- 昨天你去哪了？(Zuó tiān nǐ qù nǎ le?) — Where did you go yesterday?

## Notes

昨 uses 日 (sun/day) as semantic component and 乍 (zhà) as phonetic hint.
