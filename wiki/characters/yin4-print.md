# 印 Print, seal, mark

- **Character**: 印
- **Pinyin**: yìn
- **Meaning**: print, seal, mark
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 印象 (yìn xiàng) — impression
- 脚印 (jiǎo yìn) — footprint

## Example Sentences

- 这次旅行给我留下了深刻的印象。(Zhè cì lǚ xíng gěi wǒ liú xià le shēn kè de yìn xiàng.) — This trip left me with a deep impression.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: print, seal, impression, India.
