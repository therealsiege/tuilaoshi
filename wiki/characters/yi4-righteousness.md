# 义 Righteousness

- **Character**: 义
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: righteousness, justice, meaning
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 意义 (yì yì) — meaning, significance
- 正义 (zhèng yì) — justice

## Example Sentences

- 这项工作很有意义。(Zhè xiàng gōng zuò hěn yǒu yì yì.) — This work is very meaningful.

## Notes

义 simplified from 義. Originally depicted a ceremonial weapon, representing the moral obligation to act righteously.
