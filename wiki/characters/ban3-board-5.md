# 板 Splint

- **Character**: 板
- **Pinyin**: bǎn
- **Meaning**: board, plank
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 夹板 (jiā bǎn) — splint
- 木板 (mù bǎn) — wooden board
- 黑板 (hēi bǎn) — blackboard

## Example Sentences

- 夹板可以防止骨头移位。(Jiā bǎn kě yǐ fáng zhǐ gǔ tou yí wèi.) — A splint can prevent bone displacement.

## Notes

Rigid support; used in fracture management.
