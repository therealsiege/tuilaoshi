# 质 Quality

- **Character**: 质
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: quality, nature, substance
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 质量 (zhì liàng) — quality
- 物质 (wù zhì) — substance

## Example Sentences

- 产品质量很好。(Chǎn pǐn zhì liàng hěn hǎo.) — The product quality is very good.

## Notes

质 with shell/money radical suggests inherent value, essential for quality terminology.
