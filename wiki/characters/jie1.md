# 接 To Receive

- **Character**: 接
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: to receive, to connect, to catch, to meet
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 接受 (jiē shòu) — to accept
- 直接 (zhí jiē) — direct, directly

## Example Sentences

- 我去机场接你。(Wǒ qù jī chǎng jiē nǐ.) — I'll pick you up at the airport.

## Notes

接 has multiple related meanings: to receive, to connect/join, to catch (a ball), to meet/pick up someone.
