# 爸 Dad

- **Character**: 爸
- **Pinyin**: bà
- **Meaning**: father, dad
- **Radical**: 父 (father)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 爸爸 (bà ba) — dad
- 爸妈 (bà mā) — parents (informal)

## Example Sentences

- 我爸爸是医生。(Wǒ bà ba shì yī shēng.) — My dad is a doctor.

## Notes

爸 uses 父 (father) on top and 巴 (bā) as phonetic component. 爸爸 is the informal, affectionate word for father.
