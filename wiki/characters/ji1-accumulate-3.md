# 积 Accumulate, amass, product

- **Character**: 积
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: accumulate, amass, product
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 堆积 (duī jī) — accumulate, pile up
- 沉积 (chén jī) — sedimentation, deposit

## Example Sentences

- 河底堆积了大量泥沙。(Hé dǐ duī jī le dà liàng ní shā.) — A large amount of sediment has accumulated at the river bottom.

## Notes

Used for accumulation and sedimentation
