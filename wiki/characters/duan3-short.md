# 短 Short

- **Character**: 短
- **Pinyin**: duǎn
- **Meaning**: short (length)
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 短裤 (duǎn kù) — shorts
- 缩短 (suō duǎn) — to shorten

## Example Sentences

- 这条裙子太短了。(Zhè tiáo qún zi tài duǎn le.) — This skirt is too short.

## Notes

短 refers to short in length (opposite of 长). For short in height, use 矮 (ǎi).
