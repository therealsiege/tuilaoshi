# 验 Examine, test, prove effective

- **Character**: 验
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: examine, test, prove effective
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 检验 (jiǎnyàn) — test, examine
- 验证 (yànzhèng) — verify, validate

## Example Sentences

- 实验验证了这个理论。(Shíyàn yànzhèng le zhège lǐlùn.) — The experiment verified this theory.

## Notes

Common term for testing, examination, or verification.
