# 泵 Pump

- **Character**: 泵
- **Pinyin**: bèng
- **Meaning**: pump
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 水泵 (shuǐ bèng) — water pump
- 气泵 (qì bèng) — air pump

## Example Sentences

- 冷却系统需要一个泵。(Lěng què xì tǒng xū yào yī gè bèng.) — The cooling system needs a pump.

## Notes

Used in 水泵 (water pump), 真空泵 (vacuum pump), mechanical systems
