# 攀 Climb

- **Character**: 攀
- **Pinyin**: pān
- **Meaning**: to climb, clamber
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 攀登 (pān dēng) — to climb, scale
- 攀岩 (pān yán) — rock climbing

## Example Sentences

- 攀岩需要力量和技巧。(Pānyán xūyào lìliàng hé jìqiǎo.) — Rock climbing requires strength and skill.

## Notes

Climbing; rock climbing and mountaineering
