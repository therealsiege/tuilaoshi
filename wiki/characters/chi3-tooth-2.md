# 齿 Tooth

- **Character**: 齿
- **Pinyin**: chǐ
- **Meaning**: tooth, gear
- **Radical**: 齿 (tooth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 齿轮 (chǐ lún) — gear
- 牙齿 (yá chǐ) — tooth

## Example Sentences

- 齿轮坏了。(Chǐ lún huài le.) — The gear is broken.

## Notes

齿 originally meant tooth, naturally extends to gear teeth in machinery.
