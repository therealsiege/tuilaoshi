# 获 Obtain, capture, reap

- **Character**: 获
- **Pinyin**: huò
- **Meaning**: obtain, capture, reap
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 获得 (huòdé) — obtain, gain
- 捕获 (bǔhuò) — capture

## Example Sentences

- 他获得了诺贝尔奖。(Tā huòdé le Nuòbèi'ěr jiǎng.) — He won the Nobel Prize.

## Notes

Formal term for obtaining and capturing.
