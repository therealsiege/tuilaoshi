# 邃 Profound

- **Character**: 邃
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: deep, profound
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 深邃 (shēn suì) — deep, profound
- 幽邃 (yōu suì) — secluded and deep

## Example Sentences

- 他的眼神深邃。(Tā de yǎn shén shēn suì.) — His gaze is profound.

## Notes

邃 has walk 辶 with 穴 (cave), going deep into a cave, hence profound.
