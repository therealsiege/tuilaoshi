# 易 Easy

- **Character**: 易
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: easy, to change
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 容易 (róng yì) — easy
- 交易 (jiāo yì) — transaction, trade

## Example Sentences

- 说起来容易做起来难。(Shuō qǐ lái róng yì zuò qǐ lái nán.) — Easier said than done.

## Notes

易 originally meant to change/exchange. In 容易 means easy. 易经 (I Ching) = Book of Changes.
