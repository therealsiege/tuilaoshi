# 店 Shop

- **Character**: 店
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: shop, store
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 商店 (shāng diàn) — shop, store
- 饭店 (fàn diàn) — restaurant

## Example Sentences

- 这家店很大。(Zhè jiā diàn hěn dà.) — This shop is very big.

## Notes

店 means shop. 书店 = bookstore. 酒店 = hotel.
