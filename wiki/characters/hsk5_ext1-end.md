# 端 End, Proper, 端正 Upright

- **Character**: 端
- **Pinyin**: duān
- **Meaning**: end, proper, 端正 upright
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 端正 (duān zhèng) — upright, proper
- 弊端 (bì duān) — abuse, drawback
- 终端 (zhōng duān) — terminal

## Example Sentences

- 他态度端正。(Tā tàidù duānzhèng.) — His attitude is proper.

## Notes

Versatile character meaning end/extremity, or proper/upright. Used in both physical (终端 terminal) and moral (端正 upright) contexts.
