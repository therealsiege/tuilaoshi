# 控 Control, accuse, charge

- **Character**: 控
- **Pinyin**: kòng
- **Meaning**: control, accuse, charge
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 控制 (kòngzhì) — control
- 控诉 (kòngsù) — accuse, denounce

## Example Sentences

- 受害者控诉罪犯。(Shòuhàizhě kòngsù zuìfàn.) — The victim accused the criminal.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: control and legal accusation.
