# 幡 Banner, streamer

- **Character**: 幡
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: banner, streamer
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 幡然 (fān rán) — suddenly, abruptly
- 经幡 (jīng fān) — prayer flag

## Example Sentences

- 寺庙里挂着经幡。(Sì miào lǐ guà zhe jīng fān.) — Prayer flags hang in the temple.

## Notes

Less common in modern business. 幡然悔悟 means suddenly repent.
