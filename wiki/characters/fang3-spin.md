# 纺 To Spin

- **Character**: 纺
- **Pinyin**: fǎng
- **Meaning**: to spin (thread)
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纺织 (fǎng zhī) — spinning and weaving, textiles
- 纺纱 (fǎng shā) — to spin yarn

## Example Sentences

- 纺织业是重要产业。(Fǎngzhīyè shì zhòngyào chǎnyè.) — The textile industry is an important industry.

## Notes

纺 combines silk 糹 with 方 (square/direction), representing the directional twisting in spinning thread.
