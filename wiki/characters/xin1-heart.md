# 心 Heart

- **Character**: 心
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: heart, mind, center
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 开心 (kāi xīn) — happy
- 小心 (xiǎo xīn) — be careful

## Example Sentences

- 我很开心。(Wǒ hěn kāi xīn.) — I am very happy.

## Notes

心 is a pictograph of a heart. As a radical it appears as 忄 on the left or 心 at the bottom of characters related to emotions.
