# 瞅 To look at, to see, to watch

- **Character**: 瞅
- **Pinyin**: chǒu
- **Meaning**: to look at, to see, to watch
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瞅见 (chǒu jiàn) — to see, to catch sight of
- 瞅着 (chǒu zhe) — to look at

## Example Sentences

- 你瞅什么呢？(Nǐ chǒu shénme ne?) — What are you looking at?

## Notes

Colloquial northern dialect word for looking.
