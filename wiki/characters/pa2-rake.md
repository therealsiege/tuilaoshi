# 耙 Rake, harrow

- **Character**: 耙
- **Pinyin**: pá
- **Meaning**: rake, harrow
- **Radical**: 耒 (plow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耙子 (pá zi) — rake
- 钉耙 (dīng pá) — rake

## Example Sentences

- 用耙子把地耙平。(Yòng pá zi bǎ dì pá píng.) — Use a rake to level the ground.

## Notes

Agricultural tool for leveling soil
