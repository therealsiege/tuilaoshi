# 镀 Plate

- **Character**: 镀
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: plate, electroplate
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 镀金 (dù jīn) — gold-plated
- 电镀 (diàn dù) — electroplate

## Example Sentences

- 这个戒指是镀金的，不是纯金。(Zhège jièzhi shì dùjīn de, bù shì chún jīn.) — This ring is gold-plated, not pure gold.

## Notes

镀 means to plate or coat with metal, often for decoration or protection from corrosion.
