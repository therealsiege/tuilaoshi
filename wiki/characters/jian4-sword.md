# 剑 Sword

- **Character**: 剑
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: sword
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 刻舟求剑 (kè zhōu qiú jiàn) — cling to outdated methods
- 宝剑 (bǎo jiàn) — treasured sword

## Example Sentences

- 宝剑锋从磨砺出。(Bǎo jiàn fēng cóng mólì chū.) — A sharp sword comes from grinding.

## Notes

The sword in 刻舟求剑; also used in martial arts contexts.
