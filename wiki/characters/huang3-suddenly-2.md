# 恍 Suddenly

- **Character**: 恍
- **Pinyin**: huǎng
- **Meaning**: suddenly, as if, absent-minded
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恍然大悟 (huǎng rán dà wù) — suddenly realize
- 恍惚 (huǎng hū) — in a trance

## Example Sentences

- 他恍然大悟，终于明白了。(Tā huǎng rán dà wù, zhōngyú míngbai le.) — He suddenly realized and finally understood.

## Notes

Used in 恍然大悟: the sudden flash of understanding.
