# 兑 Exchange, convert, cash

- **Character**: 兑
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: exchange, convert, cash
- **Radical**: 儿 (son)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 兑换 (duì huàn) — to exchange, convert
- 兑现 (duì xiàn) — to cash, honor (promise)

## Example Sentences

- 我需要兑换一些外币。(Wǒ xū yào duì huàn yī xiē wài bì.) — I need to exchange some foreign currency.

## Notes

Essential in finance and currency exchange. 兑付 means to pay. 汇兑 means remittance.
