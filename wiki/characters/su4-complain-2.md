# 诉 Complain, sue, tell

- **Character**: 诉
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: complain, sue, tell
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诉讼 (sùsòng) — lawsuit, litigation
- 起诉 (qǐsù) — sue, prosecute

## Example Sentences

- 他向法院提起诉讼。(Tā xiàng fǎyuàn tíqǐ sùsòng.) — He filed a lawsuit with the court.

## Notes

Legal term for lawsuits and complaints.
