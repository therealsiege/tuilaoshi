# 录 To Record

- **Character**: 录
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: to record, to copy
- **Radical**: 彐 (snout)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 记录 (jì lù) — to record
- 录音 (lù yīn) — to record sound

## Example Sentences

- 请把会议内容录下来。(Qǐng bǎ huì yì nèi róng lù xià lái.) — Please record the meeting content.

## Notes

录 simplified from 錄. Originally meaning to copy or transcribe information.
