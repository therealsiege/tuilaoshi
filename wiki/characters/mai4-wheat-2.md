# 麦 Wheat, barley, oats

- **Character**: 麦
- **Pinyin**: mài
- **Meaning**: wheat, barley, oats
- **Radical**: 麦 (wheat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 小麦 (xiǎo mài) — wheat
- 麦子 (mài zi) — wheat

## Example Sentences

- 北方以种植小麦为主。(Běifāng yǐ zhòngzhí xiǎomài wéi zhǔ.) — The north primarily cultivates wheat.

## Notes

Wheat and related grain crops. Major staple in northern China, contrasting with rice in the south.
