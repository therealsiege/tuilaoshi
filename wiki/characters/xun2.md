# 循 To Follow

- **Character**: 循
- **Pinyin**: xún
- **Meaning**: to follow, to abide by
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 循环 (xún huán) — to circulate, cycle
- 遵循 (zūn xún) — to follow, to abide by

## Example Sentences

- 我们要遵循自然规律。(Wǒ men yào zūn xún zì rán guī lǜ.) — We must follow natural laws.

## Notes

循 combines stepping 彳 and 盾 (shield), representing following a protected path or established pattern.
