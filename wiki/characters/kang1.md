# 康 Healthy, well-being, peace

- **Character**: 康
- **Pinyin**: kāng
- **Meaning**: healthy, well-being, peace
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 健康 (jiàn kāng) — healthy
- 康复 (kāng fù) — recover

## Example Sentences

- 保持健康的生活方式很重要。(Bǎo chí jiàn kāng de shēng huó fāng shì hěn zhòng yào.) — Maintaining a healthy lifestyle is important.
