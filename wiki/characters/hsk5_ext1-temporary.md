# 暂 Temporary, For The Time Being, 暂时 Temporary

- **Character**: 暂
- **Pinyin**: zàn
- **Meaning**: temporary, for the time being, 暂时 temporary
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 暂时 (zàn shí) — temporary, for now
- 暂且 (zàn qiě) — for the moment
- 短暂 (duǎn zàn) — brief, short

## Example Sentences

- 我暂时住在朋友家。(Wǒ zànshí zhù zài péngyǒu jiā.) — I'm temporarily staying at a friend's house.

## Notes

Common term for temporary or provisional status. 暂时 is very frequently used for temporary situations.
