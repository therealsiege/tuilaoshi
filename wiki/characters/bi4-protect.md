# 庇 Protect, shelter, cover

- **Character**: 庇
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: protect, shelter, cover
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 庇护 (bì hù) — shelter, protect
- 庇佑 (bì yòu) — bless and protect

## Example Sentences

- 愿神灵庇佑你。(Yuàn shén líng bì yòu nǐ.) — May the gods bless and protect you.

## Notes

Divine or human protection
