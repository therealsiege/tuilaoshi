# 肠 Intestine, bowel

- **Character**: 肠
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: intestine, bowel
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肠道 (cháng dào) — intestinal tract
- 大肠 (dà cháng) — large intestine

## Example Sentences

- 肠道健康对人体很重要。(Cháng dào jiàn kāng duì rén tǐ hěn zhòng yào.) — Intestinal health is very important to the human body.
