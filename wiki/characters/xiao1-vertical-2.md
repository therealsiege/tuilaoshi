# 箫 Bamboo Flute

- **Character**: 箫
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: vertical bamboo flute
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 洞箫 (dòng xiāo) — vertical flute
- 排箫 (pái xiāo) — panpipes

## Example Sentences

- 箫声悠扬动听。(Xiāo shēng yōu yáng dòng tīng.) — The flute sound is melodious and pleasant.

## Notes

箫 is an end-blown flute, different from the transverse 笛.
