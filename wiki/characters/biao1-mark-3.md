# 标 Mark

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, standard
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 标准 (biāo zhǔn) — standard
- 目标 (mù biāo) — goal, target

## Example Sentences

- 你的目标是什么？(Nǐ de mù biāo shì shén me?) — What is your goal?

## Notes

标 means sign, mark, or standard. 标志 = symbol/logo, 标题 = title/headline.
