# 背 To Carry On Back

- **Character**: 背
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: to carry on back, back (body part)
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 背包 (bēi bāo) — backpack
- 背后 (bèi hòu) — behind

## Example Sentences

- 他背着书包上学。(Tā bēi zhe shū bāo shàng xué.) — He carries a backpack to school.

## Notes

背 shows person carrying on back, can mean to carry (bēi) or back (bèi).
