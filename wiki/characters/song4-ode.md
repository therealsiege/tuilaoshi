# 颂 Ode, eulogy, praise

- **Character**: 颂
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: ode, eulogy, praise
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 颂扬 (sòng yáng) — praise, extol
- 赞颂 (zàn sòng) — praise, eulogize

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗颂扬了劳动人民的伟大。(Zhè shǒu shī sòngyáng le láodòng rénmín de wěidà.) — This poem praises the greatness of working people.

## Notes

One of the four categories in the Book of Songs (诗经). Represents formal praise and eulogy.
