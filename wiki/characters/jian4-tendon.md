# 腱 Tendon, sinew

- **Character**: 腱
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: tendon, sinew
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 肌腱 (jī jiàn) — tendon
- 腱鞘 (jiàn qiào) — tendon sheath

## Example Sentences

- 运动员的肌腱拉伤了。(Yùndòngyuán de jījiàn lāshāng le.) — The athlete strained a tendon.

## Notes

Anatomical term for tendons connecting muscle to bone.
