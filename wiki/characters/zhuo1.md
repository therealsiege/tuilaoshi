# 桌 Table

- **Character**: 桌
- **Pinyin**: zhuō
- **Meaning**: table, desk
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 桌子 (zhuō zi) — table
- 书桌 (shū zhuō) — desk

## Example Sentences

- 书在桌子上。(Shū zài zhuō zi shàng.) — The book is on the table.

## Notes

桌 uses the wood radical 木 because tables are traditionally made of wood. Measure word is 张 (zhāng).
