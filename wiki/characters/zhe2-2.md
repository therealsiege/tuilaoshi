# 折 折扣, break, discount

- **Character**: 折
- **Pinyin**: zhé
- **Meaning**: 折扣, break, discount
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 折扣 (zhé kòu) — discount
- 打折 (dǎ zhé) — give a discount

## Example Sentences

- 商店打八折。(Shāng diàn dǎ bā zhé.) — The store gives a 20% discount.

## Notes

Note: 八折 = 80% of price = 20% off. 折旧 means depreciation.
