# 绿 Green

- **Character**: 绿
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: green
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绿色 (lǜ sè) — green color
- 绿茶 (lǜ chá) — green tea

## Example Sentences

- 春天树叶变绿了。(Chūn tiān shù yè biàn lǜ le.) — The tree leaves turn green in spring.

## Notes

绿 uses the silk radical, reflecting the production of green dye from silk fabrics. Symbolizes nature, life, and environmental protection.
