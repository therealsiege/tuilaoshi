# 沉 To Sink

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: to sink, to submerge, heavy, deep
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沉默 (chén mò) — silent
- 下沉 (xià chén) — to sink

## Example Sentences

- 石头沉到水底了。(Shí tou chén dào shuǐ dǐ le.) — The stone sank to the bottom of the water.

## Notes

沉 means to sink or submerge. Also describes沉y or deep states (沉默 silence, 沉思 contemplation).
