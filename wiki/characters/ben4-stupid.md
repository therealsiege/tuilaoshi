# 笨 Stupid

- **Character**: 笨
- **Pinyin**: bèn
- **Meaning**: stupid, clumsy
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 笨拙 (bèn zhuō) — clumsy
- 愚笨 (yú bèn) — stupid

## Example Sentences

- 别叫我笨。(Bié jiào wǒ bèn.) — Don't call me stupid.

## Notes

笨 shows bamboo 竹 and 本 (origin/base), originally heavy bamboo, extended to dull or clumsy.
