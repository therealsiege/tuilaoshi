# 携 To carry, to take along, to bring

- **Character**: 携
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: to carry, to take along, to bring
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 携带 (xié dài) — to carry, to bring
- 携手 (xié shǒu) — hand in hand, to work together

## Example Sentences

- 请勿携带危险物品上飞机。(Qǐng wù xiédài wēixiǎn wùpǐn shàng fēijī.) — Please do not bring dangerous items onto the plane.

## Notes

More formal than 带, often used in official contexts.
