# 桶 Bucket

- **Character**: 桶
- **Pinyin**: tǒng
- **Meaning**: bucket, barrel
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 水桶 (shuǐtǒng) — water bucket
- 垃圾桶 (lājītǒng) — trash can

## Example Sentences

- 请把垃圾扔进垃圾桶。(Qǐng bǎ lājī rēng jìn lājītǒng.) — Please throw the trash in the trash can.

## Notes

Common household container. 垃圾桶 (trash can) is essential vocabulary. Also measure word.
