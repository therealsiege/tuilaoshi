# 饮 Drink, beverage

- **Character**: 饮
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: drink, beverage
- **Radical**: 食 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 饮料 (yǐn liào) — beverage, drink
- 饮酒 (yǐn jiǔ) — drink wine

## Example Sentences

- 他一饮而尽。(Tā yī yǐn ér jìn.) — He drank it down in one gulp.

## Notes

Formal term for drinking; 喝 is more colloquial.
