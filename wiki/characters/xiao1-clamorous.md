# 嚣 Clamorous, arrogant

- **Character**: 嚣
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: clamorous, arrogant
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 嚣张 (xiāozhāng) — arrogant, aggressive
- 甚嚣尘上 (shènxiāo chénshàng) — clamorous, rampant

## Example Sentences

- 他的态度很嚣张。(Tā de tàidù hěn xiāozhāng.) — His attitude is very arrogant.

## Notes

Literary term for clamor or arrogance.
