# 巍 Lofty

- **Character**: 巍
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: lofty, towering, majestic
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 巍峨 (wēi é) — towering
- 巍然 (wēi rán) — imposingly

## Example Sentences

- 巍峨的山峰耸入云天。(Wēi é de shān fēng sǒng rù yún tiān.) — The towering peaks rise into the clouds.

## Notes

巍 has mountain 山 at top with 魏 (Wei kingdom), grandly imposing.
