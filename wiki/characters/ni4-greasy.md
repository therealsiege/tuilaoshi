# 腻 Greasy, smooth, bored with, 膩 (traditional)

- **Character**: 腻
- **Pinyin**: nì
- **Meaning**: greasy, smooth, bored with, 膩 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 油腻 (yóu nì) — greasy, oily
- 细腻 (xì nì) — fine, delicate

## Example Sentences

- 这道菜太油腻了。(Zhè dào cài tài yóunì le.) — This dish is too greasy.

## Notes

Greasy texture or feeling fed up with something.
