# 妨 To hinder, harm, prevent

- **Character**: 妨
- **Pinyin**: fáng
- **Meaning**: to hinder, harm, prevent
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 妨碍 (fáng ài) — to hinder, obstruct
- 无妨 (wú fáng) — no harm, might as well

## Example Sentences

- 试一试也无妨。(Shì yī shì yě wúfáng.) — There's no harm in trying.

## Notes

To hinder or prevent, also 'no harm' in 无妨.
