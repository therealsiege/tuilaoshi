# 量 Quantity

- **Character**: 量
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: quantity, amount, to measure
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 数量 (shù liàng) — quantity
- 质量 (zhì liàng) — quality

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要增加产量。(Wǒ men xū yào zēng jiā chǎn liàng.) — We need to increase production quantity.

## Notes

量 can be pronounced liàng (quantity, to measure) or liáng (to measure). Paired with 质 in 质量 (quality).
