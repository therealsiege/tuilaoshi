# 锣 Gong

- **Character**: 锣
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: gong
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锣鼓 (luó gǔ) — gongs and drums
- 敲锣 (qiāo luó) — beat a gong

## Example Sentences

- 锣鼓喧天庆祝节日。(Luó gǔ xuān tiān qìng zhù jié rì.) — Gongs and drums resound to celebrate the festival.

## Notes

Traditional percussion instrument in Chinese music.
