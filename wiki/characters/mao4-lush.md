# 茂 Lush

- **Character**: 茂
- **Pinyin**: mào
- **Meaning**: lush, luxuriant
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 茂盛 (mào shèng) — lush
- 茂密 (mào mì) — dense (vegetation)

## Example Sentences

- 这片森林很茂密。(Zhè piàn sēn lín hěn mào mì.) — This forest is very dense.

## Notes

茂 has grass 艹 over 戊 (fifth heavenly stem), flourishing vegetation.
