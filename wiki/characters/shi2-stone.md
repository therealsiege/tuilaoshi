# 石 Stone

- **Character**: 石
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: stone, rock
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 石头 (shí tou) — stone, rock
- 宝石 (bǎo shí) — gem, precious stone

## Example Sentences

- 这块石头很重。(Zhè kuài shí tou hěn zhòng.) — This stone is very heavy.

## Notes

石 depicts a stone under a cliff (厂). It's both a character and a common radical in stone-related characters.
