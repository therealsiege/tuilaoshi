# 瞧 To look, to see

- **Character**: 瞧
- **Pinyin**: qiáo
- **Meaning**: to look, to see
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 瞧见 (qiáo jiàn) — to see, to catch sight of
- 瞧不起 (qiáo bu qǐ) — to look down on

## Example Sentences

- 你瞧，那是什么？(Nǐ qiáo, nà shì shénme?) — Look, what's that?

## Notes

Colloquial for看 (to look/see).
