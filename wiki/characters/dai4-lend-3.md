# 贷 Loan

- **Character**: 贷
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: to lend, to borrow, loan
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan
- 信贷 (xìn dài) — credit

## Example Sentences

- 他向银行贷款买房。(Tā xiàng yín háng dài kuǎn mǎi fáng.) — He took out a bank loan to buy a house.

## Notes

贷 combines 代 (substitute) and 贝 (money).
