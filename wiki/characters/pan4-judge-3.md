# 判 To Judge

- **Character**: 判
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: to judge, to sentence
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge
- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee

## Example Sentences

- 你不能轻易判断别人。(Nǐ bù néng qīng yì pàn duàn bié rén.) — You cannot easily judge others.

## Notes

判 combines 半 (half) and knife 刂, originally meaning to cut in half/decide.
