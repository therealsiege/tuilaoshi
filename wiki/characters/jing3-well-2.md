# 井 Well

- **Character**: 井
- **Pinyin**: jǐng
- **Meaning**: well (water)
- **Radical**: 二 (two)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 井底之蛙 (jǐng dǐ zhī wā) — narrow-minded person
- 水井 (shuǐ jǐng) — water well

## Example Sentences

- 不要做井底之蛙。(Bú yào zuò jǐng dǐ zhī wā.) — Don't be a frog at the bottom of a well.

## Notes

Used in 井底之蛙: the well that limits the frog's view of the sky.
