# 检 Examine

- **Character**: 检
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: examine, inspect
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — to examine, inspect
- 体检 (tǐ jiǎn) — physical examination
- 检测 (jiǎn cè) — to test, detect

## Example Sentences

- 定期体检很重要。(Dìng qī tǐ jiǎn hěn zhòng yào.) — Regular physical examinations are very important.

## Notes

Inspection and testing; diagnostic process.
