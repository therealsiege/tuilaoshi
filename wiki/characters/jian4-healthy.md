# 健 Healthy

- **Character**: 健
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: healthy, strong
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 健康 (jiàn kāng) — healthy, health
- 健身 (jiàn shēn) — fitness, to work out

## Example Sentences

- 运动使人身体健康。(Yùndòng shǐ rén shēntǐ jiànkāng.) — Exercise makes people healthy.

## Notes

Health and fitness; core concept in exercise and wellness
