# 鸥 Seagull

- **Character**: 鸥
- **Pinyin**: ōu
- **Meaning**: seagull
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 海鸥 (hǎi ōu) — seagull
- 鸥鸟 (ōu niǎo) — gull

## Example Sentences

- 海边有许多海鸥在飞翔。(Hǎi biān yǒu xǔ duō hǎi ōu zài fēi xiáng.) — Many seagulls are flying by the sea.

## Notes

Symbol of freedom and coastal nature
