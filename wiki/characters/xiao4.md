# 笑 To Laugh

- **Character**: 笑
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: to laugh, to smile
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 笑话 (xiào huà) — joke
- 微笑 (wēi xiào) — smile

## Example Sentences

- 她总是在笑。(Tā zǒng shì zài xiào.) — She is always smiling.

## Notes

笑 has bamboo (竹) on top and 夭 below. The bamboo suggests the sound of laughter rustling like bamboo.
