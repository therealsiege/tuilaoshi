# 哀 Grief

- **Character**: 哀
- **Pinyin**: āi
- **Meaning**: grief, sorrow, to pity
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 悲哀 (bēi āi) — grieved
- 哀悼 (āi dào) — to mourn

## Example Sentences

- 我们对他的去世深表哀悼。(Wǒ men duì tā de qù shì shēn biǎo āi dào.) — We deeply mourn his passing.

## Notes

哀 shows mouth 口 beneath clothing, depicting someone crying out in grief with lowered head.
