# 许 To Allow

- **Character**: 许
- **Pinyin**: xǔ
- **Meaning**: to allow, perhaps, to promise
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 也许 (yě xǔ) — perhaps
- 许多 (xǔ duō) — many

## Example Sentences

- 也许他不来了。(Yě xǔ tā bù lái le.) — Perhaps he won't come.

## Notes

许 means to permit or perhaps. 也许 = maybe/perhaps. 许多 = many.
