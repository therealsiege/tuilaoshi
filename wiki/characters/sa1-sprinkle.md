# 撒 To sprinkle, to scatter

- **Character**: 撒
- **Pinyin**: sā
- **Meaning**: to sprinkle, to scatter
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撒盐 (sā yán) — to sprinkle salt
- 撒谎 (sā huǎng) — to lie, to tell a lie

## Example Sentences

- 在菜上撒一点葱花。(Zài cài shàng sā yīdiǎn cōnghuā.) — Sprinkle some chopped scallions on the dish.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: sā (sprinkle) and sǎ (let go). Hand radical.
