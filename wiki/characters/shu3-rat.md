# 鼠 Rat

- **Character**: 鼠
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: rat, mouse
- **Radical**: 鼠 (rat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 老鼠 (lǎo shǔ) — rat, mouse
- 鼠年 (shǔ nián) — Year of the Rat

## Example Sentences

- 我看见了一只老鼠。(Wǒ kàn jiàn le yī zhī lǎo shǔ.) — I saw a mouse.

## Notes

鼠 is first animal in Chinese zodiac. 老鼠 adds 老 prefix like 老虎.
