# 煤 Coal

- **Character**: 煤
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: coal
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 煤炭 (méi tàn) — coal
- 煤矿 (méi kuàng) — coal mine

## Example Sentences

- 煤是重要的能源。(Méi shì zhòng yào de néng yuán.) — Coal is an important energy source.

## Notes

煤 means coal. Used in煤炭 (coal), 煤矿 (coal mine), 煤气 (coal gas).
