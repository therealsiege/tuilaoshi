# 瑕 Flaw, blemish in jade

- **Character**: 瑕
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: flaw, blemish in jade
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 瑕疵 (xiá cī) — flaw, defect
- 白璧微瑕 (bái bì wēi xiá) — a slight flaw in an otherwise perfect thing

## Example Sentences

- 这件作品略有瑕疵，但瑕不掩瑜。(Zhè jiàn zuò pǐn lüè yǒu xiá cī, dàn xiá bù yǎn yú.) — This work has minor flaws, but the merits outweigh the defects.

## Notes

Originally meant flaw in jade; now means any defect.
