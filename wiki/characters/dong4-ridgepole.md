# 栋 Ridgepole, building, measure word

- **Character**: 栋
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: ridgepole, building, measure word
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栋梁 (dòng liáng) — pillar, backbone
- 一栋楼 (yī dòng lóu) — one building

## Example Sentences

- 他是国家的栋梁之才。(Tā shì guó jiā de dòng liáng zhī cái.) — He is a pillar of the nation.

## Notes

Literal meaning is ridgepole; metaphorically means pillar or backbone.
