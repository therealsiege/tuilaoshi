# 矿 Mine, Mineral, 矿产 Mineral Resources

- **Character**: 矿
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: mine, mineral, 矿产 mineral resources
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 矿产 (kuàng chǎn) — mineral resources
- 矿山 (kuàng shān) — mine
- 煤矿 (méi kuàng) — coal mine

## Example Sentences

- 这个地区矿产资源丰富。(Zhège dìqū kuàngchǎn zīyuán fēngfù.) — This area is rich in mineral resources.

## Notes

Industrial term for mines and minerals. 矿产 (mineral resources) is key term in resource and economic discussions.
