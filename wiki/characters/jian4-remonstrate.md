# 谏 Remonstrate, admonish

- **Character**: 谏
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: remonstrate, admonish
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 进谏 (jìnjiàn) — offer advice to superior
- 谏言 (jiànyán) — words of admonition

## Example Sentences

- 古代忠臣常向君王进谏。(Gǔdài zhōngchén cháng xiàng jūnwáng jìnjiàn.) — Ancient loyal ministers often remonstrated with the emperor.

## Notes

Classical term for advisors offering frank advice to rulers.
