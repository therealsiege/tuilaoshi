# 尝 To Taste

- **Character**: 尝
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: to taste, to try, to experience
- **Radical**: 小 (small)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 尝试 (cháng shì) — to try
- 品尝 (pǐn cháng) — to taste

## Example Sentences

- 你尝尝这个菜。(Nǐ cháng cháng zhè ge cài.) — Try this dish.

## Notes

尝 means to taste or try. 尝试 = to attempt. 尝尝 (reduplicated) means 'have a taste.'
