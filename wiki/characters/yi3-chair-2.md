# 椅 Chair

- **Character**: 椅
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: chair
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 椅子 (yǐ zi) — chair
- 轮椅 (lún yǐ) — wheelchair

## Example Sentences

- 请坐椅子。(Qǐng zuò yǐ zi.) — Please sit on the chair.

## Notes

椅 with wood radical 木. 椅子 standard word for chair. Furniture made from wood.
