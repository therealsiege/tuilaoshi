# 悔 To Regret

- **Character**: 悔
- **Pinyin**: huǐ
- **Meaning**: to regret, repent
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 后悔 (hòu huǐ) — to regret
- 忏悔 (chàn huǐ) — to repent, confess

## Example Sentences

- 他很后悔当初的决定。(Tā hěn hòuhuǐ dāngchū de juédìng.) — He regrets his decision back then.

## Notes

悔 combines heart 心 with 每 (each), suggesting repeated mental reflection on past actions.
