# 涧 Mountain stream, ravine

- **Character**: 涧
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: mountain stream, ravine
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 山涧 (shān jiàn) — mountain stream
- 溪涧 (xī jiàn) — stream

## Example Sentences

- 山涧里流淌着清泉。(Shān jiàn lǐ liú tǎng zhe qīng quán.) — Clear springs flow in the mountain stream.

## Notes

Stream between mountains
