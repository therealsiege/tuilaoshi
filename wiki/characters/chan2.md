# 缠 To wind around, to wrap, to pester

- **Character**: 缠
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: to wind around, to wrap, to pester
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 纠缠 (jiū chán) — to be entangled, to pester
- 缠绕 (chán rào) — to twine, to wind

## Example Sentences

- 藤蔓缠绕在树上。(Téngwàn chánrào zài shù shang.) — The vines wind around the tree.

## Notes

Can mean physical wrapping or persistent bothering.
