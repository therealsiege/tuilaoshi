# 漆 Lacquer

- **Character**: 漆
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: lacquer, paint, to paint
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 油漆 (yóu qī) — paint
- 漆黑 (qī hēi) — pitch-black

## Example Sentences

- 墙上刷了油漆。(Qiáng shàng shuā le yóu qī.) — The wall has been painted.

## Notes

漆 shows water 氵 representing liquid lacquer or paint coating.
