# 膏 Ointment

- **Character**: 膏
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: ointment, paste, grease
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 药膏 (yào gāo) — ointment
- 牙膏 (yá gāo) — toothpaste

## Example Sentences

- 他涂了一些药膏。(Tā tú le yì xiē yào gāo.) — He applied some ointment.

## Notes

膏 has 高 (tall) over meat 月, rich fatty layer.
