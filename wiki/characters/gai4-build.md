# 盖 Build, cover

- **Character**: 盖
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: build, cover
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盖房 (gài fáng) — build a house
- 覆盖 (fù gài) — cover

## Example Sentences

- 他们打算盖新房。(Tā men dǎ suàn gài xīn fáng.) — They plan to build a new house.

## Notes

Also means affix seal (盖章). 重盖 means rebuild.
