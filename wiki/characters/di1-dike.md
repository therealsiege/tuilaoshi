# 堤 Dike, embankment, levee

- **Character**: 堤
- **Pinyin**: dī
- **Meaning**: dike, embankment, levee
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 堤坝 (dī bà) — dam, embankment
- 河堤 (hé dī) — river embankment

## Example Sentences

- 河堤保护城市免受洪水侵袭。(Hé dī bǎo hù chéng shì miǎn shòu hóng shuǐ qīn xí.) — The river embankment protects the city from floods.
