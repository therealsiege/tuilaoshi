# 淹 To Flood

- **Character**: 淹
- **Pinyin**: yān
- **Meaning**: to flood, to submerge, to drown
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淹没 (yān mò) — to submerge
- 淹死 (yān sǐ) — to drown

## Example Sentences

- 洪水淹没了村庄。(Hóng shuǐ yān mò le cūn zhuāng.) — The flood submerged the village.

## Notes

淹 has water 氵 with 奄 (suddenly), sudden flooding.
