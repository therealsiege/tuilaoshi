# 质 Quality

- **Character**: 质
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: quality, nature, substance
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 质量 (zhì liàng) — quality
- 本质 (běn zhì) — essence

## Example Sentences

- 这个产品质量很好。(Zhè ge chǎn pǐn zhì liàng hěn hǎo.) — This product is of good quality.

## Notes

质 simplified from 質. Shell 贝 value, essential nature.
