# 壶 Pot

- **Character**: 壶
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: pot, kettle, flask
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 茶壶 (chá hú) — teapot
- 水壶 (shuǐ hú) — kettle

## Example Sentences

- 茶壶里的水开了。(Chá hú lǐ de shuǐ kāi le.) — The water in the teapot is boiling.

## Notes

壶 simplified from 壺. Depicts a vessel for holding liquids.
