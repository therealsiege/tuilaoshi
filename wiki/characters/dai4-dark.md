# 黛 Dark Blue

- **Character**: 黛
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: dark blue-black, eyebrow
- **Radical**: 黑 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 黛绿 (dài lǜ) — dark green
- 远黛 (yuǎn dài) — distant mountains

## Example Sentences

- 远山如黛，很美。(Yuǎn shān rú dài, hěn měi.) — The distant mountains are dark blue-green and beautiful.

## Notes

黛 has black 黑 with 代 (to replace), dark blue-black pigment.
