# 航 To Navigate

- **Character**: 航
- **Pinyin**: háng
- **Meaning**: to navigate, to sail, aviation
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 航天 (háng tiān) — space flight
- 航空 (háng kōng) — aviation

## Example Sentences

- 这是一次航天任务。(Zhè shì yí cì háng tiān rèn wu.) — This is a space mission.

## Notes

航 has boat 舟 with 亢 (high), sailing through air or water.
