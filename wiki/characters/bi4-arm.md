# 臂 Arm

- **Character**: 臂
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: arm
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 手臂 (shǒubì) — arm
- 臂膀 (bìbǎng) — arm

## Example Sentences

- 他的手臂很强壮。(Tā de shǒubì hěn qiángzhuàng.) — His arms are very strong.

## Notes

Usually appears as 手臂 or 臂膀. The 月 radical indicates a body part.
