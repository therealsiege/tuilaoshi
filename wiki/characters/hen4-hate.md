# 恨 To Hate

- **Character**: 恨
- **Pinyin**: hèn
- **Meaning**: to hate, regret
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 仇恨 (chóu hèn) — hatred
- 痛恨 (tòng hèn) — to hate bitterly

## Example Sentences

- 我恨透了这种天气。(Wǒ hèntòu le zhè zhǒng tiānqì.) — I absolutely hate this kind of weather.

## Notes

恨 combines 艮 (stubborn) and heart 心, representing stubborn negative feelings — hatred or regret.
