# 清 Clear, pure, clean

- **Character**: 清
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: clear, pure, clean
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 清澈 (qīng chè) — clear, limpid
- 清晰 (qīng xī) — clear, distinct

## Example Sentences

- 这里的水很清澈。(Zhè lǐ de shuǐ hěn qīng chè.) — The water here is very clear.

## Notes

Transparency and purity
