# 迹 Trace, track, sign

- **Character**: 迹
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: trace, track, sign
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 痕迹 (hén jì) — trace, mark
- 足迹 (zú jì) — footprint, track

## Example Sentences

- 雪地上留下了一串脚印的痕迹。(Xuě dì shàng liú xià le yī chuàn jiǎo yìn de hén jì.) — A series of footprint traces were left in the snow.

## Notes

Also used for historical sites (古迹).
