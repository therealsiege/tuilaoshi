# 感 To Feel

- **Character**: 感
- **Pinyin**: gǎn
- **Meaning**: to feel, feeling, sense
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 感觉 (gǎn jué) — feeling
- 感谢 (gǎn xiè) — to thank

## Example Sentences

- 我感觉不舒服。(Wǒ gǎn jué bù shū fú.) — I don't feel well.

## Notes

感 is used in many emotion words: 感动 (moved), 感兴趣 (interested), 感冒 (cold/flu).
