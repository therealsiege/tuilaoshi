# 攻 Attack

- **Character**: 攻
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: attack
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 攻击 (gōng jī) — attack
- 进攻 (jìn gōng) — attack

## Example Sentences

- 系统遭受网络攻击。(Xì tǒng zāo shòu wǎng luò gōng jī.) — The system suffered a cyber attack.

## Notes

Used in 攻击 (attack), 攻破 (breach), cybersecurity
