# 政 Politics, Government, 政府 Government

- **Character**: 政
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: politics, government, 政府 government
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 政府 (zhèng fǔ) — government
- 政策 (zhèng cè) — policy
- 政治 (zhèng zhì) — politics

## Example Sentences

- 地方政府负责城市管理。(Dìfāng zhèngfǔ fùzé chéngshì guǎnlǐ.) — Local governments are responsible for city management.

## Notes

Fundamental character in political and governmental contexts. 政府, 政策, and 政治 are among the most common political terms.
