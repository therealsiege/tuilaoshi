# 出 To Go Out

- **Character**: 出
- **Pinyin**: chū
- **Meaning**: to go out, to come out
- **Radical**: 凵 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 出去 (chū qù) — to go out
- 出来 (chū lái) — to come out

## Example Sentences

- 我们出去走走吧。(Wǒ men chū qù zǒu zǒu ba.) — Let's go out for a walk.

## Notes

出 shows a foot stepping out of an enclosure. Opposite of 进 (to enter).
