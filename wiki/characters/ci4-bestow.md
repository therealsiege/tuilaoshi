# 赐 To Bestow

- **Character**: 赐
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: to bestow, grant
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赏赐 (shǎng cì) — to reward, bestow
- 赐予 (cì yǔ) — to grant, bestow

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝赐予他一块土地。(Huángdì cìyǔ tā yī kuài tǔdì.) — The emperor granted him a piece of land.

## Notes

赐 combines shell/money 贝 with 易 (change), representing valuable gifts from superiors.
