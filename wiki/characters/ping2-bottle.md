# 瓶 Bottle

- **Character**: 瓶
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: bottle, vase, measure word
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 瓶子 (píng zi) — bottle
- 一瓶 (yī píng) — one bottle

## Example Sentences

- 买一瓶水。(Mǎi yī píng shuǐ.) — Buy a bottle of water.

## Notes

瓶 uses tile radical 瓦. Also measure word for bottled items.
