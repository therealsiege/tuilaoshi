# 愧 Ashamed, guilty

- **Character**: 愧
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: ashamed, guilty
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed, abashed
- 愧疚 (kuì jiù) — guilty, ashamed

## Example Sentences

- 他对不起朋友感到很愧疚。(Tā duìbuqǐ péngyou gǎndào hěn kuìjiù.) — He feels very guilty toward his friend.

## Notes

Often used to express regret or guilt over one's actions.
