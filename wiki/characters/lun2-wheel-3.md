# 轮 Wheel, gear

- **Character**: 轮
- **Pinyin**: lún
- **Meaning**: wheel, gear
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 齿轮 (chǐ lún) — gear
- 轮子 (lún zi) — wheel

## Example Sentences

- 这个齿轮需要更换。(Zhè gè chǐ lún xū yào gēng huàn.) — This gear needs to be replaced.

## Notes

Used in 齿轮 (gear), 轮胎 (tire), 轮流 (take turns)
