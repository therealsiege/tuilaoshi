# 大 Big

- **Character**: 大
- **Pinyin**: dà
- **Meaning**: big, large, great
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 大学 (dà xué) — university
- 大家 (dà jiā) — everyone

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间很大。(Zhè ge fángjiān hěn dà.) — This room is very big.

## Notes

大 is a pictograph of a person with arms and legs spread wide.
