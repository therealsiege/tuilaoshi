# 怅 Melancholy

- **Character**: 怅
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: disappointed, melancholy
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 惆怅 (chóu chàng) — melancholy
- 怅然 (chàng rán) — disappointed

## Example Sentences

- 他惆怅地望着远方。(Tā chóu chàng de wàng zhe yuǎn fāng.) — He gazed into the distance with melancholy.

## Notes

怅 simplified from 悵. Heart 忄 with 长 (long), prolonged sadness.
