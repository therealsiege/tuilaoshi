# 旨 Purpose, imperial decree, delicious

- **Character**: 旨
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: purpose, imperial decree, delicious
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 宗旨 (zōng zhǐ) — purpose, aim
- 旨意 (zhǐ yì) — imperial decree

## Example Sentences

- 这是我们组织的宗旨。(Zhè shì wǒ men zǔ zhī de zōng zhǐ.) — This is the purpose of our organization.

## Notes

Historically used for imperial edicts, now means purpose or aim
