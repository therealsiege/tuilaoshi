# 清 Clear, pure, Qing Dynasty

- **Character**: 清
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: clear, pure, Qing Dynasty
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 清楚 (qīng chu) — clear, distinct
- 清洁 (qīng jié) — clean

## Example Sentences

- 我说得很清楚。(Wǒ shuō de hěn qīngchu.) — I spoke very clearly.

## Notes

Clarity, purity, or the Qing Dynasty.
