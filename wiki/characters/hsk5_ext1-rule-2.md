# 规 Rule, Regulation, 规定 Regulation

- **Character**: 规
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: rule, regulation, 规定 regulation
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 规定 (guī dìng) — regulation, stipulate
- 规则 (guī zé) — rule
- 规划 (guī huà) — plan, layout

## Example Sentences

- 学校有严格的规定。(Xuéxiào yǒu yángé de guīdìng.) — The school has strict regulations.

## Notes

Common in formal and official contexts for rules and regulations. 规定 is standard term for regulations and stipulations.
