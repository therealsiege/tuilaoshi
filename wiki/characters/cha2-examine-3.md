# 察 Examine, observe, inspect

- **Character**: 察
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: examine, observe, inspect
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 观察 (guānchá) — observe
- 检察 (jiǎnchá) — procuratorate

## Example Sentences

- 警察正在观察嫌疑人。(Jǐngchá zhèngzài guānchá xiányírén.) — The police are observing the suspect.

## Notes

Formal term for examination and observation.
