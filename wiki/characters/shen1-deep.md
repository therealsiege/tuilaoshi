# 深 Deep

- **Character**: 深
- **Pinyin**: shēn
- **Meaning**: deep, profound
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 深夜 (shēn yè) — late at night
- 深刻 (shēn kè) — profound

## Example Sentences

- 这个湖很深。(Zhè ge hú hěn shēn.) — This lake is very deep.

## Notes

深 with water radical for deep water. Also means profound: 深刻 (profound). Opposite: 浅.
