# 腐 Rot, corrupt, tofu

- **Character**: 腐
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: rot, corrupt, tofu
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 腐败 (fǔbài) — corrupt, rotten
- 豆腐 (dòufu) — tofu

## Example Sentences

- 官员腐败损害国家利益。(Guānyuán fǔbài sǔnhài guójiā lìyì.) — Official corruption damages national interests.

## Notes

Means rotting or corruption; also used in the word for tofu.
