# 遮 To cover, to hide, to block

- **Character**: 遮
- **Pinyin**: zhē
- **Meaning**: to cover, to hide, to block
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 遮挡 (zhē dǎng) — to block, to obstruct
- 遮盖 (zhē gài) — to cover, to hide

## Example Sentences

- 树木遮住了阳光。(Shùmù zhēzhù le yángguāng.) — The trees blocked the sunlight.

## Notes

Emphasizes blocking or hiding from view.
