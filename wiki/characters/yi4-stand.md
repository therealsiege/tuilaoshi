# 屹 Stand firm, towering

- **Character**: 屹
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: stand firm, towering
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 屹立 (yìlì) — stand firm, stand towering
- 屹然不动 (yìrán bùdòng) — stand firm and unmoved

## Example Sentences

- 纪念碑屹立在广场中央。(Jìniànbēi yìlì zài guǎngchǎng zhōngyāng.) — The monument stands firm in the center of the square.

## Notes

Literary term emphasizing firmness and stability in standing.
