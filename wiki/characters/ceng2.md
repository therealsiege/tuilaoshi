# 层 Layer

- **Character**: 层
- **Pinyin**: céng
- **Meaning**: layer, floor, story, stratum
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 一层 (yī céng) — one floor/layer
- 层次 (céng cì) — level, hierarchy

## Example Sentences

- 我住在三层。(Wǒ zhù zài sān céng.) — I live on the third floor.

## Notes

层 represents layers or floors. Measure word for floors of buildings and layers of things.
