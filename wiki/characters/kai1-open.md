# 开 To Open

- **Character**: 开
- **Pinyin**: kāi
- **Meaning**: to open, to start, to drive
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 打开 (dǎ kāi) — to open
- 开始 (kāi shǐ) — to begin

## Example Sentences

- 请开门。(Qǐng kāi mén.) — Please open the door.

## Notes

开 depicts two hands opening a bolt. Many uses: open doors, drive vehicles, start activities, bloom.
