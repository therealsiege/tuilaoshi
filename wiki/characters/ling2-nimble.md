# 灵 Nimble

- **Character**: 灵
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: nimble, clever
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灵活 (líng huó) — flexible, agile
- 灵敏 (líng mǐn) — sensitive, quick

## Example Sentences

- 运动员的身体很灵活。(Yùndòngyuán de shēntǐ hěn línghuó.) — Athletes' bodies are very flexible.

## Notes

Nimbleness and flexibility; key for gymnastics and martial arts
