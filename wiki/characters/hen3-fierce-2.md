# 狠 Fierce

- **Character**: 狠
- **Pinyin**: hěn
- **Meaning**: fierce, ruthless
- **Radical**: 犭 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 凶狠 (xiōng hěn) — fierce, ferocious
- 狠心 (hěn xīn) — ruthless

## Example Sentences

- 比赛时要狠一点。(Bǐsài shí yào hěn yīdiǎn.) — Be a bit fiercer during competition.

## Notes

Fierceness; aggressive competitive spirit
