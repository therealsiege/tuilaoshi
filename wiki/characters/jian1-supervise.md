# 监 Supervise, prison, watch

- **Character**: 监
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: supervise, prison, watch
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 监狱 (jiānyù) — prison, jail
- 监督 (jiāndū) — supervise, monitor

## Example Sentences

- 罪犯在监狱服刑。(Zuìfàn zài jiānyù fúxíng.) — The criminal is serving a sentence in prison.

## Notes

Used for supervision and prison.
