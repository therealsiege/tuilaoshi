# 界 World

- **Character**: 界
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: boundary, world, field
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 世界 (shì jiè) — world
- 边界 (biān jiè) — boundary

## Example Sentences

- 世界很大。(Shì jiè hěn dà.) — The world is very big.

## Notes

界 means a boundary or domain. 世界 = world, 科学界 = scientific community.
