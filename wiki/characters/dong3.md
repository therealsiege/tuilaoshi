# 懂 To Understand

- **Character**: 懂
- **Pinyin**: dǒng
- **Meaning**: to understand
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 听懂 (tīng dǒng) — to understand (by hearing)
- 看懂 (kàn dǒng) — to understand (by reading)

## Example Sentences

- 你听懂了吗？(Nǐ tīng dǒng le ma?) — Did you understand?

## Notes

懂 is used as a result complement: 听懂 (heard and understood), 看懂 (read and understood).
