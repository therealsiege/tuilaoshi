# 娶 To marry (of man), to take a wife

- **Character**: 娶
- **Pinyin**: qǔ
- **Meaning**: to marry (of man), to take a wife
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 娶妻 (qǔ qī) — to take a wife
- 娶亲 (qǔ qīn) — to get married (of man)

## Example Sentences

- 他今年娶了个漂亮的妻子。(Tā jīnnián qǔ le ge piàoliang de qīzi.) — He married a beautiful wife this year.

## Notes

Specifically for a man marrying; counterpart is 嫁.
