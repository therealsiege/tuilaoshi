# 垛 Pile, stack (of bricks, etc.)

- **Character**: 垛
- **Pinyin**: duǒ
- **Meaning**: pile, stack (of bricks, etc.)
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 草垛 (cǎo duǒ) — haystack
- 一垛砖 (yī duǒ zhuān) — a stack of bricks

## Example Sentences

- 院子里有一垛柴火。(Yuànzi lǐ yǒu yī duǒ cháihuo.) — There is a stack of firewood in the yard.

## Notes

Specifically for stacks of construction materials or hay.
