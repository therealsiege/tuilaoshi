# 群 Group

- **Character**: 群
- **Pinyin**: qún
- **Meaning**: crowd, group, flock
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 群众 (qún zhòng) — the masses
- 人群 (rén qún) — crowd

## Example Sentences

- 广场上聚集了一大群人。(Guǎng chǎng shàng jù jí le yí dà qún rén.) — A large crowd gathered in the square.

## Notes

群 sheep 羊 plus 君 (ruler), flock following leader.
