# 贫 Poor, poverty

- **Character**: 贫
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: poor, poverty
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贫穷 (pín qióng) — poor, impoverished
- 贫困 (pín kùn) — poverty-stricken

## Example Sentences

- 政府帮助贫困地区发展。(Zhèng fǔ bāng zhù pín kùn dì qū fā zhǎn.) — The government helps poverty-stricken areas develop.

## Notes

扶贫 means poverty alleviation. Opposite of 富.
