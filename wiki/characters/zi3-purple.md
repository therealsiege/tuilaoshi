# 紫 Purple

- **Character**: 紫
- **Pinyin**: zǐ
- **Meaning**: purple, violet
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 紫色 (zǐ sè) — purple color
- 紫禁城 (zǐ jìn chéng) — Forbidden City

## Example Sentences

- 紫色很漂亮。(Zǐ sè hěn piào liang.) — Purple is very beautiful.

## Notes

紫 was an imperial color in ancient China. 紫禁城 (Forbidden City) was the imperial palace in Beijing.
