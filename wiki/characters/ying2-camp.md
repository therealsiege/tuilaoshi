# 营 Camp

- **Character**: 营
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: camp, battalion, to operate
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 经营 (jīng yíng) — to operate, manage
- 营业 (yíng yè) — to do business

## Example Sentences

- 这家店每天营业到晚上十点。(Zhè jiā diàn měi tiān yíng yè dào wǎn shàng shí diǎn.) — This shop operates until 10 PM every day.

## Notes

营 is simplified from 營. Originally meant military camp; now also business operations.
