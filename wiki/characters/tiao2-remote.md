# 迢 Remote, far

- **Character**: 迢
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: remote, far
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 迢迢 (tiáo tiáo) — very far, a long way
- 千里迢迢 (qiān lǐ tiáo tiáo) — from afar, from a great distance

## Example Sentences

- 他千里迢迢来看我。(Tā qiān lǐ tiáotiáo lái kàn wǒ.) — He came from afar to see me.

## Notes

Literary term for great distance, often used in pairs.
