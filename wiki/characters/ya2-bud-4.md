# 芽 Bud

- **Character**: 芽
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: bud, sprout
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 发芽 (fā yá) — to sprout
- 豆芽 (dòu yá) — bean sprout

## Example Sentences

- 种子开始发芽了。(Zhǒng zi kāi shǐ fā yá le.) — The seeds have started to sprout.

## Notes

芽 has grass 艹 with 牙 (tooth/tusk), a sharp new growth.
