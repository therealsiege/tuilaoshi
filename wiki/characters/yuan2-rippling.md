# 湲 Rippling water

- **Character**: 湲
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: rippling water
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 潺湲 (chán yuán) — flowing gently
- 湲湲 (yuán yuán) — rippling

## Example Sentences

- 溪水潺湲而下。(Xī shuǐ chán yuán ér xià.) — Stream water flows gently downward.

## Notes

Gentle rippling water flow
