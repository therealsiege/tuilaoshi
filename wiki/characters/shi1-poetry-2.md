# 诗 Poetry

- **Character**: 诗
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: poetry, poem
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诗歌 (shī gē) — poetry
- 古诗 (gǔ shī) — classical poetry

## Example Sentences

- 他喜欢读唐诗。(Tā xǐ huān dú táng shī.) — He likes to read Tang poetry.

## Notes

诗 is the primary word for poetry, especially classical Chinese poetry.
