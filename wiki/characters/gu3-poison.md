# 蛊 To Bewitch

- **Character**: 蛊
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: venomous insect, to bewitch
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 蛊惑 (gǔ huò) — to bewitch
- 放蛊 (fàng gǔ) — to cast a spell

## Example Sentences

- 不要被他的话蛊惑了。(Bú yào bèi tā de huà gǔ huò le.) — Don't be bewitched by his words.

## Notes

蛊 depicts insects 虫 in a vessel 皿, ancient poisonous magic practice.
