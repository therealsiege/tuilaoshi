# 纷 Numerous, confused, dispute

- **Character**: 纷
- **Pinyin**: fēn
- **Meaning**: numerous, confused, dispute
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纠纷 (jiū fēn) — dispute
- 纷纷 (fēn fēn) — one after another

## Example Sentences

- 大家纷纷发表意见。(Dà jiā fēn fēn fā biǎo yì jiàn.) — Everyone expressed their opinions one after another.

## Notes

Indicates disorder or multitude. 纷乱 means chaotic. 缤纷 means colorful.
