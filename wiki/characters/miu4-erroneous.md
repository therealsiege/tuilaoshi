# 谬 Erroneous, absurd, error

- **Character**: 谬
- **Pinyin**: miù
- **Meaning**: erroneous, absurd, error
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 谬误 (miù wù) — error, fallacy
- 荒谬 (huāng miù) — absurd, preposterous

## Example Sentences

- 这种说法完全是荒谬的。(Zhè zhǒng shuō fǎ wán quán shì huāng miù de.) — This statement is completely absurd.

## Notes

Used in formal contexts to denote logical or factual errors
