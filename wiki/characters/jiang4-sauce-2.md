# 酱 Sauce, jam, brown-red

- **Character**: 酱
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: sauce, jam, brown-red
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 酱油 (jiàng yóu) — soy sauce
- 酱色 (jiàng sè) — brown-red color

## Example Sentences

- 这道菜需要加一点酱油。(Zhè dào cài xū yào jiā yī diǎn jiàng yóu.) — This dish needs a bit of soy sauce.

## Notes

Primary meaning is sauce; also a reddish-brown color.
