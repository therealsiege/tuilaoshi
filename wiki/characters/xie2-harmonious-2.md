# 谐 Harmonious, resonance

- **Character**: 谐
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: harmonious, resonance
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谐波 (xié bō) — harmonic
- 和谐 (hé xié) — harmonious

## Example Sentences

- 谐波失真很小。(Xié bō shī zhēn hěn xiǎo.) — Harmonic distortion is minimal.

## Notes

Used in 谐波 (harmonic), 谐振 (resonance), electronics
