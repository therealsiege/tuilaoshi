# 扶 To support with hand, to help sb up

- **Character**: 扶
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: to support with hand, to help sb up
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 扶手 (fú shǒu) — handrail, armrest
- 搀扶 (chān fú) — to support with the arm

## Example Sentences

- 请扶老人上车。(Qǐng fú lǎorén shàngchē.) — Please help the elderly person onto the bus.

## Notes

Commonly used for supporting or helping someone physically.
