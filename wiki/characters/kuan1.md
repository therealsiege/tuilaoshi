# 宽 Wide

- **Character**: 宽
- **Pinyin**: kuān
- **Meaning**: wide, broad, lenient
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽广 (kuān guǎng) — wide, broad
- 宽容 (kuān róng) — tolerant

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很宽。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn kuān.) — This road is very wide.

## Notes

宽 simplified from 寬. Roof radical 宀 with spacious interior, representing width and generosity.
