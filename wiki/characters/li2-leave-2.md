# 离 Separate

- **Character**: 离
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: leave, separate
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 隔离 (gé lí) — to isolate, quarantine
- 分离 (fēn lí) — to separate
- 离开 (lí kāi) — to leave

## Example Sentences

- 隔离期间不能外出。(Gé lí qī jiān bù néng wài chū.) — Cannot go out during quarantine.

## Notes

Separation or departure; quarantine component.
