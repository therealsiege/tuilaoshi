# 罐 Cup

- **Character**: 罐
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: jar, can
- **Radical**: 缶 (jar)
- **Stroke count**: 23

## Compounds

- 拔罐 (bá guàn) — cupping therapy
- 罐头 (guàn tou) — canned food
- 火罐 (huǒ guàn) — fire cupping

## Example Sentences

- 他后背有拔罐的印记。(Tā hòu bèi yǒu bá guàn de yìn jì.) — He has cupping marks on his back.

## Notes

Container; used in cupping therapy.
