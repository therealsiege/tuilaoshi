# 渗 Seep, permeate

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: seep, permeate
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèntòu) — permeate, infiltrate
- 渗漏 (shènlòu) — seep, leak

## Example Sentences

- 雨水渗透了地面。(Yǔshuǐ shèntòu le dìmiàn.) — Rainwater permeated the ground.

## Notes

Common term for seeping or permeation, both literal and figurative.
