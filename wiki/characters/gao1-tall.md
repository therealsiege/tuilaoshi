# 高 Tall

- **Character**: 高
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: tall, high
- **Radical**: 高 (tall)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 高兴 (gāo xìng) — happy
- 提高 (tí gāo) — to improve, to raise

## Example Sentences

- 他很高。(Tā hěn gāo.) — He is very tall.

## Notes

高 pictograph of tall building/tower. 高兴 (happy) literally 'high spirits.' Opposite: 矮.
