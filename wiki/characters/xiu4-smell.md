# 嗅 Smell

- **Character**: 嗅
- **Pinyin**: xiù
- **Meaning**: smell, sniff
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 嗅觉 (xiùjué) — sense of smell
- 嗅到 (xiù dào) — smell, detect

## Example Sentences

- 狗的嗅觉很灵敏。(Gǒu de xiùjué hěn língmǐn.) — Dogs have a very keen sense of smell.

## Notes

Smelling action. Mouth radical. 嗅觉 is the sense of smell. Common with animals and detection.
