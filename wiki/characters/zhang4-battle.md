# 仗 Battle

- **Character**: 仗
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: battle, to rely on
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 打仗 (dǎ zhàng) — to fight a battle
- 依仗 (yī zhàng) — to rely on

## Example Sentences

- 两军正在打仗。(Liǎng jūn zhèng zài dǎ zhàng.) — The two armies are fighting.

## Notes

仗 means battle or to rely on; in 对仗 refers to parallelism in poetry.
