# 难 Difficult

- **Character**: 难
- **Pinyin**: nán
- **Meaning**: difficult, hard
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 困难 (kùn nán) — difficulty, difficult
- 难过 (nán guò) — sad, upset

## Example Sentences

- 中文很难吗？(Zhōng wén hěn nán ma?) — Is Chinese very difficult?

## Notes

难 simplified from 難. Opposite of 容易 (easy). Also read nàn (disaster, calamity).
