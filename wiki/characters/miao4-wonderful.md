# 妙 Wonderful, subtle, ingenious

- **Character**: 妙
- **Pinyin**: miào
- **Meaning**: wonderful, subtle, ingenious
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 美妙 (měi miào) — wonderful, beautiful
- 奇妙 (qí miào) — wonderful, marvelous

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个妙计。(Zhè shì yī ge miào jì.) — This is a brilliant plan.

## Notes

Indicates wonderfulness or cleverness. 妙不可言 means too wonderful for words. 妙龄 means young and beautiful.
