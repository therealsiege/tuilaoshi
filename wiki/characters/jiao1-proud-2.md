# 骄 Proud, arrogant

- **Character**: 骄
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: proud, arrogant
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 骄傲 (jiāo'ào) — proud, arrogant
- 骄阳 (jiāoyáng) — scorching sun

## Example Sentences

- 不要骄傲自满。(Bùyào jiāo'ào zìmǎn.) — Don't be arrogant and complacent.

## Notes

Common term for pride or arrogance, can be positive or negative.
