# 才 Only Then

- **Character**: 才
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: just now, only then, talent
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 人才 (rén cái) — talent, talented person
- 刚才 (gāng cái) — just now

## Example Sentences

- 他九点才来。(Tā jiǔ diǎn cái lái.) — He didn't come until nine o'clock.

## Notes

才 implies 'later than expected' or 'only if.' Contrast with 就 which implies 'sooner than expected.'
