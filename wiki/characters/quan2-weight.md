# 权 Weight, right, power

- **Character**: 权
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: weight, right, power
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 权重 (quán zhòng) — weight (neural network)
- 权限 (quán xiàn) — permission

## Example Sentences

- 调整神经网络的权重。(Tiáo zhěng shén jīng wǎng luò de quán zhòng.) — Adjust the weights of the neural network.

## Notes

Used in 权重 (weight), 权限 (permission), machine learning terminology
