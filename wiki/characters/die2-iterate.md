# 迭 Iterate, alternate

- **Character**: 迭
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: iterate, alternate
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 迭代 (dié dài) — iterate, iteration
- 更迭 (gēng dié) — change, replace

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要快速迭代产品。(Wǒ men xū yào kuài sù dié dài chǎn pǐn.) — We need to iterate the product quickly.

## Notes

Used in 迭代 (iteration), essential for agile development and algorithms
