# 叨 To Chatter

- **Character**: 叨
- **Pinyin**: dāo
- **Meaning**: to chatter, talkative (also tāo)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 唠叨 (láo dao) — to nag
- 絮叨 (xù dao) — long-winded

## Example Sentences

- 他说话很絮叨。(Tā shuō huà hěn xù dao.) — He talks in a very long-winded way.

## Notes

叨 shows mouth 口 and knife 刀, as dao means to chatter, as tāo means to receive favor.
