# 灌 To irrigate, pour, fill

- **Character**: 灌
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: to irrigate, pour, fill
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 灌溉 (guàn gài) — to irrigate
- 灌输 (guàn shū) — to instill, inculcate

## Example Sentences

- 农民正在灌溉农田。(Nóngmín zhèngzài guàngài nóngtián.) — Farmers are irrigating the fields.

## Notes

To pour, irrigate, or fill; also to instill ideas.
