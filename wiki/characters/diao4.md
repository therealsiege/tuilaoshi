# 吊 To hang, to suspend, to mourn

- **Character**: 吊
- **Pinyin**: diào
- **Meaning**: to hang, to suspend, to mourn
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 吊灯 (diào dēng) — chandelier, hanging lamp
- 吊唁 (diào yàn) — to condole, to offer condolences

## Example Sentences

- 天花板上吊着一盏灯。(Tiānhuābǎn shàng diàozhe yī zhǎn dēng.) — A lamp is hanging from the ceiling.

## Notes

Can mean to hang/suspend or to mourn/condole.
