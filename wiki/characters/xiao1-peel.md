# 削 To peel, to pare, to cut

- **Character**: 削
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: to peel, to pare, to cut
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 削皮 (xiāo pí) — to peel
- 削减 (xuē jiǎn) — to cut, to reduce

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈在削苹果皮。(Māma zài xiāo píngguǒ pí.) — Mom is peeling an apple.

## Notes

Pronounced xiāo for peeling; xuē for削减 (to cut/reduce).
