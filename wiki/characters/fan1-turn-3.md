# 翻 Turn over, translate

- **Character**: 翻
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: turn over, translate
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 翻新 (fān xīn) — renovate, refurbish
- 翻译 (fān yì) — translate

## Example Sentences

- 旧房翻新后像新的一样。(Jiù fáng fān xīn hòu xiàng xīn de yī yàng.) — After renovation, the old house looks new.

## Notes

翻修 means renovate. 翻盖 means rebuild.
