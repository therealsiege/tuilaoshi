# 牍 Wooden Tablet

- **Character**: 牍
- **Pinyin**: dú
- **Meaning**: wooden tablets for writing
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 文牍 (wén dú) — official documents
- 简牍 (jiǎn dú) — bamboo and wooden slips

## Example Sentences

- 简牍是古代的书写材料。(Jiǎn dú shì gǔ dài de shū xiě cái liào.) — Bamboo and wooden slips were ancient writing materials.

## Notes

牍 simplified from 牘. Wood 片 with 卖 phonetic, wooden writing tablets.
