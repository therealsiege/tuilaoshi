# 羁 Halter, bridle, restrain

- **Character**: 羁
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: halter, bridle, restrain
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 羁绊 (jī bàn) — fetters, ties
- 羁押 (jī yā) — detention

## Example Sentences

- 不要被世俗的羁绊所困。(Bù yào bèi shì sú de jī bàn suǒ kùn.) — Don't be trapped by worldly fetters.

## Notes

Restraint and confinement, literal or metaphorical
