# 降 Downgrade, descend

- **Character**: 降
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: downgrade, descend
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 降级 (jiàng jí) — downgrade
- 下降 (xià jiàng) — decrease

## Example Sentences

- 如果有问题可以降级版本。(Rú guǒ yǒu wèn tí kě yǐ jiàng jí bǎn běn.) — If there are problems, you can downgrade the version.

## Notes

Used in 降级 (downgrade), opposite of 升级 (upgrade)
