# 挂 To Hang

- **Character**: 挂
- **Pinyin**: guà
- **Meaning**: to hang, to put up, to suspend
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挂号 (guà hào) — to register
- 挂念 (guà niàn) — to worry about

## Example Sentences

- 墙上挂着一幅画。(Qiáng shàng guà zhe yī fú huà.) — A painting is hanging on the wall.

## Notes

挂 means to hang or suspend. Also used for registering (挂号), hanging up phone (挂电话).
