# 拙 Clumsy, awkward

- **Character**: 拙
- **Pinyin**: zhuō
- **Meaning**: clumsy, awkward
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 笨拙 (bènzhuō) — clumsy, awkward
- 拙劣 (zhuōliè) — clumsy, inferior

## Example Sentences

- 他的手艺很笨拙。(Tā de shǒuyì hěn bènzhuō.) — His craftsmanship is very clumsy.

## Notes

Describes clumsiness or awkwardness, often used humbly in self-reference.
