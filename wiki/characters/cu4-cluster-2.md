# 簇 Cluster

- **Character**: 簇
- **Pinyin**: cù
- **Meaning**: cluster, bunch, to gather
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 簇拥 (cù yōng) — to cluster around
- 花簇 (huā cù) — flower cluster

## Example Sentences

- 人群簇拥着明星。(Rén qún cù yōng zhe míng xīng.) — The crowd clustered around the star.

## Notes

簇 has bamboo 竹 with 族 (clan), tight grouping like bamboo shoots.
