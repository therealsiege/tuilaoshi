# 上 Up

- **Character**: 上
- **Pinyin**: shàng
- **Meaning**: up, on, above, to go up
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 上午 (shàng wǔ) — morning
- 上班 (shàng bān) — go to work

## Example Sentences

- 书在桌子上。(Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.) — The book is on the table.

## Notes

上 is an ideograph showing a mark above a baseline, meaning 'above.' Opposite of 下.
