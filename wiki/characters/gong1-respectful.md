# 恭 Respectful

- **Character**: 恭
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: respectful, reverent
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恭喜 (gōng xǐ) — congratulations
- 恭敬 (gōng jìng) — respectful

## Example Sentences

- 恭喜发财！(Gōng xǐ fā cái!) — Wishing you prosperity! (New Year greeting)

## Notes

恭 shows two hands 共 over a heart 心, representing reverent attitude with sincere heart.
