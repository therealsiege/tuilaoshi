# 怕 To Fear

- **Character**: 怕
- **Pinyin**: pà
- **Meaning**: to fear, to be afraid of
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 害怕 (hài pà) — to be afraid
- 恐怕 (kǒng pà) — I'm afraid that

## Example Sentences

- 别怕！(Bié pà!) — Don't be afraid!

## Notes

怕 uses the heart radical. 恐怕 is softer, meaning 'I'm afraid that...' (expressing worry, not fear).
