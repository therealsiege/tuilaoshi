# 富 Rich

- **Character**: 富
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: rich, wealthy, abundant
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 丰富 (fēng fù) — rich, abundant
- 财富 (cái fù) — wealth

## Example Sentences

- 中国文化很丰富。(Zhōng guó wén huà hěn fēng fù.) — Chinese culture is very rich.

## Notes

富 shows a roof (宀) over treasure — wealth stored at home. 富有 = wealthy.
