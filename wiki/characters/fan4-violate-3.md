# 犯 Violate

- **Character**: 犯
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: to violate, foul
- **Radical**: 犭 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 犯规 (fàn guī) — to foul, violate rules
- 犯错 (fàn cuò) — to make mistake

## Example Sentences

- 他犯规了，被罚下场。(Tā fànguī le, bèi fá xià chǎng.) — He fouled and was sent off.

## Notes

Fouling; rule violations
