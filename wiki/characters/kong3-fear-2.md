# 恐 Fear

- **Character**: 恐
- **Pinyin**: kǒng
- **Meaning**: fear, dread, to fear
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恐怖 (kǒng bù) — terror
- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear

## Example Sentences

- 孩子对黑暗感到恐惧。(Hái zi duì hēi àn gǎn dào kǒng jù.) — The child feels fear of darkness.

## Notes

恐 combines 巩 (to strengthen) and 心 (heart), heart gripped tightly.
