# 稿 Manuscript, draft, straw

- **Character**: 稿
- **Pinyin**: gǎo
- **Meaning**: manuscript, draft, straw
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 稿件 (gǎo jiàn) — manuscript, draft
- 草稿 (cǎo gǎo) — rough draft

## Example Sentences

- 我还在修改演讲稿。(Wǒ hái zài xiū gǎi yǎn jiǎng gǎo.) — I'm still revising my speech draft.

## Notes

Common in writing and publishing contexts. 投稿 means to submit a manuscript.
