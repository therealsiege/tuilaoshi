# 障 To block, obstruct, barrier, 障 (traditional)

- **Character**: 障
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: to block, obstruct, barrier, 障 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle, barrier
- 保障 (bǎo zhàng) — to guarantee, safeguard

## Example Sentences

- 我们要克服一切障碍。(Wǒmen yào kèfú yīqiè zhàng'ài.) — We must overcome all obstacles.

## Notes

Obstacle or barrier, also to block.
