# 判 To Judge

- **Character**: 判
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: to judge, to sentence, to decide
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge, to determine
- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee, judge

## Example Sentences

- 法官做出了判决。(Fǎ guān zuò chū le pàn jué.) — The judge made a verdict.

## Notes

判 combines division (半) and knife (刂), suggesting decisive judgment.
