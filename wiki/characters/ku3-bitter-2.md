# 苦 Bitter

- **Character**: 苦
- **Pinyin**: kǔ
- **Meaning**: bitter, hardship, suffering
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 辛苦 (xīn kǔ) — hard work
- 痛苦 (tòng kǔ) — painful

## Example Sentences

- 辛苦了！(Xīn kǔ le!) — You've worked hard!

## Notes

辛苦了 is a common expression acknowledging someone's hard work. 吃苦 means to endure hardship.
