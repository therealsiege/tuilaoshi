# 煌 Brilliant

- **Character**: 煌
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: brilliant, shining
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 辉煌 (huī huáng) — splendid, brilliant
- 金碧辉煌 (jīn bì huī huáng) — splendid and magnificent

## Example Sentences

- 大厅装饰得金碧辉煌。(Dàtīng zhuāngshì de jīn bì huī huáng.) — The hall is decorated magnificently.

## Notes

煌 combines fire 火 with 皇 (emperor), representing the brilliant radiance associated with imperial grandeur.
