# 配 Match, distribute

- **Character**: 配
- **Pinyin**: pèi
- **Meaning**: match, distribute
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 配送 (pèi sòng) — deliver, distribute
- 分配 (fēn pèi) — allocate, distribute

## Example Sentences

- 公司提供免费配送服务。(Gōng sī tí gōng miǎn fèi pèi sòng fú wù.) — The company provides free delivery service.

## Notes

配货 means prepare goods for delivery. 配置 means allocate/configure.
