# 恕 To forgive, to pardon

- **Character**: 恕
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: to forgive, to pardon
- **Radical**: 心(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽恕 (kuān shù) — to forgive, to pardon
- 恕罪 (shù zuì) — to beg forgiveness

## Example Sentences

- 请宽恕他的过错。(Qǐng kuānshù tā de guòcuò.) — Please forgive his mistakes.

## Notes

More formal term for forgiveness, often used in written language.
