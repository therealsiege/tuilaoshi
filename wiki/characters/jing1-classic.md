# 经 Classic, scripture, pass through

- **Character**: 经
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: classic, scripture, pass through
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 经书 (jīng shū) — Confucian classics
- 佛经 (fó jīng) — Buddhist sutras

## Example Sentences

- 他每天都读佛经。(Tā měi tiān dōu dú fó jīng.) — He reads Buddhist sutras every day.

## Notes

Refers to canonical texts in Confucianism, Buddhism, etc.
