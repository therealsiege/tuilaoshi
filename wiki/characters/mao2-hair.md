# 毛 Hair

- **Character**: 毛
- **Pinyin**: máo
- **Meaning**: hair, fur, wool, feather
- **Radical**: 毛 (hair)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 毛衣 (máo yī) — sweater
- 羽毛 (yǔ máo) — feather

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着一件毛衣。(Tā chuān zhe yī jiàn máo yī.) — She is wearing a sweater.

## Notes

毛 means hair, fur, or wool. Used in毛衣 (sweater), 毛巾 (towel), surname Mao.
