# 蔑 To Despise

- **Character**: 蔑
- **Pinyin**: miè
- **Meaning**: to despise, to slight
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蔑视 (miè shì) — to despise, to scorn
- 轻蔑 (qīng miè) — to disdain

## Example Sentences

- 他用蔑视的眼光看着我。(Tā yòng miè shì de yǎn guāng kàn zhe wǒ.) — He looked at me with contemptuous eyes.

## Notes

蔑 expresses strong disdain or contempt. Often paired with 视 (to view) or 轻 (light, slight).
