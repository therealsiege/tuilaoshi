# 陡 Steep, sudden

- **Character**: 陡
- **Pinyin**: dǒu
- **Meaning**: steep, sudden
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 陡峭 (dǒuqiào) — steep, precipitous
- 陡然 (dǒurán) — suddenly, abruptly

## Example Sentences

- 山路很陡峭。(Shānlù hěn dǒuqiào.) — The mountain path is very steep.

## Notes

Describes steepness or suddenness.
