# 挥 Wave, wield, disperse

- **Character**: 挥
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: wave, wield, disperse
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挥发 (huī fā) — volatilize, evaporate
- 发挥 (fā huī) — bring into play

## Example Sentences
