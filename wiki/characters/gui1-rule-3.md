# 规 Rule

- **Character**: 规
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: rule, regulation, to plan
- **Radical**: 见 (to see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 规定 (guī dìng) — regulation
- 规则 (guī zé) — rule

## Example Sentences

- 学校有严格的规定。(Xué xiào yǒu yán gé de guī dìng.) — The school has strict regulations.

## Notes

规 simplified from 規. To see 见 with compass, measuring/rules.
