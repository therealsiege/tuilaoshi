# 满 Full

- **Character**: 满
- **Pinyin**: mǎn
- **Meaning**: full, satisfied, to fill
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 满意 (mǎn yì) — satisfied
- 满足 (mǎn zú) — satisfied, to satisfy

## Example Sentences

- 杯子满了。(Bēi zi mǎn le.) — The cup is full.

## Notes

满 means full or satisfied. 满意 = satisfied (with quality). 满足 = content/fulfilled.
