# 简 Simple

- **Character**: 简
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: simple, brief
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 简单 (jiǎn dān) — simple
- 简历 (jiǎn lì) — resume

## Example Sentences

- 这个很简单。(Zhè ge hěn jiǎn dān.) — This is very simple.

## Notes

简单 is the most common compound. 简体 means simplified (as in simplified Chinese characters).
