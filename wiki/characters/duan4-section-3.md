# 段 Section

- **Character**: 段
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: section, paragraph
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 段落 (duàn luò) — paragraph
- 片段 (piàn duàn) — fragment

## Example Sentences

- 这段音乐很动听。(Zhè duàn yīn yuè hěn dòng tīng.) — This section of music is very pleasant.

## Notes

段 means section or passage, used for divisions of music or text.
