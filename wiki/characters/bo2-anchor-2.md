# 泊 To Anchor

- **Character**: 泊
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: to anchor, to moor, lake
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 停泊 (tíng bó) — to anchor
- 湖泊 (hú pō) — lake

## Example Sentences

- 船在港口停泊。(Chuán zài gǎng kǒu tíng bó.) — The ship is anchored at the port.

## Notes

泊 as bó means to moor/anchor. As pō it appears in 湖泊 (lake).
