# 躺 To Lie Down

- **Character**: 躺
- **Pinyin**: tǎng
- **Meaning**: to lie down, to recline
- **Radical**: 身 (body)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 躺下 (tǎng xià) — to lie down
- 躺着 (tǎng zhe) — lying down

## Example Sentences

- 他躺在床上休息。(Tā tǎng zài chuáng shàng xiū xī.) — He is lying in bed resting.

## Notes

躺 uses body radical 身 with 尚 (still), representing the body in a still horizontal position.
