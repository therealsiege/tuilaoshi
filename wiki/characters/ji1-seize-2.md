# 缉 Seize, arrest, sew

- **Character**: 缉
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: seize, arrest, sew
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缉拿 (jīná) — arrest, apprehend
- 通缉 (tōngjī) — wanted (by police)

## Example Sentences

- 警方通缉逃犯。(Jǐngfāng tōngjī táofàn.) — Police are hunting for the fugitive.

## Notes

Police term for arresting; also means to sew.
