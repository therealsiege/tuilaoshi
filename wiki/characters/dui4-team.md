# 队 Team

- **Character**: 队
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: team, group, line (queue)
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 排队 (pái duì) — to line up
- 队伍 (duì wǔ) — team, troops

## Example Sentences

- 请排队等候。(Qǐng pái duì děng hòu.) — Please line up and wait.

## Notes

队 is simplified from 隊. 排队 = line up, 球队 = sports team.
