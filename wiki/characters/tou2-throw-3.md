# 投 Throw

- **Character**: 投
- **Pinyin**: tóu
- **Meaning**: to throw, toss
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 投篮 (tóu lán) — to shoot (basketball)
- 投球 (tóu qiú) — to pitch, throw ball

## Example Sentences

- 他投篮命中率很高。(Tā tóulán mìngzhònglǜ hěn gāo.) — His shooting percentage is very high.

## Notes

Throwing and shooting; key action in basketball and baseball
