# 解 解除, explain, solve

- **Character**: 解
- **Pinyin**: jiě
- **Meaning**: 解除, explain, solve
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 解除 (jiě chú) — 解除, remove
- 解决 (jiě jué) — solve, resolve

## Example Sentences

- 双方同意解除合同。(Shuāng fāng tóng yì jiě chú hé tong.) — Both parties agree to terminate the contract.

## Notes

解约 means cancel a contract. 解雇 means dismiss/fire.
