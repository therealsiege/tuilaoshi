# 梆 Clapper

- **Character**: 梆
- **Pinyin**: bāng
- **Meaning**: watchman's clapper
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 梆子 (bāng zi) — wooden clappers
- 梆梆 (bāng bāng) — clapping sound

## Example Sentences

- 梆子打出清脆的节奏。(Bāng zi dǎ chū qīng cuì de jié zòu.) — The clappers produce a crisp rhythm.

## Notes

梆 refers to wooden clappers used in traditional music and opera.
