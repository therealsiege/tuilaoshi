# 旧 Old (Used)

- **Character**: 旧
- **Pinyin**: jiù
- **Meaning**: old, used, former
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 旧书 (jiù shū) — used book
- 依旧 (yī jiù) — as before

## Example Sentences

- 这是一本旧书。(Zhè shì yī běn jiù shū.) — This is an old book.

## Notes

旧 means 'old' in the sense of 'used/former' (旧衣服, old clothes), unlike 老 which means 'old' in age.
