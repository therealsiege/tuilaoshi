# 浮 To Float

- **Character**: 浮
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: to float, superficial
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浮动 (fú dòng) — to float
- 漂浮 (piāo fú) — to float

## Example Sentences

- 木头在水面上漂浮。(Mù tou zài shuǐ miàn shàng piāo fú.) — The wood is floating on the water.

## Notes

浮 water 氵 plus 孚 (to trust), floating on surface.
