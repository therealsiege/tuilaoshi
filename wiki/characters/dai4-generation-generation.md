# 代 Generation

- **Character**: 代
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: generation, era, to substitute
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 时代 (shí dài) — era
- 代表 (dài biǎo) — to represent

## Example Sentences

- 他代表我们班发言。(Tā dài biǎo wǒ men bān fā yán.) — He spoke on behalf of our class.

## Notes

代 means generation or substitution. 现代 = modern, 古代 = ancient.
