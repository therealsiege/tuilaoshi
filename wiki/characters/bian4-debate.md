# 辩 To Debate

- **Character**: 辩
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: to debate, to argue, to distinguish
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 辩论 (biàn lùn) — to debate
- 争辩 (zhēng biàn) — to argue

## Example Sentences

- 他们在辩论这个话题。(Tā men zài biàn lùn zhè gè huà tí.) — They are debating this topic.

## Notes

辩 simplified from 辯. Shows辛 (bitter) on both sides of speech 言, representing the sharp exchange of arguments.
