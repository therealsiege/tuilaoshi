# 辉 Brilliance, splendor

- **Character**: 辉
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: brilliance, splendor
- **Radical**: 光 (light)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 光辉 (guāng huī) — radiance, glory
- 辉煌 (huī huáng) — brilliant, splendid

## Example Sentences

- 这座宫殿金碧辉煌。(Zhè zuò gōng diàn jīn bì huī huáng.) — This palace is resplendent and magnificent.

## Notes

Often describes brilliant light or glorious achievements.
