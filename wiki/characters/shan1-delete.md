# 删 Delete, remove

- **Character**: 删
- **Pinyin**: shān
- **Meaning**: delete, remove
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 删除 (shān chú) — delete, remove
- 删改 (shān gǎi) — delete and revise

## Example Sentences

- 请删除这一条款。(Qǐng shān chú zhè yī tiáo kuǎn.) — Please delete this clause.

## Notes

删节 means abridge. 删减 means cut/delete.
