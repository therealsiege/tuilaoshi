# 漂 To Float

- **Character**: 漂
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: to float, to drift, bleach
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漂浮 (piāo fú) — to float
- 漂亮 (piào liang) — beautiful

## Example Sentences

- 叶子在水面上漂浮。(Yè zi zài shuǐ miàn shàng piāo fú.) — Leaves float on the water surface.

## Notes

漂 has water 氵 with 票 (ticket), drifting on water.
