# 补 补充, mend, supply

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: 补充, mend, supply
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 补充 (bǔ chōng) — 补充
- 补偿 (bǔ cháng) — compensate

## Example Sentences

- 需要签订补充协议。(Xū yào qiān dìng bǔ chōng xié yì.) — Need to sign a supplementary agreement.

## Notes

补贴 is subsidy. 补救 means remedy.
