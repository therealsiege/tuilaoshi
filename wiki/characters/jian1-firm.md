# 坚 Firm

- **Character**: 坚
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: firm, hard, solid
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 坚持不懈 (jiān chí bù xiè) — persevere relentlessly
- 坚强 (jiān qiáng) — strong, firm

## Example Sentences

- 坚持不懈才能成功。(Jiān chí bù xiè cái néng chénggōng.) — Only through perseverance can you succeed.

## Notes

Used in 坚持不懈: firm and unyielding persistence.
