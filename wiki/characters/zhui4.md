# 坠 To fall, to drop, to weigh down

- **Character**: 坠
- **Pinyin**: zhuì
- **Meaning**: to fall, to drop, to weigh down
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 坠落 (zhuì luò) — to fall, to drop
- 下坠 (xià zhuì) — to fall, to sink

## Example Sentences

- 飞机坠落在山区。(Fēijī zhuìluò zài shānqū.) — The airplane crashed in the mountains.

## Notes

Implies falling or dropping, often with weight.
