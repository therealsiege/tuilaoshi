# 名 Name

- **Character**: 名
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: name, fame, rank
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 排名 (pái míng) — ranking, to rank
- 名次 (míng cì) — ranking, position

## Example Sentences

- 她获得了第一名。(Tā huòdé le dì yī míng.) — She won first place.

## Notes

Ranking and fame; placement in competition
