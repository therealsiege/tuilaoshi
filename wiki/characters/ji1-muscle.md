# 肌 Muscle

- **Character**: 肌
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: muscle, flesh
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 肌肉 (jī ròu) — muscle
- 肌肤 (jī fū) — skin

## Example Sentences

- 运动可以增强肌肉。(Yùn dòng kě yǐ zēng qiáng jī ròu.) — Exercise can strengthen muscles.

## Notes

肌 uses meat/moon radical 月, specifically referring to muscle tissue as opposed to general flesh (肉).
