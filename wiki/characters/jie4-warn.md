# 诫 Warn, admonish

- **Character**: 诫
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: warn, admonish
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 告诫 (gàojiè) — warn, admonish
- 训诫 (xùnjiè) — admonish, reprimand

## Example Sentences

- 父亲告诫我要诚实守信。(Fùqīn gàojiè wǒ yào chéngshí shǒuxìn.) — My father admonished me to be honest and trustworthy.

## Notes

Emphasizes moral or disciplinary warnings.
