# 摄 To Photograph

- **Character**: 摄
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: to take (photo), to absorb
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摄影 (shè yǐng) — photography
- 拍摄 (pāi shè) — to shoot (film)

## Example Sentences

- 他喜欢摄影。(Tā xǐ huān shè yǐng.) — He likes photography.

## Notes

摄 simplified from 攝. Hand radical 扌 representing capturing or gathering in.
