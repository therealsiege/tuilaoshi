# 钝 Blunt, dull, obtuse

- **Character**: 钝
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: blunt, dull, obtuse
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钝角 (dùn jiǎo) — obtuse angle
- 迟钝 (chí dùn) — slow, dull

## Example Sentences

- 钝角大于九十度小于一百八十度。(Dùn jiǎo dà yú jiǔ shí dù xiǎo yú yī bǎi bā shí dù.) — An obtuse angle is greater than 90 degrees and less than 180 degrees.

## Notes

Not sharp, wide angle
