# 愤 Indignant

- **Character**: 愤
- **Pinyin**: fèn
- **Meaning**: indignant, angry
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 愤怒 (fèn nù) — angry
- 义愤 (yì fèn) — righteous indignation

## Example Sentences

- 他非常愤怒。(Tā fēi cháng fèn nù.) — He is very angry.

## Notes

愤 simplified from 憤. Heart 忄 with 贲 phonetic, heart boiling with anger.
