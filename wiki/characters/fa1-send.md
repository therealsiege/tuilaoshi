# 发 To Send

- **Character**: 发
- **Pinyin**: fā
- **Meaning**: to send, to emit, to develop; hair (fà)
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 发现 (fā xiàn) — to discover
- 头发 (tóu fà) — hair

## Example Sentences

- 我发现了一个问题。(Wǒ fā xiàn le yī ge wèn tí.) — I discovered a problem.

## Notes

发 as fā: send, emit, develop (发展, 发现). As fà: hair (头发). Simplified from two characters: 發 and 髮.
