# 还 Return, repay

- **Character**: 还
- **Pinyin**: huán
- **Meaning**: return, repay
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 归还 (guī huán) — return, give back
- 还款 (huán kuǎn) — repay a loan

## Example Sentences

- 请按时还款。(Qǐng àn shí huán kuǎn.) — Please repay on time.

## Notes

Different from 还 (hái = still). 还钱 means pay back money.
