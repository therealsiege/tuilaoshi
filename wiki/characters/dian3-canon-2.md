# 典 Canon, pawn

- **Character**: 典
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: canon, pawn
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 典当 (diǎn dàng) — pawn
- 典型 (diǎn xíng) — typical, model

## Example Sentences

- 他把手表拿去典当了。(Tā bǎ shǒu biǎo ná qù diǎn dàng le.) — He pawned his watch.

## Notes

典当行 is pawn shop. Has multiple meanings: classical/pawn.
