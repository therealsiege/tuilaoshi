# 冲 Rush, dash, rinse

- **Character**: 冲
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: rush, dash, rinse
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冲刷 (chōng shuā) — wash away, erode
- 冲击 (chōng jī) — impact, assault

## Example Sentences

- 洪水冲毁了桥梁。(Hóng shuǐ chōng huǐ le qiáo liáng.) — The flood washed away the bridge.
