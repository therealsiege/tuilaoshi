# 绵 Continuous, soft

- **Character**: 绵
- **Pinyin**: mián
- **Meaning**: continuous, soft
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 连绵 (liánmián) — continuous, unbroken
- 绵延 (miányán) — stretch in an unbroken chain

## Example Sentences

- 山脉绵延数千里。(Shānmài miányán shù qiān lǐ.) — The mountain range stretches for thousands of miles.

## Notes

Describes continuous, unbroken extension or soft texture like silk.
