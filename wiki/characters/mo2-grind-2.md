# 磨 To Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: to grind, to rub, to wear down (also mò)
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 磨练 (mó liàn) — to temper, to steel
- 石磨 (shí mò) — millstone

## Example Sentences

- 他经过多年磨练成功了。(Tā jīng guò duō nián mó liàn chéng gōng le.) — He succeeded after years of tempering.

## Notes

磨 as mó means to grind/polish, as mò means millstone. Stone radical 石 representing grinding.
