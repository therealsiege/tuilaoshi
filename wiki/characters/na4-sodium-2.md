# 钠 Sodium

- **Character**: 钠
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: sodium
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钠盐 (nà yán) — sodium salt
- 氯化钠 (lǜ huà nà) — sodium chloride

## Example Sentences

- 氯化钠就是我们常说的食盐。(Lǜhuànà jiù shì wǒmen cháng shuō de shíyán.) — Sodium chloride is what we commonly call table salt.

## Notes

钠 is sodium, a reactive metal. Sodium compounds like salt are essential for life.
