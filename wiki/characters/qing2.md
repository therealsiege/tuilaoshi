# 情 — Feeling / Emotion

- **Character**: 情
- **Pinyin**: qíng
- **Meaning**: feeling, emotion; situation, condition; affection
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 事情 (shìqíng) — matter, thing, affair
- 感情 (gǎnqíng) — feeling, emotion, affection
- 心情 (xīnqíng) — mood, state of mind
- 情况 (qíngkuàng) — situation, condition
- 爱情 (àiqíng) — romantic love
- 表情 (biǎoqíng) — facial expression

## Example Sentences

- 这件事情很重要。(Zhè jiàn shìqíng hěn zhòngyào.) — This matter is very important.
- 今天我的心情很好。(Jīntiān wǒ de xīnqíng hěn hǎo.) — I'm in a good mood today.
- 他们两个人的感情很深。(Tāmen liǎng ge rén de gǎnqíng hěn shēn.) — The two of them have deep feelings for each other.

## Notes

情 combines the heart radical 忄with 青 (qīng, blue-green) as its phonetic component. The heart radical signals that this character deals with the inner emotional world. 情 appears in an enormous range of compounds covering human feelings (感情, 爱情), mental states (心情, 热情 rèqíng — enthusiasm), and situations (事情, 情况). 事情 (matter/thing) is one of the most frequently used nouns in Chinese — more formal than 事 (shì) alone. The character captures a fundamental concept in Chinese thought: that feelings and situations are intertwined, both flowing from the heart.
