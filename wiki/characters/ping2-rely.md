# 凭 To Rely On

- **Character**: 凭
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: to rely on, based on, proof
- **Radical**: 几 (table)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 凭借 (píng jiè) — to rely on
- 凭证 (píng zhèng) — proof, certificate

## Example Sentences

- 他凭自己的努力成功了。(Tā píng zì jǐ de nǔ lì chéng gōng le.) — He succeeded by his own efforts.

## Notes

凭 shows leaning on a table 几, representing relying on or using as evidence.
