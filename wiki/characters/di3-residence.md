# 邸 Residence

- **Character**: 邸
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: residence, official building
- **Radical**: 阝(city)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 府邸 (fǔ dǐ) — mansion
- 官邸 (guān dǐ) — official residence

## Example Sentences

- 首相官邸在市中心。(Shǒu xiàng guān dǐ zài shì zhōng xīn.) — The Prime Minister's residence is in the city center.

## Notes

邸 with city radical for high-status residences, mansions and official buildings.
