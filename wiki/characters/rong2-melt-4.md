# 融 To Melt

- **Character**: 融
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: to melt, to blend, finance
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 金融 (jīn róng) — finance
- 融化 (róng huà) — to melt

## Example Sentences

- 冰在阳光下融化了。(Bīng zài yáng guāng xià róng huà le.) — The ice melted in the sunlight.

## Notes

融 originally depicted an insect transforming; extended to melting and blending.
