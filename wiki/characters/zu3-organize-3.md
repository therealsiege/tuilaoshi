# 组 To Organize

- **Character**: 组
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to organize, group, section
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 组织 (zǔ zhī) — organization
- 小组 (xiǎo zǔ) — group

## Example Sentences

- 他们组织了一次活动。(Tā men zǔ zhī le yí cì huó dòng.) — They organized an activity.

## Notes

组 simplified from 組. Silk 纟 threads woven together.
