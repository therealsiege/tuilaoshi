# 绊 Trip

- **Character**: 绊
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to trip, stumble
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绊倒 (bàn dǎo) — to trip and fall
- 羁绊 (jī bàn) — fetter, bond

## Example Sentences

- 他被对手绊倒了。(Tā bèi duìshǒu bàn dǎo le.) — He was tripped by his opponent.

## Notes

Tripping; takedown technique
