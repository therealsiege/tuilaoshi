# 羊 Sheep

- **Character**: 羊
- **Pinyin**: yáng
- **Meaning**: sheep, goat
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 亡羊补牢 (wáng yáng bǔ láo) — better late than never
- 山羊 (shān yáng) — goat

## Example Sentences

- 牧羊人在山上放羊。(Mùyángrén zài shān shàng fàng yáng.) — The shepherd tends sheep on the mountain.

## Notes

The sheep in 亡羊补牢; also a zodiac animal and radical.
