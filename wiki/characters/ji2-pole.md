# 极 Pole, extreme

- **Character**: 极
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: pole, extreme
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 电极 (diàn jí) — electrode
- 极性 (jí xìng) — polarity

## Example Sentences

- 电池有正负极。(Diàn chí yǒu zhèng fù jí.) — Batteries have positive and negative poles.

## Notes

Used in 电极 (electrode), 极性 (polarity), 磁极 (magnetic pole)
