# 锡 Tin

- **Character**: 锡
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: tin
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 焊锡 (hàn xī) — solder
- 锡纸 (xī zhǐ) — tin foil

## Example Sentences

- 用焊锡连接电路。(Yòng hàn xī lián jiē diàn lù.) — Connect the circuit with solder.

## Notes

锡 with metal radical for tin, essential in soldering and electronics.
