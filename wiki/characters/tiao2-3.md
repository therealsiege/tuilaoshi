# 条 条款, clause, strip

- **Character**: 条
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: 条款, clause, strip
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 条款 (tiáo kuǎn) — clause, provision
- 条件 (tiáo jiàn) — condition, term

## Example Sentences

- 请仔细阅读合同条款。(Qǐng zǐ xì yuè dú hé tong tiáo kuǎn.) — Please read the contract clauses carefully.

## Notes

条目 means item/entry. 条例 means regulations.
