# 率 Rate, ratio

- **Character**: 率
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: rate, ratio
- **Radical**: 玄 (profound)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 速率 (sù lǜ) — rate
- 效率 (xiào lǜ) — efficiency

## Example Sentences

- 传输速率很高。(Chuán shū sù lǜ hěn gāo.) — The transmission rate is high.

## Notes

Used in 速率 (rate), 效率 (efficiency), 频率 (frequency)
