# 昏 Unconscious

- **Character**: 昏
- **Pinyin**: hūn
- **Meaning**: unconscious, faint
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 昏迷 (hūn mí) — coma, unconscious
- 昏倒 (hūn dǎo) — to faint
- 昏厥 (hūn jué) — to faint, syncope

## Example Sentences

- 他昏迷了三天。(Tā hūn mí le sān tiān.) — He was unconscious for three days.

## Notes

Loss of consciousness; medical emergency.
