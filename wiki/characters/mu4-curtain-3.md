# 幕 Curtain

- **Character**: 幕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: curtain, act
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 幕布 (mù bù) — curtain
- 开幕 (kāi mù) — opening

## Example Sentences

- 幕布拉开了。(Mù bù lā kāi le.) — The curtain was drawn open.

## Notes

幕 with cloth radical for large curtain, used in theater and ceremonies.
