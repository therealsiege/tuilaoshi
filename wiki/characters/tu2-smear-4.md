# 涂 To Smear

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: to smear, to apply, to scribble
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear
- 涂料 (tú liào) — coating

## Example Sentences

- 他在墙上涂油漆。(Tā zài qiáng shàng tú yóu qī.) — He paints on the wall.

## Notes

涂 simplified from 塗. Water 氵 spreading, to apply coating.
