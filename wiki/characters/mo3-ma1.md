# 抹 Smear

- **Character**: 抹
- **Pinyin**: mǒ/mā
- **Meaning**: wipe, smear
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to apply, smear
- 抹药 (mǒ yào) — to apply medicine
- 擦抹 (cā mǒ) — to wipe

## Example Sentences

- 抹上消炎药膏。(Mǒ shang xiāo yán yào gāo.) — Apply anti-inflammatory ointment.

## Notes

Wiping or spreading; medication application.
