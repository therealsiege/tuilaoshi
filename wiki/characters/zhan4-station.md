# 站 Station, website

- **Character**: 站
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: station, website
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 网站 (wǎng zhàn) — website
- 站点 (zhàn diǎn) — site, station

## Example Sentences

- 这个网站很有用。(Zhè gè wǎng zhàn hěn yǒu yòng.) — This website is very useful.

## Notes

Used in 网站 (website), 站点 (site), 车站 (station)
