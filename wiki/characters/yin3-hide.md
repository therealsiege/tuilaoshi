# 隐 To hide, conceal, 隱 (traditional)

- **Character**: 隐
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: to hide, conceal, 隱 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 隐藏 (yǐn cáng) — to hide, conceal
- 隐私 (yǐn sī) — privacy

## Example Sentences

- 他隐藏了真相。(Tā yǐncángle zhēnxiàng.) — He concealed the truth.

## Notes

To hide, conceal, or keep private.
