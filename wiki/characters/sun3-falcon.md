# 隼 Falcon, hawk

- **Character**: 隼
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: falcon, hawk
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 游隼 (yóu sǔn) — peregrine falcon
- 隼鸟 (sǔn niǎo) — falcon

## Example Sentences

- 隼是一种猛禽。(Sǔn shì yī zhǒng měng qín.) — Falcons are birds of prey.
