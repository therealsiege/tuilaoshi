# 鼠 Mouse

- **Character**: 鼠
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: rat, mouse
- **Radical**: 鼠 (rat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鼠标 (shǔ biāo) — mouse (computer)
- 老鼠 (lǎo shǔ) — rat, mouse

## Example Sentences

- 我的鼠标坏了。(Wǒ de shǔ biāo huài le.) — My mouse is broken.

## Notes

鼠 is the zodiac rat, borrowed for computer mouse due to shape resemblance.
