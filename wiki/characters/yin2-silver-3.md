# 银 Silver

- **Character**: 银
- **Pinyin**: yín
- **Meaning**: silver, bank
- **Radical**: 金 (gold)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 银行 (yín háng) — bank
- 银色 (yín sè) — silver color

## Example Sentences

- 我要去银行取钱。(Wǒ yào qù yín háng qǔ qián.) — I need to go to the bank to withdraw money.

## Notes

银 simplified from 銀. Metal 金 plus phonetic 艮.
