# 鬼 Ghost

- **Character**: 鬼
- **Pinyin**: guǐ
- **Meaning**: ghost, spirit
- **Radical**: 鬼 (ghost)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 鬼魂 (guǐ hún) — ghost, spirit
- 魔鬼 (mó guǐ) — demon

## Example Sentences

- 小孩子不要讲鬼故事。(Xiǎo hái zi bù yào jiǎng guǐ gù shì.) — Children shouldn't tell ghost stories.

## Notes

鬼 is both a radical and character. Can mean ghost, crafty, or be used in compounds for suspicious behavior.
