# 疆 Border, boundary, territory

- **Character**: 疆
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: border, boundary, territory
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 疆域 (jiāng yù) — territory, domain
- 边疆 (biān jiāng) — border region

## Example Sentences

- 保卫边疆是军人的职责。(Bǎo wèi biān jiāng shì jūn rén de zhí zé.) — Defending the borders is a soldier's duty.

## Notes

Important concept in territorial integrity and sovereignty
