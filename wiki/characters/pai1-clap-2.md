# 拍 Clap

- **Character**: 拍
- **Pinyin**: pāi
- **Meaning**: clap, beat, time
- **Radical**: 手(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拍手 (pāi shǒu) — clap hands
- 节拍 (jié pāi) — beat, meter

## Example Sentences

- 观众跟着音乐的节拍鼓掌。(Guānzhòng gēn zhe yīnyuè de jiépāi gǔzhǎng.) — The audience clapped along with the beat of the music.

## Notes

拍 means to clap or beat. In music, it refers to the beat or time signature.
