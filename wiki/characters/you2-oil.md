# 油 Oil

- **Character**: 油
- **Pinyin**: yóu
- **Meaning**: oil, grease, petroleum
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 石油 (shí yóu) — petroleum
- 酱油 (jiàng yóu) — soy sauce

## Example Sentences

- 炒菜需要油。(Chǎo cài xū yào yóu.) — Stir-frying needs oil.

## Notes

油 means oil. 加油 = add fuel, cheer on. 汽油 = gasoline.
