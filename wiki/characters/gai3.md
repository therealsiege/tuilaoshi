# 改 To Alter

- **Character**: 改
- **Pinyin**: gǎi
- **Meaning**: to change, to alter, to correct
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 改变 (gǎi biàn) — to change
- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — to revise

## Example Sentences

- 我想改一下计划。(Wǒ xiǎng gǎi yī xià jì huà.) — I want to change the plan a bit.

## Notes

改 implies deliberate modification or correction, as in 改错 (correct mistakes) or 改革 (reform).
