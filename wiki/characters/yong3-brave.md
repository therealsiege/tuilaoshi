# 勇 Brave

- **Character**: 勇
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: brave, courageous
- **Radical**: 力 (strength)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 见义勇为 (jiàn yì yǒng wéi) — act bravely for justice
- 勇敢 (yǒng gǎn) — brave

## Example Sentences

- 他勇敢地救了那个孩子。(Tā yǒnggǎn de jiù le nà ge háizi.) — He bravely saved that child.

## Notes

The courage in 见义勇为; also used in 勇敢 (brave) and 勇气 (courage).
