# 四 Four

- **Character**: 四
- **Pinyin**: sì
- **Meaning**: four
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 四月 (sì yuè) — April
- 四季 (sì jì) — four seasons

## Example Sentences

- 一年有四个季节。(Yī nián yǒu sì gè jì jié.) — There are four seasons in a year.

## Notes

四 sounds like 死 (sǐ, death), so it is considered unlucky. Many buildings skip the 4th floor.
