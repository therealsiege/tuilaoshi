# 垠 Boundary, limit, bank

- **Character**: 垠
- **Pinyin**: yín
- **Meaning**: boundary, limit, bank
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 无垠 (wú yín) — boundless
- 一望无垠 (yī wàng wú yín) — boundless as far as the eye can see

## Example Sentences

- 大海一望无垠。(Dà hǎi yī wàng wú yín.) — The ocean is boundless.

## Notes

Often used in poetic descriptions of vast landscapes
