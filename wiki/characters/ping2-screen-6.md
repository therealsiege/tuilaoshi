# 屏 Screen

- **Character**: 屏
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: screen
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen
- 荧屏 (yíng píng) — screen (TV)

## Example Sentences

- 广告出现在大屏幕上。(Guǎng gào chū xiàn zài dà píng mù shàng.) — Advertisements appear on the big screen.

## Notes

Different from 屏 (bǐng = hold breath). 银屏 means silver screen.
