# 捡 To Pick Up

- **Character**: 捡
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: to pick up, to collect
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捡起 (jiǎn qǐ) — to pick up
- 捡到 (jiǎn dào) — to find

## Example Sentences

- 我在地上捡到了一个钱包。(Wǒ zài dì shàng jiǎn dào le yí gè qián bāo.) — I picked up a wallet on the ground.

## Notes

捡 simplified from 撿. Hand 扌 selecting and gathering.
