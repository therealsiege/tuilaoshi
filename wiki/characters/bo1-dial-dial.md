# 拨 To Dial

- **Character**: 拨
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to dial, to poke, to stir
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拨打 (bō dǎ) — to dial (phone)
- 拨款 (bō kuǎn) — to allocate funds

## Example Sentences

- 请拨打这个电话。(Qǐng bō dǎ zhè ge diàn huà.) — Please dial this number.

## Notes

拨 means to dial or poke. 拨款 = allocate funds, 拨乱反正 = restore order.
