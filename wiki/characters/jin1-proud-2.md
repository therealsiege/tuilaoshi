# 矜 Proud, pity

- **Character**: 矜
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: proud, pity
- **Radical**: 矛 (spear)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 矜持 (jīnchí) — reserved, restrained
- 自矜 (zìjīn) — self-conceited

## Example Sentences

- 她很矜持。(Tā hěn jīnchí.) — She is very reserved.

## Notes

Literary term for pride or reservedness; also means to pity.
