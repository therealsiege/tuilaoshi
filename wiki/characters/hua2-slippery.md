# 滑 Slippery, smooth, to slip

- **Character**: 滑
- **Pinyin**: huá
- **Meaning**: slippery, smooth, to slip
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 光滑 (guāng huá) — smooth, glossy
- 滑稽 (huá jī) — funny, comical

## Example Sentences

- 地面很滑，小心摔倒。(Dìmiàn hěn huá, xiǎoxīn shuāidǎo.) — The floor is slippery, be careful not to fall.

## Notes

Slippery or smooth surface, or to slide.
