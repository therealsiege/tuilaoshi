# 跪 To Kneel

- **Character**: 跪
- **Pinyin**: guì
- **Meaning**: to kneel
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跪下 (guì xià) — to kneel down
- 下跪 (xià guì) — to kneel

## Example Sentences

- 他跪在地上祈祷。(Tā guì zài dì shàng qí dǎo.) — He knelt on the ground to pray.

## Notes

跪 combines foot 足 and 危 (danger), representing kneeling position.
