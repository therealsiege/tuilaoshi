# 喷 Spurt, spray, spout

- **Character**: 喷
- **Pinyin**: pēn
- **Meaning**: spurt, spray, spout
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喷发 (pēn fā) — erupt, spurt
- 喷泉 (pēn quán) — fountain

## Example Sentences

- 火山喷发出大量岩浆。(Huǒ shān pēn fā chū dà liàng yán jiāng.) — The volcano erupted with a large amount of magma.
