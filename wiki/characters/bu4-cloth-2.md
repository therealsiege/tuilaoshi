# 布 Cloth, announce, spread

- **Character**: 布
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: cloth, announce, spread
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 公布 (gōng bù) — to announce publicly
- 宣布 (xuān bù) — to declare, announce

## Example Sentences

- 法院公布了判决结果。(Fǎ yuàn gōng bù le pàn jué jié guǒ.) — The court announced the verdict.

## Notes

Originally cloth/fabric, extended to spreading/announcing information. 分布 means distribution. 布置 means to arrange.
