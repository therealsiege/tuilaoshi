# 筏 Raft

- **Character**: 筏
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: raft
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 竹筏 (zhú fá) — bamboo raft
- 木筏 (mù fá) — wooden raft

## Example Sentences

- 他们乘竹筏过河。(Tā men chéng zhú fá guò hé.) — They crossed the river on a bamboo raft.

## Notes

筏 has bamboo 竹 with 伐 (to cut), bamboo poles lashed for floating.
