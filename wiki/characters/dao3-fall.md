# 倒 To Fall

- **Character**: 倒
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: to fall, to collapse, to pour
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倒下 (dǎo xià) — to fall down
- 倒塌 (dǎo tā) — to collapse

## Example Sentences

- 树被风吹倒了。(Shù bèi fēng chuī dǎo le.) — The tree was blown down by wind.

## Notes

倒 combines person 亻 and 到 (arrive), can mean to fall (dǎo) or to pour (dào).
