# 柔 Soft, gentle, supple

- **Character**: 柔
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: soft, gentle, supple
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柔软 (róu ruǎn) — soft, flexible
- 温柔 (wēn róu) — gentle, tender

## Example Sentences

- 她说话的声音温柔动听。(Tā shuō huà de shēng yīn wēn róu dòng tīng.) — Her speaking voice is gentle and pleasant to hear.

## Notes

Describes physical softness or gentle character.
