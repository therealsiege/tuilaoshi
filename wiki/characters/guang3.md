# 犷 Rough, boorish, uncouth

- **Character**: 犷
- **Pinyin**: guǎng
- **Meaning**: rough, boorish, uncouth
- **Radical**: 犭 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 粗犷 (cū guǎng) — rough, rugged
- 犷悍 (guǎng hàn) — rough and fierce

## Example Sentences

- 他的性格粗犷豪放。(Tā de xìng gé cū guǎng háo fàng.) — His character is rough and unrestrained.

## Notes

Often used to describe rough or bold character traits.
