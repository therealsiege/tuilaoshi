# 玉 Jade

- **Character**: 玉
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: jade
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 玉石 (yù shí) — jade
- 玉米 (yù mǐ) — corn

## Example Sentences

- 她戴着一块玉。(Tā dài zhe yī kuài yù.) — She is wearing a piece of jade.

## Notes

玉 means jade, highly valued in Chinese culture. Used in玉石 (jade), 玉米 (corn, literally 'jade grain').
