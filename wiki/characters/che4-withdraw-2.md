# 撤 Withdraw, remove

- **Character**: 撤
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: withdraw, remove
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撤销 (chè xiāo) — cancel, revoke
- 撤回 (chè huí) — withdraw, retract

## Example Sentences

- 公司决定撤销这个决定。(Gōng sī jué dìng chè xiāo zhè ge jué dìng.) — The company decided to revoke this decision.

## Notes

撤诉 means withdraw a lawsuit. 撤资 means withdraw investment.
