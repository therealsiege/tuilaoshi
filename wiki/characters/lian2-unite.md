# 联 To Unite

- **Character**: 联
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: to unite, to join, even
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 联合 (lián hé) — to unite
- 联系 (lián xì) — contact

## Example Sentences

- 我会和你保持联系。(Wǒ huì hé nǐ bǎo chí lián xì.) — I will keep in touch with you.

## Notes

联 simplified from 聯. Ears 耳 connected by threads, linking together.
