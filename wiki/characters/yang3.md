# 养 To raise, to keep, to support

- **Character**: 养
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: to raise, to keep, to support
- **Radical**: 丷(eight)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 养育 (yǎng yù) — to raise, to bring up
- 营养 (yíng yǎng) — nutrition, nourishment

## Example Sentences

- 他在家养了一只猫。(Tā zài jiā yǎng le yī zhī māo.) — He keeps a cat at home.

## Notes

Common verb for raising children, pets, or supporting.
