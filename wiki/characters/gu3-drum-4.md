# 鼓 Drum

- **Character**: 鼓
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: drum, to beat
- **Radical**: 鼓 (drum)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 打鼓 (dǎ gǔ) — to beat a drum
- 鼓舞 (gǔ wǔ) — to inspire

## Example Sentences

- 他在台上打鼓。(Tā zài tái shàng dǎ gǔ.) — He is drumming on stage.

## Notes

鼓 is both the instrument and the action of beating it.
