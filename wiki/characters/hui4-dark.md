# 晦 Dark

- **Character**: 晦
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: dark, obscure
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 晦涩 (huì sè) — obscure, abstruse
- 隐晦 (yǐn huì) — veiled, obscure

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章的语言很晦涩。(Zhè piān wén zhāng de yǔ yán hěn huì sè.) — The language of this article is very obscure.

## Notes

晦 uses sun radical 日, meaning dark or the last day of lunar month. Extended to mean obscure or unclear.
