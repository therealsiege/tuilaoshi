# 端 End

- **Character**: 端
- **Pinyin**: duān
- **Meaning**: end, terminal, proper
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 终端 (zhōng duān) — terminal
- 客户端 (kè hù duān) — client

## Example Sentences

- 请重启终端设备。(Qǐng chóng qǐ zhōng duān shè bèi.) — Please restart the terminal device.

## Notes

端 is crucial in technology: 前端 (frontend), 后端 (backend), 服务器端 (server-side).
