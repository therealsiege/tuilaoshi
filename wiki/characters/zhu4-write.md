# 著 Write, literary work, obvious

- **Character**: 著
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: write, literary work, obvious
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 著名 (zhùmíng) — famous, well-known
- 著作 (zhùzuò) — literary work, writings

## Example Sentences

- 他是著名的作家。(Tā shì zhùmíng de zuòjiā.) — He is a famous writer.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: writing, works, and being obvious or famous.
