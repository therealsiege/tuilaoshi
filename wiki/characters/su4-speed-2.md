# 速 Speed

- **Character**: 速
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: speed
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 速度 (sù dù) — speed
- 高速 (gāo sù) — high speed

## Example Sentences

- 下载速度很快。(Xià zài sù dù hěn kuài.) — The download speed is fast.

## Notes

Used in 速度 (speed), 速率 (rate), 高速 (high speed)
