# 粗 Coarse

- **Character**: 粗
- **Pinyin**: cū
- **Meaning**: coarse, rough, thick
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粗糙 (cū cāo) — rough, coarse
- 粗心 (cū xīn) — careless

## Example Sentences

- 他做事太粗心了。(Tā zuò shì tài cū xīn le.) — He is too careless in his work.

## Notes

粗 combines rice 米 and 且 (moreover), representing unrefined or rough texture.
