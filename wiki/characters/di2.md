# 嘀 To Mutter

- **Character**: 嘀
- **Pinyin**: dí
- **Meaning**: to mutter, to whisper
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 嘀咕 (dí gū) — to mutter, to grumble
- 嘀嗒 (dī dā) — tick-tock

## Example Sentences

- 他在那儿嘀咕什么呢？(Tā zài nà r dí gū shén me ne?) — What is he muttering about there?

## Notes

嘀 shows mouth 口 and 啇 (commerce), representing quiet speech or muttering.
