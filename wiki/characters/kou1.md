# 抠 To Dig Out

- **Character**: 抠
- **Pinyin**: kōu
- **Meaning**: to dig out, to pick, stingy
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抠门 (kōu mén) — stingy
- 抠字眼 (kōu zì yǎn) — to nitpick

## Example Sentences

- 他很抠门，不愿意花钱。(Tā hěn kōu mén, bù yuàn yì huā qián.) — He is very stingy and unwilling to spend money.

## Notes

抠 simplified from 摳. Hand 扌 digging motion.
