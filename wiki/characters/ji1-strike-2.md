# 击 To Strike

- **Character**: 击
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to strike, to hit, to attack
- **Radical**: 凵 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 打击 (dǎ jī) — to strike, to hit
- 击鼓 (jī gǔ) — to beat a drum

## Example Sentences

- 打击乐器包括鼓和锣。(Dǎ jī yuè qì bāo kuò gǔ hé luó.) — Percussion instruments include drums and gongs.

## Notes

击 means to strike or hit, more formal than 打.
