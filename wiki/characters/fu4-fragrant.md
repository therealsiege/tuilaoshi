# 馥 Fragrant, aromatic

- **Character**: 馥
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: fragrant, aromatic
- **Radical**: 香 (fragrance)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 馥郁 (fù yù) — fragrant, aromatic
- 芬馥 (fēn fù) — fragrant

## Example Sentences

- 花园里百花盛开，芳香馥郁。(Huā yuán lǐ bǎi huā shèng kāi, fāng xiāng fù yù.) — The garden is in full bloom, fragrant and aromatic.

## Notes

Literary term for rich, pleasant fragrance.
