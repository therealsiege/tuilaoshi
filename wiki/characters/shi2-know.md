# 识 Knowledge

- **Character**: 识
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: to know, to recognize, knowledge
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 认识 (rèn shí) — to know (a person)
- 知识 (zhī shí) — knowledge

## Example Sentences

- 认识你很高兴。(Rèn shí nǐ hěn gāo xìng.) — Nice to meet you.

## Notes

识 is simplified from 識. It primarily appears in compounds: 认识, 知识, 常识 (common sense).
