# 繁 Complicated, numerous, propagate

- **Character**: 繁
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: complicated, numerous, propagate
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 繁殖 (fán zhí) — reproduce, breed
- 繁荣 (fán róng) — prosperous, flourishing

## Example Sentences

- 许多动物在春季繁殖。(Xǔ duō dòng wù zài chūn jì fán zhí.) — Many animals reproduce in spring.

## Notes

Used for reproduction and complexity
