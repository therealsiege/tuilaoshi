# 蠕 To Squirm

- **Character**: 蠕
- **Pinyin**: rú
- **Meaning**: to squirm, to wriggle
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 蠕动 (rú dòng) — to squirm
- 蠕虫 (rú chóng) — worm

## Example Sentences

- 毛毛虫在叶子上蠕动。(Máo mao chóng zài yè zi shàng rú dòng.) — The caterpillar squirmed on the leaf.

## Notes

蠕 has insect 虫 with 需 (to need), slow creeping movement.
