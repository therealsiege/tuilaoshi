# 磨 To Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: to grind, to polish
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 磨练 (mó liàn) — to temper oneself
- 折磨 (zhé mó) — to torture

## Example Sentences

- 困难磨练了他的意志。(Kùn nán mó liàn le tā de yì zhì.) — Difficulties tempered his will.

## Notes

磨 as mó means to grind or polish. As mò it means millstone. Extended to mean wear down or torment.
