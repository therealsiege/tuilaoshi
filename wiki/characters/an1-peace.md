# 安 Peace

- **Character**: 安
- **Pinyin**: ān
- **Meaning**: peace, safe, calm
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 安全 (ān quán) — safe, safety
- 安静 (ān jìng) — quiet

## Example Sentences

- 请安静。(Qǐng ān jìng.) — Please be quiet.

## Notes

安 shows a woman (女) under a roof (宀) — a woman at home is peaceful.
