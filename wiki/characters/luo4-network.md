# 络 Network, connect

- **Character**: 络
- **Pinyin**: luò
- **Meaning**: network, connect
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 网络 (wǎng luò) — network
- 联络 (lián luò) — contact, communicate

## Example Sentences

- 无线网络很快。(Wú xiàn wǎng luò hěn kuài.) — The wireless network is fast.

Used in 网络 (network), 脉络 (context), networking terminology
