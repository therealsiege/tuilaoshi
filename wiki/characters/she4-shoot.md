# 射 Shoot, emit, radiate

- **Character**: 射
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: shoot, emit, radiate
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 辐射 (fú shè) — radiation
- 反射 (fǎn shè) — reflection, reflex

## Example Sentences

- 太阳辐射是地球能量的主要来源。(Tài yáng fú shè shì dì qiú néng liàng de zhǔ yào lái yuán.) — Solar radiation is the main source of Earth's energy.

## Notes

Common in physics for radiation and emission
