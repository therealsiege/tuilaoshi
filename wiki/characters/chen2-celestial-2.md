# 辰 Celestial

- **Character**: 辰
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: celestial bodies, time
- **Radical**: 辰(morning)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 星辰 (xīng chén) — stars
- 生辰 (shēng chén) — birthday

## Example Sentences

- 夜空中星辰闪烁。(Yè kōng zhōng xīngchén shǎnshuò.) — Stars twinkle in the night sky.

## Notes

辰 refers to celestial bodies or time periods. One of the twelve Earthly Branches in Chinese calendar.
