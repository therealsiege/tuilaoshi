# 赦 To Pardon

- **Character**: 赦
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: to pardon, amnesty
- **Radical**: 赤 (red)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 大赦 (dà shè) — amnesty
- 特赦 (tè shè) — special pardon

## Example Sentences

- 国家宣布大赦。(Guó jiā xuān bù dà shè.) — The nation announced a general amnesty.

## Notes

赦 represents forgiveness and pardon from punishment.
