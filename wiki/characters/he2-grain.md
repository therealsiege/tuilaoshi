# 禾 Grain, rice, cereal

- **Character**: 禾
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: grain, rice, cereal
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 禾苗 (hé miáo) — rice seedlings
- 嘉禾 (jiā hé) — auspicious grain

## Example Sentences

- 禾苗在阳光下茁壮成长。(Hé miáo zài yáng guāng xià zhuó zhuàng chéng zhǎng.) — Rice seedlings grow vigorously in the sunlight.

## Notes

Radical representing grain crops
