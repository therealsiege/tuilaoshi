# 摔 Fall

- **Character**: 摔
- **Pinyin**: shuāi
- **Meaning**: to fall, throw down
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 摔跤 (shuāi jiāo) — wrestling
- 摔倒 (shuāi dǎo) — to fall down

## Example Sentences

- 摔跤是古老的格斗运动。(Shuāijiāo shì gǔlǎo de gédòu yùndòng.) — Wrestling is an ancient combat sport.

## Notes

Throwing and wrestling; grappling sport
