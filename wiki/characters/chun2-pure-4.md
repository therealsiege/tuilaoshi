# 淳 Pure

- **Character**: 淳
- **Pinyin**: chún
- **Meaning**: pure, simple, honest
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淳朴 (chún pǔ) — simple and honest
- 淳厚 (chún hòu) — sincere

## Example Sentences

- 他有淳朴的性格。(Tā yǒu chún pǔ de xìng gé.) — He has a simple and honest character.

## Notes

淳 has water 氵 with 享 (to enjoy), pure unmixed essence.
