# 篷 Canopy

- **Character**: 篷
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: canopy, covering
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 帐篷 (zhàng篷 péng) — tent
- 天篷 (tiān péng) — canopy

## Example Sentences

- 我们搭起帐篷。(Wǒ men dā qǐ zhàng péng.) — We set up a tent.

## Notes

篷 with bamboo radical for bamboo/fabric canopy, temporary covers.
