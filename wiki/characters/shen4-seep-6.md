# 渗 To Seep

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: to seep, to infiltrate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate
- 渗入 (shèn rù) — to seep into

## Example Sentences

- 雨水渗入了墙壁。(Yǔ shuǐ shèn rù le qiáng bì.) — Rainwater seeped into the wall.

## Notes

渗 simplified from 滲. Water 氵 plus 参 phonetic.
