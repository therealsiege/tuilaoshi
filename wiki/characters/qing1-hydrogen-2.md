# 氢 Hydrogen

- **Character**: 氢
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: hydrogen
- **Radical**: 气(gas)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 氢气 (qīng qì) — hydrogen gas
- 氢弹 (qīng dàn) — hydrogen bomb

## Example Sentences

- 氢是最轻的化学元素。(Qīng shì zuì qīng de huàxué yuánsù.) — Hydrogen is the lightest chemical element.

## Notes

氢 is hydrogen, the most abundant element in the universe. The gas radical indicates it's a gas.
