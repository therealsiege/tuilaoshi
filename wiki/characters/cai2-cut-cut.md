# 裁 To Cut

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut, to tailor, to judge, to reduce
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee, judge
- 裁员 (cái yuán) — to lay off workers

## Example Sentences

- 裁判做出了判决。(Cái pàn zuò chū le pàn jué.) — The referee made a ruling.

## Notes

裁 means to cut/tailor or to judge. 裁判 = referee, 裁缝 = tailor.
