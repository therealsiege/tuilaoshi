# 痉 Spasm

- **Character**: 痉
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: spasm
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 痉挛 (jìng luán) — spasm, convulsion
- 抽痉 (chōu jìng) — convulsion

## Example Sentences

- 肌肉痉挛很痛。(Jī ròu jìng luán hěn tòng.) — Muscle spasms are very painful.

## Notes

Involuntary muscle contraction; can indicate nerve irritation.
