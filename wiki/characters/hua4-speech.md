# 话 Words

- **Character**: 话
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: speech, words, language
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 说话 (shuō huà) — to speak
- 电话 (diàn huà) — telephone

## Example Sentences

- 你说的话我听不懂。(Nǐ shuō de huà wǒ tīng bù dǒng.) — I don't understand what you said.

## Notes

话 combines the speech radical 讠with 舌 (tongue). It means spoken words or language.
