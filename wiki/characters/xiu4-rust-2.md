# 锈 Rust

- **Character**: 锈
- **Pinyin**: xiù
- **Meaning**: rust, to rust
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 生锈 (shēng xiù) — to rust
- 铁锈 (tiě xiù) — rust

## Example Sentences

- 这把刀生锈了。(Zhè bǎ dāo shēng xiù le.) — This knife has rusted.

## Notes

锈 simplified from 鏽. Metal 钅 plus 秀 (elegant), metal deteriorating.
