# 糖 Sugar

- **Character**: 糖
- **Pinyin**: táng
- **Meaning**: sugar, candy, sweet
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 白糖 (bái táng) — white sugar
- 糖果 (táng guǒ) — candy

## Example Sentences

- 我不喜欢吃糖。(Wǒ bù xǐ huān chī táng.) — I don't like eating candy.

## Notes

糖 means sugar or candy. 糖水 = sweet soup. 红糖 = brown sugar.
