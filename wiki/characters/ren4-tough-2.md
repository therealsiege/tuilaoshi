# 韧 Ligament

- **Character**: 韧
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: tough, pliable
- **Radical**: 韦 (soft leather)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 韧带 (rèn dài) — ligament
- 坚韧 (jiān rèn) — tenacious, tough
- 柔韧 (róu rèn) — flexible, supple

## Example Sentences

- 他膝盖韧带受伤了。(Tā xī gài rèn dài shòu shāng le.) — His knee ligament was injured.

## Notes

Describes toughness; 韧带 (ligament) connects bones.
