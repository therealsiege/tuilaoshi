# 免 免除, avoid, exempt

- **Character**: 免
- **Pinyin**: miǎn
- **Meaning**: 免除, avoid, exempt
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 免费 (miǎn fèi) — free of charge
- 豁免 (huō miǎn) — exempt, waive

## Example Sentences

- 运费免费。(Yùn fèi miǎn fèi.) — Shipping is free.

## Notes

免税 means tax-free. 减免 means reduce or remit.
