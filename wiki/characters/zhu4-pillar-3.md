# 柱 Pillar

- **Character**: 柱
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: pillar, column
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柱子 (zhù zi) — pillar
- 圆柱 (yuán zhù) — cylinder

## Example Sentences

- 大厅里有很多柱子。(Dà tīng lǐ yǒu hěn duō zhù zi.) — There are many pillars in the hall.

## Notes

柱 with wood radical for pillar/column, supporting structure.
