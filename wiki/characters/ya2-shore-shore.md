# 涯 Shore

- **Character**: 涯
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: shore, cliff, limit
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 天涯 (tiān yá) — ends of the earth
- 生涯 (shēng yá) — career

## Example Sentences

- 学海无涯。(Xué hǎi wú yá.) — The sea of learning has no shore.

## Notes

涯 has water 氵 with 崖 phonetic, the boundary of water meeting land.
