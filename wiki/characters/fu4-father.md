# 父 Father

- **Character**: 父
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: father
- **Radical**: 父 (father)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 父亲 (fù qīn) — father (formal)
- 父母 (fù mǔ) — parents

## Example Sentences

- 我的父亲是老师。(Wǒ de fù qīn shì lǎo shī.) — My father is a teacher.

## Notes

父亲 is the formal word for father. 爸爸 is informal. 父母 = parents.
