# 肘 Elbow

- **Character**: 肘
- **Pinyin**: zhǒu
- **Meaning**: elbow
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肘击 (zhǒu jī) — elbow strike
- 胳膊肘 (gē bo zhǒu) — elbow

## Example Sentences

- 泰拳允许使用肘击。(Tàiquán yǔnxǔ shǐyòng zhǒujī.) — Muay Thai allows elbow strikes.

## Notes

Elbow; elbow strikes in martial arts
