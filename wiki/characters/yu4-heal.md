# 愈 Heal, recover, the more

- **Character**: 愈
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: heal, recover, the more
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痊愈 (quán yù) — recover completely
- 愈加 (yù jiā) — all the more

## Example Sentences

- 他的病已经痊愈了。(Tā de bìng yǐ jīng quán yù le.) — He has completely recovered from his illness.
