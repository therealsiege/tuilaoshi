# 酬 Reward, compensate, toast

- **Character**: 酬
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: reward, compensate, toast
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 报酬 (bào chóu) — remuneration, reward
- 酬劳 (chóu láo) — to reward, payment

## Example Sentences

- 他得到了应有的报酬。(Tā dé dào le yīng yǒu de bào chóu.) — He received the remuneration he deserved.

## Notes

Means compensation or reward. 酬谢 means to thank with a gift. 应酬 means social engagement.
