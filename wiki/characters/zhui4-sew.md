# 缀 Sew, stitch, decorate

- **Character**: 缀
- **Pinyin**: zhuì
- **Meaning**: sew, stitch, decorate
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 点缀 (diǎn zhuì) — embellish, decorate
- 缀合 (zhuì hé) — stitch together

## Example Sentences

- 几朵白云点缀着蓝天。(Jǐ duǒ bái yún diǎn zhuì zhe lán tiān.) — A few white clouds embellish the blue sky.

## Notes

Attaching decorations or connecting pieces
