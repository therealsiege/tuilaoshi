# 滩 Beach

- **Character**: 滩
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: beach, shoal
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 海滩 (hǎitān) — beach
- 沙滩 (shātān) — sandy beach

## Example Sentences

- 我们去海滩玩。(Wǒmen qù hǎitān wán.) — We're going to play at the beach.

## Notes

Coastal vocabulary. Water radical. 海滩 and 沙滩 are popular vacation destinations. Tourist context.
