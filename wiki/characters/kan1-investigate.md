# 勘 Investigate, survey, collate

- **Character**: 勘
- **Pinyin**: kān
- **Meaning**: investigate, survey, collate
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 勘察 (kān chá) — to survey, investigate
- 勘误 (kān wù) — to correct errors, errata

## Example Sentences

- 工程师正在现场勘察地形。(Gōng chéng shī zhèng zài xiàn chǎng kān chá dì xíng.) — The engineers are surveying the terrain on site.

## Notes

Used for surveying land or investigating carefully. 勘误表 is an errata sheet.
