# 翎 Feather

- **Character**: 翎
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: feather, plume
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 翎毛 (líng máo) — feather
- 雉翎 (zhì líng) — pheasant feather

## Example Sentences

- 孔雀的翎毛很美丽。(Kǒng què de líng máo hěn měi lì.) — The peacock's plumage is beautiful.

## Notes

翎 has feather 羽 with 令 (command), long ornamental feather.
