# 占 To Occupy

- **Character**: 占
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: to occupy, to take up, to constitute
- **Radical**: 卜 (divination)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 占领 (zhàn lǐng) — to occupy
- 占据 (zhàn jù) — to occupy, to hold

## Example Sentences

- 这个项目占用了很多时间。(Zhè ge xiàng mù zhàn yòng le hěn duō shí jiān.) — This project took up a lot of time.

## Notes

占 means to occupy space or time. Can also be zhān meaning to divine or占ticate.
