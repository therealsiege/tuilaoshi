# 星 Star

- **Character**: 星
- **Pinyin**: xīng
- **Meaning**: star, heavenly body
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 星星 (xīng xing) — star
- 卫星 (wèi xīng) — satellite

## Example Sentences

- 夜空中有很多星星。(Yè kōng zhōng yǒu hěn duō xīng xing.) — There are many stars in the night sky.

## Notes

星 has sun 日 with 生 (life), celestial bodies that shine.
