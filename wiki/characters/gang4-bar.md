# 杠 Bar

- **Character**: 杠
- **Pinyin**: gàng
- **Meaning**: bar, pole
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 单杠 (dān gàng) — horizontal bar
- 双杠 (shuāng gàng) — parallel bars

## Example Sentences

- 他在单杠上做动作。(Tā zài dāngàng shàng zuò dòngzuò.) — He performs moves on the horizontal bar.

## Notes

Bars used in gymnastics equipment
