# 在 At/In/Exist

- **Character**: 在
- **Pinyin**: zài
- **Meaning**: at, in, on; to exist, to be (located)
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 现在 (xiànzài) — now, at present
- 在家 (zài jiā) — at home
- 存在 (cúnzài) — to exist, existence
- 正在 (zhèngzài) — in the process of (progressive marker)
- 不在 (bú zài) — not present, absent

## Example Sentences

- 他在学校。(Tā zài xuéxiào.) — He is at school.
- 你在做什么？(Nǐ zài zuò shénme?) — What are you doing?
- 书在桌子上。(Shū zài zhuōzi shàng.) — The book is on the table.

## Notes

在 functions as both a verb ("to be located") and a preposition ("at, in, on"). When placed before a verb, it indicates an ongoing action (similar to English "-ing"): 我在吃饭 (wǒ zài chī fàn, "I am eating"). This progressive usage can be strengthened with 正 to form 正在. The character combines 土 (earth) with a phonetic element, suggesting a fixed place on the ground.
