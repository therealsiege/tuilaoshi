# 那 That

- **Character**: 那
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: that, then
- **Radical**: 阝(city)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 那里 (nà lǐ) — there
- 那个 (nà ge) — that one

## Example Sentences

- 那是什么？(Nà shì shénme?) — What is that?

## Notes

那 pairs with 这 (this). 那 can also be read as nèi in colloquial speech before measure words.
