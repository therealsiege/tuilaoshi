# 逸 To escape, leisure, ease

- **Character**: 逸
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to escape, leisure, ease
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 安逸 (ān yì) — easy and comfortable
- 逃逸 (táo yì) — to escape, to flee

## Example Sentences

- 他过着安逸的生活。(Tā guòzhe ānyì de shēnghuó.) — He lives a comfortable and easy life.

## Notes

Can mean to escape or refer to leisurely comfort.
