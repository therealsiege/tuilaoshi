# 厂 Factory

- **Character**: 厂
- **Pinyin**: chǎng
- **Meaning**: factory, works
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 工厂 (gōng chǎng) — factory
- 厂长 (chǎng zhǎng) — factory director

## Example Sentences

- 他在工厂工作。(Tā zài gōng chǎng gōng zuò.) — He works in a factory.

## Notes

厂 means factory. Also a radical. 厂家 = manufacturer.
