# 卫 Guard, defend, protect

- **Character**: 卫
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: guard, defend, protect
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 保卫 (bǎo wèi) — defend, safeguard
- 卫生 (wèi shēng) — hygiene, health

## Example Sentences

- 士兵保卫国家安全。(Shì bīng bǎo wèi guó jiā ān quán.) — Soldiers defend national security.

## Notes

Military defense and protection
