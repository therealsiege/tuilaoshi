# 籍 Native Place

- **Character**: 籍
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: native place, record, membership
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 国籍 (guó jí) — nationality
- 户籍 (hù jí) — household registration

## Example Sentences

- 你的国籍是哪里？(Nǐ de guó jí shì nǎ lǐ?) — What is your nationality?

## Notes

籍 contains bamboo radical 竹, as ancient records were kept on bamboo slips. Represents official registration.
