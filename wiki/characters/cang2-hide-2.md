# 藏 To Hide

- **Character**: 藏
- **Pinyin**: cáng
- **Meaning**: to hide, to store; Tibet (zàng)
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 收藏 (shōu cáng) — to collect
- 西藏 (xī zàng) — Tibet

## Example Sentences

- 他把礼物藏起来了。(Tā bǎ lǐ wù cáng qǐ lái le.) — He hid the gift.

## Notes

藏 as cáng: to hide/store. As zàng: Tibet (西藏), Buddhist scriptures (大藏经).
