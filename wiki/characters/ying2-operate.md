# 营 Operate, camp, battalion

- **Character**: 营
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: operate, camp, battalion
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 经营 (jīngyíng) — operate, manage
- 营业 (yíngyè) — do business, be open for business

## Example Sentences

- 这家商店每天营业十小时。(Zhè jiā shāngdiàn měitiān yíngyè shí xiǎoshí.) — This store is open for business ten hours every day.

## Notes

Common in business contexts for operations and management.
