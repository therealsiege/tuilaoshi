# 疏 Sparse

- **Character**: 疏
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: thin, sparse, distant
- **Radical**: 疋 (bolt)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 疏忽 (shū hū) — to neglect, careless
- 稀疏 (xī shū) — sparse

## Example Sentences

- 由于疏忽，我犯了错误。(Yóu yú shū hū, wǒ fàn le cuò wù.) — Due to carelessness, I made a mistake.

## Notes

疏 shows 疋 (foot) and channels, representing clearing passages or things spread apart.
