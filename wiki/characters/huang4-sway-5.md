# 晃 To Sway

- **Character**: 晃
- **Pinyin**: huàng
- **Meaning**: to sway, to flash, to dazzle
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晃动 (huàng dòng) — to sway
- 摇晃 (yáo huàng) — to shake

## Example Sentences

- 船在水上晃动。(Chuán zài shuǐ shàng huàng dòng.) — The boat sways on the water.

## Notes

晃 has sun 日 with 晃 (bright), dazzling light, to sway.
