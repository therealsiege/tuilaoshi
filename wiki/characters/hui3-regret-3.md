# 悔 To Regret

- **Character**: 悔
- **Pinyin**: huǐ
- **Meaning**: to regret, to repent
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 后悔 (hòu huǐ) — to regret
- 悔恨 (huǐ hèn) — remorse

## Example Sentences

- 他后悔了。(Tā hòu huǐ le.) — He regrets it.

## Notes

悔 has heart 忄 with 每 (each), heart regretting every time thinking about it.
