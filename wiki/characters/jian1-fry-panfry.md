# 煎 To Pan-Fry

- **Character**: 煎
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: to pan-fry, to simmer
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 煎饼 (jiān bǐng) — pancake
- 煎熬 (jiān áo) — to torment

## Example Sentences

- 她煎了一个鸡蛋。(Tā jiān le yí gè jī dàn.) — She fried an egg.

## Notes

煎 has fire 灬 at bottom, cooking by shallow frying.
