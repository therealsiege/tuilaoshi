# 化 To Transform

- **Character**: 化
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to transform, to influence, -ize, culture
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 文化 (wén huà) — culture
- 变化 (biàn huà) — change

## Example Sentences

- 现代化很重要。(Xiàn dài huà hěn zhòng yào.) — Modernization is very important.

## Notes

化 as a suffix creates verbs or nouns of transformation (现代化 modernize, 绿化 greening, 美化 beautify).
