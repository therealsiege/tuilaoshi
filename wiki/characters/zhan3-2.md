# 展 展览, unfold, develop

- **Character**: 展
- **Pinyin**: zhǎn
- **Meaning**: 展览, unfold, develop
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 展览 (zhǎn lǎn) — exhibition
- 展示 (zhǎn shì) — display, show

## Example Sentences

- 公司参加贸易展览会。(Gōng sī cān jiā mào yì zhǎn lǎn huì.) — The company participates in the trade exhibition.

## Notes

展会 means exhibition/trade show. 展台 is exhibition booth.
