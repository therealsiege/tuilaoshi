# 惬 Satisfied, comfortable

- **Character**: 惬
- **Pinyin**: qiè
- **Meaning**: satisfied, comfortable
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惬意 (qiè yì) — pleased, satisfied
- 惬心 (qiè xīn) — satisfying

## Example Sentences

- 午后的阳光很惬意。(Wǔ hòu de yáng guāng hěn qiè yì.) — The afternoon sunshine is very pleasant.

## Notes

Contentment and satisfaction
