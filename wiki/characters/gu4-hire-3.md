# 雇 To Hire

- **Character**: 雇
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: to hire, to employ
- **Radical**: 隹 (short-tailed bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 雇员 (gù yuán) — employee
- 雇主 (gù zhǔ) — employer

## Example Sentences

- 公司雇了一位新员工。(Gōng sī gù le yí wèi xīn yuán gōng.) — The company hired a new employee.

## Notes

雇 originally related to a type of bird; extended meaning to hire.
