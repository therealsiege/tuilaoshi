# 氧 Oxygen

- **Character**: 氧
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: oxygen
- **Radical**: 气 (gas)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 氧气 (yǎng qì) — oxygen
- 二氧化碳 (èr yǎng huà tàn) — carbon dioxide

## Example Sentences

- 人类需要氧气才能生存。(Rén lèi xū yào yǎng qì cái néng shēng cún.) — Humans need oxygen to survive.

## Notes

Chemical element oxygen, essential for life
