# 弄 To Play With

- **Character**: 弄
- **Pinyin**: nòng
- **Meaning**: to play with, to do
- **Radical**: 廾 (hands)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 弄错 (nòng cuò) — to get wrong
- 摆弄 (bǎi nòng) — to fiddle with

## Example Sentences

- 他在弄琴弦。(Tā zài nòng qín xián.) — He is adjusting the strings.

## Notes

弄 means to fiddle with or manipulate; also read as lòng in 弄堂 (alley).
