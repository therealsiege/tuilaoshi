# 隔 To Separate

- **Character**: 隔
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: to separate, to隔开, partition
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 隔壁 (gé bì) — next door
- 隔开 (gé kāi) — to separate

## Example Sentences

- 隔壁住着一家人。(Gé bì zhù zhe yī jiā rén.) — A family lives next door.

## Notes

隔 represents physical or temporal separation between things.
