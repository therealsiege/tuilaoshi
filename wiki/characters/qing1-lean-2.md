# 倾 To Lean

- **Character**: 倾
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: to lean, to incline, to pour out
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倾向 (qīng xiàng) — tendency
- 倾斜 (qīng xié) — to incline

## Example Sentences

- 他有学习语言的倾向。(Tā yǒu xué xí yǔ yán de qīng xiàng.) — He has a tendency to study languages.

## Notes

倾 simplified from 傾. Person radical 亻 representing leaning or tilting.
