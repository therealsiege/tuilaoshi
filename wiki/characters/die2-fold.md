# 叠 To fold, to pile up, to repeat

- **Character**: 叠
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: to fold, to pile up, to repeat
- **Radical**: 又(again)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 重叠 (chóng dié) — to overlap, to duplicate
- 折叠 (zhé dié) — to fold

## Example Sentences

- 请把衣服叠好。(Qǐng bǎ yīfu dié hǎo.) — Please fold the clothes properly.

## Notes

Used for folding or stacking items.
