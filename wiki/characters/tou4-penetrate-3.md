# 透 Penetrate, pass through, thorough

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: penetrate, pass through, thorough
- **Radical**: 辵 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — permeate, infiltrate
- 透明 (tòu míng) — transparent

## Example Sentences

- 阳光透过窗户照进来。(Yáng guāng tòu guò chuāng hu zhào jìn lái.) — Sunlight shines in through the window.
