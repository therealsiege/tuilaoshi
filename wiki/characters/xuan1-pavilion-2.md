# 轩 Pavilion

- **Character**: 轩
- **Pinyin**: xuān
- **Meaning**: pavilion, high, carriage
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 轩敞 (xuān chǎng) — spacious
- 书轩 (shū xuān) — study

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间很轩敞。(Zhè ge fáng jiān hěn xuān chǎng.) — This room is very spacious.

## Notes

轩 originally meant high-fronted carriage, now for elevated pavilions and study rooms.
