# 庶 Common people, numerous

- **Character**: 庶
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: common people, numerous
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 庶民 (shù mín) — common people
- 庶务 (shù wù) — general affairs

## Example Sentences

- 庶民百姓都期盼太平。(Shù mín bǎi xìng dōu qī pàn tài píng.) — The common people all hope for peace.

## Notes

Classical term for ordinary citizens in ancient China
