# 改 改变, correct, alter

- **Character**: 改
- **Pinyin**: gǎi
- **Meaning**: 改变, correct, alter
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — modify, revise
- 改变 (gǎi biàn) — 改变, alter

## Example Sentences

- 合同条款需要修改。(Hé tong tiáo kuǎn xū yào xiū gǎi.) — Contract clauses need to be revised.

## Notes

改进 means improve. 变更 means alter/change.
