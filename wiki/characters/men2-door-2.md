# 门 Door

- **Character**: 门
- **Pinyin**: mén
- **Meaning**: door, gate,门
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 大门 (dà mén) — gate
- 门口 (mén kǒu) — doorway

## Example Sentences

- 关上门。(Guān shàng mén.) — Close the door.

## Notes

门 is the simplified radical and character for door/gate, fundamental building element.
