# 算 Count, calculate

- **Character**: 算
- **Pinyin**: suàn
- **Meaning**: count, calculate
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 计算 (jì suàn) — calculate
- 结算 (jié suàn) — settle accounts

## Example Sentences

- 月底结算工资。(Yuè dǐ jié suàn gōng zī.) — Settle wages at the end of the month.

## Notes

算账 means settle accounts. 清算 means liquidate/settle.
