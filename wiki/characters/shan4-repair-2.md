# 缮 Repair, copy

- **Character**: 缮
- **Pinyin**: shàn
- **Meaning**: repair, copy
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 修缮 (xiū shàn) — repair, renovate
- 缮写 (shàn xiě) — copy, transcribe

## Example Sentences

- 古建筑需要定期修缮。(Gǔ jiàn zhù xū yào dìng qī xiū shàn.) — Ancient buildings need regular repairs.

## Notes

Formal term for maintenance. 缮本 means fair copy.
