# 挡 To block, to obstruct, to resist

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, to obstruct, to resist
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to stop, to resist
- 遮挡 (zhē dǎng) — to block, to obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 请不要挡住门口。(Qǐng bùyào dǎngzhù ménkǒu.) — Please don't block the doorway.

## Notes

Common verb for blocking or obstructing.
