# 售 Sell, vend

- **Character**: 售
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: sell, vend
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 销售 (xiāo shòu) — to sell, sales
- 出售 (chū shòu) — to售售售售售

## Example Sentences

- 这家商店销售各种商品。(Zhè jiā shāng diàn xiāo shòu gè zhǒng shāng pǐn.) — This store sells various commodities.

## Notes

Formal term for selling. 售货员 means sales clerk. 售票 means to sell tickets. 零售 means retail.
