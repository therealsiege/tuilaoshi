# 攒 To save, to accumulate

- **Character**: 攒
- **Pinyin**: zǎn
- **Meaning**: to save, to accumulate
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 22

## Compounds

- 积攒 (jī zǎn) — to save up, to accumulate
- 攒钱 (zǎn qián) — to save money

## Example Sentences

- 他攒钱买房子。(Tā zǎn qián mǎi fángzi.) — He is saving money to buy a house.

## Notes

Pronounced zǎn for saving; cuán for gathering together.
