# 佰 Hundred

- **Character**: 佰
- **Pinyin**: bǎi
- **Meaning**: hundred (formal/financial)
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 壹佰 (yī bǎi) — one hundred (formal)
- 佰元 (bǎi yuán) — hundred yuan

## Example Sentences

- 请写壹佰元整。(Qǐng xiě yī bǎi yuán zhěng.) — Please write one hundred yuan exactly.

## Notes

佰 is the formal financial form of 百 (hundred), used on checks/receipts.
