# 汹 Turbulent

- **Character**: 汹
- **Pinyin**: xiōng
- **Meaning**: torrential, turbulent
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 汹涌 (xiōng yǒng) — turbulent
- 来势汹汹 (lái shì xiōng xiōng) — menacing

## Example Sentences

- 海浪汹涌澎湃。(Hǎi làng xiōng yǒng péng pài.) — The waves surge turbulently.

## Notes

汹 simplified from 洶. Water 氵 fierce and violent.
