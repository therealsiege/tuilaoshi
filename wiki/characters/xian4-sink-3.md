# 陷 To Sink

- **Character**: 陷
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: to sink, to trap, to fall into
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陷入 (xiàn rù) — to fall into
- 陷阱 (xiàn jǐng) — trap

## Example Sentences

- 他陷入了困境。(Tā xiàn rù le kùn jìng.) — He fell into difficulty.

## Notes

陷 combines mound 阝 and 臽 (pit), representing falling into a hole or trap.
