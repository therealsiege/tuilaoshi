# 演 To perform, to act, to practice, to develop

- **Character**: 演
- **Pinyin**: yǎn
- **Meaning**: to perform, to act, to演习, to演绎
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 演出 (yǎn chū) — to perform, performance
- 演奏 (yǎn zòu) — to perform, to play (music)

## Example Sentences

- 他在电影中演主角。(Tā zài diànyǐng zhōng yǎn zhǔjué.) — He plays the lead role in the movie.

## Notes

Broad term for performing, acting, or demonstrating.
