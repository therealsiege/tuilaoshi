# 办 — Handle / Do

- **Character**: 办
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to handle, to do, to manage
- **Radical**: 力 (power/strength)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 办法 (bànfǎ) — method, way (to do something)
- 办公室 (bàngōngshì) — office
- 办事 (bànshì) — to handle affairs
- 办理 (bànlǐ) — to handle, to process (official matters)
- 举办 (jǔbàn) — to hold, to host (an event)

## Example Sentences

- 你有什么办法？(Nǐ yǒu shénme bànfǎ?) — Do you have any ideas / What can you do about it?
- 他在办公室工作。(Tā zài bàngōngshì gōngzuò.) — He works in an office.
- 这件事很难办。(Zhè jiàn shì hěn nán bàn.) — This matter is hard to handle.

## Notes

办 is a simplified form of the traditional 辦. The character's structure features 力 (strength/power) at its core, flanked by two dots representing effort applied from both sides. The idea is that handling affairs requires exertion of force. 办法 (method/way) is an extremely high-frequency compound and often appears in the negative: 没办法 (méi bànfǎ) meaning "there's no way" or "can't be helped."
