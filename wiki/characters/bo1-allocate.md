# 拨 To Allocate

- **Character**: 拨
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to allocate, to dial, to poke
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拨打 (bō dǎ) — to dial
- 拨款 (bō kuǎn) — to allocate funds

## Example Sentences

- 请拨打这个电话号码。(Qǐng bō dǎ zhè ge diàn huà hào mǎ.) — Please dial this phone number.

## Notes

拨 simplified from 撥. Hand 扌 plus 发 phonetic.
