# 扭 Twist

- **Character**: 扭
- **Pinyin**: niǔ
- **Meaning**: to twist, wrench
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 扭伤 (niǔ shāng) — sprain, strain
- 扭转 (niǔ zhuǎn) — to turn around

## Example Sentences

- 运动时要小心别扭伤了。(Yùndòng shí yào xiǎoxīn bié niǔshāng le.) — Be careful not to sprain anything while exercising.

## Notes

Twisting; sprains and turning motions
