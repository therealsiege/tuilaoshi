# 刃 Blade, edge

- **Character**: 刃
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: blade, edge
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 刀刃 (dāo rèn) — knife edge, blade
- 刃口 (rèn kǒu) — cutting edge

## Example Sentences

- 这把刀的刀刃很锋利。(Zhè bǎ dāo de dāorèn hěn fēnglì.) — This knife's blade is very sharp.

## Notes

Refers to the cutting edge of a blade.
