# 顶 Top, peak, carry on head

- **Character**: 顶
- **Pinyin**: dǐng
- **Meaning**: top, peak, carry on head
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 屋顶 (wū dǐng) — roof, rooftop
- 山顶 (shān dǐng) — mountain peak

## Example Sentences

- 我们终于爬到了山顶。(Wǒ men zhōng yú pá dào le shān dǐng.) — We finally climbed to the mountain peak.

## Notes

Means top or to carry on head. Also measure word for hats (一顶帽子). 顶替 means to substitute.
