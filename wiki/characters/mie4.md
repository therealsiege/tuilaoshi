# 灭 To Extinguish

- **Character**: 灭
- **Pinyin**: miè
- **Meaning**: to extinguish, destroy
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 消灭 (xiāo miè) — to eliminate, wipe out
- 灭火 (miè huǒ) — to put out a fire

## Example Sentences

- 请把灯灭了。(Qǐng bǎ dēng miè le.) — Please turn off the light.

## Notes

灭 shows something covering fire, representing extinguishing or destroying.
