# 族 Ethnic Group

- **Character**: 族
- **Pinyin**: zú
- **Meaning**: race, nationality, ethnic group
- **Radical**: 方 (direction)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 民族 (mín zú) — nationality, ethnic group
- 家族 (jiā zú) — family clan

## Example Sentences

- 中国有五十六个民族。(Zhōng guó yǒu wǔ shí liù ge mín zú.) — China has 56 ethnic groups.

## Notes

族 combines 方 (banner) and 矢 (arrow), suggesting people gathered under one banner.
