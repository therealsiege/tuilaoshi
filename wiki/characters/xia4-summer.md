# 夏 Summer

- **Character**: 夏
- **Pinyin**: xià
- **Meaning**: summer
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 夏天 (xià tiān) — summer
- 夏季 (xià jì) — summer season

## Example Sentences

- 夏天很热。(Xià tiān hěn rè.) — Summer is very hot.

## Notes

夏 was also the name of China's first dynasty (Xia Dynasty). The character suggests a person laboring under the hot sun.
