# 凝 To congeal, concentrate, gaze

- **Character**: 凝
- **Pinyin**: níng
- **Meaning**: to congeal, concentrate, gaze
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 凝视 (níng shì) — to gaze fixedly, stare
- 凝聚 (níng jù) — to condense, cohere

## Example Sentences

- 她凝视着远方，若有所思。(Tā níngshìzhe yuǎnfāng, ruò yǒu suǒ sī.) — She gazed into the distance, lost in thought.

## Notes

Can mean physical condensation or concentrated attention/focus.
