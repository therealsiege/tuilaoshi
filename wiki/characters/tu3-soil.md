# 土 Soil

- **Character**: 土
- **Pinyin**: tǔ
- **Meaning**: soil, earth, land, local
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 土地 (tǔ dì) — land
- 泥土 (ní tǔ) — soil, mud

## Example Sentences

- 花需要土壤。(Huā xū yào tǔ rǎng.) — Flowers need soil.

## Notes

土 means earth or soil. Radical for earth-related characters. Used in土地 (land), 本土 (native).
