# 件 Item

- **Character**: 件
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: item, piece, measure word
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 一件事 (yī jiàn shì) — one matter
- 案件 (àn jiàn) — case

## Example Sentences

- 这是一件重要的事情。(Zhè shì yī jiàn zhòng yào de shì qing.) — This is an important matter.

## Notes

件 is a common measure word for matters, clothing, luggage, and件s件 件. Combines person and cow (分), suggesting division into pieces.
