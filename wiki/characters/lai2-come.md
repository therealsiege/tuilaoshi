# 来 To Come

- **Character**: 来
- **Pinyin**: lái
- **Meaning**: to come
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 来到 (lái dào) — to arrive
- 原来 (yuán lái) — originally

## Example Sentences

- 请进来！(Qǐng jìn lái!) — Please come in!

## Notes

来 is the opposite of 去 (to go). As a complement it indicates motion toward the speaker.
