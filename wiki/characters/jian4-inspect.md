# 鉴 To Inspect

- **Character**: 鉴
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: to inspect, mirror, example
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鉴定 (jiàn dìng) — to appraise
- 借鉴 (jiè jiàn) — to learn from

## Example Sentences

- 专家鉴定了这幅画。(Zhuān jiā jiàn dìng le zhè fú huà.) — Experts appraised this painting.

## Notes

鉴 is simplified from 鑑. Originally meant mirror, extended to careful examination.
