# 窗 Window

- **Character**: 窗
- **Pinyin**: chuāng
- **Meaning**: window
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 窗户 (chuāng hu) — window
- 窗帘 (chuāng lián) — curtain

## Example Sentences

- 请打开窗户。(Qǐng dǎ kāi chuāng hu.) — Please open the window.

## Notes

窗 has cave 穴 with opening, hole in wall for light and air.
