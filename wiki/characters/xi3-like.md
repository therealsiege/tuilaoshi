# 喜 To Like

- **Character**: 喜
- **Pinyin**: xǐ
- **Meaning**: to like, joy, happiness
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喜欢 (xǐ huān) — to like
- 欢喜 (huān xǐ) — joyful

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢中国菜。(Wǒ xǐ huān zhōng guó cài.) — I like Chinese food.

## Notes

喜 depicts a drum (壴) and a mouth (口) — making joyful sounds. 双喜 (double happiness) is a wedding symbol.
