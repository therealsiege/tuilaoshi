# 瘦 Thin

- **Character**: 瘦
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: thin, lean
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘦弱 (shòu ruò) — thin and weak
- 减瘦 (jiǎn shòu) — to lose weight

## Example Sentences

- 她很瘦。(Tā hěn shòu.) — She is very thin.

## Notes

瘦 with sickness radical suggests being too thin. Opposite of 胖 (fat). Can be positive or negative.
