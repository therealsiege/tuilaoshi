# 笑 Laugh, smile, ridicule

- **Character**: 笑
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: laugh, smile, ridicule
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 大笑 (dà xiào) — laugh heartily
- 微笑 (wēi xiào) — smile

## Example Sentences

- 她笑得很开心。(Tā xiào de hěn kāi xīn.) — She laughed very happily.

## Notes

Laughter, smiling, and mockery
