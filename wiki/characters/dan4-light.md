# 淡 Light

- **Character**: 淡
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: light, pale, tasteless
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淡水 (dàn shuǐ) — fresh water
- 冷淡 (lěng dàn) — cold, indifferent

## Example Sentences

- 这个汤味道很淡。(Zhè gè tāng wèi dào hěn dàn.) — This soup tastes very bland.

## Notes

淡 shows water 氵 and 炎 (flame), representing water diluting or weakening intensity.
