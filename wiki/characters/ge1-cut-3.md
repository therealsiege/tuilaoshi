# 割 To Cut

- **Character**: 割
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: to cut, to mow
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 割草 (gē cǎo) — to mow grass
- 切割 (qiē gē) — to cut

## Example Sentences

- 农民在割麦子。(Nóng mín zài gē mài zi.) — Farmers are harvesting wheat.

## Notes

割 combines knife radical 刂 with 害 (harm), representing cutting or harvesting.
