# 校 Proofread, check, collate

- **Character**: 校
- **Pinyin**: jiào
- **Meaning**: proofread, check, collate
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 校对 (jiào duì) — to proofread
- 校正 (jiào zhèng) — to proofread and correct

## Example Sentences

- 出版前必须仔细校对文稿。(Chū bǎn qián bì xū zǐ xì jiào duì wén gǎo.) — The manuscript must be carefully proofread before publication.

## Notes

When pronounced jiào, means to proofread or check. As xiào, means school. Important distinction in publishing.
