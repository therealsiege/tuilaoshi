# 喊 To Shout

- **Character**: 喊
- **Pinyin**: hǎn
- **Meaning**: to shout, to yell
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大喊 (dà hǎn) — to shout loudly
- 呼喊 (hū hǎn) — to shout

## Example Sentences

- 他大声喊我的名字。(Tā dà shēng hǎn wǒ de míng zì.) — He loudly shouted my name.

## Notes

喊 combines mouth 口 and 咸 (all), representing calling out loudly to reach everyone.
