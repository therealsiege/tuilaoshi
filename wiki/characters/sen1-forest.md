# 森 Forest

- **Character**: 森
- **Pinyin**: sēn
- **Meaning**: forest, dense
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 森林 (sēn lín) — forest
- 森严 (sēn yán) — strict, tight (security)

## Example Sentences

- 这片森林很大。(Zhè piàn sēn lín hěn dà.) — This forest is very large.

## Notes

森 shows three trees (木木木), representing a dense forest. The character structure itself depicts the concept.
