# 绎 To Interpret

- **Character**: 绎
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to interpret, to elaborate
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 演绎 (yǎn yì) — to deduce, to perform
- 络绎不绝 (luò yì bù jué) — in an endless stream

## Example Sentences

- 游客络绎不绝。(Yóu kè luò yì bù jué.) — Tourists come in an endless stream.

## Notes

绎 simplified from 繹. Silk 纟 with 睪, unraveling threads of meaning.
