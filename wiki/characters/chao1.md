# 超 To Exceed

- **Character**: 超
- **Pinyin**: chāo
- **Meaning**: to exceed, to surpass, super
- **Radical**: 走 (run)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 超过 (chāo guò) — to exceed, to surpass
- 超市 (chāo shì) — supermarket

## Example Sentences

- 他超过了我。(Tā chāo guò le wǒ.) — He surpassed me.

## Notes

超 indicates exceeding limits or surpassing. Modern usage includes 超 as prefix meaning 'super' (超级 super, 超人 superman).
