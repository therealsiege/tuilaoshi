# 敏 Quick, nimble, sensitive

- **Character**: 敏
- **Pinyin**: mǐn
- **Meaning**: quick, nimble, sensitive
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 过敏 (guò mǐn) — allergy, allergic
- 敏感 (mǐn gǎn) — sensitive

## Example Sentences

- 他对花粉过敏。(Tā duì huā fěn guò mǐn.) — He is allergic to pollen.

## Notes

Used for allergies and sensitivity
