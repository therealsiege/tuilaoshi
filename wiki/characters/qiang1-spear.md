# 枪 Spear, gun

- **Character**: 枪
- **Pinyin**: qiāng
- **Meaning**: spear, gun
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 手枪 (shǒu qiāng) — pistol
- 长枪 (cháng qiāng) — spear, rifle

## Example Sentences

- 士兵们正在练习射击。(Shìbīng men zhèngzài liànxí shèjī.) — The soldiers are practicing shooting.

## Notes

Originally meant spear (with wood radical), now primarily means gun. Still used for traditional spears in martial arts contexts.
