# 赠 Give as present

- **Character**: 赠
- **Pinyin**: zèng
- **Meaning**: give as present
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赠送 (zèngsòng) — give as a present
- 赠品 (zèngpǐn) — gift, complimentary item

## Example Sentences

- 公司赠送给客户礼品。(Gōngsī zèngsòng gěi kèhù lǐpǐn.) — The company gives gifts to customers.

## Notes

Common term for giving presents or gifts.
