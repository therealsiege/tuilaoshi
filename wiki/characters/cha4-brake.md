# 刹 Brake

- **Character**: 刹
- **Pinyin**: chà
- **Meaning**: brake; temple (chà); instant (shā)
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刹车 (shā chē) — brake
- 刹那 (chà nà) — an instant

## Example Sentences

- 赶紧踩刹车！(Gǎn jǐn cǎi shā chē!) — Hit the brakes quickly!

## Notes

刹 has multiple readings: shā (brake), chà (Buddhist temple, 刹那 = instant).
