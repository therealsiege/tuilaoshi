# 通 To pass through, connect, understand

- **Character**: 通
- **Pinyin**: tōng
- **Meaning**: to pass through, connect, understand
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 交通 (jiāo tōng) — traffic, transportation
- 沟通 (gōu tōng) — to communicate

## Example Sentences

- 我们要加强沟通。(Wǒmen yào jiāqiáng gōutōng.) — We need to strengthen communication.

## Notes

To pass through, connect, or understand.
