# 瑚 Coral

- **Character**: 瑚
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: coral
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 珊瑚 (shān hú) — coral
- 红珊瑚 (hóng shān hú) — red coral

## Example Sentences

- 红珊瑚很珍贵。(Hóng shān hú hěn zhēn guì.) — Red coral is very precious.

## Notes

瑚 uses jade radical 玉, only appears with 珊 to form the word for coral.
