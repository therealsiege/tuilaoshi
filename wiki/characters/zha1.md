# 扎 To Bind

- **Character**: 扎
- **Pinyin**: zhā
- **Meaning**: to prick, to tie, to bind, to plunge into
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 扎实 (zhā shi) — solid, sturdy
- 包扎 (bāo zā) — to bind up, to bandage

## Example Sentences

- 她把头发扎起来了。(Tā bǎ tóu fa zā qǐ lái le.) — She tied up her hair.

## Notes

扎 can be zhā (to prick, to bind), zā (to tie), or zhá (to struggle). Most commonly zā for tying hair.
