# 震 To Shake

- **Character**: 震
- **Pinyin**: zhèn
- **Meaning**: to shake, to震, to excite
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 地震 (dì zhèn) — earthquake
- 震动 (zhèn dòng) — to震, to vibrate

## Example Sentences

- 这里发生了地震。(Zhè lǐ fā shēng le dì zhèn.) — An earthquake occurred here.

## Notes

震 shows rain 雨 with 辰 (heavenly body), representing thunder震 shaking the earth.
