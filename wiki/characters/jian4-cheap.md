# 贱 Cheap

- **Character**: 贱
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: cheap, lowly, mean
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 低贱 (dī jiàn) — lowly
- 贵贱 (guì jiàn) — high and low status

## Example Sentences

- 人人平等，不分贵贱。(Rén rén píng děng, bù fēn guì jiàn.) — All people are equal, regardless of status.

## Notes

贱 simplified from 賤. Uses shell/money radical 贝, indicating low value or cost.
