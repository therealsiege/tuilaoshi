# 铺 Shop, store

- **Character**: 铺
- **Pinyin**: pù
- **Meaning**: shop, store
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 店铺 (diàn pù) — shop, store
- 铺面 (pù miàn) — shop front

## Example Sentences

- 商业街有很多店铺。(Shāng yè jiē yǒu hěn duō diàn pù.) — The commercial street has many shops.

## Notes

Different from 铺 (pū = spread). 铺租 means shop rent.
