# 尿 Urine

- **Character**: 尿
- **Pinyin**: niào
- **Meaning**: urine, urinate
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 小便 (xiǎo biàn) — to urinate, urine
- 尿频 (niào pín) — frequent urination
- 糖尿病 (táng niào bìng) — diabetes

## Example Sentences

- 尿频可能是感染的症状。(Niào pín kě néng shì gǎn rǎn de zhèng zhuàng.) — Frequent urination may be a symptom of infection.

## Notes

Urinary function; important diagnostic indicator.
