# 教 To Teach

- **Character**: 教
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: to teach; teaching (jiào)
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 教室 (jiào shì) — classroom
- 教育 (jiào yù) — education

## Example Sentences

- 谁教你中文？(Shéi jiāo nǐ zhōng wén?) — Who teaches you Chinese?

## Notes

教 is read jiāo when meaning 'to teach' and jiào when meaning 'teaching/religion' (as in 教室, 教育).
