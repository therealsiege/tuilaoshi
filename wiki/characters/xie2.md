# 邪 Evil

- **Character**: 邪
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: evil, heretical
- **Radical**: 阝(city)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 邪恶 (xié è) — evil, sinister
- 邪教 (xié jiào) — cult, heresy

## Example Sentences

- 我们要抵制邪恶势力。(Wǒ men yào dǐ zhì xié è shì lì.) — We must resist evil forces.

## Notes

邪 combines 牙 (tooth) and city radical 阝, originally meaning slanted. Extended to mean deviant or evil.
