# 湾 Bay, gulf, cove

- **Character**: 湾
- **Pinyin**: wān
- **Meaning**: bay, gulf, cove
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 海湾 (hǎi wān) — bay, gulf
- 港湾 (gǎng wān) — harbor, port

## Example Sentences

- 这个海湾风景优美。(Zhè ge hǎi wān fēng jǐng yōu měi.) — This bay has beautiful scenery.

## Notes

Curved inlets of water
