# 舵 Rudder, helm

- **Character**: 舵
- **Pinyin**: duò
- **Meaning**: rudder, helm
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 舵手 (duò shǒu) — helmsman
- 掌舵 (zhǎng duò) — to steer, take the helm

## Example Sentences

- 船长掌舵驶向远方。(Chuán zhǎng zhǎng duò shǐ xiàng yuǎn fāng.) — The captain steered the ship toward the distance.

## Notes

Means rudder or helm. Metaphorically used for leadership and guidance.
