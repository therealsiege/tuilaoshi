# 阻 To Hinder

- **Character**: 阻
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to hinder, to obstruct
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 阻止 (zǔ zhǐ) — to prevent
- 阻碍 (zǔ ài) — to obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 没有什么能阻止我。(Méi yǒu shén me néng zǔ zhǐ wǒ.) — Nothing can stop me.

## Notes

阻 shows the mound radical 阝 and 且 (moreover), depicting a hill blocking the path and preventing further progress.
