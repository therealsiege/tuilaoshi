# 萎 Atrophy

- **Character**: 萎
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: wither, atrophy
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 萎缩 (wěi suō) — atrophy, wither
- 枯萎 (kū wěi) — to wither
- 萎靡 (wěi mǐ) — dispirited, listless

## Example Sentences

- 长期不运动会导致肌肉萎缩。(Cháng qī bù yùn dòng huì dǎo zhì jī ròu wěi suō.) — Long-term lack of exercise can lead to muscle atrophy.

## Notes

Tissue wasting; seen in disuse or neurological conditions.
