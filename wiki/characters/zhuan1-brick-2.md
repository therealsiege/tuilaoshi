# 砖 Brick

- **Character**: 砖
- **Pinyin**: zhuān
- **Meaning**: brick
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砖头 (zhuān tóu) — brick
- 瓷砖 (cí zhuān) — ceramic tile

## Example Sentences

- 用砖建造房子。(Yòng zhuān jiàn zào fáng zi.) — Build a house with bricks.

## Notes

砖 with stone radical for brick, fundamental building material.
