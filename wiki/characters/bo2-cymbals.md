# 钹 Cymbals

- **Character**: 钹
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: cymbals
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钹子 (bó zi) — cymbals
- 铙钹 (náo bó) — cymbals

## Example Sentences

- 钹声清脆悦耳。(Bó shēng qīng cuì yuè ěr.) — The cymbal sound is crisp and pleasant to the ear.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese percussion instrument.
