# 向 Towards

- **Character**: 向
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: towards, to face, direction
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 方向 (fāng xiàng) — direction
- 向前 (xiàng qián) — forward

## Example Sentences

- 他向我问好。(Tā xiàng wǒ wèn hǎo.) — He greeted me.

## Notes

向 shows directionality, both physical (towards a place) and metaphorical (towards a person when speaking or acting).
