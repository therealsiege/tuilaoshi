# 肿 To swell, swollen, 腫 (traditional)

- **Character**: 肿
- **Pinyin**: zhǒng
- **Meaning**: to swell, swollen, 腫 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肿胀 (zhǒng zhàng) — swollen
- 红肿 (hóng zhǒng) — red and swollen

## Example Sentences

- 她的脚踝肿了。(Tā de jiǎo huái zhǒng le.) — Her ankle is swollen.

## Notes

Medical term for swelling and inflammation.
