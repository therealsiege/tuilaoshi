# 祥 Auspicious, 吉祥 Auspicious

- **Character**: 祥
- **Pinyin**: xiáng
- **Meaning**: auspicious, 吉祥 auspicious
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 吉祥 (jí xiáng) — auspicious, lucky
- 祥和 (xiáng hé) — peaceful, harmonious
- 慈祥 (cí xiáng) — kindly

## Example Sentences

- 过年时人们互相祝福吉祥如意。(Guònián shí rénmen hùxiāng zhùfú jíxiáng rúyì.) — During New Year, people wish each other auspiciousness and good fortune.

## Notes

Traditional term for good fortune and auspiciousness. 吉祥 common in greetings and blessings, especially during festivals.
