# 爬 Climb

- **Character**: 爬
- **Pinyin**: pá
- **Meaning**: climb, crawl
- **Radical**: 爪 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 爬山 (pá shān) — climb mountain
- 爬起来 (pá qǐlái) — climb up, get up

## Example Sentences

- 我们周末去爬山。(Wǒmen zhōumò qù pá shān.) — We're going mountain climbing this weekend.

## Notes

Climbing or crawling. Claw radical. 爬山 is popular outdoor activity. Common movement verb.
