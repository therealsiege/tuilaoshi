# 了 Completed Action

- **Character**: 了
- **Pinyin**: le
- **Meaning**: completed action particle
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 好了 (hǎo le) — okay now, done
- 来了 (lái le) — has come

## Example Sentences

- 我吃了饭。(Wǒ chī le fàn.) — I ate.

## Notes

了 has two main functions: as an aspect particle after verbs (completed action) and as a sentence-final particle (change of state).
