# 赞 To praise, to support, to sponsor

- **Character**: 赞
- **Pinyin**: zàn
- **Meaning**: to praise, to support, to sponsor
- **Radical**: 贝(shell)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 赞美 (zàn měi) — to admire, to praise
- 赞成 (zàn chéng) — to approve, to endorse

## Example Sentences

- 大家都赞美她的勇气。(Dàjiā dōu zànměi tā de yǒngqì.) — Everyone praises her courage.

## Notes

Common verb for praising or expressing approval.
