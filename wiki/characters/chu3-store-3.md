# 储 To Store

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: to store, to save
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 存储 (cún chǔ) — storage
- 储存 (chǔ cún) — to store

## Example Sentences

- 这个硬盘储存容量很大。(Zhè ge yìng pán chǔ cún róng liàng hěn dà.) — This hard disk has large storage capacity.

## Notes

储 specifically emphasizes the act of storing for future use, common in technology contexts.
