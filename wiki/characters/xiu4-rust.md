# 锈 Rust

- **Character**: 锈
- **Pinyin**: xiù
- **Meaning**: rust, to rust
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 生锈 (shēng xiù) — to rust
- 锈蚀 (xiù shí) — to corrode

## Example Sentences

- 铁门生锈了。(Tiě mén shēng xiù le.) — The iron gate has rusted.

## Notes

锈 simplified from 鏽. Metal radical 钅 with秀 (elegant), representing metal degradation.
