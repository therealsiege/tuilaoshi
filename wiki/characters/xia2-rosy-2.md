# 霞 Clouds

- **Character**: 霞
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: rosy clouds, sunset glow
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 朝霞 (zhāo xiá) — morning glow
- 晚霞 (wǎn xiá) — sunset glow

## Example Sentences

- 天边出现了美丽的晚霞。(Tiān biān chū xiàn le měi lì de wǎn xiá.) — Beautiful sunset clouds appeared on the horizon.

## Notes

霞 has rain 雨 with 叚 phonetic, colorful clouds at dawn/dusk.
