# 忘 To Forget

- **Character**: 忘
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: to forget
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忘记 (wàng jì) — to forget
- 忘了 (wàng le) — forgot

## Example Sentences

- 我忘了带伞。(Wǒ wàng le dài sǎn.) — I forgot to bring an umbrella.

## Notes

忘 combines 亡 (to perish) and 心 (heart) — something that has died in the heart. Opposite of 记 (remember).
