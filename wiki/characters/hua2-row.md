# 划 Row

- **Character**: 划
- **Pinyin**: huá
- **Meaning**: to row, paddle
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 划船 (huá chuán) — to row a boat
- 划水 (huá shuǐ) — to paddle, swim stroke

## Example Sentences

- 他们在湖上划船。(Tāmen zài hú shàng huáchuán.) — They are rowing on the lake.

## Notes

Rowing and paddling; water sports movement
