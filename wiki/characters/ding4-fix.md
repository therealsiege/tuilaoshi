# 定 To Determine

- **Character**: 定
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: to fix, to determine, definite, certainly
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 决定 (jué dìng) — to decide
- 一定 (yī dìng) — certain, surely

## Example Sentences

- 我们一定会成功。(Wǒ men yī dìng huì chéng gōng.) — We will definitely succeed.

## Notes

定 shows a roof over正 (correct), representing something settled or determined. Very common in many compounds.
