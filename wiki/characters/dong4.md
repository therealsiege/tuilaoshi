# 洞 Cave

- **Character**: 洞
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: cave, hole
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 山洞 (shān dòng) — cave
- 洞察 (dòng chá) — to see clearly, insight

## Example Sentences

- 他们发现了一个山洞。(Tāmen fāxiàn le yīgè shāndòng.) — They discovered a cave.

## Notes

洞 combines water 氵 with 同 (same/together), originally meaning a tunnel through which water flows.
