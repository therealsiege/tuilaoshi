# 宽 Wide, lenient, relax

- **Character**: 宽
- **Pinyin**: kuān
- **Meaning**: wide, lenient, relax
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽容 (kuān róng) — tolerant, lenient
- 宽大 (kuān dà) — spacious, lenient

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该宽容待人。(Wǒ men yīng gāi kuān róng dài rén.) — We should be tolerant toward people.

## Notes

Means wide or lenient. 宽恕 means to forgive. 宽敞 means spacious. 宽心 means to feel relieved.
