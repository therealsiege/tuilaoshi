# 廉 Honest, incorruptible

- **Character**: 廉
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: honest, incorruptible
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 廉洁 (lián jié) — honest, clean (of officials)
- 清廉 (qīng lián) — honest and upright

## Example Sentences

- 清正廉洁是公务员的基本要求。(Qīngzhèng liánjié shì gōngwùyuán de jīběn yāoqiú.) — Integrity and incorruptibility are basic requirements for civil servants.

## Notes

Refers to honesty and freedom from corruption, especially for officials. Important virtue in governance.
