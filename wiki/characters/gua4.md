# 挂 To Hang

- **Character**: 挂
- **Pinyin**: guà
- **Meaning**: to hang, to be worried about
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挂号 (guà hào) — to register (at hospital/post office)
- 牵挂 (qiān guà) — to worry about

## Example Sentences

- 把外套挂在那里。(Bǎ wài tào guà zài nà lǐ.) — Hang the coat there.

## Notes

挂 means to hang or suspend. Metaphorically means to worry (挂念 to miss someone, 牵挂 to be concerned about).
