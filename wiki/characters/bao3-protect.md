# 保 To Protect

- **Character**: 保
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: to protect, to keep, to guarantee
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 保护 (bǎo hù) — to protect
- 保证 (bǎo zhèng) — to guarantee

## Example Sentences

- 我们要保护环境。(Wǒ men yào bǎo hù huán jìng.) — We must protect the environment.

## Notes

保 means to protect or keep safe. 保险 = insurance, 保持 = maintain.
