# 霖 Continuous rain, heavy rain

- **Character**: 霖
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: continuous rain, heavy rain
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 甘霖 (gān lín) — welcome rain, timely rain
- 霖雨 (lín yǔ) — continuous rain

## Example Sentences

- 久旱逢甘霖。(Jiǔ hàn féng gānlín.) — After a long drought, timely rain arrives.

## Notes

Continuous or heavy rain, often used metaphorically for blessings.
