# 口 Mouth

- **Character**: 口
- **Pinyin**: kǒu
- **Meaning**: mouth, opening, measure word
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 人口 (rén kǒu) — population
- 出口 (chū kǒu) — exit

## Example Sentences

- 中国有很大的人口。(Zhōng guó yǒu hěn dà de rén kǒu.) — China has a very large population.

## Notes

口 is a pictograph of an open mouth. As a measure word it counts family members: 三口人.
