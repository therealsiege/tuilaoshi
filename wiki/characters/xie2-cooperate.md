# 协 Cooperate, harmonize, assist

- **Character**: 协
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: cooperate, harmonize, assist
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 协议 (xié yì) — agreement, protocol
- 协商 (xié shāng) — to negotiate, consult

## Example Sentences

- 经过协商，双方达成了协议。(Jīng guò xié shāng, shuāng fāng dá chéng le xié yì.) — After negotiation, both sides reached an agreement.

## Notes

Essential for business and diplomacy. 协调 means to coordinate. 协助 means to assist. 妥协 means to compromise.
