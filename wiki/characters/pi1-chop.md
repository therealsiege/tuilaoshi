# 劈 To chop, to split, to hack

- **Character**: 劈
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: to chop, to split, to hack
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 劈柴 (pī chái) — to chop firewood
- 劈开 (pī kāi) — to split open

## Example Sentences

- 他在院子里劈柴。(Tā zài yuànzi lǐ pī chái.) — He is chopping firewood in the yard.

## Notes

Used for chopping or splitting with force.
