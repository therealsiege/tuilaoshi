# 仁 Kernel, benevolence

- **Character**: 仁
- **Pinyin**: rén
- **Meaning**: kernel, benevolence
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 杏仁 (xìng rén) — almond
- 仁义 (rén yì) — benevolence and righteousness

## Example Sentences

- 杏仁很有营养。(Xìngrén hěn yǒu yíngyǎng.) — Almonds are very nutritious.

## Notes

Key Confucian virtue. Also means nut kernel. Person radical.
