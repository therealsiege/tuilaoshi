# 椅 Chair

- **Character**: 椅
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: chair
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 椅子 (yǐ zi) — chair
- 轮椅 (lún yǐ) — wheelchair

## Example Sentences

- 请搬一把椅子。(Qǐng bān yī bǎ yǐ zi.) — Please bring a chair.

## Notes

椅 uses the wood radical 木 and 奇 (qí) as phonetic. Measure word is 把 (bǎ) or 张 (zhāng).
