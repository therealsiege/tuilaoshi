# 慌 Flustered

- **Character**: 慌
- **Pinyin**: huāng
- **Meaning**: nervous, flustered
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 慌张 (huāng zhāng) — flustered
- 惊慌 (jīng huāng) — panicked

## Example Sentences

- 别慌张，慢慢来。(Bié huāng zhāng, màn man lái.) — Don't panic, take it slow.

## Notes

慌 heart 忄 plus 荒 (waste/wild), heart running wild.
