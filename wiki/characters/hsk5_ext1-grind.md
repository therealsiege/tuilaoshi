# 研 Grind, Research, 研究 Research

- **Character**: 研
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: grind, research, 研究 research
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 研究 (yán jiū) — research, study
- 研发 (yán fā) — research and develop
- 研讨 (yán tǎo) — discuss, seminar

## Example Sentences

- 公司投资研发新产品。(Gōngsī tóuzī yánfā xīn chǎnpǐn.) — The company invests in developing new products.

## Notes

Originally meant 'grind' but now primarily used in academic contexts. 研究生 (graduate student) and 研发 (R&D) are common terms.
