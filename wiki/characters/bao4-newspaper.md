# 报 To Report

- **Character**: 报
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: newspaper, to report, to repay
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 报纸 (bào zhǐ) — newspaper
- 报名 (bào míng) — to sign up

## Example Sentences

- 你看今天的报纸了吗？(Nǐ kàn jīn tiān de bào zhǐ le ma?) — Did you read today's newspaper?

## Notes

报 means report, newspaper, or repay. 报道 = news report, 报答 = to repay a kindness.
