# 冷 Cold

- **Character**: 冷
- **Pinyin**: lěng
- **Meaning**: cold, cool
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 冷静 (lěng jìng) — calm, cool-headed
- 寒冷 (hán lěng) — cold (weather)

## Example Sentences

- 今天很冷。(Jīn tiān hěn lěng.) — Today is very cold.

## Notes

冷 with ice radical 冫. Opposite of 热 (hot). 冷静 = calm (cool and quiet).
