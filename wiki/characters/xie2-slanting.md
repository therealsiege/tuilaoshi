# 斜 Slanting

- **Character**: 斜
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: slanting, inclined
- **Radical**: 斗 (dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 倾斜 (qīng xié) — to incline
- 斜坡 (xié pō) — slope

## Example Sentences

- 路面是斜的。(Lù miàn shì xié de.) — The road surface is slanted.

## Notes

斜 combines dipper 斗 and phonetic component, representing something tilted at an angle.
