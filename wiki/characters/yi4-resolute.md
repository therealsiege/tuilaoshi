# 毅 Resolute, persevering

- **Character**: 毅
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: resolute, persevering
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 毅力 (yì lì) — willpower, perseverance
- 坚毅 (jiān yì) — firm and persistent

## Example Sentences

- 成功需要坚强的毅力。(Chénggōng xūyào jiānqiáng de yìlì.) — Success requires strong perseverance.

## Notes

Represents determination and resolve. The character structure suggests cutting through obstacles with determination.
