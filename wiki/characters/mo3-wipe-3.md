# 抹 To Wipe

- **Character**: 抹
- **Pinyin**: mǒ
- **Meaning**: to wipe, to smear, to erase
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抹布 (mā bù) — rag
- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear

## Example Sentences

- 用抹布擦桌子。(Yòng mā bù cā zhuō zi.) — Use a rag to wipe the table.

## Notes

抹 hand 扌 plus 末 (end), wiping away.
