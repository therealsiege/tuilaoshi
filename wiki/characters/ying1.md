# 应 Should

- **Character**: 应
- **Pinyin**: yīng
- **Meaning**: should, ought to; to respond (yìng)
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 应该 (yīng gāi) — should
- 反应 (fǎn yìng) — reaction

## Example Sentences

- 你应该早点睡。(Nǐ yīng gāi zǎo diǎn shuì.) — You should go to bed earlier.

## Notes

应 as yīng means 'should' (应该). As yìng means 'to respond/answer' (应用, 反应).
