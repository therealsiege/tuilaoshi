# 书 Book

- **Character**: 书
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: book
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 读书 (dú shū) — to study
- 书店 (shū diàn) — bookstore

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很有意思。(Zhè běn shū hěn yǒu yì si.) — This book is very interesting.

## Notes

书 is greatly simplified from 書. The traditional form shows a hand holding a brush writing.
