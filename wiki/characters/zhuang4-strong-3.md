# 壮 Strong, robust, magnificent

- **Character**: 壮
- **Pinyin**: zhuàng
- **Meaning**: strong, robust, magnificent
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 壮丽 (zhuàng lì) — magnificent, majestic
- 悲壮 (bēi zhuàng) — solemn and stirring

## Example Sentences

- 山河壮丽，令人心旷神怡。(Shān hé zhuàng lì, lìng rén xīn kuàng shén yí.) — The mountains and rivers are magnificent, refreshing one's spirit.

## Notes

Describes physical strength or magnificent qualities.
