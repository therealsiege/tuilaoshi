# 避 To avoid, shun, evade

- **Character**: 避
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to avoid, shun, evade
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 避免 (bì miǎn) — to avoid, prevent
- 回避 (huí bì) — to evade, avoid

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该避免犯同样的错误。(Wǒmen yīnggāi bìmiǎn fàn tóngyàng de cuòwù.) — We should avoid making the same mistake.

## Notes

To avoid or evade, often used for prevention.
