# 朗 Clear, bright, loud

- **Character**: 朗
- **Pinyin**: lǎng
- **Meaning**: clear, bright, loud
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 明朗 (mínglǎng) — clear, bright
- 开朗 (kāilǎng) — cheerful, outgoing

## Example Sentences

- 她性格很开朗。(Tā xìnggé hěn kāilǎng.) — She has a very cheerful personality.

## Notes

Common for clarity, brightness, or cheerful disposition.
