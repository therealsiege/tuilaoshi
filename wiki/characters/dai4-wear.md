# 戴 To Wear

- **Character**: 戴
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: to wear (accessories), to put on
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 戴帽子 (dài mào zi) — to wear a hat
- 佩戴 (pèi dài) — to wear

## Example Sentences

- 他戴着眼镜。(Tā dài zhe yǎn jìng.) — He is wearing glasses.

## Notes

戴 for accessories on head/hands: hats, glasses, jewelry, watches. Different from 穿 (body clothing).
