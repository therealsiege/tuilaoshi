# 光 Light

- **Character**: 光
- **Pinyin**: guāng
- **Meaning**: light, ray, glory, only, bare
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阳光 (yáng guāng) — sunshine
- 时光 (shí guāng) — time, days

## Example Sentences

- 阳光很好。(Yáng guāng hěn hǎo.) — The sunshine is nice.

## Notes

光 means light or glory. 光 alone can mean 'only' or 'bare': 光说不做 (all talk, no action).
