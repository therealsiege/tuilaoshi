# 补 To Patch

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: to patch, to补充, to补偿
- **Radical**: 衤(clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 补充 (bǔ chōng) — to补充
- 补习 (bǔ xí) — to make up (study)

## Example Sentences

- 我需要补充说明一下。(Wǒ xū yào bǔ chōng shuō míng yī xià.) — I need to add some explanation.

## Notes

补 is simplified from 補. The clothes radical suggests patching or filling gaps.
