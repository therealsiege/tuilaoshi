# 雕 To Carve

- **Character**: 雕
- **Pinyin**: diāo
- **Meaning**: to carve, to engrave
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — to carve
- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture

## Example Sentences

- 他在木头上雕刻图案。(Tā zài mù tou shàng diāo kè tú àn.) — He carved patterns on the wood.

## Notes

雕 originally depicted a bird of prey, extended to mean carving with precision and detail.
