# 惧 To Fear

- **Character**: 惧
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: to fear, to be afraid
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear, dread
- 畏惧 (wèi jù) — to fear

## Example Sentences

- 他内心充满恐惧。(Tā nèi xīn chōng mǎn kǒng jù.) — His heart is filled with fear.

## Notes

惧 simplified from 懼. Heart 忄 experiencing fear or dread.
