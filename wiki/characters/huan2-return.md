# 还 To return, to give back, to repay

- **Character**: 还
- **Pinyin**: huán
- **Meaning**: to return, to give back, to repay
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 还钱 (huán qián) — to repay money
- 归还 (guī huán) — to return, to give back

## Example Sentences

- 我明天会还你钱。(Wǒ míngtiān huì huán nǐ qián.) — I will repay you tomorrow.

## Notes

Pronounced huán for returning; hái for still/yet.
