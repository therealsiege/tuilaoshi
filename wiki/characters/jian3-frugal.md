# 俭 Frugal, thrifty

- **Character**: 俭
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: frugal, thrifty
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 节俭 (jié jiǎn) — frugal, thrifty
- 勤俭 (qín jiǎn) — hardworking and thrifty

## Example Sentences

- 他生活很节俭。(Tā shēng huó hěn jié jiǎn.) — He lives very frugally.

## Notes

Traditional virtue. Opposite of 奢. 俭朴 means simple and frugal.
