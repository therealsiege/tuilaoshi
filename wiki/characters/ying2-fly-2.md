# 蝇 Fly (insect)

- **Character**: 蝇
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: fly (insect)
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 苍蝇 (cāng ying) — fly
- 果蝇 (guǒ yíng) — fruit fly

## Example Sentences

- 房间里有一只苍蝇。(Fángjiān lǐ yǒu yī zhī cāngying.) — There is a fly in the room.

## Notes

Common household pest. Insect radical on the left side.
