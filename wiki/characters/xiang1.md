# 箱 Trunk

- **Character**: 箱
- **Pinyin**: xiāng
- **Meaning**: box, chest, case, trunk
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 箱子 (xiāng zi) — box, suitcase
- 冰箱 (bīng xiāng) — refrigerator

## Example Sentences

- 我有两个箱子。(Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè xiāng zi.) — I have two suitcases.

## Notes

箱 with bamboo radical, boxes originally made from bamboo. 冰箱 = refrigerator (ice box).
