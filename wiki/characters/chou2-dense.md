# 稠 Dense, thick, crowded

- **Character**: 稠
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: dense, thick, crowded
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 稠密 (chóu mì) — dense, thick
- 浓稠 (nóng chóu) — thick, concentrated

## Example Sentences

- 粥太稠了，加点水。(Zhōu tài chóu le, jiā diǎn shuǐ.) — The porridge is too thick, add some water.

## Notes

Dense or thick consistency, especially of liquids.
