# 裁 To cut out, to cut fabric, to judge

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut out, to cut fabric, to judge
- **Radical**: 衣(clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁缝 (cái feng) — tailor
- 裁员 (cái yuán) — to lay off staff

## Example Sentences

- 她去裁缝店做衣服。(Tā qù cáiféng diàn zuò yīfu.) — She went to the tailor shop to have clothes made.

## Notes

Used for cutting fabric or making judgments/decisions.
