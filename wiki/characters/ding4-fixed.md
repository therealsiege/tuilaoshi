# 定 Fixed

- **Character**: 定
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: fixed, to decide
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 决定 (jué dìng) — to decide
- 定音鼓 (dìng yīn gǔ) — timpani

## Example Sentences

- 定音鼓能调音高。(Dìng yīn gǔ néng tiáo yīn gāo.) — Timpani can be tuned to different pitches.

## Notes

定 means fixed or to decide; 定音鼓 are tunable drums.
