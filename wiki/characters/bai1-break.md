# 掰 To Break

- **Character**: 掰
- **Pinyin**: bāi
- **Meaning**: to break with hands, to split
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 掰开 (bāi kāi) — to break apart
- 掰断 (bāi duàn) — to snap

## Example Sentences

- 把面包掰成两半。(Bǎ miàn bāo bāi chéng liǎng bàn.) — Break the bread in half.

## Notes

掰 two hands 手 pulling apart 分, breaking motion.
