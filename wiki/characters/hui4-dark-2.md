# 晦 Dark, obscure, last day of lunar month

- **Character**: 晦
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: dark, obscure, last day of lunar month
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 晦涩 (huìsè) — obscure, abstruse
- 晦暗 (huì'àn) — dim, gloomy

## Example Sentences

- 天色晦暗。(Tiānsè huì'àn.) — The sky is dim.

## Notes

Literary term for darkness or obscurity, both literal and figurative.
