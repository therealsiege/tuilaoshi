# 献 To offer, to present, to dedicate

- **Character**: 献
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: to offer, to present, to dedicate
- **Radical**: 犬(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 奉献 (fèng xiàn) — to dedicate, to devote
- 贡献 (gòng xiàn) — to contribute, contribution

## Example Sentences

- 他为国家奉献了一生。(Tā wèi guójiā fèngxiàn le yīshēng.) — He dedicated his life to the country.

## Notes

Expresses offering or dedicating with respect.
