# 漱 To rinse, gargle

- **Character**: 漱
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: to rinse, gargle
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漱口 (shù kǒu) — to rinse mouth, gargle
- 盥漱 (guàn shù) — to wash up

## Example Sentences

- 饭后要漱口。(Fàn hòu yào shùkǒu.) — You should rinse your mouth after meals.

## Notes

To rinse or gargle, especially the mouth.
