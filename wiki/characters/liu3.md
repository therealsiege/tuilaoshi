# 柳 Willow

- **Character**: 柳
- **Pinyin**: liǔ
- **Meaning**: Willow constellation, willow
- **Radical**: 木(wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柳宿 (liǔ xiù) — Willow constellation
- 柳树 (liǔ shù) — willow tree

## Example Sentences

- 柳宿代表南方朱雀的嘴部。(Liǔ xiù dàibiǎo nánfāng zhūquè de zuǐ bù.) — Willow constellation represents the beak of the Southern Vermillion Bird.

## Notes

柳 means willow tree. In astronomy, the Willow constellation, part of the Vermillion Bird pattern.
