# 筋 Tendon, Muscle, 筋骨 Muscles And Bones

- **Character**: 筋
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: tendon, muscle, 筋骨 muscles and bones
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 筋骨 (jīn gǔ) — muscles and bones
- 抽筋 (chōu jīn) — cramp
- 脑筋 (nǎo jīn) — brains, mind

## Example Sentences

- 运动前要活动筋骨。(Yùndòng qián yào huódòng jīngǔ.) — You should loosen up your muscles before exercising.

## Notes

Term for tendons and muscles. 抽筋 (cramp) is common complaint. 脑筋 figuratively means mental capacity or thinking.
