# 承 To Bear

- **Character**: 承
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to bear, to承 to承tain
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 轴承 (zhóu chéng) — bearing
- 承担 (chéng dān) — to承 bear

## Example Sentences

- 他承担了全部责任。(Tā chéng dān le quán bù zé rèn.) — He bore all the responsibility.

## Notes

承 shows hands supporting from below, perfect for bearings and承 undertaking responsibilities.
