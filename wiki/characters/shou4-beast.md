# 兽 Beast, animal

- **Character**: 兽
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: beast, animal
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 野兽 (yě shòu) — wild beast
- 猛兽 (měng shòu) — fierce animal

## Example Sentences

- 森林里生活着各种野兽。(Sēn lín lǐ shēng huó zhe gè zhǒng yě shòu.) — Various wild beasts live in the forest.

## Notes

General term for mammals and large animals
