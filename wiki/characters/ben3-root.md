# 本 Root

- **Character**: 本
- **Pinyin**: běn
- **Meaning**: root, origin, this; measure word for books
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 本来 (běn lái) — originally
- 基本 (jī běn) — basic

## Example Sentences

- 我买了三本书。(Wǒ mǎi le sān běn shū.) — I bought three books.

## Notes

本 is the measure word for books. Also means 'origin/root' as in 本来 (originally), 日本 (Japan = sun origin).
