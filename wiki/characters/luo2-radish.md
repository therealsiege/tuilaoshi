# 萝 Radish, turnip

- **Character**: 萝
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: radish, turnip
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 萝卜 (luó bo) — radish
- 胡萝卜 (hú luó bo) — carrot

## Example Sentences

- 胡萝卜对眼睛好。(Húluóbo duì yǎnjing hǎo.) — Carrots are good for the eyes.

## Notes

萝卜 is radish, 胡萝卜 is carrot. Notice the pattern with 菠萝 (pineapple).
