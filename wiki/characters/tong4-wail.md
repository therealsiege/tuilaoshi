# 恸 To Wail

- **Character**: 恸
- **Pinyin**: tòng
- **Meaning**: to wail, to cry in grief
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恸哭 (tòng kū) — to wail
- 悲恸 (bēi tòng) — grieved

## Example Sentences

- 她悲恸欲绝。(Tā bēi tòng yù jué.) — She was overcome with grief.

## Notes

恸 simplified from 慟. Heart 忄 with 动 (move), heart moved to extreme grief.
