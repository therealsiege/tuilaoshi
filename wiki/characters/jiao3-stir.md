# 搅 To stir, to mix, to disturb

- **Character**: 搅
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: to stir, to mix, to disturb
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir, to agitate
- 打搅 (dǎ jiǎo) — to disturb, to bother

## Example Sentences

- 请把鸡蛋搅拌均匀。(Qǐng bǎ jīdàn jiǎobàn jūnyún.) — Please stir the eggs evenly.

## Notes

Common in cooking contexts and for disturbance.
