# 焦 Joule, burnt

- **Character**: 焦
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: joule, burnt
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 焦耳 (jiāo ěr) — joule
- 焦点 (jiāo diǎn) — focus

## Example Sentences

- 能量以焦耳计量。(Néng liàng yǐ jiāo ěr jì liàng.) — Energy is measured in joules.

## Notes

Used in 焦耳 (joule), energy measurement unit
