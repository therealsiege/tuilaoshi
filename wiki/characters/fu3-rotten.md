# 腐 Rotten

- **Character**: 腐
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: rotten, corrupt, tofu
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 腐败 (fǔ bài) — corruption, rotten
- 豆腐 (dòu fǔ) — tofu

## Example Sentences

- 反对腐败。(Fǎn duì fǔ bài.) — Oppose corruption.

## Notes

腐 shows meat 肉 in a container 府, representing rotting or fermentation (as in tofu).
