# 恍 Seemingly, as if

- **Character**: 恍
- **Pinyin**: huǎng
- **Meaning**: seemingly, as if
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恍惚 (huǎnghū) — absent-minded, in a trance
- 恍然大悟 (huǎngrán dàwù) — suddenly see the light

## Example Sentences

- 他恍然大悟。(Tā huǎngrán dàwù.) — He suddenly understood.

## Notes

Literary term for seeming or sudden realization.
