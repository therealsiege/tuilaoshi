# 岩 Rock

- **Character**: 岩
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: rock, cliff
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 岩石 (yán shí) — rock
- 岩浆 (yán jiāng) — lava, magma

## Example Sentences

- 登山者爬上了陡峭的岩壁。(Dēng shān zhě pá shàng le dǒu qiào de yán bì.) — The climber scaled the steep cliff face.

## Notes

岩 has mountain 山 under stone 石, solid rock formations.
