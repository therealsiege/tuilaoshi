# 涌 Surge, gush, emerge

- **Character**: 涌
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: surge, gush, emerge
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涌现 (yǒngxiàn) — emerge, spring up
- 汹涌 (xiōngyǒng) — turbulent, surging

## Example Sentences

- 人群涌向广场。(Rénqún yǒng xiàng guǎngchǎng.) — The crowd surged toward the square.

## Notes

Describes surging movements of water or crowds.
