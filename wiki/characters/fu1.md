# 肤 Skin

- **Character**: 肤
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: skin
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 皮肤 (pí fū) — skin
- 肌肤 (jī fū) — skin

## Example Sentences

- 她的皮肤很光滑。(Tā de pí fū hěn guāng huá.) — Her skin is very smooth.

## Notes

肤 specifically means skin, often paired with 皮 in 皮肤. Used metaphorically for superficial understanding.
