# 痴 Dementia

- **Character**: 痴
- **Pinyin**: chī
- **Meaning**: silly, dementia
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痴呆 (chī dāi) — dementia
- 白痴 (bái chī) — idiot
- 痴迷 (chī mí) — infatuated, obsessed

## Example Sentences

- 老年痴呆症影响记忆。(Lǎo nián chī dāi zhèng yǐng xiǎng jì yì.) — Alzheimer's disease affects memory.

## Notes

Cognitive impairment; dementia conditions.
