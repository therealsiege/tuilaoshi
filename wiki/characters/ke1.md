# 科 Department

- **Character**: 科
- **Pinyin**: kē
- **Meaning**: department, branch, science
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 科学 (kē xué) — science
- 科技 (kē jì) — technology

## Example Sentences

- 他学的是计算机科学。(Tā xué de shì jì suàn jī kē xué.) — He studies computer science.

## Notes

科 originally meant to measure grain; extended to classification and academic subjects.
