# 赈 Relieve, provide disaster relief

- **Character**: 赈
- **Pinyin**: zhèn
- **Meaning**: relieve, provide disaster relief
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 赈灾 (zhènzāi) — provide disaster relief
- 赈济 (zhènjì) — provide relief

## Example Sentences

- 国家向灾区赈济粮食。(Guójiā xiàng zāiqū zhènjì liángshi.) — The state provides grain relief to disaster areas.

## Notes

Specifically refers to disaster relief and aid.
