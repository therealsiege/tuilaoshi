# 售 Sell

- **Character**: 售
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: sell
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 出售 (chūshòu) — sell, offer for sale
- 销售 (xiāoshòu) — sell, market

## Example Sentences

- 这家店出售各种商品。(Zhè jiā diàn chūshòu gèzhǒng shāngpǐn.) — This store sells various goods.

## Notes

Formal term for selling, commonly used in business contexts.
