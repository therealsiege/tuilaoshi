# 雁 Wild goose

- **Character**: 雁
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: wild goose
- **Radical**: 隹 (short-tailed bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大雁 (dà yàn) — wild goose
- 雁群 (yàn qún) — flock of geese

## Example Sentences

- 秋天大雁往南飞。(Qiūtiān dàyàn wǎng nán fēi.) — In autumn, wild geese fly south.

## Notes

Known for V-formation flight. 隹 radical for bird.
