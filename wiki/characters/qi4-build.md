# 砌 To Lay Bricks

- **Character**: 砌
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: to build by piling, to lay bricks
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砌墙 (qì qiáng) — to build a wall
- 堆砌 (duī qì) — to pile up

## Example Sentences

- 工人在砌墙。(Gōng rén zài qì qiáng.) — The workers are building a wall.

## Notes

砌 has stone 石 with 切 (to cut), stacking cut stones.
