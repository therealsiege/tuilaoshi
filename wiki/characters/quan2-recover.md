# 痊 Recover, heal

- **Character**: 痊
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: recover, heal
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 痊愈 (quán yù) — recover
- 痊可 (quán kě) — be cured

## Example Sentences

- 祝你早日痊愈。(Zhù nǐ zǎo rì quán yù.) — Wish you a speedy recovery.
