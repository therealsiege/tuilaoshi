# 推 Push

- **Character**: 推
- **Pinyin**: tuī
- **Meaning**: to push
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 推铅球 (tuī qiān qiú) — to put the shot
- 推进 (tuī jìn) — to advance

## Example Sentences

- 他用力推出铅球。(Tā yònglì tuī chū qiānqiú.) — He powerfully put the shot.

## Notes

Pushing motion; shot put technique
