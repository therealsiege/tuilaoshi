# 浊 Muddy

- **Character**: 浊
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: muddy, turbid, impure
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浑浊 (hún zhuó) — turbid
- 污浊 (wū zhuó) — dirty

## Example Sentences

- 河水变得浑浊。(Hé shuǐ biàn de hún zhuó.) — The river water became turbid.

## Notes

浊 simplified from 濁. Water 氵 mixed with impurities.
