# 术 Technique

- **Character**: 术
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: technique, art, method
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 技术 (jì shù) — technology
- 魔术 (mó shù) — magic

## Example Sentences

- 他的魔术表演很精彩。(Tā de mó shù biǎo yǎn hěn jīng cǎi.) — His magic performance is very wonderful.

## Notes

术 means technique, art, or method; appears in many skill-related compounds.
