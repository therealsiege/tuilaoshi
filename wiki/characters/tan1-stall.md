# 摊 Stall

- **Character**: 摊
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: stall, booth, to spread out
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摊位 (tān wèi) — booth, stall
- 地摊 (dì tān) — street stall

## Example Sentences

- 市场上有很多小摊。(Shì chǎng shàng yǒu hěn duō xiǎo tān.) — There are many small stalls in the market.

## Notes

摊 simplified from 攤. Hand radical 扌 with 难, representing spreading things out with hands, like goods on a stall.
