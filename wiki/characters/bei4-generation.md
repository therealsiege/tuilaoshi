# 辈 Generation

- **Character**: 辈
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: generation, lifetime, class
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 辈分 (bèi fèn) — seniority in family
- 前辈 (qián bèi) — senior, predecessor

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的前辈。(Tā shì wǒ de qián bèi.) — He is my senior/predecessor.

## Notes

辈 simplified from 輩. Vehicle radical 车 with people, representing groups moving through time together as generations.
