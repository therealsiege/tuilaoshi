# 黄 Yellow

- **Character**: 黄
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: yellow, surname
- **Radical**: 黄 (yellow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 黄色 (huáng sè) — yellow
- 黄梅 (huáng méi) — Huangmei (opera)

## Example Sentences

- 黄梅戏起源于安徽。(Huáng méi xì qǐ yuán yú ān huī.) — Huangmei opera originated in Anhui.

## Notes

黄 means yellow; 黄梅戏 is a popular folk opera from Anhui.
