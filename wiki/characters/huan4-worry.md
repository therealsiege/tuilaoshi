# 患 Worry

- **Character**: 患
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: worry, suffer from, disaster
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 患者 (huàn zhě) — patient, sufferer
- 忧患 (yōu huàn) — suffering, distress

## Example Sentences

- 他患了重病。(Tā huàn le zhòng bìng.) — He suffers from a serious illness.

## Notes

患 shows two hands 串 over a heart 心, representing worry and affliction weighing on the heart.
