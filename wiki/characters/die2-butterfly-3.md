# 蝶 Butterfly

- **Character**: 蝶
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: butterfly
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 蝶泳 (dié yǒng) — butterfly stroke
- 蝴蝶 (hú dié) — butterfly

## Example Sentences

- 蝶泳是最难的泳姿。(Diéyǒng shì zuì nán de yǒngzī.) — Butterfly is the most difficult swimming stroke.

## Notes

Butterfly; butterfly stroke in swimming
