# 留 Stay, remain, keep

- **Character**: 留
- **Pinyin**: liú
- **Meaning**: stay, remain, keep
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 保留 (bǎoliú) — retain, reserve
- 拘留 (jūliú) — detain

## Example Sentences

- 请留下联系方式。(Qǐng liúxià liánxì fāngshì.) — Please leave your contact information.

## Notes

Very common for staying and keeping.
