# 攫 Seize, grab

- **Character**: 攫
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: seize, grab
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 23

## Compounds

- 攫取 (juéqǔ) — seize, grab
- 攫夺 (juéduó) — seize, plunder

## Example Sentences

- 不法分子攫取了大量财富。(Bùfǎ fènzǐ juéqǔ le dàliàng cáifù.) — Criminals seized a large amount of wealth.

## Notes

Suggests forceful, predatory seizing, often with negative connotation.
