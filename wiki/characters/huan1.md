# 欢 Joyous

- **Character**: 欢
- **Pinyin**: huān
- **Meaning**: joyous, happy
- **Radical**: 欠 (yawn)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 喜欢 (xǐ huān) — to like
- 欢迎 (huān yíng) — to welcome

## Example Sentences

- 欢迎你来中国！(Huān yíng nǐ lái zhōng guó!) — Welcome to China!

## Notes

欢 is simplified from 歡. Almost always appears in compound words like 喜欢 and 欢迎.
