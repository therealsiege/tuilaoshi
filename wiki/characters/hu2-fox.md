# 狐 Fox

- **Character**: 狐
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: fox
- **Radical**: 犭 (animal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 狐假虎威 (hú jiǎ hǔ wēi) — bully using another's power
- 狐狸 (hú li) — fox

## Example Sentences

- 他不过是狐假虎威罢了。(Tā búguò shì hú jiǎ hǔ wēi bà le.) — He's just bullying others by borrowing someone else's power.

## Notes

Used in 狐假虎威: the fox uses the tiger's fearsome reputation.
