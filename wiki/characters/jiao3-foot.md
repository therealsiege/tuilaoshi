# 脚 Foot

- **Character**: 脚
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: foot, leg
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 脚步 (jiǎo bù) — footstep
- 赤脚 (chì jiǎo) — barefoot

## Example Sentences

- 我的脚很疼。(Wǒ de jiǎo hěn téng.) — My feet hurt.

## Notes

脚 simplified from 腳, with flesh radical. 脚步 = footstep. 赤脚 = barefoot.
