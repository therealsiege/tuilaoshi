# 哭 To Cry

- **Character**: 哭
- **Pinyin**: kū
- **Meaning**: to cry, to weep
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哭泣 (kū qì) — to weep
- 痛哭 (tòng kū) — to cry bitterly

## Example Sentences

- 别哭了。(Bié kū le.) — Stop crying.

## Notes

哭 has two mouths (口口) on top and 犬 (dog) below — like two mouths wailing.
