# 建 To Build

- **Character**: 建
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: to build, to construct, to establish
- **Radical**: 廴 (stride)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 建设 (jiàn shè) — to build, construction
- 建筑 (jiàn zhù) — building, architecture

## Example Sentences

- 我们正在建一座新楼。(Wǒ men zhèng zài jiàn yī zuò xīn lóu.) — We are building a new building.

## Notes

建 combines廴 (to walk) with聿 (brush), representing establishing or constructing something.
