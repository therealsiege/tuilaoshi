# 滴 Drop, drip

- **Character**: 滴
- **Pinyin**: dī
- **Meaning**: drop, drip
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 水滴 (shuǐ dī) — water drop
- 一滴 (yī dī) — one drop

## Example Sentences

- 水滴石穿，绳锯木断。(Shuǐ dī shí chuān, shéng jù mù duàn.) — Dripping water penetrates stone, sawing rope cuts wood.

## Notes

Single drop, used in perseverance proverb
