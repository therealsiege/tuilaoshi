# 垒 Base

- **Character**: 垒
- **Pinyin**: lěi
- **Meaning**: base (baseball), rampart
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 垒球 (lěi qiú) — softball
- 本垒 (běn lěi) — home base

## Example Sentences

- 垒球和棒球很相似。(Lěiqiú hé bàngqiú hěn xiāngsì.) — Softball and baseball are very similar.
