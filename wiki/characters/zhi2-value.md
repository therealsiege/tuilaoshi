# 值 Value

- **Character**: 值
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: value, worth, to be worth
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 价值 (jià zhí) — value
- 值得 (zhí dé) — to be worth

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很值得一读。(Zhè běn shū hěn zhí dé yī dú.) — This book is well worth reading.

## Notes

值 shows person 亻 and 直 (straight), representing upright worth or equivalent value.
