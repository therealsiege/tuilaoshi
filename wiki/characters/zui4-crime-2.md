# 罪 Crime

- **Character**: 罪
- **Pinyin**: zuì
- **Meaning**: crime, sin, guilt
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 犯罪 (fàn zuì) — to commit a crime
- 有罪 (yǒu zuì) — guilty

## Example Sentences

- 他没有犯罪。(Tā méi yǒu fàn zuì.) — He didn't commit a crime.

## Notes

罪 shows a net (law) over wrong (非), representing crime or guilt.
