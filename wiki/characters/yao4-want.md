# 要 To Want

- **Character**: 要
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: to want, to need, will, important
- **Radical**: 西 (west)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 重要 (zhòng yào) — important
- 需要 (xū yào) — to need

## Example Sentences

- 我要一杯水。(Wǒ yào yī bēi shuǐ.) — I want a glass of water.

## Notes

要 has multiple uses: 'to want' (我要), 'will/going to' (要下雨了), 'need to' (你要学习). 不要 = don't.
