# 可 Can

- **Character**: 可
- **Pinyin**: kě
- **Meaning**: can, may, -able
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 可以 (kě yǐ) — can, may
- 可能 (kě néng) — possible

## Example Sentences

- 可以进来吗？(Kě yǐ jìn lái ma?) — May I come in?

## Notes

可以 indicates permission or possibility. 可爱 means 'cute' (able to be loved). 可是 means 'but.'
