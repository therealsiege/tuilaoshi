# 淹 To Flood

- **Character**: 淹
- **Pinyin**: yān
- **Meaning**: to flood, to submerge
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淹没 (yān mò) — to submerge, to flood
- 淹死 (yān sǐ) — to drown

## Example Sentences

- 洪水淹没了整个村庄。(Hóng shuǐ yān mò le zhěng gè cūn zhuāng.) — The flood submerged the entire village.

## Notes

淹 specifically relates to flooding or drowning, with connotations of being overwhelmed by water.
