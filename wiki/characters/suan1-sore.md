# 酸 Sore

- **Character**: 酸
- **Pinyin**: suān
- **Meaning**: sore, sour
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 酸痛 (suān tòng) — sore, aching
- 腰酸 (yāo suān) — lower back soreness
- 酸胀 (suān zhàng) — sore and swollen

## Example Sentences

- 运动后肌肉酸痛。(Yùn dòng hòu jī ròu suān tòng.) — Muscles are sore after exercise.

## Notes

Soreness; muscle fatigue or strain.
