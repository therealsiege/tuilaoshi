# 翳 Shade

- **Character**: 翳
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: shade, to obscure, cataract
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 遮翳 (zhē yì) — to shade
- 云翳 (yún yì) — cloudiness

## Example Sentences

- 树荫遮翳了阳光。(Shù yīn zhē yì le yáng guāng.) — The tree shade blocks the sunlight.

## Notes

翳 has feather 羽 over 殹 (medical), covering that obscures light.
