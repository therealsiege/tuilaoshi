# 碗 Bowl

- **Character**: 碗
- **Pinyin**: wǎn
- **Meaning**: bowl
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 饭碗 (fànwǎn) — rice bowl
- 碗筷 (wǎnkuài) — bowl and chopsticks

## Example Sentences

- 请给我一个碗。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yī gè wǎn.) — Please give me a bowl.

## Notes

Basic dining vocabulary. Measure word is 个. The stone radical suggests ceramic material.
