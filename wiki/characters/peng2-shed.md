# 棚 Shed, shack, booth

- **Character**: 棚
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: shed, shack, booth
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大棚 (dà péng) — greenhouse, large shed
- 棚户 (péng hù) — shanty, shack

## Example Sentences

- 农民在大棚里种植蔬菜。(Nóng mín zài dà péng lǐ zhòng zhí shū cài.) — Farmers grow vegetables in the greenhouse.

## Notes

Refers to simple structures like sheds or greenhouses. 棚架 means trellis or scaffolding.
