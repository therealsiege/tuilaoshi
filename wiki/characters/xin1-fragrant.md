# 馨 Fragrant, warm

- **Character**: 馨
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: fragrant, warm
- **Radical**: 香 (fragrance)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 温馨 (wēn xīn) — warm, cozy
- 馨香 (xīn xiāng) — fragrance

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间布置得温馨舒适。(Zhè gè fáng jiān bù zhì de wēn xīn shū shì.) — This room is arranged warmly and comfortably.

## Notes

Can mean fragrant or warm/cozy feelings.
