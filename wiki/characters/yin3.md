# 隐 To Hide

- **Character**: 隐
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: to hide, concealed
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 隐藏 (yǐn cáng) — to hide, to conceal
- 隐私 (yǐn sī) — privacy

## Example Sentences

- 他隐瞒了真相。(Tā yǐn mán le zhēn xiàng.) — He concealed the truth.

## Notes

隐 uses mound radical 阝, suggesting something hidden behind or beneath. Used for physical hiding or concealment.
