# 预 Beforehand, in advance

- **Character**: 预
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: beforehand, in advance
- **Radical**: 页 (head)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 预算 (yùsuàn) — budget
- 预测 (yùcè) — forecast, predict

## Example Sentences

- 政府公布了年度预算。(Zhèngfǔ gōngbù le niándù yùsuàn.) — The government announced the annual budget.

## Notes

Used for planning and advance preparation.
