# 际 Border

- **Character**: 际
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: border, edge, occasion,际rsonal relations
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 实际 (shí jì) — actual
- 国际 (guó jì) — international

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个国际会议。(Zhè shì yī ge guó jì huì yì.) — This is an际ternational conference.

## Notes

际 originally meant border, extended to occasions and relations (人际 interpersonal). Common in compound际.
