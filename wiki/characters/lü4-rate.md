# 率 Rate

- **Character**: 率
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: rate, ratio, to lead
- **Radical**: 玄 (dark)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 效率 (xiào lǜ) — efficiency
- 概率 (gài lǜ) — probability

## Example Sentences

- 我们要提高工作效率。(Wǒ men yào tí gāo gōng zuò xiào lǜ.) — We need to improve work efficiency.

## Notes

率 can be lǜ (rate, ratio) or shuài (to lead). Common in efficiency, probability, and rate contexts.
