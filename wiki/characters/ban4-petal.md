# 瓣 Petal

- **Character**: 瓣
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: petal, segment, valve
- **Radical**: 瓜 (melon)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 花瓣 (huā bàn) — petal
- 蒜瓣 (suàn bàn) — garlic clove

## Example Sentences

- 花瓣落了一地。(Huā bàn luò le yí dì.) — Petals fell all over the ground.

## Notes

瓣 has two 辛 (bitter) flanking 瓜 (melon), segments of a fruit.
