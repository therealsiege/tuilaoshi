# 传 传播, pass, transmit

- **Character**: 传
- **Pinyin**: chuán
- **Meaning**: 传播, pass, transmit
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 传播 (chuán bō) — spread, disseminate
- 传递 (chuán dì) — 传达, convey

## Example Sentences

- 广告通过媒体传播。(Guǎng gào tōng guò méi tǐ chuán bō.) — Advertisements are spread through media.

## Notes

传媒 means media. 传真 means fax.
