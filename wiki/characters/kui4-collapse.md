# 溃 Collapse, be routed

- **Character**: 溃
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: collapse, be routed
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 溃败 (kuìbài) — be utterly defeated
- 崩溃 (bēngkuì) — collapse, break down

## Example Sentences

- 敌军溃不成军。(Díjūn kuì bù chéng jūn.) — The enemy army was completely routed.

## Notes

Describes complete collapse or defeat, often in military contexts.
