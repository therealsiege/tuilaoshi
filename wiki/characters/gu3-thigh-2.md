# 股 Share

- **Character**: 股
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: thigh, share, strand
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 股票 (gǔ piào) — stock, share
- 股东 (gǔ dōng) — shareholder

## Example Sentences

- 他买了一些股票。(Tā mǎi le yī xiē gǔ piào.) — He bought some stocks.

## Notes

股 originally meant thigh; extended to portions and shares in business.
