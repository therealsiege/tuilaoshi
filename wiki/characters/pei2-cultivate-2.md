# 培 To Cultivate

- **Character**: 培
- **Pinyin**: péi
- **Meaning**: to cultivate, to train
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 培养 (péi yǎng) — to cultivate, to train
- 培训 (péi xùn) — to train

## Example Sentences

- 学校培养了很多优秀人才。(Xué xiào péi yǎng le hěn duō yōu xiù rén cái.) — The school has cultivated many outstanding talents.

## Notes

培 combines earth 土 and 咅 (phonetic), representing mounding earth around plants to help them grow.
