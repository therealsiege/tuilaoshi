# 喝 To Drink

- **Character**: 喝
- **Pinyin**: hē
- **Meaning**: to drink
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喝水 (hē shuǐ) — to drink water
- 喝茶 (hē chá) — to drink tea
- 喝酒 (hē jiǔ) — to drink alcohol
- 好喝 (hǎo hē) — tasty (for drinks)
- 喝醉 (hē zuì) — to get drunk

## Example Sentences

- 你想喝什么？(Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?) — What would you like to drink?
- 中国人喜欢喝茶。(Zhōngguó rén xǐhuān hē chá.) — Chinese people like to drink tea.
- 多喝水对身体好。(Duō hē shuǐ duì shēntǐ hǎo.) — Drinking more water is good for your health.

## Notes

喝 uses the mouth radical 口 plus 曷 (hé) as a phonetic component. When pronounced hè (fourth tone), it means "to shout" as in 喝彩 (hècǎi, "to cheer/applaud"). The pattern for taste adjectives mirrors 吃: 好喝 (hǎo hē, "tastes good" for drinks) parallels 好吃 (hǎo chī, "tastes good" for food). Tea culture runs deep in China -- 喝茶 can also refer to a social gathering or, colloquially, being called in for police questioning.
