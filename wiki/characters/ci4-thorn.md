# 刺 Thorn, sting, stab

- **Character**: 刺
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: thorn, sting, stab
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刺激 (cì jī) — stimulate, irritate
- 荆棘 (jīng jí) — thorn, bramble

## Example Sentences

- 玫瑰的茎上有刺。(Méi gui de jīng shàng yǒu cì.) — Rose stems have thorns.
