# 倡 Initiate, Advocate, 倡导 Advocate

- **Character**: 倡
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: initiate, advocate, 倡导 advocate
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倡导 (chàng dǎo) — advocate, promote
- 倡议 (chàng yì) — propose, initiative
- 首倡 (shǒu chàng) — initiate, be the first to propose

## Example Sentences

- 我们倡导绿色生活方式。(Wǒmen chàngdǎo lǜsè shēnghuó fāngshì.) — We advocate a green lifestyle.

## Notes

Formal term commonly used in official contexts for advocacy and promotion of ideas, policies, or initiatives.
