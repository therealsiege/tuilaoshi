# 晚 Late

- **Character**: 晚
- **Pinyin**: wǎn
- **Meaning**: late, evening
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 晚上 (wǎn shàng) — evening, night
- 晚饭 (wǎn fàn) — dinner

## Example Sentences

- 晚上见！(Wǎn shàng jiàn!) — See you tonight!

## Notes

晚 with sun radical. Opposite of 早. 晚安 = good night.
