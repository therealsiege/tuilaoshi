# 着 To touch, be affected by, 著 (traditional)

- **Character**: 着
- **Pinyin**: zháo
- **Meaning**: to touch, be affected by, 著 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 着急 (zháo jí) — worried, anxious
- 着火 (zháo huǒ) — to catch fire

## Example Sentences

- 别着急，慢慢来。(Bié zháojí, màn man lái.) — Don't worry, take your time.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: zháo (touch/ignite), zhe (aspect), zhuó (wear).
