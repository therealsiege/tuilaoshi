# 轴 Axis, shaft

- **Character**: 轴
- **Pinyin**: zhóu
- **Meaning**: axis, shaft
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 主轴 (zhǔ zhóu) — spindle, main axis
- 轴承 (zhóu chéng) — bearing

## Example Sentences

- 机器的主轴需要润滑。(Jī qì de zhǔ zhóu xū yào rùn huá.) — The machine's spindle needs lubrication.

## Notes

Used in 主轴 (spindle), 轴承 (bearing), X轴/Y轴/Z轴 (X/Y/Z axis)
