# 宝 Treasure

- **Character**: 宝
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: treasure, precious
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 宝贝 (bǎo bèi) — treasure, baby
- 数来宝 (shǔ lái bǎo) — rhythmic talk

## Example Sentences

- 这是我的宝贝。(Zhè shì wǒ de bǎo bèi.) — This is my treasure.

## Notes

宝 means treasure or precious; in 数来宝 part of the performance name.
