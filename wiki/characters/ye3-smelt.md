# 冶 To Smelt

- **Character**: 冶
- **Pinyin**: yě
- **Meaning**: to smelt, seductive
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 冶炼 (yě liàn) — to smelt
- 妖冶 (yāo yě) — seductive, bewitching

## Example Sentences

- 这个工厂冶炼铁矿石。(Zhè ge gōng chǎng yě liàn tiě kuàng shí.) — This factory smelts iron ore.

## Notes

冶 primarily means to smelt metals. Also used in literary contexts to describe seductive beauty.
