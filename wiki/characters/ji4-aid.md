# 济 To aid, to help, to relieve

- **Character**: 济
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to aid, to help, to relieve
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 救济 (jiù jì) — to relieve, relief aid
- 经济 (jīng jì) — economy, economic

## Example Sentences

- 政府救济灾民。(Zhèngfǔ jiùjì zāimín.) — The government provides relief to disaster victims.

## Notes

Can mean to aid/help or be part of 经济 (economy).
