# 插 To Insert

- **Character**: 插
- **Pinyin**: chā
- **Meaning**: to insert, to stick in, to plug in
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 插入 (chā rù) — to insert
- 插座 (chā zuò) — electrical outlet

## Example Sentences

- 请把钥匙插进锁里。(Qǐng bǎ yào shi chā jìn suǒ lǐ.) — Please insert the key into the lock.

## Notes

插 means to insert or stick something into something else. Used for plugging in, inserting, and cutting in line.
