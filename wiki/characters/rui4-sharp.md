# 锐 Sharp, keen, acute

- **Character**: 锐
- **Pinyin**: ruì
- **Meaning**: sharp, keen, acute
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 敏锐 (mǐnruì) — keen, acute
- 锐利 (ruìlì) — sharp, incisive

## Example Sentences

- 他观察很敏锐。(Tā guānchá hěn mǐnruì.) — His observation is very acute.

## Notes

Common formal term for sharpness or keenness.
