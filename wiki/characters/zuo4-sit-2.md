# 坐 To Sit

- **Character**: 坐
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: to sit
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 请坐 (qǐng zuò) — please sit
- 坐下 (zuò xià) — to sit down

## Example Sentences

- 请坐下。(Qǐng zuò xià.) — Please sit down.

## Notes

坐 shows two people 人人 on the earth 土, representing people sitting on the ground.
