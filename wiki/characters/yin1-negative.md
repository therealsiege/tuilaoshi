# 阴 Negative, yin

- **Character**: 阴
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: negative, yin
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阴极 (yīn jí) — cathode, negative pole
- 阴天 (yīn tiān) — cloudy day

## Example Sentences

- 阴极射线管。(Yīn jí shè xiàn guǎn.) — Cathode ray tube.

## Notes

Used in 阴极 (cathode), 负阴 (negative), electrical polarity
