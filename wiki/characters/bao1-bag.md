# 包 Bag

- **Character**: 包
- **Pinyin**: bāo
- **Meaning**: bag, to wrap, package
- **Radical**: 勹 (wrap)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 书包 (shū bāo) — schoolbag
- 红包 (hóng bāo) — red envelope

## Example Sentences

- 我的书包在桌子上。(Wǒ de shū bāo zài zhuō zi shàng.) — My schoolbag is on the table.

## Notes

包 is a pictograph of a baby in the womb — wrapped up. Very versatile: 包子, 面包, 书包.
