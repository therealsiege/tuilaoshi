# 起 To Rise

- **Character**: 起
- **Pinyin**: qǐ
- **Meaning**: to rise, to get up, to start
- **Radical**: 走 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 起来 (qǐ lái) — to get up
- 一起 (yī qǐ) — together

## Example Sentences

- 快起来！(Kuài qǐ lái!) — Get up quickly!

## Notes

起 as a complement indicates upward movement or beginning: 站起来 (stand up), 想起来 (recall). 对不起 = 'sorry.'
