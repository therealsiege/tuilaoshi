# 灰 Ash

- **Character**: 灰
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: ash, dust, gray
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 灰尘 (huī chén) — dust
- 灰色 (huī sè) — gray

## Example Sentences

- 他穿着灰色的衣服。(Tā chuān zhe huī sè de yī fu.) — He is wearing gray clothes.

## Notes

灰 means ash, dust, or gray color. Used in灰尘 (dust), 灰心 (discouraged), colors (灰色).
