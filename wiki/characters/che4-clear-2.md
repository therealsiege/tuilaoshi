# 澈 Clear

- **Character**: 澈
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: clear, limpid, transparent
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 清澈 (qīng chè) — limpid
- 澄澈 (chéng chè) — clear

## Example Sentences

- 湖水清澈见底。(Hú shuǐ qīng chè jiàn dǐ.) — The lake water is so clear you can see the bottom.

## Notes

澈 has water 氵 with 彻 (thorough), completely transparent water.
