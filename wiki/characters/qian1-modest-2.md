# 谦 Modest, humble, 謙 (traditional)

- **Character**: 谦
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: modest, humble, 謙 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 谦虚 (qiān xū) — modest, humble
- 谦逊 (qiān xùn) — modest, unassuming

## Example Sentences

- 他为人很谦虚。(Tā wéirén hěn qiānxū.) — He is very modest as a person.

## Notes

Virtue of modesty and humility.
