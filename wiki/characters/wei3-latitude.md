# 纬 Latitude, weft

- **Character**: 纬
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: latitude, weft
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纬度 (wěi dù) — latitude
- 经纬 (jīng wěi) — longitude and latitude

## Example Sentences

- 赤道的纬度是零度。(Chì dào de wěi dù shì líng dù.) — The equator is at zero degrees latitude.

## Notes

Geographic coordinate
