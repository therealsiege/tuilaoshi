# 眷 Family

- **Character**: 眷
- **Pinyin**: juàn
- **Meaning**: family, to care about
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 眷恋 (juàn liàn) — to be attached to
- 家眷 (jiā juàn) — family members

## Example Sentences

- 他很眷恋家乡。(Tā hěn juàn liàn jiā xiāng.) — He is very attached to his hometown.

## Notes

眷 has eye 目 below 卷 (scroll), watching over one's family.
