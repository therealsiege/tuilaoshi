# 鄙 Rustic

- **Character**: 鄙
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: rustic, despise
- **Radical**: 阝(city)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 卑鄙 (bēi bǐ) — despicable
- 鄙视 (bǐ shì) — to despise

## Example Sentences

- 我鄙视这种不诚实的行为。(Wǒ bǐ shì zhè zhǒng bù chéng shí de xíng wéi.) — I despise this kind of dishonest behavior.

## Notes

鄙 uses city radical 阝, originally meaning countryside (vs. city), extended to mean uncultured or contemptible.
