# 完 To Finish

- **Character**: 完
- **Pinyin**: wán
- **Meaning**: to finish, complete, whole
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 完成 (wán chéng) — to complete
- 完全 (wán quán) — completely

## Example Sentences

- 作业做完了。(Zuò yè zuò wán le.) — The homework is done.

## Notes

完 as a complement indicates completion: 吃完 (finished eating), 看完 (finished watching), 做完 (finished doing).
