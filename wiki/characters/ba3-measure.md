# 把 To Hold

- **Character**: 把
- **Pinyin**: bǎ
- **Meaning**: measure word for things with handles, to hold, ba-construction particle
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 一把 (yī bǎ) — a handful, one (for things with handles)
- 把握 (bǎ wò) — to grasp, to seize

## Example Sentences

- 他把书放在桌子上。(Tā bǎ shū fàng zài zhuō zi shàng.) — He placed the book on the table.

## Notes

把 in the ba-construction (把字句) moves the object before the verb to emphasize the result of an action. Essential for intermediate Chinese grammar.
