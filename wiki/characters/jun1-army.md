# 军 Army

- **Character**: 军
- **Pinyin**: jūn
- **Meaning**: army, military, troops
- **Radical**: 冖 (cover)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 军队 (jūn duì) — army, troops
- 空军 (kōng jūn) — air force

## Example Sentences

- 他在军队服役三年。(Tā zài jūn duì fú yì sān nián.) — He served in the military for three years.

## Notes

军 is simplified from 軍, which showed a chariot surrounded by soldiers.
