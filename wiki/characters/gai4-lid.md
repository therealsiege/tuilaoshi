# 盖 Lid

- **Character**: 盖
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: lid, cover
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盖子 (gàizi) — lid
- 锅盖 (guōgài) — pot lid

## Example Sentences

- 请把锅盖盖上。(Qǐng bǎ guōgài gài shàng.) — Please put the lid on the pot.

## Notes

Both noun (lid) and verb (to cover). The 皿 radical suggests covering a dish or container.
