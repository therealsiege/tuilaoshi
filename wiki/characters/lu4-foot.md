# 麓 Foot of a mountain

- **Character**: 麓
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: foot of a mountain
- **Radical**: 鹿 (deer)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 山麓 (shān lù) — foot of a mountain
- 岳麓山 (Yuè lù shān) — Yuelu Mountain

## Example Sentences

- 这座寺庙建在山麓。(Zhè zuò sì miào jiàn zài shān lù.) — This temple is built at the foot of the mountain.

## Notes

Opposite of 巅 (summit); used in place names.
