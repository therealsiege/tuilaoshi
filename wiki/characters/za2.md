# 杂 Mixed

- **Character**: 杂
- **Pinyin**: zá
- **Meaning**: mixed, miscellaneous
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 复杂 (fù zá) — complicated
- 杂志 (zá zhì) — magazine

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题很复杂。(Zhè gè wèn tí hěn fù zá.) — This problem is very complicated.

## Notes

杂 simplified from 雜. Originally showed different colored silks mixed together.
