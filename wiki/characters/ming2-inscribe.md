# 铭 Inscribe, engrave, motto

- **Character**: 铭
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: inscribe, engrave, motto
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铭记 (míng jì) — engrave on one's mind
- 座右铭 (zuò yòu míng) — motto

## Example Sentences

- 我们要铭记历史，珍惜和平。(Wǒ men yào míng jì lì shǐ, zhēn xī hé píng.) — We must remember history and cherish peace.

## Notes

Often used for deeply remembering or inscriptions.
