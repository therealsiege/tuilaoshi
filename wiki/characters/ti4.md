# 替 To Replace

- **Character**: 替
- **Pinyin**: tì
- **Meaning**: to replace, to substitute, for, on behalf of
- **Radical**: 曰 (say)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 代替 (dài tì) — to replace
- 替换 (tì huàn) — to substitute

## Example Sentences

- 他替我做了这件事。(Tā tì wǒ zuò le zhè jiàn shì.) — He did this thing for me.

## Notes

替 means to substitute or act on behalf of someone. Used as preposition meaning 'for' or 'on behalf of'.
