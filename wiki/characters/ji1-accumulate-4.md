# 积 Accumulate, product, integral

- **Character**: 积
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: accumulate, product, integral
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 面积 (miàn jī) — area
- 积分 (jī fēn) — integral, integration

## Example Sentences

- 计算这个图形的面积。(Jì suàn zhè ge tú xíng de miàn jī.) — Calculate the area of this figure.

## Notes

Multiplication and accumulation
