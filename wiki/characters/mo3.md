# 抹 To Wipe

- **Character**: 抹
- **Pinyin**: mǒ
- **Meaning**: to wipe, to smear (also mā, mò)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear
- 抹布 (mā bù) — rag, cloth

## Example Sentences

- 用抹布擦桌子。(Yòng mā bù cā zhuō zi.) — Wipe the table with a cloth.

## Notes

抹 as mǒ means to wipe/smear, as mā means to wipe, as mò in抹茶 (matcha). Hand 扌 with末 (end).
