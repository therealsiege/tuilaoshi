# 骇 Terrify, shock

- **Character**: 骇
- **Pinyin**: hài
- **Meaning**: terrify, shock
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 骇人听闻 (hàirén tīngwén) — shocking, appalling
- 惊骇 (jīnghài) — shocked, horrified

## Example Sentences

- 这个消息令人震骇。(Zhège xiāoxi lìng rén zhènhài.) — This news is shocking.

## Notes

Emphasizes extreme shock or terror.
