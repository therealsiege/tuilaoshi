# 块 Piece

- **Character**: 块
- **Pinyin**: kuài
- **Meaning**: piece, lump, yuan (colloquial)
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 一块 (yī kuài) — one yuan; one piece
- 一块儿 (yī kuàir) — together

## Example Sentences

- 这个五块钱。(Zhè ge wǔ kuài qián.) — This costs five yuan.

## Notes

块 is the colloquial measure word for money (yuan). Formally it is 元 (yuán). Also means 'piece/chunk.'
