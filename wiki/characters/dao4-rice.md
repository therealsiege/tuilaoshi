# 稻 Rice plant, paddy

- **Character**: 稻
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: rice plant, paddy
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 水稻 (shuǐ dào) — rice
- 稻田 (dào tián) — rice paddy

## Example Sentences

- 南方种植水稻历史悠久。(Nán fāng zhòng zhí shuǐ dào lì shǐ yōu jiǔ.) — The south has a long history of growing rice.

## Notes

Main staple crop in southern China
