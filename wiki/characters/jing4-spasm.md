# 痉 Spasm, 痙 (traditional)

- **Character**: 痉
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: spasm, 痙 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 痉挛 (jìng luán) — spasm, cramp
- 抽痉 (chōu jìng) — to have convulsions

## Example Sentences

- 他腿部肌肉痉挛了。(Tā tuǐbù jīròu jìngluán le.) — He had a muscle spasm in his leg.

## Notes

Medical term for spasms, used in compound 痉挛.
