# 复 To return, repeat, recover, 復 (traditional)

- **Character**: 复
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to return, repeat, recover, 復 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 夂 (go slowly)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恢复 (huī fù) — to recover, restore
- 康复 (kāng fù) — to recover

## Example Sentences

- 他正在恢复健康。(Tā zhèngzài huīfù jiànkāng.) — He is recovering his health.

## Notes

To return, repeat, or recover; also complex/complicated.
