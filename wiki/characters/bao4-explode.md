# 爆 To Explode

- **Character**: 爆
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: to explode, to burst
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 爆炸 (bào zhà) — to explode
- 爆发 (bào fā) — to erupt

## Example Sentences

- 战争爆发了。(Zhàn zhēng bào fā le.) — War broke out.

## Notes

爆 uses the fire radical. 爆炸 = explosion, 爆发 = outbreak.
