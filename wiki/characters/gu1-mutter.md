# 咕 To Mutter

- **Character**: 咕
- **Pinyin**: gū
- **Meaning**: to mutter, rumble (onomatopoeia)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 嘀咕 (dí gū) — to mutter
- 咕噜 (gū lū) — rumbling sound

## Example Sentences

- 肚子咕咕叫了。(Dù zi gū gū jiào le.) — My stomach is rumbling.

## Notes

咕 combines mouth 口 and 古 (ancient), often used in onomatopoeia for murmuring sounds.
