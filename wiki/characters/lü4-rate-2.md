# 率 Rate

- **Character**: 率
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: rate, ratio, to lead
- **Radical**: 玄 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 频率 (pín lǜ) — frequency
- 效率 (xiào lǜ) — efficiency

## Example Sentences

- 这个效率很高。(Zhè ge xiào lǜ hěn gāo.) — This efficiency is very high.

## Notes

率 can also be read as shuài meaning to lead, but lǜ for rate is more common in tech.
