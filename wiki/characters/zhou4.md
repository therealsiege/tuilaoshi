# 宙 Universe, cosmos

- **Character**: 宙
- **Pinyin**: zhòu
- **Meaning**: universe, cosmos
- **Radical**: 宇 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 时空宇宙 (shí kōng yǔ zhòu) — spacetime universe
