# 蓝 Blue

- **Character**: 蓝
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: blue
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蓝色 (lán sè) — blue color
- 天蓝 (tiān lán) — sky blue

## Example Sentences

- 天空是蓝色的。(Tiān kōng shì lán sè de.) — The sky is blue.

## Notes

蓝 originally referred to the indigo plant (hence grass radical 艹) from which blue dye was extracted.
