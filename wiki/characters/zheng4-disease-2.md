# 症 Disease, illness, symptom

- **Character**: 症
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: disease, illness, symptom
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 症状 (zhèng zhuàng) — symptom
- 病症 (bìng zhèng) — disease, illness

## Example Sentences

- 请详细描述您的症状。(Qǐng xiángxì miáoshù nín de zhèng zhuàng.) — Please describe your symptoms in detail.

## Notes

Disease or symptoms. Used to describe medical conditions and their manifestations. Also pronounced zhēng in some contexts.
