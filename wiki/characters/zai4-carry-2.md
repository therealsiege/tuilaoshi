# 载 To Carry

- **Character**: 载
- **Pinyin**: zài
- **Meaning**: to carry, to load
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 载重 (zài zhòng) — load capacity
- 下载 (xià zài) — to download

## Example Sentences

- 这辆车可以载六个人。(Zhè liàng chē kě yǐ zài liù gè rén.) — This car can carry six people.

## Notes

载 shows vehicle carrying loads, representing transportation and loading.
