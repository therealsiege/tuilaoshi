# 吃 To Eat

- **Character**: 吃
- **Pinyin**: chī
- **Meaning**: to eat
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 吃饭 (chī fàn) — to eat (a meal)
- 好吃 (hǎo chī) — delicious

## Example Sentences

- 你吃了吗？(Nǐ chī le ma?) — Have you eaten?

## Notes

你吃了吗？ is a common greeting, equivalent to 'How are you?' reflecting the cultural importance of food.
