# 莲 Lotus

- **Character**: 莲
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: lotus
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 莲花 (lián huā) — lotus flower
- 荷花 (hé huā) — lotus

## Example Sentences

- 池塘里开满了莲花。(Chí táng lǐ kāi mǎn le lián huā.) — The pond is full of blooming lotuses.

## Notes

莲 simplified from 蓮. Grass radical 艹 representing the sacred aquatic plant.
