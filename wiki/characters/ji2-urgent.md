# 急 Urgent

- **Character**: 急
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: urgent, anxious, impatient
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 着急 (zháo jí) — anxious
- 紧急 (jǐn jí) — urgent

## Example Sentences

- 别着急，慢慢来。(Bié zháo jí, màn màn lái.) — Don't worry, take your time.

## Notes

急 shows a hand reaching over heart 心, representing an anxious heart in urgent situations.
