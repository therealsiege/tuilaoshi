# 逊 Modest, yielding, inferior

- **Character**: 逊
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: modest, yielding, inferior
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 谦逊 (qiān xùn) — modest
- 逊色 (xùn sè) — inferior, less impressive

## Example Sentences

- 他为人谦逊，深受大家尊敬。(Tā wéirén qiānxùn, shēn shòu dàjiā zūnjìng.) — He is modest in character and deeply respected by everyone.

## Notes

Represents yielding and humility. Often paired with 谦 to emphasize modest deportment.
