# 琶 琵琶 (pipa, second character)

- **Character**: 琶
- **Pinyin**: pá
- **Meaning**: 琵琶 (pipa, second character)
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琵琶 (pí pá) — pipa, Chinese lute
- 琵琶骨 (pí pá gǔ) — shoulder blade

## Example Sentences

- 琵琶是中国传统乐器之一。(Pí pá shì Zhōng guó chuán tǒng yuè qì zhī yī.) — The pipa is one of the traditional Chinese instruments.

## Notes

Always used in combination as 琵琶.
