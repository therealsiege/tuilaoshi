# 叔 Uncle

- **Character**: 叔
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: uncle (father's younger brother)
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 叔叔 (shū shu) — uncle
- 大叔 (dà shū) — uncle (informal)

## Example Sentences

- 我叔叔住在北京。(Wǒ shū shu zhù zài běi jīng.) — My uncle lives in Beijing.

## Notes

叔 specifically father's younger brother. Reduplicates to 叔叔. Can address middle-aged men.
