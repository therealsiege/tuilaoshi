# 恩 Grace

- **Character**: 恩
- **Pinyin**: ēn
- **Meaning**: grace, kindness
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恩惠 (ēn huì) — favor, grace
- 感恩 (gǎn ēn) — to be grateful

## Example Sentences

- 我们要学会感恩。(Wǒmen yào xuéhuì gǎn'ēn.) — We should learn to be grateful.

## Notes

恩 combines cause 因 with heart 心, representing kindness that causes gratitude in the heart.
