# 薪 Salary

- **Character**: 薪
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: salary, firewood
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 薪水 (xīn shuǐ) — salary
- 薪酬 (xīn chóu) — remuneration

## Example Sentences

- 他的薪水很高。(Tā de xīn shuǐ hěn gāo.) — His salary is very high.

## Notes

Originally meant firewood; extended to salary via 薪俸.
