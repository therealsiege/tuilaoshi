# 洒 To Sprinkle

- **Character**: 洒
- **Pinyin**: sǎ
- **Meaning**: to sprinkle, to spray, to spill
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 洒水 (sǎ shuǐ) — to sprinkle water
- 喷洒 (pēn sǎ) — to spray

## Example Sentences

- 他在地上洒水。(Tā zài dì shàng sǎ shuǐ.) — He is sprinkling water on the ground.

## Notes

洒 means to sprinkle or spray liquids. Used for watering, spilling, and spraying.
