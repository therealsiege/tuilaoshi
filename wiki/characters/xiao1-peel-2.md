# 削 To Peel

- **Character**: 削
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: to peel, to pare, to削削削to cut
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 削皮 (xiāo pí) — to peel
- 削弱 (xuē ruò) — to weaken

## Example Sentences

- 她在削苹果。(Tā zài xiāo píng guǒ.) — She is peeling an apple.

## Notes

削 can be xiāo (to peel, to pare) or xuē (to cut, to削削). As xiāo, used for peeling fruit.
