# 鹿 Deer

- **Character**: 鹿
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: deer
- **Radical**: 鹿 (deer)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 指鹿为马 (zhǐ lù wéi mǎ) — deliberately distort truth
- 梅花鹿 (méihuā lù) — sika deer

## Example Sentences

- 指鹿为马是颠倒黑白的行为。(Zhǐ lù wéi mǎ shì diāndǎo hēibái de xíngwéi.) — Calling a deer a horse is distorting right and wrong.

## Notes

Used in 指鹿为马: the deer that the eunuch Zhao Gao called a horse to test loyalty.
