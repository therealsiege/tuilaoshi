# 调 Tone, tune, to transfer, 調 (traditional)

- **Character**: 调
- **Pinyin**: diào
- **Meaning**: tone, tune, to transfer, 調 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 调查 (diào chá) — to investigate
- 曲调 (qǔ diào) — tune, melody

## Example Sentences

- 他说话的语调很温和。(Tā shuōhuà de yǔdiào hěn wēnhé.) — His tone of voice is very gentle.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: diào (tone/tune) and tiáo (to adjust/transfer).
