# 罚 Punish, penalize, fine

- **Character**: 罚
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: punish, penalize, fine
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 惩罚 (chéng fá) — to punish
- 罚款 (fá kuǎn) — to fine, penalty

## Example Sentences

- 违规停车将被罚款。(Wéi guī tíng chē jiāng bèi fá kuǎn.) — Illegal parking will result in a fine.

## Notes

Common in legal and administrative contexts. 处罚 means to punish. 罚金 means monetary penalty.
