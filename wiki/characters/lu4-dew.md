# 露 Dew, 露, reveal

- **Character**: 露
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: dew,露, reveal
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 露水 (lù shuǐ) — dew
- 朝露 (zhāo lù) — morning dew

## Example Sentences

- 草叶上挂着晶莹的露水。(Cǎo yè shàng guà zhe jīng yíng de lù shuǐ.) — Crystal clear dew hangs on the grass leaves.

## Notes

Water condensation on surfaces
