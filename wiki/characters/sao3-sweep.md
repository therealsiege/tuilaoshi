# 扫 To sweep, clear away

- **Character**: 扫
- **Pinyin**: sǎo
- **Meaning**: to sweep, clear away
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 打扫 (dǎ sǎo) — to clean, sweep
- 扫除 (sǎo chú) — to sweep, clear away

## Example Sentences

- 我每天早上打扫房间。(Wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang dǎ sǎo fáng jiān.) — I clean my room every morning.

## Notes

Common verb for sweeping. 扫兴 means to dampen spirits. 扫描 means to scan.
