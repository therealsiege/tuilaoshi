# 纶 Silk thread, imperial orders

- **Character**: 纶
- **Pinyin**: lún
- **Meaning**: silk thread, imperial orders
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 经纶 (jīng lún) — statecraft, administration
- 满腹经纶 (mǎn fù jīng lún) — full of knowledge

## Example Sentences

- 他满腹经纶，治国有方。(Tā mǎn fù jīng lún, zhì guó yǒu fāng.) — He is full of knowledge and governs well.

## Notes

Classical term related to governance and statecraft
