# 髻 Hair Bun

- **Character**: 髻
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: hair bun, topknot
- **Radical**: 髟 (long hair)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 发髻 (fà jì) — hair bun
- 丸髻 (wán jì) — round bun

## Example Sentences

- 她梳了一个高发髻。(Tā shū le yí gè gāo fà jì.) — She put her hair up in a high bun.

## Notes

髻 has long hair 髟 with 吉 (auspicious), hair elegantly arranged.
