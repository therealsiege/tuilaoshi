# 飞 To Fly

- **Character**: 飞
- **Pinyin**: fēi
- **Meaning**: to fly
- **Radical**: 飞 (fly)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 飞机 (fēi jī) — airplane
- 起飞 (qǐ fēi) — to take off

## Example Sentences

- 飞机几点起飞？(Fēi jī jǐ diǎn qǐ fēi?) — What time does the plane take off?

## Notes

飞 is simplified from 飛, depicting a bird in flight.
