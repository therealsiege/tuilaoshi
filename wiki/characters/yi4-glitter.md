# 熠 Glitter, shine

- **Character**: 熠
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: glitter, shine
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 熠熠 (yì yì) — glistening, sparkling
- 熠熠生辉 (yì yì shēng huī) — shine brilliantly

## Example Sentences

- 她的眼睛熠熠发光。(Tā de yǎn jīng yì yì fā guāng.) — Her eyes sparkle with light.

## Notes

Literary term often used in 熠熠生辉.
