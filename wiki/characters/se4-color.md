# 色 Color

- **Character**: 色
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: color, look, appearance
- **Radical**: 色 (color)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 颜色 (yán sè) — color
- 红色 (hóng sè) — red

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么颜色？(Zhè shì shén me yán sè?) — What color is this?

## Notes

色 means color or appearance. Also means desire/lust in Buddhist usage.
