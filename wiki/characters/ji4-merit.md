# 绩 Merit, achievement

- **Character**: 绩
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: merit, achievement
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 业绩 (yè jì) — performance, achievement
- 成绩 (chéng jì) — achievement, result

## Example Sentences

- 公司今年的业绩很好。(Gōng sī jīn nián de yè jì hěn hǎo.) — The company's performance this year is very good.

## Notes

Essential for performance reviews. 绩效 means performance/effectiveness.
