# 损 Loss, damage

- **Character**: 损
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: loss, damage
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 损失 (sǔn shī) — loss
- 损坏 (sǔn huài) — damage

## Example Sentences

- 损失函数需要优化。(Sǔn shī hán shù xū yào yōu huà.) — The loss function needs optimization.
