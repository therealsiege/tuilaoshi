# 畅 Smooth

- **Character**: 畅
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: smooth, free, unimpeded
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 畅通 (chàng tōng) — unblocked
- 畅销 (chàng xiāo) — bestselling

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很畅销。(Zhè běn shū hěn chàng xiāo.) — This book is a bestseller.

## Notes

畅 means smooth or free-flowing. 畅谈 = to chat freely, 畅快 = carefree.
