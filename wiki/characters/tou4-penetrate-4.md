# 透 To penetrate, thorough, transparent

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: to penetrate, thorough, transparent
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 透明 (tòu míng) — transparent
- 看透 (kàn tòu) — to see through

## Example Sentences

- 玻璃是透明的。(Bōli shì tòumíng de.) — Glass is transparent.

## Notes

To penetrate or see through, thoroughness.
