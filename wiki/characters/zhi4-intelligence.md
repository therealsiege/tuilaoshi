# 智 Intelligence, wisdom

- **Character**: 智
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: intelligence, wisdom
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 智能 (zhì néng) — intelligent, AI
- 人工智能 (rén gōng zhì néng) — artificial intelligence

## Example Sentences

- 人工智能发展很快。(Rén gōng zhì néng fā zhǎn hěn kuài.) — Artificial intelligence is developing rapidly.

## Notes

Essential for 智能 (AI), 人工智能 (artificial intelligence), 智慧 (wisdom)
