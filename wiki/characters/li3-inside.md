# 里 Inside

- **Character**: 里
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: inside, in, Chinese mile
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 这里 (zhè lǐ) — here
- 那里 (nà lǐ) — there

## Example Sentences

- 你家里有几口人？(Nǐ jiā lǐ yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?) — How many people are in your family?

## Notes

里 has two meanings: 'inside' (simplified from 裡/裏) and a unit of distance (about 500 meters).
