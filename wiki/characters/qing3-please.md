# 请 Please

- **Character**: 请
- **Pinyin**: qǐng
- **Meaning**: please, to invite, to treat
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 请问 (qǐng wèn) — excuse me, may I ask
- 请客 (qǐng kè) — to treat someone

## Example Sentences

- 请坐。(Qǐng zuò.) — Please sit down.

## Notes

请 is a polite word used to make requests. 请 + verb is the standard polite imperative.
