# 蕴 To contain, accumulate, 蘊 (traditional)

- **Character**: 蕴
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: to contain, accumulate, 蘊 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 蕴含 (yùn hán) — to contain, embody
- 蕴藏 (yùn cáng) — to hold in store, contain

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗蕴含着深刻的哲理。(Zhè shǒu shī yùnhánzhe shēnkè de zhélǐ.) — This poem contains profound philosophy.

## Notes

Used for abstract containment of meaning, resources, or potential.
