# 暮 Dusk

- **Character**: 暮
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: dusk, evening, late
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 暮色 (mù sè) — dusk
- 日暮 (rì mù) — sunset

## Example Sentences

- 暮色降临，华灯初上。(Mù sè jiàng lín, huá dēng chū shàng.) — Dusk falls and lights come on.

## Notes

暮 has sun 日 with 莫 (don't), sun setting in the grass.
