# 束 Bind, bundle, restrain

- **Character**: 束
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: bind, bundle, restrain
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 束缚 (shù fù) — bind, fetter
- 约束 (yuē shù) — restrain, bind

## Example Sentences

- 不要被传统观念束缚。(Bù yào bèi chuán tǒng guān niàn shù fù.) — Don't be bound by traditional concepts.

## Notes

Physical binding or mental constraint
