# 厚 Thick

- **Character**: 厚
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: thick, deep, generous
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 厚积薄发 (hòu jī bó fā) — build up before breaking through
- 深厚 (shēn hòu) — deep, profound

## Example Sentences

- 成功需要厚积薄发。(Chénggōng xūyào hòu jī bó fā.) — Success requires extensive accumulation before a breakthrough.

## Notes

Used in 厚积薄发: thick/deep accumulation of knowledge before unleashing it.
