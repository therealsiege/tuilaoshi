# 骸 Bone

- **Character**: 骸
- **Pinyin**: hái
- **Meaning**: bone, skeleton
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 骸骨 (hái gǔ) — skeleton, bones
- 遗骸 (yí hái) — remains, skeletal remains

## Example Sentences

- 考古学家发现了古代遗骸。(Kǎo gǔ xué jiā fā xiàn le gǔ dài yí hái.) — Archaeologists discovered ancient skeletal remains.

## Notes

Literary term for bones; often used in forensic or archaeological contexts.
