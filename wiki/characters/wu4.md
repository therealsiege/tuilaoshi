# 误 Mistake, miss, delay

- **Character**: 误
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: mistake, miss, delay
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 误会 (wù huì) — misunderstanding
- 耽误 (dān wù) — to delay, hold up

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个误会,请让我解释。(Zhè shì yī ge wù huì, qǐng ràng wǒ jiě shì.) — This is a misunderstanding, please let me explain.

## Notes

Indicates mistakes or delays. 误点 means behind schedule. 失误 means error or slip.
