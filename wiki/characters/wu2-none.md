# 无 None, not have, without

- **Character**: 无
- **Pinyin**: wú
- **Meaning**: none, not have, without
- **Radical**: 无 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 无为 (wú wéi) — non-action (Taoist concept)
- 无常 (wú cháng) — impermanence

## Example Sentences

- 老子提倡无为而治。(Lǎo zǐ tí chàng wú wéi ér zhì.) — Laozi advocated governing through non-action.

## Notes

Central to Taoist philosophy of wu wei (non-action)
