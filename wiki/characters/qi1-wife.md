# 妻 Wife

- **Character**: 妻
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: wife
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 妻子 (qī zi) — wife
- 夫妻 (fū qī) — husband and wife

## Example Sentences

- 他和妻子很恩爱。(Tā hé qī zi hěn ēn ài.) — He and his wife are very loving.

## Notes

妻 is a formal word for wife. 妻子 is the standard term. 老婆 is colloquial.
