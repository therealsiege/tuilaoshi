# 坝 Dam

- **Character**: 坝
- **Pinyin**: bà
- **Meaning**: dam, embankment
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 大坝 (dà bà) — large dam
- 堤坝 (dī bà) — dike

## Example Sentences

- 三峡大坝很壮观。(Sān xiá dà bà hěn zhuàng guān.) — The Three Gorges Dam is magnificent.

## Notes

坝 simplified from 壩. Earth 土 barrier, water control structure.
