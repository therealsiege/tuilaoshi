# 半 Half

- **Character**: 半
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: half, semi-
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 一半 (yī bàn) — one half
- 半天 (bàn tiān) — half a day, a long time

## Example Sentences

- 现在三点半。(Xiàn zài sān diǎn bàn.) — It's 3:30 now.

## Notes

半 shows a division of something into two parts. 半天 colloquially means 'a long time.'
