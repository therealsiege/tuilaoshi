# 渺 Tiny

- **Character**: 渺
- **Pinyin**: miǎo
- **Meaning**: tiny, remote, vague
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 渺小 (miǎo xiǎo) — tiny, insignificant
- 渺茫 (miǎo máng) — dim, vague

## Example Sentences

- 人类在宇宙中显得很渺小。(Rénlèi zài yǔzhòu zhōng xiǎnde hěn miǎoxiǎo.) — Humans seem very tiny in the universe.

## Notes

渺 combines water 氵 with 眇 (tiny), suggesting something as small and distant as water on the horizon.
