# 祸 Disaster

- **Character**: 祸
- **Pinyin**: huò
- **Meaning**: disaster, misfortune
- **Radical**: 礻(spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 祸害 (huò hài) — disaster, bane
- 灾祸 (zāi huò) — calamity

## Example Sentences

- 这会带来灾祸。(Zhè huì dài lái zāi huò.) — This will bring calamity.

## Notes

祸 shows spirit/altar radical 礻 with 咼 (crooked), representing divine displeasure bringing misfortune.
