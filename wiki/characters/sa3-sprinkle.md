# 洒 To Sprinkle

- **Character**: 洒
- **Pinyin**: sǎ
- **Meaning**: to sprinkle, to spray
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 洒水 (sǎ shuǐ) — to sprinkle water
- 潇洒 (xiāo sǎ) — elegant and free

## Example Sentences

- 园丁在花园里洒水。(Yuán dīng zài huā yuán lǐ sǎ shuǐ.) — The gardener sprinkles water in the garden.

## Notes

洒 simplified from 灑. Water 氵 being scattered.
