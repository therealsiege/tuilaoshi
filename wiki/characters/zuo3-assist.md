# 佐 Assist, assistant, left

- **Character**: 佐
- **Pinyin**: zuǒ
- **Meaning**: assist, assistant, left
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 辅佐 (fǔ zuǒ) — assist, support
- 佐证 (zuǒ zhèng) — supporting evidence

## Example Sentences

- 他辅佐君王治理国家。(Tā fǔ zuǒ jūn wáng zhì lǐ guó jiā.) — He assists the king in governing the country.

## Notes

Historical term for ministerial assistance
