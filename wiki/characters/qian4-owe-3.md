# 欠 Owe, lack

- **Character**: 欠
- **Pinyin**: qiàn
- **Meaning**: owe, lack
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 欠款 (qiàn kuǎn) — debt, money owed
- 拖欠 (tuō qiàn) — default on payment

## Example Sentences

- 他还欠我一千块钱。(Tā hái qiàn wǒ yī qiān kuài qián.) — He still owes me a thousand yuan.

## Notes

欠条 is IOU. 欠债 means owe a debt.
