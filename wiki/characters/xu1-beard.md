# 须 Beard

- **Character**: 须
- **Pinyin**: xū
- **Meaning**: beard, must,须根
- **Radical**: 彡 (hair)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胡须 (hú xū) — beard
- 须根 (xū gēn) — fibrous roots

## Example Sentences

- 植物的须根吸收水分。(Zhí wù de xū gēn xī shōu shuǐ fèn.) — The plant's fibrous roots absorb water.

## Notes

须 simplified from 鬚. Whiskers 彡 with 页 (head), fine root hairs.
