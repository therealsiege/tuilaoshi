# 箭 Arrow

- **Character**: 箭
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: arrow, rocket
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 火箭 (huǒ jiàn) — rocket
- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow

## Example Sentences

- 火箭发射成功。(Huǒ jiàn fā shè chéng gōng.) — The rocket launched successfully.

## Notes

箭 has bamboo 竹 with 前 (forward), bamboo shaft flying forward.
