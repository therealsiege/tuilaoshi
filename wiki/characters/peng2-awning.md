# 篷 Awning, canopy, sail

- **Character**: 篷
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: awning, canopy, sail
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 帐篷 (zhàng peng) — tent
- 篷布 (péng bù) — tarpaulin, canvas

## Example Sentences

- 雨篷可以遮挡阳光和雨水。(Yǔ péng kě yǐ zhē dǎng yáng guāng hé yǔ shuǐ.) — The awning can block sunlight and rain.

## Notes

Used for various coverings: awnings, canopies, sails. 敞篷车 means convertible car.
