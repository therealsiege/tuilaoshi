# 箭 Arrow

- **Character**: 箭
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: arrow
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow
- 射箭 (shè jiàn) — archery

## Example Sentences

- 她的箭术很高超。(Tā de jiànshù hěn gāochāo.) — Her archery skills are excellent.

## Notes

Arrow for archery; projectile weapon
