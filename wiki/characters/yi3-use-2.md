# 以 To Use

- **Character**: 以
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: to use, by means of, in order to
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 以后 (yǐ hòu) — after, later
- 可以 (kě yǐ) — can, may

## Example Sentences

- 以我的经验来看，这是对的。(Yǐ wǒ de jīng yàn lái kàn, zhè shì duì de.) — In my experience, this is correct.

## Notes

以 shows a person with a tool, representing using something or taking an action by means of something.
