# 废 Abolish, waste, useless

- **Character**: 废
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: abolish, waste, useless
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 废除 (fèichú) — abolish, repeal
- 废话 (fèihuà) — nonsense, waste words

## Example Sentences

- 这个法律已被废除。(Zhège fǎlǜ yǐ bèi fèichú.) — This law has been abolished.

## Notes

Means waste material, abolishing, or uselessness.
