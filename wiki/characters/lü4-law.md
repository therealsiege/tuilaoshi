# 律 Law

- **Character**: 律
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: law, rule, discipline
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 法律 (fǎ lǜ) — law
- 纪律 (jì lǜ) — discipline

## Example Sentences

- 我们必须遵守法律。(Wǒ men bì xū zūn shǒu fǎ lǜ.) — We must obey the law.

## Notes

律 originally related to musical pitch/rhythm, extended to rules and laws that regulate behavior.
