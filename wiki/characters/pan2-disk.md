# 盘 Disk, plate

- **Character**: 盘
- **Pinyin**: pán
- **Meaning**: disk, plate
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 硬盘 (yìng pán) — hard disk
- 光盘 (guāng pán) — CD, optical disc

## Example Sentences

- 硬盘已经满了。(Yìng pán yǐ jīng mǎn le.) — The hard disk is full.

## Notes

Used in 硬盘 (hard disk), 光盘 (CD), 磁盘 (disk)
