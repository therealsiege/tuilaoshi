# 刑 Punishment, sentence, torture

- **Character**: 刑
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: punishment, sentence, torture
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刑罚 (xíngfá) — penalty, punishment
- 判刑 (pànxíng) — sentence (punishment)

## Example Sentences

- 罪犯被判刑五年。(Zuìfàn bèi pànxíng wǔ nián.) — The criminal was sentenced to five years.

## Notes

Legal term for criminal punishment and sentencing.
