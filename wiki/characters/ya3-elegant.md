# 雅 Elegant

- **Character**: 雅
- **Pinyin**: yǎ
- **Meaning**: elegant, refined
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 优雅 (yōu yǎ) — elegant, graceful
- 文雅 (wén yǎ) — refined, cultured

## Example Sentences

- 她举止很优雅。(Tā jǔ zhǐ hěn yōu yǎ.) — Her manners are very elegant.

## Notes

雅 shows bird 隹 with 牙 (tooth), representing refined or standard (as in standard speech).
