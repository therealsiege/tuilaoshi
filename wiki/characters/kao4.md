# 靠 To Lean

- **Character**: 靠
- **Pinyin**: kào
- **Meaning**: to lean on, to depend on, near
- **Radical**: 非 (wrong)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 依靠 (yī kào) — to rely on
- 靠近 (kào jìn) — near, to approach

## Example Sentences

- 你可以靠我。(Nǐ kě yǐ kào wǒ.) — You can rely on me.

## Notes

靠 shows告 (to tell) with非 (not), representing leaning on something reliable when things go wrong, hence to depend on.
