# 蜗 Snail

- **Character**: 蜗
- **Pinyin**: wō
- **Meaning**: snail
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蜗牛 (wō niú) — snail
- 蜗居 (wō jū) — cramped dwelling

## Example Sentences

- 蜗牛爬得很慢。(Wōniú pá de hěn màn.) — Snails crawl very slowly.

## Notes

蜗牛 means snail. 蜗居 means small cramped living space. Insect radical.
