# 诉 Tell, complain, sue

- **Character**: 诉
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: tell, complain, sue
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诉说 (sùshuō) — tell, recount
- 控诉 (kòngsù) — accuse, denounce

## Example Sentences

- 他向法院起诉。(Tā xiàng fǎyuàn qǐsù.) — He filed a lawsuit.

## Notes

Common for telling, complaining, or legal action.
