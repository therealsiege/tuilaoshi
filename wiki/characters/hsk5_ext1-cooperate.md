# 协 Cooperate, 协助 Assist

- **Character**: 协
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: cooperate,协助 assist
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 协助 (xié zhù) — assist, help
- 协调 (xié tiáo) — coordinate
- 协议 (xié yì) — agreement, protocol

## Example Sentences

- 各部门要协调工作。(Gè bùmén yào xiétiáo gōngzuò.) — All departments must coordinate their work.

## Notes

Common in professional and organizational contexts for cooperation and coordination. 协议 (agreement) is standard term for formal agreements.
