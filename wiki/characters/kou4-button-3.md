# 扣 To Button

- **Character**: 扣
- **Pinyin**: kòu
- **Meaning**: to button, to buckle, to deduct
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 纽扣 (niǔ kòu) — button
- 扣除 (kòu chú) — to deduct

## Example Sentences

- 他扣上了外套的纽扣。(Tā kòu shàng le wài tào de niǔ kòu.) — He buttoned up his coat.

## Notes

扣 has hand 扌 with 口 (mouth/opening), fastening together.
