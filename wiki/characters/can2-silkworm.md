# 蚕 Silkworm

- **Character**: 蚕
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: silkworm
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 蚕丝 (cán sī) — silk
- 养蚕 (yǎng cán) — to raise silkworms

## Example Sentences

- 中国养蚕有几千年的历史。(Zhōngguó yǎng cán yǒu jǐ qiān nián de lìshǐ.) — China has thousands of years of history raising silkworms.

## Notes

Critical to silk production. Insect radical indicates larva type.
