# 惘 Disappointed, at a loss

- **Character**: 惘
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: disappointed, at a loss
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惘然 (wǎngrán) — frustrated, disappointed
- 怅惘 (chàngwǎng) — distracted and disappointed

## Example Sentences

- 他惘然若失。(Tā wǎngrán ruò shī.) — He felt lost and disappointed.

## Notes

Literary term for feeling lost or disappointed.
