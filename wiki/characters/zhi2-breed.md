# 殖 Breed, reproduce, colonize

- **Character**: 殖
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: breed, reproduce, colonize
- **Radical**: 歹 (death)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 殖民 (zhí mín) — to colonize
- 繁殖 (fán zhí) — to breed, reproduce

## Example Sentences

- 这种动物繁殖能力很强。(Zhè zhǒng dòng wù fán zhí néng lì hěn qiáng.) — This animal has strong reproductive ability.

## Notes

Means to breed or multiply. 殖民地 means colony. Used in biology and history.
