# 拷 Torture, beat, question

- **Character**: 拷
- **Pinyin**: kǎo
- **Meaning**: torture, beat, question
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拷问 (kǎowèn) — torture for confession
- 拷打 (kǎodǎ) — torture, beat

## Example Sentences

- 古代官府拷打犯人。(Gǔdài guānfǔ kǎodǎ fànrén.) — Ancient authorities tortured criminals.

## Notes

Formal term for torture or harsh interrogation.
