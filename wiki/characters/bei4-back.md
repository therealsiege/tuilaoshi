# 背 Back

- **Character**: 背
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: back (of body), to carry on back, to recite
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 背后 (bèi hòu) — behind, at the back
- 背包 (bēi bāo) — backpack

## Example Sentences

- 我要背这些单词。(Wǒ yào bèi zhè xiē dān cí.) — I need to memorize these words.

## Notes

背 has two pronunciations: bèi (back, to recite/memorize) and bēi (to carry on back). The two meanings connect to the idea of the back.
