# 灼 Burn

- **Character**: 灼
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: burn, scorch
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灼伤 (zhuó shāng) — burn injury
- 灼热 (zhuó rè) — burning hot
- 灼痛 (zhuó tòng) — burning pain

## Example Sentences

- 化学品灼伤需要立即冲洗。(Huà xué pǐn zhuó shāng xū yào lì jí chōng xǐ.) — Chemical burns need immediate rinsing.

## Notes

Thermal or chemical injury; requires emergency treatment.
