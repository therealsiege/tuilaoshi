# 斧 Axe

- **Character**: 斧
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: axe, hatchet
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 斧头 (fǔ tou) — axe
- 斧子 (fǔ zi) — hatchet

## Example Sentences

- 他用斧头砍柴。(Tā yòng fǔ tou kǎn chái.) — He chops firewood with an axe.

## Notes

斧 combines 父 (father) and 斤 (axe/catty), the tool of labor.
