# 敬 To Respect

- **Character**: 敬
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: to respect, to salute
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 尊敬 (zūn jìng) — to revere
- 敬礼 (jìng lǐ) — to salute

## Example Sentences

- 他很敬业。(Tā hěn jìng yè.) — He is very dedicated to his work.

## Notes

敬 shows the hand radical 攵 acting on 苟 (carefully), representing careful respectful action.
