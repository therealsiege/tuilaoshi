# 列 Row

- **Character**: 列
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: row, column, to list
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 列表 (liè biǎo) — list
- 系列 (xì liè) — series

## Example Sentences

- 请查看列表。(Qǐng chá kàn liè biǎo.) — Please check the list.

## Notes

列 with knife radical suggests cutting into rows, perfect for lists and columns.
