# 曲 Song

- **Character**: 曲
- **Pinyin**: qǔ
- **Meaning**: song, tune
- **Radical**: 曰 (say)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 歌曲 (gē qǔ) — song
- 乐曲 (yuè qǔ) — musical composition

## Example Sentences

- 这首曲子很动听。(Zhè shǒu qǔ zi hěn dòng tīng.) — This tune is very pleasant.

## Notes

曲 (qǔ) means song or tune; also read as qū meaning curved.
