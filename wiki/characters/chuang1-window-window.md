# 窗 Window

- **Character**: 窗
- **Pinyin**: chuāng
- **Meaning**: window
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 窗户 (chuāng hù) — window
- 窗帘 (chuāng lián) — curtain

## Example Sentences

- 请把窗户打开。(Qǐng bǎ chuāng hù dǎ kāi.) — Please open the window.

## Notes

窗户 is the colloquial form; 窗 alone is more literary.
