# 蛾 Moth

- **Character**: 蛾
- **Pinyin**: é
- **Meaning**: moth
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 飞蛾 (fēi é) — moth
- 蚕蛾 (cán é) — silkworm moth

## Example Sentences

- 飞蛾扑火比喻自取灭亡。(Fēi'é pūhuǒ bǐyù zìqǔ mièwáng.) — A moth flying into a flame metaphorically means self-destruction.

## Notes

Used in idiom 飞蛾扑火. Insect radical indicates insect type.
