# 涌 Surge, gush, pour

- **Character**: 涌
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: surge, gush, pour
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涌出 (yǒng chū) — gush out, pour out
- 泉涌 (quán yǒng) — spring forth

## Example Sentences

- 泉水从地下涌出。(Quán shuǐ cóng dì xià yǒng chū.) — Spring water gushes out from underground.

## Notes

Water flowing forcefully
