# 真 True

- **Character**: 真
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: true, real, really
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 真的 (zhēn de) — really, truly
- 认真 (rèn zhēn) — serious, earnest

## Example Sentences

- 真的吗？(Zhēn de ma?) — Really?

## Notes

真 contrasts with 假 (jiǎ, false/fake). 真的 is a very common colloquial intensifier.
