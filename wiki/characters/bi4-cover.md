# 蔽 To cover, to shield, to screen

- **Character**: 蔽
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to cover, to shield, to screen
- **Radical**: 艹(grass)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 隐蔽 (yǐn bì) — to conceal, hidden
- 遮蔽 (zhē bì) — to cover, to shade

## Example Sentences

- 士兵隐蔽在树林中。(Shìbīng yǐnbì zài shùlín zhōng.) — The soldiers concealed themselves in the woods.

## Notes

Often used for concealment or protection.
