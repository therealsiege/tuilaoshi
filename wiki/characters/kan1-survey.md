# 勘 Survey, investigate

- **Character**: 勘
- **Pinyin**: kān
- **Meaning**: survey, investigate
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 勘察 (kānchá) — survey, prospect
- 勘探 (kāntàn) — explore, prospect

## Example Sentences

- 地质队勘探矿藏。(Dìzhìduì kāntàn kuàngcáng.) — The geological team prospects for minerals.

## Notes

Technical term for surveying or investigating, often in geological contexts.
