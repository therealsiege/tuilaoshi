# 辰 Celestial

- **Character**: 辰
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: celestial bodies, time, occasion
- **Radical**: 辰 (morning)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 星辰 (xīng chén) — stars
- 时辰 (shí chen) — time

## Example Sentences

- 满天星辰闪烁。(Mǎn tiān xīng chén shǎn shuò.) — The sky is full of twinkling stars.

## Notes

辰 depicts agricultural tools, also the 5th earthly branch.
