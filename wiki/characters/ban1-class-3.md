# 班 Class

- **Character**: 班
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: class, team, shift, squad
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 上班 (shàng bān) — to go to work
- 班级 (bān jí) — class (in school)

## Example Sentences

- 我每天八点上班。(Wǒ měi tiān bā diǎn shàng bān.) — I go to work at 8 o'clock every day.

## Notes

班 originally meant to divide jade; extended to dividing people into groups or shifts.
