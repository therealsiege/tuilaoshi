# 筑 Build

- **Character**: 筑
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: build, ancient stringed instrument
- **Radical**: 竹(bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 建筑 (jiàn zhù) — building, architecture
- 筑路 (zhù lù) — build roads

## Example Sentences

- 工人们正在筑路。(Gōngrénmen zhèngzài zhù lù.) — Workers are building the road.

## Notes

筑 means to build or construct. Also refers to an ancient stringed instrument similar to a zither.
