# 广 Wide, extensive

- **Character**: 广
- **Pinyin**: guǎng
- **Meaning**: wide, extensive
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 广告 (guǎng gào) — advertisement
- 广泛 (guǎng fàn) — extensive, widespread

## Example Sentences

- 这个广告很有创意。(Zhè ge guǎng gào hěn yǒu chuàng yì.) — This advertisement is very creative.

## Notes

广播 means broadcast. 广场 means plaza/square.
