# 拍 Hit

- **Character**: 拍
- **Pinyin**: pāi
- **Meaning**: to hit, clap, racket
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 球拍 (qiú pāi) — racket
- 拍打 (pāi dǎ) — to pat, slap

## Example Sentences

- 他买了一个新网球拍。(Tā mǎi le yī gè xīn wǎngqiú pāi.) — He bought a new tennis racket.

## Notes

Hitting and rackets; used for racquet sports
