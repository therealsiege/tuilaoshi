# 蕊 Stamen

- **Character**: 蕊
- **Pinyin**: ruǐ
- **Meaning**: stamen, pistil
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 花蕊 (huā ruǐ) — stamen
- 雄蕊 (xióng ruǐ) — male stamen

## Example Sentences

- 蜜蜂在花蕊上采蜜。(Mì fēng zài huā ruǐ shàng cǎi mì.) — The bee collects honey from the stamen.

## Notes

蕊 has grass 艹 over 心 (heart) three times, center of flower.
