# 周 Week

- **Character**: 周
- **Pinyin**: zhōu
- **Meaning**: week, cycle, circumference
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 星期 (xīng qī) — week
- 周末 (zhōu mò) — weekend

## Example Sentences

- 这周我很忙。(Zhè zhōu wǒ hěn máng.) — I'm very busy this week.

## Notes

周 means week or cycle. 周围 = surroundings. 周年 = anniversary.
