# 苗 Vaccine

- **Character**: 苗
- **Pinyin**: miáo
- **Meaning**: seedling; vaccine
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 疫苗 (yì miáo) — vaccine
- 接种疫苗 (jiē zhǒng yì miáo) — to vaccinate
- 幼苗 (yòu miáo) — seedling

## Example Sentences

- 儿童应该按时接种疫苗。(Ér tóng yīng gāi àn shí jiē zhǒng yì miáo.) — Children should be vaccinated on schedule.

## Notes

Vaccine preparation; preventive medicine.
