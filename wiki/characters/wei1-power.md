# 威 Might

- **Character**: 威
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: power, might, prestige
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 狐假虎威 (hú jiǎ hǔ wēi) — bully using another's power
- 威胁 (wēi xié) — to threaten

## Example Sentences

- 老虎的威风令人害怕。(Lǎohǔ de wēifēng lìng rén hàipà.) — The tiger's might is frightening.

## Notes

Used in 狐假虎威: the tiger's might or authority that the fox borrows.
