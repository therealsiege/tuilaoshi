# 施 Implement, Bestow, 实施 Implement

- **Character**: 施
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: implement, bestow, 实施 implement
- **Radical**: 方 (direction)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 实施 (shí shī) — implement, carry out
- 施工 (shī gōng) — construct
- 措施 (cuò shī) — measure, step

## Example Sentences

- 新政策将于下月实施。(Xīn zhèngcè jiāng yú xià yuè shíshī.) — The new policy will be implemented next month.

## Notes

Common in official and formal contexts for implementing policies or carrying out actions. 实施 is standard term for policy implementation.
