# 赐 Bestow, grant (from superior)

- **Character**: 赐
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: bestow, grant (from superior)
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赏赐 (shǎng cì) — grant a reward, bestow
- 恩赐 (ēn cì) — favor, grace

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝赐给功臣大量财宝。(Huángdì cìgěi gōngchén dàliàng cáibǎo.) — The emperor bestowed abundant treasures upon his meritorious officials.

## Notes

Means to grant or bestow from a superior to inferior. Emphasizes the hierarchical nature of the gift.
