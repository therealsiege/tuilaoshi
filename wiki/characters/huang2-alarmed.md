# 惶 Alarmed

- **Character**: 惶
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: alarmed, anxious, fearful
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惶恐 (huáng kǒng) — terrified
- 惊惶 (jīng huáng) — alarmed

## Example Sentences

- 他感到惶恐不安。(Tā gǎn dào huáng kǒng bù ān.) — He felt terrified and uneasy.

## Notes

惶 has heart 忄 with 皇 (emperor), heart in a flutter before authority.
