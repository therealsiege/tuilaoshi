# 亭 Pavilion, kiosk

- **Character**: 亭
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: pavilion, kiosk
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 凉亭 (liáng tíng) — pavilion, gazebo
- 亭子 (tíng zi) — pavilion

## Example Sentences

- 公园里有一座古色古香的凉亭。(Gōng yuán lǐ yǒu yī zuò gǔ sè gǔ xiāng de liáng tíng.) — There is an antique-style pavilion in the park.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese garden structure for rest and viewing.
