# 约 Treaty, agreement, 约

- **Character**: 约
- **Pinyin**: yuē
- **Meaning**: treaty, agreement,约
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 条约 (tiáo yuē) — treaty
- 契约 (qì yuē) — contract, agreement

## Example Sentences

- 双方签署了贸易条约。(Shuāng fāng qiān shǔ le mào yì tiáo yuē.) — Both sides signed a trade treaty.

## Notes

Essential character in legal documents. Also means to约 (make appointment). 违约 means to break a contract.
