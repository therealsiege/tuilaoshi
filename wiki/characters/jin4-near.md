# 近 Near

- **Character**: 近
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: near, close
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 最近 (zuì jìn) — recently, lately
- 附近 (fù jìn) — nearby, vicinity

## Example Sentences

- 最近怎么样？(Zuì jìn zěn me yàng?) — How have you been lately?

## Notes

近 with walking radical - close enough to walk. Opposite of 远 (far).
