# 还 Still

- **Character**: 还
- **Pinyin**: hái
- **Meaning**: still, yet, also; to return (huán)
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 还是 (hái shì) — or; still
- 还有 (hái yǒu) — also, in addition

## Example Sentences

- 你还要别的吗？(Nǐ hái yào bié de ma?) — Do you want anything else?

## Notes

还 as hái means 'still/yet/also.' As huán it means 'to return/give back.' 还是 in questions means 'or.'
