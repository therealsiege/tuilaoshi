# 显 Display, show

- **Character**: 显
- **Pinyin**: xiǎn
- **Meaning**: display, show
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 显示 (xiǎn shì) — display
- 显卡 (xiǎn kǎ) — graphics card

## Example Sentences

- 屏幕上显示错误信息。(Píng mù shàng xiǎn shì cuò wù xìn xī.) — Error message displays on the screen.

## Notes

Used in 显示 (display), 显卡 (graphics card), 显示器 (monitor)
