# 标 Mark, sign, standard

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, standard
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 坐标 (zuò biāo) — coordinate
- 目标 (mù biāo) — target, goal

## Example Sentences

- 在坐标系中标出这个点。(Zài zuò biāo xì zhōng biāo chū zhè ge diǎn.) — Mark this point on the coordinate system.
