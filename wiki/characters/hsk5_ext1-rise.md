# 涨 Rise, Go Up, 上涨 Rise

- **Character**: 涨
- **Pinyin**: zhǎng
- **Meaning**: rise, go up, 上涨 rise
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 上涨 (shàng zhǎng) — rise, go up
- 涨价 (zhǎng jià) — price increase
- 高涨 (gāo zhǎng) — run high, upsurge

## Example Sentences

- 房价一直在上涨。(Fángjià yīzhí zài shàngzhǎng.) — Housing prices keep rising.

## Notes

Also pronounced zhàng when meaning swell/bloat. As zhǎng, essential economic term for price/level increases.
