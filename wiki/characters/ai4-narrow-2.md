# 隘 Narrow pass, defile

- **Character**: 隘
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: narrow pass, defile
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 关隘 (guān ài) — strategic pass
- 险隘 (xiǎn ài) — dangerous pass

## Example Sentences

- 这个关隘易守难攻。(Zhè gè guān ài yì shǒu nán gōng.) — This strategic pass is easy to defend and hard to attack.

## Notes

Strategically important geographic features in Chinese history.
