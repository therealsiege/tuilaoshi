# 倾 To Lean

- **Character**: 倾
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: to lean, to incline, to collapse
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倾向 (qīng xiàng) — tendency, inclination
- 倾听 (qīng tīng) — to listen attentively

## Example Sentences

- 我倾向于支持这个计划。(Wǒ qīng xiàng yú zhī chí zhè ge jì huà.) — I tend to support this plan.

## Notes

倾 combines person 亻and phonetic component, suggesting leaning or inclining. Used metaphorically for tendency or devotion.
