# 岭 Mountain ridge, range

- **Character**: 岭
- **Pinyin**: lǐng
- **Meaning**: mountain ridge, range
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 山岭 (shān lǐng) — mountain ridge
- 岭南 (Lǐng nán) — south of the Five Ridges (Guangdong)

## Example Sentences

- 翻过这座山岭就到了目的地。(Fān guò zhè zuò shān lǐng jiù dào le mù dì dì.) — Once you cross this mountain ridge, you'll reach the destination.

## Notes

Common in place names like 五岭 (Five Ridges).
