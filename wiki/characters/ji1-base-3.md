# 基 Base, foundation, fund

- **Character**: 基
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: base, foundation, fund
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 基金 (jī jīn) — fund, foundation
- 基础 (jī chǔ) — foundation, base

## Example Sentences

- 他投资了几个基金。(Tā tóu zī le jǐ ge jī jīn.) — He invested in several funds.

## Notes

Means foundation or base. 基本 means basic. 基层 means grassroots level. 基因 means gene.
