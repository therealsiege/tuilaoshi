# 赋 Poetic Prose

- **Character**: 赋
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: poetic prose, to bestow
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赋予 (fù yǔ) — to bestow, to endow
- 辞赋 (cí fù) — rhapsody

## Example Sentences

- 汉代的辞赋文学很发达。(Hàn dài de cí fù wén xué hěn fā dá.) — Rhapsodic literature flourished in the Han dynasty.

## Notes

赋 refers to a genre of poetic prose (fu); also means to bestow.
