# 落 To fall, to drop, to lag behind

- **Character**: 落
- **Pinyin**: luò
- **Meaning**: to fall, to drop, to lag behind
- **Radical**: 艹(grass)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 落下 (luò xià) — to fall, to drop
- 降落 (jiàng luò) — to descend, to land

## Example Sentences

- 树叶落在地上。(Shùyè luò zài dì shang.) — The leaves fall on the ground.

## Notes

Common verb for falling; also pronounced lào/là in some contexts.
