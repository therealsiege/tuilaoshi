# 该 Should

- **Character**: 该
- **Pinyin**: gāi
- **Meaning**: should, ought to
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 应该 (yīng gāi) — should
- 活该 (huó gāi) — serves you right

## Example Sentences

- 你该走了。(Nǐ gāi zǒu le.) — You should go now.

## Notes

该 means 'should/ought to' — similar to 应该 but slightly less formal. 该 alone can replace 应该.
