# 议 To Discuss

- **Character**: 议
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to discuss,议论, proposal
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 会议 (huì yì) — meeting, conference
- 建议 (jiàn yì) — suggestion

## Example Sentences

- 我们开会议讨论这个问题。(Wǒ men kāi huì yì tǎo lùn zhè ge wèn tí.) — We held a meeting to discuss this issue.

## Notes

议 is simplified from 議. Uses the speech radical for discussion and deliberation.
