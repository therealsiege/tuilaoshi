# 检 Inspect, examine, check

- **Character**: 检
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: inspect, examine, check
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎnchá) — inspect, check
- 检察 (jiǎnchá) — procuratorate

## Example Sentences

- 检察院负责公诉。(Jiǎncháyuàn fùzé gōngsù.) — The procuratorate is responsible for public prosecution.

## Notes

Common for inspection and examination; used in legal contexts.
