# 舶 Ocean-going vessel

- **Character**: 舶
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: ocean-going vessel
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 船舶 (chuán bó) — ships, vessels
- 舶来品 (bó lái pǐn) — imported goods

## Example Sentences

- 港口停泊着许多船舶。(Gǎng kǒu tíng bó zhe xǔ duō chuán bó.) — Many vessels are moored in the harbor.

## Notes

Specifically refers to large sea-going ships.
