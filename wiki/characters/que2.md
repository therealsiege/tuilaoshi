# 瘸 Lame

- **Character**: 瘸
- **Pinyin**: qué
- **Meaning**: lame, crippled
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 瘸子 (qué zi) — lame person
- 瘸腿 (qué tuǐ) — lame leg

## Example Sentences

- 他的腿瘸了。(Tā de tuǐ qué le.) — His leg is lame.

## Notes

瘸 combines sickness 疒 with 辟 (open), representing a disability affecting walking.
