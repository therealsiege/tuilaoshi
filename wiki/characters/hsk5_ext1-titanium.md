# 钛 Titanium, 钛合金 Titanium Alloy

- **Character**: 钛
- **Pinyin**: tài
- **Meaning**: titanium, 钛合金 titanium alloy
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钛合金 (tài hé jīn) — titanium alloy
- 钛金 (tài jīn) — titanium
- 钛钢 (tài gāng) — titanium steel

## Example Sentences

- 钛合金强度高、重量轻。(Tài héjīn qiángdù gāo, zhòngliàng qīng.) — Titanium alloy has high strength and light weight.

## Notes

Modern high-tech metal. 钛合金 used in aerospace, medical implants, and high-end consumer products.
