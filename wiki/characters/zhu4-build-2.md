# 筑 Build, construct

- **Character**: 筑
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: build, construct
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 建筑 (jiàn zhù) — building, architecture
- 构筑 (gòu zhù) — build, construct

## Example Sentences

- 建筑公司承包了这个项目。(Jiàn zhù gōng sī chéng bāo le zhè ge xiàng mù.) — The construction company contracted this project.

## Notes

建筑师 is architect. 建筑业 is construction industry.
