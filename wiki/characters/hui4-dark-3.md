# 晦 Obscure

- **Character**: 晦
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: dark, obscure
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 晦涩 (huì sè) — obscure
- 隐晦 (yǐn huì) — veiled

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章晦涩难懂。(Zhè piān wén zhāng huì sè nán dǒng.) — This article is obscure and hard to understand.

## Notes

晦 has sun 日 with 每 (each), sun hidden each day, hence obscure.
