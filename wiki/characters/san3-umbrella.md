# 伞 Umbrella

- **Character**: 伞
- **Pinyin**: sǎn
- **Meaning**: umbrella, parasol
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 雨伞 (yǔ sǎn) — umbrella
- 阳伞 (yáng sǎn) — parasol

## Example Sentences

- 下雨了，带伞吧。(Xià yǔ le, dài sǎn ba.) — It's raining, take an umbrella.

## Notes

伞 simplified from 傘, pictograph showing umbrella canopy. 雨伞 = rain umbrella.
