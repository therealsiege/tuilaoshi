# 准 Accurate

- **Character**: 准
- **Pinyin**: zhǔn
- **Meaning**: accurate, standard
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 准确 (zhǔn què) — accurate, precise
- 瞄准 (miáo zhǔn) — to aim at

## Example Sentences

- 他射击很准。(Tā shèjī hěn zhǔn.) — His shooting is very accurate.

## Notes

Accuracy and precision; critical for shooting and targeting sports
