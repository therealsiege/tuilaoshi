# 释 Release, explain, Buddhism

- **Character**: 释
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: release, explain, Buddhism
- **Radical**: 釆 (distinguish)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 释放 (shì fàng) — to release, set free
- 解释 (jiě shì) — to explain

## Example Sentences

- 囚犯被提前释放了。(Qiú fàn bèi tí qián shì fàng le.) — The prisoner was released early.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: release, explain, or Buddhist. 释怀 means to feel relieved. 诠释 means to interpret.
