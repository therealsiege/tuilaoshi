# 标 Mark, sign, bid, standard

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, bid, standard
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 标准 (biāozhǔn) — standard, criterion
- 投标 (tóubiāo) — submit a bid

## Example Sentences

- 这是行业标准。(Zhè shì hángyè biāozhǔn.) — This is the industry standard.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: mark, standard, bid.
