# 危 Rooftop

- **Character**: 危
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: Rooftop constellation, danger
- **Radical**: ⺁(seal)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 危宿 (wēi xiù) — Rooftop constellation
- 危险 (wēi xiǎn) — danger

## Example Sentences

- 危宿象征着屋顶的危险位置。(Wēi xiù xiàngzhēng zhe wūdǐng de wēixiǎn wèizhì.) — Rooftop constellation symbolizes the dangerous position of a rooftop.

## Notes

危 means danger or precarious. In astronomy, the Rooftop constellation, suggesting the danger of being on a roof.
