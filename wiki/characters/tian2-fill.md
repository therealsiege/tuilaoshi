# 填 To Fill

- **Character**: 填
- **Pinyin**: tián
- **Meaning**: to fill, to stuff, to fill in (a form)
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 填写 (tián xiě) — to fill in (a form)
- 填空 (tián kòng) — fill in the blank

## Example Sentences

- 请填写这个表格。(Qǐng tián xiě zhè ge biǎo gé.) — Please fill in this form.

## Notes

填 means to fill in or stuff. Very common for填写 (filling in forms) and填空 (fill-in-the-blank exercises).
