# 抵 Resist, mortgage

- **Character**: 抵
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: resist, mortgage
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抵押 (dǐ yā) — mortgage
- 抵抗 (dǐ kàng) — resist

## Example Sentences

- 房子可以抵押贷款。(Fáng zi kě yǐ dǐ yā dài kuǎn.) — Houses can be mortgaged for loans.

## Notes

抵押品 is collateral. 抵押权 is mortgage right.
