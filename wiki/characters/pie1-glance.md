# 瞥 To glance, to glimpse

- **Character**: 瞥
- **Pinyin**: piē
- **Meaning**: to glance, to glimpse
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 瞥见 (piē jiàn) — to catch sight of, to glimpse
- 一瞥 (yī piē) — a glance

## Example Sentences

- 我瞥见他走过街角。(Wǒ piējiàn tā zǒuguò jiējiǎo.) — I caught a glimpse of him walking past the street corner.

## Notes

Describes quick, brief looks.
