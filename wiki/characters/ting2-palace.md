# 廷 Palace courtyard, royal court

- **Character**: 廷
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: palace courtyard, royal court
- **Radical**: 廴 (move)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 朝廷 (cháo tíng) — imperial court
- 宫廷 (gōng tíng) — imperial palace, royal court

## Example Sentences

- 大臣们在朝廷上讨论国家大事。(Dàchén men zài cháotíng shàng tǎolùn guójiā dàshì.) — Ministers discussed state affairs at the imperial court.

## Notes

Refers to the courtyard of the palace where the emperor held court. By extension, the royal government.
