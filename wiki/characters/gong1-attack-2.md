# 攻 To Attack

- **Character**: 攻
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: to attack, to assault
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 攻击 (gōng jī) — to attack
- 进攻 (jìn gōng) — to attack

## Example Sentences

- 他发起了攻击。(Tā fā qǐ le gōng jī.) — He launched an attack.

## Notes

攻 has 工 (work) with 攵 (strike), working to break through defenses.
