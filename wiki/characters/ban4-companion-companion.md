# 伴 Companion

- **Character**: 伴
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: companion, partner, to accompany
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 伙伴 (huǒ bàn) — partner
- 陪伴 (péi bàn) — to accompany

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的好伙伴。(Tā shì wǒ de hǎo huǒ bàn.) — He is my good partner.

## Notes

伴 means companion or to accompany. 伴侣 = spouse/partner.
