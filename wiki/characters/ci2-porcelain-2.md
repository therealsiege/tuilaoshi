# 瓷 Porcelain

- **Character**: 瓷
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: porcelain, china
- **Radical**: 瓦(tile)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 瓷器 (cí qì) — porcelain, chinaware
- 青瓷 (qīng cí) — celadon

## Example Sentences

- 景德镇以烧制瓷器而闻名。(Jǐngdézhèn yǐ shāozhì cíqì ér wénmíng.) — Jingdezhen is famous for firing porcelain.

## Notes

瓷 specifically refers to porcelain, a high-quality ceramic. China (the country) is named after china (porcelain).
