# 短 Short

- **Character**: 短
- **Pinyin**: duǎn
- **Meaning**: short (length)
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 短信 (duǎn xìn) — text message
- 长短 (cháng duǎn) — length

## Example Sentences

- 这条裤子太短了。(Zhè tiáo kù zi tài duǎn le.) — These pants are too short.

## Notes

短 with arrow 矢 radical. Opposite of 长 (long). 短信 = SMS (short message).
