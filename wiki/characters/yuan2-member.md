# 员 Member

- **Character**: 员
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: member, personnel, staff
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 人员 (rén yuán) — personnel, staff
- 成员 (chéng yuán) — member

## Example Sentences

- 公司有五十名员工。(Gōng sī yǒu wǔ shí míng yuán gōng.) — The company has fifty employees.

## Notes

员 is simplified from 員. Often used as a suffix for occupations and roles.
