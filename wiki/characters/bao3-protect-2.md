# 保 Protect, guarantee, keep

- **Character**: 保
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: protect,保证, keep
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 保护 (bǎohù) — protect
- 取保 (qǔbǎo) — bail

## Example Sentences

- 嫌疑人取保候审。(Xiányírén qǔbǎo hòushěn.) — The suspect was released on bail pending trial.

## Notes

Common for protection and guarantees.
