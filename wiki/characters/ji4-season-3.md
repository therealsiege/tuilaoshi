# 季 Season

- **Character**: 季
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: season, quarter
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 赛季 (sài jì) — season (sports)
- 季军 (jì jūn) — third place

## Example Sentences

- 这个赛季他们表现出色。(Zhège sàijì tāmen biǎoxiàn chūsè.) — They performed excellently this season.

## Notes
