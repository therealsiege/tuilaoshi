# 津 Ferry, allowance, saliva

- **Character**: 津
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: ferry, allowance, saliva
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 津贴 (jīntiē) — allowance, subsidy
- 天津 (Tiānjīn) — Tianjin (city)

## Example Sentences

- 员工每月有交通津贴。(Yuángōng měi yuè yǒu jiāotōng jīntiē.) — Employees have a monthly transportation allowance.

## Notes

Used for allowances and subsidies; also in place names.
