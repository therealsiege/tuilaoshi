# 岸 Shore

- **Character**: 岸
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: shore, bank, coast
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 海岸 (hǎi àn) — coast
- 河岸 (hé àn) — riverbank

## Example Sentences

- 船靠岸了。(Chuán kào àn le.) — The boat docked.

## Notes

岸 means shore or bank of a river/sea.
