# 堵 To Block

- **Character**: 堵
- **Pinyin**: dǔ
- **Meaning**: to block, to堵塞, wall
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 堵塞 (dǔ sè) — to堵塞
- 堵车 (dǔ chē) — traffic jam

## Example Sentences

- 路上堵车了。(Lù shàng dǔ chē le.) — There's a traffic jam on the road.

## Notes

堵 uses the earth radical, suggesting blocking with solid matter like walls.
