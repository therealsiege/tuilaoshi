# 铸 Cast metal, forge

- **Character**: 铸
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: cast metal, forge
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铸造 (zhù zào) — cast, found
- 铸铁 (zhù tiě) — cast iron

## Example Sentences

- 这口大钟是明朝铸造的。(Zhè kǒu dà zhōng shì Míng cháo zhù zào de.) — This large bell was cast during the Ming Dynasty.

## Notes

Specifically for casting metal objects.
