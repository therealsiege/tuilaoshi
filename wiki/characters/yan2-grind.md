# 研 To grind, to research, to study

- **Character**: 研
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: to grind, to研究, to研磨
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 研究 (yán jiū) — to research, to study
- 钻研 (zuān yán) — to study intensively

## Example Sentences

- 他正在研究这个问题。(Tā zhèngzài yánjiū zhège wèntí.) — He is researching this problem.

## Notes

Often used for academic research or fine grinding.
