# 检 Check, inspect

- **Character**: 检
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: check, inspect
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — check, inspect
- 检测 (jiǎn cè) — detect, test

## Example Sentences

- 请检查代码中的错误。(Qǐng jiǎn chá dài mǎ zhōng de cuò wù.) — Please check the errors in the code.

## Notes

Used in 检查 (check), 检测 (detect), 检索 (retrieve), important for testing
