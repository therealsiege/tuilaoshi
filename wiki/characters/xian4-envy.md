# 羡 To envy, to admire

- **Character**: 羡
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: to envy, to admire
- **Radical**: 羊(sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 羡慕 (xiàn mù) — to envy, to admire
- 艳羡 (yàn xiàn) — to admire enviously

## Example Sentences

- 大家都羡慕她的幸福生活。(Dàjiā dōu xiànmù tā de xìngfú shēnghuó.) — Everyone envies her happy life.

## Notes

Often paired with 慕 to form 羡慕.
