# 罚 To Punish

- **Character**: 罚
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: to punish, to penalize, fine
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 处罚 (chǔ fá) — to punish
- 罚款 (fá kuǎn) — fine, penalty

## Example Sentences

- 违反规定要受到处罚。(Wéi fǎn guī dìng yào shòu dào chǔ fá.) — Violating the rules will result in punishment.

## Notes

罚 is simplified from 罰. Shows punishment under law (net symbolizes law).
