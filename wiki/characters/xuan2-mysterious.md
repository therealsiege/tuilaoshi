# 玄 Mysterious, profound, black

- **Character**: 玄
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: mysterious, profound, black
- **Radical**: 玄 (dark)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 玄妙 (xuán miào) — mysterious, abstruse
- 玄学 (xuán xué) — metaphysics

## Example Sentences

- 道家思想深奥玄妙。(Dào jiā sī xiǎng shēn ào xuán miào.) — Daoist thought is profound and mysterious.

## Notes

Literary term for mystery and profundity. 玄关 means entrance hall. Associated with Daoism.
