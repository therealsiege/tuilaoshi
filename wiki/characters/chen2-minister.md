# 臣 Minister, subject, official

- **Character**: 臣
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: minister, subject, official
- **Radical**: 臣 (minister)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 大臣 (dà chén) — minister of state
- 臣子 (chén zǐ) — official, subject

## Example Sentences

- 忠臣宁死不屈。(Zhōng chén nìng sǐ bù qū.) — A loyal minister would rather die than submit.

## Notes

Represents a government minister or subject of the emperor. The character depicts a downward-looking eye, suggesting submission.
