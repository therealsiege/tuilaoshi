# 炎 Inflammation

- **Character**: 炎
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: inflammation
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 发炎 (fā yán) — to become inflamed
- 肺炎 (fèi yán) — pneumonia
- 消炎 (xiāo yán) — anti-inflammatory

## Example Sentences

- 伤口发炎了，需要消炎药。(Shāng kǒu fā yán le, xū yào xiāo yán yào.) — The wound is inflamed and needs anti-inflammatory medication.

## Notes

Suffix for inflammation conditions; appears in many disease names.
