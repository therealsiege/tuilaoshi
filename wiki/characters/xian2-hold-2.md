# 衔 Hold in mouth, rank, title

- **Character**: 衔
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: hold in mouth, rank, title
- **Radical**: 行 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 军衔 (jūn xián) — military rank
- 头衔 (tóu xián) — title

## Example Sentences

- 他的军衔是上校。(Tā de jūn xián shì shàng xiào.) — His military rank is colonel.

## Notes

Used for official titles and ranks
