# 坦 Flat, smooth, frank

- **Character**: 坦
- **Pinyin**: tǎn
- **Meaning**: flat, smooth, frank
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 坦率 (tǎn shuài) — frank, candid
- 坦白 (tǎn bái) — confess, be frank

## Example Sentences

- 他坦率地承认了自己的错误。(Tā tǎn shuài de chéng rèn le zì jǐ de cuò wù.) — He frankly admitted his mistake.

## Notes

Originally meant flat terrain; now commonly means frank.
