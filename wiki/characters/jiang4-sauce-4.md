# 酱 Sauce

- **Character**: 酱
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: sauce, paste, jam
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine vessel)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 酱油 (jiàng yóu) — soy sauce
- 果酱 (guǒ jiàng) — jam

## Example Sentences

- 请给我一些酱油。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yì xiē jiàng yóu.) — Please give me some soy sauce.

## Notes

酱 simplified from 醬. Wine vessel 酉 suggests fermented condiment.
