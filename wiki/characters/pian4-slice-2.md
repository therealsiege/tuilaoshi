# 片 Slice

- **Character**: 片
- **Pinyin**: piàn
- **Meaning**: slice, piece, chip
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 芯片 (xīn piàn) — chip
- 照片 (zhào piàn) — photograph

## Example Sentences

- 给我看看那张照片。(Gěi wǒ kàn kàn nà zhāng zhào piàn.) — Let me see that photograph.

## Notes

片 as a flat piece is perfect for chips, photos, and tablets.
