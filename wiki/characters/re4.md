# 热 Hot

- **Character**: 热
- **Pinyin**: rè
- **Meaning**: hot, heat
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 热情 (rè qíng) — enthusiastic
- 发热 (fā rè) — to have fever

## Example Sentences

- 今天很热。(Jīn tiān hěn rè.) — Today is very hot.

## Notes

热 simplified from 熱, with four dots fire radical 灬. Opposite of 冷 (cold).
