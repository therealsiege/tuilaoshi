# 最 Most

- **Character**: 最
- **Pinyin**: zuì
- **Meaning**: most, -est (superlative)
- **Radical**: 曰 (say)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 最好 (zuì hǎo) — best
- 最后 (zuì hòu) — finally, last

## Example Sentences

- 他是最好的学生。(Tā shì zuì hǎo de xué shēng.) — He is the best student.

## Notes

最 is the superlative marker: 最大 (biggest), 最好 (best), 最重要 (most important).
