# 荒 Wasteland, absurd, famine

- **Character**: 荒
- **Pinyin**: huāng
- **Meaning**: wasteland, absurd, famine
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荒谬 (huāng miù) — absurd
- 荒唐 (huāng táng) — absurd, fantastic

## Example Sentences

- 这个想法太荒唐了。(Zhè gè xiǎng fǎ tài huāng táng le.) — This idea is too absurd.

## Notes

Describes both physical desolation and logical absurdity
