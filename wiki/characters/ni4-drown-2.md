# 溺 Drown

- **Character**: 溺
- **Pinyin**: nì
- **Meaning**: to drown
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 溺水 (nì shuǐ) — to drown
- 沉溺 (chén nì) — to indulge in

## Example Sentences

- 游泳时要注意安全，防止溺水。(Yóuyǒng shí yào zhùyì ānquán, fángzhǐ nìshuǐ.) — Pay attention to safety when swimming to prevent drowning.

## Notes

Drowning; water safety concern
