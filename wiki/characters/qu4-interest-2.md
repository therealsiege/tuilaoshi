# 趣 Interest, delight, 趨 (to hasten)

- **Character**: 趣
- **Pinyin**: qù
- **Meaning**: interest, delight,趨 (to hasten)
- **Radical**: 走 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 有趣 (yǒu qù) — interesting, amusing
- 兴趣 (xìng qù) — interest, hobby

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很有趣。(Zhè běn shū hěn yǒuqù.) — This book is very interesting.

## Notes

Common character for interest, fun, and what appeals to people.
