# 练 Practice, train

- **Character**: 练
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: practice, train
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 训练 (xùn liàn) — training
- 练习 (liàn xí) — practice

## Example Sentences

- 神经网络正在训练中。(Shén jīng wǎng luò zhèng zài xùn liàn zhōng.) — The neural network is training.

## Notes

Used in 训练 (training), 练习 (practice), AI/ML terminology
