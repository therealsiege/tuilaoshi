# 释 Release, explain, Buddhism

- **Character**: 释
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: release, explain, Buddhism
- **Radical**: 采 (pick)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 释放 (shìfàng) — release, set free
- 假释 (jiǎshì) — parole

## Example Sentences

- 人质被释放了。(Rénzhì bèi shìfàng le.) — The hostages were released.

## Notes

Used for releasing and explaining; also relates to Buddhism.
