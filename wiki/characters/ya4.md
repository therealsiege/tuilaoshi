# 亚 Second

- **Character**: 亚
- **Pinyin**: yà
- **Meaning**: second place, Asia
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 亚军 (yà jūn) — second place, runner-up
- 亚洲 (Yà zhōu) — Asia

## Example Sentences

- 我们队获得了亚军。(Wǒmen duì huòdé le yàjūn.) — Our team won second place.

## Notes

Second place in competition; also means Asia
