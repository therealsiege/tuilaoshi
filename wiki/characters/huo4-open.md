# 豁 Open, crack, exempt

- **Character**: 豁
- **Pinyin**: huò
- **Meaning**: open, crack, exempt
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 豁达 (huòdá) — open-minded, optimistic
- 豁免 (huòmiǎn) — exempt, waive

## Example Sentences

- 他性格豁达。(Tā xìnggé huòdá.) — He is open-minded.

## Notes

Literary term for openness of mind or exemption.
