# 健 Healthy

- **Character**: 健
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: healthy, strong
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 健康 (jiàn kāng) — healthy
- 健身 (jiàn shēn) — to work out

## Example Sentences

- 祝你身体健康！(Zhù nǐ shēn tǐ jiàn kāng!) — Wishing you good health!

## Notes

健康 is the standard word for health/healthy. 健身房 means gym.
