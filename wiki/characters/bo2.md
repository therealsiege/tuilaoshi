# 驳 To Refute

- **Character**: 驳
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: to refute, to reject, parti-colored
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 反驳 (fǎn bó) — to refute
- 批驳 (pī bó) — to criticize and refute

## Example Sentences

- 他反驳了那个观点。(Tā fǎn bó le nà gè guān diǎn.) — He refuted that viewpoint.

## Notes

驳 simplified from 駁. Uses horse radical 马, originally meaning multicolored horse, extended to mean contradicting or refuting.
