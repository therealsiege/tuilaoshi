# 璀 Brilliant

- **Character**: 璀
- **Pinyin**: cuǐ
- **Meaning**: brilliant, lustrous
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 璀璨 (cuǐ càn) — bright, brilliant, dazzling
- 璀璨夺目 (cuǐ càn duó mù) — dazzlingly brilliant

## Example Sentences

- 夜空中星星璀璨。(Yè kōng zhōng xīng xīng cuǐ càn.) — Stars are brilliant in the night sky.

## Notes

璀 uses jade radical 玉, always paired with 璨 to describe dazzling brilliance or radiance.
