# 残 Incomplete

- **Character**: 残
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: incomplete, disabled, cruel, to damage
- **Radical**: 歹 (bad)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 残疾 (cán jí) — disability
- 残忍 (cán rěn) — cruel

## Example Sentences

- 不要残忍地对待动物。(Bú yào cán rěn de duì dài dòng wù.) — Don't treat animals cruelly.

## Notes

残 means damaged, incomplete, or cruel. 残疾人 = person with disability.
