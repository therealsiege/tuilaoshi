# 铸 Cast

- **Character**: 铸
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: cast (metal), mint
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铸造 (zhù zào) — cast, founding
- 铸铁 (zhù tiě) — cast iron

## Example Sentences

- 这座铜像是用失蜡法铸造的。(Zhè zuò tóngxiàng shì yòng shīlà fǎ zhùzào de.) — This bronze statue was cast using the lost-wax method.

## Notes

铸 refers to casting molten metal into molds. Ancient China excelled at bronze casting.
