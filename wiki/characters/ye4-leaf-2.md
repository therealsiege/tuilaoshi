# 叶 Leaf

- **Character**: 叶
- **Pinyin**: yè
- **Meaning**: leaf, surname Ye
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 叶公好龙 (yè gōng hào lóng) — profess love but actually fear
- 树叶 (shù yè) — tree leaf

## Example Sentences

- 他只是叶公好龙罢了。(Tā zhǐ shì yè gōng hào lóng bà le.) — He just professes to love it but doesn't really.

## Notes

In 叶公好龙, 叶 is a surname (Yè); Lord Ye loved dragon art but fled when a real dragon appeared.
