# 进 Advance, enter, 进

- **Character**: 进
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: advance, enter,进
- **Radical**: 辵 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 进化 (jìn huà) — evolution, evolve
- 前进 (qián jìn) — advance, move forward

## Example Sentences

- 达尔文提出了进化论。(Dá ěr wén tí chū le jìn huà lùn.) — Darwin proposed the theory of evolution.

Used in evolution and advancement
