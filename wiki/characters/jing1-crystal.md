# 晶 Crystal

- **Character**: 晶
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: crystal, bright
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 水晶 (shuǐ jīng) — crystal
- 结晶 (jié jīng) — to crystallize

## Example Sentences

- 这块水晶很漂亮。(Zhè kuài shuǐjīng hěn piàoliang.) — This crystal is very beautiful.

## Notes

晶 shows three suns 日日日, representing extreme brightness and clarity like crystals.
