# 插 Insert, plug

- **Character**: 插
- **Pinyin**: chā
- **Meaning**: insert, plug
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 插入 (chā rù) — insert
- 插槽 (chā cáo) — slot

## Example Sentences

- 请把USB线插入电脑。(Qǐng bǎ USB xiàn chā rù diàn nǎo.) — Please insert the USB cable into the computer.

## Notes

Used in 插入 (insert), 插槽 (slot), 插件 (plugin)
