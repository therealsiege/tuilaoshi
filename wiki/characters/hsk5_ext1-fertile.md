# 沃 Fertile, Irrigate, 肥沃 Fertile

- **Character**: 沃
- **Pinyin**: wò
- **Meaning**: fertile, irrigate, 肥沃 fertile
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肥沃 (féi wò) — fertile
- 沃土 (wò tǔ) — fertile land
- 沃野 (wò yě) — fertile field

## Example Sentences

- 这片土地很肥沃。(Zhè piàn tǔdì hěn féiwò.) — This land is very fertile.

## Notes

Agricultural term describing fertile, productive land. 肥沃 (fertile) is essential for describing agricultural quality.
