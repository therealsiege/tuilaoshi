# 透 Dialysis

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: penetrate, thorough
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 透析 (tòu xī) — dialysis
- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate
- 透明 (tòu míng) — transparent

## Example Sentences

- 肾衰竭患者需要透析。(Shèn shuāi jié huàn zhě xū yào tòu xī.) — Kidney failure patients need dialysis.

## Notes

Penetration; dialysis filters blood artificially.
