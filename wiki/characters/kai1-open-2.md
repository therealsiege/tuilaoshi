# 开 To open, start, drive, 開 (traditional)

- **Character**: 开
- **Pinyin**: kāi
- **Meaning**: to open, start, drive, 開 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 开始 (kāi shǐ) — to begin, start
- 打开 (dǎ kāi) — to open

## Example Sentences

- 会议马上开始。(Huìyì mǎshàng kāishǐ.) — The meeting will start soon.

## Notes

Very common character for opening, starting, or driving.
