# 检 To Check

- **Character**: 检
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: to check, to examine
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — to inspect
- 体检 (tǐ jiǎn) — physical exam

## Example Sentences

- 医生给我做了检查。(Yī shēng gěi wǒ zuò le jiǎn chá.) — The doctor gave me an examination.

## Notes

检查 is the general word for checking or inspecting anything.
