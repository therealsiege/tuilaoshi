# 牍 Wooden tablet, documents

- **Character**: 牍
- **Pinyin**: dú
- **Meaning**: wooden tablet, documents
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 文牍 (wén dú) — official documents
- 案牍 (àn dú) — documents, files

## Example Sentences

- 古代用竹牍记录文字。(Gǔ dài yòng zhú dú jì lù wén zì.) — Ancient people used bamboo tablets to record writing.

## Notes

Ancient writing materials, bamboo or wooden strips
