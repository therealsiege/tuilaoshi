# 投 Throw, invest, submit

- **Character**: 投
- **Pinyin**: tóu
- **Meaning**: throw, invest, submit
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 投资 (tóuzī) — invest, investment
- 投标 (tóubiāo) — submit a bid

## Example Sentences

- 公司计划投资新项目。(Gōngsī jìhuà tóuzī xīn xiàngmù.) — The company plans to invest in new projects.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: throw, invest, submit.
