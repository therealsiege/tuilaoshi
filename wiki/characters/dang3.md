# 挡 To Block

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, to obstruct, to keep off
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to obstruct, to block
- 挡住 (dǎng zhù) — to block, to cover

## Example Sentences

- 树挡住了阳光。(Shù dǎng zhù le yáng guāng.) — The tree blocks the sunlight.

## Notes

挡 means to block or obstruct physically. In vehicles, 挡 refers to gear (一挡 first gear, 二挡 second gear).
