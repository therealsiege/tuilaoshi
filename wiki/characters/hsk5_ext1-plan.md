# 划 Plan, Delimit, 计划 Plan

- **Character**: 划
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: plan, delimit, 计划 plan
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 计划 (jì huà) — plan
- 规划 (guī huà) — plan, layout
- 策划 (cè huà) — plan, scheme

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要规划未来的发展方向。(Wǒmen xūyào guīhuà wèilái de fāzhǎn fāngxiàng.) — We need to plan future development directions.

## Notes

Also pronounced huá when meaning 'row (a boat)'. In planning contexts, always pronounced huà. Essential in business and government contexts.
