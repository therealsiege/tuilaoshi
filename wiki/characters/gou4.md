# 构 To Construct

- **Character**: 构
- **Pinyin**: gòu
- **Meaning**: to construct, structure, to form
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 结构 (jiē gòu) — structure
- 构成 (gòu chéng) — to constitute

## Example Sentences

- 这座建筑的结构很特别。(Zhè zuò jiàn zhù de jiē gòu hěn tè bié.) — The structure of this building is very special.

## Notes

构 means to construct or form a structure. Essential in architectural and organizational contexts.
