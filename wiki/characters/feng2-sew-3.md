# 缝 Sew

- **Character**: 缝
- **Pinyin**: féng
- **Meaning**: sew, stitch
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缝纫 (féng rèn) — sewing
- 裁缝 (cái féng) — tailor

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈正在缝补衣服。(Māma zhèngzài féng bǔ yīfu.) — Mom is mending clothes.

## Notes

缝 (féng) means to sew. Also pronounced fèng meaning crack or seam.
