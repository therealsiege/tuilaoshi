# 宪 Constitution

- **Character**: 宪
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: constitution, law
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 宪法 (xiàn fǎ) — constitution
- 立宪 (lì xiàn) — constitutional

## Example Sentences

- 宪法是国家的根本法。(Xiàn fǎ shì guó jiā de gēn běn fǎ.) — The constitution is the fundamental law of the nation.

## Notes

宪 is simplified from 憲. Represents supreme law and constitutional principles.
