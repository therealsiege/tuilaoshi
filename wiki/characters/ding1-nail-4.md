# 钉 Nail

- **Character**: 钉
- **Pinyin**: dīng
- **Meaning**: nail, spike
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 钉子 (dīng zi) — nail
- 螺丝钉 (luó sī dīng) — screw

## Example Sentences

- 他用锤子把钉子钉进了木板。(Tā yòng chuízi bǎ dīngzi dīng jìn le mùbǎn.) — He hammered the nail into the wooden board.

## Notes

钉 (dīng) is a nail or spike. As a verb (dìng) it means to nail or fasten.
