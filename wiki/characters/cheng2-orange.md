# 橙 Orange

- **Character**: 橙
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: orange (color/fruit)
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 橙色 (chéng sè) — orange color
- 橙汁 (chéng zhī) — orange juice

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢橙色。(Wǒ xǐ huan chéng sè.) — I like orange (color).

## Notes

橙 refers to both the orange fruit and the color. Uses the tree/wood radical 木 as oranges grow on trees.
