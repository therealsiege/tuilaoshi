# 染 Dye

- **Character**: 染
- **Pinyin**: rǎn
- **Meaning**: dye, stain
- **Radical**: 木(wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 染色 (rǎn sè) — dye, dyeing
- 污染 (wū rǎn) — pollute, contaminate

## Example Sentences

- 这块布被染成了蓝色。(Zhè kuài bù bèi rǎn chéng le lán sè.) — This cloth was dyed blue.

## Notes

染 means to dye or stain. Extended meaning includes contamination and infection.
