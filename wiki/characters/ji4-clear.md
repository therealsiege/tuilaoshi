# 霁 Clear Up

- **Character**: 霁
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to clear up (weather)
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 霁雨 (jì yǔ) — rain clearing up
- 雪霁 (xuě jì) — snow clearing

## Example Sentences

- 雨后天霁，出现了彩虹。(Yǔ hòu tiān jì, chū xiàn le cǎi hóng.) — After the rain cleared, a rainbow appeared.

## Notes

霁 simplified from 霽. Rain 雨 with 齐 (even), clearing skies.
