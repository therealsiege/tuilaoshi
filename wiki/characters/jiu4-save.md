# 救 To Save

- **Character**: 救
- **Pinyin**: jiù
- **Meaning**: to save, rescue
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 救命 (jiù mìng) — save a life, help
- 拯救 (zhěng jiù) — to save, rescue

## Example Sentences

- 他救了一个落水的孩子。(Tā jiù le yīgè luòshuǐ de háizi.) — He saved a drowning child.

## Notes

救 combines 求 (to求) with 攵 (strike), representing forceful求 or intervention to save someone.
