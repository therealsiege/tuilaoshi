# 矿 Mine

- **Character**: 矿
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: mine, ore, mineral
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 矿山 (kuàng shān) — mine
- 矿物 (kuàng wù) — mineral

## Example Sentences

- 这座山有很多矿产。(Zhè zuò shān yǒu hěn duō kuàng chǎn.) — This mountain has many mineral resources.

## Notes

矿 means mine or mineral. Used in矿山 (mine), 矿泉水 (mineral water), 矿产 (mineral resources).
