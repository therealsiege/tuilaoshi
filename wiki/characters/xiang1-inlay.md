# 镶 To Inlay

- **Character**: 镶
- **Pinyin**: xiāng
- **Meaning**: to inlay, to embed
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 镶嵌 (xiāng qiàn) — to inlay, to set
- 镶边 (xiāng biān) — to edge, to border

## Example Sentences

- 戒指上镶嵌着宝石。(Jiè zhǐ shàng xiāng qiàn zhe bǎo shí.) — The ring is set with gems.

## Notes

镶 simplified from 鑲. Metal radical 钅 representing inlaying precious materials.
