# 灰 Ash

- **Character**: 灰
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: ash, lime, gray
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 石灰 (shí huī) — lime
- 灰色 (huī sè) — gray color

## Example Sentences

- 传统建筑中常用石灰作为粘合剂。(Chuántǒng jiànzhú zhōng cháng yòng shíhuī zuòwéi zhānhéjì.) — Traditional architecture often uses lime as a binder.

## Notes

灰 means ash, lime, or the color gray. Lime is important in traditional construction and ceramics.
