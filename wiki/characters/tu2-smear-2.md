# 涂 To smear, apply, scribble

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: to smear, apply, scribble
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear, daub
- 糊涂 (hú tu) — confused, muddled

## Example Sentences

- 她在面包上涂了一层黄油。(Tā zài miàn bāo shàng tú le yī céng huáng yóu.) — She spread a layer of butter on the bread.

## Notes

Means to smear or apply. 涂鸦 means graffiti. 生灵涂炭 means people in dire straits.
