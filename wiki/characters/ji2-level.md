# 级 Level

- **Character**: 级
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: level, grade, rank
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 年级 (nián jí) — grade (school)
- 高级 (gāo jí) — advanced

## Example Sentences

- 你上几年级？(Nǐ shàng jǐ nián jí?) — What grade are you in?

## Notes

级 is used for levels and grades: 初级 (beginner), 中级 (intermediate), 高级 (advanced).
