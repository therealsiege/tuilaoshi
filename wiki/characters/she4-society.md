# 社 Society

- **Character**: 社
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: society, organization, association
- **Radical**: 示 (show)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 社会 (shè huì) — society
- 公司 (gōng sī) — company

## Example Sentences

- 我们生活在一个复杂的社会。(Wǒ men shēng huó zài yī ge fù zá de shè huì.) — We live in a complex society.

## Notes

社 originally meant altar to the earth god, evolved to mean community or society. Core character for social concepts.
