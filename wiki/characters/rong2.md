# 熔 To Smelt

- **Character**: 熔
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: to smelt, to melt
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熔化 (róng huà) — to melt
- 熔炉 (róng lú) — furnace, melting pot

## Example Sentences

- 金属在高温下熔化。(Jīn shǔ zài gāo wēn xià róng huà.) — Metals melt at high temperatures.

## Notes

熔 uses fire radical 火, specifically refers to melting metals or materials through heat.
