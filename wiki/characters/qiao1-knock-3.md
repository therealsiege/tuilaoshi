# 敲 To Strike

- **Character**: 敲
- **Pinyin**: qiāo
- **Meaning**: to knock, to strike
- **Radical**: 攴 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 敲门 (qiāo mén) — to knock on door
- 敲鼓 (qiāo gǔ) — to beat a drum

## Example Sentences

- 他在敲鼓。(Tā zài qiāo gǔ.) — He is beating the drum.

## Notes

敲 means to strike or knock, used for percussion instruments.
