# 干 To Do

- **Character**: 干
- **Pinyin**: gàn
- **Meaning**: to do, to work; dry (gān)
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 干什么 (gàn shén me) — what are you doing
- 能干 (néng gàn) — capable

## Example Sentences

- 你在干什么？(Nǐ zài gàn shén me?) — What are you doing?

## Notes

干 as gàn means to do (colloquial). As gān means dry. 干净 = clean. Simplified from 幹 and 乾.
