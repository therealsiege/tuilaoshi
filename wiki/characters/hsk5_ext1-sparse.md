# 稀 Sparse, Rare, 稀少 Scarce

- **Character**: 稀
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: sparse, rare, 稀少 scarce
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 稀少 (xī shǎo) — scarce, rare
- 稀有 (xī yǒu) — rare
- 稀饭 (xī fàn) — porridge

## Example Sentences

- 这种动物非常稀有。(Zhè zhǒng dòngwù fēicháng xīyǒu.) — This animal is very rare.

## Notes

Opposite of 密 (dense). Describes sparseness or rarity. 稀饭 (porridge) is exception - watery/thin rice.
