# 怠 Idle, negligent, slack

- **Character**: 怠
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: idle, negligent, slack
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 怠慢 (dài màn) — to neglect, to slight
- 懈怠 (xiè dài) — to slack off

## Example Sentences

- 工作不能懈怠。(Gōngzuò bù néng xièdài.) — You cannot be negligent in work.

## Notes

Negative character for idleness and negligence.
