# 巨 Huge

- **Character**: 巨
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: huge, gigantic
- **Radical**: 工 (work)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 巨大 (jù dà) — huge, enormous
- 巨人 (jù rén) — giant

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个巨大的成功。(Zhè shì yīgè jùdà de chénggōng.) — This is a huge success.

## Notes

巨 originally depicted a carpenter's square, extended to mean large or巨.
