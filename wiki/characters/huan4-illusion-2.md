# 幻 Illusion

- **Character**: 幻
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: illusion, fantasy
- **Radical**: 幺 (tiny)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 幻觉 (huàn jué) — hallucination
- 幻想 (huàn xiǎng) — fantasy, illusion
- 幻听 (huàn tīng) — auditory hallucination

## Example Sentences

- 精神病患者可能出现幻觉。(Jīng shén bìng huàn zhě kě néng chū xiàn huàn jué.) — Mental illness patients may experience hallucinations.

## Notes

False perceptions; psychotic symptom.
