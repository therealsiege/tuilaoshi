# 崩 To Collapse

- **Character**: 崩
- **Pinyin**: bēng
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to burst
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 崩溃 (bēng kuì) — to collapse
- 崩塌 (bēng tā) — to crumble

## Example Sentences

- 他快要崩溃了。(Tā kuài yào bēng kuì le.) — He is about to break down.

## Notes

崩 depicts mountain 山 splitting apart, two 朋 sounds.
