# 缝 To Sew

- **Character**: 缝
- **Pinyin**: féng
- **Meaning**: to sew, stitch
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缝纫 (féng rèn) — to sew
- 缝补 (féng bǔ) — to mend

## Example Sentences

- 她在缝衣服。(Tā zài féng yīfu.) — She is sewing clothes.

## Notes

缝 can be read as féng (to sew) or fèng (gap, crack). Combines silk 糹 with 逢 (to meet), suggesting joining threads.
