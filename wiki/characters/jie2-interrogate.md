# 诘 Interrogate, question closely

- **Character**: 诘
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: interrogate, question closely
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诘问 (jiéwèn) — question closely, interrogate
- 反诘 (fǎnjié) — retort, ask in reply

## Example Sentences

- 警察诘问嫌疑人。(Jǐngchá jiéwèn xiányírén.) — The police interrogated the suspect.

## Notes

Formal term for close questioning or interrogation.
