# 仲 Middle, mediate, second (brother)

- **Character**: 仲
- **Pinyin**: zhòng
- **Meaning**: middle, mediate, second (brother)
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 仲裁 (zhòng cái) — arbitration
- 仲夏 (zhòng xià) — midsummer

## Example Sentences

- 双方同意进行仲裁。(Shuāng fāng tóng yì jìn xíng zhòng cái.) — Both parties agreed to arbitration.

## Notes

Used in arbitration and as ordinal (second brother). 仲介 means intermediary.
