# 桨 Oar, paddle

- **Character**: 桨
- **Pinyin**: jiǎng
- **Meaning**: oar, paddle
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 船桨 (chuán jiǎng) — oar
- 划桨 (huá jiǎng) — to row, paddle

## Example Sentences

- 他们用力划桨向前进。(Tā men yòng lì huá jiǎng xiàng qián jìn.) — They rowed hard forward.

## Notes

Means oar or paddle. 螺旋桨 means propeller. Used in water sports and boating.
