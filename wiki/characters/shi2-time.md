# 时 Time

- **Character**: 时
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: time, hour, season
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 时间 (shí jiān) — time
- 小时 (xiǎo shí) — hour

## Example Sentences

- 现在什么时候？(Xiàn zài shén me shí hòu?) — What time is it now?

## Notes

时 is simplified from 時. 时候 means 'time/moment,' 小时 means 'hour,' 有时候 means 'sometimes.'
