# 纤 Fine

- **Character**: 纤
- **Pinyin**: xiān
- **Meaning**: fine, delicate, fiber
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 纤维 (xiān wéi) — fiber
- 纤细 (xiān xì) — slender, fine

## Example Sentences

- 她的手指很纤细。(Tā de shǒuzhǐ hěn xiānxì.) — Her fingers are very slender.

## Notes

纤 combines silk 糹 with 千 (thousand), suggesting fine threads. Can also be read as qiàn in 纤夫 (tracker, boat puller).
