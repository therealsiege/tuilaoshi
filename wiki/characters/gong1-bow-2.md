# 弓 Bow (weapon)

- **Character**: 弓
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: bow (weapon)
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow
- 弩弓 (nǔ gōng) — crossbow

## Example Sentences

- 射箭需要很强的臂力和准确度。(Shè jiàn xūyào hěn qiáng de bì lì hé zhǔnquè dù.) — Archery requires strong arm strength and accuracy.

## Notes

Ancient ranged weapon. The character is pictographic, showing the curved shape of a bow. A radical in many weapon-related characters.
