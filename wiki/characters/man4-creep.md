# 蔓 Creep, spread

- **Character**: 蔓
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: creep, spread
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蔓延 (mànyán) — spread, extend
- 藤蔓 (téngwàn) — vine

## Example Sentences

- 火势迅速蔓延。(Huǒshì xùnsù mànyán.) — The fire spread rapidly.

## Notes

Describes spreading like vines, has two pronunciations: màn and wàn.
