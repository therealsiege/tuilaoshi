# 实 Real

- **Character**: 实
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: real, true, solid, fruit
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 确实 (què shí) — indeed
- 实际 (shí jì) — actual, reality

## Example Sentences

- 实际上，这很简单。(Shí jì shàng, zhè hěn jiǎn dān.) — Actually, this is very simple.

## Notes

实 shows a house full of things (实ull), representing reality or实stance. Opposite of虚 (empty, false).
