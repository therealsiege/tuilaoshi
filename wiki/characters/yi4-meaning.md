# 意 Meaning, idea, intention

- **Character**: 意
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: meaning, idea, intention
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 意思 (yì si) — meaning
- 意义 (yì yì) — significance

## Example Sentences

- 这句话有深刻的意义。(Zhè jù huà yǒu shēn kè de yì yì.) — This sentence has profound meaning.
