# 倒 To Pour

- **Character**: 倒
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to pour, to fall down, to倒倒, contrary
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倒水 (dào shuǐ) — to pour water
- 跌倒 (diē dǎo) — to fall down

## Example Sentences

- 请给我倒杯水。(Qǐng gěi wǒ dào bēi shuǐ.) — Please pour me a glass of water.

## Notes

倒 can be dào (to pour, to fall) or dǎo (upside down, contrary). As dào, used for pouring liquids.
