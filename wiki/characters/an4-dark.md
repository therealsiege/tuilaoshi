# 暗 Dark

- **Character**: 暗
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: dark, dim, secret
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 黑暗 (hēi àn) — dark
- 昏暗 (hūn àn) — dim

## Example Sentences

- 房间里很暗。(Fáng jiān lǐ hěn àn.) — The room is very dark.

## Notes

暗 has sun 日 with 音 (sound), darkness where light is hidden.
