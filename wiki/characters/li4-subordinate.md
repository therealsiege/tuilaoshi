# 隶 Subordinate, be attached to, clerical script

- **Character**: 隶
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: subordinate, be attached to, clerical script
- **Radical**: 隶 (slave)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 隶属 (lì shǔ) — to be subordinate to
- 隶书 (lì shū) — clerical script

## Example Sentences

- 这个部门隶属于总公司。(Zhè ge bù mén lì shǔ yú zǒng gōng sī.) — This department is subordinate to the head office.

## Notes

Indicates subordination or affiliation. 隶书 is an important style of Chinese calligraphy.
