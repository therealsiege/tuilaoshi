# 花 Flower

- **Character**: 花
- **Pinyin**: huā
- **Meaning**: flower, to spend
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 花园 (huā yuán) — garden
- 花钱 (huā qián) — to spend money

## Example Sentences

- 这些花很漂亮。(Zhè xiē huā hěn piào liàng.) — These flowers are very beautiful.

## Notes

花 means both 'flower' and 'to spend (money/time).' 花时间 (spend time), 花钱 (spend money).
