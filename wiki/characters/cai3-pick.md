# 采 To Pick

- **Character**: 采
- **Pinyin**: cǎi
- **Meaning**: to pick, to collect, to mine
- **Radical**: 爫 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 采取 (cǎi qǔ) — to adopt (measures)
- 采访 (cǎi fǎng) — to interview

## Example Sentences

- 记者采访了他。(Jì zhě cǎi fǎng le tā.) — The reporter interviewed him.

## Notes

采 means to pick or collect. 采取 = to adopt, 采购 = to purchase.
