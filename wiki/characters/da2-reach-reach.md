# 达 To Reach

- **Character**: 达
- **Pinyin**: dá
- **Meaning**: to reach, to arrive, to express
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 到达 (dào dá) — to arrive
- 表达 (biǎo dá) — to express

## Example Sentences

- 我们到达了目的地。(Wǒ men dào dá le mù dì dì.) — We arrived at the destination.

## Notes

达 means to reach or attain. 发达 = developed, 达到 = to achieve/reach.
