# 曦 Sunlight

- **Character**: 曦
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: sunlight, dawn
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 晨曦 (chén xī) — dawn
- 朝曦 (zhāo xī) — morning light

## Example Sentences

- 晨曦照亮了大地。(Chén xī zhào liàng le dà dì.) — Dawn illuminates the earth.

## Notes

曦 has sun 日 with 羲 (ancient emperor), early sunlight.
