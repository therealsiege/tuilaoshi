# 胶 Glue

- **Character**: 胶
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: glue, gum, rubber
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胶水 (jiāo shuǐ) — glue
- 橡胶 (xiàng jiāo) — rubber

## Example Sentences

- 用胶水粘好它。(Yòng jiāo shuǐ zhān hǎo tā.) — Glue it well.

## Notes

胶 simplified from 膠. Sticky substance from animal parts.
