# 胆 Gallbladder

- **Character**: 胆
- **Pinyin**: dǎn
- **Meaning**: gallbladder; courage
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胆囊 (dǎn náng) — gallbladder
- 胆结石 (dǎn jié shí) — gallstone
- 胆固醇 (dǎn gù chún) — cholesterol

## Example Sentences

- 他需要切除胆囊。(Tā xū yào qiē chú dǎn náng.) — He needs to have his gallbladder removed.

## Notes

Stores bile; also metaphorically means courage or guts.
