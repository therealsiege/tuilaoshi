# 碍 Hinder

- **Character**: 碍
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: hinder, obstruct
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle, disorder
- 妨碍 (fáng ài) — to hinder
- 阻碍 (zǔ ài) — to obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 情感障碍影响日常生活。(Qíng gǎn zhàng ài yǐng xiǎng rì cháng shēng huó.) — Mood disorders affect daily life.

## Notes

Interference; functional impairment.
