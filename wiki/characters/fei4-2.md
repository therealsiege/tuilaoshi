# 废 废除, abolish, waste

- **Character**: 废
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: 废除, abolish, waste
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 废除 (fèi chú) — 废除, repeal
- 作废 (zuò fèi) — become invalid

## Example Sentences

- 旧合同已经作废。(Jiù hé tong yǐ jīng zuò fèi.) — The old contract has been invalidated.

## Notes

废弃 means discard. 废止 means abrogate.
