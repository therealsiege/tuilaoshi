# 渲 Render, wash (in painting)

- **Character**: 渲
- **Pinyin**: xuàn
- **Meaning**: render, wash (in painting)
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 渲染 (xuànrǎn) — render, exaggerate
- 渲泄 (xuànxiè) — give vent to

## Example Sentences

- 媒体渲染了这个事件。(Méitǐ xuànrǎn le zhège shìjiàn.) — The media hyped up this incident.

## Notes

Literary term for rendering in art or exaggerating.
