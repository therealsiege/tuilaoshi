# 鸡 Chicken

- **Character**: 鸡
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: chicken
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 鸡蛋 (jī dàn) — chicken egg
- 公鸡 (gōng jī) — rooster

## Example Sentences

- 我早餐吃了一个鸡蛋。(Wǒ zǎocān chīle yī gè jīdàn.) — I ate an egg for breakfast.

## Notes

One of the 12 Chinese zodiac animals. Very common character.
