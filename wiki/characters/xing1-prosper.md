# 兴 Prosper, thrive

- **Character**: 兴
- **Pinyin**: xīng
- **Meaning**: prosper,興旺
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 兴旺 (xīng wàng) — prosperous, thriving
- 复兴 (fù xīng) — revive, renaissance

## Example Sentences

- 生意很兴旺。(Shēng yì hěn xīng wàng.) — Business is very prosperous.

## Notes

Different from 兴 (xìng = interest). 振兴 means revitalize.
