# 兼 Simultaneously

- **Character**: 兼
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: simultaneously, to hold two jobs
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 兼职 (jiān zhí) — part-time job
- 兼顾 (jiān gù) — to give consideration to

## Example Sentences

- 他在大学里兼职。(Tā zài dà xué lǐ jiān zhí.) — He works part-time at the university.

## Notes

兼 depicts holding two things at once.
