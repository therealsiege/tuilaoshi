# 延 Extend, prolong, invite

- **Character**: 延
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: extend, prolong, invite
- **Radical**: 廴 (move)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 延长 (yáncháng) — extend, prolong
- 蔓延 (mànyán) — spread, extend

## Example Sentences

- 会议延长了一小时。(Huìyì yáncháng le yī xiǎoshí.) — The meeting was extended by an hour.

## Notes

Common character for extension, prolongation, or invitation.
