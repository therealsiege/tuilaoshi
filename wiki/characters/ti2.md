# 题 Topic

- **Character**: 题
- **Pinyin**: tí
- **Meaning**: topic, question, problem
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 问题 (wèn tí) — question, problem
- 题目 (tí mù) — title, topic

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题很难。(Zhè ge wèn tí hěn nán.) — This question is very difficult.

## Notes

题 is used in many academic contexts: 题目 (title/topic), 问题 (question/problem), 做题 (do exercises).
