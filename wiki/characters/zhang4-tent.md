# 帐 Tent

- **Character**: 帐
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: tent, mosquito net, account
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 帐篷 (zhàng péng) — tent
- 蚊帐 (wén zhàng) — mosquito net

## Example Sentences

- 蚊帐能防蚊子。(Wén zhàng néng fáng wén zi.) — Mosquito nets can prevent mosquitos.

## Notes

帐 with cloth radical for cloth tent/curtain, also borrowed for accounts (ledgers).
