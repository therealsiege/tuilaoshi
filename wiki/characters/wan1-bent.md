# 弯 Bent

- **Character**: 弯
- **Pinyin**: wān
- **Meaning**: bent, curved, to bend
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 弯曲 (wān qū) — curved, bent
- 转弯 (zhuǎn wān) — to turn a corner

## Example Sentences

- 前面路弯了。(Qián miàn lù wān le.) — The road ahead curves.

## Notes

弯 means curved or to bend. Used for roads curving (转弯 turning), bending body (弯腰 to bend over).
