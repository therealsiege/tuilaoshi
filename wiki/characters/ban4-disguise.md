# 扮 To Disguise

- **Character**: 扮
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to disguise, to play (a role), to dress up
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 扮演 (bàn yǎn) — to play (a role)
- 打扮 (dǎ bàn) — to dress up

## Example Sentences

- 她打扮得很漂亮。(Tā dǎ bàn de hěn piào liàng.) — She dressed up very beautifully.

## Notes

扮演 = to play a role (in a movie/play). 打扮 = to dress up/groom.
