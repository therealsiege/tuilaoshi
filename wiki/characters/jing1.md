# 经 — Pass Through / Experience

- **Character**: 经
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: to pass through, to experience; classic, scripture
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 已经 (yǐjīng) — already
- 经常 (jīngcháng) — often, frequently
- 经验 (jīngyàn) — experience
- 经过 (jīngguò) — to pass through; process
- 经济 (jīngjì) — economy, economics
- 经理 (jīnglǐ) — manager

## Example Sentences

- 我经常去那家餐厅。(Wǒ jīngcháng qù nà jiā cāntīng.) — I often go to that restaurant.
- 他有很多工作经验。(Tā yǒu hěn duō gōngzuò jīngyàn.) — He has a lot of work experience.
- 我们经过了一个很漂亮的公园。(Wǒmen jīngguò le yī ge hěn piàoliang de gōngyuán.) — We passed through a very beautiful park.

## Notes

经 (simplified from 經) carries the silk radical 纟, originally relating to the lengthwise threads on a loom (the "warp"). This idea of continuous threads running through something extended metaphorically to mean "to pass through" and "constant, regular" — hence 经常 (often). It also came to mean "classic" or "scripture" (something that runs through time), as in 圣经 (Shèngjīng, the Bible). 经济 (economy) is a borrowing from Japanese, itself derived from the classical Chinese phrase 经世济民 (govern the world and aid the people).
