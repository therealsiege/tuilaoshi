# 复 Recover

- **Character**: 复
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: recover, return
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 康复 (kāng fù) — to recover, rehabilitation
- 恢复 (huī fù) — to restore, recover
- 复原 (fù yuán) — to recover, recuperate

## Example Sentences

- 病人已经完全康复了。(Bìng rén yǐ jīng wán quán kāng fù le.) — The patient has completely recovered.

## Notes

Return to health; rehabilitation process.
