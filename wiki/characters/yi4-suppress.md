# 抑 To Suppress

- **Character**: 抑
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to restrain, to suppress
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抑制 (yì zhì) — to inhibit
- 压抑 (yā yì) — to depress

## Example Sentences

- 他抑制不住内心的喜悦。(Tā yì zhì bù zhù nèi xīn de xǐ yuè.) — He cannot suppress his inner joy.

## Notes

抑 hand 扌 holding down 卬, forcefully restraining.
