# 芜 Overgrown, luxuriant weeds

- **Character**: 芜
- **Pinyin**: wú
- **Meaning**: overgrown, luxuriant weeds
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 荒芜 (huāng wú) — waste, uncultivated
- 芜杂 (wú zá) — confused, jumbled

## Example Sentences

- 田地荒芜长满了杂草。(Tián dì huāng wú zhǎng mǎn le zá cǎo.) — The fields are overgrown with weeds.

Wild vegetation and neglect
