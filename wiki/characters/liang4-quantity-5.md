# 量 Quantity, amount

- **Character**: 量
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: quantity, amount
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 流量 (liú liàng) — traffic, flow
- 数量 (shù liàng) — quantity

## Example Sentences

- 数据量太大了。(Shù jù liàng tài dà le.) — The data volume is too large.

## Notes

Used in 流量 (traffic), 数量 (quantity), 容量 (capacity)
