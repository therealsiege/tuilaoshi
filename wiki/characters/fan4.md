# 饭 Meal

- **Character**: 饭
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: meal, rice (cooked)
- **Radical**: 饣(food)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吃饭 (chī fàn) — to eat
- 米饭 (mǐ fàn) — cooked rice

## Example Sentences

- 你吃饭了吗？(Nǐ chī fàn le ma?) — Have you eaten?

## Notes

饭 specifically means cooked rice, but 吃饭 is the general expression for 'to eat a meal.'
