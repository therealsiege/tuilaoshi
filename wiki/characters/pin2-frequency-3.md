# 频 Frequency

- **Character**: 频
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: frequency
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 频率 (pín lǜ) — frequency
- 频道 (pín dào) — channel

## Example Sentences

- 信号频率很高。(Xìn hào pín lǜ hěn gāo.) — The signal frequency is high.

## Notes

Used in 频率 (frequency), 频段 (frequency band), 射频 (RF)
