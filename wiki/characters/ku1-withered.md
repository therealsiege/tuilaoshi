# 枯 Withered

- **Character**: 枯
- **Pinyin**: kū
- **Meaning**: withered, dried up
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 枯萎 (kū wěi) — to wither
- 枯燥 (kū zào) — dull, dry

## Example Sentences

- 这棵树枯了。(Zhè kē shù kū le.) — This tree has withered.

## Notes

枯 combines wood 木 and 古 (ancient), a tree dried to old age.
