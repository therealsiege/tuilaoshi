# 果 Fruit

- **Character**: 果
- **Pinyin**: guǒ
- **Meaning**: fruit, result
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 水果 (shuǐ guǒ) — fruit
- 如果 (rú guǒ) — if

## Example Sentences

- 你喜欢吃什么水果？(Nǐ xǐ huān chī shén me shuǐ guǒ?) — What fruit do you like to eat?

## Notes

果 is a pictograph of fruit on a tree. It extends to mean 'result/outcome' as in 结果 (jiē guǒ, result).
