# 翠 Emerald, kingfisher green

- **Character**: 翠
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: emerald, kingfisher green
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 翠绿 (cuì lǜ) — emerald green
- 翠鸟 (cuì niǎo) — kingfisher

## Example Sentences

- 山上树木翠绿欲滴。(Shān shàng shù mù cuì lǜ yù dī.) — The trees on the mountain are lush emerald green.

## Notes

Vivid green color; also refers to kingfisher.
