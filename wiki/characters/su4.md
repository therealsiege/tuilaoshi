# 速 Fast

- **Character**: 速
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: fast, speed, velocity
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 速度 (sù dù) — speed
- 迅速 (xùn sù) — rapid

## Example Sentences

- 他的速度很快。(Tā de sù dù hěn kuài.) — His speed is very fast.

## Notes

速 shows walk 辶 and 束 (to bind quickly), representing swift movement that happens in an instant.
