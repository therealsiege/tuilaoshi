# 颤 To Tremble

- **Character**: 颤
- **Pinyin**: chàn
- **Meaning**: to tremble, to shiver (also zhàn)
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to tremble, to shiver
- 发颤 (fā chàn) — to tremble

## Example Sentences

- 他冷得直颤抖。(Tā lěng de zhí chàn dǒu.) — He was shivering from cold.

## Notes

颤 simplified from 顫. As chàn means tremble, as zhàn means vibrate. Page/head radical 页 representing shaking.
