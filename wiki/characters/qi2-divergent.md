# 歧 Divergent, branching, discriminate

- **Character**: 歧
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: divergent, branching, discriminate
- **Radical**: 止 (stop)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 歧视 (qí shì) — to discriminate
- 分歧 (fēn qí) — divergence, difference

## Example Sentences

- 种族歧视是一个严重的社会问题。(Zhǒng zú qí shì shì yī ge yán zhòng de shè huì wèn tí.) — Racial discrimination is a serious social problem.

## Notes

Indicates branching or divergence. 歧途 means wrong path. Common in social and moral contexts.
