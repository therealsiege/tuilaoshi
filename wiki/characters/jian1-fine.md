# 缣 Fine Silk

- **Character**: 缣
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: fine silk
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缣帛 (jiān bó) — fine silk
- 缣素 (jiān sù) — white silk

## Example Sentences

- 古人在缣帛上书写。(Gǔ rén zài jiān bó shàng shū xiě.) — The ancients wrote on fine silk.

## Notes

缣 simplified from 縑. Silk 纟 with 兼 (to combine), double-thread silk.
