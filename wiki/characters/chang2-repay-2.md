# 偿 Repay, compensate

- **Character**: 偿
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: repay, compensate
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 补偿 (bǔcháng) — compensate, make up
- 赔偿 (péicháng) — compensate, pay damages

## Example Sentences

- 公司赔偿了他的损失。(Gōngsī péicháng le tā de sǔnshī.) — The company compensated him for his losses.

## Notes

Formal term for repayment or compensation.
