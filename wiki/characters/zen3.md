# 怎 How

- **Character**: 怎
- **Pinyin**: zěn
- **Meaning**: how, why
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 怎么 (zěn me) — how
- 怎样 (zěn yàng) — how, what kind

## Example Sentences

- 这个怎么做？(Zhè ge zěn me zuò?) — How do you do this?

## Notes

怎 almost always appears with 么 as 怎么 (how) or 样 as 怎样 (what kind/how).
