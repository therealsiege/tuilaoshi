# 挠 Scratch

- **Character**: 挠
- **Pinyin**: náo
- **Meaning**: scratch
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挠痒 (náo yǎng) — scratch an itch
- 挠头 (náo tóu) — scratch head (puzzled)

## Example Sentences

- 蚊子咬了我，很痒，我想挠。(Wénzi yǎo le wǒ, hěn yǎng, wǒ xiǎng náo.) — A mosquito bit me, it's very itchy, I want to scratch.

## Notes

Scratching action. Hand radical. 挠头 metaphorically means puzzled. Common for relief from itching.
