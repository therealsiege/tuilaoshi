# 护 Protect

- **Character**: 护
- **Pinyin**: hù
- **Meaning**: to protect, guard
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 保护 (bǎo hù) — to protect
- 护具 (hù jù) — protective gear

## Example Sentences

- 运动员穿戴护具防止受伤。(Yùndòngyuán chuāndài hùjù fángzhǐ shòushāng.) — Athletes wear protective gear to prevent injury.

## Notes

Protection; used for guards and protective equipment
