# 注 To pour, focus, annotate

- **Character**: 注
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to pour, focus, annotate
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 注意 (zhù yì) — to pay attention
- 关注 (guān zhù) — to follow with interest, pay attention to

## Example Sentences

- 请注意安全。(Qǐng zhùyì ānquán.) — Please pay attention to safety.

## Notes

Very common character for attention, focus, and concentration.
