# 淀 Shallow lake, to settle, sediment

- **Character**: 淀
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: shallow lake, to settle, sediment
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 沉淀 (chén diàn) — to settle, sediment
- 淀粉 (diàn fěn) — starch

## Example Sentences

- 液体中有沉淀物。(Yètǐ zhōng yǒu chéndiàn wù.) — There is sediment in the liquid.

## Notes

Can refer to sediment or shallow lakes.
