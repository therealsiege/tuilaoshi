# 系 System, tie, department

- **Character**: 系
- **Pinyin**: xì
- **Meaning**: system, tie, department
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 太阳系 (tài yáng xì) — solar system
- 星系 (xīng xì) — galaxy

## Example Sentences

- 太阳系有八大行星。(Tài yáng xì yǒu bā dà xíng xīng.) — The solar system has eight major planets.

## Notes

Used for systems in astronomy and organization
