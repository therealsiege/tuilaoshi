# 雾 Fog

- **Character**: 雾
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: fog, mist
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 大雾 (dà wù) — heavy fog
- 雾气 (wù qì) — fog, mist

## Example Sentences

- 今天有大雾。(Jīn tiān yǒu dà wù.) — There is heavy fog today.

## Notes

雾 means fog or mist. Used in weather descriptions, 雾霾 (smog), 迷雾 (heavy fog).
