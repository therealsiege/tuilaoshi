# 笙 笙笙笙

- **Character**: 笙
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: 笙笙笙
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 笙歌 (shēng gē) — 笙笙笙笙
- 芦笙 (lú shēng) — 笙笙

## Example Sentences

- 宴会上笙歌悠扬。(Yàn huì shàng shēng gē yōu yáng.) — At the banquet, the music of reed pipes and songs was melodious.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese reed wind instrument.
