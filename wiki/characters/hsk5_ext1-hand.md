# 递 Hand Over, Pass, 递增 Increase Progressively

- **Character**: 递
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: hand over, pass, 递增 increase progressively
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 递增 (dì zēng) — increase progressively
- 递减 (dì jiǎn) — decrease progressively
- 传递 (chuán dì) — transmit, pass on

## Example Sentences

- 请把盐递给我。(Qǐng bǎ yán dì gěi wǒ.) — Please pass me the salt.

## Notes

Common verb for passing or handing things. 递增/递减 are technical terms for progressive increase/decrease in statistics.
