# 候 Time/Wait

- **Character**: 候
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: to wait, time, season
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 时候 (shí hòu) — time, moment
- 气候 (qì hòu) — climate

## Example Sentences

- 什么时候出发？(Shén me shí hòu chū fā?) — When do we leave?

## Notes

候 means 'to wait' or 'time/season.' Almost always appears in compounds: 时候, 气候, 等候.
