# 雨 Rain

- **Character**: 雨
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: rain
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 下雨 (xià yǔ) — to rain
- 雨天 (yǔ tiān) — rainy day

## Example Sentences

- 今天下雨了。(Jīn tiān xià yǔ le.) — It's raining today.

## Notes

雨 pictograph showing raindrops falling from clouds. Used as radical in weather characters.
