# 雅 Elegant, refined

- **Character**: 雅
- **Pinyin**: yǎ
- **Meaning**: elegant, refined
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 优雅 (yōu yǎ) — elegant, graceful
- 雅致 (yǎ zhì) — elegant, refined

## Example Sentences

- 她举止很优雅。(Tā jǔzhǐ hěn yōuyǎ.) — Her manner is very elegant.

## Notes

Elegance and refinement in taste and behavior.
