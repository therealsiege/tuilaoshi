# 恐 Terror

- **Character**: 恐
- **Pinyin**: kǒng
- **Meaning**: fear, terror
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear, dread
- 恐怖 (kǒng bù) — terror, horror
- 恐慌 (kǒng huāng) — panic

## Example Sentences

- 恐慌发作很可怕。(Kǒng huāng fā zuò hěn kě pà.) — Panic attacks are very frightening.

## Notes

Fear emotion; appears in anxiety disorders.
