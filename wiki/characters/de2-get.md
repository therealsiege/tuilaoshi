# 得 Gain

- **Character**: 得
- **Pinyin**: dé
- **Meaning**: to get, to obtain, to gain
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 一举两得 (yī jǔ liǎng dé) — kill two birds with one stone
- 得到 (dé dào) — to obtain

## Example Sentences

- 这个方法一举两得。(Zhège fāngfǎ yī jǔ liǎng dé.) — This method kills two birds with one stone.

## Notes

In 一举两得, 得 means to gain; also read de as a complement particle or děi meaning must.
