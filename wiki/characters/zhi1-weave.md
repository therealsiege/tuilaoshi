# 织 To Weave

- **Character**: 织
- **Pinyin**: zhī
- **Meaning**: to weave, knit
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 编织 (biān zhī) — to weave, knit
- 组织 (zǔ zhī) — to organize, organization

## Example Sentences

- 她在织毛衣。(Tā zài zhī máoyī.) — She is knitting a sweater.

## Notes

织 combines silk 糹 with 只 (only), representing the weaving of threads.
