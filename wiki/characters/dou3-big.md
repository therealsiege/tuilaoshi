# 斗 Dipper

- **Character**: 斗
- **Pinyin**: dǒu
- **Meaning**: Big Dipper, measure
- **Radical**: 斗(dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 北斗 (běi dǒu) — Big Dipper
- 斗牛 (dǒu niú) — bullfighting

## Example Sentences

- 北斗七星是北半球最显著的星座。(Běidǒu qī xīng shì běi bànqiú zuì xiǎnzhù de xīngzuò.) — The Big Dipper is the most prominent constellation in the Northern Hemisphere.

## Notes

斗 (dǒu) is the Big Dipper constellation or a measure. As dòu it means to fight or struggle.
