# 丞 Assist, deputy, minister

- **Character**: 丞
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: assist, deputy, minister
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 丞相 (chéng xiàng) — prime minister
- 县丞 (xiàn chéng) — county magistrate's assistant

## Example Sentences

- 丞相是古代的重要官职。(Chéng xiàng shì gǔ dài de zhòng yào guān zhí.) — Prime minister was an important official position in ancient times.

## Notes

Ancient official title, especially in 丞相 (prime minister)
