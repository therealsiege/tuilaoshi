# 窥 To peep, to spy on, to pry

- **Character**: 窥
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: to peep, to spy on, to pry
- **Radical**: 穴(cave)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 窥探 (kuī tàn) — to pry, to spy
- 窥视 (kuī shì) — to peep at, to spy on

## Example Sentences

- 不要窥探别人的隐私。(Bùyào kuītàn biérén de yǐnsī.) — Don't pry into other people's privacy.

## Notes

Implies secretive or improper looking.
