# 乘 To Ride

- **Character**: 乘
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to ride, to multiply
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 乘车 (chéng chē) — to ride in a vehicle
- 乘客 (chéng kè) — passenger

## Example Sentences

- 我每天乘地铁上班。(Wǒ měi tiān chéng dì tiě shàng bān.) — I take the subway to work every day.

## Notes

乘 shows a person climbing onto something tall, representing mounting or riding. Also means multiplication in mathematics.
