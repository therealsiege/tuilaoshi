# 禀 Report, endowed with

- **Character**: 禀
- **Pinyin**: bǐng
- **Meaning**: report, endowed with
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 禀报 (bǐngbào) — report to superior
- 禀赋 (bǐngfù) — natural endowment

## Example Sentences

- 他禀赋异常聪慧。(Tā bǐngfù yìcháng cōnghuì.) — He is endowed with exceptional intelligence.

## Notes

Classical term for reporting to superiors or natural gifts.
