# 鸾 Mythical bird, phoenix

- **Character**: 鸾
- **Pinyin**: luán
- **Meaning**: mythical bird, phoenix
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 鸾凤 (luán fèng) — mythical phoenixes
- 鸾鸟 (luán niǎo) — mythical bird

## Example Sentences

- 传说中鸾凤和鸣，象征美满婚姻。(Chuán shuō zhōng luán fèng hé míng, xiàng zhēng měi mǎn hūn yīn.) — In legend, phoenixes singing together symbolize a happy marriage.

## Notes

Mythical bird often paired with phoenix
