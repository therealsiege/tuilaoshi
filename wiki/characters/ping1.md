# 乒 Ping

- **Character**: 乒
- **Pinyin**: pīng
- **Meaning**: ping (sound), table tennis
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 乒乓球 (pīng pāng qiú) — table tennis, ping pong
- 乒乓 (pīng pāng) — ping pong (sound)

## Example Sentences

- 中国的乒乓球队很强。(Zhōngguó de pīngpāng qiú duì hěn qiáng.) — China's table tennis team is very strong.

## Notes

Onomatopoeia for the sound of table tennis; always used with 乓
