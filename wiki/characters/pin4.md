# 聘 To engage, hire, betroth

- **Character**: 聘
- **Pinyin**: pìn
- **Meaning**: to engage, hire, betroth
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 聘请 (pìn qǐng) — to engage, invite
- 招聘 (zhāo pìn) — to recruit

## Example Sentences

- 学校聘请了一位著名教授。(Xué xiào pìn qǐng le yī wèi zhù míng jiào shòu.) — The school engaged a famous professor.

## Notes

Formal term for hiring or engaging. 聘书 means letter of appointment. More formal than 雇.
