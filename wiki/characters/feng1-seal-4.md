# 封 Seal, confer title, fief

- **Character**: 封
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: seal, confer title, fief
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 封建 (fēng jiàn) — feudal
- 分封 (fēn fēng) — enfeoff

## Example Sentences

- 周朝实行分封制度。(Zhōu cháo shíxíng fēnfēng zhìdù.) — The Zhou Dynasty implemented the feudal system.

## Notes

Means to seal, confer titles, or grant fiefs. The character shows hands planting a tree to mark territory boundaries.
