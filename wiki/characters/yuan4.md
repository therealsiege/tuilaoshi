# 院 Courtyard

- **Character**: 院
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: courtyard, institution
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 医院 (yī yuàn) — hospital
- 学院 (xué yuàn) — college

## Example Sentences

- 医院在哪里？(Yī yuàn zài nǎ lǐ?) — Where is the hospital?

## Notes

院 refers to an enclosed yard or an institution. 法院 (court), 电影院 (cinema).
