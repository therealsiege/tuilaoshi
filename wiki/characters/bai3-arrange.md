# 摆 To Arrange

- **Character**: 摆
- **Pinyin**: bǎi
- **Meaning**: to arrange, to place, to swing
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摆放 (bǎi fàng) — to arrange, to display
- 摆脱 (bǎi tuō) — to break away from

## Example Sentences

- 桌子摆好了。(Zhuō zi bǎi hǎo le.) — The table is set.

## Notes

摆 involves arranging or positioning objects deliberately. Can also mean to swing or sway.
