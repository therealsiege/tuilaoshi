# 望 Hope

- **Character**: 望
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: to gaze, to hope, to expect
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 喜出望外 (xǐ chū wàng wài) — pleasantly surprised
- 希望 (xī wàng) — to hope

## Example Sentences

- 我们都望着远方。(Wǒmen dōu wàng zhe yuǎnfāng.) — We all gazed into the distance.

## Notes

In 喜出望外, 望 means expectation; joy that goes beyond what was expected.
