# 册 册子, volume, register

- **Character**: 册
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: 册子, volume, register
- **Radical**: 丨 (line)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 手册 (shǒu cè) — handbook, manual
- 画册 (huà cè) — album, picture book

## Example Sentences

- 请参考员工手册。(Qǐng cān kǎo yuán gōng shǒu cè.) — Please refer to the employee handbook.

## Notes

Used for booklets, manuals, and registers. 注册 means register/registration.
