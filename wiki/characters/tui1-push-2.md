# 推 Push, promote

- **Character**: 推
- **Pinyin**: tuī
- **Meaning**: push, promote
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 推广 (tuī guǎng) — promote, popularize
- 推销 (tuī xiāo) — promote sales, peddle

## Example Sentences

- 公司正在推广新产品。(Gōng sī zhèng zài tuī guǎng xīn chǎn pǐn.) — The company is promoting new products.

## Notes

推介 means promote/recommend. 推出 means launch.
