# 估 Estimate, appraise

- **Character**: 估
- **Pinyin**: gū
- **Meaning**: estimate, appraise
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 估计 (gū jì) — estimate, reckon
- 评估 (píng gū) — assess, evaluate

## Example Sentences

- 需要评估资产价值。(Xū yào píng gū zī chǎn jià zhí.) — Need to assess asset value.

## Notes

估价 means appraise/valuation. 估算 means rough estimate.
