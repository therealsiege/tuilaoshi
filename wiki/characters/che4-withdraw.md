# 撤 Withdraw, remove, dismiss

- **Character**: 撤
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: withdraw, remove, dismiss
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撤销 (chèxiāo) — cancel, revoke
- 撤诉 (chèsù) — withdraw a lawsuit

## Example Sentences

- 原告撤诉了。(Yuángào chèsù le.) — The plaintiff withdrew the lawsuit.

## Notes

Formal term for withdrawal and dismissal.
