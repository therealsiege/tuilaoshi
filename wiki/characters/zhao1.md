# 招 Beckon, recruit, invite

- **Character**: 招
- **Pinyin**: zhāo
- **Meaning**: beckon, recruit, invite
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 招标 (zhāobiāo) — invite bids
- 招聘 (zhāopìn) — recruit

## Example Sentences

- 政府招标建设项目。(Zhèngfǔ zhāobiāo jiànshè xiàngmù.) — The government invited bids for construction projects.

## Notes

Used for recruiting, inviting, and beckoning.
