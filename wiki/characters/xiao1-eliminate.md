# 消 Eliminate

- **Character**: 消
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: eliminate, digest
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 消毒 (xiāo dú) — to disinfect
- 消除 (xiāo chú) — to eliminate
- 消化 (xiāo huà) — to digest

## Example Sentences

- 手术室需要严格消毒。(Shǒu shù shì xū yào yán gé xiāo dú.) — Operating rooms require strict disinfection.

## Notes

Elimination; disinfection process.
