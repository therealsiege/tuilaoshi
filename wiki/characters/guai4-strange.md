# 怪 Strange

- **Character**: 怪
- **Pinyin**: guài
- **Meaning**: strange, to blame, monster
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奇怪 (qí guài) — strange
- 责怪 (zé guài) — to blame

## Example Sentences

- 别怪我没提醒你。(Bié guài wǒ méi tí xǐng nǐ.) — Don't blame me for not reminding you.

## Notes

怪 shows heart 心 and 圣 (holy/strange), representing unusual feelings or phenomena.
