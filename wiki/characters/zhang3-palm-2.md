# 掌 Palm

- **Character**: 掌
- **Pinyin**: zhǎng
- **Meaning**: palm, to control
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 手掌 (shǒu zhǎng) — palm
- 鼓掌 (gǔ zhǎng) — to applaud

## Example Sentences

- 武术中掌法很重要。(Wǔshù zhōng zhǎngfǎ hěn zhòngyào.) — Palm techniques are very important in martial arts.

## Notes

Palm of hand; martial arts palm strikes
