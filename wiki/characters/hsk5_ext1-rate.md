# 率 Rate, Ratio, 效率 Efficiency

- **Character**: 率
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: rate, ratio, 效率 efficiency
- **Radical**: 玄 (dark)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 效率 (xiào lǜ) — efficiency
- 比率 (bǐ lǜ) — ratio
- 概率 (gài lǜ) — probability

## Example Sentences

- 我们要提高工作效率。(Wǒmen yào tígāo gōngzuò xiàolǜ.) — We must improve work efficiency.

## Notes

Also pronounced shuài when meaning 'lead' (as in 率领 - lead). As lǜ, commonly used for rates and ratios in statistics and efficiency.
