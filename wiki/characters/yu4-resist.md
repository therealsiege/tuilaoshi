# 御 Resist, imperial, drive

- **Character**: 御
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: resist, imperial, drive
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 抵御 (dǐ yù) — resist, withstand

## Example Sentences

- 这件衣服可以御寒。(Zhè jiàn yī fu kě yǐ yù hán.) — This clothing can keep out the cold.
