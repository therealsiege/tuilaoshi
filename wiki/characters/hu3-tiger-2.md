# 虎 Tiger

- **Character**: 虎
- **Pinyin**: hǔ
- **Meaning**: tiger
- **Radical**: 虍 (tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 老虎 (lǎo hǔ) — tiger
- 虎年 (hǔ nián) — Year of the Tiger

## Example Sentences

- 老虎是百兽之王。(Lǎo hǔ shì bǎi shòu zhī wáng.) — The tiger is king of all beasts.

## Notes

虎 is a pictograph. 老虎 is the colloquial form. 骑虎难下 = hard to back down once started.
