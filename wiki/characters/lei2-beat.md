# 擂 To Beat

- **Character**: 擂
- **Pinyin**: léi
- **Meaning**: to beat (drum), to grind
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 擂鼓 (léi gǔ) — to beat drums
- 擂台 (lèi tái) — arena

## Example Sentences

- 战鼓擂响，士气大振。(Zhàn gǔ léi xiǎng, shì qì dà zhèn.) — The war drums beat, morale soared.

## Notes

擂 emphasizes vigorous beating of drums; also read as lèi in 擂台.
