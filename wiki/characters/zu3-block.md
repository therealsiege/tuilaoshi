# 阻 To block, hinder, obstruct

- **Character**: 阻
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to block, hinder, obstruct
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 阻止 (zǔ zhǐ) — to prevent, stop
- 阻碍 (zǔ ài) — to hinder, obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 没有什么能阻止我们前进。(Méiyǒu shénme néng zǔzhǐ wǒmen qiánjìn.) — Nothing can stop us from moving forward.

## Notes

To block, hinder, or prevent.
