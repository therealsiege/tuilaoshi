# 闪 To Flash

- **Character**: 闪
- **Pinyin**: shǎn
- **Meaning**: to flash, dodge
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 闪电 (shǎn diàn) — lightning
- 闪光 (shǎn guāng) — to flash

## Example Sentences

- 天空中闪过一道闪电。(Tiānkōng zhōng shǎnguò yī dào shǎndiàn.) — A bolt of lightning flashed across the sky.

## Notes

闪 shows a person人 glimpsed through a door 门, representing quick flashing movement.
