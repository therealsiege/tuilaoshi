# 汤 Soup, decoction, hot water

- **Character**: 汤
- **Pinyin**: tāng
- **Meaning**: soup, decoction, hot water
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 汤药 (tāng yào) — decoction, medicinal soup
- 汤剂 (tāng jì) — decoction

## Example Sentences

- 这副汤药需要煎煮半小时。(Zhè fù tāng yào xūyào jiān zhǔ bàn xiǎoshí.) — This herbal decoction needs to be boiled for half an hour.

## Notes

Medicinal decoction made by boiling herbs. The most common form of traditional Chinese medicine preparation.
