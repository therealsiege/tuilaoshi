# 缥 Dim, pale blue

- **Character**: 缥
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: dim, pale blue
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缥缈 (piāomiǎo) — dimly discernible, vague
- 虚无缥缈 (xūwú piāomiǎo) — illusory, unreal

## Example Sentences

- 远山缥缈如画。(Yuǎn shān piāomiǎo rú huà.) — The distant mountains are dimly visible like a painting.

## Notes

Literary term for pale blue color or vagueness, always used with 缈.
