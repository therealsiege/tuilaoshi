# 奶 Milk

- **Character**: 奶
- **Pinyin**: nǎi
- **Meaning**: milk, breast
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 牛奶 (niú nǎi) — milk
- 奶茶 (nǎi chá) — milk tea

## Example Sentences

- 我每天喝牛奶。(Wǒ měi tiān hē niú nǎi.) — I drink milk every day.

## Notes

奶 means milk. 奶奶 = paternal grandmother. 奶粉 = milk powder.
