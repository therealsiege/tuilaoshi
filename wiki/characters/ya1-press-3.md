# 压 Press, pressure

- **Character**: 压
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: press, pressure
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 压力 (yā lì) — pressure
- 冲压 (chōng yā) — stamping, pressing

## Example Sentences

- 冲压机生产金属件。(Chōng yā jī shēng chǎn jīn shǔ jiàn.) — Stamping machines produce metal parts.

## Notes

Used in 压力 (pressure), 冲压 (stamping), 电压 (voltage)
