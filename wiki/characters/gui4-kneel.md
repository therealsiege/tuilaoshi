# 跪 To Kneel

- **Character**: 跪
- **Pinyin**: guì
- **Meaning**: to kneel
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跪下 (guì xià) — to kneel down
- 下跪 (xià guì) — to kneel

## Example Sentences

- 他跪下请求原谅。(Tā guì xià qǐng qiú yuán liàng.) — He knelt down to ask for forgiveness.

## Notes

跪 uses foot radical 足 with 危 (danger/precarious), depicting the precarious bent-knee position of kneeling.
