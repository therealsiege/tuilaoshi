# 铃 Bell

- **Character**: 铃
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: bell, ring
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 铃铛 (líng dāng) — small bell
- 门铃 (mén líng) — doorbell

## Example Sentences

- 风铃发出清脆的声音。(Fēng líng fā chū qīng cuì de shēng yīn.) — The wind chimes made a crisp sound.

## Notes

铃 refers to small bells that ring when shaken.
