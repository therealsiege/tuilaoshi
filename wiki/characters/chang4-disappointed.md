# 怅 Disappointed, distressed

- **Character**: 怅
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: disappointed, distressed
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 惆怅 (chóuchàng) — melancholy, disconsolate
- 怅惘 (chàngwǎng) — distracted and disappointed

## Example Sentences

- 他心中惆怅不已。(Tā xīnzhōng chóuchàng bùyǐ.) — He felt deeply melancholy.

## Notes

Literary term for disappointment or melancholy.
