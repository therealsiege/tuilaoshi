# 姑 Aunt

- **Character**: 姑
- **Pinyin**: gū
- **Meaning**: aunt (father's sister)
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 姑姑 (gū gu) — aunt (paternal)
- 姑娘 (gū niang) — girl, young woman

## Example Sentences

- 我姑姑很年轻。(Wǒ gū gu hěn nián qīng.) — My aunt is very young.

## Notes

姑 specifically father's sister. 姑娘 means girl/young woman (different usage).
