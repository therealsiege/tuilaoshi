# 仪 Instrument, appearance, ceremony

- **Character**: 仪
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: instrument, appearance, ceremony
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 仪器 (yí qì) — instrument, apparatus
- 仪式 (yí shì) — ceremony

## Example Sentences

- 实验室里有很多精密仪器。(Shí yàn shì lǐ yǒu hěn duō jīng mì yí qì.) — There are many precision instruments in the laboratory.

## Notes

Measuring and scientific devices
