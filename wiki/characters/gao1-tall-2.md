# 高 High

- **Character**: 高
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: tall, high
- **Radical**: 高 (tall)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 跳高 (tiào gāo) — high jump
- 高度 (gāo dù) — height

## Example Sentences

- 她擅长跳高。(Tā shàncháng tiàogāo.) — She is good at high jump.

## Notes

Height; high jump and altitude
