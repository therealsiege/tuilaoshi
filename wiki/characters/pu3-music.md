# 谱 Music Score

- **Character**: 谱
- **Pinyin**: pǔ
- **Meaning**: music score, genealogy
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 乐谱 (yuè pǔ) — music score
- 曲谱 (qǔ pǔ) — musical notation

## Example Sentences

- 他正在看乐谱。(Tā zhèng zài kàn yuè pǔ.) — He is reading the music score.

## Notes

谱 refers to musical scores or genealogies.
