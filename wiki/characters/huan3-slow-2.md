# 缓 Slow, postpone, defer

- **Character**: 缓
- **Pinyin**: huǎn
- **Meaning**: slow, postpone, defer
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缓慢 (huǎnmàn) — slow
- 缓刑 (huǎnxíng) — suspended sentence

## Example Sentences

- 法院判处缓刑。(Fǎyuàn pànchǔ huǎnxíng.) — The court imposed a suspended sentence.

## Notes

Used for slowing, postponing, and legal suspension.
