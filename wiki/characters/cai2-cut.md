# 裁 To Cut

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut, tailor, judge
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee, to judge
- 裁剪 (cái jiǎn) — to cut (cloth)

## Example Sentences

- 这块布要仔细裁剪。(Zhè kuài bù yào zǐxì cáijiǎn.) — This cloth needs to be cut carefully.

## Notes

裁 combines clothing 衣 with 𢦏 (hand with knife), representing cutting cloth for garments or making judgments.
