# 猪 Pig

- **Character**: 猪
- **Pinyin**: zhū
- **Meaning**: pig
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猪肉 (zhū ròu) — pork
- 小猪 (xiǎo zhū) — piglet

## Example Sentences

- 中国人常吃猪肉。(Zhōng guó rén cháng chī zhū ròu.) — Chinese people often eat pork.

## Notes

猪 with animal radical 犭. Pork is most common meat in Chinese cuisine.
