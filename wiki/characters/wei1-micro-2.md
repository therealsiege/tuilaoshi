# 微 Micro, tiny

- **Character**: 微
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: micro, tiny
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 微型 (wēi xíng) — miniature, micro
- 微处理器 (wēi chǔ lǐ qì) — microprocessor

## Example Sentences

- 微芯片很小。(Wēi xīn piàn hěn xiǎo.) — Microchips are very small.

## Notes

Used in 微型 (micro), 微处理器 (microprocessor), 微米 (micron)
