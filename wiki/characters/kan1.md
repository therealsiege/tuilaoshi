# 刊 Publication, print, periodical

- **Character**: 刊
- **Pinyin**: kān
- **Meaning**: publication, print, periodical
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 刊物 (kān wù) — publication
- 期刊 (qī kān) — periodical, journal

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章刊登在著名的学术期刊上。(Zhè piān wén zhāng kān dēng zài zhù míng de xué shù qī kān shàng.) — This article was published in a famous academic journal.

## Notes

Related to publishing and printing. 刊登 means to publish, 刊物 refers to publications.
