# 廊 Corridor, veranda, gallery

- **Character**: 廊
- **Pinyin**: láng
- **Meaning**: corridor, veranda, gallery
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 走廊 (zǒu láng) — corridor, hallway
- 画廊 (huà láng) — gallery

## Example Sentences

- 这座宫殿有一条长长的画廊。(Zhè zuò gōng diàn yǒu yī tiáo cháng cháng de huà láng.) — This palace has a long gallery.

## Notes

Common in traditional Chinese architecture.
