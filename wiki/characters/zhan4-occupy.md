# 占 To Occupy

- **Character**: 占
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: to occupy, constitute
- **Radical**: 卜 (divination)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 占领 (zhàn lǐng) — to occupy, seize
- 占据 (zhàn jù) — to occupy, hold

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题占用了我很多时间。(Zhège wèntí zhànyòng le wǒ hěn duō shíjiān.) — This problem took up a lot of my time.

## Notes

占 can also be read as zhān meaning 'to divine' or 'to observe'. The zhàn reading focuses on physical occupation.
