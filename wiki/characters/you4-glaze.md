# 釉 Glaze

- **Character**: 釉
- **Pinyin**: yòu
- **Meaning**: glaze (ceramics)
- **Radical**: 釆 (to distinguish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 釉料 (yòu liào) — glaze
- 上釉 (shàng yòu) — to glaze

## Example Sentences

- 这个花瓶上了釉。(Zhè gè huā píng shàng le yòu.) — This vase has been glazed.

## Notes

釉 shows釆 (distinguish) with oil/liquid, representing ceramic glaze coating.
