# 射 Shoot

- **Character**: 射
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: to shoot, emit
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 射门 (shè mén) — to shoot (at goal)
- 射箭 (shè jiàn) — archery

## Example Sentences

- 他射门得分了。(Tā shèmén défēn le.) — He scored with a shot.

## Notes

Shooting in sports; used in archery, shooting goals, etc.
