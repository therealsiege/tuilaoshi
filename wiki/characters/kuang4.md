# 况 Situation

- **Character**: 况
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: situation, condition, moreover
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 情况 (qíng kuàng) — situation, circumstances
- 况且 (kuàng qiě) — moreover, furthermore

## Example Sentences

- 你知道现在的情况吗？(Nǐ zhī dào xiàn zài de qíng kuàng ma?) — Do you know the current situation?

## Notes

况 appears in 情况 (situation) and 况且 (moreover). Essential for describing circumstances.
