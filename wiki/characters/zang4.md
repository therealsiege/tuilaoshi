# 脏 Organs

- **Character**: 脏
- **Pinyin**: zàng
- **Meaning**: internal organs
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 内脏 (nèi zàng) — internal organs
- 心脏 (xīn zàng) — heart

## Example Sentences

- 心脏是重要的内脏器官。(Xīn zàng shì zhòng yào de nèi zàng qì guān.) — The heart is an important internal organ.

## Notes

脏 as zàng means organs. As zāng (different character 脏) it means dirty. Context determines meaning.
