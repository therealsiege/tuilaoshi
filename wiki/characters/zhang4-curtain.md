# 帐 Curtain, tent, account

- **Character**: 帐
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: curtain, tent, account
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 帐篷 (zhàng peng) — tent
- 蚊帐 (wén zhàng) — mosquito net

## Example Sentences

- 我们在山上搭起了帐篷。(Wǒ men zài shān shàng dā qǐ le zhàng peng.) — We pitched a tent on the mountain.

## Notes

Means tent or curtain (with 巾 cloth radical). Different from 账 (account) which has 贝 shell radical.
