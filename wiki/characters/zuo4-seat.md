# 座 Seat

- **Character**: 座
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: seat, base, classifier for buildings
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 插座 (chā zuò) — socket
- 底座 (dǐ zuò) — base, pedestal

## Example Sentences

- 这个插座没电。(Zhè ge chā zuò méi diàn.) — This socket has no power.

## Notes

座 for seat/base naturally extends to electrical sockets and mounting bases.
