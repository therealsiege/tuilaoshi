# 菊 Chrysanthemum

- **Character**: 菊
- **Pinyin**: jú
- **Meaning**: chrysanthemum
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 菊花 (jú huā) — chrysanthemum
- 野菊 (yě jú) — wild chrysanthemum

## Example Sentences

- 秋天是赏菊的季节。(Qiū tiān shì shǎng jú de jì jié.) — Autumn is the season for viewing chrysanthemums.

## Notes

菊 shows grass 艹 with匊 (to hold in both hands), representing the precious flower.
