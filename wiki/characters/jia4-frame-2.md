# 架 Frame

- **Character**: 架
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: frame, rack, to架 架架架
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 框架 (kuàng jià) — framework
- 书架 (shū jià) — bookshelf

## Example Sentences

- 书放在书架上。(Shū fàng zài shū jià shàng.) — The books are on the bookshelf.

## Notes

架 with wood radical for架架 wooden frame, also架 measure word for planes and machines.
