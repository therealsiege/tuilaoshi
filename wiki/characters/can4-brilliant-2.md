# 灿 Brilliant

- **Character**: 灿
- **Pinyin**: càn
- **Meaning**: brilliant, bright, splendid
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灿烂 (càn làn) — brilliant, splendid
- 金灿灿 (jīn càn càn) — golden and shining

## Example Sentences

- 阳光灿烂。(Yáng guāng càn làn.) — The sunshine is brilliant.

## Notes

灿烂 means brilliant or splendid. 灿烂的笑容 = a brilliant smile.
