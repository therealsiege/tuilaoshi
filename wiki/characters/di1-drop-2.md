# 滴 Drop

- **Character**: 滴
- **Pinyin**: dī
- **Meaning**: drop, to drip, measure word
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 滴水 (dī shuǐ) — to drip water
- 一滴 (yī dī) — one drop

## Example Sentences

- 水龙头在滴水。(Shuǐ lóng tóu zài dī shuǐ.) — The faucet is dripping.

## Notes

滴 means drop or to drip. Also measure word for drops of liquid (一滴水 one drop of water).
