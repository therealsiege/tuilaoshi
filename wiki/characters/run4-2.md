# 润 润滑, moist, profit

- **Character**: 润
- **Pinyin**: rùn
- **Meaning**: 润滑, moist, profit
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 利润 (lì rùn) — profit
- 润滑 (rùn huá) — 润滑

## Example Sentences

- 利润率达到百分之二十。(Lì rùn lǜ dá dào bǎi fēn zhī èr shí.) — The profit margin reached 20 percent.

## Notes

盈利 and 利润 both mean profit. 润色 means polish/refine.
