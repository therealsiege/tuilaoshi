# 厅 Hall

- **Character**: 厅
- **Pinyin**: tīng
- **Meaning**: hall, office, department
- **Radical**: 厂 (factory)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 餐厅 (cān tīng) — dining hall, restaurant
- 大厅 (dà tīng) — lobby, hall

## Example Sentences

- 会议在大厅举行。(Huì yì zài dà tīng jǔ xíng.) — The meeting is held in the hall.

## Notes

厅 is simplified from 廳. Used for halls and provincial-level departments.
