# 铁 Iron

- **Character**: 铁
- **Pinyin**: tiě
- **Meaning**: iron
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 铁路 (tiě lù) — railway
- 钢铁 (gāng tiě) — steel

## Example Sentences

- 铁是地壳中含量最多的金属元素之一。(Tiě shì dìqiào zhōng hánliàng zuì duō de jīnshǔ yuánsù zhī yī.) — Iron is one of the most abundant metallic elements in the Earth's crust.

## Notes

铁 is iron, crucial metal for construction, tools, and biological processes like oxygen transport.
