# 片 Slice

- **Character**: 片
- **Pinyin**: piàn
- **Meaning**: slice, piece, tablet, photograph
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 照片 (zhào piàn) — photograph
- 一片 (yī piàn) — a slice, a stretch of

## Example Sentences

- 给我看看你的照片。(Gěi wǒ kàn kàn nǐ de zhào piàn.) — Show me your photos.

## Notes

片 depicts half of a tree split vertically. As a measure word, it's used for flat, thin objects or expanses.
