# 暧 Ambiguous

- **Character**: 暧
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: ambiguous, dim
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 暧昧 (ài mèi) — ambiguous
- 暧昧不明 (ài mèi bù míng) — vague and unclear

## Example Sentences

- 他们的关系很暧昧。(Tā men de guān xì hěn ài mèi.) — Their relationship is ambiguous.

## Notes

暧昧 means ambiguous or vague, often used to describe unclear romantic relationships.
