# 估 To Estimate

- **Character**: 估
- **Pinyin**: gū
- **Meaning**: to estimate, to reckon
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 估计 (gū jì) — to estimate
- 评估 (píng gū) — to evaluate

## Example Sentences

- 我估计明天会下雨。(Wǒ gū jì míng tiān huì xià yǔ.) — I estimate it will rain tomorrow.

## Notes

估计 is very common, used like 'I reckon/guess' in spoken Chinese.
