# 瑙 Agate

- **Character**: 瑙
- **Pinyin**: nǎo
- **Meaning**: agate (in 玛瑙)
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 玛瑙 (mǎ nǎo) — agate

## Example Sentences

- 这个手镯是玛瑙做的。(Zhè ge shǒu zhuó shì mǎ nǎo zuò de.) — This bracelet is made of agate.

## Notes

瑙 has jade 玉 with 囟 (brain), variegated gemstone.
