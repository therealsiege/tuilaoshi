# 额 额度, forehead, amount

- **Character**: 额
- **Pinyin**: é
- **Meaning**: 额度, forehead, amount
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 金额 (jīn é) — sum, amount
- 额外 (é wài) — extra, additional

## Example Sentences

- 交易金额很大。(Jiāo yì jīn é hěn dà.) — The transaction amount is very large.

## Notes

限额 means quota/limit. 名额 means quota of people.
