# 拍 To Clap

- **Character**: 拍
- **Pinyin**: pāi
- **Meaning**: to clap, beat (music)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拍手 (pāi shǒu) — to clap hands
- 节拍 (jié pāi) — beat, rhythm

## Example Sentences

- 大家一起拍手。(Dà jiā yì qǐ pāi shǒu.) — Everyone claps together.

## Notes

拍 has hand 扌 with 白 (white), striking with the hand.
