# 系 To Tie

- **Character**: 系
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to tie, to bind, to系系, department
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 联系 (lián xì) — to contact, connection
- 关系 (guān xì) — relationship

## Example Sentences

- 请系好安全带。(Qǐng jì hǎo ān quán dài.) — Please fasten your seatbelt.

## Notes

系 can be jì (to tie, to fasten) or xì (system, department). As jì, used for tying shoelaces, seatbelts.
