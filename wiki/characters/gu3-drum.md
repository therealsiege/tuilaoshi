# 鼓 Drum, beat, bulge

- **Character**: 鼓
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: drum, beat, bulge
- **Radical**: 鼓 (drum)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鼓励 (gǔ lì) — encourage
- 打鼓 (dǎ gǔ) — beat a drum

## Example Sentences

- 鼓声震天，气势磅礴。(Gǔ shēng zhèn tiān, qì shì páng bó.) — The drum sounds shake the sky with magnificent momentum.

## Notes

Can be noun (drum) or verb (beat, encourage, bulge).
