# 砖 Brick

- **Character**: 砖
- **Pinyin**: zhuān
- **Meaning**: brick
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砖头 (zhuān tou) — brick
- 砖块 (zhuān kuài) — brick

## Example Sentences

- 工人们正在用砖砌墙。(Gōngrénmen zhèngzài yòng zhuān qì qiáng.) — The workers are building a wall with bricks.

## Notes

砖 refers to bricks, which are fired clay blocks used in construction.
