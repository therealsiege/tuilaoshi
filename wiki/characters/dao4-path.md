# 道 Path, way, road

- **Character**: 道
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: path, way, road
- **Radical**: 辵 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 轨道 (guǐ dào) — orbit, track
- 通道 (tōng dào) — passage, channel

## Example Sentences

- 行星沿着固定的轨道运行。(Xíng xīng yán zhe gù dìng de guǐ dào yùn xíng.) — Planets move along fixed orbits.

## Notes

Path or way, used in orbit terminology
