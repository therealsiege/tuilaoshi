# 销 Sell, market

- **Character**: 销
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: sell, market
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 销售 (xiāo shòu) — sell, sales
- 促销 (cù xiāo) — promote sales

## Example Sentences

- 商店正在促销打折。(Shāng diàn zhèng zài cù xiāo dǎ zhé.) — The store is having a promotional sale.

## Notes

销量 is sales volume. 畅销 means sell well/bestselling.
