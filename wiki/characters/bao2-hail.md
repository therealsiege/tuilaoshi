# 雹 Hail

- **Character**: 雹
- **Pinyin**: báo
- **Meaning**: hail
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 冰雹 (bīng báo) — hail
- 雹灾 (báo zāi) — hail disaster

## Example Sentences

- 昨天下了一场大冰雹。(Zuó tiān xià le yì chǎng dà bīng báo.) — There was heavy hail yesterday.

## Notes

雹 has rain 雨 with 包 (to wrap), ice wrapped in layers.
