# 泄 To leak, to divulge, to let out

- **Character**: 泄
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to leak, to divulge, to let out
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泄漏 (xiè lòu) — to leak, to divulge
- 发泄 (fā xiè) — to give vent to, to let off

## Example Sentences

- 不要泄露公司机密。(Bùyào xièlòu gōngsī jīmì.) — Don't leak company secrets.

## Notes

Often used for revealing information or releasing emotions.
