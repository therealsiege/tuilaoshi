# 愧 Ashamed

- **Character**: 愧
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: ashamed, guilty
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cánkuì) — ashamed
- 羞愧 (xiūkuì) — ashamed

## Example Sentences

- 我对他感到很惭愧。(Wǒ duì tā gǎndào hěn cánkuì.) — I feel very ashamed towards him.

## Notes

Moral emotion. Heart radical indicates feeling. 惭愧 and 羞愧 are common compounds. Cultural importance.
