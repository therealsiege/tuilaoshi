# 波 Wave

- **Character**: 波
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: wave
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 电波 (diàn bō) — radio wave
- 波长 (bō cháng) — wavelength

## Example Sentences

- 电磁波传播速度很快。(Diàn cí bō chuán bō sù dù hěn kuài.) — Electromagnetic waves propagate quickly.

## Notes

Used in 电波 (radio wave), 波长 (wavelength), 脉冲波 (pulse wave)
