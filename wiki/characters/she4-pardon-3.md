# 赦 Pardon, amnesty

- **Character**: 赦
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: pardon, amnesty
- **Radical**: 赤 (red)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 特赦 (tèshè) — special pardon
- 大赦 (dàshè) — general amnesty

## Example Sentences

- 政府宣布大赦。(Zhèngfǔ xuānbù dàshè.) — The government announced a general amnesty.

## Notes

Legal term for pardon and amnesty.
