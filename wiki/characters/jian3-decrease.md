# 减 To Reduce

- **Character**: 减
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: to decrease, to reduce, to subtract
- **Radical**: 冰 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 减少 (jiǎn shǎo) — to reduce, to decrease
- 减肥 (jiǎn féi) — to lose weight

## Example Sentences

- 我要减肥。(Wǒ yào jiǎn féi.) — I need to lose weight.

## Notes

减 means to decrease or subtract. 减肥 (literally 'reduce fat') is a common word for dieting/losing weight.
