# 疗 Treat, cure, therapy

- **Character**: 疗
- **Pinyin**: liáo
- **Meaning**: treat, cure, therapy
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 治疗 (zhì liáo) — treat, cure
- 疗法 (liáo fǎ) — therapy

## Example Sentences

- 这种疗法很有效。(Zhè zhǒng liáo fǎ hěn yǒu xiào.) — This therapy is very effective.
