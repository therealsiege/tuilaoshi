# 噪 Noise, chirp, clamor

- **Character**: 噪
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: noise, chirp, clamor
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 噪音 (zào yīn) — noise
- 鼓噪 (gǔ zào) — clamor

## Example Sentences

- 城市的噪音污染很严重。(Chéng shì de zào yīn wū rǎn hěn yán zhòng.) — Noise pollution in cities is very serious.

## Notes

Unwanted sound and environmental noise
