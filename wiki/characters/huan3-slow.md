# 缓 Slow, postpone, relieve

- **Character**: 缓
- **Pinyin**: huǎn
- **Meaning**: slow, postpone, relieve
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缓慢 (huǎn màn) — slow
- 缓解 (huǎn jiě) — alleviate

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要缓解压力。(Wǒ men xū yào huǎn jiě yā lì.) — We need to alleviate stress.

## Notes

Slow pace and relief from tension
