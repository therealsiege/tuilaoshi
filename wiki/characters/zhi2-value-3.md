# 值 Value, worth, be on duty

- **Character**: 值
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: value, worth, be on duty
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 价值 (jiàzhí) — value, worth
- 值得 (zhíde) — be worth, deserve

## Example Sentences

- 这件事很值得研究。(Zhè jiàn shì hěn zhíde yánjiū.) — This matter is worth studying.

## Notes

Common for value, worth, and being on duty.
