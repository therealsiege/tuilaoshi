# 错 Wrong

- **Character**: 错
- **Pinyin**: cuò
- **Meaning**: wrong, mistake, error
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 不错 (bú cuò) — not bad, pretty good
- 错误 (cuò wù) — mistake, error

## Example Sentences

- 这道题你做错了。(Zhè dào tí nǐ zuò cuò le.) — You got this question wrong.

## Notes

不错 literally means 'not wrong' but is used as a compliment meaning 'not bad, quite good.'
