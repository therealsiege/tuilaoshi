# 淋 To drench, pour, filter

- **Character**: 淋
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: to drench, pour, filter
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淋浴 (lín yù) — shower
- 淋漓 (lín lí) — dripping wet

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢淋浴，不喜欢泡澡。(Wǒ xǐhuan línyù, bù xǐhuan pàozǎo.) — I prefer showers, not baths.

## Notes

To drench or pour water, as in showering.
