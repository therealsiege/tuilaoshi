# 锯 Saw

- **Character**: 锯
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: saw, to saw
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锯子 (jù zi) — saw
- 锯木头 (jù mù tou) — to saw wood

## Example Sentences

- 他用锯子锯木头。(Tā yòng jù zi jù mù tou.) — He saws wood with a saw.

## Notes

锯 simplified from 鋸. Metal 钅 with 居 (to reside), metal cutting tool.
