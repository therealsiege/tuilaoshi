# 署 Sign, office, arrange

- **Character**: 署
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: sign, office, arrange
- **Radical**: 网 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 签署 (qiān shǔ) — to sign officially
- 部署 (bù shǔ) — to deploy, arrange

## Example Sentences

- 政府部署了新的安全措施。(Zhèng fǔ bù shǔ le xīn de ān quán cuò shī.) — The government deployed new security measures.

## Notes

More formal than 签. 署名 means to sign one's name. 警署 means police station.
