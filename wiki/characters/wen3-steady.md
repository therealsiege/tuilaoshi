# 稳 Steady

- **Character**: 稳
- **Pinyin**: wěn
- **Meaning**: steady, stable
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 稳定 (wěn dìng) — stable, steady
- 平稳 (píng wěn) — smooth, stable

## Example Sentences

- 他的发挥很稳定。(Tā de fāhuī hěn wěndìng.) — His performance is very steady.

## Notes

Stability and steadiness; important for consistent performance
