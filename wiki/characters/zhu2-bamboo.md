# 竹 Bamboo

- **Character**: 竹
- **Pinyin**: zhú
- **Meaning**: bamboo
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 竹子 (zhú zi) — bamboo
- 竹笋 (zhú sǔn) — bamboo shoot

## Example Sentences

- 熊猫喜欢吃竹子。(Xióng māo xǐ huān chī zhú zi.) — Pandas like to eat bamboo.

## Notes

竹 is a pictograph showing two bamboo stalks with leaves, representing the plant.
