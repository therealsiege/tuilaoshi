# 挡 To Block

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, to obstruct, gear
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挡住 (dǎng zhù) — to block
- 遮挡 (zhē dǎng) — to cover, block

## Example Sentences

- 树挡住了阳光。(Shù dǎng zhù le yáng guāng.) — The tree blocked the sunlight.

## Notes

挡 is simplified from 擋. The hand radical suggests physical blocking.
