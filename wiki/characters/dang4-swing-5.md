# 荡 To Swing

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: to swing, to sway, to wash away, to clear
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荡秋千 (dàng qiū qiān) — to swing (on a swing)
- 摇荡 (yáo dàng) — to sway

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在荡秋千。(Hái zi men zài dàng qiū qiān.) — The children are swinging.

## Notes

荡 means to swing or sway. Used for swinging on swings (荡秋千), rippling water (荡漾).
