# 鸡 Chicken

- **Character**: 鸡
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: chicken
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 鸡蛋 (jī dàn) — chicken egg
- 公鸡 (gōng jī) — rooster

## Example Sentences

- 早饭我吃了鸡蛋。(Zǎo fàn wǒ chī le jī dàn.) — I ate eggs for breakfast.

## Notes

鸡 uses bird radical 鸟. 鸡蛋 (chicken egg) is standard word for 'egg' in cooking.
