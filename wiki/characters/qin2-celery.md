# 芹 Celery

- **Character**: 芹
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: celery
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 芹菜 (qín cài) — celery
- 水芹 (shuǐ qín) — water celery

## Example Sentences

- 我在炒芹菜。(Wǒ zài chǎo qíncài.) — I am stir-frying celery.

## Notes

Common vegetable. Often used in 芹菜 (full word for celery).
