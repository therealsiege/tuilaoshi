# 从 From

- **Character**: 从
- **Pinyin**: cóng
- **Meaning**: from, since, to follow
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 从来 (cóng lái) — always, never (with neg.)
- 从前 (cóng qián) — in the past

## Example Sentences

- 从这里走。(Cóng zhè lǐ zǒu.) — Go from here.

## Notes

从 is simplified from 從. It shows two people following — 'from' or 'to follow.' 从...到... means 'from...to...'
