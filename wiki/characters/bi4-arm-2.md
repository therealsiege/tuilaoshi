# 臂 Arm

- **Character**: 臂
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: arm
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 机械臂 (jī xiè bì) — robotic arm
- 手臂 (shǒu bì) — arm

## Example Sentences

- 机械臂正在焊接零件。(Jī xiè bì zhèng zài hàn jiē líng jiàn.) — The robotic arm is welding parts.

## Notes

Used in 机械臂 (robotic arm), 悬臂 (cantilever), robotics terminology
