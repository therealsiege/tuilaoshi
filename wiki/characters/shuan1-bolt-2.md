# 栓 Bolt

- **Character**: 栓
- **Pinyin**: shuān
- **Meaning**: bolt, plug, cork
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 栓塞 (shuān sè) — embolism
- 消防栓 (xiāo fáng shuān) — fire hydrant

## Example Sentences

- 他拧紧了螺栓。(Tā nǐng jǐn le luó shuān.) — He tightened the bolt.

## Notes

栓 has wood 木 with 全 (complete/whole), wooden plug or bolt.
