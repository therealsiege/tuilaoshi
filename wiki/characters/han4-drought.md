# 旱 Drought, dry

- **Character**: 旱
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: drought, dry
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 干旱 (gān hàn) — drought, arid
- 旱灾 (hàn zāi) — drought disaster

## Example Sentences

- 这个地区遭受了严重的干旱。(Zhè ge dì qū zāo shòu le yán zhòng de gān hàn.) — This region suffered severe drought.

## Notes

Lack of rainfall and water
