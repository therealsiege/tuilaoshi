# 淤 Bruise

- **Character**: 淤
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: bruise, blood stasis
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淤血 (yū xiě) — blood stasis, bruise
- 淤青 (yū qīng) — bruise
- 淤积 (yū jī) — siltation, blockage

## Example Sentences

- 摔倒后腿上有淤青。(Shuāi dǎo hòu tuǐ shàng yǒu yū qīng.) — There are bruises on the legs after falling.

## Notes

Blood pooling under skin; important in TCM diagnosis.
