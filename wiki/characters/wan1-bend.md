# 弯 Bend

- **Character**: 弯
- **Pinyin**: wān
- **Meaning**: bend, curve
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 转弯 (zhuǎn wān) — to turn, bend
- 弯道 (wān dào) — curve, bend

## Example Sentences

- 赛车手在弯道超车了。(Sàichēshǒu zài wāndào chāochē le.) — The race car driver overtook on the curve.

## Notes

Curves and bends; turning in racing
