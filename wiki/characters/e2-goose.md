# 鹅 Goose

- **Character**: 鹅
- **Pinyin**: é
- **Meaning**: goose
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 天鹅 (tiān é) — swan
- 鹅毛 (é máo) — goose feather

## Example Sentences

- 白天鹅在湖上游泳。(Bái tiān'é zài hú shàng yóuyǒng.) — White swans are swimming on the lake.

## Notes

Domesticated waterfowl. Bird radical indicates type.
