# 忠 Loyal

- **Character**: 忠
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: loyal, devoted
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 忠诚 (zhōng chéng) — loyal
- 忠实 (zhōng shí) — faithful

## Example Sentences

- 他是一个忠诚的朋友。(Tā shì yī gè zhōng chéng de péng yǒu.) — He is a loyal friend.

## Notes

忠 combines 中 (center/middle) and 心 (heart), representing keeping one's heart centered and unwavering in devotion.
