# 片 Tablet

- **Character**: 片
- **Pinyin**: piàn
- **Meaning**: tablet, slice
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 药片 (yào piàn) — tablet, pill
- 片剂 (piàn jì) — tablet (dosage form)
- 一片 (yī piàn) — one slice

## Example Sentences

- 这种药片一天吃三次。(Zhè zhǒng yào piàn yī tiān chī sān cì.) — Take this tablet three times a day.

## Notes

Flat tablet form; common dosage format.
