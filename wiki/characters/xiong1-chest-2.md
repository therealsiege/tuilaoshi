# 胸 Chest

- **Character**: 胸
- **Pinyin**: xiōng
- **Meaning**: chest, thorax, mind
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胸有成竹 (xiōng yǒu chéng zhú) — have a well-thought-out plan
- 胸怀 (xiōng huái) — mind, breadth of vision

## Example Sentences

- 他胸有成竹地走上台。(Tā xiōng yǒu chéng zhú de zǒu shàng tái.) — He walked on stage with full confidence.

## Notes

Used in 胸有成竹: having a complete bamboo painting in one's mind before starting to paint.
