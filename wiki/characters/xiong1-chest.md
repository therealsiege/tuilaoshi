# 胸 Chest

- **Character**: 胸
- **Pinyin**: xiōng
- **Meaning**: chest, breast
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胸部 (xiōngbù) — chest
- 胸口 (xiōngkǒu) — chest area

## Example Sentences

- 他感到胸口有点闷。(Tā gǎndào xiōngkǒu yǒudiǎn mēn.) — He feels some tightness in his chest.

## Notes

Important for describing body parts and medical symptoms. Often appears as 胸部 or 胸口.
