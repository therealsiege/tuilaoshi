# 解 — Solve / Understand

- **Character**: 解
- **Pinyin**: jiě (solve/understand) / jiè (escort) / xiè (surname)
- **Meaning**: to solve, to untie, to explain; to understand
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 理解 (lǐjiě) — to understand, to comprehend
- 了解 (liǎojiě) — to understand, to find out about
- 解释 (jiěshì) — to explain, explanation
- 解决 (jiějué) — to solve, to resolve
- 解答 (jiědá) — to answer, to explain
- 误解 (wùjiě) — misunderstanding

## Example Sentences

- 我想了解一下中国历史。(Wǒ xiǎng liǎojiě yīxià Zhōngguó lìshǐ.) — I'd like to learn about Chinese history.
- 这个问题很难解决。(Zhè ge wèntí hěn nán jiějué.) — This problem is hard to solve.
- 请你解释一下这个语法。(Qǐng nǐ jiěshì yīxià zhè ge yǔfǎ.) — Please explain this grammar point.

## Notes

解 is a vivid character: it shows 角 (horn) on the left, 刀 (knife) on the upper right, and 牛 (ox) on the lower right — literally depicting the act of using a knife to separate an ox's horn from its body. From "to separate, to take apart" came the meanings "to untie, to solve, to explain" — all forms of breaking something complex into understandable parts. 了解 and 理解 both mean "to understand" but differ in nuance: 了解 emphasizes knowing about or becoming familiar with something, while 理解 emphasizes deeper comprehension of reasons and meaning.
