# 优 Excellent

- **Character**: 优
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: excellent, superior, 优秀
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 优秀 (yōu xiù) — excellent
- 优点 (yōu diǎn) — advantage

## Example Sentences

- 他是一个优秀的学生。(Tā shì yī ge yōu xiù de xué shēng.) — He is an excellent student.

## Notes

优 is simplified from 優. Represents excellence and superiority.
