# 误 Mistake, Miss, 错误 Mistake

- **Character**: 误
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: mistake, miss, 错误 mistake
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 错误 (cuò wù) — mistake, error
- 耽误 (dān wù) — delay
- 误会 (wù huì) — misunderstanding

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个严重的错误。(Zhè shì yī gè yánzhòng de cuòwù.) — This is a serious mistake.

## Notes

Common character for mistakes and misunderstandings. 错误 (error), 误会 (misunderstanding), 失误 (slip-up) all essential error terms.
