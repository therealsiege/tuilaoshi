# 碎 Broken, fragmented

- **Character**: 碎
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: broken, fragmented
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 粉碎 (fěn suì) — to smash, to shatter
- 碎肉 (suì ròu) — ground meat

## Example Sentences

- 这个碗打碎了。(Zhège wǎn dǎ suì le.) — This bowl is broken.

## Notes

Can be adjective (broken) or verb (to break). Stone radical.
