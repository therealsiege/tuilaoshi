# 注 Inject, pour

- **Character**: 注
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: inject, pour
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 注入 (zhù rù) — inject, pour in
- 注塑 (zhù sù) — injection molding

## Example Sentences

- 注塑成型很常见。(Zhù sù chéng xíng hěn cháng jiàn.) — Injection molding is very common.

## Notes

Used in 注塑 (injection molding), 注入 (inject), manufacturing
