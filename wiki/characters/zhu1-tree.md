# 株 Stump

- **Character**: 株
- **Pinyin**: zhū
- **Meaning**: tree trunk, stump, measure word for plants
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 守株待兔 (shǒu zhū dài tù) — wait for a windfall
- 株连 (zhū lián) — to implicate

## Example Sentences

- 守株待兔的人终究会失败。(Shǒu zhū dài tù de rén zhōngjiū huì shībài.) — Those who wait idly for luck will ultimately fail.

## Notes

The tree stump in 守株待兔; also a measure word for trees and plants.
