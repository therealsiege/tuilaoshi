# 蹄 Hoof

- **Character**: 蹄
- **Pinyin**: tí
- **Meaning**: hoof
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 马蹄 (mǎ tí) — horse hoof
- 蹄子 (tí zi) — hoof

## Example Sentences

- 马蹄声响起来了。(Mǎ tí shēng xiǎng qǐ lái le.) — The sound of horse hooves rang out.

## Notes

蹄 has foot 足 with 帝 (emperor), hard foot of hoofed animals.
