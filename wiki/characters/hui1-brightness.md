# 辉 Brightness

- **Character**: 辉
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: brightness, splendor
- **Radical**: 光 (light)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 光辉 (guāng huī) — glory, brilliance
- 辉煌 (huī huáng) — splendid, glorious

## Example Sentences

- 这是我们辉煌的历史。(Zhè shì wǒmen huīhuáng de lìshǐ.) — This is our glorious history.

## Notes

辉 combines light 光 with 军 (army), representing the brilliant radiance of massed torches or banners.
