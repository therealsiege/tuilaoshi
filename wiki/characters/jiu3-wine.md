# 酒 Wine

- **Character**: 酒
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: wine, liquor, alcohol
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 啤酒 (pí jiǔ) — beer
- 白酒 (bái jiǔ) — Chinese liquor

## Example Sentences

- 我不会喝酒。(Wǒ bù huì hē jiǔ.) — I can't drink alcohol.

## Notes

酒 means alcohol. 红酒 = red wine. 酒吧 = bar.
