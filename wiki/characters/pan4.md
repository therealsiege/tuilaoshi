# 判 To Judge

- **Character**: 判
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: to judge, to decide, to sentence
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge
- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee, judge

## Example Sentences

- 很难判断谁对谁错。(Hěn nán pàn duàn shéi duì shéi cuò.) — It's hard to judge who's right and who's wrong.

## Notes

判 shows knife 刂 dividing 半 (half), representing the decisive cutting action of making a judgment.
