# 守 Guard

- **Character**: 守
- **Pinyin**: shǒu
- **Meaning**: to guard, to defend, to keep
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 守株待兔 (shǒu zhū dài tù) — wait for a windfall
- 守护 (shǒu hù) — to protect

## Example Sentences

- 我们不能守株待兔，要主动出击。(Wǒmen bù néng shǒu zhū dài tù, yào zhǔdòng chūjī.) — We can't just wait for luck; we must take the initiative.

## Notes

Used in 守株待兔: guarding a tree stump waiting for a rabbit to run into it.
