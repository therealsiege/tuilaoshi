# 疫 Epidemic, plague

- **Character**: 疫
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: epidemic, plague
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 疫苗 (yì miáo) — vaccine
- 防疫 (fáng yì) — epidemic prevention

## Example Sentences

- 接种疫苗可以预防疾病。(Jiē zhǒng yì miáo kě yǐ yù fáng jí bìng.) — Vaccination can prevent diseases.

## Notes

Used for epidemics and immunity
