# 侦 To Detect

- **Character**: 侦
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: to detect, to investigate
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 侦探 (zhēn tàn) — detective
- 侦查 (zhēn chá) — to investigate

## Example Sentences

- 警察正在侦查案件。(Jǐng chá zhèng zài zhēn chá àn jiàn.) — The police are investigating the case.

## Notes

侦 simplified from 偵. Person 亻 plus 贞 (faithful/to divine).
