# 拌 To Mix

- **Character**: 拌
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to mix, to toss (salad)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir, to mix
- 凉拌 (liáng bàn) — cold and dressed with sauce

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢吃凉拌菜。(Wǒ xǐ huān chī liáng bàn cài.) — I like to eat cold dishes.

## Notes

拌 combines hand 扌 and 半 (half), representing mixing ingredients together.
