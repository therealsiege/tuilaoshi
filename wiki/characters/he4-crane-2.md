# 鹤 Crane

- **Character**: 鹤
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: crane (bird)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 鹤立鸡群 (hè lì jī qún) — stand out from the crowd
- 仙鹤 (xiān hè) — red-crowned crane

## Example Sentences

- 她在人群中鹤立鸡群。(Tā zài rénqún zhōng hè lì jī qún.) — She stands out from the crowd.

## Notes

Used in 鹤立鸡群: the crane stands tall among ordinary chickens.
