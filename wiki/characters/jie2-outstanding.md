# 杰 Outstanding, hero

- **Character**: 杰
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: outstanding, hero
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 杰出 (jiéchū) — outstanding, distinguished
- 英杰 (yīngjié) — hero, outstanding person

## Example Sentences

- 他是杰出的科学家。(Tā shì jiéchū de kēxuéjiā.) — He is an outstanding scientist.

## Notes

Common term for outstanding people or achievements.
