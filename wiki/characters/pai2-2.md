# 牌 牌子, brand, plate

- **Character**: 牌
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: 牌子, brand, plate
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 品牌 (pǐn pái) — brand
- 牌子 (pái zi) — brand, sign

## Example Sentences

- 这是知名品牌。(Zhè shì zhī míng pǐn pái.) — This is a well-known brand.

## Notes

品牌管理 is brand management. 名牌 means famous brand.
