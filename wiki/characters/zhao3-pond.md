# 沼 Pond

- **Character**: 沼
- **Pinyin**: zhǎo
- **Meaning**: pond, marsh
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 沼泽 (zhǎo zé) — swamp, marsh
- 池沼 (chí zhǎo) — pond

## Example Sentences

- 这片沼泽地很危险。(Zhè piàn zhǎozédì hěn wēixiǎn.) — This swamp is dangerous.

## Notes

沼 combines water 氵 with 召 (to call), the 召 being phonetic. Represents shallow standing water.
