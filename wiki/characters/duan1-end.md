# 端 End, tip, proper, upright

- **Character**: 端
- **Pinyin**: duān
- **Meaning**: end, tip, proper,端正
- **Radical**: 立(stand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 端正 (duān zhèng) — upright, proper
- 一端 (yī duān) — one end

## Example Sentences

- 他的态度很端正。(Tā de tàidu hěn duānzhèng.) — His attitude is very proper.

## Notes

Can mean end/tip or describe proper/upright behavior.
