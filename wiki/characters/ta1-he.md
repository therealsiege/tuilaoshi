# 他 He

- **Character**: 他
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: he, him
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 他们 (tā men) — they (male)
- 其他 (qí tā) — other

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的朋友。(Tā shì wǒ de péngyǒu.) — He is my friend.

## Notes

他 uses the person radical 亻, distinguishing it from 她 (she) which uses the woman radical 女.
