# 系 Tie, Department, 中文系 Chinese Department

- **Character**: 系
- **Pinyin**: xì
- **Meaning**: 系 系 系 系 系 tie, 系 department, 中文系 Chinese department
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 系统 (xì tǒng) — system
- 关系 (guān xì) — relationship
- 中文系 (Zhōngwén xì) — Chinese department

## Example Sentences

- 她在大学中文系学习。(Tā zài dàxué Zhōngwén xì xuéxí.) — She studies in the university Chinese department.

## Notes

Also pronounced jì when meaning 'tie/bind'. In academic contexts (department), always pronounced xì. 系统 (system) is very common.
