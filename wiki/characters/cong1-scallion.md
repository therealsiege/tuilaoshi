# 葱 Scallion, green onion

- **Character**: 葱
- **Pinyin**: cōng
- **Meaning**: scallion, green onion
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大葱 (dà cōng) — scallion
- 葱花 (cōng huā) — chopped scallions

## Example Sentences

- 汤上面撒了一些葱花。(Tāng shàngmian sǎle yīxiē cōnghuā.) — Some chopped scallions are sprinkled on the soup.

## Notes

Essential garnish in Chinese cuisine. Grass radical indicates plant.
