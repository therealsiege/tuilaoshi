# 跟 With

- **Character**: 跟
- **Pinyin**: gēn
- **Meaning**: with, and, to follow
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跟着 (gēn zhe) — to follow
- 跟前 (gēn qián) — in front of

## Example Sentences

- 我跟你一起去。(Wǒ gēn nǐ yī qǐ qù.) — I'll go with you.

## Notes

跟 as a preposition means 'with' — similar to 和 but more colloquial. As a verb it means 'to follow.'
