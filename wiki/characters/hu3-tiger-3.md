# 虎 Tiger

- **Character**: 虎
- **Pinyin**: hǔ
- **Meaning**: tiger
- **Radical**: 虍 (tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 虎头蛇尾 (hǔ tóu shé wěi) — start strong, finish weak
- 老虎 (lǎo hǔ) — tiger

## Example Sentences

- 做事不能虎头蛇尾。(Zuò shì bù néng hǔ tóu shé wěi.) — Don't start strong and finish weak.

## Notes

Used in 虎头蛇尾: the tiger represents a powerful start; zodiac animal.
