# 惭 Ashamed

- **Character**: 惭
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: ashamed
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed, abashed
- 羞惭 (xiū cán) — ashamed

## Example Sentences

- 我对自己的行为感到惭愧。(Wǒ duì zìjǐ de xíngwéi gǎndào cánkuì.) — I feel ashamed of my behavior.

## Notes

Describes the internal feeling of shame or embarrassment.
