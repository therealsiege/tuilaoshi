# 榭 Pavilion

- **Character**: 榭
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: pavilion on terrace
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 水榭 (shuǐ xiè) — waterside pavilion
- 亭台楼榭 (tíng tái lóu xiè) — pavilions and towers

## Example Sentences

- 古代园林有很多亭台楼榭。(Gǔ dài yuán lín yǒu hěn duō tíng tái lóu xiè.) — Ancient gardens have many pavilions and towers.

## Notes

榭 with wood radical for elevated wooden pavilion, classical architecture.
