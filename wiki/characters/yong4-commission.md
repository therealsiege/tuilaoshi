# 佣 Commission

- **Character**: 佣
- **Pinyin**: yòng
- **Meaning**: commission
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 佣金 (yòng jīn) — commission
- 回佣 (huí yòng) — rebate, kickback

## Example Sentences

- 中介收取百分之二的佣金。(Zhōng jiè shōu qǔ bǎi fēn zhī èr de yòng jīn.) — The agent charges a 2% commission.

## Notes

佣金制 is commission system. 提成 is similar but less formal.
