# 渔 Fishing, fishery

- **Character**: 渔
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: fishing, fishery
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渔民 (yú mín) — fisherman
- 渔业 (yú yè) — fishing industry

## Example Sentences

- 渔民每天清晨出海捕鱼。(Yú mín měi tiān qīng chén chū hǎi bǔ yú.) — Fishermen go out to sea to fish every morning.

## Notes

Related to fishing. 渔船 means fishing boat. 渔翁得利 is an idiom about gaining from others' conflict.
