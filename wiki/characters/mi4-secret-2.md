# 密 Secret, dense

- **Character**: 密
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: secret,密码
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 密码 (mì mǎ) — password, cipher
- 加密 (jiā mì) — encrypt

## Example Sentences

- 请输入密码。(Qǐng shū rù mì mǎ.) — Please enter the password.

## Notes

Essential for 密码 (password), 加密 (encryption), cybersecurity
