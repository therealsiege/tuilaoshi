# 脸 Face

- **Character**: 脸
- **Pinyin**: liǎn
- **Meaning**: face
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 洗脸 (xǐ liǎn) — to wash face
- 脸色 (liǎn sè) — complexion, expression

## Example Sentences

- 她的脸红了。(Tā de liǎn hóng le.) — Her face turned red.

## Notes

脸 simplified from 臉, with flesh radical 月. 洗脸 = wash face. 脸色 = facial expression.
