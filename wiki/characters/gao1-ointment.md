# 膏 Ointment, paste, fat

- **Character**: 膏
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: ointment, paste, fat
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 牙膏 (yá gāo) — toothpaste
- 药膏 (yào gāo) — ointment, salve

## Example Sentences

- 医生给我开了药膏。(Yīshēng gěi wǒ kāile yàogāo.) — The doctor prescribed an ointment for me.

## Notes

Ointment, paste, or fat/grease.
