# 瑟 Large Zither

- **Character**: 瑟
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: large zither
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 琴瑟 (qín sè) — zithers, marital harmony
- 萧瑟 (xiāo sè) — desolate

## Example Sentences

- 琴瑟和鸣。(Qín sè hé míng.) — The zithers play in harmony.

## Notes

瑟 depicts a large ancient stringed instrument with many strings.
