# 白 White

- **Character**: 白
- **Pinyin**: bái
- **Meaning**: white, plain, clear
- **Radical**: 白 (white)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 白色 (bái sè) — white color
- 明白 (míng bai) — to understand

## Example Sentences

- 我明白了。(Wǒ míng bai le.) — I understand.

## Notes

白 depicts the sun (日) just rising, when light is white. 明白 (to understand) literally means 'bright-white/clear'.
