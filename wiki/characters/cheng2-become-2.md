# 成 Become, succeed, accomplish

- **Character**: 成
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: become, succeed, accomplish
- **Radical**: 戈 (spear)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 成本 (chéngběn) — cost
- 成功 (chénggōng) — succeed, success

## Example Sentences

- 生产成本降低了。(Shēngchǎn chéngběn jiàngdī le.) — Production costs decreased.

## Notes

Widely used for completion, success, and cost.
