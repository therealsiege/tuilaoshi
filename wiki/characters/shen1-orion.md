# 参 Orion

- **Character**: 参
- **Pinyin**: shēn
- **Meaning**: Orion constellation, ginseng
- **Radical**: 厶(private)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 参宿 (shēn xiù) — Orion constellation
- 人参 (rén shēn) — ginseng

## Example Sentences

- 参宿是冬季夜空中最明亮的星座之一。(Shēn xiù shì dōngjì yè kōng zhōng zuì míngliàng de xīngzuò zhī yī.) — Orion is one of the brightest constellations in the winter night sky.

## Notes

参 (shēn) refers to the Orion constellation. Also pronounced cān (participate) and cēn (uneven).
