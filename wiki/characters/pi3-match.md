# 匹 Match, measure word for horses

- **Character**: 匹
- **Pinyin**: pǐ
- **Meaning**: match, measure word for horses
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 匹配 (pǐpèi) — match, pair
- 匹敌 (pǐdí) — match, rival

## Example Sentences

- 他的实力无人能匹敌。(Tā de shílì wú rén néng pǐdí.) — No one can match his strength.

## Notes

Used as measure word for horses and cloth, also means matching.
