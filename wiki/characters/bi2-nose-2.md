# 鼻 Nose

- **Character**: 鼻
- **Pinyin**: bí
- **Meaning**: nose
- **Radical**: 鼻 (nose)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 鼻子 (bízi) — nose
- 鼻音 (bíyīn) — nasal sound

## Example Sentences

- 我的鼻子有点疼。(Wǒ de bízi yǒudiǎn téng.) — My nose hurts a bit.

## Notes

This character is its own radical. Usually used with 子 suffix as 鼻子 in modern Chinese.
