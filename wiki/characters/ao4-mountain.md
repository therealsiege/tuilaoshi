# 坳 Mountain pass, col, depression

- **Character**: 坳
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: mountain pass, col, depression
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 山坳 (shān ào) — mountain hollow
- 低坳 (dī ào) — depression, low-lying area

## Example Sentences

- 村庄坐落在山坳里。(Cūn zhuāng zuò luò zài shān ào lǐ.) — The village is nestled in a mountain hollow.

## Notes

Describes a concave or depressed area between mountains.
