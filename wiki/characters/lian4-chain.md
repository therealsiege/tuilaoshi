# 链 Chain

- **Character**: 链
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: chain, link
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 链接 (liàn jiē) — link (hyperlink)
- 区块链 (qū kuài liàn) — blockchain

## Example Sentences

- 点击这个链接。(Diǎn jī zhè ge liàn jiē.) — Click this link.

## Notes

链 with metal radical for chain, now essential for hyperlinks and blockchain technology.
