# 膏 Ointment

- **Character**: 膏
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: ointment, paste
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 牙膏 (yá gāo) — toothpaste
- 药膏 (yào gāo) — ointment

## Example Sentences

- 我需要买牙膏。(Wǒ xū yào mǎi yá gāo.) — I need to buy toothpaste.

## Notes

膏 with meat radical originally meant fat/grease, now for pastes and ointments.
