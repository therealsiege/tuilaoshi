# 禽 Bird, fowl, poultry

- **Character**: 禽
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: bird, fowl, poultry
- **Radical**: 禸 (track)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 家禽 (jiā qín) — poultry, domestic fowl
- 禽类 (qín lèi) — birds, fowl

## Example Sentences

- 鸡鸭鹅都是常见的家禽。(Jī yā é dōu shì cháng jiàn de jiā qín.) — Chickens, ducks, and geese are common poultry.

## Notes

Refers to birds especially domesticated ones
