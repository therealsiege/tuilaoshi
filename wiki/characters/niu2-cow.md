# 牛 Cow

- **Character**: 牛
- **Pinyin**: niú
- **Meaning**: cow, ox, bull
- **Radical**: 牛 (cow)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 牛肉 (niú ròu) — beef
- 奶牛 (nǎi niú) — dairy cow

## Example Sentences

- 我不吃牛肉。(Wǒ bù chī niú ròu.) — I don't eat beef.

## Notes

牛 pictograph showing cow's head with horns. Also slang for 'awesome': 太牛了！
