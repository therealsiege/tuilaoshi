# 聆 Listen respectfully

- **Character**: 聆
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: listen respectfully
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 聆听 (língtīng) — listen respectfully
- 聆教 (língjiào) — receive instruction

## Example Sentences

- 我聆听了教授的讲座。(Wǒ língtīng le jiàoshòu de jiǎngzuò.) — I listened respectfully to the professor's lecture.

## Notes

Formal term for respectful listening, more formal than 听.
