# 珠 Pearl

- **Character**: 珠
- **Pinyin**: zhū
- **Meaning**: pearl, bead
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 珍珠 (zhēn zhū) — pearl
- 水珠 (shuǐ zhū) — water droplet

## Example Sentences

- 露珠在叶子上闪闪发光。(Lù zhū zài yè zi shàng shǎn shǎn fā guāng.) — Dewdrops glisten on the leaves.

## Notes

珠 has jade 玉 with 朱 (vermillion), round precious gem.
