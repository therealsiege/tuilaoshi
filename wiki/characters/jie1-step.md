# 阶 Step, stairs, rank

- **Character**: 阶
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: step, stairs, rank
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阶梯 (jiē tī) — stairs, ladder
- 台阶 (tái jiē) — steps, stairs

## Example Sentences

- 他一步一步地登上石阶。(Tā yī bù yī bù de dēng shàng shí jiē.) — He climbed the stone steps one by one.

## Notes

Also means level, rank, or stage in progression.
