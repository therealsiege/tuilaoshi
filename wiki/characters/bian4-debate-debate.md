# 辩 To Debate

- **Character**: 辩
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: to debate, to argue, to distinguish
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 辩论 (biàn lùn) — debate
- 争辩 (zhēng biàn) — to argue

## Example Sentences

- 我们进行了一场辩论。(Wǒ men jìn xíng le yī chǎng biàn lùn.) — We had a debate.

## Notes

辩 means to argue or debate. Differs from 辨 (to distinguish) and 瓣 (petal).
