# 汰 Eliminate

- **Character**: 汰
- **Pinyin**: tài
- **Meaning**: to eliminate, wash out
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 淘汰 (táo tài) — to eliminate, weed out
- 淘汰赛 (táo tài sài) — elimination tournament

## Example Sentences

- 淘汰赛很残酷。(Táotàisài hěn cánkù.) — Elimination tournaments are very cruel.

## Notes

Being eliminated; losing and exiting competition
