# 铲 Shovel

- **Character**: 铲
- **Pinyin**: chǎn
- **Meaning**: shovel, to shovel
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铲子 (chǎn zi) — shovel
- 铲除 (chǎn chú) — to eradicate

## Example Sentences

- 用铲子铲雪。(Yòng chǎn zi chǎn xuě.) — Use a shovel to shovel snow.

## Notes

铲 means shovel. 铲除 = to root out/eradicate (figuratively).
