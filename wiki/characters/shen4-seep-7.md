# 渗 To Seep

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: to seep, to ooze, to permeate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate
- 渗漏 (shèn lòu) — to seep

## Example Sentences

- 水慢慢渗入土壤。(Shuǐ màn man shèn rù tǔ rǎng.) — Water slowly seeps into the soil.

## Notes

渗 simplified from 滲. Water 氵 gradually penetrating.
