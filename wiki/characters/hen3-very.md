# 很 Very

- **Character**: 很
- **Pinyin**: hěn
- **Meaning**: very, quite
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 很多 (hěn duō) — very many
- 很好 (hěn hǎo) — very good

## Example Sentences

- 她很漂亮。(Tā hěn piào liàng.) — She is very pretty.

## Notes

很 is required before adjective predicates even when 'very' isn't meant: 我很好 = 'I'm fine' (not necessarily 'very' fine).
