# 格 Pattern

- **Character**: 格
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: pattern, standard, style, to investigate
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 格式 (gé shì) — format
- 价格 (jià gé) — price

## Example Sentences

- 这个文件的格式不对。(Zhè ge wén jiàn de gé shì bú duì.) — This file's format is incorrect.

## Notes

格 shows木 (wood) crossed with力 (lines), representing patterns or standards. Very versatile character.
