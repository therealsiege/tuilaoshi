# 涩 Astringent, rough, 澀 (traditional)

- **Character**: 涩
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: astringent, rough, 澀 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 苦涩 (kǔ sè) — bitter, anguished
- 晦涩 (huì sè) — obscure, difficult

## Example Sentences

- 这种水果味道很涩。(Zhè zhǒng shuǐguǒ wèidào hěn sè.) — This fruit tastes very astringent.

## Notes

Astringent taste or rough texture, also obscure/difficult.
