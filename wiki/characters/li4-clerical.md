# 隶 Clerical Script

- **Character**: 隶
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: clerical script
- **Radical**: 隶 (slave)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 隶书 (lì shū) — clerical script
- 隶体 (lì tǐ) — clerical style

## Example Sentences

- 隶书是汉代流行的字体。(Lì shū shì Hàn dài liú xíng de zì tǐ.) — Clerical script was popular in the Han Dynasty.

## Notes

隶 simplified from 隸. Represents the clerical script style.
