# 叫 To Call

- **Character**: 叫
- **Pinyin**: jiào
- **Meaning**: to call, to be called
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 叫做 (jiào zuò) — to be called
- 大叫 (dà jiào) — to shout

## Example Sentences

- 你叫什么名字？(Nǐ jiào shénme míng zì?) — What is your name?

## Notes

叫 is used to state one's name: 我叫... (My name is...).
