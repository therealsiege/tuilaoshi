# 钙 Calcium

- **Character**: 钙
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: calcium
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钙质 (gài zhì) — calcium
- 补钙 (bǔ gài) — supplement calcium

## Example Sentences

- 牛奶富含钙质，有助于骨骼健康。(Niúnǎi fùhán gàizhì, yǒuzhù yú gǔgé jiànkāng.) — Milk is rich in calcium, which helps bone health.

## Notes

钙 is calcium, an essential mineral for bones and teeth. The metal radical indicates it's a metallic element.
