# 介 Between, introduce

- **Character**: 介
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: between, introduce
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 介绍 (jiè shào) — introduce
- 中介 (zhōng jiè) — agent, intermediary

## Example Sentences

- 通过中介租房。(Tōng guò zhōng jiè zū fáng.) — Rent a house through an agent.

## Notes

媒介 means medium. 简介 means brief introduction.
