# 泰 Safe, Peaceful, 泰国 Thailand

- **Character**: 泰
- **Pinyin**: tài
- **Meaning**: safe, peaceful, 泰国 Thailand
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 泰国 (Tài guó) — Thailand
- 泰山 (Tài Shān) — Mount Tai
- 安泰 (ān tài) — safe and peaceful

## Example Sentences

- 泰山是五岳之首。(Tài Shān shì wǔ yuè zhī shǒu.) — Mount Tai is the chief of the Five Sacred Mountains.

## Notes

Means peaceful/safe in classical Chinese. Now mainly in proper nouns: 泰国 (Thailand), 泰山 (Mount Tai).
