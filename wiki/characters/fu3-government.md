# 府 Government Office

- **Character**: 府
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: government office, prefecture
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 政府 (zhèng fǔ) — government
- 首府 (shǒu fǔ) — capital city

## Example Sentences

- 市政府在市中心。(Shì zhèng fǔ zài shì zhōng xīn.) — The city government is in the city center.

## Notes

府 uses the shelter radical 广, indicating an official building or residence.
