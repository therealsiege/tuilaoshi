# 遁 To hide, to escape, to flee

- **Character**: 遁
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: to hide, to escape, to flee
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 遁形 (dùn xíng) — to hide one's form, to vanish
- 逃遁 (táo dùn) — to flee, to escape

## Example Sentences

- 他无处可遁，只好投降。(Tā wú chù kě dùn, zhǐhǎo tóuxiáng.) — He had nowhere to escape, so he had to surrender.

## Notes

Literary term for hiding or escaping, often metaphorical.
