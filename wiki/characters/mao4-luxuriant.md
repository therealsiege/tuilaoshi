# 茂 Luxuriant, lush, flourishing

- **Character**: 茂
- **Pinyin**: mào
- **Meaning**: luxuriant, lush, flourishing
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 茂盛 (mào shèng) — luxuriant, flourishing
- 繁茂 (fán mào) — lush, luxuriant

## Example Sentences

- 森林里植被茂盛。(Sēn lín lǐ zhí bèi mào shèng.) — The vegetation in the forest is lush.

## Notes

Dense and healthy plant growth
