# 讼 Lawsuit, litigation

- **Character**: 讼
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: lawsuit, litigation
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 诉讼 (sù sòng) — lawsuit
- 讼讼 (mín sòng) — civil lawsuit

## Example Sentences

- 这个案件已进入诉讼程序。(Zhè ge àn jiàn yǐ jìn rù sù sòng chéng xù.) — This case has entered litigation proceedings.

## Notes

Specifically refers to legal proceedings. 刑讼 means criminal lawsuit. 争讼 means dispute.
