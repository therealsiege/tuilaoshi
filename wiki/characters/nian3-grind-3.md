# 碾 To Grind

- **Character**: 碾
- **Pinyin**: niǎn
- **Meaning**: to grind, to crush
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 碾米 (niǎn mǐ) — to mill rice
- 碾压 (niǎn yā) — to crush

## Example Sentences

- 用石磨碾米。(Yòng shí mò niǎn mǐ.) — Use a millstone to grind rice.

## Notes

碾 combines stone 石 and 展 (unfold), representing crushing with a rolling stone.
