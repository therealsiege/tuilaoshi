# 压 Press

- **Character**: 压
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: press, pressure
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 压力 (yālì) — pressure, stress
- 按压 (ànyā) — press down

## Example Sentences

- 工作压力很大。(Gōngzuò yālì hěn dà.) — Work pressure is very high.

## Notes

Pressing action and abstract pressure. 压力 is very common for stress. Physical and mental meanings.
