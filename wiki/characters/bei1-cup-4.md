# 杯 Cup

- **Character**: 杯
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: cup, glass
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 杯弓蛇影 (bēi gōng shé yǐng) — overly suspicious
- 干杯 (gān bēi) — cheers, bottoms up

## Example Sentences

- 别杯弓蛇影，自己吓自己。(Bié bēi gōng shé yǐng, zìjǐ xià zìjǐ.) — Don't be overly suspicious and scare yourself.

## Notes

Used in 杯弓蛇影: mistaking a bow's reflection in a cup for a snake.
