# 坝 Dam, Embankment, 大坝 Dam

- **Character**: 坝
- **Pinyin**: bà
- **Meaning**: dam, embankment, 大坝 dam
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 大坝 (dà bà) — dam
- 水坝 (shuǐ bà) — dam
- 堤坝 (dī bà) — dike, dam

## Example Sentences

- 三峡大坝是世界上最大的水利工程之一。(Sānxiá Dàbà shì shìjiè shàng zuì dà de shuǐlì gōngchéng zhī yī.) — The Three Gorges Dam is one of the world's largest water conservancy projects.

## Notes

Infrastructure term for dams and embankments. 三峡大坝 is China's famous dam project on the Yangtze River.
