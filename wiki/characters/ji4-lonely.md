# 寂 Lonely

- **Character**: 寂
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: lonely, quiet
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 寂寞 (jìmò) — lonely
- 寂静 (jìjìng) — quiet, silent

## Example Sentences

- 一个人在家很寂寞。(Yī gè rén zài jiā hěn jìmò.) — Being alone at home is very lonely.

## Notes

Loneliness feeling. Roof radical suggests isolation indoors. 寂寞 is emotional loneliness.
