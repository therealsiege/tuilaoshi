# 璃 Colored glaze, glass

- **Character**: 璃
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: colored glaze, glass
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 玻璃 (bō li) — glass
- 琉璃 (liú lí) — colored glaze

## Example Sentences

- 寺庙的琉璃瓦在阳光下闪闪发光。(Sì miào de liú lí wǎ zài yáng guāng xià shǎn shǎn fā guāng.) — The glazed tiles of the temple gleamed in the sunlight.

## Notes

Originally referred to colored glaze, now mainly in compound words.
