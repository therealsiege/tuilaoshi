# 累 Tired

- **Character**: 累
- **Pinyin**: lèi
- **Meaning**: tired, exhausted
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 累了 (lèi le) — tired
- 劳累 (láo lèi) — fatigued

## Example Sentences

- 我今天很累。(Wǒ jīn tiān hěn lèi.) — I'm very tired today.

## Notes

累 as lèi means tired. As lěi means to accumulate (积累). As léi means to tie up.
