# 削 Peel

- **Character**: 削
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: peel, cut
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 削减 (xuē jiǎn) — cut down, reduce
- 剥削 (bō xuē) — exploit

## Example Sentences

- 他用刀削铅笔。(Tā yòng dāo xiāo qiānbǐ.) — He sharpened the pencil with a knife.

## Notes

削 (xiāo) means to peel or whittle. As xuē it appears in compounds meaning to reduce or cut.
