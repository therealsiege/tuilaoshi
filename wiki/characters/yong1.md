# 拥 To embrace, to hug, to crowd, to support

- **Character**: 拥
- **Pinyin**: yōng
- **Meaning**: to embrace, to hug, to crowd, to support
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拥抱 (yōng bào) — to embrace, hug
- 拥有 (yōng yǒu) — to possess, to own

## Example Sentences

- 他们热情地拥抱在一起。(Tāmen rèqíng de yōngbào zài yīqǐ.) — They warmly embraced each other.

## Notes

Common verb for embracing or possessing something.
