# 屏 Screen

- **Character**: 屏
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: screen, to shield
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen (computer, TV)
- 屏障 (píng zhàng) — barrier, shield

## Example Sentences

- 手机屏幕坏了。(Shǒu jī píng mù huài le.) — The phone screen is broken.

## Notes

屏 can be píng (screen) or bǐng (to hold one's breath). Shows shelter radical 尸 with items arranged, representing a protective screen.
