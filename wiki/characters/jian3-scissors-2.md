# 剪 Scissors

- **Character**: 剪
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: scissors, to cut, to clip
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 剪刀 (jiǎn dāo) — scissors
- 剪纸 (jiǎn zhǐ) — paper cutting

## Example Sentences

- 她在剪头发。(Tā zài jiǎn tóu fa.) — She is cutting hair.

## Notes

剪 means scissors or to cut with scissors. Used for haircuts, paper cutting (剪纸, traditional art).
