# 渴 Thirsty

- **Character**: 渴
- **Pinyin**: kě
- **Meaning**: thirsty
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 望梅止渴 (wàng méi zhǐ kě) — console with false hope
- 口渴 (kǒu kě) — thirsty

## Example Sentences

- 我很渴，想喝水。(Wǒ hěn kě, xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — I'm very thirsty and want to drink water.

## Notes

Used in 望梅止渴: the thirst that plum gazing temporarily alleviates.
