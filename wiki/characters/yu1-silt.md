# 淤 Silt up, clog, muddy

- **Character**: 淤
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: silt up, clog, muddy
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淤泥 (yū ní) — silt, mud
- 淤积 (yū jī) — silt up, deposit

## Example Sentences

- 河道被淤泥堵塞了。(Hé dào bèi yū ní dǔ sè le.) — The river channel was blocked by silt.
