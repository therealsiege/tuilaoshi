# 都 All

- **Character**: 都
- **Pinyin**: dōu
- **Meaning**: all, both, even
- **Radical**: 阝(city)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 都是 (dōu shì) — all are
- 首都 (shǒu dū) — capital city

## Example Sentences

- 我们都是学生。(Wǒ men dōu shì xué shēng.) — We are all students.

## Notes

都 as dōu means 'all/both' and must come AFTER the subject. As dū it means 'capital city.'
