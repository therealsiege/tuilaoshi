# 晋 Advance

- **Character**: 晋
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to advance, promote
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晋级 (jìn jí) — to advance to next round
- 晋升 (jìn shēng) — to promote

## Example Sentences

- 他们成功晋级决赛。(Tāmen chénggōng jìnjí juésài.) — They successfully advanced to the finals.

## Notes

Advancing; progressing to next round
