# 颗 Classifier for small round objects

- **Character**: 颗
- **Pinyin**: kē
- **Meaning**: classifier for small round objects
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 一颗心 (yī kē xīn) — a heart
- 颗粒 (kē lì) — granule, pellet

## Example Sentences

- 我吃了三颗葡萄。(Wǒ chīle sān kē pútao.) — I ate three grapes.

## Notes

Measure word for hearts, teeth, stars, beads, etc. Page radical.
