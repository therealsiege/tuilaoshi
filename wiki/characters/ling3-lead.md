# 领 To Lead

- **Character**: 领
- **Pinyin**: lǐng
- **Meaning**: to lead, collar, receive
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 领导 (lǐng dǎo) — leader, to lead
- 领取 (lǐng qǔ) — to receive, collect

## Example Sentences

- 他是我们的领导。(Tā shì wǒ men de lǐng dǎo.) — He is our leader.

## Notes

领 is simplified from 領. Originally meant collar/neck, extended to leadership (at the head).
