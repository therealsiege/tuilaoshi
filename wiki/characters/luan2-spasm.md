# 挛 Spasm, twins, 攣 (traditional)

- **Character**: 挛
- **Pinyin**: luán
- **Meaning**: spasm, twins, 攣 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 痉挛 (jìng luán) — spasm, cramp
- 双胞胎 (shuāng bāo tāi) — twins

## Example Sentences

- 寒冷会引起肌肉痉挛。(Hánlěng huì yǐnqǐ jīròu jìngluán.) — Cold can cause muscle spasms.

## Notes

Used in compound 痉挛 for spasms and cramps.
