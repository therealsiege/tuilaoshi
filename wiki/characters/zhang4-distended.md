# 胀 Bloated

- **Character**: 胀
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: distended, bloated
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肿胀 (zhǒng zhàng) — swelling
- 膨胀 (péng zhàng) — to expand, swell
- 腹胀 (fù zhàng) — abdominal distension

## Example Sentences

- 吃太多会腹胀。(Chī tài duō huì fù zhàng.) — Eating too much causes abdominal bloating.

## Notes

Describes fullness or swelling; common GI complaint.
