# 荡 Oscillate, swing

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: oscillate, swing
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 振荡 (zhèn dàng) — oscillate
- 荡漾 (dàng yàng) — ripple

## Example Sentences

- 振荡电路工作正常。(Zhèn dàng diàn lù gōng zuò zhèng cháng.) — The oscillator circuit works normally.

## Notes

Used in 振荡 (oscillation), 振荡器 (oscillator), electronics
