# 犁 Plow

- **Character**: 犁
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: plow, to plow
- **Radical**: 牛 (cow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 犁地 (lí dì) — to plow
- 犁田 (lí tián) — to plow a field

## Example Sentences

- 农民在犁地。(Nóng mín zài lí dì.) — The farmer is plowing the field.

## Notes

犁 has cow 牛 at bottom with 利 (sharp), ox-drawn cutting tool.
