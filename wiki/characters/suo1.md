# 缩 To shrink, withdraw, 縮 (traditional)

- **Character**: 缩
- **Pinyin**: suō
- **Meaning**: to shrink, withdraw, 縮 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 缩小 (suō xiǎo) — to reduce, shrink
- 萎缩 (wěi suō) — to atrophy

## Example Sentences

- 这件毛衣洗后缩水了。(Zhè jiàn máoyī xǐ hòu suōshuǐ le.) — This sweater shrank after washing.

## Notes

Physical shrinking or metaphorical withdrawal/contraction.
