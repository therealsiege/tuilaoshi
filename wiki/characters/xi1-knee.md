# 膝 Knee

- **Character**: 膝
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: knee
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 膝盖 (xī gài) — kneecap, knee
- 膝关节 (xī guān jié) — knee joint

## Example Sentences

- 他的膝盖受伤了。(Tā de xī gài shòu shāng le.) — His knee was injured.

## Notes

膝 specifically refers to the knee joint, an important body part for movement and posture.
