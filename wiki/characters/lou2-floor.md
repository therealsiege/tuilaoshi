# 楼 Floor

- **Character**: 楼
- **Pinyin**: lóu
- **Meaning**: floor, building, storey
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 楼上 (lóu shàng) — upstairs
- 大楼 (dà lóu) — building

## Example Sentences

- 我住在三楼。(Wǒ zhù zài sān lóu.) — I live on the third floor.

## Notes

楼 is used for floors: 一楼 (ground floor), 二楼 (2nd floor). In China, 一楼 = ground floor.
