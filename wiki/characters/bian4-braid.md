# 辫 Braid

- **Character**: 辫
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: braid, pigtail
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 辫子 (biàn zi) — braid, pigtail
- 编辫子 (biān biàn zi) — to braid hair

## Example Sentences

- 她扎了两条辫子。(Tā zhā le liǎng tiáo biàn zi.) — She tied two braids.

## Notes

辫 has 辛辛 (two bitter) flanking 纟 (silk), intertwined strands.
