# 抖 To Shake

- **Character**: 抖
- **Pinyin**: dǒu
- **Meaning**: to shake, to tremble
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 发抖 (fā dǒu) — to shiver
- 抖动 (dǒu dòng) — to shake

## Example Sentences

- 他冷得发抖。(Tā lěng de fā dǒu.) — He is shivering from cold.

## Notes

抖 hand 扌 plus 斗 (to fight), shaking motion.
