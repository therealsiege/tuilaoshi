# 螺 Snail

- **Character**: 螺
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: snail, spiral, screw
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 螺丝 (luó sī) — screw
- 田螺 (tián luó) — river snail

## Example Sentences

- 需要用螺丝刀。(Xū yào yòng luó sī dāo.) — You need to use a screwdriver.

## Notes

螺 has insect 虫 with 累 (to accumulate), spiral shell creature.
