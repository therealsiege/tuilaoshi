# 鸦 Crow, raven

- **Character**: 鸦
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: crow, raven
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 乌鸦 (wū yā) — crow
- 鸦雀无声 (yā què wú shēng) — dead silence

## Example Sentences

- 教室里鸦雀无声。(Jiào shì lǐ yā què wú shēng.) — The classroom was dead silent.

## Notes

Used in expressions about silence
