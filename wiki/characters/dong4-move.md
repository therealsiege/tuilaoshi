# 动 To Move

- **Character**: 动
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: to move, action, movement
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 运动 (yùn dòng) — sports, exercise
- 活动 (huó dòng) — activity

## Example Sentences

- 别动！(Bié dòng!) — Don't move!

## Notes

动 means to move. 感动 = moved (emotionally). 动物 = animal (moving creature).
