# 蟹 Crab

- **Character**: 蟹
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: crab
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 螃蟹 (páng xiè) — crab
- 蟹肉 (xiè ròu) — crab meat

## Example Sentences

- 秋天是吃螃蟹的季节。(Qiūtiān shì chī pángxiè de jìjié.) — Autumn is the season for eating crabs.

## Notes

Delicacy in Chinese cuisine. Insect radical for arthropod.
