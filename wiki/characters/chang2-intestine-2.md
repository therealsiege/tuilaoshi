# 肠 Intestine

- **Character**: 肠
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: intestine
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肠胃 (cháng wèi) — gastrointestinal
- 大肠 (dà cháng) — large intestine
- 小肠 (xiǎo cháng) — small intestine

## Example Sentences

- 肠胃炎很常见。(Cháng wèi yán hěn cháng jiàn.) — Gastroenteritis is very common.

## Notes

Part of digestive system; appears in many GI disorder terms.
