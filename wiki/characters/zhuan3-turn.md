# 转 To Turn

- **Character**: 转
- **Pinyin**: zhuǎn
- **Meaning**: to turn, to rotate, to transfer
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 转身 (zhuǎn shēn) — to turn around
- 转变 (zhuǎn biàn) — to change

## Example Sentences

- 请向右转。(Qǐng xiàng yòu zhuǎn.) — Please turn right.

## Notes

转 simplified from 轉. Vehicle radical 车 with专 (special), representing rotating or转turning.
