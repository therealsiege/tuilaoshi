# 赚 Earn, make a profit

- **Character**: 赚
- **Pinyin**: zhuàn
- **Meaning**: earn, make a profit
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赚钱 (zhuànqián) — make money, earn money
- 盈利 (yínglì) — profit

## Example Sentences

- 他今年赚了不少钱。(Tā jīnnián zhuàn le bùshǎo qián.) — He made quite a bit of money this year.

## Notes

Common colloquial term for earning money or making profit.
