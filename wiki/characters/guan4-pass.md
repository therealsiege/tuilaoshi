# 贯 Pass through, consistent

- **Character**: 贯
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: pass through, consistent
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贯彻 (guànchè) — implement, carry through
- 一贯 (yīguàn) — consistent, all along

## Example Sentences

- 要贯彻执行政策。(Yào guànchè zhíxíng zhèngcè.) — Must implement and carry out the policy.

## Notes

Common formal term for passing through or consistency.
