# 流 To Flow

- **Character**: 流
- **Pinyin**: liú
- **Meaning**: to flow, to drift, stream, current
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 流动 (liú dòng) — to flow
- 交流 (jiāo liú) — to exchange

## Example Sentences

- 河水在流动。(Hé shuǐ zài liú dòng.) — The river water is flowing.

## Notes

流 means to flow or stream. Used for water flowing, spreading (流传), and wandering (流浪).
