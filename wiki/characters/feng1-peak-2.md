# 峰 Peak, summit, hump

- **Character**: 峰
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: peak, summit, hump
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 山峰 (shān fēng) — mountain peak
- 峰峦 (fēng luán) — peaks and ridges

## Example Sentences

- 珠穆朗玛峰是世界最高峰。(Zhū mù lǎng mǎ fēng shì shì jiè zuì gāo fēng.) — Mount Everest is the world's highest peak.

## Notes

Also used for peaks in graphs or camel humps.
