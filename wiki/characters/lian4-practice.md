# 练 To Practice

- **Character**: 练
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to practice, to train
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 练习 (liàn xí) — to practice
- 训练 (xùn liàn) — to train

## Example Sentences

- 多练习就会进步。(Duō liàn xí jiù huì jìn bù.) — Practice more and you'll improve.

## Notes

练习 is both a verb (to practice) and a noun (exercise/drill). 熟练 means proficient.
