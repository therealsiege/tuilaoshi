# 孝 Filial piety

- **Character**: 孝
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: filial piety
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 孝顺 (xiào shùn) — filial, obedient to parents
- 孝道 (xiào dào) — filial duty

## Example Sentences

- 百善孝为先。(Bǎi shàn xiào wéi xiān.) — Of all virtues, filial piety is the most important.

## Notes

Shows an old person supported by a child, representing the duty of children to care for parents. Central to Confucian family values.
