# 皮 Skin

- **Character**: 皮
- **Pinyin**: pí
- **Meaning**: skin, leather, surface
- **Radical**: 皮 (skin)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 皮肤 (pí fū) — skin
- 皮革 (pí gé) — leather

## Example Sentences

- 她的皮肤很好。(Tā de pí fū hěn hǎo.) — Her skin is very good.

## Notes

皮 means skin or leather. Used in皮肤 (skin), 皮包 (leather bag), 调皮 (naughty).
