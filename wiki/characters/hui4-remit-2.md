# 汇 Remit, converge, exchange rate

- **Character**: 汇
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: remit, converge, exchange rate
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汇率 (huì lǜ) — exchange rate
- 汇款 (huì kuǎn) — to remit money

## Example Sentences

- 今天的汇率是多少？(Jīn tiān de huì lǜ shì duō shao?) — What's today's exchange rate?

## Notes

Key finance term. 外汇 means foreign exchange. 汇总 means to sum up. 词汇 means vocabulary.
