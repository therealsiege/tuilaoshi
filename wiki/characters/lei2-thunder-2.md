# 雷 Thunder

- **Character**: 雷
- **Pinyin**: léi
- **Meaning**: thunder
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 打雷 (dǎ léi) — thunder
- 雷雨 (léiyǔ) — thunderstorm

## Example Sentences

- 外面在打雷。(Wàimiàn zài dǎ léi.) — It's thundering outside.

## Notes

Weather phenomenon. 打雷 is the verb form. Often appears with 闪电. Rain radical indicates storm.
