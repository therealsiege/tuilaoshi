# 溜 To slip away, to slide, smooth

- **Character**: 溜
- **Pinyin**: liū
- **Meaning**: to slip away, to slide, smooth
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 溜走 (liū zǒu) — to slip away
- 光溜溜 (guāng liū liū) — smooth, slippery

## Example Sentences

- 趁人不注意，他溜走了。(Chèn rén bù zhùyì, tā liūzǒu le.) — When no one was paying attention, he slipped away.

## Notes

Can mean to slip away or describe smoothness.
