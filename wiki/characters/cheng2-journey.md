# 程 Program

- **Character**: 程
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: journey, procedure, program
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 程序 (chéng xù) — procedure, program
- 课程 (kè chéng) — course

## Example Sentences

- 他正在学习编程。(Tā zhèng zài xué xí biān chéng.) — He is learning programming.

## Notes

程 grain 禾 plus 呈 (to present), measured distance.
