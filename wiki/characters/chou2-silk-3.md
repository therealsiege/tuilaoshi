# 绸 Silk

- **Character**: 绸
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: silk, to bind, to wrap
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 未雨绸缪 (wèi yǔ chóu móu) — prepare in advance
- 丝绸 (sī chóu) — silk

## Example Sentences

- 未雨绸缪才是明智之举。(Wèi yǔ chóu móu cái shì míngzhì zhī jǔ.) — Preparing in advance is the wise move.

## Notes

Used in 未雨绸缪: to bind and repair before rain comes; preparation.
