# 鲨 Shark

- **Character**: 鲨
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: shark
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 鲨鱼 (shā yú) — shark
- 鲨鱼鳍 (shā yú qí) — shark fin

## Example Sentences

- 大海里有很多鲨鱼。(Dàhǎi lǐ yǒu hěn duō shāyú.) — There are many sharks in the ocean.

## Notes

Apex predator of the ocean. Fish radical indicates marine life.
