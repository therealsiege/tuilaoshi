# 捏 To Pinch

- **Character**: 捏
- **Pinyin**: niē
- **Meaning**: to pinch, to mold
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捏造 (niē zào) — to fabricate
- 捏泥人 (niē ní rén) — to mold clay figures

## Example Sentences

- 孩子在捏橡皮泥。(Hái zi zài niē xiàng pí ní.) — The child is molding plasticine.

## Notes

捏 combines hand 扌 and 圼 (phonetic), representing pinching or molding with fingers.
