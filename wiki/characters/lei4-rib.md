# 肋 Rib, side

- **Character**: 肋
- **Pinyin**: lèi
- **Meaning**: rib, side
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 肋骨 (lèi gǔ) — rib
- 两肋 (liǎng lèi) — both sides

## Example Sentences

- 他摔断了一根肋骨。(Tā shuāi duànle yī gēn lèigǔ.) — He broke a rib in the fall.

## Notes

Anatomical term for ribs, the bones protecting the chest.
