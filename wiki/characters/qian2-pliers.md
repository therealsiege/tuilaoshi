# 钳 Pliers, pincers, clamp

- **Character**: 钳
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: pliers, pincers, clamp
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钳子 (qián zi) — pliers
- 火钳 (huǒ qián) — fire tongs

## Example Sentences

- 用钳子夹住这个螺丝。(Yòng qián zi jiā zhù zhè gè luó sī.) — Grip this screw with pliers.

## Notes

Tool for gripping or clamping
