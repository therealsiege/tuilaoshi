# 炮 Cannon, artillery

- **Character**: 炮
- **Pinyin**: pào
- **Meaning**: cannon, artillery
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 大炮 (dà pào) — cannon
- 炮火 (pào huǒ) — artillery fire

## Example Sentences

- 鸦片战争期间，英军用炮火攻击中国海岸。(Yāpiàn Zhànzhēng qījiān, Yīng jūn yòng pàohuǒ gōngjī Zhōngguó hǎi'àn.) — During the Opium War, British forces attacked the Chinese coast with artillery fire.

## Notes

Heavy weapons that fire projectiles using explosive force. The fire radical indicates the explosive propulsion.
