# 啃 To Gnaw

- **Character**: 啃
- **Pinyin**: kěn
- **Meaning**: to gnaw, to nibble
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 啃骨头 (kěn gǔ tou) — to gnaw on bones

## Example Sentences

- 他在啃苹果。(Tā zài kěn píng guǒ.) — He is biting into an apple.

## Notes

啃 shows mouth 口 and 艮 (stubborn), representing persistent biting or gnawing.
