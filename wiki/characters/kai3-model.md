# 楷 Model, regular script

- **Character**: 楷
- **Pinyin**: kǎi
- **Meaning**: model, regular script
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 楷模 (kǎimó) — model, example
- 楷书 (kǎishū) — regular script

## Example Sentences

- 他是年轻人的楷模。(Tā shì niánqīngrén de kǎimó.) — He is a role model for young people.

## Notes

Refers both to exemplary models and the regular style of Chinese calligraphy.
