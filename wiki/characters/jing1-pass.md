# 经 To Pass Through

- **Character**: 经
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: to pass through, classic, scripture, to manage
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 经常 (jīng cháng) — often, frequently
- 经济 (jīng jì) — economy

## Example Sentences

- 我经常去那家餐厅。(Wǒ jīng cháng qù nà jiā cān tīng.) — I often go to that restaurant.

## Notes

经 shows silk threads (warp in weaving), representing things running through or enduring. Very versatile character.
