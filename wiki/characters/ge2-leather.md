# 革 Leather

- **Character**: 革
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: leather, to reform, to革革革
- **Radical**: 革 (leather)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 革命 (gé mìng) — revolution
- 改革 (gǎi gé) — to reform

## Example Sentences

- 中国在进行改革开放。(Zhōng guó zài jìn xíng gǎi gé kāi fàng.) — China is carrying out reform and opening up.

## Notes

革 means leather or to reform. Used in革命 (revolution), 改革 (reform), 皮革 (leather).
