# 骇 To Shock

- **Character**: 骇
- **Pinyin**: hài
- **Meaning**: to shock, to horrify
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 震骇 (zhèn hài) — to震 and horrify
- 骇人 (hài rén) — shocking, horrifying

## Example Sentences

- 这个消息令人震惊。(Zhè gè xiāo xī lìng rén zhèn jīng.) — This news is shocking.

## Notes

骇 simplified from 駭. Horse radical 马 representing a startled horse, extended to shock or horror.
