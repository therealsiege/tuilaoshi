# 俯 To bow one's head, look down

- **Character**: 俯
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: to bow one's head, look down
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 俯视 (fǔ shì) — to overlook, look down at
- 俯瞰 (fǔ kàn) — to overlook from above

## Example Sentences

- 他俯下身子看地图。(Tā fǔ xià shēnzi kàn dìtú.) — He bent down to look at the map.

## Notes

Opposite of 仰; indicates downward gaze or bowing posture.
