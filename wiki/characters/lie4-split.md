# 裂 To Split

- **Character**: 裂
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: to split, to crack
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 分裂 (fēn liè) — to split, to divide
- 破裂 (pò liè) — to rupture

## Example Sentences

- 墙上出现了裂缝。(Qiáng shàng chū xiàn le liè fèng.) — Cracks appeared on the wall.

## Notes

裂 depicts clothing being torn apart. Used for physical splitting or metaphorical division.
