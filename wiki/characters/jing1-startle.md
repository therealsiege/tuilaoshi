# 惊 To Startle

- **Character**: 惊
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: to startle, to shock, to surprise
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 吃惊 (chī jīng) — to be startled
- 惊讶 (jīng yà) — surprised

## Example Sentences

- 我很吃惊。(Wǒ hěn chī jīng.) — I'm very surprised.

## Notes

惊 simplified from 驚. Heart 忄 experiencing shock, like a startled horse.
