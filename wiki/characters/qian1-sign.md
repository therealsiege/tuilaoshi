# 签 Sign, affix signature

- **Character**: 签
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: sign, affix signature
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 签署 (qiān shǔ) — to sign (official documents)
- 签字 (qiān zì) — to sign, signature

## Example Sentences

- 总统签署了新法案。(Zǒng tǒng qiān shǔ le xīn fǎ àn.) — The president signed the new bill.

## Notes

Essential for legal and business transactions. 签名 means signature. 签证 means visa.
