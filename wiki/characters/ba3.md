# 把 To Hold

- **Character**: 把
- **Pinyin**: bǎ
- **Meaning**: to hold; BA construction particle
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 把握 (bǎ wò) — to grasp, certainty
- 一把 (yī bǎ) — one (handful)

## Example Sentences

- 请把门关上。(Qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng.) — Please close the door.

## Notes

把 introduces the BA construction: 把 + object + verb + complement. It moves the object before the verb for disposal/handling.
