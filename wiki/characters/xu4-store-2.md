# 蓄 Store up, save, grow

- **Character**: 蓄
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: store up, save, grow
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 储蓄 (chǔxù) — save, savings
- 蓄积 (xùjī) — accumulate, store up

## Example Sentences

- 要蓄积力量等待时机。(Yào xùjī lìliàng děngdài shíjī.) — One should accumulate strength and wait for the opportunity.

## Notes

Used for accumulation and saving, both literally and figuratively.
