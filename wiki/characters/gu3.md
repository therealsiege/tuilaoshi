# 谷 Valley

- **Character**: 谷
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: valley, grain
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 山谷 (shān gǔ) — valley
- 峡谷 (xiá gǔ) — canyon, gorge

## Example Sentences

- 山谷里很安静。(Shāngǔ lǐ hěn ānjìng.) — It's very quiet in the valley.

## Notes

谷 depicts an opening between mountains. Also means grain, representing crops grown in valleys.
