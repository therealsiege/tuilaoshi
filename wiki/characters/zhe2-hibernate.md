# 蛰 To Hibernate

- **Character**: 蛰
- **Pinyin**: zhé
- **Meaning**: to hibernate, to sting
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 蛰伏 (zhé fú) — to lie dormant
- 惊蛰 (jīng zhé) — Awakening of Insects (solar term)

## Example Sentences

- 惊蛰过后春天就来了。(Jīng zhé guò hòu chūn tiān jiù lái le.) — After the Awakening of Insects, spring arrives.

## Notes

蛰 simplified from 蟄. Insect 虫 with 执 (to hold), insects dormant underground.
