# 枫 Maple

- **Character**: 枫
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: maple
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 枫树 (fēng shù) — maple tree
- 枫叶 (fēng yè) — maple leaf

## Example Sentences

- 秋天枫叶变红了。(Qiū tiān fēng yè biàn hóng le.) — Maple leaves turn red in autumn.

## Notes

枫 simplified from 楓. Wood radical 木 with风 (wind), representing the tree whose leaves flutter in wind.
