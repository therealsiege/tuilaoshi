# 漂 To Float

- **Character**: 漂
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: to float, to drift
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漂流 (piāo liú) — to drift
- 漂浮 (piāo fú) — to float

## Example Sentences

- 树叶在水面上漂浮。(Shù yè zài shuǐ miàn shàng piāo fú.) — Leaves float on the water surface.

## Notes

漂 has three pronunciations: piāo (float), piǎo (bleach), piào (pretty). Context determines meaning.
