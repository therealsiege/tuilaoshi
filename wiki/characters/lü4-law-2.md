# 律 Law

- **Character**: 律
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: law, regulation,律师
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 法律 (fǎ lǜ) — law
- 规律 (guī lǜ) — pattern, law

## Example Sentences

- 每个人都要遵守法律。(Měi ge rén dōu yào zūn shǒu fǎ lǜ.) — Everyone must obey the law.

## Notes

律 combines movement and writing, representing established rules and laws.
