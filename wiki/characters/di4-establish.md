# 缔 To Found

- **Character**: 缔
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: to conclude, to found
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缔造 (dì zào) — to create, to found
- 缔结 (dì jié) — to conclude (treaty)

## Example Sentences

- 两国缔结了和平条约。(Liǎng guó dì jié le hé píng tiáo yuē.) — The two countries concluded a peace treaty.

## Notes

缔 simplified from 締. Silk 纟 with 帝 (emperor), binding an agreement.
