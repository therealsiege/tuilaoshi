# 和 Harmony

- **Character**: 和
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: to join in singing, harmony
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 和声 (hè shēng) — harmony (music)
- 唱和 (chàng hè) — to sing in response

## Example Sentences

- 两人一唱一和，配合默契。(Liǎng rén yī chàng yī hè, pèi hé mò qì.) — The two sang in harmony with perfect coordination.

## Notes

和 (hè) means to harmonize or join in singing; different from 和 (hé) meaning 'and'.
