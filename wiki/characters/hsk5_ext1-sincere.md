# 挚 Sincere, Intimate, 真挚 Sincere

- **Character**: 挚
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: sincere, intimate, 真挚 sincere
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 真挚 (zhēn zhì) — sincere, genuine
- 挚友 (zhì yǒu) — close friend
- 诚挚 (chéng zhì) — sincere

## Example Sentences

- 他们是真挚的朋友。(Tāmen shì zhēnzhì de péngyǒu.) — They are genuine friends.

## Notes

Literary term for deep sincerity and genuine emotion. 真挚 expresses authentic feelings, often used in formal or emotional contexts.
