# 艇 Boat

- **Character**: 艇
- **Pinyin**: tǐng
- **Meaning**: boat, vessel
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赛艇 (sài tǐng) — rowing, crew
- 快艇 (kuài tǐng) — speedboat

## Example Sentences

- 赛艇是奥运会项目。(Sàitǐng shì Àoyùnhuì xiàngmù.) — Rowing is an Olympic sport.
