# 氮 Nitrogen

- **Character**: 氮
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: nitrogen
- **Radical**: 气 (gas)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 氮气 (dàn qì) — nitrogen gas
- 液氮 (yè dàn) — liquid nitrogen

## Example Sentences

- 空气中大约百分之七十八是氮气。(Kōng qì zhōng dà yuē bǎi fēn zhī qī shí bā shì dàn qì.) — About 78% of air is nitrogen.

## Notes

Chemical element nitrogen, major component of air
