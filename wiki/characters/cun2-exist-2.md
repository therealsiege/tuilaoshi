# 存 To Store

- **Character**: 存
- **Pinyin**: cún
- **Meaning**: to exist, to store, to deposit
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 存储 (cún chǔ) — to store, storage
- 保存 (bǎo cún) — to save, preserve

## Example Sentences

- 别忘了保存文件。(Bié wàng le bǎo cún wén jiàn.) — Don't forget to save the file.

## Notes

存 appears in all storage contexts: banking, computing, and warehousing.
