# 蒸 To steam, to evaporate

- **Character**: 蒸
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: to steam, to evaporate
- **Radical**: 艹(grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蒸汽 (zhēng qì) — steam, vapor
- 蒸饭 (zhēng fàn) — to steam rice

## Example Sentences

- 她在蒸包子。(Tā zài zhēng bāozi.) — She is steaming buns.

## Notes

Common cooking method in Chinese cuisine.
