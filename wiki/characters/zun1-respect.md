# 尊 To Respect

- **Character**: 尊
- **Pinyin**: zūn
- **Meaning**: to respect, to honor
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 尊重 (zūn zhòng) — to respect
- 尊敬 (zūn jìng) — to revere

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该尊重每个人的意见。(Wǒ men yīng gāi zūn zhòng měi gè rén de yì jiàn.) — We should respect everyone's opinions.

## Notes

尊 combines 酋 (wine vessel) and 寸 (hand), originally depicting offering wine with both hands as a sign of respect.
