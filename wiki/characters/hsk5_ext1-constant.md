# 恒 Constant, Permanent, 恒心 Perseverance

- **Character**: 恒
- **Pinyin**: héng
- **Meaning**: constant, permanent, 恒心 perseverance
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恒心 (héng xīn) — perseverance
- 永恒 (yǒng héng) — eternal
- 恒温 (héng wēn) — constant temperature

## Example Sentences

- 成功需要恒心。(Chénggōng xūyào héngxīn.) — Success requires perseverance.

## Notes

Formal term for constancy and permanence. 恒心 (perseverance) is virtue term. 恒星 (fixed star) in astronomy.
