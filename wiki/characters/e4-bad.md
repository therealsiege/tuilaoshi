# 噩 Bad

- **Character**: 噩
- **Pinyin**: è
- **Meaning**: bad, nightmare
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 噩梦 (è mèng) — nightmare
- 噩耗 (è hào) — bad news (death)

## Example Sentences

- 昨晚我做了一个噩梦。(Zuó wǎn wǒ zuò le yī gè è mèng.) — Last night I had a nightmare.

## Notes

噩 has a complex structure with four 口 mouths, suggesting overwhelming bad news or frightening dreams.
