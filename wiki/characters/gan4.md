# 干 Trunk, stem, do

- **Character**: 干
- **Pinyin**: gàn
- **Meaning**: trunk, stem, do
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 树干 (shù gàn) — tree trunk
- 主干 (zhǔ gàn) — trunk, main part

## Example Sentences

- 粗壮的树干支撑着整棵树。(Cū zhuàng de shù gàn zhī chēng zhe zhěng kē shù.) — The thick trunk supports the entire tree.

## Notes

Main trunk of tree
