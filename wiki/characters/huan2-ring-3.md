# 环 Ring, cycle

- **Character**: 环
- **Pinyin**: huán
- **Meaning**: ring, cycle
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 循环 (xún huán) — cycle
- 环境 (huán jìng) — environment

## Example Sentences

- 水循环系统正常。(Shuǐ xún huán xì tǒng zhèng cháng.) — The water circulation system is normal.

## Notes

Used in 循环 (cycle), 环境 (environment), 环保 (environmental protection)
