# 哗 Clamor, noise, crashing sound

- **Character**: 哗
- **Pinyin**: huá
- **Meaning**: clamor, noise, crashing sound
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哗然 (huá rán) — in an uproar
- 哗啦 (huā lā) — crash, clatter

## Example Sentences

- 这个消息引起了哗然。(Zhè gè xiāo xi yǐn qǐ le huá rán.) — This news caused an uproar.

## Notes

Sudden loud noise or public commotion
