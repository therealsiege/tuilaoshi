# 陈 Arrange, state, old

- **Character**: 陈
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: arrange, state, old
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 陈述 (chénshù) — state, present
- 陈旧 (chénjiù) — old-fashioned, obsolete

## Example Sentences

- 他向法官陈述了案情。(Tā xiàng fǎguān chénshù le ànqíng.) — He presented the case to the judge.

## Notes

Used in formal contexts for presenting facts or statements.
