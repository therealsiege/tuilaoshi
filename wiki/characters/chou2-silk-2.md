# 绸 Silk

- **Character**: 绸
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: silk, satin
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 丝绸 (sī chóu) — silk
- 绸缎 (chóu duàn) — silk and satin

## Example Sentences

- 丝绸之路促进了东西方的贸易。(Sīchóu zhī lù cùjìn le dōngxīfāng de màoyì.) — The Silk Road promoted trade between East and West.

## Notes

绸 is a general term for silk fabrics. China has been famous for silk production for millennia.
