# 捡 Pick up

- **Character**: 捡
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: pick up
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捡到 (jiǎn dào) — find, pick up
- 捡起 (jiǎnqǐ) — pick up

## Example Sentences

- 我捡到一个钱包。(Wǒ jiǎn dào yī gè qiánbāo.) — I found/picked up a wallet.

## Notes

Picking up from ground. Hand radical. Common for found objects. Often with 到 to indicate completion.
