# 卿 Minister, you (classical)

- **Character**: 卿
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: minister, you (classical)
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 公卿 (gōng qīng) — high officials
- 卿相 (qīng xiàng) — ministers and prime ministers

## Example Sentences

- 爱卿所言甚是。(Ài qīng suǒ yán shèn shì.) — What you say is very true, dear minister.

## Notes

Ancient official rank and term of endearment
