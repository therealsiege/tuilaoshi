# 蹦 Hop

- **Character**: 蹦
- **Pinyin**: bèng
- **Meaning**: hop, bounce
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 蹦跳 (bèngtiào) — hop and jump
- 蹦蹦跳跳 (bèngbèng-tiàotiào) — hopping around

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们蹦蹦跳跳地玩。(Háizimen bèngbèng-tiàotiào de wán.) — The children are playing, hopping around.

## Notes

Hopping or bouncing. Foot radical. Playful, energetic movement. Often with children or animals.
