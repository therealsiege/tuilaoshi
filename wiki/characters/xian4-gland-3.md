# 腺 Gland

- **Character**: 腺
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: gland
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腺体 (xiàn tǐ) — gland
- 甲状腺 (jiǎ zhuàng xiàn) — thyroid gland
- 前列腺 (qián liè xiàn) — prostate gland

## Example Sentences

- 甲状腺功能异常需要治疗。(Jiǎ zhuàng xiàn gōng néng yì cháng xū yào zhì liáo.) — Abnormal thyroid function requires treatment.

## Notes

Secretes hormones or other substances; important in endocrinology.
