# 偶 Accidental, even number, spouse, puppet

- **Character**: 偶
- **Pinyin**: ǒu
- **Meaning**: accidental, even number, spouse, puppet
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偶然 (ǒu rán) — accidental, by chance
- 配偶 (pèi ǒu) — spouse

## Example Sentences

- 我们偶然在街上相遇。(Wǒmen ǒurán zài jiē shàng xiāngyù.) — We met by chance on the street.

## Notes

Multiple meanings including chance, spouse, or even numbers.
