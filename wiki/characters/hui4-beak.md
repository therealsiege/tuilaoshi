# 喙 Beak

- **Character**: 喙
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: beak, bill
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 鸟喙 (niǎo huì) — bird's beak
- 喙部 (huì bù) — beak area

## Example Sentences

- 鹦鹉的喙很坚硬。(Yīng wǔ de huì hěn jiān yìng.) — The parrot's beak is very hard.

## Notes

喙 has mouth 口 with 彖 (piglet), bird's pointed bill.
