# 案 Case

- **Character**: 案
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: case, legal case, file
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 案件 (àn jiàn) — case, legal case
- 方案 (fāng àn) — plan, scheme

## Example Sentences

- 警察在调查这个案件。(Jǐng chá zài diào chá zhè ge àn jiàn.) — The police are investigating this case.

## Notes

案 originally meant table or desk; extended to files and legal cases.
