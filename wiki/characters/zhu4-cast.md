# 铸 To Cast

- **Character**: 铸
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to cast, to forge
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铸造 (zhù zào) — to cast, to mold
- 铸成 (zhù chéng) — to forge, to form

## Example Sentences

- 这座雕像是铜铸的。(Zhè zuò diāo xiàng shì tóng zhù de.) — This statue is cast in bronze.

## Notes

铸 uses metal radical 金, referring to pouring molten metal into molds to create objects.
