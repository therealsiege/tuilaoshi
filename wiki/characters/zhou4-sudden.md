# 骤 Sudden, abrupt

- **Character**: 骤
- **Pinyin**: zhòu
- **Meaning**: sudden, abrupt
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 骤然 (zhòurán) — suddenly, abruptly
- 急骤 (jízhòu) — rapid, sudden

## Example Sentences

- 天气骤然变冷。(Tiānqì zhòurán biàn lěng.) — The weather suddenly turned cold.

## Notes

Literary term emphasizing the suddenness of change.
