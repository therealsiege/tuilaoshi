# 锤 Hammer, mallet

- **Character**: 锤
- **Pinyin**: chuí
- **Meaning**: hammer, mallet
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锤子 (chuí zi) — hammer
- 铁锤 (tiě chuí) — iron hammer

## Example Sentences

- 他用锤子钉钉子。(Tā yòng chuí zi dīng dīng zi.) — He hammers nails with a hammer.

## Notes

Striking tool, also political symbol
