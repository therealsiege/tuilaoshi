# 议 To Discuss

- **Character**: 议
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to discuss, to suggest, opinion
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 会议 (huì yì) — meeting
- 建议 (jiàn yì) — to suggest

## Example Sentences

- 我有个建议。(Wǒ yǒu gè jiàn yì.) — I have a suggestion.

## Notes

议 simplified from 議. Uses speech radical 讠 with 义 (righteousness), representing speaking about proper courses of action.
