# 附 附加, attach, add

- **Character**: 附
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: 附加, attach, add
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 附件 (fù jiàn) — attachment, annex
- 附加 (fù jiā) — 附加, additional

## Example Sentences

- 请查看邮件附件。(Qǐng chá kàn yóu jiàn fù jiàn.) — Please check the email attachment.

## Notes

附录 is appendix. 附属 means subsidiary/affiliated.
