# 瘟 Epidemic, plague, pestilence

- **Character**: 瘟
- **Pinyin**: wēn
- **Meaning**: epidemic, plague, pestilence
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘟疫 (wēn yì) — plague, epidemic
- 瘟神 (wēn shén) — god of plague, pest

## Example Sentences

- 古代瘟疫常常造成大量人口死亡。(Gǔdài wēnyì chángcháng zàochéng dàliàng rénkǒu sǐwáng.) — Ancient epidemics often caused massive loss of life.

## Notes

Epidemic diseases, especially highly contagious ones. Historical plagues had devastating effects on Chinese society.
