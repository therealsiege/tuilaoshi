# 钉 Nail

- **Character**: 钉
- **Pinyin**: dīng
- **Meaning**: nail, to nail, to follow closely
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 钉子 (dīng zi) — nail
- 图钉 (tú dīng) — thumbtack

## Example Sentences

- 墙上钉了几个钉子。(Qiáng shàng dīng le jǐ ge dīng zi.) — Several nails were driven into the wall.

## Notes

钉 can be dīng (nail, to nail) or dìng (to rivet, to follow). As dīng, used for nails and钉ing things.
