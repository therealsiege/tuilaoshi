# 慈 Compassionate, merciful, kind

- **Character**: 慈
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: compassionate, merciful, kind
- **Radical**: 心(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 慈善 (cí shàn) — charity, benevolent
- 慈祥 (cí xiáng) — kindly, benevolent

## Example Sentences

- 她有一颗慈善的心。(Tā yǒu yī kē císhàn de xīn.) — She has a charitable heart.

## Notes

Expresses compassion and kindness, often maternal.
