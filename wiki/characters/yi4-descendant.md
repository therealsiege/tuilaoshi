# 裔 Descendant

- **Character**: 裔
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: descendant, offspring
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 后裔 (hòu yì) — descendant
- 华裔 (huá yì) — ethnic Chinese

## Example Sentences

- 他是华裔美国人。(Tā shì huá yì měi guó rén.) — He is a Chinese American.

## Notes

裔 shows clothing 衣 with trailing hem, symbolizing descendants who come after like a garment's train.
