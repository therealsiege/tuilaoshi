# 链 Chain, link

- **Character**: 链
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: chain, link
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 链接 (liàn jiē) — link, hyperlink
- 区块链 (qū kuài liàn) — blockchain

## Example Sentences

- 请点击这个链接。(Qǐng diǎn jī zhè gè liàn jiē.) — Please click this link.

## Notes

Used in 链接 (link), 区块链 (blockchain), 链条 (chain)
