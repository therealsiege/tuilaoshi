# 坠 To Fall

- **Character**: 坠
- **Pinyin**: zhuì
- **Meaning**: to fall, to drop
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 坠子 (zhuì zi) — ballad singing (with sanxian)
- 下坠 (xià zhuì) — to fall

## Example Sentences

- 坠子是河南的曲艺形式。(Zhuì zi shì hé nán de qǔ yì xíng shì.) — Zhuizi is a Henan form of narrative ballad.

## Notes

坠 means to fall; 坠子 refers to a ballad genre with sanxian accompaniment.
