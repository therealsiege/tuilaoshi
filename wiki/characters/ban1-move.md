# 搬 To Move

- **Character**: 搬
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: to move, to carry, to transport
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 搬家 (bān jiā) — to move house
- 搬运 (bān yùn) — to transport

## Example Sentences

- 我们明天搬家。(Wǒ men míng tiān bān jiā.) — We're moving house tomorrow.

## Notes

搬 involves moving heavy or bulky objects, especially furniture. 搬家 (moving house) is a very common life event.
