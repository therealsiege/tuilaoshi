# 祸 Disaster, calamity, curse

- **Character**: 祸
- **Pinyin**: huò
- **Meaning**: disaster, calamity, curse
- **Radical**: 礻 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 祸害 (huò hài) — disaster, scourge
- 灾祸 (zāi huò) — calamity

## Example Sentences

- 祸不单行，福不双至。(Huò bù dān xíng, fú bù shuāng zhì.) — Misfortunes never come singly.

## Notes

Common in proverbs about disaster and fortune
