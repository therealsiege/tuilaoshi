# 锡 Tin, 锡箔 Tinfoil

- **Character**: 锡
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: tin, 锡箔 tinfoil
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锡箔 (xī bó) — tinfoil
- 锡纸 (xī zhǐ) — aluminum foil (colloquial)
- 焊锡 (hàn xī) — solder

## Example Sentences

- 用锡纸包好食物再烤。(Yòng xīzhǐ bāo hǎo shíwù zài kǎo.) — Wrap the food in foil before baking.

## Notes

Metal element tin. 锡纸 is colloquial term for aluminum foil in cooking contexts, though technically incorrect.
