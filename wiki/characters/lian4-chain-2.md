# 链 Chain

- **Character**: 链
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: chain, link
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 项链 (xiàng liàn) — necklace
- 铁链 (tiě liàn) — iron chain

## Example Sentences

- 她戴着一条金项链。(Tā dài zhe yì tiáo jīn xiàng liàn.) — She wears a gold necklace.

## Notes

链 simplified from 鏈. Metal 钅 links connected together.
