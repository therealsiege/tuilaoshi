# 磨 To Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: to grind, to polish, to wear down
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 磨练 (mó liàn) — to temper, to toughen
- 折磨 (zhé mó) — to torment

## Example Sentences

- 经验需要时间来磨练。(Jīng yàn xū yào shí jiān lái mó liàn.) — Experience needs time to be honed.

## Notes

磨 shows stone 石 and hemp 麻, representing grinding stones used to process tough materials.
