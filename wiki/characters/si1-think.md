# 思 To Think

- **Character**: 思
- **Pinyin**: sī
- **Meaning**: to think, thought
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 意思 (yì si) — meaning
- 思想 (sī xiǎng) — thought, ideology

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么意思？(Zhè shì shén me yì si?) — What does this mean?

## Notes

思 combines 田 (field/brain) and 心 (heart) — thinking with both mind and heart.
