# 讨 Demand, ask for, discuss

- **Character**: 讨
- **Pinyin**: tǎo
- **Meaning**: demand, ask for, discuss
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 讨论 (tǎolùn) — discuss
- 讨债 (tǎozhài) — demand repayment of debt

## Example Sentences

- 债主来讨债。(Zhàizhǔ lái tǎozhài.) — The creditor came to demand repayment.

## Notes

Used for demanding, asking, and discussing.
