# 简 — Simple

- **Character**: 简
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: simple, brief; bamboo slip (historical)
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 简单 (jiǎndān) — simple, easy
- 简化 (jiǎnhuà) — to simplify
- 简体 (jiǎntǐ) — simplified (characters)
- 简介 (jiǎnjiè) — brief introduction
- 简历 (jiǎnlì) — resume, CV
- 简称 (jiǎnchēng) — abbreviation, short name

## Example Sentences

- 这道题很简单。(Zhè dào tí hěn jiǎndān.) — This question is very simple.
- 你能简单地说一下吗？(Nǐ néng jiǎndān de shuō yīxià ma?) — Can you explain it briefly?
- 简体字比繁体字容易写。(Jiǎntǐzì bǐ fántǐzì róngyì xiě.) — Simplified characters are easier to write than traditional characters.

## Notes

简 carries the bamboo radical 竹 at the top because it originally referred to bamboo slips used for writing in ancient China. Since bamboo slips were narrow, writing on them required brevity — hence the meaning "brief, simple." The compound 简单 (simple) is extremely common in everyday speech. 简体字 (simplified characters) refers to the writing system used in mainland China, which is what this wiki uses. The character itself is somewhat ironic: despite meaning "simple," it has 13 strokes, making it one of the more complex characters at this level.
