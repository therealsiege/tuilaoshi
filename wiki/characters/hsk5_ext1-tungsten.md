# 钨 Tungsten, 钨丝 Tungsten Filament

- **Character**: 钨
- **Pinyin**: wū
- **Meaning**: tungsten, 钨丝 tungsten filament
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钨丝 (wū sī) — tungsten filament
- 钨钢 (wū gāng) — tungsten steel
- 钨矿 (wū kuàng) — tungsten ore

## Example Sentences

- 传统灯泡使用钨丝发光。(Chuántǒng dēngpào shǐyòng wūsī fāguāng.) — Traditional light bulbs use tungsten filaments to emit light.

## Notes

Industrial metal tungsten. 钨丝 (tungsten filament) was standard in incandescent bulbs before LED technology.
