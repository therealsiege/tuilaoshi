# 安 Peaceful, safe, to settle

- **Character**: 安
- **Pinyin**: ān
- **Meaning**: peaceful, safe, to安全 settle
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 安全 (ān quán) — safe, secure
- 安慰 (ān wèi) — to comfort

## Example Sentences

- 注意安全。(Zhùyì ānquán.) — Pay attention to safety.

## Notes

Core character for peace, safety, and settling/installing.
