# 粉 Powder

- **Character**: 粉
- **Pinyin**: fěn
- **Meaning**: powder, pink
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 粉碎 (fěn suì) — to smash to pieces
- 粉红 (fěn hóng) — pink

## Example Sentences

- 敌人的阴谋被粉碎了。(Dí rén de yīn móu bèi fěn suì le.) — The enemy's plot was crushed.

## Notes

粉 uses rice radical 米, originally meaning rice powder. Extended to any powder and the color pink.
