# 换 To Change

- **Character**: 换
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: to change, to exchange, to switch
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 交换 (jiāo huàn) — to exchange
- 兑换 (duì huàn) — to convert (currency)

## Example Sentences

- 我想换一些人民币。(Wǒ xiǎng huàn yī xiē rén mín bì.) — I want to exchange some RMB.

## Notes

换 means to exchange or replace. Common for changing clothes, currency, or switching things.
