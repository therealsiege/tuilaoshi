# 务 Service, affair

- **Character**: 务
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: service, affair
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 服务 (fú wù) — service
- 任务 (rèn wu) — task, mission

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个重要的任务。(Zhè shì yī gè zhòng yào de rèn wu.) — This is an important task.

## Notes

Used in 服务 (service), 任务 (task), 业务 (business)
