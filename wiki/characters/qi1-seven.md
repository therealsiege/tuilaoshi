# 七 Seven

- **Character**: 七
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: seven
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 七月 (qī yuè) — July
- 七十 (qī shí) — seventy

## Example Sentences

- 一个星期有七天。(Yī gè xīng qī yǒu qī tiān.) — A week has seven days.

## Notes

七 was originally a cross shape. The Qixi Festival (七夕) on the 7th day of the 7th month is Chinese Valentine's Day.
