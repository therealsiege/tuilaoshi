# 排 Row

- **Character**: 排
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: row, arrange, volleyball
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 排球 (pái qiú) — volleyball
- 排队 (pái duì) — to line up, queue

## Example Sentences

- 她是学校排球队的队长。(Tā shì xuéxiào páiqiú duì de duìzhǎng.) — She is the captain of the school volleyball team.

## Notes

Used in volleyball; also means to arrange or line up
