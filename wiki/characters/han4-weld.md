# 焊 To Weld

- **Character**: 焊
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: to weld, to solder
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 焊接 (hàn jiē) — to weld
- 电焊 (diàn hàn) — electric welding

## Example Sentences

- 工人正在焊接钢管。(Gōng rén zhèng zài hàn jiē gāng guǎn.) — Workers are welding steel pipes.

## Notes

焊 uses fire radical 火, referring to joining metals by heating them until they fuse together.
