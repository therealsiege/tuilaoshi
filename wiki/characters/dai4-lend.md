# 贷 To lend, to borrow, loan

- **Character**: 贷
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: to lend, to borrow, loan
- **Radical**: 贝(shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan, to provide a loan
- 信贷 (xìn dài) — credit, loan

## Example Sentences

- 他向银行贷款买房。(Tā xiàng yínháng dàikuǎn mǎi fáng.) — He took out a bank loan to buy a house.

## Notes

Commonly used in financial contexts for loans.
