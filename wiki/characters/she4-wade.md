# 涉 Wade, involve, experience

- **Character**: 涉
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: wade, involve, experience
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涉及 (shèjí) — involve, relate to
- 涉嫌 (shèxián) — be suspected of

## Example Sentences

- 此案涉及多个部门。(Cǐ àn shèjí duō gè bùmén.) — This case involves multiple departments.

## Notes

Originally meant to wade through water, now commonly used for involvement.
