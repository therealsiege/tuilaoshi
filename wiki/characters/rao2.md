# 饶 To forgive, to spare, rich, plentiful

- **Character**: 饶
- **Pinyin**: ráo
- **Meaning**: to forgive, to spare, rich, plentiful
- **Radical**: 饣(food)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 饶恕 (ráo shù) — to forgive, to pardon
- 求饶 (qiú ráo) — to beg for mercy

## Example Sentences

- 他求对方饶了他。(Tā qiú duìfāng ráo le tā.) — He begged the other party to spare him.

## Notes

Can mean to forgive or to be abundant/plentiful.
