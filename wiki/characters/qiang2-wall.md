# 墙 Wall

- **Character**: 墙
- **Pinyin**: qiáng
- **Meaning**: wall
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 墙壁 (qiáng bì) — wall
- 长城 (cháng chéng) — Great Wall

## Example Sentences

- 墙上有一幅画。(Qiáng shàng yǒu yī fú huà.) — There's a painting on the wall.

## Notes

墙 simplified from 牆, uses earth radical 土. Walls traditionally made of packed earth.
