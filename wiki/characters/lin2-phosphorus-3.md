# 磷 Phosphorus

- **Character**: 磷
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: phosphorus
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 磷肥 (lín féi) — phosphate fertilizer
- 磷酸 (lín suān) — phosphoric acid

## Example Sentences

- 磷是植物生长的必需元素。(Lín shì zhíwù shēngzhǎng de bìxū yuánsù.) — Phosphorus is an essential element for plant growth.

## Notes

磷 is phosphorus, important for fertilizers and biological processes. The stone radical indicates mineral sources.
