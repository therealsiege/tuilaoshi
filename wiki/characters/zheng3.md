# 整 Whole, tidy, arrange

- **Character**: 整
- **Pinyin**: zhěng
- **Meaning**: whole, tidy, arrange
- **Radical**: 攵 (rap)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 整理 (zhěnglǐ) — arrange, tidy up
- 整体 (zhěngtǐ) — whole, entirety

## Example Sentences

- 请整理一下文件。(Qǐng zhěnglǐ yīxià wénjiàn.) — Please organize the documents.

## Notes

Common for整 and tidying.
