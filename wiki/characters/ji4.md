# 记 — Remember / Record

- **Character**: 记
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to remember, to record; a record, a note
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 记得 (jìde) — to remember
- 忘记 (wàngjì) — to forget
- 记住 (jìzhù) — to remember firmly, to memorize
- 笔记 (bǐjì) — notes (written)
- 日记 (rìjì) — diary
- 记者 (jìzhě) — journalist, reporter

## Example Sentences

- 你还记得他吗？(Nǐ hái jìde tā ma?) — Do you still remember him?
- 我把这个词忘记了。(Wǒ bǎ zhè ge cí wàngjì le.) — I forgot this word.
- 请把这些记住。(Qǐng bǎ zhèxiē jìzhù.) — Please memorize these.

## Notes

记 (simplified from 記) combines the speech radical 讠with 己 (jǐ, self), suggesting "words of the self" — a record of one's own thoughts and experiences. The character covers both the act of committing to memory (记住) and the written product of recording (日记, 笔记). The pair 记得/忘记 (remember/forget) is fundamental for daily conversation. Note the complement pattern: 记住 uses 住 as a resultative complement meaning the remembering is firmly established.
