# 泊 To Moor

- **Character**: 泊
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: to moor, to anchor
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 停泊 (tíng bó) — to anchor, to berth
- 泊位 (bó wèi) — berth, parking space

## Example Sentences

- 船只停泊在港口。(Chuán zhǐ tíng bó zài gǎng kǒu.) — The ships are anchored in the harbor.

## Notes

泊 shows water 氵 and white 白, originally referring to still, clear water where boats rest.
