# 靶 Target

- **Character**: 靶
- **Pinyin**: bǎ
- **Meaning**: target, bull's-eye
- **Radical**: 革 (leather)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 靶子 (bǎ zi) — target
- 射靶 (shè bǎ) — target shooting

## Example Sentences

- 箭射中了靶心。(Jiàn shè zhòng le bǎxīn.) — The arrow hit the bull's-eye.

## Notes

Target for archery and shooting sports
