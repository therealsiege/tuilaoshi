# 怒 Angry, rage

- **Character**: 怒
- **Pinyin**: nù
- **Meaning**: angry, rage
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 愤怒 (fèn nù) — angry, indignant
- 发怒 (fā nù) — to get angry

## Example Sentences

- 他怒气冲冲地离开了。(Tā nùqì chōngchōng de líkāi le.) — He left in a rage.

## Notes

Core character for anger and rage, very commonly used.
