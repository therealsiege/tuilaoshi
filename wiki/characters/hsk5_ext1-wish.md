# 愿 Wish, Willing, 愿意 Willing

- **Character**: 愿
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: wish, willing, 愿意 willing
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 愿意 (yuàn yì) — willing, want
- 心愿 (xīn yuàn) — wish, aspiration
- 祝愿 (zhù yuàn) — wish, hope

## Example Sentences

- 我愿意帮助你。(Wǒ yuànyì bāngzhù nǐ.) — I'm willing to help you.

## Notes

Common verb for willingness and wishes. 愿意 (willing) is essential for expressing consent and desire in everyday speech.
