# 自 Self, from, since

- **Character**: 自
- **Pinyin**: zì
- **Meaning**: self, from, since
- **Radical**: 自 (self)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 自己 (zìjǐ) — self, oneself
- 自诉 (zìsù) — private prosecution

## Example Sentences

- 受害者提起自诉。(Shòuhàizhě tíqǐ zìsù.) — The victim filed a private prosecution.

## Notes

Very common character for self and related concepts.
