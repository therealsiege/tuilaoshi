# 带 Band, bring

- **Character**: 带
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: band, bring
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 带宽 (dài kuān) — bandwidth
- 频带 (pín dài) — frequency band

## Example Sentences

- 带宽不够。(Dài kuān bù gòu.) — The bandwidth is insufficient.

## Notes

Used in 带宽 (bandwidth), 磁带 (tape), 携带 (carry)
