# 网 Net

- **Character**: 网
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: net, network
- **Radical**: 网 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 网球 (wǎng qiú) — tennis
- 上网 (shàng wǎng) — to go online

## Example Sentences

- 网球比赛很精彩。(Wǎngqiú bǐsài hěn jīngcǎi.) — The tennis match is very exciting.

## Notes

Used in tennis and other net sports; also means internet/network
