# 措 Arrange, 措施 Measure

- **Character**: 措
- **Pinyin**: cuò
- **Meaning**: arrange,措施 measure
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 措施 (cuò shī) — measure, step
- 举措 (jǔ cuò) — move, measure
- 筹措 (chóu cuò) — raise (funds)

## Example Sentences

- 政府采取了有效措施控制疫情。(Zhèngfǔ cǎiqǔ le yǒuxiào cuòshī kòngzhì yìqíng.) — The government took effective measures to control the epidemic.

## Notes

Almost exclusively used in 措施 (measures/steps), a very common term in official announcements and policy documents.
