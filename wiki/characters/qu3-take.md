# 取 Take, get, fetch

- **Character**: 取
- **Pinyin**: qǔ
- **Meaning**: take, get, fetch
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 取得 (qǔdé) — obtain, get
- 取保 (qǔbǎo) — bail

## Example Sentences

- 他取得了很大成就。(Tā qǔdé le hěn dà chéngjiù.) — He achieved great accomplishments.

## Notes

Very common for taking and obtaining.
