# 臻 Arrive, reach perfection

- **Character**: 臻
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: arrive, reach perfection
- **Radical**: 至 (arrive)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 臻于完善 (zhēn yú wánshàn) — reach perfection
- 日臻成熟 (rì zhēn chéngshú) — become increasingly mature

## Example Sentences

- 他的技艺日臻完美。(Tā de jìyì rì zhēn wánměi.) — His craftsmanship is approaching perfection.

## Notes

Literary term often used to describe gradual improvement toward perfection.
