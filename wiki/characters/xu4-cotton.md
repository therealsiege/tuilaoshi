# 絮 Cotton Wadding

- **Character**: 絮
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: cotton wadding, long-winded
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 棉絮 (mián xù) — cotton wadding
- 絮叨 (xù dao) — to nag, to ramble

## Example Sentences

- 她总是絮絮叨叨的。(Tā zǒng shì xù xù dāo dāo de.) — She always rambles on and on.

## Notes

絮 has silk 糸 below 如 (like), soft fibrous material like silk.
