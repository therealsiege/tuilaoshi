# 琴 Stringed Instrument

- **Character**: 琴
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: stringed instrument
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琴书 (qín shū) — narrative ballad with string accompaniment
- 钢琴 (gāng qín) — piano

## Example Sentences

- 琴书是用琴伴奏的说唱。(Qín shū shì yòng qín bàn zòu de shuō chàng.) — Qinshu is narrative singing with string accompaniment.

## Notes

琴 means stringed instrument; 琴书 is ballad singing with dulcimer.
