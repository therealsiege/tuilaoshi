# 哑 Mute

- **Character**: 哑
- **Pinyin**: yǎ
- **Meaning**: mute, dumb, hoarse
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 哑巴 (yǎ ba) — mute person
- 哑口无言 (yǎ kǒu wú yán) — speechless

## Example Sentences

- 他嗓子哑了。(Tā sǎngzi yǎ le.) — His voice is hoarse.

## Notes

哑 combines mouth 口 with 亚 (second), representing impaired speech function.
