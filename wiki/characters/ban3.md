# 版 Edition

- **Character**: 版
- **Pinyin**: bǎn
- **Meaning**: edition, version, page
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 版本 (bǎn běn) — version
- 出版 (chū bǎn) — to publish

## Example Sentences

- 这是最新版本。(Zhè shì zuì xīn bǎn běn.) — This is the latest version.

## Notes

版 means edition or version. 出版 = to publish.
