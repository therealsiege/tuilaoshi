# 雇 To hire, employ

- **Character**: 雇
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: to hire, employ
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 雇佣 (gù yōng) — to employ, hire
- 雇员 (gù yuán) — employee

## Example Sentences

- 公司雇佣了十名新员工。(Gōng sī gù yōng le shí míng xīn yuán gōng.) — The company hired ten new employees.

## Notes

Common verb for hiring or employing. 雇主 means employer. 受雇 means to be employed.
