# 含 To Contain

- **Character**: 含
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: to contain, to hold in mouth
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 包含 (bāo hán) — to contain
- 含义 (hán yì) — meaning, implication

## Example Sentences

- 这句话含义很深。(Zhè jù huà hán yì hěn shēn.) — This sentence has deep meaning.

## Notes

含 shows mouth 口 with 今 (now), representing holding something in the mouth or containing.
