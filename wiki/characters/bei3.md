# 北 North

- **Character**: 北
- **Pinyin**: běi
- **Meaning**: north
- **Radical**: 匕 (spoon)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 北京 (běi jīng) — Beijing
- 北方 (běi fāng) — the North

## Example Sentences

- 北京是中国的首都。(Běi jīng shì zhōng guó de shǒu dū.) — Beijing is the capital of China.

## Notes

北 originally showed two people back to back — turning one's back, hence the idea of the direction behind you (north, when facing south).
