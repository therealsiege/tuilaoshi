# 缓 Slow, buffer

- **Character**: 缓
- **Pinyin**: huǎn
- **Meaning**: slow, buffer
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缓存 (huǎn cún) — cache, buffer
- 缓冲 (huǎn chōng) — buffer

## Example Sentences

- 清除浏览器缓存可以解决问题。(Qīng chú liú lǎn qì huǎn cún kě yǐ jiě jué wèn tí.) — Clearing the browser cache can solve the problem.

## Notes

Used in 缓存 (cache), 缓冲 (buffer), important for performance optimization
