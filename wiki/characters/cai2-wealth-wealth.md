# 财 Wealth

- **Character**: 财
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: wealth, money, property
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 财产 (cái chǎn) — property
- 财务 (cái wù) — finance

## Example Sentences

- 他管理公司的财务。(Tā guǎn lǐ gōng sī de cái wù.) — He manages the company's finances.

## Notes

财 uses the shell radical 贝 (shells were ancient currency). 发财 = get rich.
