# 兆 Omen

- **Character**: 兆
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: omen, million
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 预兆 (yù zhào) — omen, portent
- 吉兆 (jí zhào) — good omen

## Example Sentences

- 这是个好兆头。(Zhè shì ge hǎo zhào tóu.) — This is a good omen.

## Notes

兆 originally depicted cracks in oracle bones used for divination. Also means million in modern usage.
