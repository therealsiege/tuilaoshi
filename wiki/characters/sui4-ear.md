# 穗 Ear of grain, tassel

- **Character**: 穗
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: ear of grain, tassel
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 麦穗 (mài suì) — ear of wheat
- 穗子 (suì zi) — tassel, ear

## Example Sentences

- 麦田里的麦穗黄了。(Màitián lǐ de màisuì huáng le.) — The wheat ears in the field have turned yellow.

## Notes

Short for Guangzhou (广州). Grain radical shows agricultural origin.
