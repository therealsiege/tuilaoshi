# 带 Belt

- **Character**: 带
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: belt, to carry, to lead
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 盘带 (pán dài) — to dribble
- 带球 (dài qiú) — to carry the ball

## Example Sentences

- 前锋带球突破防线。(Qiánfēng dài qiú túpò fángxiàn.) — The forward carries the ball through the defense.

## Notes

Carrying the ball; advancing with possession
