# 涉 Wade, involve, experience

- **Character**: 涉
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: wade, involve, experience
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涉及 (shè jí) — involve, relate to
- 跋涉 (bá shè) — trek, trudge

## Example Sentences

- 这个案件涉及多个部门。(Zhè gè àn jiàn shè jí duō gè bù mén.) — This case involves multiple departments.

## Notes

Original meaning is to wade; now commonly means to involve.
