# 睛 Eye

- **Character**: 睛
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: eye, eyeball
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 画龙点睛 (huà lóng diǎn jīng) — add the finishing touch
- 眼睛 (yǎn jīng) — eye

## Example Sentences

- 这个标题起到了画龙点睛的作用。(Zhège biāotí qǐdào le huà lóng diǎn jīng de zuòyòng.) — This title served as the perfect finishing touch.

## Notes

Used in 画龙点睛: dotting the eyes of a painted dragon brings it to life.
