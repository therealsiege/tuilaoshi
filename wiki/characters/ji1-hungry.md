# 饥 Hungry

- **Character**: 饥
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: hungry
- **Radical**: 饣 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 饥饿 (jī è) — hungry, hunger
- 饥渴 (jī kě) — hungry and thirsty
- 饥荒 (jī huāng) — famine

## Example Sentences

- 低血糖会感到饥饿。(Dī xiě táng huì gǎn dào jī è.) — Low blood sugar makes you feel hungry.

## Notes

Hunger sensation; metabolic indicator.
