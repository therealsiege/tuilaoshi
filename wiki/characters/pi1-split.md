# 劈 To Split

- **Character**: 劈
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: to split, to chop
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 劈开 (pī kāi) — to split open
- 劈叉 (pǐ chà) — to do the splits

## Example Sentences

- 他一掌劈开木板。(Tā yì zhǎng pī kāi mù bǎn.) — He split the wooden board with one chop.

## Notes

劈 has 辟 (to open) with knife 刂, cutting forcefully downward.
