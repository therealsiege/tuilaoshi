# 铣 Mill, milling

- **Character**: 铣
- **Pinyin**: xǐ
- **Meaning**: mill, milling
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铣床 (xǐ chuáng) — milling machine
- 铣削 (xǐ xiāo) — milling

## Example Sentences

- 铣床加工金属零件。(Xǐ chuáng jiā gōng jīn shǔ líng jiàn.) — Milling machines process metal parts.

## Notes

Used in 铣床 (milling machine), 铣削 (milling), CNC machining
