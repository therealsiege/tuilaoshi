# 扩 To Expand

- **Character**: 扩
- **Pinyin**: kuò
- **Meaning**: to expand, to enlarge, to扩大
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扩大 (kuò dà) — to expand
- 扩展 (kuò zhǎn) — to extend

## Example Sentences

- 公司正在扩大规模。(Gōng sī zhèng zài kuò dà guī mó.) — The company is expanding its scale.

## Notes

扩 is simplified from 擴. The hand radical suggests active expansion.
