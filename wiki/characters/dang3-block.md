# 挡 To Block

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, to resist, gear
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to block
- 挡住 (dǎng zhù) — to block, to keep out

## Example Sentences

- 树挡住了阳光。(Shù dǎng zhù le yáng guāng.) — The tree blocked the sunlight.

## Notes

挡 uses hand 扌 and 当 (to serve as), representing using one's hand to block or ward off something.
