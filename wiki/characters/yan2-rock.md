# 岩 Rock, crag

- **Character**: 岩
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: rock, crag
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 岩石 (yán shí) — rock
- 火山岩 (huǒ shān yán) — volcanic rock

## Example Sentences

- 这种岩石形成于数百万年前。(Zhè zhǒng yán shí xíng chéng yú shù bǎi wàn nián qián.) — This rock formed millions of years ago.

## Notes

Fundamental term in geology for rocks
