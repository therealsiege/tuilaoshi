# 瘫 Paralyzed, down

- **Character**: 瘫
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: paralyzed, down
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘫痪 (tān huàn) — paralyzed, down (system)
- 系统瘫痪 (xì tǒng tān huàn) — system down

## Example Sentences

- 网络瘫痪了。(Wǎng luò tān huàn le.) — The network is down.

## Notes

Used in 瘫痪 (system down), network failure terminology
