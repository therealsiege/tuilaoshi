# 蕈 Mushroom

- **Character**: 蕈
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: mushroom, fungus
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 蕈菌 (xùn jūn) — mushroom
- 毒蕈 (dú xùn) — poisonous mushroom

## Example Sentences

- 森林里有很多野生蕈类。(Sēn lín lǐ yǒu hěn duō yě shēng xùn lèi.) — There are many wild mushrooms in the forest.

## Notes

蕈 has grass 艹 with 覃 (to extend), umbrella-shaped fungi.
