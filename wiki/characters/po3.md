# 叵 Cannot, not

- **Character**: 叵
- **Pinyin**: pǒ
- **Meaning**: cannot, not
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 叵测 (pǒ cè) — unfathomable, unpredictable
- 居心叵测 (jū xīn pǒ cè) — harbor evil intentions

## Example Sentences

- 此人居心叵测。(Cǐ rén jū xīn pǒ cè.) — This person harbors evil intentions.

## Notes

Classical character mostly used in the phrase 叵测 (unfathomable)
