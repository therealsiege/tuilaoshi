# 涂 Apply

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: apply, smear
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to apply, smear
- 涂药 (tú yào) — to apply medicine
- 涂层 (tú céng) — coating

## Example Sentences

- 在患处涂抹药膏。(Zài huàn chù tú mǒ yào gāo.) — Apply ointment to the affected area.

## Notes

Spreading or coating; topical application.
