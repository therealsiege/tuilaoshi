# 析 Analyze

- **Character**: 析
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: analyze, separate
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 透析 (tòu xī) — dialysis
- 分析 (fēn xī) — to analyze
- 解析 (jiě xī) — to analyze, parse

## Example Sentences

- 血液透析每周三次。(Xiě yè tòu xī měi zhōu sān cì.) — Hemodialysis is done three times a week.

## Notes

Separation and analysis; component of dialysis term.
