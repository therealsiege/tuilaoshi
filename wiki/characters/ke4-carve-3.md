# 刻 Carve

- **Character**: 刻
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: to carve, quarter hour, moment
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刻舟求剑 (kè zhōu qiú jiàn) — cling to outdated methods
- 刻苦 (kè kǔ) — hardworking

## Example Sentences

- 这种做法无异于刻舟求剑。(Zhè zhǒng zuòfǎ wúyì yú kè zhōu qiú jiàn.) — This approach is no different from clinging to outdated methods.

## Notes

Used in 刻舟求剑: carving a mark on the boat to find a dropped sword.
