# 漆 Lacquer

- **Character**: 漆
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: lacquer, paint
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 油漆 (yóu qī) — paint, lacquer
- 漆器 (qī qì) — lacquerware

## Example Sentences

- 中国的漆器工艺历史悠久。(Zhōngguó de qīqì gōngyì lìshǐ yōujiǔ.) — Chinese lacquerware has a long history.

## Notes

漆 refers to lacquer or paint. Chinese lacquerware is a traditional decorative art.
