# 挠 Yield

- **Character**: 挠
- **Pinyin**: náo
- **Meaning**: to yield, to scratch, to bend
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 百折不挠 (bǎi zhé bù náo) — indomitable
- 阻挠 (zǔ náo) — to obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 任何困难都不能使他屈挠。(Rènhé kùnnan dōu bù néng shǐ tā qū náo.) — No difficulty can make him yield.

## Notes

Used in 百折不挠: not yielding; 不挠 means unbending, unyielding.
