# 阀 Valve, powerful person

- **Character**: 阀
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: valve, powerful person
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阀门 (fá mén) — valve
- 阀值 (fá zhí) — threshold value

## Example Sentences

- 请关闭水管的阀门。(Qǐng guān bì shuǐ guǎn de fá mén.) — Please close the water valve.

## Notes

Technical term for valve. Also used historically for powerful families (军阀 warlord).
