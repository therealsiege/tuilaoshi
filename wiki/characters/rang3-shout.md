# 嚷 To Shout

- **Character**: 嚷
- **Pinyin**: rǎng
- **Meaning**: to shout, to make a racket
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 叫嚷 (jiào rǎng) — to shout, to yell
- 嚷嚷 (rǎng rang) — to make noise

## Example Sentences

- 别在这里大声嚷嚷。(Bié zài zhè lǐ dà shēng rǎng rǎng.) — Don't make a racket here.

## Notes

嚷 combines mouth 口 with 襄 (assist), representing loud vocal commotion.
