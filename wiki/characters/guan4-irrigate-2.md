# 灌 Irrigate, pour, fill

- **Character**: 灌
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: irrigate, pour, fill
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 灌溉 (guàn gài) — irrigate
- 灌水 (guàn shuǐ) — pour water, flood

## Example Sentences

- 良好的灌溉系统对农业很重要。(Liánghǎo de guàngài xìtǒng duì nóngyè hěn zhòngyào.) — Good irrigation systems are very important for agriculture.

## Notes

Irrigating crops or pouring liquids. China developed sophisticated irrigation systems in ancient times.
