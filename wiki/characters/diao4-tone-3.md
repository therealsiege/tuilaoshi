# 调 Tone

- **Character**: 调
- **Pinyin**: diào
- **Meaning**: tone, melody, tune
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 曲调 (qǔ diào) — tune, melody
- 调子 (diào zi) — tune, tone

## Example Sentences

- 这首歌的曲调很优美。(Zhè shǒu gē de qǔdiào hěn yōuměi.) — The melody of this song is very beautiful.

## Notes

调 (diào) refers to tone or melody in music. Also pronounced tiáo meaning to adjust or transfer.
