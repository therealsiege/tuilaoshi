# 决 To Decide

- **Character**: 决
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to decide, decisive
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 决定 (jué dìng) — to decide
- 解决 (jiě jué) — to solve

## Example Sentences

- 你决定了吗？(Nǐ jué dìng le ma?) — Have you decided?

## Notes

决 relates to water breaking through — hence 'decisive.' 决赛 = final (competition).
