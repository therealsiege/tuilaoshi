# 桥 Bridge

- **Character**: 桥
- **Pinyin**: qiáo
- **Meaning**: bridge
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 桥梁 (qiáo liáng) — bridge
- 大桥 (dà qiáo) — large bridge

## Example Sentences

- 他们正在建一座大桥。(Tā men zhèng zài jiàn yí zuò dà qiáo.) — They are building a large bridge.

## Notes

桥 simplified from 橋. Wood 木 structure spanning water.
