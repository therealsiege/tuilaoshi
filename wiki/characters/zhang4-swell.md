# 胀 To swell, distend, 脹 (traditional)

- **Character**: 胀
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: to swell, distend, 脹 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 膨胀 (péng zhàng) — to expand, swell
- 肿胀 (zhǒng zhàng) — swollen

## Example Sentences

- 吃太多会感觉腹胀。(Chī tài duō huì gǎnjué fù zhàng.) — Eating too much will make you feel bloated.

## Notes

Swelling or expansion, often from internal pressure.
