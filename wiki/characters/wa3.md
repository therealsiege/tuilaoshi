# 瓦 Tile

- **Character**: 瓦
- **Pinyin**: wǎ
- **Meaning**: tile, earthenware
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 瓦解 (wǎ jiě) — to disintegrate, to fall apart
- 砖瓦 (zhuān wǎ) — bricks and tiles

## Example Sentences

- 这个组织已经瓦解了。(Zhè ge zǔ zhī yǐ jīng wǎ jiě le.) — This organization has already disintegrated.

## Notes

瓦 is both a radical and character meaning tile. 瓦解 literally means 'to break apart like tiles,' used for organizational collapse.
