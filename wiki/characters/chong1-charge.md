# 充 Charge, fill

- **Character**: 充
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: charge, fill
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 充电 (chōng diàn) — charge (battery)
- 充满 (chōng mǎn) — fill

## Example Sentences

- 手机需要充电。(Shǒu jī xū yào chōng diàn.) — The phone needs charging.

## Notes

Used in 充电 (charge), 充电器 (charger), 充满 (full)
