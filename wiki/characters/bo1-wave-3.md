# 波 Wave

- **Character**: 波
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: wave, undulation
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 波长 (bō cháng) — wavelength
- 微波 (wēi bō) — microwave

## Example Sentences

- 微波炉很方便。(Wēi bō lú hěn fāng biàn.) — The microwave oven is very convenient.

## Notes

波 with water radical for wave extends to electromagnetic waves and oscillations.
