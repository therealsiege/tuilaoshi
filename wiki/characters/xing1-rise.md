# 兴 Rise, flourish, start

- **Character**: 兴
- **Pinyin**: xīng
- **Meaning**: rise, flourish,興 興 興 start
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 兴旺 (xīng wàng) — prosperous, thriving
- 兴盛 (xīng shèng) — prosperous

## Example Sentences

- 这个行业正在兴旺发展。(Zhè ge háng yè zhèng zài xīng wàng fā zhǎn.) — This industry is thriving and developing.

## Notes

As xīng means to prosper or rise. As xìng means interest or mood. 复兴 means renaissance.
