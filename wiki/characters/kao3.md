# 考 To Test

- **Character**: 考
- **Pinyin**: kǎo
- **Meaning**: to test, to examine
- **Radical**: 耂 (old)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 考试 (kǎo shì) — exam
- 考虑 (kǎo lǜ) — to consider

## Example Sentences

- 明天有考试。(Míng tiān yǒu kǎo shì.) — There's an exam tomorrow.

## Notes

考 originally meant 'old/elder' (related to 老). The meaning shifted to 'examine/test' — elders testing the young.
