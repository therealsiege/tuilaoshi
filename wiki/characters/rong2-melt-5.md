# 熔 Melt

- **Character**: 熔
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: melt, smelt
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熔化 (róng huà) — melt, fuse
- 熔炉 (róng lú) — furnace, melting pot

## Example Sentences

- 金属在高温下会熔化。(Jīnshǔ zài gāowēn xià huì rónghuà.) — Metal melts at high temperatures.

## Notes

熔 means to melt or smelt, especially metals. The fire radical indicates the heating process involved.
