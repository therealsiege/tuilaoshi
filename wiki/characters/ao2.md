# 熬 To Endure

- **Character**: 熬
- **Pinyin**: áo
- **Meaning**: to endure, to boil slowly
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire dots)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熬夜 (áo yè) — to stay up late
- 煎熬 (jiān áo) — torment

## Example Sentences

- 别熬夜了。(Bié áo yè le.) — Don't stay up late.

## Notes

熬 means to cook slowly or to endure. 熬夜 = burn the midnight oil.
