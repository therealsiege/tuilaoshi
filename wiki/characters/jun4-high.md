# 峻 High and steep, severe

- **Character**: 峻
- **Pinyin**: jùn
- **Meaning**: high and steep, severe
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 险峻 (xiǎnjùn) — precipitous, steep
- 严峻 (yánjùn) — severe, stern

## Example Sentences

- 形势严峻。(Xíngshì yánjùn.) — The situation is severe.

## Notes

Describes steep mountains or severe situations.
