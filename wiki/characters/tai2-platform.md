# 台 Platform

- **Character**: 台
- **Pinyin**: tái
- **Meaning**: platform, stage, table
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 舞台 (wǔ tái) — stage
- 平台 (píng tái) — platform

## Example Sentences

- 他站在舞台上。(Tā zhàn zài wǔ tái shàng.) — He stands on the stage.

## Notes

台 for raised platform, also used as measure word for machines and performances.
