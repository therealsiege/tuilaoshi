# 翼 Wing

- **Character**: 翼
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: wing, flank
- **Radical**: 羽(feather)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 翼宿 (yì xiù) — Wings constellation
- 机翼 (jī yì) — aircraft wing

## Example Sentences

- 翼宿是二十八宿之一。(Yì xiù shì èrshíbā xiù zhī yī.) — Wings is one of the 28 lunar mansions.

## Notes

翼 means wing or flank. In astronomy, refers to the Wings constellation, one of the 28 mansions.
