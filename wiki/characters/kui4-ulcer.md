# 溃 Ulcer

- **Character**: 溃
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: ulcer, fester
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 溃疡 (kuì yáng) — ulcer
- 溃烂 (kuì làn) — festering, ulceration
- 胃溃疡 (wèi kuì yáng) — gastric ulcer

## Example Sentences

- 他患有胃溃疡。(Tā huàn yǒu wèi kuì yáng.) — He has a gastric ulcer.

## Notes

Describes tissue breakdown; common in GI and skin conditions.
