# 数 To Count

- **Character**: 数
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: to count, to enumerate
- **Radical**: 攴 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 数字 (shù zì) — number
- 数来宝 (shǔ lái bǎo) — rhythmic comic talk

## Example Sentences

- 数来宝是一种快板艺术。(Shǔ lái bǎo shì yī zhǒng kuài bǎn yì shù.) — Shulaibao is a form of rhythmic comic performance.

## Notes

数 (shǔ) means to count; also read as shù meaning number.
