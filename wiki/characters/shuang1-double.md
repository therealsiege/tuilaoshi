# 双 Double

- **Character**: 双
- **Pinyin**: shuāng
- **Meaning**: double, pair, both
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 双方 (shuāng fāng) — both sides
- 双簧 (shuāng huáng) — two-person comic show

## Example Sentences

- 双簧是两人配合的表演。(Shuāng huáng shì liǎng rén pèi hé de biǎo yǎn.) — Shuanghuang is a performance requiring two people's coordination.

## Notes

双 means double or pair; 双簧 is a comic act with one speaking, one acting.
