# 亩 Mu (unit of area)

- **Character**: 亩
- **Pinyin**: mǔ
- **Meaning**: mu (unit of area)
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 亩产 (mǔ chǎn) — yield per mu
- 英亩 (yīng mǔ) — acre

## Example Sentences

- 这块地有十亩。(Zhè kuài dì yǒu shí mǔ.) — This plot of land is ten mu.

## Notes

1 mu = ~666.7 m². Traditional Chinese land measurement unit.
