# 羁 Halter, restrain, detain

- **Character**: 羁
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: halter, restrain, detain
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 羁押 (jīyā) — detain, hold in custody
- 羁绊 (jībàn) — tie, fetter

## Example Sentences

- 嫌疑人被羁押。(Xiányírén bèi jīyā.) — The suspect was detained.

## Notes

Legal term for detention; literary term for restraint.
