# 盛 To ladle, to serve (food)

- **Character**: 盛
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to ladle, to serve (food)
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盛饭 (chéng fàn) — to serve rice
- 盛汤 (chéng tāng) — to ladle soup

## Example Sentences

- 请给我盛一碗饭。(Qǐng gěi wǒ chéng yī wǎn fàn.) — Please serve me a bowl of rice.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: chéng (ladle) and shèng (flourishing). Dish radical.
