# 萎 To Wilt

- **Character**: 萎
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: to wilt, to wither
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 枯萎 (kū wěi) — to wither
- 萎缩 (wěi suō) — to shrink

## Example Sentences

- 花在太阳下萎了。(Huā zài tài yáng xià wěi le.) — The flowers wilted in the sun.

## Notes

萎 has grass 艹 on top with 委 (to entrust/limp), plants drooping.
