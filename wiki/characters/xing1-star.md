# 星 Star, planet

- **Character**: 星
- **Pinyin**: xīng
- **Meaning**: star, planet
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 星球 (xīng qiú) — celestial body, planet
- 恒星 (héng xīng) — fixed star

## Example Sentences

- 夜空中有无数的星星。(Yè kōng zhōng yǒu wú shù de xīng xing.) — There are countless stars in the night sky.

## Notes

Used in astronomy for stars and celestial bodies
