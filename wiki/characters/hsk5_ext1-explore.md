# 探 Explore, Probe, 探索 Explore

- **Character**: 探
- **Pinyin**: tàn
- **Meaning**: explore, probe, 探索 explore
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 探索 (tàn suǒ) — explore, seek
- 探讨 (tàn tǎo) — explore, investigate
- 探测 (tàn cè) — probe, detect

## Example Sentences

- 科学家们正在探索新的治疗方法。(Kēxuéjiāmen zhèngzài tànsuǒ xīn de zhìliáo fāngfǎ.) — Scientists are exploring new treatment methods.

## Notes

Common in research and scientific contexts. Implies active investigation and seeking of knowledge or understanding.
