# 视 To Look

- **Character**: 视
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to look at, vision
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 电视 (diàn shì) — television
- 视频 (shì pín) — video

## Example Sentences

- 我在看电视。(Wǒ zài kàn diàn shì.) — I am watching TV.

## Notes

视 means to look at or vision; 电视 literally means 'electric vision'.
