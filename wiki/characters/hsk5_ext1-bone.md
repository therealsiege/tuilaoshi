# 骨 Bone, 骨头 Bone

- **Character**: 骨
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: bone, 骨头 bone
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 骨头 (gǔ tou) — bone
- 骨折 (gǔ zhé) — fracture
- 骨气 (gǔ qì) — moral integrity

## Example Sentences

- 他摔断了胳膊骨头。(Tā shuāi duàn le gēbo gǔtou.) — He broke his arm bone in the fall.

## Notes

Basic anatomical term for bone. 骨折 (fracture) is common medical term. 骨气 figuratively means integrity/backbone.
