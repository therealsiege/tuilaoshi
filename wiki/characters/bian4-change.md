# 变 Change, transform, vary

- **Character**: 变
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: change, transform, vary
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 变化 (biàn huà) — change, variation
- 突变 (tū biàn) — mutation

## Example Sentences

- 基因突变可能导致新的性状。(Jī yīn tū biàn kě néng dǎo zhì xīn de xìng zhuàng.) — Gene mutations may lead to new traits.

## Notes

Used for changes and mutations in biology
