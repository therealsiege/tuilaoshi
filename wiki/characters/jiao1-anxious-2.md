# 焦 Anxious

- **Character**: 焦
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: anxious, burnt
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 焦虑 (jiāo lǜ) — anxiety, anxious
- 焦急 (jiāo jí) — anxious, worried
- 焦点 (jiāo diǎn) — focus, focal point

## Example Sentences

- 她患有焦虑症。(Tā huàn yǒu jiāo lǜ zhèng.) — She suffers from anxiety disorder.

## Notes

Mental state of worry; common mental health issue.
