# 喘 Gasp

- **Character**: 喘
- **Pinyin**: chuǎn
- **Meaning**: pant, gasp
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 哮喘 (xiào chuǎn) — asthma
- 气喘 (qì chuǎn) — to pant, asthma
- 喘息 (chuǎn xī) — to gasp for breath

## Example Sentences

- 他跑步后喘得厉害。(Tā pǎo bù hòu chuǎn de lì hai.) — He's panting heavily after running.

## Notes

Labored breathing; respiratory distress.
