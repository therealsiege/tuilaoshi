# 畏 To fear, respect, shrink from

- **Character**: 畏
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: to fear, respect, shrink from
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 畏惧 (wèi jù) — to fear
- 敬畏 (jìng wèi) — to revere, to awe

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该敬畏自然。(Wǒmen yīnggāi jìngwèi zìrán.) — We should respect and revere nature.

## Notes

Can mean fear or respectful awe, depending on context.
