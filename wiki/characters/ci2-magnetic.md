# 磁 Magnetic, magnetism

- **Character**: 磁
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: magnetic, magnetism
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 磁场 (cí chǎng) — magnetic field
- 磁铁 (cí tiě) — magnet

## Example Sentences

- 地球有一个强大的磁场。(Dì qiú yǒu yī ge qiáng dà de cí chǎng.) — The Earth has a powerful magnetic field.

## Notes

Essential in physics for magnetic phenomena
