# 备 — Prepare

- **Character**: 备
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: to prepare, to equip; equipment
- **Radical**: 夂 (go/follow)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 准备 (zhǔnbèi) — to prepare, preparation
- 备课 (bèikè) — to prepare a lesson
- 设备 (shèbèi) — equipment, facilities
- 备用 (bèiyòng) — spare, backup, reserve
- 预备 (yùbèi) — to prepare, preliminary
- 具备 (jùbèi) — to possess, to be equipped with

## Example Sentences

- 我正在准备明天的考试。(Wǒ zhèngzài zhǔnbèi míngtiān de kǎoshì.) — I'm preparing for tomorrow's exam.
- 老师每天都要备课。(Lǎoshī měitiān dōu yào bèikè.) — Teachers need to prepare lessons every day.
- 这个实验室的设备很先进。(Zhè ge shíyànshì de shèbèi hěn xiānjìn.) — The equipment in this lab is very advanced.

## Notes

备 (simplified from 備) originally depicted arrows in a quiver — having supplies ready and equipped for use. The core idea is "having things in readiness." Its most common appearance is in 准备 (zhǔnbèi, to prepare), which pairs 准 (standard/ready) with 备 (equipped) for a compound meaning "to get things to the proper state of readiness." In academic contexts, 备课 (prepare a lesson) is commonly heard. The character also appears in modern technical vocabulary through 设备 (equipment/device).
