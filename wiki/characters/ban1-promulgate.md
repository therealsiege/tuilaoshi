# 颁 Promulgate, issue, award

- **Character**: 颁
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: promulgate, issue, award
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 颁布 (bān bù) — to promulgate, issue
- 颁发 (bān fā) — to issue, award

## Example Sentences

- 政府颁布了新的环保法规。(Zhèng fǔ bān bù le xīn de huán bǎo fǎ guī.) — The government promulgated new environmental regulations.

## Notes

Used for official issuance of laws, decrees, or awards. 颁奖 means to present awards.
