# 沃 Fertile, irrigate, rich

- **Character**: 沃
- **Pinyin**: wò
- **Meaning**: fertile, irrigate, rich
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肥沃 (féi wò) — fertile
- 沃土 (wò tǔ) — fertile soil

## Example Sentences

- 沃土适合种植农作物。(Wò tǔ shì hé zhòng zhí nóng zuò wù.) — Fertile soil is suitable for growing crops.
