# 鸡 Chicken

- **Character**: 鸡
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: chicken
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 鸡蛋 (jī dàn) — egg
- 鸡肉 (jī ròu) — chicken meat

## Example Sentences

- 我要一个鸡蛋。(Wǒ yào yī gè jī dàn.) — I want an egg.

## Notes

鸡 is simplified from 雞. 鸡蛋 literally means 'chicken egg' and is the default word for 'egg.'
