# 空 Empty, air, void

- **Character**: 空
- **Pinyin**: kōng
- **Meaning**: empty, air, void
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 空虚 (kōng xū) — empty, void
- 空性 (kōng xìng) — emptiness (Buddhist)

## Example Sentences

- 佛教讲究色即是空。(Fó jiào jiǎng jiù sè jí shì kōng.) — Buddhism teaches that form is emptiness.

## Notes

Core Buddhist concept of emptiness or śūnyatā
