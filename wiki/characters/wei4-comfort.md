# 慰 To comfort, console

- **Character**: 慰
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: to comfort, console
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 安慰 (ān wèi) — to comfort, console
- 慰问 (wèi wèn) — to express sympathy

## Example Sentences

- 她安慰了受伤的孩子。(Tā ānwèile shòushāng de háizi.) — She comforted the injured child.

## Notes

Expression of comfort and consolation to those suffering or distressed.
