# 宝 Treasure, precious

- **Character**: 宝
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: treasure, precious
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 瑰宝 (guī bǎo) — treasure, gem
- 宝贵 (bǎo guì) — valuable, precious

## Example Sentences

- 时间是最宝贵的财富。(Shí jiān shì zuì bǎo guì de cái fù.) — Time is the most precious wealth.

## Notes

Common character for treasure and precious things.
