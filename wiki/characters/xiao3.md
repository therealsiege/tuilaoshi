# 小 Small/Little

- **Character**: 小
- **Pinyin**: xiǎo
- **Meaning**: small, little, young
- **Radical**: 小 (small)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 小学 (xiǎoxué) — elementary school
- 小时 (xiǎoshí) — hour
- 小心 (xiǎoxīn) — to be careful, watch out
- 小姐 (xiǎojiě) — Miss, young lady
- 大小 (dàxiǎo) — size
- 小吃 (xiǎochī) — snack, street food

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间太小了。(Zhège fángjiān tài xiǎo le.) — This room is too small.
- 小心！(Xiǎoxīn!) — Be careful!
- 我有一个小妹妹。(Wǒ yǒu yí gè xiǎo mèimei.) — I have a little sister.

## Notes

小 is a pictographic character depicting something being divided into small pieces — the central stroke splits downward into two smaller parts. It is itself a radical (radical 42) and appears as a component in many characters like 少 (shǎo, few), 尖 (jiān, sharp/pointed), and 尘 (chén, dust). As a prefix before surnames (小王, 小李), it serves as a familiar, affectionate form of address for someone younger or of similar age.
