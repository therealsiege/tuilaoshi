# 脸 Face

- **Character**: 脸
- **Pinyin**: liǎn
- **Meaning**: face
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 洗脸 (xǐ liǎn) — wash face
- 脸红 (liǎn hóng) — blush, face red

## Example Sentences

- 她每天早上洗脸。(Tā měi tiān zǎoshang xǐ liǎn.) — She washes her face every morning.

## Notes

The radical 月 originally meant 'moon' but here represents 'flesh/body part'. Essential body vocabulary.
