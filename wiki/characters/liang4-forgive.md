# 谅 To forgive, to understand

- **Character**: 谅
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: to forgive, to understand
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谅解 (liàng jiě) — to understand, to forgive
- 原谅 (yuán liàng) — to forgive, to excuse

## Example Sentences

- 请原谅我的错误。(Qǐng yuánliàng wǒ de cuòwù.) — Please forgive my mistake.

## Notes

Emphasizes understanding and forgiving others.
