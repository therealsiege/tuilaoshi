# 累 Tired

- **Character**: 累
- **Pinyin**: lèi
- **Meaning**: tired, weary
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 很累 (hěn lèi) — very tired
- 疲累 (pí lèi) — exhausted

## Example Sentences

- 我今天很累。(Wǒ jīn tiān hěn lèi.) — I'm very tired today.

## Notes

累 shows 田 (fields) piled up - accumulated labor causes tiredness. Also read lěi (accumulate).
