# 涩 Astringent

- **Character**: 涩
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: astringent, obscure
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晦涩 (huì sè) — obscure
- 苦涩 (kǔ sè) — bitter

## Example Sentences

- 这个柿子很涩。(Zhè gè shì zi hěn sè.) — This persimmon is very astringent.

## Notes

涩 simplified from 澀. Water 氵 with rough texture, not smooth flowing.
