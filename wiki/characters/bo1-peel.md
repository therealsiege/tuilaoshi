# 剥 To Peel

- **Character**: 剥
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to peel, to strip, to shell
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 剥夺 (bō duó) — to deprive
- 剥皮 (bāo pí) — to peel

## Example Sentences

- 帮我剥个橘子。(Bāng wǒ bāo ge jú zi.) — Help me peel an orange.

## Notes

剥 as bō is formal (剥夺 = deprive). As bāo is colloquial (剥花生 = shell peanuts).
