# 她 She

- **Character**: 她
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: she, her
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 她们 (tā men) — they (female)
- 她的 (tā de) — her, hers

## Example Sentences

- 她是我的妈妈。(Tā shì wǒ de māmā.) — She is my mother.

## Notes

她 was created in the early 20th century to distinguish 'she' from 'he' (他) in written Chinese. Both are pronounced tā.
