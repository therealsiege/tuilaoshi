# 沉 To sink, heavy, deep

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: to sink, heavy, deep
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沉重 (chén zhòng) — heavy, serious
- 沉默 (chén mò) — silent

## Example Sentences

- 石头沉到了水底。(Shítou chén dàole shuǐ dǐ.) — The stone sank to the bottom of the water.

## Notes

To sink or be heavy/deep, also silence.
