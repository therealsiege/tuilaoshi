# 漂 Pretty

- **Character**: 漂
- **Pinyin**: piào
- **Meaning**: pretty, beautiful (piào); to float (piāo)
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漂亮 (piào liàng) — beautiful
- 漂浮 (piāo fú) — to float

## Example Sentences

- 她很漂亮。(Tā hěn piào liàng.) — She is very beautiful.

## Notes

漂亮 is the most common word for beautiful/pretty. 漂 as piāo means to float.
