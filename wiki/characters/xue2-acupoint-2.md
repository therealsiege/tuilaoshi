# 穴 Acupoint

- **Character**: 穴
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: acupoint, cave
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 穴位 (xué wèi) — acupuncture point
- 洞穴 (dòng xué) — cave
- 针穴 (zhēn xué) — acupoint

## Example Sentences

- 针灸治疗要找准穴位。(Zhēn jiǔ zhì liáo yào zhǎo zhǔn xué wèi.) — Acupuncture treatment requires finding the right acupoints.

## Notes

Energy points on body; TCM meridian system.
