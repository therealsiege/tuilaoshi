# 代 Generation, substitute

- **Character**: 代
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: generation,代
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 代码 (dài mǎ) — code
- 迭代 (dié dài) — iterate

## Example Sentences
