# 禁 Prohibit, forbid, endure

- **Character**: 禁
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: prohibit, forbid, endure
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 禁止 (jìn zhǐ) — prohibit, forbid
- 禁令 (jìn lìng) — prohibition order

## Example Sentences

- 这里禁止吸烟。(Zhè lǐ jìn zhǐ xī yān.) — Smoking is prohibited here.

## Notes

Also read as jīn meaning to endure
