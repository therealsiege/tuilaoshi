# 慢 Slow

- **Character**: 慢
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: slow
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 慢慢 (màn man) — slowly
- 慢走 (màn zǒu) — take care (when seeing someone off)

## Example Sentences

- 请说慢一点。(Qǐng shuō màn yī diǎn.) — Please speak more slowly.

## Notes

慢 uses the heart radical 忄. 慢慢来 (take your time) is a common expression. Opposite of 快.
