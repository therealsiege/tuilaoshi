# 基 Foundation

- **Character**: 基
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: base, foundation, basic
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 基金 (jī jīn) — fund
- 基础 (jī chǔ) — foundation

## Example Sentences

- 教育是国家发展的基础。(Jiào yù shì guó jiā fā zhǎn de jī chǔ.) — Education is the foundation of national development.

## Notes

基 earth 土 supporting 其 (vessel), solid foundation.
