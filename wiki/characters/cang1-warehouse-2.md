# 仓 Warehouse, storehouse

- **Character**: 仓
- **Pinyin**: cāng
- **Meaning**: warehouse, storehouse
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 仓库 (cāng kù) — warehouse
- 仓储 (cāng chǔ) — warehousing, storage

## Example Sentences

- 货物存放在仓库里。(Huò wù cún fàng zài cāng kù lǐ.) — Goods are stored in the warehouse.

## Notes

仓管 is warehouse management. 入仓 means put in warehouse.
