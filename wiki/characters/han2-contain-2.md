# 涵 To contain, cultivate

- **Character**: 涵
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: to contain, cultivate
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 涵养 (hán yǎng) — to cultivate, conserve
- 内涵 (nèi hán) — connotation, substance

## Example Sentences

- 这个词的内涵很丰富。(Zhège cí de nèihán hěn fēngfù.) — This word has rich connotations.

## Notes

Often used for cultural cultivation and inner meaning or substance.
