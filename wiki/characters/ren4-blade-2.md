# 刃 Blade, edge, knife

- **Character**: 刃
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: blade, edge, knife
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 刀刃 (dāo rèn) — blade, cutting edge
- 刃口 (rèn kǒu) — edge of blade

## Example Sentences

- 刀刃要保持锋利。(Dāo rèn yào bǎo chí fēng lì.) — The blade must be kept sharp.

## Notes

Cutting edge
