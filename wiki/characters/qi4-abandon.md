# 弃 To Abandon

- **Character**: 弃
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: to abandon, discard
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 放弃 (fàng qì) — to give up, abandon
- 废弃 (fèi qì) — to discard

## Example Sentences

- 他决定放弃这个计划。(Tā juédìng fàngqì zhège jìhuà.) — He decided to abandon this plan.

## Notes

弃 shows a child in a basket with two hands, representing the act of abandoning an infant.
