# 缆 Cable, rope

- **Character**: 缆
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: cable, rope
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 电缆 (diàn lǎn) — power cable
- 光缆 (guāng lǎn) — optical cable

## Example Sentences

- 这条光缆连接两个城市。(Zhè tiáo guāng lǎn lián jiē liǎng gè chéng shì.) — This optical cable connects two cities.

## Notes

Used in 电缆 (power cable), 光缆 (optical cable), heavy-duty cables
