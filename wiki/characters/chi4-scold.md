# 斥 To Scold

- **Character**: 斥
- **Pinyin**: chì
- **Meaning**: to scold, to expel
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 斥责 (chì zé) — to scold, to rebuke
- 排斥 (pái chì) — to reject, to exclude

## Example Sentences

- 老师严厉斥责了那个学生。(Lǎo shī yán lì chì zé le nà ge xué shēng.) — The teacher severely rebuked that student.

## Notes

斥 indicates direct, sharp criticism. Can also mean to expel or reject.
