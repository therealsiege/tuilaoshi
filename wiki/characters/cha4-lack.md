# 差 To Lack

- **Character**: 差
- **Pinyin**: chà
- **Meaning**: to lack, poor, difference; to send (chāi)
- **Radical**: 工 (work)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 差不多 (chà bu duō) — almost
- 差别 (chā bié) — difference

## Example Sentences

- 差不多了。(Chà bu duō le.) — Almost done.

## Notes

差 has multiple readings: chà (poor/lacking), chā (difference), chāi (to send on errand).
