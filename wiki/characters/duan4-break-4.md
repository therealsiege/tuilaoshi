# 断 Break

- **Character**: 断
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: break, cut off
- **Radical**: 斤 (ax)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 戒断 (jiè duàn) — withdrawal (from drugs)
- 中断 (zhōng duàn) — to interrupt
- 断绝 (duàn jué) — to sever

## Example Sentences

- 戒断反应很痛苦。(Jiè duàn fǎn yìng hěn tòng kǔ.) — Withdrawal symptoms are very painful.

## Notes

Severance; withdrawal from substances.
