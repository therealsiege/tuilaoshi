# 厌 Dislike

- **Character**: 厌
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: dislike, be fed up
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 讨厌 (tǎoyàn) — dislike, annoying
- 厌烦 (yànfán) — be fed up

## Example Sentences

- 我讨厌下雨天。(Wǒ tǎoyàn xià yǔ tiān.) — I dislike rainy days.

## Notes

Negative feeling. 讨厌 is very common for expressing dislike. Can be adjective or verb.
