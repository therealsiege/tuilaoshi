# 晶 Crystal

- **Character**: 晶
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: crystal, bright
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 水晶 (shuǐ jīng) — crystal
- 结晶 (jié jīng) — crystallization

## Example Sentences

- 这块水晶非常纯净。(Zhè kuài shuǐ jīng fēi cháng chún jìng.) — This crystal is very pure.

## Notes

晶 has three 日 (sun), brilliant crystalline structure.
