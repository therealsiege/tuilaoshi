# 舆 Carriage, public opinion

- **Character**: 舆
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: carriage, public opinion
- **Radical**: 臼 (mortar)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 舆论 (yú lùn) — public opinion
- 舆情 (yú qíng) — public sentiment

## Example Sentences

- 舆论对政策的影响很大。(Yú lùn duì zhèng cè de yǐng xiǎng hěn dà.) — Public opinion has a great influence on policy.

## Notes

Originally meant 'carriage', now primarily used in 舆论 (public opinion). Common in media and political contexts.
