# 滞 Stagnant, sluggish, unsold

- **Character**: 滞
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: stagnant, sluggish, unsold
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 停滞 (tíng zhì) — to stagnate
- 滞留 (zhì liú) — to be detained, held up

## Example Sentences

- 经济发展不能停滞不前。(Jīng jì fā zhǎn bù néng tíng zhì bù qián.) — Economic development cannot stagnate.

## Notes

Indicates lack of flow or progress. 滞销 means poor sales. 呆滞 means dull or lifeless.
