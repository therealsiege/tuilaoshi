# 塔 Tower

- **Character**: 塔
- **Pinyin**: tǎ
- **Meaning**: tower, pagoda
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 宝塔 (bǎo tǎ) — pagoda
- 铁塔 (tiě tǎ) — iron tower

## Example Sentences

- 这座塔很高。(Zhè zuò tǎ hěn gāo.) — This tower is very tall.

## Notes

塔 originally from Sanskrit stupa, now for all tall vertical structures.
