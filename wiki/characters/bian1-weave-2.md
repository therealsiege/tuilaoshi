# 编 Weave

- **Character**: 编
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: weave, braid, compile
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 编辑 (biān jí) — edit, compile
- 编织 (biān zhī) — weave, knit

## Example Sentences

- 她会编竹篮。(Tā huì biān zhú lán.) — She knows how to weave bamboo baskets.

## Notes

编 originally meant to weave or braid, extended to mean compile or edit (weaving information together).
