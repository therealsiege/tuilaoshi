# 属 Belong to, category, genus

- **Character**: 属
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: belong to, category, genus
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 属于 (shǔ yú) — to belong to
- 金属 (jīn shǔ) — metal

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题属于技术范畴。(Zhè ge wèn tí shǔ yú jì shù fàn chóu.) — This problem belongs to the technical category.

## Notes

Common verb meaning to belong to. Also used in taxonomy (genus) and for relatives (亲属).
