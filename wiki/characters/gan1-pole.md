# 杆 Pole, rod, stick

- **Character**: 杆
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: pole, rod, stick
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 旗杆 (qí gān) — flagpole
- 电线杆 (diàn xiàn gān) — utility pole

## Example Sentences

- 校园里的旗杆很高。(Xiào yuán lǐ de qí gān hěn gāo.) — The flagpole on campus is very tall.

## Notes

Pronounced gān for poles and rods, gǎn for rod-shaped items like pens (笔杆). Common in compound words.
