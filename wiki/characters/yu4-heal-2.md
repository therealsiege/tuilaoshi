# 愈 To heal, recover, more

- **Character**: 愈
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to heal, recover, more
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痊愈 (quán yù) — to recover fully
- 愈来愈 (yù lái yù) — more and more

## Example Sentences

- 他的病已经痊愈了。(Tā de bìng yǐjīng quányù le.) — He has fully recovered from his illness.

## Notes

Medical recovery or comparative 'more and more'.
