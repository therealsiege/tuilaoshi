# 猜 To Guess

- **Character**: 猜
- **Pinyin**: cāi
- **Meaning**: to guess, to suspect
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猜测 (cāi cè) — to guess
- 猜想 (cāi xiǎng) — to suppose

## Example Sentences

- 你能猜出答案吗？(Nǐ néng cāi chū dá àn ma?) — Can you guess the answer?

## Notes

猜 uses dog/beast radical 犭 with 青 (unclear), representing uncertainty like a wary animal, hence to guess or suspect.
