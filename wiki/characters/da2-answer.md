# 答 To Answer

- **Character**: 答
- **Pinyin**: dá
- **Meaning**: to answer, to reply
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 回答 (huí dá) — to answer
- 答案 (dá àn) — answer

## Example Sentences

- 请回答这个问题。(Qǐng huí dá zhè ge wèn tí.) — Please answer this question.

## Notes

答 is read dá in 回答 (to answer) and dā in 答应 (to agree/promise).
