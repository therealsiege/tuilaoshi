# 菌 Fungus, bacterium, germ

- **Character**: 菌
- **Pinyin**: jūn
- **Meaning**: fungus, bacterium, germ
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 细菌 (xì jūn) — bacteria, germ
- 病菌 (bìng jūn) — harmful bacteria, pathogen

## Example Sentences

- 洗手可以减少细菌传播。(Xǐ shǒu kě yǐ jiǎn shǎo xì jūn chuán bō.) — Washing hands can reduce the spread of bacteria.

## Notes

Used for bacteria and fungi
