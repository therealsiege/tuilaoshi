# 你 You

- **Character**: 你
- **Pinyin**: nǐ
- **Meaning**: you (singular)
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 你好 (nǐ hǎo) — hello
- 你们 (nǐmen) — you (plural)
- 你的 (nǐ de) — your, yours
- 您 (nín) — you (polite form)

## Example Sentences

- 你叫什么名字？(Nǐ jiào shénme míngzì?) — What is your name?
- 你是哪国人？(Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?) — What country are you from?
- 你想喝什么？(Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?) — What would you like to drink?

## Notes

你 combines 亻 (person radical) with 尔, an old word meaning "you." The polite form 您 adds 心 (heart) below 你, literally "you in my heart," expressing respect. 你 is informal and used among friends, family, and peers.
