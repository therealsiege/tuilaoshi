# 荫 Shade, shelter, protect

- **Character**: 荫
- **Pinyin**: yìn
- **Meaning**: shade, shelter, protect
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 树荫 (shù yìn) — shade of tree
- 荫庇 (yìn bì) — shelter, protect

## Example Sentences

- 我们在树荫下乘凉。(Wǒ men zài shù yìn xià chéng liáng.) — We enjoy the cool under the tree shade.

## Notes

Physical shade or protective influence
