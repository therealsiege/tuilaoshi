# 据 According To

- **Character**: 据
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: according to, evidence
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 根据 (gēn jù) — according to
- 数据 (shù jù) — data

## Example Sentences

- 据说他明天来。(Jù shuō tā míng tiān lái.) — It is said he'll come tomorrow.

## Notes

据说 means 'it is said/reportedly.' 根据 means 'according to.'
