# 煮 To boil, to cook

- **Character**: 煮
- **Pinyin**: zhǔ
- **Meaning**: to boil, to cook
- **Radical**: 灬(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 煮饭 (zhǔ fàn) — to cook rice
- 煮熟 (zhǔ shú) — to boil until cooked

## Example Sentences

- 水开了，可以煮面了。(Shuǐ kāi le, kěyǐ zhǔ miàn le.) — The water is boiling, we can cook the noodles now.

## Notes

General term for boiling or cooking in liquid.
