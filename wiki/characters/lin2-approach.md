# 临 To Approach

- **Character**: 临
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: to approach, to face, on the point of
- **Radical**: 丨 (line)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 临时 (lín shí) — temporary, at the time
- 面临 (miàn lín) — to face, to confront

## Example Sentences

- 我们面临很多挑战。(Wǒ men miàn lín hěn duō tiǎo zhàn.) — We face many challenges.

## Notes

临 simplified from 臨. Originally showed looking down from a height, extended to approaching or facing.
