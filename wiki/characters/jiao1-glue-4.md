# 胶 Gelatin

- **Character**: 胶
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: glue, gelatin
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胶囊 (jiāo náng) — capsule
- 橡胶 (xiàng jiāo) — rubber
- 胶水 (jiāo shuǐ) — glue

## Example Sentences

- 胶囊比药片容易吞咽。(Jiāo náng bǐ yào piàn róng yì tūn yàn.) — Capsules are easier to swallow than tablets.

## Notes

Gelatin material; used for capsules.
