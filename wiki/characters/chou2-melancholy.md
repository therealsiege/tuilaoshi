# 惆 Melancholy

- **Character**: 惆
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: melancholy, sad
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惆怅 (chóu chàng) — melancholy
- 惆然 (chóu rán) — sadly

## Example Sentences

- 他感到一阵惆怅。(Tā gǎn dào yí zhèn chóu chàng.) — He felt a wave of melancholy.

## Notes

惆 has heart 忄 with 周 (cycle), emotions going in circles.
