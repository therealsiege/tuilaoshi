# 植 To plant, to grow

- **Character**: 植
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: to plant, to grow
- **Radical**: 木(wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 植物 (zhí wù) — plant, vegetation
- 种植 (zhòng zhí) — to plant, to grow

## Example Sentences

- 这里种植了很多花卉。(Zhèlǐ zhòngzhí le hěn duō huāhuì.) — Many flowers are planted here.

## Notes

More formal than 栽, often used in scientific contexts.
