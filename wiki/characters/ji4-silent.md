# 寂 Silent

- **Character**: 寂
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: lonely, still, silent
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 寂寞 (jì mò) — lonely
- 寂静 (jì jìng) — quiet, silent

## Example Sentences

- 夜晚很寂静。(Yè wǎn hěn jì jìng.) — The night is very quiet.

## Notes

寂 has roof 宀 with 叔 (uncle/harvest), stillness within shelter.
