# 阻 To Block

- **Character**: 阻
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to block, to obstruct, to hinder
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 阻止 (zǔ zhǐ) — to prevent
- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to block

## Example Sentences

- 没有什么能阻止我。(Méi yǒu shén me néng zǔ zhǐ wǒ.) — Nothing can stop me.

## Notes

阻 shows a mound blocking the way, representing obstruction.
