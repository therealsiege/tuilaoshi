# 今 Now

- **Character**: 今
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: now, today, present
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 今天 (jīn tiān) — today
- 今年 (jīn nián) — this year

## Example Sentences

- 今天星期几？(Jīn tiān xīng qī jǐ?) — What day is today?

## Notes

今 means 'the present time.' Combined with time words: 今天 (today), 今年 (this year), 今晚 (tonight).
