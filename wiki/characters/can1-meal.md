# 餐 Meal

- **Character**: 餐
- **Pinyin**: cān
- **Meaning**: meal, food
- **Radical**: 食 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 餐厅 (cān tīng) — restaurant
- 早餐 (zǎo cān) — breakfast

## Example Sentences

- 我们去餐厅吃饭吧。(Wǒ men qù cān tīng chī fàn ba.) — Let's go eat at the restaurant.

## Notes

餐 is more formal than 饭 for meals. 午餐 = lunch, 晚餐 = dinner.
