# 虑 Worry

- **Character**: 虑
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: worry, concern
- **Radical**: 虍 (tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 焦虑 (jiāo lǜ) — anxiety, anxious
- 考虑 (kǎo lǜ) — to consider
- 忧虑 (yōu lǜ) — to worry

## Example Sentences

- 不要过度焦虑。(Bú yào guò dù jiāo lǜ.) — Don't be overly anxious.

## Notes

Thinking with worry; component of anxiety.
