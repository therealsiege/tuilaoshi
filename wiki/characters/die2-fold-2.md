# 叠 Fold

- **Character**: 叠
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: fold, pile up
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 叠衣服 (dié yīfu) — fold clothes
- 叠被子 (dié bèizi) — fold the quilt

## Example Sentences

- 请把衣服叠好。(Qǐng bǎ yīfu dié hǎo.) — Please fold the clothes neatly.

## Notes

Essential household task. Common with 衣服 and 被子. The repetition in the character suggests folding.
