# 汇 To Remit

- **Character**: 汇
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: to converge, to remit
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汇率 (huì lǜ) — exchange rate
- 汇款 (huì kuǎn) — to remit money

## Example Sentences

- 今天的汇率是多少？(Jīn tiān de huì lǜ shì duō shao?) — What is today's exchange rate?

## Notes

汇 simplified from 匯. Waters converging, extended to money flowing.
