# 补 Mend

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: to mend, to patch, to supplement
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 亡羊补牢 (wáng yáng bǔ láo) — better late than never
- 补充 (bǔ chōng) — to supplement

## Example Sentences

- 亡羊补牢还来得及。(Wáng yáng bǔ láo hái lái de jí.) — It's still not too late to fix things.

## Notes

The mending action in 亡羊补牢; uses the clothing radical since it originally meant patching clothes.
