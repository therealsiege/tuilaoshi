# 堆 To pile, to stack, heap, pile

- **Character**: 堆
- **Pinyin**: duī
- **Meaning**: to pile, to stack, heap, pile
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 堆积 (duī jī) — to pile up, to accumulate
- 一堆 (yī duī) — a pile, a heap

## Example Sentences

- 地上堆着一堆沙子。(Dì shàng duīzhe yī duī shāzi.) — There is a pile of sand on the ground.

## Notes

Common word for piling or heaps of things.
