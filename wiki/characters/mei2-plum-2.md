# 梅 Plum

- **Character**: 梅
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: plum blossom
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 梅花 (méi huā) — plum blossom
- 黄梅 (huáng méi) — Huangmei

## Example Sentences

- 梅花在冬天开放。(Méi huā zài dōng tiān kāi fàng.) — Plum blossoms bloom in winter.

## Notes

梅 means plum blossom; in 黄梅戏 refers to the Huangmei region.
