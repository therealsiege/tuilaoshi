# 缺 Lack, Deficiency, 缺点 Shortcoming

- **Character**: 缺
- **Pinyin**: quē
- **Meaning**: lack, deficiency, 缺点 shortcoming
- **Radical**: 缶 (jar)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 缺点 (quē diǎn) — shortcoming, defect
- 缺少 (quē shǎo) — lack
- 缺乏 (quē fá) — be short of

## Example Sentences

- 每个人都有缺点。(Měi gèrén dōu yǒu quēdiǎn.) — Everyone has shortcomings.

## Notes

Common character for expressing lack or deficiency. 缺点 (shortcoming) is essential for criticism and evaluation.
