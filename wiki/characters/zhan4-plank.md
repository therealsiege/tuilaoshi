# 栈 Warehouse

- **Character**: 栈
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: plank road, warehouse, inn
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栈道 (zhàn dào) — plank road
- 货栈 (huò zhàn) — warehouse

## Example Sentences

- 古代有很多栈道。(Gǔ dài yǒu hěn duō zhàn dào.) — There were many plank roads in ancient times.

## Notes

栈 simplified from 棧. Wood 木 structure, elevated walkway.
