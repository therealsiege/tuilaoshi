# 锄 Hoe

- **Character**: 锄
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: hoe, to hoe
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锄头 (chú tou) — hoe
- 锄草 (chú cǎo) — to hoe weeds

## Example Sentences

- 他拿着锄头去锄草。(Tā ná zhe chú tou qù chú cǎo.) — He took a hoe to weed.

## Notes

锄 simplified from 鋤. Metal 钅 with 助 (to help), the helper tool for farming.
