# 营 Camp, Operate, 经营 Operate

- **Character**: 营
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: camp, operate, 经营 operate
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 经营 (jīng yíng) — operate, manage
- 营业 (yíng yè) — do business
- 阵营 (zhèn yíng) — camp, bloc

## Example Sentences

- 这家商店每天营业到晚上十点。(Zhè jiā shāngdiàn měitiān yíngyè dào wǎnshang shí diǎn.) — This store does business until 10 PM every day.

## Notes

Common in business contexts for operations and management. 经营 (operate/manage) is essential business terminology.
