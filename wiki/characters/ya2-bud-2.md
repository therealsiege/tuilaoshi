# 芽 Bud, sprout

- **Character**: 芽
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: bud, sprout
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 发芽 (fāyá) — germinate, sprout
- 豆芽 (dòuyá) — bean sprouts

## Example Sentences

- 种子已经发芽了。(Zhǒngzi yǐjīng fāyá le.) — The seeds have already sprouted.

## Notes

Common term for buds or sprouts of plants.
