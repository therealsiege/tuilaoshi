# 粤 Guangdong

- **Character**: 粤
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: Guangdong, Cantonese
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 粤剧 (yuè jù) — Cantonese opera
- 粤语 (yuè yǔ) — Cantonese language

## Example Sentences

- 粤剧是广东的传统戏曲。(Yuè jù shì guǎng dōng de chuán tǒng xì qǔ.) — Cantonese opera is traditional opera from Guangdong.

## Notes

粤 refers to Guangdong; 粤剧 is Cantonese opera.
