# 竞 Compete, contend

- **Character**: 竞
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: compete, contend
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 竞争 (jìng zhēng) — to compete, competition
- 竞标 (jìng biāo) — to bid competitively

## Example Sentences

- 市场竞争非常激烈。(Shì chǎng jìng zhēng fēi cháng jī liè.) — Market competition is very fierce.

## Notes

Essential business term. 竞赛 means competition/contest. 竞选 means to run for election.
