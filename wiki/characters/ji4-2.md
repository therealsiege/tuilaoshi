# 计 计算, plan, count

- **Character**: 计
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: 计算, plan, count
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 计算 (jì suàn) — calculate, compute
- 会计 (kuài jì) — accounting, accountant

## Example Sentences

- 会计部门负责财务计算。(Kuài jì bù mén fù zé cái wù jì suàn.) — The accounting department is responsible for financial calculations.

## Notes

统计 means statistics. 设计 means design.
