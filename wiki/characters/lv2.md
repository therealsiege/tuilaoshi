# 驴 Donkey

- **Character**: 驴
- **Pinyin**: lǘ
- **Meaning**: donkey
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 毛驴 (máo lǘ) — donkey
- 驴肉 (lǘ ròu) — donkey meat

## Example Sentences

- 他骑着一头驴。(Tā qízhe yī tóu lǘ.) — He is riding a donkey.

## Notes

Horse radical indicates equine family. Used as work animal historically.
