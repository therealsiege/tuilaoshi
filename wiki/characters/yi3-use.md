# 以 By Means Of

- **Character**: 以
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: to use, by means of, in order to
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 可以 (kě yǐ) — can, may
- 以后 (yǐ hòu) — after, later

## Example Sentences

- 以后再说吧。(Yǐ hòu zài shuō ba.) — Let's talk about it later.

## Notes

以 is a classical word still productive in modern compounds: 以前 (before), 以后 (after), 以为 (to think/assume).
