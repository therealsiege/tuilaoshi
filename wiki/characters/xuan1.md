# 喧 Noisy, clamor

- **Character**: 喧
- **Pinyin**: xuān
- **Meaning**: noisy, clamor
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喧哗 (xuān huá) — noisy, clamorous
- 喧闹 (xuān nào) — noisy

## Example Sentences

- 请不要在图书馆喧哗。(Qǐng bù yào zài tú shū guǎn xuān huá.) — Please don't make noise in the library.

## Notes

Loud noise and clamor
