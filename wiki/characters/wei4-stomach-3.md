# 胃 Stomach

- **Character**: 胃
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: stomach
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胃病 (wèi bìng) — stomach disease
- 胃痛 (wèi tòng) — stomachache
- 胃口 (wèi kǒu) — appetite

## Example Sentences

- 我胃疼，吃不下饭。(Wǒ wèi téng, chī bu xià fàn.) — My stomach hurts, I can't eat.

## Notes

Main digestive organ; frequently mentioned in digestive health contexts.
