# 票 Ticket

- **Character**: 票
- **Pinyin**: piào
- **Meaning**: ticket
- **Radical**: 西 (west)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 车票 (chē piào) — ticket (for vehicle)
- 电影票 (diàn yǐng piào) — movie ticket

## Example Sentences

- 我买了一张票。(Wǒ mǎi le yī zhāng piào.) — I bought a ticket.

## Notes

票 means ticket. 机票 = plane ticket. 门票 = entrance ticket.
