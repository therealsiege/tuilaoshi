# 卫 Hygiene

- **Character**: 卫
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: guard, hygiene
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 卫生 (wèi shēng) — hygiene, sanitation
- 保卫 (bǎo wèi) — to defend
- 门卫 (mén wèi) — gatekeeper

## Example Sentences

- 注意个人卫生。(Zhù yì gè rén wèi shēng.) — Pay attention to personal hygiene.

## Notes

Protection or hygiene; health maintenance.
