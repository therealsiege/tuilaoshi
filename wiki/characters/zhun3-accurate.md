# 准 Accurate

- **Character**: 准
- **Pinyin**: zhǔn
- **Meaning**: accurate, standard, to allow
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 准备 (zhǔn bèi) — to prepare
- 标准 (biāo zhǔn) — standard

## Example Sentences

- 你准备好了吗？(Nǐ zhǔn bèi hǎo le ma?) — Are you ready?

## Notes

准备 is one of the most common compounds with 准. 准确 means 'accurate,' 标准 means 'standard.'
