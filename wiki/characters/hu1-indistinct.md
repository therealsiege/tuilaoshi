# 惚 Indistinct, absent-minded

- **Character**: 惚
- **Pinyin**: hū
- **Meaning**: indistinct, absent-minded
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 恍惚 (huǎnghū) — absent-minded, in a trance
- 精神恍惚 (jīngshén huǎnghū) — be in a trance

## Example Sentences

- 他精神恍惚。(Tā jīngshén huǎnghū.) — He is absent-minded.

## Notes

Literary term for absent-mindedness, always used with 恍.
