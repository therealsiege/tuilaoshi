# 章 Chapter

- **Character**: 章
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: chapter, section, seal
- **Radical**: 立 (to stand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 章程 (zhāng chéng) — regulations
- 文章 (wén zhāng) — article

## Example Sentences

- 请阅读公司章程。(Qǐng yuè dú gōng sī zhāng chéng.) — Please read the company regulations.

## Notes

章 combines 音 (sound) and 十 (ten), organized sections.
