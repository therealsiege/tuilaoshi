# 独 Alone

- **Character**: 独
- **Pinyin**: dú
- **Meaning**: alone, single
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 孤独 (gūdú) — lonely
- 独自 (dúzì) — alone, by oneself

## Example Sentences

- 他独自一人生活。(Tā dúzì yī rén shēnghuó.) — He lives alone by himself.

## Notes

Aloneness. Dog radical. 孤独 is loneliness, 独自 is being alone physically. Important distinction.
