# 爬 Crawl, climb, creep

- **Character**: 爬
- **Pinyin**: pá
- **Meaning**: crawl, climb, creep
- **Radical**: 爪 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 爬行 (pá xíng) — crawl, creep
- 爬虫 (pá chóng) — reptile

## Example Sentences

- 蛇是典型的爬行动物。(Shé shì diǎn xíng de pá xíng dòng wù.) — Snakes are typical reptiles.
