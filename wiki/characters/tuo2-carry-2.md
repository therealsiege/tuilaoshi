# 驮 To Carry On Back

- **Character**: 驮
- **Pinyin**: tuó
- **Meaning**: to carry on back (animals)
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 驮运 (tuó yùn) — to transport by pack animal
- 驮子 (tuó zi) — pack, load

## Example Sentences

- 马驮着货物过河。(Mǎ tuó zhe huò wù guò hé.) — The horse carries goods across the river.

## Notes

驮 simplified from 馱. Horse radical 马 representing animals carrying loads on back.
