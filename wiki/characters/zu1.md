# 租 Rent, lease

- **Character**: 租
- **Pinyin**: zū
- **Meaning**: rent, lease
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 租金 (zū jīn) — rent
- 出租 (chū zū) — rent out

## Example Sentences

- 这套房子月租五千元。(Zhè tào fáng zi yuè zū wǔ qiān yuán.) — This apartment rents for 5,000 yuan per month.

## Notes

房租 is house rent. 租赁 is more formal term for lease.
