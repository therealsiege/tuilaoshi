# 容 To Contain

- **Character**: 容
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: to contain, capacity, to tolerate
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 电容 (diàn róng) — capacitance, capacitor
- 容量 (róng liàng) — capacity

## Example Sentences

- 这个硬盘容量很大。(Zhè ge yìng pán róng liàng hěn dà.) — This hard disk has large capacity.

## Notes

容 suggests containing/holding, essential for capacity and capacitance terms.
