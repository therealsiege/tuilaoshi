# 罐 Jar

- **Character**: 罐
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: jar, can
- **Radical**: 缶 (jar)
- **Stroke count**: 23

## Compounds

- 罐头 (guàntou) — canned food
- 水罐 (shuǐguàn) — water jar

## Example Sentences

- 他打开了一罐可乐。(Tā dǎkāi le yī guàn kělè.) — He opened a can of Coke.

## Notes

Used for both traditional jars and modern cans. 罐头 is canned food. Also measure word for canned items.
