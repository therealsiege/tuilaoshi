# 亿 Hundred million

- **Character**: 亿
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: hundred million
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 亿万 (yì wàn) — millions upon millions
- 数亿 (shù yì) — hundreds of millions

## Example Sentences

- 中国有十几亿人口。(Zhōng guó yǒu shí jǐ yì rén kǒu.) — China has over a billion people.

## Notes

Major number unit in Chinese counting system
