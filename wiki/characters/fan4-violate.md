# 犯 To Violate

- **Character**: 犯
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: to violate, to offend, criminal
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 罪犯 (zuì fàn) — criminal
- 侵犯 (qīn fàn) — to infringe

## Example Sentences

- 警察抓住了罪犯。(Jǐng chá zhuā zhù le zuì fàn.) — The police caught the criminal.

## Notes

犯 combines dog radical and violation, suggesting transgression.
