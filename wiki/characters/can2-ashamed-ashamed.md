# 惭 Ashamed

- **Character**: 惭
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: ashamed, humiliated
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed
- 羞惭 (xiū cán) — ashamed

## Example Sentences

- 我感到很惭愧。(Wǒ gǎn dào hěn cán kuì.) — I feel very ashamed.

## Notes

惭愧 means ashamed or guilty. Uses the heart radical for emotions.
