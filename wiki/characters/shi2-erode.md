# 蚀 Erode, corrode, eclipse

- **Character**: 蚀
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: erode, corrode, eclipse
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 侵蚀 (qīn shí) — erosion, corrosion
- 腐蚀 (fǔ shí) — corrode, corrupt

## Example Sentences

- 风和水会侵蚀岩石。(Fēng hé shuǐ huì qīn shí yán shí.) — Wind and water erode rocks.

## Notes

Used for erosion and corrosion processes
