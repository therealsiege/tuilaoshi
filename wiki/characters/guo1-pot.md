# 锅 Pot

- **Character**: 锅
- **Pinyin**: guō
- **Meaning**: pot, pan, wok
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 火锅 (huǒ guō) — hot pot
- 炒锅 (chǎo guō) — wok

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢吃火锅。(Wǒ xǐ huān chī huǒ guō.) — I like to eat hot pot.

## Notes

锅 with metal radical 钅. 火锅 is popular Chinese communal cooking style.
