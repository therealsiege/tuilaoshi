# 意 Meaning

- **Character**: 意
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: meaning, idea, intention
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 意思 (yì si) — meaning
- 注意 (zhù yì) — to pay attention

## Example Sentences

- 请注意安全。(Qǐng zhù yì ān quán.) — Please pay attention to safety.

## Notes

意 combines 音 (sound) and 心 (heart) — the meaning in one's heart. Very productive in compounds.
