# 氲 Dense vapor

- **Character**: 氲
- **Pinyin**: yūn
- **Meaning**: dense vapor
- **Radical**: 气 (air)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 氤氲 (yīnyūn) — dense mist, rich atmosphere
- 烟氲 (yānyūn) — misty, hazy

## Example Sentences

- 山间烟氲缭绕。(Shānjiān yānyūn liáorào.) — Mist swirls among the mountains.

## Notes

Classical term for dense vapor or mist, typically paired with 氤.
