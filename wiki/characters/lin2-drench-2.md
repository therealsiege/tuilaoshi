# 淋 To drench, to drizzle

- **Character**: 淋
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: to drench, to drizzle
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淋雨 (lín yǔ) — to be caught in the rain
- 淋浴 (lín yù) — shower

## Example Sentences

- 在蛋糕上淋一些巧克力酱。(Zài dàngāo shàng lín yīxiē qiǎokèlì jiàng.) — Drizzle some chocolate sauce on the cake.

## Notes

Can mean rain on or pour sauce. Water radical.
