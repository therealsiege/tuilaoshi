# 闲 Idle, leisure, unoccupied

- **Character**: 闲
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: idle, leisure, unoccupied
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 空闲 (kòng xián) — free time
- 休闲 (xiū xián) — leisure

## Example Sentences

- 我周末有空闲时间。(Wǒ zhōu mò yǒu kòng xián shí jiān.) — I have free time on weekends.

## Notes

Free time and leisurely state
