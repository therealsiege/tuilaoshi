# 漫 To overflow, free, unrestrained

- **Character**: 漫
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: to overflow, free, unrestrained
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漫长 (màn cháng) — very long, endless
- 浪漫 (làng màn) — romantic

## Example Sentences

- 这是一段漫长的旅程。(Zhè shì yī duàn màncháng de lǚchéng.) — This is a very long journey.

## Notes

Can mean overflowing, leisurely, or extending far.
