# 禅 Meditation, Zen Buddhism

- **Character**: 禅
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: meditation, Zen Buddhism
- **Radical**: 礻 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 禅宗 (chán zōng) — Zen Buddhism
- 参禅 (cān chán) — practice meditation

## Example Sentences

- 他在寺庙里参禅悟道。(Tā zài sì miào lǐ cān chán wù dào.) — He practices meditation in the temple.

## Notes

Central concept in Chinese Buddhism, also read as shàn meaning abdication
