# 枪 Gun

- **Character**: 枪
- **Pinyin**: qiāng
- **Meaning**: gun, spear, firearm
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 手枪 (shǒu qiāng) — pistol
- 步枪 (bù qiāng) — rifle

## Example Sentences

- 士兵拿着枪。(Shì bīng ná zhe qiāng.) — The soldier is holding a gun.

## Notes

枪 originally meant spear (wooden weapon, hence 木 radical), but now primarily means gun or firearm.
