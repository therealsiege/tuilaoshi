# 成 To Become

- **Character**: 成
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to become, to succeed, finished
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 成功 (chéng gōng) — to succeed
- 成为 (chéng wéi) — to become

## Example Sentences

- 他成功了！(Tā chéng gōng le!) — He succeeded!

## Notes

成 is very productive: 成功 (succeed), 成为 (become), 完成 (complete), 成绩 (grades).
