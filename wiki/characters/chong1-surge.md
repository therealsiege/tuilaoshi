# 冲 Surge, rush

- **Character**: 冲
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: surge, rush
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 脉冲 (mài chōng) — pulse
- 冲击 (chōng jī) — impact

## Example Sentences

- 脉冲宽度可调。(Mài chōng kuān dù kě tiáo.) — The pulse width is adjustable.

## Notes

Used in 脉冲 (pulse), 冲击 (impact), digital electronics
