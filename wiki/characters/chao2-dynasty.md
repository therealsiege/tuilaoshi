# 朝 Dynasty

- **Character**: 朝
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: dynasty, toward; morning (zhāo)
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 朝代 (cháo dài) — dynasty
- 朝着 (cháo zhe) — toward

## Example Sentences

- 朝这个方向走。(Cháo zhè ge fāng xiàng zǒu.) — Walk in this direction.

## Notes

朝 as cháo: dynasty, toward. As zhāo: morning (朝阳 = morning sun).
