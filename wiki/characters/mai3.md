# 买 To Buy

- **Character**: 买
- **Pinyin**: mǎi
- **Meaning**: to buy
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 买东西 (mǎi dōng xī) — to go shopping
- 购买 (gòu mǎi) — to purchase

## Example Sentences

- 我想买这本书。(Wǒ xiǎng mǎi zhè běn shū.) — I want to buy this book.

## Notes

买 (mǎi, buy) and 卖 (mài, sell) differ by only one stroke and one tone — a common source of confusion.
