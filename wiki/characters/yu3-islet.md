# 屿 Islet, small island

- **Character**: 屿
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: islet, small island
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 岛屿 (dǎo yǔ) — island
- 小屿 (xiǎo yǔ) — small island

## Example Sentences

- 这片海域有许多岛屿。(Zhè piàn hǎi yù yǒu xǔ duō dǎo yǔ.) — This sea area has many islands.
