# 舞 Dance

- **Character**: 舞
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: to dance, dance
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — to dance
- 舞蹈 (wǔ dǎo) — dance

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢跳舞。(Tā xǐ huan tiào wǔ.) — She likes to dance.

## Notes

舞 means dance or to dance. Used in跳舞 (to dance), 舞台 (stage), 舞会 (ball, dance party).
