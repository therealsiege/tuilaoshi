# 离 Leave, separate, from

- **Character**: 离
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: leave, separate, from
- **Radical**: 离 (leave)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 离子 (lí zǐ) — ion
- 分离 (fēn lí) — separate, detach

## Example Sentences

- 这个过程可以分离出纯净物质。(Zhè ge guò chéng kě yǐ fēn lí chū chún jìng wù zhì.) — This process can separate out pure substances.

## Notes

Key character in chemistry for ions and separation
