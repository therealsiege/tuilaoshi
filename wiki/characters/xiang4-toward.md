# 向 Toward

- **Character**: 向
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: toward, direction
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 方向 (fāng xiàng) — direction
- 向前 (xiàng qián) — forward

## Example Sentences

- 向前走。(Xiàng qián zǒu.) — Walk forward.

## Notes

向 originally depicted a house with a window facing a direction. It indicates direction of movement or facing.
