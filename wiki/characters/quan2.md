# 泉 Spring

- **Character**: 泉
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: spring, fountain
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 温泉 (wēn quán) — hot spring
- 泉水 (quán shuǐ) — spring water

## Example Sentences

- 这里有一个温泉。(Zhèlǐ yǒu yīgè wēnquán.) — There is a hot spring here.

## Notes

泉 shows water 白 emerging from between rocks 𠂊, representing a natural spring.
