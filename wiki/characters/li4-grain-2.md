# 粒 Grain, granule

- **Character**: 粒
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: grain, granule
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 米粒 (mǐ lì) — grain of rice
- 颗粒 (kē lì) — granule, pellet

## Example Sentences

- 一粒米也不能浪费。(Yī lì mǐ yě bù néng làngfèi.) — Not even one grain of rice should be wasted.

## Notes

Measure word for small round objects. Rice radical shows origin.
