# 菠 Spinach

- **Character**: 菠
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: spinach
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 菠菜 (bō cài) — spinach
- 菠萝 (bō luó) — pineapple

## Example Sentences

- 菠菜含有很多铁。(Bōcài hányǒu hěn duō tiě.) — Spinach contains a lot of iron.

## Notes

Used in both 菠菜 (spinach) and 菠萝 (pineapple). Grass radical for plants.
