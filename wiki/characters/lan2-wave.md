# 澜 Wave

- **Character**: 澜
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: wave, billows, ripple
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 波澜 (bō lán) — waves
- 澜沧江 (Lán cāng jiāng) — Lancang River

## Example Sentences

- 湖面波澜壮阔。(Hú miàn bō lán zhuàng kuò.) — The lake surface is covered with magnificent waves.

## Notes

澜 simplified from 瀾. Water 氵 with grand waves.
