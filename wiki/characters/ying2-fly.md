# 蝇 Fly

- **Character**: 蝇
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: fly (insect)
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 苍蝇 (cāng ying) — fly
- 果蝇 (guǒ yíng) — fruit fly

## Example Sentences

- 厨房里有苍蝇。(Chú fáng lǐ yǒu cāng ying.) — There are flies in the kitchen.

## Notes

蝇 simplified from 蠅. Insect radical 虫 representing the common fly.
