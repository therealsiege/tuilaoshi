# 抽 To Pull Out

- **Character**: 抽
- **Pinyin**: chōu
- **Meaning**: to pull out, to extract, drawer
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抽屉 (chōu tì) — drawer
- 抽烟 (chōu yān) — to smoke

## Example Sentences

- 请把抽屉打开。(Qǐng bǎ chōu tì dǎ kāi.) — Please open the drawer.

## Notes

抽 shows hand 扌 and 由 (from), representing the action of pulling something out from within.
