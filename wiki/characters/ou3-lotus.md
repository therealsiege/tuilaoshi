# 藕 Lotus root

- **Character**: 藕
- **Pinyin**: ǒu
- **Meaning**: lotus root
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 莲藕 (lián ǒu) — lotus root
- 藕片 (ǒu piàn) — lotus root slices

## Example Sentences

- 凉拌藕片很爽口。(Liángbàn ǒu piàn hěn shuǎngkǒu.) — Cold lotus root slices are very refreshing.

## Notes

Crunchy vegetable from lotus plant. Grass radical indicates plant origin.
