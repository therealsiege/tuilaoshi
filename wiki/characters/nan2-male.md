# 男 Man

- **Character**: 男
- **Pinyin**: nán
- **Meaning**: male, man
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 男人 (nán rén) — man
- 男孩 (nán hái) — boy

## Example Sentences

- 那个男人是谁？(Nà ge nán rén shì shéi?) — Who is that man?

## Notes

男 combines 田 (field) and 力 (strength) — one who works the fields with strength.
