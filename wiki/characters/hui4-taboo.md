# 讳 Taboo

- **Character**: 讳
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: taboo, to avoid mentioning
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 忌讳 (jì huì) — taboo
- 讳莫如深 (huì mò rú shēn) — to make a deep secret of

## Example Sentences

- 在中国文化中有些话题是忌讳的。(Zài zhōng guó wén huà zhōng yǒu xiē huà tí shì jì huì de.) — In Chinese culture some topics are taboo.

## Notes

讳 indicates things that should not be spoken about, especially names of deceased emperors or ancestors.
