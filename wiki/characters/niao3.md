# 鸟 Bird

- **Character**: 鸟
- **Pinyin**: niǎo
- **Meaning**: bird
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 小鸟 (xiǎo niǎo) — small bird
- 鸟类 (niǎo lèi) — birds (category)

## Example Sentences

- 树上有很多鸟。(Shù shàng yǒu hěn duō niǎo.) — There are many birds in the tree.

## Notes

鸟 simplified from 鳥, pictograph of bird. Used as radical in many bird-related characters.
