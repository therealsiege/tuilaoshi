# 充 To Fill

- **Character**: 充
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: to fill, to充满, abundant
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 充满 (chōng mǎn) — to be filled with
- 充足 (chōng zú) — abundant

## Example Sentences

- 房间里充满了欢笑。(Fáng jiān lǐ chōng mǎn le huān xiào.) — The room was filled with laughter.

## Notes

充 shows growth and fullness, representing filling or abundance.
