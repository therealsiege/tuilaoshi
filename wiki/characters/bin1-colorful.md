# 缤 Colorful

- **Character**: 缤
- **Pinyin**: bīn
- **Meaning**: colorful, numerous
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 缤纷 (bīn fēn) — colorful, diverse
- 五彩缤纷 (wǔ cǎi bīn fēn) — colorful, vibrant

## Example Sentences

- 秋天的树叶五彩缤纷。(Qiū tiān de shù yè wǔ cǎi bīn fēn.) — Autumn leaves are colorful.

## Notes

缤纷 means colorful and varied. Almost always used in the phrase 五彩缤纷.
