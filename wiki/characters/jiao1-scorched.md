# 焦 Scorched, worried, anxious

- **Character**: 焦
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: scorched, worried, anxious
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 焦虑 (jiāo lǜ) — anxious, worried
- 焦急 (jiāo jí) — anxious, worried

## Example Sentences

- 她为考试焦虑不安。(Tā wèi kǎoshì jiāolǜ bù'ān.) — She is anxious about the exam.

## Notes

Originally meant burnt/scorched, now commonly used for anxiety.
