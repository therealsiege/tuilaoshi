# 付 Pay, hand over

- **Character**: 付
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: pay, hand over
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 支付 (zhīfù) — pay
- 付款 (fùkuǎn) — pay, payment

## Example Sentences

- 请用现金支付。(Qǐng yòng xiànjīn zhīfù.) — Please pay in cash.

## Notes

Common term for payment and delivery.
