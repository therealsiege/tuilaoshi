# 吼 To Roar

- **Character**: 吼
- **Pinyin**: hǒu
- **Meaning**: to roar, to howl
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吼叫 (hǒu jiào) — to roar, to howl
- 狂吼 (kuáng hǒu) — to roar wildly

## Example Sentences

- 狮子在吼叫。(Shī zi zài hǒu jiào.) — The lion is roaring.

## Notes

吼 shows mouth 口 and 孔 (hole/opening), representing deep powerful vocal sounds.
