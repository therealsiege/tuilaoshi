# 底 Bottom

- **Character**: 底
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: bottom, base, end (of month/year)
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 到底 (dào dǐ) — after all, finally
- 年底 (nián dǐ) — end of year

## Example Sentences

- 年底我要回家。(Nián dǐ wǒ yào huí jiā.) — I'm going home at the end of the year.

## Notes

底 means bottom or end. 月底 = end of month. 彻底 = thorough.
