# 跌 Fall, drop

- **Character**: 跌
- **Pinyin**: diē
- **Meaning**: fall, drop
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 下跌 (xià diē) — fall, drop
- 跌价 (diē jià) — fall in price

## Example Sentences

- 房价下跌了。(Fáng jià xià diē le.) — Housing prices fell.

## Notes

暴跌 means plummet. Opposite of 涨.
