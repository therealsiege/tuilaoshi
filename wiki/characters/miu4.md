# 谬 Absurd, erroneous, false

- **Character**: 谬
- **Pinyin**: miù
- **Meaning**: absurd, erroneous, false
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 谬论 (miù lùn) — fallacy, absurd theory
- 荒谬 (huāng miù) — absurd, preposterous

## Example Sentences

- 这种说法完全是荒谬的。(Zhè zhǒng shuō fǎ wán quán shì huāng miù de.) — This statement is completely absurd.

## Notes

Indicates serious logical errors or absurdity. More severe than simple mistakes (错误).
