# 听 To Listen

- **Character**: 听
- **Pinyin**: tīng
- **Meaning**: to listen, to hear
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 好听 (hǎo tīng) — pleasant to hear
- 听说 (tīng shuō) — to hear that, it is said

## Example Sentences

- 你听得懂吗？(Nǐ tīng de dǒng ma?) — Can you understand (what you hear)?

## Notes

听 is greatly simplified from the traditional 聽 which had the ear radical 耳.
