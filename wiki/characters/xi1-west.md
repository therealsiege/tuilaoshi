# 西 West

- **Character**: 西
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: west
- **Radical**: 西 (west)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 东西 (dōng xī) — thing
- 西方 (xī fāng) — the West

## Example Sentences

- 太阳从西边落下。(Tài yáng cóng xī biān luò xià.) — The sun sets in the west.

## Notes

西 originally depicted a bird nest — birds return to nest at dusk, in the west.
