# 碎 Shattered

- **Character**: 碎
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: to shatter, broken, trivial
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 粉碎 (fěn suì) — to smash
- 碎片 (suì piàn) — fragment

## Example Sentences

- 玻璃碎了一地。(Bō li suì le yí dì.) — Glass shattered all over the floor.

## Notes

碎 combines stone 石 and 卒 (soldier/end), stone broken to pieces.
