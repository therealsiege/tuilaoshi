# 段 Section

- **Character**: 段
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: section, segment, period
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阶段 (jiē duàn) — stage, phase
- 片段 (piàn duàn) — fragment, segment

## Example Sentences

- 这个阶段很重要。(Zhè ge jiē duàn hěn zhòng yào.) — This stage is very important.

## Notes

段 means section/segment, useful in code segments, time periods, and text passages.
