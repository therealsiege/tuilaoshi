# 被 Passive Marker

- **Character**: 被
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: passive marker, quilt, by
- **Radical**: 衤(clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 被动 (bèi dòng) — passive
- 被子 (bèi zi) — quilt

## Example Sentences

- 他被老师批评了。(Tā bèi lǎo shī pī píng le.) — He was criticized by the teacher.

## Notes

被 creates passive voice sentences. The original meaning 'quilt' relates to the idea of being covered or affected by something.
