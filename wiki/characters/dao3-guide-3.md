# 导 To Guide

- **Character**: 导
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: to guide, lead, direct
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 指导 (zhǐ dǎo) — to guide, instruct
- 导师 (dǎo shī) — mentor, advisor

## Example Sentences

- 老师指导我们做实验。(Lǎo shī zhǐ dǎo wǒ men zuò shí yàn.) — The teacher guides us in doing experiments.

## Notes

导 is simplified from 導. Combines movement and guidance.
