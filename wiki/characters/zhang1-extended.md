# 张 Net

- **Character**: 张
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: Extended Net constellation, spread
- **Radical**: 弓(bow)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 张宿 (zhāng xiù) — Extended Net constellation
- 张开 (zhāng kāi) — spread out

## Example Sentences

- 张宿象征着张开的网。(Zhāng xiù xiàngzhēng zhe zhāngkāi de wǎng.) — Extended Net symbolizes a spread-out net.

## Notes

张 means to spread or open. In astronomy, the Extended Net constellation, representing a spread hunting net.
