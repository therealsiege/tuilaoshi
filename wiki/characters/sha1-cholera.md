# 痧 Sha

- **Character**: 痧
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: cholera; sha
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 刮痧 (guā shā) — gua sha therapy
- 痧症 (shā zhèng) — cholera-like illness

## Example Sentences

- 刮痧后皮肤会发红。(Guā shā hòu pí fū huì fā hóng.) — The skin becomes red after gua sha.

## Notes

TCM pathogenic factor; appears in therapy name.
