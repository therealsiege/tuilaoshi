# 伟 Great, mighty, extraordinary

- **Character**: 伟
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: great, mighty, extraordinary
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 伟大 (wěi dà) — great, mighty
- 宏伟 (hóng wěi) — grand, magnificent

## Example Sentences

- 毛泽东是一位伟大的领袖。(Máo Zé Dōng shì yī wèi wěi dà de lǐng xiù.) — Mao Zedong was a great leader.

## Notes

Common character for greatness. 伟人 means great person. Often used in names.
