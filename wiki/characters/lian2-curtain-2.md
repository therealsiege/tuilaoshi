# 帘 Curtain

- **Character**: 帘
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: curtain, screen
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 窗帘 (chuāng lián) — window curtain
- 门帘 (mén lián) — door curtain

## Example Sentences

- 拉上窗帘。(Lā shàng chuāng lián.) — Draw the curtains.

## Notes

帘 with cloth radical for帘 hanging curtains and screens.
