# 垄 Ridge, furrow, monopolize

- **Character**: 垄
- **Pinyin**: lǒng
- **Meaning**: ridge, furrow, monopolize
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 垄断 (lǒng duàn) — monopolize
- 田垄 (tián lǒng) — field ridge

## Example Sentences

- 土豆种在垄上。(Tǔdòu zhòng zài lǒng shàng.) — Potatoes are planted on ridges.

## Notes

Raised ridges in fields where crops are planted. Extended meaning of monopoly comes from controlling all ridges/land.
