# 重 Weight, heavy

- **Character**: 重
- **Pinyin**: zhòng
- **Meaning**: weight, heavy
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 权重 (quán zhòng) — weight
- 重要 (zhòng yào) — important

## Example Sentences

- 权重初始化很重要。(Quán zhòng chū shǐ huà hěn zhòng yào.) — Weight initialization is important.

## Notes

Used in 权重 (weight), 重要 (important), neural network parameters
