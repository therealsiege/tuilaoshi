# 泻 Diarrhea

- **Character**: 泻
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: diarrhea, drain
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 腹泻 (fù xiè) — diarrhea
- 泄泻 (xiè xiè) — diarrhea
- 倾泻 (qīng xiè) — to pour down

## Example Sentences

- 吃坏东西会腹泻。(Chī huài dōng xi huì fù xiè.) — Eating spoiled food can cause diarrhea.

## Notes

Loose stools; common GI complaint.
