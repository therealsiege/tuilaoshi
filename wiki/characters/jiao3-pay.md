# 缴 Pay, hand in

- **Character**: 缴
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: pay, hand in
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缴纳 (jiǎo nà) — pay (taxes, fees)
- 缴费 (jiǎo fèi) — pay fees

## Example Sentences

- 公司按时缴纳税款。(Gōng sī àn shí jiǎo nà shuì kuǎn.) — The company pays taxes on time.

## Notes

Formal term for paying taxes and fees. 上缴 means turn over/pay to higher authority.
