# 购 Purchase, buy

- **Character**: 购
- **Pinyin**: gòu
- **Meaning**: purchase, buy
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 购买 (gòu mǎi) — purchase, buy
- 采购 (cǎi gòu) — purchase, procure

## Example Sentences

- 公司采购部负责买办公用品。(Gōng sī cǎi gòu bù fù zé mǎi bàn gōng yòng pǐn.) — The company procurement department is responsible for buying office supplies.

## Notes

网购 means online shopping. 订购 means order/subscribe.
