# 薄 Thin

- **Character**: 薄
- **Pinyin**: báo
- **Meaning**: thin, weak (also bó, bò)
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 薄弱 (bó ruò) — weak, frail
- 刻薄 (kè bó) — unkind, harsh

## Example Sentences

- 这张纸很薄。(Zhè zhāng zhǐ hěn báo.) — This paper is very thin.

## Notes

薄 as báo means thin (物理), as bó means weak/slight, as bò in 薄荷 (mint). Grass radical 艹 with 溥 (spread).
