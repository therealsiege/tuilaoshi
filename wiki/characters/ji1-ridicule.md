# 讥 To Ridicule

- **Character**: 讥
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to ridicule, to satirize
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 讥讽 (jī fěng) — to sneer at
- 讥笑 (jī xiào) — to ridicule

## Example Sentences

- 他们讥讽我的想法。(Tā men jī fěng wǒ de xiǎng fǎ.) — They sneered at my ideas.

## Notes

讥 uses speech radical 讠, similar to 讽 but often more cutting or sarcastic in tone.
