# 凭 Rely on, proof

- **Character**: 凭
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: rely on, proof
- **Radical**: 几 (table)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 凭证 (píng zhèng) — proof, certificate
- 凭据 (píng jù) — evidence, voucher

## Example Sentences

- 凭证明可以领取货物。(Píng zhèng míng kě yǐ lǐng qǔ huò wù.) — Goods can be collected with proof.

## Notes

会计凭证 are accounting vouchers, essential for financial records.
