# 钹 Cymbals

- **Character**: 钹
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: cymbals
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 铜钹 (tóng bó) — brass cymbals
- 钹声 (bó shēng) — sound of cymbals

## Example Sentences

- 乐队中传来清脆的钹声。(Yuèduì zhōng chuán lái qīngcuì de bó shēng.) — The crisp sound of cymbals came from the band.

## Notes

钹 refers to cymbals, metal percussion instruments struck together. The metal radical 钅 indicates the material.
