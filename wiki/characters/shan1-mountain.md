# 山 Mountain

- **Character**: 山
- **Pinyin**: shān
- **Meaning**: mountain, hill
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 山区 (shān qū) — mountain area
- 爬山 (pá shān) — to climb mountains

## Example Sentences

- 我们去爬山吧。(Wǒ men qù pá shān ba.) — Let's go mountain climbing.

## Notes

山 is a pictograph showing three peaks. Famous mountains include 泰山 (Tai Shan) and 黄山 (Huangshan).
