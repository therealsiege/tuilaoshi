# 仰 Face upward

- **Character**: 仰
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: to face upward
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 仰泳 (yǎng yǒng) — backstroke
- 仰望 (yǎng wàng) — to look up at

## Example Sentences

- 仰泳时要注意呼吸。(Yǎngyǒng shí yào zhùyì hūxī.) — Pay attention to breathing when doing backstroke.

## Notes

Facing upward; backstroke swimming
