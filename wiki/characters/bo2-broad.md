# 博 Broad

- **Character**: 博
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: broad, extensive, to win, to gamble
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 博物馆 (bó wù guǎn) — museum
- 博士 (bó shì) — PhD, doctor

## Example Sentences

- 我们去博物馆吧。(Wǒ men qù bó wù guǎn ba.) — Let's go to the museum.

## Notes

博 means broad or extensive. 博士 = PhD, 博客 = blog.
