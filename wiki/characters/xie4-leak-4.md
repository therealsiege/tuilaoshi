# 泄 To Leak

- **Character**: 泄
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to leak, to divulge, to release
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泄露 (xiè lòu) — to leak (information)
- 发泄 (fā xiè) — to vent

## Example Sentences

- 不能泄露机密。(Bù néng xiè lòu jī mì.) — Cannot leak classified information.

## Notes

泄 has water 氵 with 世 (world), water flowing out, to release.
