# 蜥 Lizard

- **Character**: 蜥
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: lizard
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蜥蜴 (xī yì) — lizard
- 蜥蜴类 (xī yì lèi) — lizard species

## Example Sentences

- 蜥蜴在石头上晒太阳。(Xī yì zài shí tou shàng shài tài yáng.) — The lizard is sunbathing on the rock.

## Notes

蜥 has insect 虫 with 析 (to analyze/split), the darting reptile.
