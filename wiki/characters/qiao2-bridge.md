# 桥 Bridge

- **Character**: 桥
- **Pinyin**: qiáo
- **Meaning**: bridge
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 大桥 (dà qiáo) — large bridge
- 桥梁 (qiáo liáng) — bridge

## Example Sentences

- 我们过桥吧。(Wǒ men guò qiáo ba.) — Let's cross the bridge.

## Notes

桥 means bridge. 天桥 = overpass. 立交桥 = flyover.
