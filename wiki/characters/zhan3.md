# 斩 To behead, to cut off

- **Character**: 斩
- **Pinyin**: zhǎn
- **Meaning**: to behead, to cut off
- **Radical**: 斤(axe)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 斩断 (zhǎn duàn) — to sever, to cut off
- 斩首 (zhǎn shǒu) — to behead

## Example Sentences

- 他决心斩断一切后路。(Tā juéxīn zhǎnduàn yīqiè hòulù.) — He is determined to cut off all retreat.

## Notes

Stronger than 砍, often implies execution or decisive cutting.
