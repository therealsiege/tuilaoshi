# 源 Source

- **Character**: 源
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: source, origin
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 电源 (diàn yuán) — power source
- 资源 (zī yuán) — resource

## Example Sentences

- 请检查电源连接。(Qǐng jiǎn chá diàn yuán lián jiē.) — Please check the power connection.

## Notes

源 with water radical suggests spring/source, now for all kinds of sources and resources.
