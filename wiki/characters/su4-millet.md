# 粟 Millet, grain

- **Character**: 粟
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: millet, grain
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 粟米 (sù mǐ) — millet
- 沧海一粟 (cāng hǎi yī sù) — a drop in the ocean

## Example Sentences

- 人类在宇宙中如沧海一粟。(Rén lèi zài yǔ zhòu zhōng rú cāng hǎi yī sù.) — Humanity is like a drop in the ocean in the universe.

## Notes

Used in famous metaphor for insignificance
