# 掰 To break with hands

- **Character**: 掰
- **Pinyin**: bāi
- **Meaning**: to break with hands
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 掰开 (bāi kāi) — to break open
- 掰断 (bāi duàn) — to snap, to break

## Example Sentences

- 把馒头掰成两半。(Bǎ mántou bāi chéng liǎng bàn.) — Break the steamed bun in half.

## Notes

Two hand radicals show breaking with both hands.
