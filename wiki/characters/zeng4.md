# 赠 To Give

- **Character**: 赠
- **Pinyin**: zèng
- **Meaning**: to give as a gift
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赠送 (zèng sòng) — to give as a gift
- 赠品 (zèng pǐn) — gift, present

## Example Sentences

- 他赠送了我一本书。(Tā zèngsòng le wǒ yī běn shū.) — He gave me a book as a gift.

## Notes

赠 combines shell/money 贝 with 曾 (already), representing giving something of value.
