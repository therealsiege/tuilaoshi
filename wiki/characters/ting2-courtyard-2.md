# 庭 Courtyard

- **Character**: 庭
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: courtyard, court
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 庭院 (tíng yuàn) — courtyard
- 法庭 (fǎ tíng) — court

## Example Sentences

- 庭院里种了很多花。(Tíng yuàn lǐ zhòng le hěn duō huā.) — Many flowers are planted in the courtyard.

## Notes

庭 with shelter radical for courtyard, both residential and legal courts.
