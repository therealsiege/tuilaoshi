# 愧 Ashamed

- **Character**: 愧
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: ashamed, guilty
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed
- 愧疚 (kuì jiù) — guilty, remorseful

## Example Sentences

- 我对他感到很愧疚。(Wǒ duì tā gǎndào hěn kuìjiù.) — I feel very guilty toward him.

## Notes

愧 combines heart 心 with 鬼 (ghost), suggesting the haunting feeling of shame or guilt.
