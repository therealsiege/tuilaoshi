# 罄 Exhausted

- **Character**: 罄
- **Pinyin**: qìng
- **Meaning**: exhausted, used up
- **Radical**: 缶 (jar)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 罄竹难书 (qìng zhú nán shū) — too numerous to record
- 一罄 (yī qìng) — all used up

## Example Sentences

- 他的罪行罄竹难书。(Tā de zuì xíng qìng zhú nán shū.) — His crimes are too numerous to record.

## Notes

罄 means exhausted or used up; often in the idiom 罄竹难书.
