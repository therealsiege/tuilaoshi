# 床 Bed

- **Character**: 床
- **Pinyin**: chuáng
- **Meaning**: bed
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 床上 (chuáng shàng) — on the bed
- 起床 (qǐ chuáng) — to get up

## Example Sentences

- 我每天七点起床。(Wǒ měi tiān qī diǎn qǐ chuáng.) — I get up at seven every day.

## Notes

床 uses the shelter radical 广 and 木 (wood). 起床 is the common expression for getting out of bed.
