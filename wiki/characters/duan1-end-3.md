# 端 End, terminal

- **Character**: 端
- **Pinyin**: duān
- **Meaning**: end, terminal
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 终端 (zhōng duān) — terminal
- 前端 (qián duān) — front-end

## Example Sentences

- 我是一个前端开发者。(Wǒ shì yī gè qián duān kāi fā zhě.) — I am a front-end developer.

## Notes

Critical for 前端 (front-end), 后端 (back-end), 终端 (terminal), 客户端 (client)
