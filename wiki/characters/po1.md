# 坡 Slope

- **Character**: 坡
- **Pinyin**: pō
- **Meaning**: slope, hillside
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 山坡 (shān pō) — hillside, slope
- 斜坡 (xié pō) — slope, incline

## Example Sentences

- 车慢慢爬上了山坡。(Chē mànman pá shàng le shānpō.) — The car slowly climbed up the slope.

## Notes

坡 combines earth 土 with 皮 (skin/surface), representing the sloping surface of terrain.
