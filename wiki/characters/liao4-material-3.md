# 料 Material, stuff

- **Character**: 料
- **Pinyin**: liào
- **Meaning**: material, stuff
- **Radical**: 斗 (dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 材料 (cái liào) — material
- 原料 (yuán liào) — raw material

## Example Sentences

- 这种材料很耐用。(Zhè zhǒng cái liào hěn nài yòng.) — This material is very durable.

Used in 材料 (material), 原料 (raw material), 塑料 (plastic)
