# 突 Sudden, 突, protrude

- **Character**: 突
- **Pinyin**: tū
- **Meaning**: sudden,突, protrude
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 突变 (tū biàn) — mutation
- 突然 (tū rán) — sudden, suddenly

## Example Sentences

- 基因突变是进化的重要机制。(Jī yīn tū biàn shì jìn huà de zhòng yào jī zhì.) — Gene mutation is an important mechanism of evolution.

## Notes

Used for sudden changes and mutations
