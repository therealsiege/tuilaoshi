# 储 Store, save

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: store, save
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 储蓄 (chǔxù) — save, savings
- 储备 (chǔbèi) — reserve, stockpile

## Example Sentences

- 他有良好的储蓄习惯。(Tā yǒu liánghǎo de chǔxù xíguàn.) — He has good savings habits.

## Notes

Common in banking and finance for savings and reserves.
