# 虹 Rainbow

- **Character**: 虹
- **Pinyin**: hóng
- **Meaning**: rainbow
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 彩虹 (cǎihóng) — rainbow
- 虹桥 (hóngqiáo) — rainbow bridge

## Example Sentences

- 雨后出现了一道彩虹。(Yǔ hòu chūxiàn le yī dào cǎihóng.) — A rainbow appeared after the rain.

## Notes

Beautiful weather phenomenon. Usually appears as 彩虹. Despite insect radical, means rainbow in modern usage.
