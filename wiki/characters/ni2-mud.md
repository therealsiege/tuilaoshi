# 泥 Mud

- **Character**: 泥
- **Pinyin**: ní
- **Meaning**: mud, clay
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泥土 (ní tǔ) — soil, clay
- 陶泥 (táo ní) — pottery clay

## Example Sentences

- 陶工用泥巴捏成了一个碗。(Táogōng yòng níba niē chéng le yī gè wǎn.) — The potter molded a bowl from clay.

## Notes

泥 means mud or clay. Essential material for pottery and ceramics.
