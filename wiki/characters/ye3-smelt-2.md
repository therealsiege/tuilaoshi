# 冶 Smelt metal, alluring

- **Character**: 冶
- **Pinyin**: yě
- **Meaning**: smelt metal, alluring
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 冶炼 (yě liàn) — smelt, refine
- 陶冶 (táo yě) — cultivate, mold

## Example Sentences

- 这家工厂专门冶炼铜矿。(Zhè jiā gōng chǎng zhuān mén yě liàn tóng kuàng.) — This factory specializes in smelting copper ore.

## Notes

Primary meaning is smelting metal; also used metaphorically.
