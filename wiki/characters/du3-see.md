# 睹 To See

- **Character**: 睹
- **Pinyin**: dǔ
- **Meaning**: to see, to observe
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 目睹 (mù dǔ) — to witness
- 有目共睹 (yǒu mù gòng dǔ) — obvious to all

## Example Sentences

- 我亲眼目睹了这件事。(Wǒ qīn yǎn mù dǔ le zhè jiàn shì.) — I witnessed this with my own eyes.

## Notes

睹 has eye 目 with 者 (one who), one who sees directly.
