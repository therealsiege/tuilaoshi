# 庸 Mediocre, ordinary, use

- **Character**: 庸
- **Pinyin**: yōng
- **Meaning**: mediocre, ordinary, use
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 平庸 (píng yōng) — mediocre
- 中庸 (zhōng yōng) — the mean, moderation

## Example Sentences

- 他是个平庸的人。(Tā shì gè píng yōng de rén.) — He is a mediocre person.

## Notes

中庸 is a key Confucian concept of the golden mean
