# 稀 Thin, watery, sparse

- **Character**: 稀
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: thin, watery, sparse
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 稀饭 (xī fàn) — thin porridge, congee
- 稀少 (xī shǎo) — scarce, rare

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢喝稀饭。(Wǒ xǐhuan hē xīfàn.) — I like to eat thin porridge.

## Notes

Opposite of 稠. Can mean watery or sparse. Grain radical.
