# 罐 Jar, pot, tin

- **Character**: 罐
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: jar, pot, tin
- **Radical**: 缶 (jar)
- **Stroke count**: 23

## Compounds

- 罐头 (guàn tou) — can, tinned food
- 茶罐 (chá guàn) — tea jar

## Example Sentences

- 他买了几罐罐头。(Tā mǎi le jǐ guàn guàn tou.) — He bought several cans of food.

## Notes

Container for storage or canned goods
