# 嶙 Jagged rocks

- **Character**: 嶙
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: jagged rocks
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 嶙峋 (línxún) — craggy, jagged
- 怪石嶙峋 (guàishí línxún) — grotesque rocks standing in rows

## Example Sentences

- 山上怪石嶙峋。(Shān shàng guàishí línxún.) — Grotesque rocks jut from the mountain.

## Notes

Literary term for jagged, protruding rocks, always used with 峋.
