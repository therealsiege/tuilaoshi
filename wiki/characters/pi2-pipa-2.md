# 琵 Pipa

- **Character**: 琵
- **Pinyin**: pí
- **Meaning**: pipa (part of)
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琵琶 (pí pá) — pipa (lute)
- 琵琶行 (pí pá xíng) — Song of the Pipa

## Example Sentences

- 她弹琵琶弹得很好。(Tā tán pí pá tán de hěn hǎo.) — She plays the pipa very well.

## Notes

琵 only appears in 琵琶, a four-stringed Chinese lute.
