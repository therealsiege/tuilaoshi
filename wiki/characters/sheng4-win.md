# 胜 To Win

- **Character**: 胜
- **Pinyin**: shèng
- **Meaning**: to win, victory, superior
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胜利 (shèng lì) — victory
- 名胜 (míng shèng) — famous scenic spot

## Example Sentences

- 最后我们胜利了。(Zuì hòu wǒ men shèng lì le.) — In the end we were victorious.

## Notes

胜 simplified from 勝. Shows strength 力 prevailing over flesh 月, representing superiority and victory.
