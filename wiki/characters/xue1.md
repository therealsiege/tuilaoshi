# 削 To Cut

- **Character**: 削
- **Pinyin**: xuē
- **Meaning**: to cut, reduce
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 削减 (xuē jiǎn) — to cut, reduce
- 剥削 (bō xuē) — to exploit

## Example Sentences

- 政府决定削减开支。(Zhèngfǔ juédìng xuējiǎn kāizhī.) — The government decided to cut expenses.

## Notes

削 can be read as xuē (to cut) or xiāo (to peel with a knife). Combines 肖 (resemble) with knife 刂.
