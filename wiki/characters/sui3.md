# 髓 Marrow

- **Character**: 髓
- **Pinyin**: suǐ
- **Meaning**: marrow, essence
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 骨髓 (gǔ suǐ) — bone marrow
- 脊髓 (jǐ suǐ) — spinal cord

## Example Sentences

- 骨髓移植可以治疗某些疾病。(Gǔ suǐ yí zhí kě yǐ zhì liáo mǒu xiē jí bìng.) — Bone marrow transplants can treat certain diseases.

## Notes

髓 uses bone radical 骨, referring to marrow inside bones or the essential core of something.
