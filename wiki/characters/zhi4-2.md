# 制 System, make, restrain

- **Character**: 制
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: 制度,制造, restrain
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 制度 (zhìdù) — system, institution
- 控制 (kòngzhì) — control

## Example Sentences

- 需要建立完善的制度。(Xūyào jiànlì wánshàn de zhìdù.) — Need to establish a sound system.

## Notes

Very common for systems, manufacturing, and restraint.
