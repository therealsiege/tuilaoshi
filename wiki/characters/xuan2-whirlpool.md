# 漩 Whirlpool

- **Character**: 漩
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: whirlpool, eddy
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漩涡 (xuán wō) — whirlpool
- 漩流 (xuán liú) — eddy

## Example Sentences

- 水中形成了漩涡。(Shuǐ zhōng xíng chéng le xuán wō.) — A whirlpool formed in the water.

## Notes

漩 has water 氵 with 旋 (to rotate), swirling water.
