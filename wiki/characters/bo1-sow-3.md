# 播 Sow, broadcast, spread

- **Character**: 播
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: sow, broadcast, spread
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 播种 (bō zhǒng) — sow seeds
- 广播 (guǎng bō) — broadcast

## Example Sentences

- 春播秋收是农业生产的规律。(Chūn bō qiū shōu shì nóngyè shēngchǎn de guīlǜ.) — Sowing in spring and harvesting in autumn is the law of agricultural production.

## Notes

Scattering seeds for planting. Also means to broadcast or disseminate information widely.
