# 辞 Resign, dismiss

- **Character**: 辞
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: resign, dismiss
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 辞职 (cí zhí) — resign
- 辞退 (cí tuì) — dismiss, fire

## Example Sentences

- 他决定辞职去创业。(Tā jué dìng cí zhí qù chuàng yè.) — He decided to resign to start a business.

## Notes

Used for both voluntary resignation and dismissal. 辞呈 is a resignation letter.
