# 新 New

- **Character**: 新
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: new
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 新年 (xīn nián) — new year
- 新闻 (xīn wén) — news

## Example Sentences

- 新年快乐！(Xīn nián kuài lè!) — Happy New Year!

## Notes

新 combines 亲 (close) and 斤 (axe) — chopping fresh wood. Opposite of 旧 (old, used).
