# 侯 Marquis, nobleman

- **Character**: 侯
- **Pinyin**: hóu
- **Meaning**: marquis, nobleman
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 诸侯 (zhū hóu) — feudal lords
- 王侯 (wáng hóu) — princes and marquises

## Example Sentences

- 春秋时期，诸侯割据，战乱不断。(Chūnqiū shíqī, zhūhóu gējù, zhànluàn bùduàn.) — During the Spring and Autumn period, feudal lords ruled separate territories and wars were constant.

## Notes

A noble rank in ancient China, second tier of the five-rank system. Feudal lords who governed territories under the emperor.
