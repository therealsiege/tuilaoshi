# 枢 Pivot, hub, center

- **Character**: 枢
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: pivot, hub, center
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 枢纽 (shū niǔ) — hub, pivotal point
- 中枢 (zhōng shū) — center, pivot

## Example Sentences

- 这座城市是该地区的交通枢纽。(Zhè zuò chéng shì shì gāi dì qū de jiāo tōng shū niǔ.) — This city is a transportation hub for the region.

## Notes

Indicates central pivoting point or hub. Common in transportation and organizational contexts.
