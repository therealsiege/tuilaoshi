# 种 Kind

- **Character**: 种
- **Pinyin**: zhǒng
- **Meaning**: kind, type, species, seed
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 各种 (gè zhǒng) — various
- 种类 (zhǒng lèi) — category, type

## Example Sentences

- 这里有很多种水果。(Zhè lǐ yǒu hěn duō zhǒng shuǐ guǒ.) — There are many kinds of fruit here.

## Notes

种 can be zhǒng (kind/type) or zhòng (to plant). As zhǒng, it's a common measure word for types/categories.
