# 忙 Busy

- **Character**: 忙
- **Pinyin**: máng
- **Meaning**: busy
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 帮忙 (bāng máng) — to help
- 忙碌 (máng lù) — busy

## Example Sentences

- 最近很忙。(Zuì jìn hěn máng.) — I've been very busy lately.

## Notes

忙 combines heart 忄 and 亡 (lost/flee). Busy = heart running away! Opposite: 闲 (free/idle).
