# 筵 Banquet

- **Character**: 筵
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: banquet, feast
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 筵席 (yán xí) — banquet
- 喜筵 (xǐ yán) — wedding feast

## Example Sentences

- 天下没有不散的筵席。(Tiān xià méi yǒu bú sàn de yán xí.) — All good things must come to an end.

## Notes

筵 has bamboo 竹 with 延 (to extend), originally bamboo mats for seating at banquets.
