# 坐 To Sit

- **Character**: 坐
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: to sit, to take (transport)
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 请坐 (qǐng zuò) — please sit
- 坐车 (zuò chē) — to ride a vehicle

## Example Sentences

- 请坐！(Qǐng zuò!) — Please have a seat!

## Notes

坐 depicts two people sitting on the ground. Also means 'to take' transportation: 坐飞机 (take a plane).
