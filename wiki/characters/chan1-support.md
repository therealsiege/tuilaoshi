# 搀 Support with hand, mix

- **Character**: 搀
- **Pinyin**: chān
- **Meaning**: support with hand, mix
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搀扶 (chānfú) — support with hand
- 搀和 (chānhuo) — mix, blend

## Example Sentences

- 他搀扶老人过马路。(Tā chānfú lǎorén guò mǎlù.) — He helped the elderly person cross the street.

## Notes

Common term for supporting by the arm or mixing things.
