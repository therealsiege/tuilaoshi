# 和 And

- **Character**: 和
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: and, with, harmonious
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 和平 (hé píng) — peace
- 温和 (wēn hé) — gentle, mild

## Example Sentences

- 我和他是朋友。(Wǒ hé tā shì péng yǒu.) — He and I are friends.

## Notes

和 connects nouns ('and') but NOT clauses or sentences. For clauses use 而 or rephrase. Also means 'harmonious.'
