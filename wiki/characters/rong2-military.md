# 戎 Military, arms, warfare

- **Character**: 戎
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: military, arms, warfare
- **Radical**: 戈 (spear)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 戎装 (róng zhuāng) — military uniform
- 投笔从戎 (tóu bǐ cóng róng) — give up civil pursuits for a military career

## Example Sentences

- 他决定投笔从戎，保卫国家。(Tā juédìng tóubǐ cóng róng, bǎowèi guójiā.) — He decided to give up his scholarly pursuits and join the military to defend the country.

## Notes

Literary term for military affairs or warfare. Often appears in classical expressions related to military service.
