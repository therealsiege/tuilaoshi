# 障 Barrier

- **Character**: 障
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: barrier, obstacle
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle
- 保障 (bǎo zhàng) — to guarantee

## Example Sentences

- 语言不应该成为障碍。(Yǔ yán bù yīng gāi chéng wéi zhàng ài.) — Language should not become a barrier.

## Notes

障 combines mound 阝 and 章 (chapter/section), representing a physical barrier that divides or separates sections.
