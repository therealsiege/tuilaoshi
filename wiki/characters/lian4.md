# 炼 To Refine

- **Character**: 炼
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to refine, to smelt
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 冶炼 (yě liàn) — to smelt
- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — to exercise

## Example Sentences

- 石油需要提炼才能使用。(Shí yóu xū yào tí liàn cái néng shǐ yòng.) — Oil needs to be refined before use.

## Notes

炼 uses fire radical 火, meaning to purify through heating or metaphorically to refine skills.
