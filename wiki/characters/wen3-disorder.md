# 紊 Disorder

- **Character**: 紊
- **Pinyin**: wěn
- **Meaning**: disorder, chaos
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 紊乱 (wěn luàn) — disorder, chaos
- 紊流 (wěn liú) — turbulent flow

## Example Sentences

- 内分泌紊乱影响情绪。(Nèi fēn mì wěn luàn yǐng xiǎng qíng xù.) — Endocrine disorder affects mood.

## Notes

State of disarray; system dysfunction.
