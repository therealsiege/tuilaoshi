# 宽 Wide, bandwidth

- **Character**: 宽
- **Pinyin**: kuān
- **Meaning**: wide, bandwidth
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 带宽 (dài kuān) — bandwidth
- 宽度 (kuān dù) — width

## Example Sentences

- 这个网络很宽。(Zhè gè wǎng luò hěn kuān.) — This network has high bandwidth.

## Notes

Used in 带宽 (bandwidth), 宽带 (broadband), 宽度 (width)
