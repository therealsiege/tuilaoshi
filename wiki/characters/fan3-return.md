# 反 Return

- **Character**: 反
- **Pinyin**: fǎn
- **Meaning**: to return, opposite, to rebel
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 举一反三 (jǔ yī fǎn sān) — learn by analogy
- 反对 (fǎn duì) — to oppose

## Example Sentences

- 他反对这个提议。(Tā fǎnduì zhège tíyì.) — He opposes this proposal.

## Notes

In 举一反三, 反 means to infer or deduce the reverse/remaining.
