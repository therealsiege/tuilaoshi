# 寂 Lonely, Silent, 寂寞 Lonely

- **Character**: 寂
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: lonely, silent, 寂寞 lonely
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 寂寞 (jì mò) — lonely
- 寂静 (jì jìng) — quiet, silent
- 孤寂 (gū jì) — lonely, solitary

## Example Sentences

- 夜晚很寂静。(Yèwǎn hěn jìjìng.) — The night is very quiet.

## Notes

Literary term for silence and loneliness. 寂寞 (lonely) describes emotional isolation, 寂静 (silent) describes absence of sound.
