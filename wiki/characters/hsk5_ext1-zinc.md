# 锌 Zinc, 锌合金 Zinc Alloy

- **Character**: 锌
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: zinc, 锌合金 zinc alloy
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锌合金 (xīn hé jīn) — zinc alloy
- 补锌 (bǔ xīn) — zinc supplement
- 锌板 (xīn bǎn) — zinc plate

## Example Sentences

- 儿童需要补锌促进发育。(Értóng xūyào bǔ xīn cùjìn fāyù.) — Children need zinc supplements to promote development.

## Notes

Metal element zinc. Used in industrial contexts (合金 alloy) and health contexts (补锌 zinc supplementation).
