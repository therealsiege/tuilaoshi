# 铃 Bell

- **Character**: 铃
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: bell, weight
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哑铃 (yǎ líng) — dumbbell
- 杠铃 (gàng líng) — barbell

## Example Sentences

- 他每天举哑铃锻炼。(Tā měi tiān jǔ yǎlíng duànliàn.) — He lifts dumbbells every day to exercise.

## Notes

Weight training equipment; bells and dumbbells
