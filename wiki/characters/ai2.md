# 癌 Cancer

- **Character**: 癌
- **Pinyin**: ái
- **Meaning**: cancer
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 癌症 (ái zhèng) — cancer
- 肺癌 (fèi ái) — lung cancer

## Example Sentences

- 医生在研究癌症。(Yī shēng zài yán jiū ái zhèng.) — Doctors are researching cancer.

## Notes

癌 has sickness 疒 with 嵒 (rocky/craggy), growths like rocky formations.
