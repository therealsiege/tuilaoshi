# 刚 Just Now

- **Character**: 刚
- **Pinyin**: gāng
- **Meaning**: just now, just, barely
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刚才 (gāng cái) — just now
- 刚刚 (gāng gāng) — just a moment ago

## Example Sentences

- 他刚走。(Tā gāng zǒu.) — He just left.

## Notes

刚 indicates something that happened very recently. 刚才 refers to a moment ago.
