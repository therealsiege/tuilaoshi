# 笛 Flute

- **Character**: 笛
- **Pinyin**: dí
- **Meaning**: flute, whistle
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 笛子 (dí zi) — bamboo flute
- 长笛 (cháng dí) — flute (Western)

## Example Sentences

- 他吹笛子吹得很棒。(Tā chuī dí zi chuī de hěn bàng.) — He plays the flute very well.

## Notes

笛 refers to transverse flutes, played horizontally across the mouth.
