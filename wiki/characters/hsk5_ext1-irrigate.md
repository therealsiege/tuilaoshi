# 灌 Irrigate, Pour, 灌溉 Irrigate

- **Character**: 灌
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: irrigate, pour, 灌溉 irrigate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 灌溉 (guàn gài) — irrigate
- 浇灌 (jiāo guàn) — water, irrigate
- 灌输 (guàn shū) — instill, inculcate

## Example Sentences

- 农民用水泵灌溉农田。(Nóngmín yòng shuǐbèng guàngài nóngtián.) — Farmers use pumps to irrigate farmland.

## Notes

Agricultural term for irrigation. 灌溉 is essential for farming. Also used figuratively in 灌输 (instill ideas/knowledge).
