# 烘 To bake, to roast, to dry by fire

- **Character**: 烘
- **Pinyin**: hōng
- **Meaning**: to bake, to roast, to dry by fire
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烘干 (hōng gān) — to dry with heat
- 烘烤 (hōng kǎo) — to bake

## Example Sentences

- 面包在烤箱里烘着。(Miànbāo zài kǎoxiāng lǐ hōng zhe.) — The bread is baking in the oven.

## Notes

Often used for baking bread and pastries. Related to 烤 but emphasizes heat drying.
