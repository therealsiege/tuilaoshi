# 篆 Seal Script

- **Character**: 篆
- **Pinyin**: zhuàn
- **Meaning**: seal script
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 篆书 (zhuàn shū) — seal script
- 篆刻 (zhuàn kè) — seal engraving

## Example Sentences

- 他擅长篆刻。(Tā shàn cháng zhuàn kè.) — He excels at seal engraving.

## Notes

篆 has bamboo 竹 with 彖 phonetic, ancient seal script style.
