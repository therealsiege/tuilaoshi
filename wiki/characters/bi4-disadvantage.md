# 弊 Disadvantage

- **Character**: 弊
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: disadvantage, fraud, abuse
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 弊端 (bì duān) — drawback
- 利弊 (lì bì) — pros and cons

## Example Sentences

- 每件事都有利弊。(Měi jiàn shì dōu yǒu lì bì.) — Everything has pros and cons.

## Notes

弊 means disadvantage or malpractice. 利弊 = advantages and disadvantages.
