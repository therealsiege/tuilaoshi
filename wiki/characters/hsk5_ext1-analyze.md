# 析 Analyze, Separate, 分析 Analyze

- **Character**: 析
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: analyze, separate, 分析 analyze
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 分析 (fēn xī) — analyze
- 解析 (jiě xī) — analyze, parse
- 剖析 (pōu xī) — analyze in depth

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要仔细分析这些数据。(Wǒmen xūyào zǐxì fēnxī zhèxiē shùjù.) — We need to carefully analyze this data.

## Notes

Critical character in academic and professional contexts. 分析 (analyze) is one of the most common words in research and business.
