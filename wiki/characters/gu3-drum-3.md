# 鼓 Drum

- **Character**: 鼓
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: drum, beat
- **Radical**: 鼓(drum)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 打鼓 (dǎ gǔ) — beat a drum
- 鼓励 (gǔ lì) — encourage

## Example Sentences

- 他在乐队里打鼓。(Tā zài yuèduì lǐ dǎ gǔ.) — He plays drums in the band.

## Notes

鼓 is a drum, one of the oldest musical instruments. Also means to encourage or bulge.
