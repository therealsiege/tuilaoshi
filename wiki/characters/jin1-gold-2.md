# 金 Gold, metal, money

- **Character**: 金
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: gold, metal, money
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 基金 (jī jīn) — fund
- 现金 (xiàn jīn) — cash

## Example Sentences

- 请问你们接受现金支付吗？(Qǐng wèn nǐ men jiē shòu xiàn jīn zhī fù ma?) — Do you accept cash payment?

## Notes

Radical itself, meaning gold or metal. 黄金 means gold. 奖金 means bonus. 资金 means funds.
