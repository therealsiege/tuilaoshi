# 状 Appearance

- **Character**: 状
- **Pinyin**: zhuàng
- **Meaning**: appearance, form, state, certificate
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 状态 (zhuàng tài) — state
- 形状 (xíng zhuàng) — shape

## Example Sentences

- 他现在的状态很好。(Tā xiàn zài de zhuàng tài hěn hǎo.) — His current state is very good.

## Notes

状 represents appearance or condition. Common in describing states, shapes, and situations.
