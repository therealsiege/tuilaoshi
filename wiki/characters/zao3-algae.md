# 藻 Algae

- **Character**: 藻
- **Pinyin**: zǎo
- **Meaning**: algae, seaweed
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 海藻 (hǎi zǎo) — seaweed
- 水藻 (shuǐ zǎo) — aquatic plants

## Example Sentences

- 池塘里长满了水藻。(Chí táng lǐ zhǎng mǎn le shuǐ zǎo.) — The pond is full of algae.

## Notes

藻 has grass 艹 with 澡 (to bathe), aquatic plants.
