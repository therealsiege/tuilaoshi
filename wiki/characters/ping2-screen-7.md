# 屏 Screen

- **Character**: 屏
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: screen
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen
- 触屏 (chù píng) — touchscreen

## Example Sentences

- 屏幕太暗了。(Píng mù tài àn le.) — The screen is too dark.

## Notes

Used in 屏幕 (screen), 显示屏 (display screen), 触摸屏 (touchscreen)
