# 徐 Slow, gentle, surname

- **Character**: 徐
- **Pinyin**: xú
- **Meaning**: slow, gentle, surname
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 徐徐 (xú xú) — slowly, gently
- 清风徐来 (qīng fēng xú lái) — gentle breeze comes

## Example Sentences

- 清风徐来，水波不兴。(Qīng fēng xú lái, shuǐ bō bù xīng.) — A gentle breeze comes, and the water is calm.

## Notes

Slow and gentle manner, also a common surname
