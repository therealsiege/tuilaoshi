# 放 Release

- **Character**: 放
- **Pinyin**: fàng
- **Meaning**: to release, to let go, to bloom
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 心花怒放 (xīn huā nù fàng) — be overjoyed
- 开放 (kāi fàng) — to open up

## Example Sentences

- 春天花儿都开放了。(Chūntiān huā er dōu kāifàng le.) — In spring all the flowers bloom.

## Notes

In 心花怒放, 放 means to bloom or burst open with joy.
