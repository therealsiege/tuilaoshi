# 贬 To Demote

- **Character**: 贬
- **Pinyin**: biǎn
- **Meaning**: to demote, to reduce
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贬低 (biǎn dī) — to belittle, to depreciate
- 贬值 (biǎn zhí) — to devalue

## Example Sentences

- 你不应该贬低自己的能力。(Nǐ bù yīng gāi biǎn dī zì jǐ de néng lì.) — You shouldn't belittle your own abilities.

## Notes

贬 uses money radical 贝, originally meaning to reduce value. Opposite of 褒 (to praise).
