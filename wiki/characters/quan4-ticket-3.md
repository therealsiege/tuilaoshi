# 券 Ticket, bond, certificate

- **Character**: 券
- **Pinyin**: quàn
- **Meaning**: ticket, bond, certificate
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 债券 (zhàiquàn) — bond, debenture
- 股券 (gǔquàn) — stock certificate

## Example Sentences

- 政府发行了新的债券。(Zhèngfǔ fāxíng le xīn de zhàiquàn.) — The government issued new bonds.

## Notes

Used for tickets, bonds, and various financial certificates.
