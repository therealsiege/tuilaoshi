# 航 Navigate, sail, aviation

- **Character**: 航
- **Pinyin**: háng
- **Meaning**: navigate, sail, aviation
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 航行 (háng xíng) — navigate, sail
- 航空 (háng kōng) — aviation

## Example Sentences

- 这艘船将航行到太平洋。(Zhè sōu chuán jiāng háng xíng dào Tài píng yáng.) — This ship will sail to the Pacific Ocean.

## Notes

Used for both water and air navigation.
