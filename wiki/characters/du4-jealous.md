# 妒 Jealous

- **Character**: 妒
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: jealous, envy
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 嫉妒 (jídù) — jealous
- 妒忌 (dùjì) — jealous

## Example Sentences

- 不要妒忌别人。(Bùyào dùjì biérén.) — Don't be jealous of others.

## Notes

Paired with 嫉 as 嫉妒. Woman radical. Despite radical, applies to all people. Negative emotion vocabulary.
