# 笔 Pen

- **Character**: 笔
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: pen, writing instrument
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 笔记 (bǐ jì) — notes
- 铅笔 (qiān bǐ) — pencil

## Example Sentences

- 你有笔吗？(Nǐ yǒu bǐ ma?) — Do you have a pen?

## Notes

笔 has bamboo radical because ancient brushes were made from bamboo. 毛笔 = calligraphy brush.
