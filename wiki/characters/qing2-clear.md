# 晴 Sunny

- **Character**: 晴
- **Pinyin**: qíng
- **Meaning**: clear weather, sunny
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 晴天 (qíng tiān) — sunny day
- 天晴 (tiān qíng) — clear weather

## Example Sentences

- 明天是晴天。(Míng tiān shì qíng tiān.) — Tomorrow will be sunny.

## Notes

晴 combines sun 日 and 青 (blue/clear). Sunny days have clear blue sky.
