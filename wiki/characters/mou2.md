# 缪 Bind

- **Character**: 缪
- **Pinyin**: móu
- **Meaning**: to bind, to repair, to plan
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 未雨绸缪 (wèi yǔ chóu móu) — prepare in advance
- 绸缪 (chóu móu) — to make preparations

## Example Sentences

- 凡事都要未雨绸缪。(Fánshì dōu yào wèi yǔ chóu móu.) — One should always prepare in advance.

## Notes

Used in 未雨绸缪: to repair or prepare; appears almost exclusively in this idiom.
