# 贴 Patch

- **Character**: 贴
- **Pinyin**: tiē
- **Meaning**: stick, paste
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 膏贴 (gāo tiē) — medicated plaster
- 创可贴 (chuāng kě tiē) — band-aid
- 贴纸 (tiē zhǐ) — sticker

## Example Sentences

- 贴上止痛膏药。(Tiē shang zhǐ tòng gāo yào.) — Apply a pain-relief patch.

## Notes

Adhesive application; patches and plasters.
