# 利 利益, sharp, benefit

- **Character**: 利
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: 利益, sharp, benefit
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 利润 (lì rùn) — 利益
- 利益 (lì yì) — 利益, interest

## Example Sentences

- 公司今年利润增长了。(Gōng sī jīn nián lì rùn zēng zhǎng le.) — Company profits increased this year.

## Notes

毛利 is gross profit, 净利 is net profit. Core financial metric.
