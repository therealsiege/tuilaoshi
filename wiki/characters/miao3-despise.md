# 藐 To Despise

- **Character**: 藐
- **Pinyin**: miǎo
- **Meaning**: to despise, tiny
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 藐视 (miǎo shì) — to despise, to look down on
- 藐小 (miǎo xiǎo) — tiny, insignificant

## Example Sentences

- 我们不能藐视任何对手。(Wǒ men bù néng miǎo shì rèn hé duì shǒu.) — We cannot despise any opponent.

## Notes

藐 combines grass 艹 and 貌 (appearance), suggesting something appearing insignificant. Similar meaning to 蔑.
