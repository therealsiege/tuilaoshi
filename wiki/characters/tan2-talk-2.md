# 谈 Talk, chat

- **Character**: 谈
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: talk, chat
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谈话 (tánhuà) — talk, conversation
- 谈论 (tánlùn) — discuss

## Example Sentences

- 我们谈了很多。(Wǒmen tán le hěn duō.) — We talked a lot.

## Notes

Common character for talking or chatting.
