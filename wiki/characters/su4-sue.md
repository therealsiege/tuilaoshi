# 诉 To Sue

- **Character**: 诉
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: to sue, to tell, to complain
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诉讼 (sù sòng) — lawsuit
- 告诉 (gào sù) — to tell

## Example Sentences

- 他决定起诉对方。(Tā jué dìng qǐ sù duì fāng.) — He decided to sue the other party.

## Notes

诉 simplified from 訴. Speech 讠 plus 斥 (to scold).
