# 情 Emotion

- **Character**: 情
- **Pinyin**: qíng
- **Meaning**: emotion, feeling
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 感情 (gǎn qíng) — feeling, emotion
- 道情 (dào qíng) — Taoist ballad

## Example Sentences

- 他们之间感情很深。(Tā men zhī jiān gǎn qíng hěn shēn.) — Their feelings for each other are deep.

## Notes

情 means emotion or feeling; appears in many emotional and artistic terms.
