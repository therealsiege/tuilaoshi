# 别 — Don't / Other

- **Character**: 别
- **Pinyin**: bié
- **Meaning**: don't; other, another; to part
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 别人 (biéren) — other people, others
- 别的 (bié de) — other, else
- 特别 (tèbié) — special, especially
- 别处 (biéchù) — elsewhere
- 告别 (gàobié) — to say goodbye, to bid farewell
- 区别 (qūbié) — difference, distinction

## Example Sentences

- 别忘了带伞。(Bié wàng le dài sǎn.) — Don't forget to bring an umbrella.
- 别人都走了，只有我还在。(Biéren dōu zǒu le, zhǐyǒu wǒ hái zài.) — Everyone else has left, only I'm still here.
- 这个地方特别漂亮。(Zhè ge dìfang tèbié piàoliang.) — This place is especially beautiful.

## Notes

别 serves double duty in Chinese. As an adverb before a verb, it means "don't" and is the standard way to form negative imperatives: 别走 (don't go), 别说 (don't say). As an adjective/determiner, it means "other" or "another," as in 别人 and 别的. The character contains the knife radical 刂 on the right, possibly reflecting the original meaning of "to separate" or "to part," from which both "other" (separated, distinct) and "don't" (cut off an action) evolved. 特别 (especially) is one of the most useful adverbs at this level.
