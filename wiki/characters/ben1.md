# 奔 To run, to hurry, to rush

- **Character**: 奔
- **Pinyin**: bēn
- **Meaning**: to run, to hurry, to rush
- **Radical**: 大(big)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奔跑 (bēn pǎo) — to run
- 奔腾 (bēn téng) — to gallop, to surge

## Example Sentences

- 马儿在草原上奔跑。(Mǎ'er zài cǎoyuán shàng bēnpǎo.) — The horses are running on the grassland.

## Notes

Pronounced bēn for running; bèn for heading toward.
