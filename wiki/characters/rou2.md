# 柔 Soft

- **Character**: 柔
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: soft, gentle, flexible
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柔软 (róu ruǎn) — soft, supple
- 温柔 (wēn róu) — gentle, tender

## Example Sentences

- 她的性格很温柔。(Tā de xìnggé hěn wēnróu.) — Her personality is very gentle.

## Notes

柔 shows a spear 矛 bent by wood 木, representing flexibility and gentleness.
