# 虎 Tiger

- **Character**: 虎
- **Pinyin**: hǔ
- **Meaning**: tiger
- **Radical**: 虍 (tiger)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 老虎 (lǎo hǔ) — tiger
- 虎年 (hǔ nián) — Year of the Tiger

## Example Sentences

- 老虎很危险。(Lǎo hǔ hěn wēi xiǎn.) — Tigers are dangerous.

## Notes

虎 shows stripes of tiger. 老虎 adds 老 (old/venerable) showing respect for powerful animal.
