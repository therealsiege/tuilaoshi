# 罪 Crime, guilt, sin

- **Character**: 罪
- **Pinyin**: zuì
- **Meaning**: crime, guilt, sin
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 犯罪 (fàn zuì) — to commit a crime
- 有罪 (yǒu zuì) — guilty

## Example Sentences

- 被告被判定有罪。(Bèi gào bèi pàn dìng yǒu zuì.) — The defendant was found guilty.

## Notes

Fundamental legal term. 无罪 means innocent. 罪犯 means criminal. 罪行 means crime, criminal act.
