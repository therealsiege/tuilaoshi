# 记 To Remember

- **Character**: 记
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to remember, to record
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 记住 (jì zhù) — to remember
- 日记 (rì jì) — diary

## Example Sentences

- 你记住了吗？(Nǐ jì zhù le ma?) — Did you remember?

## Notes

记住 means to remember firmly. 忘记 means to forget. 笔记 means notes.
