# 存 Store, save

- **Character**: 存
- **Pinyin**: cún
- **Meaning**: store, save
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 存储 (cún chǔ) — storage
- 内存 (nèi cún) — memory, RAM

## Example Sentences

- 请保存文件。(Qǐng bǎo cún wén jiàn.) — Please save the file.

## Notes

Fundamental for 存储 (storage), 内存 (memory), 缓存 (cache), 保存 (save)
