# 瓦 Tile

- **Character**: 瓦
- **Pinyin**: wǎ
- **Meaning**: tile, earthenware
- **Radical**: 瓦(tile)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 瓦片 (wǎ piàn) — tile shard
- 砖瓦 (zhuān wǎ) — bricks and tiles

## Example Sentences

- 传统的中国房子屋顶铺着瓦片。(Chuántǒng de Zhōngguó fángzi wūdǐng pū zhe wǎpiàn.) — Traditional Chinese houses have roofs covered with tiles.

## Notes

瓦 refers to roof tiles or earthenware. It is also a radical meaning tile.
