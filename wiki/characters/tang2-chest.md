# 膛 Cavity

- **Character**: 膛
- **Pinyin**: táng
- **Meaning**: chest cavity, barrel
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 胸膛 (xiōng táng) — chest
- 枪膛 (qiāng táng) — gun barrel

## Example Sentences

- 他拍着胸膛保证。(Tā pāi zhe xiōng táng bǎo zhèng.) — He patted his chest in guarantee.

## Notes

膛 refers to hollow body cavities or barrel-like spaces, like chest cavity or gun barrel.
