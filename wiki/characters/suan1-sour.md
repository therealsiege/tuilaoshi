# 酸 Sour

- **Character**: 酸
- **Pinyin**: suān
- **Meaning**: sour, acid
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 酸奶 (suān nǎi) — yogurt
- 酸甜 (suān tián) — sweet and sour

## Example Sentences

- 柠檬很酸。(Níng méng hěn suān.) — Lemons are very sour.

## Notes

酸 with wine radical 酉, wine turns sour. 酸奶 = yogurt. 糖醋 = sweet and sour.
