# 绝 Absolute

- **Character**: 绝
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to exhaust, absolute, quatrain
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绝句 (jué jù) — quatrain
- 绝对 (jué duì) — absolute

## Example Sentences

- 绝句是四句的诗。(Jué jù shì sì jù de shī.) — A quatrain is a four-line poem.

## Notes

绝 means absolute or to exhaust; 绝句 is a four-line regulated verse.
