# 怨 To Resent

- **Character**: 怨
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: to resent, complain
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 抱怨 (bào yuàn) — to complain
- 怨恨 (yuàn hèn) — to resent

## Example Sentences

- 他总是抱怨工作太累。(Tā zǒngshì bàoyuàn gōngzuò tài lèi.) — He always complains that work is too tiring.

## Notes

怨 combines evening 夕 and heart 心, suggesting lingering grievance in the heart.
