# 延 Extend, Delay, 延长 Extend

- **Character**: 延
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: extend, delay, 延长 extend
- **Radical**: 廴 (extend)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 延长 (yán cháng) — extend, prolong
- 延期 (yán qī) — postpone
- 拖延 (tuō yán) — delay

## Example Sentences

- 会议延期到下周。(Huìyì yánqī dào xià zhōu.) — The meeting is postponed to next week.

## Notes

Formal term for extension and postponement. 延长 (extend), 延期 (postpone), 延续 (continue) are common in official contexts.
