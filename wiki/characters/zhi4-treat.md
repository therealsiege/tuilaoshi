# 治 Cure

- **Character**: 治
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: treat, cure
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 治疗 (zhì liáo) — to treat, treatment
- 治愈 (zhì yù) — to cure
- 医治 (yī zhì) — to treat (medically)

## Example Sentences

- 这种病能治好吗？(Zhè zhǒng bìng néng zhì hǎo ma?) — Can this disease be cured?

## Notes

To govern or treat; fundamental medical verb.
