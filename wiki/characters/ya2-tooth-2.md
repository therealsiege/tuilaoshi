# 牙 Tooth

- **Character**: 牙
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: tooth
- **Radical**: 牙 (tooth)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 牙齿 (yáchǐ) — teeth
- 刷牙 (shuā yá) — brush teeth

## Example Sentences

- 我每天刷两次牙。(Wǒ měi tiān shuā liǎng cì yá.) — I brush my teeth twice a day.

## Notes

Simple character for tooth. Remember 刷牙 (brush teeth) is an essential daily routine phrase.
