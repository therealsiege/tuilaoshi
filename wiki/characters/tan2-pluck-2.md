# 弹 To Pluck

- **Character**: 弹
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: to pluck, to play (instrument)
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 弹琴 (tán qín) — to play stringed instrument
- 弹钢琴 (tán gāng qín) — to play piano

## Example Sentences

- 她正在弹吉他。(Tā zhèng zài tán jí tā.) — She is playing the guitar.

## Notes

弹 simplified from 彈. Bow radical 弓 representing plucking strings like drawing a bow.
