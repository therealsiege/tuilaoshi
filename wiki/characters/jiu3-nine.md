# 九 Nine

- **Character**: 九
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: nine
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 九月 (jiǔ yuè) — September
- 九十 (jiǔ shí) — ninety

## Example Sentences

- 他今年九十岁。(Tā jīn nián jiǔ shí suì.) — He is ninety years old this year.

## Notes

九 sounds like 久 (jiǔ, long-lasting), so it symbolizes longevity. The Emperor used 9 extensively.
