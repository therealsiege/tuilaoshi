# 燕 Swallow (bird)

- **Character**: 燕
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: swallow (bird)
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 燕子 (yàn zi) — swallow
- 燕窝 (yàn wō) — bird's nest

## Example Sentences

- 春天燕子从南方飞回来。(Chūntiān yànzi cóng nánfāng fēi huílai.) — In spring, swallows fly back from the south.

## Notes

Herald of spring. Also name of ancient state. Top part resembles bird shape.
