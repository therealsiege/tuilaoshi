# 辣 Spicy

- **Character**: 辣
- **Pinyin**: là
- **Meaning**: spicy, hot (flavor)
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 辣椒 (là jiāo) — chili pepper
- 麻辣 (má là) — numbing and spicy

## Example Sentences

- 四川菜很辣。(Sì chuān cài hěn là.) — Sichuan food is very spicy.

## Notes

辣 combines bitter 辛 and 束 (bundle), intensely pungent.
