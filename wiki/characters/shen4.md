# 慎 Cautious

- **Character**: 慎
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: cautious, careful
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 谨慎 (jǐn shèn) — cautious
- 慎重 (shèn zhòng) — prudent

## Example Sentences

- 做决定要谨慎。(Zuò jué dìng yào jǐn shèn.) — One must be cautious when making decisions.

## Notes

慎 shows heart 心 beneath 真 (true), representing sincere carefulness and vigilance in one's conduct.
