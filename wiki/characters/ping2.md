# 评 To Comment

- **Character**: 评
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: to comment, to review, to judge
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 评论 (píng lùn) — to comment
- 批评 (pī píng) — to criticize

## Example Sentences

- 请评论一下这篇文章。(Qǐng píng lùn yī xià zhè piān wén zhāng.) — Please comment on this article.

## Notes

评 simplified from 評. Uses speech radical 讠 with 平 (balanced), representing fair balanced speech in evaluation.
