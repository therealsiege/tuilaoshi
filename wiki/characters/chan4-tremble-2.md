# 颤 To Tremble

- **Character**: 颤
- **Pinyin**: chàn
- **Meaning**: to tremble, to shiver
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to tremble
- 颤动 (chàn dòng) — to vibrate

## Example Sentences

- 他冷得颤抖。(Tā lěng de chàn dǒu.) — He trembled from cold.

## Notes

颤 simplified from 顫. Head radical 页 representing trembling or shivering.
