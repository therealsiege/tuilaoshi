# 远 Far

- **Character**: 远
- **Pinyin**: yuǎn
- **Meaning**: far, distant
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 遥远 (yáo yuǎn) — distant, remote
- 远方 (yuǎn fāng) — far away, distant place

## Example Sentences

- 学校离这儿很远。(Xué xiào lí zhè r hěn yuǎn.) — The school is very far from here.

## Notes

远 simplified from 遠. Opposite of 近 (near). 远方 means distant place.
