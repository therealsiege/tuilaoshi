# 筝 筝筝筝

- **Character**: 筝
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: 筝筝筝
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 古筝 (gǔ zhēng) — 筝筝
- 筝曲 (zhēng qǔ) — 筝筝

## Example Sentences

- 她正在学习古筝。(Tā zhèng zài xué xí gǔ zhēng.) — She is currently learning the guzheng.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese stringed instrument with movable bridges.
