# 耙 Rake

- **Character**: 耙
- **Pinyin**: pá
- **Meaning**: rake, to rake
- **Radical**: 耒 (plow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耙子 (pá zi) — rake
- 耙地 (pá dì) — to rake the field

## Example Sentences

- 他用耙子耙地。(Tā yòng pá zi pá dì.) — He rakes the field with a rake.

## Notes

耙 has plow 耒 with 巴 (to cling to), farm tool for leveling soil.
