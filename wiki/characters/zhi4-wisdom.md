# 智 Wisdom, intelligence

- **Character**: 智
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: wisdom, intelligence
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 智慧 (zhì huì) — wisdom
- 智力 (zhì lì) — intelligence

## Example Sentences

- 智者千虑，必有一失。(Zhì zhě qiān lǜ, bì yǒu yī shī.) — Even the wise make mistakes after a thousand deliberations.

## Notes

Represents wisdom and knowledge. One of the five Confucian virtues, emphasizing the importance of discernment and understanding.
