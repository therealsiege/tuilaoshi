# 狂 Mad, wild, arrogant

- **Character**: 狂
- **Pinyin**: kuáng
- **Meaning**: mad, wild, arrogant
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 疯狂 (fēngkuáng) — crazy, mad
- 狂妄 (kuángwàng) — arrogant, presumptuous

## Example Sentences

- 他的想法很狂妄。(Tā de xiǎngfǎ hěn kuángwàng.) — His ideas are very arrogant.

## Notes

Common term for madness, wildness, or arrogance.
