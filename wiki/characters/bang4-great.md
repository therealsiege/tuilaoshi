# 棒 Great

- **Character**: 棒
- **Pinyin**: bàng
- **Meaning**: stick, club, great, fantastic
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 棒球 (bàng qiú) — baseball
- 冰棒 (bīng bàng) — popsicle

## Example Sentences

- 你真棒！(Nǐ zhēn bàng!) — You're great!

## Notes

棒 colloquially means awesome/great. Also means a stick or rod.
