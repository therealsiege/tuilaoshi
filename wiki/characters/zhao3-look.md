# 找 To Look For

- **Character**: 找
- **Pinyin**: zhǎo
- **Meaning**: to look for, to seek, to find
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 找到 (zhǎo dào) — to find
- 寻找 (xún zhǎo) — to search for

## Example Sentences

- 你在找什么？(Nǐ zài zhǎo shén me?) — What are you looking for?

## Notes

找 uses hand radical because searching involves reaching with hands. 找到 adds result complement.
