# 储 Store, save

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: store, save
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 储存 (chǔ cún) — store, reserve
- 储蓄 (chǔ xù) — save money, savings

## Example Sentences

- 银行鼓励人们储蓄。(Yín háng gǔ lì rén men chǔ xù.) — Banks encourage people to save.

## Notes

储备 means reserve/stockpile. 储户 means depositor.
