# 娜 Graceful, elegant

- **Character**: 娜
- **Pinyin**: nuó
- **Meaning**: graceful, elegant
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 婀娜 (ē nuó) — graceful, lithe
- 袅娜 (niǎo nuó) — graceful, delicate

## Example Sentences

- 舞者的身姿袅娜动人。(Wǔ zhě de shēn zī niǎo nuó dòng rén.) — The dancer's figure is gracefully enchanting.

## Notes

Also pronounced nà as a transliteration or in names.
