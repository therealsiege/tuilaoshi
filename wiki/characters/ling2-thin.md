# 绫 Thin silk, damask

- **Character**: 绫
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: thin silk, damask
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绫罗 (líng luó) — silks
- 绫绢 (líng juàn) — silk fabrics

## Example Sentences

- 这件衣服是用绫罗绸缎做的。(Zhè jiàn yī fú shì yòng líng luó chóu duàn zuò de.) — This garment is made of fine silks and satins.

## Notes

Type of patterned silk fabric.
