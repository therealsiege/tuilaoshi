# 颤 To Tremble

- **Character**: 颤
- **Pinyin**: chàn
- **Meaning**: to tremble, to shiver
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to tremble
- 颤动 (chàn dòng) — to vibrate

## Example Sentences

- 他的手在颤抖。(Tā de shǒu zài chàn dǒu.) — His hands are trembling.

## Notes

颤 simplified from 顫. Head 页 shaking motion.
