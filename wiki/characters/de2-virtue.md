# 德 Virtue, morality, ethics, kindness

- **Character**: 德
- **Pinyin**: dé
- **Meaning**: virtue, morality, ethics, kindness
- **Radical**: 彳(step)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 道德 (dào dé) — morality, ethics
- 品德 (pǐn dé) — moral character

## Example Sentences

- 他是个有道德的人。(Tā shì ge yǒu dàodé de rén.) — He is a moral person.

## Notes

Fundamental character for virtue and moral character.
