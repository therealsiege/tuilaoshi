# 髦 Fashionable

- **Character**: 髦
- **Pinyin**: máo
- **Meaning**: fashionable, bang (hair)
- **Radical**: 髟 (long hair)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 时髦 (shí máo) — fashionable
- 赶时髦 (gǎn shí máo) — to follow fashion

## Example Sentences

- 她穿得很时髦。(Tā chuān de hěn shí máo.) — She dresses very fashionably.

## Notes

髦 has long hair 髟 with 毛 (hair/fur), originally bangs/forelocks, now means trendy.
