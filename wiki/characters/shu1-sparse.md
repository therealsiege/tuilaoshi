# 疏 Sparse, distant, to dredge

- **Character**: 疏
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: sparse, distant, to dredge
- **Radical**: 疋 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 疏忽 (shū hu) — to neglect, careless
- 疏远 (shū yuǎn) — to drift apart, estranged

## Example Sentences

- 由于疏忽，他犯了错误。(Yóuyú shūhu, tā fànle cuòwù.) — Due to carelessness, he made a mistake.

## Notes

Can mean sparse/thin or careless/negligent depending on context.
