# 淡 Bland, light, weak

- **Character**: 淡
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: bland, light, weak
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 清淡 (qīng dàn) — light, bland
- 淡水 (dàn shuǐ) — fresh water

## Example Sentences

- 医生建议吃清淡的食物。(Yīshēng jiànyì chī qīngdàn de shíwù.) — The doctor suggests eating light food.

## Notes

Opposite of 咸. Can mean bland taste or light color. Water radical.
