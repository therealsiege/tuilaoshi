# 闷 Depressed

- **Character**: 闷
- **Pinyin**: mèn
- **Meaning**: bored, depressed; stuffy (mēn)
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 闷闷不乐 (mèn mèn bù lè) — depressed
- 烦闷 (fán mèn) — anguished

## Example Sentences

- 他心里很闷。(Tā xīn lǐ hěn mèn.) — He feels very depressed.

## Notes

闷 shows heart 心 trapped behind a gate 门, representing oppressed spirits. As mēn it means stuffy (air).
