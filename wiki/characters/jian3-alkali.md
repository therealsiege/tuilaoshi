# 碱 Alkali

- **Character**: 碱
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: alkali, base
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 碱性 (jiǎn xìng) — alkaline
- 酸碱 (suān jiǎn) — acid and alkali

## Example Sentences

- 肥皂水是碱性的。(Féizào shuǐ shì jiǎnxìng de.) — Soapy water is alkaline.

## Notes

碱 refers to alkali or base in chemistry, opposite of acid. The stone radical reflects mineral origins.
