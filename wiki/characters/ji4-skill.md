# 技 Skill

- **Character**: 技
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: skill, technique
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 技术 (jì shù) — technology
- 杂技 (zá jì) — acrobatics

## Example Sentences

- 他的技术很高超。(Tā de jì shù hěn gāo chāo.) — His skills are very advanced.

## Notes

技 means skill or technique; appears in many performance-related terms.
