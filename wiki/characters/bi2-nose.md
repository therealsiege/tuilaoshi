# 鼻 Nose

- **Character**: 鼻
- **Pinyin**: bí
- **Meaning**: nose
- **Radical**: 鼻 (nose)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 鼻子 (bí zi) — nose
- 鼻涕 (bí tì) — nasal mucus, snot

## Example Sentences

- 我的鼻子不舒服。(Wǒ de bí zi bù shū fu.) — My nose is uncomfortable.

## Notes

鼻 pictograph of nose and breathing. 鼻子 complete word. 鼻涕 = runny nose.
