# 拔 Extract

- **Character**: 拔
- **Pinyin**: bá
- **Meaning**: pull out, extract
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拔罐 (bá guàn) — cupping therapy
- 拔牙 (bá yá) — to extract a tooth
- 拔出 (bá chū) — to pull out

## Example Sentences

- 拔罐可以缓解肌肉疼痛。(Bá guàn kě yǐ huǎn jiě jī ròu téng tòng.) — Cupping can relieve muscle pain.

## Notes

Pulling or extraction; TCM therapy or dental.
