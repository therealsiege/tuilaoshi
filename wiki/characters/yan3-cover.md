# 掩 Cover

- **Character**: 掩
- **Pinyin**: yǎn
- **Meaning**: to cover, to conceal
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn ěr dào líng) — self-deception
- 掩盖 (yǎn gài) — to cover up

## Example Sentences

- 掩耳盗铃只是自欺欺人。(Yǎn ěr dào líng zhǐ shì zìqī qīrén.) — Covering your ears to steal a bell is just self-deception.

## Notes

Used in 掩耳盗铃: covering one's ears while stealing a bell, thinking others won't hear.
