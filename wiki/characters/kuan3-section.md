# 款 Clause

- **Character**: 款
- **Pinyin**: kuǎn
- **Meaning**: section, fund, style, clause
- **Radical**: 欠 (to owe)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 款项 (kuǎn xiàng) — funds
- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan

## Example Sentences

- 请仔细阅读每一款。(Qǐng zǐ xì yuè dú měi yī kuǎn.) — Please read each clause carefully.

## Notes

款 combines 士 (scholar) and 欠 (to owe), formal document sections.
