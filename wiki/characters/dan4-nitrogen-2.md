# 氮 Nitrogen

- **Character**: 氮
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: nitrogen
- **Radical**: 气(gas)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 氮气 (dàn qì) — nitrogen gas
- 氮肥 (dàn féi) — nitrogen fertilizer

## Example Sentences

- 空气中约78%是氮气。(Kōngqì zhōng yuē bǎifēn zhī qīshíbā shì dànqì.) — About 78% of air is nitrogen.

## Notes

氮 is nitrogen, the most abundant gas in Earth's atmosphere. Essential for proteins and DNA.
