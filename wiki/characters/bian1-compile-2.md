# 编 Compile, arrange

- **Character**: 编
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: compile, arrange
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 编程 (biān chéng) — programming
- 编译 (biān yì) — compile

## Example Sentences

- 我在学习编程。(Wǒ zài xué xí biān chéng.) — I am learning programming.

## Notes

Essential for 编程 (programming), 编译 (compile), 编码 (encoding)
