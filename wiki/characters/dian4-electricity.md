# 电 Electricity

- **Character**: 电
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: electricity, electric
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 电话 (diàn huà) — telephone
- 电脑 (diàn nǎo) — computer

## Example Sentences

- 我的电话号码是多少？(Wǒ de diàn huà hào mǎ shì duō shǎo?) — What is my phone number?

## Notes

电 originally depicted lightning striking a field. It simplified from 電.
