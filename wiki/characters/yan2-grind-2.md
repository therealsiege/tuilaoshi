# 研 To Grind

- **Character**: 研
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: to grind, to study
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 研究 (yán jiū) — to research
- 研磨 (yán mó) — to grind

## Example Sentences

- 他在研究这个问题。(Tā zài yán jiū zhè gè wèn tí.) — He is researching this problem.

## Notes

研 combines stone 石 and 开 (open), originally grinding with stone, extended to thorough study.
