# 筛 Screen

- **Character**: 筛
- **Pinyin**: shāi
- **Meaning**: sieve, screen
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 筛查 (shāi chá) — screening, to screen
- 筛选 (shāi xuǎn) — to select, screen
- 过筛 (guò shāi) — to sieve

## Example Sentences

- 癌症筛查可以早期发现。(Ái zhèng shāi chá kě yǐ zǎo qī fā xiàn.) — Cancer screening can detect it early.

## Notes

Selective testing; early detection strategy.
