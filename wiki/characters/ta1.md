# 他 He/Him

- **Character**: 他
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: he, him
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 他们 (tāmen) — they, them (masculine or mixed)
- 他的 (tā de) — his
- 他人 (tārén) — others, other people
- 其他 (qítā) — other, else

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的朋友。(Tā shì wǒ de péngyǒu.) — He is my friend.
- 他在中国工作。(Tā zài Zhōngguó gōngzuò.) — He works in China.
- 你认识他吗？(Nǐ rènshí tā ma?) — Do you know him?

## Notes

他 uses the person radical 亻 plus 也 (yě, "also") as a phonetic component. In spoken Chinese, 他 (he), 她 (she), and 它 (it) are all pronounced tā. The written distinction between 他 and 她 was introduced in the early 20th century under Western influence; classical Chinese used 他 for all genders.
