# 肘 Elbow

- **Character**: 肘
- **Pinyin**: zhǒu
- **Meaning**: elbow
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 胳膊肘 (gē bo zhǒu) — elbow
- 肘击 (zhǒu jī) — elbow strike

## Example Sentences

- 他用肘击攻击对手。(Tā yòng zhǒu jī gōng jī duì shǒu.) — He attacks his opponent with an elbow strike.

## Notes

肘 has flesh 月 with 寸 (inch), the bend of the arm.
