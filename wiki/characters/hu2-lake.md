# 湖 Lake

- **Character**: 湖
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: lake
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 湖泊 (hú pō) — lake
- 西湖 (xī hú) — West Lake (famous in Hangzhou)

## Example Sentences

- 这个湖很美。(Zhè gè hú hěn měi.) — This lake is very beautiful.

## Notes

湖 uses the water radical. Famous lakes include 西湖 (West Lake) in Hangzhou and 青海湖 (Qinghai Lake).
