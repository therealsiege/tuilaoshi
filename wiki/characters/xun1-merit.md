# 勋 Merit, meritorious deed

- **Character**: 勋
- **Pinyin**: xūn
- **Meaning**: merit, meritorious deed
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 勋章 (xūn zhāng) — medal, decoration
- 功勋 (gōng xūn) — meritorious service

## Example Sentences

- 他获得了一枚勋章。(Tā huò dé le yī méi xūn zhāng.) — He received a medal.

## Notes

Recognition of distinguished service or achievement
