# 藉 To rely on, excuse, pad

- **Character**: 藉
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: to rely on, excuse, pad
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 慰藉 (wèi jiè) — comfort, consolation
- 借藉 (jiè jiè) — to use as pretext

## Example Sentences

- 音乐是他心灵的慰藉。(Yīnyuè shì tā xīnlíng de wèijiè.) — Music is his spiritual comfort.

## Notes

Often used in compound 慰藉 for emotional comfort or consolation.
