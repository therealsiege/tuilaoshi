# 朝 Dynasty, imperial court

- **Character**: 朝
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: dynasty, imperial court
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 朝廷 (cháo tíng) — imperial court
- 朝代 (cháo dài) — dynasty

## Example Sentences

- 清朝是中国最后一个封建王朝。(Qīng cháo shì Zhōngguó zuìhòu yī gè fēngjiàn wángcháo.) — The Qing Dynasty was China's last feudal dynasty.

## Notes

Represents a dynasty or the imperial court. Also pronounced zhāo meaning morning. The character shows moon and sun.
