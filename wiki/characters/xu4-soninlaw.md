# 婿 Son-in-law

- **Character**: 婿
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: son-in-law
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 女婿 (nǚ xù) — son-in-law
- 姑爷 (gū ye) — son-in-law

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的女婿。(Tā shì wǒ de nǚxù.) — He is my son-in-law.

## Notes

Specifically refers to daughter's husband.
