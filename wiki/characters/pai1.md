# 拍 To Pat

- **Character**: 拍
- **Pinyin**: pāi
- **Meaning**: to pat, to take (photo)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拍照 (pāi zhào) — to take a photo
- 拍手 (pāi shǒu) — to clap

## Example Sentences

- 我想在这里拍张照片。(Wǒ xiǎng zài zhè lǐ pāi zhāng zhào piàn.) — I want to take a photo here.

## Notes

拍 combines hand 扌 and 白 (white/clear), representing striking with palm.
