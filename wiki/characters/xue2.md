# 学 To Study/Learn

- **Character**: 学
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: to study, to learn; learning, knowledge
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 学生 (xuéshēng) — student
- 学校 (xuéxiào) — school
- 学习 (xuéxí) — to study, to learn
- 大学 (dàxué) — university
- 学会 (xuéhuì) — to master, to learn (successfully)
- 同学 (tóngxué) — classmate

## Example Sentences

- 我在学中文。(Wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén.) — I am studying Chinese.
- 他在大学学习。(Tā zài dàxué xuéxí.) — He studies at a university.
- 学中文难不难？(Xué Zhōngwén nán bù nán?) — Is studying Chinese hard?

## Notes

学 (simplified from 學) shows hands (top strokes) guiding a child (子) under a roof, depicting the act of teaching/learning. The distinction between 学 and 习 (xí): 学 emphasizes acquiring new knowledge, while 习 emphasizes practicing and reviewing. Together, 学习 covers the full learning process. Confucius said: 学而时习之，不亦说乎 — "To learn and then practice regularly, is this not a joy?"
