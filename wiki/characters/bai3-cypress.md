# 柏 Cypress

- **Character**: 柏
- **Pinyin**: bǎi
- **Meaning**: cypress, cedar (also bó)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 松柏 (sōng bǎi) — pine and cypress
- 柏树 (bǎi shù) — cypress tree

## Example Sentences

- 松柏四季常青。(Sōng bǎi sì jì cháng qīng.) — Pines and cypresses are evergreen.

## Notes

柏 as bǎi means cypress, as bó in柏林 (Berlin). Wood 木 with白 (white), representing the pale wood.
