# 靠 Lean on, rely on, near

- **Character**: 靠
- **Pinyin**: kào
- **Meaning**: lean on, rely on, near
- **Radical**: 非 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 靠近 (kào jìn) — near, close to
- 依靠 (yī kào) — rely on, depend on

## Example Sentences

- 船慢慢靠岸了。(Chuán màn màn kào àn le.) — The boat slowly came to shore.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: lean, rely, approach, or come near.
