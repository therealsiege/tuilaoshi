# 愧 Ashamed

- **Character**: 愧
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: ashamed, abashed
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed
- 愧疚 (kuì jiù) — guilt

## Example Sentences

- 他心怀愧疚。(Tā xīn huái kuì jiù.) — He feels guilty.

## Notes

愧 has heart 忄 with 鬼 (ghost), heart haunted by guilt.
