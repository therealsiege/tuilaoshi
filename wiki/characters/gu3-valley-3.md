# 谷 Valley

- **Character**: 谷
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: valley
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 山谷 (shāngǔ) — valley
- 峡谷 (xiágǔ) — canyon

## Example Sentences

- 山谷里很安静。(Shāngǔ lǐ hěn ānjìng.) — It's very quiet in the valley.

## Notes

Geography vocabulary. The character is its own radical. Often paired with 山 as 山谷.
