# 砚 Inkstone

- **Character**: 砚
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: inkstone
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砚台 (yàn tái) — inkstone
- 笔砚 (bǐ yàn) — writing tools

## Example Sentences

- 这块砚台很珍贵。(Zhè kuài yàn tái hěn zhēn guì.) — This inkstone is very precious.

## Notes

砚 simplified from 硯. Stone 石 with 见 (to see), stone slab for grinding ink.
