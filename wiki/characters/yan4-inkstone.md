# 砚 Inkstone

- **Character**: 砚
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: inkstone, ink slab
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砚台 (yàn tái) — inkstone

## Example Sentences

- 砚台是文房四宝之一。(Yàn tái shì wén fáng sì bǎo zhī yī.) — The inkstone is one of the Four Treasures of the Study.

## Notes

砚 means inkstone, used for grinding ink. One of the Four Treasures of the Study (笔墨纸砚).
