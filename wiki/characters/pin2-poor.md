# 贫 Poor, lacking, deficient

- **Character**: 贫
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: poor, lacking, deficient
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贫瘠 (pín jí) — barren, poor
- 贫穷 (pín qióng) — poor, impoverished

## Example Sentences

- 贫瘠的土地需要改良。(Pín jí de tǔ dì xū yào gǎi liáng.) — Barren land needs improvement.

## Notes

Lack of nutrients or resources
