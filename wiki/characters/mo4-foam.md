# 沫 Foam, froth, bubble

- **Character**: 沫
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: foam, froth, bubble
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泡沫 (pào mò) — foam, bubble
- 唾沫 (tuò mo) — saliva, spit

## Example Sentences

- 搅拌产生了大量泡沫。(Jiǎo bàn chǎn shēng le dà liàng pào mò.) — Stirring produced a lot of foam.
