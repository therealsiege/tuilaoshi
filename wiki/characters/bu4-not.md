# 不 Not

- **Character**: 不
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: not, no
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 不好 (bù hǎo) — not good
- 不是 (bú shì) — is not

## Example Sentences

- 我不知道。(Wǒ bù zhīdào.) — I don't know.

## Notes

不 changes to 2nd tone (bú) before a 4th tone syllable.
