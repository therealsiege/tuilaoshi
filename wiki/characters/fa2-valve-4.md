# 阀 Valve

- **Character**: 阀
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: valve
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 阀门 (fá mén) — valve
- 电子阀 (diàn zǐ fá) — electronic valve

## Example Sentences

- 这个阀门控制流量。(Zhè gè fá mén kòng zhì liú liàng.) — This valve controls the flow.

## Notes

Used in 阀门 (valve), 电子管 (vacuum tube), control mechanisms
