# 补 Mend, patch, supplement

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: mend, patch, supplement
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 补贴 (bǔ tiē) — subsidy
- 补充 (bǔ chōng) — to supplement

## Example Sentences

- 需要补充更多的证据。(Xū yào bǔ chōng gèng duō de zhèng jù.) — More evidence needs to be supplemented.

## Notes

Means to mend or supplement. 弥补 means to make up for. 补偿 means compensation. 补习 means tutoring.
