# 劈 Chop

- **Character**: 劈
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: to chop, split
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 劈叉 (pī chà) — to do the splits
- 劈砍 (pī kǎn) — to chop

## Example Sentences

- 她能轻松地劈叉。(Tā néng qīngsōng de pīchà.) — She can do the splits easily.

## Notes

Chopping motion and splits; used in martial arts and gymnastics
