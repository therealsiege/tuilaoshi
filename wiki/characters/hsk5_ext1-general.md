# 概 General, Approximate, 概况 Situation Overview

- **Character**: 概
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: general, approximate,概况 situation overview
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 概括 (gài kuò) — summarize, generalize
- 大概 (dà gài) — probably, roughly
- 概念 (gài niàn) — concept, idea

## Example Sentences

- 请概括一下主要内容。(Qǐng gài kuò yīxià zhǔyào nèiróng.) — Please summarize the main content.

## Notes

Used extensively in academic writing to express general ideas or approximations. Common in phrases like 概况 (general situation) and 概述 (overview).
