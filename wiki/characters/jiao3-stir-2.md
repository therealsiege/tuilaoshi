# 搅 Stir, disturb, mix

- **Character**: 搅
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: stir, disturb, mix
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — stir, mix
- 搅动 (jiǎo dòng) — stir, agitate

## Example Sentences

- 用勺子搅动溶液。(Yòng sháo zi jiǎo dòng róng yè.) — Stir the solution with a spoon.

## Notes

Stirring and agitating
