# 扛 To Carry On Shoulder

- **Character**: 扛
- **Pinyin**: káng
- **Meaning**: to carry on shoulder
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扛着 (káng zhe) — shouldering
- 扛起 (káng qǐ) — to shoulder

## Example Sentences

- 他扛着一袋米。(Tā káng zhe yí dài mǐ.) — He carries a bag of rice on his shoulder.

## Notes

扛 combines hand 扌 and 工 (work), representing carrying heavy loads on shoulder.
