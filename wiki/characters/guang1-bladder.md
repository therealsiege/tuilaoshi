# 胱 Bladder

- **Character**: 胱
- **Pinyin**: guāng
- **Meaning**: bladder
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 膀胱 (páng guāng) — urinary bladder
- 膀胱炎 (páng guāng yán) — cystitis

## Example Sentences

- 膀胱炎会引起尿频。(Páng guāng yán huì yǐn qǐ niào pín.) — Cystitis can cause frequent urination.

## Notes

Used in bladder terminology
