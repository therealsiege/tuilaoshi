# 沟 Ditch

- **Character**: 沟
- **Pinyin**: gōu
- **Meaning**: ditch, gully, groove
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 水沟 (shuǐ gōu) — ditch, drain
- 沟通 (gōu tōng) — to communicate

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要加强沟通。(Wǒmen xūyào jiāqiáng gōutōng.) — We need to strengthen communication.

## Notes

沟 combines water 氵 with 冓 (to construct), representing channels for water flow or communication.
