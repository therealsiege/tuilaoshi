# 拍 To Pat

- **Character**: 拍
- **Pinyin**: pāi
- **Meaning**: to pat, to clap, beat (music)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拍手 (pāi shǒu) — to clap hands
- 节拍 (jié pāi) — beat, rhythm

## Example Sentences

- 观众为她鼓掌拍手。(Guān zhòng wèi tā gǔ zhǎng pāi shǒu.) — The audience applauded for her.

## Notes

拍 means to pat or clap; in music refers to beats.
