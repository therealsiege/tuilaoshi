# 邑 City, town, state

- **Character**: 邑
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: city, town, state
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 城邑 (chéng yì) — city, town
- 都邑 (dū yì) — capital city

## Example Sentences

- 古代的城邑都有城墙保护。(Gǔdài de chéngyì dōu yǒu chéngqiáng bǎohù.) — Ancient cities were all protected by city walls.
