# 椽 Rafter

- **Character**: 椽
- **Pinyin**: chuán
- **Meaning**: rafter
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 椽子 (chuán zi) — rafter
- 房椽 (fáng chuán) — roof rafter

## Example Sentences

- 传统建筑的椽子很讲究。(Chuán tǒng jiàn zhù de chuán zi hěn jiǎng jiù.) — Rafters in traditional buildings are very particular.

## Notes

椽 has wood 木 with 彖 phonetic, slanted roof supports.
