# 歪 Crooked

- **Character**: 歪
- **Pinyin**: wāi
- **Meaning**: crooked, askew
- **Radical**: 不 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 歪曲 (wāi qū) — to distort
- 歪斜 (wāi xié) — crooked

## Example Sentences

- 画挂歪了。(Huà guà wāi le.) — The painting is hanging crooked.

## Notes

歪 combines 不 (not) and 正 (straight), representing something not straight or proper.
