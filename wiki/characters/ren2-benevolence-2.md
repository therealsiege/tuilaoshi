# 仁 Benevolence, humanity

- **Character**: 仁
- **Pinyin**: rén
- **Meaning**: benevolence, humanity
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 仁慈 (rén cí) — benevolent, kind
- 仁爱 (rén ài) — love and kindness

## Example Sentences

- 孔子认为仁是最高的道德标准。(Kǒngzǐ rènwéi rén shì zuìgāo de dàodé biāozhǔn.) — Confucius believed that benevolence is the highest moral standard.

## Notes

Core Confucian virtue meaning benevolence or humaneness. The character combines 人 (person) and 二 (two), suggesting interpersonal relationships.
