# 喷 To Spray

- **Character**: 喷
- **Pinyin**: pēn
- **Meaning**: to spray, to spurt, to spout
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喷泉 (pēn quán) — fountain
- 喷射 (pēn shè) — to jet

## Example Sentences

- 火山喷发了。(Huǒ shān pēn fā le.) — The volcano erupted.

## Notes

喷 simplified from 噴. Mouth 口 ejecting forcefully, to spray.
