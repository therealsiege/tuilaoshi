# 傲 Proud, haughty

- **Character**: 傲
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: proud, haughty
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 骄傲 (jiāo'ào) — proud, arrogant
- 傲慢 (àomàn) — arrogant, haughty

## Example Sentences

- 他态度傲慢。(Tā tàidù àomàn.) — His attitude is arrogant.

## Notes

Common term for pride or haughtiness, usually negative.
