# 牢 Pen

- **Character**: 牢
- **Pinyin**: láo
- **Meaning**: pen, prison, firm
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 亡羊补牢 (wáng yáng bǔ láo) — better late than never
- 牢固 (láo gù) — firm, secure

## Example Sentences

- 牢固的基础很重要。(Láogù de jīchǔ hěn zhòngyào.) — A solid foundation is very important.

## Notes

The animal pen in 亡羊补牢; also means prison or firm/secure.
