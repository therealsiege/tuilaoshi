# 引 Lead, Guide, 引导 Guide

- **Character**: 引
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: lead, guide, 引导 guide
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 引导 (yǐn dǎo) — guide, lead
- 引起 (yǐn qǐ) — cause, arouse
- 吸引 (xī yǐn) — attract

## Example Sentences

- 这件事引起了公众的关注。(Zhè jiàn shì yǐnqǐ le gōngzhòng de guānzhù.) — This matter aroused public attention.

## Notes

Common in both literal (lead/guide) and figurative (cause/attract) contexts. 引起 (cause) is very frequently used.
