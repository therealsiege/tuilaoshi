# 嶂 Cliff, precipice, screen-like peak

- **Character**: 嶂
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: cliff, precipice, screen-like peak
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 层峦叠嶂 (céng luán dié zhàng) — range upon range of mountains
- 叠嶂 (dié zhàng) — overlapping peaks

## Example Sentences

- 这里层峦叠嶂，风景壮丽。(Zhè lǐ céng luán dié zhàng, fēng jǐng zhuàng lì.) — Here the mountains rise range upon range, with magnificent scenery.

## Notes

Often used in the phrase 层峦叠嶂.
