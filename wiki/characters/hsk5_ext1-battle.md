# 阵 Battle Array, Burst, 阵营 Camp

- **Character**: 阵
- **Pinyin**: zhèn
- **Meaning**: battle array, burst, 阵营 camp
- **Radical**: ⻏ (city)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阵营 (zhèn yíng) — camp, bloc
- 一阵 (yī zhèn) — a burst of
- 阵地 (zhèn dì) — position, front

## Example Sentences

- 忽然刮起一阵大风。(Hūrán guā qǐ yī zhèn dà fēng.) — Suddenly a gust of strong wind blew.

## Notes

Used both in military contexts (battle formation) and as measure word for bursts of activity (一阵风 - a gust of wind).
