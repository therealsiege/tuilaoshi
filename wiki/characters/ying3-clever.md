# 颖 Clever, bright, tip

- **Character**: 颖
- **Pinyin**: yǐng
- **Meaning**: clever, bright, tip
- **Radical**: 页 (head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 聪颖 (cōngyǐng) — intelligent, bright
- 新颖 (xīnyǐng) — novel, new

## Example Sentences

- 这个想法很新颖。(Zhège xiǎngfǎ hěn xīnyǐng.) — This idea is very novel.

## Notes

Common for intelligence or novelty.
