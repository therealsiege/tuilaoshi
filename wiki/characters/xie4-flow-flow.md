# 泻 To Flow

- **Character**: 泻
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to flow swiftly, diarrhea
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 倾泻 (qīng xiè) — to pour down
- 腹泻 (fù xiè) — diarrhea

## Example Sentences

- 瀑布倾泻而下。(Pù bù qīng xiè ér xià.) — The waterfall pours down.

## Notes

泻 simplified from 瀉. Water 氵 flowing rapidly downward.
