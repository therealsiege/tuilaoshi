# 豁 Open-minded, to open, crack

- **Character**: 豁
- **Pinyin**: huò
- **Meaning**: open-minded, to open, crack
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 豁达 (huò dá) — open-minded, optimistic
- 豁然 (huò rán) — suddenly enlightened

## Example Sentences

- 他为人很豁达。(Tā wéirén hěn huòdá.) — He is very open-minded.

## Notes

Open-mindedness or sudden clarity; also huō (crack/gap).
