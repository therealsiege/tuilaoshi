# 岸 Shore

- **Character**: 岸
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: shore, bank
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 岸边 (àn biān) — waterside, shore
- 上岸 (shàng àn) — to go ashore

## Example Sentences

- 他游到了对岸。(Tā yóu dào le duì àn.) — He swam to the opposite shore.

## Notes

Shore and bank; swimming destinations
