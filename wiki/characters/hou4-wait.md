# 候 Wait, time, season

- **Character**: 候
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: wait, time, season
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 时候 (shíhou) — time, moment
- 候审 (hòushěn) — await trial

## Example Sentences

- 嫌疑人候审期间不得离开。(Xiányírén hòushěn qījiān bùdé líkāi.) — The suspect may not leave while awaiting trial.

## Notes

Used for waiting and time periods.
