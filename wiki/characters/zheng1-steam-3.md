# 蒸 To Steam

- **Character**: 蒸
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: to steam, to evaporate
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蒸汽 (zhēng qì) — steam
- 蒸发 (zhēng fā) — to evaporate

## Example Sentences

- 她蒸了一条鱼。(Tā zhēng le yì tiáo yú.) — She steamed a fish.

## Notes

蒸 has grass 艹 on top, relating to the ancient meaning of steaming plants.
