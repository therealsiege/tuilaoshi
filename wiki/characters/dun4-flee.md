# 遁 To flee, escape, hide

- **Character**: 遁
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: to flee, escape, hide
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 逃遁 (táo dùn) — to flee, escape
- 隐遁 (yǐn dùn) — to live in seclusion

## Example Sentences

- 罪犯逃遁无踪。(Zuìfàn táodùn wú zōng.) — The criminal fled without a trace.

## Notes

To flee or escape, often used in literary contexts.
