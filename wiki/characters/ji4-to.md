# 计 To Calculate

- **Character**: 计
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to计计计, to plan, meter
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 设计 (shè jì) — to design
- 计划 (jì huà) — plan

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要做个计划。(Wǒ men xū yào zuò ge jì huà.) — We need to make a plan.

## Notes

计 means to calculate or plan. Common in设计 (design), 计划 (plan), 计算 (calculate).
