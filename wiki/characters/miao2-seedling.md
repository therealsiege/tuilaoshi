# 苗 Seedling, young plant, vaccine

- **Character**: 苗
- **Pinyin**: miáo
- **Meaning**: seedling, young plant, vaccine
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 疫苗 (yì miáo) — vaccine
- 树苗 (shù miáo) — sapling

## Example Sentences

- 儿童应该按时接种疫苗。(Ér tóng yīng gāi àn shí jiē zhǒng yì miáo.) — Children should be vaccinated on time.

## Notes

Used for vaccines and seedlings
