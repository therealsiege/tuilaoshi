# 梦 Dream

- **Character**: 梦
- **Pinyin**: mèng
- **Meaning**: dream
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 做梦 (zuò mèng) — to dream
- 梦想 (mèng xiǎng) — dream, to dream of

## Example Sentences

- 我昨晚做了个奇怪的梦。(Wǒ zuó wǎn zuò le gè qí guài de mèng.) — I had a strange dream last night.

## Notes

梦 simplified from 夢. Contains wood/tree 木, ancient Chinese believed dreams occurred in darkness under trees.
