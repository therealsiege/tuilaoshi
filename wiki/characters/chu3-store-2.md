# 储 To Store

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: to store, to save
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 储蓄 (chǔ xù) — savings
- 储存 (chǔ cún) — to store

## Example Sentences

- 你应该多储蓄一些钱。(Nǐ yīng gāi duō chǔ xù yì xiē qián.) — You should save more money.

## Notes

储 simplified from 儲. Person 亻 plus 诸 phonetic component.
