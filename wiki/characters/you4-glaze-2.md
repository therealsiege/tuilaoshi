# 釉 Glaze

- **Character**: 釉
- **Pinyin**: yòu
- **Meaning**: glaze
- **Radical**: 釆(distinguish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 釉料 (yòu liào) — glaze material
- 上釉 (shàng yòu) — apply glaze

## Example Sentences

- 这个花瓶的釉色非常漂亮。(Zhège huāpíng de yòusè fēicháng piàoliang.) — The glaze color of this vase is very beautiful.

## Notes

釉 is the glaze applied to ceramics before firing. Different glazes create different colors and textures.
