# 材 Material

- **Character**: 材
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: material, timber, talent
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 材料 (cái liào) — material
- 木材 (mù cái) — timber

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要什么材料？(Wǒ men xū yào shén me cái liào?) — What materials do we need?

## Notes

材 originally meant timber. Extended to any material or even talent (人才).
