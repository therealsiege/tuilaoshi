# 杆 Pole

- **Character**: 杆
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: pole, rod, bar
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 旗杆 (qí gān) — flagpole
- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing

## Example Sentences

- 广场上立着一根旗杆。(Guǎng chǎng shàng lì zhe yì gēn qí gān.) — A flagpole stands in the square.

## Notes

杆 has wood 木 with 干 (dry/stem), a wooden pole or rod.
