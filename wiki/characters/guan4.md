# 贯 To Pierce Through

- **Character**: 贯
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: to pierce through, native place
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贯彻 (guàn chè) — to implement
- 籍贯 (jí guàn) — ancestral home

## Example Sentences

- 我们要贯彻这个政策。(Wǒ men yào guàn chè zhè gè zhèng cè.) — We must implement this policy.

## Notes

贯 simplified from 貫. Originally showed shells strung together, representing continuity and connection.
