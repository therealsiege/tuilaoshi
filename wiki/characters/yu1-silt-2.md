# 淤 Silt

- **Character**: 淤
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: silt, to silt up
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淤泥 (yū ní) — silt, mud
- 淤积 (yū jī) — to silt up

## Example Sentences

- 河底有很多淤泥。(Hé dǐ yǒu hěn duō yū ní.) — There is a lot of silt at the river bottom.

## Notes

淤 has water 氵 with 於 (at/in), sediment accumulating in water.
