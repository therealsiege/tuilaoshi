# 存 To exist, to store, to deposit

- **Character**: 存
- **Pinyin**: cún
- **Meaning**: to exist, to store, to deposit
- **Radical**: 子(child)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 存在 (cún zài) — to exist, existence
- 储存 (chǔ cún) — to store, to deposit

## Example Sentences

- 我要去银行存钱。(Wǒ yào qù yínháng cún qián.) — I need to go to the bank to deposit money.

## Notes

Common verb for存在 (existing) or depositing.
