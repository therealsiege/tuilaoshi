# 唱 To sing

- **Character**: 唱
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: to sing
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 唱歌 (chàng gē) — to sing (a song)
- 合唱 (hé chàng) — chorus, to chorus

## Example Sentences

- 她唱得很好听。(Tā chàng de hěn hǎotīng.) — She sings very beautifully.

## Notes

Most common verb for singing.
