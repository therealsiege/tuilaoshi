# 滴 Drop

- **Character**: 滴
- **Pinyin**: dī
- **Meaning**: drop, to drip
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 水滴 (shuǐ dī) — water drop
- 滴水 (dī shuǐ) — to drip

## Example Sentences

- 雨水一滴一滴地落下。(Yǔ shuǐ yì dī yì dī de luò xià.) — Raindrops fall one by one.

## Notes

滴 has water 氵 with 适 phonetic, small drops falling.
