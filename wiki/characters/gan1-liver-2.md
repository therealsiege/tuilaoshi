# 肝 Liver

- **Character**: 肝
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: liver
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肝脏 (gān zàng) — liver (organ)
- 肝炎 (gān yán) — hepatitis
- 肝癌 (gān ái) — liver cancer

## Example Sentences

- 他患有乙型肝炎。(Tā huàn yǒu yǐ xíng gān yán.) — He has hepatitis B.

## Notes

Vital organ for detoxification; important in traditional Chinese medicine theory.
