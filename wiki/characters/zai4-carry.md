# 载 To Carry

- **Character**: 载
- **Pinyin**: zài
- **Meaning**: to carry, to load
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 载重 (zài zhòng) — load capacity
- 下载 (xià zài) — to download

## Example Sentences

- 这辆卡车可以载很多货物。(Zhè liàng kǎ chē kě yǐ zài hěn duō huò wù.) — This truck can carry a lot of goods.

## Notes

载 simplified from 載. Shows vehicle 车 carrying loads, representing transportation and bearing weight.
