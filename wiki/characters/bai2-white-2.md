# 白 White

- **Character**: 白
- **Pinyin**: bái
- **Meaning**: white, blank, plain, in vain
- **Radical**: 白 (white)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 白色 (bái sè) — white color
- 明白 (míng bái) — to understand

## Example Sentences

- 你明白了吗？(Nǐ míng bái le ma?) — Do you understand?

## Notes

白 depicts sun just rising, when light is white. Also means 'in vain': 白费力气 (waste effort).
