# 塞 To Stuff

- **Character**: 塞
- **Pinyin**: sāi
- **Meaning**: to stuff, to squeeze in, to block
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 塞满 (sāi mǎn) — to cram full
- 堵塞 (dǔ sè) — to block

## Example Sentences

- 箱子里塞满了衣服。(Xiāng zi lǐ sāi mǎn le yī fu.) — The suitcase is crammed full of clothes.

## Notes

塞 earth 土 blocking passages, stuffing tightly.
