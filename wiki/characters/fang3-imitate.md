# 仿 To Imitate

- **Character**: 仿
- **Pinyin**: fǎng
- **Meaning**: to imitate, to copy, seemingly
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 模仿 (mó fǎng) — to imitate
- 仿佛 (fǎng fú) — to seem, as if

## Example Sentences

- 孩子喜欢模仿大人。(Hái zi xǐ huān mó fǎng dà rén.) — Children like to imitate adults.

## Notes

仿 shows person 亻 and 方 (direction/method), representing following another's way.
