# 囚 Prisoner

- **Character**: 囚
- **Pinyin**: qiú
- **Meaning**: prisoner, to imprison
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 囚犯 (qiú fàn) — prisoner
- 囚禁 (qiú jìn) — to imprison

## Example Sentences

- 囚犯试图越狱。(Qiú fàn shì tú yuè yù.) — The prisoner attempted to break out of jail.

## Notes

囚 shows a person 人 enclosed in a box 囗, literally depicting imprisonment.
