# 锢 Lock up, confine, solidify

- **Character**: 锢
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: lock up, confine, solidify
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 禁锢 (jìn gù) — imprison, confine
- 锢蔽 (gù bì) — block, obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 不要被旧思想禁锢。(Bù yào bèi jiù sī xiǎng jìn gù.) — Don't be confined by old thoughts.

## Notes

Imprisonment or mental confinement
