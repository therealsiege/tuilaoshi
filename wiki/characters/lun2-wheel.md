# 轮 Wheel, rotate, take turns

- **Character**: 轮
- **Pinyin**: lún
- **Meaning**: wheel, rotate, take turns
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 车轮 (chē lún) — wheel
- 齿轮 (chǐ lún) — gear

## Example Sentences

- 齿轮带动整个机械运转。(Chǐ lún dài dòng zhěng gè jī xiè yùn zhuǎn.) — Gears drive the entire machinery.
