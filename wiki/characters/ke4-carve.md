# 刻 Carve, engrave, quarter hour

- **Character**: 刻
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: carve, engrave, quarter hour
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — carve, engrave
- 刻苦 (kè kǔ) — hardworking, assiduous

## Example Sentences

- 他在石碑上刻下了自己的名字。(Tā zài shí bēi shàng kè xià le zì jǐ de míng zi.) — He carved his name on the stone tablet.

## Notes

Also means a quarter hour (15 minutes).
