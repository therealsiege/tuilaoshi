# 霜 Frost, cream

- **Character**: 霜
- **Pinyin**: shuāng
- **Meaning**: frost, cream
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 霜冻 (shuāng dòng) — frost, freeze
- 冰霜 (bīng shuāng) — ice and frost

## Example Sentences

- 秋天的早晨地上有霜。(Qiū tiān de zǎo chén dì shàng yǒu shuāng.) — There is frost on the ground on autumn mornings.

## Notes

Frozen water vapor on surfaces
