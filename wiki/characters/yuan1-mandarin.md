# 鸳 Mandarin Duck

- **Character**: 鸳
- **Pinyin**: yuān
- **Meaning**: mandarin duck (male)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 鸳鸯 (yuān yāng) — mandarin ducks
- 鸳鸯锅 (yuān yāng guō) — yin-yang hot pot

## Example Sentences

- 鸳鸯象征爱情。(Yuān yāng xiàng zhēng ài qíng.) — Mandarin ducks symbolize love.

## Notes

鸳 simplified from 鴛. Bird 鸟 with 夗 phonetic, symbol of faithful love.
