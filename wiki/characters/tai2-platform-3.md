# 台 Platform, stage

- **Character**: 台
- **Pinyin**: tái
- **Meaning**: platform, stage
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 平台 (píng tái) — platform
- 电脑台 (diàn nǎo tái) — computer desk

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个开放平台。(Zhè shì yī gè kāi fàng píng tái.) — This is an open platform.

## Notes

Used in 平台 (platform), 舞台 (stage), 台式电脑 (desktop computer)
