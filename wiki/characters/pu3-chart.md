# 谱 Chart

- **Character**: 谱
- **Pinyin**: pǔ
- **Meaning**: chart, manual, music score
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 乐谱 (yuè pǔ) — music score
- 歌谱 (gē pǔ) — song score

## Example Sentences

- 他正在看乐谱练习钢琴。(Tā zhèngzài kàn yuèpǔ liànxí gāngqín.) — He is practicing piano while reading the music score.

## Notes

谱 refers to charts, manuals, or music notation. Essential for learning and performing music.
