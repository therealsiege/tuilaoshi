# 木 Wood

- **Character**: 木
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: wood, tree, wooden
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 入木三分 (rù mù sān fēn) — penetrating insight
- 木头 (mù tou) — wood, log

## Example Sentences

- 他的评论入木三分。(Tā de pínglùn rù mù sān fēn.) — His commentary is penetratingly insightful.

## Notes

In 入木三分: the wood that calligrapher Wang Xizhi's ink penetrated three-tenths of an inch deep.
