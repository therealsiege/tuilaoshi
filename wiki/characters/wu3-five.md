# 五 Five

- **Character**: 五
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: five
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 五月 (wǔ yuè) — May
- 五十 (wǔ shí) — fifty

## Example Sentences

- 一只手有五个手指。(Yī zhī shǒu yǒu wǔ gè shǒu zhǐ.) — One hand has five fingers.

## Notes

五 originally depicted the five elements (五行: metal, wood, water, fire, earth) crossing between heaven and earth.
