# 波 Wave, ripple, fluctuation

- **Character**: 波
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: wave, ripple, fluctuation
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 波浪 (bō làng) — wave
- 风波 (fēng bō) — disturbance

## Example Sentences

- 海上起了大波浪。(Hǎi shàng qǐ le dà bō làng.) — Large waves arose on the sea.

## Notes

Water waves or metaphorical fluctuations
