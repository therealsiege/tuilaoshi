# 标 Mark, sign

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 鼠标 (shǔ biāo) — mouse
- 标签 (biāo qiān) — tag, label

## Example Sentences

- 这个标签很重要。(Zhè gè biāo qiān hěn zhòng yào.) — This tag is important.

## Notes

Used in 鼠标 (mouse), 标签 (tag), 坐标 (coordinate)
