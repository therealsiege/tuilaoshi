# 鉴 Mirror, inspect, example

- **Character**: 鉴
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: mirror, inspect, example
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鉴定 (jiàndìng) — authenticate, appraise
- 借鉴 (jièjiàn) — learn from, draw lessons from

## Example Sentences

- 我们要借鉴历史教训。(Wǒmen yào jièjiàn lìshǐ jiàoxùn.) — We should learn from historical lessons.

## Notes

Used for inspection, authentication, and learning from examples.
