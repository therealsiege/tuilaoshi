# 幅 Amplitude, width

- **Character**: 幅
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: amplitude, width
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 幅度 (fú dù) — amplitude, range
- 振幅 (zhèn fú) — amplitude

## Example Sentences

- 信号振幅很小。(Xìn hào zhèn fú hěn xiǎo.) — The signal amplitude is small.

## Notes

Used in 振幅 (amplitude), 幅度 (range), signal processing
