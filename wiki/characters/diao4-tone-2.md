# 调 Tone

- **Character**: 调
- **Pinyin**: diào
- **Meaning**: tone, melody, to transfer
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 曲调 (qǔ diào) — melody
- 音调 (yīn diào) — tone

## Example Sentences

- 这首歌的曲调很优美。(Zhè shǒu gē de qǔ diào hěn yōu měi.) — The melody of this song is very beautiful.

## Notes

调 (diào) means tone or melody; also read as tiáo meaning to adjust.
