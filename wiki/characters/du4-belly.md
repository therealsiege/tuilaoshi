# 肚 Belly

- **Character**: 肚
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: belly, stomach
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肚子 (dù zi) — belly, stomach
- 肚皮 (dù pí) — belly

## Example Sentences

- 我肚子疼。(Wǒ dù zi téng.) — My stomach hurts.

## Notes

肚 with flesh radical 月. 肚子 complete word for belly/stomach. 肚子饿 = hungry.
