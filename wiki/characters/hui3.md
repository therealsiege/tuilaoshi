# 毁 To Destroy

- **Character**: 毁
- **Pinyin**: huǐ
- **Meaning**: to destroy, to ruin
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 毁坏 (huǐ huài) — to destroy
- 销毁 (xiāo huǐ) — to destroy

## Example Sentences

- 大火毁坏了建筑。(Dà huǒ huǐ huài le jiàn zhù.) — The fire destroyed the building.

## Notes

毁 simplified from 毀. Shows weapon 殳 attacking 臼 (mortar), representing complete destruction.
