# 苗 Seedling

- **Character**: 苗
- **Pinyin**: miáo
- **Meaning**: seedling, sprout
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 禾苗 (hé miáo) — rice seedling
- 疫苗 (yì miáo) — vaccine

## Example Sentences

- 农民在种禾苗。(Nóng mín zài zhòng hé miáo.) — The farmers are planting rice seedlings.

## Notes

苗 combines grass 艹 and 田 (field), young plants in the field.
