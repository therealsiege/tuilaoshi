# 谋 Scheme, Plan, 计谋 Stratagem

- **Character**: 谋
- **Pinyin**: móu
- **Meaning**: scheme, plan, 计谋 stratagem
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 计谋 (jì móu) — stratagem, scheme
- 谋求 (móu qiú) — seek, try to get
- 阴谋 (yīn móu) — conspiracy, plot

## Example Sentences

- 他们正在谋求合作机会。(Tāmen zhèngzài móuqiú hézuò jīhuì.) — They are seeking cooperation opportunities.

## Notes

Can have negative connotations when meaning 'scheme' but neutral in 谋求 (seek). Often used in strategic planning contexts.
