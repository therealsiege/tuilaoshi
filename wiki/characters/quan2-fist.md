# 拳 Fist

- **Character**: 拳
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: fist, boxing
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 拳头 (quán tou) — fist
- 太极拳 (tài jí quán) — tai chi

## Example Sentences

- 他练太极拳。(Tā liàn tài jí quán.) — He practices tai chi.

## Notes

拳 has hand 手 below 卷 (to roll), closed hand forming a fist.
