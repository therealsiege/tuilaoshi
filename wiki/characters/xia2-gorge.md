# 峡 Gorge, strait, canyon

- **Character**: 峡
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: gorge, strait, canyon
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 海峡 (hǎi xiá) — strait, channel
- 峡谷 (xiá gǔ) — canyon, gorge

## Example Sentences

- 台湾海峡连接东海和南海。(Tái wān hǎi xiá lián jiē dōng hǎi hé nán hǎi.) — The Taiwan Strait connects the East China Sea and the South China Sea.

## Notes

Narrow passages between mountains or water
