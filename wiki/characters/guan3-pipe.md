# 管 Pipe

- **Character**: 管
- **Pinyin**: guǎn
- **Meaning**: pipe, tube, to manage
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 管乐 (guǎn yuè) — wind music
- 管弦乐 (guǎn xián yuè) — orchestral music

## Example Sentences

- 他演奏管乐器。(Tā yǎn zòu guǎn yuè qì.) — He plays wind instruments.

## Notes

管 refers to tubular wind instruments; also means to manage or control.
