# 缎 Satin

- **Character**: 缎
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: satin
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 缎子 (duàn zi) — satin
- 绫罗绸缎 (líng luó chóu duàn) — silks and satins

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着一件红色缎子旗袍。(Tā chuān zhe yī jiàn hóng sè duàn zi qí páo.) — She wore a red satin qipao.

## Notes

Fine silk fabric with glossy surface.
