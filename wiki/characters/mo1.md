# 摸 Touch

- **Character**: 摸
- **Pinyin**: mō
- **Meaning**: touch, feel
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摸索 (mōsuǒ) — grope, feel about
- 触摸 (chùmō) — touch

## Example Sentences

- 请不要摸这个展品。(Qǐng bùyào mō zhège zhǎnpǐn.) — Please don't touch this exhibit.

## Notes

Touching action. Hand radical. Common in warnings and exploration contexts. Sensory verb.
