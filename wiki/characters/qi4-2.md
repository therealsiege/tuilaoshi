# 契 契约, deed, agree

- **Character**: 契
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: 契约, deed, agree
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 契约 (qì yuē) — contract, deed
- 地契 (dì qì) — land deed

## Example Sentences

- 房契是房屋所有权的证明。(Fáng qì shì fáng wū suǒ yǒu quán de zhèng míng.) — The property deed is proof of house ownership.

## Notes

契机 means opportunity. 默契 means tacit understanding.
