# 牛 Ox

- **Character**: 牛
- **Pinyin**: niú
- **Meaning**: Ox constellation, ox
- **Radical**: 牛(ox)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 牛宿 (niú xiù) — Ox constellation
- 牵牛 (qiān niú) — Altair star

## Example Sentences

- 牛宿包含了著名的牵牛星。(Niú xiù bāohán le zhùmíng de qiānniú xīng.) — Ox constellation contains the famous Altair star.

## Notes

牛 means ox or cow. In astronomy, the Ox constellation, containing Altair from the Cowherd and Weaver Girl legend.
