# 笏 Court Tablet

- **Character**: 笏
- **Pinyin**: hù
- **Meaning**: tablet held at court
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 朝笏 (cháo hù) — court tablet
- 笏板 (hù bǎn) — tablet

## Example Sentences

- 大臣手持朝笏上朝。(Dà chén shǒu chí cháo hù shàng cháo.) — The minister held a court tablet while attending court.

## Notes

笏 has bamboo 竹 with 勿 (not), bamboo tablet for notes at court.
