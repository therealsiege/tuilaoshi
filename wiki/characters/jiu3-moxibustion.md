# 灸 Moxibustion, cauterize

- **Character**: 灸
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: moxibustion, cauterize
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 针灸 (zhēn jiǔ) — acupuncture and moxibustion
- 艾灸 (ài jiǔ) — moxibustion

## Example Sentences

- 针灸是中医的重要疗法。(Zhēn jiǔ shì zhōng yī de zhòng yào liáo fǎ.) — Acupuncture is an important treatment in Traditional Chinese Medicine.

## Notes

Key TCM therapy using heat from burning herbs
