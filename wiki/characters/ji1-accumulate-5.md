# 积 Accumulate

- **Character**: 积
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to accumulate
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 积分 (jī fēn) — points, score
- 积累 (jī lěi) — to accumulate

## Example Sentences

- 他们队积分最高。(Tāmen duì jīfēn zuì gāo.) — Their team has the highest points.

## Notes

Accumulating; points and scores
