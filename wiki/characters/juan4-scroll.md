# 卷 Scroll, volume, roll up

- **Character**: 卷
- **Pinyin**: juàn
- **Meaning**: scroll, volume, roll up
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 书卷 (shū juàn) — books, scrolls
- 卷宗 (juàn zōng) — file, dossier

## Example Sentences

- 他手不释卷，博览群书。(Tā shǒu bù shì juàn, bó lǎn qún shū.) — He always has a book in hand, reading widely.

## Notes

Also read as juǎn meaning to roll up
