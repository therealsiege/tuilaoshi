# 抽 To Draw Out

- **Character**: 抽
- **Pinyin**: chōu
- **Meaning**: to draw out, to pull, to smoke
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抽烟 (chōu yān) — to smoke
- 抽象 (chōu xiàng) — abstract

## Example Sentences

- 这里不能抽烟。(Zhè lǐ bù néng chōu yān.) — You can't smoke here.

## Notes

抽 means to draw/pull out. 抽烟 = smoke cigarettes, 抽签 = draw lots, 抽水 = pump water.
