# 窝 Nest, den, pit, harbor

- **Character**: 窝
- **Pinyin**: wō
- **Meaning**: nest, den, pit, harbor
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 窝藏 (wō cáng) — to harbor, hide
- 被窝 (bèi wō) — bed, quilt

## Example Sentences

- 小狗在被窝里睡觉。(Xiǎo gǒu zài bèi wō lǐ shuì jiào.) — The puppy is sleeping in the quilt.

## Notes

Means nest or cozy place. 窝火 means to feel vexed. 窝囊 means spineless or frustrating.
