# 滴 Drop

- **Character**: 滴
- **Pinyin**: dī
- **Meaning**: drop, drip
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 滴眼液 (dī yǎn yè) — eye drops
- 点滴 (diǎn dī) — IV drip
- 滴入 (dī rù) — to drip into

## Example Sentences

- 每天滴三次眼药水。(Měi tiān dī sān cì yǎn yào shuǐ.) — Use eye drops three times daily.

## Notes

Drop administration; eyes, ears, IV.
