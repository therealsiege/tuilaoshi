# 盖 To cover, lid, to build, to affix seal

- **Character**: 盖
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: to cover, lid, to build, to affix seal
- **Radical**: 皿(dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盖子 (gài zi) — lid, cover
- 覆盖 (fù gài) — to cover, to blanket

## Example Sentences

- 请把锅盖盖上。(Qǐng bǎ guō gài gài shang.) — Please put the lid on the pot.

## Notes

Versatile character for covering or building.
