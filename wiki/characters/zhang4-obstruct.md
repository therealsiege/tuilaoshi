# 障 Disorder

- **Character**: 障
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: obstruct, barrier
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle, disorder
- 故障 (gù zhàng) — malfunction
- 屏障 (píng zhàng) — barrier

## Example Sentences

- 学习障碍需要早期干预。(Xué xí zhàng ài xū yào zǎo qī gān yù.) — Learning disorders require early intervention.

## Notes

Blockage or impairment; disorder suffix.
