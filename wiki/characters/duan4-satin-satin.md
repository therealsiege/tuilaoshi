# 缎 Satin

- **Character**: 缎
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: satin
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 绸缎 (chóu duàn) — silk and satin
- 锦缎 (jǐn duàn) — brocade

## Example Sentences

- 这件旗袍是绸缎做的。(Zhè jiàn qí páo shì chóu duàn zuò de.) — This cheongsam is made of silk and satin.

## Notes

缎 simplified from 緞. Silk 纟 with 段 (section), fine woven fabric.
