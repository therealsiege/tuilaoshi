# 磨 Grind, polish, wear down

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: grind, polish, wear down
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 琢磨 (zhuó mó) — polish, refine
- 磨练 (mó liàn) — temper, steel

## Example Sentences

- 他的技艺是经过多年磨练的。(Tā de jì yì shì jīng guò duō nián mó liàn de.) — His skills were honed over many years.

## Notes

Also pronounced mò in some contexts.
