# 孢 Spore

- **Character**: 孢
- **Pinyin**: bāo
- **Meaning**: spore
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 孢子 (bāo zǐ) — spore
- 芽孢 (yá bāo) — spore

## Example Sentences

- 蕨类植物通过孢子繁殖。(Jué lèi zhí wù tōng guò bāo zǐ fán zhí.) — Ferns reproduce through spores.

## Notes

孢 has child 子 with 包 (to wrap), reproductive cell wrapped in shell.
