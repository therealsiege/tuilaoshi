# 狗 Dog

- **Character**: 狗
- **Pinyin**: gǒu
- **Meaning**: dog
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 小狗 (xiǎo gǒu) — puppy
- 狗肉 (gǒu ròu) — dog meat

## Example Sentences

- 这只狗很可爱。(Zhè zhī gǒu hěn kě ài.) — This dog is very cute.

## Notes

狗 with dog radical 犭. Dogs are 属狗 in Chinese zodiac, one of 12 animals.
