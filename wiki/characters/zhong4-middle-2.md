# 仲 Middle, second, arbitrate

- **Character**: 仲
- **Pinyin**: zhòng
- **Meaning**: middle, second, arbitrate
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 仲裁 (zhòngcái) — arbitration
- 仲夏 (zhòngxià) — midsummer

## Example Sentences

- 双方同意仲裁解决争议。(Shuāngfāng tóngyì zhòngcái jiějué zhēngyì.) — Both parties agreed to resolve the dispute through arbitration.

## Notes

Legal term for arbitration; also means middle or second.
