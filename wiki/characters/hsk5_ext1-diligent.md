# 勤 Diligent, Frequent, 勤奋 Diligent

- **Character**: 勤
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: diligent, frequent, 勤奋 diligent
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 勤奋 (qín fèn) — diligent, hardworking
- 勤劳 (qín láo) — industrious
- 殷勤 (yīn qín) — attentive

## Example Sentences

- 他是个勤奋的学生。(Tā shì gè qínfèn de xuéshēng.) — He is a diligent student.

## Notes

Core virtue character representing diligence and hard work. 勤奋 and 勤劳 are essential terms for describing work ethic.
