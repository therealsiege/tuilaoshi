# 孕 Pregnant, pregnancy

- **Character**: 孕
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: pregnant, pregnancy
- **Radical**: 子 (child)

## Compounds

- 怀孕 (huái yùn) — pregnant, pregnancy
