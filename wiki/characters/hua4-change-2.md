# 化 To Transform

- **Character**: 化
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to change, to transform, -化ize
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 变化 (biàn huà) — change
- 文化 (wén huà) — culture

## Example Sentences

- 天气变化很快。(Tiān qì biàn huà hěn kuài.) — The weather changes quickly.

## Notes

化 shows a person transforming, used as a suffix for -化ization or transformation.
