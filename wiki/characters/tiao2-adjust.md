# 调 Adjust, mediate, transfer

- **Character**: 调
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: adjust, mediate, transfer
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 调解 (tiáojiě) — mediate, reconcile
- 调查 (diàochá) — investigate

## Example Sentences

- 法官调解双方纠纷。(Fǎguān tiáojiě shuāngfāng jiūfēn.) — The judge mediated the dispute between both parties.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: tiáo (adjust, mediate) and diào (transfer, tune).
