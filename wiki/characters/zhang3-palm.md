# 掌 Palm

- **Character**: 掌
- **Pinyin**: zhǎng
- **Meaning**: palm, to control
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 手掌 (shǒu zhǎng) — palm
- 掌握 (zhǎng wò) — to grasp

## Example Sentences

- 他用手掌击打。(Tā yòng shǒu zhǎng jī dǎ.) — He strikes with his palm.

## Notes

掌 has hand 手 below 尚 (esteem), palm of the hand.
