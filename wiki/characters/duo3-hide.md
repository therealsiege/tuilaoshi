# 躲 To hide, avoid

- **Character**: 躲
- **Pinyin**: duǒ
- **Meaning**: to hide, avoid
- **Radical**: 身 (body)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 躲藏 (duǒ cáng) — to hide
- 躲避 (duǒ bì) — to hide, take shelter

## Example Sentences

- 他躲在门后。(Tā duǒ zài mén hòu.) — He hid behind the door.

## Notes

To hide or take shelter from something.
