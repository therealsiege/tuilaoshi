# 章 Chapter, Seal, 文章 Article

- **Character**: 章
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: chapter, seal, 文章 article
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 文章 (wén zhāng) — article, essay
- 章节 (zhāng jié) — chapters and sections
- 规章 (guī zhāng) — rules and regulations

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章写得很好。(Zhè piān wénzhāng xiě de hěn hǎo.) — This article is very well written.

## Notes

Common in both academic writing (章节 - chapters) and administrative contexts (规章 - regulations, 印章 - seal).
