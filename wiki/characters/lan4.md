# 烂 Rotten

- **Character**: 烂
- **Pinyin**: làn
- **Meaning**: rotten, messy, brilliant
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 灿烂 (càn làn) — brilliant, splendid
- 腐烂 (fǔ làn) — to rot, decay

## Example Sentences

- 这个苹果烂了。(Zhège píngguǒ làn le.) — This apple is rotten.

## Notes

烂 combines fire 火 with 兰 (orchid), representing burning until烂 or overripe. Has paradoxical meanings of both brilliant and rotten.
