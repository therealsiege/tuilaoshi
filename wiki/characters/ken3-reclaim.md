# 垦 Reclaim land, cultivate

- **Character**: 垦
- **Pinyin**: kěn
- **Meaning**: reclaim land, cultivate
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 开垦 (kāi kěn) — to reclaim (land)
- 垦荒 (kěn huāng) — to open up wasteland

## Example Sentences

- 他们开垦了大片荒地。(Tā men kāi kěn le dà piàn huāng dì.) — They reclaimed large areas of wasteland.

## Notes

Specifically refers to opening up new land for cultivation. 农垦 means land reclamation.
