# 浅 Shallow

- **Character**: 浅
- **Pinyin**: qiǎn
- **Meaning**: shallow
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 浅层 (qiǎn céng) — shallow layer
- 深浅 (shēn qiǎn) — depth

## Example Sentences

- 浅层网络比较简单。(Qiǎn céng wǎng luò bǐ jiào jiǎn dān.) — Shallow networks are relatively simple.

## Notes

Used in 浅层 (shallow), opposite of 深 (deep), neural network layers
