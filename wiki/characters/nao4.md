# 闹 Noisy, make noise, cause trouble

- **Character**: 闹
- **Pinyin**: nào
- **Meaning**: noisy, make noise, cause trouble
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 热闹 (rè nao) — lively, bustling
- 闹钟 (nào zhōng) — alarm clock

## Example Sentences

- 街上很热闹。(Jiē shàng hěn rè nao.) — The street is very lively.

## Notes

Noise, commotion, and liveliness
