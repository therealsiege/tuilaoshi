# 灯 Lamp

- **Character**: 灯
- **Pinyin**: dēng
- **Meaning**: lamp, light
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 电灯 (diàn dēng) — electric light
- 开灯 (kāi dēng) — turn on light

## Example Sentences

- 请开灯。(Qǐng kāi dēng.) — Please turn on the light.

## Notes

灯 simplified from 燈, with fire radical 火. Lights originally from fire/flame.
