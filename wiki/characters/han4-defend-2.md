# 捍 Defend, guard, protect

- **Character**: 捍
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: defend, guard, protect
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捍卫 (hàn wèi) — defend, safeguard
- 捍御 (hàn yù) — defend against

## Example Sentences

- 捍卫祖国的尊严。(Hàn wèi zǔ guó de zūn yán.) — Defend the dignity of the motherland.

## Notes

Strong defense and protection
