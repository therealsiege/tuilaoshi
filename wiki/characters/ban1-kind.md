# 般 Kind

- **Character**: 般
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: sort, kind, like
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 一般 (yī bān) — ordinary, generally
- 百般 (bǎi bān) — in every way

## Example Sentences

- 一般来说，他很准时。(Yī bān lái shuō, tā hěn zhǔn shí.) — Generally speaking, he is punctual.

## Notes

一般 is extremely common meaning 'ordinary' or 'generally.'
