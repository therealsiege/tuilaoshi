# 因 Because

- **Character**: 因
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: because, cause, reason
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 因为 (yīn wèi) — because
- 原因 (yuán yīn) — reason

## Example Sentences

- 因为下雨，所以我没去。(Yīn wèi xià yǔ, suǒ yǐ wǒ méi qù.) — Because it rained, I didn't go.

## Notes

因为...所以... is the standard because-therefore construction. Unlike English, both parts are often included.
