# 马 Horse

- **Character**: 马
- **Pinyin**: mǎ
- **Meaning**: horse
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 马上 (mǎ shàng) — immediately
- 马路 (mǎ lù) — road

## Example Sentences

- 我马上来。(Wǒ mǎ shàng lái.) — I'm coming right away.

## Notes

马 is simplified from 馬. 马上 literally means 'on horseback' but now means 'immediately.' Also a common surname.
