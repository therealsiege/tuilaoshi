# 剧 Drama

- **Character**: 剧
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: drama, severe
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 戏剧 (xì jù) — drama
- 剧本 (jù běn) — script

## Example Sentences

- 这部戏剧很感人。(Zhè bù xì jù hěn gǎn rén.) — This drama is very moving.

## Notes

剧 means drama or severe; used in theatrical and entertainment contexts.
