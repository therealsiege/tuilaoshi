# 汇 Remit, converge, exchange rate

- **Character**: 汇
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: remit, converge, exchange rate
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汇率 (huìlǜ) — exchange rate
- 汇款 (huìkuǎn) — remit money

## Example Sentences

- 今天的汇率是多少？(Jīntiān de huìlǜ shì duōshao?) — What is today's exchange rate?

## Notes

Key financial term for exchange rates and remittances.
