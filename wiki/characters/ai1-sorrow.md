# 哀 Sorrow

- **Character**: 哀
- **Pinyin**: āi
- **Meaning**: sorrow, grief, pity
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 悲哀 (bēi āi) — sorrowful
- 哀悼 (āi dào) — to mourn

## Example Sentences

- 大家为他感到悲哀。(Dà jiā wèi tā gǎn dào bēi āi.) — Everyone felt sorrow for him.

## Notes

哀 expresses deep grief. 喜怒哀乐 = joy, anger, sorrow, happiness (the four emotions).
