# 傍 Near

- **Character**: 傍
- **Pinyin**: bàng
- **Meaning**: near, beside, approaching
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 傍晚 (bàng wǎn) — dusk, evening
- 依傍 (yī bàng) — to rely on

## Example Sentences

- 傍晚的风景很美。(Bàng wǎn de fēng jǐng hěn měi.) — The evening scenery is beautiful.

## Notes

傍晚 means dusk or early evening.
