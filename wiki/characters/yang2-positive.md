# 阳 Positive, yang

- **Character**: 阳
- **Pinyin**: yáng
- **Meaning**: positive, yang
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阳极 (yáng jí) — anode, positive pole
- 太阳 (tài yáng) — sun

## Example Sentences

- 连接阳极端子。(Lián jiē yáng jí duān zǐ.) — Connect the positive terminal.

## Notes

Used in 阳极 (anode), 正阳 (positive), electrical polarity
