# 稠 Thick

- **Character**: 稠
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: thick, dense, viscous
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 稠密 (chóu mì) — dense
- 浓稠 (nóng chóu) — thick (liquid)

## Example Sentences

- 这个粥太稠了。(Zhè ge zhōu tài chóu le.) — This porridge is too thick.

## Notes

稠 combines grain 禾 and 周 (cycle/thorough), grain packed densely.
