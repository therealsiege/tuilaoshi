# 行 To Walk

- **Character**: 行
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: to walk, semi-cursive script
- **Radical**: 行 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 行书 (xíng shū) — running script
- 行走 (xíng zǒu) — to walk

## Example Sentences

- 行书介于楷书和草书之间。(Xíng shū jiè yú kǎi shū hé cǎo shū zhī jiān.) — Running script is between regular and cursive script.

## Notes

行 depicts a crossroads, walking between places, semi-cursive style.
