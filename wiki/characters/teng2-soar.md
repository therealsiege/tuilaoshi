# 腾 To soar, to gallop, to make room

- **Character**: 腾
- **Pinyin**: téng
- **Meaning**: to soar, to gallop, to make room
- **Radical**: 月(moon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 奔腾 (bēn téng) — to gallop, to surge
- 腾空 (téng kōng) — to soar, to fly

## Example Sentences

- 老鹰在天空中腾飞。(Lǎoyīng zài tiānkōng zhōng téngfēi.) — The eagle soars in the sky.

## Notes

Can mean to soar, make room, or rise rapidly.
