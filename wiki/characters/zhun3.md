# 准 — Allow / Standard

- **Character**: 准
- **Pinyin**: zhǔn
- **Meaning**: to allow, to permit; standard, accurate; quasi-
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 准备 (zhǔnbèi) — to prepare, preparation
- 准时 (zhǔnshí) — on time, punctual
- 准确 (zhǔnquè) — accurate, precise
- 批准 (pīzhǔn) — to approve, to ratify
- 标准 (biāozhǔn) — standard, criterion
- 不准 (bùzhǔn) — not allowed; inaccurate

## Example Sentences

- 你准备好了吗？(Nǐ zhǔnbèi hǎo le ma?) — Are you ready?
- 他每天都准时上课。(Tā měitiān dōu zhǔnshí shàngkè.) — He is on time for class every day.
- 这里不准吸烟。(Zhèlǐ bùzhǔn xīyān.) — Smoking is not allowed here.

## Notes

准 (simplified from 準) contains the ice radical 冫, which in this case is a phonetic borrowing rather than semantically meaningful. The character originally related to water levels (a level water surface being "standard"), from which meanings of "accurate, standard" developed. The meaning "to allow/permit" is a further extension. 准备 (to prepare) is one of the highest-frequency compounds with this character and appears constantly in Integrated Chinese. The pattern V + 好了 after 准备 (准备好了) means "finished preparing / ready."
