# 蹲 To Squat

- **Character**: 蹲
- **Pinyin**: dūn
- **Meaning**: to squat, to crouch
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 蹲下 (dūn xià) — to squat down
- 蹲着 (dūn zhe) — squatting

## Example Sentences

- 他蹲在地上。(Tā dūn zài dì shàng.) — He is squatting on the ground.

## Notes

蹲 uses foot radical 足 with 尊 (honorable), representing a respectful waiting posture in traditional culture.
