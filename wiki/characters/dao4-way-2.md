# 道 Way, path, Taoism

- **Character**: 道
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: way, path, Taoism
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 道教 (dào jiào) — Taoism
- 道德 (dào dé) — morality, ethics

## Example Sentences

- 道家讲究自然无为。(Dào jiā jiǎng jiù zì rán wú wéi.) — Taoism emphasizes naturalness and non-action.

## Notes

Core concept in Chinese philosophy, the fundamental way of the universe
