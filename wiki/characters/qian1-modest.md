# 谦 Modest

- **Character**: 谦
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: modest, humble
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 谦虚 (qiān xū) — modest
- 谦逊 (qiān xùn) — humble

## Example Sentences

- 他是个很谦虚的人。(Tā shì gè hěn qiān xū de rén.) — He is a very modest person.

## Notes

谦 combines speech 讠 and 兼 (concurrent), suggesting speaking of oneself in diminished terms while acknowledging others.
