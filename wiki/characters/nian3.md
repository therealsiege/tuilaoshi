# 碾 To Grind

- **Character**: 碾
- **Pinyin**: niǎn
- **Meaning**: to grind, to roll over
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 碾压 (niǎn yā) — to crush, to roll over
- 石碾 (shí niǎn) — stone roller

## Example Sentences

- 用石磨碾碎谷物。(Yòng shí mò niǎn suì gǔ wù.) — Use a stone mill to grind grain.

## Notes

碾 uses stone radical 石, specifically referring to grinding with a roller or crushing by rolling over.
