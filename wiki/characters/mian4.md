# 面 Face

- **Character**: 面
- **Pinyin**: miàn
- **Meaning**: face, surface, side, aspect, noodles
- **Radical**: 面 (face)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 方面 (fāng miàn) — aspect
- 面条 (miàn tiáo) — noodles

## Example Sentences

- 从各方面来看都很好。(Cóng gè fāng miàn lái kàn dōu hěn hǎo.) — It looks good from all aspects.

## Notes

面 depicts a face, extended to surface, side, aspect. Also means flour/noodles (面条).
