# 泡 Bubble

- **Character**: 泡
- **Pinyin**: pào
- **Meaning**: bubble, foam, to soak
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泡沫 (pào mò) — foam
- 浸泡 (jìn pào) — to soak

## Example Sentences

- 水里有很多泡沫。(Shuǐ lǐ yǒu hěn duō pào mò.) — There are many bubbles in the water.

## Notes

泡 has water 氵 with 包 (to wrap), bubble enclosing air.
