# 耀 To Shine

- **Character**: 耀
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: to shine, dazzle
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 闪耀 (shǎn yào) — to shine, sparkle
- 照耀 (zhào yào) — to shine, illuminate

## Example Sentences

- 星星在夜空中闪耀。(Xīngxing zài yèkōng zhōng shǎnyào.) — Stars shine in the night sky.

## Notes

耀 combines light 光 and feathers 羽, representing the dazzling display like brilliant plumage.
