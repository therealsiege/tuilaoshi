# 襟 Lapel, front of garment

- **Character**: 襟
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: lapel, front of garment
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 胸襟 (xiōng jīn) — breadth of mind
- 对襟 (duì jīn) — buttoned in front

## Example Sentences

- 他胸襟开阔，不计小节。(Tā xiōng jīn kāi kuò, bù jì xiǎo jié.) — He has a broad mind and doesn't mind trifles.

## Notes

Front of clothing or metaphor for mindset
