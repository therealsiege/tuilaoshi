# 绘 To Draw

- **Character**: 绘
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: to draw, to paint
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绘画 (huì huà) — painting, drawing
- 描绘 (miáo huì) — to depict

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢绘画。(Tā xǐ huān huì huà.) — She likes painting.

## Notes

绘 simplified from 繪. Silk radical 纟 originally referring to embroidering colorful patterns.
