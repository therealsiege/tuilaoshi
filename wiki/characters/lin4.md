# 赁 Rent, hire

- **Character**: 赁
- **Pinyin**: lìn
- **Meaning**: rent, hire
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 租赁 (zū lìn) — lease, rent
- 赁用 (lìn yòng) — hire, rent

## Example Sentences

- 租赁合同期限三年。(Zū lìn hé tong qī xiàn sān nián.) — The lease contract term is three years.

## Notes

融资租赁 is financial leasing. More formal than 租.
