# 稷 Millet, grain god

- **Character**: 稷
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: millet, grain god
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 社稷 (shè jì) — state, nation
- 稷神 (jì shén) — grain deity

## Example Sentences

- 社稷代表国家和人民。(Shè jì dài biǎo guó jiā hé rén mín.) — Shèjì represents the state and the people.

## Notes

In 社稷, represents the altar of grain god, metonym for state
