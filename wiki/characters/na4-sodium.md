# 钠 Sodium

- **Character**: 钠
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: sodium
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钠盐 (nà yán) — sodium salt
- 氯化钠 (lǜ huà nà) — sodium chloride, salt

## Example Sentences

- 食盐的主要成分是氯化钠。(Shí yán de zhǔ yào chéng fèn shì lǜ huà nà.) — The main component of table salt is sodium chloride.

## Notes

Chemical element, essential electrolyte
