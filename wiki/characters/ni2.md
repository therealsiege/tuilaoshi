# 泥 Mud

- **Character**: 泥
- **Pinyin**: ní
- **Meaning**: mud, paste, mashed
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泥土 (ní tǔ) — mud, soil
- 水泥 (shuǐ ní) — cement

## Example Sentences

- 雨后地上都是泥。(Yǔ hòu dì shàng dōu shì ní.) — After the rain the ground is all muddy.

## Notes

泥 means mud or paste. Used in水泥 (cement), 泥土 (mud), 拘泥 (to stick rigidly to).
