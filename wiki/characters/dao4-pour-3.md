# 倒 To pour, to fall

- **Character**: 倒
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to pour, to fall
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倒水 (dào shuǐ) — to pour water
- 倒下 (dǎo xià) — to fall down

## Example Sentences

- 请给我倒杯茶。(Qǐng gěi wǒ dào bēi chá.) — Please pour me a cup of tea.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: dào (pour) and dǎo (fall, reverse). Person radical.
