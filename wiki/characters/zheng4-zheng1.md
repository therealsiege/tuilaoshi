# 正 Correct

- **Character**: 正
- **Pinyin**: zhèng/zhēng
- **Meaning**: correct, proper
- **Radical**: 止 (stop)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 矫正 (jiǎo zhèng) — to correct, rectify
- 纠正 (jiū zhèng) — to correct
- 正常 (zhèng cháng) — normal

## Example Sentences

- 身体指标都正常。(Shēn tǐ zhǐ biāo dōu zhèng cháng.) — All body indicators are normal.

## Notes

Normalcy and correction; medical standard.
