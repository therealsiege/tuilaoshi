# 楼 Building

- **Character**: 楼
- **Pinyin**: lóu
- **Meaning**: building, floor
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 楼层 (lóu céng) — floor (of building)
- 楼房 (lóu fáng) — multi-story building

## Example Sentences

- 办公室在三楼。(Bàn gōng shì zài sān lóu.) — The office is on the third floor.

## Notes

楼 with wood radical originally meant wooden楼 multi-story building, now for all tall buildings.
