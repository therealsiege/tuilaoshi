# 肯 To Agree

- **Character**: 肯
- **Pinyin**: kěn
- **Meaning**: to agree, to consent, to be willing
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肯定 (kěn dìng) — to affirm, definitely
- 不肯 (bù kěn) — to refuse

## Example Sentences

- 他肯定会来。(Tā kěn dìng huì lái.) — He will definitely come.

## Notes

肯 originally meant肉en meat off bones (willing effort), now means willing or affirmative. 肯定 is very common.
