# 级 Level, grade

- **Character**: 级
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: level, grade
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 升级 (shēng jí) — upgrade
- 高级 (gāo jí) — advanced

## Example Sentences

- 这是高级功能。(Zhè shì gāo jí gōng néng.) — This is an advanced feature.

## Notes

Used in 升级 (upgrade), 等级 (level), 高级 (advanced)
