# 撰 Compose, write

- **Character**: 撰
- **Pinyin**: zhuàn
- **Meaning**: compose, write
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撰写 (zhuàn xiě) — to write, compose
- 编撰 (biān zhuàn) — to compile and write

## Example Sentences

- 他撰写了一部关于中国历史的著作。(Tā zhuàn xiě le yī bù guān yú Zhōng guó lì shǐ de zhù zuò.) — He wrote a work about Chinese history.

## Notes

Formal term for writing, often used for scholarly or official documents. More literary than 写.
