# 及 Reach, and, in time

- **Character**: 及
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: reach, and, in time
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 及时 (jíshí) — in time, timely
- 涉及 (shèjí) — involve, relate to

## Example Sentences

- 请及时完成作业。(Qǐng jíshí wánchéng zuòyè.) — Please complete the homework in time.

## Notes

Means reaching, connecting, or 'and' in formal contexts.
