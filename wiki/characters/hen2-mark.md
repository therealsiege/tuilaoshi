# 痕 Mark, trace, scar

- **Character**: 痕
- **Pinyin**: hén
- **Meaning**: mark, trace, scar
- **Radical**: 疒 (illness)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 痕迹 (hén jì) — trace, mark
- 伤痕 (shāng hén) — scar, trauma

## Example Sentences

- 墙上还留有地震的痕迹。(Qiáng shàng hái liú yǒu dì zhèn de hén jì.) — Traces of the earthquake still remain on the wall.

## Notes

Can be physical marks or metaphorical traces.
