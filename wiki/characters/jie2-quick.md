# 捷 Quick

- **Character**: 捷
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: quick, nimble, victory
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 敏捷 (mǐn jié) — agile, quick
- 快捷 (kuài jié) — fast and convenient

## Example Sentences

- 他动作敏捷。(Tā dòng zuò mǐn jié.) — His movements are agile.

## Notes

捷 shows hand 扌 and 疌 (levels/tiers), representing quick achievement or victory.
