# 绝 To Cut Off

- **Character**: 绝
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to cut off, absolute, extremely
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绝对 (jué duì) — absolute
- 拒绝 (jù jué) — to refuse

## Example Sentences

- 这绝对不可能。(Zhè jué duì bù kě néng.) — This is absolutely impossible.

## Notes

绝 simplified from 絕. Silk thread 纟 being cut, representing severance or extreme degree.
