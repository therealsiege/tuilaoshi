# 义 Righteousness, justice

- **Character**: 义
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: righteousness, justice
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 正义 (zhèng yì) — justice, righteousness
- 义务 (yì wù) — duty, obligation

## Example Sentences

- 见义勇为是中华民族的传统美德。(Jiàn yì yǒng wéi shì Zhōnghuá mínzú de chuántǒng měidé.) — Acting bravely for a just cause is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation.

## Notes

Represents righteousness and moral correctness. In traditional Chinese ethics, one of the five cardinal virtues.
