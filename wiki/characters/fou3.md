# 否 Not

- **Character**: 否
- **Pinyin**: fǒu
- **Meaning**: to negate,否t, whether or not
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 是否 (shì fǒu) — whether or not
- 否则 (fǒu zé) — otherwise

## Example Sentences

- 你是否同意这个计划？(Nǐ shì fǒu tóng yì zhè ge jì huà?) — Do you agree with this plan or not?

## Notes

否 represents negation. 是否 (whether or not) and 否则 (否herwise) are extremely common.
