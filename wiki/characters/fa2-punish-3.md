# 罚 To Fine

- **Character**: 罚
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: to punish, to fine
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 罚款 (fá kuǎn) — fine
- 处罚 (chǔ fá) — to penalize

## Example Sentences

- 违规停车要罚款。(Wéi guī tíng chē yào fá kuǎn.) — Illegal parking results in a fine.

## Notes

罚 simplified from 罰. Net 罒 catching the wrong 詈 (scold).
