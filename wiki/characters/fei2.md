# 肥 Fat

- **Character**: 肥
- **Pinyin**: féi
- **Meaning**: fat, fertile
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肥胖 (féi pàng) — obese, fat
- 肥料 (féi liào) — fertilizer

## Example Sentences

- 这块肉太肥了。(Zhè kuài ròu tài féi le.) — This meat is too fatty.

## Notes

肥 combines meat 月 with 巴 (to hope for), originally representing well-fed livestock.
