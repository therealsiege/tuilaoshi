# 晰 Clear, distinct

- **Character**: 晰
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: clear, distinct
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 清晰 (qīng xī) — clear, distinct
- 明晰 (míng xī) — clear, explicit

## Example Sentences

- 照片很清晰。(Zhàopiàn hěn qīngxī.) — The photo is very clear.

## Notes

Clarity and distinctness, used in compound 清晰.
