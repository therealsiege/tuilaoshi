# 缠 To Wind

- **Character**: 缠
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: to wind, to entangle
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 缠绕 (chán rào) — to twine, to wind
- 纠缠 (jiū chán) — to pester

## Example Sentences

- 藤蔓缠绕着树干。(Téng màn chán rào zhe shù gàn.) — Vines wind around the tree trunk.

## Notes

缠 simplified from 纏. Silk radical 纟 representing binding or wrapping tightly.
