# 消 To Disappear

- **Character**: 消
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: to消消消, to spend, to need
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 消息 (xiāo xi) — news, information
- 消费 (xiāo fèi) — to consume

## Example Sentences

- 你听到这个消息了吗？(Nǐ tīng dào zhè ge xiāo xi le ma?) — Did you hear this news?

## Notes

消 means to消tinguish, vanish, or spend. Common in 消息 (news), 消费 (consumption), 取消 (cancel).
