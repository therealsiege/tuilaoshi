# 铜 Copper, Bronze, 青铜 Bronze

- **Character**: 铜
- **Pinyin**: tóng
- **Meaning**: copper, bronze, 青铜 bronze
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 青铜 (qīng tóng) — bronze
- 铜矿 (tóng kuàng) — copper mine
- 铜牌 (tóng pái) — bronze medal

## Example Sentences

- 他在奥运会上获得了铜牌。(Tā zài Àoyùnhuì shàng huòdé le tóngpái.) — He won a bronze medal at the Olympics.

## Notes

Metal element copper/bronze. 青铜 (bronze) is historical significance, 铜牌 (bronze medal) in sports competitions.
