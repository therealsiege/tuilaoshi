# 然 So, like that, right

- **Character**: 然
- **Pinyin**: rán
- **Meaning**: so, like that, right
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 当然 (dāng rán) — of course
- 自然 (zì rán) — natural

## Example Sentences

- 这是自然而然的结果。(Zhè shì zì rán ér rán de jié guǒ.) — This is a natural result.

## Notes

Affirmative particle and suffix meaning in that manner
