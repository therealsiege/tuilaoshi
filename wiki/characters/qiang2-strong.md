# 强 Strong

- **Character**: 强
- **Pinyin**: qiáng
- **Meaning**: strong, powerful, to强化
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 强大 (qiáng dà) — powerful
- 加强 (jiā qiáng) — to strengthen

## Example Sentences

- 我们要加强学习。(Wǒ men yào jiā qiáng xué xí.) — We need to strengthen our studies.

## Notes

强 is simplified from 強. Also read as qiǎng (to force) or jiàng (stubborn).
