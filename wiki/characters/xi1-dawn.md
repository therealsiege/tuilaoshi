# 曦 Dawn

- **Character**: 曦
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: dawn, morning sun
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 晨曦 (chén xī) — dawn
- 曦光 (xī guāng) — morning light

## Example Sentences

- 晨曦中的城市很美。(Chén xī zhōng de chéng shì hěn měi.) — The city is beautiful in the dawn light.

## Notes

曦 has sun 日 with 羲 phonetic, first light of day.
