# 聘 To Hire

- **Character**: 聘
- **Pinyin**: pìn
- **Meaning**: to hire, to engage
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 聘请 (pìn qǐng) — to engage, to hire
- 招聘 (zhāo pìn) — to recruit

## Example Sentences

- 学校招聘新老师。(Xué xiào zhāo pìn xīn lǎo shī.) — The school is recruiting new teachers.

## Notes

聘 combines ear 耳 and 甹 (phonetic).
