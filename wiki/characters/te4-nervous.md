# 忑 Uneasy

- **Character**: 忑
- **Pinyin**: tè
- **Meaning**: nervous, uneasy
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忐忑不安 (tǎn tè bù ān) — uneasy, anxious
- 忐忑 (tǎn tè) — nervous

## Example Sentences

- 她心里忐忑不安。(Tā xīnli tǎn tè bù ān.) — She felt uneasy inside.

## Notes

Used in 忐忑不安: heart going down (忑); always paired with 忐 to show a fluttering heart.
