# 朽 Rotten

- **Character**: 朽
- **Pinyin**: xiǔ
- **Meaning**: rotten, decayed
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 腐朽 (fǔ xiǔ) — rotten, decayed
- 不朽 (bù xiǔ) — immortal

## Example Sentences

- 木头腐朽了。(Mù tou fǔ xiǔ le.) — The wood has rotted.

## Notes

朽 shows wood 木 with丂 (breath gone), representing decayed or rotten wood.
