# 碎 Broken

- **Character**: 碎
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: broken, fragmented
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 粉碎 (fěn suì) — to smash to pieces
- 破碎 (pò suì) — broken

## Example Sentences

- 杯子摔碎了。(Bēi zi shuāi suì le.) — The cup shattered.

## Notes

碎 combines stone 石 and 卒 (soldier/finish), representing stones broken into fragments.
