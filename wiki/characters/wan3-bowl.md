# 碗 Bowl

- **Character**: 碗
- **Pinyin**: wǎn
- **Meaning**: bowl
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 饭碗 (fàn wǎn) — rice bowl
- 一碗 (yī wǎn) — one bowl

## Example Sentences

- 给我一碗饭。(Gěi wǒ yī wǎn fàn.) — Give me a bowl of rice.

## Notes

碗 uses stone radical 石 because bowls were originally made from stone/clay.
