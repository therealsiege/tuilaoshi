# 损 Damage

- **Character**: 损
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: damage, injury
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 损伤 (sǔn shāng) — injury, damage
- 损害 (sǔn hài) — to harm, damage
- 破损 (pò sǔn) — damaged, broken

## Example Sentences

- 长期吸烟损害肺部健康。(Cháng qī xī yān sǔn hài fèi bù jiàn kāng.) — Long-term smoking damages lung health.

## Notes

Harm or impairment; used for organs and tissues.
