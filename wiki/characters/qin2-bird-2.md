# 禽 Bird

- **Character**: 禽
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: bird, fowl
- **Radical**: 禸 (track)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 家禽 (jiā qín) — poultry
- 禽类 (qín lèi) — birds

## Example Sentences

- 农场养了很多家禽。(Nóng chǎng yǎng le hěn duō jiā qín.) — The farm raises a lot of poultry.

## Notes

禽 has capture radical with bird, domesticated birds.
