# 边 Side

- **Character**: 边
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: side, edge, border
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 旁边 (páng biān) — beside
- 里边 (lǐ biān) — inside

## Example Sentences

- 学校在邮局旁边。(Xué xiào zài yóu jú páng biān.) — The school is next to the post office.

## Notes

边 is simplified from 邊. It's a suffix for direction/location words: 上边, 下边, 左边, 右边, 前边, 后边.
