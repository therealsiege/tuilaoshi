# 抑 To Restrain

- **Character**: 抑
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: to抑制, to restrain, to depress
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抑制 (yì zhì) — to抑制
- 压抑 (yā yì) — depressed

## Example Sentences

- 要抑制通货膨胀。(Yào yì zhì tōng huò péng zhàng.) — Inflation must be抑制d.

## Notes

抑 shows hand pressing down, representing restraint or抑制.
