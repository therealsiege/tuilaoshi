# 兹 This, now

- **Character**: 兹
- **Pinyin**: zī
- **Meaning**: this, now
- **Radical**: 丷 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 赫兹 (hè zī) — hertz
- 兹事体大 (zī shì tǐ dà) — this is significant

## Example Sentences

- 处理器主频3千兆赫兹。(Chǔ lǐ qì zhǔ pín sān qiān zhào hè zī.) — The processor frequency is 3 gigahertz.

## Notes

Part of 赫兹 (hertz), frequency measurement
