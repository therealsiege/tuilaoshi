# 平 Flat

- **Character**: 平
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: flat, level, even
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 平安 (píng ān) — safe and sound
- 平仄 (píng zè) — level and oblique tones

## Example Sentences

- 古诗讲究平仄。(Gǔ shī jiǎng jiū píng zè.) — Classical poetry emphasizes tonal patterns.

## Notes

平 means flat or level; in poetry refers to level tones in tonal patterns.
