# 咬 To Bite

- **Character**: 咬
- **Pinyin**: yǎo
- **Meaning**: to bite, to gnaw
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 咬牙 (yǎo yá) — to clench teeth
- 叮咬 (dīng yǎo) — to sting, to bite

## Example Sentences

- 狗咬了他一口。(Gǒu yǎo le tā yī kǒu.) — The dog bit him.

## Notes

咬 combines mouth 口 and 交 (to交), representing teeth coming together to bite.
