# 举 To Raise

- **Character**: 举
- **Pinyin**: jǔ
- **Meaning**: to raise, to lift, to elect
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 举手 (jǔ shǒu) — to raise hand
- 举办 (jǔ bàn) — to hold (an event)

## Example Sentences

- 请举手回答。(Qǐng jǔ shǒu huí dá.) — Please raise your hand to answer.

## Notes

举 means to lift or hold up. 举办 = to host/organize, 举例 = to give an example.
