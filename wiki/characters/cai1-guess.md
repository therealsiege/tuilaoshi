# 猜 To Guess

- **Character**: 猜
- **Pinyin**: cāi
- **Meaning**: to guess
- **Radical**: 犭(animal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猜测 (cāi cè) — to speculate
- 猜谜 (cāi mí) — to solve riddles

## Example Sentences

- 你猜猜看。(Nǐ cāi cāi kàn.) — Take a guess.

## Notes

猜 is often reduplicated: 猜猜 (guess, guess). 猜谜语 = solve riddles.
