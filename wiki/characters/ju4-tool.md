# 具 Tool, utensil, possess

- **Character**: 具
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: tool, utensil, possess
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 工具 (gōng jù) — tool, instrument
- 器具 (qì jù) — utensil, apparatus

## Example Sentences

- 他具备专业技能。(Tā jù bèi zhuān yè jì néng.) — He possesses professional skills.
