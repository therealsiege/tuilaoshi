# 谈 To Talk

- **Character**: 谈
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: to talk, to discuss, to chat
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谈话 (tán huà) — to talk, conversation
- 谈判 (tán pàn) — negotiation

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要谈一谈。(Wǒ men xū yào tán yī tán.) — We need to talk.

## Notes

谈 is simplified from 談. The speech radical indicates verbal communication.
