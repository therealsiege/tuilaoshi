# 层 Floor

- **Character**: 层
- **Pinyin**: céng
- **Meaning**: layer, floor, story
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 楼层 (lóu céng) — floor (of building)
- 上层 (shàng céng) — upper layer

## Example Sentences

- 我住在三层。(Wǒ zhù zài sān céng.) — I live on the third floor.

## Notes

层 means layer or floor. 一层 = first floor. 层次 = level, gradation.
