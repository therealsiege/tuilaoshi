# 朦 Dim moonlight, hazy

- **Character**: 朦
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: dim moonlight, hazy
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 朦胧 (ménglóng) — hazy, dim
- 朦朦胧胧 (méngméng lónglóng) — dimly, hazily

## Example Sentences

- 月色朦胧。(Yuèsè ménglóng.) — The moonlight is hazy.

## Notes

Literary term for haziness or dim light, always paired with 胧.
