# 蚂 Ant, grasshopper

- **Character**: 蚂
- **Pinyin**: mǎ
- **Meaning**: ant, grasshopper
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 蚂蚁 (mǎ yǐ) — ant
- 蚂蚱 (mà zha) — grasshopper

## Example Sentences

- 地上有很多蚂蚁。(Dì shàng yǒu hěn duō mǎyǐ.) — There are many ants on the ground.

## Notes

Used in 蚂蚁 (ant) and 蚂蚱 (grasshopper). Insect radical.
