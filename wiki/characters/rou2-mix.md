# 糅 Mix, blend

- **Character**: 糅
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: mix, blend
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 糅合 (róuhé) — blend, mix
- 杂糅 (záróu) — mix together

## Example Sentences

- 文章糅合了多种观点。(Wénzhāng róuhé le duō zhǒng guāndiǎn.) — The article blends multiple viewpoints.

## Notes

Formal term for mixing or blending, often in abstract contexts.
