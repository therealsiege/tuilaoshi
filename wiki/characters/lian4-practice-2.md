# 练 To Practice

- **Character**: 练
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to practice, to train
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 练习 (liàn xí) — to practice
- 训练 (xùn liàn) — to train

## Example Sentences

- 我每天练习汉字。(Wǒ měi tiān liàn xí hàn zì.) — I practice Chinese characters every day.

## Notes

练 simplified from 練. Silk radical 纟 originally meaning to refine silk by repeated processing.
