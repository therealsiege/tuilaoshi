# 防 To Prevent

- **Character**: 防
- **Pinyin**: fáng
- **Meaning**: to prevent, to defend
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 预防 (yù fáng) — to prevent
- 防止 (fáng zhǐ) — to prevent

## Example Sentences

- 要预防疾病的传播。(Yào yù fáng jí bìng de chuán bō.) — We must prevent the spread of disease.

## Notes

防 shows mound 阝 and 方 (direction), originally depicting earthen fortifications built to defend against attack from all directions.
