# 容 Capacity, contain

- **Character**: 容
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: capacity, contain
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 容量 (róng liàng) — capacity
- 电容 (diàn róng) — capacitor

## Example Sentences

- 电池容量很大。(Diàn chí róng liàng hěn dà.) — The battery capacity is large.

## Notes

Used in 容量 (capacity), 电容 (capacitor), 内容 (content)
