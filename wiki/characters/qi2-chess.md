# 棋 Chess

- **Character**: 棋
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: chess, board game
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 下棋 (xià qí) — to play chess
- 象棋 (xiàng qí) — Chinese chess

## Example Sentences

- 我们下棋吧。(Wǒ men xià qí ba.) — Let's play chess.

## Notes

棋 means chess or board game. Used in下棋 (play chess), 象棋 (Chinese chess), 围棋 (go).
