# 旋 Rotate

- **Character**: 旋
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: to rotate, revolve
- **Radical**: 方 (square)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 旋转 (xuán zhuǎn) — to rotate, spin
- 盘旋 (pán xuán) — to circle, hover

## Example Sentences

- 花样滑冰运动员快速旋转。(Huāyàng huábīng yùndòngyuán kuàisù xuánzhuǎn.) — Figure skaters spin rapidly.

## Notes

Spinning and rotation; key for gymnastics and figure skating
