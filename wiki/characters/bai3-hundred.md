# 百 Hundred

- **Character**: 百
- **Pinyin**: bǎi
- **Meaning**: hundred
- **Radical**: 白 (white)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 一百 (yī bǎi) — one hundred
- 百分之 (bǎi fēn zhī) — percent

## Example Sentences

- 这本书一百块。(Zhè běn shū yī bǎi kuài.) — This book costs 100 yuan.

## Notes

百 literally means 'one hundred.' 百分之... means 'percent': 百分之五十 = 50%.
