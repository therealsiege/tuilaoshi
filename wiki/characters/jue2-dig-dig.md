# 掘 To Dig

- **Character**: 掘
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to dig, to excavate
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 发掘 (fā jué) — to excavate
- 挖掘 (wā jué) — to dig

## Example Sentences

- 考古学家发掘了古墓。(Kǎo gǔ xué jiā fā jué le gǔ mù.) — The archaeologists excavated an ancient tomb.

## Notes

掘 has hand 扌 with 屈 (to bend), digging into the ground.
