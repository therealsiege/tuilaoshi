# 睡 To Sleep

- **Character**: 睡
- **Pinyin**: shuì
- **Meaning**: to sleep
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 睡觉 (shuì jiào) — to sleep
- 睡眠 (shuì mián) — sleep

## Example Sentences

- 我通常十一点睡觉。(Wǒ tōng cháng shí yī diǎn shuì jiào.) — I usually go to sleep at eleven.

## Notes

睡 shows eye 目 with 垂 (to droop), depicting drooping eyelids in sleep.
