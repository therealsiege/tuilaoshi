# 执 To execute, to grasp, to hold

- **Character**: 执
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: to execute, to grasp, to hold
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执行 (zhí xíng) — to implement, to execute
- 坚持 (jiān chí) — to persist, to insist on

## Example Sentences

- 我们要严格执行规定。(Wǒmen yào yángé zhíxíng guīdìng.) — We must strictly implement the regulations.

## Notes

Common in formal contexts for carrying out tasks or policies.
