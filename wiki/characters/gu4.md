# 固 Solid, firm, stubborn

- **Character**: 固
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: solid, firm, stubborn
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 固定 (gù dìng) — fixed, regular
- 顽固 (wán gù) — stubborn, obstinate

## Example Sentences

- 他很固执,不愿意改变。(Tā hěn gù zhí, bù yuàn yì gǎi biàn.) — He is very stubborn and unwilling to change.

## Notes

Can be positive (firm, solid) or negative (stubborn). 固然 means admittedly or of course.
