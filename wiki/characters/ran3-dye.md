# 染 To Dye

- **Character**: 染
- **Pinyin**: rǎn
- **Meaning**: to dye, to catch (disease)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 染色 (rǎn sè) — to dye
- 感染 (gǎn rǎn) — to infect

## Example Sentences

- 她把头发染成了红色。(Tā bǎ tóu fà rǎn chéng le hóng sè.) — She dyed her hair red.

## Notes

染 shows wood 木 (for dye vats) with water 氵, representing the dyeing process.
