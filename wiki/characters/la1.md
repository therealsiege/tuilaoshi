# 拉 To Pull

- **Character**: 拉
- **Pinyin**: lā
- **Meaning**: to pull, to drag, to play (string instrument)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拉开 (lā kāi) — to pull open
- 拉车 (lā chē) — to pull a cart

## Example Sentences

- 请拉开窗帘。(Qǐng lā kāi chuāng lián.) — Please pull open the curtains.

## Notes

拉 is the opposite of 推 (to push). Also used for playing stringed instruments like violin (拉小提琴).
