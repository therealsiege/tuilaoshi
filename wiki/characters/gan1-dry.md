# 干 Dry

- **Character**: 干
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: dry; to do (gàn); stem
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 干净 (gān jìng) — clean
- 干活 (gàn huó) — to work

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间很干净。(Zhè ge fáng jiān hěn gān jìng.) — This room is very clean.

## Notes

干 as gān means dry/clean. As gàn means to do/work. Simplified from both 乾 and 幹.
