# 核 Nucleus, examine, pit

- **Character**: 核
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: nucleus, examine, pit
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 核心 (héxīn) — core, nucleus
- 审核 (shěnhé) — examine, check

## Example Sentences

- 这是问题的核心。(Zhè shì wèntí de héxīn.) — This is the core of the problem.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: nucleus/core, examination, and fruit pit.
