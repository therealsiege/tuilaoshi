# 止 Stop, cease

- **Character**: 止
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: stop, cease
- **Radical**: 止 (stop)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 停止 (tíng zhǐ) — stop, cease
- 禁止 (jìn zhǐ) — prohibit, forbid

## Example Sentences

- 合同到期自动终止。(Hé tong dào qī zì dòng zhōng zhǐ.) — The contract terminates automatically upon expiration.

## Notes

中止 means suspend. 截止 means deadline.
