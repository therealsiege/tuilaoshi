# 本 Root, origin, capital, this

- **Character**: 本
- **Pinyin**: běn
- **Meaning**: root, origin, capital, this
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 成本 (chéngběn) — cost
- 资本 (zīběn) — capital

## Example Sentences

- 公司需要更多资本。(Gōngsī xūyào gèng duō zīběn.) — The company needs more capital.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: root, origin, capital, book, this.
