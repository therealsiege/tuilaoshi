# 勺 Spoon

- **Character**: 勺
- **Pinyin**: sháo
- **Meaning**: spoon, ladle
- **Radical**: 勹 (wrap)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 勺子 (sháo zi) — spoon
- 汤勺 (tāng sháo) — soup spoon

## Example Sentences

- 给我一个勺子。(Gěi wǒ yī gè sháo zi.) — Give me a spoon.

## Notes

勺 pictograph of ladle. 勺子 standard word for spoon. 汤勺 = soup spoon.
