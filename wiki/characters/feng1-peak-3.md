# 峰 Peak

- **Character**: 峰
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: peak, summit
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 山峰 (shānfēng) — mountain peak
- 高峰 (gāofēng) — peak, summit

## Example Sentences

- 他们爬到了山峰顶部。(Tāmen pá dào le shānfēng dǐngbù.) — They climbed to the mountain peak.

## Notes

Mountain vocabulary. Mountain radical. 高峰 also means peak/rush hour in traffic context.
