# 钞 Paper money, bill

- **Character**: 钞
- **Pinyin**: chāo
- **Meaning**: paper money, bill
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钞票 (chāo piào) — banknote, bill
- 现钞 (xiàn chāo) — cash

## Example Sentences

- 请收好钞票和发票。(Qǐng shōu hǎo chāo piào hé fā piào.) — Please keep the cash and receipt.

## Notes

伪钞 means counterfeit money. 美钞 means US dollar bills.
