# 簿 Register, book

- **Character**: 簿
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: register, book
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 账簿 (zhàng bù) — account book
- 簿记 (bù jì) — bookkeeping

## Example Sentences

- 会计负责记账簿。(Kuài jì fù zé jì zhàng bù.) — The accountant is responsible for keeping the account books.

## Notes

Traditional accounting term. 簿记 is bookkeeping profession.
