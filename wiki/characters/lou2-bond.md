# 娄 Bond

- **Character**: 娄
- **Pinyin**: lóu
- **Meaning**: Bond constellation, rope
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 娄宿 (lóu xiù) — Bond constellation
- 娄子 (lóu zi) — trouble

## Example Sentences

- 娄宿是西方白虎的第二宿。(Lóu xiù shì xīfāng báihǔ de dì èr xiù.) — Bond is the second mansion of the Western White Tiger.

## Notes

娄 refers to the Bond constellation. The name suggests binding or gathering together.
