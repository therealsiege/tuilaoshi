# 卑 Low

- **Character**: 卑
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: low, base, humble
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卑鄙 (bēi bǐ) — mean, despicable
- 自卑 (zì bēi) — inferiority complex

## Example Sentences

- 他的行为很卑鄙。(Tā de xíng wéi hěn bēi bǐ.) — His behavior is very despicable.

## Notes

卑 originally depicted a low servant. Used for both physical lowness and moral baseness.
