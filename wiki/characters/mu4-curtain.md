# 幕 Curtain

- **Character**: 幕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: curtain, screen, act (of play)
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 幕布 (mù bù) — curtain
- 开幕 (kāi mù) — to open (ceremony)

## Example Sentences

- 开幕式将在明天举行。(Kāi mù shì jiāng zài míng tiān jǔ xíng.) — The opening ceremony will be held tomorrow.

## Notes

幕 shows cloth radical 巾 with 莫 (sunset), originally a cloth cover used at dusk, now means curtain or screen.
