# 策 策略, plan, scheme

- **Character**: 策
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: 策略, plan, scheme
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 策略 (cè lüè) — strategy, tactic
- 策划 (cè huà) — plan, scheme

## Example Sentences

- 营销策划很重要。(Yíng xiāo cè huà hěn zhòng yào.) — Marketing planning is very important.

## Notes

政策 means policy. 对策 means countermeasure.
