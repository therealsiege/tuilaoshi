# 家 Home/Family/-ist

- **Character**: 家
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: home, family; specialist, -ist (suffix)
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 家人 (jiārén) — family members
- 家庭 (jiātíng) — family, household
- 国家 (guójiā) — country, nation
- 大家 (dàjiā) — everyone
- 作家 (zuòjiā) — writer, author
- 回家 (huíjiā) — to go home

## Example Sentences

- 我想回家。(Wǒ xiǎng huíjiā.) — I want to go home.
- 你家有几口人？(Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?) — How many people are in your family?
- 大家好！(Dàjiā hǎo!) — Hello everyone!

## Notes

家 combines the roof radical 宀 on top with 豕 (shǐ, pig) below — a pig under a roof. In ancient China, pigs were kept inside the household compound, so their presence beneath a roof signified a home. As a suffix, 家 indicates a specialist or expert in a field: 作家 (writer), 画家 (painter), 科学家 (scientist), 音乐家 (musician). The measure word for 家 when counting families or businesses is itself 家.
