# 唠 To Chat

- **Character**: 唠
- **Pinyin**: láo
- **Meaning**: to chat, to nag
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 唠叨 (láo dao) — to nag, to chatter
- 唠嗑 (láo kè) — to chat

## Example Sentences

- 别老唠叨了。(Bié lǎo láo dao le.) — Stop nagging.

## Notes

唠 simplified from 嘮. Mouth radical 口 representing continuous talking or chattering.
