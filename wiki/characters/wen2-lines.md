# 纹 Pattern

- **Character**: 纹
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: lines, veins, pattern
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纹理 (wén lǐ) — texture, grain
- 指纹 (zhǐ wén) — fingerprint

## Example Sentences

- 用指纹解锁。(Yòng zhǐ wén jiě suǒ.) — Unlock with fingerprint.

## Notes

纹 with silk radical suggests woven patterns, now for all patterns and textures.
