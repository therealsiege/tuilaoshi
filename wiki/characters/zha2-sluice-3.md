# 闸 Sluice

- **Character**: 闸
- **Pinyin**: zhá
- **Meaning**: sluice, floodgate, switch
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 闸门 (zhá mén) — sluice gate
- 开闸 (kāi zhá) — to open sluice

## Example Sentences

- 他们打开了水闸。(Tā men dǎ kāi le shuǐ zhá.) — They opened the water gate.

## Notes

闸 simplified from 閘. Gate 门 controlling flow, dam gate.
