# 总 General

- **Character**: 总
- **Pinyin**: zǒng
- **Meaning**: general, total, always
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 总是 (zǒng shì) — always
- 总统 (zǒng tǒng) — president

## Example Sentences

- 他总是很忙。(Tā zǒng shì hěn máng.) — He is always very busy.

## Notes

总 is simplified from 總, meaning to gather together or overall.
