# 番 Time, Turn, 一番 Once

- **Character**: 番
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: time, turn, 一番 once
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 一番 (yī fān) — a round of, a bout of
- 几番 (jǐ fān) — several times
- 番茄 (fān qié) — tomato

## Example Sentences

- 经过一番努力，他成功了。(Jīngguò yī fān nǔlì, tā chénggōng le.) — After some effort, he succeeded.

## Notes

Classifier for actions or efforts. 一番 describes a round or bout of activity. Also appears in 番茄 (tomato).
