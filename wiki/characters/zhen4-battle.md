# 阵 Battle formation, array

- **Character**: 阵
- **Pinyin**: zhèn
- **Meaning**: battle formation, array
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阵地 (zhèn dì) — position, front
- 阵营 (zhèn yíng) — camp, group

## Example Sentences

- 两军对阵，各显神通。(Liǎng jūn duì zhèn, gè xiǎn shéntōng.) — The two armies face off in formation, each displaying their skills.

## Notes

Military formation or battle array. Also used as a measure word for periods of time or bursts (一阵风 - a gust of wind).
