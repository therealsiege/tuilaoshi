# 碑 Stone tablet, monument

- **Character**: 碑
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: stone tablet, monument
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 石碑 (shí bēi) — stone tablet
- 纪念碑 (jì niàn bēi) — monument

## Example Sentences

- 这座石碑记载了这个城市的历史。(Zhè zuò shíbēi jìzǎi le zhège chéngshì de lìshǐ.) — This stone tablet records the history of this city.

## Notes

Stone monuments often inscribed with historical records or commemorative texts. Important medium for preserving Chinese calligraphy.
