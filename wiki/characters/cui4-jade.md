# 翠 Jade Green

- **Character**: 翠
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: jade green, emerald
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 翡翠 (fěi cuì) — jade
- 翠绿 (cuì lǜ) — emerald green

## Example Sentences

- 山上一片翠绿。(Shān shàng yí piàn cuì lǜ.) — The mountain is covered in emerald green.

## Notes

翠 has feather 羽 with 卒 (soldier), kingfisher green color.
