# 放 To Put

- **Character**: 放
- **Pinyin**: fàng
- **Meaning**: to put, to release, to let go
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 放心 (fàng xīn) — to feel at ease
- 放假 (fàng jià) — to have a holiday

## Example Sentences

- 放心吧！(Fàng xīn ba!) — Don't worry!

## Notes

放 is versatile: 放下 (put down), 放心 (relax), 放假 (have a vacation), 放学 (school lets out).
