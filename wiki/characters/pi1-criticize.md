# 批 To Criticize

- **Character**: 批
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: to criticize, batch, to approve
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 批评 (pī píng) — to criticize
- 批准 (pī zhǔn) — to approve

## Example Sentences

- 老师批改了作业。(Lǎo shī pī gǎi le zuò yè.) — The teacher corrected the homework.

## Notes

批 shows hand 扌 and 比 (to compare), representing using the hand to mark or evaluate in batches.
