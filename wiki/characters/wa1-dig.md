# 挖 To dig, to excavate, to scoop out

- **Character**: 挖
- **Pinyin**: wā
- **Meaning**: to dig, to excavate, to scoop out
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挖掘 (wā jué) — to excavate, to dig
- 挖洞 (wā dòng) — to dig a hole

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在沙滩上挖沙子。(Háizimen zài shātān shàng wā shāzi.) — The children are digging in the sand on the beach.

## Notes

General term for digging, more casual than 掘.
