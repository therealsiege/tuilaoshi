# 扩 Expand, enlarge, extend

- **Character**: 扩
- **Pinyin**: kuò
- **Meaning**: expand, enlarge, extend
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扩散 (kuò sàn) — diffuse, spread
- 扩大 (kuò dà) — expand, enlarge

## Example Sentences

- 气体会自然扩散。(Qì tǐ huì zì rán kuò sàn.) — Gases naturally diffuse.
