# 符 Symbol

- **Character**: 符
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: talisman, symbol, to conform to
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 符合 (fú hé) — to conform to
- 符号 (fú hào) — symbol

## Example Sentences

- 这不符合要求。(Zhè bù fú hé yāo qiú.) — This doesn't meet the requirements.

## Notes

符合 means to conform to or match. 符号 means symbol or sign.
