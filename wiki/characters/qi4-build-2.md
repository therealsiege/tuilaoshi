# 砌 Build with bricks

- **Character**: 砌
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: build with bricks
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砌墙 (qì qiáng) — build a wall
- 砖砌 (zhuān qì) — brick-built

## Example Sentences

- 工人在砌墙。(Gōng rén zài qì qiáng.) — Workers are building a wall.

## Notes

砌砖 means lay bricks. 石砌 means stone-built.
