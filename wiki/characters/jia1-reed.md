# 笳 Reed Pipe

- **Character**: 笳
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: reed pipe (ancient)
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 胡笳 (hú jiā) — nomadic reed pipe
- 笳鼓 (jiā gǔ) — pipe and drum

## Example Sentences

- 胡笳十八拍是著名的古曲。(Hú jiā shí bā pāi shì zhù míng de gǔ qǔ.) — Eighteen Songs of a Nomad Flute is a famous ancient tune.

## Notes

笳 was used by northern nomadic peoples, often mentioned in classical poetry.
