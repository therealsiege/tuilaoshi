# 钉 Nail

- **Character**: 钉
- **Pinyin**: dīng
- **Meaning**: nail, to nail
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 钉子 (dīng zi) — nail
- 图钉 (tú dīng) — thumbtack

## Example Sentences

- 钉在墙上。(Dīng zài qiáng shàng.) — Nail it to the wall.

## Notes

钉 as dīng means nail; as dìng means to follow closely. Essential fastening terminology.
