# 憋 To Suppress

- **Character**: 憋
- **Pinyin**: biē
- **Meaning**: to suppress, to hold back, to feel stifled
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 憋气 (biē qì) — to hold breath
- 憋屈 (biē qū) — to feel stifled

## Example Sentences

- 别憋着，说出来。(Bié biē zhe, shuō chū lái.) — Don't hold it in, speak up.

## Notes

憋 means to suppress or hold in. Colloquial and expressive.
