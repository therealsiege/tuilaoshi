# 隧 Tunnel

- **Character**: 隧
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: tunnel, underground passage
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 隧道 (suì dào) — tunnel
- 海底隧道 (hǎi dǐ suì dào) — undersea tunnel

## Example Sentences

- 火车穿过隧道。(Huǒ chē chuān guò suì dào.) — The train passes through the tunnel.

## Notes

隧 has mound 阝 with 遂 (to follow through), passage through earth.
