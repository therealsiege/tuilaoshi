# 三 Three

- **Character**: 三
- **Pinyin**: sān
- **Meaning**: three
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 三级跳 (sān jí tiào) — triple jump
- 三分球 (sān fēn qiú) — three-point shot

## Example Sentences

- 三级跳是跳跃项目。(Sānjítiào shì tiàoyuè xiàngmù.) — Triple jump is a jumping event.

## Notes

Number three; triple jump and three-pointers
