# 钝 Blunt, dull, stupid

- **Character**: 钝
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: blunt, dull, stupid
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 钝器 (dùn qì) — blunt instrument
- 愚钝 (yú dùn) — dull, stupid

## Example Sentences

- 这把刀太钝了。(Zhè bǎ dāo tài dùn le.) — This knife is too blunt.

## Notes

Opposite of sharp, for objects or intelligence
