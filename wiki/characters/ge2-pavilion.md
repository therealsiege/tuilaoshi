# 阁 Pavilion, cabinet, chamber

- **Character**: 阁
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: pavilion, cabinet, chamber
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 楼阁 (lóu gé) — tower, pavilion
- 内阁 (nèi gé) — cabinet (government)

## Example Sentences

- 滕王阁是中国古代建筑的杰作。(Téng Wáng Gé shì Zhōng guó gǔ dài jiàn zhù de jié zuò.) — The Pavilion of Prince Teng is a masterpiece of ancient Chinese architecture.

## Notes

Famous pavilions include 滕王阁, 岳阳楼.
