# 寓 To Reside

- **Character**: 寓
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to reside, to imply
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 寓言 (yù yán) — fable, allegory
- 公寓 (gōng yù) — apartment

## Example Sentences

- 这个故事寓意深刻。(Zhè ge gù shì yù yì shēn kè.) — This story has profound implications.

## Notes

寓 under roof radical 宀 originally meant temporary residence, extended to meaning implicit or allegorical.
