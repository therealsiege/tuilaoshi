# 瘥 Recover from illness

- **Character**: 瘥
- **Pinyin**: chài
- **Meaning**: recover from illness
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 病瘥 (bìng chài) — recover from illness
- 瘥愈 (chài yù) — recover

## Example Sentences

- 他的病终于瘥了。(Tā de bìng zhōng yú chài le.) — He finally recovered from his illness.

## Notes

Classical term for recovery from illness
