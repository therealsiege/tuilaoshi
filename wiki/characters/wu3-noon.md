# 午 Noon

- **Character**: 午
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: noon, midday
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 上午 (shàng wǔ) — morning
- 下午 (xià wǔ) — afternoon

## Example Sentences

- 中午我们一起吃饭。(Zhōng wǔ wǒ men yī qǐ chī fàn.) — Let's eat lunch together at noon.

## Notes

午 is the 7th earthly branch, corresponding to the horse and the hours 11am-1pm (noon).
