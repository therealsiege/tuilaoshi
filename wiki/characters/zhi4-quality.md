# 质 Quality

- **Character**: 质
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: quality, nature, substance
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 质量 (zhì liàng) — quality
- 物质 (wù zhì) — matter, substance

## Example Sentences

- 这个产品的质量很好。(Zhè ge chǎn pǐn de zhì liàng hěn hǎo.) — This product's quality is very good.

## Notes

质 represents the inherent nature or quality of something. Essential for discussing standards and characteristics.
