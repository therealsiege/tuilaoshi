# 蓄 To Store Up

- **Character**: 蓄
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: to store up, to grow (beard)
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 储蓄 (chǔ xù) — to save
- 积蓄 (jī xù) — savings

## Example Sentences

- 他有一些积蓄。(Tā yǒu yī xiē jī xù.) — He has some savings.

## Notes

蓄 shows grass 艹 growing and accumulating over time, representing gradual storage or accumulation.
