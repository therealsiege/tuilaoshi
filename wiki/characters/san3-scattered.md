# 散 Scattered

- **Character**: 散
- **Pinyin**: sǎn
- **Meaning**: scattered, loose, prose
- **Radical**: 攴 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 散文 (sǎn wén) — prose
- 散步 (sàn bù) — to take a walk

## Example Sentences

- 他写散文写得很好。(Tā xiě sǎn wén xiě de hěn hǎo.) — He writes prose very well.

## Notes

散 (sǎn) means scattered or prose; also read as sàn meaning to disperse.
