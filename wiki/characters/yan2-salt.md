# 盐 Salt

- **Character**: 盐
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: salt
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 食盐 (shí yán) — table salt
- 盐水 (yán shuǐ) — salt water

## Example Sentences

- 菜里面没有盐。(Cài lǐ miàn méi yǒu yán.) — There's no salt in the dish.

## Notes

盐 means salt. 盐巴 = salt (colloquial). 盐湖 = salt lake.
