# 购 Purchase, buy

- **Character**: 购
- **Pinyin**: gòu
- **Meaning**: purchase, buy
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 购买 (gòu mǎi) — to purchase
- 采购 (cǎi gòu) — to purchase, procurement

## Example Sentences

- 公司采购了新设备。(Gōng sī cǎi gòu le xīn shè bèi.) — The company purchased new equipment.

## Notes

Formal term for purchasing. 购物 means shopping. 订购 means to order goods.
