# 堕 Fall, degenerate

- **Character**: 堕
- **Pinyin**: duò
- **Meaning**: fall, degenerate
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 堕落 (duòluò) — degenerate, become depraved
- 堕胎 (duòtāi) — abortion

## Example Sentences

- 不要堕落成罪犯。(Bùyào duòluò chéng zuìfàn.) — Don't degenerate into a criminal.

## Notes

Describes moral or physical falling, degradation.
