# 庞 Huge

- **Character**: 庞
- **Pinyin**: páng
- **Meaning**: huge, enormous
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 庞大 (páng dà) — huge, enormous
- 庞然大物 (páng rán dà wù) — colossus

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个庞大的系统。(Zhè shì yīgè pángdà de xìtǒng.) — This is an enormous system.

## Notes

庞 combines shelter 广 with dragon 龙, suggesting something huge like a dragon.
