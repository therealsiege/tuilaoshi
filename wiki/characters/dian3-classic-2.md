# 典 Classic text, standard, ceremony

- **Character**: 典
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: classic text, standard, ceremony
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 经典 (jīng diǎn) — classics, classical
- 典礼 (diǎn lǐ) — ceremony

## Example Sentences

- 这是一部经典著作，值得反复阅读。(Zhè shì yī bù jīngdiǎn zhùzuò, zhídé fǎnfù yuèdú.) — This is a classic work worth reading repeatedly.

## Notes

Represents canonical texts and authoritative standards. The character shows books (册) on a stand, symbolizing important texts.
