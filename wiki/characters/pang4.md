# 胖 Fat

- **Character**: 胖
- **Pinyin**: pàng
- **Meaning**: fat, plump
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 肥胖 (féi pàng) — obese
- 胖子 (pàng zi) — fat person

## Example Sentences

- 他比以前胖了。(Tā bǐ yǐqián pàng le.) — He is fatter than before.

## Notes

胖 combines meat 月 with 半 (half), though the connection is not entirely clear. Opposite of 瘦.
