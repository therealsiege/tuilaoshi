# 胖 Fat

- **Character**: 胖
- **Pinyin**: pàng
- **Meaning**: fat, plump
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 发胖 (fā pàng) — to gain weight
- 肥胖 (féi pàng) — obese

## Example Sentences

- 我最近胖了。(Wǒ zuì jìn pàng le.) — I've gained weight recently.

## Notes

胖 uses flesh radical 月. More polite than 肥 (fat, used for animals/objects). Opposite: 瘦.
