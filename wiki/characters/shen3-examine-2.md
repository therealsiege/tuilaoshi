# 审 To examine, try, 審 (traditional)

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: to examine, try,審 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěn chá) — to examine, investigate
- 审批 (shěn pī) — to examine and approve

## Example Sentences

- 法院正在审理这个案子。(Fǎyuàn zhèngzài shěnlǐ zhège ànzi.) — The court is trying this case.

## Notes

Common in legal and administrative contexts for examination and review.
