# 沉 To Sink

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: to sink, heavy, deep
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 下沉 (xià chén) — to sink
- 沉默 (chén mò) — silent

## Example Sentences

- 石头沉入水底。(Shí tou chén rù shuǐ dǐ.) — The stone sinks to the bottom.

## Notes

沉 has water 氵 with 冘 (to sink), descending below water.
