# 运 Transport, luck

- **Character**: 运
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: transport, luck
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 运输 (yùn shū) — transport
- 运费 (yùn fèi) — freight, shipping cost

## Example Sentences

- 运费由买方承担。(Yùn fèi yóu mǎi fāng chéng dān.) — Shipping costs are borne by the buyer.

## Notes

物流 is logistics. 运营 means operate/operation.
