# 聪 Intelligent, acute hearing

- **Character**: 聪
- **Pinyin**: cōng
- **Meaning**: intelligent, acute hearing
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 聪明 (cōngmíng) — intelligent, clever
- 聪慧 (cōnghuì) — intelligent, bright

## Example Sentences

- 他很聪明。(Tā hěn cōngmíng.) — He is very intelligent.

## Notes

Common character for intelligence or acute hearing.
