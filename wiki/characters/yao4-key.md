# 钥 Key

- **Character**: 钥
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: key (in 钥匙)
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钥匙 (yào shi) — key

## Example Sentences

- 我找不到钥匙了。(Wǒ zhǎo bù dào yào shi le.) — I can't find my keys.

## Notes

钥 only used in compound 钥匙 (key). Metal radical 钅 because keys are metal.
