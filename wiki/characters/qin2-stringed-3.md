# 琴 Zither

- **Character**: 琴
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: stringed instrument, zither
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 对牛弹琴 (duì niú tán qín) — cast pearls before swine
- 钢琴 (gāng qín) — piano

## Example Sentences

- 她会弹钢琴。(Tā huì tán gāngqín.) — She can play the piano.

## Notes

The instrument in 对牛弹琴; one of the four traditional arts (琴棋书画).
