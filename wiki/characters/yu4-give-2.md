# 育 Give birth, raise, educate

- **Character**: 育
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: give birth, raise, educate
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 生育 (shēng yù) — give birth, reproduce
- 教育 (jiào yù) — educate, education

## Example Sentences

- 哺乳动物生育后会照顾幼崽。(Bǔ rǔ dòng wù shēng yù hòu huì zhào gù yòu zǎi.) — Mammals care for their young after giving birth.

## Notes

Used for birth and nurturing
