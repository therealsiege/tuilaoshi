# 开 To Open

- **Character**: 开
- **Pinyin**: kāi
- **Meaning**: to open, to start, to drive
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 开始 (kāi shǐ) — to begin
- 开车 (kāi chē) — to drive

## Example Sentences

- 请开门。(Qǐng kāi mén.) — Please open the door.

## Notes

开 is simplified from 開. It is extremely versatile: 开门 (open door), 开车 (drive), 开会 (hold a meeting), 开心 (happy).
