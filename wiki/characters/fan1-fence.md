# 藩 Fence, vassal state

- **Character**: 藩
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: fence, vassal state
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 藩属 (fān shǔ) — vassal, dependency
- 藩王 (fān wáng) — vassal king

## Example Sentences

- 明清时期设立了许多藩属国。(Míng Qīng shíqī shèlì le xǔduō fānshǔ guó.) — During the Ming and Qing dynasties, many vassal states were established.

## Notes

Originally meant fence or barrier. Extended to mean vassal states that formed a protective barrier around China.
