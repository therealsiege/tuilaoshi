# 篱 Fence

- **Character**: 篱
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: fence, hedge
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 篱笆 (lí ba) — fence
- 竹篱 (zhú lí) — bamboo fence

## Example Sentences

- 院子外面有篱笆。(Yuàn zi wài miàn yǒu lí ba.) — There is a fence outside the yard.

## Notes

篱 simplified from 籬. Bamboo 竹 with 离 (to leave/separate), bamboo barrier.
