# 来 To Come

- **Character**: 来
- **Pinyin**: lái
- **Meaning**: to come
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 来自 (láizì) — to come from
- 原来 (yuánlái) — originally, as it turns out
- 出来 (chūlái) — to come out
- 回来 (huílái) — to come back
- 将来 (jiānglái) — future, in the future
- 起来 (qǐlái) — to get up; (used as complement)

## Example Sentences

- 你来这里做什么？(Nǐ lái zhèlǐ zuò shénme?) — What did you come here to do?
- 他从北京来。(Tā cóng Běijīng lái.) — He comes from Beijing.
- 春天来了。(Chūntiān lái le.) — Spring has come.

## Notes

来 is the simplified form of 來, which in oracle bone script depicted a stalk of wheat — originally meaning "wheat" (now written 麦). The meaning shifted to "to come" through phonetic borrowing. As a directional complement, 来 indicates motion toward the speaker, forming the core pair with 去 (qù). It also appears in grammatical constructions like 来 + verb (purpose: "come to do something").
