# 应 Respond, should, answer

- **Character**: 应
- **Pinyin**: yìng
- **Meaning**: respond, should, answer
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 反应 (fǎn yìng) — reaction, response
- 应该 (yīng gāi) — should, ought to

## Example Sentences

- 身体对药物有反应。(Shēn tǐ duì yào wù yǒu fǎn yìng.) — The body has a reaction to the medication.

## Notes

Used for responses and reactions
