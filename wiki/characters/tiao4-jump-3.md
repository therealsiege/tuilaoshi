# 跳 Jump

- **Character**: 跳
- **Pinyin**: tiào
- **Meaning**: to jump, leap
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跳高 (tiào gāo) — high jump
- 跳远 (tiào yuǎn) — long jump

## Example Sentences

- 她跳得很高。(Tā tiào de hěn gāo.) — She jumps very high.

## Notes

Used in jumping events, dancing, and general jumping actions
