# 通 Through, open, communicate

- **Character**: 通
- **Pinyin**: tōng
- **Meaning**: through, open, communicate
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 通知 (tōngzhī) — notify, notice
- 通缉 (tōngjī) — wanted (by police)

## Example Sentences

- 这条路不通。(Zhè tiáo lù bù tōng.) — This road is not passable.

## Notes

Very common for通 and communication.
