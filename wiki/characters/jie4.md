# 界 Boundary

- **Character**: 界
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: boundary, scope, world, circle
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 世界 (shì jiè) — world
- 边界 (biān jiè) — boundary

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个美丽的世界。(Zhè shì yī ge měi lì de shì jiè.) — This is a beautiful world.

## Notes

界 shows fields divided, representing boundaries or circles. Essential in世界 (world) and for describing boundaries.
