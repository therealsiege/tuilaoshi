# 疵 Flaw, defect, fault

- **Character**: 疵
- **Pinyin**: cī
- **Meaning**: flaw, defect, fault
- **Radical**: 疒 (illness)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 瑕疵 (xiá cī) — flaw, defect
- 无疵 (wú cī) — flawless

## Example Sentences

- 这块布料没有任何瑕疵。(Zhè kuài bù liào méi yǒu rèn hé xiá cī.) — This fabric has no flaws whatsoever.

## Notes

Almost always used in the compound 瑕疵.
