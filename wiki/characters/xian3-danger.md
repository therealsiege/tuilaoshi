# 险 Danger

- **Character**: 险
- **Pinyin**: xiǎn
- **Meaning**: danger, risky, narrow
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 危险 (wēi xiǎn) — danger
- 保险 (bǎo xiǎn) — insurance

## Example Sentences

- 我买了保险。(Wǒ mǎi le bǎo xiǎn.) — I bought insurance.

## Notes

险 simplified from 險. Shows mound 阝 and 佥 (all/together), depicting steep mountain terrain where danger lurks.
