# 杆 Pole, rod, staff

- **Character**: 杆
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: pole, rod, staff
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing, balustrade
- 旗杆 (qí gān) — flagpole

## Example Sentences

- 广场上立着一根高高的旗杆。(Guǎng chǎng shàng lì zhe yī gēn gāo gāo de qí gān.) — A tall flagpole stands in the square.

## Notes

Also pronounced gǎn for certain meanings like rifle barrel.
