# 痛 Pain, Ache, 头痛 Headache

- **Character**: 痛
- **Pinyin**: tòng
- **Meaning**: pain, ache, 头痛 headache
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 头痛 (tóu tòng) — headache
- 疼痛 (téng tòng) — pain, ache
- 痛苦 (tòng kǔ) — suffering, pain

## Example Sentences

- 我头痛得厉害。(Wǒ tóutòng de lìhài.) — I have a severe headache.

## Notes

Common character for physical and emotional pain. 头痛, 肚子痛 (stomachache), 痛苦 (suffering) are all frequently used.
