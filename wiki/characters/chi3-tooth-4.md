# 齿 Tooth

- **Character**: 齿
- **Pinyin**: chǐ
- **Meaning**: tooth, gear tooth, cog
- **Radical**: 齿 (tooth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 牙齿 (yá chǐ) — teeth
- 齿轮 (chǐ lún) — gear

## Example Sentences

- 他有一口整齐的牙齿。(Tā yǒu yì kǒu zhěng qí de yá chǐ.) — He has a set of neat teeth.

## Notes

齿 simplified from 齒. Depicts teeth in the mouth.
