# 漆 Paint, lacquer

- **Character**: 漆
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: paint, lacquer
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 油漆 (yóu qī) — paint
- 漆黑 (qī hēi) — pitch-black

## Example Sentences

- 这面墙需要刷油漆。(Zhè miàn qiáng xū yào shuā yóu qī.) — This wall needs to be painted.

## Notes

涂漆 means apply paint. 喷漆 means spray paint.
