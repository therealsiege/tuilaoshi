# 册 Volume

- **Character**: 册
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: book, volume, measure word for books
- **Radical**: 冂 (wide)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 画册 (huà cè) — picture album
- 手册 (shǒu cè) — handbook

## Example Sentences

- 请翻开第一册。(Qǐng fān kāi dì yī cè.) — Please open volume one.

## Notes

册 is a pictograph of bamboo slips tied together — ancient books. Used as measure word for volumes.
