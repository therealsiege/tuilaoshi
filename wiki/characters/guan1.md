# 关 — Close / Related

- **Character**: 关
- **Pinyin**: guān
- **Meaning**: to close, to shut; to concern, to be related to; a pass, a barrier
- **Radical**: 八 (eight) / 关 (as simplified radical)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 关系 (guānxì) — relationship, connection
- 关于 (guānyú) — about, regarding, concerning
- 关心 (guānxīn) — to care about, to be concerned
- 关门 (guānmén) — to close a door
- 有关 (yǒuguān) — related to, concerning
- 关键 (guānjiàn) — key, crucial

## Example Sentences

- 请把门关上。(Qǐng bǎ mén guānshang.) — Please close the door.
- 这件事跟你没有关系。(Zhè jiàn shì gēn nǐ méiyǒu guānxì.) — This matter has nothing to do with you.
- 父母很关心孩子的学习。(Fùmǔ hěn guānxīn háizi de xuéxí.) — Parents care a lot about their children's studies.

## Notes

关 (simplified from 關) originally depicted a door bolt, hence "to close/shut." The extended meaning of "barrier, pass" comes from mountain passes with gates. The further extension to "relate, concern" follows the logic that things on either side of a barrier are connected through it. 关系 (relationship) is one of the most culturally loaded words in Chinese — it encompasses personal relationships, social connections, and the concept of networking that is central to Chinese social life. 关于 is the standard way to say "about/regarding" in formal contexts.
