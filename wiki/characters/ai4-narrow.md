# 隘 Narrow pass, defile

- **Character**: 隘
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: narrow pass, defile
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 关隘 (guān ài) — mountain pass
- 要隘 (yào ài) — strategic pass

## Example Sentences

- 这里是重要的关隘。(Zhè lǐ shì zhòng yào de guān ài.) — This is an important mountain pass.

## Notes

Strategic narrow passages in mountains
