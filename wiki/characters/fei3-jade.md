# 翡 Jade

- **Character**: 翡
- **Pinyin**: fěi
- **Meaning**: jade (in 翡翠)
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 翡翠 (fěi cuì) — jade, jadeite

## Example Sentences

- 她有一只翡翠手镯。(Tā yǒu yì zhī fěi cuì shǒu zhuó.) — She has a jade bracelet.

## Notes

翡 has feather 羽 with 非, kingfisher bird; jade named for its color.
