# 豫 Henan

- **Character**: 豫
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: Henan, beforehand
- **Radical**: 豕 (pig)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 豫剧 (yù jù) — Henan opera
- 犹豫 (yóu yù) — to hesitate

## Example Sentences

- 豫剧是河南的代表剧种。(Yù jù shì hé nán de dài biǎo jù zhǒng.) — Henan opera is the representative opera of Henan.

## Notes

豫 refers to Henan province; 豫剧 is Henan opera.
