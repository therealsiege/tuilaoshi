# 稳 Stable, steady, sure

- **Character**: 稳
- **Pinyin**: wěn
- **Meaning**: stable, steady, sure
- **Radical**: 禾(grain)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 稳定 (wěn dìng) — stable, steady
- 平稳 (píng wěn) — smooth, steady

## Example Sentences

- 这把椅子很稳。(Zhè bǎ yǐzi hěn wěn.) — This chair is very stable.

## Notes

Describes physical stability or reliable character.
