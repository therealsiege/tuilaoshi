# 拔 To Pull Out

- **Character**: 拔
- **Pinyin**: bá
- **Meaning**: to pull out, extract
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拔牙 (bá yá) — to extract a tooth
- 选拔 (xuǎn bá) — to select, choose

## Example Sentences

- 他去医院拔牙了。(Tā qù yīyuàn báyá le.) — He went to the hospital to have a tooth pulled.

## Notes

拔 combines hand 扌 with 犮 (to violate), representing forcefully pulling something out.
