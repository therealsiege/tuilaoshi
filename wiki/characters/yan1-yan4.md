# 咽 Pharynx

- **Character**: 咽
- **Pinyin**: yān/yàn
- **Meaning**: pharynx; to swallow
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 咽喉 (yān hóu) — pharynx and larynx, throat
- 咽炎 (yān yán) — pharyngitis
- 吞咽 (tūn yàn) — to swallow

## Example Sentences

- 咽炎患者吞咽困难。(Yān yán huàn zhě tūn yàn kùn nan.) — Pharyngitis patients have difficulty swallowing.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: yān (pharynx) and yàn (swallow).
