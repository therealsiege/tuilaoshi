# 涝 Waterlogging, flood

- **Character**: 涝
- **Pinyin**: lào
- **Meaning**: waterlogging, flood
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 水涝 (shuǐ lào) — waterlogging, flood
- 涝灾 (lào zāi) — flood disaster

## Example Sentences

- 连续暴雨造成了严重的涝灾。(Lián xù bào yǔ zào chéng le yán zhòng de lào zāi.) — Continuous heavy rain caused severe flooding.

## Notes

Excessive water accumulation
