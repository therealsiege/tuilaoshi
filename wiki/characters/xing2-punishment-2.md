# 刑 Punishment

- **Character**: 刑
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: punishment, penalty, sentence
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刑法 (xíng fǎ) — criminal law
- 死刑 (sǐ xíng) — death penalty

## Example Sentences

- 他被判处有期徒刑。(Tā bèi pàn chǔ yǒu qī tú xíng.) — He was sentenced to a fixed-term imprisonment.

## Notes

刑 shows punishment with a knife, representing legal penalties.
