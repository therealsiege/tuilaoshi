# 侦 Scout, detect, investigate

- **Character**: 侦
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: scout, detect, investigate
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 侦查 (zhēnchá) — investigate (criminal case)
- 侦探 (zhēntàn) — detective

## Example Sentences

- 警方正在侦查案件。(Jǐngfāng zhèngzài zhēnchá ànjiàn.) — The police are investigating the case.

## Notes

Police and legal term for criminal investigation.
