# 焊 Weld, solder

- **Character**: 焊
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: weld, solder
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 焊接 (hàn jiē) — welding
- 电焊 (diàn hàn) — electric welding

## Example Sentences

- 机器人正在焊接。(Jī qì rén zhèng zài hàn jiē.) — The robot is welding.

## Notes

Used in 焊接 (welding), 焊工 (welder), manufacturing
