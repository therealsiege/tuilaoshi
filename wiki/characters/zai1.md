# 灾 Disaster

- **Character**: 灾
- **Pinyin**: zāi
- **Meaning**: disaster, calamity
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灾难 (zāi nàn) — disaster
- 火灾 (huǒ zāi) — fire disaster

## Example Sentences

- 地震是自然灾难。(Dì zhèn shì zì rán zāi nàn.) — Earthquakes are natural disasters.

## Notes

灾 simplified from 災. Contains fire radical 火, as fire was one of the most destructive disasters in ancient times.
