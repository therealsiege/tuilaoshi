# 猛 Fierce

- **Character**: 猛
- **Pinyin**: měng
- **Meaning**: fierce, sudden, vigorous
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猛烈 (měng liè) — fierce
- 勇猛 (yǒng měng) — brave and fierce

## Example Sentences

- 风刮得很猛。(Fēng guā de hěn měng.) — The wind is blowing fiercely.

## Notes

猛 uses dog/beast radical 犭 with 孟 (eldest), representing fierce animal-like strength and intensity.
