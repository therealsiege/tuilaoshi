# 中 Hit the mark, win (bid)

- **Character**: 中
- **Pinyin**: zhòng
- **Meaning**: hit the mark, win (bid)
- **Radical**: 丨 (line)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 中标 (zhòngbiāo) — win a bid
- 中奖 (zhòngjiǎng) — win a prize

## Example Sentences

- 我们公司中标了。(Wǒmen gōngsī zhòngbiāo le.) — Our company won the bid.

## Notes

Pronunciation zhòng means to hit or win; zhōng means middle/center.
