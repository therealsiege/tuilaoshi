# 国 Country/Nation

- **Character**: 国
- **Pinyin**: guó
- **Meaning**: country, nation, state
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 中国 (Zhōngguó) — China
- 美国 (Měiguó) — United States
- 国家 (guójiā) — country, nation
- 外国 (wàiguó) — foreign country
- 国际 (guójì) — international

## Example Sentences

- 你是哪国人？(Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?) — What country are you from?
- 中国是一个大国。(Zhōngguó shì yí gè dà guó.) — China is a large country.
- 他去过很多国家。(Tā qù guò hěn duō guójiā.) — He has been to many countries.

## Notes

国 is the simplified form of 國. The traditional form shows 囗 (enclosure/border) surrounding 或 (a region defended by a weapon 戈). The simplified form places 玉 (jade, a symbol of treasure) inside 囗, suggesting "the precious territory within borders." Most country names in Chinese use 国: 英国 (Yīngguó, UK), 法国 (Fǎguó, France), 德国 (Déguó, Germany).
