# 觑 To look at, to glance at

- **Character**: 觑
- **Pinyin**: qù
- **Meaning**: to look at, to glance at
- **Radical**: 见(see)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 小觑 (xiǎo qù) — to look down on, to underestimate
- 面面相觑 (miàn miàn xiāng qù) — to look at each other in dismay

## Example Sentences

- 他们面面相觑，不知所措。(Tāmen miànmiàn-xiāngqù, bù zhī suǒ cuò.) — They looked at each other in dismay, not knowing what to do.

## Notes

Literary term for looking, often in idioms.
