# 城 City

- **Character**: 城
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: city, town, wall
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 城市 (chéng shì) — city
- 长城 (cháng chéng) — Great Wall

## Example Sentences

- 这个城市很大。(Zhè ge chéng shì hěn dà.) — This city is very big.

## Notes

城 originally meant city wall, extended to mean the city itself.
