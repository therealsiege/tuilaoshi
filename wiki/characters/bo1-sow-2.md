# 播 To Broadcast

- **Character**: 播
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to sow, to broadcast
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 广播 (guǎng bō) — broadcast
- 播放 (bō fàng) — to broadcast

## Example Sentences

- 电台正在播放音乐。(Diàn tái zhèng zài bō fàng yīn yuè.) — The radio station is broadcasting music.

## Notes

播 combines hand 扌 with 番 (turn), originally scattering seeds, extended to broadcasting.
