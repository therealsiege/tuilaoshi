# 也 Also

- **Character**: 也
- **Pinyin**: yě
- **Meaning**: also, too
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 也许 (yě xǔ) — perhaps
- 也好 (yě hǎo) — that's fine too

## Example Sentences

- 我也是学生。(Wǒ yě shì xué shēng.) — I am also a student.

## Notes

也 always comes before the verb, after the subject. 我也去 (I'll go too), not *我去也.
