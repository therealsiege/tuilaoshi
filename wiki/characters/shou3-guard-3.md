# 守 Guard

- **Character**: 守
- **Pinyin**: shǒu
- **Meaning**: to guard, defend
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 守门员 (shǒu mén yuán) — goalkeeper
- 防守 (fáng shǒu) — to defend

## Example Sentences

- 他是个优秀的守门员。(Tā shì gè yōuxiù de shǒuményuán.) — He is an excellent goalkeeper.

## Notes

Defending and guarding in sports; key defensive term
