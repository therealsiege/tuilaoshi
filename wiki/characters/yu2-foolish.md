# 愚 Foolish

- **Character**: 愚
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: foolish, stupid
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 愚公移山 (yú gōng yí shān) — perseverance conquers all
- 愚蠢 (yú chǔn) — foolish, stupid

## Example Sentences

- 愚公移山的精神令人敬佩。(Yú gōng yí shān de jīngshén lìng rén jìngpèi.) — The spirit of the Foolish Old Man moving mountains is admirable.

## Notes

Used in 愚公移山: the seemingly foolish old man whose persistence moved mountains.
