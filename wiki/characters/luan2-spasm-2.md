# 挛 Cramp

- **Character**: 挛
- **Pinyin**: luán
- **Meaning**: spasm, cramp
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 痉挛 (jìng luán) — spasm, convulsion
- 抽挛 (chōu luán) — cramp, spasm

## Example Sentences

- 小腿抽挛很常见。(Xiǎo tuǐ chōu luán hěn cháng jiàn.) — Calf cramps are very common.

## Notes

Painful muscle contractions; often nocturnal.
