# 晴 Clear, sunny, fine

- **Character**: 晴
- **Pinyin**: qíng
- **Meaning**: clear, sunny, fine
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 晴天 (qíng tiān) — sunny day
- 晴朗 (qíng lǎng) — clear, sunny

## Example Sentences

- 今天是个晴朗的好天气。(Jīn tiān shì ge qíng lǎng de hǎo tiān qì.) — Today is a clear and beautiful day.

## Notes

Clear weather conditions
