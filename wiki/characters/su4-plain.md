# 素 Plain

- **Character**: 素
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: plain, vegetarian, element
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 素质 (sù zhì) — quality, caliber
- 朴素 (pǔ sù) — simple, plain

## Example Sentences

- 他的素质很高。(Tā de sù zhì hěn gāo.) — His quality/caliber is very high.

## Notes

素 shows raw silk threads 糸, representing the natural, unbleached state, hence plain or basic.
