# 蛇 Snake

- **Character**: 蛇
- **Pinyin**: shé
- **Meaning**: snake, serpent
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 蛇年 (shé nián) — Year of the Snake
- 毒蛇 (dú shé) — poisonous snake

## Example Sentences

- 我怕蛇。(Wǒ pà shé.) — I'm afraid of snakes.

## Notes

蛇 uses insect radical 虫. One of 12 zodiac animals, represents wisdom and mystery.
