# 拆 To Demolish

- **Character**: 拆
- **Pinyin**: chāi
- **Meaning**: to tear open, to demolish
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拆开 (chāi kāi) — to open (a package)
- 拆除 (chāi chú) — to demolish

## Example Sentences

- 他拆开了礼物。(Tā chāi kāi le lǐ wù.) — He opened the gift.

## Notes

拆 means to take apart or open up. 拆迁 = demolition and relocation.
