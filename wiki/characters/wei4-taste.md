# 味 Taste, flavor, smell

- **Character**: 味
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: taste, flavor, smell
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 味道 (wèi dào) — taste, flavor, smell
- 意味 (yì wèi) — to signify, meaning

## Example Sentences

- 这道菜味道不错。(Zhè dào cài wèidào bùcuò.) — This dish tastes good.

## Notes

Used for physical tastes and abstract flavors or implications.
