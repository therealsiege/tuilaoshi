# 燕 Swallow (bird), Yan (state)

- **Character**: 燕
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: swallow (bird), Yan (state)
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 燕子 (yàn zi) — swallow
- 燕国 (yàn guó) — Yan state

## Example Sentences

- 春天燕子从南方飞回来了。(Chūn tiān yàn zi cóng nán fāng fēi huí lái le.) — In spring, swallows fly back from the south.

## Notes

Also name of ancient state during Warring States period
