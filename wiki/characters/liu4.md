# 六 Six

- **Character**: 六
- **Pinyin**: liù
- **Meaning**: six
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 六月 (liù yuè) — June
- 六十 (liù shí) — sixty

## Example Sentences

- 他六岁了。(Tā liù suì le.) — He is six years old.

## Notes

六 is considered a lucky number in Chinese culture. 六六大顺 means 'everything goes smoothly.'
