# 犀 Rhinoceros, sharp

- **Character**: 犀
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: rhinoceros, sharp
- **Radical**: 牛 (ox)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 犀利 (xī lì) — sharp, incisive
- 犀牛 (xī niú) — rhinoceros

## Example Sentences

- 他的观点十分犀利。(Tā de guān diǎn shí fēn xī lì.) — His views are very sharp.

## Notes

Sharpness and keenness
