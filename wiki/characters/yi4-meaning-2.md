# 意 Meaning, intention, idea

- **Character**: 意
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: meaning, intention, idea
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 意思 (yì si) — meaning, intention
- 注意 (zhù yì) — to pay attention

## Example Sentences

- 你的意思我明白了。(Nǐ de yìsi wǒ míngbai le.) — I understand what you mean.

## Notes

Fundamental character for meaning, intention, and mental concepts.
