# 纠 Correct, investigate, entangle

- **Character**: 纠
- **Pinyin**: jiū
- **Meaning**: correct, investigate, entangle
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 纠纷 (jiū fēn) — dispute, dispute
- 纠正 (jiū zhèng) — to correct

## Example Sentences

- 双方发生了纠纷。(Shuāng fāng fā shēng le jiū fēn.) — A dispute arose between the two parties.

## Notes

Used for disputes and corrections. 纠察 means to patrol and inspect. 纠缠 means to pester.
