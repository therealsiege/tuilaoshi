# 废 Waste, discard

- **Character**: 废
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: waste, discard
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 废料 (fèi liào) — waste material
- 废品 (fèi pǐn) — waste product

## Example Sentences

- 废料可以回收。(Fèi liào kě yǐ huí shōu.) — Waste material can be recycled.

## Notes

Used in 废料 (waste), 废弃 (discard), 废品 (scrap)
