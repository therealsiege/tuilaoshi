# 衷 Inner Feelings, 由衷 Heartfelt

- **Character**: 衷
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: inner feelings, 由衷 heartfelt
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 由衷 (yóu zhōng) — heartfelt, sincere
- 衷心 (zhōng xīn) — heartfelt, wholehearted
- 苦衷 (kǔ zhōng) — secret trouble

## Example Sentences

- 我表示由衷的感谢。(Wǒ biǎoshì yóuzhōng de gǎnxiè.) — I express my heartfelt thanks.

## Notes

Formal literary term for sincere feelings from the heart. 由衷 and 衷心 express genuine, heartfelt emotions.
