# 瓣 Petal

- **Character**: 瓣
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: petal, segment
- **Radical**: 瓜 (melon)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 花瓣 (huā bàn) — petal
- 蒜瓣 (suàn bàn) — garlic clove

## Example Sentences

- 玫瑰花瓣很柔软。(Méi gui huā bàn hěn róu ruǎn.) — Rose petals are very soft.

## Notes

瓣 has melon 瓜 with 辡 phonetic, segmented parts of flower or fruit.
