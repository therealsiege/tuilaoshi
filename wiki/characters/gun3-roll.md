# 滚 Roll

- **Character**: 滚
- **Pinyin**: gǔn
- **Meaning**: to roll, tumble
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 翻滚 (fān gǔn) — to roll, tumble
- 滚动 (gǔn dòng) — to roll

## Example Sentences

- 球滚到了场外。(Qiú gǔn dào le chǎngwài.) — The ball rolled out of bounds.

## Notes

Rolling motion; used in tumbling and ball movement
