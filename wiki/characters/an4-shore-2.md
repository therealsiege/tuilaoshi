# 岸 Shore

- **Character**: 岸
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: shore, bank, coast
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 岸边 (àn biān) — waterside
- 海岸 (hǎi àn) — seashore

## Example Sentences

- 他站在河岸边。(Tā zhàn zài hé àn biān.) — He stands by the river bank.

## Notes

岸 has mountain 山 with cliff 厂, high bank or shore.
