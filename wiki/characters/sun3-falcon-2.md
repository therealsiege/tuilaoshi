# 隼 Falcon

- **Character**: 隼
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: falcon
- **Radical**: 隹 (short-tailed bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 游隼 (yóu sǔn) — peregrine falcon
- 隼鸟 (sǔn niǎo) — falcon

## Example Sentences

- 隼是速度最快的鸟。(Sǔn shì sù dù zuì kuài de niǎo.) — Falcons are the fastest birds.

## Notes

隼 has bird 隹 below 十 (ten), a bird of prey.
