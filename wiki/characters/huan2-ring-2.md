# 环 Ring

- **Character**: 环
- **Pinyin**: huán
- **Meaning**: ring, to surround, environment
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 环境 (huán jìng) — environment
- 环绕 (huán rào) — to surround

## Example Sentences

- 我们要保护环境。(Wǒ men yào bǎo hù huán jìng.) — We must protect the environment.

## Notes

环 simplified from 環. Jade 王 ring, circular shape.
