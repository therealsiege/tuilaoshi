# 裁 To Cut

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut (cloth), to reduce, to judge
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁剪 (cái jiǎn) — to tailor
- 制裁 (zhì cái) — sanctions

## Example Sentences

- 裁缝在裁剪布料。(Cái féng zài cái jiǎn bù liào.) — The tailor is cutting fabric.

## Notes

裁 combines 衣 (clothing) with knife, representing cutting cloth for tailoring.
