# 钻 To drill, to bore, to get into

- **Character**: 钻
- **Pinyin**: zuān
- **Meaning**: to drill, to bore, to get into
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钻研 (zuān yán) — to study intensively
- 钻孔 (zuān kǒng) — to drill a hole

## Example Sentences

- 他在钻研数学难题。(Tā zài zuānyán shùxué nántí.) — He is intensively studying difficult math problems.

## Notes

Pronounced zuān for drilling; zuàn for diamond.
