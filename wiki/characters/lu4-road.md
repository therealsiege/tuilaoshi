# 路 Road

- **Character**: 路
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: road, path, way
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 马路 (mǎ lù) — street
- 道路 (dào lù) — road, path

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很长。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn cháng.) — This road is very long.

## Notes

路 combines 足 (foot) and 各 (each). Roads are where each person walks. Common in compounds for street/road.
