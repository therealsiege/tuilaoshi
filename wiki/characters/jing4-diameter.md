# 径 Diameter, path, direct

- **Character**: 径
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: diameter, path, direct
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 直径 (zhí jìng) — diameter
- 半径 (bàn jìng) — radius

## Example Sentences

- 圆的直径是半径的两倍。(Yuán de zhí jìng shì bàn jìng de liǎng bèi.) — The diameter of a circle is twice the radius.

## Notes

Measurement across circle
