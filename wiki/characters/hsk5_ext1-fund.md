# 款 Fund, Clause, 条款 Clause

- **Character**: 款
- **Pinyin**: kuǎn
- **Meaning**: fund, clause, 条款 clause
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 条款 (tiáo kuǎn) — clause, article
- 款项 (kuǎn xiàng) — sum of money
- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan

## Example Sentences

- 请仔细阅读合同条款。(Qǐng zǐxì yuèdú hétong tiáokuǎn.) — Please read the contract clauses carefully.

## Notes

Commonly used in financial contexts (款项 - funds) and legal documents (条款 - clauses). Also means 'style' or 'model' for products.
