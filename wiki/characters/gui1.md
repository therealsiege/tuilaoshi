# 规 Rule

- **Character**: 规
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: rule, regulation, to advise
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 规定 (guī dìng) — regulation, to stipulate
- 规则 (guī zé) — rule, regulation

## Example Sentences

- 这是学校的规定。(Zhè shì xué xiào de guī dìng.) — This is the school's regulation.

## Notes

规 contains 见 (to see), representing guidelines or standards that should be observed.
