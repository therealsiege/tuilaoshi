# 雪 Snow

- **Character**: 雪
- **Pinyin**: xuě
- **Meaning**: snow
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 下雪 (xià xuě) — to snow
- 雪花 (xuě huā) — snowflake

## Example Sentences

- 冬天会下雪。(Dōng tiān huì xià xuě.) — It snows in winter.

## Notes

雪 combines rain 雨 and hand sweeping 彐 - snow you must sweep away.
