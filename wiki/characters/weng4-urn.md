# 瓮 Urn, jar, earthen pot

- **Character**: 瓮
- **Pinyin**: wèng
- **Meaning**: urn, jar, earthen pot
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 瓮中捉鳖 (wèng zhōng zhuō biē) — catch a turtle in a jar (easy prey)
- 水瓮 (shuǐ wèng) — water urn

## Example Sentences

- 他们已经是瓮中之鳖了。(Tā men yǐ jīng shì wèng zhōng zhī biē le.) — They are already like turtles in a jar (trapped).

## Notes

Large earthen jar; used in idioms.
