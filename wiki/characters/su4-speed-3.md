# 速 Speed

- **Character**: 速
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: speed, fast
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 速度 (sù dù) — speed, velocity
- 快速 (kuài sù) — fast, rapid

## Example Sentences

- 他跑步的速度很快。(Tā pǎobù de sùdù hěn kuài.) — His running speed is very fast.

## Notes

Speed and velocity in sports; critical for racing and timed events
