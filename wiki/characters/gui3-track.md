# 轨 Track, orbit, rail

- **Character**: 轨
- **Pinyin**: guǐ
- **Meaning**: track, orbit, rail
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 轨道 (guǐ dào) — orbit, track
- 铁轨 (tiě guǐ) — railway track

## Example Sentences

- 卫星沿着轨道运行。(Wèi xīng yán zhe guǐ dào yùn xíng.) — The satellite moves along its orbit.

## Notes

Used for orbits in astronomy and tracks
