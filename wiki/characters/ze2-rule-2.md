# 则 Rule

- **Character**: 则
- **Pinyin**: zé
- **Meaning**: rule, then, in that case
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 规则 (guī zé) — rule
- 原则 (yuán zé) — principle

## Example Sentences

- 我们要遵守游戏规则。(Wǒ men yào zūn shǒu yóu xì guī zé.) — We must follow the game rules.

## Notes

则 simplified from 則. Knife 刂 cutting 贝 (cowrie), marking standards.
