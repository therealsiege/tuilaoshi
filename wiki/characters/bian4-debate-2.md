# 辩 Debate, argue, defend

- **Character**: 辩
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: debate, argue, defend
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 辩护 (biàn hù) — to defend (in court)
- 辩论 (biàn lùn) — to debate

## Example Sentences

- 律师为被告辩护。(Lǜ shī wèi bèi gào biàn hù.) — The lawyer defended the defendant.

## Notes

Used in legal defense and debates. 辩解 means to explain or justify. 答辩 means to reply to a charge.
