# 草 Grass

- **Character**: 草
- **Pinyin**: cǎo
- **Meaning**: grass, straw, draft
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 草地 (cǎo dì) — grassland, lawn
- 草稿 (cǎo gǎo) — draft, sketch

## Example Sentences

- 草地上有很多花。(Cǎo dì shàng yǒu hěn duō huā.) — There are many flowers on the lawn.

## Notes

草 uses the grass radical 艹. Also means draft/rough version (草稿 draft manuscript, 草书 cursive script).
