# 词 Word

- **Character**: 词
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: word, term, lyrics
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 生词 (shēng cí) — new word
- 词语 (cí yǔ) — words and phrases

## Example Sentences

- 今天学了几个生词。(Jīn tiān xué le jǐ ge shēng cí.) — Today we learned a few new words.

## Notes

词 refers to individual words or song lyrics. 词典 is a dictionary of words.
