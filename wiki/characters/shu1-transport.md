# 输 Transport, lose

- **Character**: 输
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: transport, lose
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 运输 (yùn shū) — transport
- 输出 (shū chū) — export, output

## Example Sentences

- 公司主要做进出口运输。(Gōng sī zhǔ yào zuò jìn chū kǒu yùn shū.) — The company mainly does import-export transportation.

## Notes

输入 means import/input. 运输业 is transportation industry.
