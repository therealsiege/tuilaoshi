# 浮 Float

- **Character**: 浮
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: to float
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 漂浮 (piāo fú) — to float, drift
- 浮力 (fú lì) — buoyancy

## Example Sentences

- 学游泳先要学会浮在水面上。(Xué yóuyǒng xiān yào xuéhuì fú zài shuǐmiàn shàng.) — To learn swimming, first learn to float on the water surface.

## Notes

Floating; fundamental swimming skill
