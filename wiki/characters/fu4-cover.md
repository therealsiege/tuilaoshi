# 覆 To Cover

- **Character**: 覆
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to cover, to overturn
- **Radical**: 覀 (west)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 颠覆 (diān fù) — to overturn
- 覆盖 (fù gài) — to cover

## Example Sentences

- 雪覆盖了整个城市。(Xuě fù gài le zhěng gè chéng shì.) — Snow covered the entire city.

## Notes

覆 originally depicted something turned over to cover. Used in both literal (covering) and figurative (overturning) contexts.
