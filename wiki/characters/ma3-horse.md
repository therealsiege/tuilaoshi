# 马 Horse

- **Character**: 马
- **Pinyin**: mǎ
- **Meaning**: horse
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 马上 (mǎ shàng) — immediately, right away
- 骑马 (qí mǎ) — to ride a horse

## Example Sentences

- 我马上就来。(Wǒ mǎ shàng jiù lái.) — I'll be right there.

## Notes

马 simplified from 馬, pictograph. 马上 literally 'on horseback' = immediately.
