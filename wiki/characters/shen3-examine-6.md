# 审 Examine, try, review

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: examine, try,审
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěnchá) — examine, review
- 审判 (shěnpàn) — trial, try (in court)

## Example Sentences

- 法院正在审理此案。(Fǎyuàn zhèngzài shěnlǐ cǐ àn.) — The court is trying this case.

## Notes

Legal term for examination and trial.
