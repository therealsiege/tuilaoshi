# 徙 Move, migrate, shift

- **Character**: 徙
- **Pinyin**: xǐ
- **Meaning**: move, migrate, shift
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 迁徙 (qiān xǐ) — migrate
- 徙居 (xǐ jū) — move residence

## Example Sentences

- 鲑鱼会长距离迁徙产卵。(Guī yú huì cháng jù lí qiān xǐ chǎn luǎn.) — Salmon migrate long distances to spawn.
