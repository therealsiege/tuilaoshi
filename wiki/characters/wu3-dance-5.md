# 舞 Dance

- **Character**: 舞
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: to dance, dance
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 龙飞凤舞 (lóng fēi fèng wǔ) — lively, flamboyant
- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — to dance

## Example Sentences

- 她的舞姿很优美。(Tā de wǔzī hěn yōuměi.) — Her dancing posture is very graceful.

## Notes

Used in 龙飞凤舞: describes the dancing motion of the phoenix.
