# 抹 To Wipe

- **Character**: 抹
- **Pinyin**: mǒ
- **Meaning**: to wipe, to smear, to erase
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to apply
- 抹布 (mā bù) — cleaning cloth

## Example Sentences

- 她在脸上抹了护肤霜。(Tā zài liǎn shàng mǒ le hù fū shuāng.) — She applied skin cream on her face.

## Notes

抹 can be mǒ (to wipe, to smear), mā (to wipe), or mò (to plaster). Most commonly mǒ for applying substances.
