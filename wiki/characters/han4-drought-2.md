# 旱 Drought

- **Character**: 旱
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: drought, dry land
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 干旱 (gān hàn) — drought
- 旱灾 (hàn zāi) — drought disaster

## Example Sentences

- 今年发生了严重的干旱。(Jīn nián fā shēng le yán zhòng de gān hàn.) — There was a severe drought this year.

## Notes

旱 has sun 日 over 干 (dry), lack of water, drought.
