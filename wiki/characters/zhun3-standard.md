# 准 Standard

- **Character**: 准
- **Pinyin**: zhǔn
- **Meaning**: standard, accurate, to allow, certainly
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 标准 (biāo zhǔn) — standard
- 准备 (zhǔn bèi) — to prepare

## Example Sentences

- 你准备好了吗？(Nǐ zhǔn bèi hǎo le ma?) — Are you ready?

## Notes

准 represents accuracy or standard. Common in 标准 (standard), 准备 (prepare), and as permission (准许).
