# 涂 To Smear

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: to smear, to apply, to paint
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to apply, to smear
- 涂料 (tú liào) — paint, coating

## Example Sentences

- 他在墙上涂油漆。(Tā zài qiáng shàng tú yóu qī.) — He is painting on the wall.

## Notes

涂 means to apply or smear substances like paint, cream, or paste onto surfaces.
