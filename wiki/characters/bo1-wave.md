# 波 Wave, ripple

- **Character**: 波
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: wave, ripple
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 波长 (bō cháng) — wavelength
- 声波 (shēng bō) — sound wave

## Example Sentences

- 光以波的形式传播。(Guāng yǐ bō de xíng shì chuán bō.) — Light propagates in the form of waves.

## Notes

Fundamental concept in wave physics
