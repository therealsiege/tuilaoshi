# 拧 To Twist

- **Character**: 拧
- **Pinyin**: níng
- **Meaning**: to twist, to wring
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拧干 (níng gān) — to wring dry
- 拧开 (níng kāi) — to unscrew

## Example Sentences

- 请把毛巾拧干。(Qǐng bǎ máo jīn níng gān.) — Please wring out the towel.

## Notes

拧 simplified from 擰. Hand radical 扌 representing twisting motion with hands.
