# 莹 Luster

- **Character**: 莹
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: luster, bright and clean
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晶莹 (jīng yíng) — sparkling, crystal clear
- 莹润 (yíng rùn) — lustrous

## Example Sentences

- 露珠晶莹剔透。(Lùzhū jīngyíng tītòu.) — The dewdrops are sparkling and crystal clear.

## Notes

莹 combines grass 艹 with jade 玉 components, representing the lustrous quality of polished jade or gems.
