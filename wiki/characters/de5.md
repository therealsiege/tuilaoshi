# 的 Possessive/Adjective Marker

- **Character**: 的
- **Pinyin**: de
- **Meaning**: possessive particle; adjective marker; nominalizer
- **Radical**: 白 (white)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 我的 (wǒ de) — my, mine
- 目的 (mùdì) — purpose, goal
- 有的 (yǒu de) — some (of them)
- 是...的 (shì...de) — emphasis construction for past events
- 真的 (zhēn de) — really, truly

## Example Sentences

- 这是我的书。(Zhè shì wǒ de shū.) — This is my book.
- 红色的花很漂亮。(Hóngsè de huā hěn piàoliàng.) — The red flowers are beautiful.
- 他说的对。(Tā shuō de duì.) — What he said is correct.

## Notes

的 is the single most frequently used character in modern Chinese. It serves three main roles: (1) possessive marker (我的 "my"), (2) adjective-to-noun linker (漂亮的花 "beautiful flower"), (3) nominalizer turning verbs/clauses into noun phrases (吃的 "something to eat"). Don't confuse with 地 (de, adverb marker) or 得 (de, complement marker).
