# 抛 Toss

- **Character**: 抛
- **Pinyin**: pāo
- **Meaning**: to toss, throw
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抛球 (pāo qiú) — to toss a ball
- 抛弃 (pāo qì) — to abandon

## Example Sentences

- 他把球高高抛起。(Tā bǎ qiú gāogāo pāo qǐ.) — He tossed the ball high up.

## Notes

Tossing upward; used in serves and throws
