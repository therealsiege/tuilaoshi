# 画 To Draw

- **Character**: 画
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to draw, to paint, painting, picture
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 画画 (huà huà) — to draw, to paint
- 油画 (yóu huà) — oil painting

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢画画。(Tā xǐ huān huà huà.) — She likes to draw.

## Notes

画 functions as both verb (to draw/paint) and noun (painting/drawing). The character itself depicts a hand drawing boundaries.
