# 麻 Numb, hemp, sesame

- **Character**: 麻
- **Pinyin**: má
- **Meaning**: numb, hemp, sesame
- **Radical**: 麻 (hemp)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 麻木 (má mù) — numb, insensitive
- 芝麻 (zhī ma) — sesame

## Example Sentences

- 我的腿坐麻了。(Wǒ de tuǐ zuò má le.) — My leg has gone numb from sitting.

## Notes

Numbness sensation, also hemp plant and sesame.
