# 函 Letter

- **Character**: 函
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: envelope, letter
- **Radical**: 凵 (receptacle)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 函件 (hán jiàn) — letter
- 信函 (xìn hán) — letter

## Example Sentences

- 他寄来了一封信函。(Tā jì lái le yì fēng xìn hán.) — He sent a letter.

## Notes

函 has receptacle 凵 containing something, envelope or letter.
