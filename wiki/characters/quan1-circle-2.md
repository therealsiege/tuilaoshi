# 圈 Circle

- **Character**: 圈
- **Pinyin**: quān
- **Meaning**: circle, ring, to circle
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 圆圈 (yuán quān) — circle
- 画圈 (huà quān) — to draw circles

## Example Sentences

- 他在地图上画了一个圈。(Tā zài dì tú shàng huà le yí gè quān.) — He drew a circle on the map.

## Notes

圈 has enclosure 囗 with 卷 (scroll), circular boundary.
