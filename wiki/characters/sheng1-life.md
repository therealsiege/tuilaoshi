# 生 Life

- **Character**: 生
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: life, birth
- **Radical**: 生 (life)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 卫生 (wèi shēng) — hygiene, sanitation
- 医生 (yī shēng) — doctor
- 生病 (shēng bìng) — to get sick

## Example Sentences

- 讲究卫生可以预防疾病。(Jiǎng jiu wèi shēng kě yǐ yù fáng jí bìng.) — Practicing hygiene can prevent disease.

## Notes

Life or birth; fundamental health concept.
