# 宽 Wide, broad, lenient

- **Character**: 宽
- **Pinyin**: kuān
- **Meaning**: wide, broad, lenient
- **Radical**: 宀(roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽阔 (kuān kuò) — wide, broad
- 宽容 (kuān róng) — tolerant, lenient

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间很宽敞。(Zhège fángjiān hěn kuānchang.) — This room is very spacious.

## Notes

Used for width or leniency/tolerance.
