# 螂 Mantis, cockroach

- **Character**: 螂
- **Pinyin**: láng
- **Meaning**: mantis, cockroach
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 螳螂 (táng láng) — mantis
- 蟑螂 (zhāng láng) — cockroach

## Example Sentences

- 厨房里有蟑螂。(Chúfáng lǐ yǒu zhāngláng.) — There are cockroaches in the kitchen.

## Notes

Used in 螳螂 (mantis) and 蟑螂 (cockroach). Insect radical.
