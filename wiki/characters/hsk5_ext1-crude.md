# 陋 Crude, Shabby, 简陋 Simple And Crude

- **Character**: 陋
- **Pinyin**: lòu
- **Meaning**: crude, shabby, 简陋 simple and crude
- **Radical**: ⻏ (city)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 简陋 (jiǎn lòu) — simple and crude
- 陋习 (lòu xí) — bad habit
- 丑陋 (chǒu lòu) — ugly

## Example Sentences

- 这里的设施比较简陋。(Zhèlǐ de shèshī bǐjiào jiǎnlòu.) — The facilities here are rather crude.

## Notes

Describes poor quality, crudeness, or bad customs. Often used to criticize outdated or improper practices.
