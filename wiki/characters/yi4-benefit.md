# 益 Benefit

- **Character**: 益
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: benefit, profit, to increase
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 利益 (lì yì) — benefit, interest
- 有益 (yǒu yì) — beneficial

## Example Sentences

- 这对你很有益。(Zhè duì nǐ hěn yǒu yì.) — This is very beneficial to you.

## Notes

益 shows water overflowing a dish, representing增ase or benefit. Used for advantages and profits.
