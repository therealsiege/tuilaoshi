# 改 To Change

- **Character**: 改
- **Pinyin**: gǎi
- **Meaning**: to change, to alter, to correct
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 改变 (gǎi biàn) — to change
- 改正 (gǎi zhèng) — to correct

## Example Sentences

- 情况已经改变了。(Qíng kuàng yǐ jīng gǎi biàn le.) — The situation has changed.

## Notes

改 shows forceful action to make changes or corrections.
