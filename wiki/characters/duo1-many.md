# 多 Many

- **Character**: 多
- **Pinyin**: duō
- **Meaning**: many, much, more
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 多少 (duō shǎo) — how many, how much
- 很多 (hěn duō) — very many

## Example Sentences

- 这里人很多。(Zhè lǐ rén hěn duō.) — There are many people here.

## Notes

多 originally showed two pieces of meat (夕夕), suggesting abundance. Opposite of 少.
