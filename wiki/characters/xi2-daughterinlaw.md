# 媳 Daughter-in-law, son's wife

- **Character**: 媳
- **Pinyin**: xí
- **Meaning**: daughter-in-law, son's wife
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 媳妇 (xí fù) — daughter-in-law, wife
- 儿媳 (ér xí) — daughter-in-law

## Example Sentences

- 她是我的儿媳。(Tā shì wǒ de érxí.) — She is my daughter-in-law.

## Notes

Specifically refers to son's wife.
