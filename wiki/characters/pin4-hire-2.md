# 聘 Hire, engage

- **Character**: 聘
- **Pinyin**: pìn
- **Meaning**: hire, engage
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 聘请 (pìn qǐng) — engage, hire
- 招聘 (zhāo pìn) — recruit, hire

## Example Sentences

- 我们正在招聘新员工。(Wǒ men zhèng zài zhāo pìn xīn yuán gōng.) — We are recruiting new employees.

## Notes

Used in formal hiring contexts. 聘书 is an appointment letter.
