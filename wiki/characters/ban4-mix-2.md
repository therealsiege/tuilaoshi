# 拌 Mix, blend, stir

- **Character**: 拌
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: mix, blend, stir
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — stir, mix
- 拌匀 (bàn yún) — mix evenly

## Example Sentences

- 把这些材料搅拌均匀。(Bǎ zhè xiē cái liào jiǎo bàn jūn yún.) — Mix these materials evenly.

## Notes

Process of mixing substances
