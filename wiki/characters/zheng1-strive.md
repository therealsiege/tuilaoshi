# 争 To Strive

- **Character**: 争
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: to strive, to compete, to argue
- **Radical**: ⺈ (down stroke)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 竞争 (jìng zhēng) — to compete
- 争论 (zhēng lùn) — to argue

## Example Sentences

- 他们在争论这个问题。(Tā men zài zhēng lùn zhè gè wèn tí.) — They are arguing about this issue.

## Notes

争 simplified from 爭. Shows two hands pulling on an object from opposite sides, representing competition or conflict.
