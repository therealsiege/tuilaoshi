# 伤 Injury

- **Character**: 伤
- **Pinyin**: shāng
- **Meaning**: injury, wound
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 受伤 (shòu shāng) — to be injured
- 伤口 (shāng kǒu) — wound
- 烧伤 (shāo shāng) — burn injury

## Example Sentences

- 他受伤了，需要包扎伤口。(Tā shòu shāng le, xū yào bāo zā shāng kǒu.) — He's injured and needs the wound bandaged.

## Notes

General term for injury; very common in medical contexts.
