# 铸 Cast, mold

- **Character**: 铸
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: cast, mold
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铸造 (zhù zào) — casting, foundry
- 铸件 (zhù jiàn) — casting (part)

## Example Sentences

- 铸造工艺很复杂。(Zhù zào gōng yì hěn fù zá.) — Casting technology is complex.

## Notes

Used in 铸造 (casting), 铸铁 (cast iron), manufacturing
