# 腾 To Soar

- **Character**: 腾
- **Pinyin**: téng
- **Meaning**: to soar, to gallop, to vacate
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 奔腾 (bēn téng) — to surge
- 腾空 (téng kōng) — to soar

## Example Sentences

- 马儿在草原上奔腾。(Mǎ ér zài cǎo yuán shàng bēn téng.) — Horses gallop on the grassland.

## Notes

腾 simplified from 騰. Originally showing a horse leaping, representing soaring or rising.
