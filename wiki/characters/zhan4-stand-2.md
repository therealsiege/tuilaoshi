# 站 Station

- **Character**: 站
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: to stand, station, stop
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 车站 (chē zhàn) — station
- 站台 (zhàn tái) — platform

## Example Sentences

- 我在车站等你。(Wǒ zài chē zhàn děng nǐ.) — I'll wait for you at the station.

## Notes

站 means to stand or station. 火车站 = train station. 网站 = website.
