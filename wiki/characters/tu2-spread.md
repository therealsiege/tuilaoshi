# 涂 To spread, to apply

- **Character**: 涂
- **Pinyin**: tú
- **Meaning**: to spread, to apply
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear, to daub
- 涂黄油 (tú huáng yóu) — to spread butter

## Example Sentences

- 在面包上涂一层黄油。(Zài miànbāo shàng tú yī céng huángyóu.) — Spread a layer of butter on the bread.

## Notes

Used for spreading substances. Water radical from liquid origin.
