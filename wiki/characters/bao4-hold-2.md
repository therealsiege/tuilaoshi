# 抱 Hold

- **Character**: 抱
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: hold, hug
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拥抱 (yōngbào) — hug, embrace
- 抱歉 (bàoqiàn) — sorry

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈抱着孩子。(Māma bàozhe háizi.) — Mom is holding the child.

## Notes

Holding or hugging. Hand radical. 拥抱 is embrace, 抱歉 is apology. Warm, affectionate verb.
