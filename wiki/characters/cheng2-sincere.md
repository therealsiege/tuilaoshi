# 诚 Sincere

- **Character**: 诚
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: sincere, honest
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诚实 (chéng shí) — honest
- 真诚 (zhēn chéng) — sincere

## Example Sentences

- 诚实是最重要的品质。(Chéng shí shì zuì zhòng yào de pǐn zhì.) — Honesty is the most important quality.

## Notes

诚 simplified from 誠. Combines speech 讠 with 成 (complete), representing complete truthfulness in one's words.
