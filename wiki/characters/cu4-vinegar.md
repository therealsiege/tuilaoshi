# 醋 Vinegar

- **Character**: 醋
- **Pinyin**: cù
- **Meaning**: vinegar, jealousy
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine vessel)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 醋酸 (cù suān) — acetic acid
- 吃醋 (chī cù) — to be jealous

## Example Sentences

- 她吃醋了。(Tā chī cù le.) — She got jealous.

## Notes

醋 has wine vessel 酉, representing fermented liquid. 吃醋 = jealousy.
