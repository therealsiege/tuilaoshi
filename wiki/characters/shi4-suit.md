# 适 To Suit

- **Character**: 适
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to suit, to fit, appropriate
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 适合 (shì hé) — to suit, to fit
- 舒适 (shū shì) — comfortable

## Example Sentences

- 这件衣服很适合你。(Zhè jiàn yī fu hěn shì hé nǐ.) — This piece of clothing suits you very well.

## Notes

适 indicates suitability or appropriateness. Common in 适合 (to suit), 适应 (to adapt), 合适 (suitable).
