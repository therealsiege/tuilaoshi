# 扑 Pounce

- **Character**: 扑
- **Pinyin**: pū
- **Meaning**: to pounce, dive
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 扑球 (pū qiú) — to dive for ball (goalkeeper)
- 扑救 (pū jiù) — to make a save

## Example Sentences

- 守门员扑出了点球。(Shǒuményuán pū chū le diǎnqiú.) — The goalkeeper saved the penalty kick.

## Notes

Diving and pouncing; goalkeeper saves
