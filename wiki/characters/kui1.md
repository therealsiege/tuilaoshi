# 亏 To Lose

- **Character**: 亏
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: to lose, deficit, deficient
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 亏损 (kuī sǔn) — loss, deficit
- 吃亏 (chī kuī) — to suffer losses

## Example Sentences

- 这笔生意亏了。(Zhè bǐ shēng yì kuī le.) — This business deal resulted in a loss.

## Notes

亏 simplified from 虧. Originally showed a partial or deficient state, representing loss or shortage.
