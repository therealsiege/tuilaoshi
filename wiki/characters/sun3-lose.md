# 损 Lose, harm

- **Character**: 损
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: lose, harm
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 损失 (sǔn shī) — loss
- 损坏 (sǔn huài) — damage

## Example Sentences

- 地震造成巨大经济损失。(Dì zhèn zào chéng jù dà jīng jì sǔn shī.) — The earthquake caused huge economic losses.

## Notes

损益 means profit and loss. 损耗 means wear and tear/loss.
