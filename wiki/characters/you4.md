# 右 Right

- **Character**: 右
- **Pinyin**: yòu
- **Meaning**: right (side)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 右边 (yòu biān) — right side
- 左右 (zuǒ yòu) — approximately; left and right

## Example Sentences

- 往右转。(Wǎng yòu zhuǎn.) — Turn right.

## Notes

右 originally depicted a hand and a mouth — the right hand used for eating. Opposite of 左 (left).
