# 松 Loose

- **Character**: 松
- **Pinyin**: sōng
- **Meaning**: loose, to loosen, pine tree
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 放松 (fàng sōng) — to relax
- 轻松 (qīng sōng) — relaxed, easy

## Example Sentences

- 周末我喜欢放松一下。(Zhōu mò wǒ xǐ huān fàng sōng yī xià.) — On weekends I like to relax.

## Notes

松 shows wood 木 and 公 (public), as pine trees stand freely. Also means loose or relaxed.
