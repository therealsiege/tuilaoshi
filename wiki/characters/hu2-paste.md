# 糊 Paste, muddled, burnt, 糊 (traditional)

- **Character**: 糊
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: paste, muddled, burnt, 糊 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 糊涂 (hú tu) — muddled, confused
- 糊糊 (hú hu) — paste, gruel

## Example Sentences

- 别糊涂了，好好想想。(Bié hútu le, hǎohao xiǎngxiang.) — Don't be confused, think it through.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: hú (paste/confused), hū (perfunctory), hù (paste).
