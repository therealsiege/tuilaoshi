# 塑 Model, mold, plastic

- **Character**: 塑
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: model, mold, plastic
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture
- 塑造 (sù zào) — mold, shape

## Example Sentences

- 作家成功地塑造了一个复杂的人物形象。(Zuò jiā chéng gōng de sù zào le yī gè fù zá de rén wù xíng xiàng.) — The writer successfully created a complex character.

## Notes

Used for sculpture and shaping/molding concepts.
