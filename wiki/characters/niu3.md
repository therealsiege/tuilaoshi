# 纽 Button, knot, bond

- **Character**: 纽
- **Pinyin**: niǔ
- **Meaning**: button, knot, bond
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纽扣 (niǔ kòu) — button
- 枢纽 (shū niǔ) — hub, pivotal point

## Example Sentences

- 这件衬衫的纽扣掉了一个。(Zhè jiàn chèn shān de niǔ kòu diào le yī ge.) — This shirt lost a button.

## Notes

Means button or connecting point. 纽约 (New York) uses this character phonetically.
