# 败 To Be Defeated

- **Character**: 败
- **Pinyin**: bài
- **Meaning**: to be defeated, to fail
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 失败 (shī bài) — to fail
- 战败 (zhàn bài) — to be defeated in battle

## Example Sentences

- 失败是成功之母。(Shī bài shì chéng gōng zhī mǔ.) — Failure is the mother of success.

## Notes

败 simplified from 敗. Shows shells/money 贝 being struck 攵, representing loss of wealth in defeat.
