# 籽 Seed

- **Character**: 籽
- **Pinyin**: zǐ
- **Meaning**: seed
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 菜籽 (cài zǐ) — rapeseed
- 籽粒 (zǐ lì) — seed, grain

## Example Sentences

- 西瓜里有很多籽。(Xīguā lǐ yǒu hěn duō zǐ.) — There are many seeds in the watermelon.

## Notes

Specifically refers to plant seeds. Rice radical indicates grain origin.
