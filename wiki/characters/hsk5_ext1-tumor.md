# 瘤 Tumor, 肿瘤 Tumor

- **Character**: 瘤
- **Pinyin**: liú
- **Meaning**: tumor, 肿瘤 tumor
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 肿瘤 (zhǒng liú) — tumor
- 瘤子 (liú zi) — tumor, lump
- 脑瘤 (nǎo liú) — brain tumor

## Example Sentences

- 医生发现了一个良性肿瘤。(Yīshēng fāxiàn le yī gè liángxìng zhǒngliú.) — The doctor found a benign tumor.

## Notes

Medical term specifically for tumors and growths. 肿瘤 distinguishes between benign (良性) and malignant (恶性) types.
