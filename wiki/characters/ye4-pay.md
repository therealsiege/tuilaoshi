# 谒 Pay respects, visit

- **Character**: 谒
- **Pinyin**: yè
- **Meaning**: pay respects, visit
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 拜谒 (bàiyè) — pay respects to
- 谒见 (yèjiàn) — have an audience with

## Example Sentences

- 他前往拜谒先烈墓。(Tā qiánwǎng bàiyè xiānliè mù.) — He went to pay respects at the martyrs' tomb.

## Notes

Formal term for visiting superiors or paying respects to the deceased.
