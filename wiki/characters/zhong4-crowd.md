# 众 Crowd, many, multitude

- **Character**: 众
- **Pinyin**: zhòng
- **Meaning**: crowd, many, multitude
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 群众 (qún zhòng) — the masses
- 大众 (dà zhòng) — the public

## Example Sentences

- 众人拾柴火焰高。(Zhòng rén shí chái huǒ yàn gāo.) — Many hands make light work.

## Notes

Three people radicals stacked, representing a crowd
