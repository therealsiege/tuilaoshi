# 松 Pine tree, loose, to loosen, 鬆 (traditional)

- **Character**: 松
- **Pinyin**: sōng
- **Meaning**: pine tree, loose, to loosen, 鬆 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 放松 (fàng sōng) — to relax, loosen
- 松懈 (sōng xiè) — to relax, slacken

## Example Sentences

- 工作累了要放松一下。(Gōngzuò lèi le yào fàngsōng yīxià.) — When tired from work, you should relax.

## Notes

Can refer to pine tree or the state of being loose/relaxed.
