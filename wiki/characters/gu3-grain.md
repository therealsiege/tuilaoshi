# 谷 Grain, valley, cereal

- **Character**: 谷
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: grain, valley, cereal
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 谷物 (gǔ wù) — grain, cereal
- 五谷 (wǔ gǔ) — five grains

## Example Sentences

- 五谷丰登是农民最大的愿望。(Wǔ gǔ fēng dēng shì nóngmín zuì dà de yuànwàng.) — Bountiful harvests of the five grains is farmers' greatest wish.

## Notes

Grains or cereals. 五谷 traditionally refers to five main grains: rice, millet, wheat, beans, and hemp/barley.
