# 笃 Devoted, Deep, 笃信 Firmly Believe

- **Character**: 笃
- **Pinyin**: dǔ
- **Meaning**: devoted, deep, 笃信 firmly believe
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 笃信 (dǔ xìn) — firmly believe
- 深笃 (shēn dǔ) — deep, profound
- 笃定 (dǔ dìng) — certain, confident

## Example Sentences

- 他笃信自己的理念。(Tā dǔxìn zìjǐ de lǐniàn.) — He firmly believes in his ideals.

## Notes

Formal term expressing deep conviction and steadfast belief. Often used for serious devotion to principles or faith.
