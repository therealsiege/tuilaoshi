# 叉 Fork

- **Character**: 叉
- **Pinyin**: chā
- **Meaning**: fork, cross
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 劈叉 (pī chà) — to do the splits
- 叉子 (chā zi) — fork

## Example Sentences

- 体操运动员的劈叉动作很标准。(Tǐcāo yùndòngyuán de pīchà dòngzuò hěn biāozhǔn.) — The gymnast's split is very standard.

## Notes

Crossing and splitting; used in gymnastics for splits
