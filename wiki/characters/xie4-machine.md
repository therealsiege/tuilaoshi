# 械 Machine, mechanical

- **Character**: 械
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: machine, mechanical
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 机械 (jī xiè) — machinery, mechanical
- 机械臂 (jī xiè bì) — robotic arm

## Example Sentences

- 机械工程师设计机器人。(Jī xiè gōng chéng shī shè jì jī qì rén.) — Mechanical engineers design robots.

## Notes

Used in 机械 (machinery), 机械臂 (robotic arm), 机械化 (mechanization)
