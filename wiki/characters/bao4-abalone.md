# 鲍 Abalone

- **Character**: 鲍
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: abalone
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鲍鱼 (bào yú) — abalone
- 干鲍 (gān bào) — dried abalone

## Example Sentences

- 鲍鱼是很贵的海鲜。(Bàoyú shì hěn guì de hǎixiān.) — Abalone is expensive seafood.

## Notes

Luxury seafood item. Also a surname. Fish radical.
