# 韧 Tough, pliable, 韌 (traditional)

- **Character**: 韧
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: tough, pliable, 韌 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 韦 (leather)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 韧性 (rèn xìng) — toughness, resilience
- 坚韧 (jiān rèn) — tough, tenacious

## Example Sentences

- 他性格很坚韧。(Tā xìnggé hěn jiānrèn.) — He has a tenacious character.

## Notes

Physical toughness or metaphorical resilience and tenacity.
