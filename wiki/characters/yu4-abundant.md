# 裕 Abundant, rich

- **Character**: 裕
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: abundant, rich
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 富裕 (fùyù) — prosperous, well-off
- 宽裕 (kuānyù) — well-off, comfortably off

## Example Sentences

- 他家境很富裕。(Tā jiājìng hěn fùyù.) — His family is very prosperous.

## Notes

Formal term for abundance or prosperity.
