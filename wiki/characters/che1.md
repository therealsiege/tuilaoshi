# 车 Car

- **Character**: 车
- **Pinyin**: chē
- **Meaning**: car, vehicle
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 火车 (huǒ chē) — train
- 汽车 (qì chē) — automobile

## Example Sentences

- 我坐车去上班。(Wǒ zuò chē qù shàng bān.) — I take a car to work.

## Notes

车 is simplified from 車, a pictograph of a chariot viewed from above.
