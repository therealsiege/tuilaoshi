# 目 Eye

- **Character**: 目
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: eye, item, list
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 目前 (mù qián) — currently
- 节目 (jié mù) — program, show

## Example Sentences

- 目前情况不错。(Mù qián qíng kuàng bú cuò.) — The current situation is not bad.

## Notes

目 is a pictograph of an eye turned sideways. The colloquial word for eye is 眼睛 (yǎn jīng).
