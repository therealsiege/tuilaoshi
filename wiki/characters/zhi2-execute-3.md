# 执 Execute, grasp, persist

- **Character**: 执
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: execute, grasp, persist
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执行 (zhí xíng) — to execute, implement
- 执着 (zhí zhuó) — persistent, stubborn

## Example Sentences

- 法院执行判决。(Fǎ yuàn zhí xíng pàn jué.) — The court executes the verdict.

## Notes

Means to carry out or persist. 执法 means law enforcement. 固执 means stubborn.
