# 顾 To Look After

- **Character**: 顾
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: to look after, to attend to
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 顾客 (gù kè) — customer
- 照顾 (zhào gù) — to take care of

## Example Sentences

- 请照顾好自己。(Qǐng zhào gù hǎo zì jǐ.) — Please take care of yourself.

## Notes

顾 means to look after or attend to. 顾客 = customer (one you attend to).
