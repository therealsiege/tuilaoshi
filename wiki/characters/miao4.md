# 妙 Wonderful

- **Character**: 妙
- **Pinyin**: miào
- **Meaning**: wonderful, ingenious
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 美妙 (měi miào) — beautiful, wonderful
- 巧妙 (qiǎo miào) — clever, ingenious

## Example Sentences

- 这个想法真妙。(Zhè gè xiǎng fǎ zhēn miào.) — This idea is truly wonderful.

## Notes

妙 combines woman 女 and young 少, originally meaning delicate beauty, extended to wonderful or ingenious.
