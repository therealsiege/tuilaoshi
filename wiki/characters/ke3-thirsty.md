# 渴 Thirsty

- **Character**: 渴
- **Pinyin**: kě
- **Meaning**: thirsty
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 口渴 (kǒu kě) — thirsty
- 干渴 (gān kě) — parched
- 渴望 (kě wàng) — to yearn for

## Example Sentences

- 糖尿病患者经常口渴。(Táng niào bìng huàn zhě jīng cháng kǒu kě.) — Diabetes patients are often thirsty.

## Notes

Thirst sensation; dehydration or diabetes sign.
