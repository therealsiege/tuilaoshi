# 据 According to, evidence

- **Character**: 据
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: according to, evidence
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 根据 (gēn jù) — according to, basis
- 凭据 (píng jù) — evidence, proof

## Example Sentences

- 请保留好付款凭据。(Qǐng bǎo liú hǎo fù kuǎn píng jù.) — Please keep the payment receipt.

## Notes

Important for documentation. 数据 means data, 票据 means bills/notes.
