# 哺 To Feed

- **Character**: 哺
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: to feed, to nurse
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哺乳 (bǔ rǔ) — to breastfeed
- 哺育 (bǔ yù) — to nurture

## Example Sentences

- 母亲哺育孩子。(Mǔ qīn bǔ yù hái zi.) — The mother nurtures the child.

## Notes

哺 means to feed or nurse. 哺乳动物 = mammal (nursing animal).
