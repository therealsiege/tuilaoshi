# 膏 Ointment, paste, grease

- **Character**: 膏
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: ointment, paste, grease
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 药膏 (yào gāo) — ointment, salve
- 牙膏 (yá gāo) — toothpaste

## Example Sentences

- 这种药膏对皮肤病很有效。(Zhè zhǒng yào gāo duì pífū bìng hěn yǒuxiào.) — This ointment is very effective for skin diseases.

## Notes

Paste-like medicine or substance. In TCM, medicinal pastes are applied topically or taken internally.
