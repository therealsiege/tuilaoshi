# 度 Degree, measure

- **Character**: 度
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: degree, measure
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 维度 (wéi dù) — dimension
- 速度 (sù dù) — speed

## Example Sentences

- 角度很重要。(Jiǎo dù hěn zhòng yào.) — The angle is important.

## Notes

Used in 维度 (dimension), 速度 (speed), 温度 (temperature)
