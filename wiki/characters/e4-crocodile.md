# 鳄 Crocodile, alligator

- **Character**: 鳄
- **Pinyin**: è
- **Meaning**: crocodile, alligator
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 鳄鱼 (è yú) — crocodile, alligator
- 鳄鱼皮 (è yú pí) — crocodile skin

## Example Sentences

- 河里有鳄鱼,很危险。(Hé lǐ yǒu èyú, hěn wēixiǎn.) — There are crocodiles in the river, it's dangerous.

## Notes

Dangerous reptile. Fish radical for aquatic animal.
