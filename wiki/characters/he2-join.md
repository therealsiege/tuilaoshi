# 合 To Join

- **Character**: 合
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: to join, to fit, to combine, together
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 合作 (hé zuò) — to cooperate
- 适合 (shì hé) — to suit

## Example Sentences

- 我们可以合作完成这个项目。(Wǒ men kě yǐ hé zuò wán chéng zhè ge xiàng mù.) — We can cooperate to complete this project.

## Notes

合 shows a lid closing on a container, representing joining or fitting together. Used in many compounds.
