# 露 Dew

- **Character**: 露
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: dew, reveal
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 露水 (lùshuǐ) — dew
- 暴露 (bàolù) — expose

## Example Sentences

- 早晨草上有露水。(Zǎochén cǎo shàng yǒu lùshuǐ.) — There's dew on the grass in the morning.

## Notes

Two meanings: dew (lù) and to reveal/expose. Rain radical for weather meaning. Common in poetry.
