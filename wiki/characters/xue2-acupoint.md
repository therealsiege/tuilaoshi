# 穴 Acupoint, cave, hole

- **Character**: 穴
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: acupoint, cave, hole
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 穴位 (xué wèi) — acupuncture point
- 洞穴 (dòng xué) — cave

## Example Sentences

- 针灸需要准确找到穴位。(Zhēnjiǔ xūyào zhǔnquè zhǎodào xué wèi.) — Acupuncture requires accurately locating the acupoints.

## Notes

Acupuncture points on the body where qi flows. Also means cave or hole. Over 360 acupoints are recognized in TCM.
