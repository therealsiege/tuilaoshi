# 综 Synthesize, Sum Up, 综合 Comprehensive

- **Character**: 综
- **Pinyin**: zōng
- **Meaning**: synthesize, sum up, 综合 comprehensive
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 综合 (zōng hé) — comprehensive, synthesize
- 综述 (zōng shù) — summary, review
- 错综复杂 (cuò zōng fù zá) — intricate and complex

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要综合考虑各种因素。(Wǒmen xūyào zōnghé kǎolǜ gè zhǒng yīnsù.) — We need to comprehensively consider various factors.

## Notes

Key character in academic contexts. 综合 (comprehensive) is extremely common in formal writing and research papers.
