# 冰 Ice

- **Character**: 冰
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: ice
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冰箱 (bīng xiāng) — refrigerator
- 冰淇淋 (bīng qí lín) — ice cream

## Example Sentences

- 请给我加冰。(Qǐng gěi wǒ jiā bīng.) — Please add ice.

## Notes

冰 uses the ice radical 冫. 冰箱 = refrigerator, 滑冰 = ice skating.
