# 境 Border, Situation, 环境 Environment

- **Character**: 境
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: border, situation, 环境 environment
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 环境 (huán jìng) — environment
- 境况 (jìng kuàng) — circumstances
- 入境 (rù jìng) — enter a country

## Example Sentences

- 我们要保护自然环境。(Wǒmen yào bǎohù zìrán huánjìng.) — We must protect the natural environment.

## Notes

Very common character. 环境 (environment) is one of the most frequently used words in modern Chinese.
