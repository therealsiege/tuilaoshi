# 猎 Hunt, pursue

- **Character**: 猎
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: hunt, pursue
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猎人 (liè rén) — hunter
- 狩猎 (shòu liè) — to hunt

## Example Sentences

- 猎人在森林里追踪猎物。(Liè rén zài sēn lín lǐ zhuī zōng liè wù.) — The hunter tracked prey in the forest.

## Notes

Means to hunt or pursue. 猎奇 means to seek novelty. 猎头 means headhunter (recruitment).
