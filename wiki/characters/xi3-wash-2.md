# 洗 Wash

- **Character**: 洗
- **Pinyin**: xǐ
- **Meaning**: wash
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 洗衣服 (xǐ yīfu) — wash clothes
- 洗澡 (xǐzǎo) — take a bath

## Example Sentences

- 我需要洗衣服。(Wǒ xūyào xǐ yīfu.) — I need to wash clothes.

## Notes

Very common daily verb. 洗澡 and 洗脸 are daily routines. Water radical indicates washing action.
