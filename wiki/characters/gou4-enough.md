# 够 Enough

- **Character**: 够
- **Pinyin**: gòu
- **Meaning**: enough, sufficient
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 足够 (zú gòu) — sufficient
- 不够 (bú gòu) — not enough

## Example Sentences

- 时间不够了。(Shí jiān bú gòu le.) — There's not enough time.

## Notes

够 means enough or sufficient. 够了 = that's enough. 能够 = to be able to.
