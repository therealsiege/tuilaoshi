# 蒙 Cover, deceive, Mongolia

- **Character**: 蒙
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: cover, deceive, Mongolia
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蒙骗 (méng piàn) — to deceive, dupe
- 启蒙 (qǐ méng) — to enlighten, initiate

## Example Sentences

- 不要试图蒙骗老师。(Bù yào shì tú méng piàn lǎo shī.) — Don't try to deceive the teacher.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: méng (cover, deceive), měng (Mongolia), mēng (unconscious). Very versatile character.
