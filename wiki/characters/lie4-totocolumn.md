# 列 To List

- **Character**: 列
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: to列列列to列列列column, row
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 列车 (liè chē) — train
- 列表 (liè biǎo) — list, table

## Example Sentences

- 请列出你的要求。(Qǐng liè chū nǐ de yāo qiú.) — Please list your requirements.

## Notes

列 means to arrange in rows or list. Used in列车 (train, literally 'row vehicle').
