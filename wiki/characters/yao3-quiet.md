# 窈 Quiet, secluded, refined

- **Character**: 窈
- **Pinyin**: yǎo
- **Meaning**: quiet, secluded, refined
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 窈窕 (yǎo tiǎo) — slender and graceful
- 窈窕淑女 (yǎo tiǎo shū nǚ) — fair maiden

## Example Sentences

- 窈窕淑女，君子好逑。(Yǎo tiǎo shū nǚ, jūn zǐ hǎo qiú.) — A fair maiden is a good match for a gentleman.

## Notes

Famous from the Book of Songs (诗经).
