# 减 To Reduce

- **Character**: 减
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: to reduce, to减少, to subtract
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 减少 (jiǎn shǎo) — to reduce
- 减肥 (jiǎn féi) — to lose weight

## Example Sentences

- 我们要减少浪费。(Wǒ men yào jiǎn shǎo làng fèi.) — We need to reduce waste.

## Notes

减 is simplified from 減. Represents reduction and subtraction.
