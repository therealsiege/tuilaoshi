# 妙 Wonderful, ingenious, subtle

- **Character**: 妙
- **Pinyin**: miào
- **Meaning**: wonderful, ingenious, subtle
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 美妙 (měi miào) — wonderful, beautiful
- 曼妙 (màn miào) — graceful, wonderful

## Example Sentences

- 这个主意真是妙不可言。(Zhè gè zhǔ yi zhēn shì miào bù kě yán.) — This idea is truly wonderful beyond words.

## Notes

Expresses wonderfulness, ingenuity, or subtlety.
