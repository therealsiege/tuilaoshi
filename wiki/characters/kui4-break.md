# 溃 To Break Down

- **Character**: 溃
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: to break down, to fester
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 崩溃 (bēng kuì) — to collapse
- 溃败 (kuì bài) — to be routed, to suffer crushing defeat

## Example Sentences

- 敌军溃不成军。(Dí jūn kuì bù chéng jūn.) — The enemy army was completely routed.

## Notes

溃 uses water radical 氵, suggesting something breaking through like a dam bursting. Used for military defeat or systemic breakdown.
