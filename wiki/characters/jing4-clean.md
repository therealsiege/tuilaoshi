# 净 Clean

- **Character**: 净
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: clean, net (weight)
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 干净 (gān jìng) — clean
- 净重 (jìng zhòng) — net weight

## Example Sentences

- 房间很干净。(Fáng jiān hěn gān jìng.) — The room is very clean.

## Notes

净 simplified from 淨. Water radical with 争 (compete), originally meaning clear water without impurities.
