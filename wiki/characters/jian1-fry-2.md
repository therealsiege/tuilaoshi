# 煎 To fry, to pan-fry

- **Character**: 煎
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: to fry, to pan-fry
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 煎蛋 (jiān dàn) — fried egg
- 煎饼 (jiān bǐng) — pancake

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢煎鸡蛋当早餐。(Wǒ xǐhuan jiān jīdàn dāng zǎocān.) — I like to fry eggs for breakfast.

## Notes

Common cooking method. The fire radical indicates heat-related cooking.
