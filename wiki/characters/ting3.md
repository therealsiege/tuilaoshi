# 艇 Small boat, submarine

- **Character**: 艇
- **Pinyin**: tǐng
- **Meaning**: small boat, submarine
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 潜艇 (qián tǐng) — submarine
- 游艇 (yóu tǐng) — yacht

## Example Sentences

- 他们乘游艇出海游玩。(Tā men chéng yóu tǐng chū hǎi yóu wán.) — They went out to sea on a yacht.

## Notes

For smaller vessels. 快艇 means speedboat. 潜水艇 means submarine.
