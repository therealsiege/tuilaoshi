# 钟 Bell

- **Character**: 钟
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: bell, clock
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钟声 (zhōng shēng) — bell sound
- 编钟 (biān zhōng) — bronze bells (musical instrument)

## Example Sentences

- 编钟是中国古代的乐器。(Biān zhōng shì zhōng guó gǔ dài de yuè qì.) — The bianzhong is an ancient Chinese musical instrument.

## Notes

钟 refers to both bells (musical) and clocks (timekeeping).
