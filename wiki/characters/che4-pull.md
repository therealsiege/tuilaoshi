# 掣 Pull, drag, hinder

- **Character**: 掣
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: pull, drag, hinder
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 牵掣 (qiān chè) — hinder, hamper
- 掣肘 (chè zhǒu) — hold back, hamper

## Example Sentences

- 他处处受人掣肘。(Tā chù chù shòu rén chè zhǒu.) — He is hampered by others everywhere.

## Notes

Obstruction and interference
