# 已 — Already

- **Character**: 已
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: already, to cease, to stop
- **Radical**: 己 (self)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 已经 (yǐjīng) — already
- 已知 (yǐzhī) — known, already known
- 已往 (yǐwǎng) — in the past, formerly
- 早已 (zǎoyǐ) — long ago, long since
- 已故 (yǐgù) — deceased, late (formal)

## Example Sentences

- 我已经吃过了。(Wǒ yǐjīng chī guò le.) — I've already eaten.
- 他们已经到了机场。(Tāmen yǐjīng dào le jīchǎng.) — They've already arrived at the airport.
- 电影已经开始了。(Diànyǐng yǐjīng kāishǐ le.) — The movie has already started.

## Notes

已 is visually similar to 己 (jǐ, self) and 巳 (sì, the sixth Earthly Branch), but they differ in whether the middle horizontal stroke extends past the right vertical. In 已, the stroke extends slightly — a detail worth memorizing. In practice, 已 almost always appears in the compound 已经 (already), which is one of the most important time adverbs for expressing completed actions. The pattern 已经...了 is the standard way to indicate something has already happened.
