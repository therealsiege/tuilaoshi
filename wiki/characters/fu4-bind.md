# 缚 To bind, to tie

- **Character**: 缚
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to bind, to tie
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 束缚 (shù fù) — to bind, to restrict
- 绑缚 (bǎng fù) — to tie up, to bind

## Example Sentences

- 不要被旧观念束缚。(Bùyào bèi jiù guānniàn shùfù.) — Don't be bound by old ideas.

## Notes

Often used figuratively for restrictions or constraints.
