# 刻 To Carve

- **Character**: 刻
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: to carve, to engrave, quarter hour
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — to carve
- 刻苦 (kè kǔ) — hardworking

## Example Sentences

- 现在三点一刻。(Xiàn zài sān diǎn yī kè.) — It's 3:15 now.

## Notes

刻 shows knife radical 刂, representing cutting or engraving. Also used for quarter hours.
