# 隐 Hidden

- **Character**: 隐
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: to hide, concealed
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 隐藏 (yǐn cáng) — to hide
- 隐私 (yǐn sī) — privacy

## Example Sentences

- 他把秘密隐藏了起来。(Tā bǎ mì mì yǐn cáng le qǐ lái.) — He hid the secret.

## Notes

隐 simplified from 隱. Mound radical 阝 plus components meaning concealment.
