# 刹 Brake

- **Character**: 刹
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: to brake, stop
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刹车 (shā chē) — to brake
- 急刹 (jí shā) — emergency brake

## Example Sentences

- 滑雪时要学会刹车。(Huáxuě shí yào xuéhuì shāchē.) — When skiing, you must learn to brake.

## Notes

Braking; stopping in skiing and cycling
