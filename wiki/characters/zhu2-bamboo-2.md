# 竹 Bamboo

- **Character**: 竹
- **Pinyin**: zhú
- **Meaning**: bamboo
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 竹子 (zhú zi) — bamboo
- 竹笛 (zhú dí) — bamboo flute

## Example Sentences

- 竹笛是用竹子做的。(Zhú dí shì yòng zhú zi zuò de.) — The bamboo flute is made from bamboo.

## Notes

竹 means bamboo; the radical for many musical instruments.
