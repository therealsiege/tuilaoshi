# 愁 Worried

- **Character**: 愁
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: worried, anxious
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 发愁 (fā chóu) — worry
- 愁眉苦脸 (chóuméikǔliǎn) — worried expression

## Example Sentences

- 他为工作发愁。(Tā wèi gōngzuò fā chóu.) — He's worried about work.

## Notes

Worry emotion. Heart radical. 发愁 means to worry. Common in describing mental state.
