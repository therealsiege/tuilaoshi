# 助 To help, to assist, assistance

- **Character**: 助
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to help, to assist, assistance
- **Radical**: 力(power)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 帮助 (bāng zhù) — to help, to assist
- 助手 (zhù shǒu) — assistant, helper

## Example Sentences

- 谢谢你的帮助。(Xièxie nǐ de bāngzhù.) — Thank you for your help.

## Notes

Common verb and noun for help or assistance.
