# 寓 To Imply

- **Character**: 寓
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to reside, to imply
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 寓言 (yù yán) — fable
- 寓意 (yù yì) — implied meaning

## Example Sentences

- 这个故事寓意深刻。(Zhè gè gù shì yù yì shēn kè.) — This story has a profound meaning.

## Notes

寓 has roof 宀 with 禺 phonetic, dwelling place, extended to hidden meaning.
