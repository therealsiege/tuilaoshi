# 账 Account, debt

- **Character**: 账
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: account, debt
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 账户 (zhàng hù) — account
- 账单 (zhàng dān) — bill, statement

## Example Sentences

- 请检查你的银行账户。(Qǐng jiǎn chá nǐ de yín háng zhàng hù.) — Please check your bank account.

## Notes

Core financial term. 记账 means bookkeeping, 结账 means settle accounts.
