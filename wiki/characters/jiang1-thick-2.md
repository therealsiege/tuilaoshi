# 浆 Thick liquid, starch, 漿 (traditional)

- **Character**: 浆
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: thick liquid, starch, 漿 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 豆浆 (dòu jiāng) — soy milk
- 浆糊 (jiāng hú) — paste

## Example Sentences

- 早餐喝豆浆很健康。(Zǎocān hē dòujiāng hěn jiànkāng.) — Drinking soy milk for breakfast is healthy.

## Notes

Thick liquid or paste-like substance.
