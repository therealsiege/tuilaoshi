# 奴 Slave, servant

- **Character**: 奴
- **Pinyin**: nú
- **Meaning**: slave, servant
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 奴隶 (nú lì) — slave
- 奴役 (nú yì) — to enslave

## Example Sentences

- 奴隶制度早已被废除。(Nú lì zhì dù zǎo yǐ bèi fèi chú.) — The slave system was abolished long ago.

## Notes

Means slave. Often used metaphorically (守财奴 miser, 房奴 mortgage slave).
