# 晨 Morning

- **Character**: 晨
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: morning, dawn
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 早晨 (zǎo chén) — morning
- 清晨 (qīng chén) — early morning

## Example Sentences

- 他每天早晨跑步。(Tā měi tiān zǎo chén pǎo bù.) — He runs every morning.

## Notes

晨 has sun 日 rising over 辰 (celestial), morning dawn.
