# 豪 Bold, heroic, grand

- **Character**: 豪
- **Pinyin**: háo
- **Meaning**: bold, heroic, grand
- **Radical**: 豕 (pig)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 豪放 (háo fàng) — bold and unconstrained
- 豪迈 (háo mài) — bold, heroic

## Example Sentences

- 他的诗风豪放不羁。(Tā de shī fēng háo fàng bù jī.) — His poetry style is bold and unrestrained.

## Notes

Positive connotation of boldness and magnificence.
