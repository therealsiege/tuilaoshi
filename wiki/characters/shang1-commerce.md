# 商 Commerce, merchant, discuss

- **Character**: 商
- **Pinyin**: shāng
- **Meaning**: commerce, merchant, discuss
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 商业 (shāng yè) — commerce, business
- 商品 (shāng pǐn) — commodity, goods

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个繁荣的商业区。(Zhè shì yī ge fán róng de shāng yè qū.) — This is a prosperous commercial district.

## Notes

Fundamental commerce term. 商人 means merchant. 商量 means to discuss. 商务 means business affairs.
