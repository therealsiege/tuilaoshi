# 屿 Islet, small island

- **Character**: 屿
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: islet, small island
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 岛屿 (dǎo yǔ) — islands
- 鼓浪屿 (Gǔ làng yǔ) — Gulangyu Island

## Example Sentences

- 这片海域散布着许多小岛屿。(Zhè piàn hǎi yù sàn bù zhe xǔ duō xiǎo dǎo yǔ.) — This sea area is dotted with many small islands.

## Notes

Specifically for small islands; common in place names.
