# 翻 Turn Over, Translate, 翻译 Translate

- **Character**: 翻
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: turn over, translate, 翻译 translate
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 翻译 (fān yì) — translate
- 翻倍 (fān bèi) — double
- 翻阅 (fān yuè) — look through

## Example Sentences

- 销售额比去年翻了一番。(Xiāoshòu'é bǐ qùnián fān le yī fān.) — Sales doubled compared to last year.

## Notes

Versatile verb meaning turn over, flip, translate, or double. 翻译 (translate) is essential; 翻倍/翻番 means double/multiply.
