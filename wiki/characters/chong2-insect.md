# 虫 Insect

- **Character**: 虫
- **Pinyin**: chóng
- **Meaning**: insect, worm
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 昆虫 (kūn chóng) — insect
- 害虫 (hài chóng) — pest

## Example Sentences

- 花园里有很多昆虫。(Huā yuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō kūn chóng.) — There are many insects in the garden.

## Notes

虫 simplified from 蟲. Originally pictograph of a snake or worm, now general term for insects.
