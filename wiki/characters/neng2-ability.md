# 能 Ability, capable

- **Character**: 能
- **Pinyin**: néng
- **Meaning**: ability, capable
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 智能 (zhì néng) — intelligent
- 功能 (gōng néng) — function

## Example Sentences

- 这个功能很强大。(Zhè gè gōng néng hěn qiáng dà.) — This function is very powerful.

## Notes

Used in 智能 (intelligence), 功能 (function), 能力 (ability)
