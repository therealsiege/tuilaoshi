# 款 Sum of money, section, style

- **Character**: 款
- **Pinyin**: kuǎn
- **Meaning**: sum of money, section, style
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 款项 (kuǎnxiàng) — sum of money, fund
- 贷款 (dàikuǎn) — loan

## Example Sentences

- 请尽快支付这笔款项。(Qǐng jǐnkuài zhīfù zhè bǐ kuǎnxiàng.) — Please pay this sum of money as soon as possible.

## Notes

Common term for money amounts, funds, and also styles of goods.
