# 编 To Weave

- **Character**: 编
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: to weave, compile, edit
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 编辑 (biān jí) — to edit, editor
- 编织 (biān zhī) — to weave, knit

## Example Sentences

- 他在编写一本书。(Tā zài biānxiě yī běn shū.) — He is writing a book.

## Notes

编 combines silk 糹 with 扁 (flat), representing the weaving of flat materials or the compilation of texts.
