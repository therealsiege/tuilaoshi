# 窑 Kiln, cave dwelling

- **Character**: 窑
- **Pinyin**: yáo
- **Meaning**: kiln, cave dwelling
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 窑洞 (yáo dòng) — cave dwelling
- 瓷窑 (cí yáo) — porcelain kiln

## Example Sentences

- 景德镇以瓷窑闻名。(Jǐng dé zhèn yǐ cí yáo wén míng.) — Jingdezhen is famous for its porcelain kilns.

## Notes

Used for ceramic kilns and cave dwellings.
