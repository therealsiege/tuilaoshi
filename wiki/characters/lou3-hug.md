# 搂 To hug, to embrace, to hold in arms

- **Character**: 搂
- **Pinyin**: lǒu
- **Meaning**: to hug, to embrace, to hold in arms
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搂抱 (lǒu bào) — to hug, to embrace
- 搂住 (lǒu zhù) — to hold tight

## Example Sentences

- 她紧紧搂住了孩子。(Tā jǐnjǐn lǒuzhù le háizi.) — She held the child tightly in her arms.

## Notes

Similar to 抱 but emphasizes tighter, more protective holding.
