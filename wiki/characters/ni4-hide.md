# 匿 To hide, conceal

- **Character**: 匿
- **Pinyin**: nì
- **Meaning**: to hide, conceal
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 匿名 (nì míng) — anonymous
- 隐匿 (yǐn nì) — to hide, conceal

## Example Sentences

- 他匿名发表了文章。(Tā nìmíng fābiǎole wénzhāng.) — He published the article anonymously.

## Notes

To hide or conceal, often used in formal contexts.
