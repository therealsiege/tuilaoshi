# 惧 Fear

- **Character**: 惧
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: fear, dread
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 恐惧 (kǒng jù) — fear, dread
- 畏惧 (wèi jù) — to fear
- 惧怕 (jù pà) — to be afraid

## Example Sentences

- 他有社交恐惧症。(Tā yǒu shè jiāo kǒng jù zhèng.) — He has social phobia.

## Notes

Intense fear; basis of phobia disorders.
