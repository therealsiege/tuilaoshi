# 猴 Monkey

- **Character**: 猴
- **Pinyin**: hóu
- **Meaning**: monkey
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 猴子 (hóu zi) — monkey
- 猴年 (hóu nián) — Year of the Monkey

## Example Sentences

- 猴子很聪明。(Hóu zi hěn cōng míng.) — Monkeys are very clever.

## Notes

猴 with animal radical. 孙悟空 (Monkey King) is famous character in Journey to the West.
