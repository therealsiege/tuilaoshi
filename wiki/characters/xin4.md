# 信 Trust, faith, honesty

- **Character**: 信
- **Pinyin**: xìn
- **Meaning**: trust, faith, honesty
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 信用 (xìn yòng) — credit, trustworthiness
- 诚信 (chéng xìn) — honesty, integrity

## Example Sentences

- 做生意最重要的是讲信用。(Zuò shēngyì zuì zhòngyào de shì jiǎng xìn yòng.) — The most important thing in business is maintaining credibility.

## Notes

Combines 人 (person) and 言 (speech), indicating that one's words should be trustworthy. A fundamental Confucian virtue.
