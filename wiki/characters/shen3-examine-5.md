# 审 To Examine

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: to examine, to try (a case)
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审判 (shěn pàn) — to try (a case)
- 审查 (shěn chá) — to examine

## Example Sentences

- 法院正在审理此案。(Fǎ yuàn zhèng zài shěn lǐ cǐ àn.) — The court is trying this case.

## Notes

审 simplified from 審. Roof 宀 plus 番 (to examine carefully).
