# 哀 Grief, sorrow, to grieve

- **Character**: 哀
- **Pinyin**: āi
- **Meaning**: grief, sorrow, to grieve
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 悲哀 (bēi āi) — sad, sorrowful
- 哀悼 (āi dào) — to mourn, to grieve

## Example Sentences

- 他们为逝去的亲人哀悼。(Tāmen wèi shìqù de qīnrén āidào.) — They mourn for their deceased relatives.

## Notes

Strong term for grief or mourning.
