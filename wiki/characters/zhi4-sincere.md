# 挚 Sincere, close

- **Character**: 挚
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: sincere, close
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 真挚 (zhēnzhì) — sincere, genuine
- 挚友 (zhìyǒu) — close friend

## Example Sentences

- 他们是挚友。(Tāmen shì zhìyǒu.) — They are close friends.

## Notes

Formal literary term for sincerity or closeness.
