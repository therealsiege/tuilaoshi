# 硕 Large, master's degree

- **Character**: 硕
- **Pinyin**: shuò
- **Meaning**: large, master's degree
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 硕士 (shuòshì) — master's degree
- 硕大 (shuòdà) — huge, enormous

## Example Sentences

- 他获得了硕士学位。(Tā huòdé le shuòshì xuéwèi.) — He obtained a master's degree.

## Notes

Formal term for large size or master's degree.
