# 津 Ford, ferry, saliva

- **Character**: 津
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: ford, ferry, saliva
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 津津有味 (jīn jīn yǒu wèi) — with great relish
- 津贴 (jīn tiē) — allowance, subsidy

## Example Sentences

- 他津津有味地讲述着旅行经历。(Tā jīn jīn yǒu wèi de jiǎng shù zhe lǚ xíng jīng lì.) — He recounted his travel experiences with great relish.

## Notes

Common in place names like 天津 (Tianjin).
