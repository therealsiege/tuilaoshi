# 震 Shake, 震, quake

- **Character**: 震
- **Pinyin**: zhèn
- **Meaning**: shake,震, quake
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 地震 (dì zhèn) — earthquake
- 震动 (zhèn dòng) — vibrate, shake

## Example Sentences

- 这次地震造成了严重破坏。(Zhè cì dì zhèn zào chéng le yán zhòng pò huài.) — This earthquake caused severe damage.
