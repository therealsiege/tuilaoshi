# 诉 Complain, sue, tell

- **Character**: 诉
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: complain, sue, tell
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诉讼 (sù sòng) — lawsuit, litigation
- 起诉 (qǐ sù) — to sue, prosecute

## Example Sentences

- 他向法院提起诉讼。(Tā xiàng fǎ yuàn tí qǐ sù sòng.) — He filed a lawsuit with the court.

## Notes

Essential legal term. 告诉 means to tell. 上诉 means to appeal. 申诉 means to appeal or complain.
