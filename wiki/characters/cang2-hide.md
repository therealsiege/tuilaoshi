# 藏 To hide, store, 藏 (traditional)

- **Character**: 藏
- **Pinyin**: cáng
- **Meaning**: to hide, store, 藏 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 隐藏 (yǐn cáng) — to hide, conceal
- 收藏 (shōu cáng) — to collect, store

## Example Sentences

- 他收藏了很多邮票。(Tā shōucángle hěn duō yóupiào.) — He has collected many stamps.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: cáng (hide/store), Zàng (Tibet/Tibetan).
