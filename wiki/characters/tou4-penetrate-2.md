# 透 To penetrate, to pass through, thorough

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: to penetrate, to pass through, thorough
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 透明 (tòu míng) — transparent, clear
- 看透 (kàn tòu) — to see through, to understand thoroughly

## Example Sentences

- 阳光透过窗户照进来。(Yángguāng tòuguò chuānghù zhào jìnlái.) — Sunlight shines through the window.

## Notes

Implies complete penetration or thorough understanding.
