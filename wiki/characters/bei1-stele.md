# 碑 Stone Tablet

- **Character**: 碑
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: stele, stone tablet, monument
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 纪念碑 (jì niàn bēi) — monument
- 碑文 (bēi wén) — inscription

## Example Sentences

- 广场上有一座纪念碑。(Guǎng chǎng shàng yǒu yī zuò jì niàn bēi.) — There is a monument in the square.

## Notes

碑 is a stone tablet with inscriptions. 口碑 = public reputation (word of mouth).
