# 束 Bundle

- **Character**: 束
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: to tie, bundle, measure word
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 束缚 (shù fù) — to bind, to restrain
- 一束花 (yī shù huā) — a bunch of flowers

## Example Sentences

- 他送了我一束花。(Tā sòng le wǒ yī shù huā.) — He gave me a bunch of flowers.

## Notes

束 shows wood tied together, used for bundles and as a measure word.
