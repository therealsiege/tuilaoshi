# 桩 Stake

- **Character**: 桩
- **Pinyin**: zhuāng
- **Meaning**: stake, pile, measure word for events
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 木桩 (mù zhuāng) — wooden stake
- 一桩事 (yì zhuāng shì) — an affair

## Example Sentences

- 地上打了很多木桩。(Dì shàng dǎ le hěn duō mù zhuāng.) — Many wooden stakes were driven into the ground.

## Notes

桩 simplified from 樁. Wood 木 plus fixed point, pile foundation.
