# 麻 Hemp

- **Character**: 麻
- **Pinyin**: má
- **Meaning**: hemp, flax, numb, pockmarked
- **Radical**: 麻 (hemp)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 麻烦 (má fan) — trouble, troublesome
- 芝麻 (zhī ma) — sesame

## Example Sentences

- 真麻烦。(Zhēn má fan.) — What a hassle.

## Notes

麻 means hemp or numb. Used in麻烦 (trouble), 麻木 (numb), 芝麻 (sesame).
