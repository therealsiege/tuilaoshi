# 猖 Rampant, wild

- **Character**: 猖
- **Pinyin**: chāng
- **Meaning**: rampant, wild
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猖狂 (chāngkuáng) — rampant, savage
- 猖獗 (chāngjué) — rampant, unchecked

## Example Sentences

- 犯罪活动很猖狂。(Fànzuì huódòng hěn chāngkuáng.) — Criminal activities are rampant.

## Notes

Literary term for rampancy or savagery, usually negative.
