# 会 Can/Will/Meeting

- **Character**: 会
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: can (learned ability), will (future), meeting
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 会议 (huìyì) — meeting, conference
- 机会 (jīhuì) — opportunity, chance
- 社会 (shèhuì) — society
- 开会 (kāihuì) — to hold a meeting
- 学会 (xuéhuì) — to learn (to master); learned society

## Example Sentences

- 你会说中文吗？(Nǐ huì shuō Zhōngwén ma?) — Can you speak Chinese?
- 明天会下雨。(Míngtiān huì xiàyǔ.) — It will rain tomorrow.
- 我们下午开会。(Wǒmen xiàwǔ kāihuì.) — We have a meeting this afternoon.

## Notes

会 is the simplified form of 會. It carries two major functions: as an auxiliary verb expressing learned ability ("can/know how to") or future possibility ("will"), and as a noun meaning "meeting" or "gathering." Compare with 能 (néng), which expresses innate ability or permission. 会说中文 means "can speak Chinese" (learned skill), while 能说中文 implies having the capacity or permission to speak it.
