# 赚 To Earn

- **Character**: 赚
- **Pinyin**: zhuàn
- **Meaning**: to earn, to make a profit
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赚钱 (zhuàn qián) — to make money
- 赚取 (zhuàn qǔ) — to earn

## Example Sentences

- 他今年赚了不少钱。(Tā jīn nián zhuàn le bù shǎo qián.) — He made quite a bit of money this year.

## Notes

赚 simplified from 賺. Shell/money radical 贝 indicating financial gain or profit.
