# 路 Road

- **Character**: 路
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: road, path, route
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 马路 (mǎ lù) — road, street
- 路上 (lù shàng) — on the road

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很长。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn cháng.) — This road is very long.

## Notes

路 means road or path. 一路 = all the way. 走路 = to walk.
