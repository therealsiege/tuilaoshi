# 医 Medicine

- **Character**: 医
- **Pinyin**: yī
- **Meaning**: medicine, doctor
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 医生 (yī shēng) — doctor
- 医院 (yī yuàn) — hospital

## Example Sentences

- 我要去看医生。(Wǒ yào qù kàn yī shēng.) — I need to see a doctor.

## Notes

医 is simplified from 醫. The original form included 酉 (wine jar) because medicine was historically alcohol-based.
