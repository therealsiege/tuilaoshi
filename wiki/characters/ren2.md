# 人 Person

- **Character**: 人
- **Pinyin**: rén
- **Meaning**: person, people, human
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 人们 (rén men) — people (plural)
- 中国人 (Zhōngguó rén) — Chinese person
- 好人 (hǎo rén) — good person
- 大人 (dà rén) — adult

## Example Sentences

- 他是中国人。(Tā shì Zhōngguó rén.) — He is Chinese.
- 这里有很多人。(Zhèlǐ yǒu hěn duō rén.) — There are many people here.

## Notes

人 is one of the simplest and most common radicals. It's a pictograph of a person walking, showing two legs in stride.
