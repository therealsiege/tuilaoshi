# 渗 To Seep

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: to seep, to permeate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate, to infiltrate
- 渗入 (shèn rù) — to seep into

## Example Sentences

- 水渗透到了墙里。(Shuǐ shèn tòu dào le qiáng lǐ.) — Water seeped into the wall.

## Notes

渗 uses water radical 氵, indicating gradual penetration of liquid or influence.
