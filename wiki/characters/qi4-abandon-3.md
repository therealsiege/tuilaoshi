# 弃 Abandon, discard

- **Character**: 弃
- **Pinyin**: qì
- **Meaning**: abandon, discard
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 废弃 (fèi qì) — abandon, discard
- 抛弃 (pāo qì) — abandon

## Example Sentences

- 废弃物需要处理。(Fèi qì wù xū yào chǔ lǐ.) — Waste needs to be processed.

## Notes

Used in 废弃 (discard), 放弃 (give up), waste management
