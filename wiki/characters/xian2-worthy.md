# 贤 Worthy, virtuous, talented

- **Character**: 贤
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: worthy, virtuous, talented
- **Radical**: 贝(shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贤惠 (xián huì) — virtuous (of women)
- 圣贤 (shèng xián) — sage

## Example Sentences

- 她是个贤惠的妻子。(Tā shì ge xiánhuì de qīzi.) — She is a virtuous wife.

## Notes

Often used to describe virtuous or talented people.
