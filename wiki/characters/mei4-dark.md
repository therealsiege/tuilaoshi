# 昧 Dark, obscure, ignorant

- **Character**: 昧
- **Pinyin**: mèi
- **Meaning**: dark, obscure, ignorant
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 愚昧 (yú mèi) — ignorant, benighted
- 昧心 (mèi xīn) — against one's conscience

## Example Sentences

- 不能因为愚昧而拒绝科学。(Bù néng yīn wèi yú mèi ér jù jué kē xué.) — One should not reject science out of ignorance.

## Notes

Indicates darkness or ignorance. 暧昧 means ambiguous or equivocal. 昧良心 means unconscionable.
