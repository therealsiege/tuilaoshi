# 遭 To Suffer

- **Character**: 遭
- **Pinyin**: zāo
- **Meaning**: to suffer, to encounter (misfortune)
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 遭遇 (zāo yù) — to encounter, experience
- 遭受 (zāo shòu) — to suffer

## Example Sentences

- 他遭受了很大的损失。(Tā zāo shòu le hěn dà de sǔn shī.) — He suffered great losses.

## Notes

遭 combines movement 辶 and 曹 (group), often used for encountering misfortune or difficulties.
