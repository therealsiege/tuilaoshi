# 碾 To Grind

- **Character**: 碾
- **Pinyin**: niǎn
- **Meaning**: to grind, to crush
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 碾压 (niǎn yā) — to run over, to crush

## Example Sentences

- 用石磨碾谷物。(Yòng shí mò niǎn gǔ wù.) — Grind grain with a stone mill.

## Notes

碾 combines stone 石 and展 (unfold), representing grinding or crushing with heavy stone.
