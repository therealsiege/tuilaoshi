# 瓜 Melon, gourd

- **Character**: 瓜
- **Pinyin**: guā
- **Meaning**: melon, gourd
- **Radical**: 瓜 (melon)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 西瓜 (xī guā) — watermelon
- 黄瓜 (huáng guā) — cucumber

## Example Sentences

- 夏天吃西瓜很解渴。(Xiàtiān chī xīguā hěn jiěkě.) — Eating watermelon in summer is very thirst-quenching.

## Notes

This character is itself a radical. Used in many melon/gourd names.
