# 丸 Pill, pellet

- **Character**: 丸
- **Pinyin**: wán
- **Meaning**: pill, pellet
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 药丸 (yào wán) — pill
- 弹丸 (dàn wán) — pellet, bullet

## Example Sentences

- 中药常制成药丸服用。(Zhōng yào cháng zhì chéng yào wán fúyòng.) — Chinese medicine is often made into pills for consumption.

## Notes

Small round pill. Traditional Chinese medicine often uses pills made from concentrated herbal extracts.
