# 阂 Obstruction, barrier, 閡 (traditional)

- **Character**: 阂
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: obstruction, barrier, 閡 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 隔阂 (gé hé) — estrangement, misunderstanding
- 阂阻 (hé zǔ) — obstruction

## Example Sentences

- 他们之间有隔阂。(Tāmen zhījiān yǒu géhé.) — There is estrangement between them.

## Notes

Barrier or estrangement, often used in 隔阂.
