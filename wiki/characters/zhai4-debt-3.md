# 债 Debt, owe

- **Character**: 债
- **Pinyin**: zhài
- **Meaning**: debt, owe
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 债务 (zhàiwù) — debt, liability
- 欠债 (qiànzhài) — owe a debt

## Example Sentences

- 他还清了所有债务。(Tā huánqīng le suǒyǒu zhàiwù.) — He paid off all his debts.

## Notes

Key financial and legal term for debt and obligations.
