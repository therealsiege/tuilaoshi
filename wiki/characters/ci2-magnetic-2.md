# 磁 Magnetic

- **Character**: 磁
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: magnetic
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 磁场 (cí chǎng) — magnetic field
- 电磁 (diàn cí) — electromagnetic

## Example Sentences

- 电磁波传播信息。(Diàn cí bō chuán bō xìn xī.) — Electromagnetic waves transmit information.

## Notes

Used in 磁场 (magnetic field), 电磁 (electromagnetic), 磁盘 (magnetic disk)
