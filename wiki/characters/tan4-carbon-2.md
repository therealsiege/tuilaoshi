# 碳 Carbon

- **Character**: 碳
- **Pinyin**: tàn
- **Meaning**: carbon
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 碳水化合物 (tàn shuǐ huà hé wù) — carbohydrate
- 二氧化碳 (èr yǎng huà tàn) — carbon dioxide

## Example Sentences

- 碳是所有有机物的基础元素。(Tàn shì suǒyǒu yǒujīwù de jīchǔ yuánsù.) — Carbon is the fundamental element of all organic compounds.

## Notes

碳 is carbon, essential element for all life. Forms the basis of organic chemistry.
