# 圃 Garden, nursery, plot

- **Character**: 圃
- **Pinyin**: pǔ
- **Meaning**: garden, nursery, plot
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 苗圃 (miáo pǔ) — plant nursery
- 花圃 (huā pǔ) — flower garden

## Example Sentences

- 这个苗圃培育各种树苗。(Zhège miáopǔ péiyù gèzhǒng shù miáo.) — This nursery cultivates various tree seedlings.

## Notes

Enclosed garden or nursery for cultivating plants. The enclosure radical suggests a bounded growing area.
