# 透 Penetrate, thorough, transparent

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: penetrate, thorough, transparent
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 透明 (tòumíng) — transparent, open
- 看透 (kàntòu) — see through, understand thoroughly

## Example Sentences

- 玻璃是透明的。(Bōli shì tòumíng de.) — Glass is transparent.

## Notes

Common character for penetration, thoroughness, or transparency.
