# 恤 Sympathize, relieve

- **Character**: 恤
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: sympathize, relieve
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 抚恤 (fǔxù) — give relief payment
- 体恤 (tǐxù) — show consideration for

## Example Sentences

- 政府抚恤烈士家属。(Zhèngfǔ fǔxù lièshì jiāshǔ.) — The government provides relief payments to martyrs' families.

## Notes

Formal term for showing sympathy and providing relief or compensation.
