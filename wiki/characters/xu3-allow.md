# 许 Allow, promise

- **Character**: 许
- **Pinyin**: xǔ
- **Meaning**: allow, promise
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 许可 (xǔ kě) — permit, allow
- 允许 (yǔn xǔ) — permit, allow

## Example Sentences

- 需要申请许可证。(Xū yào shēn qǐng xǔ kě zhèng.) — Need to apply for a permit.

## Notes

许可证 is permit/license. Used in regulatory contexts.
