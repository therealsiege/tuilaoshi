# 馆 Building

- **Character**: 馆
- **Pinyin**: guǎn
- **Meaning**: building, shop, embassy
- **Radical**: 饣(food)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 图书馆 (tú shū guǎn) — library
- 饭馆 (fàn guǎn) — restaurant

## Example Sentences

- 我去图书馆了。(Wǒ qù tú shū guǎn le.) — I went to the library.

## Notes

馆 originally meant guesthouse, now for public buildings and specialized shops.
