# 槽 Groove

- **Character**: 槽
- **Pinyin**: cáo
- **Meaning**: groove, trough, manger
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 水槽 (shuǐ cáo) — sink, water trough
- 槽糕 (zāo gāo) — in a mess, too bad

## Example Sentences

- 厨房的水槽堵住了。(Chú fáng de shuǐ cáo dǔ zhù le.) — The kitchen sink is clogged.

## Notes

槽 combines wood 木 and 曹 (group), originally wooden troughs for feeding animals, now any groove or channel.
