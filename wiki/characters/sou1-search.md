# 搜 Search, seek

- **Character**: 搜
- **Pinyin**: sōu
- **Meaning**: search, seek
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搜索 (sōusuǒ) — search
- 搜查 (sōuchá) — search (by police)

## Example Sentences

- 警察搜查了现场。(Jǐngchá sōuchá le xiànchǎng.) — Police searched the scene.

## Notes

Common for searching and seeking.
