# 户 Door

- **Character**: 户
- **Pinyin**: hù
- **Meaning**: door, household, family
- **Radical**: 户 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 窗户 (chuāng hu) — window
- 户口 (hù kǒu) — household registration

## Example Sentences

- 这个村有三百户人家。(Zhè ge cūn yǒu sān bǎi hù rén jiā.) — This village has three hundred households.

## Notes

户 simplified from 戶. Depicts a door or gate, household unit.
