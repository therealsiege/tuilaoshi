# 煎 Decoct

- **Character**: 煎
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: decoct, fry
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 煎药 (jiān yào) — to decoct medicine
- 煎熬 (jiān áo) — to suffer, torment
- 煎炸 (jiān zhá) — to fry

## Example Sentences

- 中药需要煎煮后服用。(Zhōng yào xū yào jiān zhǔ hòu fú yòng.) — Chinese medicine needs to be decocted before taking.

## Notes

Boiling herbs; traditional preparation method.
