# 裂 To Crack

- **Character**: 裂
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: to crack, to split
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裂开 (liè kāi) — to crack open
- 分裂 (fēn liè) — to split

## Example Sentences

- 墙壁裂开了。(Qiáng bì liè kāi le.) — The wall cracked open.

## Notes

裂 shows clothing 衣 being torn apart by 列 (to arrange/split).
