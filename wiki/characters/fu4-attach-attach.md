# 附 Near

- **Character**: 附
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to attach, near
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 附近 (fù jìn) — nearby
- 附件 (fù jiàn) — attachment

## Example Sentences

- 附近有银行吗？(Fù jìn yǒu yín háng ma?) — Is there a bank nearby?

## Notes

附近 is one of the most common compounds, meaning 'nearby/in the vicinity.'
