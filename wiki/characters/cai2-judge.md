# 裁 Judge, decide, cut out

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: judge, decide, cut out
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁决 (cái jué) — to rule, adjudicate
- 仲裁 (zhòng cái) — arbitration

## Example Sentences

- 法官裁决被告有罪。(Fǎ guān cái jué bèi gào yǒu zuì.) — The judge ruled the defendant guilty.

## Notes

Core legal term for making judgments. 裁判 means referee or umpire. 裁员 means layoffs.
