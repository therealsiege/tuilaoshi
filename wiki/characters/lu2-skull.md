# 颅 Skull, cranium, 顱 (traditional)

- **Character**: 颅
- **Pinyin**: lú
- **Meaning**: skull, cranium, 顱 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 头颅 (tóu lú) — head, skull
- 颅骨 (lú gǔ) — skull bone, cranium

## Example Sentences

- 医生检查了患者的颅骨。(Yīshēng jiǎnchále huànzhě de lúgǔ.) — The doctor examined the patient's skull.

## Notes

Medical term for skull or cranium.
