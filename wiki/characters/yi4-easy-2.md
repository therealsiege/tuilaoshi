# 易 Easy, change, trade

- **Character**: 易
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: easy, change, trade
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贸易 (mào yì) — trade
- 交易 (jiāo yì) — transaction, deal

## Example Sentences

- 股票交易市场今天很活跃。(Gǔ piào jiāo yì shì chǎng jīn tiān hěn huó yuè.) — The stock trading market is very active today.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: trade, change, easy. 容易 means easy. 变易 means to change.
