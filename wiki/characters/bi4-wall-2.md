# 壁 Wall

- **Character**: 壁
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: wall, cliff
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 墙壁 (qiáng bì) — wall
- 壁纸 (bì zhǐ) — wallpaper

## Example Sentences

- 墙壁需要粉刷。(Qiáng bì xū yào fěn shuā.) — The wall needs to be painted.

## Notes

壁 originally meant cliff face, now commonly used for walls of buildings.
