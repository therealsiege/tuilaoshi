# 布 Cloth

- **Character**: 布
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: cloth, fabric, to announce, to spread
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 布置 (bù zhì) — to arrange
- 宣布 (xuān bù) — to announce

## Example Sentences

- 老师布置了作业。(Lǎo shī bù zhì le zuò yè.) — The teacher assigned homework.

## Notes

布 means cloth or to spread/announce. 布置 = arrange/decorate, 发布 = to release/publish.
