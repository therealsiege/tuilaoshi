# 项 项目, item, neck

- **Character**: 项
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: 项目, item, neck
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 项目 (xiàng mù) — 项目, item
- 条项 (tiáo xiàng) — clause, provision

## Example Sentences

- 合同有二十个条项。(Hé tong yǒu èr shí ge tiáo xiàng.) — The contract has twenty clauses.

## Notes

事项 means matter/item. 款项 means sum of money.
