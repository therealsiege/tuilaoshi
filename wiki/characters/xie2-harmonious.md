# 谐 Harmonious, humorous

- **Character**: 谐
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: harmonious, humorous
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 和谐 (hé xié) — harmonious
- 诙谐 (huī xié) — humorous

## Example Sentences

- 社会需要和谐发展。(Shè huì xū yào hé xié fā zhǎn.) — Society needs harmonious development.

## Notes

Harmony in relationships and humorous manner
