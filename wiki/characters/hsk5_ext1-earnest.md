# 恳 Earnest, Sincere, 恳切 Earnest

- **Character**: 恳
- **Pinyin**: kěn
- **Meaning**: earnest, sincere, 恳切 earnest
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恳切 (kěn qiè) — earnest, sincere
- 恳求 (kěn qiú) — implore, entreat
- 诚恳 (chéng kěn) — sincere, honest

## Example Sentences

- 他态度很诚恳。(Tā tàidù hěn chéngkěn.) — His attitude is very sincere.

## Notes

Formal term expressing earnestness and sincerity. Often used in serious requests or expressions of genuine intent.
