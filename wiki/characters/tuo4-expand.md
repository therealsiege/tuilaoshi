# 拓 Expand, open up, make rubbing

- **Character**: 拓
- **Pinyin**: tuò
- **Meaning**: expand, open up, make rubbing
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 开拓 (kāi tuò) — to open up, expand
- 拓展 (tuò zhǎn) — to expand, develop

## Example Sentences

- 公司正在开拓海外市场。(Gōng sī zhèng zài kāi tuò hǎi wài shì chǎng.) — The company is expanding into overseas markets.

## Notes

Means to expand or pioneer. Also pronounced tà in 拓片 (rubbing of inscriptions).
