# 红 Red

- **Character**: 红
- **Pinyin**: hóng
- **Meaning**: red
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 红色 (hóng sè) — red color
- 红茶 (hóng chá) — black tea

## Example Sentences

- 她穿红色的衣服。(Tā chuān hóng sè de yī fu.) — She's wearing red clothes.

## Notes

红 in Chinese culture symbolizes luck, prosperity, and celebration. Interestingly, 红茶 means 'black tea' (red from the color of the brewed liquid).
