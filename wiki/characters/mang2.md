# 盲 Blind

- **Character**: 盲
- **Pinyin**: máng
- **Meaning**: blind
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 盲人 (máng rén) — blind person
- 盲目 (máng mù) — blind, blindly

## Example Sentences

- 不要盲目相信别人。(Bùyào mángmù xiāngxìn biéren.) — Don't blindly trust others.

## Notes

盲 combines 亡 (lost) with eye 目, representing eyes that have lost their function.
