# 馅 Filling, stuffing

- **Character**: 馅
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: filling, stuffing
- **Radical**: 食 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 馅饼 (xiàn bǐng) — pie
- 肉馅 (ròu xiàn) — meat filling

## Example Sentences

- 饺子里的馅是什么?(Jiǎozi lǐ de xiàn shì shénme?) — What is the filling in the dumplings?

## Notes

Essential for discussing dumplings, buns, pies. Food radical indicates edible filling.
