# 书 Book/To Write

- **Character**: 书
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: book; to write
- **Radical**: 乛 (hook)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 书本 (shūběn) — book (physical copy)
- 看书 (kànshū) — to read a book
- 书法 (shūfǎ) — calligraphy
- 图书馆 (túshūguǎn) — library
- 书店 (shūdiàn) — bookstore
- 读书 (dúshū) — to study, to read

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢看书。(Wǒ xǐhuān kànshū.) — I like reading books.
- 图书馆里有很多书。(Túshūguǎn lǐ yǒu hěn duō shū.) — There are many books in the library.
- 这本书很有意思。(Zhè běn shū hěn yǒu yìsi.) — This book is very interesting.

## Notes

书 is the simplified form of 書, which was originally a pictograph of a hand holding a brush writing on a surface. The simplification reduced it from 10 strokes to just 4. The dual meaning of "book" (noun) and "to write" (verb) reflects the inseparability of writing and texts in Chinese culture. The art of writing characters, 书法 (shūfǎ, calligraphy), remains one of the most revered traditional arts. The measure word for books is 本 (běn): 一本书.
