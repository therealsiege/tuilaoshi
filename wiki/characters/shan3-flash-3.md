# 闪 To Flash

- **Character**: 闪
- **Pinyin**: shǎn
- **Meaning**: to flash, to dodge, to sprain
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 闪电 (shǎn diàn) — lightning
- 闪光 (shǎn guāng) — to flash

## Example Sentences

- 天空闪过一道闪电。(Tiān kōng shǎn guò yí dào shǎn diàn.) — A bolt of lightning flashed across the sky.

## Notes

闪 simplified from 閃. Light 人 glimpsed through door 门, quick flash.
