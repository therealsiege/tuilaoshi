# 截 Intercept, cut off

- **Character**: 截
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: intercept, cut off
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 截取 (jié qǔ) — intercept, capture
- 拦截 (lán jié) — intercept

## Example Sentences

- 拦截恶意流量。(Lán jié è yì liú liàng.) — Intercept malicious traffic.

## Notes

Used in 截取 (intercept), 拦截 (block), 截图 (screenshot)
