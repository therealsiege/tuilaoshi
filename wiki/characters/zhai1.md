# 摘 To Pick

- **Character**: 摘
- **Pinyin**: zhāi
- **Meaning**: to pick, to pluck, to take off
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 摘花 (zhāi huā) — to pick flowers
- 摘要 (zhāi yào) — abstract

## Example Sentences

- 她在花园里摘花。(Tā zài huā yuán lǐ zhāi huā.) — She is picking flowers in the garden.

## Notes

摘 hand 扌 plus 啇 (to announce), selecting and taking.
