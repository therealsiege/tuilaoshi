# 算 Calculate, compute, count

- **Character**: 算
- **Pinyin**: suàn
- **Meaning**: calculate, compute, count
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 计算 (jì suàn) — calculate, compute
- 算法 (suàn fǎ) — algorithm

## Example Sentences

- 用计算器算这道题。(Yòng jì suàn qì suàn zhè dào tí.) — Use a calculator to solve this problem.
