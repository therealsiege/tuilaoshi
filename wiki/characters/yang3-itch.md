# 痒 Itch

- **Character**: 痒
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: itch
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 痒痒 (yǎng yang) — itchy
- 瘙痒 (sào yǎng) — itching, pruritus
- 止痒 (zhǐ yǎng) — to stop itching

## Example Sentences

- 皮肤过敏会很痒。(Pí fū guò mǐn huì hěn yǎng.) — Skin allergies can be very itchy.

## Notes

Common symptom in dermatology and allergies.
