# 羽 Feather

- **Character**: 羽
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: feather, wing
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 羽毛球 (yǔmáo qiú) — badminton
- 羽绒 (yǔ róng) — down (feathers)

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢打羽毛球锻炼身体。(Wǒ xǐhuan dǎ yǔmáoqiú duànliàn shēntǐ.) — I like playing badminton to exercise.

## Notes

Refers to feathers; used in badminton (feather ball)
