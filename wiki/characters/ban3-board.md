# 板 Board

- **Character**: 板
- **Pinyin**: bǎn
- **Meaning**: board, plank, plate, stiff
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 黑板 (hēi bǎn) — blackboard
- 木板 (mù bǎn) — plank

## Example Sentences

- 老师在黑板上写字。(Lǎo shī zài hēi bǎn shàng xiě zì.) — The teacher is writing on the blackboard.

## Notes

板 means board or plank. Used in黑板 (blackboard), 木板 (wooden board), 板凳 (bench).
