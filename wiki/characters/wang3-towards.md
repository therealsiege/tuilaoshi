# 往 Towards

- **Character**: 往
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: towards, to go, in the direction of
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 往前 (wǎng qián) — forward, ahead
- 来往 (lái wǎng) — to come and go

## Example Sentences

- 往前走，然后右转。(Wǎng qián zǒu, rán hòu yòu zhuǎn.) — Go forward, then turn right.

## Notes

往 emphasizes the direction of movement more than 向. Often used with verbs of motion.
