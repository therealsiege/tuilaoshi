# 年 Year

- **Character**: 年
- **Pinyin**: nián
- **Meaning**: year
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 今年 (jīnnián) — this year
- 明年 (míngnián) — next year
- 去年 (qùnián) — last year
- 年轻 (niánqīng) — young
- 新年 (xīnnián) — new year
- 年纪 (niánjì) — age

## Example Sentences

- 今年是二〇二六年。(Jīnnián shì èr líng èr liù nián.) — This year is 2026.
- 你今年多大了？(Nǐ jīnnián duō dà le?) — How old are you this year?
- 新年快乐！(Xīnnián kuàilè!) — Happy New Year!

## Notes

年 originally depicted a person carrying a bundle of grain, representing the harvest — a cycle completed once per year. The connection between "harvest" and "year" reflects the agricultural roots of Chinese civilization, where the annual grain harvest marked the passage of time. In oracle bone inscriptions, 年 was written as a person bent under a sheaf of grain. It is also the name of a mythical beast (年兽 Niánshòu) in the legend behind Chinese New Year celebrations.
