# 妄 Absurd, rash, false

- **Character**: 妄
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: absurd, rash, false
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 狂妄 (kuángwàng) — arrogant, presumptuous
- 妄想 (wàngxiǎng) — vain hope, wishful thinking

## Example Sentences

- 不要妄想一夜成功。(Bùyào wàngxiǎng yīyè chénggōng.) — Don't wishfully think you'll succeed overnight.

## Notes

Literary term for absurdity, rashness, or falseness.
