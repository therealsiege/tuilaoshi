# 砂 Sand

- **Character**: 砂
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: sand, grit
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砂石 (shā shí) — sand and gravel
- 砂纸 (shā zhǐ) — sandpaper

## Example Sentences

- 制陶需要加入适量的砂。(Zhì táo xūyào jiārù shìliàng de shā.) — Making pottery requires adding an appropriate amount of sand.

## Notes

砂 refers to sand or grit. The stone radical distinguishes it from 沙 (sand on beaches).
