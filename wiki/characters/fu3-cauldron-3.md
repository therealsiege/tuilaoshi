# 釜 Cauldron

- **Character**: 釜
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: cauldron, pot
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 破釜沉舟 (pò fǔ chén zhōu) — burn one's bridges
- 釜底抽薪 (fǔ dǐ chōu xīn) — remove firewood from under the pot

## Example Sentences

- 他破釜沉舟，决定放手一搏。(Tā pò fǔ chén zhōu, juédìng fàngshǒu yī bó.) — He burned his bridges and decided to go all in.

## Notes

Used in 破釜沉舟: the cooking pots that Xiang Yu destroyed to show no retreat.
