# 停 To Stop

- **Character**: 停
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: to stop, to halt, to park
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 停止 (tíng zhǐ) — to stop
- 停车 (tíng chē) — to park

## Example Sentences

- 请不要在这里停车。(Qǐng bù yào zài zhè lǐ tíng chē.) — Please don't park here.

## Notes

停 combines person 亻 and 亭 (pavilion), representing a person stopping to rest at a pavilion.
