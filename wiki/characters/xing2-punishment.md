# 刑 Punishment, sentence, penalty

- **Character**: 刑
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: punishment, sentence, penalty
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刑罚 (xíng fá) — penalty, punishment
- 刑事 (xíng shì) — criminal (law)

## Example Sentences

- 他因盗窃受到刑事处罚。(Tā yīn dào qiè shòu dào xíng shì chǔ fá.) — He received criminal punishment for theft.

## Notes

Essential legal term for criminal punishment. 死刑 means capital punishment. 有期徒刑 means fixed-term imprisonment.
