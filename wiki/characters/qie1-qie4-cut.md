# 切 Palpate

- **Character**: 切
- **Pinyin**: qiē/qiè
- **Meaning**: cut; palpate
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 切脉 (qiè mài) — to feel the pulse
- 切诊 (qiè zhěn) — palpation (TCM)
- 亲切 (qīn qiè) — cordial

## Example Sentences

- 中医通过切脉诊断疾病。(Zhōng yī tōng guò qiè mài zhěn duàn jí bìng.) — TCM diagnoses diseases by feeling the pulse.

## Notes

Palpation; fourth TCM diagnostic method.
