# 邃 Deep, profound, far-reaching

- **Character**: 邃
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: deep, profound, far-reaching
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 深邃 (shēn suì) — deep, profound
- 幽邃 (yōu suì) — serene and deep

## Example Sentences

- 他的眼神深邃而神秘。(Tā de yǎn shén shēn suì ér shén mì.) — His gaze is deep and mysterious.

## Notes

Literary term for depth and profundity. Often used to describe eyes, thought, or spaces.
