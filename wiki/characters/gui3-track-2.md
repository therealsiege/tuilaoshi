# 轨 Orbit

- **Character**: 轨
- **Pinyin**: guǐ
- **Meaning**: track, orbit, rail
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 轨道 (guǐ dào) — orbit, track
- 铁轨 (tiě guǐ) — railway track

## Example Sentences

- 卫星进入轨道。(Wèi xīng jìn rù guǐ dào.) — The satellite enters orbit.

## Notes

轨 simplified from 軌. Vehicle 车 track, path to follow.
