# 腿 Leg

- **Character**: 腿
- **Pinyin**: tuǐ
- **Meaning**: leg
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 大腿 (dàtuǐ) — thigh
- 小腿 (xiǎotuǐ) — calf, lower leg

## Example Sentences

- 跑步后我的腿很酸。(Pǎobù hòu wǒ de tuǐ hěn suān.) — My legs are very sore after running.

## Notes

Basic body part vocabulary. Used for describing movement, exercise, and physical condition.
