# 钳 Pliers

- **Character**: 钳
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: pliers, pincers
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钳子 (qián zi) — pliers, pincers
- 火钳 (huǒ qián) — fire tongs

## Example Sentences

- 工人用钳子夹住了铁丝。(Gōngrén yòng qiánzi jiā zhù le tiěsī.) — The worker gripped the wire with pliers.

## Notes

钳 refers to pliers, tongs, or pincers. Essential tool for gripping and manipulating metal objects.
