# 尘 Dust

- **Character**: 尘
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: dust, dirt, this world
- **Radical**: 小 (small)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 灰尘 (huī chén) — dust
- 尘土 (chén tǔ) — dust

## Example Sentences

- 桌上有很多灰尘。(Zhuō shàng yǒu hěn duō huī chén.) — There is a lot of dust on the table.

## Notes

尘 means dust or dirt. Used in灰尘 (dust), 尘土 (dust and dirt), 红尘 (the mortal world).
