# 擦 To Wipe

- **Character**: 擦
- **Pinyin**: cā
- **Meaning**: to wipe, to rub, to apply
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 擦桌子 (cā zhuō zi) — to wipe table
- 摩擦 (mó cā) — friction

## Example Sentences

- 请擦干净桌子。(Qǐng cā gān jìng zhuō zi.) — Please wipe the table clean.

## Notes

擦 hand 扌 plus 察 (to examine), wiping to clean.
