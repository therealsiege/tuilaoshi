# 醒 To Wake

- **Character**: 醒
- **Pinyin**: xǐng
- **Meaning**: to wake up, to become aware
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 清醒 (qīng xǐng) — sober, clear-headed
- 醒来 (xǐng lái) — to wake up

## Example Sentences

- 我早上六点醒来。(Wǒ zǎo shang liù diǎn xǐng lái.) — I wake up at six in the morning.

## Notes

醒 shows wine vessel 酉 and 星 (star), representing becoming sober or regaining consciousness like stars becoming visible.
