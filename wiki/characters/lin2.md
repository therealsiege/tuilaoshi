# 林 Woods

- **Character**: 林
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: forest, woods, circle
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 森林 (sēn lín) — forest
- 树林 (shù lín) — woods, grove

## Example Sentences

- 我们在树林里散步。(Wǒ men zài shù lín lǐ sàn bù.) — We're taking a walk in the woods.

## Notes

林 shows two trees (木木), meaning a small forest. Metaphorically used for 'circles' (文艺林 literary circles). Very common surname.
