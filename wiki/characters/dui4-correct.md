# 对 Correct

- **Character**: 对
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: correct, toward, opposite
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 对不起 (duì bu qǐ) — sorry
- 对面 (duì miàn) — opposite

## Example Sentences

- 对不起，我来晚了。(Duì bu qǐ, wǒ lái wǎn le.) — Sorry, I came late.

## Notes

对 is versatile: as an adjective (correct), preposition (toward), and in 对不起 (sorry, literally 'cannot face').
