# 背 Back

- **Character**: 背
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: back, carry on back
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 背部 (bèibù) — back (body part)
- 背包 (bēibāo) — backpack

## Example Sentences

- 我的背很疼。(Wǒ de bèi hěn téng.) — My back hurts a lot.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: bèi (back, behind) and bēi (carry on back). Context determines usage.
