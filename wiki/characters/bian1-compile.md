# 编 To Compile

- **Character**: 编
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: to compile, to weave, to edit
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 编辑 (biān jí) — to edit, editor
- 编写 (biān xiě) — to compile

## Example Sentences

- 他在编辑一本书。(Tā zài biān jí yī běn shū.) — He is editing a book.

## Notes

编 uses the silk radical — weaving threads together. Extended to compiling/editing.
