# 弓 Bow

- **Character**: 弓
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: bow (weapon)
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 杯弓蛇影 (bēi gōng shé yǐng) — overly suspicious
- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow

## Example Sentences

- 他会射弓箭。(Tā huì shè gōngjiàn.) — He can shoot a bow and arrow.

## Notes

Used in 杯弓蛇影: the bow whose reflection looks like a snake; itself a radical.
