# 变 To Change

- **Character**: 变
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: to change, to become
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 变化 (biàn huà) — change, transformation
- 改变 (gǎi biàn) — to change, to alter

## Example Sentences

- 天气变冷了。(Tiān qì biàn lěng le.) — The weather has gotten cold.

## Notes

变 indicates transformation or change of state. Often pairs with 化 in 变化 to mean general change.
