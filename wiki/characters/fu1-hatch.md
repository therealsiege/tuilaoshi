# 孵 Hatch, incubate

- **Character**: 孵
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: hatch, incubate
- **Radical**: 卵 (egg)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 孵化 (fū huà) — hatch, incubate
- 孵蛋 (fū dàn) — incubate eggs

## Example Sentences

- 鸟类通过孵化繁殖后代。(Niǎo lèi tōng guò fū huà fán zhí hòu dài.) — Birds reproduce their offspring through hatching.
