# 粘 Sticky

- **Character**: 粘
- **Pinyin**: zhān
- **Meaning**: sticky, to stick, to paste, to glue
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粘贴 (zhān tiē) — to paste
- 粘性 (nián xìng) — viscosity

## Example Sentences

- 这个胶水很粘。(Zhè ge jiāo shuǐ hěn zhān.) — This glue is very sticky.

## Notes

粘 can be zhān (to stick, sticky) or nián (sticky, glutinous). As zhān, describes adhesiveness.
