# 赢 To Win

- **Character**: 赢
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: to win, to profit
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 赢得 (yíng dé) — to win
- 输赢 (shū yíng) — win or lose

## Example Sentences

- 我们队赢了比赛。(Wǒ men duì yíng le bǐ sài.) — Our team won the match.

## Notes

赢 is complex, combining elements meaning 'to gain/profit,' with the shell/money radical 贝 indicating wealth gained through victory.
