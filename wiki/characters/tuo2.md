# 驼 Camel

- **Character**: 驼
- **Pinyin**: tuó
- **Meaning**: camel, hunchbacked
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 骆驼 (luò tuó) — camel
- 驼背 (tuó bèi) — hunchback

## Example Sentences

- 沙漠里有很多骆驼。(Shā mò lǐ yǒu hěn duō luò tuó.) — There are many camels in the desert.

## Notes

驼 simplified from 駝. Horse radical 马 with它 (it), representing the camel's humped shape.
