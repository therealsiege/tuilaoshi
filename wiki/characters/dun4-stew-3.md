# 炖 To Stew

- **Character**: 炖
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: to stew, to simmer
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 炖肉 (dùn ròu) — stewed meat
- 慢炖 (màn dùn) — slow-cook

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈炖了一锅汤。(Māma dùn le yì guō tāng.) — Mom stewed a pot of soup.

## Notes

炖 simplified from 燉. Fire 火 plus 屯 (to store up), slow cooking.
