# 膝 Knee

- **Character**: 膝
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: knee
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 膝盖 (xīgài) — knee
- 膝部 (xībù) — knee area

## Example Sentences

- 他摔倒时伤了膝盖。(Tā shuāidǎo shí shāng le xīgài.) — He hurt his knee when he fell.

## Notes

Almost always appears as 膝盖 in modern Chinese. Important for describing injuries and movement.
