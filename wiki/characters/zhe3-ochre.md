# 赭 Ochre, reddish brown

- **Character**: 赭
- **Pinyin**: zhě
- **Meaning**: ochre, reddish brown
- **Radical**: 赤 (red)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 赭石 (zhě shí) — ochre, hematite
- 赭色 (zhě sè) — ochre color

## Example Sentences

- 这片土地呈现赭红色。(Zhè piàn tǔ dì chéng xiàn zhě hóng sè.) — This land appears ochre red.

## Notes

Natural earth pigment color; reddish brown.
