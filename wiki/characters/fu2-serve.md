# 服 Serve, obey, clothing

- **Character**: 服
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: serve, obey, clothing
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 服装 (fúzhuāng) — clothing
- 服刑 (fúxíng) — serve a sentence

## Example Sentences

- 罪犯正在监狱服刑。(Zuìfàn zhèngzài jiānyù fúxíng.) — The criminal is serving a sentence in prison.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: serve, obey, clothing, take (medicine).
