# 盖 Cover, affix seal

- **Character**: 盖
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: cover, affix seal
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盖章 (gài zhāng) — affix seal, stamp
- 覆盖 (fù gài) — cover, coverage

## Example Sentences

- 文件需要盖章才有效。(Wén jiàn xū yào gài zhāng cái yǒu xiào.) — Documents need to be stamped to be valid.

## Notes

Official seals (章) are crucial in Chinese business culture.
