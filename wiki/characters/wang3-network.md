# 网 Network, net

- **Character**: 网
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: network, net
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 网络 (wǎng luò) — network
- 上网 (shàng wǎng) — go online

## Example Sentences

- 网络连接不稳定。(Wǎng luò lián jiē bù wěn dìng.) — The network connection is unstable.

## Notes

Essential for 网络 (network), 互联网 (internet), 网站 (website)
