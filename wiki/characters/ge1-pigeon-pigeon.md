# 鸽 Pigeon

- **Character**: 鸽
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: pigeon, dove
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 鸽子 (gē zi) — pigeon
- 和平鸽 (hé píng gē) — peace dove

## Example Sentences

- 广场上有很多鸽子。(Guǎng chǎng shàng yǒu hěn duō gē zi.) — There are many pigeons in the square.

## Notes

鸽 simplified from 鴿. Bird 鸟 with 合 (to close/join), the social bird.
