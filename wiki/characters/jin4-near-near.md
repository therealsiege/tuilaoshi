# 近 Near

- **Character**: 近
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: near, close, recent
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 附近 (fù jìn) — nearby
- 最近 (zuì jìn) — recently

## Example Sentences

- 最近怎么样？(Zuì jìn zěn me yàng?) — How have you been lately?

## Notes

近 is opposite of 远 (far). 最近 = recently/lately.
