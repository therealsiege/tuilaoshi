# 痪 Paralysis, 瘓 (traditional)

- **Character**: 痪
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: paralysis, 瘓 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 瘫痪 (tān huàn) — paralyzed
- 痪病 (huàn bìng) — paralytic disease

## Example Sentences

- 脑血栓可能导致瘫痪。(Nǎo xuèshuān kěnéng dǎozhì tānhuàn.) — Cerebral thrombosis may lead to paralysis.

## Notes

Used primarily in the compound 瘫痪 for paralysis.
