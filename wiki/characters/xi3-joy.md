# 喜 Joy

- **Character**: 喜
- **Pinyin**: xǐ
- **Meaning**: joy, happiness, to like
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喜出望外 (xǐ chū wàng wài) — pleasantly surprised
- 欢喜 (huān xǐ) — happy, joyful

## Example Sentences

- 收到礼物，他喜出望外。(Shōu dào lǐwù, tā xǐ chū wàng wài.) — Receiving the gift, he was pleasantly surprised.

## Notes

Used in 喜出望外: joy that exceeds expectations.
