# 慢 Slow

- **Character**: 慢
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: slow
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 慢慢 (màn màn) — slowly
- 减慢 (jiǎn màn) — to slow down

## Example Sentences

- 请说慢一点。(Qǐng shuō màn yī diǎn.) — Please speak a little slower.

## Notes

慢 shows heart 心 with 曼 (extended), representing a calm unhurried heart at ease, hence slow.
