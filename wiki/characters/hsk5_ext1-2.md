# 约 Arrange, Treaty, 条约 Treaty

- **Character**: 约
- **Pinyin**: yuē
- **Meaning**: 约 约 约 约 约 arrange,约 约 约 arrange, treaty, 条约 treaty
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 条约 (tiáo yuē) — treaty
- 约定 (yuē dìng) — arrange, agree on
- 大约 (dà yuē) — approximately

## Example Sentences

- 两国签署了和平条约。(Liǎng guó qiānshǔ le hépíng tiáoyuē.) — The two countries signed a peace treaty.

## Notes

Versatile character meaning both 'arrange/appointment' and 'treaty/agreement'. Also means 'approximately' in some contexts.
