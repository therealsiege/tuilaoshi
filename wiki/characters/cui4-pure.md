# 粹 Pure, essence, unmixed

- **Character**: 粹
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: pure, essence, unmixed
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 精粹 (jīng cuì) — essence, quintessence
- 纯粹 (chún cuì) — pure, unadulterated

## Example Sentences

- 这是纯粹的科学问题。(Zhè shì chúncuì de kēxué wèntí.) — This is a purely scientific question.

## Notes

Purity and essence without mixture.
