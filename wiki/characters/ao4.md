# 奥 Profound, mysterious, Olympics

- **Character**: 奥
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: profound, mysterious, Olympics
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 奥秘 (ào mì) — mystery, secret
- 深奥 (shēn ào) — profound, abstruse

## Example Sentences

- 这个理论很深奥,不容易理解。(Zhè ge lǐ lùn hěn shēn ào, bù róng yì lǐ jiě.) — This theory is very profound and not easy to understand.

## Notes

Means profound or mysterious. 奥运会 means Olympics. 奥妙 means mystery or secret.
