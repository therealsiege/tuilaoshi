# 借 To Borrow

- **Character**: 借
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: to borrow, to lend, to use as pretext
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 借口 (jiè kǒu) — excuse, pretext
- 借钱 (jiè qián) — to borrow money

## Example Sentences

- 我可以借你的笔吗？(Wǒ kě yǐ jiè nǐ de bǐ ma?) — Can I borrow your pen?

## Notes

借 combines person 亻 and 昔 (past), representing taking something temporarily like drawing from the past.
