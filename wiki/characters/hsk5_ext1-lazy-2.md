# 惰 Lazy, Indolent, 懒惰 Lazy

- **Character**: 惰
- **Pinyin**: duò
- **Meaning**: lazy, indolent, 懒惰 lazy
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 懒惰 (lǎn duò) — lazy
- 惰性 (duò xìng) — inertia, laziness
- 怠惰 (dài duò) — idle, lazy

## Example Sentences

- 懒惰是成功的敌人。(Lǎnduò shì chénggōng de dírén.) — Laziness is the enemy of success.

## Notes

Negative character for laziness. 懒惰 is common term for lazy behavior. 惰性 means inertia or lazy tendency.
