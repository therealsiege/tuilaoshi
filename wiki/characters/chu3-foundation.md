# 础 Foundation, base

- **Character**: 础
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: foundation, base
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 基础 (jī chǔ) — foundation, basis
- 柱础 (zhù chǔ) — column base

## Example Sentences

- 打好基础是成功的关键。(Dǎ hǎo jī chǔ shì chéng gōng de guān jiàn.) — Laying a good foundation is the key to success.

## Notes

Literally the stone base of a pillar; broadly means foundation.
