# 瓦 Watt, tile

- **Character**: 瓦
- **Pinyin**: wǎ
- **Meaning**: watt, tile
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 瓦特 (wǎ tè) — watt
- 千瓦 (qiān wǎ) — kilowatt

## Example Sentences

- 功率是1000瓦。(Gōng lǜ shì yī qiān wǎ.) — The power is 1000 watts.

## Notes

Used in 瓦特 (watt), 千瓦 (kilowatt), power measurement
