# 步 Step

- **Character**: 步
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: step, pace, walk
- **Radical**: 止 (stop)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 进步 (jìn bù) — progress
- 散步 (sàn bù) — to take a walk

## Example Sentences

- 你进步了！(Nǐ jìn bù le!) — You've improved!

## Notes

步 means step or pace. 跑步 = to jog, 散步 = take a walk, 步行 = on foot.
