# 釜 Cauldron, pot

- **Character**: 釜
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: cauldron, pot
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 釜底抽薪 (fǔ dǐ chōu xīn) — pull firewood from under the cauldron
- 破釜沉舟 (pò fǔ chén zhōu) — burn one's boats

## Example Sentences

- 他破釜沉舟，决心背水一战。(Tā pò fǔ chén zhōu, jué xīn bèi shuǐ yī zhàn.) — He burned his boats, determined to fight with his back to the wall.

## Notes

Ancient cooking vessel, used in idioms
