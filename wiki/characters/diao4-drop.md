# 掉 To Drop

- **Character**: 掉
- **Pinyin**: diào
- **Meaning**: to drop, to fall, to lose
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掉了 (diào le) — dropped, lost
- 丢掉 (diū diào) — to throw away

## Example Sentences

- 我的手机掉了！(Wǒ de shǒu jī diào le!) — I dropped/lost my phone!

## Notes

掉 as a complement means removal: 忘掉 (forget completely), 扔掉 (throw away), 擦掉 (wipe off).
