# 仰 To look up, admire, rely on

- **Character**: 仰
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: to look up, admire, rely on
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 仰望 (yǎng wàng) — to look up at, admire
- 信仰 (xìn yǎng) — faith, belief

## Example Sentences

- 孩子仰着头看天空。(Háizi yǎngzhe tóu kàn tiānkōng.) — The child looked up at the sky.

## Notes

Common in expressions of respect, admiration, or physical upward orientation.
