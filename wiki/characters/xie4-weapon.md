# 械 Weapon, instrument, shackles

- **Character**: 械
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: weapon, instrument, shackles
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 机械 (jī xiè) — machinery, mechanical
- 器械 (qì xiè) — apparatus, equipment

## Example Sentences

- 机械工程是重要的学科。(Jī xiè gōng chéng shì zhòng yào de xué kē.) — Mechanical engineering is an important discipline.
