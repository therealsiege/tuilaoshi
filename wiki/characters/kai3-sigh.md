# 慨 Sigh, generous

- **Character**: 慨
- **Pinyin**: kǎi
- **Meaning**: sigh, generous
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 慷慨 (kāng kǎi) — generous, vehement
- 感慨 (gǎn kǎi) — sigh with emotion

## Example Sentences

- 看到这一幕，他不禁感慨万千。(Kàn dào zhè yī mù, tā bù jīn gǎn kǎi wàn qiān.) — Seeing this scene, he couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

## Notes

Used in compounds related to emotion and generosity.
