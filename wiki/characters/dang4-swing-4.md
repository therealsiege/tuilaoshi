# 荡 Swing, sweep, dissolute

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: swing, sweep, dissolute
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荡漾 (dàngyàng) — ripple
- 浩浩荡荡 (hàohào dàngdàng) — vast and mighty

## Example Sentences

- 湖水荡漾。(Húshuǐ dàngyàng.) — The lake water ripples.

## Notes

Common for swinging motion or vastness; also means dissolute.
