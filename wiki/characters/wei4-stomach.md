# 胃 Stomach

- **Character**: 胃
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: stomach
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胃口 (wèi kǒu) — appetite
- 胃病 (wèi bìng) — stomach disease

## Example Sentences

- 吃太快会伤胃。(Chī tài kuài huì shāng wèi.) — Eating too fast can hurt the stomach.
