# 基 Base, foundation

- **Character**: 基
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: base, foundation
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 基础 (jīchǔ) — foundation, basis
- 基本 (jīběn) — basic, fundamental

## Example Sentences

- 学好基础很重要。(Xué hǎo jīchǔ hěn zhòngyào.) — Learning the basics well is important.

## Notes

Common character for foundations and fundamentals.
