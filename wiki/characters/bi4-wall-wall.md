# 壁 Wall

- **Character**: 壁
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: wall, rampart, cliff face
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 墙壁 (qiáng bì) — wall
- 壁画 (bì huà) — mural

## Example Sentences

- 这幅壁画很精美。(Zhè fú bì huà hěn jīng měi.) — This mural is exquisite.

## Notes

壁 has earth 土 with 辟 (to open), vertical surface or cliff.
