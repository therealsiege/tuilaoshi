# 有 To Have

- **Character**: 有
- **Pinyin**: yǒu
- **Meaning**: to have, there is
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 没有 (méi yǒu) — don't have
- 所有 (suǒ yǒu) — all

## Example Sentences

- 我有两个孩子。(Wǒ yǒu liǎng ge háizi.) — I have two children.

## Notes

有 is negated with 没 (méi), not 不. 没有 means 'don't have' or 'there isn't.'
