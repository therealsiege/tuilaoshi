# 道 Way

- **Character**: 道
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: way, path, principle, to say
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 知道 (zhī dào) — to know
- 道路 (dào lù) — road

## Example Sentences

- 你知道吗？(Nǐ zhī dào ma?) — Do you know?

## Notes

道 is a central concept in Chinese philosophy (Daoism). It means 'the Way' — the natural order of the universe.
