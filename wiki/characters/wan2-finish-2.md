# 完 Complete

- **Character**: 完
- **Pinyin**: wán
- **Meaning**: to finish, whole, complete
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 完成 (wán chéng) — to complete
- 完全 (wán quán) — completely

## Example Sentences

- 我完成了作业。(Wǒ wán chéng le zuò yè.) — I completed my homework.

## Notes

完 shows completeness under a roof, representing wholeness and completion.
