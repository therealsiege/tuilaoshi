# 圈 Circle

- **Character**: 圈
- **Pinyin**: quān
- **Meaning**: circle, ring, to encircle, group
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 圆圈 (yuán quān) — circle
- 一圈 (yī quān) — one circuit

## Example Sentences

- 我们绕着公园走了一圈。(Wǒ men rào zhe gōng yuán zǒu le yī quān.) — We walked one circuit around the park.

## Notes

圈 can be quān (circle, measure word for circuits) or juàn (pen for animals). As quān, very common for circular movements.
