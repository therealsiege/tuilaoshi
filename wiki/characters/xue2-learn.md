# 学 Learn, study

- **Character**: 学
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: learn, study
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 学习 (xué xí) — learn, study
- 机器学习 (jī qì xué xí) — machine learning

## Example Sentences

- 机器学习是AI的核心。(Jī qì xué xí shì AI de hé xīn.) — Machine learning is the core of AI.

## Notes

Used in 学习 (learning), 机器学习 (machine learning), 深度学习 (deep learning)
