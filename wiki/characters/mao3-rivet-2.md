# 铆 Rivet

- **Character**: 铆
- **Pinyin**: mǎo
- **Meaning**: rivet, to rivet
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 铆钉 (mǎo dīng) — rivet
- 铆接 (mǎo jiē) — to rivet

## Example Sentences

- 用铆钉固定钢板。(Yòng mǎo dīng gù dìng gāng bǎn.) — Use rivets to secure the steel plate.

## Notes

铆 simplified from 鉚. Metal 钅 with 卯 (earthly branch), metal fastener.
