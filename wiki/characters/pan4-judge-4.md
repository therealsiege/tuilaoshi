# 判 Judge, decide, sentence

- **Character**: 判
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: judge, decide, sentence
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 判决 (pànjué) — judgment, verdict
- 审判 (shěnpàn) — trial

## Example Sentences

- 法官宣布判决。(Fǎguān xuānbù pànjué.) — The judge announced the verdict.

## Notes

Legal term for judgment and sentencing.
