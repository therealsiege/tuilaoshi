# 掘 To dig, to excavate

- **Character**: 掘
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to dig, to excavate
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 挖掘 (wā jué) — to excavate, to dig
- 发掘 (fā jué) — to excavate, to explore

## Example Sentences

- 考古队在挖掘古墓。(Kǎogǔ duì zài wājué gǔmù.) — The archaeological team is excavating the ancient tomb.

## Notes

Often used for formal excavation or digging projects.
