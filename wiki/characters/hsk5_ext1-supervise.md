# 督 Supervise, 监督 Supervise

- **Character**: 督
- **Pinyin**: dū
- **Meaning**: supervise, 监督 supervise
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 监督 (jiān dū) — supervise, oversee
- 督促 (dū cù) — urge, supervise
- 总督 (zǒng dū) — governor-general

## Example Sentences

- 家长要监督孩子学习。(Jiāzhǎng yào jiāndū háizi xuéxí.) — Parents should supervise their children's studies.

## Notes

Formal term for supervision and oversight. 监督 (supervise) is common in organizational and governmental contexts.
