# 罩 To cover, to shade, cover, shade

- **Character**: 罩
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: to cover, to shade, cover, shade
- **Radical**: 罒(net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 口罩 (kǒu zhào) — face mask
- 灯罩 (dēng zhào) — lampshade

## Example Sentences

- 出门请戴口罩。(Chūmén qǐng dài kǒuzhào.) — Please wear a face mask when going out.

## Notes

Often used for protective or enclosing covers.
