# 论 To Discuss

- **Character**: 论
- **Pinyin**: lùn
- **Meaning**: to discuss, theory, treatise
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 讨论 (tǎo lùn) — to discuss
- 理论 (lǐ lùn) — theory

## Example Sentences

- 我们讨论了这个问题。(Wǒ men tǎo lùn le zhè gè wèn tí.) — We discussed this problem.

## Notes

论 simplified from 論. Uses speech radical 讠, representing systematic reasoned discourse.
