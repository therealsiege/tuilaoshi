# 手 Hand

- **Character**: 手
- **Pinyin**: shǒu
- **Meaning**: hand
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 手机 (shǒu jī) — cellphone
- 左手 (zuǒ shǒu) — left hand

## Example Sentences

- 请举手。(Qǐng jǔ shǒu.) — Please raise your hand.

## Notes

手 is a pictograph of five fingers. As a radical (扌) it appears in action-related characters: 打, 拿, 推.
