# 被 By (Passive)

- **Character**: 被
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: by (passive), quilt
- **Radical**: 衤(clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 被子 (bèi zi) — quilt
- 被动 (bèi dòng) — passive

## Example Sentences

- 我的手机被偷了。(Wǒ de shǒu jī bèi tōu le.) — My phone was stolen.

## Notes

被 marks the passive voice: Subject + 被 + (agent) + verb. The passive in Chinese often implies something unwanted happened.
