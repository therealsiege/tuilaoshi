# 搁 To Place

- **Character**: 搁
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: to place, to put aside
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搁置 (gē zhì) — to shelve
- 搁浅 (gē qiǎn) — to run aground

## Example Sentences

- 把书搁在桌子上。(Bǎ shū gē zài zhuō zi shàng.) — Put the book on the table.

## Notes

搁 simplified from 擱. Hand 扌 placing something down.
