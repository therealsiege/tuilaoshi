# 侪 Peer, equal

- **Character**: 侪
- **Pinyin**: chái
- **Meaning**: peer, equal
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 同侪 (tóngchái) — peer, fellow
- 侪辈 (cháibèi) — peers, contemporaries

## Example Sentences

- 他在同侪中出类拔萃。(Tā zài tóngchái zhōng chūlèibácuì.) — He stands out among his peers.

## Notes

Literary term for people of equal status or age group.
