# 中 Middle/Center/China

- **Character**: 中
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: middle, center, among; China (abbreviation)
- **Radical**: 丨 (line)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 中国 (Zhōngguó) — China
- 中文 (Zhōngwén) — Chinese language (written)
- 中间 (zhōngjiān) — middle, between
- 中午 (zhōngwǔ) — noon, midday
- 中学 (zhōngxué) — middle school

## Example Sentences

- 中国很大。(Zhōngguó hěn dà.) — China is very large.
- 他在房间中间。(Tā zài fángjiān zhōngjiān.) — He is in the middle of the room.
- 中午我们一起吃饭吧。(Zhōngwǔ wǒmen yìqǐ chī fàn ba.) — Let's eat lunch together at noon.

## Notes

中 depicts an arrow hitting the center of a target, or a line passing through the middle of a rectangle. China's name for itself, 中国 (Zhōngguó), literally means "Middle Kingdom," reflecting the ancient view of China as the center of the civilized world. When pronounced zhòng (fourth tone), it means "to hit the mark" as in 中奖 (zhòngjiǎng, "to win a prize").
