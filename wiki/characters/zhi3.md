# 指 Finger

- **Character**: 指
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: finger, to point
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 手指 (shǒu zhǐ) — finger
- 指导 (zhǐ dǎo) — to guide

## Example Sentences

- 我的手指受伤了。(Wǒ de shǒu zhǐ shòu shāng le.) — My finger is injured.

## Notes

指 with hand radical. 手指 = finger. As verb means to point/indicate.
