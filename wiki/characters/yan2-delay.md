# 延 Delay, extend

- **Character**: 延
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: delay, extend
- **Radical**: 廴 (move)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 延迟 (yán chí) — latency, delay
- 延长 (yán cháng) — extend

## Example Sentences

- 网络延迟很高。(Wǎng luò yán chí hěn gāo.) — The network latency is high.

## Notes

Used in 延迟 (latency), 延长 (extend), network performance terminology
