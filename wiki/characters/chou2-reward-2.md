# 酬 Reward, remuneration

- **Character**: 酬
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: reward, remuneration
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 报酬 (bào chóu) — remuneration, reward
- 薪酬 (xīn chóu) — salary, pay

## Example Sentences

- 他的工作报酬很高。(Tā de gōng zuò bào chóu hěn gāo.) — His work remuneration is very high.

## Notes

酬劳 means reward for labor. 酬金 means monetary reward.
