# 玛 Agate

- **Character**: 玛
- **Pinyin**: mǎ
- **Meaning**: agate (in 玛瑙)
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 玛瑙 (mǎ nǎo) — agate

## Example Sentences

- 玛瑙是一种半透明宝石。(Mǎ nǎo shì yì zhǒng bàn tòu míng bǎo shí.) — Agate is a type of translucent gemstone.

## Notes

玛 simplified from 瑪. Jade 玉 with 马 (horse), used in 玛瑙.
