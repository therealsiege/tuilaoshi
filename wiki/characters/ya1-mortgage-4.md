# 押 Mortgage, deposit

- **Character**: 押
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: mortgage, deposit
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抵押 (dǐ yā) — mortgage
- 押金 (yā jīn) — deposit

## Example Sentences

- 租房要交押金。(Zū fáng yào jiāo yā jīn.) — Renting requires paying a deposit.

## Notes

押金 is security deposit. 押运 means escort (goods).
