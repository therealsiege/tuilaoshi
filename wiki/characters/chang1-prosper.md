# 昌 Prosperous

- **Character**: 昌
- **Pinyin**: chāng
- **Meaning**: prosperous, flourishing
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 昌盛 (chāng shèng) — prosperous
- 南昌 (nán chāng) — Nanchang (city)

## Example Sentences

- 国家繁荣昌盛。(Guó jiā fán róng chāng shèng.) — The country is prosperous and flourishing.

## Notes

昌 means prosperous. Used in city names like 南昌. 两个日 = double sun = bright and flourishing.
