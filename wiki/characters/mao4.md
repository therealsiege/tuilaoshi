# 贸 Trade

- **Character**: 贸
- **Pinyin**: mào
- **Meaning**: trade, to trade
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贸易 (mào yì) — trade
- 外贸 (wài mào) — foreign trade

## Example Sentences

- 两国开展贸易合作。(Liǎng guó kāi zhǎn mào yì hé zuò.) — The two countries are developing trade cooperation.

## Notes

贸 simplified from 貿. Contains shell/money radical 贝, reflecting exchange of goods and currency.
