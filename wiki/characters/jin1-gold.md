# 金 Gold

- **Character**: 金
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: gold, metal, money
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 金钱 (jīn qián) — money
- 黄金 (huáng jīn) — gold

## Example Sentences

- 金钱不是一切。(Jīn qián bú shì yī qiè.) — Money isn't everything.

## Notes

金 is one of the five elements (五行) in Chinese philosophy. Also a common surname. The traditional form is also 金.
