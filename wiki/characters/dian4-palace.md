# 殿 Palace Hall

- **Character**: 殿
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: palace hall, temple
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 宫殿 (gōng diàn) — palace
- 殿堂 (diàn táng) — hall, palace

## Example Sentences

- 故宫有很多宫殿。(Gù gōng yǒu hěn duō gōng diàn.) — The Forbidden City has many palace halls.

## Notes

殿 has rear 尸 (body) with 殳 (weapon), grand ceremonial building.
