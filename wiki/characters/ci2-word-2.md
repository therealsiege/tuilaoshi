# 词 Word

- **Character**: 词
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: word, term, lyrics, a type of poetry
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 词语 (cí yǔ) — word, term
- 歌词 (gē cí) — lyrics

## Example Sentences

- 这个词是什么意思？(Zhè ge cí shì shén me yì si?) — What does this word mean?

## Notes

词 means word or a type of Song dynasty poetry (词牌). Used in词语 (word), 歌词 (lyrics).
