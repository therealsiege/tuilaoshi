# 举 Lift

- **Character**: 举
- **Pinyin**: jǔ
- **Meaning**: to lift, to cite, to elect
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 举一反三 (jǔ yī fǎn sān) — learn by analogy
- 举行 (jǔ xíng) — to hold (event)

## Example Sentences

- 好学生能举一反三。(Hǎo xuéshēng néng jǔ yī fǎn sān.) — Good students can learn by analogy.

## Notes

Used in 举一反三: give one example and infer three; from Confucius.
