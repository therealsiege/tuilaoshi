# 罚 Punish, fine, penalty

- **Character**: 罚
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: punish, fine, penalty
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 处罚 (chǔfá) — punish, penalize
- 罚款 (fákuǎn) — fine, penalty

## Example Sentences

- 违规者将被罚款。(Wéiguīzhě jiāng bèi fákuǎn.) — Violators will be fined.

## Notes

Legal term for punishment and fines.
