# 牵 Lead, pull, involve

- **Character**: 牵
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: lead, pull, involve
- **Radical**: 牛 (cow)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 牵引 (qiān yǐn) — traction, draw
- 牵挂 (qiān guà) — worry about

## Example Sentences

- 我很牵挂远方的家人。(Wǒ hěn qiān guà yuǎn fāng de jiā rén.) — I worry about my family far away.

## Notes

Physical pulling or emotional connection
