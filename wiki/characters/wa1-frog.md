# 蛙 Frog

- **Character**: 蛙
- **Pinyin**: wā
- **Meaning**: frog
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 青蛙 (qīng wā) — frog
- 牛蛙 (niú wā) — bullfrog

## Example Sentences

- 池塘里有很多青蛙。(Chítáng lǐ yǒu hěn duō qīngwā.) — There are many frogs in the pond.

## Notes

Amphibian but uses insect radical. Common in rice paddies.
