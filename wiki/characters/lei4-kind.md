# 类 Category

- **Character**: 类
- **Pinyin**: lèi
- **Meaning**: kind, type, class, category
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 种类 (zhǒng lèi) — type, category
- 分类 (fēn lèi) — to classify

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要把这些东西分类。(Wǒ men xū yào bǎ zhè xiē dōng xi fēn lèi.) — We need to classify these things.

## Notes

类 shows rice + dog, representing categorization. Essential for discussing types and classifications.
