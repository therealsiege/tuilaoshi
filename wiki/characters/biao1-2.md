# 标 标志, mark, sign

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: 标志, mark, sign
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 商标 (shāng biāo) — trademark, brand
- 标志 (biāo zhì) — 标志, symbol

## Example Sentences

- 这是注册商标。(Zhè shì zhù cè shāng biāo.) — This is a registered trademark.

## Notes

标识 means sign/logo. 标语 means slogan.
