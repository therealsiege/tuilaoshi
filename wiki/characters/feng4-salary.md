# 俸 Salary, stipend

- **Character**: 俸
- **Pinyin**: fèng
- **Meaning**: salary, stipend
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 俸禄 (fèng lù) — official's salary
- 薪俸 (xīn fèng) — salary, pay

## Example Sentences

- 清官不拿俸禄之外的钱财。(Qīng guān bù ná fènglù zhī wài de qiáncái.) — Honest officials don't take money beyond their salary.

## Notes

Specifically refers to the official salary paid to government employees. Often paired with 禄 to emphasize official compensation.
