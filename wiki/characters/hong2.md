# 红 Red

- **Character**: 红
- **Pinyin**: hóng
- **Meaning**: red
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 红色 (hóng sè) — red (color)
- 红包 (hóng bāo) — red envelope

## Example Sentences

- 中国人喜欢红色。(Zhōng guó rén xǐ huān hóng sè.) — Chinese people like the color red.

## Notes

红 symbolizes luck and joy in Chinese culture. 红包 (red envelopes with money) are given during holidays and celebrations.
