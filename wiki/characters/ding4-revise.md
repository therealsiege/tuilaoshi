# 订 Revise, subscribe, book

- **Character**: 订
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: revise, subscribe, book
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 修订 (xiū dìng) — to revise (documents)
- 订阅 (dìng yuè) — to subscribe

## Example Sentences

- 国会修订了新法案。(Guó huì xiū dìng le xīn fǎ àn.) — Congress revised the new bill.
