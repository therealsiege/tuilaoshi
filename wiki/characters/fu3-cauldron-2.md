# 釜 Cauldron, pot

- **Character**: 釜
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: cauldron, pot
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 釜底抽薪 (fǔ dǐ chōu xīn) — remove firewood from under the cauldron
- 破釜沉舟 (pò fǔ chén zhōu) — burn one's boats

## Example Sentences

- 要从根本上解决问题，必须釜底抽薪。(Yào cóng gēn běn shàng jiě jué wèn tí, bì xū fǔ dǐ chōu xīn.) — To solve the problem fundamentally, one must remove the firewood from under the cauldron.

## Notes

Ancient cooking vessel; used in idioms.
