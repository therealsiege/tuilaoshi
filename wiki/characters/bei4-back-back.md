# 背 Back

- **Character**: 背
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: back, to carry on back, to recite (bèi)
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 背后 (bèi hòu) — behind
- 背景 (bèi jǐng) — background

## Example Sentences

- 背后的故事很感人。(Bèi hòu de gù shì hěn gǎn rén.) — The story behind it is touching.

## Notes

背 as bèi means back. As bēi means to carry on one's back.
