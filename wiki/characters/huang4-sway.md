# 晃 To Sway

- **Character**: 晃
- **Pinyin**: huàng
- **Meaning**: to sway, to dazzle (also huǎng)
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晃动 (huàng dòng) — to sway, to shake
- 摇晃 (yáo huàng) — to rock, to shake

## Example Sentences

- 灯在风中摇晃。(Dēng zài fēng zhōng yáo huàng.) — The lamp swayed in the wind.

## Notes

晃 as huàng means to sway, as huǎng means to flash by. Sun 日 representing dazzling light or swaying.
