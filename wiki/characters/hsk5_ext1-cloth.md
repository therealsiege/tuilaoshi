# 布 Cloth, Announce, 公布 Announce

- **Character**: 布
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: cloth, announce, 公布 announce
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 公布 (gōng bù) — announce, make public
- 颁布 (bān bù) — promulgate, issue
- 分布 (fēn bù) — distribute, spread

## Example Sentences

- 学校公布了考试成绩。(Xuéxiào gōngbù le kǎoshì chéngjì.) — The school announced the exam results.

## Notes

Originally meant cloth, now commonly used in compounds meaning 'announce' or 'distribute'. 公布 (announce) is very common in official contexts.
