# 键 Key

- **Character**: 键
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: key
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 键盘 (jiàn pán) — keyboard
- 按键 (àn jiàn) — key, button

## Example Sentences

- 请按回车键。(Qǐng àn huí chē jiàn.) — Please press the Enter key.

## Notes

Used in 键盘 (keyboard), 按键 (key), 快捷键 (shortcut key)
