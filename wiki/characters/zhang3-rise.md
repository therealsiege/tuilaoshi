# 涨 Rise, go up

- **Character**: 涨
- **Pinyin**: zhǎng
- **Meaning**: rise, go up
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涨价 (zhǎng jià) — price increase
- 上涨 (shàng zhǎng) — rise, go up

## Example Sentences

- 股票价格上涨了。(Gǔ piào jià gé shàng zhǎng le.) — Stock prices went up.

## Notes

Different from 涨 (zhàng = swell). Opposite of 跌.
