# 鸥 Seagull

- **Character**: 鸥
- **Pinyin**: ōu
- **Meaning**: gull, seagull
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 海鸥 (hǎi ōu) — seagull
- 鸥群 (ōu qún) — flock of gulls

## Example Sentences

- 海边有很多海鸥。(Hǎi biān yǒu hěn duō hǎi ōu.) — There are many seagulls by the sea.

## Notes

鸥 simplified from 鷗. Bird 鸟 with 区 (area/district).
