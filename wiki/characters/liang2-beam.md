# 梁 Beam

- **Character**: 梁
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: beam, bridge
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 桥梁 (qiáo liáng) — bridge
- 栋梁 (dòng liáng) — pillar (person)

## Example Sentences

- 他是国家的栋梁。(Tā shì guó jiā de dòng liáng.) — He is a pillar of the nation.

## Notes

梁 shows wood 木 with water 水 beneath, originally referring to bridges spanning water, extended to roof beams.
