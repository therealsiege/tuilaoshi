# 渣 Slag, residue

- **Character**: 渣
- **Pinyin**: zhā
- **Meaning**: slag, residue
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 炉渣 (lú zhā) — slag
- 渣滓 (zhā zǐ) — dregs, residue

## Example Sentences

- 炉渣需要清理。(Lú zhā xū yào qīng lǐ.) — The slag needs to be cleaned.

## Notes

Used in 炉渣 (slag), 废渣 (waste residue), industrial waste
