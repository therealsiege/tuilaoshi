# 环 Ring

- **Character**: 环
- **Pinyin**: huán
- **Meaning**: ring, loop, environment
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 环境 (huán jìng) — environment
- 循环 (xún huán) — cycle, to circulate

## Example Sentences

- 保护环境很重要。(Bǎo hù huán jìng hěn zhòng yào.) — Protecting the environment is important.

## Notes

环 with jade radical for ring, essential in environment and循 循loop terminology.
