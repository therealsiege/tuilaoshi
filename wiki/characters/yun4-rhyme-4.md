# 韵 Rhyme

- **Character**: 韵
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: rhyme, charm, appeal
- **Radical**: 音 (sound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 韵律 (yùn lǜ) — rhythm, meter
- 押韵 (yā yùn) — to rhyme

## Example Sentences

- 古诗讲究韵律。(Gǔ shī jiǎng jiū yùn lǜ.) — Classical poetry emphasizes rhythm and rhyme.

## Notes

韵 refers to rhyme in poetry or charm and appeal in general.
