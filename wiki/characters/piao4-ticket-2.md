# 票 Ticket, ballot, note

- **Character**: 票
- **Pinyin**: piào
- **Meaning**: ticket, ballot, note
- **Radical**: 示 (示)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 发票 (fā piào) — invoice, receipt
- 票据 (piào jù) — bill, note

## Example Sentences

- 请给我开发票。(Qǐng gěi wǒ kāi fā piào.) — Please give me an invoice.

## Notes

发票 is essential for tax deductions and reimbursement in China.
