# 防 Prevent, protect

- **Character**: 防
- **Pinyin**: fáng
- **Meaning**: 防御, protect
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 防火墙 (fáng huǒ qiáng) — firewall
- 防护 (fáng hù) — protect

## Example Sentences

- 防火墙阻止攻击。(Fáng huǒ qiáng zǔ zhǐ gōng jī.) — The firewall blocks attacks.

## Notes

Essential for 防火墙 (firewall), 防护 (protection), security
