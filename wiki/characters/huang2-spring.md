# 簧 Spring, reed

- **Character**: 簧
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: spring, reed
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 弹簧 (tán huáng) — spring (mechanical)
- 簧片 (huáng piàn) — reed

## Example Sentences

- 弹簧可以储存机械能。(Tán huáng kě yǐ chǔ cún jī xiè néng.) — Springs can store mechanical energy.
