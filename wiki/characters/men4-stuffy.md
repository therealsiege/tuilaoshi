# 闷 Stuffy, bored, sealed, 悶 (traditional)

- **Character**: 闷
- **Pinyin**: mèn
- **Meaning**: stuffy, bored, sealed, 悶 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 烦闷 (fán mèn) — bored, depressed
- 闷热 (mēn rè) — muggy, sultry

## Example Sentences

- 今天天气很闷热。(Jīntiān tiānqì hěn mēnrè.) — The weather is very muggy today.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: mèn (bored/depressed) and mēn (stuffy/sealed).
