# 张 Open, stretch, exaggerate

- **Character**: 张
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: open, stretch, exaggerate
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 夸张 (kuāzhāng) — exaggerate
- 嚣张 (xiāozhāng) — arrogant, aggressive

## Example Sentences

- 他的话太夸张了。(Tā de huà tài kuāzhāng le.) — His words are too exaggerated.

## Notes

Common character for opening, spreading, or exaggeration.
