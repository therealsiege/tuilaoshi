# 澎 Roaring water, surge

- **Character**: 澎
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: roaring water, surge
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 澎湃 (péngpài) — surge, seethe
- 心潮澎湃 (xīncháo péngpài) — heart surging with emotion

## Example Sentences

- 海浪澎湃汹涌。(Hǎilàng péngpài xiōngyǒng.) — The waves surge tumultuously.

## Notes

Usually used in the compound 澎湃 to describe surging water or emotions.
