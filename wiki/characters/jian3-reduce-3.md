# 减 Reduce, subtract

- **Character**: 减
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: reduce, subtract
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 减少 (jiǎn shǎo) — reduce, decrease
- 削减 (xuē jiǎn) — cut, reduce

## Example Sentences

- 公司减少了开支。(Gōng sī jiǎn shǎo le kāi zhī.) — The company reduced expenses.

## Notes

减价 means reduce price. 减免 means reduce or remit.
