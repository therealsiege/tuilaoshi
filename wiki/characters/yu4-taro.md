# 芋 Taro

- **Character**: 芋
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: taro
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 芋头 (yù tou) — taro
- 香芋 (xiāng yù) — fragrant taro

## Example Sentences

- 芋头可以蒸着吃。(Yùtou kěyǐ zhēng zhe chī.) — Taro can be eaten steamed.

## Notes

Popular root vegetable in Chinese cuisine. Used in desserts and savory dishes.
