# 动 Move, change

- **Character**: 动
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: move, change
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 波动 (bō dòng) — fluctuate
- 变动 (biàn dòng) — change

## Example Sentences

- 市场价格波动频繁。(Shì chǎng jià gé bō dòng pín fán.) — Market prices fluctuate frequently.

## Notes

流动 means flow/mobile. 活动 means activity.
