# 撞 Hit

- **Character**: 撞
- **Pinyin**: zhuàng
- **Meaning**: hit, collide
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撞车 (zhuàng chē) — car crash
- 撞到 (zhuàng dào) — bump into

## Example Sentences

- 他不小心撞到了墙。(Tā bù xiǎoxīn zhuàng dào le qiáng.) — He accidentally bumped into the wall.

## Notes

Collision or impact. Hand radical. 撞车 is car accident. Common for accidents and impacts.
