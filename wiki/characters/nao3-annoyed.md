# 恼 Annoyed, vexed, 惱 (traditional)

- **Character**: 恼
- **Pinyin**: nǎo
- **Meaning**: annoyed, vexed, 惱 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 烦恼 (fán nǎo) — worried, vexed
- 恼火 (nǎo huǒ) — to get angry

## Example Sentences

- 这件事让我很烦恼。(Zhè jiàn shì ràng wǒ hěn fánnǎo.) — This matter vexes me.

## Notes

Describes annoyance, vexation, or being troubled by something.
