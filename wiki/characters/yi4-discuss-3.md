# 议 Discuss, opinion

- **Character**: 议
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: discuss, opinion
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 会议 (huì yì) — meeting, conference
- 建议 (jiàn yì) — suggest, proposal

## Example Sentences

- 董事会召开紧急会议。(Dǒng shì huì zhào kāi jǐn jí huì yì.) — The board convened an emergency meeting.

## Notes

议案 is motion/bill. 议价 means negotiate price.
