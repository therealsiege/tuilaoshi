# 黛 Black, eyebrow paint

- **Character**: 黛
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: black, eyebrow paint
- **Radical**: 黑 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 黛色 (dài sè) — dark green or black
- 粉黛 (fěn dài) — face powder and eyebrow paint

## Example Sentences

- 远山如黛，景色优美。(Yuǎn shān rú dài, jǐng sè yōu měi.) — The distant mountains are dark green, the scenery is beautiful.

## Notes

Poetic term for dark colors, especially in landscape.
