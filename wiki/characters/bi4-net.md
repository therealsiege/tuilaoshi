# 毕 Net

- **Character**: 毕
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: Net constellation, complete
- **Radical**: 比(compare)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 毕宿 (bì xiù) — Net constellation
- 毕业 (bì yè) — graduate

## Example Sentences

- 毕宿在西方白虎七宿中代表猎网。(Bì xiù zài xīfāng báihǔ qī xiù zhōng dàibiǎo liè wǎng.) — Net constellation represents a hunting net in the Western White Tiger mansions.

## Notes

毕 means to complete or finish. In astronomy, the Net constellation, resembling a hunting net.
