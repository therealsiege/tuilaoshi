# 护 Nurse

- **Character**: 护
- **Pinyin**: hù
- **Meaning**: protect, nurse
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 护士 (hù shi) — nurse
- 护理 (hù lǐ) — nursing care
- 保护 (bǎo hù) — to protect

## Example Sentences

- 护士每天检查病人。(Hù shi měi tiān jiǎn chá bìng rén.) — Nurses check on patients every day.

## Notes

Care and protection; essential healthcare role.
