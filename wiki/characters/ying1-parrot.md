# 鹦 Parrot

- **Character**: 鹦
- **Pinyin**: yīng
- **Meaning**: parrot
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 鹦鹉 (yīng wǔ) — parrot
- 鹦鹉学舌 (yīng wǔ xué shé) — to parrot

## Example Sentences

- 鹦鹉会学人说话。(Yīng wǔ huì xué rén shuō huà.) — Parrots can learn to speak.

## Notes

鹦 simplified from 鸚. Bird 鸟 with 婴 (infant), bird that mimics speech.
