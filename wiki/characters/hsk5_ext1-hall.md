# 殿 Hall, Palace, 宫殿 Palace

- **Character**: 殿
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: hall, palace, 宫殿 palace
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 宫殿 (gōng diàn) — palace
- 殿堂 (diàn táng) — hall, palace
- 大殿 (dà diàn) — main hall

## Example Sentences

- 故宫有很多宏伟的宫殿。(Gùgōng yǒu hěn duō hóngwěi de gōngdiàn.) — The Forbidden City has many magnificent palaces.

## Notes

Formal term for palace halls and grand buildings. Used in both imperial (宫殿) and religious (佛殿 Buddhist hall) contexts.
