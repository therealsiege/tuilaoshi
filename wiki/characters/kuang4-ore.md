# 矿 Mineral

- **Character**: 矿
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: ore, mine, mineral
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 矿物 (kuàng wù) — mineral
- 煤矿 (méi kuàng) — coal mine

## Example Sentences

- 这个地区有丰富的矿产资源。(Zhè ge dì qū yǒu fēng fù de kuàng chǎn zī yuán.) — This region has abundant mineral resources.

## Notes

矿 simplified from 礦. Stone 石 with 广 (broad), underground mineral deposits.
