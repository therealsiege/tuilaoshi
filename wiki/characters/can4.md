# 灿 Brilliant

- **Character**: 灿
- **Pinyin**: càn
- **Meaning**: brilliant, splendid
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灿烂 (càn làn) — bright, brilliant
- 鲜灿 (xiān càn) — bright and colorful

## Example Sentences

- 阳光灿烂。(Yángguāng cànlàn.) — The sun is brilliant.

## Notes

灿 combines fire 火 with 山 (mountain), suggesting brilliant light shining from mountaintops.
