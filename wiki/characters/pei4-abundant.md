# 沛 Abundant, copious

- **Character**: 沛
- **Pinyin**: pèi
- **Meaning**: abundant, copious
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 充沛 (chōngpèi) — abundant, plentiful
- 精力充沛 (jīnglì chōngpèi) — energetic, vigorous

## Example Sentences

- 他精力充沛地工作。(Tā jīnglì chōngpèi de gōngzuò.) — He works energetically.

## Notes

Literary term for abundance, commonly used in 充沛.
