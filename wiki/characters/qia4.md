# 恰 Exactly

- **Character**: 恰
- **Pinyin**: qià
- **Meaning**: exactly, just, appropriate
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恰好 (qià hǎo) — just right, exactly
- 恰当 (qià dàng) — appropriate

## Example Sentences

- 时间恰好，我们走吧。(Shí jiān qià hǎo, wǒ men zǒu ba.) — The timing is just right, let's go.

## Notes

恰 combines heart 心 and 合 (join), representing a feeling of perfect fit or appropriateness.
