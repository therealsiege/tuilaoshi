# 穷 Poor

- **Character**: 穷
- **Pinyin**: qióng
- **Meaning**: poor, exhausted
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 贫穷 (pín qióng) — poor
- 穷人 (qióng rén) — poor person

## Example Sentences

- 他出身于一个贫穷的家庭。(Tā chū shēn yú yī gè pín qióng de jiā tíng.) — He came from a poor family.

## Notes

穷 simplified from 窮. Shows cave 穴 and bent body 弓, depicting someone confined to a cave dwelling in poverty.
