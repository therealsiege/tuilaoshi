# 蹦 To jump, to bounce, to hop

- **Character**: 蹦
- **Pinyin**: bèng
- **Meaning**: to jump, to bounce, to hop
- **Radical**: 足(foot)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 蹦跳 (bèng tiào) — to hop, to jump
- 活蹦乱跳 (huó bèng luàn tiào) — lively, full of energy

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在院子里蹦蹦跳跳。(Háizimen zài yuànzi lǐ bèngbeng-tiàotiào.) — The children are hopping and jumping in the yard.

## Notes

Often used for playful or energetic jumping.
