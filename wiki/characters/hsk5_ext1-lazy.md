# 怠 Lazy, Idle, 怠慢 Neglect

- **Character**: 怠
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: lazy, idle, 怠慢 neglect
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 怠慢 (dài màn) — neglect, cold-shoulder
- 懈怠 (xiè dài) — slack off, slacken
- 怠工 (dài gōng) — slow down, work slowdown

## Example Sentences

- 学习不能懈怠。(Xuéxí bù néng xièdài.) — One must not slack off in studying.

## Notes

Negative term for laziness and neglect. 怠慢 can mean neglecting guests/duties. 懈怠 is slacking off or losing diligence.
