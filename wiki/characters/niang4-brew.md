# 酿 Brew, make wine, cause

- **Character**: 酿
- **Pinyin**: niàng
- **Meaning**: brew, make wine, cause
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 酝酿 (yùn niàng) — brew, ferment
- 酿酒 (niàng jiǔ) — make wine

## Example Sentences

- 这家酒厂酿造传统白酒。(Zhè jiā jiǔ chǎng niàng zào chuán tǒng bái jiǔ.) — This distillery brews traditional Chinese liquor.

## Notes

Also means to cause or lead to (as in 酿成灾祸).
