# 倾 Lean, incline, pour out

- **Character**: 倾
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: lean, incline, pour out
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倾听 (qīngtīng) — listen attentively
- 倾诉 (qīngsù) — pour out (thoughts)

## Example Sentences

- 他倾诉了心中的烦恼。(Tā qīngsù le xīnzhōng de fánnǎo.) — He poured out his troubles.

## Notes

Common for leaning, pouring, or expressing emotions fully.
