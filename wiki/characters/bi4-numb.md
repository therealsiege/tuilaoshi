# 痹 Numbness

- **Character**: 痹
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: numbness, paralysis
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 麻痹 (má bì) — paralysis, numb
- 风痹 (fēng bì) — rheumatism

## Example Sentences

- 不要麻痹大意。(Bú yào má bì dà yì.) — Don't be careless.

## Notes

痹 has sickness 疒 with 畀 (to give), loss of sensation/movement.
