# 颂 To Praise

- **Character**: 颂
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: to praise, ode
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 颂扬 (sòng yáng) — to praise
- 赞颂 (zàn sòng) — to eulogize

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗颂扬了英雄的事迹。(Zhè shǒu shī sòng yáng le yīng xióng de shì jì.) — This poem praises the hero's deeds.

## Notes

颂 means to praise or an ode; often used in formal or literary contexts.
