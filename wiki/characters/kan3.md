# 砍 To chop, to cut, to hack

- **Character**: 砍
- **Pinyin**: kǎn
- **Meaning**: to chop, to cut, to hack
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砍伐 (kǎn fá) — to cut down, to fell
- 砍树 (kǎn shù) — to chop down trees

## Example Sentences

- 工人正在砍树。(Gōngrén zhèngzài kǎn shù.) — The workers are cutting down trees.

## Notes

Common verb for chopping or cutting down.
