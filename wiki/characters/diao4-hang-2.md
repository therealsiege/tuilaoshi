# 吊 Hang

- **Character**: 吊
- **Pinyin**: diào
- **Meaning**: to hang, suspend
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 吊环 (diào huán) — rings (gymnastics)
- 悬吊 (xuán diào) — to suspend

## Example Sentences

- 运动员在吊环上完成动作。(Yùndòngyuán zài diàohuán shàng wánchéng dòngzuò.) — The athlete completes moves on the rings.

## Notes

Hanging and suspension; rings apparatus
