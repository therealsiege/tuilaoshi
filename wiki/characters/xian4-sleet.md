# 霰 Sleet

- **Character**: 霰
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: sleet, graupel
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 霰弹 (xiàn dàn) — shotgun pellet
- 雪霰 (xuě xiàn) — snow pellets

## Example Sentences

- 天上下起了霰。(Tiān shàng xià qǐ le xiàn.) — Sleet started falling from the sky.

## Notes

霰 has rain 雨 with 散 (scatter), small ice pellets.
