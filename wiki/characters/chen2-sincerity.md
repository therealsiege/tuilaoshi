# 忱 Sincerity, genuine feeling

- **Character**: 忱
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: sincerity, genuine feeling
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 热忱 (rèchén) — enthusiasm, zeal
- 诚忱 (chéngchén) — sincerity

## Example Sentences

- 他工作充满热忱。(Tā gōngzuò chōngmǎn rèchén.) — He works with great enthusiasm.

## Notes

Formal literary term for sincerity or genuine feelings.
