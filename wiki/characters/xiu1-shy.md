# 羞 Shy

- **Character**: 羞
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: shy, ashamed
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 害羞 (hài xiū) — shy, bashful
- 羞愧 (xiū kuì) — ashamed

## Example Sentences

- 她是个很害羞的女孩。(Tā shì gè hěn hàixiū de nǚhái.) — She is a very shy girl.

## Notes

羞 combines sheep 羊 with hand offering food, representing the embarrassment of offering something inadequate.
