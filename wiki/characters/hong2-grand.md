# 宏 Grand, magnificent, macro

- **Character**: 宏
- **Pinyin**: hóng
- **Meaning**: grand, magnificent, macro
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 宏伟 (hóng wěi) — grand, magnificent
- 宏观 (hóng guān) — macro, macroscopic

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个宏伟的建筑。(Zhè shì yī ge hóng wěi de jiàn zhú.) — This is a magnificent building.

## Notes

Indicates grandness or magnificence. 宏观经济 means macroeconomics. 宽宏大量 means magnanimous.
