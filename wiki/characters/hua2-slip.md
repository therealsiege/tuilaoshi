# 滑 Slip, slide, smooth

- **Character**: 滑
- **Pinyin**: huá
- **Meaning**: slip, slide, smooth
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 滑坡 (huá pō) — landslide
- 滑动 (huá dòng) — slide, slip

## Example Sentences

- 大雨后发生了滑坡。(Dà yǔ hòu fā shēng le huá pō.) — A landslide occurred after the heavy rain.

## Notes

Sliding and slipping movements
