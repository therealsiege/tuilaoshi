# 幕 Curtain

- **Character**: 幕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: curtain, screen, act (theater)
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 幕布 (mù bù) — curtain
- 开幕 (kāi mù) — to open (ceremony)

## Example Sentences

- 演出即将开幕。(Yǎn chū jí jiāng kāi mù.) — The performance is about to begin.

## Notes

幕 has 莫 (do not/dusk) over cloth 巾, curtain that blocks view.
