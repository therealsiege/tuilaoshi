# 面 Face

- **Character**: 面
- **Pinyin**: miàn
- **Meaning**: face, surface, side, noodles
- **Radical**: 面 (face)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 前面 (qián miàn) — in front
- 面条 (miàn tiáo) — noodles

## Example Sentences

- 学校在前面。(Xué xiào zài qián miàn.) — The school is up ahead.

## Notes

面 has many uses: face (面子), surface (表面), side (前面), noodles (面条).
