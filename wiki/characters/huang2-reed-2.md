# 簧 Reed

- **Character**: 簧
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: reed (in instruments)
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 簧片 (huáng piàn) — reed
- 双簧 (shuāng huáng) — two-person comic act

## Example Sentences

- 笙管中有簧片振动发声。(Shēng guǎn zhōng yǒu huáng piàn zhèn dòng fā shēng.) — The reed in the sheng vibrates to produce sound.

## Notes

簧 is the vibrating reed in wind instruments; also in 双簧 (comic dialogue).
