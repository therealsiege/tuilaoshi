# 碍 To Hinder

- **Character**: 碍
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: to hinder, to obstruct
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle
- 妨碍 (fáng ài) — to hinder

## Example Sentences

- 请不要妨碍交通。(Qǐng bú yào fáng ài jiāo tōng.) — Please don't obstruct traffic.

## Notes

碍 uses the stone radical — a stone blocking the path.
