# 逼 To Force

- **Character**: 逼
- **Pinyin**: bī
- **Meaning**: to force, to press, to compel
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 逼迫 (bī pò) — to force, coerce
- 逼近 (bī jìn) — to approach

## Example Sentences

- 别逼我。(Bié bī wǒ.) — Don't force me.

## Notes

逼 means to press or force. Colloquially, 牛逼 is strong slang for 'awesome.'
