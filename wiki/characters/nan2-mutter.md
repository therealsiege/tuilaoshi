# 喃 To Mutter

- **Character**: 喃
- **Pinyin**: nán
- **Meaning**: to mutter, to murmur
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喃喃 (nán nán) — to murmur
- 呢喃 (ní nán) — to murmur, to whisper

## Example Sentences

- 他喃喃自语。(Tā nán nán zì yǔ.) — He muttered to himself.

## Notes

喃 combines mouth 口 with 南 (south), representing soft murmuring sounds.
