# 署 Office

- **Character**: 署
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: office, to sign, to arrange
- **Radical**: 网 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 签署 (qiān shǔ) — to sign
- 部署 (bù shǔ) — to deploy, arrange

## Example Sentences

- 双方签署了协议。(Shuāng fāng qiān shǔ le xié yì.) — Both parties signed the agreement.

## Notes

署 combines net and person, suggesting organization and official duties.
