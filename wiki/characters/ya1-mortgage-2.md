# 押 To Mortgage

- **Character**: 押
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: to mortgage, to rhyme
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 押韵 (yā yùn) — to rhyme
- 抵押 (dǐ yā) — to mortgage

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗押韵很工整。(Zhè shǒu shī yā yùn hěn gōng zhěng.) — This poem rhymes very neatly.

## Notes

押 means to mortgage or pawn; in literature means to rhyme.
