# 积 To accumulate, to pile up, product

- **Character**: 积
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to accumulate, to pile up, product
- **Radical**: 禾(grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 积累 (jī lěi) — to accumulate, to build up
- 积极 (jī jí) — positive, active

## Example Sentences

- 长期积累经验很重要。(Chángqī jīlěi jīngyàn hěn zhòngyào.) — Accumulating experience over time is important.

## Notes

Used for accumulation or积极 (positive/active).
