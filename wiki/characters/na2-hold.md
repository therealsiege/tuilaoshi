# 拿 Hold, take, seize

- **Character**: 拿
- **Pinyin**: ná
- **Meaning**: hold, take, seize
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 拿来 (nálái) — bring
- 缉拿 (jīná) — arrest

## Example Sentences

- 请把书拿来。(Qǐng bǎ shū nálái.) — Please bring the book.

## Notes

Very common for holding and taking.
