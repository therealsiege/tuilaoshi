# 地 Ground

- **Character**: 地
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: ground, land, place
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 地方 (dì fāng) — place
- 土地 (tǔ dì) — land

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么地方？(Zhè shì shén me dì fāng?) — What place is this?

## Notes

地 as dì means earth/land; as de is the adverbial particle. Fundamental character.
