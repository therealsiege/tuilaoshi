# 舌 Tongue

- **Character**: 舌
- **Pinyin**: shé
- **Meaning**: tongue
- **Radical**: 舌 (tongue)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 舌头 (shétou) — tongue
- 火舌 (huǒshé) — tongue of flame

## Example Sentences

- 他咬到自己的舌头了。(Tā yǎodào zìjǐ de shétou le.) — He bit his own tongue.

## Notes

Usually appears as 舌头 in speech. The character depicts a tongue extending from a mouth.
