# 切 Cut

- **Character**: 切
- **Pinyin**: qiē/qiè
- **Meaning**: cut, slice
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 切除 (qiē chú) — to excise, remove
- 切开 (qiē kāi) — to cut open
- 一切 (yī qiè) — everything

## Example Sentences

- 医生切除了病变组织。(Yī shēng qiē chú le bìng biàn zǔ zhī.) — The doctor excised the diseased tissue.

## Notes

To cut; qiē for cutting, qiè for close/urgent.
