# 踩 To Step On

- **Character**: 踩
- **Pinyin**: cǎi
- **Meaning**: to step on, to trample
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 踩踏 (cǎi tà) — to trample
- 踩刹车 (cǎi shā chē) — to step on the brake

## Example Sentences

- 别踩草地！(Bié cǎi cǎo dì!) — Don't step on the grass!

## Notes

踩 means to step on or tread. Foot radical 足 indicates foot-related action.
