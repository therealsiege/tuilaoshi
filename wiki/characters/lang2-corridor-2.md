# 廊 Corridor

- **Character**: 廊
- **Pinyin**: láng
- **Meaning**: corridor, porch
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 走廊 (zǒu láng) — corridor
- 画廊 (huà láng) — gallery

## Example Sentences

- 走廊很长。(Zǒu láng hěn cháng.) — The corridor is very long.

## Notes

廊 with shelter radical for covered walkway, corridors and galleries.
