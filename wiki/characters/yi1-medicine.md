# 医 Medicine

- **Character**: 医
- **Pinyin**: yī
- **Meaning**: medicine, doctor
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 医生 (yī shēng) — doctor
- 医院 (yī yuàn) — hospital
- 中医 (zhōng yī) — traditional Chinese medicine

## Example Sentences

- 他是一名医生。(Tā shì yī míng yī shēng.) — He is a doctor.

## Notes

Core character for medicine and healing professions.
