# 笙 Reed Pipe

- **Character**: 笙
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: reed pipe instrument
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 笙歌 (shēng gē) — music and singing
- 芦笙 (lú shēng) — reed pipe

## Example Sentences

- 他吹笙吹得很好。(Tā chuī shēng chuī de hěn hǎo.) — He plays the reed pipe very well.

## Notes

笙 is a traditional Chinese wind instrument made of bamboo pipes.
