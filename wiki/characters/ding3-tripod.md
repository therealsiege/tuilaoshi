# 鼎 Tripod, peak, eminent

- **Character**: 鼎
- **Pinyin**: dǐng
- **Meaning**: tripod, peak, eminent
- **Radical**: 鼎 (tripod)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 鼎盛 (dǐng shèng) — at the height of power
- 问鼎 (wèn dǐng) — aspire to the throne

## Example Sentences

- 唐朝是中国历史上的鼎盛时期。(Táng cháo shì zhōng guó lì shǐ shàng de dǐng shèng shí qī.) — The Tang Dynasty was the peak period in Chinese history.

## Notes

Ancient bronze vessel, symbol of power
