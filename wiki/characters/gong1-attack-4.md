# 攻 Attack

- **Character**: 攻
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: to attack, assault
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 进攻 (jìn gōng) — to attack, offense
- 攻击 (gōng jī) — to attack

## Example Sentences

- 球队加强了进攻。(Qiúduì jiāqiáng le jìngōng.) — The team strengthened their offense.

## Notes

Offensive play in sports; opposite of 守 (defend)
