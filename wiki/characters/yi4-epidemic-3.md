# 疫 Epidemic

- **Character**: 疫
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: epidemic, plague
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 疫情 (yì qíng) — epidemic situation
- 瘟疫 (wēn yì) — plague, pestilence
- 防疫 (fáng yì) — epidemic prevention

## Example Sentences

- 新冠疫情影响全球。(Xīn guān yì qíng yǐng xiǎng quán qiú.) — The COVID-19 pandemic affected the whole world.

## Notes

Infectious disease outbreak; public health concern.
