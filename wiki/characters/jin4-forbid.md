# 禁 To Forbid

- **Character**: 禁
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to forbid, to prohibit, taboo
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 禁止 (jìn zhǐ) — to prohibit
- 严禁 (yán jìn) — strictly禁止

## Example Sentences

- 这里禁止吸烟。(Zhè lǐ jìn zhǐ xī yān.) — Smoking is禁止d here.

## Notes

禁 also read as jīn meaning 'to endure'. Represents prohibitions and taboos.
