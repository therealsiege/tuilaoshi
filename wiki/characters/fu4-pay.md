# 付 To Pay

- **Character**: 付
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to pay, to hand over
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 付款 (fù kuǎn) — to pay
- 支付 (zhī fù) — to pay

## Example Sentences

- 请付款。(Qǐng fù kuǎn.) — Please pay.

## Notes

付 shows person 亻 and hand 寸, representing handing over payment.
