# 导 To Guide

- **Character**: 导
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: to guide, to lead, to conduct
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 领导 (lǐng dǎo) — leader
- 导游 (dǎo yóu) — tour guide

## Example Sentences

- 导游带我们参观了故宫。(Dǎo yóu dài wǒ men cān guān le gù gōng.) — The tour guide took us to visit the Forbidden City.

## Notes

导 is simplified from 導. 指导 = instruct, 导致 = to cause/lead to.
