# 理 — Reason / Manage

- **Character**: 理
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: reason, logic; to manage, to put in order; texture, grain
- **Radical**: 王 (jade, written as 玉)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 道理 (dàolǐ) — reason, principle, truth
- 理解 (lǐjiě) — to understand, to comprehend
- 管理 (guǎnlǐ) — to manage, management
- 办理 (bànlǐ) — to handle, to process
- 心理 (xīnlǐ) — psychology, mentality
- 理想 (lǐxiǎng) — ideal, aspiration

## Example Sentences

- 你说得有道理。(Nǐ shuō de yǒu dàolǐ.) — What you said makes sense.
- 我不理解他为什么这样做。(Wǒ bù lǐjiě tā wèishénme zhèyàng zuò.) — I don't understand why he did this.
- 她的理想是当医生。(Tā de lǐxiǎng shì dāng yīshēng.) — Her ideal is to become a doctor.

## Notes

理 combines the jade radical 王/玉 with 里 (lǐ, village/inside) as a phonetic component. The original meaning was "to work jade" — polishing and cutting along the natural grain of the stone. From this came the abstract meanings: the "grain" or natural pattern of things is their inherent reason or logic (道理), and working with that pattern is managing or putting things in order (管理, 办理). The expression 有道理 (makes sense, literally "has reason") and 没道理 (unreasonable) are extremely common in conversation. 理解 pairs with 解 (solve/untangle) to mean "understand."
