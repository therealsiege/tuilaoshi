# 绛 Deep red, purple

- **Character**: 绛
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: deep red, purple
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绛红 (jiàng hóng) — deep red
- 绛紫 (jiàng zǐ) — dark purple

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着一件绛红色的旗袍。(Tā chuān zhe yī jiàn jiàng hóng sè de qí páo.) — She wore a deep red qipao.

## Notes

Traditional color term for deep red or purple.
