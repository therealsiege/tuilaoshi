# 关 To Close

- **Character**: 关
- **Pinyin**: guān
- **Meaning**: to close, to shut, to turn off
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 关门 (guān mén) — to close door
- 关系 (guān xì) — relationship

## Example Sentences

- 请关灯。(Qǐng guān dēng.) — Please turn off the light.

## Notes

关 originally meant gate/barrier. Now means to close/turn off. Opposite of 开.
