# 壑 Ravine

- **Character**: 壑
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: gully, ravine
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 沟壑 (gōu hè) — ravine
- 壑谷 (hè gǔ) — valley

## Example Sentences

- 山中有很多沟壑。(Shān zhōng yǒu hěn duō gōu hè.) — There are many ravines in the mountains.

## Notes

壑 originally depicted a stream carving through earth 土.
