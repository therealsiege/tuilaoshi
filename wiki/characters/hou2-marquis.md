# 侯 Marquis, noble

- **Character**: 侯
- **Pinyin**: hóu
- **Meaning**: marquis, noble
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 诸侯 (zhū hóu) — feudal lords
- 王侯 (wáng hóu) — princes and marquises

## Example Sentences

- 春秋时期诸侯争霸。(Chūn qiū shí qī zhū hóu zhēng bà.) — During the Spring and Autumn period, feudal lords competed for supremacy.

## Notes

Second rank in traditional Chinese nobility system
