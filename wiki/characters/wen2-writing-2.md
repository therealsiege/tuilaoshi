# 文 Writing

- **Character**: 文
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: writing, culture, language
- **Radical**: 文 (script)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 文章 (wén zhāng) — article
- 中文 (zhōng wén) — Chinese language

## Example Sentences

- 他的文章写得很好。(Tā de wén zhāng xiě de hěn hǎo.) — His articles are written very well.

## Notes

文 means writing, culture, or civil; fundamental character in literary terms.
