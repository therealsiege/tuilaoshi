# 批 Batch

- **Character**: 批
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: to批评,批准, batch, measure word
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 批准 (pī zhǔn) — to approve
- 批评 (pī píng) — to criticize

## Example Sentences

- 计划已经批准了。(Jì huà yǐ jīng pī zhǔn le.) — The plan has been approved.

## Notes

批 shows hand action, used for批准 批评 and as a measure word for batches.
