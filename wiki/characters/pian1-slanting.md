# 偏 Slanting

- **Character**: 偏
- **Pinyin**: piān
- **Meaning**: slanting, biased, to偏偏偏偏偏偏偏偏偏偏one side
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偏见 (piān jiàn) — prejudice
- 偏向 (piān xiàng) — to incline toward

## Example Sentences

- 我们不应该有偏见。(Wǒ men bù yīng gāi yǒu piān jiàn.) — We shouldn't have prejudice.

## Notes

偏 combines person 亻 and 扁 (flat), representing leaning to one side or being partial.
