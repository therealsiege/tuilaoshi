# 氧 Oxygen

- **Character**: 氧
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: oxygen
- **Radical**: 气(gas)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 氧气 (yǎng qì) — oxygen
- 氧化 (yǎng huà) — oxidation

## Example Sentences

- 我们呼吸需要氧气。(Wǒmen hūxī xūyào yǎngqì.) — We need oxygen to breathe.

## Notes

氧 is oxygen, essential for life and combustion. The gas radical 气 indicates it's a gas.
