# 匾 Horizontal inscribed board

- **Character**: 匾
- **Pinyin**: biǎn
- **Meaning**: horizontal inscribed board
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 匾额 (biǎn é) — horizontal inscribed board
- 牌匾 (pái biǎn) — signboard, plaque

## Example Sentences

- 大门上挂着一块金字匾额。(Dà mén shàng guà zhe yī kuài jīn zì biǎn é.) — A horizontal board with golden characters hangs above the gate.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese architectural decoration.
