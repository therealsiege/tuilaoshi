# 料 Material

- **Character**: 料
- **Pinyin**: liào
- **Meaning**: material, to expect
- **Radical**: 斗 (dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 材料 (cái liào) — material
- 资料 (zī liào) — data, information

## Example Sentences

- 准备好所有材料。(Zhǔn bèi hǎo suǒ yǒu cái liào.) — Prepare all the materials.

## Notes

料 originally meant to measure grain, now for materials and料料料 料预dictions.
