# 盗 Steal

- **Character**: 盗
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to steal, thief, robber
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn ěr dào líng) — self-deception
- 盗窃 (dào qiè) — to steal, theft

## Example Sentences

- 盗窃是违法行为。(Dàoqiè shì wéifǎ xíngwéi.) — Theft is against the law.

## Notes

The stealing action in 掩耳盗铃; also means thief or robber.
