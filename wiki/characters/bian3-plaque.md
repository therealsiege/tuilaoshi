# 匾 Plaque, horizontal board

- **Character**: 匾
- **Pinyin**: biǎn
- **Meaning**: plaque, horizontal board
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 匾额 (biǎn é) — horizontal inscribed board
- 牌匾 (pái biǎn) — shop sign

## Example Sentences

- 老店门上挂着金字匾额。(Lǎo diàn mén shàng guà zhe jīn zì biǎn é.) — A golden inscribed board hangs on the old shop.

## Notes

Traditional shop signage. 题匾 means inscribe a plaque.
