# 脉 Pulse, vein, artery

- **Character**: 脉
- **Pinyin**: mài
- **Meaning**: pulse, vein, artery
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 脉搏 (mài bó) — pulse
- 把脉 (bǎ mài) — feel the pulse

## Example Sentences

- 中医通过把脉诊断疾病。(Zhōngyī tōngguò bǎ mài zhěnduàn jíbìng.) — Traditional Chinese medicine diagnoses diseases by feeling the pulse.

## Notes

Pulse or blood vessels. Pulse diagnosis is fundamental in traditional Chinese medicine, believed to reveal the body's condition.
