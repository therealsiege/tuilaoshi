# 惶 Anxious, nervous, fearful

- **Character**: 惶
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: anxious, nervous, fearful
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惶恐 (huáng kǒng) — terrified, nervous
- 惊惶 (jīng huáng) — alarmed, scared

## Example Sentences

- 他惶恐不安地等待结果。(Tā huángkǒng bù'ān de děngdài jiéguǒ.) — He waited nervously for the result.

## Notes

Emotional term for anxiety, nervousness, or fearfulness.
