# 惆 Distressed, depressed

- **Character**: 惆
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: distressed, depressed
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惆怅 (chóuchàng) — melancholy, disconsolate
- 惆惆 (chóuchóu) — worried, distressed

## Example Sentences

- 离别时心情惆怅。(Líbié shí xīnqíng chóuchàng.) — One feels melancholy when parting.

## Notes

Literary term for melancholy, always paired with 怅.
