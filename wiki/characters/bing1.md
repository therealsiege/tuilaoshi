# 冰 Ice

- **Character**: 冰
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: ice, to freeze
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冰箱 (bīng xiāng) — refrigerator
- 冰淇淋 (bīng qí lín) — ice cream

## Example Sentences

- 水变成冰了。(Shuǐ biàn chéng bīng le.) — The water has turned into ice.

## Notes

冰 combines water (水) with ice radical (冫), representing frozen water. 冰箱 (refrigerator) literally means 'ice box'.
