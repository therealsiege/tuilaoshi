# 巍 Lofty, towering

- **Character**: 巍
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: lofty, towering
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 巍峨 (wēi'é) — lofty, towering
- 巍然 (wēirán) — lofty and firm

## Example Sentences

- 巍峨的山峰耸入云霄。(Wēi'é de shānfēng sǒng rù yúnxiāo.) — The lofty peaks tower into the clouds.

## Notes

Literary term for lofty, majestic mountains or buildings.
