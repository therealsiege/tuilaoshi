# 弹 Play

- **Character**: 弹
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: to play (instrument), to bounce
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 对牛弹琴 (duì niú tán qín) — cast pearls before swine
- 弹琴 (tán qín) — to play a stringed instrument

## Example Sentences

- 跟他解释这些简直是对牛弹琴。(Gēn tā jiěshì zhèxiē jiǎnzhí shì duì niú tán qín.) — Explaining this to him is like casting pearls before swine.

## Notes

Used in 对牛弹琴: playing music to a cow, meaning the audience cannot appreciate it.
