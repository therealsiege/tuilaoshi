# 爱 To Love

- **Character**: 爱
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: to love, love
- **Radical**: 爫 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 爱好 (ài hào) — hobby
- 爱情 (ài qíng) — romantic love

## Example Sentences

- 我爱你。(Wǒ ài nǐ.) — I love you.

## Notes

爱 is simplified from 愛, which had 心 (heart) in the middle.
