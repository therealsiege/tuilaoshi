# 虹 Rainbow

- **Character**: 虹
- **Pinyin**: hóng
- **Meaning**: rainbow
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 彩虹 (cǎi hóng) — rainbow
- 霓虹 (ní hóng) — neon

## Example Sentences

- 雨后天空出现了彩虹。(Yǔ hòu tiān kōng chū xiàn le cǎi hóng.) — A rainbow appeared in the sky after the rain.

## Notes

虹 has insect 虫 with 工 (work), arc of light in sky.
