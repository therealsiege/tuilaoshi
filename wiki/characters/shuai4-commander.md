# 帅 Commander, marshal, handsome

- **Character**: 帅
- **Pinyin**: shuài
- **Meaning**: commander, marshal, handsome
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 元帅 (yuán shuài) — marshal
- 统帅 (tǒng shuài) — commander-in-chief

## Example Sentences

- 他被任命为三军统帅。(Tā bèi rènmìng wéi sān jūn tǒng shuài.) — He was appointed commander-in-chief of the three armed forces.

## Notes

Military commander or marshal. In modern usage, also means handsome. In Chinese chess, the general piece.
