# 揉 To Knead

- **Character**: 揉
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: to knead, to rub
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揉面 (róu miàn) — to knead dough
- 揉眼睛 (róu yǎn jīng) — to rub eyes

## Example Sentences

- 她在揉面团。(Tā zài róu miàn tuán.) — She is kneading the dough.

## Notes

揉 combines hand 扌 and 柔 (soft), representing kneading or rubbing to soften.
