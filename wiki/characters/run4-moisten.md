# 润 Moisten, profit, smooth

- **Character**: 润
- **Pinyin**: rùn
- **Meaning**: moisten, profit, smooth
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 利润 (lìrùn) — profit
- 润滑 (rùnhuá) — lubricate

## Example Sentences

- 这家公司利润丰厚。(Zhè jiā gōngsī lìrùn fēnghòu.) — This company has substantial profits.

## Notes

Used for profit in business; also means to moisten or lubricate.
