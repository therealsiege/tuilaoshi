# 巩 To strengthen, consolidate, 鞏 (traditional)

- **Character**: 巩
- **Pinyin**: gǒng
- **Meaning**: to strengthen, consolidate, 鞏 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 工 (work)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 巩固 (gǒng gù) — to consolidate, strengthen
- 巩膜 (gǒng mó) — sclera (white of eye)

## Example Sentences

- 我们要巩固学习成果。(Wǒmen yào gǒnggù xuéxí chéngguǒ.) — We should consolidate our learning achievements.

## Notes

To strengthen or consolidate; also the sclera of the eye.
