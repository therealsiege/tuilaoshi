# 执 Hold, execute

- **Character**: 执
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: hold, execute
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执行 (zhí xíng) — execute, implement
- 执照 (zhí zhào) — license

## Example Sentences

- 公司严格执行规章制度。(Gōng sī yán gé zhí xíng guī zhāng zhì dù.) — The company strictly implements rules and regulations.

## Notes

执行董事 is executive director, 执行总裁 is CEO.
