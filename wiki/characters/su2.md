# 俗 Custom

- **Character**: 俗
- **Pinyin**: sú
- **Meaning**: custom, vulgar, common
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 风俗 (fēng sú) — custom
- 通俗 (tōng sú) — popular, common

## Example Sentences

- 这是当地的风俗习惯。(Zhè shì dāng dì de fēng sú xí guàn.) — This is the local custom.

## Notes

俗 shows person 亻 and valley 谷, representing common people or popular customs.
