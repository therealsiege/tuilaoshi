# 津 Ferry, saliva, allowance

- **Character**: 津
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: ferry, saliva, allowance
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 津贴 (jīn tiē) — allowance, subsidy
- 天津 (Tiān jīn) — Tianjin (city)

## Example Sentences

- 员工可以领取交通津贴。(Yuán gōng kě yǐ lǐng qǔ jiāo tōng jīn tiē.) — Employees can receive transportation allowances.

## Notes

津 originally meant ferry crossing. 津津有味 means with great relish. 津液 means saliva.
