# 蚊 Mosquito

- **Character**: 蚊
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: mosquito
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 蚊子 (wén zi) — mosquito
- 蚊虫 (wén chóng) — mosquitoes

## Example Sentences

- 夏天蚊子很多。(Xià tiān wén zi hěn duō.) — There are many mosquitoes in summer.

## Notes

蚊 combines insect 虫 and文 (pattern), representing mosquitoes with patterned wings.
