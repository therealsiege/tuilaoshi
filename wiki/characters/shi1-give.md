# 施 To give, to exert, to carry out

- **Character**: 施
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: to give, to施加, to carry out
- **Radical**: 方(square)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 施工 (shī gōng) — construction, to construct
- 实施 (shí shī) — to implement, to carry out

## Example Sentences

- 这条路正在施工。(Zhè tiáo lù zhèngzài shīgōng.) — This road is under construction.

## Notes

Used for giving, implementing, or carrying out actions.
