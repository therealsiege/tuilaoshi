# 大 Big, large, great

- **Character**: 大
- **Pinyin**: dà
- **Meaning**: big, large, great
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 大学 (dàxué) — university
- 大赦 (dàshè) — general amnesty

## Example Sentences

- 这是一件大事。(Zhè shì yī jiàn dàshì.) — This is a major matter.

## Notes

Very common basic character for size and importance.
