# 踝 Ankle

- **Character**: 踝
- **Pinyin**: huái
- **Meaning**: ankle
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 脚踝 (jiǎo huái) — ankle
- 踝骨 (huái gǔ) — ankle bone

## Example Sentences

- 她扭伤了脚踝。(Tā niǔ shāng le jiǎo huái.) — She sprained her ankle.

## Notes

踝 has foot 足 with 果 (fruit), the rounded bone at the ankle.
