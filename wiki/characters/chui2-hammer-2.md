# 锤 Hammer

- **Character**: 锤
- **Pinyin**: chuí
- **Meaning**: hammer, to hammer
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 铁锤 (tiě chuí) — iron hammer
- 锤子 (chuí zi) — hammer

## Example Sentences

- 他用锤子钉钉子。(Tā yòng chuí zi dīng dīng zi.) — He uses a hammer to drive nails.

## Notes

锤 simplified from 錘. Metal radical 钅 representing a striking tool.
