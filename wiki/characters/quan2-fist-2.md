# 拳 Fist

- **Character**: 拳
- **Pinyin**: quán
- **Meaning**: fist, boxing
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 拳击 (quán jī) — boxing
- 太极拳 (tài jí quán) — tai chi

## Example Sentences

- 他是拳击冠军。(Tā shì quánjī guànjūn.) — He is the boxing champion.

## Notes

Used in boxing and martial arts; fundamental combat term
