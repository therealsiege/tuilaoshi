# 顷 Hectare, moment

- **Character**: 顷
- **Pinyin**: qǐng
- **Meaning**: hectare, moment
- **Radical**: 頁 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 公顷 (gōng qǐng) — hectare
- 顷刻 (qǐng kè) — moment, instant

## Example Sentences

- 农场面积一百公顷。(Nóng chǎng miàn jī yī bǎi gōng qǐng.) — The farm area is 100 hectares.

## Notes

1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 15 mu. 顷刻间 means in an instant.
