# 股 Thigh, share, strand

- **Character**: 股
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: thigh, share, strand
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 股票 (gǔ piào) — stock, share
- 股东 (gǔ dōng) — shareholder

## Example Sentences

- 他是公司的主要股东之一。(Tā shì gōng sī de zhǔ yào gǔ dōng zhī yī.) — He is one of the company's major shareholders.

## Notes

Essential in stock market contexts. 股份 means shares. 股市 means stock market. Also measure word for strands.
