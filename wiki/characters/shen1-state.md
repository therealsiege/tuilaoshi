# 申 State, explain, apply

- **Character**: 申
- **Pinyin**: shēn
- **Meaning**: state, explain,申请
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 申请 (shēnqǐng) — apply for
- 申诉 (shēnsù) — appeal, complain

## Example Sentences

- 他向上级申诉。(Tā xiàng shàngjí shēnsù.) — He appealed to higher authorities.

## Notes

Formal term for stating, explaining, and申请ing.
