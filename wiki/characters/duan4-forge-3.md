# 锻 To Forge

- **Character**: 锻
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: to forge, to temper
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — to exercise
- 锻造 (duàn zào) — to forge

## Example Sentences

- 每天锻炼身体很重要。(Měi tiān duàn liàn shēn tǐ hěn zhòng yào.) — Exercising every day is important.

## Notes

锻 simplified from 鍛. Metal 钅 with 段 (section), hammering metal.
