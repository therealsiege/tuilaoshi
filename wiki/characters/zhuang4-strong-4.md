# 壮 Strong

- **Character**: 壮
- **Pinyin**: zhuàng
- **Meaning**: strong, robust
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 强壮 (qiáng zhuàng) — strong, sturdy
- 壮大 (zhuàng dà) — to strengthen

## Example Sentences

- 锻炼让他变得更强壮。(Duànliàn ràng tā biàn de gèng qiángzhuàng.) — Exercise makes him stronger.

## Notes

Robustness and strength; physical build
