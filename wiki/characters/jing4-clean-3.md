# 净 Clean

- **Character**: 净
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: clean, pure
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 干净 (gān jìng) — clean
- 净化 (jìng huà) — to purify
- 洁净 (jié jìng) — clean, sanitary

## Example Sentences

- 保持环境干净很重要。(Bǎo chí huán jìng gān jìng hěn zhòng yào.) — Keeping the environment clean is very important.

## Notes

Cleanliness; hygiene standard.
