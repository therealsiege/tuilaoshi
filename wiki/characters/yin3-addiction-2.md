# 瘾 Addiction

- **Character**: 瘾
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: addiction, craving
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 上瘾 (shàng yǐn) — to be addicted
- 毒瘾 (dú yǐn) — drug addiction
- 烟瘾 (yān yǐn) — nicotine addiction

## Example Sentences

- 他戒烟很难，因为烟瘾很大。(Tā jiè yān hěn nán, yīn wèi yān yǐn hěn dà.) — It's hard for him to quit smoking because his nicotine addiction is severe.

## Notes

Compulsive craving; substance or behavioral.
