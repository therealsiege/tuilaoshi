# 党 Party

- **Character**: 党
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: party, political party
- **Radical**: 儿 (son)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 政党 (zhèng dǎng) — political party
- 党员 (dǎng yuán) — party member

## Example Sentences

- 他是一名党员。(Tā shì yì míng dǎng yuán.) — He is a party member.

## Notes

党 simplified from 黨. Originally 尚 (esteem) in darkness, forming alliance.
