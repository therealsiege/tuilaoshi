# 溃 Collapse, break down

- **Character**: 溃
- **Pinyin**: kuì
- **Meaning**: collapse, break down
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 崩溃 (bēng kuì) — crash
- 溃败 (kuì bài) — collapse

## Example Sentences

- 系统快要崩溃了。(Xì tǒng kuài yào bēng kuì le.) — The system is about to crash.

## Notes

Used in 崩溃 (crash), 溃败 (collapse), failure terminology
