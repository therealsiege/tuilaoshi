# 句 Sentence

- **Character**: 句
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: sentence, measure word for sentences
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 句子 (jù zi) — sentence
- 一句话 (yī jù huà) — one sentence

## Example Sentences

- 请说一句中文。(Qǐng shuō yī jù zhōng wén.) — Please say a sentence in Chinese.

## Notes

句 is the measure word for sentences and phrases: 一句话 (one sentence).
