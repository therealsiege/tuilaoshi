# 鸣 To Sound

- **Character**: 鸣
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: to sound, to ring, to cry
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 鸣叫 (míng jiào) — to cry out (birds)
- 共鸣 (gòng míng) — resonance

## Example Sentences

- 鸟儿在树上鸣叫。(Niǎo ér zài shù shàng míng jiào.) — Birds are singing in the trees.

## Notes

鸣 describes sounds made by birds, animals, or instruments.
