# 潭 Deep Pool

- **Character**: 潭
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: deep pool, pond
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潭水 (tán shuǐ) — pool water
- 深潭 (shēn tán) — deep pool

## Example Sentences

- 山脚下有一个深潭。(Shān jiǎo xià yǒu yí gè shēn tán.) — There is a deep pool at the foot of the mountain.

## Notes

潭 has water 氵 with 覃 (to reach deeply), very deep water.
