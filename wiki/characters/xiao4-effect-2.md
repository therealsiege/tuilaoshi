# 效 Effect

- **Character**: 效
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: effect, to imitate, to devote
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 效果 (xiào guǒ) — effect, result
- 有效 (yǒu xiào) — effective

## Example Sentences

- 这个方法很有效。(Zhè gè fāng fǎ hěn yǒu xiào.) — This method is very effective.

## Notes

效 combines 交 (to intersect) and strike 攵, representing producing a result through action.
