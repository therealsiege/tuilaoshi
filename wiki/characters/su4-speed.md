# 速 Speed

- **Character**: 速
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: speed, fast
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 速度 (sù dù) — speed
- 高速 (gāo sù) — high-speed

## Example Sentences

- 网速太慢了。(Wǎng sù tài màn le.) — The internet speed is too slow.

## Notes

速 with walk radical suggests rapid movement, fundamental to speed terminology.
