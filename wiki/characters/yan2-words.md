# 言 Words

- **Character**: 言
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: words, speech, to say
- **Radical**: 言 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 语言 (yǔ yán) — language
- 五言 (wǔ yán) — five-character (verse)

## Example Sentences

- 五言诗每句五个字。(Wǔ yán shī měi jù wǔ gè zì.) — Five-character verse has five characters per line.

## Notes

言 means words or speech; in poetry refers to characters per line.
