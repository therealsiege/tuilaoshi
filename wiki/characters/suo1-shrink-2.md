# 缩 To Shrink

- **Character**: 缩
- **Pinyin**: suō
- **Meaning**: to shrink, to withdraw
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 缩小 (suō xiǎo) — to reduce
- 收缩 (shōu suō) — to contract

## Example Sentences

- 衣服洗后缩小了。(Yī fú xǐ hòu suō xiǎo le.) — The clothes shrank after washing.

## Notes

缩 simplified from 縮. Silk radical 纟 representing fabric shrinking or contracting.
