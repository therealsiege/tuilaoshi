# 琅 Gem-like stone, tinkling sound

- **Character**: 琅
- **Pinyin**: láng
- **Meaning**: gem-like stone, tinkling sound
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 琳琅 (lín láng) — beautiful gems
- 琅琅 (láng láng) — sonorous, clear sound

## Example Sentences

- 他琅琅的读书声传遍整个院子。(Tā láng láng de dú shū shēng chuán biàn zhěng gè yuàn zi.) — His sonorous reading voice echoed throughout the courtyard.

## Notes

Often describes clear, resonant sounds.
