# 铝 Aluminum, 铝合金 Aluminum Alloy

- **Character**: 铝
- **Pinyin**: lǚ
- **Meaning**: aluminum, 铝合金 aluminum alloy
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铝合金 (lǚ hé jīn) — aluminum alloy
- 铝材 (lǚ cái) — aluminum material
- 铝箔 (lǚ bó) — aluminum foil

## Example Sentences

- 铝合金门窗很受欢迎。(Lǚ héjīn ménchuāng hěn shòu huānyíng.) — Aluminum alloy doors and windows are very popular.

## Notes

Modern industrial metal. 铝合金 (aluminum alloy) common in construction and manufacturing contexts.
