# 释 To Release

- **Character**: 释
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to release, to explain, to interpret
- **Radical**: 釆 (distinguish)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 释放 (shì fàng) — to release
- 解释 (jiě shì) — to explain

## Example Sentences

- 请解释一下这个词的意思。(Qǐng jiě shì yī xià zhè ge cí de yì si.) — Please explain the meaning of this word.

## Notes

释 is simplified from 釋. Means to release or explain.
