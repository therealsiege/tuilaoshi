# 天 Sky

- **Character**: 天
- **Pinyin**: tiān
- **Meaning**: sky, day, heaven
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 今天 (jīn tiān) — today
- 天气 (tiān qì) — weather

## Example Sentences

- 今天天气很好。(Jīn tiān tiān qì hěn hǎo.) — The weather is good today.

## Notes

天 shows a line above 大 (person), representing what is above humans — the sky.
