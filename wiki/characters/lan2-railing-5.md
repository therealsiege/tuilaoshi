# 栏 Railing

- **Character**: 栏
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: railing, fence, column
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing
- 围栏 (wéi lán) — fence

## Example Sentences

- 楼梯有栏杆保护。(Lóu tī yǒu lán gān bǎo hù.) — The stairs have railings for protection.

## Notes

栏 simplified from 欄. Wood 木 plus barrier, wooden railing.
