# 句 Sentence

- **Character**: 句
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: sentence, phrase
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 句子 (jù zi) — sentence
- 一句话 (yī jù huà) — one sentence

## Example Sentences

- 这句话我不懂。(Zhè jù huà wǒ bù dǒng.) — I don't understand this sentence.

## Notes

句 is measure word for sentences. 造句 = make sentences.
