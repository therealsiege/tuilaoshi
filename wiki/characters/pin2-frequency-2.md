# 频 Frequency

- **Character**: 频
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: frequency, repeated
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 频率 (pín lǜ) — frequency
- 频繁 (pín fán) — frequent

## Example Sentences

- 这个频率太高了。(Zhè ge pín lǜ tài gāo le.) — This frequency is too high.

## Notes

频 originally meant repeated visits, now standard for frequency in all technical contexts.
