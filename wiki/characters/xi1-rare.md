# 稀 Rare

- **Character**: 稀
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: rare, thin, sparse
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 稀少 (xī shǎo) — rare
- 稀饭 (xī fàn) — porridge

## Example Sentences

- 这种动物很稀少。(Zhè zhǒng dòng wù hěn xī shǎo.) — This kind of animal is very rare.

## Notes

稀 combines grain 禾 and 希 (hope/rare), few grains scattered thinly.
