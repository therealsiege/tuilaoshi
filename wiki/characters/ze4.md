# 仄 Oblique

- **Character**: 仄
- **Pinyin**: zè
- **Meaning**: oblique tones, narrow
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 平仄 (píng zè) — level and oblique tones
- 仄声 (zè shēng) — oblique tones

## Example Sentences

- 写律诗要注意平仄。(Xiě lǜ shī yào zhù yì píng zè.) — When writing regulated verse, one must pay attention to tonal patterns.

## Notes

仄 refers to oblique tones (rising, falling, entering) in classical poetry.
