# 氏 Clan

- **Character**: 氏
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: clan, family name, maiden name
- **Radical**: 氏 (clan)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 姓氏 (xìng shì) — surname
- 摄氏 (shè shì) — Celsius

## Example Sentences

- 你知道自己的姓氏来源吗？(Nǐ zhī dào zì jǐ de xìng shì lái yuán ma?) — Do you know the origin of your surname?

## Notes

氏 represents a clan or family name, often used in formal contexts or compound terms.
