# 彪 Young Tiger

- **Character**: 彪
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: young tiger, striped, extraordinary
- **Radical**: 彡 (hair)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 彪悍 (biāo hàn) — valiant
- 彪炳 (biāo bǐng) — shining, brilliant

## Example Sentences

- 他体格彪悍。(Tā tǐ gé biāo hàn.) — He has a sturdy physique.

## Notes

彪 depicts tiger stripes. Used metaphorically for bold or robust qualities.
