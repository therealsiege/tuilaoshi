# 基 Base, foundation, radical

- **Character**: 基
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: base, foundation, radical
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 基因 (jī yīn) — gene
- 基础 (jī chǔ) — foundation, basis

## Example Sentences

- 基因决定了生物的遗传特征。(Jī yīn jué dìng le shēng wù de yí chuán tè zhēng.) — Genes determine the hereditary characteristics of organisms.
