# 挑 To Lift

- **Character**: 挑
- **Pinyin**: tiāo
- **Meaning**: to lift, to provoke
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挑战 (tiǎo zhàn) — to challenge
- 挑衅 (tiǎo xìn) — to provoke

## Example Sentences

- 他挑起了对方的武器。(Tā tiāo qǐ le duì fāng de wǔ qì.) — He lifted up his opponent's weapon.

## Notes

挑 has hand 扌 with 兆 (omen), lifting upward with the hand.
