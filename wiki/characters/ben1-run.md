# 奔 To Run

- **Character**: 奔
- **Pinyin**: bēn
- **Meaning**: to run, to rush, to head for
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奔跑 (bēn pǎo) — to run
- 奔波 (bēn bō) — to rush about

## Example Sentences

- 他奔向终点。(Tā bēn xiàng zhōng diǎn.) — He ran toward the finish line.

## Notes

奔 as bēn means to run/rush. As bèn means to head for (a destination).
