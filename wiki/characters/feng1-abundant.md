# 丰 Abundant

- **Character**: 丰
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: abundant, rich, harvest
- **Radical**: 丨 (vertical)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 丰富 (fēng fù) — rich, abundant
- 丰收 (fēng shōu) — bumper harvest

## Example Sentences

- 这里的文化很丰富。(Zhè lǐ de wén huà hěn fēng fù.) — The culture here is very rich.

## Notes

丰 is simplified from 豐. 丰富 is the most common compound meaning 'abundant/rich.'
