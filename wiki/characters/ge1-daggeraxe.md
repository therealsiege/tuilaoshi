# 戈 Dagger-axe, halberd

- **Character**: 戈
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: dagger-axe, halberd
- **Radical**: 戈 (spear)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 干戈 (gān gē) — weapons of war, warfare
- 倒戈 (dǎo gē) — turn one's weapon around, defect

## Example Sentences

- 化干戈为玉帛意思是化战争为和平。(Huà gāngē wéi yùbó yìsi shì huà zhànzhēng wéi hépíng.) — Turning weapons into jade and silk means transforming war into peace.

## Notes

An ancient Chinese weapon, a dagger on a long shaft. The character itself is a radical appearing in many military-related characters.
