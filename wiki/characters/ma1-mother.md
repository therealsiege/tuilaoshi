# 妈 Mom

- **Character**: 妈
- **Pinyin**: mā
- **Meaning**: mother, mom
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 妈妈 (mā ma) — mom
- 爸妈 (bà mā) — parents (informal)

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈在做饭。(Māmā zài zuò fàn.) — Mom is cooking.

## Notes

妈 uses 女 (woman) radical plus 马 (mǎ, horse) as phonetic. 妈妈 is informal; 母亲 (mǔ qīn) is formal.
