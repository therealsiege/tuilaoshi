# 膀 Bladder, upper arm

- **Character**: 膀
- **Pinyin**: páng/bǎng
- **Meaning**: bladder, upper arm
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 膀胱 (páng guāng) — urinary bladder
- 肩膀 (jiān bǎng) — shoulder

## Example Sentences

- 膀胱负责储存尿液。(Páng guāng fù zé chǔ cún niào yè.) — The bladder is responsible for storing urine.

## Notes

Part of urinary system
