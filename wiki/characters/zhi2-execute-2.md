# 执 To execute, grasp, stubborn, 執 (traditional)

- **Character**: 执
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: to execute, grasp, stubborn, 執 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执行 (zhí xíng) — to execute, implement
- 固执 (gù zhí) — stubborn

## Example Sentences

- 他性格很固执。(Tā xìnggé hěn gùzhí.) — He has a stubborn character.

## Notes

To grasp/execute or stubborn persistence.
