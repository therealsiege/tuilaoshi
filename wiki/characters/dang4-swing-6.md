# 荡 To Swing

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: to swing, to sway
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荡漾 (dàng yàng) — to ripple
- 摇荡 (yáo dàng) — to swing

## Example Sentences

- 湖水荡漾着波纹。(Hú shuǐ dàng yàng zhe bō wén.) — The lake water ripples with waves.

## Notes

荡 simplified from 蕩. Grass radical 艹 representing swaying or washing away.
