# 锋 Edge, blade, front

- **Character**: 锋
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: edge, blade, front
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锋面 (fēng miàn) — front (weather)
- 刀锋 (dāo fēng) — blade edge

## Example Sentences

- 冷锋带来降温天气。(Lěng fēng dài lái jiàng wēn tiān qì.) — A cold front brings cooler weather.

## Notes

Sharp edge or weather front
