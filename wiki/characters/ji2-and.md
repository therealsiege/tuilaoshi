# 及 And

- **Character**: 及
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: and, to reach, in time for
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 以及 (yǐ jí) — as well as
- 及时 (jí shí) — timely

## Example Sentences

- 我学习中文以及日语。(Wǒ xué xí zhōng wén yǐ jí rì yǔ.) — I study Chinese as well as Japanese.

## Notes

及 shows a hand reaching to catch someone, representing reaching or including.
