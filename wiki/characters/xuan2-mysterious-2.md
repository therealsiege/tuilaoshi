# 玄 Mysterious, profound, dark

- **Character**: 玄
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: mysterious, profound, dark
- **Radical**: 玄 (dark)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 玄妙 (xuán miào) — mysterious, abstruse
- 玄学 (xuán xué) — metaphysics

## Example Sentences

- 道家的思想很玄妙。(Dào jiā de sī xiǎng hěn xuán miào.) — Taoist thought is very mysterious.

## Notes

Key concept in Taoist philosophy, appears in Dao De Jing
