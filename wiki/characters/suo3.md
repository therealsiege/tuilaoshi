# 所 Place

- **Character**: 所
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: place, that which
- **Radical**: 户 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 所以 (suǒ yǐ) — therefore
- 所有 (suǒ yǒu) — all

## Example Sentences

- 所以你不去了？(Suǒ yǐ nǐ bù qù le?) — So you're not going?

## Notes

所 is a classical word. 所以 means 'therefore.' 所有 means 'all.' 所 + verb creates a relative clause.
