# 接 Receive

- **Character**: 接
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: receive, connect
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 接种 (jiē zhǒng) — to vaccinate, inoculate
- 接受 (jiē shòu) — to accept
- 接触 (jiē chù) — to contact

## Example Sentences

- 请到医院接种疫苗。(Qǐng dào yī yuàn jiē zhǒng yì miáo.) — Please go to the hospital for vaccination.

## Notes

Receive or connect; vaccination administration.
