# 恳 Earnest, sincere

- **Character**: 恳
- **Pinyin**: kěn
- **Meaning**: earnest, sincere
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恳切 (kěnqiè) — earnest, sincere
- 诚恳 (chéngkěn) — sincere, honest

## Example Sentences

- 他态度很诚恳。(Tā tàidù hěn chéngkěn.) — His attitude is very sincere.

## Notes

Formal term for earnestness or sincerity.
