# 管 To Manage

- **Character**: 管
- **Pinyin**: guǎn
- **Meaning**: to manage, tube, pipe, regardless of
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 管理 (guǎn lǐ) — to manage
- 不管 (bù guǎn) — regardless

## Example Sentences

- 不管怎样，我都会去。(Bù guǎn zěn yàng, wǒ dōu huì qù.) — Regardless of what happens, I'll go.

## Notes

管 means tube/pipe or to manage. 不管...都... = regardless of...still...
