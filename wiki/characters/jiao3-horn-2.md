# 角 Horn

- **Character**: 角
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: horn, corner, angle
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 号角 (hào jiǎo) — bugle, horn
- 角色 (jué sè) — role

## Example Sentences

- 号角声响起，军队开始前进。(Hào jiǎo shēng xiǎng qǐ, jūn duì kāi shǐ qián jìn.) — The horn sounded, and the army began to advance.

## Notes

角 has multiple meanings: horn (instrument), corner, angle; also read as jué in 角色.
