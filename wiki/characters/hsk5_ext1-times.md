# 倍 Times, -Fold, 加倍 Double

- **Character**: 倍
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: times, -fold, 加倍 double
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 加倍 (jiā bèi) — double
- 倍数 (bèi shù) — multiple
- 成倍 (chéng bèi) — multiply

## Example Sentences

- 这个数字是那个的两倍。(Zhège shùzì shì nàge de liǎng bèi.) — This number is twice that one.

## Notes

Essential for expressing multiples and proportional increases. Used in mathematical and comparative contexts.
