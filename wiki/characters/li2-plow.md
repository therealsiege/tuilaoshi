# 犁 Plow, plough

- **Character**: 犁
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: plow, plough
- **Radical**: 牛 (cow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 犁田 (lí tián) — plow fields
- 犁地 (lí dì) — plow land

## Example Sentences

- 农民正在犁田准备播种。(Nóng mín zhèng zài lí tián zhǔn bèi bō zhǒng.) — Farmers are plowing fields preparing to sow.
