# 散 Scatter, disperse, loose

- **Character**: 散
- **Pinyin**: sàn
- **Meaning**: scatter, disperse, loose
- **Radical**: 攵 (rap)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 分散 (fēnsàn) — disperse, scatter
- 散布 (sànbù) — spread, disseminate

## Example Sentences

- 人群渐渐散去。(Rénqún jiànjiàn sànqù.) — The crowd gradually dispersed.

## Notes

Has two pronunciations: sàn (scatter) and sǎn (loose).
