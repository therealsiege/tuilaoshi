# 阁 Pavilion

- **Character**: 阁
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: pavilion, cabinet
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 楼阁 (lóu gé) — pavilion
- 内阁 (nèi gé) — cabinet (government)

## Example Sentences

- 这座楼阁建于明代。(Zhè zuò lóu gé jiàn yú Míng dài.) — This pavilion was built in the Ming dynasty.

## Notes

阁 simplified from 閣. Gate 门 with 各 (each), multi-story structure.
