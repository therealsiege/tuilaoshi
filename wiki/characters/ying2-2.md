# 营 营业, camp, manage

- **Character**: 营
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: 营业, camp, manage
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 营业 (yíng yè) — do business, operate
- 经营 (jīng yíng) — manage, operate

## Example Sentences

- 商店每天营业到晚上十点。(Shāng diàn měi tiān yíng yè dào wǎn shang shí diǎn.) — The store operates until 10 PM every day.

## Notes

营业额 is business volume/turnover. 营业时间 is business hours.
