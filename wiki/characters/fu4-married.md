# 妇 Married woman, wife

- **Character**: 妇
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: married woman, wife
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 夫妇 (fū fù) — married couple
- 妇女 (fù nǚ) — women

## Example Sentences

- 这对夫妇结婚十年了。(Zhè duì fūfù jiéhūn shí nián le.) — This couple has been married for ten years.

## Notes

Refers to married women or women in general.
