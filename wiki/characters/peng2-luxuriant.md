# 蓬 Luxuriant, fluffy, disheveled

- **Character**: 蓬
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: luxuriant, fluffy, disheveled
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蓬勃 (péng bó) — vigorous, flourishing
- 蓬松 (péng sōng) — fluffy, puffy

## Example Sentences

- 经济呈现蓬勃发展的态势。(Jīng jì chéng xiàn péng bó fā zhǎn de tài shì.) — The economy shows a vigorous development trend.

## Notes

Indicates vigorous growth. 蓬头垢面 means disheveled appearance. 蓬莱 is a mythical place.
