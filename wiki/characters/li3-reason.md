# 理 Reason, manage, principle

- **Character**: 理
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: reason, manage, principle
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 道理 (dàoli) — reason, principle
- 管理 (guǎnlǐ) — manage

## Example Sentences

- 这个道理很简单。(Zhège dàoli hěn jiǎndān.) — This principle is very simple.

## Notes

Very common for reason, principles, and management.
