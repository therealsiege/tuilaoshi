# 章 Chapter

- **Character**: 章
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: chapter, section, seal
- **Radical**: 音 (sound)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 章节 (zhāng jié) — chapters and sections
- 文章 (wén zhāng) — article

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗分为三章。(Zhè shǒu shī fēn wéi sān zhāng.) — This poem is divided into three chapters.

## Notes

章 means chapter or section; also refers to seals and regulations.
