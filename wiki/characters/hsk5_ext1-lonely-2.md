# 寞 Lonely, 寂寞 Lonely

- **Character**: 寞
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: lonely, 寂寞 lonely
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 寂寞 (jì mò) — lonely
- 孤寞 (gū mò) — lonely
- 冷寞 (lěng mò) — cold and desolate

## Example Sentences

- 一个人在异国他乡会感到寂寞。(Yī gèrén zài yìguó tāxiāng huì gǎndào jìmò.) — Being alone in a foreign land makes one feel lonely.

## Notes

Almost always appears in compound 寂寞 (lonely). Rarely used independently in modern Chinese.
