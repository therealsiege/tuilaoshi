# 注 Inject

- **Character**: 注
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: inject, pour
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 注射 (zhù shè) — to inject, injection
- 灌注 (guàn zhù) — to perfuse, infuse
- 注意 (zhù yì) — to pay attention

## Example Sentences

- 护士给病人注射了药物。(Hù shi gěi bìng rén zhù shè le yào wù.) — The nurse injected the patient with medication.

## Notes

Injection or infusion; common medical procedure.
