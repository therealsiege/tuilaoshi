# 舱 Cabin

- **Character**: 舱
- **Pinyin**: cāng
- **Meaning**: cabin, compartment, hold
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 船舱 (chuán cāng) — cabin
- 货舱 (huò cāng) — cargo hold

## Example Sentences

- 宇航员进入太空舱。(Yǔ háng yuán jìn rù tài kōng cāng.) — Astronauts enter the space capsule.

## Notes

舱 simplified from 艙. Boat 舟 compartment for storage.
