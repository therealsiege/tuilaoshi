# 池 Pool

- **Character**: 池
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: pool, pond
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 游泳池 (yóu yǒng chí) — swimming pool
- 泳池 (yǒng chí) — swimming pool

## Example Sentences

- 这个游泳池有五十米长。(Zhège yóuyǒng chí yǒu wǔshí mǐ cháng.) — This swimming pool is fifty meters long.
