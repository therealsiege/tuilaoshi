# 且 Moreover

- **Character**: 且
- **Pinyin**: qiě
- **Meaning**: moreover, and, for the time being
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 而且 (ér qiě) — moreover
- 并且 (bìng qiě) — and, moreover

## Example Sentences

- 他聪明而且勤奋。(Tā cōng míng ér qiě qín fèn.) — He is smart and moreover diligent.

## Notes

且 originally depicted an ancestral tablet on an altar, later borrowed for its grammatical functions.
