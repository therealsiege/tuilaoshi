# 寒 Cold

- **Character**: 寒
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: cold, winter, poor
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 寒假 (hán jià) — winter vacation
- 寒冷 (hán lěng) — cold

## Example Sentences

- 寒假你去哪？(Hán jià nǐ qù nǎ?) — Where are you going for winter break?

## Notes

寒 is more literary than 冷 for cold. 寒假 = winter vacation, 暑假 = summer vacation.
