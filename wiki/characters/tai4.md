# 太 Too

- **Character**: 太
- **Pinyin**: tài
- **Meaning**: too, excessively, very
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 太太 (tài tài) — Mrs., wife
- 太阳 (tài yáng) — sun

## Example Sentences

- 太贵了！(Tài guì le!) — Too expensive!

## Notes

太 + adjective + 了 is a common pattern meaning 'too...' — 太好了 (great!), 太贵了 (too expensive).
