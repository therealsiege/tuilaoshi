# 拌 To mix, to toss

- **Character**: 拌
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: to mix, to toss
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir, to mix
- 凉拌 (liáng bàn) — cold dressed with sauce

## Example Sentences

- 凉拌黄瓜很清爽。(Liángbàn huángguā hěn qīngshuǎng.) — Cold cucumber salad is very refreshing.

## Notes

Common cooking method in Chinese cuisine. Hand radical.
