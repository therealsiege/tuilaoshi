# 警 Police

- **Character**: 警
- **Pinyin**: jǐng
- **Meaning**: to warn, alert, police
- **Radical**: 言 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 警察 (jǐng chá) — police
- 警告 (jǐng gào) — warning

## Example Sentences

- 警察在调查这个案件。(Jǐng chá zài diào chá zhè ge àn jiàn.) — The police are investigating this case.

## Notes

警 combines 言 (words) and 敬 (respect), suggesting serious warnings.
