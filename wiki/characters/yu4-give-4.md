# 育 Give birth, raise, educate

- **Character**: 育
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: give birth, raise, educate
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 教育 (jiàoyù) — educate, education
- 培育 (péiyù) — cultivate, foster

## Example Sentences

- 学校教育很重要。(Xuéxiào jiàoyù hěn zhòngyào.) — School education is important.

## Notes

Common term for giving birth, raising, or educating.
