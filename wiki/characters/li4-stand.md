# 立 Stand

- **Character**: 立
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: to stand
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 倒立 (dào lì) — handstand
- 站立 (zhàn lì) — to stand

## Example Sentences

- 运动员单脚站立保持平衡。(Yùndòngyuán dān jiǎo zhànlì bǎochí pínghéng.) — The athlete stands on one foot to maintain balance.

## Notes

Standing position; fundamental body position
