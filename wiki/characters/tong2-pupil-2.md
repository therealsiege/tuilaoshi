# 瞳 Pupil

- **Character**: 瞳
- **Pinyin**: tóng
- **Meaning**: pupil of the eye
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 瞳孔 (tóng kǒng) — pupil (of eye)
- 瞳仁 (tóng rén) — pupil
- 瞳距 (tóng jù) — pupillary distance

## Example Sentences

- 医生检查了他的瞳孔反应。(Yī shēng jiǎn chá le tā de tóng kǒng fǎn yìng.) — The doctor examined his pupillary response.

## Notes

Controls light entry into eye; examined in neurological assessments.
