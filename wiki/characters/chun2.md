# 纯 Pure

- **Character**: 纯
- **Pinyin**: chún
- **Meaning**: pure, unmixed, genuine
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纯洁 (chún jié) — pure, clean
- 单纯 (dān chún) — simple, pure

## Example Sentences

- 这是纯天然的产品。(Zhè shì chún tiān rán de chǎn pǐn.) — This is a pure natural product.

## Notes

纯 simplified from 純. Silk radical 纟 indicates unmixed, high-quality material.
