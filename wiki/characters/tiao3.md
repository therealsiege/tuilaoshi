# 窕 Slender, delicate

- **Character**: 窕
- **Pinyin**: tiǎo
- **Meaning**: slender, delicate
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 窈窕 (yǎo tiǎo) — slender and graceful
- 苗条 (miáo tiáo) — slender, slim

## Example Sentences

- 她身材苗条匀称。(Tā shēn cái miáo tiáo yún chèn.) — Her figure is slender and well-proportioned.

## Notes

Used to describe slender, graceful figure.
