# 敏 Nimble

- **Character**: 敏
- **Pinyin**: mǐn
- **Meaning**: nimble, agile
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 敏捷 (mǐn jié) — agile, nimble
- 灵敏 (ling mǐn) — sensitive, quick

## Example Sentences

- 体操运动员动作敏捷。(Tǐcāo yùndòngyuán dòngzuò mǐnjié.) — Gymnasts' movements are agile.

## Notes

Agility and quickness; important for reactive sports
