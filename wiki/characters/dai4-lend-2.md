# 贷 Lend, loan, borrow

- **Character**: 贷
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: lend, loan, borrow
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan
- 信贷 (xìn dài) — credit, lending

## Example Sentences

- 我向银行申请了贷款。(Wǒ xiàng yín háng shēn qǐng le dài kuǎn.) — I applied for a loan from the bank.

## Notes

Banking term for loans. 借贷 means borrowing and lending. 按揭贷款 means mortgage loan.
