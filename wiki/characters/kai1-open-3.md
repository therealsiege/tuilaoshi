# 开 Open, start, operate

- **Character**: 开
- **Pinyin**: kāi
- **Meaning**: open, start, operate
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 开标 (kāibiāo) — open bids
- 开始 (kāishǐ) — begin, start

## Example Sentences

- 明天上午开标。(Míngtiān shàngwǔ kāibiāo.) — Bids will be opened tomorrow morning.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: open, start, operate, drive.
