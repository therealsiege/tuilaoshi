# 秋 Autumn

- **Character**: 秋
- **Pinyin**: qiū
- **Meaning**: autumn, fall
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 秋天 (qiū tiān) — autumn
- 中秋 (zhōng qiū) — Mid-Autumn

## Example Sentences

- 秋天的叶子很美。(Qiū tiān de yè zi hěn měi.) — The autumn leaves are beautiful.

## Notes

秋 combines 禾 (grain) and 火 (fire) — grain ready for harvest. 中秋节 is the Mid-Autumn Festival.
