# 弊 Disadvantage, Harm, 弊端 Abuse

- **Character**: 弊
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: disadvantage, harm, 弊端 abuse
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 弊端 (bì duān) — abuse, malpractice
- 利弊 (lì bì) — advantages and disadvantages
- 弊病 (bì bìng) — malady, defect

## Example Sentences

- 我们要分析利弊。(Wǒmen yào fēnxī lìbì.) — We must analyze the pros and cons.

## Notes

Formal term for disadvantages and abuses. 利弊 (pros and cons) is common in decision-making contexts.
