# 慕 Admire

- **Character**: 慕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: admire, yearn for
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 羡慕 (xiànmù) — envy, admire
- 爱慕 (àimù) — adore

## Example Sentences

- 我很羡慕她的才华。(Wǒ hěn xiànmù tā de cáihuá.) — I really admire her talent.

## Notes

Positive emotion. Heart radical. 羡慕 is very common. Important for expressing admiration.
