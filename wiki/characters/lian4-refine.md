# 炼 Refine, smelt, temper

- **Character**: 炼
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: refine, smelt, temper
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 冶炼 (yě liàn) — smelt, refine
- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — exercise, temper

## Example Sentences

- 古代炼金术士试图把铅炼成金子。(Gǔ dài liàn jīn shù shì shì tú bǎ qiān liàn chéng jīn zi.) — Ancient alchemists tried to turn lead into gold.

## Notes

Used for refining metals and metaphorically for tempering character.
