# 卓 Outstanding, eminent

- **Character**: 卓
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: outstanding, eminent
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卓越 (zhuóyuè) — outstanding, excellence
- 卓著 (zhuózhù) — outstanding, remarkable

## Example Sentences

- 他有卓越的领导才能。(Tā yǒu zhuóyuè de lǐngdǎo cáinéng.) — He has outstanding leadership ability.

## Notes

Formal term for excellence or outstanding achievement.
