# 盟 Alliance, oath, league

- **Character**: 盟
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: alliance, oath, league
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 联盟 (lián méng) — alliance, coalition
- 盟约 (méng yuē) — pact, covenant

## Example Sentences

- 这些国家组成了军事联盟。(Zhè xiē guó jiā zǔ chéng le jūn shì lián méng.) — These countries formed a military alliance.

## Notes

Historically refers to oath-taking ceremonies. 盟友 means ally. Common in international relations.
