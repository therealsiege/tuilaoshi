# 膝 Knee

- **Character**: 膝
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: knee
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 膝盖 (xī gài) — knee
- 膝击 (xī jī) — knee strike

## Example Sentences

- 格斗中膝盖是有力的武器。(Gédòu zhōng xīgài shì yǒulì de wǔqì.) — In combat, the knee is a powerful weapon.
