# 缈 Dim, distant, indistinct

- **Character**: 缈
- **Pinyin**: miǎo
- **Meaning**: dim, distant, indistinct
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缥缈 (piāomiǎo) — dimly discernible, vague
- 渺缈 (miǎomiǎo) — vast and dim

## Example Sentences

- 歌声缥缈动人。(Gēshēng piāomiǎo dòngrén.) — The singing is ethereal and moving.

## Notes

Literary term for indistinctness or vastness, often paired with 缥.
