# 悠 Leisurely, long, far

- **Character**: 悠
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: leisurely, long, far
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 悠久 (yōu jiǔ) — long, age-old
- 悠闲 (yōu xián) — leisurely

## Example Sentences

- 他过着悠闲的生活。(Tā guò zhe yōu xián de shēng huó.) — He lives a leisurely life.

## Notes

Unhurried pace or long duration
