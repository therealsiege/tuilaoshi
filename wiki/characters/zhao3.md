# 找 To Look For

- **Character**: 找
- **Pinyin**: zhǎo
- **Meaning**: to look for, to find, to give change
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 找到 (zhǎo dào) — to find
- 找钱 (zhǎo qián) — to give change

## Example Sentences

- 我在找我的钥匙。(Wǒ zài zhǎo wǒ de yào shi.) — I'm looking for my keys.

## Notes

找 means 'to look for.' 找到 means 'to find' (successfully). 找钱 means 'to give change.'
