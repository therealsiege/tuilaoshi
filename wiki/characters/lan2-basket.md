# 篮 Basket

- **Character**: 篮
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: basket
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 篮球 (lán qiú) — basketball
- 篮板 (lán bǎn) — backboard, rebound

## Example Sentences

- 我们每天下午打篮球。(Wǒmen měi tiān xiàwǔ dǎ lánqiú.) — We play basketball every afternoon.

## Notes

Used in basketball terminology; also means basket for carrying items
