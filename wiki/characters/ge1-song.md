# 歌 Song

- **Character**: 歌
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: song, to sing
- **Radical**: 欠 (yawn)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 唱歌 (chàng gē) — to sing
- 歌曲 (gē qǔ) — song

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢唱歌。(Tā xǐ huān chàng gē.) — She likes to sing.

## Notes

歌 means song. 唱歌 = sing songs. 歌手 = singer.
