# 抖 Shake

- **Character**: 抖
- **Pinyin**: dǒu
- **Meaning**: shake, tremble
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to tremble, shiver
- 发抖 (fā dǒu) — to shiver
- 抖动 (dǒu dòng) — to shake, vibrate

## Example Sentences

- 他冷得发抖。(Tā lěng de fā dǒu.) — He's shivering from cold.

## Notes

Shaking or trembling; involuntary movement.
