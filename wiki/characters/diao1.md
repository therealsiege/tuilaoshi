# 雕 To Carve

- **Character**: 雕
- **Pinyin**: diāo
- **Meaning**: to carve, engrave
- **Radical**: 隹 (short-tailed bird)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — to carve, engrave
- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture

## Example Sentences

- 这块木头雕刻得很精细。(Zhè kuài mùtou diāokè de hěn jīngxì.) — This wood is carved very finely.

## Notes

雕 originally depicted a kind of eagle, extended to mean carving with precision like a bird of prey.
