# 奥 Profound

- **Character**: 奥
- **Pinyin**: ào
- **Meaning**: profound, mysterious, Olympic
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 奥秘 (ào mì) — mystery
- 奥运会 (ào yùn huì) — Olympics

## Example Sentences

- 奥运会四年一次。(Ào yùn huì sì nián yī cì.) — The Olympics are held every four years.

## Notes

奥 is used for Olympics (奥运) and mysteries (奥秘).
