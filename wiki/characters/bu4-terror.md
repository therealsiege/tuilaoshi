# 怖 Terror

- **Character**: 怖
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: terror, fear
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 恐怖 (kǒng bù) — terror, horrible
- 可怖 (kě bù) — frightful

## Example Sentences

- 那是一部恐怖电影。(Nà shì yī bù kǒng bù diàn yǐng.) — That's a horror movie.

## Notes

怖 shows heart 忄 and 布 (spread), representing fear spreading through the心.
