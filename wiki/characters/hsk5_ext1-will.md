# 将 Will, About To, 将来 Future

- **Character**: 将
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: will, about to, 将来 future
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 将来 (jiāng lái) — future
- 将要 (jiāng yào) — will, be about to
- 即将 (jí jiāng) — be about to

## Example Sentences

- 他将来想当医生。(Tā jiānglái xiǎng dāng yīshēng.) — He wants to be a doctor in the future.

## Notes

Also pronounced jiàng (general, commander). As jiāng, formal auxiliary verb for future tense, more literary than 会.
