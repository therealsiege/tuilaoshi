# 异 Different, strange, unusual

- **Character**: 异
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: different, strange, unusual
- **Radical**: 廾 (hands)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 变异 (biàn yì) — variation, mutation
- 差异 (chā yì) — difference, divergence

## Example Sentences

- 物种之间存在很大的差异。(Wù zhǒng zhī jiān cún zài hěn dà de chā yì.) — There are great differences between species.

## Notes

Used for variation and differences
