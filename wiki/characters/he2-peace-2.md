# 和 Peace, harmony, and

- **Character**: 和
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: peace, harmony, and
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 和平 (hépíng) — peace
- 和解 (héjiě) — reconcile, settle

## Example Sentences

- 双方达成和解。(Shuāngfāng dáchéng héjiě.) — Both parties reached a settlement.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations and meanings: hé (peace, and), huó (mix), huò (mix), hú (mahjong).
