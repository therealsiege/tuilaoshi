# 联 Couplet

- **Character**: 联
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: couplet, to connect
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 对联 (duì lián) — couplet
- 春联 (chūn lián) — Spring Festival couplets

## Example Sentences

- 春节时家家贴对联。(Chūn jié shí jiā jiā tiē duì lián.) — Every family posts couplets during Spring Festival.

## Notes

联 refers to couplets; also means to connect or unite.
