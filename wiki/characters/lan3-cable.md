# 缆 Cable, hawser, mooring rope

- **Character**: 缆
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: cable, hawser, mooring rope
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缆绳 (lǎn shéng) — cable, hawser
- 电缆 (diàn lǎn) — electric cable

## Example Sentences

- 工人正在铺设电缆。(Gōng rén zhèng zài pū shè diàn lǎn.) — Workers are laying electric cables.

## Notes

Means thick rope or cable. Used for ships (mooring cables) and electricity (power cables).
