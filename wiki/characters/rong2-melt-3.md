# 融 Melt, fuse, harmonious

- **Character**: 融
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: melt, fuse, harmonious
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 融合 (rónghé) — fuse, integrate
- 融化 (rónghuà) — melt, thaw

## Example Sentences

- 冰雪融化了。(Bīngxuě rónghuà le.) — The ice and snow melted.

## Notes

Common character for melting or fusion, both physical and abstract.
