# 为 For

- **Character**: 为
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: for, because of; to do (wéi)
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 因为 (yīn wèi) — because
- 认为 (rèn wéi) — to think

## Example Sentences

- 为什么？(Wèi shén me?) — Why?

## Notes

为 is read wèi as preposition 'for' (为什么, 因为) and wéi as verb 'to do/act as' (认为, 成为).
