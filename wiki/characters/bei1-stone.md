# 碑 Stone tablet, monument

- **Character**: 碑
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: stone tablet, monument
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 石碑 (shí bēi) — stone tablet
- 墓碑 (mù bēi) — tombstone

## Example Sentences

- 这块石碑有一千多年的历史。(Zhè kuài shí bēi yǒu yī qiān duō nián de lì shǐ.) — This stone tablet is over a thousand years old.

## Notes

Important historical records carved on stone
