# 铲 Shovel

- **Character**: 铲
- **Pinyin**: chǎn
- **Meaning**: shovel, to shovel
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铁铲 (tiě chǎn) — iron shovel
- 铲子 (chǎn zi) — shovel

## Example Sentences

- 他用铲子铲雪。(Tā yòng chǎn zi chǎn xuě.) — He shovels snow with a shovel.

## Notes

铲 simplified from 鏟. Metal 钅 with 产 (to produce), metal digging tool.
