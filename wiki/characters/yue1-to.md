# 约 To Restrict

- **Character**: 约
- **Pinyin**: yuē
- **Meaning**: to约束, to restrict, appointment
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 约束 (yuē shù) — to restrain
- 制约 (zhì yuē) — to restrict

## Example Sentences

- 父母要约束孩子的行为。(Fù mǔ yào yuē shù hái zi de xíng wéi.) — Parents need to restrain their children's behavior.

## Notes

约 (same character as before) also means restriction and约束.
