# 税 Tax

- **Character**: 税
- **Pinyin**: shuì
- **Meaning**: tax, duty
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 税收 (shuì shōu) — tax revenue
- 纳税 (nà shuì) — to pay taxes

## Example Sentences

- 公民有义务纳税。(Gōng mín yǒu yì wù nà shuì.) — Citizens have an obligation to pay taxes.

## Notes

税 shows grain 禾 and 兑 (to exchange), originally representing grain paid as tax, later generalized to all taxation.
