# 缓 Slow

- **Character**: 缓
- **Pinyin**: huǎn
- **Meaning**: slow, to postpone, to relax
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缓慢 (huǎn màn) — slow
- 延缓 (yán huǎn) — to postpone

## Example Sentences

- 进展比较缓慢。(Jìn zhǎn bǐ jiào huǎn màn.) — Progress is relatively slow.

## Notes

缓 shows silk thread 纟 and 爰 (to lead slowly), representing the gradual gentle progress of drawing out silk.
