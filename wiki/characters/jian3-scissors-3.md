# 剪 Scissors

- **Character**: 剪
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: scissors, to cut
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 剪刀 (jiǎn dāo) — scissors
- 裁剪 (cái jiǎn) — to tailor

## Example Sentences

- 我用剪刀剪纸。(Wǒ yòng jiǎn dāo jiǎn zhǐ.) — I cut paper with scissors.

## Notes

剪 shows two knives working together, representing scissors or cutting action.
