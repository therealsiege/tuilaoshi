# 胧 Hazy

- **Character**: 胧
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: hazy (in 朦胧)
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 朦胧 (méng lóng) — hazy
- 胧月 (lóng yuè) — hazy moon

## Example Sentences

- 远处的山影朦胧不清。(Yuǎn chù de shān yǐng méng lóng bù qīng.) — The mountain shadows in the distance are hazy.

## Notes

胧 simplified from 朧. Moon 月 with 龙 (dragon), indistinct appearance.
