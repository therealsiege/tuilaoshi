# 盾 Shield

- **Character**: 盾
- **Pinyin**: dùn
- **Meaning**: shield
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 自相矛盾 (zì xiāng máo dùn) — self-contradictory
- 矛盾 (máo dùn) — contradiction

## Example Sentences

- 矛盾无处不在。(Máodùn wúchù bú zài.) — Contradictions are everywhere.

## Notes

Used in 自相矛盾: the impenetrable shield; 矛盾 now means contradiction in general.
