# 涓 Tiny stream, trickle

- **Character**: 涓
- **Pinyin**: juān
- **Meaning**: tiny stream, trickle
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涓涓 (juān juān) — trickling
- 涓滴 (juān dī) — tiny bit

## Example Sentences

- 涓涓细流汇成大海。(Juān juān xì liú huì chéng dà hǎi.) — Tiny streams gather to form the sea.

## Notes

Very small flow, used for accumulation metaphor
