# 鲸 Whale

- **Character**: 鲸
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: whale
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 鲸鱼 (jīng yú) — whale
- 蓝鲸 (lán jīng) — blue whale

## Example Sentences

- 蓝鲸是世界上最大的动物。(Lánjīng shì shìjiè shàng zuì dà de dòngwù.) — The blue whale is the largest animal in the world.

## Notes

Largest animal on earth. Fish radical though it's a mammal.
