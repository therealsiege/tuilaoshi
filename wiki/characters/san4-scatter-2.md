# 散 To scatter, disperse, loose

- **Character**: 散
- **Pinyin**: sàn
- **Meaning**: to scatter, disperse, loose
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 分散 (fēn sàn) — to disperse, decentralize
- 散步 (sàn bù) — to take a walk

## Example Sentences

- 会议结束后大家就散了。(Huìyì jiéshù hòu dàjiā jiù sàn le.) — Everyone dispersed after the meeting ended.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: sàn (to scatter) and sǎn (loose/not bundled).
