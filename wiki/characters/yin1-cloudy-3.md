# 阴 Cloudy

- **Character**: 阴
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: cloudy, overcast, shade
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阴天 (yīn tiān) — cloudy day
- 阴影 (yīn yǐng) — shadow

## Example Sentences

- 今天阴天，可能要下雨。(Jīn tiān yīn tiān, kě néng yào xià yǔ.) — It's cloudy today, it might rain.

## Notes

阴 simplified from 陰. Mound 阝 with 月 (moon), dark side without sun.
