# 缀 Sew

- **Character**: 缀
- **Pinyin**: zhuì
- **Meaning**: sew, stitch, patch
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 点缀 (diǎn zhuì) — embellish, decorate
- 缀补 (zhuì bǔ) — patch, mend

## Example Sentences

- 她在衣服上缀了几颗珍珠。(Tā zài yīfu shàng zhuì le jǐ kē zhēnzhū.) — She sewed several pearls onto the clothing.

## Notes

缀 means to attach or embellish by sewing. Extended meaning includes decorative additions.
