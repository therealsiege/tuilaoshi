# 是 To Be/Is

- **Character**: 是
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to be, is, am, are; yes, correct
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 是的 (shì de) — yes, that's right
- 但是 (dànshì) — but, however
- 还是 (háishì) — or (in questions); still
- 不是 (bú shì) — is not
- 要是 (yàoshì) — if, in case

## Example Sentences

- 我是美国人。(Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.) — I am American.
- 这是什么？(Zhè shì shénme?) — What is this?
- 他不是老师，他是学生。(Tā bú shì lǎoshī, tā shì xuéshēng.) — He is not a teacher; he is a student.

## Notes

是 originally meant "straight, correct" (日 sun + 正 straight in its ancient form). Its use as a copula ("to be") developed later. Unlike English "to be," 是 is not used with adjectives — you say 我很好 (wǒ hěn hǎo) "I am good," not *我是好. 是 links nouns: 我是学生 "I am a student."
