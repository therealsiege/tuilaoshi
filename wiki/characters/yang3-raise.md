# 养 To raise, nourish, support, 養 (traditional)

- **Character**: 养
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: to raise, nourish, support, 養 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 营养 (yíng yǎng) — nutrition
- 养生 (yǎng shēng) — to preserve health

## Example Sentences

- 多吃蔬菜有营养。(Duō chī shūcài yǒu yíngyǎng.) — Eating more vegetables is nutritious.

## Notes

To raise, nourish, cultivate, or maintain health.
