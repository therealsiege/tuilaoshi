# 裁 To Cut

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut, to reduce, to judge
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁员 (cái yuán) — to lay off staff
- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee

## Example Sentences

- 公司决定裁员。(Gōng sī jué dìng cái yuán.) — The company decided to lay off staff.

## Notes

裁 originally meant cutting cloth, extended to cutting staff and making judgments.
