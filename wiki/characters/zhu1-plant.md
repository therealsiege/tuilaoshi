# 株 Plant

- **Character**: 株
- **Pinyin**: zhū
- **Meaning**: plant, tree trunk
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 一株树 (yì zhū shù) — a tree
- 株距 (zhū jù) — plant spacing

## Example Sentences

- 院子里种了几株花。(Yuàn zi lǐ zhòng le jǐ zhū huā.) — A few plants were planted in the courtyard.

## Notes

株 has wood 木 with 朱 (vermillion), individual plant or tree.
