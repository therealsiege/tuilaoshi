# 谢 To Thank

- **Character**: 谢
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: to thank
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 谢谢 (xiè xiè) — thank you
- 感谢 (gǎn xiè) — to be grateful

## Example Sentences

- 谢谢你的帮助。(Xiè xiè nǐ de bāng zhù.) — Thank you for your help.

## Notes

谢 also has meanings of 'to wither' and 'to decline.' As a surname it is one of the most common in China.
