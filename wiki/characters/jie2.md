# 截 To Cut Off

- **Character**: 截
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: to cut off, to intercept
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 截止 (jié zhǐ) — deadline, to end
- 拦截 (lán jié) — to intercept

## Example Sentences

- 报名截止日期是明天。(Bào míng jié zhǐ rì qī shì míng tiān.) — The registration deadline is tomorrow.

## Notes

截 shows a halberd 戈 cutting through 隹 (bird), representing the action of cutting across or intercepting.
