# 育 To give birth, to raise, to educate

- **Character**: 育
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to give birth, to raise, to educate
- **Radical**: 月(moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 教育 (jiào yù) — education, to educate
- 哺育 (bǔ yù) — to feed, to nurture

## Example Sentences

- 父母教育孩子很重要。(Fùmǔ jiàoyù háizi hěn zhòngyào.) — Parents educating their children is very important.

## Notes

Broad term for raising, educating, or nurturing.
