# 句 Sentence

- **Character**: 句
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: sentence, measure word for sentences
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 句子 (jù zi) — sentence
- 一句话 (yī jù huà) — one sentence

## Example Sentences

- 这句话很重要。(Zhè jù huà hěn zhòng yào.) — This sentence is very important.

## Notes

句 is both a noun (sentence) and a measure word for sentences. Essential for language learning discussions.
