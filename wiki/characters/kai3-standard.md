# 楷 Standard Script

- **Character**: 楷
- **Pinyin**: kǎi
- **Meaning**: standard script, model
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 楷书 (kǎi shū) — regular script
- 楷模 (kǎi mó) — model

## Example Sentences

- 楷书是最规范的字体。(Kǎi shū shì zuì guī fàn de zì tǐ.) — Regular script is the most standardized style.

## Notes

楷 has wood 木 with 皆 (all), model or standard script style.
