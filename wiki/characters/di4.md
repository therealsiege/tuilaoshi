# 地 Earth

- **Character**: 地
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: earth, ground, land; -ly (de)
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 地方 (dì fāng) — place
- 地铁 (dì tiě) — subway

## Example Sentences

- 这个地方很美。(Zhè ge dì fāng hěn měi.) — This place is very beautiful.

## Notes

地 as dì means 'earth/ground/place.' As de it's an adverbial particle: 高兴地说 (said happily).
