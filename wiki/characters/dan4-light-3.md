# 淡 Light

- **Character**: 淡
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: light, pale, bland, indifferent
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淡水 (dàn shuǐ) — fresh water
- 平淡 (píng dàn) — plain

## Example Sentences

- 这汤味道很淡。(Zhè tāng wèi dào hěn dàn.) — This soup tastes very bland.

## Notes

淡 has water 氵 with 炎 (flame), water diluted, light in color/flavor.
