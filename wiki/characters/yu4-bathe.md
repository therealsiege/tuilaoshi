# 浴 To bathe, bath

- **Character**: 浴
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to bathe, bath
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 洗浴 (xǐ yù) — to bathe
- 浴室 (yù shì) — bathroom

## Example Sentences

- 浴室里有热水。(Yùshì lǐ yǒu rè shuǐ.) — There's hot water in the bathroom.

## Notes

To bathe or take a bath, also bathroom.
