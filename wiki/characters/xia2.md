# 辖 Govern, control, jurisdiction

- **Character**: 辖
- **Pinyin**: xiá
- **Meaning**: govern, control, jurisdiction
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 管辖 (guǎn xiá) — to have jurisdiction over
- 辖区 (xiá qū) — administrative area

## Example Sentences

- 这个案件不在我们的管辖范围内。(Zhè ge àn jiàn bù zài wǒ men de guǎn xiá fàn wéi nèi.) — This case is not within our jurisdiction.

## Notes

Indicates administrative control or jurisdiction. Common in legal and governmental contexts.
