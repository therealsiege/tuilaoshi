# 莫 Do not, none, desert

- **Character**: 莫
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: do not, none, desert
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 莫非 (mò fēi) — can it be that
- 莫名 (mò míng) — inexplicable

## Example Sentences

- 莫名其妙的事情发生了。(Mò míng qí miào de shì qíng fā shēng le.) — Something inexplicable happened.

## Notes

Classical negative particle, also used in modern compounds
