# 比 To Compare

- **Character**: 比
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: to compare, than
- **Radical**: 比 (compare)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 比较 (bǐ jiào) — to compare; relatively
- 比赛 (bǐ sài) — competition

## Example Sentences

- 他比我高。(Tā bǐ wǒ gāo.) — He is taller than me.

## Notes

比 is used for comparisons: A 比 B + adjective. 比较 means 'comparatively' or 'to compare.'
