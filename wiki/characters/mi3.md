# 米 Rice

- **Character**: 米
- **Pinyin**: mǐ
- **Meaning**: rice, meter
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 米饭 (mǐ fàn) — cooked rice
- 玉米 (yù mǐ) — corn

## Example Sentences

- 我要一碗米饭。(Wǒ yào yī wǎn mǐ fàn.) — I want a bowl of rice.

## Notes

米 means uncooked rice and also the unit meter. 米饭 = cooked rice. 大米 = uncooked rice.
