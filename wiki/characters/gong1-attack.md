# 攻 To Attack

- **Character**: 攻
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: to attack, to assault
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 进攻 (jìn gōng) — to attack
- 攻击 (gōng jī) — to attack

## Example Sentences

- 敌人发动了进攻。(Dí rén fā dòng le jìn gōng.) — The enemy launched an attack.

## Notes

攻 shows工 (work/craft) with strike radical 攵, representing applying force skillfully in attack.
