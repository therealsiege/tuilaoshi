# 腔 Cavity

- **Character**: 腔
- **Pinyin**: qiāng
- **Meaning**: cavity, accent, tune
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 口腔 (kǒu qiāng) — oral cavity
- 秦腔 (qín qiāng) — Qinqiang opera

## Example Sentences

- 他说话带点南方腔。(Tā shuō huà dài diǎn nán fāng qiāng.) — He speaks with a bit of a southern accent.

## Notes

腔 means cavity or accent; in opera refers to musical style.
