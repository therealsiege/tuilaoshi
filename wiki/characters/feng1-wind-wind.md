# 风 Wind

- **Character**: 风
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: wind, style, custom
- **Radical**: 风 (wind)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 风景 (fēng jǐng) — scenery
- 台风 (tái fēng) — typhoon

## Example Sentences

- 今天风很大。(Jīn tiān fēng hěn dà.) — The wind is strong today.

## Notes

风 is simplified from 風. It also means style (风格) and custom (风俗).
