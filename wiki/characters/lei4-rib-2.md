# 肋 Rib

- **Character**: 肋
- **Pinyin**: lèi
- **Meaning**: rib
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 肋骨 (lèi gǔ) — rib bone
- 肋间 (lèi jiān) — intercostal
- 两肋 (liǎng lèi) — both sides of the chest

## Example Sentences

- 他摔断了两根肋骨。(Tā shuāi duàn le liǎng gēn lèi gǔ.) — He broke two ribs from the fall.

## Notes

Protects chest cavity organs; common in trauma descriptions.
