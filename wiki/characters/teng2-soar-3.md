# 腾 Soar

- **Character**: 腾
- **Pinyin**: téng
- **Meaning**: to soar, jump
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腾空 (téng kōng) — to soar, jump in air
- 奔腾 (bēn téng) — to gallop

## Example Sentences

- 他腾空跳起投篮。(Tā téngkōng tiào qǐ tóulán.) — He jumped into the air to shoot.

## Notes

Leaping and soaring; dramatic aerial movement
