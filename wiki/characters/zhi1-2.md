# 支 支付, branch, support

- **Character**: 支
- **Pinyin**: zhī
- **Meaning**: 支付, branch, support
- **Radical**: 支 (branch)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 支出 (zhī chū) — expenditure
- 开支 (kāi zhī) — expenses

## Example Sentences

- 公司每月支出很大。(Gōng sī měi yuè zhī chū hěn dà.) — The company's monthly expenditure is very large.

## Notes

支票 means check. 财政支出 is fiscal expenditure.
