# 查 To Check

- **Character**: 查
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: to check, to examine, to investigate
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — to inspect, check
- 调查 (diào chá) — to investigate

## Example Sentences

- 医生给我检查身体。(Yī shēng gěi wǒ jiǎn chá shēn tǐ.) — The doctor examined my body.

## Notes

查 represents thorough examination and investigation.
