# 化 — Change / Transform

- **Character**: 化
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to change, to transform; -ize, -ification (suffix)
- **Radical**: 亻(person) / 匕 (spoon/dagger)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 文化 (wénhuà) — culture
- 变化 (biànhuà) — change, transformation
- 现代化 (xiàndàihuà) — modernization
- 化学 (huàxué) — chemistry
- 简化 (jiǎnhuà) — to simplify
- 消化 (xiāohuà) — to digest

## Example Sentences

- 这几年城市的变化很大。(Zhè jǐ nián chéngshì de biànhuà hěn dà.) — The city has changed a lot in recent years.
- 她学的是化学。(Tā xué de shì huàxué.) — She studies chemistry.
- 中国的现代化发展很快。(Zhōngguó de xiàndàihuà fāzhǎn hěn kuài.) — China's modernization is developing rapidly.

## Notes

化 shows a person (亻) and an inverted person (匕), depicting transformation — a person turning into something else. As a suffix, 化 works like English "-ize" or "-ification," converting nouns and adjectives into verbs or abstract nouns: 现代 (modern) becomes 现代化 (modernization), 简 (simple) becomes 简化 (simplify). This productive suffix appears throughout academic, political, and everyday vocabulary, making it one of the most versatile characters at the HSK4 level.
