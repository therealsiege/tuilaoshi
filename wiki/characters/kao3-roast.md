# 烤 To roast, to bake, to toast

- **Character**: 烤
- **Pinyin**: kǎo
- **Meaning**: to roast, to bake, to toast
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烤箱 (kǎo xiāng) — oven
- 烧烤 (shāo kǎo) — barbecue

## Example Sentences

- 我们去吃烧烤吧。(Wǒmen qù chī shāokǎo ba.) — Let's go eat barbecue.

## Notes

Used for roasting, baking, or grilling.
