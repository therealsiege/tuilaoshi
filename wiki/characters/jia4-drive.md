# 驾 To Drive

- **Character**: 驾
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: to drive, to pilot
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 驾驶 (jià shǐ) — to drive
- 驾照 (jià zhào) — driver's license

## Example Sentences

- 他正在学习驾驶汽车。(Tā zhèng zài xué xí jià shǐ qì chē.) — He is learning to drive a car.

## Notes

驾 simplified from 駕. Contains horse radical 马, originally meaning to control horses, now extended to driving vehicles.
