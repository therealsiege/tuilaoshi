# 萎 To wither, wilt, atrophy

- **Character**: 萎
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: to wither, wilt, atrophy
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 萎缩 (wěi suō) — to atrophy, wither
- 枯萎 (kū wěi) — to wither, wilt

## Example Sentences

- 缺水使植物枯萎了。(Quē shuǐ shǐ zhíwù kūwěi le.) — Lack of water caused the plants to wither.

## Notes

Physical wilting of plants or medical atrophy of tissues.
