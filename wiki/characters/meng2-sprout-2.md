# 萌 Sprout, budding

- **Character**: 萌
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: sprout, budding
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 萌芽 (méngyá) — sprout, bud
- 萌生 (méngshēng) — burgeon, spring up

## Example Sentences

- 新思想开始萌芽。(Xīn sīxiǎng kāishǐ méngyá.) — New ideas began to sprout.

## Notes

Literary term for sprouting or budding, literal or figurative.
