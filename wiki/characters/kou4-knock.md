# 叩 To Knock

- **Character**: 叩
- **Pinyin**: kòu
- **Meaning**: to knock, to kowtow
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 叩门 (kòu mén) — to knock on door
- 叩头 (kòu tóu) — to kowtow

## Example Sentences

- 他轻轻叩门。(Tā qīng qīng kòu mén.) — He knocked gently on the door.

## Notes

叩 is a formal or literary word for knocking.
