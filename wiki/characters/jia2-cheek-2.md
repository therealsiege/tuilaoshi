# 颊 Cheek

- **Character**: 颊
- **Pinyin**: jiá
- **Meaning**: cheek
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 脸颊 (liǎn jiá) — cheek
- 口颊 (kǒu jiá) — oral cavity and cheek
- 颊肿 (jiá zhǒng) — swollen cheek

## Example Sentences

- 他脸颊肿了。(Tā liǎn jiá zhǒng le.) — His cheek is swollen.

## Notes

Side of face; can indicate dental or facial infections.
