# 烟 Smoke

- **Character**: 烟
- **Pinyin**: yān
- **Meaning**: smoke, cigarette, tobacco
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 吸烟 (xī yān) — to smoke
- 香烟 (xiāng yān) — cigarette

## Example Sentences

- 这里禁止吸烟。(Zhè lǐ jìn zhǐ xī yān.) — Smoking is prohibited here.

## Notes

烟 means smoke or cigarette. Common in吸烟 (smoking), 烟雾 (smoke/fog), 香烟 (cigarette).
