# 幽 Secluded

- **Character**: 幽
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: secluded, serene, hidden, dark
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 幽静 (yōu jìng) — quiet and secluded
- 幽默 (yōu mò) — humor

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个幽静的地方。(Zhè shì yí gè yōu jìng de dì fāng.) — This is a quiet and secluded place.

## Notes

幽 has two 幺 (small) with mountain 山, deeply hidden valley.
