# 揉 To knead, to rub

- **Character**: 揉
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: to knead, to rub
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揉面 (róu miàn) — to knead dough
- 揉眼睛 (róu yǎnjing) — to rub eyes

## Example Sentences

- 做馒头要先揉面。(Zuò mántou yào xiān róu miàn.) — To make steamed buns, you first need to knead the dough.

## Notes

Important for bread and dumpling making. Hand radical.
