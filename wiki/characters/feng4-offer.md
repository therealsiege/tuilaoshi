# 奉 To offer, to receive (orders), to follow

- **Character**: 奉
- **Pinyin**: fèng
- **Meaning**: to offer, to receive (orders), to奉行
- **Radical**: 大(big)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 奉献 (fèng xiàn) — to dedicate, to devote
- 信奉 (xìn fèng) — to believe in, to have faith in

## Example Sentences

- 我们要奉行诚实的原则。(Wǒmen yào fèngxíng chéngshí de yuánzé.) — We should follow the principle of honesty.

## Notes

Respectful term for offering or following orders/principles.
