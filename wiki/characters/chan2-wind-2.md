# 缠 To Wind

- **Character**: 缠
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: to wind, to coil, to pester, to tangle
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 缠绕 (chán rào) — to twine, to wind
- 纠缠 (jiū chán) — to pester

## Example Sentences

- 电线缠在一起了。(Diàn xiàn chán zài yī qǐ le.) — The wires are tangled together.

## Notes

缠 means to wrap or wind around. Also means to pester or bother persistently.
