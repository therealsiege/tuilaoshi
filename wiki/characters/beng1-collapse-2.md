# 崩 To Collapse

- **Character**: 崩
- **Pinyin**: bēng
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to崩fall apart
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 崩溃 (bēng kuì) — to collapse
- 山崩 (shān bēng) — landslide

## Example Sentences

- 他的精神快要崩溃了。(Tā de jīng shén kuài yào bēng kuì le.) — His spirit is about to collapse.

## Notes

崩 combines mountain 山 and 朋 (friend), originally meaning mountain collapse.
