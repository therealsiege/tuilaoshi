# 认 To Recognize

- **Character**: 认
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: to recognize, to acknowledge
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 认识 (rèn shí) — to know (a person)
- 认为 (rèn wéi) — to think, to consider

## Example Sentences

- 你认识他吗？(Nǐ rèn shí tā ma?) — Do you know him?

## Notes

认识 means to know/recognize a person (contrast with 知道 which is to know a fact).
