# 对 Correct/Toward/Pair

- **Character**: 对
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: correct, right; toward, to; pair, couple
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 对不起 (duìbuqǐ) — sorry, excuse me
- 对面 (duìmiàn) — opposite side, across from
- 对话 (duìhuà) — dialogue, conversation
- 不对 (búduì) — incorrect, wrong
- 对比 (duìbǐ) — contrast, comparison
- 对象 (duìxiàng) — target, object; partner (colloquial)

## Example Sentences

- 你说得对。(Nǐ shuō de duì.) — What you said is correct.
- 对不起，我迟到了。(Duìbuqǐ, wǒ chídào le.) — Sorry, I'm late.
- 他对我很好。(Tā duì wǒ hěn hǎo.) — He is very good to me.

## Notes

对 is the simplified form of 對. The right component 寸 (inch) originally suggested precise measurement, connecting to the meaning of "correct" or "matching." As a preposition, 对 introduces the target or object of an attitude or action (对我好, 对这个问题). The extremely common phrase 对不起 literally means "cannot face (you)" — an expression of apology. As an adjective it simply means "correct" (对/不对).
