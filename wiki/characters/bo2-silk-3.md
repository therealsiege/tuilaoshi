# 帛 Silk

- **Character**: 帛
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: silk, silk fabric
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 布帛 (bù bó) — cloth and silk
- 帛书 (bó shū) — silk manuscript

## Example Sentences

- 马王堆出土了帛书。(Mǎ wáng duī chū tǔ le bó shū.) — Silk manuscripts were unearthed at Mawangdui.

## Notes

帛 has white 白 above cloth 巾, white silk fabric for writing.
