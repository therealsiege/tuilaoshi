# 分 Divide

- **Character**: 分
- **Pinyin**: fēn
- **Meaning**: to divide, score
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 得分 (dé fēn) — to score points
- 积分 (jī fēn) — points, score

## Example Sentences

- 他在比赛中得了很多分。(Tā zài bǐsài zhōng dé le hěn duō fēn.) — He scored many points in the game.

## Notes

Scoring; points and divisions
