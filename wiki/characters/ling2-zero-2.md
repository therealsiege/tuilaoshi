# 零 Zero

- **Character**: 零
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: zero, fractional, odd
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 零钱 (líng qián) — change (money)
- 零食 (líng shí) — snack

## Example Sentences

- 今天零下五度。(Jīn tiān líng xià wǔ dù.) — It's minus 5 degrees today.

## Notes

零 means zero. 零下 = below zero. Also means scattered/fractional: 零食 = snack.
