# 箭 Arrow

- **Character**: 箭
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: arrow
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow
- 火箭 (huǒ jiàn) — rocket

## Example Sentences

- 百步穿杨形容箭法高超。(Bǎi bù chuān yáng xíngróng jiàn fǎ gāochāo.) — Piercing a willow leaf at a hundred paces describes superior archery skills.

## Notes

Projectile shot from a bow. The bamboo radical indicates arrows were traditionally made with bamboo shafts.
