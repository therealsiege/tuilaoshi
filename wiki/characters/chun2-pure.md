# 纯 Pure, unmixed, simple, 純 (traditional)

- **Character**: 纯
- **Pinyin**: chún
- **Meaning**: pure, unmixed, simple, 純 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纯洁 (chún jié) — pure, chaste
- 单纯 (dān chún) — simple, pure

## Example Sentences

- 她心地纯洁。(Tā xīndì chúnjié.) — She has a pure heart.

## Notes

Purity without mixture or contamination.
