# 兆 Omen, million, trillion

- **Character**: 兆
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: omen, million, trillion
- **Radical**: 儿 (human legs)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 预兆 (yù zhào) — omen, portent
- 兆头 (zhào tou) — sign, omen

## Example Sentences

- 这是个好兆头。(Zhè shì gè hǎo zhào tou.) — This is a good omen.

## Notes

Also used as a large number unit (million/trillion)
