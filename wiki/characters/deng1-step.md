# 蹬 Step

- **Character**: 蹬
- **Pinyin**: dēng
- **Meaning**: to step on, pedal
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 蹬腿 (dēng tuǐ) — to kick legs
- 蹬车 (dēng chē) — to pedal bicycle

## Example Sentences

- 游泳时要用力蹬腿。(Yóuyǒng shí yào yònglì dēng tuǐ.) — Kick your legs hard when swimming.

## Notes

Kicking and pedaling; propulsive leg motion
