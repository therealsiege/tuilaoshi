# 放 Release, discharge

- **Character**: 放
- **Pinyin**: fàng
- **Meaning**: release, discharge
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 放电 (fàng diàn) — discharge
- 释放 (shì fàng) — release

## Example Sentences

- 电池正在放电。(Diàn chí zhèng zài fàng diàn.) — The battery is discharging.

## Notes

Used in 放电 (discharge), 释放 (release), 开放 (open)
