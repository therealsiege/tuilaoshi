# 寓 To reside, contain (meaning)

- **Character**: 寓
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to reside, contain (meaning)
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 寓意 (yù yì) — moral, implied meaning
- 公寓 (gōng yù) — apartment

## Example Sentences

- 这个故事寓意深刻。(Zhège gùshi yùyì shēnkè.) — This story has a profound moral.

## Notes

Used for dwelling places and symbolic meanings in stories or art.
