# 策 Strategy

- **Character**: 策
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: strategy, plan, policy
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 政策 (zhèng cè) — policy
- 策略 (cè lüè) — strategy

## Example Sentences

- 这个政策很有效。(Zhè ge zhèng cè hěn yǒu xiào.) — This policy is very effective.

## Notes

策 originally meant a bamboo whip (for spurring a horse). Extended to plan/strategy.
