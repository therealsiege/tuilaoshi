# 壁 Wall

- **Character**: 壁
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: wall, cliff
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 墙壁 (qiáng bì) — wall
- 壁画 (bì huà) — mural

## Example Sentences

- 墙壁上有一幅壁画。(Qiáng bì shàng yǒu yī fú bì huà.) — There is a mural on the wall.

## Notes

壁 means wall (more literary than 墙). 壁画 = mural painting.
