# 秦 Qin

- **Character**: 秦
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: Qin (dynasty), Shaanxi
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 秦朝 (qín cháo) — Qin dynasty
- 秦腔 (qín qiāng) — Qinqiang opera

## Example Sentences

- 秦腔是陕西最古老的戏曲。(Qín qiāng shì shǎn xī zuì gǔ lǎo de xì qǔ.) — Qinqiang is Shaanxi's oldest opera.

## Notes

秦 refers to Qin state/dynasty or Shaanxi; 秦腔 is a rough and vigorous opera.
