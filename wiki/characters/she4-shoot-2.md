# 射 Inject

- **Character**: 射
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: shoot, inject
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 注射 (zhù shè) — to inject, injection
- 射线 (shè xiàn) — ray, radiation
- 发射 (fā shè) — to launch

## Example Sentences

- 肌肉注射比静脉注射简单。(Jī ròu zhù shè bǐ jìng mài zhù shè jiǎn dān.) — Intramuscular injection is simpler than intravenous injection.

## Notes

Injection delivery; parenteral route.
