# 粉 Powder, pink

- **Character**: 粉
- **Pinyin**: fěn
- **Meaning**: powder, pink
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 面粉 (miàn fěn) — flour
- 粉红 (fěn hóng) — pink

## Example Sentences

- 做蛋糕需要面粉。(Zuò dàngāo xūyào miànfěn.) — Making cake requires flour.

## Notes

Can mean powder or pink color. Rice radical from rice powder origin.
