# 荡 Swing, wash away, dissolute

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: swing, wash away, dissolute
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荡然无存 (dàng rán wú cún) — completely wiped out
- 浩浩荡荡 (hào hào dàng dàng) — vast and mighty

## Example Sentences

- 军队浩浩荡荡地前进。(Jūn duì hào hào dàng dàng de qián jìn.) — The army advances in a vast and mighty manner.

## Notes

Swinging motion or moral dissolution
