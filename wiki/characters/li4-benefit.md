# 利 Benefit, profit, sharp

- **Character**: 利
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: benefit, profit, sharp
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 利润 (lìrùn) — profit
- 利益 (lìyì) — benefit, interest

## Example Sentences

- 公司的利润增长了。(Gōngsī de lìrùn zēngzhǎng le.) — The company's profit increased.

## Notes

Fundamental character for profit, benefit, and advantage.
