# 逸 Escape, leisure, excel

- **Character**: 逸
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: escape, leisure, excel
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 安逸 (ān yì) — easy and comfortable
- 逸闻 (yì wén) — anecdote

## Example Sentences

- 他过着安逸的生活。(Tā guò zhe ān yì de shēng huó.) — He lives a comfortable life.

## Notes

Carefree ease or outstanding excellence
