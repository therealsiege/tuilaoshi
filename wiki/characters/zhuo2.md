# 浊 Turbid

- **Character**: 浊
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: turbid, muddy, impure
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浑浊 (hún zhuó) — turbid, muddy
- 污浊 (wū zhuó) — dirty, filthy

## Example Sentences

- 河水变得浑浊了。(Hé shuǐ biàn de hún zhuó le.) — The river water has become turbid.

## Notes

浊 simplified from 濁. Water radical 氵 with 蜀, representing muddy or impure water.
