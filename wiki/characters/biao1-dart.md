# 镖 Dart, bodyguard, escort

- **Character**: 镖
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: dart, bodyguard, escort
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 飞镖 (fēi biāo) — dart
- 保镖 (bǎo biāo) — bodyguard

## Example Sentences

- 他雇了两个保镖保护自己的安全。(Tā gù le liǎng gè bǎobiāo bǎohù zìjǐ de ānquán.) — He hired two bodyguards to protect his safety.

## Notes

Originally a throwing weapon (dart). Extended to mean bodyguards who escorted valuables in ancient times (走镖).
