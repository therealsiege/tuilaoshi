# 澎 Surging

- **Character**: 澎
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: sound of waves, surging
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 澎湃 (péng pài) — surging
- 澎湖 (Péng hú) — Penghu

## Example Sentences

- 心潮澎湃。(Xīn cháo péng pài.) — The heart surges with emotion.

## Notes

澎 has water 氵 with 彭 (sound), crashing wave sounds.
