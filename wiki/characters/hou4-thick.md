# 厚 Thick

- **Character**: 厚
- **Pinyin**: hòu
- **Meaning**: thick, deep, generous
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 厚度 (hòu dù) — thickness
- 深厚 (shēn hòu) — deep, profound

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很厚。(Zhè běn shū hěn hòu.) — This book is very thick.

## Notes

厚 shows cliff radical 厂 with 日 (sun) and layers, representing substantial depth or thickness.
