# 吃 To Eat

- **Character**: 吃
- **Pinyin**: chī
- **Meaning**: to eat
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 吃饭 (chī fàn) — to eat (a meal)
- 好吃 (hǎo chī) — delicious, tasty
- 吃惊 (chījīng) — to be surprised, startled
- 吃亏 (chīkuī) — to suffer a loss, to be at a disadvantage
- 小吃 (xiǎochī) — snack, street food

## Example Sentences

- 你吃饭了吗？(Nǐ chī fàn le ma?) — Have you eaten? (common greeting)
- 我想吃中国菜。(Wǒ xiǎng chī Zhōngguó cài.) — I want to eat Chinese food.
- 这个不好吃。(Zhè ge bù hǎo chī.) — This doesn't taste good.

## Notes

吃 combines 口 (mouth) with the phonetic component 乞 (qǐ). The phrase 你吃了吗 (nǐ chī le ma, "have you eaten?") is a traditional Chinese greeting, reflecting the cultural importance of food. 吃 also appears in figurative expressions: 吃苦 (chīkǔ, "to endure hardship," lit. "eat bitterness"), 吃醋 (chīcù, "to be jealous," lit. "eat vinegar").
