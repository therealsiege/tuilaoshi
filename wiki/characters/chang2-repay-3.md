# 偿 Repay, compensate

- **Character**: 偿
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: repay, compensate
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偿还 (cháng huán) — repay, pay back
- 补偿 (bǔ cháng) — compensate

## Example Sentences

- 他按时偿还贷款。(Tā àn shí cháng huán dài kuǎn.) — He repays the loan on time.

## Notes

赔偿 means compensation for damages. 无偿 means free of charge.
