# 笔 Pen

- **Character**: 笔
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: pen, brush, writing instrument
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 毛笔 (máo bǐ) — writing brush
- 铅笔 (qiān bǐ) — pencil

## Example Sentences

- 我需要一支笔。(Wǒ xū yào yī zhī bǐ.) — I need a pen.

## Notes

笔 means pen or brush. Used in毛笔 (brush), 铅笔 (pencil), 钢笔 (fountain pen). Measure word for sums of money.
