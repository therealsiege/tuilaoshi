# 挪 To Move

- **Character**: 挪
- **Pinyin**: nuó
- **Meaning**: to move, to shift
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挪动 (nuó dòng) — to move
- 挪用 (nuó yòng) — to embezzle

## Example Sentences

- 请把椅子挪一下。(Qǐng bǎ yǐ zi nuó yí xià.) — Please move the chair a bit.

## Notes

挪 hand 扌 plus 那 phonetic, moving aside.
