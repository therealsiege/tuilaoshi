# 具 Tool

- **Character**: 具
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: tool, equipment
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 护具 (hù jù) — protective gear
- 工具 (gōng jù) — tool

## Example Sentences

- 比赛时必须佩戴护具。(Bǐsài shí bìxū pèidài hùjù.) — Protective gear must be worn during competition.

## Notes

Equipment and gear; tools for sports
