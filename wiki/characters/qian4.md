# 嵌 To Embed

- **Character**: 嵌
- **Pinyin**: qiàn
- **Meaning**: to inlay, to embed
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 镶嵌 (xiāng qiàn) — to inlay
- 嵌入 (qiàn rù) — to embed

## Example Sentences

- 墙上嵌入了大理石。(Qiáng shàng qiàn rù le dà lǐ shí.) — Marble is embedded in the wall.

## Notes

嵌 shows mountain 山 with陷 (sink), representing fitting something into a space.
