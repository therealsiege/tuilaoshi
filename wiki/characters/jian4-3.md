# 鉴 鉴别, mirror, examine

- **Character**: 鉴
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: 鉴别, mirror, examine
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鉴定 (jiàn dìng) — appraise, authenticate
- 鉴别 (jiàn bié) — 鉴别, differentiate

## Example Sentences

- 专家鉴定古董真伪。(Zhuān jiā jiàn dìng gǔ dǒng zhēn wěi.) — Experts authenticate antiques.

## Notes

借鉴 means use for reference. 鉴于 means in view of.
