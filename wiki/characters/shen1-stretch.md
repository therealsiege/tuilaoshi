# 伸 To Stretch

- **Character**: 伸
- **Pinyin**: shēn
- **Meaning**: to stretch, to extend, to伸出
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 伸手 (shēn shǒu) — to stretch out hand
- 延伸 (yán shēn) — to extend

## Example Sentences

- 他伸手拿起了书。(Tā shēn shǒu ná qǐ le shū.) — He reached out and picked up the book.

## Notes

伸 combines person and stretching, representing physical extension.
