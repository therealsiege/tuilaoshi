# 锻 Forge, temper

- **Character**: 锻
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: forge, temper
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 锻造 (duàn zào) — forging
- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — exercise, temper

## Example Sentences

- 锻造可以提高强度。(Duàn zào kě yǐ tí gāo qiáng dù.) — Forging can increase strength.

## Notes

Used in 锻造 (forging), 锻件 (forged part), metal working
