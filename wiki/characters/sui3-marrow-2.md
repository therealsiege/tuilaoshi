# 髓 Marrow, essence

- **Character**: 髓
- **Pinyin**: suǐ
- **Meaning**: marrow, essence
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 骨髓 (gǔ suǐ) — bone marrow
- 精髓 (jīng suǐ) — essence, quintessence

## Example Sentences

- 这本书包含了儒家思想的精髓。(Zhè běn shū bāohánle Rújiā sīxiǎng de jīngsuǐ.) — This book contains the essence of Confucian thought.

## Notes

Bone marrow or metaphorical essence/quintessence.
