# 守 To Guard

- **Character**: 守
- **Pinyin**: shǒu
- **Meaning**: to guard, to defend
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 防守 (fáng shǒu) — to defend
- 守卫 (shǒu wèi) — to guard

## Example Sentences

- 他采取防守姿态。(Tā cǎi qǔ fáng shǒu zī tài.) — He assumes a defensive posture.

## Notes

守 has roof 宀 with 寸 (inch), guarding the home.
