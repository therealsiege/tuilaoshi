# 想 To Think

- **Character**: 想
- **Pinyin**: xiǎng
- **Meaning**: to think, to want, to miss
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 想法 (xiǎng fǎ) — idea
- 思想 (sī xiǎng) — thought, ideology

## Example Sentences

- 我想喝水。(Wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — I want to drink water.

## Notes

想 combines 相 (mutual/appearance) and 心 (heart) — what the heart contemplates.
