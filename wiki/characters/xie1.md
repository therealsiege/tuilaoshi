# 些 Some

- **Character**: 些
- **Pinyin**: xiē
- **Meaning**: some, a few
- **Radical**: 二 (two)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 一些 (yī xiē) — some
- 这些 (zhè xiē) — these

## Example Sentences

- 我买了一些水果。(Wǒ mǎi le yī xiē shuǐ guǒ.) — I bought some fruit.

## Notes

些 indicates a small or indefinite quantity. 有些 = somewhat. 哪些 = which ones.
