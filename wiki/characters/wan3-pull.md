# 挽 To pull, to draw, to roll up, to coil

- **Character**: 挽
- **Pinyin**: wǎn
- **Meaning**: to pull, to draw, to roll up, to coil
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 挽救 (wǎn jiù) — to save, to remedy
- 挽回 (wǎn huí) — to retrieve, to redeem

## Example Sentences

- 医生努力挽救病人的生命。(Yīshēng nǔlì wǎnjiù bìngrén de shēngmìng.) — The doctor tried hard to save the patient's life.

## Notes

Often used figuratively for saving or retrieving situations.
