# 渗 Seep, permeate, ooze

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: seep, permeate, ooze
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — permeate, infiltrate
- 渗漏 (shèn lòu) — seep, leak

## Example Sentences

- 水慢慢渗透进土壤。(Shuǐ màn màn shèn tòu jìn tǔ rǎng.) — Water slowly seeps into the soil.

## Notes

Gradual penetration
