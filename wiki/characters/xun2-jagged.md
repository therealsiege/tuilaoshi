# 峋 Jagged, uneven

- **Character**: 峋
- **Pinyin**: xún
- **Meaning**: jagged, uneven
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 嶙峋 (línxún) — craggy, jagged
- 瘦骨嶙峋 (shòu gǔ línxún) — skinny, all skin and bones

## Example Sentences

- 他瘦骨嶙峋。(Tā shòu gǔ línxún.) — He is all skin and bones.

## Notes

Literary term for jaggedness, used both for rocks and extreme thinness.
