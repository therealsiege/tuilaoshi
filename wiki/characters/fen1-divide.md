# 分 To Divide

- **Character**: 分
- **Pinyin**: fēn
- **Meaning**: to divide, minute, mark, cent
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 分数 (fēn shù) — score, grade
- 分钟 (fēn zhōng) — minute

## Example Sentences

- 我得了90分。(Wǒ dé le jiǔ shí fēn.) — I got 90 points.

## Notes

分 means to divide or minute. 部分 = part. 分别 = to part, separately.
