# 静 Quiet, Still, 安静 Quiet

- **Character**: 静
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: quiet, still, 安静 quiet
- **Radical**: 青 (blue)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 安静 (ān jìng) — quiet
- 平静 (píng jìng) — calm, peaceful
- 寂静 (jì jìng) — silent

## Example Sentences

- 图书馆里很安静。(Túshūguǎn lǐ hěn ānjìng.) — The library is very quiet.

## Notes

Common character for quietness and stillness. 安静 (quiet) is everyday term, 平静 (calm) describes peaceful state.
