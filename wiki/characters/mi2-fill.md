# 弥 Fill, full, more

- **Character**: 弥
- **Pinyin**: mí
- **Meaning**: fill, full, more
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 弥补 (míbǔ) — make up for, remedy
- 弥漫 (mímàn) — fill the air, permeate

## Example Sentences

- 雾气弥漫了整个山谷。(Wùqì mímàn le zhěnggè shāngǔ.) — Mist filled the entire valley.

## Notes

Often used in compounds describing pervasive spreading or filling.
