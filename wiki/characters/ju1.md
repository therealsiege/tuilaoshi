# 拘 To Detain

- **Character**: 拘
- **Pinyin**: jū
- **Meaning**: to detain, to restrain
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拘留 (jū liú) — to detain
- 拘束 (jū shù) — constrained, ill at ease

## Example Sentences

- 警察拘留了嫌疑人。(Jǐng chá jū liú le xián yí rén.) — The police detained the suspect.

## Notes

拘 uses hand radical 扌, suggesting physical restraint. Can be legal detention or psychological constraint.
