# 魅 Charm

- **Character**: 魅
- **Pinyin**: mèi
- **Meaning**: charm, enchantment, demon
- **Radical**: 鬼 (ghost)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 魅力 (mèi lì) — charm, charisma
- 鬼魅 (guǐ mèi) — demon, evil spirit

## Example Sentences

- 她很有魅力。(Tā hěn yǒu mèi lì.) — She is very charming.

## Notes

魅 combines ghost 鬼 and 未 (not yet), originally referring to supernatural charm or enchantment.
