# 掐 Pinch

- **Character**: 掐
- **Pinyin**: qiā
- **Meaning**: pinch, nip
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掐住 (qiā zhù) — pinch, grip
- 掐死 (qiāsǐ) — strangle

## Example Sentences

- 他被虫子咬了一口，很掐。(Tā bèi chóngzi yǎo le yī kǒu, hěn qiā.) — He was bitten by an insect and it really pinches.

## Notes

Pinching with fingernails or sharp pressure. Hand radical. Can be painful or precise action.
