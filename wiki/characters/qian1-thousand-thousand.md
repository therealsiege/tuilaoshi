# 仟 Thousand

- **Character**: 仟
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: thousand (formal/financial)
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 壹仟 (yī qiān) — one thousand (formal)
- 仟元 (qiān yuán) — thousand yuan

## Example Sentences

- 支票上写着壹仟元。(Zhī piào shàng xiě zhe yī qiān yuán.) — The check says one thousand yuan.

## Notes

仟 is the formal financial form of 千 (thousand), used on checks.
