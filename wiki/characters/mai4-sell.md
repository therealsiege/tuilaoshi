# 卖 To Sell

- **Character**: 卖
- **Pinyin**: mài
- **Meaning**: to sell
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 出卖 (chū mài) — to sell, to betray
- 卖完 (mài wán) — sold out

## Example Sentences

- 这家店卖水果。(Zhè jiā diàn mài shuǐ guǒ.) — This store sells fruit.

## Notes

卖 has more strokes than 买 - selling requires more effort! Top component 十 represents cross/exchange.
