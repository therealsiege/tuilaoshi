# 济 To Aid

- **Character**: 济
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: to help, to aid, to cross a river, economy
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 经济 (jīng jì) — economy
- 救济 (jiù jì) — to provide relief

## Example Sentences

- 中国的经济发展很快。(Zhōng guó de jīng jì fā zhǎn hěn kuài.) — China's economy is developing rapidly.

## Notes

济 originally meant to cross water, extended to helping people cross difficulties. Key component of 经济 (economy).
