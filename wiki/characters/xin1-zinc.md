# 锌 Zinc

- **Character**: 锌
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: zinc
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锌合金 (xīn hé jīn) — zinc alloy
- 补锌 (bǔ xīn) — supplement zinc

## Example Sentences

- 锌是人体必需的微量元素。(Xīn shì réntǐ bìxū de wēiliàng yuánsù.) — Zinc is an essential trace element for the human body.

## Notes

锌 is zinc, important for immune function and used in galvanization to prevent rust.
