# 拉 To Pull

- **Character**: 拉
- **Pinyin**: lā
- **Meaning**: to pull, to play (bowed instrument)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拉琴 (lā qín) — to play a bowed instrument
- 二胡拉 (èr hú lā) — to play erhu

## Example Sentences

- 他在拉二胡。(Tā zài lā èr hú.) — He is playing the erhu.

## Notes

拉 means to pull; used for bowed string instruments like violin or erhu.
