# 深 Deep, profound, dark

- **Character**: 深
- **Pinyin**: shēn
- **Meaning**: deep, profound, dark
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 深刻 (shēn kè) — profound, deep
- 深度 (shēn dù) — depth

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个深刻的问题。(Zhè shì yī ge shēn kè de wèn tí.) — This is a profound question.

## Notes

Very common character for depth, both physical and metaphorical. 深入 means thorough or in-depth.
