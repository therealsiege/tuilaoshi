# 贸 Trade, exchange

- **Character**: 贸
- **Pinyin**: mào
- **Meaning**: trade, exchange
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贸易 (màoyì) — trade, commerce
- 外贸 (wàimào) — foreign trade

## Example Sentences

- 中美贸易关系很重要。(Zhōng-Měi màoyì guānxì hěn zhòngyào.) — Sino-US trade relations are very important.

## Notes

Core character for trade and commerce, especially in international contexts.
