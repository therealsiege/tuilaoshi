# 帛 Silk, cloth

- **Character**: 帛
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: silk, cloth
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 丝帛 (sī bó) — silk
- 财帛 (cái bó) — wealth, money

## Example Sentences

- 古代用丝帛作为货币。(Gǔ dài yòng sī bó zuò wéi huò bì.) — In ancient times, silk was used as currency.

## Notes

Classical term for silk; also used for wealth.
