# 赫 Prominent, illustrious

- **Character**: 赫
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: prominent, illustrious
- **Radical**: 赤 (red)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 显赫 (xiǎnhè) — illustrious, prominent
- 赫赫有名 (hèhè yǒumíng) — famous, illustrious

## Example Sentences

- 他出身显赫的家族。(Tā chūshēn xiǎnhè de jiāzú.) — He comes from an illustrious family.

## Notes

Literary term for prominence or illustriousness.
