# 剁 To Chop

- **Character**: 剁
- **Pinyin**: duò
- **Meaning**: to chop, to mince
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 剁肉 (duò ròu) — to mince meat
- 剁碎 (duò suì) — to chop fine

## Example Sentences

- 把肉剁碎。(Bǎ ròu duò suì.) — Chop the meat fine.

## Notes

剁 朵 (flower) plus knife 刂, repeated chopping motion.
