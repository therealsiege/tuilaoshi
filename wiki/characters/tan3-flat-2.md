# 坦 Flat, calm, candid

- **Character**: 坦
- **Pinyin**: tǎn
- **Meaning**: flat, calm, candid
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 坦白 (tǎnbái) — frank, candid
- 平坦 (píngtǎn) — flat, level

## Example Sentences

- 他很坦白。(Tā hěn tǎnbái.) — He is very frank.

## Notes

Common for flatness or frankness/candor.
