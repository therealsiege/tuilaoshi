# 蛇 Snake

- **Character**: 蛇
- **Pinyin**: shé
- **Meaning**: snake, serpent
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 画蛇添足 (huà shé tiān zú) — to ruin by overdoing
- 蛇年 (shé nián) — Year of the Snake

## Example Sentences

- 他画蛇添足，把好事弄砸了。(Tā huà shé tiān zú, bǎ hǎo shì nòng zá le.) — He ruined things by overdoing it.

## Notes

Used in 画蛇添足: drawing a snake and adding feet — doing something unnecessary that ruins the result.
