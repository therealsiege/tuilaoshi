# 故 Old

- **Character**: 故
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: old, reason, therefore,故事
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 故事 (gù shì) — story
- 故意 (gù yì) — deliberately

## Example Sentences

- 给我讲个故事吧。(Gěi wǒ jiǎng ge gù shì ba.) — Tell me a story.

## Notes

故 means 'old/former' or 'reason.' 故事 (story), 故意 (deliberately), 故乡 (hometown).
