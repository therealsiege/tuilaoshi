# 量 Measure, quantity, estimate

- **Character**: 量
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: measure, quantity, estimate
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 数量 (shùliàng) — quantity
- 量刑 (liàngxíng) — mete out punishment

## Example Sentences

- 法官根据情节量刑。(Fǎguān gēnjù qíngjié liàngxíng.) — The judge metes out punishment based on circumstances.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: liàng (measure, estimate) and liáng (measure).
