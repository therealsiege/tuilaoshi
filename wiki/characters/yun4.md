# 蕴 To Accumulate

- **Character**: 蕴
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: to accumulate, to contain
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 蕴含 (yùn hán) — to contain, to embody
- 蕴藏 (yùn cáng) — to hold in store

## Example Sentences

- 这片土地蕴藏着丰富的矿产。(Zhè piàn tǔ dì yùn cáng zhe fēng fù de kuàng chǎn.) — This land holds abundant mineral resources.

## Notes

蕴 suggests something stored or accumulated over time, like nutrients in soil or wisdom in a person.
