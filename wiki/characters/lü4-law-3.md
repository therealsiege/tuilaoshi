# 律 Law

- **Character**: 律
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: law, rhythm, pitch
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 法律 (fǎ lǜ) — law
- 音律 (yīn lǜ) — musical pitch

## Example Sentences

- 这首曲子音律优美。(Zhè shǒu qǔ zi yīn lǜ yōu měi.) — This tune has beautiful pitch.

## Notes

律 means law or regulations; in music refers to pitch and rhythm.
