# 膊 Arm

- **Character**: 膊
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: arm (in 胳膊)
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 胳膊 (gē bo) — arm
- 赤膊 (chì bó) — bare-chested

## Example Sentences

- 胳膊很有力。(Gē bo hěn yǒu lì.) — The arms are very strong.

## Notes

膊 mainly used in 胳膊 (arm). 赤膊 = shirtless, bare-chested.
