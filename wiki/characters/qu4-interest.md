# 趣 Interest, interesting, delight

- **Character**: 趣
- **Pinyin**: qù
- **Meaning**: interest, interesting, delight
- **Radical**: 走 (run)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 兴趣 (xìng qù) — interest
- 有趣 (yǒu qù) — interesting

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很有趣。(Zhè běn shū hěn yǒu qù.) — This book is very interesting.

## Notes

Interest, amusement, and delight
