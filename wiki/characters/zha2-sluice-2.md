# 闸 Gate

- **Character**: 闸
- **Pinyin**: zhá
- **Meaning**: sluice gate, switch
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 闸门 (zhá mén) — sluice gate
- 电闸 (diàn zhá) — electric switch

## Example Sentences

- 拉下电闸。(Lā xià diàn zhá.) — Pull down the circuit breaker.

## Notes

闸 with door radical for gate, used for水 sluices and electrical switches.
