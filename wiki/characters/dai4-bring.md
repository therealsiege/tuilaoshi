# 带 To Bring

- **Character**: 带
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: to bring, to carry, belt, band
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 带来 (dài lái) — to bring
- 皮带 (pí dài) — belt

## Example Sentences

- 请带上你的护照。(Qǐng dài shàng nǐ de hù zhào.) — Please bring your passport.

## Notes

带 means both 'to bring/carry' and 'belt/band.' 带孩子 can mean 'to bring children' or 'to look after children.'
