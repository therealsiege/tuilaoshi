# 绸 Silk, satin

- **Character**: 绸
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: silk, satin
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 丝绸 (sī chóu) — silk
- 绸缎 (chóu duàn) — silks and satins

## Example Sentences

- 中国的丝绸闻名世界。(Zhōng guó de sī chóu wén míng shì jiè.) — Chinese silk is world-famous.

## Notes

General term for silk fabrics.
