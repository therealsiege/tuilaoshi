# 知 To Know

- **Character**: 知
- **Pinyin**: zhī
- **Meaning**: to know
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 知道 (zhī dào) — to know
- 知识 (zhī shí) — knowledge

## Example Sentences

- 我不知道。(Wǒ bù zhī dào.) — I don't know.

## Notes

知 combines 矢 (arrow, swift) and 口 (mouth) — words that hit the mark, hence 'to know.' 知道 is the most common form.
