# 雄 Male, grand, powerful

- **Character**: 雄
- **Pinyin**: xióng
- **Meaning**: male, grand, powerful
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 雄伟 (xióngwěi) — grand, magnificent
- 雄壮 (xióngzhuàng) — majestic, imposing

## Example Sentences

- 雄伟的长城令人赞叹。(Xióngwěi de Chángchéng lìng rén zàntàn.) — The magnificent Great Wall is awe-inspiring.

## Notes

Denotes male gender and also grand, powerful qualities.
