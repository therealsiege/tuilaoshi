# 蝉 Cicada

- **Character**: 蝉
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: cicada
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蝉鸣 (chán míng) — cicada chirping
- 金蝉 (jīn chán) — golden cicada

## Example Sentences

- 夏天蝉声很大。(Xià tiān chán shēng hěn dà.) — The cicada sounds are loud in summer.

## Notes

蝉 simplified from 蟬. Insect 虫 with 单 (single), the solitary singing insect.
