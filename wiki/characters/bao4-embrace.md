# 抱 To Embrace

- **Character**: 抱
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: to hold, to embrace, to hug
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拥抱 (yōng bào) — to embrace
- 抱怨 (bào yuàn) — to complain

## Example Sentences

- 别抱怨了。(Bié bào yuàn le.) — Stop complaining.

## Notes

抱怨 means to complain. 抱歉 means sorry.
