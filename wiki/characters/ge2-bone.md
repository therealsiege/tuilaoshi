# 骼 Bone, skeleton

- **Character**: 骼
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: bone, skeleton
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 骨骼 (gǔgé) — skeleton, framework
- 骸骼 (háigé) — skeleton

## Example Sentences

- 人体骨骼共有206块骨头。(Réntǐ gǔgé gòng yǒu èrbǎilíngliù kuài gǔtou.) — The human skeleton has 206 bones.

## Notes

Technical term used in anatomy and biology.
