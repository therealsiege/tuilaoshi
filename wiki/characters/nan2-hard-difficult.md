# 难 Difficult

- **Character**: 难
- **Pinyin**: nán
- **Meaning**: difficult, hard
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 困难 (kùn nán) — difficulty
- 难过 (nán guò) — sad

## Example Sentences

- 中文很难学。(Zhōng wén hěn nán xué.) — Chinese is hard to learn.

## Notes

难 as nán: difficult. As nàn: disaster (灾难). 难过 means sad, not 'hard to pass.'
