# 了 Completion/Change Marker

- **Character**: 了
- **Pinyin**: le / liǎo
- **Meaning**: completion aspect marker (le); change of state marker (le); to finish, able (liǎo)
- **Radical**: 了 (self)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 了解 (liǎojiě) — to understand, to find out
- 为了 (wèile) — in order to, for the sake of
- 受不了 (shòu bù liǎo) — cannot stand, unbearable
- 算了 (suàn le) — forget it, never mind
- 好了 (hǎo le) — all done, okay now

## Example Sentences

- 我吃了饭。(Wǒ chī le fàn.) — I ate / I have eaten.
- 下雨了。(Xià yǔ le.) — It started raining. (change of state)
- 太贵了！(Tài guì le!) — Too expensive!

## Notes

了 is one of the trickiest particles in Chinese. As a verb suffix (verb + 了), it marks completed action. As a sentence-final particle, it signals a change of state or new situation. These two uses can stack: 他来了 can mean both "he came" (completion) and "he's here now" (new situation). When pronounced liǎo, it means "to finish" or "able to," as in 吃不了 (chī bù liǎo, "can't finish eating").
