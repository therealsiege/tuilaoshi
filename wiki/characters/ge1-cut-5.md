# 割 Cut

- **Character**: 割
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: cut, excise
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 切割 (qiē gē) — to cut
- 割除 (gē chú) — to excise, remove surgically
- 分割 (fēn gē) — to divide, partition

## Example Sentences

- 需要割除肿瘤。(Xū yào gē chú zhǒng liú.) — The tumor needs to be excised.

## Notes

Surgical cutting; removal of tissue.
