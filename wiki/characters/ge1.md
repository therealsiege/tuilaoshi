# 哥 Older Brother

- **Character**: 哥
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: older brother
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哥哥 (gē ge) — older brother
- 大哥 (dà gē) — eldest brother

## Example Sentences

- 我有一个哥哥。(Wǒ yǒu yī gè gē ge.) — I have an older brother.

## Notes

哥 reduplicates to 哥哥. Can address unrelated older males respectfully as 哥.
