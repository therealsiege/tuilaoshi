# 陶 Pottery

- **Character**: 陶
- **Pinyin**: táo
- **Meaning**: pottery, ceramics
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陶瓷 (táo cí) — ceramics, pottery
- 陶器 (táo qì) — pottery, earthenware

## Example Sentences

- 中国的陶瓷工艺闻名世界。(Zhōngguó de táocí gōngyì wénmíng shìjiè.) — Chinese ceramics are world-renowned.

## Notes

陶 refers to pottery or earthenware. China has a long history of ceramic production.
