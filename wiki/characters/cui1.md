# 摧 To Destroy

- **Character**: 摧
- **Pinyin**: cuī
- **Meaning**: to destroy, break
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 摧毁 (cuī huǐ) — to destroy, demolish
- 摧残 (cuī cán) — to devastate

## Example Sentences

- 地震摧毁了许多建筑。(Dìzhèn cuīhuǐ le xǔduō jiànzhù.) — The earthquake destroyed many buildings.

## Notes

摧 combines hand 扌 with 崔 (high), suggesting forcefully bringing down something high.
