# 泊 Anchor, moor, berth

- **Character**: 泊
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: anchor, moor, berth
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 停泊 (tíng bó) — anchor, berth
- 漂泊 (piāo bó) — wander, drift

## Example Sentences

- 船在港口停泊了一夜。(Chuán zài gǎng kǒu tíng bó le yī yè.) — The ship was moored in the harbor for one night.

## Notes

Also pronounced pō in 湖泊 (lake).
