# 翻 To Flip

- **Character**: 翻
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: to flip, to turn over, to translate
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 翻译 (fān yì) — to translate
- 翻倒 (fān dǎo) — to overturn

## Example Sentences

- 他是一名翻译。(Tā shì yì míng fān yì.) — He is a translator.

## Notes

翻 combines 番 (turn) and feather 羽, representing turning over like a bird flipping in flight.
