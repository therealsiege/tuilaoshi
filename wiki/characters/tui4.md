# 褪 Fade, take off, shed

- **Character**: 褪
- **Pinyin**: tuì
- **Meaning**: fade, take off, shed
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 褪色 (tuì sè) — fade
- 褪去 (tuì qù) — fade away

## Example Sentences

- 这件衣服褪色了。(Zhè jiàn yī fu tuì sè le.) — This garment has faded.

## Notes

Loss of color or removal of clothing
