# 出 To Exit

- **Character**: 出
- **Pinyin**: chū
- **Meaning**: to go out, to exit, to produce
- **Radical**: 凵 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 出去 (chū qù) — to go out
- 出来 (chū lái) — to come out

## Example Sentences

- 我要出去一下。(Wǒ yào chū qù yī xià.) — I need to go out for a bit.

## Notes

出 shows something emerging from a container. Opposite of 进. Common in directional complements.
