# 我 I

- **Character**: 我
- **Pinyin**: wǒ
- **Meaning**: I, me
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 我们 (wǒ men) — we, us
- 自我 (zì wǒ) — self

## Example Sentences

- 我是学生。(Wǒ shì xuéshēng.) — I am a student.

## Notes

我 originally depicted a hand holding a weapon, possibly meaning 'I defend myself.'
