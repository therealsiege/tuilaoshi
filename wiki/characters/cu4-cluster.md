# 簇 Cluster

- **Character**: 簇
- **Pinyin**: cù
- **Meaning**: cluster, bunch
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 一簇花 (yì cù huā) — a cluster of flowers
- 簇拥 (cù yōng) — to crowd around

## Example Sentences

- 路边有一簇野花。(Lù biān yǒu yì cù yě huā.) — There is a cluster of wildflowers by the road.

## Notes

簇 has bamboo 竹 top with 族 (clan), a group gathered together.
