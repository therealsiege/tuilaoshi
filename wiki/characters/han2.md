# 涵 To Contain

- **Character**: 涵
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: to contain, to include
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 涵养 (hán yǎng) — self-restraint, cultivation
- 内涵 (nèi hán) — connotation

## Example Sentences

- 这个词有很深的文化内涵。(Zhè ge cí yǒu hěn shēn de wén huà nèi hán.) — This word has deep cultural connotations.

## Notes

涵 uses water radical 氵, suggesting something that holds or encompasses like water in a container.
