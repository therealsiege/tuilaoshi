# 旁 Beside

- **Character**: 旁
- **Pinyin**: páng
- **Meaning**: side, beside
- **Radical**: 方 (square)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 旁边 (páng biān) — beside
- 身旁 (shēn páng) — at one's side

## Example Sentences

- 坐在我旁边。(Zuò zài wǒ páng biān.) — Sit beside me.

## Notes

旁边 is the most common compound meaning 'beside/next to.'
