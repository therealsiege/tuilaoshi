# 裳 Skirt, lower garment

- **Character**: 裳
- **Pinyin**: shang
- **Meaning**: skirt, lower garment
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 衣裳 (yī shang) — clothing
- 红裳 (hóng cháng) — red skirt

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着美丽的衣裳。(Tā chuān zhe měi lì de yī shang.) — She wears beautiful clothing.

## Notes

Also read as cháng, classical term for lower garment
