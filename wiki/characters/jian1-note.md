# 笺 Note

- **Character**: 笺
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: note, letter paper
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 信笺 (xìn jiān) — letter paper
- 便笺 (biàn jiān) — note pad

## Example Sentences

- 他在信笺上写了一封信。(Tā zài xìn jiān shàng xiě le yì fēng xìn.) — He wrote a letter on the stationery.

## Notes

笺 simplified from 箋. Bamboo 竹 with 戋 (small), bamboo or paper slips for writing.
