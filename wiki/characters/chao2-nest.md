# 巢 Nest, lair, den

- **Character**: 巢
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: nest, lair, den
- **Radical**: 巛 (river)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 鸟巢 (niǎo cháo) — bird's nest
- 巢穴 (cháo xué) — lair, den

## Example Sentences

- 鸟儿在树上筑巢。(Niǎo er zài shù shàng zhù cháo.) — Birds build nests in trees.

## Notes

Means nest or lair. 鸟巢 is also the nickname for Beijing's National Stadium.
