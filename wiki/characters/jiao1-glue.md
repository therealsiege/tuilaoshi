# 胶 Glue

- **Character**: 胶
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: glue, rubber, sticky
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胶水 (jiāo shuǐ) — glue
- 橡胶 (xiàng jiāo) — rubber

## Example Sentences

- 用胶水把它粘住。(Yòng jiāo shuǐ bǎ tā zhān zhù.) — Stick it with glue.

## Notes

胶 means glue or rubber. Used in胶水 (glue), 橡胶 (rubber), 口香糖 (chewing gum).
