# 碍 To hinder, obstruct, 礙 (traditional)

- **Character**: 碍
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: to hinder, obstruct, 礙 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 妨碍 (fáng ài) — to hinder, obstruct
- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle, barrier

## Example Sentences

- 不要妨碍别人工作。(Bùyào fáng'ài biérén gōngzuò.) — Don't hinder others' work.

## Notes

To hinder or obstruct, often in compounds.
