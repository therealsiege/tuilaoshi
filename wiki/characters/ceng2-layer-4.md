# 层 Layer

- **Character**: 层
- **Pinyin**: céng
- **Meaning**: layer, stratum, floor
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 阶层 (jiē céng) — social stratum
- 层次 (céng cì) — level

## Example Sentences

- 这座楼有二十层。(Zhè zuò lóu yǒu èr shí céng.) — This building has twenty floors.

## Notes

层 simplified from 層. Depicts multiple levels stacked.
