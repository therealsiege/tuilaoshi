# 挣 To earn, to make (money)

- **Character**: 挣
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: to earn, to make (money)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挣钱 (zhèng qián) — to earn money
- 挣扎 (zhēng zhá) — to struggle

## Example Sentences

- 他每个月挣五千元。(Tā měi gè yuè zhèng wǔ qiān yuán.) — He earns five thousand yuan every month.

## Notes

Pronounced zhèng for earning money; zhēng for struggling.
