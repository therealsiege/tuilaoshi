# 涌 To Gush

- **Character**: 涌
- **Pinyin**: yǒng
- **Meaning**: to gush, to surge
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涌现 (yǒng xiàn) — to emerge in large numbers
- 汹涌 (xiōng yǒng) — turbulent, surging

## Example Sentences

- 泉水从地下涌出。(Quán shuǐ cóng dì xià yǒng chū.) — Spring water gushed from underground.

## Notes

涌 describes forceful upward or outward movement, like water surging or crowds gathering.
