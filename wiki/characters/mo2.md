# 魔 Demon

- **Character**: 魔
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: demon, magic
- **Radical**: 鬼 (ghost)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 魔鬼 (mó guǐ) — devil, demon
- 魔法 (mó fǎ) — magic

## Example Sentences

- 他像着了魔一样工作。(Tā xiàng zháo le mó yī yàng gōng zuò.) — He works as if possessed.

## Notes

魔 uses ghost radical 鬼, relating to demons or supernatural powers. Can be negative (demon) or neutral (magic).
