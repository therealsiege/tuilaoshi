# 进 To Enter

- **Character**: 进
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to enter, to advance
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 进来 (jìn lái) — to come in
- 进去 (jìn qù) — to go in

## Example Sentences

- 请进！(Qǐng jìn!) — Please come in!

## Notes

进 means entering/advancing. Directional complements 进来 (in toward speaker) and 进去 (in away from speaker).
