# 审 To Examine

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: to examine, to judge, to try
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěn chá) — to examine, review
- 审判 (shěn pàn) — trial, to try

## Example Sentences

- 法院将审判这个案子。(Fǎ yuàn jiāng shěn pàn zhè ge àn zi.) — The court will try this case.

## Notes

审 is simplified from 審. Represents careful examination and judgment.
