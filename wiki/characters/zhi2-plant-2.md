# 植 Implant

- **Character**: 植
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: plant, implant
- **Radical**: 木 (wood/tree)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 移植 (yí zhí) — to transplant
- 种植 (zhòng zhí) — to plant, implant
- 植物 (zhí wù) — plant

## Example Sentences

- 他接受了心脏移植手术。(Tā jiē shòu le xīn zàng yí zhí shǒu shù.) — He underwent a heart transplant surgery.

## Notes

Transplantation; organ or tissue grafting.
