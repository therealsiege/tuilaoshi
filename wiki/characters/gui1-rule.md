# 规 Rule

- **Character**: 规
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: rule, regulation, compass
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 规定 (guī dìng) — regulation
- 规律 (guī lǜ) — pattern, law

## Example Sentences

- 你要遵守规定。(Nǐ yào zūn shǒu guī dìng.) — You must follow the rules.

## Notes

规 means rule or pattern. 规模 = scale, 规划 = to plan.
