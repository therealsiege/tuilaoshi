# 讯 Question, interrogate, news

- **Character**: 讯
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: question, interrogate, news
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 审讯 (shěnxùn) — interrogate, try
- 讯问 (xùnwèn) — question, interrogate

## Example Sentences

- 法官审讯被告。(Fǎguān shěnxùn bèigào.) — The judge interrogated the defendant.

## Notes

Formal term for interrogation or news, also means inquire.
