# 棉 Cotton

- **Character**: 棉
- **Pinyin**: mián
- **Meaning**: cotton
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 棉花 (mián hua) — cotton
- 棉衣 (mián yī) — cotton-padded clothes

## Example Sentences

- 这件衣服是棉的。(Zhè jiàn yī fu shì mián de.) — This piece of clothing is made of cotton.

## Notes

棉 means cotton. Used in棉花 (cotton plant/fiber), 棉衣 (cotton-padded clothing).
