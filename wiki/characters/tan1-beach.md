# 滩 Beach, shoal, rapids

- **Character**: 滩
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: beach, shoal, rapids
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 沙滩 (shā tān) — sandy beach
- 海滩 (hǎi tān) — beach

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在沙滩上玩耍。(Hái zi men zài shā tān shàng wán shuǎ.) — Children play on the beach.

## Notes

Shallow water areas and beaches
