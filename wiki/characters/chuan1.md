# 穿 To Wear

- **Character**: 穿
- **Pinyin**: chuān
- **Meaning**: to wear, to put on, to pierce
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 穿衣服 (chuān yī fú) — to get dressed
- 穿过 (chuān guò) — to pass through

## Example Sentences

- 今天穿什么？(Jīn tiān chuān shén me?) — What should I wear today?

## Notes

穿 means 'to wear' for clothing on the body (not hats or accessories). Also means 'to pierce through.'
