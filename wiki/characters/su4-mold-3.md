# 塑 To Mold

- **Character**: 塑
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: to mold, to shape, plastic
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 塑料 (sù liào) — plastic
- 塑造 (sù zào) — to mold, to shape

## Example Sentences

- 艺术家塑造了一个雕像。(Yì shù jiā sù zào le yí gè diāo xiàng.) — The artist molded a statue.

## Notes

塑 combines earth 土 with phonetic component, representing shaping clay or other materials.
