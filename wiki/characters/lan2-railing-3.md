# 栏 Column

- **Character**: 栏
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: railing, column (in document)
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栏目 (lán mù) — column, section
- 专栏 (zhuān lán) — special column

## Example Sentences

- 菜单栏在上面。(Cài dān lán zài shàng miàn.) — The menu bar is at the top.

## Notes

栏 originally meant railing/fence, now used for columns and sections in documents and UI.
