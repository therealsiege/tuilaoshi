# 处 Deal with, punish, place

- **Character**: 处
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: deal with, punish, place
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 处理 (chǔlǐ) — handle, deal with
- 处罚 (chǔfá) — punish, penalize

## Example Sentences

- 如何处理这个问题？(Rúhé chǔlǐ zhège wèntí?) — How to handle this problem?

## Notes

Two pronunciations: chǔ (deal with, place) and chù (place, location).
