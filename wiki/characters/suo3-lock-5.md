# 锁 Lock

- **Character**: 锁
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: lock
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锁定 (suǒ dìng) — lock, fix
- 解锁 (jiě suǒ) — unlock

## Example Sentences

- 系统已被锁定。(Xì tǒng yǐ bèi suǒ dìng.) — The system has been locked.

## Notes

Used in 锁定 (lock), 解锁 (unlock), 加锁 (lock)
