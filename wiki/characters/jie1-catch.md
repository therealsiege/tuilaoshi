# 接 Catch

- **Character**: 接
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: to catch, receive
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 接球 (jiē qiú) — to catch a ball
- 接力 (jiē lì) — relay

## Example Sentences

- 他接住了球。(Tā jiē zhù le qiú.) — He caught the ball.

## Notes

Catching and receiving; used in relay races and ball sports
