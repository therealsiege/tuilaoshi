# 睡 To Sleep

- **Character**: 睡
- **Pinyin**: shuì
- **Meaning**: to sleep
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 睡觉 (shuì jiào) — to sleep
- 睡着 (shuì zháo) — to fall asleep

## Example Sentences

- 我每天十一点睡觉。(Wǒ měi tiān shí yī diǎn shuì jiào.) — I go to sleep at eleven every day.

## Notes

睡 uses the eye radical 目 — eyes closed in sleep. 睡觉 is the common expression; 睡 alone is also used.
