# 兴 Interest

- **Character**: 兴
- **Pinyin**: xìng
- **Meaning**: mood, interest; to rise
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 高兴 (gāo xìng) — happy
- 兴趣 (xìng qù) — interest

## Example Sentences

- 认识你很高兴。(Rèn shí nǐ hěn gāo xìng.) — Nice to meet you.

## Notes

兴 is read xìng in 高兴 (happy) and 兴趣 (interest), but xīng in 兴起 (to rise) and 复兴 (revival).
