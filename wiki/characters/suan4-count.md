# 算 Count, calculate, consider

- **Character**: 算
- **Pinyin**: suàn
- **Meaning**: count, calculate, consider
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 计算 (jìsuàn) — calculate, compute
- 结算 (jiésuàn) — settle accounts

## Example Sentences

- 请帮我计算一下总价。(Qǐng bāng wǒ jìsuàn yīxià zǒngjià.) — Please help me calculate the total price.

## Notes

Common for calculation and accounting.
