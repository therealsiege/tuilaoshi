# 种 To plant, to grow, to cultivate

- **Character**: 种
- **Pinyin**: zhòng
- **Meaning**: to plant, to grow, to种植
- **Radical**: 禾(grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 种植 (zhòng zhí) — to plant, to grow
- 种地 (zhòng dì) — to farm, to till land

## Example Sentences

- 农民在田里种庄稼。(Nóngmín zài tián lǐ zhòng zhuāngjia.) — The farmers are planting crops in the field.

## Notes

Pronounced zhòng for planting; zhǒng for kind/species.
