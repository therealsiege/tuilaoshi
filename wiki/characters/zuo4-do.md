# 做 To Do

- **Character**: 做
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: to do, to make
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 做饭 (zuò fàn) — to cook
- 做事 (zuò shì) — to work, do things

## Example Sentences

- 你在做什么？(Nǐ zài zuò shénme?) — What are you doing?

## Notes

做 is the general verb for 'to do/make.' Compare with 作 (zuò) which is more literary.
