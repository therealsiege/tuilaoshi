# 轸 Chariot

- **Character**: 轸
- **Pinyin**: zhěn
- **Meaning**: chariot constellation, crossboard
- **Radical**: 车(cart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 轸宿 (zhěn xiù) — Chariot constellation
- 国轸 (guó zhěn) — national concern

## Example Sentences

- 轸宿是南方朱雀七宿之一。(Zhěn xiù shì nánfāng zhūquè qī xiù zhī yī.) — Chariot is one of the seven mansions of the Southern Vermillion Bird.

## Notes

轸 originally meant the crossboard at the rear of a chariot. In astronomy, the Chariot constellation.
