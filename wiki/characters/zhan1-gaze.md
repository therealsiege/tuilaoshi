# 瞻 To gaze, look upward, look forward

- **Character**: 瞻
- **Pinyin**: zhān
- **Meaning**: to gaze, look upward, look forward
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 瞻仰 (zhān yǎng) — to revere, pay respects
- 高瞻远瞩 (gāo zhān yuǎn zhǔ) — to show great foresight

## Example Sentences

- 我们去瞻仰烈士墓。(Wǒmen qù zhānyǎng lièshì mù.) — We went to pay respects at the martyrs' cemetery.

## Notes

Often used in formal contexts for respectful viewing or forward-looking vision.
