# 禁 Prohibit

- **Character**: 禁
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to prohibit, ban
- **Radical**: 示 (sign)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 禁区 (jìn qū) — forbidden zone, penalty area
- 禁止 (jìn zhǐ) — to prohibit

## Example Sentences

- 球进入了禁区。(Qiú jìnrù le jìnqū.) — The ball entered the penalty area.

## Notes

Prohibition; forbidden areas and banned actions
