# 逾 Exceed, go beyond

- **Character**: 逾
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: exceed, go beyond
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 逾期 (yú qī) — overdue, exceed time limit
- 逾越 (yú yuè) — exceed, overstep

## Example Sentences

- 逾期还款会产生罚息。(Yú qī huán kuǎn huì chǎn shēng fá xī.) — Overdue repayment will incur penalty interest.

## Notes

逾期未还 means overdue and unpaid. Important in credit management.
