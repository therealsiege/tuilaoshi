# 痴 Infatuated

- **Character**: 痴
- **Pinyin**: chī
- **Meaning**: stupid, silly, infatuated
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痴迷 (chī mí) — obsessed
- 白痴 (bái chī) — idiot

## Example Sentences

- 他痴迷于音乐。(Tā chī mí yú yīn yuè.) — He is obsessed with music.

## Notes

痴 sickness 疒 plus 疑 (doubt), mental confusion.
