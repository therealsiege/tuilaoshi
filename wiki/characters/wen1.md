# 温 Warm, mild, to review, 溫 (traditional)

- **Character**: 温
- **Pinyin**: wēn
- **Meaning**: warm, mild, to review, 溫 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 温度 (wēn dù) — temperature
- 温柔 (wēn róu) — gentle, tender

## Example Sentences

- 她性格很温柔。(Tā xìnggé hěn wēnróu.) — She has a gentle character.

## Notes

Warm temperature or gentle/mild character.
