# 帅 Handsome, Commander, 帅气 Handsome

- **Character**: 帅
- **Pinyin**: shuài
- **Meaning**: handsome, commander, 帅气 handsome
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 帅气 (shuài qì) — handsome
- 元帅 (yuán shuài) — marshal
- 统帅 (tǒng shuài) — commander-in-chief

## Example Sentences

- 这个男孩长得很帅。(Zhège nánhái zhǎng de hěn shuài.) — This boy is very handsome.

## Notes

Colloquial term for handsome/good-looking. Also means commander/marshal in military contexts. Very common in everyday speech.
