# 卫 To Guard

- **Character**: 卫
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: to guard, to defend, satellite
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 卫星 (wèi xīng) — satellite
- 保卫 (bǎo wèi) — to defend

## Example Sentences

- 士兵保卫国家。(Shì bīng bǎo wèi guó jiā.) — Soldiers defend the country.

## Notes

卫 simplified from 衛. To guard and protect.
