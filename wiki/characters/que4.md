# 确 Sure

- **Character**: 确
- **Pinyin**: què
- **Meaning**: true, accurate, sure, real
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 确实 (què shí) — indeed, really
- 正确 (zhèng què) — correct

## Example Sentences

- 这确实是个好主意。(Zhè què shí shì ge hǎo zhǔ yi.) — This is indeed a good idea.

## Notes

确 contains stone radical, representing solidity and certainty. Essential for expressing certainty and correctness.
