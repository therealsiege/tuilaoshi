# 拿 To Take

- **Character**: 拿
- **Pinyin**: ná
- **Meaning**: to take, to hold, to bring
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 拿走 (ná zǒu) — to take away
- 拿来 (ná lái) — to bring

## Example Sentences

- 请把书拿过来。(Qǐng bǎ shū ná guò lái.) — Please bring the book over.

## Notes

拿 means to take or hold in one's hand. 拿手 means good at (something).
