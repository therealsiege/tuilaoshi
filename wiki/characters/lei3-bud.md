# 蕾 Bud, flower bud

- **Character**: 蕾
- **Pinyin**: lěi
- **Meaning**: bud, flower bud
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 花蕾 (huā lěi) — flower bud
- 含苞待放 (hán bāo dài fàng) — in bud

## Example Sentences

- 花蕾即将绽放。(Huā lěi jí jiāng zhàn fàng.) — The flower buds are about to bloom.
