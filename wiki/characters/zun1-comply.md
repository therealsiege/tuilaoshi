# 遵 To Comply With

- **Character**: 遵
- **Pinyin**: zūn
- **Meaning**: to comply with, to follow
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 遵守 (zūn shǒu) — to observe, to abide by
- 遵循 (zūn xún) — to follow

## Example Sentences

- 每个人都应该遵守法律。(Měi gè rén dōu yīng gāi zūn shǒu fǎ lǜ.) — Everyone should abide by the law.

## Notes

遵 shows movement 辶 with 尊 (respect), representing following respectfully or adhering to rules.
