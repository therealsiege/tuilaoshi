# 偶 Accidental, Even Number, 偶尔 Occasionally

- **Character**: 偶
- **Pinyin**: ǒu
- **Meaning**: accidental, even number, 偶尔 occasionally
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偶尔 (ǒu ěr) — occasionally, once in a while
- 偶然 (ǒu rán) — accidental, by chance
- 配偶 (pèi ǒu) — spouse

## Example Sentences

- 我偶尔去看电影。(Wǒ ǒu'ěr qù kàn diànyǐng.) — I occasionally go to the movies.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: occasional/accidental (偶尔, 偶然), even number (奇偶 odd/even), and mate/spouse (配偶).
