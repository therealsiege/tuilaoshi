# 匙 Spoon

- **Character**: 匙
- **Pinyin**: shi
- **Meaning**: spoon, key (in 钥匙)
- **Radical**: 匕 (spoon)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 钥匙 (yào shi) — key
- 汤匙 (tāng chí) — soup spoon

## Example Sentences

- 钥匙在哪儿？(Yào shi zài nǎr?) — Where are the keys?

## Notes

匙 means spoon (chí) or used in 钥匙 (key, shi neutral tone). Spoon radical 匕.
