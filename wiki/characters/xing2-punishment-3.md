# 刑 Punishment

- **Character**: 刑
- **Pinyin**: xíng
- **Meaning**: punishment, sentence
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刑法 (xíng fǎ) — criminal law
- 刑罚 (xíng fá) — punishment

## Example Sentences

- 他被判了三年刑。(Tā bèi pàn le sān nián xíng.) — He was sentenced to three years.

## Notes

刑 combines 开 and knife 刂, representing legal punishment.
