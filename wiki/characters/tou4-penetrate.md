# 透 To Penetrate

- **Character**: 透
- **Pinyin**: tòu
- **Meaning**: to penetrate, thorough
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate
- 透明 (tòu míng) — transparent

## Example Sentences

- 阳光透过窗户照进来。(Yáng guāng tòu guò chuāng hù zhào jìn lái.) — Sunlight shone in through the window.

## Notes

透 indicates complete penetration or thoroughness. Used both literally and metaphorically.
