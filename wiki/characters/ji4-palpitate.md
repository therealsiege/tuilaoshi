# 悸 Palpitate

- **Character**: 悸
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: palpitate, pounding (heart)
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 心悸 (xīn jì) — palpitation
- 悸动 (jì dòng) — to throb

## Example Sentences

- 他心有余悸。(Tā xīn yǒu yú jì.) — He still has lingering fears.

## Notes

悸 has heart 忄 with 季 (season), heart beating anxiously.
