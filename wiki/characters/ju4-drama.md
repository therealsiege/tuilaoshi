# 剧 Drama

- **Character**: 剧
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: drama, play, opera, severe
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 戏剧 (xì jù) — drama, play
- 电视剧 (diàn shì jù) — TV series

## Example Sentences

- 这部电视剧很受欢迎。(Zhè bù diàn shì jù hěn shòu huān yíng.) — This TV series is very popular.

## Notes

剧 means drama or severe. Used in戏剧 (drama), 电视剧 (TV series), 剧烈 (intense).
