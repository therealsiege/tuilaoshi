# 委 To Entrust

- **Character**: 委
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: to entrust, committee
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 委员 (wěi yuán) — committee member
- 委托 (wěi tuō) — to entrust

## Example Sentences

- 他被委任为主席。(Tā bèi wěi rèn wéi zhǔ xí.) — He was appointed as chairman.

## Notes

委 combines 禾 (grain) and 女 (woman), entrusting responsibility.
