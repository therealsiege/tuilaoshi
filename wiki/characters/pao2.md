# 刨 Plane

- **Character**: 刨
- **Pinyin**: páo
- **Meaning**: plane (tool), dig
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 刨子 (páo zi) — plane (tool)
- 刨床 (páo chuáng) — planer (machine)

## Example Sentences

- 木匠用刨子把木板刨平。(Mùjiàng yòng páozi bǎ mùbǎn páo píng.) — The carpenter planed the board smooth with a plane.

## Notes

刨 (páo) means to plane or dig. Also pronounced bào meaning to peel.
