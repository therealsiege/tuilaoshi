# 亡 To Die

- **Character**: 亡
- **Pinyin**: wáng
- **Meaning**: to die, perish, lose
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 死亡 (sǐ wáng) — death
- 灭亡 (miè wáng) — to perish, be destroyed

## Example Sentences

- 这个王朝最终灭亡了。(Zhège wángcháo zuìzhōng mièwáng le.) — This dynasty eventually perished.

## Notes

亡 depicts a person hidden or gone, representing death or disappearance.
