# 瓦 Tile

- **Character**: 瓦
- **Pinyin**: wǎ
- **Meaning**: tile, watt
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 瓦片 (wǎ piàn) — roof tile
- 千瓦 (qiān wǎ) — kilowatt

## Example Sentences

- 屋顶的瓦掉了。(Wū dǐng de wǎ diào le.) — The roof tiles fell off.

## Notes

瓦 as roof tile, also borrowed for watt (unit of power) in modern usage.
