# 媲 Match, compare favorably

- **Character**: 媲
- **Pinyin**: pì
- **Meaning**: match, compare favorably
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 媲美 (pìměi) — compare favorably with, rival
- 相媲美 (xiāng pìměi) — be comparable to

## Example Sentences

- 这里的风景可以媲美桂林。(Zhèlǐ de fēngjǐng kěyǐ pìměi Guìlín.) — The scenery here rivals that of Guilin.

## Notes

Literary term for favorable comparison, often used for quality matching.
