# 舅 Uncle

- **Character**: 舅
- **Pinyin**: jiù
- **Meaning**: uncle (mother's brother)
- **Radical**: 臼 (mortar)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 舅舅 (jiù jiu) — uncle (maternal)
- 舅妈 (jiù mā) — aunt (wife of mother's brother)

## Example Sentences

- 舅舅来看我了。(Jiù jiu lái kàn wǒ le.) — Uncle came to visit me.

## Notes

舅 specifically mother's brother. Chinese kinship terms distinguish maternal/paternal.
