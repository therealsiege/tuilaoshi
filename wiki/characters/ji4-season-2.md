# 季 Season

- **Character**: 季
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: season, quarter (of year)
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 季节 (jì jié) — season
- 四季 (sì jì) — four seasons

## Example Sentences

- 春天是我最喜欢的季节。(Chūn tiān shì wǒ zuì xǐ huān de jì jié.) — Spring is my favorite season.

## Notes

季 means season. 季度 = quarter (business). 淡季 = off-season.
