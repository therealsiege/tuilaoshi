# 泅 Swim

- **Character**: 泅
- **Pinyin**: qiú
- **Meaning**: swim
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泅水 (qiú shuǐ) — swim
- 泅渡 (qiú dù) — swim across

## Example Sentences

- 他泅水渡过了这条河。(Tā qiú shuǐ dù guò le zhè tiáo hé.) — He swam across this river.

## Notes

Formal or literary term for swimming; 游泳 is more common.
