# 颂 To praise, to laud, ode, hymn

- **Character**: 颂
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: to praise, to laud, ode, hymn
- **Radical**: 页(page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 歌颂 (gē sòng) — to extol, to praise
- 赞颂 (zàn sòng) — to praise, to eulogize

## Example Sentences

- 诗歌颂扬了英雄的事迹。(Shī gēsòng le yīngxióng de shìjì.) — The poem praises the hero's deeds.

## Notes

Used for praising or songs/poems of praise.
