# 不 Not/No

- **Character**: 不
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: not, no (negation)
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 不好 (bù hǎo) — not good, bad
- 不是 (bú shì) — is not
- 不客气 (bú kèqì) — you're welcome
- 不错 (búcuò) — not bad, pretty good
- 不行 (bù xíng) — not okay, won't work

## Example Sentences

- 我不知道。(Wǒ bù zhīdào.) — I don't know.
- 他不喜欢喝咖啡。(Tā bù xǐhuān hē kāfēi.) — He doesn't like to drink coffee.
- 今天不冷。(Jīntiān bù lěng.) — It's not cold today.

## Notes

不 is the standard negator for present and future actions and for adjectives. For past actions, use 没 (méi) instead. 不 has a special tone sandhi rule: it is normally fourth tone (bù), but changes to second tone (bú) before another fourth-tone syllable — e.g., 不是 is pronounced bú shì, not bù shì.
