# 衔 To Hold In Mouth

- **Character**: 衔
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: to hold in the mouth, to harbor
- **Radical**: 行 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 衔接 (xián jiē) — to link up

## Example Sentences

- 鸟儿嘴里衔着一根树枝。(Niǎo er zuǐ lǐ xián zhe yī gēn shù zhī.) — The bird held a twig in its mouth.

## Notes

衔 shows行 (go) with metal 金, originally a horse's bit, extended to holding in mouth.
