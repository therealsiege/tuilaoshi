# 阁 Pavilion

- **Character**: 阁
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: pavilion, cabinet
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阁楼 (gé lóu) — attic, loft
- 内阁 (nèi gé) — cabinet (government)

## Example Sentences

- 阁楼上有杂物。(Gé lóu shàng yǒu zá wù.) — There are sundries in the attic.

## Notes

阁 with door radical for elevated pavilion, also used for government cabinet.
