# 零 零售, zero, odd

- **Character**: 零
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: 零售, zero, odd
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 零售 (líng shòu) — 零售
- 零钱 (líng qián) — change, small money

## Example Sentences

- 批发价比零售价便宜。(Pī fā jià bǐ líng shòu jià pián yi.) — Wholesale price is cheaper than retail price.

## Notes

零售商 is retailer. Opposite of 批发 (wholesale).
