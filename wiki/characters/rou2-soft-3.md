# 柔 Soft

- **Character**: 柔
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: soft, gentle
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柔软 (róu ruǎn) — soft, supple
- 柔道 (róu dào) — judo

## Example Sentences

- 柔道讲究以柔克刚。(Róudào jiǎngjiù yǐ róu kè gāng.) — Judo emphasizes overcoming hardness with softness.

## Notes

Softness and flexibility; judo martial art
