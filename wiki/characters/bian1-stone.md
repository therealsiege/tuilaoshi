# 砭 Stone needle, criticize

- **Character**: 砭
- **Pinyin**: biān
- **Meaning**: stone needle, criticize
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 针砭 (zhēn biān) — acupuncture, point out
- 砭石 (biān shí) — stone needle

## Example Sentences

- 他针砭时弊，直言不讳。(Tā zhēn biān shí bì, zhí yán bù huì.) — He criticizes current evils and speaks frankly.

## Notes

Ancient stone needles, now metaphor for criticism
