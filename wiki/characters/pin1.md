# 拼 Fight

- **Character**: 拼
- **Pinyin**: pīn
- **Meaning**: to piece together, fight hard
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拼搏 (pīn bó) — to struggle, fight
- 拼命 (pīn mìng) — desperately

## Example Sentences

- 他在场上全力拼搏。(Tā zài chǎng shàng quánlì pīnbó.) — He fights with all his strength on the field.

## Notes

Fighting spirit; giving maximum effort
