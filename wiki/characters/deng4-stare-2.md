# 瞪 To Stare

- **Character**: 瞪
- **Pinyin**: dèng
- **Meaning**: to stare, to glare
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 瞪眼 (dèng yǎn) — to glare
- 瞪着 (dèng zhe) — staring at

## Example Sentences

- 他生气地瞪着我。(Tā shēng qì de dèng zhe wǒ.) — He glared at me angrily.

## Notes

瞪 has eye 目 with 登 (to ascend), eyes raised wide open.
