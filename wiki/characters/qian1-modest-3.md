# 谦 Modest, humble

- **Character**: 谦
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: modest, humble
- **Radical**: 讲 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 谦虚 (qiān xū) — modest, humble
- 谦逊 (qiān xùn) — modest and courteous

## Example Sentences

- 满招损，谦受益。(Mǎn zhāo sǔn, qiān shòu yì.) — Pride invites loss, humility brings benefit.

## Notes

Humility and modesty, valued highly in Chinese culture. The character emphasizes modest speech and behavior.
