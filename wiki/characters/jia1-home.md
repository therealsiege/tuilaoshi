# 家 Home

- **Character**: 家
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: home, family
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 大家 (dà jiā) — everyone
- 国家 (guó jiā) — country

## Example Sentences

- 我想回家。(Wǒ xiǎng huí jiā.) — I want to go home.

## Notes

家 shows a pig (豕) under a roof (宀) — in ancient China, pigs were kept inside the home.
