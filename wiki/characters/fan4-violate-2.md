# 犯 To Commit

- **Character**: 犯
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: to violate, to commit (crime)
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 犯法 (fàn fǎ) — to break the law
- 罪犯 (zuì fàn) — criminal

## Example Sentences

- 犯法要受到惩罚。(Fàn fǎ yào shòu dào chéng fá.) — Breaking the law will be punished.

## Notes

犯 dog 犭 plus 㔾 (to encroach), transgressing boundaries.
