# 参 To Participate

- **Character**: 参
- **Pinyin**: cān
- **Meaning**: to participate, to join
- **Radical**: 厶 (private)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 参加 (cān jiā) — to participate
- 参观 (cān guān) — to visit

## Example Sentences

- 你参加了比赛吗？(Nǐ cān jiā le bǐ sài ma?) — Did you participate in the competition?

## Notes

参 also reads shēn as in 人参 (ginseng).
