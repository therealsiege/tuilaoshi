# 滨 Shore, bank, edge

- **Character**: 滨
- **Pinyin**: bīn
- **Meaning**: shore, bank, edge
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 海滨 (hǎi bīn) — seashore, seaside
- 河滨 (hé bīn) — riverside

## Example Sentences

- 我们在海滨度过了一个愉快的假期。(Wǒ men zài hǎi bīn dù guò le yī gè yú kuài de jià qī.) — We spent a pleasant vacation at the seaside.

## Notes

Common in place names like 哈尔滨 (Harbin).
