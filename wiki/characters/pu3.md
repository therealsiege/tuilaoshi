# 朴 Simple

- **Character**: 朴
- **Pinyin**: pǔ
- **Meaning**: simple, unadorned, sincere
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 朴素 (pǔ sù) — simple, plain
- 朴实 (pǔ shí) — plain, honest

## Example Sentences

- 她的穿着很朴素。(Tā de chuān zhuó hěn pǔ sù.) — Her clothing is very plain.

## Notes

朴 shows wood 木 with 卜 (divination), originally unworked wood, representing simplicity and naturalness.
