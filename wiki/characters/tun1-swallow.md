# 吞 To Swallow

- **Character**: 吞
- **Pinyin**: tūn
- **Meaning**: to swallow, to gulp down
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吞咽 (tūn yàn) — to swallow
- 吞噬 (tūn shì) — to devour

## Example Sentences

- 他一口吞下了药片。(Tā yī kǒu tūn xià le yào piàn.) — He swallowed the pill in one gulp.

## Notes

吞 shows mouth 口 above sky/heaven 天, representing taking something into the mouth whole.
