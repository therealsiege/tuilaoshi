# 洞 Cave

- **Character**: 洞
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: cave, hole
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 山洞 (shān dòng) — cave
- 洞箫 (dòng xiāo) — vertical bamboo flute

## Example Sentences

- 洞箫的音色幽雅。(Dòng xiāo de yīn sè yōu yǎ.) — The dongxiao has an elegant tone.

## Notes

洞 means cave or hole; 洞箫 is a vertical end-blown flute.
