# 拨 To dial, to allocate, to stir

- **Character**: 拨
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to dial, to allocate, to拨动
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拨款 (bō kuǎn) — to allocate funds
- 拨号 (bō hào) — to dial a number

## Example Sentences

- 请拨打这个电话号码。(Qǐng bō dǎ zhège diànhuà hàomǎ.) — Please dial this phone number.

## Notes

Used for dialing phones or allocating resources.
