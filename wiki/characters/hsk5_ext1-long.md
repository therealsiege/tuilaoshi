# 久 Long Time, 很久 A Long Time

- **Character**: 久
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: long time, 很久 a long time
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 很久 (hěn jiǔ) — a long time
- 长久 (cháng jiǔ) — long-lasting
- 久远 (jiǔ yuǎn) — remote, ancient

## Example Sentences

- 我们很久没见了。(Wǒmen hěn jiǔ méi jiàn le.) — We haven't seen each other for a long time.

## Notes

Common term for extended time duration. 很久 (very long) and 好久 (quite long) are everyday expressions.
