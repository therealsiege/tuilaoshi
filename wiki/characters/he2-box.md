# 盒 Box

- **Character**: 盒
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: box, case, measure word
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盒子 (hé zi) — box
- 一盒 (yī hé) — one box

## Example Sentences

- 这是一盒巧克力。(Zhè shì yī hé qiǎo kè lì.) — This is a box of chocolates.

## Notes

盒 with dish radical 皿. Also measure word for boxed items.
