# 诡 Sly, cunning, strange

- **Character**: 诡
- **Pinyin**: guǐ
- **Meaning**: sly, cunning, strange
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诡计 (guǐ jì) — trick, ruse
- 诡异 (guǐ yì) — strange, mysterious

## Example Sentences

- 他中了敌人的诡计。(Tā zhòng le dí rén de guǐ jì.) — He fell for the enemy's trick.

## Notes

Describes cunning schemes or strangely unsettling things
