# 棚 Shed

- **Character**: 棚
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: shed, shack, canopy
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大棚 (dà péng) — greenhouse
- 车棚 (chē péng) — carport

## Example Sentences

- 他们在大棚里种菜。(Tā men zài dà péng lǐ zhòng cài.) — They grow vegetables in the greenhouse.

## Notes

棚 has wood 木 with 朋 (friend), wooden shelter structure.
