# 汉 Han Chinese

- **Character**: 汉
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: Han (Chinese), man
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汉字 (hàn zì) — Chinese character
- 汉语 (hàn yǔ) — Chinese language

## Example Sentences

- 汉字很有意思。(Hàn zì hěn yǒu yì si.) — Chinese characters are very interesting.

## Notes

汉 refers to the Han dynasty and the Han ethnic majority. 汉字 = Chinese characters.
