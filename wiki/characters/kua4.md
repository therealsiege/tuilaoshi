# 跨 Stride

- **Character**: 跨
- **Pinyin**: kuà
- **Meaning**: to stride, step over
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跨栏 (kuà lán) — hurdles
- 跨越 (kuà yuè) — to stride across

## Example Sentences

- 跨栏需要很好的节奏感。(Kuàlán xūyào hěn hǎo de jiézòugǎn.) — Hurdles require good rhythm.

## Notes

Striding over obstacles; hurdles
