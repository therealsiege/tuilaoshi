# 磨 Grind

- **Character**: 磨
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: grind, polish
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 磨刀 (mó dāo) — sharpen a knife
- 磨练 (mó liàn) — temper, steel oneself

## Example Sentences

- 他在磨刀石上磨刀。(Tā zài módāoshí shàng mó dāo.) — He sharpened his knife on a whetstone.

## Notes

磨 (mó) means to grind, polish, or sharpen. As mò it refers to a millstone or mill.
