# 押 Mortgage, pawn, detain

- **Character**: 押
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: mortgage, pawn, detain
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抵押 (dǐ yā) — to mortgage
- 押金 (yā jīn) — deposit, security

## Example Sentences

- 租房需要交押金。(Zū fáng xū yào jiāo yā jīn.) — Renting a house requires paying a deposit.

## Notes

Used for collateral and deposits. 押运 means to escort (valuables). 扣押 means to detain.
