# 廉 Honest, cheap

- **Character**: 廉
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: honest, cheap
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 廉价 (lián jià) — cheap, low-priced
- 清廉 (qīng lián) — honest, incorruptible

## Example Sentences

- 这里可以买到廉价商品。(Zhè lǐ kě yǐ mǎi dào lián jià shāng pǐn.) — You can buy cheap goods here.

## Notes

廉洁 means honest/clean government. Has both meanings: cheap and honest.
