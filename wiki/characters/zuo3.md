# 左 Left

- **Character**: 左
- **Pinyin**: zuǒ
- **Meaning**: left (side)
- **Radical**: 工 (work)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 左边 (zuǒ biān) — left side
- 左右 (zuǒ yòu) — approximately

## Example Sentences

- 往左走。(Wǎng zuǒ zǒu.) — Go left.

## Notes

左 originally depicted a hand and a tool — the left hand used for work. 左右 also means 'approximately.'
