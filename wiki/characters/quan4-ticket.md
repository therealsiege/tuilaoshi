# 券 Ticket, certificate, bond

- **Character**: 券
- **Pinyin**: quàn
- **Meaning**: ticket, certificate, bond
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 债券 (zhài quàn) — bond, debenture
- 证券 (zhèng quàn) — securities

## Example Sentences

- 证券市场波动很大。(Zhèng quàn shì chǎng bō dòng hěn dà.) — The securities market is very volatile.

## Notes

Used for various certificates and tickets. 优惠券 means coupon. 入场券 means admission ticket.
