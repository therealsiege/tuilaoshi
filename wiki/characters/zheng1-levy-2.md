# 征 Levy, collect

- **Character**: 征
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: levy, collect
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 征税 (zhēng shuì) — levy taxes
- 征收 (zhēng shōu) — levy, collect

## Example Sentences

- 政府对进口商品征税。(Zhèng fǔ duì jìn kǒu shāng pǐn zhēng shuì.) — The government levies taxes on imported goods.

## Notes

征用 means requisition. 征集 means collect/solicit.
