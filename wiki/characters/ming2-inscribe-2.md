# 铭 Inscribe, engrave, motto

- **Character**: 铭
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: inscribe, engrave, motto
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铭记 (míng jì) — engrave in one's memory
- 座右铭 (zuò yòu míng) — motto

## Example Sentences

- 我们要铭记历史，珍惜和平。(Wǒmen yào míngjì lìshǐ, zhēnxī hépíng.) — We must remember history and cherish peace.

## Notes

Originally text inscribed on bronze or stone. Now means to remember deeply or a personal motto.
