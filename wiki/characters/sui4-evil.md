# 祟 Evil Spirit

- **Character**: 祟
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: evil spirit, haunting
- **Radical**: 示 (to show)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 作祟 (zuò suì) — to cause trouble, to haunt
- 鬼祟 (guǐ suì) — sneaky, furtive

## Example Sentences

- 他的行为很鬼祟。(Tā de xíng wéi hěn guǐ suì.) — His behavior is very sneaky.

## Notes

祟 uses altar radical 示, relating to supernatural harm or malicious spirits causing misfortune.
