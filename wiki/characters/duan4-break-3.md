# 断 To Break

- **Character**: 断
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: to break, to snap, to decide
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 中断 (zhōng duàn) — to interrupt
- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge

## Example Sentences

- 绳子断了。(Shéng zi duàn le.) — The rope broke.

## Notes

断 simplified from 斷. Shows axe 斤 cutting threads, representing breaking or severing.
