# 卤 To stew in soy sauce, brine

- **Character**: 卤
- **Pinyin**: lǔ
- **Meaning**: to stew in soy sauce, brine
- **Radical**: 卜 (divination)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 卤肉 (lǔ ròu) — braised meat
- 卤蛋 (lǔ dàn) — tea egg, marinated egg

## Example Sentences

- 我买了一些卤豆腐。(Wǒ mǎile yīxiē lǔ dòufu.) — I bought some braised tofu.

## Notes

Popular Chinese cooking method using soy sauce and spices. Creates savory dishes.
