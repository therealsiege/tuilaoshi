# 免 Exempt, avoid, 免

- **Character**: 免
- **Pinyin**: miǎn
- **Meaning**: exempt, avoid,免
- **Radical**: 免 (exempt)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 免疫 (miǎn yì) — immunity, immune
- 避免 (bì miǎn) — avoid, prevent

## Example Sentences

- 人体的免疫系统保护我们免受疾病侵害。(Rén tǐ de miǎn yì xì tǒng bǎo hù wǒ men miǎn shòu jí bìng qīn hài.) — The body's immune system protects us from disease.

## Notes

Used for immunity and exemption
