# 的 Of

- **Character**: 的
- **Pinyin**: de
- **Meaning**: of, possessive particle
- **Radical**: 白 (white)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 目的 (mù dì) — purpose
- 有的 (yǒu de) — some

## Example Sentences

- 这是我的书。(Zhè shì wǒ de shū.) — This is my book.

## Notes

的 is the most common character in Chinese, used as a possessive and attributive particle.
