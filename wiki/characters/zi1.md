# 觜 Beak

- **Character**: 觜
- **Pinyin**: zī
- **Meaning**: Beak constellation, mouth
- **Radical**: 角(horn)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 觜宿 (zī xiù) — Beak constellation
- 鸟觜 (niǎo zuǐ) — bird's beak

## Example Sentences

- 觜宿是西方白虎的嘴部。(Zī xiù shì xīfāng báihǔ de zuǐ bù.) — Beak constellation is the mouth part of the Western White Tiger.

## Notes

觜 (zī) refers to the Beak constellation. Also pronounced zuǐ meaning mouth or beak.
