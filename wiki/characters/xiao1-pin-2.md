# 销 Pin

- **Character**: 销
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: pin, to melt, to sell
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 销售 (xiāo shòu) — to sell
- 销毁 (xiāo huǐ) — to destroy

## Example Sentences

- 这个产品销售很好。(Zhè ge chǎn pǐn xiāo shòu hěn hǎo.) — This product sells well.

## Notes

销 simplified from 銷. Metal 钅 pin, also to melt away.
