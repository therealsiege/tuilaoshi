# 推 To Push

- **Character**: 推
- **Pinyin**: tuī
- **Meaning**: to push, to shove, to infer
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 推开 (tuī kāi) — to push open
- 推迟 (tuī chí) — to postpone

## Example Sentences

- 请推门进来。(Qǐng tuī mén jìn lái.) — Please push the door and come in.

## Notes

推 is paired with 拉 (pull/push). Also means to infer or deduce (推理 reasoning, 推测 speculation).
