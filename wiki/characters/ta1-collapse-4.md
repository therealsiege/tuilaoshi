# 塌 To Collapse

- **Character**: 塌
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to cave in
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 倒塌 (dǎo tā) — to collapse
- 坍塌 (tān tā) — to cave in

## Example Sentences

- 那栋楼倒塌了。(Nà dòng lóu dǎo tā le.) — That building collapsed.

## Notes

塌 combines earth 土 and 塔 phonetic, structures falling to ground.
