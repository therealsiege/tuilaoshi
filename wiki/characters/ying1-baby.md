# 婴 Baby, infant

- **Character**: 婴
- **Pinyin**: yīng
- **Meaning**: baby, infant
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 婴儿 (yīng ér) — baby, infant
- 婴幼儿 (yīng yòu ér) — infants and young children

## Example Sentences

- 婴儿需要悉心照料。(Yīng ér xū yào xī xīn zhào liào.) — Babies need careful care.
