# 凤 Phoenix

- **Character**: 凤
- **Pinyin**: fèng
- **Meaning**: phoenix
- **Radical**: 几 (table)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 龙飞凤舞 (lóng fēi fèng wǔ) — lively, flamboyant
- 凤凰 (fèng huáng) — phoenix

## Example Sentences

- 凤凰是中国的吉祥鸟。(Fènghuáng shì zhōngguó de jíxiáng niǎo.) — The phoenix is an auspicious bird in China.

## Notes

Used in 龙飞凤舞: the phoenix complements the dragon as yin to yang.
