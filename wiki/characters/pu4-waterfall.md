# 瀑 Waterfall

- **Character**: 瀑
- **Pinyin**: pù
- **Meaning**: waterfall
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 瀑布 (pù bù) — waterfall
- 飞瀑 (fēi pù) — 飞瀑 waterfall

## Example Sentences

- 那个瀑布很壮观。(Nàge pùbù hěn zhuàngguān.) — That waterfall is spectacular.

## Notes

瀑 combines water 氵 with 暴 (violent), representing violently falling water.
