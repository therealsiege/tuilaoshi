# 奎 Legs

- **Character**: 奎
- **Pinyin**: kuí
- **Meaning**: Legs constellation, stride
- **Radical**: 大(big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 奎宿 (kuí xiù) — Legs constellation
- 奎星 (kuí xīng) — Kui star

## Example Sentences

- 奎宿是西方白虎七宿的第一宿。(Kuí xiù shì xīfāng báihǔ qī xiù de dì yī xiù.) — Legs is the first mansion of the Western White Tiger.

## Notes

奎 refers to the Legs constellation in the 28 mansions. Also associated with literary talent in traditional culture.
