# 浮 To Float

- **Character**: 浮
- **Pinyin**: fú
- **Meaning**: to float, superficial
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浮现 (fú xiàn) — to emerge
- 漂浮 (piāo fú) — to float

## Example Sentences

- 木头浮在水面上。(Mù tou fú zài shuǐ miàn shàng.) — Wood floats on the water surface.

## Notes

浮 has water 氵 with 孚 (to trust), buoyancy keeping afloat.
