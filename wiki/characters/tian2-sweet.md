# 甜 Sweet

- **Character**: 甜
- **Pinyin**: tián
- **Meaning**: sweet
- **Radical**: 甘 (sweet)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 甜点 (tián diǎn) — dessert
- 香甜 (xiāng tián) — fragrant and sweet

## Example Sentences

- 这个蛋糕很甜。(Zhè ge dàn gāo hěn tián.) — This cake is very sweet.

## Notes

甜 uses sweet radical 甘. One of five basic tastes. 甜点 = dessert.
