# 琳 Beautiful jade

- **Character**: 琳
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: beautiful jade
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琳琅 (lín láng) — beautiful gems
- 琳琅满目 (lín láng mǎn mù) — dazzling array

## Example Sentences

- 展厅里琳琅满目的艺术品让人目不暇接。(Zhǎn tīng lǐ lín láng mǎn mù de yì shù pǐn ràng rén mù bù xiá jiē.) — The dazzling array of artworks in the hall was overwhelming.

## Notes

Often used in the phrase 琳琅满目 (dazzling variety).
