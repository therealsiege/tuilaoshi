# 换 Exchange, change

- **Character**: 换
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: exchange, change
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 交换 (jiāo huàn) — exchange
- 更换 (gēng huàn) — replace, change

## Example Sentences

- 商品有质量问题可以换。(Shāng pǐn yǒu zhì liàng wèn tí kě yǐ huàn.) — Products with quality issues can be exchanged.

## Notes

换货 means exchange goods. 退换货 is return and exchange policy.
