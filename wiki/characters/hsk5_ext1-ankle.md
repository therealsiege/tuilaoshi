# 踝 Ankle, 脚踝 Ankle

- **Character**: 踝
- **Pinyin**: huái
- **Meaning**: ankle, 脚踝 ankle
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 脚踝 (jiǎo huái) — ankle
- 踝骨 (huái gǔ) — anklebone
- 踝关节 (huái guān jié) — ankle joint

## Example Sentences

- 他扭伤了脚踝。(Tā niǔshāng le jiǎohuái.) — He sprained his ankle.

## Notes

Specific term for ankle. Common in medical and sports injury contexts. 脚踝 is the standard term.
