# 审 Examine, try, inspect

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: examine, try, inspect
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěnchá) — examine, investigate
- 审判 (shěnpàn) — try, bring to trial

## Example Sentences

- 法院审理了这个案件。(Fǎyuàn shěnlǐ le zhège ànjiàn.) — The court tried this case.

## Notes

Common formal term for examination, trial, or inspection.
