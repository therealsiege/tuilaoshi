# 磋 To Deliberate

- **Character**: 磋
- **Pinyin**: cuō
- **Meaning**: to deliberate, to polish
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 切磋 (qiē cuō) — to learn from each other
- 磋商 (cuō shāng) — to consult, to negotiate

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要切磋技艺。(Wǒ men xū yào qiē cuō jì yì.) — We need to learn from each other's skills.

## Notes

磋 shows stone 石 with 差 (difference), originally polishing stones, extended to refining through discussion.
