# 戏 Play

- **Character**: 戏
- **Pinyin**: xì
- **Meaning**: play, drama, opera
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 戏剧 (xì jù) — drama, theater
- 京剧 (jīng jù) — Beijing opera

## Example Sentences

- 他喜欢看戏。(Tā xǐ huān kàn xì.) — He likes to watch opera.

## Notes

戏 refers to theatrical performances, especially traditional Chinese opera.
