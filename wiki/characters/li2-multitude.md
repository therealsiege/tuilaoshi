# 黎 Multitude, black

- **Character**: 黎
- **Pinyin**: lí
- **Meaning**: multitude, black
- **Radical**: 黍 (millet)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 黎民 (lí mín) — the people
- 黎明 (lí míng) — dawn

## Example Sentences

- 黎民百姓安居乐业。(Lí mín bǎi xìng ān jū lè yè.) — The people live and work in peace.

## Notes

Often used in 黎民 (the masses), classical literary term
