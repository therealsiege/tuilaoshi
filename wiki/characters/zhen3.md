# 诊 Examine, diagnose

- **Character**: 诊
- **Pinyin**: zhěn
- **Meaning**: examine, diagnose
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诊断 (zhěn duàn) — diagnose
- 门诊 (mén zhěn) — outpatient service

## Example Sentences

- 医生给他做了详细的诊断。(Yī shēng gěi tā zuò le xiáng xì de zhěn duàn.) — The doctor gave him a thorough diagnosis.
