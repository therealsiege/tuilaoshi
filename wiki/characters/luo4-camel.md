# 骆 Camel

- **Character**: 骆
- **Pinyin**: luò
- **Meaning**: camel
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 骆驼 (luò tuo) — camel
- 骆驼队 (luò tuo duì) — camel caravan

## Example Sentences

- 骆驼能在沙漠里生存。(Luòtuo néng zài shāmò lǐ shēngcún.) — Camels can survive in the desert.

## Notes

Usually used in compound 骆驼. Horse radical for large domesticated animal.
