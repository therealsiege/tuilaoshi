# 衷 Inner feelings, heartfelt

- **Character**: 衷
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: inner feelings, heartfelt
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 衷心 (zhōng xīn) — heartfelt, cordial
- 苦衷 (kǔ zhōng) — secret trouble

## Example Sentences

- 我向你表示衷心的感谢。(Wǒ xiàng nǐ biǎo shì zhōng xīn de gǎn xiè.) — I express my heartfelt thanks to you.

## Notes

Refers to sincere inner feelings or hidden difficulties
