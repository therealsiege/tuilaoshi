# 迁 Move, migrate, change

- **Character**: 迁
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: move, migrate, change
- **Radical**: 辵 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 迁移 (qiān yí) — migrate, move
- 迁徙 (qiān xǐ) — migrate (of birds)

## Example Sentences

- 候鸟每年都要迁徙。(Hòu niǎo měi nián dōu yào qiān xǐ.) — Migratory birds migrate every year.

## Notes

Used for animal migration
