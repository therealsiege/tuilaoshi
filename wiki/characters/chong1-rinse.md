# 冲 To rinse, rush, dash, 沖 (traditional)

- **Character**: 冲
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: to rinse, rush, dash, 沖 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冲洗 (chōng xǐ) — to rinse, wash
- 冲动 (chōng dòng) — impulsive

## Example Sentences

- 用水冲洗一下。(Yòng shuǐ chōngxǐ yīxià.) — Rinse it with water.

## Notes

To rinse, rush, or dash; also impulsive.
