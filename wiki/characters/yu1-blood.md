# 瘀 Stasis

- **Character**: 瘀
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: blood stasis
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 瘀血 (yū xiě) — blood stasis
- 瘀伤 (yū shāng) — bruise, contusion
- 瘀斑 (yū bān) — ecchymosis, bruise

## Example Sentences

- 中医认为瘀血会导致疼痛。(Zhōng yī rèn wéi yū xiě huì dǎo zhì téng tòng.) — TCM believes blood stasis causes pain.

## Notes

Central concept in TCM pathology; treated with herbs.
