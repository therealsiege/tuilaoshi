# 保 Protect, guarantee

- **Character**: 保
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: protect, guarantee
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 保险 (bǎo xiǎn) — insurance
- 保证 (bǎo zhèng) — guarantee

## Example Sentences

- 产品有一年保修期。(Chǎn pǐn yǒu yī nián bǎo xiū qī.) — The product has a one-year warranty.

## Notes

保修 is warranty. 保质期 is shelf life/expiration date.
