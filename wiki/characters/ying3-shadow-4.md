# 影 Shadow

- **Character**: 影
- **Pinyin**: yǐng
- **Meaning**: shadow, reflection, movie
- **Radical**: 彡 (hair)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 杯弓蛇影 (bēi gōng shé yǐng) — overly suspicious
- 电影 (diàn yǐng) — movie

## Example Sentences

- 我们去看电影吧。(Wǒmen qù kàn diànyǐng ba.) — Let's go see a movie.

## Notes

Used in 杯弓蛇影: the shadow or reflection that causes fear.
