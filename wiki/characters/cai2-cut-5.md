# 裁 To Cut

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut, to judge, to reduce
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee
- 裁员 (cái yuán) — layoffs

## Example Sentences

- 公司决定裁员。(Gōng sī jué dìng cái yuán.) — The company decided to lay off employees.

## Notes

裁 depicts cutting cloth, extended to mean judging/reducing.
