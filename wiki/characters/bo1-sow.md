# 播 To Broadcast

- **Character**: 播
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to sow, to broadcast, to spread
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 广播 (guǎng bō) — broadcast
- 播放 (bō fàng) — to play (media)

## Example Sentences

- 请播放那首歌。(Qǐng bō fàng nà shǒu gē.) — Please play that song.

## Notes

播 means to sow seeds or broadcast. 直播 = live broadcast/streaming.
