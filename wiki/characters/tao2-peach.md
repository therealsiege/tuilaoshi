# 桃 Peach

- **Character**: 桃
- **Pinyin**: táo
- **Meaning**: peach
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 桃子 (táo zi) — peach
- 桃花 (táo huā) — peach blossom

## Example Sentences

- 春天桃花盛开。(Chūn tiān táo huā shèng kāi.) — Peach blossoms bloom in spring.

## Notes

桃 combines wood 木 and兆 (omen), representing the peach tree, symbol of longevity.
