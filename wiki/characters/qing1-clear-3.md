# 清 Clear, settle, pure

- **Character**: 清
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: clear, settle, pure
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 清楚 (qīngchu) — clear, distinct
- 清算 (qīngsuàn) — liquidate, settle

## Example Sentences

- 公司进行清算。(Gōngsī jìnxíng qīngsuàn.) — The company is undergoing liquidation.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: clear, pure, settle accounts.
