# 捣 To pound, to beat, to disturb

- **Character**: 捣
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: to pound, to beat, to disturb
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捣乱 (dǎo luàn) — to make trouble
- 捣蒜 (dǎo suàn) — to pound garlic

## Example Sentences

- 不要在课堂上捣乱。(Bùyào zài kètáng shàng dǎoluàn.) — Don't make trouble in class.

## Notes

Can mean physical pounding or causing disruption.
