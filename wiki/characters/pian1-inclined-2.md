# 偏 Biased

- **Character**: 偏
- **Pinyin**: piān
- **Meaning**: inclined to one side, biased
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偏见 (piān jiàn) — prejudice
- 偏偏 (piān piān) — contrary to expectation

## Example Sentences

- 不要对人有偏见。(Bú yào duì rén yǒu piān jiàn.) — Don't be prejudiced against people.

## Notes

偏 person 亻 plus 扁 (flat), leaning to one side.
