# 凡 Ordinary

- **Character**: 凡
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: ordinary, all, every
- **Radical**: 几 (table)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 凡是 (fán shì) — every, all
- 平凡 (píng fán) — ordinary

## Example Sentences

- 凡是参加的人都得到了礼物。(Fán shì cān jiā de rén dōu dé dào le lǐ wù.) — Everyone who participated received a gift.

## Notes

凡 originally represented a tray or plate, extended to mean commonplace or universal.
