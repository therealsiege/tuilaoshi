# 獗 Wild, violent, unrestrained

- **Character**: 獗
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: wild, violent, unrestrained
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 猖獗 (chāngjué) — rampant, unchecked
- 横行猖獗 (héngxíng chāngjué) — run wild

## Example Sentences

- 疾病猖獗流行。(Jíbìng chāngjué liúxíng.) — The disease is rampant.

## Notes

Literary term for wildness or unchecked spread, always used with 猖.
