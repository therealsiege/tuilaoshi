# 说 To Say

- **Character**: 说
- **Pinyin**: shuō
- **Meaning**: to say, to speak
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 说话 (shuō huà) — to speak
- 小说 (xiǎo shuō) — novel

## Example Sentences

- 请说慢一点。(Qǐng shuō màn yī diǎn.) — Please speak a little slower.

## Notes

说 uses the speech radical 讠. It can also be read as shuì meaning 'to persuade' in classical Chinese.
