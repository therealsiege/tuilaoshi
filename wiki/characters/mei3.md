# 每 Every

- **Character**: 每
- **Pinyin**: měi
- **Meaning**: every, each
- **Radical**: 母 (mother)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 每天 (měi tiān) — every day
- 每个 (měi gè) — every, each

## Example Sentences

- 我每天学中文。(Wǒ měi tiān xué zhōng wén.) — I study Chinese every day.

## Notes

每 combines 母 with a stroke on top. 每 + time word + 都 is a common pattern: 每天都去 (go every day).
