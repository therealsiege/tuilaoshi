# 恋 Love

- **Character**: 恋
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: love, romance
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恋爱 (liàn'ài) — be in love
- 初恋 (chūliàn) — first love

## Example Sentences

- 他们在恋爱。(Tāmen zài liàn'ài.) — They are in love.

## Notes

Romantic love. Heart radical. 恋爱 is common term for dating/being in love. Youth culture vocabulary.
