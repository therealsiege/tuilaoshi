# 漆 Paint

- **Character**: 漆
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: lacquer, paint
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 油漆 (yóu qī) — paint
- 喷漆 (pēn qī) — to spray paint

## Example Sentences

- 墙需要刷油漆。(Qiáng xū yào shuā yóu qī.) — The wall needs to be painted.

## Notes

漆 originally meant lacquer from漆 tree sap, now for all paints and finishes.
