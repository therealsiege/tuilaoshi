# 肉 Meat

- **Character**: 肉
- **Pinyin**: ròu
- **Meaning**: meat, flesh
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 猪肉 (zhū ròu) — pork
- 牛肉 (niú ròu) — beef

## Example Sentences

- 你吃不吃肉？(Nǐ chī bù chī ròu?) — Do you eat meat?

## Notes

肉 is a pictograph of a piece of meat. Without a qualifier, 肉 usually refers to pork in China.
