# 修 Repair, revise, cultivate

- **Character**: 修
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: repair, revise, cultivate
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — to revise, amend
- 修订 (xiū dìng) — to revise (documents)

## Example Sentences

- 委员会修改了宪法条款。(Wěi yuán huì xiū gǎi le xiàn fǎ tiáo kuǎn.) — The committee amended the constitutional provisions.

## Notes

Common in legal document revision. 修理 means to repair. 修养 means self-cultivation. 进修 means further study.
