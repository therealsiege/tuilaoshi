# 丙 Third

- **Character**: 丙
- **Pinyin**: bǐng
- **Meaning**: third (heavenly stem), grade C
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 丙方 (bǐng fāng) — Party C (in contracts)
- 丙等 (bǐng děng) — grade C

## Example Sentences

- 甲乙丙三方签约。(Jiǎ yǐ bǐng sān fāng qiān yuē.) — Three parties A, B, and C signed the contract.

## Notes

丙 is the third heavenly stem. Used for ordering: 甲 (A), 乙 (B), 丙 (C).
