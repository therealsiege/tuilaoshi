# 身 Body

- **Character**: 身
- **Pinyin**: shēn
- **Meaning**: body, oneself
- **Radical**: 身 (body)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 身体 (shēn tǐ) — body, health
- 身边 (shēn biān) — at one's side

## Example Sentences

- 身体健康最重要。(Shēn tǐ jiàn kāng zuì zhòng yào.) — Health is the most important thing.

## Notes

身 is a pictograph of a pregnant woman's body, representing the physical body.
