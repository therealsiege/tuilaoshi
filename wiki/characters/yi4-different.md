# 异 Different

- **Character**: 异
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: different, strange, unusual
- **Radical**: 廾 (two hands)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 差异 (chā yì) — difference
- 异常 (yì cháng) — abnormal

## Example Sentences

- 今天天气异常炎热。(Jīn tiān tiān qì yì cháng yán rè.) — The weather is unusually hot today.

## Notes

异 simplified from 異. Shows different 田 (field) held 廾 (hands), representing difference or异ness.
