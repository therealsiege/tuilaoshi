# 生 Life/Birth/Student

- **Character**: 生
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: life, birth; to be born, to grow; student; raw, uncooked
- **Radical**: 生 (life)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 学生 (xuéshēng) — student
- 生日 (shēngrì) — birthday
- 生活 (shēnghuó) — life, livelihood
- 先生 (xiānshēng) — Mr., sir, teacher
- 生气 (shēngqì) — angry, to get angry
- 出生 (chūshēng) — to be born

## Example Sentences

- 你的生日是几月几号？(Nǐ de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?) — When is your birthday?
- 他在北京出生。(Tā zài Běijīng chūshēng.) — He was born in Beijing.
- 别生气了。(Bié shēngqì le.) — Don't be angry anymore.

## Notes

生 depicts a plant sprouting from the ground, representing the emergence of life. It is both a radical and a standalone character with an unusually wide range of meanings: life, birth, student, raw/uncooked, unfamiliar. The meaning "student" comes from the idea of someone whose knowledge is still growing. 生 as "raw" contrasts with 熟 (shú, "cooked/familiar").
