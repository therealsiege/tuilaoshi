# 则 Rule, Then, 否则 Otherwise

- **Character**: 则
- **Pinyin**: zé
- **Meaning**: rule, then, 否则 otherwise
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 否则 (fǒu zé) — otherwise
- 规则 (guī zé) — rule, regulation
- 原则 (yuán zé) — principle

## Example Sentences

- 要遵守交通规则，否则会有危险。(Yào zūnshǒu jiāotōng guīzé, fǒuzé huì yǒu wēixiǎn.) — Follow traffic rules, otherwise it will be dangerous.

## Notes

Versatile character used both as conjunction (then, otherwise) and in formal compounds about rules and principles.
