# 洗 To wash, cleanse, develop (film)

- **Character**: 洗
- **Pinyin**: xǐ
- **Meaning**: to wash, cleanse, develop (film)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 洗澡 (xǐ zǎo) — to bathe
- 清洗 (qīng xǐ) — to wash, clean

## Example Sentences

- 我每天洗澡。(Wǒ měitiān xǐzǎo.) — I bathe every day.

## Notes

To wash or cleanse, very common character.
