# 懒 Lazy, 懶 (traditional)

- **Character**: 懒
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: lazy, 懶 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 懒惰 (lǎn duò) — lazy, idle
- 偷懒 (tōu lǎn) — to be lazy, slack off

## Example Sentences

- 他很懒，什么都不想做。(Tā hěn lǎn, shénme dōu bù xiǎng zuò.) — He is very lazy and doesn't want to do anything.

## Notes

Common character for laziness and lack of motivation.
