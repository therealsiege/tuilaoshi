# 龙 Dragon

- **Character**: 龙
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: dragon
- **Radical**: 龙 (dragon)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 龙年 (lóng nián) — Year of the Dragon
- 恐龙 (kǒng lóng) — dinosaur

## Example Sentences

- 龙是中国的象征。(Lóng shì zhōng guó de xiàng zhēng.) — The dragon is a symbol of China.

## Notes

龙 simplified from 龍. Dragons are auspicious in Chinese culture, unlike Western tradition.
