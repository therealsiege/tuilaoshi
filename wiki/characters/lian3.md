# 脸 Face

- **Character**: 脸
- **Pinyin**: liǎn
- **Meaning**: face
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 洗脸 (xǐ liǎn) — to wash one's face
- 脸色 (liǎn sè) — complexion, expression

## Example Sentences

- 她的脸很漂亮。(Tā de liǎn hěn piào liang.) — Her face is very pretty.

## Notes

脸 is the modern word for face. Also used in expressions like 丢脸 (to lose face), reflecting the cultural importance of face/reputation.
