# 模 Model

- **Character**: 模
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: model, pattern, to imitate (also mú)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 模仿 (mó fǎng) — to imitate
- 模型 (mó xíng) — model

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个飞机模型。(Zhè shì yī gè fēi jī mó xíng.) — This is an airplane model.

## Notes

模 as mó means model/imitate, as mú means mold. Wood radical 木 with 莫 (not/dusk), originally a wooden mold.
