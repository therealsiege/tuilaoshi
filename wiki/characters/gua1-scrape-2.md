# 刮 Scrape

- **Character**: 刮
- **Pinyin**: guā
- **Meaning**: scrape
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刮痧 (guā shā) — gua sha therapy
- 刮风 (guā fēng) — to blow (wind)
- 刮胡子 (guā hú zi) — to shave

## Example Sentences

- 刮痧可以促进血液循环。(Guā shā kě yǐ cù jìn xiě yè xún huán.) — Gua sha can promote blood circulation.

## Notes

Scraping technique; TCM skin therapy.
