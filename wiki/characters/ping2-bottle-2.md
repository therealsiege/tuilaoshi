# 瓶 Bottle

- **Character**: 瓶
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: bottle
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 瓶子 (píngzi) — bottle
- 水瓶 (shuǐpíng) — water bottle

## Example Sentences

- 她买了一瓶水。(Tā mǎi le yī píng shuǐ.) — She bought a bottle of water.

## Notes

Common container word. Also used as measure word: 一瓶啤酒 (a bottle of beer).
