# 瘫 Paralysis

- **Character**: 瘫
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: paralysis
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘫痪 (tān huàn) — paralysis
- 截瘫 (jié tān) — paraplegia
- 偏瘫 (piān tān) — hemiplegia

## Example Sentences

- 中风可能导致偏瘫。(Zhōng fēng kě néng dǎo zhì piān tān.) — Stroke may lead to hemiplegia.

## Notes

Loss of motor function; serious neurological condition.
