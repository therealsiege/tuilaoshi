# 芯 Core

- **Character**: 芯
- **Pinyin**: xīn
- **Meaning**: core, chip, wick
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 芯片 (xīn piàn) — chip
- 笔芯 (bǐ xīn) — pen refill

## Example Sentences

- 这款手机使用最新的芯片。(Zhè kuǎn shǒu jī shǐ yòng zuì xīn de xīn piàn.) — This phone uses the latest chip.

## Notes

芯 grass 艹 plus 心 (heart), central core.
