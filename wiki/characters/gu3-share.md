# 股 Stock

- **Character**: 股
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: share, stock, thigh
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 股票 (gǔ piào) — stock
- 股东 (gǔ dōng) — shareholder

## Example Sentences

- 他买了很多股票。(Tā mǎi le hěn duō gǔ piào.) — He bought a lot of stocks.

## Notes

Original meaning was thigh; extended to shares/portions.
