# 如 If

- **Character**: 如
- **Pinyin**: rú
- **Meaning**: if, like, as
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 如果 (rú guǒ) — if
- 比如 (bǐ rú) — for example

## Example Sentences

- 如果下雨，我就不去了。(Rú guǒ xià yǔ, wǒ jiù bù qù le.) — If it rains, I won't go.

## Notes

如果...就... is the standard if-then construction. 比如 introduces examples, similar to 'for instance.'
