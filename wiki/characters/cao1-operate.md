# 操 To Operate

- **Character**: 操
- **Pinyin**: cāo
- **Meaning**: to operate, exercise, to grasp
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 操作 (cāo zuò) — to operate
- 做操 (zuò cāo) — to do exercises

## Example Sentences

- 请按照说明操作。(Qǐng àn zhào shuō míng cāo zuò.) — Please operate according to the instructions.

## Notes

操 means to operate or handle. 操场 = playground/sports field. 操心 = to worry.
