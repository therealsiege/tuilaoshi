# 峭 Steep, harsh

- **Character**: 峭
- **Pinyin**: qiào
- **Meaning**: steep, harsh
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陡峭 (dǒuqiào) — steep, precipitous
- 峭壁 (qiàobì) — cliff, precipice

## Example Sentences

- 悬崖峭壁令人胆寒。(Xuányá qiàobì lìng rén dǎn hán.) — The steep cliffs are terrifying.

## Notes

Literary term for steepness, often of cliffs and mountains.
