# 菜 Dish

- **Character**: 菜
- **Pinyin**: cài
- **Meaning**: vegetable, dish, cuisine
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 中国菜 (zhōng guó cài) — Chinese food
- 蔬菜 (shū cài) — vegetables

## Example Sentences

- 你喜欢什么菜？(Nǐ xǐ huān shén me cài?) — What dishes do you like?

## Notes

菜 means both 'vegetable' and 'dish/cuisine.' 点菜 means 'to order food' at a restaurant.
