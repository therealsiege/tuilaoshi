# 粘 Sticky, to stick, to paste

- **Character**: 粘
- **Pinyin**: nián
- **Meaning**: sticky, to stick, to paste
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粘贴 (nián tiē) — to paste, stick
- 粘性 (nián xìng) — viscosity, stickiness

## Example Sentences

- 胶水很粘。(Jiāoshuǐ hěn nián.) — The glue is very sticky.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: nián (sticky), zhān (to glue/stick).
