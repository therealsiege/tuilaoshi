# 泼 To Splash

- **Character**: 泼
- **Pinyin**: pō
- **Meaning**: to splash, to spill, vigorous
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泼水 (pō shuǐ) — to splash water
- 活泼 (huó pō) — lively

## Example Sentences

- 他把水泼在地上。(Tā bǎ shuǐ pō zài dì shàng.) — He splashed the water on the ground.

## Notes

泼 means to splash or pour. Also means vigorous or lively (活泼). Used in泼水节 (Water Splashing Festival).
