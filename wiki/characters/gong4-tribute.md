# 贡 Tribute

- **Character**: 贡
- **Pinyin**: gòng
- **Meaning**: tribute, to contribute
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 贡献 (gòng xiàn) — to contribute, contribution
- 进贡 (jìn gòng) — to pay tribute

## Example Sentences

- 他为社会做出了巨大贡献。(Tā wèi shèhuì zuòchū le jùdà gòngxiàn.) — He made great contributions to society.

## Notes

贡 combines work 工 with shell/money 贝, representing tribute or contributions offered to superiors.
