# 让 To Let

- **Character**: 让
- **Pinyin**: ràng
- **Meaning**: to let, to allow, to make, to yield
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 让步 (ràng bù) — to give way, to yield
- 让开 (ràng kāi) — to step aside

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈让我去买东西。(Mā ma ràng wǒ qù mǎi dōng xi.) — Mom asked me to go shopping.

## Notes

让 forms causative sentences (making someone do something). Can also indicate passive voice in colloquial speech.
