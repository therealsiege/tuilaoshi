# 修 Fix, repair

- **Character**: 修
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: fix, repair
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 修复 (xiū fù) — repair, fix
- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — modify

## Example Sentences

- 我已经修复了这个漏洞。(Wǒ yǐ jīng xiū fù le zhè gè lòu dòng.) — I have fixed this bug.

## Notes

Used in 修复 (fix), 修改 (modify), 维修 (maintain), critical for debugging
