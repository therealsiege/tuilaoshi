# 组 Group

- **Character**: 组
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to organize, group, team, to form
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 组织 (zǔ zhī) — organization
- 小组 (xiǎo zǔ) — group, team

## Example Sentences

- 我们成立了一个小组。(Wǒ men chéng lì le yī ge xiǎo zǔ.) — We formed a small group.

## Notes

组 shows silk threads organized together, representing groups or组ganizations. Measure word for sets/groups.
