# 垮 To Collapse

- **Character**: 垮
- **Pinyin**: kuǎ
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to break down
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 垮台 (kuǎ tái) — to collapse, to fall
- 拖垮 (tuō kuǎ) — to drag down

## Example Sentences

- 他的身体被累垮了。(Tā de shēn tǐ bèi lèi kuǎ le.) — His body broke down from exhaustion.

## Notes

垮 combines earth 土 and 夸 (boast), representing structure or system falling apart.
