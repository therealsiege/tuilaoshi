# 忡 Anxious, worried

- **Character**: 忡
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: anxious, worried
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忧心忡忡 (yōuxīn chōngchōng) — deeply worried, anxious
- 忡忡 (chōngchōng) — worried, anxious

## Example Sentences

- 他忧心忡忡地等待消息。(Tā yōuxīn chōngchōng de děngdài xiāoxi.) — He waited anxiously for news.

## Notes

Literary term for anxiety, typically used in the phrase 忧心忡忡.
