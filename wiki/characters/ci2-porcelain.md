# 瓷 Porcelain

- **Character**: 瓷
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: porcelain, china
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陶瓷 (táo cí) — ceramics
- 瓷器 (cí qì) — porcelain

## Example Sentences

- 这个瓷器很精美。(Zhè gè cí qì hěn jīng měi.) — This porcelain is very exquisite.

## Notes

瓷 combines次 (next) and tile 瓦, representing fine ceramic ware.
