# 灸 Moxibustion

- **Character**: 灸
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: moxibustion
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 针灸 (zhēn jiǔ) — acupuncture and moxibustion
- 艾灸 (ài jiǔ) — moxibustion

## Example Sentences

- 针灸是传统中医疗法。(Zhēn jiǔ shì chuán tǒng zhōng yī liáo fǎ.) — Acupuncture and moxibustion are traditional Chinese medicine therapies.

## Notes

Heat therapy; burning herbs on acupoints.
