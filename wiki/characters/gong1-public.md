# 公 Public, official, just

- **Character**: 公
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: public, official, just
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 公司 (gōngsī) — company
- 公诉 (gōngsù) — public prosecution

## Example Sentences

- 检察机关提起公诉。(Jiǎnchá jīguān tíqǐ gōngsù.) — The procuratorate initiated public prosecution.

## Notes

Very common character for public, official, and fair.
