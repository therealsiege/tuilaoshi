# 疫 Epidemic, pestilence

- **Character**: 疫
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: epidemic, pestilence
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 疫情 (yì qíng) — epidemic situation
- 防疫 (fáng yì) — epidemic prevention

## Example Sentences

- 政府采取了严格的防疫措施。(Zhèngfǔ cǎiqǔ le yángé de fángyì cuòshī.) — The government has taken strict epidemic prevention measures.

## Notes

Epidemic disease requiring public health response. Modern usage emphasizes prevention and control measures.
