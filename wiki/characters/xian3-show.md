# 显 Show, obvious, manifest

- **Character**: 显
- **Pinyin**: xiǎn
- **Meaning**: show, obvious, manifest
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 显示 (xiǎnshì) — show, display
- 明显 (míngxiǎn) — obvious, clear

## Example Sentences

- 结果很明显。(Jiéguǒ hěn míngxiǎn.) — The result is very obvious.

## Notes

Common character for showing, displaying, or being obvious.
