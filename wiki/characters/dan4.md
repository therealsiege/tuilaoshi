# 蛋 Egg

- **Character**: 蛋
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: egg
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 鸡蛋 (jī dàn) — egg
- 蛋糕 (dàn gāo) — cake

## Example Sentences

- 蛋糕很好吃。(Dàn gāo hěn hǎo chī.) — The cake is delicious.

## Notes

蛋 uses the insect radical 虫 because in ancient classification, eggs were associated with creatures. 蛋糕 means cake.
