# 薄 Thin

- **Character**: 薄
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: thin, slight, meager
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 厚积薄发 (hòu jī bó fā) — build up before breaking through
- 薄弱 (bó ruò) — weak, frail

## Example Sentences

- 冰很薄，不能在上面走。(Bīng hěn báo, bù néng zài shàngmiàn zǒu.) — The ice is thin; you can't walk on it.

## Notes

In 厚积薄发, 薄 means thinly or gradually; also read báo for physical thinness.
