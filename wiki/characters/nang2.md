# 囊 Capsule

- **Character**: 囊
- **Pinyin**: náng
- **Meaning**: sac, bag
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 22

## Compounds

- 胶囊 (jiāo náng) — capsule
- 胆囊 (dǎn náng) — gallbladder
- 囊肿 (náng zhǒng) — cyst

## Example Sentences

- 每日服用一粒胶囊。(Měi rì fú yòng yī lì jiāo náng.) — Take one capsule daily.

## Notes

Sac or pouch; medication or anatomical.
