# 跌 Fall, Drop, 下跌 Fall

- **Character**: 跌
- **Pinyin**: diē
- **Meaning**: fall, drop, 下跌 fall
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 下跌 (xià diē) — fall, drop
- 跌倒 (diē dǎo) — fall down
- 暴跌 (bào diē) — plummet

## Example Sentences

- 股票价格大幅下跌。(Gǔpiào jiàgé dà fú xiàdiē.) — Stock prices fell sharply.

## Notes

Used for both physical falling and price/value drops. Essential economic term alongside 涨 (rise) for market movements.
