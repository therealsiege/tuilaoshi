# 代 Generation

- **Character**: 代
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: generation, era, to replace
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 时代 (shí dài) — era, age
- 代表 (dài biǎo) — to represent

## Example Sentences

- 我们生活在一个新时代。(Wǒ men shēng huó zài yī gè xīn shí dài.) — We live in a new era.

## Notes

代 shows person 亻 and weapon 弋, representing one generation replacing another, like changing guards.
