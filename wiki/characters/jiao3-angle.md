# 角 Angle, corner, horn

- **Character**: 角
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: angle, corner, horn
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 角度 (jiǎo dù) — angle
- 直角 (zhí jiǎo) — right angle

## Example Sentences

- 这个角是九十度。(Zhè ge jiǎo shì jiǔ shí dù.) — This angle is ninety degrees.

## Notes

Geometric angle measurement
