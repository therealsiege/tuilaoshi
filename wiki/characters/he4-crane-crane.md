# 鹤 Crane

- **Character**: 鹤
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: crane (bird)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 仙鹤 (xiān hè) — crane
- 鹤立鸡群 (hè lì jī qún) — to stand out from the crowd

## Example Sentences

- 白鹤是长寿的象征。(Bái hè shì cháng shòu de xiàng zhēng.) — The white crane symbolizes longevity.

## Notes

鹤 simplified from 鶴. Bird 鸟 with 隺 phonetic, the elegant crane.
