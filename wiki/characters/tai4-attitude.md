# 态 Attitude

- **Character**: 态
- **Pinyin**: tài
- **Meaning**: attitude, manner, appearance, state
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 态度 (tài dù) — attitude
- 状态 (zhuàng tài) — state, condition

## Example Sentences

- 你的态度很重要。(Nǐ de tài dù hěn zhòng yào.) — Your attitude is very important.

## Notes

态 combines heart with能 (ability), representing态titude or state of mind. Essential for discussing attitudes and conditions.
