# 饭 Meal

- **Character**: 饭
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: cooked rice, meal
- **Radical**: 食 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 米饭 (mǐ fàn) — cooked rice
- 吃饭 (chī fàn) — to eat

## Example Sentences

- 你吃饭了吗？(Nǐ chī fàn le ma?) — Have you eaten?

## Notes

饭 means cooked rice or meal. 午饭 = lunch. 晚饭 = dinner.
