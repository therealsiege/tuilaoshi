# 系 — System / Department

- **Character**: 系
- **Pinyin**: xì (system/department) / jì (to tie, to fasten)
- **Meaning**: system, department; to tie, to connect
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 关系 (guānxì) — relationship, connection
- 系统 (xìtǒng) — system
- 联系 (liánxì) — to contact, connection
- 中文系 (Zhōngwén xì) — Chinese department (at a university)
- 体系 (tǐxì) — system, framework
- 系列 (xìliè) — series

## Example Sentences

- 你是哪个系的学生？(Nǐ shì nǎ ge xì de xuésheng?) — Which department are you a student in?
- 请跟我联系。(Qǐng gēn wǒ liánxì.) — Please contact me.
- 这个系统很复杂。(Zhè ge xìtǒng hěn fùzá.) — This system is very complex.

## Notes

系 derives from the silk radical 糸, reflecting the original meaning of "to tie, to bind" (threads of silk being tied together). From binding comes "connection" (关系, 联系) and from an organized set of connections comes "system" (系统, 体系). In academic contexts, 系 means "department" — 中文系, 历史系 (history department), 数学系 (math department). This is a common topic in Integrated Chinese Level 2, where students discuss their majors and departments. Note the alternate reading jì for the literal meaning "to tie/fasten," though xì is far more common in modern usage.
