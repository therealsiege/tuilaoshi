# 饼 Cake

- **Character**: 饼
- **Pinyin**: bǐng
- **Meaning**: cake, biscuit, flat bread
- **Radical**: 饣(food)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 月饼 (yuè bǐng) — mooncake
- 饼干 (bǐng gān) — cracker, biscuit

## Example Sentences

- 中秋节要吃月饼。(Zhōng qiū jié yào chī yuè bǐng.) — You eat mooncakes during Mid-Autumn Festival.

## Notes

饼 means flat bread or cake. 月饼 = mooncake (eaten during Mid-Autumn Festival).
