# 快 Fast

- **Character**: 快
- **Pinyin**: kuài
- **Meaning**: fast, quick, happy
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 快乐 (kuài lè) — happy
- 加快 (jiā kuài) — to speed up

## Example Sentences

- 时间过得很快。(Shí jiān guò de hěn kuài.) — Time passes very quickly.

## Notes

快 shows heart 心 with 夬 (decisive), representing a heart that acts quickly and decisively, hence fast and cheerful.
