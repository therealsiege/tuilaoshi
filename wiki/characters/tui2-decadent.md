# 颓 Decadent, ruined, dispirited

- **Character**: 颓
- **Pinyin**: tuí
- **Meaning**: decadent, ruined, dispirited
- **Radical**: 页 (head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 颓废 (tuífèi) — decadent, dispirited
- 颓丧 (tuísàng) — dejected, dispirited

## Example Sentences

- 他情绪颓丧。(Tā qíngxù tuísàng.) — His spirits are dejected.

## Notes

Describes collapse, ruin, or dispirited mental state.
