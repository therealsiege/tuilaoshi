# 容 Contain, tolerate, appearance

- **Character**: 容
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: contain, tolerate, appearance
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 容纳 (róng nà) — to contain, accommodate
- 宽容 (kuān róng) — tolerant

## Example Sentences

- 这个房间可以容纳50人。(Zhè ge fáng jiān kě yǐ róng nà wǔ shí rén.) — This room can accommodate 50 people.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: to contain, tolerate, or appearance. 容易 means easy. 容貌 means appearance.
