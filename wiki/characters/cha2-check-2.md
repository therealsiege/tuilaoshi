# 查 Check, search

- **Character**: 查
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: check, search
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 检查 (jiǎn chá) — check
- 查询 (chá xún) — query, search

## Example Sentences

- 请查询数据库。(Qǐng chá xún shù jù kù.) — Please query the database.

## Notes

Essential for 检查 (check), 查询 (query), 排查 (troubleshoot)
