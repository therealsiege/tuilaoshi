# 阅 To read, review, 閱 (traditional)

- **Character**: 阅
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: to read, review, 閱 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 阅读 (yuè dú) — to read
- 阅览 (yuè lǎn) — to read, peruse

## Example Sentences

- 我每天阅读一小时。(Wǒ měitiān yuèdú yī xiǎoshí.) — I read for an hour every day.

## Notes

Formal term for reading, often used in compounds for reading and reviewing.
