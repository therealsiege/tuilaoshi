# 悯 To pity, to sympathize

- **Character**: 悯
- **Pinyin**: mǐn
- **Meaning**: to pity, to sympathize
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 怜悯 (lián mǐn) — to pity, compassion
- 悲悯 (bēi mǐn) — to feel compassion for

## Example Sentences

- 他对穷人怀有悲悯之心。(Tā duì qióngrén huáiyǒu bēimǐn zhī xīn.) — He has compassion for the poor.

## Notes

Similar to 怜, often used together.
