# 微 Micro, tiny, minute

- **Character**: 微
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: micro, tiny, minute
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 微分 (wēi fēn) — differential, differentiation
- 微小 (wēi xiǎo) — tiny, minute

## Example Sentences

- 微积分是高等数学的基础。(Wēi jī fēn shì gāo děng shù xué de jī chǔ.) — Calculus is the foundation of advanced mathematics.

## Notes

Infinitesimal quantities
