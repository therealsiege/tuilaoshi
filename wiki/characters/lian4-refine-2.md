# 炼 To Refine

- **Character**: 炼
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to refine, to smelt
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — to exercise
- 炼钢 (liàn gāng) — to smelt steel

## Example Sentences

- 他每天早上锻炼身体。(Tā měi tiān zǎo shàng duàn liàn shēn tǐ.) — He exercises every morning.

## Notes

炼 simplified from 煉. Fire radical 火 representing purification through heat.
