# 砧 Anvil

- **Character**: 砧
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: anvil, chopping block
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 砧板 (zhēn bǎn) — cutting board
- 铁砧 (tiě zhēn) — anvil

## Example Sentences

- 铁匠把烧红的铁放在砧上锤打。(Tiějiàng bǎ shāohóng de tiě fàng zài zhēn shàng chuídǎ.) — The blacksmith placed the red-hot iron on the anvil and hammered it.

## Notes

砧 is an anvil for metalworking or a chopping block for food. The stone radical reflects its hard surface.
