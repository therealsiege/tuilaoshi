# 愤 Angry, indignant, 憤 (traditional)

- **Character**: 愤
- **Pinyin**: fèn
- **Meaning**: angry, indignant, 憤 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 愤怒 (fèn nù) — angry, indignant
- 气愤 (qì fèn) — indignant, furious

## Example Sentences

- 他对这种不公正现象感到愤怒。(Tā duì zhè zhǒng bù gōngzhèng xiànxiàng gǎndào fènnù.) — He feels angry about this injustice.

## Notes

Strong emotion of anger, often righteous indignation.
