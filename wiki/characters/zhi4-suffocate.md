# 窒 To Suffocate

- **Character**: 窒
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: to obstruct, to suffocate
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 窒息 (zhì xī) — to suffocate
- 窒碍 (zhì ài) — to obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 烟雾让人窒息。(Yān wù ràng rén zhì xī.) — The smoke was suffocating.

## Notes

窒 has cave 穴 with 至 (to arrive at), blocked passage.
