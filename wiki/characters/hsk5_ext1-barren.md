# 瘠 Barren, Thin, 贫瘠 Barren

- **Character**: 瘠
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: barren, thin, 贫瘠 barren
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 贫瘠 (pín jí) — barren, infertile
- 瘠薄 (jí bó) — poor (of land)
- 瘠土 (jí tǔ) — barren land

## Example Sentences

- 这片土地比较贫瘠。(Zhè piàn tǔdì bǐjiào pínjí.) — This land is rather barren.

## Notes

Agricultural term for infertile or barren land. Opposite of 肥沃 (fertile). Used to describe poor agricultural conditions.
