# 终 End, final

- **Character**: 终
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: end, final
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 终止 (zhōng zhǐ) — terminate, end
- 年终 (nián zhōng) — year-end

## Example Sentences

- 合同即将终止。(Hé tong jí jiāng zhōng zhǐ.) — The contract will terminate soon.

## Notes

终结 means end/conclude. 终身 means lifelong.
