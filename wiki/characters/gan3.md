# 感 Feel, sense, touch

- **Character**: 感
- **Pinyin**: gǎn
- **Meaning**: feel, sense, touch
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 感染 (gǎn rǎn) — infect, infection
- 敏感 (mǐn gǎn) — sensitive

## Example Sentences

- 伤口容易感染。(Shāng kǒu róng yì gǎn rǎn.) — Wounds are easily infected.

Used for感 and infection
