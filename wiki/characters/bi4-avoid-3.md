# 避 Avoid

- **Character**: 避
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to avoid, evade
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 躲避 (duǒ bì) — to avoid, evade
- 闪避 (shǎn bì) — to dodge

## Example Sentences

- 他敏捷地闪避开来。(Tā mǐnjié de shǎnbì kāilái.) — He nimbly dodged away.

## Notes

Avoidance; evasive tactics
