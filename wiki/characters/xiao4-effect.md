# 效 Effect

- **Character**: 效
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: effect, efficacy, to imitate
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 效果 (xiào guǒ) — effect, result
- 有效 (yǒu xiào) — effective

## Example Sentences

- 这个方法很有效。(Zhè ge fāng fǎ hěn yǒu xiào.) — This method is very effective.

## Notes

效 represents effect or efficacy, from the idea of achieving results. Key for discussing effectiveness.
