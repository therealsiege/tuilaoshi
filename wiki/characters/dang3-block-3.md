# 挡 To block, resist, gear, 擋 (traditional)

- **Character**: 挡
- **Pinyin**: dǎng
- **Meaning**: to block, resist, gear, 擋 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阻挡 (zǔ dǎng) — to stop, obstruct
- 挡住 (dǎng zhù) — to block, stop

## Example Sentences

- 别挡住我的视线。(Bié dǎngzhù wǒ de shìxiàn.) — Don't block my view.

## Notes

To block or resist, also gear in vehicles.
