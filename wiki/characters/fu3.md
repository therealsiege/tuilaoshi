# 腑 Organs

- **Character**: 腑
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: internal organs
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 肺腑 (fèi fǔ) — bottom of one's heart
- 腑脏 (fǔ zàng) — internal organs

## Example Sentences

- 这是我的肺腑之言。(Zhè shì wǒ de fèi fǔ zhī yán.) — These are words from the bottom of my heart.

## Notes

腑 refers to hollow organs in Traditional Chinese Medicine, as opposed to 脏 (solid organs).
