# 潜 Dive

- **Character**: 潜
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: to dive, submerge
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潜水 (qián shuǐ) — to dive, scuba diving
- 潜伏 (qián fú) — to hide, lurk

## Example Sentences

- 他喜欢潜水探索海底。(Tā xǐhuan qiánshuǐ tànsuǒ hǎidǐ.) — He likes scuba diving to explore the ocean floor.

## Notes

Diving underwater; scuba and free diving
