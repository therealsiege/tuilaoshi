# 回 To Return

- **Character**: 回
- **Pinyin**: huí
- **Meaning**: to return, to go back
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 回来 (huí lái) — to come back
- 回去 (huí qù) — to go back

## Example Sentences

- 我想回家。(Wǒ xiǎng huí jiā.) — I want to go home.

## Notes

回 is a pictograph of a whirlpool — going around and returning to the starting point.
