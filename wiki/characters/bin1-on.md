# 濒 On the Verge

- **Character**: 濒
- **Pinyin**: bīn
- **Meaning**: on the verge of, close to
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 濒临 (bīn lín) — on the verge of
- 濒危 (bīn wēi) — endangered

## Example Sentences

- 这种动物濒临灭绝。(Zhè zhǒng dòng wù bīn lín miè jué.) — This species is on the verge of extinction.

## Notes

濒 means on the brink of. 濒危 = endangered (species).
