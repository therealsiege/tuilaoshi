# 趴 To Lie Prone

- **Character**: 趴
- **Pinyin**: pā
- **Meaning**: to lie on one's stomach, to lean on
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 趴下 (pā xià) — to lie down (on stomach)
- 趴着 (pā zhe) — lying prone

## Example Sentences

- 他趴在桌子上睡着了。(Tā pā zài zhuō zi shàng shuì zháo le.) — He fell asleep lying on the desk.

## Notes

趴 shows foot 足 and八 (eight/spread), representing the body spread out face-down in a prone position.
