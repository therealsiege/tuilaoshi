# 缉 To Seize

- **Character**: 缉
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: to seize, to arrest
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缉拿 (jī ná) — to arrest
- 通缉 (tōng jī) — to put on wanted list

## Example Sentences

- 警方通缉这名逃犯。(Jǐng fāng tōng jī zhè míng táo fàn.) — The police put this fugitive on the wanted list.

## Notes

缉 originally meant to sew together (silk radical 纟), extended to mean piecing together clues and arresting.
