# 等 To Wait

- **Character**: 等
- **Pinyin**: děng
- **Meaning**: to wait, class, grade, etc.
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 等等 (děng děng) — wait a moment; and so on
- 等待 (děng dài) — to wait for

## Example Sentences

- 请等一下。(Qǐng děng yī xià.) — Please wait a moment.

## Notes

等 after list items means 'etc.' 等一下 and 等等 both mean 'wait a moment.'
