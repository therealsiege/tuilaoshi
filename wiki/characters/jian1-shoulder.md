# 肩 Shoulder

- **Character**: 肩
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: shoulder
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肩膀 (jiān bǎng) — shoulder
- 并肩 (bìng jiān) — side by side, shoulder to shoulder

## Example Sentences

- 他拍了拍我的肩膀。(Tā pāi le pāi wǒ de jiān bǎng.) — He patted my shoulder.

## Notes

肩 appears as 肩膀 in modern usage. 肩负 means to shoulder responsibility.
