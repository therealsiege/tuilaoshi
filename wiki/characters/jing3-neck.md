# 颈 Neck

- **Character**: 颈
- **Pinyin**: jǐng
- **Meaning**: neck
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 颈部 (jǐng bù) — neck area
- 颈椎 (jǐng zhuī) — cervical vertebra
- 脖颈 (bó jǐng) — neck

## Example Sentences

- 长期低头会导致颈椎病。(Cháng qī dī tóu huì dǎo zhì jǐng zhuī bìng.) — Long-term bowing of the head can lead to cervical spondylosis.

## Notes

Connects head to body; frequently appears in orthopedic contexts.
