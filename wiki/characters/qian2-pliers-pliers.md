# 钳 Pliers

- **Character**: 钳
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: pliers, pincers, to clamp
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 钳子 (qián zi) — pliers
- 钳制 (qián zhì) — to restrain

## Example Sentences

- 用钳子拔出钉子。(Yòng qián zi bá chū dīng zi.) — Use pliers to pull out the nail.

## Notes

钳 simplified from 鉗. Metal 钅 with 甘 (sweet), gripping tool.
