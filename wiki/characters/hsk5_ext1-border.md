# 疆 Border, Boundary, 新疆 Xinjiang

- **Character**: 疆
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: border, boundary, 新疆 Xinjiang
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 边疆 (biān jiāng) — border, frontier
- 疆域 (jiāng yù) — territory
- 新疆 (Xīn jiāng) — Xinjiang

## Example Sentences

- 军队保卫边疆地区。(Jūnduì bǎowèi biānjiāng dìqū.) — The army defends the border regions.

## Notes

Formal term for national borders and frontiers. Most commonly seen in 新疆 (Xinjiang) and 边疆 (frontier/border).
