# 那 That

- **Character**: 那
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: that
- **Radical**: 阝 (city)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 那个 (nà ge) — that one
- 那里 (nàlǐ) — there, that place
- 那些 (nàxiē) — those
- 那样 (nàyàng) — that way, like that
- 那儿 (nàr) — there (colloquial, northern)

## Example Sentences

- 那是谁？(Nà shì shéi?) — Who is that?
- 那个人是我的老师。(Nà ge rén shì wǒ de lǎoshī.) — That person is my teacher.
- 你去过那里吗？(Nǐ qù guò nàlǐ ma?) — Have you been there?

## Notes

那 pairs with 这 (zhè, "this") as the demonstrative pair. In casual speech it is often pronounced nèi before measure words. 那 can also function as a conjunction meaning "then, in that case": 那我们走吧 (nà wǒmen zǒu ba, "then let's go"). Like 这, it requires a measure word before a noun: 那本书 (nà běn shū), not *那书.
