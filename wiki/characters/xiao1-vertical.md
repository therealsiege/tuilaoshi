# 箫 Vertical bamboo flute

- **Character**: 箫
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: vertical bamboo flute
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 洞箫 (dòng xiāo) — vertical bamboo flute
- 箫声 (xiāo shēng) — flute sound

## Example Sentences

- 远处传来悠扬的箫声。(Yuǎn chù chuán lái yōu yáng de xiāo shēng.) — The melodious sound of the vertical flute came from afar.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese bamboo flute played vertically.
