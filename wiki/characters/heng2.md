# 衡 Weigh

- **Character**: 衡
- **Pinyin**: héng
- **Meaning**: to weigh, balance
- **Radical**: 行 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 平衡 (píng héng) — balance
- 均衡 (jūn héng) — balanced

## Example Sentences

- 保持身体平衡很重要。(Bǎochí shēntǐ pínghéng hěn zhòngyào.) — Maintaining body balance is very important.

## Notes

Balance; equilibrium in movement
