# 刺 To Stab

- **Character**: 刺
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: to stab, to pierce
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刺杀 (cì shā) — to assassinate
- 刺客 (cì kè) — assassin

## Example Sentences

- 他用剑刺向敌人。(Tā yòng jiàn cì xiàng dí rén.) — He stabbed at the enemy with a sword.

## Notes

刺 has 朿 (thorn) with knife 刂, piercing with a sharp point.
