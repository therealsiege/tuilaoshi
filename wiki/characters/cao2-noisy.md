# 嘈 Noisy, confused noise

- **Character**: 嘈
- **Pinyin**: cáo
- **Meaning**: noisy, confused noise
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 嘈杂 (cáo zá) — noisy, confused
- 嘈嘈 (cáo cáo) — clamorous

## Example Sentences

- 市场里很嘈杂。(Shì chǎng lǐ hěn cáo zá.) — The market is very noisy.
