# 绿 Green

- **Character**: 绿
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: green
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绿色 (lǜ sè) — green color
- 绿茶 (lǜ chá) — green tea

## Example Sentences

- 春天树叶都是绿色的。(Chūn tiān shù yè dōu shì lǜ sè de.) — In spring the leaves are all green.

## Notes

绿 uses silk radical, from green-dyed silk. Note pinyin is lǜ with ü umlaut.
