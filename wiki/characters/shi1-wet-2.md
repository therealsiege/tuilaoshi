# 湿 Wet

- **Character**: 湿
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: wet, moist, damp
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 潮湿 (cháo shī) — damp
- 湿度 (shī dù) — humidity

## Example Sentences

- 地面很湿。(Dì miàn hěn shī.) — The ground is very wet.

## Notes

湿 simplified from 濕. Water 氵 permeating, moisture.
