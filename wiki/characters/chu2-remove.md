# 除 To Remove

- **Character**: 除
- **Pinyin**: chú
- **Meaning**: to remove, except, to divide
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 除了 (chú le) — except, besides
- 除非 (chú fēi) — unless

## Example Sentences

- 除了他，大家都来了。(Chú le tā, dà jiā dōu lái le.) — Everyone came except him.

## Notes

除了...以外 means 'except for' or 'besides' depending on context.
