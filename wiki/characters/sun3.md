# 损 To Damage

- **Character**: 损
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: to damage, to lose
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 损失 (sǔn shī) — loss
- 损害 (sǔn hài) — to harm

## Example Sentences

- 地震造成了巨大的损失。(Dì zhèn zào chéng le jù dà de sǔn shī.) — The earthquake caused huge losses.

## Notes

损 simplified from 損. Shows hand 扌 and 员, representing reduction or diminishment through action.
