# 喝 To Drink

- **Character**: 喝
- **Pinyin**: hē
- **Meaning**: to drink
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 喝水 (hē shuǐ) — to drink water
- 喝茶 (hē chá) — to drink tea

## Example Sentences

- 你想喝什么？(Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?) — What would you like to drink?

## Notes

喝 uses the mouth radical 口. It can also be read as hè meaning 'to shout.'
