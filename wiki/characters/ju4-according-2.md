# 据 According To

- **Character**: 据
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: according to, basis, to occupy
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 数据 (shù jù) — data
- 根据 (gēn jù) — according to, basis

## Example Sentences

- 根据最新数据分析。(Gēn jù zuì xīn shù jù fēn xī.) — According to the latest data analysis.

## Notes

据 with hand radical suggests grasping evidence, essential in data and basis terminology.
