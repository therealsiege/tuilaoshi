# 域 Domain

- **Character**: 域
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: domain, field, region
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 领域 (lǐng yù) — field, domain
- 区域 (qū yù) — region, area

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个新的领域。(Zhè shì yī ge xīn de lǐng yù.) — This is a new field/domain.

## Notes

域 represents a defined area or domain. Common in academic and technical contexts for fields of study or regions.
