# 肾 Kidney

- **Character**: 肾
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: kidney
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肾脏 (shèn zàng) — kidney (organ)
- 肾炎 (shèn yán) — nephritis
- 肾结石 (shèn jié shí) — kidney stone

## Example Sentences

- 他需要做肾移植手术。(Tā xū yào zuò shèn yí zhí shǒu shù.) — He needs a kidney transplant surgery.

## Notes

Filters blood and produces urine; crucial in TCM kidney essence theory.
