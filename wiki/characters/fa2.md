# 伐 To Cut Down

- **Character**: 伐
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: to cut down, attack
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 砍伐 (kǎn fá) — to cut down trees
- 讨伐 (tǎo fá) — to send a punitive expedition

## Example Sentences

- 禁止砍伐树木。(Jìnzhǐ kǎnfá shùmù.) — Cutting down trees is prohibited.

## Notes

伐 shows a person with a weapon (戈), representing the action of attacking or chopping.
