# 曜 Radiance

- **Character**: 曜
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: bright, glorious, daylight
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 七曜 (qī yào) — seven luminaries
- 日曜日 (rì yào rì) — Sunday (Japanese)

## Example Sentences

- 太阳放射光曜。(Tài yáng fàng shè guāng yào.) — The sun radiates brilliance.

## Notes

曜 has sun 日 with 翟 (pheasant feathers), radiant sunlight.
