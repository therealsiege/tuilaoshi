# 航 To Navigate

- **Character**: 航
- **Pinyin**: háng
- **Meaning**: to navigate, to sail
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 航空 (háng kōng) — aviation
- 航班 (háng bān) — scheduled flight

## Example Sentences

- 我订了明天的航班。(Wǒ dìng le míng tiān de háng bān.) — I booked tomorrow's flight.

## Notes

航 combines boat 舟 and 亢 (high), representing vessels traveling through water or air on high journeys.
