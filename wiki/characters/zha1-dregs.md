# 渣 Dregs, residue

- **Character**: 渣
- **Pinyin**: zhā
- **Meaning**: dregs, residue
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 豆渣 (dòu zhā) — bean dregs, okara
- 渣滓 (zhā zǐ) — dregs, scum

## Example Sentences

- 做豆浆会剩下豆渣。(Zuò dòujiāng huì shèngxià dòuzhā.) — Making soy milk leaves bean dregs.

## Notes

Refers to leftover solids after extraction. Water radical.
