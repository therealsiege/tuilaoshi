# 扣 To Button

- **Character**: 扣
- **Pinyin**: kòu
- **Meaning**: to button, to deduct, buckle
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扣除 (kòu chú) — to deduct
- 折扣 (zhé kòu) — discount

## Example Sentences

- 这个有折扣吗？(Zhè ge yǒu zhé kòu ma?) — Is there a discount on this?

## Notes

扣 with hand radical suggests fastening, extended to deductions and discounts in business.
