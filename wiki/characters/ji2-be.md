# 嫉 To Be Jealous

- **Character**: 嫉
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: to be jealous, envy
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 嫉妒 (jí dù) — jealousy
- 嫉恨 (jí hèn) — to hate out of jealousy

## Example Sentences

- 不要嫉妒他人的才华。(Bùyào jídù tārén de cáihuá.) — Don't be jealous of others' talents.

## Notes

嫉 combines woman 女 with disease 疾, suggesting jealousy as a kind of emotional illness.
