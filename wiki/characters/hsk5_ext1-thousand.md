# 千 Thousand, 成千上万 Thousands Upon Thousands

- **Character**: 千
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: thousand, 成千上万 thousands upon thousands
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 千万 (qiān wàn) — ten million, be sure to
- 成千上万 (chéng qiān shàng wàn) — thousands upon thousands
- 千克 (qiān kè) — kilogram

## Example Sentences

- 千万别忘记。(Qiānwàn bié wàngjì.) — Be absolutely sure not to forget.

## Notes

Basic large number unit (1,000). 千万 has dual meanings: ten million numerically, and 'absolutely must/mustn't' as an adverb.
