# 钱 Money

- **Character**: 钱
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: money
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 花钱 (huā qián) — to spend money
- 有钱 (yǒu qián) — rich, wealthy

## Example Sentences

- 这个多少钱？(Zhè ge duō shǎo qián?) — How much does this cost?

## Notes

钱 uses the metal radical because coins were originally made of metal. Also a common surname.
