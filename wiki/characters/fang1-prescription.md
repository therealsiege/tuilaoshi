# 方 Prescription, direction, square

- **Character**: 方
- **Pinyin**: fāng
- **Meaning**: prescription, direction, square
- **Radical**: 方 (square)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 药方 (yào fāng) — prescription
- 方子 (fāng zi) — prescription, recipe

## Example Sentences

- 医生开了一个中药方子。(Yīshēng kāi le yī gè zhōng yào fāng zi.) — The doctor prescribed a traditional Chinese medicine formula.

## Notes

Medical prescription or formula. In TCM, prescriptions carefully balance multiple herbs for specific conditions.
