# 歌 Song

- **Character**: 歌
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: song, to sing
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 唱歌 (chàng gē) — to sing
- 歌曲 (gē qǔ) — song

## Example Sentences

- 她在唱歌。(Tā zài chàng gē.) — She is singing.

## Notes

歌 means song or to sing. Used in唱歌 (to sing), 歌手 (singer), 国歌 (national anthem).
