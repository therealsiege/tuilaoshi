# 婉 Graceful, tactful, mild

- **Character**: 婉
- **Pinyin**: wǎn
- **Meaning**: graceful, tactful, mild
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 婉转 (wǎn zhuǎn) — tactful, melodious
- 委婉 (wěi wǎn) — tactful, mild

## Example Sentences

- 她用委婉的方式表达了自己的意见。(Tā yòng wěi wǎn de fāng shì biǎo dá le zì jǐ de yì jiàn.) — She expressed her opinion in a tactful manner.

## Notes

Describes tactful speech or graceful manner.
