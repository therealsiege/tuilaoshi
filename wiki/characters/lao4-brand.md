# 烙 Brand, burn, bake

- **Character**: 烙
- **Pinyin**: lào
- **Meaning**: brand, burn, bake
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烙印 (lào yìn) — brand, stigma
- 烙饼 (lào bǐng) — baked flatbread

## Example Sentences

- 这段经历在他心中烙下了深深的印记。(Zhè duàn jīng lì zài tā xīn zhōng lào xià le shēn shēn de yìn jì.) — This experience left a deep brand on his heart.

## Notes

Can mean branding or cooking method.
