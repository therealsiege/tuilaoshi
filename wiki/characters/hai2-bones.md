# 骸 Bones, skeleton

- **Character**: 骸
- **Pinyin**: hái
- **Meaning**: bones, skeleton
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 骸骨 (háigǔ) — skeleton, remains
- 尸骸 (shīhái) — corpse, remains

## Example Sentences

- 考古学家发现了古人的骸骨。(Kǎogǔxuéjiā fāxiàn le gǔrén de háigǔ.) — Archaeologists discovered ancient human remains.

## Notes

Literary term for skeletal remains, often used in formal contexts.
