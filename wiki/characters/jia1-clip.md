# 夹 To Clip

- **Character**: 夹
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: to clip, to pinch, to sandwich, to mix
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 夹子 (jiā zi) — clip, clamp
- 夹杂 (jiā zá) — to mix, to mingle

## Example Sentences

- 他用筷子夹菜。(Tā yòng kuài zi jiā cài.) — He picks up food with chopsticks.

## Notes

夹 can be jiā (to clip, to pick up with chopsticks) or jiá (narrow). As jiā, essential for using chopsticks.
