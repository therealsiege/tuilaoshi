# 刺 To stab, to prick, thorn, splinter

- **Character**: 刺
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: to stab, to prick, thorn, splinter
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刺激 (cì jī) — to stimulate, irritate
- 讽刺 (fěng cì) — to satirize, sarcasm

## Example Sentences

- 玫瑰花茎上有刺。(Méiguī huā jīng shàng yǒu cì.) — Rose stems have thorns.

## Notes

Can be a verb (to stab) or noun (thorn).
