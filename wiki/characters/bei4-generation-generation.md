# 辈 Generation

- **Character**: 辈
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: generation, lifetime
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 前辈 (qián bèi) — elder, senior
- 一辈子 (yī bèi zi) — a lifetime

## Example Sentences

- 这辈子我最想去中国。(Zhè bèi zi wǒ zuì xiǎng qù zhōng guó.) — In this lifetime, I most want to go to China.

## Notes

辈 indicates generational position. 长辈 = elder, 晚辈 = younger generation.
