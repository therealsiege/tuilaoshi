# 给 To Give/For

- **Character**: 给
- **Pinyin**: gěi
- **Meaning**: to give; for, to (preposition)
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 给予 (jǐyǔ) — to give, to grant (formal)
- 送给 (sònggěi) — to give as a gift
- 交给 (jiāogěi) — to hand over to
- 带给 (dàigěi) — to bring to

## Example Sentences

- 请给我一杯水。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yì bēi shuǐ.) — Please give me a glass of water.
- 我给你打电话。(Wǒ gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà.) — I'll call you (on the phone).
- 这本书是给你的。(Zhè běn shū shì gěi nǐ de.) — This book is for you.

## Notes

给 is the simplified form of 給. The silk radical 纟 reflects its original meaning related to providing or supplying. In modern Chinese, 给 serves double duty: as a main verb meaning "to give" and as a preposition meaning "for/to" that introduces the recipient of an action. The prepositional use (给你打电话, 给他写信) is extremely common in spoken Chinese. Note the alternate literary pronunciation jǐ in the formal compound 给予.
