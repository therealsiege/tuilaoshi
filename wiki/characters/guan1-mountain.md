# 关 Mountain pass, close, concern

- **Character**: 关
- **Pinyin**: guān
- **Meaning**: mountain pass, close, concern
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 关口 (guān kǒu) — pass, juncture
- 山海关 (shān hǎi guān) — Shanhai Pass

## Example Sentences

- 山海关是长城的东端起点。(Shān hǎi guān shì cháng chéng de dōng duān qǐ diǎn.) — Shanhai Pass is the eastern end of the Great Wall.

Famous historical mountain passes in China
