# 次 Time (Occurrence)

- **Character**: 次
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: time (occurrence), next, second
- **Radical**: 欠 (yawn)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 一次 (yī cì) — once
- 下次 (xià cì) — next time

## Example Sentences

- 下次再来。(Xià cì zài lái.) — Come again next time.

## Notes

次 is a measure word for occurrences: 一次 (once), 两次 (twice), 三次 (three times), 每次 (every time).
