# 额 Amount

- **Character**: 额
- **Pinyin**: é
- **Meaning**: forehead, amount, quota
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 金额 (jīn é) — amount of money
- 数额 (shù é) — amount

## Example Sentences

- 这笔交易的金额很大。(Zhè bǐ jiāo yì de jīn é hěn dà.) — The amount of this transaction is very large.

## Notes

额 simplified from 額. Head 页 plus phonetic, amount tallied.
