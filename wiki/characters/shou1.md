# 收 Receive, collect, harvest

- **Character**: 收
- **Pinyin**: shōu
- **Meaning**: receive, collect, harvest
- **Radical**: 攵 (rap)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 收入 (shōurù) — income, revenue
- 验收 (yànshōu) — check and accept

## Example Sentences

- 他的年收入很高。(Tā de nián shōurù hěn gāo.) — His annual income is very high.

## Notes

Common for receiving, collecting, and harvesting.
