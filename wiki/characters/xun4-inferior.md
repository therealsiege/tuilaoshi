# 逊 Inferior, modest, to yield, 遜 (traditional)

- **Character**: 逊
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: inferior, modest, to yield, 遜 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 谦逊 (qiān xùn) — modest, humble
- 逊色 (xùn sè) — inferior to

## Example Sentences

- 他的表现不逊于专业选手。(Tā de biǎoxiàn bù xùn yú zhuānyè xuǎnshǒu.) — His performance is not inferior to professional players.

## Notes

Modesty or inferiority in comparison.
