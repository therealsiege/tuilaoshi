# 绊 Trip, stumble, hinder

- **Character**: 绊
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: trip, stumble, hinder
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 绊倒 (bàn dǎo) — trip and fall
- 羁绊 (jī bàn) — fetters, ties

## Example Sentences

- 他被石头绊倒了。(Tā bèi shí tou bàn dǎo le.) — He tripped over a stone.

## Notes

Physical tripping or metaphorical obstruction
