# 早 Early

- **Character**: 早
- **Pinyin**: zǎo
- **Meaning**: early, morning, good morning
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 早上 (zǎo shàng) — morning
- 早饭 (zǎo fàn) — breakfast

## Example Sentences

- 早上好！(Zǎo shàng hǎo!) — Good morning!

## Notes

早 combines 日 (sun) and 十 (ten) — the sun just above the horizon. 早 alone can serve as a greeting: 'Morning!'
