# 梯 Ladder, stairs

- **Character**: 梯
- **Pinyin**: tī
- **Meaning**: ladder, stairs
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 阶梯 (jiē tī) — stairs, ladder
- 电梯 (diàn tī) — elevator

## Example Sentences

- 他爬上梯子修理屋顶。(Tā pá shàng tī zi xiū lǐ wū dǐng.) — He climbed up the ladder to repair the roof.

## Notes

Used for various types of ladders and staircases.
