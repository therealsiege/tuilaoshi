# 备 To Prepare

- **Character**: 备
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: to prepare, to equip
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 准备 (zhǔn bèi) — to prepare
- 设备 (shè bèi) — equipment

## Example Sentences

- 我在准备考试。(Wǒ zài zhǔn bèi kǎo shì.) — I am preparing for the exam.

## Notes

备 is simplified from 備. It means 'to prepare/equip.' 设备 means 'equipment,' 备注 means 'remarks/notes.'
