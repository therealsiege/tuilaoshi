# 加 To Add

- **Character**: 加
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: to add, to increase, plus
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 参加 (cān jiā) — to participate
- 增加 (zēng jiā) — to increase

## Example Sentences

- 请加我微信。(Qǐng jiā wǒ wēi xìn.) — Please add me on WeChat.

## Notes

加 means to add or increase. 加油 (jiā yóu) literally means 'add fuel' but is used as encouragement: 'Go for it!'
