# 创 Wound

- **Character**: 创
- **Pinyin**: chuāng
- **Meaning**: wound, trauma
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 创伤 (chuāng shāng) — trauma, wound
- 创口 (chuāng kǒu) — wound
- 刀创 (dāo chuāng) — knife wound

## Example Sentences

- 心理创伤需要时间治愈。(Xīn lǐ chuāng shāng xū yào shí jiān zhì yù.) — Psychological trauma takes time to heal.

## Notes

More formal than 伤; used for both physical and psychological trauma.
