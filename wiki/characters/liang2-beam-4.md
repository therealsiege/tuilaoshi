# 梁 Beam

- **Character**: 梁
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: beam, bridge
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 房梁 (fáng liáng) — roof beam
- 桥梁 (qiáo liáng) — bridge

## Example Sentences

- 房梁上雕刻着精美的图案。(Fáng liáng shàng diāo kè zhe jīng měi de tú àn.) — The roof beam is carved with exquisite patterns.

## Notes

梁 has wood 木 with 刅 and 氺 (water), horizontal structural member.
