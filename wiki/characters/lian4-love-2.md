# 恋 To love, long for, 戀 (traditional)

- **Character**: 恋
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to love, long for, 戀 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恋爱 (liàn ài) — to be in love
- 留恋 (liú liàn) — to be reluctant to leave

## Example Sentences

- 他们正在恋爱。(Tāmen zhèngzài liàn'ài.) — They are in love.

## Notes

Romantic love or nostalgic attachment.
