# 脉 Pulse, vein

- **Character**: 脉
- **Pinyin**: mài
- **Meaning**: pulse, vein
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 脉冲 (mài chōng) — pulse
- 脉搏 (mài bó) — pulse (heartbeat)

## Example Sentences

- 脉冲信号很规则。(Mài chōng xìn hào hěn guī zé.) — The pulse signal is regular.

## Notes

Used in 脉冲 (pulse), digital signals, electronics
