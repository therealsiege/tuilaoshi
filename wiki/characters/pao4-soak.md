# 泡 Bubble

- **Character**: 泡
- **Pinyin**: pào
- **Meaning**: to soak, bubble, foam
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泡沫 (pào mò) — foam
- 泡茶 (pào chá) — to brew tea

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢泡茶喝。(Tā xǐ huān pào chá hē.) — She likes to brew tea.

## Notes

泡 combines water 氵 and 包 (to wrap), water enclosing air = bubble.
