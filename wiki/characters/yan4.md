# 咽 To Swallow

- **Character**: 咽
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: to swallow (also yān: throat, yè: choke)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 吞咽 (tūn yàn) — to swallow
- 咽喉 (yān hóu) — throat

## Example Sentences

- 我喉咙痛，很难咽东西。(Wǒ hóu lóng tòng, hěn nán yàn dōng xī.) — My throat hurts, it's hard to swallow things.

## Notes

咽 as yàn means to swallow, as yān means throat, as yè means to choke. Mouth 口 with 因 (cause).
