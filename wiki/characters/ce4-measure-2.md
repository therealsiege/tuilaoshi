# 测 To Measure

- **Character**: 测
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: to measure, to survey, to test
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 测量 (cè liáng) — to measure
- 探测 (tàn cè) — to probe

## Example Sentences

- 工人测量了距离。(Gōng rén cè liáng le jù lí.) — The workers measured the distance.

## Notes

测 simplified from 測. Water 氵 depth measurement, to gauge.
