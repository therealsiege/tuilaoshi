# 厥 Syncope

- **Character**: 厥
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: faint, lose consciousness
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 昏厥 (hūn jué) — to faint, syncope
- 晕厥 (yūn jué) — syncope, fainting

## Example Sentences

- 患者突然晕厥。(Huàn zhě tū rán yūn jué.) — The patient suddenly fainted.

## Notes

Medical term for fainting; temporary loss of consciousness.
