# 夹 Clamp, clip

- **Character**: 夹
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: clamp, clip
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 夹子 (jiā zi) — clip, clamp
- 夹具 (jiā jù) — fixture, clamp

## Example Sentences

- 夹具固定工件。(Jiā jù gù dìng gōng jiàn.) — The fixture holds the workpiece.

## Notes

Used in 夹具 (fixture), 夹子 (clamp), robotics and manufacturing
