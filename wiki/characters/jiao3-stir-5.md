# 搅 To Stir

- **Character**: 搅
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: to stir, to disturb
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir
- 搅乱 (jiǎo luàn) — to disturb

## Example Sentences

- 用勺子搅拌咖啡。(Yòng sháo zi jiǎo bàn kā fēi.) — Stir the coffee with a spoon.

## Notes

搅 simplified from 攪. Hand 扌 plus mixing motion.
