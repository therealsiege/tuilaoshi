# 横 Horizontal, unruly, unexpected

- **Character**: 横
- **Pinyin**: hèng
- **Meaning**: horizontal, unruly, unexpected
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 横行 (héng xíng) — rampage
- 纵横 (zòng héng) — vertical and horizontal

## Example Sentences

- 土匪在村里横行霸道。(Tǔ fěi zài cūn lǐ héng xíng bà dào.) — Bandits run wild in the village.

## Notes

Also read as héng, horizontal direction or domineering behavior
