# 精 Essence

- **Character**: 精
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: essence, refined,精
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 精神 (jīng shén) — spirit, energy
- 精彩 (jīng cǎi) — brilliant, wonderful

## Example Sentences

- 这是一场精彩的表演。(Zhè shì yī chǎng jīng cǎi de biǎo yǎn.) — This is a wonderful performance.

## Notes

精 shows rice 米 refined through selection 青, representing pure essence or refined quality.
