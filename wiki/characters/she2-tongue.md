# 舌 Tongue

- **Character**: 舌
- **Pinyin**: shé
- **Meaning**: tongue
- **Radical**: 舌 (tongue)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 舌头 (shé tou) — tongue
- 口舌 (kǒu shé) — dispute, argument

## Example Sentences

- 舌头尝味道。(Shé tou cháng wèi dào.) — The tongue tastes flavors.

## Notes

舌 pictograph of tongue sticking out. 舌头 complete word for tongue.
