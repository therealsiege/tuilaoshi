# 照 Photo, license, shine

- **Character**: 照
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: 照片, license, shine
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 执照 (zhí zhào) — license, permit
- 牌照 (pái zhào) — license plate, license

## Example Sentences

- 这家店没有营业执照。(Zhè jiā diàn méi yǒu yíng yè zhí zhào.) — This shop doesn't have a business license.

## Notes

营业执照 is required for all businesses in China.
