# 船 Boat

- **Character**: 船
- **Pinyin**: chuán
- **Meaning**: boat, ship
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 小船 (xiǎo chuán) — small boat
- 船长 (chuán zhǎng) — captain

## Example Sentences

- 我们坐船去岛上。(Wǒ men zuò chuán qù dǎo shàng.) — We take a boat to the island.

## Notes

船 means boat or ship. 轮船 = steamship. 渔船 = fishing boat.
