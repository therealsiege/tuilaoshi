# 辟 Open up, refute, monarch

- **Character**: 辟
- **Pinyin**: pì
- **Meaning**: open up, refute, monarch
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 开辟 (kāi pì) — to open up, establish
- 辟谣 (pì yáo) — to refute a rumor

## Example Sentences

- 政府开辟了一条新的公路。(Zhèng fǔ kāi pì le yī tiáo xīn de gōng lù.) — The government opened up a new highway.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations and meanings. As pì: open up, refute. As bì: monarch, avoid.
