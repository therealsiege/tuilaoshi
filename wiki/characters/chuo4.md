# 啜 Sip, sob

- **Character**: 啜
- **Pinyin**: chuò
- **Meaning**: sip, sob
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 啜饮 (chuò yǐn) — sip
- 啜泣 (chuò qì) — sob, weep

## Example Sentences

- 她轻轻地啜饮着热茶。(Tā qīng qīng de chuò yǐn zhe rè chá.) — She sipped the hot tea gently.

## Notes

Can mean sipping drinks or sobbing.
