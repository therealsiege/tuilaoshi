# 料 Material

- **Character**: 料
- **Pinyin**: liào
- **Meaning**: material, ingredient, to expect
- **Radical**: 斗 (dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 材料 (cái liào) — material
- 料理 (liào lǐ) — to arrange

## Example Sentences

- 这些是建筑材料。(Zhè xiē shì jiàn zhù cái liào.) — These are building materials.

## Notes

料 has rice 米 measured with dipper 斗, materials prepared.
