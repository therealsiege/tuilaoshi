# 团 Group, Organization, 团体 Organization

- **Character**: 团
- **Pinyin**: tuán
- **Meaning**: group, organization, 团体 organization
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 团体 (tuán tǐ) — organization, group
- 集团 (jí tuán) — group, bloc
- 团结 (tuán jié) — unite, solidarity

## Example Sentences

- 大家要团结合作。(Dàjiā yào tuánjié hézuò.) — Everyone should unite and cooperate.

## Notes

Common in organizational contexts. 团体 (organization), 集团 (group/conglomerate), and 团结 (unity) are frequently used.
