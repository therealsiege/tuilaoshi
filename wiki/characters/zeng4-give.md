# 赠 To give as a present, to bestow

- **Character**: 赠
- **Pinyin**: zèng
- **Meaning**: to give as a present, to bestow
- **Radical**: 贝(shell)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 赠送 (zèng sòng) — to present as a gift
- 捐赠 (juān zèng) — to donate, to contribute

## Example Sentences

- 朋友赠送我一本书。(Péngyou zèngsòng wǒ yī běn shū.) — A friend gave me a book as a gift.

## Notes

Formal term for giving gifts or bestowing.
