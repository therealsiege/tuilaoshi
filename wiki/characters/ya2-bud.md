# 芽 Bud, sprout, shoot

- **Character**: 芽
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: bud, sprout, shoot
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 发芽 (fā yá) — sprout, germinate
- 豆芽 (dòu yá) — bean sprouts

## Example Sentences

- 春天到了,树木开始发芽。(Chūn tiān dào le, shù mù kāi shǐ fā yá.) — Spring has arrived, and trees are beginning to sprout.

## Notes

Early growth stage of plants
