# 份 Portion

- **Character**: 份
- **Pinyin**: fèn
- **Meaning**: portion, share, copy
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 一份 (yī fèn) — one portion
- 身份 (shēn fèn) — identity

## Example Sentences

- 请给我一份菜单。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yī fèn cài dān.) — Please give me a menu.

## Notes

份 is a measure word for portions, copies, or shares: 一份报纸 (a copy of newspaper).
