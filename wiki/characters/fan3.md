# 反 Opposite

- **Character**: 反
- **Pinyin**: fǎn
- **Meaning**: opposite, to oppose, to reverse
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 反对 (fǎn duì) — to oppose
- 相反 (xiāng fǎn) — opposite

## Example Sentences

- 我反对这个决定。(Wǒ fǎn duì zhè ge jué dìng.) — I oppose this decision.

## Notes

反 means opposite or reverse. 反而 = on the contrary, 反复 = repeatedly.
