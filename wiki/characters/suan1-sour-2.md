# 酸 Sour

- **Character**: 酸
- **Pinyin**: suān
- **Meaning**: sour, acid
- **Radical**: 酉(wine)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 酸性 (suān xìng) — acidic
- 硫酸 (liú suān) — sulfuric acid

## Example Sentences

- 柠檬汁是酸性的。(Níngméng zhī shì suānxìng de.) — Lemon juice is acidic.

## Notes

酸 means sour or acid. In chemistry, refers to acids. The wine radical 酉 suggests fermentation.
