# 岸 Shore, bank, coast

- **Character**: 岸
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: shore, bank, coast
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 海岸 (hǎi àn) — seashore, coast
- 河岸 (hé àn) — riverbank

## Example Sentences

- 我们沿着河岸散步。(Wǒ men yán zhe hé àn sàn bù.) — We walk along the riverbank.

## Notes

Edge of water bodies
