# 绸 Silk

- **Character**: 绸
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: silk, to bind
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 丝绸 (sī chóu) — silk
- 绸缎 (chóu duàn) — silk and satin

## Example Sentences

- 丝绸之路连接了东西方。(Sī chóu zhī lù lián jiē le dōng xī fāng.) — The Silk Road connected East and West.

## Notes

绸 simplified from 綢. Silk 纟 with 周 (cycle/thorough), tightly woven silk.
