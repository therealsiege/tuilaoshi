# 缸 Vat, crock, jar

- **Character**: 缸
- **Pinyin**: gāng
- **Meaning**: vat, crock, jar
- **Radical**: 缶 (jar)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 水缸 (shuǐ gāng) — water vat
- 鱼缸 (yú gāng) — fish tank

## Example Sentences

- 院子里有一口大水缸。(Yuàn zi lǐ yǒu yī kǒu dà shuǐ gāng.) — There's a large water vat in the courtyard.
