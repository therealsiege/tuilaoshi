# 调 Adjust, debug

- **Character**: 调
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: adjust, debug
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 调试 (tiáo shì) — debug
- 调整 (tiáo zhěng) — adjust

## Example Sentences

- 我正在调试这段代码。(Wǒ zhèng zài tiáo shì zhè duàn dài mǎ.) — I am debugging this code.

## Notes

Critical for 调试 (debug), 调用 (call/invoke), 调整 (adjust)
