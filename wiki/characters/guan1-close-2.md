# 关 Close, shut, relationship

- **Character**: 关
- **Pinyin**: guān
- **Meaning**: close, shut,关系
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 关系 (guānxì) — relationship
- 关押 (guānyā) — imprison, take into custody

## Example Sentences

- 犯人被关押在看守所。(Fànrén bèi guānyā zài kānshǒusuǒ.) — The prisoner is detained in the detention center.

## Notes

Very common character with multiple meanings.
