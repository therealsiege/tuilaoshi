# 裁 Judge

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: to cut, judge
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee, judge
- 裁决 (cái jué) — to rule, adjudicate

## Example Sentences

- 裁判员举起了红牌。(Cáipànyuán jǔ qǐ le hóng pái.) — The referee raised the red card.

## Notes

Refereeing; officiating sports
