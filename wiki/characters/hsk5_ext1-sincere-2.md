# 诚 Sincere, Honest, 诚实 Honest

- **Character**: 诚
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: sincere, honest, 诚实 honest
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诚实 (chéng shí) — honest
- 真诚 (zhēn chéng) — sincere
- 诚恳 (chéng kěn) — sincere, earnest

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该诚实做人。(Wǒmen yīnggāi chéngshí zuòrén.) — We should be honest in life.

## Notes

Core virtue character representing honesty and sincerity. 诚实 (honest) and 真诚 (sincere) are fundamental moral terms.
