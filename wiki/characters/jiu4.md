# 就 Then

- **Character**: 就
- **Pinyin**: jiù
- **Meaning**: then, just, right away
- **Radical**: 尢 (lame)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 就是 (jiù shì) — precisely, exactly
- 成就 (chéng jiù) — achievement

## Example Sentences

- 我马上就来。(Wǒ mǎ shàng jiù lái.) — I'll be right there.

## Notes

就 emphasizes immediacy or certainty. It often pairs with 才 which emphasizes lateness or conditionality.
