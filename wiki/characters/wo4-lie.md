# 卧 To Lie Down

- **Character**: 卧
- **Pinyin**: wò
- **Meaning**: to lie down, to recline
- **Radical**: 卜 (divination)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卧室 (wò shì) — bedroom
- 卧床 (wò chuáng) — to be bedridden

## Example Sentences

- 他卧床休息。(Tā wò chuáng xiū xi.) — He rested in bed.

## Notes

卧 simplified from 臥. Shows person lying down, representing reclining posture.
