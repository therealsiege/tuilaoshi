# 窃 Steal, theft

- **Character**: 窃
- **Pinyin**: qiè
- **Meaning**: steal, theft
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 窃取 (qiè qǔ) — steal
- 盗窃 (dào qiè) — theft

## Example Sentences

- 数据被窃取了。(Shù jù bèi qiè qǔ le.) — The data was stolen.

## Notes

Used in 窃取 (steal), 盗窃 (theft), data security
