# 氤 Dense smoke, obscure

- **Character**: 氤
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: dense smoke, obscure
- **Radical**: 气 (air)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 氤氲 (yīnyūn) — dense mist, rich atmosphere
- 氤氲缭绕 (yīnyūn liáorào) — pervaded by dense mist

## Example Sentences

- 茶香氤氲。(Cháxiāng yīnyūn.) — The tea fragrance is rich and pervasive.

## Notes

Classical term for dense mist or smoke, always used with 氲.
