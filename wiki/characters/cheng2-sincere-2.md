# 诚 Sincere, honest, true

- **Character**: 诚
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: sincere, honest, true
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诚实 (chéngshí) — honest, truthful
- 真诚 (zhēnchéng) — sincere, genuine

## Example Sentences

- 他很诚实。(Tā hěn chéngshí.) — He is very honest.

## Notes

Common formal character for sincerity or honesty.
