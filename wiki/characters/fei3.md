# 翡 Kingfisher

- **Character**: 翡
- **Pinyin**: fěi
- **Meaning**: kingfisher, emerald
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 翡翠 (fěi cuì) — jade, emerald
- 翡翠鸟 (fěi cuì niǎo) — kingfisher

## Example Sentences

- 她戴着翡翠手镯。(Tā dài zhe fěi cuì shǒu zhuó.) — She wore a jade bracelet.

## Notes

翡 uses feather radical 羽, originally referring to a colorful kingfisher bird. Now mainly appears in 翡翠 (jade).
