# 钙 Calcium

- **Character**: 钙
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: calcium
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钙质 (gài zhì) — calcium, calcareous
- 补钙 (bǔ gài) — calcium supplement

## Example Sentences

- 牛奶含有丰富的钙。(Niú nǎi hán yǒu fēng fù de gài.) — Milk is rich in calcium.

## Notes

Essential mineral for bones and teeth
