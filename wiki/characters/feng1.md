# 风 Wind

- **Character**: 风
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: wind, style, custom
- **Radical**: 风 (wind)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 风景 (fēng jǐng) — scenery, landscape
- 台风 (tái fēng) — typhoon

## Example Sentences

- 今天风很大。(Jīn tiān fēng hěn dà.) — The wind is very strong today.

## Notes

风 simplified from 風, depicts an insect (虫) that moves with the wind. Also means style or custom (风俗 customs).
