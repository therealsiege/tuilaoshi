# 驮 Carry on back, pack animal

- **Character**: 驮
- **Pinyin**: tuó
- **Meaning**: carry on back, pack animal
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 驮运 (tuóyùn) — transport by pack animal
- 驮马 (tuómǎ) — pack horse

## Example Sentences

- 骆驼驮着货物穿越沙漠。(Luòtuo tuó zhe huòwù chuānyuè shāmò.) — Camels carry goods across the desert.

## Notes

Specifically refers to animals carrying loads on their backs.
