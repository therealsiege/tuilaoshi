# 然 So

- **Character**: 然
- **Pinyin**: rán
- **Meaning**: so, like that, correct
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire dots)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 当然 (dāng rán) — of course
- 虽然 (suī rán) — although

## Example Sentences

- 虽然很难，但是我会努力。(Suī rán hěn nán, dàn shì wǒ huì nǔ lì.) — Although it's difficult, I will work hard.

## Notes

然 is extremely productive in forming adverbs and conjunctions: 当然, 虽然, 突然, 自然, 忽然.
