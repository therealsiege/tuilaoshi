# 敢 To Dare

- **Character**: 敢
- **Pinyin**: gǎn
- **Meaning**: to dare
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 勇敢 (yǒng gǎn) — brave
- 敢于 (gǎn yú) — to dare to

## Example Sentences

- 你敢不敢？(Nǐ gǎn bù gǎn?) — Do you dare?

## Notes

敢 means to dare. 勇敢 = brave/courageous.
