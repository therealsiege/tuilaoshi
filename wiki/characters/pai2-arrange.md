# 排 Arrange, troubleshoot

- **Character**: 排
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: arrange, troubleshoot
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 排序 (pái xù) — sort
- 排查 (pái chá) — troubleshoot

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要排查这个故障。(Wǒ men xū yào pái chá zhè gè gù zhàng.) — We need to troubleshoot this malfunction.

## Notes

Used in 排序 (sort), 排查 (troubleshoot), 安排 (arrange), common in debugging
