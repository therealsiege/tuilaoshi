# 伤 To Injure

- **Character**: 伤
- **Pinyin**: shāng
- **Meaning**: to injure, injury, wound
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 受伤 (shòu shāng) — to be injured
- 伤心 (shāng xīn) — sad

## Example Sentences

- 他在事故中受伤了。(Tā zài shì gù zhōng shòu shāng le.) — He was injured in the accident.

## Notes

伤 simplified from 傷. Shows person 亻 being struck, representing physical or emotional injury.
