# 映 Reflect, project

- **Character**: 映
- **Pinyin**: yìng
- **Meaning**: reflect, project
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 放映 (fàng yìng) — show (a film)
- 反映 (fǎn yìng) — reflect, mirror

## Example Sentences

- 电影院放映新片。(Diàn yǐng yuàn fàng yìng xīn piàn.) — The cinema shows new films.

## Notes

上映 means screen/show. 映像 means image/reflection.
