# 镰 Sickle, scythe

- **Character**: 镰
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: sickle, scythe
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 镰刀 (lián dāo) — sickle
- 锤子镰刀 (chuí zi lián dāo) — hammer and sickle

## Example Sentences

- 他用镰刀割麦子。(Tā yòng lián dāo gē mài zi.) — He cuts wheat with a sickle.

## Notes

Harvesting tool, also political symbol
