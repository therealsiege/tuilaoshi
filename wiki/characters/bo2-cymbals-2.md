# 钹 Cymbals

- **Character**: 钹
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: cymbals
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 铜钹 (tóng bó) — brass cymbals
- 钹声 (bó shēng) — cymbal sound

## Example Sentences

- 钹声清脆响亮。(Bó shēng qīng cuì xiǎng liàng.) — The cymbal sound is crisp and loud.

## Notes

钹 are percussion instruments played by striking two together.
