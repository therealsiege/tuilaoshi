# 师 Teacher

- **Character**: 师
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: teacher, master, expert
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 老师 (lǎo shī) — teacher
- 医师 (yī shī) — doctor

## Example Sentences

- 她是一位好老师。(Tā shì yī wèi hǎo lǎo shī.) — She is a good teacher.

## Notes

师 implies mastery and expertise. It's used as a suffix for many professions: 律师 (lawyer), 工程师 (engineer).
