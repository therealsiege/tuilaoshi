# 墩 Pier, block, lump

- **Character**: 墩
- **Pinyin**: dūn
- **Meaning**: pier, block, lump
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 桥墩 (qiáo dūn) — bridge pier
- 土墩 (tǔ dūn) — earthen mound

## Example Sentences

- 这座大桥有十几个坚固的桥墩。(Zhè zuò dà qiáo yǒu shí jǐ gè jiān gù de qiáo dūn.) — This large bridge has more than ten solid piers.

## Notes

Used for bridge piers and other supporting structures.
