# 锡 Tin

- **Character**: 锡
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: tin, to bestow
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锡纸 (xī zhǐ) — tin foil
- 焊锡 (hàn xī) — solder

## Example Sentences

- 用锡纸包好食物。(Yòng xī zhǐ bāo hǎo shí wù.) — Wrap the food in tin foil.

## Notes

锡 simplified from 錫. Metal 钅, soft silvery metal.
