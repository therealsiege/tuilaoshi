# 骡 Mule

- **Character**: 骡
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: mule
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 骡子 (luó zi) — mule
- 马骡 (mǎ luó) — horse mule

## Example Sentences

- 骡子比马更能驮东西。(Luózi bǐ mǎ gèng néng tuó dōngxi.) — Mules can carry loads better than horses.

## Notes

Hybrid of horse and donkey. Horse radical indicates relation to horses.
