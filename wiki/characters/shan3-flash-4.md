# 闪 To Flash

- **Character**: 闪
- **Pinyin**: shǎn
- **Meaning**: to flash, to dodge
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 闪躲 (shǎn duǒ) — to dodge
- 闪开 (shǎn kāi) — to get out of the way

## Example Sentences

- 他迅速闪开了。(Tā xùn sù shǎn kāi le.) — He quickly dodged.

## Notes

闪 simplified from 閃. Gate 门 with sun peeking through, quick movement.
