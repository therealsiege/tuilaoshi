# 柱 Pillar, column, post

- **Character**: 柱
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: pillar, column, post
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柱子 (zhù zi) — pillar, column
- 支柱 (zhī zhù) — pillar, mainstay

## Example Sentences

- 大殿里有十二根雕刻精美的石柱。(Dà diàn lǐ yǒu shí èr gēn diāo kè jīng měi de shí zhù.) — There are twelve exquisitely carved stone pillars in the great hall.

## Notes

Crucial load-bearing element; also used metaphorically.
