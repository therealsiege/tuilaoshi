# 钉 Nail

- **Character**: 钉
- **Pinyin**: dīng
- **Meaning**: nail, to nail, to follow
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 钉子 (dīng zi) — nail
- 钉住 (dīng zhù) — to nail down

## Example Sentences

- 他用钉子固定了木板。(Tā yòng dīng zi gù dìng le mù bǎn.) — He fixed the board with nails.

## Notes

钉 has metal 钅 with 丁 (fourth), metal spike or nail.
