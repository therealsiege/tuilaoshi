# 送 To Send

- **Character**: 送
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: to send, to deliver, to give as a gift
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 送给 (sòng gěi) — to give to
- 送走 (sòng zǒu) — to see someone off

## Example Sentences

- 我送你一个礼物。(Wǒ sòng nǐ yī gè lǐ wù.) — I'll give you a gift.

## Notes

送 has three main uses: to deliver (送快递), to give as a gift (送礼物), and to see someone off (送行).
