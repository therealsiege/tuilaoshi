# 肺 Lung

- **Character**: 肺
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: lung
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肺炎 (fèi yán) — pneumonia
- 肺癌 (fèi ái) — lung cancer
- 肺部 (fèi bù) — lung area

## Example Sentences

- 吸烟对肺有害。(Xī yān duì fèi yǒu hài.) — Smoking is harmful to the lungs.

## Notes

Primary respiratory organ; commonly appears in respiratory disease terms.
