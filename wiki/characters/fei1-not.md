# 非 Not

- **Character**: 非
- **Pinyin**: fēi
- **Meaning**: not, non-, wrong
- **Radical**: 非 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 非常 (fēi cháng) — extremely
- 是非 (shì fēi) — right and wrong

## Example Sentences

- 非常感谢！(Fēi cháng gǎn xiè!) — Thank you very much!

## Notes

非 literally means 'not' or 'wrong.' 非常 (not ordinary) has evolved to mean 'very, extremely.'
