# 茶 Tea

- **Character**: 茶
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: tea
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 喝茶 (hē chá) — to drink tea
- 茶叶 (chá yè) — tea leaves

## Example Sentences

- 你要喝茶吗？(Nǐ yào hē chá ma?) — Would you like some tea?

## Notes

茶 is one of China's most important cultural exports. The word 'tea' in most languages derives from Chinese: 茶 (chá) or its Min dialect form 'te.'
