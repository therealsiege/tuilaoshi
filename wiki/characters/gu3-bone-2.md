# 骨 Bone

- **Character**: 骨
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: bone
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 骨头 (gǔtou) — bone
- 骨折 (gǔzhé) — fracture

## Example Sentences

- 他摔断了骨头。(Tā shuāi duàn le gǔtou.) — He broke a bone in the fall.

## Notes

Essential medical vocabulary. Usually appears as 骨头 in colloquial speech. 骨折 means fracture.
