# 晕 Dizzy

- **Character**: 晕
- **Pinyin**: yūn
- **Meaning**: dizzy, faint
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 头晕 (tóu yūn) — dizzy
- 晕倒 (yūn dǎo) — to faint
- 晕车 (yūn chē) — carsick

## Example Sentences

- 我头晕，需要坐下。(Wǒ tóu yūn, xū yào zuò xià.) — I'm dizzy and need to sit down.

## Notes

Common symptom; can indicate various conditions.
