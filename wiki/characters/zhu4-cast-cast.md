# 铸 To Cast

- **Character**: 铸
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to cast (metal), to mold
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铸造 (zhù zào) — to cast (metal)
- 铸铁 (zhù tiě) — cast iron

## Example Sentences

- 这口钟是青铜铸成的。(Zhè kǒu zhōng shì qīng tóng zhù chéng de.) — This bell is cast from bronze.

## Notes

铸 simplified from 鑄. Metal 钅 with 寿 (longevity), forging enduring metal.
