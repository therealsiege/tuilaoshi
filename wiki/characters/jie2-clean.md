# 洁 Clean

- **Character**: 洁
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: clean, pure, to cleanse
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 清洁 (qīng jié) — clean, sanitary
- 纯洁 (chún jié) — pure

## Example Sentences

- 保持环境清洁很重要。(Bǎo chí huán jìng qīng jié hěn zhòng yào.) — Maintaining a clean environment is important.

## Notes

洁 simplified from 潔. Water radical 氵 representing cleanliness and purity.
