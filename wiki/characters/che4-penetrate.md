# 彻 Penetrate, thorough

- **Character**: 彻
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: penetrate, thorough
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 彻底 (chèdǐ) — thorough, complete
- 贯彻 (guànchè) — carry out, implement

## Example Sentences

- 我们要彻底解决这个问题。(Wǒmen yào chèdǐ jiějué zhège wèntí.) — We must thoroughly solve this problem.

## Notes

Describes thoroughness or penetration to the core.
