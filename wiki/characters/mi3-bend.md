# 靡 Bend, submit

- **Character**: 靡
- **Pinyin**: mǐ
- **Meaning**: bend, submit
- **Radical**: 非 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 望风披靡 (wàngfēng pīmǐ) — flee at the mere sight
- 披靡 (pīmǐ) — be swept away

## Example Sentences

- 敌军望风披靡。(Díjūn wàngfēng pīmǐ.) — The enemy fled at the mere sight.

## Notes

Literary term meaning to bend or submit; different tone from 靡 (mí).
