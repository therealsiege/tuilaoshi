# 瘤 Tumor

- **Character**: 瘤
- **Pinyin**: liú
- **Meaning**: tumor
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 肿瘤 (zhǒng liú) — tumor
- 脑瘤 (nǎo liú) — brain tumor
- 良性肿瘤 (liáng xìng zhǒng liú) — benign tumor

## Example Sentences

- 医生发现他脑部有肿瘤。(Yī shēng fā xiàn tā nǎo bù yǒu zhǒng liú.) — The doctor found a tumor in his brain.

## Notes

Abnormal growth; can be benign or malignant.
