# 钥 Key

- **Character**: 钥
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: key (lock)
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钥匙 (yào shi) — key
- 密钥 (mì yào) — encryption key

## Example Sentences

- 我忘带钥匙了。(Wǒ wàng dài yào shi le.) — I forgot to bring my key.

## Notes

钥 with metal radical for key, essential in both physical and cryptographic key contexts.
