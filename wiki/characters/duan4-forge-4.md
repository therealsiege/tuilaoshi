# 锻 Forge

- **Character**: 锻
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: forge, temper
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — exercise, temper
- 锻造 (duàn zào) — forge, forging

## Example Sentences

- 铁匠正在锻造铁器。(Tiějiàng zhèngzài duànzào tiěqì.) — The blacksmith is forging iron tools.

## Notes

锻 means to forge metal by heating and hammering. Extended to mean physical or character training.
