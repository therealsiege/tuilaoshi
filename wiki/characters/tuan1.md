# 湍 Rapid

- **Character**: 湍
- **Pinyin**: tuān
- **Meaning**: rapid water, torrential
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 湍急 (tuān jí) — rapid (current)
- 湍流 (tuān liú) — turbulent flow

## Example Sentences

- 河水湍急。(Hé shuǐ tuān jí.) — The river current is rapid.

## Notes

湍 has water 氵 with 耑 (to specialize), water flowing swiftly.
