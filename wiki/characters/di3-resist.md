# 抵 Resist, offset, mortgage

- **Character**: 抵
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: resist, offset, mortgage
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抵押 (dǐ yā) — to mortgage
- 抵抗 (dǐ kàng) — to resist

## Example Sentences

- 他用房产抵押贷款。(Tā yòng fáng chǎn dǐ yā dài kuǎn.) — He used real estate as mortgage collateral for a loan.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: to resist, offset, or mortgage. 抵达 means to arrive. 大抵 means generally.
