# 写 To Write

- **Character**: 写
- **Pinyin**: xiě
- **Meaning**: to write
- **Radical**: 冖 (cover)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 写字 (xiězì) — to write characters
- 写作 (xiězuò) — to write (compose), writing
- 写信 (xiěxìn) — to write a letter
- 描写 (miáoxiě) — to describe, description
- 写生 (xiěshēng) — to sketch from life

## Example Sentences

- 请写你的名字。(Qǐng xiě nǐ de míngzi.) — Please write your name.
- 这个字怎么写？(Zhège zì zěnme xiě?) — How do you write this character?
- 她喜欢写文章。(Tā xǐhuān xiě wénzhāng.) — She likes writing articles.

## Notes

写 is the simplified form of 寫, drastically reduced from 15 strokes to 5. The traditional form contained 宀 (roof) on top and a complex phonetic component below. The simplified version retains the cover radical 冖 on top with a streamlined bottom. In everyday Chinese, 写 specifically refers to the physical act of writing, distinguishing it from 书 (shū), which leans more toward "book" in modern usage. A common classroom question is 这个字怎么写？ — asking how to write a particular character.
