# 槽 Groove

- **Character**: 槽
- **Pinyin**: cáo
- **Meaning**: groove, trough, manger
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 水槽 (shuǐ cáo) — sink, trough
- 槽钢 (cáo gāng) — channel steel

## Example Sentences

- 他在水槽里洗菜。(Tā zài shuǐ cáo lǐ xǐ cài.) — He washes vegetables in the sink.

## Notes

槽 has wood 木 with 曹 (group), wooden trough or channel.
