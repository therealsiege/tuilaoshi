# 妄 Delusion

- **Character**: 妄
- **Pinyin**: wàng
- **Meaning**: absurd, reckless
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 妄想 (wàng xiǎng) — delusion, paranoia
- 狂妄 (kuáng wàng) — arrogant
- 妄图 (wàng tú) — to attempt in vain

## Example Sentences

- 被害妄想是精神分裂的症状。(Bèi hài wàng xiǎng shì jīng shén fēn liè de zhèng zhuàng.) — Persecutory delusions are a symptom of schizophrenia.

## Notes

False beliefs; delusional thinking.
