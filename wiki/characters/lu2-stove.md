# 炉 Stove, furnace

- **Character**: 炉
- **Pinyin**: lú
- **Meaning**: stove, furnace
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 炉子 (lú zi) — stove
- 香炉 (xiāng lú) — incense burner

## Example Sentences

- 香炉里燃着檀香。(Xiāng lú lǐ rán zhe tán xiāng.) — Sandalwood incense is burning in the censer.

## Notes

Heating or religious vessel
