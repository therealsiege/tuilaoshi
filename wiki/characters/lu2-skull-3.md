# 颅 Skull

- **Character**: 颅
- **Pinyin**: lú
- **Meaning**: skull
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 头颅 (tóu lú) — skull, head
- 颅骨 (lú gǔ) — cranium
- 颅内 (lú nèi) — intracranial

## Example Sentences

- 颅内出血很危险。(Lú nèi chū xiě hěn wēi xiǎn.) — Intracranial hemorrhage is very dangerous.

## Notes

Protects brain; critical in neurology and trauma care.
