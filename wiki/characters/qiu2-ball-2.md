# 球 Ball

- **Character**: 球
- **Pinyin**: qiú
- **Meaning**: ball, sphere, globe
- **Radical**: 王 (king/jade)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 足球 (zú qiú) — soccer
- 篮球 (lán qiú) — basketball

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢踢足球。(Wǒ xǐ huān tī zú qiú.) — I like to play soccer.

## Notes

球 means ball or sphere. 地球 = Earth (the globe). Sports: 足球, 篮球, 乒乓球.
