# 觉 To Feel

- **Character**: 觉
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to feel, to sense; sleep (jiào)
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 觉得 (jué de) — to feel, to think
- 睡觉 (shuì jiào) — to sleep

## Example Sentences

- 你觉得怎么样？(Nǐ jué de zěn me yàng?) — What do you think?

## Notes

觉 is read jué in 觉得 (to feel/think) and jiào in 睡觉 (to sleep). Two very different meanings with different tones.
