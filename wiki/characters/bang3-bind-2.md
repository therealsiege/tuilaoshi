# 绑 Bind

- **Character**: 绑
- **Pinyin**: bǎng
- **Meaning**: bind, tie
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绑扎 (bǎng zā) — to tie up, bind
- 绑带 (bǎng dài) — bandage
- 捆绑 (kǔn bǎng) — to tie up

## Example Sentences

- 用绷带绑紧伤口。(Yòng bēng dài bǎng jǐn shāng kǒu.) — Bind the wound tightly with a bandage.

## Notes

Securing with wraps or ties; stabilization.
