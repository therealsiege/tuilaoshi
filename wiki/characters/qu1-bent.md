# 曲 Bent, crooked, wrong

- **Character**: 曲
- **Pinyin**: qū
- **Meaning**: bent, crooked, wrong
- **Radical**: 曰 (say)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 弯曲 (wān qū) — curved, bent
- 曲折 (qū zhé) — winding, tortuous

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很曲折。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn qū zhé.) — This road is very winding.

## Notes

Also read as qǔ for song or melody
