# 聋 Deaf

- **Character**: 聋
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: deaf
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 聋子 (lóng zi) — deaf person
- 耳聋 (ěr lóng) — deafness

## Example Sentences

- 他的左耳有些聋。(Tā de zuǒ ěr yǒuxiē lóng.) — His left ear is somewhat deaf.

## Notes

聋 combines ear 耳 with dragon 龙, though the龙 component here is phonetic rather than semantic.
