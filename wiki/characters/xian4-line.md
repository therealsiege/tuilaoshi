# 线 Line

- **Character**: 线
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: line, thread, wire
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 在线 (zài xiàn) — online
- 电线 (diàn xiàn) — electric wire

## Example Sentences

- 他现在在线。(Tā xiàn zài zài xiàn.) — He is online now.

## Notes

线 appears in both physical (wires) and virtual (online) contexts.
