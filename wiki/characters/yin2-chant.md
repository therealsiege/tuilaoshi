# 吟 To chant, to recite, to moan

- **Character**: 吟
- **Pinyin**: yín
- **Meaning**: to chant, to recite, to moan
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吟诵 (yín sòng) — to chant, to recite
- 吟唱 (yín chàng) — to chant, to intone

## Example Sentences

- 他在吟诵古诗。(Tā zài yínsòng gǔshī.) — He is reciting ancient poetry.

## Notes

Literary term for chanting or reciting, often poetry.
