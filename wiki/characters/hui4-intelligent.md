# 慧 Intelligent, wisdom

- **Character**: 慧
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: intelligent, wisdom
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 智慧 (zhìhuì) — wisdom, intelligence
- 聪慧 (cōnghuì) — intelligent, bright

## Example Sentences

- 她很聪慧。(Tā hěn cōnghuì.) — She is very intelligent.

## Notes

Common formal term for wisdom or intelligence.
