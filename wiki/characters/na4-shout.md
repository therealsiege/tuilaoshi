# 呐 To Shout

- **Character**: 呐
- **Pinyin**: nà
- **Meaning**: to shout, suona (part of)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 唢呐 (suǒ nà) — suona
- 呐喊 (nà hǎn) — to shout

## Example Sentences

- 士兵们在呐喊。(Shì bīng men zài nà hǎn.) — The soldiers are shouting.

## Notes

呐 means to shout; in 唢呐 refers to the suona horn.
