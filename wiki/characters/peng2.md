# 朋 Friend

- **Character**: 朋
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: friend (used in 朋友)
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 朋友 (péng yǒu) — friend
- 亲朋 (qīn péng) — relatives and friends

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的好朋友。(Tā shì wǒ de hǎo péng yǒu.) — He is my good friend.

## Notes

朋 depicts two strings of shells side by side — things of the same kind, hence 'companion.' Almost always paired with 友.
