# 漆 Paint

- **Character**: 漆
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: lacquer, paint, to paint
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 油漆 (yóu qī) — paint
- 漆黑 (qī hēi) — pitch-black

## Example Sentences

- 他在刷油漆。(Tā zài shuā yóu qī.) — He is painting.

## Notes

漆 has water 氵 with tree sap 桼, lacquer from trees.
