# 层 Layer, level

- **Character**: 层
- **Pinyin**: céng
- **Meaning**: layer, level
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 层次 (céng cì) — level, hierarchy
- 隐藏层 (yǐn cáng céng) — hidden layer

## Example Sentences

- 神经网络有多少层?(Shén jīng wǎng luò yǒu duō shǎo céng?) — How many layers does the neural network have?

## Notes

Used in 层 (layer), 隐藏层 (hidden layer), neural network terminology
