# 荣 Glory, honor, flourish

- **Character**: 荣
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: glory, honor, flourish
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荣誉 (róng yù) — honor, glory
- 繁荣 (fán róng) — prosperous, booming

## Example Sentences

- 他获得了很高的荣誉。(Tā huò dé le hěn gāo de róng yù.) — He received great honor.

## Notes

Means honor or glory. 光荣 means glorious. 欣欣向荣 means flourishing.
