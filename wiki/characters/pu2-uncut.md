# 璞 Uncut Jade

- **Character**: 璞
- **Pinyin**: pú
- **Meaning**: uncut jade, unpolished
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 璞玉 (pú yù) — uncut jade
- 返璞归真 (fǎn pú guī zhēn) — return to nature

## Example Sentences

- 这是一块天然的璞玉。(Zhè shì yí kuài tiān rán de pú yù.) — This is a natural piece of uncut jade.

## Notes

璞 has jade 玉 with 菐 phonetic, raw jade in natural state.
