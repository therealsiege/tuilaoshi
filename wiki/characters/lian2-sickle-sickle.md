# 镰 Sickle

- **Character**: 镰
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: sickle
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 镰刀 (lián dāo) — sickle
- 开镰 (kāi lián) — to start harvesting

## Example Sentences

- 农民用镰刀割麦子。(Nóng mín yòng lián dāo gē mài zi.) — Farmers use sickles to cut wheat.

## Notes

镰 simplified from 鎌. Metal 钅 with 廉 (honest/cheap), the harvesting tool.
