# 渠 Canal, Ditch, 渠道 Channel

- **Character**: 渠
- **Pinyin**: qú
- **Meaning**: canal, ditch, 渠道 channel
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渠道 (qú dào) — channel, means
- 沟渠 (gōu qú) — ditch, channel
- 灌溉渠 (guàn gài qú) — irrigation canal

## Example Sentences

- 我们要拓宽销售渠道。(Wǒmen yào tuòkuān xiāoshòu qúdào.) — We need to expand sales channels.

## Notes

Literal meaning is irrigation canal, but commonly used figuratively for channels/means. 渠道 is very common in business contexts.
