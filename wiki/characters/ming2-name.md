# 名 Name

- **Character**: 名
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: name, fame, famous
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 名字 (míng zì) — name
- 有名 (yǒu míng) — famous

## Example Sentences

- 你叫什么名字？(Nǐ jiào shén me míng zì?) — What is your name?

## Notes

名 combines 夕 (evening) and 口 (mouth) — calling out names in the dark to identify people.
