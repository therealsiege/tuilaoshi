# 贷 Loan, lend

- **Character**: 贷
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: loan, lend
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan
- 信贷 (xìn dài) — credit

## Example Sentences

- 他向银行申请了贷款。(Tā xiàng yín háng shēn qǐng le dài kuǎn.) — He applied for a loan from the bank.

## Notes

房贷 is mortgage, 车贷 is car loan. Core banking term.
