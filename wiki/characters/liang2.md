# 凉 Cool

- **Character**: 凉
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: cool, cold
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 凉快 (liáng kuài) — nice and cool
- 凉水 (liáng shuǐ) — cold water

## Example Sentences

- 今天很凉快。(Jīn tiān hěn liáng kuài.) — Today is nice and cool.

## Notes

凉 with ice radical 冫. 凉快 means pleasantly cool. Between 热 (hot) and 冷 (cold).
