# 泄 Leak, release, discharge

- **Character**: 泄
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: leak, release, discharge
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泄漏 (xièlòu) — leak, divulge
- 发泄 (fāxiè) — give vent to

## Example Sentences

- 不要泄漏机密。(Bùyào xièlòu jīmì.) — Don't leak secrets.

## Notes

Common for leaking, releasing, or venting.
