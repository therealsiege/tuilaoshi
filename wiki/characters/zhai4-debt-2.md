# 债 Debt

- **Character**: 债
- **Pinyin**: zhài
- **Meaning**: debt, obligation
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 债务 (zhài wù) — debt
- 国债 (guó zhài) — national debt

## Example Sentences

- 他欠了很多债。(Tā qiàn le hěn duō zhài.) — He owes a lot of debt.

## Notes

债 simplified from 債. Person 亻 plus 责 (responsibility).
