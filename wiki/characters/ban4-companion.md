# 伴 Companion, partner, to accompany

- **Character**: 伴
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: companion, partner, to accompany
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 伙伴 (huǒ bàn) — partner, companion
- 陪伴 (péi bàn) — to accompany, to keep company

## Example Sentences

- 他是我的好伙伴。(Tā shì wǒ de hǎo huǒbàn.) — He is my good companion.

## Notes

Used for companions or accompanying someone.
