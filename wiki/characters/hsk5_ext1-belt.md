# 带 Belt, Bring, 带来 Bring

- **Character**: 带
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: belt, bring, 带来 bring
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 带来 (dài lái) — bring
- 领带 (lǐng dài) — necktie
- 携带 (xié dài) — carry, take

## Example Sentences

- 请带上你的身份证。(Qǐng dàishang nǐ de shēnfènzhèng.) — Please bring your ID card.

## Notes

Very common character with multiple meanings: bring/carry, belt/band, and lead/guide. 带来/带去 are essential directional compounds.
