# 烁 Sparkle, shine

- **Character**: 烁
- **Pinyin**: shuò
- **Meaning**: sparkle, shine
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 闪烁 (shǎn shuò) — twinkle, flicker
- 熠熠生辉 (yì yì shēng huī) — shine brilliantly

## Example Sentences

- 湖面上波光闪烁。(Hú miàn shàng bō guāng shǎn shuò.) — The waves on the lake surface sparkle with light.

## Notes

Describes sparkling or flickering light.
