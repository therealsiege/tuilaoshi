# 派 Faction

- **Character**: 派
- **Pinyin**: pài
- **Meaning**: faction, to dispatch, style
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 派别 (pài bié) — faction
- 派遣 (pài qiǎn) — to dispatch

## Example Sentences

- 公司派他去北京出差。(Gōng sī pài tā qù běi jīng chū chāi.) — The company sent him on a business trip to Beijing.

## Notes

派 water 氵 branching, streams flowing in different directions.
