# 顾 To Care For

- **Character**: 顾
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: to look after, to attend to, customer
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 照顾 (zhào gù) — to look after
- 顾客 (gù kè) — customer

## Example Sentences

- 照顾好自己。(Zhào gù hǎo zì jǐ.) — Take good care of yourself.

## Notes

顾 means to attend to or care for. 不顾 = regardless of.
