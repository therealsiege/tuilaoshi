# 词 Lyrics

- **Character**: 词
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: lyrics, words, ci poetry
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 歌词 (gē cí) — lyrics
- 宋词 (sòng cí) — Song dynasty poetry

## Example Sentences

- 这首歌的歌词很美。(Zhè shǒu gē de gē cí hěn měi.) — The lyrics of this song are very beautiful.

## Notes

词 refers to words, lyrics, or a genre of Chinese poetry (ci).
