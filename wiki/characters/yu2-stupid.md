# 愚 Stupid, foolish, deceive

- **Character**: 愚
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: stupid, foolish, deceive
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 愚蠢 (yú chǔn) — stupid, foolish
- 愚昧 (yú mèi) — ignorant

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个愚蠢的决定。(Zhè shì yī ge yú chǔn de jué dìng.) — This is a foolish decision.

## Notes

Means foolish or stupid. 愚公移山 is a famous fable about perseverance. 愚弄 means to fool or deceive.
