# 规 Rule

- **Character**: 规
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: rule, regulation, compass
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 规定 (guī dìng) — regulation, to stipulate
- 规模 (guī mó) — scale, scope

## Example Sentences

- 按照规定不能吸烟。(Àn zhào guī dìng bù néng xī yān.) — According to regulations, smoking is not allowed.

## Notes

规 is simplified from 規. Originally meant compass, extended to rules and standards.
