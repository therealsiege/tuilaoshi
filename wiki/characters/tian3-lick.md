# 舔 To Lick

- **Character**: 舔
- **Pinyin**: tiǎn
- **Meaning**: to lick
- **Radical**: 舌 (tongue)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 舔舐 (tiǎn shì) — to lick

## Example Sentences

- 小狗舔我的手。(Xiǎo gǒu tiǎn wǒ de shǒu.) — The puppy licked my hand.

## Notes

舔 combines tongue 舌 and 忝 (to disgrace), representing the action of extending the tongue to lick.
