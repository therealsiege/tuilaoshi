# 遍 Everywhere, all over, time

- **Character**: 遍
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: everywhere, all over, time
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 遍布 (biànbù) — spread all over
- 普遍 (pǔbiàn) — universal, widespread

## Example Sentences

- 这本书我读了三遍。(Zhè běn shū wǒ dú le sān biàn.) — I've read this book three times.

## Notes

Means everywhere or used as measure word for times/occurrences.
