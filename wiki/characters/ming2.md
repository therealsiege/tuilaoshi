# 明 Bright

- **Character**: 明
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: bright, clear, tomorrow
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 明天 (míng tiān) — tomorrow
- 明白 (míng bái) — to understand

## Example Sentences

- 明天见！(Míng tiān jiàn!) — See you tomorrow!

## Notes

明 combines 日 (sun) and 月 (moon) — the two brightest objects, hence 'bright, clear.'
