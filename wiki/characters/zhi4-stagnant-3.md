# 滞 Stagnant, sluggish, 滯 (traditional)

- **Character**: 滞
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: stagnant, sluggish, 滯 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 停滞 (tíng zhì) — to stagnate
- 滞留 (zhì liú) — to be detained, held up

## Example Sentences

- 经济发展停滞不前。(Jīngjì fāzhǎn tíngzhì bù qián.) — Economic development has stagnated.

## Notes

Stagnation or being held up/delayed.
