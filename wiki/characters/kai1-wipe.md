# 揩 To wipe, scrape

- **Character**: 揩
- **Pinyin**: kāi
- **Meaning**: to wipe, scrape
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揩油 (kāi yóu) — to take advantage
- 揩拭 (kāi shì) — to wipe

## Example Sentences

- 别想揩我的油。(Bié xiǎng kāi wǒ de yóu.) — Don't try to take advantage of me.

## Notes

Means to wipe. 揩油 colloquially means to take advantage or mooch. Less common than 擦.
