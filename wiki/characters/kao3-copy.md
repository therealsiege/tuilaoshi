# 拷 Copy, duplicate

- **Character**: 拷
- **Pinyin**: kǎo
- **Meaning**: copy, duplicate
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拷贝 (kǎo bèi) — copy
- 拷盘 (kǎo pán) — copy disk

## Example Sentences

- 我需要拷贝一份文件。(Wǒ xū yào kǎo bèi yī fèn wén jiàn.) — I need to copy a file.

## Notes

Used in 拷贝 (copy), often for file duplication operations
