# 赋 Endow, compose, tax

- **Character**: 赋
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: endow, compose, tax
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赋予 (fùyǔ) — endow with, bestow
- 天赋 (tiānfù) — natural gift, talent

## Example Sentences

- 这项任务赋予了他新的使命。(Zhè xiàng rènwù fùyǔ le tā xīn de shǐmìng.) — This task endowed him with a new mission.

## Notes

Classical character also referring to a type of literary composition in ancient China.
