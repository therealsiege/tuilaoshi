# 蒜 Garlic

- **Character**: 蒜
- **Pinyin**: suàn
- **Meaning**: garlic
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 大蒜 (dà suàn) — garlic
- 蒜头 (suàn tóu) — garlic bulb

## Example Sentences

- 炒菜的时候先放点蒜。(Chǎocài de shíhou xiān fàng diǎn suàn.) — When stir-frying, add some garlic first.

## Notes

Fundamental ingredient in Chinese cooking. Grass radical for plant.
