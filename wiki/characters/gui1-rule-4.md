# 规 Rule

- **Character**: 规
- **Pinyin**: guī
- **Meaning**: rule, regulation
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 规则 (guī zé) — rule, regulation
- 犯规 (fàn guī) — to foul

## Example Sentences
