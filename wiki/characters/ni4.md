# 溺 To Drown

- **Character**: 溺
- **Pinyin**: nì
- **Meaning**: to drown, to indulge
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 溺水 (nì shuǐ) — to drown
- 沉溺 (chén nì) — to indulge in

## Example Sentences

- 不要沉溺于网络游戏。(Bù yào chén nì yú wǎng luò yóu xì.) — Don't indulge in online games.

## Notes

溺 means to drown literally, but is often used metaphorically for being addicted or overly indulgent.
