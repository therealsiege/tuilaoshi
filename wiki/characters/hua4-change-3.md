# 化 Transform

- **Character**: 化
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: change, transform
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 净化 (jìng huà) — to purify
- 消化 (xiāo huà) — to digest
- 变化 (biàn huà) — change

## Example Sentences

- 空气净化器改善室内空气。(Kōng qì jìng huà qì gǎi shàn shì nèi kōng qì.) — Air purifiers improve indoor air quality.

## Notes

Transformation process; purification or digestion.
