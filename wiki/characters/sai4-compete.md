# 赛 Compete

- **Character**: 赛
- **Pinyin**: sài
- **Meaning**: compete, race, match
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 比赛 (bǐ sài) — competition, match
- 赛跑 (sài pǎo) — race, running competition

## Example Sentences

- 明天有一场重要的比赛。(Míngtiān yǒu yī chǎng zhòngyào de bǐsài.) — There is an important match tomorrow.

## Notes

Core competition character; used in all sports competitions and contests
