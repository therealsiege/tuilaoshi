# 谷 Valley, grain

- **Character**: 谷
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: valley, grain
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 山谷 (shān gǔ) — valley
- 峡谷 (xiá gǔ) — canyon, gorge

## Example Sentences

- 深谷中回荡着鸟鸣声。(Shēn gǔ zhōng huí dàng zhe niǎo míng shēng.) — Bird calls echo through the deep valley.

## Notes

Also means grain or cereal as a separate meaning.
