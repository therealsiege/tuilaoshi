# 雕 To Carve

- **Character**: 雕
- **Pinyin**: diāo
- **Meaning**: to carve, to engrave
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — to carve, to engrave
- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture

## Example Sentences

- 这座雕塑很精美。(Zhè zuò diāo sù hěn jīng měi.) — This sculpture is very exquisite.

## Notes

雕 shows bird 隹 (eagle) with周 (circumference), originally an eagle, extended to carving with precision.
