# 蓝 Blue

- **Character**: 蓝
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: blue, indigo
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 蓝色 (lán sè) — blue color
- 蓝天 (lán tiān) — blue sky

## Example Sentences

- 天空是蓝色的。(Tiān kōng shì lán sè de.) — The sky is blue.

## Notes

蓝 means blue. 蓝色 = blue color. 蓝牙 = Bluetooth.
