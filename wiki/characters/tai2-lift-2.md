# 抬 To Lift

- **Character**: 抬
- **Pinyin**: tái
- **Meaning**: to lift, to raise
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抬头 (tái tóu) — to raise head
- 抬起 (tái qǐ) — to lift up

## Example Sentences

- 请抬起头来。(Qǐng tái qǐ tóu lái.) — Please raise your head.

## Notes

抬 combines hand 扌 and 台 (platform), representing lifting upward.
