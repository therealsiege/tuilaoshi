# 丢 To Lose

- **Character**: 丢
- **Pinyin**: diū
- **Meaning**: to lose, to throw, to discard
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 丢人 (diū rén) — embarrassing
- 丢掉 (diū diào) — to discard

## Example Sentences

- 我把钥匙丢了。(Wǒ bǎ yào shi diū le.) — I lost my keys.

## Notes

丢 means to lose something. 丢人 or 丢脸 means to lose face/be embarrassing.
