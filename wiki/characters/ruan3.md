# 阮 Ruan

- **Character**: 阮
- **Pinyin**: ruǎn
- **Meaning**: ruan (lute), surname
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阮咸 (ruǎn xián) — ruanxian (instrument)
- 中阮 (zhōng ruǎn) — middle ruan

## Example Sentences

- 阮是一种弹拨乐器。(Ruǎn shì yī zhǒng tán bō yuè qì.) — The ruan is a plucked string instrument.

## Notes

阮 is a plucked string instrument similar to the pipa; also a surname.
