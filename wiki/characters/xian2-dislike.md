# 嫌 To dislike, suspicion, 嫌 (traditional)

- **Character**: 嫌
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: to dislike, suspicion, 嫌 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 嫌疑 (xián yí) — suspicion, to suspect
- 嫌弃 (xián qì) — to dislike, avoid

## Example Sentences

- 别嫌弃这个地方小。(Bié xiánqì zhège dìfang xiǎo.) — Don't dislike this place for being small.

## Notes

Can mean to dislike/mind or to suspect, depending on context.
