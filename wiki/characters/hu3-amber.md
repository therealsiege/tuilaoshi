# 琥 Amber

- **Character**: 琥
- **Pinyin**: hǔ
- **Meaning**: amber
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琥珀 (hǔ pò) — amber
- 琥珀色 (hǔ pò sè) — amber color

## Example Sentences

- 琥珀是古老的树脂化石。(Hǔ pò shì gǔ lǎo de shù zhī huà shí.) — Amber is fossilized ancient tree resin.

## Notes

琥 uses jade radical 玉, always appears with 珀 to form the word for amber.
