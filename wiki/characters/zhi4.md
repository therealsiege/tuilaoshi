# 治 To Govern

- **Character**: 治
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: to govern, manage, cure
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 政治 (zhèng zhì) — politics
- 治疗 (zhì liáo) — to treat, cure

## Example Sentences

- 这种病很难治疗。(Zhè zhǒng bìng hěn nán zhìliáo.) — This disease is hard to treat.

## Notes

治 originally meant to control water (flood control), extended to mean governance and medical treatment.
