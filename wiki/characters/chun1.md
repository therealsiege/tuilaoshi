# 春 Spring

- **Character**: 春
- **Pinyin**: chūn
- **Meaning**: spring (season)
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 春天 (chūn tiān) — spring
- 春节 (chūn jié) — Spring Festival, Chinese New Year

## Example Sentences

- 春天来了。(Chūn tiān lái le.) — Spring has arrived.

## Notes

春 combines sun (日) and vegetation sprouting (屯), representing the spring season when plants grow. 春节 is the most important Chinese holiday.
