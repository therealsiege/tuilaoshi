# 萃 To collect, gather, essence

- **Character**: 萃
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: to collect, gather, essence
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 荟萃 (huì cuì) — to gather, assemble
- 精萃 (jīng cuì) — essence, elite

## Example Sentences

- 这里荟萃了各国精英。(Zhèlǐ huìcuìle gè guó jīngyīng.) — Elites from various countries gather here.

## Notes

To gather together the best or most essential elements.
