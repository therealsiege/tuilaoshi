# 十 Ten

- **Character**: 十
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: ten
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 十分 (shí fēn) — very, extremely
- 十月 (shí yuè) — October

## Example Sentences

- 十分感谢！(Shí fēn gǎn xiè!) — Thank you very much!

## Notes

十 is a cross shape representing 'complete' — ten fingers. 十分 means 'very/extremely' (literally 'ten parts').
