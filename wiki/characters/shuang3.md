# 爽 Refreshing, straightforward, cheerful

- **Character**: 爽
- **Pinyin**: shuǎng
- **Meaning**: refreshing, straightforward, cheerful
- **Radical**: 爻 (yao)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 爽快 (shuǎng kuài) — straightforward, frank
- 豪爽 (háo shuǎng) — bold and generous

## Example Sentences

- 她性格豪爽，待人真诚。(Tā xìng gé háo shuǎng, dài rén zhēn chéng.) — She has a generous character and treats people sincerely.

## Notes

Can describe weather, feelings, or personality.
