# 爵 Noble rank, peerage, wine vessel

- **Character**: 爵
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: noble rank, peerage, wine vessel
- **Radical**: 爪 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 爵位 (jué wèi) — noble title
- 册封 (cè fēng) — confer a title

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝给他册封爵位。(Huáng dì gěi tā cè fēng jué wèi.) — The emperor conferred a noble title upon him.

## Notes

System of five noble ranks in ancient China
