# 捞 To Fish For

- **Character**: 捞
- **Pinyin**: lāo
- **Meaning**: to dredge up, to fish for
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捞鱼 (lāo yú) — to fish out
- 打捞 (dǎ lāo) — to salvage

## Example Sentences

- 从水里捞出钥匙。(Cóng shuǐ lǐ lāo chū yào shi.) — Fish the keys out of the water.

## Notes

捞 simplified from 撈. Hand 扌 scooping from water.
