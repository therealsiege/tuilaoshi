# 湃 Surge, rush

- **Character**: 湃
- **Pinyin**: pài
- **Meaning**: surge, rush
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 澎湃 (péngpài) — surge, seethe
- 汹涌澎湃 (xiōngyǒng péngpài) — turbulent and surging

## Example Sentences

- 他心情激动澎湃。(Tā xīnqíng jīdòng péngpài.) — His emotions surged with excitement.

## Notes

Primarily used in compound 澎湃 for surging motions or emotions.
