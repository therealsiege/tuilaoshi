# 昆 Kunlun

- **Character**: 昆
- **Pinyin**: kūn
- **Meaning**: Kunlun, elder brother
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 昆曲 (kūn qǔ) — Kunqu opera
- 昆仑 (kūn lún) — Kunlun Mountains

## Example Sentences

- 昆曲被誉为百戏之祖。(Kūn qǔ bèi yù wéi bǎi xì zhī zǔ.) — Kunqu opera is honored as the ancestor of all operas.

## Notes

昆 appears in 昆曲, an ancient and refined form of Chinese opera.
