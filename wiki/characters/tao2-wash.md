# 淘 Wash

- **Character**: 淘
- **Pinyin**: táo
- **Meaning**: to wash, eliminate
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淘汰 (táo tài) — to eliminate, weed out
- 淘汰赛 (táo tài sài) — elimination match

## Example Sentences

- 他们在半决赛中被淘汰了。(Tāmen zài bànjuésài zhōng bèi táotài le.) — They were eliminated in the semifinals.

## Notes

Elimination; knockout competitions
