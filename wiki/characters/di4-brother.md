# 弟 Younger Brother

- **Character**: 弟
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: younger brother
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 弟弟 (dì dì) — younger brother
- 兄弟 (xiōng dì) — brothers

## Example Sentences

- 我有一个弟弟。(Wǒ yǒu yī gè dì dì.) — I have a younger brother.

## Notes

弟 means younger brother. 弟弟 is the affectionate form. 兄弟 = brothers/bros.
