# 蹈 To Tread

- **Character**: 蹈
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: to tread, to dance
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 舞蹈 (wǔ dǎo) — dance
- 蹈海 (dǎo hǎi) — to drown oneself

## Example Sentences

- 舞蹈演员的表演很精彩。(Wǔ dǎo yǎn yuán de biǎo yǎn hěn jīng cǎi.) — The dancer's performance is wonderful.

## Notes

蹈 combines foot 足 and 舀 (to ladle), representing rhythmic stepping movements.
