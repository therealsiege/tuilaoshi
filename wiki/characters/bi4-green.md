# 碧 Blue-Green

- **Character**: 碧
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: jade green, blue-green
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 碧绿 (bì lǜ) — green, jade green
- 碧海 (bì hǎi) — azure sea

## Example Sentences

- 湖水碧绿清澈。(Hú shuǐ bì lǜ qīng chè.) — The lake water is jade green and clear.

## Notes

碧 has stone 石 with 白 (white) and 王 (king), precious green stone.
