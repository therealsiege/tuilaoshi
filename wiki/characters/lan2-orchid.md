# 兰 Orchid

- **Character**: 兰
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: orchid
- **Radical**: 丷 (two)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 兰花 (lán huā) — orchid
- 荷兰 (hé lán) — Netherlands

## Example Sentences

- 兰花很优雅。(Lán huā hěn yōu yǎ.) — Orchids are very elegant.

## Notes

兰 simplified from 蘭. Originally showed fragrant plants in doorway, representing the orchid.
