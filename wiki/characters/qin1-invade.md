# 侵 To Invade

- **Character**: 侵
- **Pinyin**: qīn
- **Meaning**: to invade, encroach
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 侵略 (qīn lüè) — to invade, aggression
- 侵犯 (qīn fàn) — to violate, infringe

## Example Sentences

- 这种行为侵犯了他人的权利。(Zhè zhǒng xíngwéi qīnfàn le tārén de quánlì.) — This behavior violates others' rights.

## Notes

侵 combines person 亻 with 帚 (broom) suggesting intrusion or invasion into another's space.
