# 断 To Break

- **Character**: 断
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: to break, to cut off, to decide
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 中断 (zhōng duàn) — to interrupt
- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge

## Example Sentences

- 电话断了。(Diàn huà duàn le.) — The phone connection was cut off.

## Notes

断 simplified from 斷. Shows an axe 斤 cutting through, representing severance or decisive action.
