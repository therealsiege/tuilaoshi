# 刷 Brush

- **Character**: 刷
- **Pinyin**: shuā
- **Meaning**: brush, scrub
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 刷子 (shuā zi) — brush
- 油刷 (yóu shuā) — paintbrush

## Example Sentences

- 他正在刷墙。(Tā zhèngzài shuā qiáng.) — He is painting the wall.

## Notes

刷 means to brush, paint, or scrub. Can refer to both the tool and the action.
