# 添 Add

- **Character**: 添
- **Pinyin**: tiān
- **Meaning**: to add, to increase
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 画蛇添足 (huà shé tiān zú) — to ruin by overdoing
- 添加 (tiān jiā) — to add, to append

## Example Sentences

- 别再画蛇添足了。(Bié zài huà shé tiān zú le.) — Stop overdoing it.

## Notes

Key character in 画蛇添足; means to add or supplement.
