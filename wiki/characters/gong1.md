# 工 Work

- **Character**: 工
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: work, labor, skill
- **Radical**: 工 (work)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 工作 (gōng zuò) — work, job
- 工人 (gōng rén) — worker

## Example Sentences

- 他的工作很忙。(Tā de gōng zuò hěn máng.) — His job is very busy.

## Notes

工 is a pictograph of a carpenter's square or ruler — a tool for work.
