# 澄 Clear, transparent, to settle

- **Character**: 澄
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: clear, transparent, to settle
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 澄清 (chéng qīng) — to clarify, clear up
- 澄澈 (chéng chè) — clear, limpid

## Example Sentences

- 我想澄清一个误会。(Wǒ xiǎng chéngqīng yī gè wùhuì.) — I want to clear up a misunderstanding.

## Notes

Clear/transparent or to clarify/settle.
