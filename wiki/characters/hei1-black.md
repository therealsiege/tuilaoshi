# 黑 Black

- **Character**: 黑
- **Pinyin**: hēi
- **Meaning**: black, dark
- **Radical**: 黑 (black)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 黑色 (hēi sè) — black color
- 黑板 (hēi bǎn) — blackboard

## Example Sentences

- 天黑了。(Tiān hēi le.) — It's getting dark.

## Notes

黑 depicts soot above a fire (灬), representing blackness from smoke and soot. Opposite of 白 (white).
