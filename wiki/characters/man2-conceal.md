# 瞒 To Conceal

- **Character**: 瞒
- **Pinyin**: mán
- **Meaning**: to conceal, to hide the truth
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 隐瞒 (yǐn mán) — to conceal
- 欺瞒 (qī mán) — to deceive

## Example Sentences

- 你不能隐瞒事实。(Nǐ bù néng yǐn mán shì shí.) — You cannot conceal the facts.

## Notes

瞒 simplified from 瞞. Eye 目 plus 满 phonetic, hiding from sight.
