# 彻 Thorough

- **Character**: 彻
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: thorough, penetrating
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 彻底 (chè dǐ) — thorough
- 贯彻 (guàn chè) — to implement

## Example Sentences

- 他彻底明白了。(Tā chè dǐ míng bái le.) — He thoroughly understood.

## Notes

彻 simplified from 徹. Step 彳 going through completely.
