# 迷 To be confused, infatuated, fan

- **Character**: 迷
- **Pinyin**: mí
- **Meaning**: to be confused, infatuated, fan
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 迷路 (mí lù) — to get lost
- 着迷 (zháo mí) — fascinated, enchanted

## Example Sentences

- 我在森林里迷路了。(Wǒ zài sēnlín lǐ mílù le.) — I got lost in the forest.

## Notes

To be lost/confused or fascinated/infatuated.
