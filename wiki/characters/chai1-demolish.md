# 拆 Demolish, dismantle

- **Character**: 拆
- **Pinyin**: chāi
- **Meaning**: demolish, dismantle
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拆除 (chāi chú) — demolish, remove
- 拆迁 (chāi qiān) — demolish and relocate

## Example Sentences

- 旧楼要拆迁了。(Jiù lóu yào chāi qiān le.) — The old building will be demolished and relocated.

## Notes

拆迁户 are residents affected by demolition. Hot topic in urban development.
