# 济 Help, relieve, ferry across

- **Character**: 济
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: help, relieve, ferry across
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 救济 (jiùjì) — relief, aid
- 经济 (jīngjì) — economy

## Example Sentences

- 政府救济贫困人口。(Zhèngfǔ jiùjì pínkùn rénkǒu.) — The government provides relief to the poor.

## Notes

Means helping across difficulties, also used in the word for economy.
