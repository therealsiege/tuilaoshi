# 冬 Winter

- **Character**: 冬
- **Pinyin**: dōng
- **Meaning**: winter
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 冬天 (dōng tiān) — winter
- 冬季 (dōng jì) — winter season

## Example Sentences

- 冬天很冷。(Dōng tiān hěn lěng.) — Winter is very cold.

## Notes

冬 shows something tied/bound at both ends - like ice binding water in winter.
