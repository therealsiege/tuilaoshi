# 笋 Bamboo shoot

- **Character**: 笋
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: bamboo shoot
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 竹笋 (zhú sǔn) — bamboo shoot
- 春笋 (chūn sǔn) — spring bamboo shoot

## Example Sentences

- 这道菜里有新鲜的竹笋。(Zhè dào cài lǐ yǒu xīnxiān de zhúsǔn.) — This dish has fresh bamboo shoots.

## Notes

Popular vegetable in Chinese cuisine. Bamboo radical clearly indicates origin.
