# 密 Dense

- **Character**: 密
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: dense, secret
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 密码 (mì mǎ) — password
- 秘密 (mì mì) — secret

## Example Sentences

- 请不要泄露密码。(Qǐng bú yào xiè lòu mì mǎ.) — Please don't leak the password.

## Notes

密 means dense/thick, naturally extending to密 密ty and encryption.
