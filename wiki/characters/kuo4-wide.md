# 阔 Wide, broad, wealthy

- **Character**: 阔
- **Pinyin**: kuò
- **Meaning**: wide, broad, wealthy
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 广阔 (guǎngkuò) — vast, broad
- 宽阔 (kuānkuò) — wide, broad

## Example Sentences

- 眼前是一片广阔的草原。(Yǎnqián shì yī piàn guǎngkuò de cǎoyuán.) — Before us is a vast grassland.

## Notes

Describes width, breadth, or wealth and generosity.
