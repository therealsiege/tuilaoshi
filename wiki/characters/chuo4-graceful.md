# 绰 Graceful, ample

- **Character**: 绰
- **Pinyin**: chuò
- **Meaning**: graceful, ample
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绰绰有余 (chuòchuò yǒuyú) — more than enough
- 绰号 (chuòhào) — nickname

## Example Sentences

- 时间绰绰有余。(Shíjiān chuòchuò yǒuyú.) — There is more than enough time.

## Notes

Literary term for gracefulness or ample sufficiency.
