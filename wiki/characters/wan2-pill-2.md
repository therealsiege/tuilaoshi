# 丸 Pill

- **Character**: 丸
- **Pinyin**: wán
- **Meaning**: pill, pellet
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 药丸 (yào wán) — pill
- 丸药 (wán yào) — pill medicine
- 弹丸 (dàn wán) — pellet, bullet

## Example Sentences

- 每天服用两粒药丸。(Měi tiān fú yòng liǎng lì yào wán.) — Take two pills daily.

## Notes

Round pill form; traditional and modern medicine.
