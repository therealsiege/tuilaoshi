# 室 Room

- **Character**: 室
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: room, office
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 教室 (jiào shì) — classroom
- 办公室 (bàn gōng shì) — office

## Example Sentences

- 他在办公室。(Tā zài bàn gōng shì.) — He is in the office.

## Notes

室 with roof radical for enclosed room, formal spaces.
