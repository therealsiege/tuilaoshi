# 鹊 Magpie

- **Character**: 鹊
- **Pinyin**: què
- **Meaning**: magpie
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 喜鹊 (xǐ què) — magpie
- 鹊桥 (què qiáo) — magpie bridge

## Example Sentences

- 喜鹊是吉祥的象征。(Xǐquè shì jíxiáng de xiàngzhēng.) — The magpie is a symbol of good fortune.

## Notes

Considered lucky in Chinese culture. Features in the Qixi Festival legend.
