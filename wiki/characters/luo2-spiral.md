# 螺 Spiral

- **Character**: 螺
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: spiral, screw, snail
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 螺丝 (luó sī) — screw
- 螺旋 (luó xuán) — spiral

## Example Sentences

- 拧紧这颗螺丝。(Níng jǐn zhè kē luó sī.) — Tighten this screw.

## Notes

螺 with insect radical from snail shell's spiral, perfect for screws and spirals.
