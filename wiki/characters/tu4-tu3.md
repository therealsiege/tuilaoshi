# 吐 Spit

- **Character**: 吐
- **Pinyin**: tù/tǔ
- **Meaning**: vomit, spit
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 呕吐 (ǒu tù) — to vomit
- 吐痰 (tǔ tán) — to spit phlegm
- 吐血 (tù xiě) — to vomit blood

## Example Sentences

- 他吐了一整夜。(Tā tù le yī zhěng yè.) — He vomited all night.

## Notes

Expel from mouth; tù for vomit, tǔ for spit.
