# 矮 Short

- **Character**: 矮
- **Pinyin**: ǎi
- **Meaning**: short (height), low
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 矮小 (ǎi xiǎo) — short and small
- 矮个子 (ǎi gè zi) — short person

## Example Sentences

- 他比我矮。(Tā bǐ wǒ ǎi.) — He is shorter than me.

## Notes

矮 means short in height (not length). Opposite of 高 (tall).
