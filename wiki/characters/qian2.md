# 前 Front

- **Character**: 前
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: front, before, ago
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 以前 (yǐ qián) — before, previously
- 前面 (qián miàn) — in front

## Example Sentences

- 学校在前面。(Xué xiào zài qián miàn.) — The school is up ahead.

## Notes

前 means both spatial 'front' and temporal 'before/ago.' Opposite of 后 (behind/after).
