# 垛 Battlements, pile, stack

- **Character**: 垛
- **Pinyin**: duǒ
- **Meaning**: battlements, pile, stack
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 城垛 (chéng duǒ) — battlements
- 垛口 (duǒ kǒu) — embrasure

## Example Sentences

- 长城上的城垛历经风雨依然坚固。(Cháng chéng shàng de chéng duǒ lì jīng fēng yǔ yī rán jiān gù.) — The battlements on the Great Wall remain solid despite wind and rain.

## Notes

Also means a pile or stack of things.
