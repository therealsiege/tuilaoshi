# 生 Life

- **Character**: 生
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: to be born, life, raw, student
- **Radical**: 生 (life)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 学生 (xué shēng) — student
- 生活 (shēng huó) — life, to live

## Example Sentences

- 他是新学生。(Tā shì xīn xué shēng.) — He is a new student.

## Notes

生 depicts a plant sprouting from the ground — the origin of life. It has many extended meanings.
