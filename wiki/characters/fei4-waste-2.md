# 废 Waste

- **Character**: 废
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: to waste, to abandon, useless
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 半途而废 (bàn tú ér fèi) — give up halfway
- 废物 (fèi wù) — waste, garbage

## Example Sentences

- 这些废纸可以回收。(Zhèxiē fèi zhǐ kěyǐ huíshōu.) — This waste paper can be recycled.

## Notes

Used in 半途而废: to abandon or discard; giving up before completion.
