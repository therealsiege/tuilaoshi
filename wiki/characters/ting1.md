# 听 To Listen/Hear

- **Character**: 听
- **Pinyin**: tīng
- **Meaning**: to listen, to hear
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 听说 (tīngshuō) — to hear it said, reportedly
- 听见 (tīngjiàn) — to hear
- 听懂 (tīngdǒng) — to understand (by listening)
- 好听 (hǎotīng) — pleasant to listen to, nice-sounding
- 听力 (tīnglì) — listening ability, listening comprehension
- 听话 (tīnghuà) — to be obedient (lit. "listen to words")

## Example Sentences

- 请你听我说。(Qǐng nǐ tīng wǒ shuō.) — Please listen to me.
- 你听见了吗？(Nǐ tīngjiàn le ma?) — Did you hear it?
- 这首歌很好听。(Zhè shǒu gē hěn hǎotīng.) — This song sounds very nice.

## Notes

听 is the simplified form of 聽, which was dramatically reduced from 22 strokes to 7. The traditional form contained the ear radical 耳 prominently, making the meaning transparent. The simplified form uses 口 (mouth) as its radical, which is less intuitive but far easier to write. With result complements, 听 specifies the outcome of listening: 听见 (heard successfully), 听懂 (understood by hearing), 听到 (managed to hear). The phrase 听不懂 (tīng bù dǒng, can't understand by listening) is one of the first survival phrases learners need.
