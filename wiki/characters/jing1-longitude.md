# 经 Longitude, pass through, classic

- **Character**: 经
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: longitude, pass through, classic
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 经度 (jīng dù) — longitude
- 经纬 (jīng wěi) — longitude and latitude

## Example Sentences

- 本初子午线的经度是零度。(Běn chū zǐ wǔ xiàn de jīng dù shì líng dù.) — The prime meridian is at zero degrees longitude.

Geographic coordinate
