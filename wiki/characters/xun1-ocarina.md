# 埙 Ocarina

- **Character**: 埙
- **Pinyin**: xūn
- **Meaning**: ocarina, ancient wind instrument
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陶埙 (táo xūn) — pottery ocarina
- 埙声 (xūn shēng) — xun sound

## Example Sentences

- 埙是中国最古老的乐器之一。(Xūn shì zhōng guó zuì gǔ lǎo de yuè qì zhī yī.) — The xun is one of China's oldest musical instruments.

## Notes

埙 is an ancient Chinese vessel flute made of clay or ceramic.
