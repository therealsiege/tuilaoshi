# 荧 Fluorescent, glimmer

- **Character**: 荧
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: fluorescent, glimmer
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荧光 (yíng guāng) — fluorescence
- 荧屏 (yíng píng) — screen (TV/computer)

## Example Sentences

- 夜晚，萤火虫发出微弱的荧光。(Yè wǎn, yíng huǒ chóng fā chū wēi ruò de yíng guāng.) — At night, fireflies emit weak fluorescent light.

## Notes

Related to fluorescence and glowing light.
