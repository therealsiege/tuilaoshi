# 先 First

- **Character**: 先
- **Pinyin**: xiān
- **Meaning**: first, before, earlier
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 先生 (xiān shēng) — Mr., sir
- 首先 (shǒu xiān) — first of all

## Example Sentences

- 请先坐。(Qǐng xiān zuò.) — Please sit down first.

## Notes

先 means first in order. 先生 = Mr./sir/husband. 先后 = one after another.
