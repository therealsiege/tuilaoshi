# 持 To hold, to grasp, to support, to maintain

- **Character**: 持
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: to hold, to grasp, to support, to maintain
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 坚持 (jiān chí) — to persist, to insist on
- 持续 (chí xù) — to continue, to persist

## Example Sentences

- 他一直坚持自己的观点。(Tā yīzhí jiānchí zìjǐ de guāndiǎn.) — He always persists in his own viewpoint.

## Notes

Used for maintaining positions, actions, or states over time.
