# 导 Conduct, guide

- **Character**: 导
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: conduct, guide
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 半导体 (bàn dǎo tǐ) — semiconductor
- 导电 (dǎo diàn) — conduct electricity

## Example Sentences

- 这种材料导电性好。(Zhè zhǒng cái liào dǎo diàn xìng hǎo.) — This material has good electrical conductivity.

## Notes

Used in 半导体 (semiconductor), 导体 (conductor), 导线 (wire)
