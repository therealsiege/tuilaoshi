# 问 To Ask

- **Character**: 问
- **Pinyin**: wèn
- **Meaning**: to ask
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 问题 (wèn tí) — question, problem
- 请问 (qǐng wèn) — excuse me, may I ask

## Example Sentences

- 我想问你一个问题。(Wǒ xiǎng wèn nǐ yī gè wèn tí.) — I want to ask you a question.

## Notes

问 combines 门 (gate/mouth opening) and 口 (mouth). Asking opens communication like a gate.
