# 现 Now

- **Character**: 现
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: now, present, current
- **Radical**: 王 (king/jade)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 现在 (xiàn zài) — now
- 出现 (chū xiàn) — to appear

## Example Sentences

- 现在几点了？(Xiàn zài jǐ diǎn le?) — What time is it now?

## Notes

现 combines 王 (jade) and 见 (see) — seeing jade implies something appearing, hence 'present, now.'
