# 久 Long Time

- **Character**: 久
- **Pinyin**: jiǔ
- **Meaning**: long (time), for a long time
- **Radical**: 丿 (slash)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 好久 (hǎo jiǔ) — a long time
- 多久 (duō jiǔ) — how long

## Example Sentences

- 好久不见！(Hǎo jiǔ bú jiàn!) — Long time no see!

## Notes

久 refers to duration of time (long ago). 好久不见 is a classic greeting.
