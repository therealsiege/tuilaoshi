# 兵 Soldier

- **Character**: 兵
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: soldier, troops, weapons
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 士兵 (shì bīng) — soldier
- 兵器 (bīng qì) — weapon

## Example Sentences

- 士兵们正在训练。(Shì bīng men zhèng zài xùn liàn.) — The soldiers are training.

## Notes

兵 shows hands holding an axe, representing weapons and soldiers.
