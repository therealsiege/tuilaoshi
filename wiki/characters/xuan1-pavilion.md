# 轩 Pavilion, high, lofty

- **Character**: 轩
- **Pinyin**: xuān
- **Meaning**: pavilion, high, lofty
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 轩敞 (xuān chǎng) — spacious and bright
- 轩窗 (xuān chuāng) — window

## Example Sentences

- 这间书房轩敞明亮。(Zhè jiān shū fáng xuān chǎng míng liàng.) — This study is spacious and bright.

## Notes

Often used in names of studios or studies.
