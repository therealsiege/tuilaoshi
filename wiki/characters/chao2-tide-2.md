# 潮 Tide

- **Character**: 潮
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: tide, damp
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潮水 (cháoshuǐ) — tide
- 高潮 (gāocháo) — high tide, climax

## Example Sentences

- 海边的潮水涨了。(Hǎibiān de cháoshuǐ zhǎng le.) — The tide at the seaside has risen.

## Notes

Ocean phenomenon and adjective for damp. Water radical. Also means trendy in modern slang.
