# 墩 Pier

- **Character**: 墩
- **Pinyin**: dūn
- **Meaning**: pier, block, foundation
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 桥墩 (qiáo dūn) — bridge pier
- 墩子 (dūn zi) — block, stump

## Example Sentences

- 这座桥有五个桥墩。(Zhè zuò qiáo yǒu wǔ gè qiáo dūn.) — This bridge has five piers.

## Notes

墩 has earth 土 with 敦 (thick/solid), solid earthen foundation.
