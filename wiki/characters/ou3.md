# 偶 Accidental

- **Character**: 偶
- **Pinyin**: ǒu
- **Meaning**: accidental, even number, idol
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 偶然 (ǒu rán) — accidental, by chance
- 配偶 (pèi ǒu) — spouse

## Example Sentences

- 我们偶然在街上遇见了。(Wǒ men ǒu rán zài jiē shàng yù jiàn le.) — We met by chance on the street.

## Notes

偶 shows person 亻 and 禺 (corner/area), originally meaning a paired figure or puppet, extended to chance pairing.
