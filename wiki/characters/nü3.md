# 女 Girl

- **Character**: 女
- **Pinyin**: nǚ
- **Meaning**: Girl constellation, woman
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 女宿 (nǚ xiù) — Girl constellation
- 女子 (nǚ zǐ) — woman

## Example Sentences

- 女宿是北方玄武七宿之一。(Nǚ xiù shì běifāng xuánwǔ qī xiù zhī yī.) — Girl is one of the seven mansions of the Northern Black Tortoise.

## Notes

女 means woman or girl. In astronomy, the Girl constellation, part of Aquarius in the northern mansions.
