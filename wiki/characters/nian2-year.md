# 年 Year

- **Character**: 年
- **Pinyin**: nián
- **Meaning**: year
- **Radical**: 干 (dry)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 今年 (jīn nián) — this year
- 新年 (xīn nián) — new year

## Example Sentences

- 今年我二十岁。(Jīn nián wǒ èr shí suì.) — I am twenty years old this year.

## Notes

年 originally depicted a person carrying grain, symbolizing the harvest cycle — one year.
