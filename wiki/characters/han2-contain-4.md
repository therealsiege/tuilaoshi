# 含 Contain

- **Character**: 含
- **Pinyin**: hán
- **Meaning**: contain, hold in mouth
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 含服 (hán fú) — to take sublingually
- 含片 (hán piàn) — lozenge
- 包含 (bāo hán) — to contain

## Example Sentences

- 让药片在舌下含服。(Ràng yào piàn zài shé xià hán fú.) — Let the tablet dissolve under the tongue.

## Notes

Sublingual administration; holds in mouth.
