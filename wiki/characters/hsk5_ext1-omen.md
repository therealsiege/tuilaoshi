# 兆 Omen, Million, 预兆 Omen

- **Character**: 兆
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: omen, million, 预兆 omen
- **Radical**: 儿 (human)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 预兆 (yù zhào) — omen, sign
- 兆头 (zhào tou) — omen
- 百万 (bǎi wàn) — million

## Example Sentences

- 这是个好兆头。(Zhè shì gè hǎo zhàotou.) — This is a good omen.

## Notes

Dual meaning: omen/sign in traditional contexts, and million (10^6) in modern usage, especially in technology (兆字节 megabyte).
