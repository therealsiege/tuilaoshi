# 腕 Wrist

- **Character**: 腕
- **Pinyin**: wàn
- **Meaning**: wrist
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 手腕 (shǒuwàn) — wrist
- 腕表 (wànbiǎo) — wristwatch

## Example Sentences

- 她戴着一块漂亮的手腕表。(Tā dàizhe yī kuài piàoliang de shǒuwàn biǎo.) — She's wearing a beautiful wristwatch.

## Notes

Usually appears as 手腕. Important for describing hand position and jewelry.
