# 缔 Tie, conclude (treaty)

- **Character**: 缔
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: tie, conclude (treaty)
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缔造 (dìzào) — create, establish
- 缔结 (dìjié) — conclude (treaty), forge

## Example Sentences

- 两国缔结了友好条约。(Liǎng guó dìjié le yǒuhǎo tiáoyuē.) — The two countries concluded a friendship treaty.

## Notes

Formal term for concluding treaties or creating/establishing.
