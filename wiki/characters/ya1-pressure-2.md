# 压 Pressure

- **Character**: 压
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: pressure, to press
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 电压 (diàn yā) — voltage
- 压力 (yā lì) — pressure, stress

## Example Sentences

- 这个电压太高。(Zhè ge diàn yā tài gāo.) — This voltage is too high.

## Notes

压 originally meant to press down, now essential for voltage and pressure measurements.
