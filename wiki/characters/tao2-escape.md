# 逃 To escape, to run away, to flee

- **Character**: 逃
- **Pinyin**: táo
- **Meaning**: to escape, to run away, to flee
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 逃跑 (táo pǎo) — to escape, to run away
- 逃避 (táo bì) — to evade, to avoid

## Example Sentences

- 犯人从监狱逃跑了。(Fànrén cóng jiānyù táopǎo le.) — The criminal escaped from prison.

## Notes

Common verb for escaping or fleeing.
