# 腺 Gland

- **Character**: 腺
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: gland
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腺体 (xiàn tǐ) — gland
- 甲状腺 (jiǎ zhuàng xiàn) — thyroid gland

## Example Sentences

- 内分泌腺分泌激素。(Nèi fēn mì xiàn fēn mì jī sù.) — Endocrine glands secrete hormones.

## Notes

腺 uses meat radical 月, specifically referring to organs that secrete substances.
