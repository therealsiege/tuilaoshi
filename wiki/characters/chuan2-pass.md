# 传 Pass on, transmit, spread

- **Character**: 传
- **Pinyin**: chuán
- **Meaning**: pass on, transmit, spread
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 遗传 (yí chuán) — heredity, genetic
- 传播 (chuán bō) — spread, disseminate

## Example Sentences

- 基因通过遗传从父母传给子女。(Jī yīn tōng guò yí chuán cóng fù mǔ chuán gěi zǐ nǚ.) — Genes are passed from parents to children through heredity.

## Notes

Used for transmission and heredity
