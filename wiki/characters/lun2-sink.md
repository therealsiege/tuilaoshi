# 沦 Sink, fall into

- **Character**: 沦
- **Pinyin**: lún
- **Meaning**: sink, fall into
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沦陷 (lúnxiàn) — fall into enemy hands
- 沦落 (lúnluò) — be reduced to, sink into

## Example Sentences

- 城市沦陷敌手。(Chéngshì lúnxiàn díshǒu.) — The city fell into enemy hands.

## Notes

Literary term for sinking or falling into undesirable situations.
