# 仆 Servant, to fall forward

- **Character**: 仆
- **Pinyin**: pú
- **Meaning**: servant, to fall forward
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 仆人 (pú rén) — servant
- 前仆后继 (qián pū hòu jì) — to advance dauntlessly

## Example Sentences

- 革命烈士前仆后继。(Gé mìng liè shì qián pū hòu jì.) — Revolutionary martyrs advanced wave upon wave.

## Notes

As pú means servant. As pū means to fall forward. 公仆 means public servant.
