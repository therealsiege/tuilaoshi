# 阶 Rank

- **Character**: 阶
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: rank, steps, stairs
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阶段 (jiē duàn) — stage
- 阶级 (jiē jí) — social class

## Example Sentences

- 社会阶层分化严重。(Shè huì jiē céng fēn huà yán zhòng.) — Social stratification is severe.

## Notes

阶 simplified from 階. Mound 阝 plus 皆 (all), steps to climb.
