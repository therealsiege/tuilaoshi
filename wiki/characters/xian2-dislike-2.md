# 嫌 To Suspect

- **Character**: 嫌
- **Pinyin**: xián
- **Meaning**: to dislike, to suspect
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 嫌疑 (xián yí) — suspicion
- 嫌弃 (xián qì) — to disdain

## Example Sentences

- 他是犯罪嫌疑人。(Tā shì fàn zuì xián yí rén.) — He is a criminal suspect.

## Notes

嫌 combines woman 女 and 兼 (simultaneously), suggesting mixed feelings.
