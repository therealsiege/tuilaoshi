# 棚 Shed

- **Character**: 棚
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: shed, canopy
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 大棚 (dà péng) — greenhouse
- 凉棚 (liáng péng) — awning

## Example Sentences

- 大棚里种菜。(Dà péng lǐ zhòng cài.) — Grow vegetables in the greenhouse.

## Notes

棚 with wood radical for temporary wooden shelter,棚 sheds and awnings.
