# 敷 Apply

- **Character**: 敷
- **Pinyin**: fū
- **Meaning**: apply, spread on
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 外敷 (wài fū) — external application
- 敷药 (fū yào) — to apply medicine
- 冷敷 (lěng fū) — cold compress

## Example Sentences

- 用冷敷缓解肿胀。(Yòng lěng fū huǎn jiě zhǒng zhàng.) — Use cold compress to relieve swelling.

## Notes

Application of compress or poultice; therapeutic.
