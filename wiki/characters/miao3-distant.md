# 邈 Distant, remote, minute

- **Character**: 邈
- **Pinyin**: miǎo
- **Meaning**: distant, remote, minute
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 渺邈 (miǎo miǎo) — distant and indistinct
- 邈远 (miǎo yuǎn) — remote, distant

## Example Sentences

- 那些记忆已经很渺远了。(Nàxiē jìyì yǐjīng hěn miǎoyuǎn le.) — Those memories are already very distant.

## Notes

Literary term for great distance in time or space.
