# 展 To Exhibit

- **Character**: 展
- **Pinyin**: zhǎn
- **Meaning**: to展展展to unfold, to display, exhibition
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 展览 (zhǎn lǎn) — exhibition
- 发展 (fā zhǎn) — to develop

## Example Sentences

- 博物馆正在展览古代文物。(Bó wù guǎn zhèng zài zhǎn lǎn gǔ dài wén wù.) — The museum is exhibiting ancient artifacts.

## Notes

展 means to unfold or display. Common in发展 (development), 展览 (exhibition).
