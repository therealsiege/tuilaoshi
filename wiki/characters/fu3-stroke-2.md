# 抚 To Stroke

- **Character**: 抚
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: to stroke, to comfort
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抚琴 (fǔ qín) — to play the zither
- 抚摸 (fǔ mō) — to stroke

## Example Sentences

- 古人抚琴以抒情怀。(Gǔ rén fǔ qín yǐ shū qíng huái.) — The ancients played the zither to express their feelings.

## Notes

抚 means to stroke gently; used poetically for playing stringed instruments.
