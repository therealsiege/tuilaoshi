# 塔 Tower, Pagoda, 宝塔 Pagoda

- **Character**: 塔
- **Pinyin**: tǎ
- **Meaning**: tower, pagoda, 宝塔 pagoda
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 宝塔 (bǎo tǎ) — pagoda
- 塔楼 (tǎ lóu) — tower
- 灯塔 (dēng tǎ) — lighthouse

## Example Sentences

- 大雁塔是西安的著名建筑。(Dà Yàn Tǎ shì Xī'ān de zhùmíng jiànzhù.) — The Giant Wild Goose Pagoda is a famous building in Xi'an.

## Notes

Term for towers and pagodas. Buddhist pagodas are iconic architectural features in Chinese temples and cities.
