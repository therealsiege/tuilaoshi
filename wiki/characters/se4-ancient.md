# 瑟 Ancient Zither

- **Character**: 瑟
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: ancient zither with 25 strings
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 瑟瑟 (sè sè) — rustling sound
- 琴瑟 (qín sè) — harmony (lit. zither and se)

## Example Sentences

- 琴瑟和鸣象征夫妻和谐。(Qín sè hé míng xiàng zhēng fū qī hé xié.) — The harmonious playing of qin and se symbolizes marital harmony.

## Notes

瑟 is an ancient Chinese zither, often paired with 琴 in classical texts.
