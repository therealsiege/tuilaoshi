# 执 To Hold

- **Character**: 执
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: to hold, to grasp, persistent
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执着 (zhí zhuó) — persistent
- 固执 (gù zhí) — stubborn

## Example Sentences

- 他很执着于自己的梦想。(Tā hěn zhí zhuó yú zì jǐ de mèng xiǎng.) — He is very persistent about his dreams.

## Notes

执 simplified from 執. Hand holding firmly.
