# 排 Row

- **Character**: 排
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: row, line, to arrange, platoon
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 排队 (pái duì) — to queue up
- 安排 (ān pái) — to arrange

## Example Sentences

- 请大家排队等候。(Qǐng dà jiā pái duì děng hòu.) — Please everyone queue up and wait.

## Notes

排 shows hands arranging things in order, creating rows or lines.
