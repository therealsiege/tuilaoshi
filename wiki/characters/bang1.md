# 帮 To Help

- **Character**: 帮
- **Pinyin**: bāng
- **Meaning**: to help
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 帮助 (bāng zhù) — to help
- 帮忙 (bāng máng) — to do a favor

## Example Sentences

- 你能帮我吗？(Nǐ néng bāng wǒ ma?) — Can you help me?

## Notes

帮 is more colloquial; 帮助 is more formal. 帮忙 is a verb-object compound meaning 'to help out.'
