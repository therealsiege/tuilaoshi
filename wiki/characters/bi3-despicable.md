# 鄙 Despicable

- **Character**: 鄙
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: despicable, to despise, rustic
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鄙视 (bǐ shì) — to despise
- 卑鄙 (bēi bǐ) — despicable, mean

## Example Sentences

- 这种行为很卑鄙。(Zhè zhǒng xíng wéi hěn bēi bǐ.) — This kind of behavior is very despicable.

## Notes

鄙 shows mound/place 阝 and 啚 (lowly), representing remote rustic areas or mean behavior.
