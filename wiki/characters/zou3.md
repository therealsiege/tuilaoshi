# 走 To Walk/To Go

- **Character**: 走
- **Pinyin**: zǒu
- **Meaning**: to walk, to go, to leave
- **Radical**: 走 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 走路 (zǒulù) — to walk (on the road)
- 走开 (zǒukāi) — to go away, to step aside
- 走廊 (zǒuláng) — corridor, hallway
- 行走 (xíngzǒu) — to walk (formal)
- 走运 (zǒuyùn) — to be lucky

## Example Sentences

- 我们走吧。(Wǒmen zǒu ba.) — Let's go.
- 他每天走路去上班。(Tā měitiān zǒulù qù shàngbān.) — He walks to work every day.
- 你走错了。(Nǐ zǒu cuò le.) — You went the wrong way.

## Notes

走 is a pictographic character showing a person in motion — the top part 土 represents the torso and the bottom resembles running legs. It is itself a radical (radical 156) and appears in many movement-related characters: 起 (qǐ, to rise), 赶 (gǎn, to hurry), 超 (chāo, to exceed), 越 (yuè, to cross). In classical Chinese, 走 meant "to run" (the modern word for run is 跑 pǎo), but in modern Chinese it has softened to mean "to walk" or "to go/leave."
