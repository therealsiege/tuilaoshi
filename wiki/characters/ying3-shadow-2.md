# 影 Shadow

- **Character**: 影
- **Pinyin**: yǐng
- **Meaning**: shadow, image, reflection, film
- **Radical**: 彡 (bristle)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 电影 (diàn yǐng) — movie
- 影响 (yǐng xiǎng) — influence

## Example Sentences

- 我们去看电影吧。(Wǒ men qù kàn diàn yǐng ba.) — Let's go watch a movie.

## Notes

影 means shadow or image. Used in电影 (movie), 影响 (influence), 摄影 (photography).
