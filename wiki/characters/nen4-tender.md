# 嫩 Tender

- **Character**: 嫩
- **Pinyin**: nèn
- **Meaning**: tender, delicate, inexperienced
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 稚嫩 (zhì nèn) — young and tender
- 娇嫩 (jiāo nèn) — delicate

## Example Sentences

- 这块肉很嫩。(Zhè kuài ròu hěn nèn.) — This meat is very tender.

## Notes

嫩 combines woman 女 with 敕 (imperial order), historically suggesting youthful delicacy.
