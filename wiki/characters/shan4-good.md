# 善 Good, virtuous, kind, to be good at

- **Character**: 善
- **Pinyin**: shàn
- **Meaning**: good, virtuous, kind, to be善于
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 善良 (shàn liáng) — kind, good-hearted
- 慈善 (cí shàn) — charity, benevolent

## Example Sentences

- 他是个善良的人。(Tā shì ge shànliáng de rén.) — He is a kind person.

## Notes

Fundamental character for goodness and virtue.
