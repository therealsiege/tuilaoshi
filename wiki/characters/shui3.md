# 水 Water

- **Character**: 水
- **Pinyin**: shuǐ
- **Meaning**: water
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 喝水 (hē shuǐ) — to drink water
- 水果 (shuǐ guǒ) — fruit

## Example Sentences

- 请给我一杯水。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yī bēi shuǐ.) — Please give me a glass of water.

## Notes

水 is a pictograph depicting flowing water. The radical form 氵appears on the left side of many water-related characters.
