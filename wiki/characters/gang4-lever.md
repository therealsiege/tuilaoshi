# 杠 Lever, pole, bar

- **Character**: 杠
- **Pinyin**: gàng
- **Meaning**: lever, pole, bar
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 杠杆 (gàng gǎn) — lever
- 单杠 (dān gàng) — horizontal bar (gymnastics)

## Example Sentences

- 杠杆原理在物理学中很重要。(Gàng gǎn yuán lǐ zài wù lǐ xué zhōng hěn zhòng yào.) — The lever principle is very important in physics.

## Notes

Means lever or bar. 抬杠 colloquially means to argue stubbornly. Used in physics and gymnastics.
