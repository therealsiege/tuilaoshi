# 矛 Spear, lance

- **Character**: 矛
- **Pinyin**: máo
- **Meaning**: spear, lance
- **Radical**: 矛 (spear)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 矛盾 (máo dùn) — contradiction
- 长矛 (cháng máo) — lance, pike

## Example Sentences

- 他的话前后矛盾。(Tā de huà qiánhòu máodùn.) — His words contradict each other.

## Notes

An ancient spear. The word 矛盾 (spear and shield) comes from the paradox of a merchant claiming to sell an unstoppable spear and impenetrable shield.
