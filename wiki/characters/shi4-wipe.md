# 拭 To wipe, scrub

- **Character**: 拭
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to wipe, scrub
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 擦拭 (cā shì) — to wipe
- 拭目以待 (shì mù yǐ dài) — wait and see

## Example Sentences

- 让我们拭目以待吧。(Ràng wǒ men shì mù yǐ dài ba.) — Let's wait and see.

## Notes

Literary term for wiping. 拭目以待 is a common idiom meaning to wait and see.
