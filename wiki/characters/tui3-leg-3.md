# 腿 Leg

- **Character**: 腿
- **Pinyin**: tuǐ
- **Meaning**: leg, thigh
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腿脚 (tuǐ jiǎo) — legs and feet
- 踢腿 (tī tuǐ) — to kick

## Example Sentences

- 他踢腿很有力。(Tā tī tuǐ hěn yǒu lì.) — His kicks are very powerful.

## Notes

腿 has flesh 月 with 退 (to retreat), leg for movement.
