# 核 Nucleus, core, pit

- **Character**: 核
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: nucleus, core, pit
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 核心 (hé xīn) — core, nucleus
- 桃核 (táo hé) — peach pit

## Example Sentences

- 这是问题的核心。(Zhè shì wèntí de héxīn.) — This is the core of the problem.

## Notes

Can mean fruit pit or nuclear. Wood radical from fruit stone origin.
