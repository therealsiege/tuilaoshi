# 胶 Glue

- **Character**: 胶
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: glue, rubber
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胶水 (jiāo shuǐ) — glue
- 橡胶 (xiàng jiāo) — rubber

## Example Sentences

- 用胶水粘住。(Yòng jiāo shuǐ zhān zhù.) — Stick it with glue.

## Notes

胶 originally meant animal glue, now for all adhesives and rubber materials.
