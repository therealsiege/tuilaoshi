# 腕 Wrist, 手腕 Wrist

- **Character**: 腕
- **Pinyin**: wàn
- **Meaning**: wrist, 手腕 wrist
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 手腕 (shǒu wàn) — wrist
- 腕表 (wàn biǎo) — wristwatch
- 腕力 (wàn lì) — wrist strength

## Example Sentences

- 她戴着漂亮的手腕表。(Tā dài zhe piàoliang de shǒuwàn biǎo.) — She wears a beautiful wristwatch.

## Notes

Term for wrist joint. 手腕 also has figurative meaning of skill/tactic in handling situations (usually in 有手腕 - skilled/tactful).
