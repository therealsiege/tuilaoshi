# 路 Road

- **Character**: 路
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: road, path, route
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 马路 (mǎ lù) — road
- 走路 (zǒu lù) — to walk

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很远。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn yuǎn.) — This road is very long.

## Notes

路 uses the foot radical 足 (abbreviated as 𧾷) — a path walked by feet. Measure word is 条 (tiáo).
