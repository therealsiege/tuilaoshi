# 股 Share, stock, thigh

- **Character**: 股
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: share, stock, thigh
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 股票 (gǔpiào) — stock, share
- 股东 (gǔdōng) — shareholder

## Example Sentences

- 他买了一些股票。(Tā mǎi le yīxiē gǔpiào.) — He bought some stocks.

## Notes

Essential finance term for shares and stocks.
