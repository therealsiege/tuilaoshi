# 臂 Arm, 手臂 Arm

- **Character**: 臂
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: arm, 手臂 arm
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 手臂 (shǒu bì) — arm
- 臂膀 (bì bǎng) — arm, shoulder
- 前臂 (qián bì) — forearm

## Example Sentences

- 他的手臂很有力。(Tā de shǒubì hěn yǒulì.) — His arms are very strong.

## Notes

Formal term for arm. More commonly used as 手臂 in everyday speech. Can also be used figuratively for support or helper.
