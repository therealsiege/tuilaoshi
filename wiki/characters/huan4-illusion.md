# 幻 Illusion, fantasy, unreal

- **Character**: 幻
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: illusion, fantasy, unreal
- **Radical**: 幺 (tiny)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 幻想 (huàn xiǎng) — fantasy, illusion
- 幻觉 (huàn jué) — hallucination

## Example Sentences

- 这只是你的幻觉。(Zhè zhǐ shì nǐ de huàn jué.) — This is just your illusion.

## Notes

Buddhist concept that phenomena are illusory
