# 粱 Sorghum, millet

- **Character**: 粱
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: sorghum, millet
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 高粱 (gāo liáng) — sorghum
- 膏粱 (gāo liáng) — rich food

## Example Sentences

- 高粱是北方重要的粮食作物。(Gāoliáng shì běifāng zhòngyào de liángshi zuòwù.) — Sorghum is an important grain crop in the north.

## Notes

Sorghum or quality millet. Used for food and also for making liquor. Part of traditional grain classifications.
