# 押 Mortgage, detain, escort

- **Character**: 押
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: mortgage, detain, escort
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抵押 (dǐyā) — mortgage
- 押送 (yāsòng) — send under escort

## Example Sentences

- 警察押送犯人到监狱。(Jǐngchá yāsòng fànrén dào jiānyù.) — Police escorted the prisoner to jail.

## Notes

Legal term for detention, escort, and mortgage.
