# 节 Festival

- **Character**: 节
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: festival, section, beat
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 节奏 (jié zòu) — rhythm
- 音节 (yīn jié) — syllable

## Example Sentences

- 这首歌节奏很快。(Zhè shǒu gē jié zòu hěn kuài.) — This song has a fast rhythm.

## Notes

节 means festival, section, or beat in music.
