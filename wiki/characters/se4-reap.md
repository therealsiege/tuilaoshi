# 穑 Reap, harvest

- **Character**: 穑
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: reap, harvest
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 稼穑 (jià sè) — sowing and reaping
- 穑事 (sè shì) — farming

## Example Sentences

- 稼穑艰难，不可不知。(Jià sè jiān nán, bù kě bù zhī.) — The hardship of farming must be known.

## Notes

Paired with 稼 to represent full farming cycle
