# 则 Rule

- **Character**: 则
- **Pinyin**: zé
- **Meaning**: rule, then, in that case
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 规则 (guī zé) — rule
- 否则 (fǒu zé) — otherwise

## Example Sentences

- 如果你不努力，否则会失败。(Rú guǒ nǐ bù nǔ lì, fǒu zé huì shī bài.) — If you don't work hard, otherwise you will fail.

## Notes

则 represents principles carved in (knife radical). Used as 'then' in conditionals and in compounds meaning rules.
