# 瓮 Urn

- **Character**: 瓮
- **Pinyin**: wèng
- **Meaning**: urn, earthen jar
- **Radical**: 瓦 (tile)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 瓮中捉鳖 (wèng zhōng zhuō biē) — catching turtles in a jar (easy catch)
- 酒瓮 (jiǔ wèng) — wine jar

## Example Sentences

- 那是一个古老的陶瓮。(Nà shì yí gè gǔ lǎo de táo wèng.) — That is an ancient ceramic urn.

## Notes

瓮 simplified from 甕. Tile 瓦 as base, large storage vessel.
