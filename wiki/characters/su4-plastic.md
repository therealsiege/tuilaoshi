# 塑 Plastic, mold

- **Character**: 塑
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: plastic, mold
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 注塑 (zhù sù) — injection molding
- 塑料 (sù liào) — plastic

## Example Sentences

- 塑料制品很多。(Sù liào zhì pǐn hěn duō.) — There are many plastic products.

## Notes

Used in 注塑 (injection molding), 塑料 (plastic), 塑造 (shape)
