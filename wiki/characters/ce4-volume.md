# 册 Volume, booklet

- **Character**: 册
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: volume, booklet
- **Radical**: 丨 (line)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 手册 (shǒu cè) — handbook, manual
- 画册 (huà cè) — picture album

## Example Sentences

- 这套百科全书共有二十册。(Zhè tào bǎikē quánshū gòng yǒu èrshí cè.) — This encyclopedia set has twenty volumes.

## Notes

The character depicts bamboo slips bound together, the ancient form of books. Now means volume or booklet.
