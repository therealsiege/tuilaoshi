# 债 Debt

- **Character**: 债
- **Pinyin**: zhài
- **Meaning**: debt, to owe
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 债务 (zhài wù) — debt
- 欠债 (qiàn zhài) — to owe a debt

## Example Sentences

- 他还清了所有的债务。(Tā huán qīng le suǒ yǒu de zhài wù.) — He paid off all his debts.

## Notes

债 simplified from 債. Shows person 亻 and 责 (responsibility), representing the obligation to repay what is owed.
