# 谈 Talk, Discuss, 谈话 Talk

- **Character**: 谈
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: talk, discuss, 谈话 talk
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谈话 (tán huà) — talk, conversation
- 谈判 (tán pàn) — negotiate
- 访谈 (fǎng tán) — interview

## Example Sentences

- 双方正在谈判合作事宜。(Shuāngfāng zhèngzài tánpàn hézuò shìyí.) — Both sides are negotiating cooperation matters.

## Notes

Common in both casual and formal contexts. 谈判 (negotiate) is key term in business and diplomatic settings.
