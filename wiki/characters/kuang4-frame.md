# 框 Frame

- **Character**: 框
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: frame, framework
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 框架 (kuàng jià) — framework
- 镜框 (jìng kuàng) — picture frame

## Example Sentences

- 这个框架很稳定。(Zhè gè kuàng jià hěn wěn dìng.) — This framework is very stable.

## Notes

框 combines wood 木 and 匡 (to rectify), representing wooden frames that define boundaries.
