# 舍 Residence, hut

- **Character**: 舍
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: residence, hut
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 宿舍 (sù shè) — dormitory
- 舍弃 (shě qì) — abandon, give up

## Example Sentences

- 学生住在宿舍。(Xué shēng zhù zài sù shè.) — Students live in dormitories.

## Notes

Different from 舍 (shě = give up). 茅舍 means thatched cottage.
