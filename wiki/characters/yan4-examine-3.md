# 验 Examine, test, verify

- **Character**: 验
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: examine, test, verify
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 验收 (yànshōu) — check and accept
- 检验 (jiǎnyàn) — test, examine

## Example Sentences

- 工程已通过验收。(Gōngchéng yǐ tōngguò yànshōu.) — The project has passed inspection and acceptance.

## Notes

Formal term for testing and verification.
