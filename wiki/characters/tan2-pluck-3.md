# 弹 To Pluck

- **Character**: 弹
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: to pluck, to play (stringed instrument)
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 弹琴 (tán qín) — to play the zither
- 弹奏 (tán zòu) — to play (music)

## Example Sentences

- 她在弹钢琴。(Tā zài tán gāng qín.) — She is playing the piano.

## Notes

弹 (tán) means to pluck or play; also read as dàn meaning bullet or to flick.
