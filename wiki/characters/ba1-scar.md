# 疤 Scar

- **Character**: 疤
- **Pinyin**: bā
- **Meaning**: scar
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 疤痕 (bā hén) — scar
- 伤疤 (shāng bā) — scar from wound

## Example Sentences

- 他脸上有一道疤痕。(Tā liǎn shàng yǒu yí dào bā hén.) — He has a scar on his face.

## Notes

疤 has sickness 疒 with 巴 (to cling to), mark that clings to skin.
