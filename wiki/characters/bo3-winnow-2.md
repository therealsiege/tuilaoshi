# 簸 To Winnow

- **Character**: 簸
- **Pinyin**: bǒ
- **Meaning**: to winnow, to sift
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 簸箕 (bò ji) — dustpan
- 颠簸 (diān bǒ) — to bump/jolt

## Example Sentences

- 路上颠簸得很厉害。(Lù shàng diān bǒ de hěn lì hài.) — The road was very bumpy.

## Notes

簸 has bamboo 竹 with 皮 (skin), bamboo tray for tossing grain.
