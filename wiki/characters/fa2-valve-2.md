# 阀 Valve

- **Character**: 阀
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: valve, 阀
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 阀门 (fá mén) — valve
- 活阀 (huó fá) — valve

## Example Sentences

- 关闭这个阀门。(Guān bì zhè ge fá mén.) — Close this valve.

## Notes

阀 with door radical suggests gate/control mechanism, standard for valves.
