# 板 Board

- **Character**: 板
- **Pinyin**: bǎn
- **Meaning**: board, plank, edition
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 黑板 (hēi bǎn) — blackboard
- 木板 (mù bǎn) — plank

## Example Sentences

- 在黑板上写字。(Zài hēi bǎn shàng xiě zì.) — Write on the blackboard.

## Notes

板 with wood radical for board/plank, also used for stiffness and editions.
