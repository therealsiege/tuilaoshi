# 肝 Liver

- **Character**: 肝
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: liver
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 肝脏 (gān zàng) — liver (organ)
- 肝炎 (gān yán) — hepatitis

## Example Sentences

- 肝脏是人体重要的解毒器官。(Gān zàng shì rén tǐ zhòng yào de jiě dú qì guān.) — The liver is an important detoxification organ in the human body.

## Notes

Major digestive organ
