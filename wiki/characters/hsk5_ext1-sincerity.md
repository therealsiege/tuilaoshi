# 忱 Sincerity, 热忱 Enthusiasm

- **Character**: 忱
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: sincerity, 热忱 enthusiasm
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 热忱 (rè chén) — enthusiasm, zeal
- 诚忱 (chéng chén) — sincerity
- 盛忱 (shèng chén) — great sincerity

## Example Sentences

- 他工作充满热忱。(Tā gōngzuò chōngmǎn rèchén.) — He works with great enthusiasm.

## Notes

Formal term for sincere feelings and enthusiasm. 热忱 (enthusiasm) is common in professional contexts describing dedication.
