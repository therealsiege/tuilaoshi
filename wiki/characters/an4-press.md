# 按 To Press

- **Character**: 按
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: to press, according to
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 按照 (àn zhào) — according to
- 按时 (àn shí) — on time

## Example Sentences

- 请按照要求做。(Qǐng àn zhào yāo qiú zuò.) — Please do it according to the requirements.

## Notes

按 means to press physically or 'according to' abstractly.
