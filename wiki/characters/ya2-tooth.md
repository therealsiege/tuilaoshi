# 牙 Tooth

- **Character**: 牙
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: tooth, teeth
- **Radical**: 牙 (tooth)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 牙齿 (yá chǐ) — tooth, teeth
- 刷牙 (shuā yá) — to brush teeth

## Example Sentences

- 我每天刷牙两次。(Wǒ měi tiān shuā yá liǎng cì.) — I brush my teeth twice a day.

## Notes

牙 pictograph of molar teeth. 牙齿 complete word. 刷牙 = brush teeth.
