# 微 Tiny, minute, micro

- **Character**: 微
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: tiny, minute, micro
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 微小 (wēi xiǎo) — tiny, minute
- 微妙 (wēi miào) — subtle, delicate

## Example Sentences

- 情况发生了微妙的变化。(Qíng kuàng fā shēng le wēi miào de biàn huà.) — The situation has undergone subtle changes.

## Notes

Indicates minuteness or subtlety. 微笑 means to smile. 微信 is WeChat. 微观 means microscopic.
