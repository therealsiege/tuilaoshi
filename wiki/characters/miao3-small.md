# 渺 Small, tiny, dim, vast

- **Character**: 渺
- **Pinyin**: miǎo
- **Meaning**: small, tiny, dim, vast
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 渺小 (miǎo xiǎo) — tiny, insignificant
- 渺茫 (miǎo máng) — slim, remote (of chances)

## Example Sentences

- 人在宇宙中是渺小的。(Rén zài yǔzhòu zhōng shì miǎoxiǎo de.) — Humans are tiny in the universe.

## Notes

Can mean tiny/insignificant or vast/distant.
