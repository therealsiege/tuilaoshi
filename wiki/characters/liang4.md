# 亮 Bright

- **Character**: 亮
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: bright, light, to shine
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 明亮 (míng liàng) — bright, well-lit
- 漂亮 (piào liang) — beautiful, pretty

## Example Sentences

- 房间很明亮。(Fángjiān hěn míngliàng.) — The room is very bright.

## Notes

亮 combines high 高 (simplified) with mouth 口, representing loud声 or bright light emanating upward.
