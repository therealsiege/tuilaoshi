# 竹 Bamboo

- **Character**: 竹
- **Pinyin**: zhú
- **Meaning**: bamboo
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 胸有成竹 (xiōng yǒu chéng zhú) — have a well-thought-out plan
- 竹子 (zhú zi) — bamboo

## Example Sentences

- 竹子象征着坚韧。(Zhúzi xiàngzhēng zhe jiānrèn.) — Bamboo symbolizes resilience.

## Notes

Used in 胸有成竹: the bamboo painting; bamboo is one of the Four Gentlemen in Chinese art.
