# 蟀 Cricket

- **Character**: 蟀
- **Pinyin**: shuài
- **Meaning**: cricket (in 蟋蟀)
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 蟋蟀 (xī shuài) — cricket

## Example Sentences

- 蟋蟀在草丛里唱歌。(Xī shuài zài cǎo cóng lǐ chàng gē.) — The cricket sings in the grass.

## Notes

蟀 has insect 虫 with 率 (to lead), used only in 蟋蟀 (cricket).
