# 盈 Full, profit, surplus

- **Character**: 盈
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: full, profit, surplus
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 盈利 (yínglì) — profit, gain
- 盈余 (yíngyú) — surplus, profit

## Example Sentences

- 公司今年盈利很多。(Gōngsī jīnnián yínglì hěn duō.) — The company made a large profit this year.

## Notes

Key financial term for profit and surplus.
