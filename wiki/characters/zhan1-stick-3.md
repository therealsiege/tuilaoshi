# 粘 Stick, paste

- **Character**: 粘
- **Pinyin**: zhān
- **Meaning**: stick, paste
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粘贴 (zhān tiē) — paste
- 复制粘贴 (fù zhì zhān tiē) — copy and paste

## Example Sentences

- 复制后请粘贴到这里。(Fù zhì hòu qǐng zhān tiē dào zhè lǐ.) — After copying, please paste it here.

## Notes

Part of 粘贴 (paste), essential clipboard operation in computing
