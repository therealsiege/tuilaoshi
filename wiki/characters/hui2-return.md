# 回 To Return

- **Character**: 回
- **Pinyin**: huí
- **Meaning**: to return, back, to reply
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 回家 (huí jiā) — to go home
- 回答 (huí dá) — to answer

## Example Sentences

- 我五点回家。(Wǒ wǔ diǎn huí jiā.) — I go home at five o'clock.

## Notes

回 shows something turning/returning within an enclosure. Also measure word for occurrences.
