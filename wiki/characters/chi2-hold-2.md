# 持 Hold

- **Character**: 持
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: to hold, to maintain, to persist
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 坚持不懈 (jiān chí bù xiè) — persevere relentlessly
- 坚持 (jiān chí) — to persist

## Example Sentences

- 他坚持每天锻炼。(Tā jiānchí měi tiān duànliàn.) — He persists in exercising every day.

## Notes

Used in 坚持不懈: to hold firm, maintain one's course.
