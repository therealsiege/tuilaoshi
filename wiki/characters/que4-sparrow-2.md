# 雀 Sparrow

- **Character**: 雀
- **Pinyin**: què
- **Meaning**: sparrow, small bird
- **Radical**: 隹 (short-tailed bird)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 麻雀 (má què) — sparrow
- 孔雀 (kǒng què) — peacock

## Example Sentences

- 麻雀虽小，五脏俱全。(Má què suī xiǎo, wǔ zàng jù quán.) — A sparrow may be small but has all organs.

## Notes

雀 has small 小 over bird 隹, a small bird. Proverb about completeness.
