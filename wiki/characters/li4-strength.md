# 力 Strength

- **Character**: 力
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: strength, power
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 力量 (lì liàng) — strength, power
- 努力 (nǔ lì) — to make effort

## Example Sentences

- 举重需要很大的力量。(Jǔzhòng xūyào hěn dà de lìliàng.) — Weightlifting requires great strength.

## Notes

Basic strength character; fundamental in power sports
