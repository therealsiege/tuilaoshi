# 万 Ten Thousand, Myriad, 万一 Just In Case

- **Character**: 万
- **Pinyin**: wàn
- **Meaning**: ten thousand, myriad, 万一 just in case
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 万一 (wàn yī) — just in case, if by chance
- 千万 (qiān wàn) — ten million, be sure to
- 亿万 (yì wàn) — millions

## Example Sentences

- 万一下雨，记得带伞。(Wànyī xià yǔ, jìdé dài sǎn.) — Just in case it rains, remember to bring an umbrella.

## Notes

Fundamental number unit (10,000) in Chinese counting system. 万 is the base for large numbers, unlike Western thousand-based system.
