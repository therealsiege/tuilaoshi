# 缝 Sew, stitch

- **Character**: 缝
- **Pinyin**: féng
- **Meaning**: sew, stitch
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缝补 (féng bǔ) — mend
- 缝纫 (féng rèn) — sewing

## Example Sentences

- 她在缝补衣服。(Tā zài féng bǔ yī fu.) — She is mending clothes.

## Notes

Also read as fèng meaning crack or gap
