# 洲 Continent, island

- **Character**: 洲
- **Pinyin**: zhōu
- **Meaning**: continent, island
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 亚洲 (Yà zhōu) — Asia
- 沙洲 (shā zhōu) — sandbar, shoal

## Example Sentences

- 亚洲是世界上人口最多的大洲。(Yà zhōu shì shì jiè shàng rén kǒu zuì duō de dà zhōu.) — Asia is the most populous continent in the world.

## Notes

Used for continents and islands in rivers.
