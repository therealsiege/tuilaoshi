# 吟 To Chant

- **Character**: 吟
- **Pinyin**: yín
- **Meaning**: to chant, to recite
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吟诗 (yín shī) — to chant poetry
- 呻吟 (shēn yín) — to groan

## Example Sentences

- 他在月下吟诗。(Tā zài yuè xià yín shī.) — He chanted poetry under the moon.

## Notes

吟 refers to reciting or chanting poetry with a melodic quality.
