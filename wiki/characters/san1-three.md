# 三 Three

- **Character**: 三
- **Pinyin**: sān
- **Meaning**: three
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 三月 (sān yuè) — March
- 三个 (sān gè) — three (of something)

## Example Sentences

- 我有三本书。(Wǒ yǒu sān běn shū.) — I have three books.

## Notes

三 is a simple ideograph of three horizontal lines representing the number three.
