# 笠 Bamboo Hat

- **Character**: 笠
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: bamboo hat, conical hat
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 斗笠 (dǒu lì) — bamboo rain hat
- 蓑笠 (suō lì) — straw cape and hat

## Example Sentences

- 渔翁戴着斗笠。(Yú wēng dài zhe dǒu lì.) — The old fisherman wears a bamboo hat.

## Notes

笠 has bamboo 竹 with 立 (stand), bamboo hat standing on head.
