# 去 To Go

- **Character**: 去
- **Pinyin**: qù
- **Meaning**: to go
- **Radical**: 厶 (private)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 出去 (chū qù) — to go out
- 过去 (guò qù) — the past

## Example Sentences

- 我们去吃饭吧。(Wǒ men qù chī fàn ba.) — Let's go eat.

## Notes

去 as a directional complement indicates motion away from the speaker, opposite of 来.
