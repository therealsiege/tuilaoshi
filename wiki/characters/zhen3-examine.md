# 诊 To examine, diagnose, 診 (traditional)

- **Character**: 诊
- **Pinyin**: zhěn
- **Meaning**: to examine, diagnose, 診 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 诊断 (zhěn duàn) — to diagnose
- 门诊 (mén zhěn) — outpatient service

## Example Sentences

- 医生诊断他得了流感。(Yīshēng zhěnduàn tā déle liúgǎn.) — The doctor diagnosed him with influenza.

## Notes

Medical examination and diagnosis.
