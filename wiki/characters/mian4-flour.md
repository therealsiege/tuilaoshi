# 面 Noodles

- **Character**: 面
- **Pinyin**: miàn
- **Meaning**: flour, noodles, face, surface
- **Radical**: 面 (face)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 面条 (miàn tiáo) — noodles
- 面包 (miàn bāo) — bread

## Example Sentences

- 我想吃面条。(Wǒ xiǎng chī miàn tiáo.) — I want to eat noodles.

## Notes

面 means flour, noodles, face, or surface. 面粉 = flour. 表面 = surface.
