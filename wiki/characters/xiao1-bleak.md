# 萧 Bleak, desolate

- **Character**: 萧
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: bleak, desolate
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 萧条 (xiāo tiáo) — depressed, bleak
- 萧瑟 (xiāo sè) — bleak, desolate

## Example Sentences

- 市场萧条。(Shì chǎng xiāo tiáo.) — The market is depressed.

## Notes

经济萧条 is economic depression. Opposite of 繁荣.
