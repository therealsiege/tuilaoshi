# 走 To Walk

- **Character**: 走
- **Pinyin**: zǒu
- **Meaning**: to walk, to go, to leave
- **Radical**: 走 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 走路 (zǒu lù) — to walk
- 走开 (zǒu kāi) — go away

## Example Sentences

- 我每天走路上班。(Wǒ měi tiān zǒu lù shàng bān.) — I walk to work every day.

## Notes

走 depicts a person (土) moving with legs (underneath). Contrast with 跑 (run) which is faster.
