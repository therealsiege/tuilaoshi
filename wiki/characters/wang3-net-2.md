# 网 Net

- **Character**: 网
- **Pinyin**: wǎng
- **Meaning**: net, network, web
- **Radical**: 网 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 网络 (wǎng luò) — network
- 上网 (shàng wǎng) — go online

## Example Sentences

- 现在网络很慢。(Xiàn zài wǎng luò hěn màn.) — The network is very slow now.

## Notes

网 is the pictograph for a net, now ubiquitous in internet terminology.
