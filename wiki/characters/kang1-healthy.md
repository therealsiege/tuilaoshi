# 康 Healthy, peaceful, abundant

- **Character**: 康
- **Pinyin**: kāng
- **Meaning**: healthy, peaceful, abundant
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 健康 (jiàn kāng) — healthy, health
- 康复 (kāng fù) — to recover, rehabilitate

## Example Sentences

- 祝你早日康复。(Zhù nǐ zǎo rì kāngfù.) — Wishing you a speedy recovery.

## Notes

Health, wellness, and prosperity.
