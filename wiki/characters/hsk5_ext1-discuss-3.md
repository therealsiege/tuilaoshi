# 议 Discuss, Proposal, 会议 Meeting

- **Character**: 议
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: discuss, proposal, 会议 meeting
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 会议 (huì yì) — meeting, conference
- 协议 (xié yì) — agreement
- 建议 (jiàn yì) — suggest, proposal

## Example Sentences

- 我建议明天开会讨论这个问题。(Wǒ jiànyì míngtiān kāihuì tǎolùn zhège wèntí.) — I suggest we hold a meeting tomorrow to discuss this issue.

## Notes

Essential character in professional contexts. 会议 (meeting) and 建议 (suggestion) are among the most common workplace terms.
