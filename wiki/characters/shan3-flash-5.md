# 闪 Flash

- **Character**: 闪
- **Pinyin**: shǎn
- **Meaning**: to flash, dodge
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 闪躲 (shǎn duǒ) — to dodge, evade
- 闪避 (shǎn bì) — to dodge

## Example Sentences

- 拳击手迅速闪开对手的进攻。(Quánjīshǒu xùnsù shǎnkāi duìshǒu de jìngōng.) — The boxer quickly dodged the opponent's attack.

## Notes

Dodging and flashing; evasive movement
