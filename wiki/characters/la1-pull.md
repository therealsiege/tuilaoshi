# 拉 To Pull

- **Character**: 拉
- **Pinyin**: lā
- **Meaning**: to pull, to drag
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拉开 (lā kāi) — to pull open
- 拉手 (lā shǒu) — handle; to hold hands

## Example Sentences

- 请拉门。(Qǐng lā mén.) — Please pull the door.

## Notes

拉 means to pull (opposite of 推, push). 拉肚子 = to have diarrhea.
