# 浆 Paste

- **Character**: 浆
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: paste, pulp, starch
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浆糊 (jiāng hú) — paste, glue
- 泥浆 (ní jiāng) — mud, slurry

## Example Sentences

- 陶工用泥浆把两个部分粘合在一起。(Táogōng yòng níjiāng bǎ liǎng gè bùfen zhānhé zài yīqǐ.) — The potter used slip to join the two parts together.

## Notes

浆 refers to paste, pulp, or liquid suspension. In ceramics, slip (泥浆) is used for joining pieces.
