# 施 Apply, bestow, carry out

- **Character**: 施
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: apply, bestow, carry out
- **Radical**: 方 (direction)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 施肥 (shī féi) — apply fertilizer
- 实施 (shí shī) — implement, carry out

## Example Sentences

- 农民正在给庄稼施肥。(Nóngmín zhèngzài gěi zhuāngjia shī féi.) — The farmers are applying fertilizer to the crops.

## Notes

To apply or implement. In agriculture, commonly refers to applying fertilizer. Also means to bestow or grant.
