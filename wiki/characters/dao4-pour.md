# 倒 To Pour

- **Character**: 倒
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to pour, to invert; to fall (dǎo)
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倒水 (dào shuǐ) — to pour water
- 摔倒 (shuāi dǎo) — to fall down

## Example Sentences

- 请给我倒杯水。(Qǐng gěi wǒ dào bēi shuǐ.) — Please pour me a glass of water.

## Notes

倒 as dào: to pour or invert. As dǎo: to fall, collapse.
