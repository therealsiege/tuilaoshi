# 诵 To Recite

- **Character**: 诵
- **Pinyin**: sòng
- **Meaning**: to recite, to chant
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 朗诵 (lǎng sòng) — to recite aloud
- 背诵 (bèi sòng) — to recite from memory

## Example Sentences

- 学生们在朗诵古诗。(Xué shēng men zài lǎng sòng gǔ shī.) — The students are reciting ancient poetry aloud.

## Notes

诵 emphasizes reciting from memory or reading aloud.
