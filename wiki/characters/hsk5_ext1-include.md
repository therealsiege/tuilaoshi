# 括 Include, Encompass, 包括 Include

- **Character**: 括
- **Pinyin**: kuò
- **Meaning**: include, encompass, 包括 include
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 包括 (bāo kuò) — include, consist of
- 概括 (gài kuò) — summarize, generalize
- 囊括 (náng kuò) — include all, embrace

## Example Sentences

- 这个项目包括三个阶段。(Zhège xiàngmù bāokuò sān gè jiēduàn.) — This project includes three stages.

## Notes

Almost always appears in compound words, most commonly 包括 (include) and 概括 (summarize). Rarely used alone in modern Chinese.
