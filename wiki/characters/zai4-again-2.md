# 再 Again, re-

- **Character**: 再
- **Pinyin**: zài
- **Meaning**: again, re-
- **Radical**: 冂 (down box)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 再生 (zài shēng) — regenerate, recycle
- 再利用 (zài lì yòng) — reuse

## Example Sentences

- 材料可以再利用。(Cái liào kě yǐ zài lì yòng.) — Materials can be reused.

## Notes

Used in 再生 (recycle), 再利用 (reuse), sustainability
