# 文 — Writing / Culture

- **Character**: 文
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: writing, script; culture, literature; civil
- **Radical**: 文 (script — itself a radical)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 中文 (Zhōngwén) — Chinese (language)
- 文化 (wénhuà) — culture
- 文学 (wénxué) — literature
- 文章 (wénzhāng) — essay, article
- 文字 (wénzì) — writing, script, characters
- 文件 (wénjiàn) — document, file

## Example Sentences

- 你的中文说得很好。(Nǐ de Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.) — Your Chinese is spoken very well.
- 我对中国文化很感兴趣。(Wǒ duì Zhōngguó wénhuà hěn gǎn xìngqù.) — I'm very interested in Chinese culture.
- 这篇文章写得不错。(Zhè piān wénzhāng xiě de búcuò.) — This article is well written.

## Notes

文 is a pictograph originally depicting a person with decorative tattoo patterns on the chest, representing "decoration" or "pattern." From this came the meanings of "writing" (patterned marks) and "culture" (the ornament of civilization), in contrast with 武 (wǔ, martial/military). 文 is both a standalone character and a radical. It appears in language names — 中文, 英文 (Yīngwén, English), 法文 (Fǎwén, French) — and is a foundational character for discussing literature, culture, and written language.
