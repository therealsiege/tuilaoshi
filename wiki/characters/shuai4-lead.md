# 率 Lead, frank, forthright

- **Character**: 率
- **Pinyin**: shuài
- **Meaning**: lead, frank,率直
- **Radical**: 玄 (dark)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 率直 (shuài zhí) — frank, straightforward
- 草率 (cǎo shuài) — careless, hasty

## Example Sentences

- 他为人率直，从不拐弯抹角。(Tā wéi rén shuài zhí, cóng bù guǎi wān mò jiǎo.) — He is forthright and never beats around the bush.

## Notes

Also pronounced lǜ meaning rate or ratio.
