# 热 Fever

- **Character**: 热
- **Pinyin**: rè
- **Meaning**: fever, heat
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 发热 (fā rè) — to have a fever
- 高热 (gāo rè) — high fever
- 退热 (tuì rè) — to reduce fever

## Example Sentences

- 他发热39度。(Tā fā rè sān shí jiǔ dù.) — He has a fever of 39 degrees.

## Notes

Elevated temperature; cardinal symptom.
