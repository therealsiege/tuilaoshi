# 限 Limit

- **Character**: 限
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: limit, boundary, to limit
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 限制 (xiàn zhì) — to limit
- 无限 (wú xiàn) — unlimited

## Example Sentences

- 时间有限。(Shí jiān yǒu xiàn.) — Time is limited.

## Notes

限 shows boundary markers on a mound, representing limits and boundaries.
