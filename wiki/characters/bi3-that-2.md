# 彼 That Other

- **Character**: 彼
- **Pinyin**: bǐ
- **Meaning**: that, the other, he/she
- **Radical**: 彳 (step)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 知己知彼 (zhī jǐ zhī bǐ) — know yourself and your enemy
- 彼此 (bǐ cǐ) — each other

## Example Sentences

- 彼此彼此。(Bǐcǐ bǐcǐ.) — Likewise; same to you.

## Notes

Used in 知己知彼: know the other/enemy; classical pronoun for the other party.
