# 书 Book

- **Character**: 书
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: book, to write
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 读书 (dú shū) — to study
- 评书 (píng shū) — storytelling

## Example Sentences

- 他在听评书。(Tā zài tīng píng shū.) — He is listening to storytelling.

## Notes

书 means book or to write; in 评书 refers to narrative storytelling.
