# 崖 Cliff

- **Character**: 崖
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: cliff, precipice
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 悬崖 (xuán yá) — cliff, precipice
- 山崖 (shān yá) — cliff

## Example Sentences

- 站在悬崖边很危险。(Zhàn zài xuányá biān hěn wēixiǎn.) — Standing at the edge of a cliff is dangerous.

## Notes

崖 combines mountain 山 with 厓 (cliff edge), representing a steep cliff face.
