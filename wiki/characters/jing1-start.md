# 惊 Alarmed

- **Character**: 惊
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: to start, to be alarmed
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惊讶 (jīng yà) — surprised
- 吃惊 (chī jīng) — shocked

## Example Sentences

- 我对这个结果很惊讶。(Wǒ duì zhè ge jié guǒ hěn jīng yà.) — I am surprised by this result.

## Notes

惊 simplified from 驚. Heart 忄 plus simplified component, startled.
