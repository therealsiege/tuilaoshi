# 蛮 Barbarous, quite, very

- **Character**: 蛮
- **Pinyin**: mán
- **Meaning**: barbarous, quite, very
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 野蛮 (yě mán) — barbarous, savage
- 蛮横 (mán hèng) — rude and unreasonable

## Example Sentences

- 他的态度很蛮横。(Tā de tài dù hěn mán hèng.) — His attitude is very rude.

## Notes

Uncivilized behavior or colloquially means quite/very
