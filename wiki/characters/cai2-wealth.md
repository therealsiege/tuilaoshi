# 财 Wealth

- **Character**: 财
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: wealth, money, property
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 财产 (cái chǎn) — property
- 发财 (fā cái) — to get rich

## Example Sentences

- 恭喜发财！(Gōng xǐ fā cái!) — Wishing you prosperity! (New Year greeting)

## Notes

财 simplified from 財. The shell/money radical 贝 clearly indicates its connection to wealth and financial matters.
