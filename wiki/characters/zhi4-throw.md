# 掷 Throw

- **Character**: 掷
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: to throw, cast
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 投掷 (tóu zhì) — to throw (track and field)
- 掷铁饼 (zhì tiě bǐng) — discus throw

## Example Sentences

- 投掷项目需要力量和技巧。(Tóuzhì xiàngmù xūyào lìliàng hé jìqiǎo.) — Throwing events require strength and technique.

## Notes

Formal throwing term in track and field events
