# 壮 Strong, robust, magnificent

- **Character**: 壮
- **Pinyin**: zhuàng
- **Meaning**: strong, robust, magnificent
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 壮观 (zhuàng guān) — spectacular, magnificent
- 强壮 (qiáng zhuàng) — strong, sturdy

## Example Sentences

- 瀑布的景象非常壮观。(Pù bù de jǐng xiàng fēi cháng zhuàng guān.) — The waterfall is a spectacular sight.

## Notes

Indicates strength or magnificence. 壮丽 means magnificent. 壮志 means lofty aspirations.
