# 欠 Owe, lack, yawn

- **Character**: 欠
- **Pinyin**: qiàn
- **Meaning**: owe, lack, yawn
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 欠债 (qiànzhài) — owe a debt
- 欠款 (qiànkuǎn) — money owed

## Example Sentences

- 他还欠我一笔钱。(Tā hái qiàn wǒ yī bǐ qián.) — He still owes me some money.

## Notes

Common for owing money or lacking something.
