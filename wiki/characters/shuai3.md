# 甩 To Fling

- **Character**: 甩
- **Pinyin**: shuǎi
- **Meaning**: to throw, to fling, to toss
- **Radical**: 用 (to use)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 甩手 (shuǎi shǒu) — to swing arms
- 甩开 (shuǎi kāi) — to fling away

## Example Sentences

- 他甩开了我的手。(Tā shuǎi kāi le wǒ de shǒu.) — He flung my hand away.

## Notes

甩 用 (to use) with throwing motion, flinging forcefully.
