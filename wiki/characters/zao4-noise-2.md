# 噪 Noise

- **Character**: 噪
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: noise
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 噪音 (zào yīn) — noise
- 噪声 (zào shēng) — noise

## Example Sentences

- 信号噪音比很重要。(Xìn hào zào yīn bǐ hěn zhòng yào.) — The signal-to-noise ratio is important.

## Notes

Used in 噪音 (noise), 噪声 (noise), signal processing
