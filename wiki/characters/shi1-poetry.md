# 诗 Poetry

- **Character**: 诗
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: poetry, poem
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 诗歌 (shī gē) — poetry, poem
- 古诗 (gǔ shī) — classical poetry

## Example Sentences

- 他会背很多古诗。(Tā huì bèi hěn duō gǔ shī.) — He can recite many classical poems.

## Notes

诗 means poetry or poem. Used in诗歌 (poetry), 诗人 (poet), 唐诗 (Tang poetry).
