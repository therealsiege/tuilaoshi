# 排 Row

- **Character**: 排
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: row, to arrange, to line up, to exclude
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 排队 (pái duì) — to queue, to line up
- 安排 (ān pái) — to arrange

## Example Sentences

- 大家在排队买票。(Dà jiā zài pái duì mǎi piào.) — Everyone is queuing to buy tickets.

## Notes

排 means to arrange in a row or排 (measure word for rows). Essential for排队 (to queue).
