# 炸 To deep-fry, to explode

- **Character**: 炸
- **Pinyin**: zhá
- **Meaning**: to deep-fry, to explode
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 油炸 (yóu zhá) — to deep-fry
- 爆炸 (bào zhà) — to explode, explosion

## Example Sentences

- 这家店炸鸡很好吃。(Zhè jiā diàn zhá jī hěn hǎochī.) — The fried chicken at this shop is very delicious.

## Notes

Can mean deep-frying food or exploding.
