# 疡 Sore

- **Character**: 疡
- **Pinyin**: yáng
- **Meaning**: ulcer, sore
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 溃疡 (kuì yáng) — ulcer
- 疮疡 (chuāng yáng) — sores and ulcers
- 口疡 (kǒu yáng) — mouth ulcer

## Example Sentences

- 口腔溃疡很疼。(Kǒu qiāng kuì yáng hěn téng.) — Mouth ulcers are very painful.

## Notes

Classical term for ulcers and sores; used in TCM.
