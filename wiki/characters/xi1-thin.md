# 稀 Thin, sparse, dilute

- **Character**: 稀
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: thin, sparse, dilute
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 稀释 (xī shì) — dilute
- 稀少 (xī shǎo) — rare, scarce

## Example Sentences

- 用水稀释这种溶液。(Yòng shuǐ xī shì zhè zhǒng róng yè.) — Dilute this solution with water.

## Notes

Low concentration
