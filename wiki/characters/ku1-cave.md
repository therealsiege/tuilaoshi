# 窟 Cave, den, hole

- **Character**: 窟
- **Pinyin**: kū
- **Meaning**: cave, den, hole
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 石窟 (shí kū) — grotto, stone cave
- 窟窿 (kū long) — hole, cavity

## Example Sentences

- 敦煌石窟是著名的佛教艺术宝库。(Dūn huáng shí kū shì zhù míng de Fó jiào yì shù bǎo kù.) — The Dunhuang Grottoes are a famous treasure house of Buddhist art.

## Notes

Indicates caves or holes, especially in cultural contexts like Buddhist grottoes. 窟窿 means hole or gap.
