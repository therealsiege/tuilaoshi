# 惆 Despondent

- **Character**: 惆
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: despondent, sad
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惆怅 (chóu chàng) — melancholy
- 惆惋 (chóu wǎn) — sorrowful

## Example Sentences

- 他惆怅了很久。(Tā chóu chàng le hěn jiǔ.) — He was melancholic for a long time.

## Notes

惆 has heart 忄 with 周 (cycle), heart going in circles with sorrow.
