# 当 To Act As

- **Character**: 当
- **Pinyin**: dāng
- **Meaning**: to act as, when, should
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 当然 (dāng rán) — of course
- 当时 (dāng shí) — at that time

## Example Sentences

- 当然可以！(Dāng rán kě yǐ!) — Of course!

## Notes

当 as dāng means 'when/to act as.' As dàng means 'to consider as' or 'to pawn.'
