# 探 To Explore

- **Character**: 探
- **Pinyin**: tàn
- **Meaning**: to explore, to probe, to visit
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 探索 (tàn suǒ) — to explore
- 探测 (tàn cè) — to probe

## Example Sentences

- 他们探索未知的领域。(Tā men tàn suǒ wèi zhī de lǐng yù.) — They explore unknown territories.

## Notes

探 has hand 扌 reaching into 穴 (cave), to probe deeply.
