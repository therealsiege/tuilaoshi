# 壑 Gully, ravine, valley

- **Character**: 壑
- **Pinyin**: hè
- **Meaning**: gully, ravine, valley
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 沟壑 (gōu hè) — gully, ravine
- 千沟万壑 (qiān gōu wàn hè) — countless gullies

## Example Sentences

- 黄土高原上千沟万壑。(Huáng tǔ gāo yuán shàng qiān gōu wàn hè.) — The Loess Plateau is crisscrossed with countless gullies.

## Notes

Commonly used to describe erosion patterns.
