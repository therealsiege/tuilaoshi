# 黄 Yellow

- **Character**: 黄
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: yellow
- **Radical**: 黄 (yellow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 黄色 (huáng sè) — yellow color
- 黄河 (huáng hé) — Yellow River

## Example Sentences

- 黄色是我最喜欢的颜色。(Huáng sè shì wǒ zuì xǐ huan de yán sè.) — Yellow is my favorite color.

## Notes

黄 was historically the imperial color in China. 黄河 (Yellow River) gets its name from the yellow silt it carries.
