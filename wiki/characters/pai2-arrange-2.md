# 排 Arrange

- **Character**: 排
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: to arrange, rank
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 排名 (pái míng) — ranking, to rank
- 排行 (pái háng) — ranking

## Example Sentences

- 他在世界排名第三。(Tā zài shìjiè páimíng dì sān.) — He ranks third in the world.

## Notes

Rankings; ordering by performance
