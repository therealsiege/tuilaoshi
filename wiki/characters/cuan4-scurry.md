# 窜 To scurry, to flee, to jump

- **Character**: 窜
- **Pinyin**: cuàn
- **Meaning**: to scurry, to flee, to jump
- **Radical**: 穴(cave)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 流窜 (liú cuàn) — to roam, to flee
- 逃窜 (táo cuàn) — to flee in disorder

## Example Sentences

- 老鼠从洞里窜了出来。(Lǎoshǔ cóng dòng lǐ cuàn le chūlái.) — The mouse scurried out of the hole.

## Notes

Implies quick, darting movement or fleeing.
