# 杆 Pole

- **Character**: 杆
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: pole, rod
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing
- 旗杆 (qí gān) — flagpole

## Example Sentences

- 旗杆很高。(Qí gān hěn gāo.) — The flagpole is very tall.

## Notes

杆 as gān means pole/rod; as gǎn means stick/rod. Essential for vertical structures.
