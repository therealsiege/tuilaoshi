# 收 Receive, collect

- **Character**: 收
- **Pinyin**: shōu
- **Meaning**: receive, collect
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 收入 (shōu rù) — income, revenue
- 收费 (shōu fèi) — charge a fee

## Example Sentences

- 公司今年收入增加了。(Gōng sī jīn nián shōu rù zēng jiā le.) — The company's income increased this year.

## Notes

回收 means recycle. 税收 means tax revenue.
