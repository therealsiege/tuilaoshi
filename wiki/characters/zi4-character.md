# 字 Character

- **Character**: 字
- **Pinyin**: zì
- **Meaning**: character, word, letter
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 汉字 (hàn zì) — Chinese character
- 写字 (xiě zì) — to write characters

## Example Sentences

- 这个字怎么写？(Zhè gè zì zěn me xiě?) — How do you write this character?

## Notes

字 originally meant a child under a roof, suggesting nurturing or education. Now refers to written characters.
