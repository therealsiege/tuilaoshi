# 怆 Mournful

- **Character**: 怆
- **Pinyin**: chuàng
- **Meaning**: mournful, sorrowful
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 悲怆 (bēi chuàng) — sorrowful
- 凄怆 (qī chuàng) — mournful

## Example Sentences

- 他的歌声悲怆动人。(Tā de gē shēng bēi chuàng dòng rén.) — His singing is movingly sorrowful.

## Notes

怆 simplified from 愴. Heart 忄 with 仓 (warehouse), heart feeling empty and sad.
