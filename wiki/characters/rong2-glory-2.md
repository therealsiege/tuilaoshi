# 荣 Glory

- **Character**: 荣
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: glory, honor, flourish
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 光荣 (guāng róng) — glory, honor
- 荣誉 (róng yù) — honor, credit

## Example Sentences

- 为国争光是我们的荣誉。(Wèi guó zhēng guāng shì wǒ men de róng yù.) — Bringing glory to the country is our honor.

## Notes

荣 is simplified from 榮. Originally depicted a flourishing tree.
