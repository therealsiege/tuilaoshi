# 罚 Punish

- **Character**: 罚
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: to punish, penalty
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 罚球 (fá qiú) — penalty kick, free throw
- 处罚 (chǔ fá) — to punish

## Example Sentences

- 裁判判了一个罚球。(Cáipàn pàn le yī gè fáqiú.) — The referee awarded a penalty.
