# 结 Knot, settle, conclude

- **Character**: 结
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: knot, settle, conclude
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 结算 (jiésuàn) — settle accounts
- 结果 (jiéguǒ) — result, outcome

## Example Sentences

- 月底结算账目。(Yuèdǐ jiésuàn zhàngmù.) — Settle the accounts at the end of the month.

## Notes

Used for settling accounts and conclusions.
