# 舞 To Dance

- **Character**: 舞
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: to dance, dance
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — to dance
- 舞蹈 (wǔ dǎo) — dance

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢跳舞。(Tā xǐ huān tiào wǔ.) — She likes to dance.

## Notes

舞 shows person with sleeves waving, representing dancing movements.
