# 韧 Tough

- **Character**: 韧
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: tough, tenacious
- **Radical**: 韦 (tanned leather)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 韧性 (rèn xìng) — tenacity, toughness
- 坚韧 (jiān rèn) — tough, resilient

## Example Sentences

- 长跑需要很强的韧性。(Chángpǎo xūyào hěn qiáng de rènxìng.) — Long-distance running requires strong tenacity.

## Notes

Toughness and resilience; mental and physical
