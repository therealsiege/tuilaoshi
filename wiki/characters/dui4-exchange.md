# 兑 To Exchange

- **Character**: 兑
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: to exchange, to cash
- **Radical**: 儿 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 兑换 (duì huàn) — to exchange, to convert
- 兑现 (duì xiàn) — to cash, to honor (promise)

## Example Sentences

- 我需要兑换一些人民币。(Wǒ xū yào duì huàn yī xiē rén mín bì.) — I need to exchange some RMB.

## Notes

兑 simplified from 兌. Originally represented pouring liquid from one vessel to another, extended to exchange.
