# 惰 Lazy, idle, inert

- **Character**: 惰
- **Pinyin**: duò
- **Meaning**: lazy, idle, inert
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 懒惰 (lǎn duò) — lazy, idle
- 惰性 (duò xìng) — inertia, laziness

## Example Sentences

- 懒惰是成功的大敌。(Lǎnduò shì chénggōng de dàdí.) — Laziness is the great enemy of success.

## Notes

Used for laziness and also inertia in physics contexts.
