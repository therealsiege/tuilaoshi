# 赔 To Compensate

- **Character**: 赔
- **Pinyin**: péi
- **Meaning**: to compensate, to pay for loss
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 赔偿 (péi cháng) — to compensate
- 赔钱 (péi qián) — to lose money

## Example Sentences

- 保险公司会赔偿损失。(Bǎo xiǎn gōng sī huì péi cháng sǔn shī.) — The insurance company will compensate for the losses.

## Notes

赔 shows shell/money 贝 and 咅 (to cultivate), representing paying compensation for losses.
