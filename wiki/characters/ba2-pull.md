# 拔 To Pull Out

- **Character**: 拔
- **Pinyin**: bá
- **Meaning**: to pull up, to pull out
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拔河 (bá hé) — tug of war
- 选拔 (xuǎn bá) — to select

## Example Sentences

- 拔河比赛很有趣。(Bá hé bǐ sài hěn yǒu qù.) — Tug of war is fun.

## Notes

拔 means to pull out or uproot.
