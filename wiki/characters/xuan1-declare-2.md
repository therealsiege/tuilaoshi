# 宣 Declare, announce

- **Character**: 宣
- **Pinyin**: xuān
- **Meaning**: declare, announce
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 宣传 (xuān chuán) — publicize, promote
- 宣布 (xuān bù) — announce, declare

## Example Sentences

- 公司加强品牌宣传。(Gōng sī jiā qiáng pǐn pái xuān chuán.) — The company strengthens brand promotion.

## Notes

宣传部 is publicity department. 宣扬 means advocate/publicize.
