# 天 Day/Sky/Heaven

- **Character**: 天
- **Pinyin**: tiān
- **Meaning**: day, sky, heaven
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 天气 (tiānqì) — weather
- 今天 (jīntiān) — today
- 明天 (míngtiān) — tomorrow
- 昨天 (zuótiān) — yesterday
- 天空 (tiānkōng) — sky
- 每天 (měitiān) — every day

## Example Sentences

- 今天天气很好。(Jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.) — The weather is very nice today.
- 你每天几点起床？(Nǐ měitiān jǐ diǎn qǐchuáng?) — What time do you get up every day?
- 明天见！(Míngtiān jiàn!) — See you tomorrow!

## Notes

天 places a horizontal stroke above 大 (dà, big/person with arms outstretched), representing what is above a person — the sky. In Chinese thought, 天 carries philosophical weight as "heaven" or the natural order, as in 天命 (tiānmìng, mandate of heaven). For counting days, 天 doubles as a measure word: 一天 (one day), 三天 (three days). The character is also a common surname (Tiān).
