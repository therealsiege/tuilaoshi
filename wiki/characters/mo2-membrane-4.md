# 膜 Membrane

- **Character**: 膜
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: membrane, film
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 薄膜 (báo mó) — membrane, thin film
- 角膜 (jiǎo mó) — cornea
- 鼓膜 (gǔ mó) — eardrum

## Example Sentences

- 角膜移植手术很复杂。(Jiǎo mó yí zhí shǒu shù hěn fù zá.) — Corneal transplant surgery is very complex.

## Notes

Thin tissue layers; appears in many anatomical structures.
