# 房 Room

- **Character**: 房
- **Pinyin**: fáng
- **Meaning**: room, house, constellation
- **Radical**: 户(door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 房间 (fáng jiān) — room
- 房宿 (fáng xiù) — Room constellation

## Example Sentences

- 房宿在二十八宿中代表天帝的宫室。(Fáng xiù zài èrshíbā xiù zhōng dàibiǎo tiāndì de gōngshì.) — Room constellation represents the celestial emperor's palace in the 28 mansions.

## Notes

房 means room or house. In astronomy, the Room constellation, one of the 28 lunar mansions.
