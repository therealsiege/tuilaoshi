# 轴 Axis, axle, shaft

- **Character**: 轴
- **Pinyin**: zhóu
- **Meaning**: axis, axle, shaft
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 轴心 (zhóu xīn) — axis, pivot
- 主轴 (zhǔ zhóu) — spindle, main shaft

## Example Sentences

- 地球绕着地轴旋转。(Dì qiú rào zhe dì zhóu xuán zhuǎn.) — The Earth rotates around its axis.

## Notes

Rotating or central shaft
