# 测 Measure, survey, conjecture

- **Character**: 测
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: measure, survey, conjecture
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 测量 (cè liáng) — measure, survey
- 推测 (tuī cè) — conjecture, infer

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要测量这块地的面积。(Wǒ men xū yào cè liáng zhè kuài dì de miàn jī.) — We need to measure the area of this land.

## Notes

Used for both physical measurement and mental conjecture
