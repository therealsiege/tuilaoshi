# 法 Law

- **Character**: 法
- **Pinyin**: fǎ
- **Meaning**: law, method, way
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 法律 (fǎ lǜ) — law
- 方法 (fāng fǎ) — method

## Example Sentences

- 这个方法很有效。(Zhè ge fāng fǎ hěn yǒu xiào.) — This method is very effective.

## Notes

法 combines water and 去 (to go/remove), representing fair judgment like water flowing level. Core character for law and methods.
