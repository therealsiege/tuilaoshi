# 哨 Whistle

- **Character**: 哨
- **Pinyin**: shào
- **Meaning**: whistle, sentry
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 口哨 (kǒu shào) — whistle (sound)
- 哨子 (shào zi) — whistle (object)

## Example Sentences

- 裁判吹响了哨子。(Cái pàn chuī xiǎng le shào zi.) — The referee blew the whistle.

## Notes

哨 refers to whistles (instrument and sound) and sentries.
