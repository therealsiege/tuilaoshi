# 印 Print, seal

- **Character**: 印
- **Pinyin**: yìn
- **Meaning**: print, seal
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 打印 (dǎ yìn) — print
- 印刷 (yìn shuā) — print, printing

## Example Sentences

- 请打印这个文件。(Qǐng dǎ yìn zhè gè wén jiàn.) — Please print this file.

## Notes

Used in 打印 (print), 打印机 (printer), 印刷 (printing)
