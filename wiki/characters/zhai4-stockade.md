# 寨 Stockade, fortress, village

- **Character**: 寨
- **Pinyin**: zhài
- **Meaning**: stockade, fortress, village
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 山寨 (shān zhài) — mountain stronghold, knockoff
- 村寨 (cūn zhài) — village

## Example Sentences

- 这个苗族村寨保存着传统文化。(Zhè gè Miáo zú cūn zhài bǎo cún zhe chuán tǒng wén huà.) — This Miao village preserves traditional culture.

## Notes

Common in minority area village names; 山寨 now means knockoff.
