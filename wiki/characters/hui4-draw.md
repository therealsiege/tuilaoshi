# 绘 To Draw

- **Character**: 绘
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: to draw, paint
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绘画 (huì huà) — to draw, painting
- 描绘 (miáo huì) — to depict, describe

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢绘画。(Tā xǐhuan huìhuà.) — She likes painting.

## Notes

绘 combines silk 糹 with 会 (to meet), representing the meeting of colored threads in ancient embroidery, extended to painting.
