# 腺 Gland

- **Character**: 腺
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: gland
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腺体 (xiàn tǐ) — gland
- 甲状腺 (jiǎ zhuàng xiàn) — thyroid gland

## Example Sentences

- 内分泌腺控制激素分泌。(Nèi fēn mì xiàn kòng zhì jī sù fēn mì.) — Endocrine glands control hormone secretion.

## Notes

Organs that secrete hormones or other substances
