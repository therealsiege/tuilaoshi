# 逗 Tease, amuse, stay

- **Character**: 逗
- **Pinyin**: dòu
- **Meaning**: tease, amuse, stay
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 逗笑 (dòu xiào) — amuse, make laugh
- 逗留 (dòu liú) — stay

## Example Sentences

- 他总是逗大家笑。(Tā zǒng shì dòu dà jiā xiào.) — He always makes everyone laugh.

## Notes

Teasing playfully or amusing someone
