# 子 Child, seed, particle

- **Character**: 子
- **Pinyin**: zǐ
- **Meaning**: child, seed, particle
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 原子 (yuán zǐ) — atom
- 分子 (fēn zǐ) — molecule

## Example Sentences

- 原子是物质的基本单位。(Yuán zǐ shì wù zhì de jī běn dān wèi.) — Atoms are the basic units of matter.

## Notes

Used as suffix for particles in physics
