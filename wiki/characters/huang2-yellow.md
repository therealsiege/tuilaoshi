# 黄 Yellow

- **Character**: 黄
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: yellow
- **Radical**: 黄 (yellow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 黄色 (huáng sè) — yellow color
- 黄河 (Huáng Hé) — Yellow River

## Example Sentences

- 这朵花是黄色的。(Zhè duǒ huā shì huáng sè de.) — This flower is yellow.

## Notes

黄 represents imperial color in ancient China. 黄河 named for its yellow-brown silt.
