# 哪 Which

- **Character**: 哪
- **Pinyin**: nǎ
- **Meaning**: which, where
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 哪里 (nǎ lǐ) — where
- 哪个 (nǎ ge) — which one

## Example Sentences

- 你去哪里？(Nǐ qù nǎ lǐ?) — Where are you going?

## Notes

哪 is the question form of 那. 哪里 also serves as a humble reply to compliments, meaning 'not at all.'
