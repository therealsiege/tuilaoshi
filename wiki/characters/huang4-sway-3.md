# 晃 To Sway

- **Character**: 晃
- **Pinyin**: huàng
- **Meaning**: to sway, to flash
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 晃动 (huàng dòng) — to sway
- 摇晃 (yáo huàng) — to rock

## Example Sentences

- 树枝在风中摇晃。(Shù zhī zài fēng zhōng yáo huàng.) — The branches sway in the wind.

## Notes

晃 combines sun 日 and light, representing flashing or swaying movement.
