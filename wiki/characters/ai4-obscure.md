# 暧 Obscure

- **Character**: 暧
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: obscure, ambiguous
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 暧昧 (ài mèi) — ambiguous, equivocal
- 暧暧 (ài ài) — dim, dusky

## Example Sentences

- 他们之间的关系很暧昧。(Tā men zhī jiān de guān xì hěn ài mèi.) — Their relationship is very ambiguous.

## Notes

暧 in 暧昧 describes unclear or potentially romantic relationships. Suggests intentional vagueness.
