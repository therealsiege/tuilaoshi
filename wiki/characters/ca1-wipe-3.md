# 擦 To Wipe

- **Character**: 擦
- **Pinyin**: cā
- **Meaning**: to wipe, to rub, to apply, to pass by
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 擦桌子 (cā zhuō zi) — to wipe the table
- 摩擦 (mó cā) — friction

## Example Sentences

- 请把桌子擦干净。(Qǐng bǎ zhuō zi cā gān jìng.) — Please wipe the table clean.

## Notes

擦 means to wipe or rub. Common for cleaning (擦桌子), applying lotion, or narrowly missing (擦肩而过).
