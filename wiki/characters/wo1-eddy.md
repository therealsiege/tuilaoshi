# 涡 Eddy

- **Character**: 涡
- **Pinyin**: wō
- **Meaning**: eddy, whirlpool, whorl
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 漩涡 (xuán wō) — whirlpool
- 涡流 (wō liú) — eddy current

## Example Sentences

- 河流中有很多涡流。(Hé liú zhōng yǒu hěn duō wō liú.) — There are many eddies in the river.

## Notes

涡 simplified from 渦. Water 氵 circulating in spirals.
