# 龙 Dragon

- **Character**: 龙
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: dragon
- **Radical**: 龙 (dragon)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 龙年 (lóng nián) — Year of the Dragon
- 恐龙 (kǒng lóng) — dinosaur

## Example Sentences

- 中国人自称龙的传人。(Zhōng guó rén zì chēng lóng de chuán rén.) — Chinese people call themselves descendants of the dragon.

## Notes

龙 is simplified from 龍. The dragon is the most auspicious creature in Chinese culture, symbolizing power and good fortune.
