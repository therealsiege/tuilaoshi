# 搅 To Stir

- **Character**: 搅
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: to stir, to disturb
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 搅拌 (jiǎo bàn) — to stir
- 打搅 (dǎ jiǎo) — to disturb

## Example Sentences

- 不好意思打搅你。(Bù hǎo yì si dǎ jiǎo nǐ.) — Sorry to disturb you.

## Notes

搅 simplified from 攪. Hand radical 扌 representing stirring or mixing action.
