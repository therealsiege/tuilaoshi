# 叉 Fork, cross, intersect

- **Character**: 叉
- **Pinyin**: chā
- **Meaning**: fork, cross, intersect
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 叉子 (chā zi) — fork (utensil)
- 交叉 (jiāo chā) — to intersect, cross

## Example Sentences

- 这两条线在中间交叉。(Zhè liǎng tiáo xiàn zài zhōng jiān jiāo chā.) — These two lines intersect in the middle.

## Notes

Basic character for fork or crossing. 刀叉 means knife and fork. Used in geometry for intersection.
