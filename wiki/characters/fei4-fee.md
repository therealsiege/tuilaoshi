# 费 Fee, cost, waste

- **Character**: 费
- **Pinyin**: fèi
- **Meaning**: fee, cost, waste
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 费用 (fèiyòng) — cost, expense
- 学费 (xuéfèi) — tuition

## Example Sentences

- 这项工程费用很高。(Zhè xiàng gōngchéng fèiyòng hěn gāo.) — This project's cost is very high.

## Notes

Common term for fees, costs, and expenses.
