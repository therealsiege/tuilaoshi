# 怨 To blame, complain, resentment

- **Character**: 怨
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: to blame, complain, resentment
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 抱怨 (bào yuàn) — to complain, grumble
- 怨恨 (yuàn hèn) — to resent, resentment

## Example Sentences

- 他总是抱怨工作太累。(Tā zǒng shì bàoyuàn gōngzuò tài lèi.) — He always complains that work is too tiring.

## Notes

Expresses complaint, resentment, or blaming others.
