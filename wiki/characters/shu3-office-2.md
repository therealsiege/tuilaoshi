# 署 Office, bureau, sign

- **Character**: 署
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: office, bureau, sign
- **Radical**: 网 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 官署 (guān shǔ) — government office
- 署名 (shǔ míng) — sign one's name

## Example Sentences

- 请在文件上署名。(Qǐng zài wénjiàn shàng shǔ míng.) — Please sign your name on the document.

## Notes

Means government office or bureau. Also means to sign or affix one's name to a document.
