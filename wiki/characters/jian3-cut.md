# 剪 Cut

- **Character**: 剪
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: cut, scissors
- **Radical**: 刀(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 剪刀 (jiǎn dāo) — scissors
- 剪裁 (jiǎn cái) — cut and tailor

## Example Sentences

- 她用剪刀剪出了美丽的窗花。(Tā yòng jiǎndāo jiǎn chū le měilì de chuāng huā.) — She cut out beautiful paper window decorations with scissors.

## Notes

剪 means to cut with scissors. Paper cutting (剪纸) is a traditional Chinese art form.
