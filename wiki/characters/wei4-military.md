# 尉 Officer

- **Character**: 尉
- **Pinyin**: wèi
- **Meaning**: military officer rank, to console
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 上尉 (shàng wèi) — captain
- 安慰 (ān wèi) — to comfort

## Example Sentences

- 他是一名上尉。(Tā shì yī míng shàng wèi.) — He is a captain.

## Notes

尉 used for junior officer ranks; also means to comfort or console.
