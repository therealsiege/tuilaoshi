# 竿 Pole

- **Character**: 竿
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: pole, rod
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 撑竿跳 (chēng gān tiào) — pole vault
- 钓竿 (diào gān) — fishing rod

## Example Sentences

- 撑竿跳需要速度和技巧。(Chēnggāntiào xūyào sùdù hé jìqiǎo.) — Pole vault requires speed and technique.

## Notes

Long pole; used in pole vault and fishing
