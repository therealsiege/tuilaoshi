# 传 Pass

- **Character**: 传
- **Pinyin**: chuán
- **Meaning**: to pass, transmit
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 传球 (chuán qiú) — to pass the ball
- 传统 (chuán tǒng) — tradition

## Example Sentences

- 他传球很准确。(Tā chuánqiú hěn zhǔnquè.) — He passes the ball very accurately.

## Notes

Passing in ball sports; also means transmit, spread
