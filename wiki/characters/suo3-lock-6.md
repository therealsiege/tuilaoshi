# 锁 Lock

- **Character**: 锁
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: lock, to lock
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锁喉 (suǒ hóu) — chokehold
- 关节锁 (guān jié suǒ) — joint lock

## Example Sentences

- 格斗中关节锁很有效。(Gédòu zhōng guānjiésuǒ hěn yǒuxiào.) — Joint locks are very effective in combat.

## Notes

Locking; submission holds in grappling
