# 惩 To Punish

- **Character**: 惩
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to punish, to penalize
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惩罚 (chéng fá) — to punish
- 惩处 (chéng chǔ) — to penalize

## Example Sentences

- 他受到了惩罚。(Tā shòu dào le chéng fá.) — He was punished.

## Notes

惩 simplified from 懲. Heart 心 below 徵 (to summon/levy).
