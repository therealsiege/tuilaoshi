# 夺 Seize, take by force, win

- **Character**: 夺
- **Pinyin**: duó
- **Meaning**: seize, take by force, win
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 夺取 (duóqǔ) — seize, capture
- 争夺 (zhēngduó) — fight for, scramble for

## Example Sentences

- 他夺得了冠军。(Tā duódé le guànjūn.) — He won the championship.

## Notes

Implies forceful or competitive acquisition.
