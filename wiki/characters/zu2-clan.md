# 族 Clan

- **Character**: 族
- **Pinyin**: zú
- **Meaning**: clan, ethnic group, tribe
- **Radical**: 方 (direction)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 民族 (mín zú) — ethnic group, nation
- 家族 (jiā zú) — clan, family

## Example Sentences

- 中国有56个民族。(Zhōng guó yǒu wǔ shí liù gè mín zú.) — China has 56 ethnic groups.

## Notes

族 combines 方 (banner) and 矢 (arrow), originally representing a group united under one banner.
