# 症 Disease, Symptom, 症状 Symptom

- **Character**: 症
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: disease, symptom, 症状 symptom
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 症状 (zhèng zhuàng) — symptom
- 病症 (bìng zhèng) — disease, illness
- 综合症 (zōng hé zhèng) — syndrome

## Example Sentences

- 这是感冒的典型症状。(Zhè shì gǎnmào de diǎnxíng zhèngzhuàng.) — These are typical symptoms of a cold.

## Notes

Medical term for diseases and symptoms. 症状 (symptom) is essential medical vocabulary.
