# 栏 Railing

- **Character**: 栏
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: railing, column (in newspaper)
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing
- 专栏 (zhuān lán) — special column

## Example Sentences

- 楼梯旁边有栏杆。(Lóu tī páng biān yǒu lán gān.) — There are railings beside the stairs.

## Notes

栏 simplified from 欄. Wood radical 木 representing wooden railings or partitions.
