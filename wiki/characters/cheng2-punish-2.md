# 惩 To Discipline

- **Character**: 惩
- **Pinyin**: chéng
- **Meaning**: to punish, to discipline
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 惩罚 (chéng fá) — punishment
- 严惩 (yán chéng) — severe punishment

## Example Sentences

- 犯罪要受到惩罚。(Fàn zuì yào shòu dào chéng fá.) — Criminals must be punished.

## Notes

惩 is simplified from 懲. The heart radical suggests moral correction.
