# 跃 Leap

- **Character**: 跃
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: to jump, leap
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 跳跃 (tiào yuè) — to jump, leap
- 飞跃 (fēi yuè) — to leap

## Example Sentences

- 运动员奋力一跃。(Yùndòngyuán fènlì yī yuè.) — The athlete leaped with all their might.

## Notes

Leaping; powerful jumping movements
