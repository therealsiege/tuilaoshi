# 依 Depend

- **Character**: 依
- **Pinyin**: yī
- **Meaning**: depend on, rely
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 依赖 (yī lài) — to depend on, dependence
- 依靠 (yī kào) — to rely on
- 依然 (yī rán) — still, as before

## Example Sentences

- 药物依赖很难克服。(Yào wù yī lài hěn nán kè fú.) — Drug dependence is very difficult to overcome.

## Notes

Dependence; physical or psychological.
