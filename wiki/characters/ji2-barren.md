# 瘠 Barren, thin, emaciated

- **Character**: 瘠
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: barren, thin, emaciated
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 贫瘠 (pín jí) — barren, poor
- 瘠薄 (jí bó) — barren, infertile

## Example Sentences

- 瘠薄的土地产量很低。(Jí bó de tǔ dì chǎn liàng hěn dī.) — Barren land has low yields.

## Notes

Infertile and unproductive
