# 材 Material

- **Character**: 材
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: material, timber, talent
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 材料 (cái liào) — material
- 木材 (mù cái) — timber

## Example Sentences

- 他是可造之材。(Tā shì kě zào zhī cái.) — He is promising talent.

## Notes

材 has wood 木 with 才 (talent), useful timber or talent.
