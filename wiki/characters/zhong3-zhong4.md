# 种 Type

- **Character**: 种
- **Pinyin**: zhǒng/zhòng
- **Meaning**: type; plant
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 接种 (jiē zhòng) — to vaccinate, inoculate
- 种类 (zhǒng lèi) — type, category
- 种植 (zhòng zhí) — to plant

## Example Sentences

- 这种疫苗很有效。(Zhè zhǒng yì miáo hěn yǒu xiào.) — This type of vaccine is very effective.

## Notes

Type or planting; zhǒng for type, zhòng in vaccination.
