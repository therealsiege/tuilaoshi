# 幼 Young

- **Character**: 幼
- **Pinyin**: yòu
- **Meaning**: young, immature
- **Radical**: 幺 (tiny)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 幼儿 (yòu ér) — young child
- 幼稚 (yòu zhì) — childish, naive

## Example Sentences

- 这个想法太幼稚了。(Zhège xiǎngfǎ tài yòuzhì le.) — This idea is too naive.

## Notes

幼 combines tiny 幺 with strength 力, representing something small and still developing strength.
