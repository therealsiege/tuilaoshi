# 儒 Confucian, scholar

- **Character**: 儒
- **Pinyin**: rú
- **Meaning**: Confucian, scholar
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 儒家 (rú jiā) — Confucianism
- 儒教 (rú jiào) — Confucianism

## Example Sentences

- 儒家思想影响了中国几千年。(Rú jiā sī xiǎng yǐng xiǎng le zhōng guó jǐ qiān nián.) — Confucian thought has influenced China for thousands of years.

## Notes

One of the three major philosophical traditions in China
