# 嵯 Lofty, uneven

- **Character**: 嵯
- **Pinyin**: cuó
- **Meaning**: lofty, uneven
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 嵯峨 (cuó'é) — lofty, towering
- 嶙峋嵯峨 (línxún cuó'é) — craggy and towering

## Example Sentences

- 山势嵯峨险峻。(Shānshì cuó'é xiǎnjùn.) — The mountains are lofty and precipitous.

## Notes

Literary term for lofty, uneven mountains, usually paired with 峨.
