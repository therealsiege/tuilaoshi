# 桩 Stake

- **Character**: 桩
- **Pinyin**: zhuāng
- **Meaning**: stake, pile, classifier
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 木桩 (mù zhuāng) — wooden stake
- 打桩 (dǎ zhuāng) — to drive piles

## Example Sentences

- 打桩机很响。(Dǎ zhuāng jī hěn xiǎng.) — The pile driver is very loud.

## Notes

桩 with wood radical for stake/pile, foundation work in construction.
