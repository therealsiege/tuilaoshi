# 缕 Strand

- **Character**: 缕
- **Pinyin**: lǚ
- **Meaning**: strand, thread
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 一缕 (yī lǚ) — a strand, a wisp
- 千丝万缕 (qiān sī wàn lǚ) — countless ties

## Example Sentences

- 一缕阳光照进了房间。(Yī lǚ yángguāng zhào jìn le fángjiān.) — A ray of sunlight shone into the room.

## Notes

缕 is a measure word for thread-like things: strands, wisps, rays. Used metaphorically for subtle connections.
