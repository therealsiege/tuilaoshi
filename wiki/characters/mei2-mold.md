# 霉 Mold

- **Character**: 霉
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: mold, mildew, unlucky
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 发霉 (fā méi) — to go moldy
- 倒霉 (dǎo méi) — unlucky

## Example Sentences

- 面包发霉了。(Miàn bāo fā méi le.) — The bread has gone moldy.

## Notes

霉 shows rain 雨 with每 (every), representing mold growing in damp conditions.
