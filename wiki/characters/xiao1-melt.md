# 销 Melt, cancel, sell, pin

- **Character**: 销
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: melt, cancel, sell, pin
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 销售 (xiāo shòu) — to sell, sales
- 销毁 (xiāo huǐ) — to destroy, cancel

## Example Sentences

- 这家公司的产品销售量很高。(Zhè jiā gōng sī de chǎn pǐn xiāo shòu liàng hěn gāo.) — This company's product sales volume is very high.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: to sell, to melt, to cancel, or a pin/bolt. Very common in business contexts.
