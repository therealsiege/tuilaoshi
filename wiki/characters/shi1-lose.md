# 失 Lose, loss

- **Character**: 失
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: lose, loss
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 损失 (sǔn shī) — loss
- 失败 (shī bài) — failure

## Example Sentences

- 训练过程中损失下降。(Xùn liàn guò chéng zhōng sǔn shī xià jiàng.) — The loss decreases during training.

## Notes

Used in 损失 (loss), 失败 (failure), 丢失 (lose)
