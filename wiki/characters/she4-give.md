# 舍 To Give Up

- **Character**: 舍
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: to give up, residence, shed
- **Radical**: 舌 (tongue)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 宿舍 (sù shè) — dormitory
- 舍不得 (shě bu dé) — to hate to part with

## Example Sentences

- 学生宿舍在那边。(Xué shēng sù shè zài nà biān.) — The student dormitory is over there.

## Notes

舍 as shè means residence; as shě means to give up/abandon.
