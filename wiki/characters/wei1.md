# 危 Danger

- **Character**: 危
- **Pinyin**: wēi
- **Meaning**: danger, peril
- **Radical**: 㔾 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 危险 (wēi xiǎn) — danger
- 危机 (wēi jī) — crisis

## Example Sentences

- 这里很危险。(Zhè lǐ hěn wēi xiǎn.) — It's very dangerous here.

## Notes

危 depicts a person 人 on a cliff 厂, representing a person in a precarious dangerous position.
