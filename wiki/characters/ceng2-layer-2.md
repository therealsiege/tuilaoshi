# 层 Layer

- **Character**: 层
- **Pinyin**: céng
- **Meaning**: layer, floor, level
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 层次 (céng cì) — level, hierarchy
- 一层 (yī céng) — one layer/floor

## Example Sentences

- 我住在五层。(Wǒ zhù zài wǔ céng.) — I live on the fifth floor.

## Notes

层 is a measure word for floors and layers. 层次 = levels/hierarchy.
