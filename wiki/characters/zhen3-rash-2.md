# 疹 Rash

- **Character**: 疹
- **Pinyin**: zhěn
- **Meaning**: rash, measles
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 皮疹 (pí zhěn) — skin rash
- 麻疹 (má zhěn) — measles

## Example Sentences

- 孩子身上出了疹子。(Hái zi shēn shàng chū le zhěn zi.) — The child broke out in a rash.

## Notes

疹 has sickness 疒 with 㐱 (to examine), symptoms appearing on skin.
