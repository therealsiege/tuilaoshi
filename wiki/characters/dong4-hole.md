# 洞 Hole, cavity

- **Character**: 洞
- **Pinyin**: dòng
- **Meaning**: hole, cavity
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 漏洞 (lòu dòng) — vulnerability
- 洞穴 (dòng xué) — cave

## Example Sentences

- 修补系统漏洞。(Xiū bǔ xì tǒng lòu dòng.) — Patch system vulnerabilities.
