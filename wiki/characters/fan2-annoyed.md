# 烦 Annoyed

- **Character**: 烦
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: annoyed, bothersome, to trouble
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 麻烦 (má fán) — troublesome
- 烦恼 (fán nǎo) — worried, troubled

## Example Sentences

- 别烦我！(Bié fán wǒ!) — Don't bother me!

## Notes

烦 combines fire and page — a burning feeling of annoyance. 麻烦 = trouble/troublesome.
