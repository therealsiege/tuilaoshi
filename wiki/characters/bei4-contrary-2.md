# 悖 Contrary, go against

- **Character**: 悖
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: contrary, go against
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 悖论 (bèi lùn) — paradox
- 悖理 (bèi lǐ) — contrary to reason

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个著名的悖论。(Zhè shì yī gè zhù míng de bèi lùn.) — This is a famous paradox.

## Notes

Common in philosophical and logical discussions
