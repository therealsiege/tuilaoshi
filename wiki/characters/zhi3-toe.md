# 趾 Toe

- **Character**: 趾
- **Pinyin**: zhǐ
- **Meaning**: toe
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 脚趾 (jiǎozhǐ) — toe
- 大脚趾 (dà jiǎozhǐ) — big toe

## Example Sentences

- 他踢到了脚趾。(Tā tī dào le jiǎozhǐ.) — He stubbed his toe.

## Notes

Similar to 指 (finger) but with 足 radical for foot. Usually appears as 脚趾.
