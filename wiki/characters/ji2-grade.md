# 级 Grade

- **Character**: 级
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: grade, level, rank
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 年级 (nián jí) — grade (in school)
- 高级 (gāo jí) — advanced

## Example Sentences

- 我上三年级。(Wǒ shàng sān nián jí.) — I'm in third grade.

## Notes

级 means grade or level. 初级 = elementary. 等级 = rank, level.
