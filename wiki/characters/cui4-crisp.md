# 脆 Crisp

- **Character**: 脆
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: crisp, brittle, fragile
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 脆弱 (cuì ruò) — fragile, vulnerable
- 干脆 (gān cuì) — simply, just

## Example Sentences

- 这个饼干很脆。(Zhège bǐnggān hěn cuì.) — This cookie is very crispy.

## Notes

脆 combines meat/月 with 危 (danger), suggesting something easily broken or fragile.
