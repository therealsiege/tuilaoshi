# 写 To Write

- **Character**: 写
- **Pinyin**: xiě
- **Meaning**: to write
- **Radical**: 冖 (cover)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 写字 (xiě zì) — to write characters
- 写作 (xiě zuò) — to write, writing

## Example Sentences

- 请写你的名字。(Qǐng xiě nǐ de míng zì.) — Please write your name.

## Notes

写 is simplified from 寫. It pairs with 读 (to read) as complementary literacy skills.
