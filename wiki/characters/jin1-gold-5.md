# 金 Gold

- **Character**: 金
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: gold, metal, money
- **Radical**: 金 (gold)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 资金 (zī jīn) — funds
- 现金 (xiàn jīn) — cash

## Example Sentences

- 公司需要筹集资金。(Gōng sī xū yào chóu jí zī jīn.) — The company needs to raise funds.

## Notes

金 pictograph of gold nuggets or metal ore.
