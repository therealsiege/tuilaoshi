# 修 To Repair

- **Character**: 修
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: to repair, to cultivate, to study
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 修理 (xiū lǐ) — to repair
- 修改 (xiū gǎi) — to revise

## Example Sentences

- 我需要修理自行车。(Wǒ xū yào xiū lǐ zì xíng chē.) — I need to repair the bicycle.

## Notes

修 combines person 亻 and 攸 (long, trailing), representing personal cultivation and refinement.
