# 阻 Resistance, block

- **Character**: 阻
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: resistance, block
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 电阻 (diàn zǔ) — resistor, resistance
- 阻止 (zǔ zhǐ) — prevent

## Example Sentences

- 电阻值是100欧姆。(Diàn zǔ zhí shì yī bǎi ōu mǔ.) — The resistance value is 100 ohms.

## Notes

Used in 电阻 (resistor), 阻力 (resistance), 阻抗 (impedance)
