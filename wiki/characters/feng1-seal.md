# 封 To Seal

- **Character**: 封
- **Pinyin**: fēng
- **Meaning**: to seal, to block
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 封闭 (fēng bì) — to seal up
- 信封 (xìn fēng) — envelope

## Example Sentences

- 这条路被封了。(Zhè tiáo lù bèi fēng le.) — This road has been sealed off.

## Notes

封 shows hands 丰 planting a tree 寸, originally meaning to grant a fief, later extending to seal/close.
