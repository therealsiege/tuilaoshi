# 缆 Cable

- **Character**: 缆
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: cable, hawser
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缆车 (lǎn chē) — cable car
- 钢缆 (gāng lǎn) — steel cable

## Example Sentences

- 缆车把游客送到山顶。(Lǎnchē bǎ yóukè sòng dào shāndǐng.) — The cable car takes tourists to the mountaintop.

## Notes

Heavy cable; cable cars and lifts
