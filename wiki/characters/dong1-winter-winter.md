# 冬 Winter

- **Character**: 冬
- **Pinyin**: dōng
- **Meaning**: winter
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 冬天 (dōng tiān) — winter
- 冬季 (dōng jì) — winter season

## Example Sentences

- 冬天很冷。(Dōng tiān hěn lěng.) — Winter is very cold.

## Notes

冬 depicts the end of a cycle. The four seasons are 春夏秋冬.
