# 汗 Sweat

- **Character**: 汗
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: sweat, perspire
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 出汗 (chū hàn) — to sweat
- 冷汗 (lěng hàn) — cold sweat
- 盗汗 (dào hàn) — night sweats

## Example Sentences

- 发烧时会大量出汗。(Fā shāo shí huì dà liàng chū hàn.) — You sweat a lot when you have a fever.

## Notes

Perspiration; thermoregulation and symptom.
