# 辨 Differentiate

- **Character**: 辨
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: distinguish, differentiate
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 辨证 (biàn zhèng) — pattern differentiation (TCM)
- 分辨 (fēn biàn) — to distinguish
- 辨认 (biàn rèn) — to identify

## Example Sentences

- 中医强调辨证论治。(Zhōng yī qiáng diào biàn zhèng lùn zhì.) — TCM emphasizes pattern differentiation and treatment.

## Notes

Differentiation; core TCM diagnostic principle.
