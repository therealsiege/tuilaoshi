# 框 Frame

- **Character**: 框
- **Pinyin**: kuāng
- **Meaning**: frame, rim
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 篮框 (lán kuāng) — basketball hoop
- 框架 (kuàng jià) — framework

## Example Sentences

- 球擦着篮框进了。(Qiú cā zhe lánkuāng jìn le.) — The ball grazed the rim and went in.

## Notes

Rim and frame; basketball hoop rim
