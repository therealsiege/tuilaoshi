# 纬 Latitude

- **Character**: 纬
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: latitude, weft (weaving)
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纬度 (wěi dù) — latitude
- 经纬 (jīng wěi) — longitude and latitude

## Example Sentences

- 北京的纬度大约是40度。(Běi jīng de wěi dù dà yuē shì sì shí dù.) — Beijing's latitude is about 40 degrees.

## Notes

纬 simplified from 緯. Silk 纟 with 韦 (leather), cross threads in weaving.
