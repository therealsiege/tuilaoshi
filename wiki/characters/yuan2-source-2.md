# 源 Source, origin

- **Character**: 源
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: source, origin
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 资源 (zī yuán) — resource
- 开源 (kāi yuán) — open source

## Example Sentences

- 这是开源软件。(Zhè shì kāi yuán ruǎn jiàn.) — This is open source software.

## Notes

Used in 资源 (resource), 开源 (open source), 源代码 (source code)
