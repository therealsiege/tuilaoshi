# 盛 Prosperous, flourishing, abundant

- **Character**: 盛
- **Pinyin**: shèng
- **Meaning**: prosperous, flourishing, abundant
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 茂盛 (mào shèng) — luxuriant
- 旺盛 (wàng shèng) — vigorous, exuberant

## Example Sentences

- 春天百花盛开。(Chūn tiān bǎi huā shèng kāi.) — In spring, hundreds of flowers bloom.

## Notes

Peak growth and abundance
