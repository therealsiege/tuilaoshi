# 踹 Kick

- **Character**: 踹
- **Pinyin**: chuài
- **Meaning**: to kick, trample
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 踹门 (chuài mén) — to kick door
- 踹腿 (chuài tuǐ) — to kick

## Example Sentences

- 武术中有侧踹的动作。(Wǔshù zhōng yǒu cè chuài de dòngzuò.) — There are side kick movements in martial arts.

## Notes

Heavy kicking; powerful kick strikes
