# 碉 Watchtower

- **Character**: 碉
- **Pinyin**: diāo
- **Meaning**: watchtower, blockhouse
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 碉堡 (diāo bǎo) — blockhouse
- 碉楼 (diāo lóu) — watchtower

## Example Sentences

- 边境上有很多碉堡。(Biān jìng shàng yǒu hěn duō diāo bǎo.) — There are many blockhouses on the border.

## Notes

碉 has stone 石 with 周 (circuit), stone defensive tower.
