# 宰 Slaughter, prime minister, govern

- **Character**: 宰
- **Pinyin**: zǎi
- **Meaning**: slaughter, prime minister, govern
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宰相 (zǎi xiàng) — prime minister
- 屠宰 (tú zǎi) — slaughter

## Example Sentences

- 他曾经担任宰相一职。(Tā céng jīng dān rèn zǎi xiàng yī zhí.) — He once served as prime minister.

## Notes

High official position and also means to butcher
