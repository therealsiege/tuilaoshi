# 嫁 To marry (of woman)

- **Character**: 嫁
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: to marry (of woman)
- **Radical**: 女(woman)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 出嫁 (chū jià) — to get married (of woman)
- 嫁人 (jià rén) — to get married (of woman)

## Example Sentences

- 她去年嫁给了他。(Tā qùnián jià gěi le tā.) — She married him last year.

## Notes

Specifically for a woman marrying; counterpart is 娶.
