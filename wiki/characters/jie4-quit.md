# 戒 Quit

- **Character**: 戒
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: quit, abstain
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 戒烟 (jiè yān) — to quit smoking
- 戒毒 (jiè dú) — to quit drugs
- 戒酒 (jiè jiǔ) — to quit drinking

## Example Sentences

- 戒毒需要专业帮助。(Jiè dú xū yào zhuān yè bāng zhù.) — Quitting drugs requires professional help.

## Notes

Cessation of addiction; recovery process.
