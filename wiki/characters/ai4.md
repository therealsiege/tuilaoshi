# 碍 To Hinder

- **Character**: 碍
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: to hinder, to obstruct
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 障碍 (zhàng ài) — obstacle
- 妨碍 (fáng ài) — to obstruct

## Example Sentences

- 这不妨碍我们的计划。(Zhè bù fáng ài wǒ men de jì huà.) — This doesn't hinder our plan.

## Notes

碍 shows stone 石 and 疑 suggesting doubt/blocking, representing a stone obstacle in one's path.
