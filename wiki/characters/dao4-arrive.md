# 到 To Arrive

- **Character**: 到
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to arrive, to reach, until
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 到达 (dào dá) — to arrive
- 看到 (kàn dào) — to see

## Example Sentences

- 我们到了。(Wǒ men dào le.) — We've arrived.

## Notes

到 as result complement indicates successful completion: 找到 (found), 买到 (bought successfully).
