# 谷 Valley, grain, cereal

- **Character**: 谷
- **Pinyin**: gǔ
- **Meaning**: valley, grain, cereal
- **Radical**: 谷 (valley)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 山谷 (shān gǔ) — valley
- 五谷 (wǔ gǔ) — five grains

## Example Sentences

- 五谷丰登是丰收的象征。(Wǔ gǔ fēng dēng shì fēng shōu de xiàng zhēng.) — Abundant grain is a symbol of a good harvest.
