# 评 To Comment

- **Character**: 评
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: to comment, to judge
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 评论 (píng lùn) — to comment
- 评书 (píng shū) — storytelling

## Example Sentences

- 评书是传统的说唱艺术。(Píng shū shì chuán tǒng de shuō chàng yì shù.) — Pingshu is a traditional narrative art.

## Notes

评 means to comment or judge; 评书 is storytelling performance art.
