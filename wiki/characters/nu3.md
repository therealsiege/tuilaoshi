# 弩 Crossbow

- **Character**: 弩
- **Pinyin**: nǔ
- **Meaning**: crossbow
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 弩弓 (nǔ gōng) — crossbow
- 弓弩 (gōng nǔ) — bows and crossbows

## Example Sentences

- 弩是中国古代重要的远程武器。(Nǔ shì Zhōngguó gǔdài zhòngyào de yuǎnchéng wǔqì.) — The crossbow was an important long-range weapon in ancient China.

## Notes

A mechanical bow that shoots bolts. Chinese crossbows were technologically advanced and played crucial military roles.
