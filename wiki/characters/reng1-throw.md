# 扔 To Throw

- **Character**: 扔
- **Pinyin**: rēng
- **Meaning**: to throw, to toss, to cast
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 扔掉 (rēng diào) — to throw away
- 乱扔 (luàn rēng) — to litter

## Example Sentences

- 别把垃圾扔在地上。(Bié bǎ lā jī rēng zài dì shàng.) — Don't throw garbage on the ground.

## Notes

扔 indicates casual throwing or discarding. 扔掉 means to throw away/discard completely.
