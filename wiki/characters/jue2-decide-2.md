# 决 Decide

- **Character**: 决
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to decide, final
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 决赛 (jué sài) — finals, final match
- 决定 (jué dìng) — to decide

## Example Sentences

- 决赛将在明天举行。(Juésài jiāng zài míngtiān jǔxíng.) — The finals will be held tomorrow.

## Notes

Finals and decisions; final rounds
