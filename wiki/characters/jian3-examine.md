# 检 To Inspect

- **Character**: 检
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: to examine, to inspect, to check
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 体检 (tǐ jiǎn) — physical examination
- 安检 (ān jiǎn) — security check

## Example Sentences

- 我去医院体检。(Wǒ qù yī yuàn tǐ jiǎn.) — I went to the hospital for a physical exam.

## Notes

检 is simplified from 檢. Represents systematic inspection and examination.
