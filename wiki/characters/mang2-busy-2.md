# 忙 Busy

- **Character**: 忙
- **Pinyin**: máng
- **Meaning**: busy
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 忙碌 (máng lù) — busy
- 帮忙 (bāng máng) — to help

## Example Sentences

- 你最近忙吗？(Nǐ zuì jìn máng ma?) — Have you been busy lately?

## Notes

忙 uses the heart radical — being busy makes the heart anxious. Opposite of 闲 (xiān, idle).
