# 散 Scatter, disperse, break up

- **Character**: 散
- **Pinyin**: sàn
- **Meaning**: scatter, disperse, break up
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 扩散 (kuò sàn) — diffuse, spread
- 散发 (sàn fā) — emit, give off

## Example Sentences

- 气味在空气中扩散。(Qì wèi zài kōng qì zhōng kuò sàn.) — The smell diffuses in the air.
