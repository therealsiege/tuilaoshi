# 蛙 Frog

- **Character**: 蛙
- **Pinyin**: wā
- **Meaning**: frog
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 蛙泳 (wā yǒng) — breaststroke
- 青蛙 (qīng wā) — frog

## Example Sentences

- 蛙泳是四种泳姿之一。(Wāyǒng shì sì zhǒng yǒngzī zhī yī.) — Breaststroke is one of the four swimming strokes.

## Notes

Frog; used in breaststroke swimming
