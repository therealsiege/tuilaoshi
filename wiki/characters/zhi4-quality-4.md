# 质 Quality, pledge

- **Character**: 质
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: quality, pledge
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 质量 (zhì liàng) — quality
- 质押 (zhì yā) — pledge

## Example Sentences

- 产品质量很重要。(Chǎn pǐn zhì liàng hěn zhòng yào.) — Product quality is very important.

## Notes

质保 is quality assurance. 质押贷款 is pledged loan.
