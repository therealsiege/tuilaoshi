# 络 Connect

- **Character**: 络
- **Pinyin**: luò
- **Meaning**: net, to connect
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 网络 (wǎng luò) — network
- 联络 (lián luò) — to contact

## Example Sentences

- 请保持联络。(Qǐng bǎo chí lián luò.) — Please stay in touch.

## Notes

络 with silk radical suggests threads connecting, essential in network terminology.
