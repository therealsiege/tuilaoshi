# 奏 To play music, to achieve, to present a memorial

- **Character**: 奏
- **Pinyin**: zòu
- **Meaning**: to play music, to achieve, to present a memorial
- **Radical**: 大(big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 演奏 (yǎn zòu) — to perform, to play (music)
- 奏效 (zòu xiào) — to prove effective, to work

## Example Sentences

- 乐队正在演奏一首交响曲。(Yuèduì zhèngzài yǎnzòu yī shǒu jiāoxiǎngqǔ.) — The orchestra is performing a symphony.

## Notes

Used for formal musical performance or achieving results.
