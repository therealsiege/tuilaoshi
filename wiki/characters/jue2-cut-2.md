# 绝 To cut off, absolute, 絕 (traditional)

- **Character**: 绝
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to cut off, absolute, 絕 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 绝对 (jué duì) — absolute, definitely
- 拒绝 (jù jué) — to refuse, reject

## Example Sentences

- 我绝对不同意。(Wǒ juéduì bù tóngyì.) — I absolutely disagree.

## Notes

To cut off or sever, also absolute/extreme.
