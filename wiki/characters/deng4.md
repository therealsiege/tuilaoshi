# 瞪 To stare, to glare

- **Character**: 瞪
- **Pinyin**: dèng
- **Meaning**: to stare, to glare
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 瞪眼 (dèng yǎn) — to stare, to glare
- 瞪着 (dèng zhe) — to stare at

## Example Sentences

- 他生气地瞪着我。(Tā shēngqì de dèngzhe wǒ.) — He glared at me angrily.

## Notes

Often implies anger or surprise in the stare.
