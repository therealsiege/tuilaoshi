# 矗 Stand tall, tower

- **Character**: 矗
- **Pinyin**: chù
- **Meaning**: stand tall, tower
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 24

## Compounds

- 矗立 (chùlì) — stand tall and upright
- 高耸矗立 (gāosǒng chùlì) — tower, stand tall

## Example Sentences

- 高楼矗立在市中心。(Gāolóu chùlì zài shì zhōngxīn.) — Tall buildings stand in the city center.

## Notes

Literary term for standing tall and erect like buildings or mountains.
