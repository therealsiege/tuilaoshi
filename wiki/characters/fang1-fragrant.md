# 芳 Fragrant, virtuous

- **Character**: 芳
- **Pinyin**: fāng
- **Meaning**: fragrant, virtuous
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 芳香 (fāng xiāng) — fragrant, aromatic
- 芬芳 (fēn fāng) — fragrant, sweet-smelling

## Example Sentences

- 春天里到处都是芬芳的花香。(Chūn tiān lǐ dào chù dōu shì fēn fāng de huā xiāng.) — In spring, fragrant flower scents are everywhere.

## Notes

Also used metaphorically for virtue or good reputation.
