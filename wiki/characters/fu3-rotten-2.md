# 腐 Rotten, corrupt, tofu

- **Character**: 腐
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: rotten, corrupt, tofu
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 腐败 (fǔ bài) — corruption, rotten
- 豆腐 (dòu fu) — tofu

## Example Sentences

- 政府要严厉打击腐败现象。(Zhèng fǔ yào yán lì dǎ jī fǔ bài xiàn xiàng.) — The government must severely crack down on corruption.

## Notes

Means rotten or corrupt. Also used in 豆腐 (tofu). 腐朽 means decayed or decadent.
