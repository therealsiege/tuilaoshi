# 施 To Apply

- **Character**: 施
- **Pinyin**: shī
- **Meaning**: to apply, carry out, grant
- **Radical**: 方 (direction)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 实施 (shí shī) — to implement, carry out
- 施工 (shī gōng) — construction

## Example Sentences

- 这项政策将于明年实施。(Zhè xiàng zhèngcè jiāng yú míngnián shíshī.) — This policy will be implemented next year.

## Notes

施 combines direction 方 with 也 (also), representing extending actions or施 outward.
