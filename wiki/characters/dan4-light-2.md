# 淡 Light, pale, insipid

- **Character**: 淡
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: light, pale, insipid
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淡水 (dàn shuǐ) — fresh water
- 冷淡 (lěng dàn) — cold, indifferent

## Example Sentences

- 这汤的味道太淡了。(Zhè tāng de wèi dào tài dàn le.) — This soup tastes too bland.

## Notes

Low intensity in color, flavor, or emotion
