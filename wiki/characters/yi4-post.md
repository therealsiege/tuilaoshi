# 驿 Post station, relay

- **Character**: 驿
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: post station, relay
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 23

## Compounds

- 驿站 (yìzhàn) — post station
- 驿马 (yìmǎ) — post horse

## Example Sentences

- 古代驿站用于传递公文。(Gǔdài yìzhàn yòngyú chuándì gōngwén.) — Ancient post stations were used to transmit official documents.

## Notes

Historical term for relay stations in ancient postal systems.
