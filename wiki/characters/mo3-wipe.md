# 抹 To wipe, smear, erase

- **Character**: 抹
- **Pinyin**: mǒ
- **Meaning**: to wipe, smear, erase
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 涂抹 (tú mǒ) — to smear
- 抹杀 (mǒ shā) — to blot out, erase

## Example Sentences

- 他用布抹去桌上的灰尘。(Tā yòng bù mǒ qù zhuō shàng de huī chén.) — He wiped the dust off the table with a cloth.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: mǒ (wipe, smear), mā (wipe), mò (plastering). 抹黑 means to smear or defame.
