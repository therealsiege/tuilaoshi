# 忽 To neglect, suddenly

- **Character**: 忽
- **Pinyin**: hū
- **Meaning**: to neglect, suddenly
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 忽视 (hū shì) — to ignore, overlook
- 忽然 (hū rán) — suddenly

## Example Sentences

- 我们不能忽视这个问题。(Wǒmen bù néng hūshì zhège wèntí.) — We cannot ignore this problem.

## Notes

Dual meaning: to neglect/overlook or suddenly/abruptly.
