# 看 To Look

- **Character**: 看
- **Pinyin**: kàn
- **Meaning**: to look, to see, to watch, to read
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 看见 (kàn jiàn) — to see
- 好看 (hǎo kàn) — good-looking

## Example Sentences

- 我在看书。(Wǒ zài kàn shū.) — I am reading a book.

## Notes

看 combines 手 (hand as a shade) over 目 (eye) — shading the eyes to look far.
