# 约 Appointment

- **Character**: 约
- **Pinyin**: yuē
- **Meaning**: to make an appointment,约定, approximately
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 约定 (yuē dìng) — to agree on
- 大约 (dà yuē) — approximately

## Example Sentences

- 我们约好了明天见面。(Wǒ men yuē hǎo le míng tiān jiàn miàn.) — We agreed to meet tomorrow.

## Notes

约 is simplified from 約. The silk radical suggests binding agreements.
