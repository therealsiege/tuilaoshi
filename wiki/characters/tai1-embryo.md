# 胎 Embryo, fetus, birth

- **Character**: 胎
- **Pinyin**: tāi
- **Meaning**: embryo, fetus, birth
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胎儿 (tāi ér) — fetus, embryo
- 怀胎 (huái tāi) — be pregnant

## Example Sentences

- 胎儿在母体内发育。(Tāi ér zài mǔ tǐ nèi fā yù.) — The fetus develops in the mother's body.

## Notes

Used for embryo and fetal development
