# 栏 Railing

- **Character**: 栏
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: railing, fence
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 栏杆 (lán gān) — railing
- 围栏 (wéi lán) — fence

## Example Sentences

- 扶着栏杆走。(Fú zhe lán gān zǒu.) — Walk holding the railing.

## Notes

栏 with wood radical for wooden railings and fences, protective barriers.
