# 珀 Amber

- **Character**: 珀
- **Pinyin**: pò
- **Meaning**: amber
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 琥珀 (hǔ pò) — amber
- 蜜珀 (mì pò) — honey amber

## Example Sentences

- 这块琥珀里有昆虫。(Zhè kuài hǔ pò lǐ yǒu kūn chóng.) — There are insects in this piece of amber.

## Notes

珀 uses jade radical 玉, rarely used alone, almost always paired with 琥 to mean amber.
