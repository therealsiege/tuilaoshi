# 垂 To hang down, to droop, to hand down

- **Character**: 垂
- **Pinyin**: chuí
- **Meaning**: to hang down, to droop, to hand down
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 垂直 (chuí zhí) — vertical, perpendicular
- 垂柳 (chuí liǔ) — weeping willow

## Example Sentences

- 柳树的枝条垂下来。(Liǔshù de zhītiáo chuí xiàlái.) — The willow branches hang down.

## Notes

Describes natural drooping or hanging downward.
