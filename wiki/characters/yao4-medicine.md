# 药 Medicine, drug, herb

- **Character**: 药
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: medicine, drug, herb
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 中药 (zhōng yào) — traditional Chinese medicine
- 草药 (cǎo yào) — herbal medicine

## Example Sentences

- 是药三分毒，不要乱吃药。(Shì yào sān fēn dú, bùyào luàn chī yào.) — All medicine has some toxicity; don't take medicine carelessly.

## Notes

Medicine or medicinal herbs. The grass radical indicates the plant origin of many traditional medicines.
