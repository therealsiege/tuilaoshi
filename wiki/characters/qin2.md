# 擒 To Capture

- **Character**: 擒
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: to capture, to seize
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 擒获 (qín huò) — to capture
- 生擒 (shēng qín) — to capture alive

## Example Sentences

- 士兵擒获了敌军首领。(Shì bīng qín huò le dí jūn shǒu lǐng.) — The soldiers captured the enemy leader.

## Notes

擒 emphasizes capturing alive, especially in military or hunting contexts. More literary than 捕.
