# 缩 Shrink

- **Character**: 缩
- **Pinyin**: suō
- **Meaning**: shrink, contract
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 萎缩 (wěi suō) — atrophy
- 收缩 (shōu suō) — to contract, shrink
- 紧缩 (jǐn suō) — to tighten, contract

## Example Sentences

- 心脏收缩将血液泵出。(Xīn zàng shōu suō jiāng xiě yè bèng chū.) — The heart contracts to pump blood out.

## Notes

Describes reduction in size; normal or pathological.
