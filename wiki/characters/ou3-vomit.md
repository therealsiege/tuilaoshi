# 呕 Vomit

- **Character**: 呕
- **Pinyin**: ǒu
- **Meaning**: vomit
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 呕吐 (ǒu tù) — to vomit
- 恶心呕吐 (ě xīn ǒu tù) — nausea and vomiting
- 呕血 (ǒu xiě) — to vomit blood

## Example Sentences

- 食物中毒会引起呕吐。(Shí wù zhòng dú huì yǐn qǐ ǒu tù.) — Food poisoning can cause vomiting.

## Notes

Forceful expulsion; GI symptom.
