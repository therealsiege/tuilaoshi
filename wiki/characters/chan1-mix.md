# 掺 Mix, mingle, adulterate

- **Character**: 掺
- **Pinyin**: chān
- **Meaning**: mix, mingle, adulterate
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掺杂 (chānzá) — mix, mingle
- 掺假 (chānjiǎ) — adulterate

## Example Sentences

- 食品中不得掺假。(Shípǐn zhōng bùdé chānjiǎ.) — Food must not be adulterated.

## Notes

Common term for mixing, often with negative connotation of adulteration.
