# 解 Untie, solve, explain

- **Character**: 解
- **Pinyin**: jiě
- **Meaning**: untie, solve, explain
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 解决 (jiějué) — solve, resolve
- 调解 (tiáojiě) — mediate

## Example Sentences

- 我们要和平解决问题。(Wǒmen yào hépíng jiějué wèntí.) — We should resolve problems peacefully.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: untie, solve, explain, understand.
