# 铃 Bell, chime

- **Character**: 铃
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: bell, chime
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 铃铛 (líng dang) — small bell
- 门铃 (mén líng) — doorbell

## Example Sentences

- 圣诞节的铃声叮当作响。(Shèng dàn jié de líng shēng dīng dāng zuò xiǎng.) — The Christmas bells jingle.

## Notes

Generic term for bells of various sizes.
