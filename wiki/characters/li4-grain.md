# 粒 Grain, particle, pellet

- **Character**: 粒
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: grain, particle, pellet
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粒子 (lì zǐ) — particle
- 颗粒 (kē lì) — granule, pellet

## Example Sentences

- 微小的粒子在空气中漂浮。(Wēi xiǎo de lì zǐ zài kōng qì zhōng piāo fú.) — Tiny particles float in the air.

## Notes

Common in physics and chemistry for describing particles
