# 金 Gold

- **Character**: 金
- **Pinyin**: jīn
- **Meaning**: gold, metal, money
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 金钱 (jīn qián) — money
- 黄金 (huáng jīn) — gold

## Example Sentences

- 这是金戒指。(Zhè shì jīn jiè zhi.) — This is a gold ring.

## Notes

金 means gold or metal. Radical for metal characters. Used in金钱 (money), 金牌 (gold medal).
