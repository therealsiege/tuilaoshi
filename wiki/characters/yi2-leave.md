# 遗 Leave behind, lose, omit

- **Character**: 遗
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: leave behind, lose, omit
- **Radical**: 辵 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 遗传 (yí chuán) — heredity, genetic
- 遗产 (yí chǎn) — heritage, legacy

## Example Sentences

- 这种疾病是遗传性的。(Zhè zhǒng jí bìng shì yí chuán xìng de.) — This disease is hereditary.
