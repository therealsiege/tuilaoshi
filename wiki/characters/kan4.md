# 看 To Look/See/Read/Watch

- **Character**: 看
- **Pinyin**: kàn
- **Meaning**: to look, to see, to read, to watch; to visit
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 看见 (kànjiàn) — to see, to catch sight of
- 看书 (kàn shū) — to read (a book)
- 好看 (hǎokàn) — good-looking, attractive
- 看法 (kànfǎ) — viewpoint, opinion
- 看病 (kàn bìng) — to see a doctor
- 看起来 (kàn qǐlái) — to look like, to appear

## Example Sentences

- 我在看书。(Wǒ zài kàn shū.) — I am reading a book.
- 你看见他了吗？(Nǐ kànjiàn tā le ma?) — Did you see him?
- 这部电影很好看。(Zhè bù diànyǐng hěn hǎokàn.) — This movie is really good.

## Notes

看 shows a hand (手) shading the eyes (目) to look into the distance. When pronounced kān (first tone), it means "to look after, to guard" as in 看门 (kānmén, "to guard the door"). 看 is versatile: 看书 (read), 看电视 (watch TV), 看朋友 (visit a friend), 看病 (see a doctor). The result complement 看见 (kànjiàn) emphasizes successful perception -- "to see" as opposed to merely "to look."
