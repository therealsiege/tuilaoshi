# 问 To Ask

- **Character**: 问
- **Pinyin**: wèn
- **Meaning**: to ask, to inquire
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 问题 (wèntí) — question, problem
- 请问 (qǐngwèn) — excuse me, may I ask
- 问好 (wènhǎo) — to send regards
- 学问 (xuéwèn) — knowledge, learning
- 访问 (fǎngwèn) — to visit (formal), to interview
- 问答 (wèndá) — questions and answers, Q&A

## Example Sentences

- 请问，火车站在哪里？(Qǐngwèn, huǒchēzhàn zài nǎlǐ?) — Excuse me, where is the train station?
- 我有一个问题。(Wǒ yǒu yí gè wèntí.) — I have a question.
- 老师问了我一个问题。(Lǎoshī wèn le wǒ yí gè wèntí.) — The teacher asked me a question.

## Notes

问 places 口 (kǒu, mouth) inside 门 (mén, door/gate) — a mouth at a door, suggesting someone standing at a gate asking questions. This vivid composition makes 问 one of the most memorable semantic-phonetic characters. The polite phrase 请问 (qǐngwèn) literally means "please ask" and is the standard way to begin any inquiry to a stranger. The compound 问题 (wèntí) serves double duty as both "question" and "problem" — context determines which meaning applies.
