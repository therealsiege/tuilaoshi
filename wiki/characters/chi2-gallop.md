# 驰 Gallop, speed

- **Character**: 驰
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: gallop, speed
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 奔驰 (bēnchí) — speed, gallop
- 驰名 (chímíng) — famous, well-known

## Example Sentences

- 骏马在草原上奔驰。(Jùnmǎ zài cǎoyuán shàng bēnchí.) — The fine horse gallops across the grassland.

## Notes

Originally meant galloping horses, now used metaphorically for speed.
