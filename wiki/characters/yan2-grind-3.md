# 研 Grind

- **Character**: 研
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: grind, research
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 研磨 (yán mó) — to grind
- 研究 (yán jiū) — research
- 研制 (yán zhì) — to develop

## Example Sentences

- 将药材研成粉末。(Jiāng yào cái yán chéng fěn mò.) — Grind the medicinal materials into powder.

## Notes

Grinding to powder; preparation method.
