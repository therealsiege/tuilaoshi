# 绯 Scarlet, deep red

- **Character**: 绯
- **Pinyin**: fēi
- **Meaning**: scarlet, deep red
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绯红 (fēi hóng) — scarlet, crimson
- 绯闻 (fēi wén) — scandal, gossip

## Example Sentences

- 夕阳把天空染成绯红色。(Xī yáng bǎ tiān kōng rǎn chéng fēi hóng sè.) — The setting sun dyed the sky crimson.

## Notes

Also used in 绯闻 (scandal), unrelated to color.
