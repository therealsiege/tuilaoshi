# 贵 Expensive

- **Character**: 贵
- **Pinyin**: guì
- **Meaning**: expensive, noble, precious
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 贵重 (guì zhòng) — valuable
- 宝贵 (bǎo guì) — precious

## Example Sentences

- 时间很宝贵。(Shí jiān hěn bǎo guì.) — Time is precious.

## Notes

贵 simplified from 貴. Contains shell/money radical 贝, indicating high value or cost.
