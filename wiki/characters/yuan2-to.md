# 援 To援

- **Character**: 援
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: to援, support
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 援助 (yuán zhù) — to援, assistance
- 支援 (zhī yuán) — to support

## Example Sentences

- 他们向灾区提供了援助。(Tāmen xiàng zāiqū tígōng le yuánzhù.) — They provided援 to the disaster area.

## Notes

援 combines hand 扌 with 爰 (pull), representing reaching out to help or援.
