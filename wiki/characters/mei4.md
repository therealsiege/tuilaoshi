# 妹 Younger Sister

- **Character**: 妹
- **Pinyin**: mèi
- **Meaning**: younger sister
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 妹妹 (mèi mei) — younger sister
- 姐妹 (jiě mèi) — sisters

## Example Sentences

- 我有一个妹妹。(Wǒ yǒu yī gè mèi mei.) — I have a younger sister.

## Notes

妹 with woman radical 女. Reduplicates to 妹妹. 姐妹 = sisters.
