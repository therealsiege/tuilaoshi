# 腱 Tendon

- **Character**: 腱
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: tendon
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 肌腱 (jī jiàn) — tendon
- 腱鞘 (jiàn qiào) — tendon sheath
- 跟腱 (gēn jiàn) — Achilles tendon

## Example Sentences

- 他跟腱撕裂了。(Tā gēn jiàn sī liè le.) — He tore his Achilles tendon.

## Notes

Connects muscle to bone; common site of sports injuries.
