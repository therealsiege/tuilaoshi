# 胳 Arm

- **Character**: 胳
- **Pinyin**: gē
- **Meaning**: arm (in 胳膊)
- **Radical**: 月 (flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 胳膊 (gē bo) — arm

## Example Sentences

- 我的胳膊酸了。(Wǒ de gē bo suān le.) — My arms are sore.

## Notes

胳 only used in compound 胳膊 (arm). With flesh radical 月.
