# 痰 Phlegm

- **Character**: 痰
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: phlegm, sputum
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痰液 (tán yè) — sputum
- 吐痰 (tǔ tán) — to spit phlegm

## Example Sentences

- 请不要随地吐痰。(Qǐng bú yào suí dì tǔ tán.) — Please don't spit anywhere.

## Notes

痰 has sickness 疒 with 炎 (inflammation) below, mucus from inflammation.
