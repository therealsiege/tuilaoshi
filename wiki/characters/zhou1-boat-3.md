# 舟 Boat

- **Character**: 舟
- **Pinyin**: zhōu
- **Meaning**: boat, vessel
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 刻舟求剑 (kè zhōu qiú jiàn) — cling to outdated methods
- 独木舟 (dú mù zhōu) — canoe

## Example Sentences

- 小舟在湖面上漂浮。(Xiǎo zhōu zài húmiàn shàng piāofú.) — A small boat floats on the lake.

## Notes

Classical word for boat; appears in 刻舟求剑. The character is itself a radical.
