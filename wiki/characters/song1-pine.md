# 松 Pine

- **Character**: 松
- **Pinyin**: sōng
- **Meaning**: pine tree, loose
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 松树 (sōng shù) — pine tree
- 松鼠 (sōng shǔ) — squirrel

## Example Sentences

- 山上长满了松树。(Shān shàng zhǎng mǎn le sōng shù.) — The mountain is covered with pine trees.

## Notes

松 shows wood 木 and公 (public), representing the松 tree standing freely.
