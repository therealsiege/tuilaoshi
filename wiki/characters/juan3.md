# 卷 To Roll

- **Character**: 卷
- **Pinyin**: juǎn
- **Meaning**: to roll up, to curl, roll
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卷起 (juǎn qǐ) — to roll up
- 卷发 (juǎn fà) — curly hair

## Example Sentences

- 她把袖子卷起来了。(Tā bǎ xiù zi juǎn qǐ lái le.) — She rolled up her sleeves.

## Notes

卷 can be juǎn (to roll up) or juàn (book, exam paper). As juǎn, used for rolling and curling.
