# 硬 Hard

- **Character**: 硬
- **Pinyin**: yìng
- **Meaning**: hard, stiff
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 坚硬 (jiān yìng) — hard, solid
- 硬化 (yìng huà) — hardening, sclerosis
- 僵硬 (jiāng yìng) — stiff, rigid

## Example Sentences

- 动脉硬化很危险。(Dòng mài yìng huà hěn wēi xiǎn.) — Arteriosclerosis is very dangerous.

## Notes

Abnormal tissue firmness; indicates various pathologies.
