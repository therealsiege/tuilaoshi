# 宪 Constitution

- **Character**: 宪
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: constitution, statute
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 宪法 (xiàn fǎ) — constitution
- 违宪 (wéi xiàn) — unconstitutional

## Example Sentences

- 宪法保护公民的权利。(Xiàn fǎ bǎo hù gōng mín de quán lì.) — The constitution protects citizens' rights.

## Notes

宪 simplified from 憲. Originally meant law/statute.
