# 灌 Infuse

- **Character**: 灌
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: irrigate, infuse
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 灌注 (guàn zhù) — to perfuse, infuse
- 灌肠 (guàn cháng) — enema
- 灌溉 (guàn gài) — to irrigate

## Example Sentences

- 手术需要心脏灌注。(Shǒu shù xū yào xīn zàng guàn zhù.) — Surgery requires cardiac perfusion.

## Notes

Fluid delivery; perfusion or irrigation.
