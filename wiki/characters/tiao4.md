# 跳 To jump, to hop, to skip

- **Character**: 跳
- **Pinyin**: tiào
- **Meaning**: to jump, to hop, to skip
- **Radical**: 足(foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — to dance
- 跳跃 (tiào yuè) — to jump, to leap

## Example Sentences

- 他跳过了那条小河。(Tā tiàoguò le nà tiáo xiǎo hé.) — He jumped over that stream.

## Notes

Most common verb for jumping or skipping.
