# 控 Control, accuse, drain

- **Character**: 控
- **Pinyin**: kòng
- **Meaning**: control, accuse, drain
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 控制 (kòngzhì) — control
- 管控 (guǎnkòng) — control and manage

## Example Sentences

- 政府加强边境管控。(Zhèngfǔ jiāqiáng biānjìng guǎnkòng.) — The government strengthened border control.

## Notes

Common for control; also legal term for accusation.
