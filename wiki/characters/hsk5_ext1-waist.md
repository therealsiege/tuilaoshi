# 腰 Waist, Lower Back, 腰痛 Backache

- **Character**: 腰
- **Pinyin**: yāo
- **Meaning**: waist, lower back, 腰痛 backache
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 腰痛 (yāo tòng) — backache
- 腰部 (yāo bù) — waist, lower back
- 弯腰 (wān yāo) — bend over

## Example Sentences

- 我今天腰痛。(Wǒ jīntiān yāotòng.) — My back hurts today.

## Notes

Common body part term for waist and lower back. 腰痛 (backache) is a frequent complaint, especially among office workers.
