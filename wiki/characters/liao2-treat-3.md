# 疗 Treat

- **Character**: 疗
- **Pinyin**: liáo
- **Meaning**: treat, cure
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 治疗 (zhì liáo) — to treat, treatment
- 疗法 (liáo fǎ) — therapy, treatment
- 医疗 (yī liáo) — medical treatment

## Example Sentences

- 这种病需要长期治疗。(Zhè zhǒng bìng xū yào cháng qī zhì liáo.) — This disease requires long-term treatment.

## Notes

Medical intervention; aims to cure or manage disease.
