# 蝶 Butterfly

- **Character**: 蝶
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: butterfly
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 蝴蝶 (hú dié) — butterfly
- 蝶泳 (dié yǒng) — butterfly stroke

## Example Sentences

- 花园里有很多蝴蝶。(Huā yuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō hú dié.) — There are many butterflies in the garden.

## Notes

蝶 has insect 虫 with 枼 phonetic, the fluttering insect.
