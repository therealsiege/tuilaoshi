# 充 To Fill

- **Character**: 充
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: to fill, to charge
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 充电 (chōng diàn) — to charge (battery)
- 充足 (chōng zú) — sufficient

## Example Sentences

- 手机需要充电了。(Shǒu jī xū yào chōng diàn le.) — The phone needs charging.

## Notes

充 means to fill up, perfect for charging batteries and filling spaces.
