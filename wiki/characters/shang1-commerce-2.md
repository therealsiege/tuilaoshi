# 商 Commerce, merchant, discuss

- **Character**: 商
- **Pinyin**: shāng
- **Meaning**: commerce, merchant, discuss
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 商业 (shāngyè) — commerce, business
- 商人 (shāngrén) — merchant, businessman

## Example Sentences

- 他经营一家商业公司。(Tā jīngyíng yī jiā shāngyè gōngsī.) — He runs a commercial company.

## Notes

Fundamental character for commerce, business, and negotiation.
