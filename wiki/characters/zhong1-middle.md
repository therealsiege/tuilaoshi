# 中 Middle

- **Character**: 中
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: middle, center, China
- **Radical**: 丨 (vertical stroke)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 中国 (zhōng guó) — China
- 中文 (zhōng wén) — Chinese language

## Example Sentences

- 我在学中文。(Wǒ zài xué zhōngwén.) — I am studying Chinese.

## Notes

中 is a pictograph of an arrow hitting the center of a target.
