# 塌 To Collapse

- **Character**: 塌
- **Pinyin**: tā
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to cave in
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 倒塌 (dǎo tā) — to collapse, to topple
- 塌陷 (tā xiàn) — to cave in

## Example Sentences

- 地震使很多房屋倒塌了。(Dì zhèn shǐ hěn duō fáng wū dǎo tā le.) — The earthquake caused many houses to collapse.

## Notes

塌 uses earth radical 土, indicating collapse or subsidence of ground or structures.
