# 卖 To Sell

- **Character**: 卖
- **Pinyin**: mài
- **Meaning**: to sell
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卖东西 (mài dōng xī) — to sell things
- 出卖 (chū mài) — to betray, to sell out

## Example Sentences

- 他在卖水果。(Tā zài mài shuǐ guǒ.) — He is selling fruit.

## Notes

卖 has an extra stroke (十) on top compared to 买, as if putting goods on display for sale.
