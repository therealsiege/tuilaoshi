# 试 To Try

- **Character**: 试
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to try, to test
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 考试 (kǎo shì) — exam
- 试试 (shì shì) — to give it a try

## Example Sentences

- 你试试看。(Nǐ shì shì kàn.) — Give it a try.

## Notes

试试 (reduplicated) means 'try it out' in a casual way. 试试看 adds 看 for 'try and see.'
