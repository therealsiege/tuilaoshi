# 宦 Official, eunuch

- **Character**: 宦
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: official, eunuch
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 宦官 (huàn guān) — eunuch
- 仕宦 (shì huàn) — be an official

## Example Sentences

- 在古代，宦官在宫廷中有很大的权力。(Zài gǔdài, huànguān zài gōngtíng zhōng yǒu hěn dà de quánlì.) — In ancient times, eunuchs had great power in the imperial court.

## Notes

Originally meant government official, but later became specifically associated with palace eunuchs who served the imperial family.
