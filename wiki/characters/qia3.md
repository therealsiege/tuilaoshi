# 卡 Checkpoint, block, card

- **Character**: 卡
- **Pinyin**: qiǎ
- **Meaning**: checkpoint, block, card
- **Radical**: 卜 (divination)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 关卡 (guān qiǎ) — checkpoint
- 卡住 (qiǎ zhù) — get stuck

## Example Sentences

- 前面有个关卡需要检查。(Qián miàn yǒu gè guān qiǎ xū yào jiǎn chá.) — There's a checkpoint ahead that requires inspection.

## Notes

Also read as kǎ for card or calorie
