# 轴 Axis

- **Character**: 轴
- **Pinyin**: zhóu
- **Meaning**: axis, axle, shaft
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 轴心 (zhóu xīn) — axis, core
- 车轴 (chē zhóu) — axle

## Example Sentences

- 这个轴承坏了。(Zhè ge zhóu chéng huài le.) — This bearing is broken.

## Notes

轴 with cart radical for axle, essential in mechanical and coordinate system terminology.
