# 旗 Flag, banner

- **Character**: 旗
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: flag, banner
- **Radical**: 方 (square)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 旗帜 (qí zhì) — flag, banner
- 国旗 (guó qí) — national flag

## Example Sentences

- 公司门口挂着旗帜。(Gōng sī mén kǒu guà zhe qí zhì.) — Flags hang at the company entrance.

## Notes

旗舰店 is flagship store. 旗号 means banner/signboard.
