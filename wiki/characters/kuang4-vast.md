# 旷 Vast, spacious, to neglect

- **Character**: 旷
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: vast, spacious, to neglect
- **Radical**: 日(sun)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 空旷 (kōng kuàng) — spacious and empty
- 旷课 (kuàng kè) — to skip class

## Example Sentences

- 这里的原野很空旷。(Zhèlǐ de yuányě hěn kōngkuàng.) — The field here is very spacious and empty.

## Notes

Can mean vast/open or to be absent/neglect.
