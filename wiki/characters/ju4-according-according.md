# 据 Evidence

- **Character**: 据
- **Pinyin**: jù
- **Meaning**: according to, evidence, data
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 数据 (shù jù) — data
- 根据 (gēn jù) — according to

## Example Sentences

- 根据报告，销售额增长了。(Gēn jù bào gào, xiāo shòu é zēng zhǎng le.) — According to the report, sales increased.

## Notes

据 simplified from 據. Hand 扌 holding evidence.
