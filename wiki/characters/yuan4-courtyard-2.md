# 院 Courtyard

- **Character**: 院
- **Pinyin**: yuàn
- **Meaning**: courtyard, institution
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 医院 (yī yuàn) — hospital
- 学院 (xué yuàn) — college

## Example Sentences

- 他在医院工作。(Tā zài yī yuàn gōng zuò.) — He works at the hospital.

## Notes

院 with mound radical for walled courtyard, extended to institutions and organizations.
