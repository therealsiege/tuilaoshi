# 概 General

- **Character**: 概
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: general, approximate
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 大概 (dà gài) — roughly, probably
- 概念 (gài niàn) — concept

## Example Sentences

- 大概要三个小时。(Dà gài yào sān ge xiǎo shí.) — It takes roughly three hours.

## Notes

大概 means approximately/probably. 概括 = to summarize.
