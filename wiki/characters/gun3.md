# 滚 Roll

- **Character**: 滚
- **Pinyin**: gǔn
- **Meaning**: roll, get lost
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 滚开 (gǔnkāi) — get lost, scram
- 滚动 (gǔndòng) — roll

## Example Sentences

- 球滚到了桌子下面。(Qiú gǔn dào le zhuōzi xiàmiàn.) — The ball rolled under the table.

## Notes

Rolling motion. Water radical. 滚开 is rude imperative (get lost). Physical and metaphorical uses.
