# 萼 Calyx

- **Character**: 萼
- **Pinyin**: è
- **Meaning**: calyx, sepal
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 花萼 (huā è) — calyx
- 萼片 (è piàn) — sepal

## Example Sentences

- 花萼保护着花朵。(Huā è bǎo hù zhe huā duǒ.) — The calyx protects the flower.

## Notes

萼 has grass 艹 with 咢 phonetic, protective outer layer of flower.
