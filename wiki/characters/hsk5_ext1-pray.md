# 祷 Pray, 祈祷 Pray

- **Character**: 祷
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: pray, 祈祷 pray
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 祈祷 (qí dǎo) — pray
- 祷告 (dǎo gào) — pray
- 祈求 (qí qiú) — pray for

## Example Sentences

- 信徒们在教堂里祈祷。(Xìntú men zài jiàotáng lǐ qídǎo.) — Believers pray in the church.

## Notes

Religious term for prayer. 祈祷 is used across religions (Christian, Buddhist, etc.) for formal prayer and supplication.
