# 各 Each

- **Character**: 各
- **Pinyin**: gè
- **Meaning**: each, every, various
- **Radical**: 夂 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 各种 (gè zhǒng) — various kinds
- 各位 (gè wèi) — everybody (polite)

## Example Sentences

- 各种各样的人都有。(Gè zhǒng gè yàng de rén dōu yǒu.) — There are all kinds of people.

## Notes

各 represents individual items going their separate ways. Essential for expressing variety and distributive meaning.
