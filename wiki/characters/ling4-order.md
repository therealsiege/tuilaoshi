# 令 Order

- **Character**: 令
- **Pinyin**: lìng
- **Meaning**: order, command, to cause
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 命令 (mìng lìng) — order, command
- 口令 (kǒu lìng) — password, command

## Example Sentences

- 将军下达了命令。(Jiāng jūn xià dá le mìng lìng.) — The general issued an order.

## Notes

令 shows a person kneeling to receive orders, representing commands.
