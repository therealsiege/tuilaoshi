# 巢 Nest, lair, den

- **Character**: 巢
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: nest, lair, den
- **Radical**: 巢 (nest)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 鸟巢 (niǎo cháo) — bird's nest
- 巢穴 (cháo xué) — nest, lair

## Example Sentences

- 燕子在屋檐下筑巢。(Yàn zi zài wū yán xià zhù cháo.) — Swallows build nests under the eaves.
