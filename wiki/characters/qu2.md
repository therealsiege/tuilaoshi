# 渠 Canal

- **Character**: 渠
- **Pinyin**: qú
- **Meaning**: canal, ditch, channel
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渠道 (qú dào) — channel
- 沟渠 (gōu qú) — irrigation ditch

## Example Sentences

- 水通过渠道流入农田。(Shuǐ tōng guò qú dào liú rù nóng tián.) — Water flows into farmland through canals.

## Notes

渠 has water 氵 with 巨 (huge), large water channel.
