# 盆 Basin

- **Character**: 盆
- **Pinyin**: pén
- **Meaning**: basin, pot
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 脸盆 (liǎnpén) — wash basin
- 花盆 (huāpén) — flower pot

## Example Sentences

- 她在脸盆里洗衣服。(Tā zài liǎnpén lǐ xǐ yīfu.) — She's washing clothes in the basin.

## Notes

Versatile household item. Can be for washing, planting, or holding water. The 皿 radical indicates container.
