# 耕 Plow, till, cultivate

- **Character**: 耕
- **Pinyin**: gēng
- **Meaning**: plow, till, cultivate
- **Radical**: 耒 (plow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耕地 (gēng dì) — plow land, arable land
- 耕种 (gēng zhòng) — plow and plant

## Example Sentences

- 春天到了，农民开始耕地播种。(Chūntiān dào le, nóngmín kāishǐ gēng dì bō zhǒng.) — Spring has arrived, and farmers begin plowing and sowing.

## Notes

Plowing land for cultivation. The character contains the plow radical (耒), reflecting its agricultural meaning.
