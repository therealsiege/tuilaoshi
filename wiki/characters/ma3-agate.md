# 玛 Agate

- **Character**: 玛
- **Pinyin**: mǎ
- **Meaning**: agate
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 玛瑙 (mǎ nǎo) — agate
- 玛瑙石 (mǎ nǎo shí) — agate stone

## Example Sentences

- 玛瑙是一种美丽的宝石。(Mǎ nǎo shì yī zhǒng měi lì de bǎo shí.) — Agate is a beautiful gemstone.

## Notes

玛 uses jade radical 玉, always appears with 瑙 to form the word for agate gemstone.
