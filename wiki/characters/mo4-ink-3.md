# 墨 Ink

- **Character**: 墨
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: ink, black
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 墨水 (mò shuǐ) — ink
- 笔墨 (bǐ mò) — pen and ink

## Example Sentences

- 笔墨纸砚是文房四宝。(Bǐ mò zhǐ yàn shì wén fáng sì bǎo.) — Brush, ink, paper, and inkstone are the four treasures of the study.

## Notes

墨 has black 黑 above earth 土, carbon black ink for writing.
