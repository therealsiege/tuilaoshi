# 季 Season

- **Character**: 季
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: season, quarter
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 季节 (jì jié) — season
- 四季 (sì jì) — four seasons

## Example Sentences

- 你最喜欢哪个季节？(Nǐ zuì xǐ huān nǎ ge jì jié?) — Which season do you like most?

## Notes

季 means a quarter of the year. 四季 are the four seasons. Also a surname.
