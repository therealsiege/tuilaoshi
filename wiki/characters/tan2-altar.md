# 坛 Altar, jar, arena

- **Character**: 坛
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: altar, jar, arena
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 天坛 (tiān tán) — Temple of Heaven
- 论坛 (lùn tán) — forum

## Example Sentences

- 天坛是明清两代祭天的地方。(Tiān tán shì míng qīng liǎng dài jì tiān de dì fāng.) — The Temple of Heaven was where emperors worshipped heaven in Ming and Qing dynasties.

## Notes

Altar for worship or earthenware jar
