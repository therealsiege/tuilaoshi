# 截 To Intercept

- **Character**: 截
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: to cut off, to intercept
- **Radical**: 戈 (spear)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 截断 (jié duàn) — to cut off
- 拦截 (lán jié) — to intercept

## Example Sentences

- 他截住了对手的攻击。(Tā jié zhù le duì shǒu de gōng jī.) — He intercepted his opponent's attack.

## Notes

截 has 雀 (bird) with 戈 (spear), cutting off with precision.
