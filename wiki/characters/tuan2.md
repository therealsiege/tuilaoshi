# 团 Group

- **Character**: 团
- **Pinyin**: tuán
- **Meaning**: group,団, to roll
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 团体 (tuán tǐ) — organization
- 团结 (tuán jié) — unity

## Example Sentences

- 我们要团结一致。(Wǒ men yào tuán jié yí zhì.) — We must unite as one.

## Notes

团 simplified from 團. Enclosure 囗 holding 才 (talent) together.
