# 昌 Prosperous, flourishing

- **Character**: 昌
- **Pinyin**: chāng
- **Meaning**: prosperous, flourishing
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 昌盛 (chāng shèng) — prosperous, thriving
- 繁荣昌盛 (fán róng chāng shèng) — prosperous and flourishing

## Example Sentences

- 祝愿国家繁荣昌盛。(Zhù yuàn guó jiā fán róng chāng shèng.) — Wish the country prosperity and flourishing.

## Notes

Literary term for prosperity. Often used in formal contexts and names. 昌明 means enlightened.
