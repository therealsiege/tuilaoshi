# 灿 Brilliant, splendid

- **Character**: 灿
- **Pinyin**: càn
- **Meaning**: brilliant, splendid
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 灿烂 (càn làn) — brilliant, splendid
- 金灿灿 (jīn càn càn) — golden and bright

## Example Sentences

- 阳光灿烂，天气晴朗。(Yáng guāng càn làn, tiān qì qíng lǎng.) — The sunshine is brilliant and the weather is clear.

## Notes

Often describes bright, radiant light or smiles.
