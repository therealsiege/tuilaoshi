# 云 Cloud

- **Character**: 云
- **Pinyin**: yún
- **Meaning**: cloud
- **Radical**: 二 (two)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 白云 (bái yún) — white cloud
- 云朵 (yún duǒ) — cloud

## Example Sentences

- 天上有很多云。(Tiān shàng yǒu hěn duō yún.) — There are many clouds in the sky.

## Notes

云 simplified from 雲, depicts swirling cloud formations. Also means 'to say' in classical Chinese.
