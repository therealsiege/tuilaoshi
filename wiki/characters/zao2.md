# 凿 To chisel, to carve

- **Character**: 凿
- **Pinyin**: záo
- **Meaning**: to chisel, to carve
- **Radical**: 凵(container)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 凿洞 (záo dòng) — to chisel a hole
- 穿凿 (chuān záo) — to bore through, far-fetched

## Example Sentences

- 工人们正在凿石头。(Gōngrénmen zhèngzài záo shítou.) — The workers are chiseling stone.

## Notes

Specifically for chiseling or carving hard materials.
