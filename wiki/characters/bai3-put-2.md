# 摆 To Place

- **Character**: 摆
- **Pinyin**: bǎi
- **Meaning**: to put, to place, to arrange, to摆ng
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 摆放 (bǎi fàng) — to arrange, to lay out
- 摇摆 (yáo bǎi) — to sway

## Example Sentences

- 桌子上摆满了书。(Zhuō zi shàng bǎi mǎn le shū.) — The table is filled with books.

## Notes

摆 means to put or arrange things in place. Common for setting out items or摆nging things.
