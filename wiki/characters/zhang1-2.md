# 章 Chapter, seal, badge

- **Character**: 章
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: 章节, seal, badge
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 章程 (zhāng chéng) — charter, regulations
- 规章 (guī zhāng) — rules and regulations

## Example Sentences

- 公司章程规定了管理制度。(Gōng sī zhāng chéng guī dìng le guǎn lǐ zhì dù.) — The company charter specifies the management system.

## Notes

公司章程 is articles of association. 合同章 is contract seal.
