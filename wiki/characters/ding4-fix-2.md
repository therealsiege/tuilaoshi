# 定 Fix, determine

- **Character**: 定
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: fix, determine
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 决定 (jué dìng) — decide
- 确定 (què dìng) — confirm, determine

## Example Sentences

- 价格已经确定。(Jià gé yǐ jīng què dìng.) — The price has been determined.

## Notes

定价 means pricing. 固定 means fixed/regular.
