# 验 To Test

- **Character**: 验
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: to examine, to test, to verify
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 经验 (jīng yàn) — experience
- 试验 (shì yàn) — experiment, test

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要做试验。(Wǒ men xū yào zuò shì yàn.) — We need to do an experiment.

## Notes

验 is simplified from 驗. Represents testing and verification through experience.
