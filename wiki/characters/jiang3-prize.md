# 奖 Prize

- **Character**: 奖
- **Pinyin**: jiǎng
- **Meaning**: prize, award, to reward
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 奖金 (jiǎng jīn) — bonus, prize money
- 获奖 (huò jiǎng) — to win a prize

## Example Sentences

- 她在比赛中获奖了。(Tā zài bǐ sài zhōng huò jiǎng le.) — She won a prize in the competition.

## Notes

奖 is simplified from 獎. Represents rewards and commendations.
