# 逐 To Chase

- **Character**: 逐
- **Pinyin**: zhú
- **Meaning**: to chase, to expel
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 逐渐 (zhú jiàn) — gradually
- 追逐 (zhuī zhú) — to chase

## Example Sentences

- 情况逐渐好转。(Qíng kuàng zhú jiàn hǎo zhuǎn.) — The situation gradually improved.

## Notes

逐 as a verb means to chase or expel. As an adverb (逐渐), it means gradually, step by step.
