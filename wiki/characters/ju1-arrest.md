# 拘 Arrest, detain, inflexible

- **Character**: 拘
- **Pinyin**: jū
- **Meaning**: arrest, detain, inflexible
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拘留 (jū liú) — detain
- 拘束 (jū shù) — constrained, ill at ease

## Example Sentences

- 他因违法被拘留了。(Tā yīn wéi fǎ bèi jū liú le.) — He was detained for breaking the law.

## Notes

Legal detention or overly formal behavior
