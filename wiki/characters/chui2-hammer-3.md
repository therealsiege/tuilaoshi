# 锤 Hammer

- **Character**: 锤
- **Pinyin**: chuí
- **Meaning**: hammer, to hammer
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锤子 (chuí zi) — hammer
- 千锤百炼 (qiān chuí bǎi liàn) — thoroughly tempered

## Example Sentences

- 他用锤子敲钉子。(Tā yòng chuí zi qiāo dīng zi.) — He hammered nails with a hammer.

## Notes

锤 simplified from 錘. Metal 钅 with 垂 (to hang down), a striking tool.
