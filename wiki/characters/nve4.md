# 疟 Malaria

- **Character**: 疟
- **Pinyin**: nüè
- **Meaning**: malaria
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 疟疾 (nüè jí) — malaria
- 疟蚊 (nüè wén) — malaria mosquito

## Example Sentences

- 疟疾在热带地区很常见。(Nüè jí zài rè dài dì qū hěn cháng jiàn.) — Malaria is common in tropical regions.

## Notes

疟 simplified from 瘧. Sickness 疒 with 虐 (cruel), a cruel disease.
