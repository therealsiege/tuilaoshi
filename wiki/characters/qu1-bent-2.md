# 曲 Bent

- **Character**: 曲
- **Pinyin**: qū
- **Meaning**: bent, curved
- **Radical**: 曰 (say)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 曲棍球 (qū gùn qiú) — field hockey, ice hockey
- 弯曲 (wān qū) — curved, bent

## Example Sentences

- 曲棍球是奥运会项目。(Qūgùnqiú shì Àoyùnhuì xiàngmù.) — Field hockey is an Olympic sport.

## Notes

Curved; curved stick in hockey
