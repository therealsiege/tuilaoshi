# 矮 Short

- **Character**: 矮
- **Pinyin**: ǎi
- **Meaning**: short, low
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 矮小 (ǎi xiǎo) — short and small
- 高矮 (gāo ǎi) — height

## Example Sentences

- 他的个子很矮。(Tā de gèzi hěn ǎi.) — He is very short.

## Notes

矮 combines arrow 矢 with 委 (bent), representing something bent down or low.
