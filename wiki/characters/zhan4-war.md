# 战 War

- **Character**: 战
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: war, battle, to fight
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 战争 (zhàn zhēng) — war
- 挑战 (tiǎo zhàn) — challenge

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个巨大的挑战。(Zhè shì yī gè jù dà de tiǎo zhàn.) — This is a huge challenge.

## Notes

战 simplified from 戰. Combines halberd 戈 and 单 suggesting combat with weapons.
