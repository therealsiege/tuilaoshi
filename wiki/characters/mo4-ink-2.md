# 墨 Ink

- **Character**: 墨
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: ink, ink stick, black
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 墨水 (mò shuǐ) — ink
- 笔墨 (bǐ mò) — pen and ink, writing

## Example Sentences

- 他用墨水写字。(Tā yòng mò shuǐ xiě zì.) — He writes with ink.

## Notes

墨 means ink or ink stick. Part of Four Treasures of the Study (文房四宝). Also means Mexico (墨西哥).
