# 策 Strategy, Plan, 政策 Policy

- **Character**: 策
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: strategy, plan, 政策 policy
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 政策 (zhèng cè) — policy
- 对策 (duì cè) — countermeasure
- 策略 (cè lüè) — strategy, tactics

## Example Sentences

- 政府发布了新的经济政策。(Zhèngfǔ fābù le xīn de jīngjì zhèngcè.) — The government issued new economic policies.

## Notes

Originally meant bamboo writing slip. Now used extensively in political and business contexts for policies and strategies.
