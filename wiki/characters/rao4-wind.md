# 绕 To wind, to coil, to revolve around, to detour

- **Character**: 绕
- **Pinyin**: rào
- **Meaning**: to wind, to coil, to revolve around, to detour
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 围绕 (wéi rào) — to revolve around, to center on
- 绕路 (rào lù) — to make a detour

## Example Sentences

- 地球绕着太阳转。(Dìqiú ràozhe tàiyáng zhuǎn.) — The Earth revolves around the sun.

## Notes

Can be used literally for winding or figuratively for topics.
