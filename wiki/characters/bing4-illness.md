# 病 Illness

- **Character**: 病
- **Pinyin**: bìng
- **Meaning**: illness, disease, sick
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 生病 (shēng bìng) — to get sick
- 病人 (bìng rén) — patient

## Example Sentences

- 他生病了。(Tā shēng bìng le.) — He got sick.

## Notes

病 uses the sickness radical 疒. 看病 = see a doctor, 毛病 = problem/flaw.
