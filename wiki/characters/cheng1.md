# 撑 To Support

- **Character**: 撑
- **Pinyin**: chēng
- **Meaning**: to support, to prop up, to pole (boat)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 支撑 (zhī chēng) — to prop up
- 撑伞 (chēng sǎn) — to hold umbrella

## Example Sentences

- 他用手撑着下巴。(Tā yòng shǒu chēng zhe xià bā.) — He propped his chin with his hand.

## Notes

撑 combines hand 扌 and 掌 (palm), representing supporting or propping up.
