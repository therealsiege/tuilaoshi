# 眼 Eye

- **Character**: 眼
- **Pinyin**: yǎn
- **Meaning**: eye
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 眼睛 (yǎn jing) — eye
- 眼镜 (yǎn jìng) — eyeglasses

## Example Sentences

- 她的眼睛很大。(Tā de yǎn jing hěn dà.) — Her eyes are very big.

## Notes

眼 with eye radical 目. 眼睛 is complete word for eye. 眼镜 = eyeglasses.
