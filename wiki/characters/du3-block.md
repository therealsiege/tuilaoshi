# 堵 To Block

- **Character**: 堵
- **Pinyin**: dǔ
- **Meaning**: to block, to stop up
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 堵车 (dǔ chē) — traffic jam
- 堵塞 (dǔ sè) — to block up

## Example Sentences

- 早上总是堵车。(Zǎo shang zǒng shì dǔ chē.) — There's always a traffic jam in the morning.

## Notes

堵 combines earth 土 and 者 (person), originally referring to building an earthen wall to block passage.
