# 围 To Surround

- **Character**: 围
- **Pinyin**: wéi
- **Meaning**: to surround, to encircle, circumference
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 范围 (fàn wéi) — scope
- 周围 (zhōu wéi) — surroundings

## Example Sentences

- 周围很安静。(Zhōu wéi hěn ān jìng.) — The surroundings are very quiet.

## Notes

围 shows an enclosure radical, representing surrounding or encircling. Essential for spatial relationships.
