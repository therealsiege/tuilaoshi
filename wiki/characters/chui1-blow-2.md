# 吹 To Blow

- **Character**: 吹
- **Pinyin**: chuī
- **Meaning**: to blow, to play (wind instrument)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吹笛 (chuī dí) — to play the flute
- 吹奏 (chuī zòu) — to play (wind instrument)

## Example Sentences

- 他吹笛子吹得很好。(Tā chuī dí zi chuī de hěn hǎo.) — He plays the flute very well.

## Notes

吹 means to blow; used for all wind instruments.
