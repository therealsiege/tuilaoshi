# 覆 To Cover

- **Character**: 覆
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to cover, to overturn
- **Radical**: 覀 (west)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 覆盖 (fù gài) — to cover
- 颠覆 (diān fù) — to subvert

## Example Sentences

- 雪覆盖了整个城市。(Xuě fù gài le zhěng gè chéng shì.) — Snow covered the entire city.

## Notes

覆 shows covering from above, representing overturning or covering completely.
