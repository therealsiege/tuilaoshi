# 汤 Soup

- **Character**: 汤
- **Pinyin**: tāng
- **Meaning**: soup, broth
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 喝汤 (hē tāng) — to drink soup
- 汤面 (tāng miàn) — noodle soup

## Example Sentences

- 这个汤很好喝。(Zhè ge tāng hěn hǎo hē.) — This soup is very good.

## Notes

汤 means soup. 鸡汤 = chicken soup. 汤匙 = soup spoon.
