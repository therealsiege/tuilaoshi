# 怜 To pity, to sympathize

- **Character**: 怜
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: to pity, to sympathize
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 可怜 (kě lián) — pitiful, poor
- 怜悯 (lián mǐn) — to pity, compassion

## Example Sentences

- 她很可怜，失去了所有亲人。(Tā hěn kělián, shīqù le suǒyǒu qīnrén.) — She is very pitiful, having lost all her relatives.

## Notes

Expresses sympathy or compassion for someone.
