# 闭 To Close

- **Character**: 闭
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to close, to shut
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 关闭 (guān bì) — to close, to shut down
- 闭嘴 (bì zuǐ) — shut up

## Example Sentences

- 请闭上眼睛。(Qǐng bì shàng yǎn jīng.) — Please close your eyes.

## Notes

闭 is more literary than 关. 闭嘴 = shut your mouth. 闭幕 = closing ceremony.
