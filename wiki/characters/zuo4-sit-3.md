# 坐 Sit, seat, travel by

- **Character**: 坐
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: sit, seat, travel by
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 坐标 (zuò biāo) — coordinate
- 坐下 (zuò xià) — sit down

## Example Sentences

- 用坐标可以确定点的位置。(Yòng zuò biāo kě yǐ què dìng diǎn de wèi zhì.) — Coordinates can determine the position of a point.
