# 贫 Poor, Poverty, 贫穷 Poor

- **Character**: 贫
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: poor, poverty, 贫穷 poor
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贫穷 (pín qióng) — poor, impoverished
- 贫困 (pín kùn) — poverty-stricken
- 贫乏 (pín fá) — lacking, meager

## Example Sentences

- 政府帮助贫困地区发展经济。(Zhèngfǔ bāngzhù pínkùn dìqū fāzhǎn jīngjì.) — The government helps poverty-stricken areas develop their economy.

## Notes

Key term for poverty and lack. 贫穷/贫困 describe economic poverty, while 贫乏 describes general lack or deficiency.
