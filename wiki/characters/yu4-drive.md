# 驭 Drive, control

- **Character**: 驭
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: drive, control
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 驾驭 (jiàyù) — control, master
- 统驭 (tǒngyù) — control, command

## Example Sentences

- 他善于驾驭复杂局面。(Tā shànyú jiàyù fùzá júmiàn.) — He is good at controlling complex situations.

## Notes

Originally meant driving horses, now used for controlling situations.
