# 亡 Lose

- **Character**: 亡
- **Pinyin**: wáng
- **Meaning**: to lose, to die, to flee
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 亡羊补牢 (wáng yáng bǔ láo) — better late than never
- 死亡 (sǐ wáng) — death

## Example Sentences

- 亡羊补牢，为时未晚。(Wáng yáng bǔ láo, wéi shí wèi wǎn.) — It's not too late to mend the pen after losing sheep.

## Notes

Used in 亡羊补牢: to lose (sheep), meaning it's better late than never to fix a problem.
