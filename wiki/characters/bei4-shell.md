# 贝 Shell, shellfish

- **Character**: 贝
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: shell, shellfish
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 贝壳 (bèi ké) — shell, seashell
- 扇贝 (shàn bèi) — scallop

## Example Sentences

- 海滩上有很多贝壳。(Hǎitān shàng yǒu hěn duō bèiké.) — There are many shells on the beach.

## Notes

This character is itself a radical. Ancient currency was shells.
