# 杯 Cup

- **Character**: 杯
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: cup, glass, measure word
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 杯子 (bēi zi) — cup, glass
- 一杯 (yī bēi) — one cup

## Example Sentences

- 我想喝一杯水。(Wǒ xiǎng hē yī bēi shuǐ.) — I want to drink a glass of water.

## Notes

杯 with wood radical, cups originally wooden. Also measure word for beverages.
