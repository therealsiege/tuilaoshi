# 怜 To Pity

- **Character**: 怜
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: to pity, to sympathize
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 怜悯 (lián mǐn) — to pity
- 可怜 (kě lián) — pitiful

## Example Sentences

- 她可怜那只小狗。(Tā kě lián nà zhī xiǎo gǒu.) — She feels sorry for that little dog.

## Notes

怜 simplified from 憐. Heart 忄 with 令 (command), heart moved by another's suffering.
