# 盘 Disk

- **Character**: 盘
- **Pinyin**: pán
- **Meaning**: plate, disk, to coil
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 键盘 (jiàn pán) — keyboard
- 硬盘 (yìng pán) — hard disk

## Example Sentences

- 硬盘空间不够了。(Yìng pán kōng jiān bù gòu le.) — There's not enough hard disk space.

## Notes

盘 from dish/plate extends to circular disks and flat surfaces like keyboards.
