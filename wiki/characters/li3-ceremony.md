# 礼 Ceremony

- **Character**: 礼
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: ceremony, gift, propriety
- **Radical**: 礻(spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 礼物 (lǐ wù) — gift
- 礼貌 (lǐ mào) — polite

## Example Sentences

- 送礼是中国的传统。(Sòng lǐ shì zhōng guó de chuán tǒng.) — Gift-giving is a Chinese tradition.

## Notes

礼 simplified from 禮. The spirit radical 礻reflects the religious origins of ceremonial rites in ancient China.
