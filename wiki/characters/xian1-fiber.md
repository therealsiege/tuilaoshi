# 纤 Fiber, fine

- **Character**: 纤
- **Pinyin**: xiān
- **Meaning**: fiber, fine
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 光纤 (guāng xiān) — optical fiber
- 纤维 (xiān wéi) — fiber

## Example Sentences

- 光纤网络覆盖全国。(Guāng xiān wǎng luò fù gài quán guó.) — Optical fiber network covers the whole country.

## Notes

Used in 光纤 (optical fiber), telecommunications
