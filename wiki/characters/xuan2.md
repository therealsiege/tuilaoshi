# 悬 To hang, to suspend, unsettled

- **Character**: 悬
- **Pinyin**: xuán
- **Meaning**: to hang, to suspend, unsettled
- **Radical**: 心(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 悬挂 (xuán guà) — to hang, to suspend
- 悬崖 (xuán yá) — cliff, precipice

## Example Sentences

- 前方有一座悬崖。(Qiánfāng yǒu yī zuò xuányá.) — There is a cliff ahead.

## Notes

Can mean hanging or describe uncertain/unsettled situations.
