# 展 To Unfold

- **Character**: 展
- **Pinyin**: zhǎn
- **Meaning**: to展开, to display, exhibition
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 展开 (zhǎn kāi) — to unfold
- 发展 (fā zhǎn) — to develop

## Example Sentences

- 经济在快速发展。(Jīng jì zài kuài sù fā zhǎn.) — The economy is developing rapidly.

## Notes

展 shows unrolling or展开ing, representing display and展开.
