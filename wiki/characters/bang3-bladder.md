# 膀 Bladder

- **Character**: 膀
- **Pinyin**: bǎng
- **Meaning**: bladder; upper arm
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/moon)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 膀胱 (bǎng guāng) — bladder
- 肩膀 (jiān bǎng) — shoulder
- 臂膀 (bì bǎng) — arm

## Example Sentences

- 膀胱炎需要抗生素治疗。(Bǎng guāng yán xū yào kàng shēng sù zhì liáo.) — Cystitis requires antibiotic treatment.

## Notes

Usually appears in 膀胱 (bladder) in medical contexts.
