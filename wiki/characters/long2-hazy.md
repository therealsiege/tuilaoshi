# 胧 Hazy, dim

- **Character**: 胧
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: hazy, dim
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 朦胧 (ménglóng) — hazy, dim
- 胧胧 (lónglóng) — hazy, misty

## Example Sentences

- 夜色朦朦胧胧。(Yèsè méngméng lónglóng.) — The night is hazy.

## Notes

Literary term for haziness, typically used with 朦.
