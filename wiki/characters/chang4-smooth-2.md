# 畅 Smooth, unhindered, free

- **Character**: 畅
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: smooth, unhindered, free
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 畅通 (chàngtōng) — unobstructed, smooth
- 畅谈 (chàngtán) — talk freely

## Example Sentences

- 交通很畅通。(Jiāotōng hěn chàngtōng.) — Traffic is smooth.

## Notes

Common character for smoothness or freedom from obstruction.
