# 揉 Rub

- **Character**: 揉
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: rub, knead
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揉眼睛 (róu yǎnjing) — rub eyes
- 揉面 (róu miàn) — knead dough

## Example Sentences

- 他揉了揉眼睛。(Tā róu le róu yǎnjing.) — He rubbed his eyes.

## Notes

Rubbing or kneading action. Hand radical. Common for eyes, dough, massage. Gentle repetitive motion.
