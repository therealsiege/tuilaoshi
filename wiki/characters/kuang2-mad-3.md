# 狂 Mad

- **Character**: 狂
- **Pinyin**: kuáng
- **Meaning**: mad, crazy
- **Radical**: 犭 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 狂躁 (kuáng zào) — manic
- 疯狂 (fēng kuáng) — crazy, insane
- 狂欢 (kuáng huān) — carnival, revelry

## Example Sentences

- 躁狂发作需要及时治疗。(Zào kuáng fā zuò xū yào jí shí zhì liáo.) — Manic episodes require timely treatment.

## Notes

Extreme excitement or madness; psychiatric term.
