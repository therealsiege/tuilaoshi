# 住 — Live / Reside

- **Character**: 住
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to live, to reside; to stop, to cease (as complement: firmly)
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 住在 (zhù zài) — to live at/in
- 住院 (zhùyuàn) — to be hospitalized
- 记住 (jìzhù) — to memorize, to remember firmly
- 抓住 (zhuāzhù) — to grab hold of
- 站住 (zhànzhù) — to stand still, halt
- 住处 (zhùchù) — residence, dwelling

## Example Sentences

- 你住在哪儿？(Nǐ zhù zài nǎr?) — Where do you live?
- 他在医院住了三天。(Tā zài yīyuàn zhù le sān tiān.) — He stayed in the hospital for three days.
- 请站住，别动！(Qǐng zhànzhù, bié dòng!) — Stop right there, don't move!

## Notes

住 combines 亻(person) with 主 (zhǔ, master/host), suggesting a person who is the master of a place — someone residing there. Beyond its primary meaning of "to live/reside," 住 is extremely productive as a resultative complement indicating that an action has been completed firmly or securely: 记住 (remember firmly), 抓住 (grab hold), 站住 (stand firm). This complement function makes 住 appear far more frequently than its basic meaning alone would suggest. The question 你住在哪儿？ is one of the most common getting-to-know-you questions.
