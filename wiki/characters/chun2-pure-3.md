# 淳 Pure, honest, simple

- **Character**: 淳
- **Pinyin**: chún
- **Meaning**: pure, honest, simple
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淳朴 (chúnpǔ) — honest and simple
- 淳厚 (chúnhòu) — honest and kindhearted

## Example Sentences

- 他性格淳朴。(Tā xìnggé chúnpǔ.) — His character is honest and simple.

## Notes

Literary term for purity, honesty, or simplicity.
