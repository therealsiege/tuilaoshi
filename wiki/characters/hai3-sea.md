# 海 Sea

- **Character**: 海
- **Pinyin**: hǎi
- **Meaning**: sea, ocean
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 上海 (shàng hǎi) — Shanghai
- 大海 (dà hǎi) — ocean

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢看海。(Wǒ xǐ huān kàn hǎi.) — I like looking at the sea.

## Notes

海 uses the water radical. 海外 = overseas, 海鲜 = seafood.
