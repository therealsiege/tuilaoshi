# 帅 Handsome

- **Character**: 帅
- **Pinyin**: shuài
- **Meaning**: handsome, commander, to lead
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 元帅 (yuán shuài) — marshal
- 统帅 (tǒng shuài) — commander-in-chief

## Example Sentences

- 这个男孩很帅。(Zhè ge nán hái hěn shuài.) — This boy is very handsome.

## Notes

帅 is simplified from 帥. Originally meant military commander; now commonly means handsome.
