# 呼 To Exhale

- **Character**: 呼
- **Pinyin**: hū
- **Meaning**: to exhale, to shout, to call
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 呼吸 (hū xī) — to breathe
- 呼叫 (hū jiào) — to call out

## Example Sentences

- 深呼吸可以放松。(Shēn hū xī kě yǐ fàng sōng.) — Deep breathing can help you relax.

## Notes

呼 shows mouth 口 and 乎 (question particle), representing calling out or breathing out.
