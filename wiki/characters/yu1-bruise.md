# 淤 Bruise

- **Character**: 淤
- **Pinyin**: yū
- **Meaning**: bruise, blood stasis
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淤血 (yū xuè) — blood stasis
- 淤青 (yū qīng) — bruise

## Example Sentences

- 他腿上有淤青。(Tā tuǐ shàng yǒu yū qīng.) — He has a bruise on his leg.

## Notes

淤 has water 氵 with 於, stagnant blood pooling under skin.
