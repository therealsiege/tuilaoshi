# 瑙 Agate

- **Character**: 瑙
- **Pinyin**: nǎo
- **Meaning**: agate
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 玛瑙 (mǎ nǎo) — agate
- 红玛瑙 (hóng mǎ nǎo) — red agate

## Example Sentences

- 她收藏了很多玛瑙。(Tā shōu cáng le hěn duō mǎ nǎo.) — She has collected a lot of agate.

## Notes

瑙 uses jade radical 玉, only appears paired with 玛 to refer to the semiprecious stone agate.
