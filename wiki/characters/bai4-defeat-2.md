# 败 Defeat

- **Character**: 败
- **Pinyin**: bài
- **Meaning**: to defeat, lose
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 失败 (shī bài) — to fail, lose
- 战败 (zhàn bài) — to be defeated

## Example Sentences

- 他们遭遇了惨败。(Tāmen zāoyù le cǎnbài.) — They suffered a crushing defeat.

## Notes

Defeat and losing; failure in competition
