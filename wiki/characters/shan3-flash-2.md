# 闪 To Flash

- **Character**: 闪
- **Pinyin**: shǎn
- **Meaning**: to flash, to dodge, to闪闪, to sprain
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 闪电 (shǎn diàn) — lightning
- 闪烁 (shǎn shuò) — to twinkle

## Example Sentences

- 天空中闪电了。(Tiān kōng zhōng shǎn diàn le.) — Lightning flashed in the sky.

## Notes

闪 means to flash or dodge. Used in闪电 (lightning), 闪开 (get out of the way), sprain (闪腰).
