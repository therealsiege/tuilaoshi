# 纱 纱, 纱纱

- **Character**: 纱
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: 纱, 纱纱
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纱布 (shā bù) — 纱纱
- 窗纱 (chuāng shā) — 纱纱

## Example Sentences

- 窗户上挂着白色的纱帘。(Chuāng hu shàng guà zhe bái sè de shā lián.) — White gauze curtains hang on the windows.
