# 翁 Old Man

- **Character**: 翁
- **Pinyin**: wēng
- **Meaning**: old man, elderly gentleman
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 塞翁失马 (sài wēng shī mǎ) — a blessing in disguise
- 老翁 (lǎo wēng) — old man

## Example Sentences

- 一位老翁在河边钓鱼。(Yī wèi lǎo wēng zài hé biān diàoyú.) — An old man fishes by the river.

## Notes

Used in 塞翁失马: the old man at the frontier whose horse ran away.
