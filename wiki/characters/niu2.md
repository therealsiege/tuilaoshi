# 牛 Cow

- **Character**: 牛
- **Pinyin**: niú
- **Meaning**: cow, ox, cattle
- **Radical**: 牛 (ox)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 牛肉 (niú ròu) — beef
- 牛奶 (niú nǎi) — milk

## Example Sentences

- 我每天喝牛奶。(Wǒ měi tiān hē niú nǎi.) — I drink milk every day.

## Notes

牛 is a pictograph of an ox head with horns. Colloquially, 牛 also means 'awesome/cool' in slang.
