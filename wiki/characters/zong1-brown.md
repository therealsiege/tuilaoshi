# 棕 Brown

- **Character**: 棕
- **Pinyin**: zōng
- **Meaning**: brown, palm
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 棕色 (zōng sè) — brown color
- 棕榈 (zōng lǘ) — palm tree

## Example Sentences

- 他有棕色的头发。(Tā yǒu zōng sè de tóu fa.) — He has brown hair.

## Notes

棕 originally referred to palm trees (which have brown fibers), and by extension the brown color itself.
