# 雷 Thunder

- **Character**: 雷
- **Pinyin**: léi
- **Meaning**: thunder
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 打雷 (dǎ léi) — to thunder
- 雷雨 (léi yǔ) — thunderstorm

## Example Sentences

- 外面在打雷。(Wài miàn zài dǎ léi.) — It's thundering outside.

## Notes

雷 combines rain 雨 and field 田. Thunder comes with rain over fields.
