# 玲 Tinkling of jade

- **Character**: 玲
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: tinkling of jade
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 玲珑 (líng lóng) — exquisite, delicate
- 小巧玲珑 (xiǎo qiǎo líng lóng) — delicate and exquisite

## Example Sentences

- 这座小巧玲珑的亭子很精致。(Zhè zuò xiǎo qiǎo líng lóng de tíng zi hěn jīng zhì.) — This delicate little pavilion is very exquisite.

## Notes

Commonly used to describe delicate, refined objects.
