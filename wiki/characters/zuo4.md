# 做 To Do/Make

- **Character**: 做
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: to do, to make
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 做饭 (zuòfàn) — to cook
- 做事 (zuòshì) — to work, to handle matters
- 做梦 (zuòmèng) — to dream
- 做法 (zuòfǎ) — method, way of doing things
- 做客 (zuòkè) — to be a guest

## Example Sentences

- 你在做什么？(Nǐ zài zuò shénme?) — What are you doing?
- 妈妈在做饭。(Māma zài zuòfàn.) — Mom is cooking.
- 我想做一个老师。(Wǒ xiǎng zuò yí gè lǎoshī.) — I want to be a teacher.

## Notes

做 contains the person radical 亻 on the left, suggesting human activity. It is often compared with 作 (zuò), which shares the same pronunciation. In modern usage, 做 is preferred for concrete actions (做饭, 做事) while 作 tends toward abstract or literary contexts (作品 work/piece, 工作 work/job). Both are HSK1-level essentials.
