# 燥 Dry

- **Character**: 燥
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: dry, parched, impatient
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 干燥 (gān zào) — dry
- 枯燥 (kū zào) — dull

## Example Sentences

- 这里的气候很干燥。(Zhè lǐ de qì hòu hěn gān zào.) — The climate here is very dry.

## Notes

燥 has fire 火 with 喿 (many birds), parched by heat.
