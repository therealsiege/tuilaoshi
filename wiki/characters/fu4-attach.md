# 附 Attach, append, be close to

- **Character**: 附
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: attach, append, be close to
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 附近 (fù jìn) — nearby, vicinity
- 附件 (fù jiàn) — attachment, accessory

## Example Sentences

- 请查看邮件附件。(Qǐng chá kàn yóu jiàn fù jiàn.) — Please check the email attachment.

## Notes

Means to attach or append. 附属 means subsidiary. 附和 means to echo or agree blindly.
