# 翻 To Turn Over

- **Character**: 翻
- **Pinyin**: fān
- **Meaning**: to turn over, to flip, to translate
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 翻译 (fān yì) — to translate
- 翻身 (fān shēn) — to turn over

## Example Sentences

- 请翻到第十页。(Qǐng fān dào dì shí yè.) — Please turn to page ten.

## Notes

翻 feather 羽 plus 番 (to repeat), flipping motion like wings.
