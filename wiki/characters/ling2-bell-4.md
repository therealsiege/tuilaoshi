# 铃 Bell

- **Character**: 铃
- **Pinyin**: líng
- **Meaning**: bell, chime
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 掩耳盗铃 (yǎn ěr dào líng) — self-deception
- 门铃 (mén líng) — doorbell

## Example Sentences

- 门铃响了。(Mén líng xiǎng le.) — The doorbell rang.

## Notes

The bell in 掩耳盗铃; refers to small bells and chimes.
