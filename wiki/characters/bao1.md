# 剥 To Peel

- **Character**: 剥
- **Pinyin**: bāo
- **Meaning**: to peel, strip
- **Radical**: 刂(knife)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 剥削 (bō xuē) — to exploit
- 剥皮 (bāo pí) — to peel, skin

## Example Sentences

- 请帮我剥这个橙子。(Qǐng bāng wǒ bāo zhège chéngzi.) — Please help me peel this orange.

## Notes

剥 can be read as bāo (to peel) or bō (to exploit). Combines knife 刂 with 彔 (record), suggesting stripping away layers.
