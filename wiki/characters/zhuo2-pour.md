# 酌 Pour wine, drink, consider

- **Character**: 酌
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: pour wine, drink, consider
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 斟酌 (zhēn zhuó) — consider, deliberate
- 酌情 (zhuó qíng) — use discretion

## Example Sentences

- 这件事需要斟酌后再做决定。(Zhè jiàn shì xū yào zhēn zhuó hòu zài zuò jué dìng.) — This matter needs to be carefully considered before making a decision.

## Notes

Original meaning is pouring/drinking wine; now means to consider.
