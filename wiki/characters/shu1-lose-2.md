# 输 Lose

- **Character**: 输
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: to lose, transport
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 输球 (shū qiú) — to lose a game
- 输赢 (shū yíng) — win or lose

## Example Sentences

- 虽然输了，但我们尽力了。(Suīrán shū le, dàn wǒmen jìnlì le.) — Although we lost, we did our best.

## Notes

Losing in competition; also means transport/transmit in other contexts
