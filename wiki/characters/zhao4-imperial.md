# 诏 Imperial decree, summon

- **Character**: 诏
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: imperial decree, summon
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 诏书 (zhàoshū) — imperial edict
- 下诏 (xiàzhào) — issue an imperial decree

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝下诏赦免囚犯。(Huángdì xiàzhào shèmiǎn qiúfàn.) — The emperor issued a decree pardoning prisoners.

## Notes

Specifically refers to edicts issued by emperors in imperial China.
