# 妒 To Envy

- **Character**: 妒
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: to envy, be jealous
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 妒忌 (dù jì) — to envy, be jealous
- 嫉妒 (jí dù) — to be jealous

## Example Sentences

- 他嫉妒别人的成功。(Tā jídù biéren de chénggōng.) — He is jealous of others' success.

## Notes

妒 combines woman 女 with 户 (door), historically associated with jealousy in polygamous households.
