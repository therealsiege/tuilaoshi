# 存 To Store

- **Character**: 存
- **Pinyin**: cún
- **Meaning**: to exist, to store, to save
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 存在 (cún zài) — to exist
- 保存 (bǎo cún) — to save, preserve

## Example Sentences

- 请保存文件。(Qǐng bǎo cún wén jiàn.) — Please save the file.

## Notes

存 means to store or exist. 存钱 = save money, 存在 = exist, 生存 = survive.
