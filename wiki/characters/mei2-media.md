# 媒 Media, matchmaker

- **Character**: 媒
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: media, matchmaker
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 媒体 (méi tǐ) — media
- 传媒 (chuán méi) — media

## Example Sentences

- 新媒体广告很流行。(Xīn méi tǐ guǎng gào hěn liú xíng.) — New media advertising is very popular.

## Notes

媒介 means medium/vehicle. 自媒体 means self-media.
