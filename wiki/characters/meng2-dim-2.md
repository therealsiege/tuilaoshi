# 朦 Hazy

- **Character**: 朦
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: dim, hazy
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 朦胧 (méng lóng) — hazy, dim
- 朦朦 (méng méng) — misty

## Example Sentences

- 月色朦胧，很浪漫。(Yuè sè méng lóng, hěn làng màn.) — The moonlight is hazy and romantic.

## Notes

朦 has moon 月 with 蒙 (to cover), obscured by mist.
