# 判 Judge, sentence, distinguish

- **Character**: 判
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: judge, sentence, distinguish
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 判决 (pàn jué) — judgment, verdict
- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge, determine

## Example Sentences

- 法院判决他三年监禁。(Fǎ yuàn pàn jué tā sān nián jiān jìn.) — The court sentenced him to three years in prison.

## Notes

Essential legal term. 判刑 means to sentence. 审判 means to try (a case). 判别 means to distinguish.
