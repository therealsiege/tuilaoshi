# 导 Lead, Guide, 领导 Leader

- **Character**: 导
- **Pinyin**: dǎo
- **Meaning**: lead, guide, 领导 leader
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 领导 (lǐng dǎo) — leader, lead
- 倡导 (chàng dǎo) — advocate
- 指导 (zhǐ dǎo) — guide, instruct

## Example Sentences

- 他是公司的领导之一。(Tā shì gōngsī de lǐngdǎo zhī yī.) — He is one of the company's leaders.

## Notes

Essential character in organizational contexts. 领导 (leader) is extremely common in business and government settings.
