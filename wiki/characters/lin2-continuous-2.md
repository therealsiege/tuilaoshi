# 霖 Continuous Rain

- **Character**: 霖
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: continuous rain
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 霖雨 (lín yǔ) — continuous rain
- 甘霖 (gān lín) — timely rain

## Example Sentences

- 久旱逢甘霖是喜事。(Jiǔ hàn féng gān lín shì xǐ shì.) — Rain after a long drought is a joyous occasion.

## Notes

霖 has rain 雨 with 林 (forest), prolonged rain like forest density.
