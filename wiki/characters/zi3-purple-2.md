# 紫 Purple

- **Character**: 紫
- **Pinyin**: zǐ
- **Meaning**: purple, violet
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 紫色 (zǐ sè) — purple color
- 紫外线 (zǐ wài xiàn) — ultraviolet rays

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢紫色。(Tā xǐ huān zǐ sè.) — She likes purple.

## Notes

紫 means purple. 紫禁城 = Forbidden City (Purple Forbidden City).
