# 撕 Tear

- **Character**: 撕
- **Pinyin**: sī
- **Meaning**: tear, rip
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撕开 (sī kāi) — tear open
- 撕破 (sīpò) — tear, rip

## Example Sentences

- 他把信撕开了。(Tā bǎ xìn sī kāi le.) — He tore the letter open.

## Notes

Tearing action. Hand radical. Common with paper or fabric. Destructive action vocabulary.
