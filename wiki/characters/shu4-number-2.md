# 数 Number

- **Character**: 数
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: number, to count
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 参数 (cān shù) — parameter
- 数据 (shù jù) — data

## Example Sentences

- 请核对数据。(Qǐng hé duì shù jù.) — Please verify the data.

## Notes

数 as shù means number/to count; as shǔ means to count things. Fundamental to all data terminology.
