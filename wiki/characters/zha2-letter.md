# 札 Letter

- **Character**: 札
- **Pinyin**: zhá
- **Meaning**: letter, note
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 书札 (shū zhá) — letter
- 手札 (shǒu zhá) — handwritten note

## Example Sentences

- 他收到了一封手札。(Tā shōu dào le yì fēng shǒu zhá.) — He received a handwritten note.

## Notes

札 has wood 木 with 乙 (second), small wooden slip for notes.
