# 裱 裱裱, 裱裱裱

- **Character**: 裱
- **Pinyin**: biǎo
- **Meaning**: 裱裱, 裱裱裱
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 装裱 (zhuāng biǎo) — 裱裱裱裱
- 裱画 (biǎo huà) — 裱裱

## Example Sentences

- 这幅字画需要重新装裱。(Zhè fú zì huà xū yào chóng xīn zhuāng biǎo.) — This calligraphy and painting needs to be remounted.

## Notes

Traditional Chinese technique for mounting calligraphy and paintings.
