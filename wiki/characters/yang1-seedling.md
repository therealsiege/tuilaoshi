# 秧 Seedling, young plant

- **Character**: 秧
- **Pinyin**: yāng
- **Meaning**: seedling, young plant
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 秧苗 (yāng miáo) — seedlings
- 插秧 (chā yāng) — transplant rice seedlings

## Example Sentences

- 农民正在田里插秧。(Nóng mín zhèng zài tián lǐ chā yāng.) — Farmers are transplanting rice seedlings in the fields.

## Notes

Especially rice seedlings in traditional farming
