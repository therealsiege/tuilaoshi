# 修 Repair, renovate

- **Character**: 修
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: repair, renovate
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 修理 (xiū lǐ) — repair, fix
- 维修 (wéi xiū) — maintain, repair

## Example Sentences

- 房子需要维修。(Fáng zi xū yào wéi xiū.) — The house needs repairs.

## Notes

装修 is renovation. 保修 is warranty/maintenance.
