# 良 Good, fine, very, excellent

- **Character**: 良
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: good, fine, very, excellent
- **Radical**: 艮(stopping)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 良好 (liáng hǎo) — good, well
- 善良 (shàn liáng) — kind, good-hearted

## Example Sentences

- 他有良好的学习习惯。(Tā yǒu liánghǎo de xuéxí xíguàn.) — He has good study habits.

## Notes

Used for goodness, quality, or favorable conditions.
