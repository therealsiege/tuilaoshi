# 法 Law

- **Character**: 法
- **Pinyin**: fǎ
- **Meaning**: law, method, way
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 法律 (fǎ lǜ) — law
- 方法 (fāng fǎ) — method

## Example Sentences

- 我们要遵守法律。(Wǒ men yào zūn shǒu fǎ lǜ.) — We must obey the law.

## Notes

法 water 氵 plus 去 (to go), flowing like water, impartial law.
