# 润 Moist

- **Character**: 润
- **Pinyin**: rùn
- **Meaning**: moist, smooth, to moisten
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 润滑 (rùn huá) — smooth, lubricated
- 湿润 (shī rùn) — moist

## Example Sentences

- 皮肤需要保持湿润。(Pí fū xū yào bǎo chí shī rùn.) — Skin needs to stay moist.

## Notes

润 simplified from 潤. Water 氵 with 闰 (intercalary), enriching moisture.
