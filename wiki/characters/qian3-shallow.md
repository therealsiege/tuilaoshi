# 浅 Shallow

- **Character**: 浅
- **Pinyin**: qiǎn
- **Meaning**: shallow, superficial
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 浅色 (qiǎn sè) — light color
- 深浅 (shēn qiǎn) — depth

## Example Sentences

- 这里的水很浅。(Zhè lǐ de shuǐ hěn qiǎn.) — The water here is very shallow.

## Notes

浅 simplified from 淺. Opposite of 深 (deep). Also means light in color.
