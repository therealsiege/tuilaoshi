# 袭 To Attack

- **Character**: 袭
- **Pinyin**: xí
- **Meaning**: to attack, raid
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 袭击 (xí jī) — to attack, raid
- 空袭 (kōng xí) — air raid

## Example Sentences

- 敌人发动了突然袭击。(Dírén fādòng le túrán xíjī.) — The enemy launched a surprise attack.

## Notes

袭 originally meant to put on clothes layer by layer, extended to mean 'to follow' and 'to attack by stealth'.
