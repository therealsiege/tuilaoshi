# 什 What

- **Character**: 什
- **Pinyin**: shén
- **Meaning**: what (used in 什么)
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 什么 (shén me) — what
- 为什么 (wèi shén me) — why

## Example Sentences

- 你叫什么名字？(Nǐ jiào shén me míng zì?) — What is your name?

## Notes

什 almost always appears in 什么 (what). On its own it can mean 'assorted' as in 什锦 (shí jǐn, assorted).
