# 缚 Bind, tie up

- **Character**: 缚
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: bind, tie up
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 束缚 (shù fù) — bind, shackle
- 缚住 (fù zhù) — tie up

## Example Sentences

- 他被绳子缚住了。(Tā bèi shéng zi fù zhù le.) — He was tied up with rope.

## Notes

Tying up with ropes or cords
