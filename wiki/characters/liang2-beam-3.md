# 梁 Beam

- **Character**: 梁
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: beam, bridge
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 横梁 (héng liáng) — crossbeam
- 桥梁 (qiáo liáng) — bridge

## Example Sentences

- 这座桥梁很坚固。(Zhè zuò qiáo liáng hěn jiān gù.) — This bridge is very sturdy.

## Notes

梁 with wood radical for wooden beam, essential structural element.
