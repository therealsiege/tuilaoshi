# 定 Stabilize

- **Character**: 定
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: fix, stable
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 固定 (gù dìng) — to fix, stabilize
- 稳定 (wěn dìng) — stable
- 决定 (jué dìng) — to decide

## Example Sentences

- 病情已经稳定。(Bìng qíng yǐ jīng wěn dìng.) — The condition has stabilized.

## Notes

Stability and fixation; important in trauma care.
