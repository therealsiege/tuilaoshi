# 略 To Omit

- **Character**: 略
- **Pinyin**: lüè
- **Meaning**: to omit, strategy
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 侵略 (qīn lüè) — aggression
- 策略 (cè lüè) — strategy

## Example Sentences

- 我略知一二。(Wǒ lüè zhī yī èr.) — I know a little about it.

## Notes

略 originally meant to survey territory, from field 田 and various 各. Extended meanings include simplification and strategy.
