# 盔 Helmet

- **Character**: 盔
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: helmet
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盔甲 (kuī jiǎ) — armor
- 头盔 (tóu kuī) — helmet

## Example Sentences

- 骑摩托车一定要戴头盔。(Qí mótuōchē yīdìng yào dài tóukuī.) — You must wear a helmet when riding a motorcycle.

## Notes

Protective headgear for soldiers or modern safety equipment. The character shows a vessel shape protecting the head.
