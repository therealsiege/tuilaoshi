# 扣 Deduct, button

- **Character**: 扣
- **Pinyin**: kòu
- **Meaning**: deduct, button
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扣除 (kòu chú) — deduct
- 折扣 (zhé kòu) — discount

## Example Sentences

- 工资扣除税款后发放。(Gōng zī kòu chú shuì kuǎn hòu fā fàng.) — Salary is paid after tax deductions.

## Notes

扣税 means withhold tax. 打扣 means give discount.
