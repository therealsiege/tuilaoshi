# 屏 Screen, shield, hold breath

- **Character**: 屏
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: screen, shield, hold breath
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen
- 屏障 (píng zhàng) — barrier, shield

## Example Sentences

- 大山成为天然屏障。(Dà shān chéng wéi tiān rán píng zhàng.) — The mountains form a natural barrier.

## Notes

Also read as bǐng meaning to hold breath or dismiss
