# 宝 Treasure

- **Character**: 宝
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: treasure, precious, jewel
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 宝贝 (bǎo bèi) — treasure, darling
- 珠宝 (zhū bǎo) — jewelry

## Example Sentences

- 这是我的宝贝。(Zhè shì wǒ de bǎo bèi.) — This is my treasure/darling.

## Notes

宝 means treasure or precious. Used in宝贝 (treasure, term of endearment), 宝石 (gemstone).
