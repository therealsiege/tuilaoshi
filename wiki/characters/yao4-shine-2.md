# 耀 Shine, dazzle, show off

- **Character**: 耀
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: shine, dazzle, show off
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 闪耀 (shǎn yào) — shine, sparkle
- 荣耀 (róng yào) — glory, honor

## Example Sentences

- 星星在夜空中闪耀。(Xīng xīng zài yè kōng zhōng shǎn yào.) — Stars sparkle in the night sky.

## Notes

Can mean shining light or showing off.
