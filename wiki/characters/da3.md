# 打 To Hit

- **Character**: 打
- **Pinyin**: dǎ
- **Meaning**: to hit, to play (ball), to make (phone call)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 打电话 (dǎ diàn huà) — to make a phone call
- 打球 (dǎ qiú) — to play ball

## Example Sentences

- 我给你打电话。(Wǒ gěi nǐ dǎ diàn huà.) — I'll call you.

## Notes

打 is extremely versatile: 打球 (play ball), 打电话 (call), 打车 (take a taxi), 打算 (plan).
