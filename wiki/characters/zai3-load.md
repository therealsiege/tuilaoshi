# 载 Load, carry

- **Character**: 载
- **Pinyin**: zǎi
- **Meaning**: load, carry
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 下载 (xià zǎi) — download
- 上载 (shàng zǎi) — upload

## Example Sentences

- 正在下载文件。(Zhèng zài xià zǎi wén jiàn.) — Downloading the file.

## Notes

Essential for 下载 (download), 上载 (upload), 加载 (load)
