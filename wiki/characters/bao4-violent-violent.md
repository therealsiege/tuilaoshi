# 暴 Violent

- **Character**: 暴
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: violent, sudden, to expose
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 暴力 (bào lì) — violence
- 暴风雨 (bào fēng yǔ) — storm

## Example Sentences

- 暴风雨要来了。(Bào fēng yǔ yào lái le.) — A storm is coming.

## Notes

暴 means violent or sudden. 暴露 = to expose, 暴发 = to break out.
