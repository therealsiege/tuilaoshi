# 藏 To hide, to conceal, to store

- **Character**: 藏
- **Pinyin**: cáng
- **Meaning**: to hide, to conceal, to store
- **Radical**: 艹(grass)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 隐藏 (yǐn cáng) — to hide, to conceal
- 收藏 (shōu cáng) — to collect, collection

## Example Sentences

- 他把钱藏在床下。(Tā bǎ qián cáng zài chuáng xià.) — He hid the money under the bed.

## Notes

Pronounced cáng for hiding; zàng for Tibet/storehouse.
