# 丧 Lose

- **Character**: 丧
- **Pinyin**: sàng
- **Meaning**: to lose, mourning, funeral
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 垂头丧气 (chuí tóu sàng qì) — dejected
- 沮丧 (jǔ sàng) — depressed

## Example Sentences

- 别丧气，再试一次。(Bié sàng qì, zài shì yī cì.) — Don't be discouraged; try again.

## Notes

In 垂头丧气, 丧 means to lose (spirit); also read sāng for mourning/funeral.
