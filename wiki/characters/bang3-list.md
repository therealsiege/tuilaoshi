# 榜 List

- **Character**: 榜
- **Pinyin**: bǎng
- **Meaning**: list, ranking
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 排行榜 (pái háng bǎng) — ranking list
- 榜首 (bǎng shǒu) — top of list

## Example Sentences

- 他们队位居积分榜榜首。(Tāmen duì wèi jū jīfēnbǎng bǎngshǒu.) — Their team is at the top of the points table.

## Notes

Rankings and leaderboards; standings
