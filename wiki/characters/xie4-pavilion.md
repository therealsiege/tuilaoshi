# 榭 Pavilion on terrace

- **Character**: 榭
- **Pinyin**: xiè
- **Meaning**: pavilion on terrace
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 水榭 (shuǐ xiè) — waterside pavilion
- 楼台亭榭 (lóu tái tíng xiè) — pavilions and towers

## Example Sentences

- 湖边的水榭是观赏风景的好地方。(Hú biān de shuǐ xiè shì guān shǎng fēng jǐng de hǎo dì fang.) — The waterside pavilion by the lake is a good place to view the scenery.

## Notes

Distinguished from 亭 by being built on a raised platform.
