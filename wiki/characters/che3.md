# 扯 To Pull

- **Character**: 扯
- **Pinyin**: chě
- **Meaning**: to pull, to tear, to chat
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 扯谎 (chě huǎng) — to lie
- 闲扯 (xián chě) — to chat idly

## Example Sentences

- 别扯我的衣服。(Bié chě wǒ de yī fu.) — Don't pull my clothes.

## Notes

扯 hand 扌 plus 止 (to stop), pulling back.
