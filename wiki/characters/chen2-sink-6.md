# 沉 Sink

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: to sink, heavy, deep
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 破釜沉舟 (pò fǔ chén zhōu) — burn one's bridges
- 沉默 (chén mò) — silence

## Example Sentences

- 船慢慢沉入水中。(Chuán mànman chén rù shuǐ zhōng.) — The ship slowly sank into the water.

## Notes

In 破釜沉舟: to sink the boats, ensuring no retreat; also means heavy or deep.
