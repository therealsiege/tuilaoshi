# 裂 Crack

- **Character**: 裂
- **Pinyin**: liè
- **Meaning**: crack, split
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 破裂 (pò liè) — to rupture, burst
- 裂开 (liè kāi) — to split open
- 撕裂 (sī liè) — to tear, lacerate

## Example Sentences

- 他韧带撕裂了。(Tā rèn dài sī liè le.) — His ligament tore.

## Notes

Tissue separation; ranges from minor cracks to major ruptures.
