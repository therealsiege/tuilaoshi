# 稼 Sow grain, crop

- **Character**: 稼
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: sow grain, crop
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 庄稼 (zhuāng jia) — crops
- 稼穑 (jià sè) — farming

## Example Sentences

- 农民种庄稼很辛苦。(Nóng mín zhòng zhuāng jia hěn xīn kǔ.) — Farmers work hard growing crops.

## Notes

Classical term for agricultural planting
