# 憧 To Long For

- **Character**: 憧
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: to long for, to look forward to
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 憧憬 (chōng jǐng) — to look forward to
- 憧憬着 (chōng jǐng zhe) — longing for

## Example Sentences

- 她憧憬着美好的未来。(Tā chōng jǐng zhe měi hǎo de wèi lái.) — She looks forward to a wonderful future.

## Notes

憧 has heart 忄 with 童 (child), childlike longing in the heart.
