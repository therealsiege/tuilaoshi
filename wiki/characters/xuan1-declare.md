# 宣 Declare, announce, proclaim

- **Character**: 宣
- **Pinyin**: xuān
- **Meaning**: declare, announce, proclaim
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 宣布 (xuānbù) — announce, declare
- 宣判 (xuānpàn) — pronounce judgment

## Example Sentences

- 法庭宣判被告有罪。(Fǎtíng xuānpàn bèigào yǒuzuì.) — The court pronounced the defendant guilty.

## Notes

Formal term for announcements and declarations.
