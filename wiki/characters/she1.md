# 奢 Extravagant, luxurious

- **Character**: 奢
- **Pinyin**: shē
- **Meaning**: extravagant, luxurious
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 奢侈 (shēchǐ) — extravagant, luxurious
- 奢华 (shēhuá) — luxurious, sumptuous

## Example Sentences

- 他过着奢侈的生活。(Tā guò zhe shēchǐ de shēnghuó.) — He lives an extravagant life.

## Notes

Describes excessive luxury or extravagance, usually negative.
