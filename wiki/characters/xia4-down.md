# 下 Down

- **Character**: 下
- **Pinyin**: xià
- **Meaning**: down, below, under, next
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 下午 (xià wǔ) — afternoon
- 下班 (xià bān) — get off work

## Example Sentences

- 请坐下。(Qǐng zuò xià.) — Please sit down.

## Notes

下 is the opposite of 上. An ideograph showing a mark below a baseline.
