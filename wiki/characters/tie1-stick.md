# 贴 Stick, paste, subsidize

- **Character**: 贴
- **Pinyin**: tiē
- **Meaning**: stick, paste, subsidize
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 津贴 (jīntiē) — allowance, subsidy
- 补贴 (bǔtiē) — subsidy, allowance

## Example Sentences

- 政府给农民补贴。(Zhèngfǔ gěi nóngmín bǔtiē.) — The government subsidizes farmers.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: stick/paste and subsidize.
