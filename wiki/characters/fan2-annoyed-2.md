# 烦 Annoyed

- **Character**: 烦
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: annoyed, irritated
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烦恼 (fánnǎo) — worry, vexation
- 麻烦 (máfan) — trouble

## Example Sentences

- 我今天心情很烦。(Wǒ jīntiān xīnqíng hěn fán.) — I'm in a very irritated mood today.

## Notes

Common daily emotion. Fire radical suggests burning irritation. 烦恼 is troubles, 麻烦 is bother.
