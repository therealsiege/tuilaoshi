# 抓 Grab, catch, arrest

- **Character**: 抓
- **Pinyin**: zhuā
- **Meaning**: grab, catch, arrest
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 抓住 (zhuāzhù) — grab, seize
- 抓紧 (zhuājǐn) — grasp firmly, hurry up

## Example Sentences

- 警察抓住了小偷。(Jǐngchá zhuāzhù le xiǎotōu.) — Police caught the thief.

## Notes

Common colloquial term for grabbing and catching.
