# 逮 Arrest, catch, seize

- **Character**: 逮
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: arrest, catch, seize
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 逮捕 (dàibǔ) — arrest
- 逮住 (dǎizhù) — catch, seize

## Example Sentences

- 警方逮捕了罪犯。(Jǐngfāng dàibǔ le zuìfàn.) — Police arrested the criminal.

## Notes

Legal term for arrest; pronunciation dǎi when meaning catch.
