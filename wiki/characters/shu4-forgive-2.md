# 恕 Forgive, excuse, pardon

- **Character**: 恕
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: forgive, excuse, pardon
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽恕 (kuān shù) — to forgive
- 恕罪 (shù zuì) — to pardon

## Example Sentences

- 请恕我直言。(Qǐng shù wǒ zhí yán.) — Please forgive my bluntness.

## Notes

Formal term for forgiveness. 恕不 means to regretfully decline. 饶恕 means to forgive.
