# 卑 Humble

- **Character**: 卑
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: low, humble, inferior
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卑鄙 (bēi bǐ) — despicable
- 自卑 (zì bēi) — inferiority complex

## Example Sentences

- 不要自卑。(Bú yào zì bēi.) — Don't feel inferior.

## Notes

卑 means low or inferior. 自卑 = inferiority complex. Opposite of 尊 (respect).
