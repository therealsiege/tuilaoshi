# 款 款项, fund, paragraph

- **Character**: 款
- **Pinyin**: kuǎn
- **Meaning**: 款项, fund, paragraph
- **Radical**: 欠 (owe)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 款项 (kuǎn xiàng) — 款项, fund
- 付款 (fù kuǎn) — pay

## Example Sentences

- 请及时付款。(Qǐng jí shí fù kuǎn.) — Please pay promptly.

## Notes

到款 means payment received. 汇款 means remit money.
