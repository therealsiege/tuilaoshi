# 带 Belt

- **Character**: 带
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: belt, band, to bring
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 频带 (pín dài) — frequency band
- 带宽 (dài kuān) — bandwidth

## Example Sentences

- 网络带宽不够。(Wǎng luò dài kuān bù gòu.) — Network bandwidth is insufficient.

## Notes

带 as belt/band naturally extends to frequency bands and bandwidth.
