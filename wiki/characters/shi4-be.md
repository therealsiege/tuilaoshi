# 嗜 To be addicted to, hobby

- **Character**: 嗜
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: to be addicted to, hobby
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 嗜好 (shì hào) — hobby, addiction
- 嗜酒 (shì jiǔ) — fond of drinking

## Example Sentences

- 他有抽烟的嗜好。(Tā yǒu chōuyān de shìhào.) — He has a smoking habit.

## Notes

Strong preference or addiction to something.
