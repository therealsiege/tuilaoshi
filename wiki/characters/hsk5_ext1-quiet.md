# 幽 Quiet, Secluded, 幽默 Humor

- **Character**: 幽
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: quiet, secluded, 幽默 humor
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 幽默 (yōu mò) — humor, humorous
- 幽静 (yōu jìng) — quiet and secluded
- 幽雅 (yōu yǎ) — serene and elegant

## Example Sentences

- 他很有幽默感。(Tā hěn yǒu yōumògǎn.) — He has a good sense of humor.

## Notes

Means secluded/quiet in classical contexts (幽静), but 幽默 (humor) is modern loanword pronunciation of English 'humor'.
