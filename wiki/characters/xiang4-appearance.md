# 相 Appearance

- **Character**: 相
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: appearance, to look at
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 相声 (xiàng shēng) — cross talk (comedy)
- 照相 (zhào xiàng) — to take a photo

## Example Sentences

- 相声是传统的曲艺形式。(Xiàng shēng shì chuán tǒng de qǔ yì xíng shì.) — Cross talk is a traditional form of comic performance.

## Notes

相 (xiàng) means appearance; also read as xiāng meaning mutual.
