# 条 Article

- **Character**: 条
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: strip, item, article
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 条件 (tiáo jiàn) — condition
- 条款 (tiáo kuǎn) — clause

## Example Sentences

- 合同中有很多条款。(Hé tóng zhōng yǒu hěn duō tiáo kuǎn.) — There are many clauses in the contract.

## Notes

条 simplified from 條. Long thin item, extended to articles/clauses.
