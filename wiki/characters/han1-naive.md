# 憨 Naive, silly, simple

- **Character**: 憨
- **Pinyin**: hān
- **Meaning**: naive, silly, simple
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 憨厚 (hānhòu) — simple and honest
- 憨态可掬 (hāntài kějū) — charmingly naive

## Example Sentences

- 他憨厚老实。(Tā hānhòu lǎoshí.) — He is simple and honest.

## Notes

Common term for naive simplicity, can be endearing.
