# 阴 Cloudy, overcast, yin

- **Character**: 阴
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: cloudy, overcast, yin
- **Radical**: 阜 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 阴天 (yīn tiān) — cloudy day
- 阴雨 (yīn yǔ) — rainy, overcast and rainy

## Example Sentences

- 今天是阴天,可能会下雨。(Jīn tiān shì yīn tiān, kě néng huì xià yǔ.) — It's cloudy today, it might rain.
