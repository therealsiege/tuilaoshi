# 掏 Take out

- **Character**: 掏
- **Pinyin**: tāo
- **Meaning**: take out, dig out
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掏出 (tāo chū) — take out
- 掏钱 (tāo qián) — pay money

## Example Sentences

- 他从口袋里掏出钥匙。(Tā cóng kǒudài lǐ tāo chū yàoshi.) — He took keys out from his pocket.

## Notes

Taking out from container. Hand radical. 掏钱 means to pay. Common for retrieving items.
