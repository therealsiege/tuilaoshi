# 嘴 Mouth

- **Character**: 嘴
- **Pinyin**: zuǐ
- **Meaning**: mouth
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 嘴巴 (zuǐ ba) — mouth
- 张嘴 (zhāng zuǐ) — to open one's mouth

## Example Sentences

- 闭上你的嘴。(Bì shàng nǐ de zuǐ.) — Shut your mouth.

## Notes

嘴 is the colloquial word for mouth, while 口 is more formal or literary. 嘴巴 is even more colloquial.
