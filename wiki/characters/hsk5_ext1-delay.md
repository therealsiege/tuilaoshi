# 耽 Delay, Indulge, 耽误 Delay

- **Character**: 耽
- **Pinyin**: dān
- **Meaning**: delay, indulge, 耽误 delay
- **Radical**: 耳 (ear)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耽误 (dān wù) — delay, hold up
- 耽搁 (dān gē) — delay
- 耽心 (dān xīn) — worry (variant of 担心)

## Example Sentences

- 别耽误时间了。(Bié dānwù shíjiān le.) — Don't waste time.

## Notes

Formal term for delay and procrastination. 耽误 and 耽搁 both mean delay/hold up, commonly used in apologizing for lateness.
