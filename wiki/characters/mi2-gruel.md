# 糜 Gruel, wasteful, rotten

- **Character**: 糜
- **Pinyin**: mí
- **Meaning**: gruel, wasteful, rotten
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 糜费 (mífèi) — waste, squander
- 糜烂 (mílàn) — rotten, decadent

## Example Sentences

- 不能糜费国家资源。(Bù néng mífèi guójiā zīyuán.) — One must not waste national resources.

## Notes

Literary term for wastefulness or decay; also means rice gruel.
