# 坯 Blank

- **Character**: 坯
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: blank, base (unfired pottery)
- **Radical**: 土(earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 毛坯 (máo pī) — rough blank
- 坯料 (pī liào) — blank material

## Example Sentences

- 陶工正在制作陶坯。(Táogōng zhèngzài zhìzuò táo pī.) — The potter is making pottery blanks.

## Notes

坯 refers to an unfinished or unfired piece, especially in ceramics. The earth radical indicates clay.
