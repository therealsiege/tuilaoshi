# 叹 To Sigh

- **Character**: 叹
- **Pinyin**: tàn
- **Meaning**: to sigh, to exclaim
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 感叹 (gǎn tàn) — to sigh with emotion
- 叹气 (tàn qì) — to sigh

## Example Sentences

- 他叹了一口气。(Tā tàn le yī kǒu qì.) — He heaved a sigh.

## Notes

叹 simplified from 嘆. Mouth radical 口 representing exhaling deeply in sigh or exclamation.
