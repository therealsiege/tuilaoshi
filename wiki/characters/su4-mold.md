# 塑 To Mold

- **Character**: 塑
- **Pinyin**: sù
- **Meaning**: to mold, model, plastic
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 塑料 (sù liào) — plastic
- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture

## Example Sentences

- 这是一件精美的雕塑。(Zhè shì yī jiàn jīngměi de diāosù.) — This is an exquisite sculpture.

## Notes

塑 combines earth 土 with 朔 (new moon), representing shaping clay like the moon's phases.
