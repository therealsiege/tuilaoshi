# 耕 Plow, till, cultivate

- **Character**: 耕
- **Pinyin**: gēng
- **Meaning**: plow, till, cultivate
- **Radical**: 耒 (plow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 耕地 (gēng dì) — to plow, arable land
- 耕种 (gēng zhòng) — to till and plant

## Example Sentences

- 农民正在田里耕地。(Nóng mín zhèng zài tián lǐ gēng dì.) — Farmers are plowing the fields.

## Notes

Fundamental farming term. 笔耕 metaphorically means writing. 深耕 means intensive cultivation.
