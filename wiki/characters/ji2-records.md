# 籍 Records, register, native place

- **Character**: 籍
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: records, register, native place
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 书籍 (shū jí) — books
- 户籍 (hù jí) — household register

## Example Sentences

- 古代典籍是研究历史的重要资料。(Gǔdài diǎnjí shì yánjiū lìshǐ de zhòngyào zīliào.) — Ancient texts are important materials for studying history.

## Notes

Originally bamboo slips used for writing. Now refers to books, records, or one's registered native place.
