# 派 Faction, Send, 派遣 Dispatch

- **Character**: 派
- **Pinyin**: pài
- **Meaning**: faction, send, 派遣 dispatch
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 派遣 (pài qiǎn) — dispatch, send
- 党派 (dǎng pài) — political party
- 气派 (qì pài) — imposing manner

## Example Sentences

- 公司派他去北京出差。(Gōngsī pài tā qù Běijīng chūchāi.) — The company sent him on a business trip to Beijing.

## Notes

Versatile character meaning both 'send/dispatch' and 'faction/school of thought' in different contexts.
