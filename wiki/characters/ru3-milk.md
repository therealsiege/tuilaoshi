# 乳 Milk, breast, nipple

- **Character**: 乳
- **Pinyin**: rǔ
- **Meaning**: milk, breast, nipple
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 乳汁 (rǔ zhī) — milk
- 哺乳 (bǔ rǔ) — breastfeed

## Example Sentences

- 母乳对婴儿最有营养。(Mǔ rǔ duì yīng ér zuì yǒu yíng yǎng.) — Breast milk is most nutritious for babies.
