# 颜 Color

- **Character**: 颜
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: face, color, countenance
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 颜色 (yán sè) — color
- 笑颜 (xiào yán) — smiling face

## Example Sentences

- 你喜欢什么颜色？(Nǐ xǐ huān shén me yán sè?) — What color do you like?

## Notes

颜 means face or color. 颜色 is the standard word for color.
