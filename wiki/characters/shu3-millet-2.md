# 黍 Millet, glutinous millet

- **Character**: 黍
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: millet, glutinous millet
- **Radical**: 黍 (millet)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 黍米 (shǔ mǐ) — millet
- 稷黍 (jì shǔ) — millet crops

## Example Sentences

- 黍是中国古代重要的粮食作物。(Shǔ shì Zhōngguó gǔdài zhòngyào de liángshi zuòwù.) — Millet was an important grain crop in ancient China.

## Notes

A type of millet, one of the ancient grains. The character itself serves as a radical in grain-related characters.
