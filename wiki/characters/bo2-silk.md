# 帛 Silk, cloth

- **Character**: 帛
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: silk, cloth
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 布帛 (bù bó) — cloth and silk
- 帛书 (bó shū) — books written on silk

## Example Sentences

- 马王堆出土了许多帛书。(Mǎ wáng duī chū tǔ le xǔ duō bó shū.) — Many silk manuscripts were unearthed at Mawangdui.

## Notes

Ancient writing material before paper was invented
