# 陷 To sink, to cave in, to trap

- **Character**: 陷
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: to sink, to cave in, to trap
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陷入 (xiàn rù) — to sink into, to get into
- 陷阱 (xiàn jǐng) — trap, pitfall

## Example Sentences

- 他陷入了困境。(Tā xiànrù le kùnjìng.) — He fell into difficulty.

## Notes

Can mean physical sinking or falling into a situation.
