# 恍 Dazed

- **Character**: 恍
- **Pinyin**: huǎng
- **Meaning**: suddenly, as if, dazed
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 恍然大悟 (huǎng rán dà wù) — to suddenly realize
- 恍惚 (huǎng hū) — in a trance

## Example Sentences

- 他恍然大悟。(Tā huǎng rán dà wù.) — He suddenly understood.

## Notes

恍 has heart 忄 with 光 (light), a flash of understanding in the heart.
