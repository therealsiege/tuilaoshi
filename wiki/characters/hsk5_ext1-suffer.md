# 患 Suffer From, 患者 Patient

- **Character**: 患
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: suffer from, 患者 patient
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 患者 (huàn zhě) — patient, sufferer
- 患病 (huàn bìng) — fall ill
- 隐患 (yǐn huàn) — hidden danger

## Example Sentences

- 他患有糖尿病。(Tā huàn yǒu tángniàobìng.) — He suffers from diabetes.

## Notes

Medical term for suffering from illness. 患者 (patient) is standard hospital terminology for people receiving treatment.
