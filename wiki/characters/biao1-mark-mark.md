# 标 Mark

- **Character**: 标
- **Pinyin**: biāo
- **Meaning**: mark, sign, to mark
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 标志 (biāo zhì) — symbol, logo
- 标题 (biāo tí) — title, headline

## Example Sentences

- 这个标志是什么意思？(Zhè ge biāo zhì shì shén me yì si?) — What does this symbol mean?

## Notes

标 can mean mark/label. 标签 = tag/label, 标本 = specimen.
