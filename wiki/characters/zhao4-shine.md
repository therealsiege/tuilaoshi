# 照 To Shine

- **Character**: 照
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: to shine, to photograph, according to
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 照片 (zhào piàn) — photograph
- 按照 (àn zhào) — according to

## Example Sentences

- 按照规定，这是不允许的。(Àn zhào guī dìng, zhè shì bù yǔn xǔ de.) — According to regulations, this is not permitted.

## Notes

照 shows fire/light 灬 beneath 召 (to summon), representing illumination or following a pattern.
