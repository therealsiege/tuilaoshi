# 菌 Bacteria

- **Character**: 菌
- **Pinyin**: jūn
- **Meaning**: bacteria, germ
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 细菌 (xì jūn) — bacteria
- 杀菌 (shā jūn) — to sterilize
- 病菌 (bìng jūn) — pathogenic bacteria

## Example Sentences

- 细菌感染需要抗生素。(Xì jūn gǎn rǎn xū yào kàng shēng sù.) — Bacterial infections require antibiotics.

## Notes

Microorganisms; infectious agents.
