# 必 Must

- **Character**: 必
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: must, certainly, necessarily
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 必须 (bì xū) — must
- 不必 (bú bì) — need not

## Example Sentences

- 你必须完成作业。(Nǐ bì xū wán chéng zuò yè.) — You must finish your homework.

## Notes

必须 = must (stronger than 应该). 必要 = necessary. 不必 = need not.
