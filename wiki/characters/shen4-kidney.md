# 肾 Kidney

- **Character**: 肾
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: kidney
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肾脏 (shèn zàng) — kidney (organ)
- 肾炎 (shèn yán) — nephritis

## Example Sentences

- 肾脏负责过滤血液。(Shèn zàng fù zé guò lǜ xuè yè.) — The kidneys are responsible for filtering blood.

## Notes

Organ for filtering and waste removal
