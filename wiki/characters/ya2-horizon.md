# 涯 Horizon, edge, limit

- **Character**: 涯
- **Pinyin**: yá
- **Meaning**: horizon, edge, limit
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 天涯 (tiān yá) — ends of the earth
- 海角天涯 (hǎi jiǎo tiān yá) — remotest corners of the earth

## Example Sentences

- 天涯海角总有亲人牵挂。(Tiān yá hǎi jiǎo zǒng yǒu qīn rén qiān guà.) — Even at the ends of the earth, family cares for you.

## Notes

Often used poetically to express distance or limits.
