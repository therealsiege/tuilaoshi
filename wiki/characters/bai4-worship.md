# 拜 To Worship

- **Character**: 拜
- **Pinyin**: bài
- **Meaning**: to worship, to visit, to salute
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 崇拜 (chóng bài) — to worship
- 拜访 (bài fǎng) — to visit

## Example Sentences

- 我明天去拜访他。(Wǒ míng tiān qù bài fǎng tā.) — I'll visit him tomorrow.

## Notes

拜 means to bow or worship. 拜年 = pay a New Year visit. 拜拜 = bye-bye (borrowed).
