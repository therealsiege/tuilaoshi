# 咳 Cough

- **Character**: 咳
- **Pinyin**: ké
- **Meaning**: cough
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 咳嗽 (ké sou) — to cough
- 咳血 (ké xiě) — to cough up blood
- 干咳 (gān ké) — dry cough

## Example Sentences

- 他咳嗽得很厉害。(Tā ké sou de hěn lì hai.) — He's coughing very badly.

## Notes

Common respiratory symptom; indicates various conditions.
