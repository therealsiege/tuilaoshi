# 盛 Flourishing, prosperous, grand

- **Character**: 盛
- **Pinyin**: shèng
- **Meaning**: flourishing, prosperous, grand
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 繁盛 (fán shèng) — prosperous, thriving
- 盛大 (shèng dà) — grand, magnificent

## Example Sentences

- 唐朝是中国历史上最繁盛的时期。(Táng cháo shì Zhōng guó lì shǐ shàng zuì fán shèng de shí qī.) — The Tang Dynasty was the most prosperous period in Chinese history.

## Notes

As shèng means prosperous or grand. As chéng means to contain. 盛会 means grand gathering.
