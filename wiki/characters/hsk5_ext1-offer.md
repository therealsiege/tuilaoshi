# 祭 Offer Sacrifice, 祭祀 Sacrifice

- **Character**: 祭
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: offer sacrifice, 祭祀 sacrifice
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 祭祀 (jì sì) — offer sacrifices
- 祭奠 (jì diàn) — hold a memorial ceremony
- 祭拜 (jì bài) — worship, pay respects

## Example Sentences

- 清明节是祭祀祖先的日子。(Qīngmíng jié shì jìsì zǔxiān de rìzi.) — Qingming Festival is a day to worship ancestors.

## Notes

Formal term for religious sacrifice and worship. 祭祀 is traditional practice of honoring ancestors and deities.
