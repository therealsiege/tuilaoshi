# 怅 Disappointed

- **Character**: 怅
- **Pinyin**: chàng
- **Meaning**: disappointed, upset
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 惆怅 (chóu chàng) — melancholy
- 怅然 (chàng rán) — regretfully

## Example Sentences

- 他怅然若失。(Tā chàng rán ruò shī.) — He felt a sense of loss.

## Notes

怅 simplified from 悵. Heart 忄 plus 长 (long), lingering sadness.
