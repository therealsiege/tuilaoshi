# 躁 Dry, parched, impetuous

- **Character**: 躁
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: dry, parched, impetuous
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 急躁 (jí zào) — impetuous, irritable
- 暴躁 (bào zào) — irritable, bad-tempered

## Example Sentences

- 他性格很急躁。(Tā xìnggé hěn jízào.) — He has an impatient temperament.

## Notes

Describes impetuous, restless, or irritable emotional states.
