# 学 To Study

- **Character**: 学
- **Pinyin**: xué
- **Meaning**: to study, to learn
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 学生 (xué shēng) — student
- 学习 (xué xí) — to study

## Example Sentences

- 我在学中文。(Wǒ zài xué zhōng wén.) — I am studying Chinese.

## Notes

学 shows hands (爫) guiding a child (子) under a roof (冖), representing teaching and learning.
