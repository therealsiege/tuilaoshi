# 沉 To Sink

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: to sink, heavy, deep
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沉重 (chén zhòng) — heavy
- 沉默 (chén mò) — silent

## Example Sentences

- 石头沉入水底。(Shí tou chén rù shuǐ dǐ.) — The stone sank to the bottom.

## Notes

沉 water 氵 plus 冘 (deep), sinking down.
