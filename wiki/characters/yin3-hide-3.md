# 隐 Hidden

- **Character**: 隐
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: to hide, concealed
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 隐藏 (yǐn cáng) — to hide
- 隐晦 (yǐn huì) — veiled

## Example Sentences

- 真相被隐藏了。(Zhēn xiàng bèi yǐn cáng le.) — The truth was hidden.

## Notes

隐 simplified from 隱. Mound 阝 hiding something from view.
