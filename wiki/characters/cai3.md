# 彩 Color

- **Character**: 彩
- **Pinyin**: cǎi
- **Meaning**: color, variety, prize
- **Radical**: 彡 (hair)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 精彩 (jīng cǎi) — wonderful
- 彩虹 (cǎi hóng) — rainbow

## Example Sentences

- 表演很精彩。(Biǎo yǎn hěn jīng cǎi.) — The performance was wonderful.

## Notes

彩 means color or splendor. 精彩 = wonderful/splendid, 彩色 = colorful.
