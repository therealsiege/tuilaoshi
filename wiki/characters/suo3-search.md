# 索 Search, seek

- **Character**: 索
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: search, seek
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 检索 (jiǎn suǒ) — search, retrieve
- 搜索 (sōu suǒ) — search

## Example Sentences

- 请在数据库中检索信息。(Qǐng zài shù jù kù zhōng jiǎn suǒ xìn xī.) — Please search for information in the database.

## Notes

Used in 检索 (search), 搜索 (search), 索引 (index)
