# 酝 Brew, ferment

- **Character**: 酝
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: brew, ferment
- **Radical**: 酉 (wine)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 酝酿 (yùn niàng) — brew, ferment
- 酝造 (yùn zào) — brew, make wine

## Example Sentences

- 这个计划正在酝酿之中。(Zhè gè jì huà zhèng zài yùn niàng zhī zhōng.) — This plan is in the process of being formulated.

## Notes

Literal meaning is brewing; often used metaphorically.
