# 裁 Cut, decide, reduce

- **Character**: 裁
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: cut, decide, reduce
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 总裁 (zǒng cái) — president, CEO
- 裁员 (cái yuán) — lay off staff

## Example Sentences

- 公司决定裁员百分之十。(Gōng sī jué dìng cái yuán bǎi fēn zhī shí.) — The company decided to lay off ten percent of staff.

## Notes

Common in executive titles and restructuring contexts.
