# 产 To Produce

- **Character**: 产
- **Pinyin**: chǎn
- **Meaning**: to produce, property, estate
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 生产 (shēng chǎn) — to produce
- 财产 (cái chǎn) — property

## Example Sentences

- 这家工厂生产汽车。(Zhè jiā gōng chǎng shēng chǎn qì chē.) — This factory produces cars.

## Notes

产 simplified from 產. Originally depicted childbirth, extended to mean production in general and property/assets.
