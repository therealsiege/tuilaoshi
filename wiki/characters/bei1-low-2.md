# 卑 Low, humble, base

- **Character**: 卑
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: low, humble, base
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 卑微 (bēi wēi) — lowly, humble
- 卑鄙 (bēi bǐ) — mean, despicable

## Example Sentences

- 他的行为很卑鄙。(Tā de xíngwéi hěn bēibǐ.) — His behavior is very despicable.

## Notes

Low status/position or base/despicable character.
