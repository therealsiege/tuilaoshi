# 银 Silver

- **Character**: 银
- **Pinyin**: yín
- **Meaning**: silver, money, bank
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 银行 (yín háng) — bank
- 银色 (yín sè) — silver color

## Example Sentences

- 我去银行取钱。(Wǒ qù yín háng qǔ qián.) — I'm going to the bank to withdraw money.

## Notes

银 uses the metal radical 钅. 银行 (bank) literally means 'silver firm', reflecting historical banking practices.
