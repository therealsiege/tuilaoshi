# 浓 Thick, concentrated, strong

- **Character**: 浓
- **Pinyin**: nóng
- **Meaning**: thick, concentrated, strong
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浓度 (nóng dù) — concentration, density
- 浓缩 (nóng suō) — concentrate, condense

## Example Sentences

- 这种溶液的浓度很高。(Zhè zhǒng róng yè de nóng dù hěn gāo.) — This solution has a high concentration.
