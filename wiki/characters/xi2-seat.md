# 席 Seat

- **Character**: 席
- **Pinyin**: xí
- **Meaning**: seat, mat, banquet
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 主席 (zhǔ xí) — chairman, president
- 出席 (chū xí) — to attend

## Example Sentences

- 他是公司主席。(Tā shì gōng sī zhǔ xí.) — He is the company chairman.

## Notes

席 originally meant woven mat for sitting; extended to position and office.
