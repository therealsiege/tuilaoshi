# 残 Cruel, incomplete, injured

- **Character**: 残
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: 残cruel, incomplete,残残injured
- **Radical**: 歹 (death)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 残忍 (cán rěn) — cruel, ruthless
- 残疾 (cán jí) — disabled

## Example Sentences

- 战争造成了许多残疾人。(Zhàn zhēng zào chéng le xǔ duō cán jí rén.) — The war created many disabled people.

## Notes

Cruelty, incompleteness, or disability
