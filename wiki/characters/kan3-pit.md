# 坎 Pit, threshold, hurdle

- **Character**: 坎
- **Pinyin**: kǎn
- **Meaning**: pit, threshold, hurdle
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 坎坷 (kǎn kě) — rough, bumpy
- 门坎 (mén kǎn) — threshold

## Example Sentences

- 他的人生道路充满坎坷。(Tā de rén shēng dào lù chōng mǎn kǎn kě.) — His life path is full of ups and downs.

## Notes

Literal meaning is threshold; often used metaphorically.
