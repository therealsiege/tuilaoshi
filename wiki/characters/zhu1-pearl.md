# 珠 Pearl

- **Character**: 珠
- **Pinyin**: zhū
- **Meaning**: pearl, bead
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 珍珠 (zhēn zhū) — pearl
- 珠宝 (zhū bǎo) — jewelry

## Example Sentences

- 她买了一串珍珠项链。(Tā mǎi le yī chuàn zhēn zhū xiàng liàn.) — She bought a pearl necklace.

## Notes

珠 means pearl or bead. Used in珍珠 (pearl), 珠宝 (jewelry), 珠江 (Pearl River).
