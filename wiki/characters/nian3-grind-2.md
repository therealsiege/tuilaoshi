# 碾 To grind, to crush, to roll

- **Character**: 碾
- **Pinyin**: niǎn
- **Meaning**: to grind, to crush, to roll
- **Radical**: 石(stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 碾压 (niǎn yā) — to crush, to run over
- 石碾 (shí niǎn) — stone roller

## Example Sentences

- 用石磨碾碎谷物。(Yòng shímò niǎnsuì gǔwù.) — Grind the grain with a stone mill.

## Notes

Used for grinding or crushing with heavy objects.
