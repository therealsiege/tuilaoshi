# 斟 Pour wine, consider

- **Character**: 斟
- **Pinyin**: zhēn
- **Meaning**: pour wine, consider
- **Radical**: 斗 (dipper)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 斟酒 (zhēn jiǔ) — pour wine
- 斟酌 (zhēn zhuó) — consider, deliberate

## Example Sentences

- 他给每位客人斟满了酒。(Tā gěi měi wèi kè rén zhēn mǎn le jiǔ.) — He filled each guest's cup with wine.

## Notes

Literal meaning is pouring; also means to consider carefully.
