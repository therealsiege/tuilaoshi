# 裹 To wrap, to bind

- **Character**: 裹
- **Pinyin**: guǒ
- **Meaning**: to wrap, to bind
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 包裹 (bāo guǒ) — package, parcel
- 裹粽子 (guǒ zòng zi) — to wrap zongzi

## Example Sentences

- 她在裹粽子。(Tā zài guǒ zòngzi.) — She is wrapping zongzi.

## Notes

Common for wrapping food like dumplings and zongzi. Clothing radical.
