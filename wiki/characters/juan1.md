# 捐 To Donate

- **Character**: 捐
- **Pinyin**: juān
- **Meaning**: to donate, contribute
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捐款 (juān kuǎn) — to donate money
- 捐赠 (juān zèng) — to donate

## Example Sentences

- 他捐了很多钱给慈善机构。(Tā juān le hěn duō qián gěi císhàn jīgòu.) — He donated a lot of money to charity.

## Notes

捐 combines hand 扌 with 肙 (phonetic), representing giving away with one's hand.
