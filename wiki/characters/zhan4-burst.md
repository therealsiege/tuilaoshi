# 绽 Burst, split, bloom

- **Character**: 绽
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: burst, split, bloom
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绽放 (zhàn fàng) — bloom
- 绽开 (zhàn kāi) — burst open

## Example Sentences

- 鲜花绽放笑脸。(Xiān huā zhàn fàng xiào liǎn.) — Fresh flowers bloom with smiling faces.

## Notes

Opening of flowers or fabric splitting
