# 碳 Carbon

- **Character**: 碳
- **Pinyin**: tàn
- **Meaning**: carbon
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 二氧化碳 (èr yǎng huà tàn) — carbon dioxide
- 碳水化合物 (tàn shuǐ huà hé wù) — carbohydrate

## Example Sentences

- 植物通过光合作用吸收二氧化碳。(Zhí wù tōng guò guāng hé zuò yòng xī shōu èr yǎng huà tàn.) — Plants absorb carbon dioxide through photosynthesis.

## Notes

Chemical element carbon, basis of organic chemistry
