# 捐 To donate, to contribute

- **Character**: 捐
- **Pinyin**: juān
- **Meaning**: to donate, to contribute
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捐款 (juān kuǎn) — to donate money
- 捐赠 (juān zèng) — to donate, to contribute

## Example Sentences

- 他向慈善机构捐了一大笔钱。(Tā xiàng císhàn jīgòu juān le yī dà bǐ qián.) — He donated a large sum to charity.

## Notes

Used for charitable donations or contributions.
