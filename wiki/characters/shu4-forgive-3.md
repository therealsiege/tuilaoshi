# 恕 Forgive, pardon

- **Character**: 恕
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: forgive, pardon
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 宽恕 (kuān shù) — forgive, pardon
- 恕罪 (shù zuì) — pardon the offense

## Example Sentences

- 己所不欲，勿施于人，这就是恕道。(Jǐ suǒ bù yù, wù shī yú rén, zhè jiùshì shù dào.) — Do not do to others what you don't want done to yourself - this is the way of forgiveness.

## Notes

Combines 如 (like, as) and 心 (heart), suggesting empathy. Represents the Confucian concept of reciprocity and forgiveness.
