# 充 Fill

- **Character**: 充
- **Pinyin**: chōng
- **Meaning**: to fill, to satisfy, sufficient
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 画饼充饥 (huà bǐng chōng jī) — feed on illusions
- 充满 (chōng mǎn) — to be full of

## Example Sentences

- 房间里充满了笑声。(Fángjiān lǐ chōngmǎn le xiàoshēng.) — The room was filled with laughter.

## Notes

In 画饼充饥: to satisfy or fill (hunger); also means sufficient or to act as.
