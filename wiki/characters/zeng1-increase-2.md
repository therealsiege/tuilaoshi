# 增 Increase, add

- **Character**: 增
- **Pinyin**: zēng
- **Meaning**: increase, add
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 增加 (zēng jiā) — increase, add
- 增长 (zēng zhǎng) — grow, increase

## Example Sentences

- 需要增加一些条款。(Xū yào zēng jiā yī xiē tiáo kuǎn.) — Need to add some clauses.

## Notes

新增 means newly added. 递增 means increase progressively.
