# 殷 Ardent, Hospitable, 殷切 Cordial

- **Character**: 殷
- **Pinyin**: yīn
- **Meaning**: ardent, hospitable, 殷切 cordial
- **Radical**: 殳 (weapon)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 殷切 (yīn qiè) — cordial, eager
- 殷勤 (yīn qín) — attentive, solicitous
- 殷实 (yīn shí) — well-off

## Example Sentences

- 他对客人非常殷勤。(Tā duì kèrén fēicháng yīnqín.) — He is very attentive to guests.

## Notes

Formal term expressing warm hospitality and eager attention. 殷切 describes earnest hopes or cordial reception.
