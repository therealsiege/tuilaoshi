# 蚀 To Erode

- **Character**: 蚀
- **Pinyin**: shí
- **Meaning**: to erode, to corrode
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 腐蚀 (fǔ shí) — to corrode
- 侵蚀 (qīn shí) — to erode

## Example Sentences

- 酸雨侵蚀了建筑物。(Suān yǔ qīn shí le jiàn zhù wù.) — Acid rain eroded the buildings.

## Notes

蚀 simplified from 蝕. Insect 虫 eating 食 (food), gradual consumption.
