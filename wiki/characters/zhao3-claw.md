# 爪 Claw

- **Character**: 爪
- **Pinyin**: zhǎo
- **Meaning**: claw, talon
- **Radical**: 爪 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 爪子 (zhǎo zi) — claw
- 鹰爪 (yīng zhǎo) — eagle talon

## Example Sentences

- 老虎的爪子很锋利。(Lǎo hǔ de zhǎo zi hěn fēng lì.) — The tiger's claws are very sharp.

## Notes

爪 depicts a hand or claw with fingers, sharp appendage.
