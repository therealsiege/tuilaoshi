# 弱 Weak

- **Character**: 弱
- **Pinyin**: ruò
- **Meaning**: weak
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 虚弱 (xū ruò) — weak, feeble
- 弱点 (ruò diǎn) — weakness

## Example Sentences

- 缺乏锻炼会让身体变弱。(Quēfá duànliàn huì ràng shēntǐ biàn ruò.) — Lack of exercise will make the body weak.

## Notes

Weakness; lack of strength
