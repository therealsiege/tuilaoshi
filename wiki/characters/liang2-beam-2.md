# 梁 Beam, bridge

- **Character**: 梁
- **Pinyin**: liáng
- **Meaning**: beam, bridge
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 栋梁 (dòng liáng) — pillar, backbone
- 桥梁 (qiáo liáng) — bridge

## Example Sentences

- 这座老房子的横梁已经有几百年历史了。(Zhè zuò lǎo fáng zi de héng liáng yǐ jīng yǒu jǐ bǎi nián lì shǐ le.) — The beams of this old house are already several hundred years old.

## Notes

Essential structural element; also a common surname.
