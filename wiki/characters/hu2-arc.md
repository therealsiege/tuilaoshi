# 弧 Arc, curve, bow

- **Character**: 弧
- **Pinyin**: hú
- **Meaning**: arc, curve, bow
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 弧线 (hú xiàn) — arc, curved line
- 弧度 (hú dù) — radian

## Example Sentences

- 画一条弧线连接两点。(Huà yī tiáo hú xiàn lián jiē liǎng diǎn.) — Draw an arc connecting the two points.

## Notes
