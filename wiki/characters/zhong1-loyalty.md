# 忠 Loyalty, devotion

- **Character**: 忠
- **Pinyin**: zhōng
- **Meaning**: loyalty, devotion
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 忠诚 (zhōng chéng) — loyal, faithful
- 忠心 (zhōng xīn) — loyalty, devotion

## Example Sentences

- 他对国家一片忠心。(Tā duì guójiā yī piàn zhōng xīn.) — He has unwavering loyalty to his country.

## Notes

Combines 中 (middle, center) and 心 (heart), suggesting wholehearted devotion. Key virtue in traditional Chinese ethics.
