# 嗽 Cough

- **Character**: 嗽
- **Pinyin**: sou
- **Meaning**: cough
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 咳嗽 (ké sou) — to cough
- 嗽口 (sou kǒu) — to gargle

## Example Sentences

- 咳嗽时请捂住嘴。(Ké sou shí qǐng wǔ zhù zuǐ.) — Please cover your mouth when coughing.

## Notes

Nearly always used in compound 咳嗽 (cough).
