# 沁 Seep, permeate, refreshing

- **Character**: 沁
- **Pinyin**: qìn
- **Meaning**: seep, permeate, refreshing
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沁人心脾 (qìn rén xīn pí) — refreshing, delightful
- 沁入 (qìn rù) — seep into

## Example Sentences

- 花香沁人心脾，令人陶醉。(Huā xiāng qìn rén xīn pí, lìng rén táo zuì.) — The flower fragrance is refreshing and intoxicating.

## Notes

Often used in 沁人心脾 to describe refreshing sensations.
