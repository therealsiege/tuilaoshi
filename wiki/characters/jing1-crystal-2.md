# 晶 Crystal, bright, lens

- **Character**: 晶
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: crystal, bright, lens
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 晶体 (jīng tǐ) — crystal, lens
- 水晶 (shuǐ jīng) — crystal

## Example Sentences

## Notes

Crystal material or the lens of the eye.
