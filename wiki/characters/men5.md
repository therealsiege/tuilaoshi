# 们 Plural Marker

- **Character**: 们
- **Pinyin**: men
- **Meaning**: plural marker for pronouns
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 我们 (wǒ men) — we
- 他们 (tā men) — they

## Example Sentences

- 你们好！(Nǐ men hǎo!) — Hello everyone!

## Notes

们 makes pronouns plural. It can also pluralize some nouns referring to people: 朋友们 (friends).
