# 踢 Kick

- **Character**: 踢
- **Pinyin**: tī
- **Meaning**: to kick
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 踢球 (tī qiú) — to kick a ball, play football
- 踢腿 (tī tuǐ) — to kick leg

## Example Sentences

- 他踢足球踢得很好。(Tā tī zúqiú tī de hěn hǎo.) — He plays soccer very well.

## Notes

Primary kicking verb; essential for football and martial arts
