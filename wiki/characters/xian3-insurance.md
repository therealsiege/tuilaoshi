# 险 Insurance, danger

- **Character**: 险
- **Pinyin**: xiǎn
- **Meaning**: insurance, danger
- **Radical**: 阝 (mound)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 保险 (bǎo xiǎn) — insurance
- 风险 (fēng xiǎn) — risk

## Example Sentences

- 投资有风险。(Tóu zī yǒu fēng xiǎn.) — Investment has risks.

## Notes

保险公司 is insurance company. 投保 means take out insurance.
