# 滞 Stagnant, sluggish, detained

- **Character**: 滞
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: stagnant, sluggish, detained
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 停滞 (tíng zhì) — stagnate
- 滞留 (zhì liú) — be detained

## Example Sentences

- 经济发展停滞不前。(Jīng jì fā zhǎn tíng zhì bù qián.) — Economic development has stagnated.

## Notes

Lack of movement or progress
