# 蚁 Ant

- **Character**: 蚁
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: ant
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 蚂蚁 (mǎ yǐ) — ant
- 白蚁 (bái yǐ) — termite

## Example Sentences

- 蚂蚁很勤劳。(Mǎyǐ hěn qínláo.) — Ants are very hardworking.

## Notes

Symbol of diligence and teamwork. Insect radical on the left.
