# 叛 To Rebel

- **Character**: 叛
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: to betray, to rebel
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 叛逆 (pàn nì) — rebellious
- 背叛 (bèi pàn) — to betray

## Example Sentences

- 他背叛了朋友的信任。(Tā bèi pàn le péng yǒu de xìn rèn.) — He betrayed his friend's trust.

## Notes

叛 combines 半 (half) and 反 (to reverse), splitting allegiance.
