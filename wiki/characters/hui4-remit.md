# 汇 To remit, to converge, to collect

- **Character**: 汇
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: to remit, to converge, to collect
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汇款 (huì kuǎn) — to remit money
- 汇率 (huì lǜ) — exchange rate

## Example Sentences

- 我需要汇款给家人。(Wǒ xūyào huìkuǎn gěi jiārén.) — I need to remit money to my family.

## Notes

Common in banking for money transfers.
