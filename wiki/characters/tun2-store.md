# 囤 To store, to hoard

- **Character**: 囤
- **Pinyin**: tún
- **Meaning**: to store, to hoard
- **Radical**: 囗(enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 囤积 (tún jī) — to hoard, to stockpile
- 囤货 (tún huò) — to hoard goods

## Example Sentences

- 不要囤积太多食物。(Bùyào túnjī tài duō shíwù.) — Don't hoard too much food.

## Notes

Implies storing or hoarding large quantities.
