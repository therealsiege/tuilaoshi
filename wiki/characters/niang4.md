# 酿 To brew, to make wine, to ferment

- **Character**: 酿
- **Pinyin**: niàng
- **Meaning**: to brew, to make wine, to ferment
- **Radical**: 酉(wine)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 酿酒 (niàng jiǔ) — to brew wine/liquor
- 酝酿 (yùn niàng) — to brew, to deliberate

## Example Sentences

- 这家工厂酿造啤酒。(Zhè jiā gōngchǎng niàngzào píjiǔ.) — This factory brews beer.

## Notes

Used for brewing alcoholic beverages or figuratively for brewing trouble.
