# 敏 Quick

- **Character**: 敏
- **Pinyin**: mǐn
- **Meaning**: quick, nimble, sharp
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 敏感 (mǐn gǎn) — sensitive
- 敏捷 (mǐn jié) — quick, agile

## Example Sentences

- 她的反应很敏捷。(Tā de fǎn yìng hěn mǐn jié.) — Her reactions are very quick.

## Notes

敏 combines 每 (every) and strike 攵, representing quick responsive action.
