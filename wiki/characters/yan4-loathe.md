# 厌 To loathe, be fed up, 厭 (traditional)

- **Character**: 厌
- **Pinyin**: yàn
- **Meaning**: to loathe, be fed up, 厭 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 厂 (cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 厌恶 (yàn wù) — to detest, loathe
- 厌烦 (yàn fán) — to be fed up with

## Example Sentences

- 我厌烦了这种生活。(Wǒ yànfán le zhè zhǒng shēnghuó.) — I'm fed up with this kind of life.

## Notes

Expresses being tired of or disgusted by something.
