# 爱 To love, to like, love, affection

- **Character**: 爱
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: to love, to like, love, affection
- **Radical**: 爫(claw)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 爱好 (ài hào) — hobby, to like
- 恋爱 (liàn ài) — to be in love, romantic love

## Example Sentences

- 我爱我的家人。(Wǒ ài wǒ de jiārén.) — I love my family.

## Notes

Fundamental character for love and affection.
