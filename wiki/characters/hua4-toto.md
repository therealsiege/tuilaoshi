# 划 To Plan

- **Character**: 划
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to划划划to plan, to row
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 计划 (jì huà) — plan
- 规划 (guī huà) — to plan

## Example Sentences

- 这是我的学习计划。(Zhè shì wǒ de xué xí jì huà.) — This is my study plan.

## Notes

划 can be huà (to划ide, to plan) or huá (to row). As huà, common in计划 (plan).
