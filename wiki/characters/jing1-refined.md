# 精 Refined, essence, spirit

- **Character**: 精
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: refined, essence, spirit
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 精神 (jīng shén) — spirit, vitality
- 精华 (jīng huá) — essence, quintessence

## Example Sentences

- 这本书是他作品的精华。(Zhè běn shū shì tā zuòpǐn de jīnghuá.) — This book is the essence of his works.

## Notes

Refined quality, essence, or mental spirit.
