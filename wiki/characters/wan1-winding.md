# 蜿 Winding, meandering

- **Character**: 蜿
- **Pinyin**: wān
- **Meaning**: winding, meandering
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蜿蜒 (wān yán) — winding, meandering
- 蜿蜒曲折 (wān yán qū zhé) — winding and tortuous

## Example Sentences

- 长城蜿蜒于群山之间。(Cháng chéng wān yán yú qún shān zhī jiān.) — The Great Wall winds among the mountains.

## Notes

Describes serpentine paths like rivers or roads
