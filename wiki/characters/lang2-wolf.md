# 狼 Wolf

- **Character**: 狼
- **Pinyin**: láng
- **Meaning**: wolf
- **Radical**: 犭 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 狼狗 (láng gǒu) — wolfhound
- 野狼 (yě láng) — wolf

## Example Sentences

- 狼是群居动物。(Láng shì qún jū dòng wù.) — Wolves are social animals.

## Notes

狼 combines dog radical 犭 and良 (good), the wild canine species.
