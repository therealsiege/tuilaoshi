# 尖 Pointed, sharp, tip

- **Character**: 尖
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: pointed, sharp, tip
- **Radical**: 小 (small)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 尖端 (jiān duān) — tip, point
- 尖锐 (jiān ruì) — sharp, keen

## Example Sentences

- 三角形有三个尖角。(Sān jiǎo xíng yǒu sān ge jiān jiǎo.) — A triangle has three pointed angles.
