# 叠 To Fold

- **Character**: 叠
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: to pile up, to fold, to repeat
- **Radical**: 又 (again)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 重叠 (chóng dié) — to overlap
- 折叠 (zhé dié) — to fold

## Example Sentences

- 她把衣服叠好了。(Tā bǎ yī fu dié hǎo le.) — She folded the clothes.

## Notes

叠 means to fold or stack neatly. Used for folding clothes, paper, and overlapping things.
