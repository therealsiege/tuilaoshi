# 纫 Thread

- **Character**: 纫
- **Pinyin**: rèn
- **Meaning**: thread a needle, sew
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 缝纫 (féng rèn) — sewing
- 穿针引线 (chuān zhēn yǐn xiàn) — thread a needle

## Example Sentences

- 她在学习缝纫技术。(Tā zài xuéxí féngrèn jìshù.) — She is learning sewing techniques.

## Notes

纫 refers to threading a needle or sewing. Often paired with 缝 in the word 缝纫.
