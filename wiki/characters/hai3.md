# 海 Sea

- **Character**: 海
- **Pinyin**: hǎi
- **Meaning**: sea, ocean
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 大海 (dà hǎi) — sea, ocean
- 上海 (shàng hǎi) — Shanghai

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢看大海。(Wǒ xǐ huān kàn dà hǎi.) — I like looking at the sea.

## Notes

海 means sea or ocean. Also used in 海外 (overseas) and 人海 (sea of people). Shanghai (上海) means 'upon the sea'.
