# 弛 To relax, loosen, unstring a bow

- **Character**: 弛
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: to relax, loosen, unstring a bow
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 松弛 (sōng chí) — to relax, slacken
- 废弛 (fèi chí) — to fall into disuse

## Example Sentences

- 肌肉需要张弛结合。(Jīròu xūyào zhāng chí jiéhé.) — Muscles need a combination of tension and relaxation.

## Notes

Often used in the compound 松弛 for physical or mental relaxation.
