# 殃 Calamity, disaster

- **Character**: 殃
- **Pinyin**: yāng
- **Meaning**: calamity, disaster
- **Radical**: 歹 (death)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 遭殃 (zāo yāng) — suffer disaster
- 殃及 (yāng jí) — bring disaster to

## Example Sentences

- 无辜的人也遭了殃。(Wú gū de rén yě zāo le yāng.) — Innocent people also suffered disaster.

## Notes

Often used in expressions about misfortune befalling others
