# 升 Upgrade, rise

- **Character**: 升
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: upgrade, rise
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 升级 (shēng jí) — upgrade
- 提升 (tí shēng) — improve, enhance

## Example Sentences

- 请升级到最新版本。(Qǐng shēng jí dào zuì xīn bǎn běn.) — Please upgrade to the latest version.

## Notes

Used in 升级 (upgrade), 提升 (improve), important for system updates
