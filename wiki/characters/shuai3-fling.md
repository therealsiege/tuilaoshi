# 甩 Fling

- **Character**: 甩
- **Pinyin**: shuǎi
- **Meaning**: to fling, swing
- **Radical**: 用 (use)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 甩手 (shuǎi shǒu) — to swing arms
- 甩开 (shuǎi kāi) — to shake off

## Example Sentences

- 他甩开了防守队员。(Tā shuǎikāi le fángshǒu duìyuán.) — He shook off the defender.

## Notes

Flinging and swinging; shaking off opponents
