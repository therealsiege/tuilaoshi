# 主 Main

- **Character**: 主
- **Pinyin**: zhǔ
- **Meaning**: main, master, owner, host
- **Radical**: 丶 (dot)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 主要 (zhǔ yào) — main, primary
- 主人 (zhǔ rén) — host, master

## Example Sentences

- 这是主要问题。(Zhè shì zhǔ yào wèn tí.) — This is the main problem.

## Notes

主 shows a lamp with a prominent flame, representing primacy or centrality.
