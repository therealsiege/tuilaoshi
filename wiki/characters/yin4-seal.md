# 印 Seal, print

- **Character**: 印
- **Pinyin**: yìn
- **Meaning**: seal, print
- **Radical**: 卩 (seal)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 印章 (yìn zhāng) — seal, stamp
- 印刷 (yìn shuā) — print, printing

## Example Sentences

- 公司的印章由法人保管。(Gōng sī de yìn zhāng yóu fǎ rén bǎo guǎn.) — The company seal is kept by the legal representative.

## Notes

Company seals have legal authority in contracts and documents.
