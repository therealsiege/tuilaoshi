# 憎 To detest, hate

- **Character**: 憎
- **Pinyin**: zēng
- **Meaning**: to detest, hate
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 憎恨 (zēng hèn) — to hate, detest
- 爱憎 (ài zēng) — love and hate

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该爱憎分明。(Wǒmen yīnggāi ài zēng fēnmíng.) — We should be clear about what we love and hate.

## Notes

Strong negative emotion, often paired with 爱 (love) as opposites.
