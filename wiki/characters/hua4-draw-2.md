# 画 To Paint

- **Character**: 画
- **Pinyin**: huà
- **Meaning**: to draw, to paint, picture, painting
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 画画 (huà huà) — to draw, to paint
- 绘画 (huì huà) — painting

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢画画。(Tā xǐ huan huà huà.) — She likes to paint.

## Notes

画 means to draw/paint or a painting. Used in画画 (to paint), 国画 (Chinese painting).
