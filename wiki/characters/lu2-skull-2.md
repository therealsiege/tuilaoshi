# 颅 Skull

- **Character**: 颅
- **Pinyin**: lú
- **Meaning**: skull, cranium
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 头颅 (tóu lú) — head, skull
- 颅骨 (lú gǔ) — skull bone

## Example Sentences

- 医生检查了他的颅骨。(Yī shēng jiǎn chá le tā de lú gǔ.) — The doctor examined his skull.

## Notes

颅 simplified from 顱. Head 页 with 卢 (hut), the bony case of the head.
