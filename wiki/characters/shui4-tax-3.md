# 税 Tax

- **Character**: 税
- **Pinyin**: shuì
- **Meaning**: tax
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 税收 (shuìshōu) — tax revenue
- 纳税 (nàshuì) — pay taxes

## Example Sentences

- 每个公民都应该纳税。(Měi gè gōngmín dōu yīnggāi nàshuì.) — Every citizen should pay taxes.

## Notes

Essential term for taxation and tax-related matters.
