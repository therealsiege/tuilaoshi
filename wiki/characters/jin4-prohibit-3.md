# 禁 To Prohibit

- **Character**: 禁
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to prohibit, to forbid
- **Radical**: 示 (to show)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 禁止 (jìn zhǐ) — to prohibit
- 严禁 (yán jìn) — strictly prohibited

## Example Sentences

- 这里禁止吸烟。(Zhè lǐ jìn zhǐ xī yān.) — Smoking is prohibited here.

## Notes

禁 altar 示 under trees 林, sacred area with restrictions.
