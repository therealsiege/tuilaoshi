# 愁 To Worry

- **Character**: 愁
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: to worry, sorrow
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 发愁 (fā chóu) — to worry
- 忧愁 (yōu chóu) — melancholy

## Example Sentences

- 他在为工作发愁。(Tā zài wèi gōng zuò fā chóu.) — He is worried about work.

## Notes

愁 shows autumn 秋 over heart 心. Autumn's declining nature evokes melancholy, representing sadness and worry.
