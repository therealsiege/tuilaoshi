# 多 Many/Much

- **Character**: 多
- **Pinyin**: duō
- **Meaning**: many, much, more
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 多少 (duōshǎo) — how many, how much
- 多么 (duōme) — how (exclamatory), what (a)
- 很多 (hěnduō) — very many, a lot
- 大多数 (dàduōshù) — the majority
- 多余 (duōyú) — surplus, unnecessary

## Example Sentences

- 你有多少钱？(Nǐ yǒu duōshǎo qián?) — How much money do you have?
- 中国人很多。(Zhōngguó rén hěn duō.) — There are many Chinese people.
- 多吃水果对身体好。(Duō chī shuǐguǒ duì shēntǐ hǎo.) — Eating more fruit is good for the body.

## Notes

多 is formed by stacking two 夕 (xī, evening) components, one atop the other. The original logic may have been that many evenings (i.e., many days) represent abundance. It pairs naturally with 少 (shǎo, few) as an antonym. Before verbs, 多 functions as an adverb meaning "more" (多吃 eat more, 多学 study more). In the question word 多少, it combines with its opposite to ask "how many/how much."
