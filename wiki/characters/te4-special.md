# 特 Special, particular, extraordinary

- **Character**: 特
- **Pinyin**: tè
- **Meaning**: special, particular, extraordinary
- **Radical**: 牛 (cow)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 特别 (tèbié) — special, particular
- 特赦 (tèshè) — special pardon

## Example Sentences

- 国家颁布特赦令。(Guójiā bānbù tèshè lìng.) — The state issued a special pardon.

## Notes

Common for special and extraordinary.
