# 宿 Lodge

- **Character**: 宿
- **Pinyin**: xiù
- **Meaning**: lodge, constellation
- **Radical**: 宀(roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 星宿 (xīng xiù) — constellation
- 二十八宿 (èr shí bā xiù) — 28 lunar mansions

## Example Sentences

- 中国古代天文学将天空划分为二十八宿。(Zhōngguó gǔdài tiānwénxué jiāng tiānkōng huàfēn wéi èrshíbā xiù.) — Ancient Chinese astronomy divided the sky into 28 lunar mansions.

## Notes

宿 (xiù) refers to constellations or lunar mansions. Also pronounced sù (lodge) and xiǔ (night).
