# 惠 Favor, benefit

- **Character**: 惠
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: favor, benefit
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 优惠 (yōu huì) — preferential, favorable
- 实惠 (shí huì) — tangible benefit, affordable

## Example Sentences

- 会员享受优惠价格。(Huì yuán xiǎng shòu yōu huì jià gé.) — Members enjoy preferential prices.

## Notes

惠顾 means patronage (polite). 互惠 means mutual benefit.
