# 盏 Small cup, lamp, measure word

- **Character**: 盏
- **Pinyin**: zhǎn
- **Meaning**: small cup, lamp, measure word
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 一盏灯 (yī zhǎn dēng) — a lamp
- 茶盏 (chá zhǎn) — teacup

## Example Sentences

- 他斟了一盏茶递给客人。(Tā zhēn le yī zhǎn chá dì gěi kè rén.) — He poured a cup of tea and passed it to the guest.

## Notes

Measure word for lamps or small cups.
