# 堂 Hall

- **Character**: 堂
- **Pinyin**: táng
- **Meaning**: hall, main room
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 课堂 (kè táng) — classroom
- 堂鼓 (táng gǔ) — hall drum

## Example Sentences

- 堂鼓放在舞台中央。(Táng gǔ fàng zài wǔ tái zhōng yāng.) — The hall drum is placed at center stage.

## Notes

堂 means hall; 堂鼓 is a large drum used in opera orchestras.
