# 勉 Strive, Encourage, 勉强 Reluctantly

- **Character**: 勉
- **Pinyin**: miǎn
- **Meaning**: strive, encourage, 勉强 reluctantly
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 勉强 (miǎn qiǎng) — reluctantly, barely
- 勉励 (miǎn lì) — encourage
- 互勉 (hù miǎn) — encourage each other

## Example Sentences

- 老师勉励学生努力学习。(Lǎoshī miǎnlì xuéshēng nǔlì xuéxí.) — The teacher encourages students to study hard.

## Notes

Character expressing effort and encouragement. 勉强 (reluctantly/barely) and 勉励 (encourage) show the range from forced effort to positive encouragement.
