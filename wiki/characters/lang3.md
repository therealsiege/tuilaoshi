# 朗 Bright, clear, loud

- **Character**: 朗
- **Pinyin**: lǎng
- **Meaning**: bright, clear, loud
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 开朗 (kāi lǎng) — cheerful, outgoing
- 朗读 (lǎng dú) — to read aloud

## Example Sentences

- 她性格很开朗。(Tā xìnggé hěn kāilǎng.) — She has a cheerful personality.

## Notes

Brightness, clarity, or loud/clear reading.
