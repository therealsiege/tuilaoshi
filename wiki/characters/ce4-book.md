# 册 Book, volume, register

- **Character**: 册
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: book,册volume, register
- **Radical**: 冂 (container)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 册子 (cè zi) — booklet, pamphlet
- 画册 (huà cè) — picture album

## Example Sentences

- 这是一本精美的画册。(Zhè shì yī běn jīng měi de huà cè.) — This is an exquisite picture album.

## Notes

Originally depicted bamboo strips bound together as a book
