# 豹 Leopard

- **Character**: 豹
- **Pinyin**: bào
- **Meaning**: leopard, panther
- **Radical**: 豸 (beast)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 豹子 (bào zi) — leopard
- 猎豹 (liè bào) — cheetah

## Example Sentences

- 豹子跑得很快。(Bào zi pǎo de hěn kuài.) — Leopards run very fast.

## Notes

豹 shows beast radical 豸 with勺 (ladle), representing the spotted big cat.
