# 覆 To cover, to overturn, to upset

- **Character**: 覆
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: to cover, to overturn, to upset
- **Radical**: 覀(west)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 覆盖 (fù gài) — to cover, to blanket
- 颠覆 (diān fù) — to overturn, to subvert

## Example Sentences

- 白雪覆盖了整个山脉。(Báixuě fùgài le zhěnggè shānmài.) — White snow covered the entire mountain range.

## Notes

More formal than 盖, often used in written language.
