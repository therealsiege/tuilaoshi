# 行 Row

- **Character**: 行
- **Pinyin**: háng
- **Meaning**: row, line, profession
- **Radical**: 行 (go)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 银行 (yín háng) — bank
- 行业 (háng yè) — industry

## Example Sentences

- 删除这一行。(Shān chú zhè yī háng.) — Delete this row.

## Notes

行 as háng means row/line/profession; as xíng means to walk/OK. Essential for spreadsheets and text.
