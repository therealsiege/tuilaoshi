# 挤 To squeeze, to push, to crowd

- **Character**: 挤
- **Pinyin**: jǐ
- **Meaning**: to squeeze, to push, to crowd
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拥挤 (yōng jǐ) — crowded, to push and squeeze
- 挤牛奶 (jǐ niúnǎi) — to milk a cow

## Example Sentences

- 早上地铁很拥挤。(Zǎoshang dìtiě hěn yōngjǐ.) — The subway is very crowded in the morning.

## Notes

Used for squeezing or crowded situations.
