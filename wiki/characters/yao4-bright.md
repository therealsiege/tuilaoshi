# 曜 Bright, dazzling, radiance

- **Character**: 曜
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: bright, dazzling, radiance
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 日曜日 (rì yào rì) — Sunday (Japanese)
- 曜变 (yào biàn) — iridescent glaze

## Example Sentences

- 这件瓷器有美丽的曜变釉色。(Zhè jiàn cí qì yǒu měi lì de yào biàn yòu sè.) — This porcelain has a beautiful iridescent glaze.

## Notes

Rare character used for celestial radiance or special glazes.
