# 堂 Hall

- **Character**: 堂
- **Pinyin**: táng
- **Meaning**: hall, main room
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 课堂 (kè táng) — classroom
- 食堂 (shí táng) — cafeteria

## Example Sentences

- 在食堂吃饭。(Zài shí táng chī fàn.) — Eat in the cafeteria.

## Notes

堂 with earth radical for main hall, formal rooms for gatherings.
