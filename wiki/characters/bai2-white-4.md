# 白 White

- **Character**: 白
- **Pinyin**: bái
- **Meaning**: white, plain, vernacular
- **Radical**: 白 (white)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 白话 (bái huà) — vernacular Chinese
- 明白 (míng bái) — to understand

## Example Sentences

- 白话文运动推广了现代汉语。(Bái huà wén yùn dòng tuī guǎng le xiàn dài hàn yǔ.) — The vernacular movement promoted modern Chinese.

## Notes

白 means white or plain; 白话 is vernacular Chinese opposed to classical.
