# 菜 Dish

- **Character**: 菜
- **Pinyin**: cài
- **Meaning**: dish, vegetable, cuisine
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 青菜 (qīng cài) — green vegetables
- 菜单 (cài dān) — menu

## Example Sentences

- 这个菜很好吃。(Zhè ge cài hěn hǎo chī.) — This dish is delicious.

## Notes

菜 means dish or vegetable. 炒菜 = stir-fry. 中国菜 = Chinese food.
