# 跃 To Jump

- **Character**: 跃
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: to jump, to leap
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 跳跃 (tiào yuè) — to jump, to leap
- 飞跃 (fēi yuè) — to leap

## Example Sentences

- 运动员跳跃过栏杆。(Yùn dòng yuán tiào yuè guò lán gān.) — The athlete leaped over the hurdle.

## Notes

跃 simplified from 躍. Foot radical 足 representing vigorous jumping movement.
