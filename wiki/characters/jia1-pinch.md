# 夹 Pinch

- **Character**: 夹
- **Pinyin**: jiā
- **Meaning**: to pinch, clip
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 夹击 (jiā jī) — to pincer attack
- 夹住 (jiā zhù) — to clamp

## Example Sentences

- 他用腿夹住对手。(Tā yòng tuǐ jiā zhù duìshǒu.) — He clamped his opponent with his legs.

## Notes

Pinching and clamping; grappling holds
