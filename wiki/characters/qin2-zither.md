# 琴 Zither

- **Character**: 琴
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: zither, stringed instrument
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 古琴 (gǔ qín) — guqin (zither)
- 钢琴 (gāng qín) — piano

## Example Sentences

- 她正在弹琴。(Tā zhèng zài tán qín.) — She is playing the zither.

## Notes

琴 refers to various stringed instruments including the traditional guqin and modern piano.
