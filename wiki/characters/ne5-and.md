# 呢 And You?

- **Character**: 呢
- **Pinyin**: ne
- **Meaning**: and you? what about?
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 你呢 (nǐ ne) — and you?
- 着呢 (zhe ne) — indicates ongoing action

## Example Sentences

- 我很好，你呢？(Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?) — I'm fine, and you?

## Notes

呢 is used to ask reciprocal questions (你呢?) and to indicate an ongoing state.
