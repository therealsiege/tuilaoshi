# 铁 Iron

- **Character**: 铁
- **Pinyin**: tiě
- **Meaning**: iron, strong, determined
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 地铁 (dì tiě) — subway, metro
- 铁路 (tiě lù) — railway

## Example Sentences

- 我坐地铁上班。(Wǒ zuò dì tiě shàng bān.) — I take the subway to work.

## Notes

铁 means iron and metaphorically anything strong or determined. 地铁 (subway) literally means 'ground iron/rail'.
