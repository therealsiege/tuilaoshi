# 穿 To wear, to put on, to pierce through

- **Character**: 穿
- **Pinyin**: chuān
- **Meaning**: to wear, to put on, to pierce through
- **Radical**: 穴(cave)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 穿着 (chuān zhuó) — dress, clothing
- 穿过 (chuān guò) — to pass through

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着一件红色的裙子。(Tā chuānzhe yī jiàn hóngsè de qúnzi.) — She is wearing a red skirt.

## Notes

Can mean to wear clothing or to pass through.
