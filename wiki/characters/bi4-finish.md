# 毕 To Finish

- **Character**: 毕
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to finish, to complete
- **Radical**: 比 (compare)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 毕业 (bì yè) — to graduate
- 毕竟 (bì jìng) — after all

## Example Sentences

- 他今年毕业。(Tā jīn nián bì yè.) — He graduates this year.

## Notes

毕业 = graduate, 毕竟 = after all/nevertheless.
