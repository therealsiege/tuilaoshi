# 买 To Buy

- **Character**: 买
- **Pinyin**: mǎi
- **Meaning**: to buy
- **Radical**: 乛 (hidden)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 买东西 (mǎi dōng xi) — to buy things, to shop
- 购买 (gòu mǎi) — to purchase

## Example Sentences

- 我想买一件衣服。(Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yī jiàn yī fu.) — I want to buy a piece of clothing.

## Notes

买 simplified from 買. Remember: 买 (buy) has fewer strokes than 卖 (sell) - buying is easier!
