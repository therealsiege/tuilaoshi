# 刀 Knife

- **Character**: 刀
- **Pinyin**: dāo
- **Meaning**: knife, blade, sword
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 刀子 (dāo zi) — knife
- 菜刀 (cài dāo) — kitchen knife

## Example Sentences

- 小心，这把刀很锋利。(Xiǎo xīn, zhè bǎ dāo hěn fēng lì.) — Be careful, this knife is very sharp.

## Notes

刀 is a pictograph of a blade. As a radical (刂), it appears in characters related to cutting or sharp objects.
