# 溉 Irrigate, 灌溉 Irrigate

- **Character**: 溉
- **Pinyin**: gài
- **Meaning**: irrigate, 灌溉 irrigate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 灌溉 (guàn gài) — irrigate
- 浇溉 (jiāo gài) — irrigate
- 溉田 (gài tián) — irrigate fields

## Example Sentences

- 这个地区的灌溉系统很先进。(Zhège dìqū de guàngài xìtǒng hěn xiānjìn.) — This area's irrigation system is very advanced.

## Notes

Almost exclusively used in 灌溉 (irrigation). Rare to see alone in modern Chinese, but essential in agricultural contexts.
