# 柔 Soft, gentle, yielding

- **Character**: 柔
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: soft, gentle, yielding
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 柔和 (róu hé) — soft, gentle
- 温柔 (wēn róu) — gentle, tender

## Example Sentences

- 以柔克刚是一种智慧。(Yǐ róu kè gāng shì yī zhǒng zhìhuì.) — Overcoming hardness with softness is a kind of wisdom.

## Notes

Represents gentleness and flexibility. In Daoist philosophy, softness is seen as ultimately stronger than rigidity.
