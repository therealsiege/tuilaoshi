# 经 Pass through, manage, classic

- **Character**: 经
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: pass through, manage, classic
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 经理 (jīnglǐ) — manager
- 经济 (jīngjì) — economy

## Example Sentences

- 他是公司经理。(Tā shì gōngsī jīnglǐ.) — He is the company manager.
