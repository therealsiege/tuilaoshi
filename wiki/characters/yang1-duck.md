# 鸯 Mandarin Duck

- **Character**: 鸯
- **Pinyin**: yāng
- **Meaning**: mandarin duck (female)
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 鸳鸯 (yuān yāng) — mandarin ducks

## Example Sentences

- 鸳鸯总是成双出现。(Yuān yāng zǒng shì chéng shuāng chū xiàn.) — Mandarin ducks always appear in pairs.

## Notes

鸯 simplified from 鴦. Bird 鸟 with 央 (center), female mandarin duck.
