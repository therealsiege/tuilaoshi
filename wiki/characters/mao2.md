# 锚 Anchor

- **Character**: 锚
- **Pinyin**: máo
- **Meaning**: anchor
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 锚地 (máo dì) — anchorage
- 起锚 (qǐ máo) — to weigh anchor

## Example Sentences

- 船只抛锚停泊在港口。(Chuán zhī pāo máo tíng bó zài gǎng kǒu.) — The ship dropped anchor and moored at the port.

## Notes

Nautical term for anchor. 抛锚 means to drop anchor or to break down (vehicle).
