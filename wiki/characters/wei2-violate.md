# 违 Violate

- **Character**: 违
- **Pinyin**: wéi
- **Meaning**: to violate, disobey
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 违规 (wéi guī) — to violate rules
- 违反 (wéi fǎn) — to violate

## Example Sentences

- 违反规则会受到处罚。(Wéifǎn guīzé huì shòudào chǔfá.) — Violating rules will be punished.

## Notes

Violating; breaking rules
