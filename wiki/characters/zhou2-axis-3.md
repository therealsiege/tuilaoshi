# 轴 Axis

- **Character**: 轴
- **Pinyin**: zhóu
- **Meaning**: axis, axle, shaft
- **Radical**: 车 (vehicle)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 轴心 (zhóu xīn) — axis
- 中轴线 (zhōng zhóu xiàn) — central axis

## Example Sentences

- 这条街是城市的中轴线。(Zhè tiáo jiē shì chéng shì de zhōng zhóu xiàn.) — This street is the city's central axis.

## Notes

轴 simplified from 軸. Vehicle 车 with central shaft.
