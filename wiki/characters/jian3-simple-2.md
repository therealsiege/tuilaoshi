# 简 Simple

- **Character**: 简
- **Pinyin**: jiǎn
- **Meaning**: simple, bamboo slip
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 简单 (jiǎn dān) — simple
- 竹简 (zhú jiǎn) — bamboo slip

## Example Sentences

- 古代用竹简记录文字。(Gǔ dài yòng zhú jiǎn jì lù wén zì.) — In ancient times, bamboo slips were used to record writing.

## Notes

简 simplified from 簡. Bamboo 竹 slips for writing, simplified form.
