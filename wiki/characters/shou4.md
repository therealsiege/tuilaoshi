# 瘦 Thin

- **Character**: 瘦
- **Pinyin**: shòu
- **Meaning**: thin, lean
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 瘦弱 (shòu ruò) — thin and weak
- 消瘦 (xiāo shòu) — to become thin

## Example Sentences

- 他最近瘦了很多。(Tā zuìjìn shòu le hěn duō.) — He has lost a lot of weight recently.

## Notes

瘦 combines sickness 疒 with 叟 (old man), suggesting the thinness associated with illness or age.
