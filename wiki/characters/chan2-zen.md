# 禅 Zen

- **Character**: 禅
- **Pinyin**: chán
- **Meaning**: Zen, meditation, Buddhist contemplation
- **Radical**: 礻(spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 禅宗 (chán zōng) — Zen Buddhism
- 坐禅 (zuò chán) — to meditate

## Example Sentences

- 他每天坐禅。(Tā měi tiān zuò chán.) — He meditates every day.

## Notes

禅 (Chán) is the Chinese origin of Japanese Zen. 禅让 (shàn ràng) = to abdicate.
