# 辞 Diction, resign, decline

- **Character**: 辞
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: diction, resign, decline
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 辞职 (cí zhí) — resign from a position
- 言辞 (yán cí) — words, diction

## Example Sentences

- 他的演讲言辞优美，打动了在场的每一个人。(Tā de yǎnjiǎng yáncí yōuměi, dǎdòng le zàichǎng de měi yī gèrén.) — His speech had beautiful diction and moved everyone present.

## Notes

Refers to refined language and rhetoric. In classical literature, 辞 refers to a type of poetic composition.
