# 项 Item

- **Character**: 项
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: item, clause, neck, measure word
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 项目 (xiàng mù) — project, item
- 事项 (shì xiàng) — matter, item

## Example Sentences

- 我们有一个新项目。(Wǒ men yǒu yī ge xīn xiàng mù.) — We have a new project.

## Notes

项 originally meant neck/nape, now commonly used for items in a list or projects. Measure word for items/clauses.
