# 疼 To ache, to love dearly, to pain

- **Character**: 疼
- **Pinyin**: téng
- **Meaning**: to ache, to love dearly, to pain
- **Radical**: 疒(sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 疼痛 (téng tòng) — pain, ache
- 心疼 (xīn téng) — to love dearly, to feel sorry for

## Example Sentences

- 妈妈很疼爱孩子。(Māma hěn téng'ài háizi.) — The mother loves her child dearly.

## Notes

Can mean physical pain or deep affection.
