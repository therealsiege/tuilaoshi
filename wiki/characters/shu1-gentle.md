# 淑 Gentle, virtuous (of women)

- **Character**: 淑
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: gentle, virtuous (of women)
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 淑女 (shū nǚ) — lady, gentlewoman
- 贤淑 (xián shū) — virtuous (of women)

## Example Sentences

- 她举止像个淑女。(Tā jǔzhǐ xiàng ge shūnǚ.) — She behaves like a lady.

## Notes

Specifically describes feminine virtue and gentleness.
