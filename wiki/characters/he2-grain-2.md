# 禾 Grain, cereal, seedling

- **Character**: 禾
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: grain, cereal, seedling
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 禾苗 (hé miáo) — grain seedlings
- 嘉禾 (jiā hé) — fine grain, auspicious crop

## Example Sentences

- 田里的禾苗长得很茂盛。(Tián lǐ de hé miáo zhǎng de hěn màoshèng.) — The grain seedlings in the field are growing luxuriantly.

## Notes

Grain crops, especially rice or millet seedlings. The character itself is a radical in many agriculture-related characters.
