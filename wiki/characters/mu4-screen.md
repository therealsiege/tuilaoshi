# 幕 Screen, curtain

- **Character**: 幕
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: screen, curtain
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen
- 荧幕 (yíng mù) — screen, monitor

## Example Sentences

- 荧幕分辨率很高。(Yíng mù fēn biàn lǜ hěn gāo.) — The screen resolution is very high.

## Notes

Used in 屏幕 (screen), 荧幕 (monitor), display terminology
