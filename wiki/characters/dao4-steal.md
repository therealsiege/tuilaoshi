# 盗 Thief

- **Character**: 盗
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to steal, thief, robber
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 盗窃 (dào qiè) — theft
- 强盗 (qiáng dào) — robber

## Example Sentences

- 这里经常有盗窃案。(Zhè lǐ jīng cháng yǒu dào qiè àn.) — There are frequent theft cases here.

## Notes

盗 depicts someone coveting what is in a dish 皿.
