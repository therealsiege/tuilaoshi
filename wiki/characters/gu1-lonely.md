# 孤 Lonely

- **Character**: 孤
- **Pinyin**: gū
- **Meaning**: lonely, solitary
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 孤独 (gūdú) — lonely
- 孤单 (gūdān) — lonely, alone

## Example Sentences

- 老人感到很孤独。(Lǎorén gǎndào hěn gūdú.) — The old person feels very lonely.

## Notes

Loneliness state. Child radical suggests orphan origin. 孤独 is existential loneliness. Deep emotion.
