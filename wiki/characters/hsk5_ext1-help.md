# 帮 Help, Gang, 帮助 Help

- **Character**: 帮
- **Pinyin**: bāng
- **Meaning**: help, gang, 帮助 help
- **Radical**: 巾 (towel)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 帮助 (bāng zhù) — help, assist
- 帮忙 (bāng máng) — help
- 帮派 (bāng pài) — gang, faction

## Example Sentences

- 谢谢你帮助我。(Xièxiè nǐ bāngzhù wǒ.) — Thank you for helping me.

## Notes

Very common verb meaning 'help'. Also used as noun for gang or group. 帮助 and 帮忙 are among the most frequently used help-related words.
