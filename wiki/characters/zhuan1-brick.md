# 砖 Brick

- **Character**: 砖
- **Pinyin**: zhuān
- **Meaning**: brick
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 砖瓦 (zhuān wǎ) — bricks and tiles
- 砖头 (zhuān tou) — brick

## Example Sentences

- 这堵墙是用红砖砌成的。(Zhè dǔ qiáng shì yòng hóng zhuān qì chéng de.) — This wall is built with red bricks.

## Notes

Fundamental building material in Chinese architecture.
