# 旷 Vast, open, neglect

- **Character**: 旷
- **Pinyin**: kuàng
- **Meaning**: vast, open, neglect
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 开旷 (kāikuàng) — open, spacious
- 旷课 (kuàngkè) — skip class

## Example Sentences

- 视野开阔旷远。(Shìyě kāikuò kuàngyuǎn.) — The vista is open and vast.

## Notes

Literary term for vastness or neglecting duties.
