# 悍 Brave, fierce, violent

- **Character**: 悍
- **Pinyin**: hàn
- **Meaning**: brave, fierce, violent
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 悍然 (hàn rán) — brazenly, flagrantly
- 强悍 (qiáng hàn) — powerful and brave

## Example Sentences

- 他悍然拒绝了要求。(Tā hàn rán jù jué le yāo qiú.) — He brazenly refused the request.

## Notes

Fierce bravery or brazenn boldness
