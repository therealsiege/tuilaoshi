# 垒 Rampart, base, pile up

- **Character**: 垒
- **Pinyin**: lěi
- **Meaning**: rampart, base, pile up
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 堡垒 (bǎo lěi) — fortress, stronghold
- 壁垒 (bì lěi) — barrier, rampart

## Example Sentences

- 他们建立了坚固的堡垒。(Tā men jiàn lì le jiān gù de bǎo lěi.) — They built a solid fortress.
