# 球 Ball, sphere, globe

- **Character**: 球
- **Pinyin**: qiú
- **Meaning**: ball, sphere, globe
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 地球 (dì qiú) — Earth
- 星球 (xīng qiú) — planet, celestial body

## Example Sentences

- 地球是我们的家园。(Dì qiú shì wǒ men de jiā yuán.) — Earth is our home.

## Notes

Used for spherical objects and planets
