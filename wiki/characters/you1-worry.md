# 忧 To worry, sad, 憂 (traditional)

- **Character**: 忧
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: to worry, sad, 憂 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忧虑 (yōu lǜ) — to worry, anxiety
- 担忧 (dān yōu) — to worry, be anxious

## Example Sentences

- 父母总是为孩子担忧。(Fùmǔ zǒng shì wèi háizi dānyōu.) — Parents always worry about their children.

## Notes

Common character for worry, sadness, and anxiety.
