# 渊 Abyss

- **Character**: 渊
- **Pinyin**: yuān
- **Meaning**: abyss, deep
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 深渊 (shēn yuān) — abyss
- 学识渊博 (xué shí yuān bó) — erudite

## Example Sentences

- 他的学识渊博。(Tā de xué shí yuān bó.) — His knowledge is profound.

## Notes

渊 simplified from 淵. Water 氵 forming a deep pool, metaphor for depth.
