# 功 Merit

- **Character**: 功
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: merit, achievement, skill
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 成功 (chéng gōng) — success
- 功夫 (gōng fu) — kung fu, skill

## Example Sentences

- 他终于成功了。(Tā zhōng yú chéng gōng le.) — He finally succeeded.

## Notes

功 combines work/labor (工) and strength (力), representing meritorious effort.
