# 闷 Gloomy

- **Character**: 闷
- **Pinyin**: mèn
- **Meaning**: gloomy, stuffy, bored
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 郁闷 (yù mèn) — gloomy
- 闷闷不乐 (mèn mèn bù lè) — unhappy

## Example Sentences

- 他整天闷闷不乐。(Tā zhěng tiān mèn mèn bù lè.) — He is unhappy all day long.

## Notes

闷 gate 门 enclosing 心 (heart), feeling trapped.
