# 腻 Greasy

- **Character**: 腻
- **Pinyin**: nì
- **Meaning**: greasy, tired of
- **Radical**: 月 (meat/flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 油腻 (yóu nì) — greasy
- 腻味 (nì wèi) — to be fed up

## Example Sentences

- 这道菜太油腻了。(Zhè dào cài tài yóu nì le.) — This dish is too greasy.

## Notes

腻 simplified from 膩. Flesh 月 plus 贰 (two/double), excessively rich.
