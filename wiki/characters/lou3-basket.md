# 篓 Basket

- **Character**: 篓
- **Pinyin**: lǒu
- **Meaning**: basket
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 竹篓 (zhú lǒu) — bamboo basket
- 字纸篓 (zì zhǐ lǒu) — wastebasket

## Example Sentences

- 她背着一个竹篓。(Tā bēi zhe yí gè zhú lǒu.) — She carries a bamboo basket on her back.

## Notes

篓 simplified from 簍. Bamboo 竹 with 娄 (frequently), woven bamboo basket.
