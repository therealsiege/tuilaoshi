# 补 Mend, supplement

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: mend, supplement
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 补充 (bǔchōng) — supplement, replenish
- 弥补 (míbǔ) — make up for, remedy

## Example Sentences

- 我需要补充一些维生素。(Wǒ xūyào bǔchōng yīxiē wéishēngsù.) — I need to supplement some vitamins.

## Notes

Common character for mending, patching, or supplementing deficiencies.
