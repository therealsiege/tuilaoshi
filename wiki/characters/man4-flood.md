# 漫 To flood, free, casual

- **Character**: 漫
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: to flood, free, casual
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漫长 (màn cháng) — very long, endless
- 浪漫 (làng màn) — romantic

## Example Sentences

- 冬天的夜晚很漫长。(Dōngtiān de yèwǎn hěn màncháng.) — Winter nights are very long.

## Notes

Can mean flooding/spreading or casual/careless attitude.
