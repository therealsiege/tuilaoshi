# 执 Execute, hold, persist

- **Character**: 执
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: execute, hold, persist
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 执行 (zhíxíng) — execute, implement
- 执法 (zhífǎ) — enforce the law

## Example Sentences

- 警察执行公务。(Jǐngchá zhíxíng gōngwù.) — Police execute official duties.

## Notes

Formal term for execution and implementation.
