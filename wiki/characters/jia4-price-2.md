# 价 Price, value

- **Character**: 价
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: price, value
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 价格 (jià gé) — price
- 价值 (jià zhí) — value

## Example Sentences

- 这个商品价格合理。(Zhè ge shāng pǐn jià gé hé lǐ.) — This product is reasonably priced.

## Notes

降价 means reduce price. 涨价 means raise price.
