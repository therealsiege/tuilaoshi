# 挥 To Wave

- **Character**: 挥
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: to wave, to wield, to scatter
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挥手 (huī shǒu) — to wave hand
- 发挥 (fā huī) — to display

## Example Sentences

- 他向我挥手告别。(Tā xiàng wǒ huī shǒu gào bié.) — He waved goodbye to me.

## Notes

挥 simplified from 揮. Hand 扌 waving motion.
