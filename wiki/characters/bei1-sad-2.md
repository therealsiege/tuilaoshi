# 悲 Sad

- **Character**: 悲
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: sad, sorrowful
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 悲伤 (bēi shāng) — sorrowful
- 悲剧 (bēi jù) — tragedy

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个悲剧。(Zhè shì yī gè bēi jù.) — This is a tragedy.

## Notes

悲 combines 非 (not) and 心 (heart) — the heart in a state of 'not right.'
