# 拉 To Pull

- **Character**: 拉
- **Pinyin**: lā
- **Meaning**: to pull, to play (violin)
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拉车 (lā chē) — to pull a cart
- 拉小提琴 (lā xiǎo tí qín) — to play violin

## Example Sentences

- 他会拉二胡。(Tā huì lā èr hú.) — He can play the erhu.

## Notes

拉 combines hand 扌 and 立 (stand), representing pulling or drawing with hand.
