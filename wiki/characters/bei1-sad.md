# 悲 Sad, sorrowful, compassionate

- **Character**: 悲
- **Pinyin**: bēi
- **Meaning**: sad, sorrowful, compassionate
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 悲伤 (bēi shāng) — sad, sorrowful
- 悲壮 (bēi zhuàng) — solemn and stirring

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗表达了悲壮的情感。(Zhè shǒu shī biǎo dá le bēi zhuàng de qíng gǎn.) — This poem expresses solemn and stirring emotions.

## Notes

Can express sadness or tragic heroism.
