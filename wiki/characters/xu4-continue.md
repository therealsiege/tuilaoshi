# 续 Continue, renew

- **Character**: 续
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: continue, renew
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 续约 (xù yuē) — renew a contract
- 继续 (jì xù) — continue

## Example Sentences

- 合同到期后可以续约。(Hé tong dào qī hòu kě yǐ xù yuē.) — The contract can be renewed after expiration.

## Notes

续期 means extend/renew. 续费 means renew payment.
