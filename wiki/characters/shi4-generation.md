# 世 Generation

- **Character**: 世
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: generation, world, era
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 世界 (shì jiè) — world
- 世纪 (shì jì) — century

## Example Sentences

- 21世纪是信息时代。(Èr shí yī shì jì shì xìn xī shí dài.) — The 21st century is the information age.

## Notes

世 shows three tens (三十), representing thirty years or one generation in ancient reckoning.
