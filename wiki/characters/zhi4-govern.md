# 治 To Govern

- **Character**: 治
- **Pinyin**: zhì
- **Meaning**: to govern, to manage, to cure
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 政治 (zhèng zhì) — politics
- 治疗 (zhì liáo) — to treat (medically)

## Example Sentences

- 医生正在治疗他的病。(Yī shēng zhèng zài zhì liáo tā de bìng.) — The doctor is treating his illness.

## Notes

治 originally meant to control water (floods), extended to governing and curing. Key in politics and medicine.
