# 鬓 Sideburns

- **Character**: 鬓
- **Pinyin**: bìn
- **Meaning**: temple hair, sideburns
- **Radical**: 髟 (long hair)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 鬓角 (bìn jiǎo) — sideburns
- 白发鬓 (bái fà bìn) — white-haired temples

## Example Sentences

- 他的鬓角已经花白了。(Tā de bìn jiǎo yǐ jīng huā bái le.) — His temples have turned grey.

## Notes

鬓 simplified from 鬢. Long hair 髟 with 宾 (guest), hair at the temples.
