# 孝 Filial Piety

- **Character**: 孝
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: filial piety
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 孝顺 (xiào shùn) — filial, obedient to parents
- 孝敬 (xiào jìng) — to show filial respect

## Example Sentences

- 孝顺父母是中国传统美德。(Xiào shùn fù mǔ shì zhōng guó chuán tǒng měi dé.) — Filial piety is a traditional Chinese virtue.

## Notes

孝 shows an old person 老 (simplified top) supported by a child 子, representing the duty of children to care for parents.
