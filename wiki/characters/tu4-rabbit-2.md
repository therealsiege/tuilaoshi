# 兔 Rabbit

- **Character**: 兔
- **Pinyin**: tù
- **Meaning**: rabbit, hare
- **Radical**: 儿 (legs)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 守株待兔 (shǒu zhū dài tù) — wait for a windfall
- 兔子 (tù zi) — rabbit

## Example Sentences

- 兔子跑得很快。(Tùzi pǎo de hěn kuài.) — Rabbits run very fast.

## Notes

The rabbit in 守株待兔; also a zodiac animal.
