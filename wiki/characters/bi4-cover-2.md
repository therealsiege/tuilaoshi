# 蔽 To cover, shelter, hide

- **Character**: 蔽
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to cover, shelter, hide
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 隐蔽 (yǐn bì) — hidden, concealed
- 遮蔽 (zhē bì) — to cover, shelter

## Example Sentences

- 树木遮蔽了阳光。(Shùmù zhēbìle yángguāng.) — The trees blocked the sunlight.

## Notes

To cover, conceal, or provide shelter.
