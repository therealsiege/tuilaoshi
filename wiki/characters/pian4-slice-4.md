# 片 Slice, chip

- **Character**: 片
- **Pinyin**: piàn
- **Meaning**: slice, chip
- **Radical**: 片 (slice)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 芯片 (xīn piàn) — chip
- 图片 (tú piàn) — image, picture

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个高性能芯片。(Zhè shì yī gè gāo xìng néng xīn piàn.) — This is a high-performance chip.

## Notes

Used in 芯片 (chip), 图片 (image), 视频片段 (video clip)
