# 孩 Child

- **Character**: 孩
- **Pinyin**: hái
- **Meaning**: child
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 孩子 (hái zi) — child
- 女孩 (nǚ hái) — girl

## Example Sentences

- 这个孩子真聪明。(Zhè ge hái zi zhēn cōng míng.) — This child is really clever.

## Notes

孩 uses the child radical 子. 男孩 = boy, 女孩 = girl.
