# 涟 Ripples, teardrops

- **Character**: 涟
- **Pinyin**: lián
- **Meaning**: ripples, teardrops
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 涟漪 (lián yī) — ripples
- 泪涟涟 (lèi lián lián) — tearful

## Example Sentences

- 她泪眼涟涟。(Tā lèi yǎn lián lián.) — Her eyes were filled with tears.

## Notes

Ripples or streaming tears
