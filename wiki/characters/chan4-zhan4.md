# 颤 Tremble

- **Character**: 颤
- **Pinyin**: chàn/zhàn
- **Meaning**: tremble, shiver
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to tremble, shiver
- 发颤 (fā chàn) — to tremble
- 寒颤 (hán zhàn) — to shiver from cold

## Example Sentences

- 高热时身体会颤抖。(Gāo rè shí shēn tǐ huì chàn dǒu.) — The body trembles during high fever.

## Notes

Shaking movement; fever or neurological sign.
