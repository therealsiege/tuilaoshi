# 唐 Tang dynasty, boastful, abrupt

- **Character**: 唐
- **Pinyin**: táng
- **Meaning**: Tang dynasty, boastful, abrupt
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 唐朝 (Táng cháo) — Tang Dynasty
- 荒唐 (huāng táng) — absurd, ridiculous

## Example Sentences

- 唐朝是中国历史上最繁荣的时期之一。(Táng cháo shì Zhōng guó lì shǐ shàng zuì fán róng de shí qī zhī yī.) — The Tang Dynasty was one of the most prosperous periods in Chinese history.

## Notes

Famous Chinese dynasty (618-907 AD). Also used in 荒唐 meaning absurd or preposterous.
