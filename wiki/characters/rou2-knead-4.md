# 揉 To Knead

- **Character**: 揉
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: to knead, to rub, to crumple
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揉面 (róu miàn) — to knead dough
- 揉眼睛 (róu yǎn jing) — to rub eyes

## Example Sentences

- 她在揉面做饺子。(Tā zài róu miàn zuò jiǎo zi.) — She is kneading dough to make dumplings.

## Notes

揉 hand 扌 plus 柔 (soft), kneading to soften.
