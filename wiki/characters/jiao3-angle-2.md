# 角 Angle, corner, horn

- **Character**: 角
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: angle, corner, horn
- **Radical**: 角 (horn)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 角落 (jiǎo luò) — corner, nook
- 岬角 (jiǎ jiǎo) — promontory

## Example Sentences

- 房间的角落里放着一张旧椅子。(Fáng jiān de jiǎo luò lǐ fàng zhe yī zhāng jiù yǐ zi.) — There's an old chair in the corner of the room.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: angle, corner, horn; also pronounced jué (role).
