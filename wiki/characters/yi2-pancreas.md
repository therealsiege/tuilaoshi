# 胰 Pancreas

- **Character**: 胰
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: pancreas
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 胰腺 (yí xiàn) — pancreas
- 胰岛素 (yí dǎo sù) — insulin

## Example Sentences

- 胰腺分泌胰岛素调节血糖。(Yí xiàn fēn mì yí dǎo sù tiáo jié xuè táng.) — The pancreas secretes insulin to regulate blood sugar.

## Notes

Organ producing insulin and digestive enzymes
