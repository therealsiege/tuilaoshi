# 飞 To Fly

- **Character**: 飞
- **Pinyin**: fēi
- **Meaning**: to fly
- **Radical**: 飞 (fly)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 飞机 (fēi jī) — airplane
- 飞行 (fēi xíng) — to fly, flight

## Example Sentences

- 鸟在天上飞。(Niǎo zài tiān shàng fēi.) — Birds are flying in the sky.

## Notes

飞 simplified from 飛. Depicts wings spreading upward. Used for all flying: birds, planes, etc.
