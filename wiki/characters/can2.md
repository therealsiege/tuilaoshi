# 惭 Ashamed

- **Character**: 惭
- **Pinyin**: cán
- **Meaning**: ashamed, abashed
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 惭愧 (cán kuì) — ashamed, abashed
- 无惭 (wú cán) — shameless

## Example Sentences

- 我感到非常惭愧。(Wǒ gǎndào fēicháng cánkuì.) — I feel very ashamed.

## Notes

惭 combines heart 心 with 斩 (cut), suggesting the cutting pain of shame in the heart.
