# 嘲 To Ridicule

- **Character**: 嘲
- **Pinyin**: cháo
- **Meaning**: to ridicule, to jeer
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 嘲笑 (cháo xiào) — to ridicule, to laugh at
- 嘲讽 (cháo fěng) — to mock

## Example Sentences

- 不要嘲笑别人的缺点。(Bù yào cháo xiào bié rén de quē diǎn.) — Don't ridicule others' shortcomings.

## Notes

嘲 uses mouth radical 口, indicating verbal mockery. More direct and harsh than 讽.
