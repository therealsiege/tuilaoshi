# 根 Root

- **Character**: 根
- **Pinyin**: gēn
- **Meaning**: root, basis; measure word for long thin things
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 根据 (gēn jù) — according to
- 根本 (gēn běn) — fundamental, at all

## Example Sentences

- 根据天气预报，明天下雨。(Gēn jù tiān qì yù bào, míng tiān xià yǔ.) — According to the forecast, it will rain tomorrow.

## Notes

根 as a measure word is used for sticks, strings, and other long thin objects.
