# 描 Trace, draw

- **Character**: 描
- **Pinyin**: miáo
- **Meaning**: trace, draw
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 扫描 (sǎo miáo) — scan
- 描述 (miáo shù) — describe

## Example Sentences

- 扫描仪正在扫描文档。(Sǎo miáo yí zhèng zài sǎo miáo wén dàng.) — The scanner is scanning the document.

## Notes

Used in 扫描 (scan), 描述 (describe), 描绘 (depict)
