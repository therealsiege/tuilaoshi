# 材 Material

- **Character**: 材
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: material, timber, talent
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 材料 (cái liào) — material
- 木材 (mù cái) — lumber

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么材料做的？(Zhè shì shén me cái liào zuò de?) — What material is this made of?

## Notes

材 with wood radical for timber, extended to all materials and talents.
