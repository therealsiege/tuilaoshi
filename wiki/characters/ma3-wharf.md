# 码 Wharf

- **Character**: 码
- **Pinyin**: mǎ
- **Meaning**: wharf,码头, number, yard
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 码头 (mǎ tóu) — wharf, dock
- 号码 (hào mǎ) — number

## Example Sentences

- 货物在码头装卸。(Huò wù zài mǎ tóu zhuāng xiè.) — Goods are loaded and unloaded at the wharf.

## Notes

码 simplified from 碼. Stone 石 measuring unit, wharf with stone steps.
