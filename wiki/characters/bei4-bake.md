# 焙 To bake, to roast

- **Character**: 焙
- **Pinyin**: bèi
- **Meaning**: to bake, to roast
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 烘焙 (hōng bèi) — to bake
- 焙烧 (bèi shāo) — to roast

## Example Sentences

- 她在学习烘焙蛋糕。(Tā zài xuéxí hōngbèi dàngāo.) — She is learning to bake cakes.

## Notes

Commonly paired with 烘 as 烘焙, the standard term for baking.
