# 珊 Coral

- **Character**: 珊
- **Pinyin**: shān
- **Meaning**: coral
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 珊瑚 (shān hú) — coral
- 珊瑚礁 (shān hú jiāo) — coral reef

## Example Sentences

- 大堡礁有美丽的珊瑚。(Dà bǎo jiāo yǒu měilì de shānhú.) — The Great Barrier Reef has beautiful coral.

## Notes

Always used with 瑚 as 珊瑚. Jade radical for precious marine organism.
