# 仁 Benevolence, humanity, kernel

- **Character**: 仁
- **Pinyin**: rén
- **Meaning**: benevolence, humanity, kernel
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 仁爱 (rén ài) — benevolent love
- 仁慈 (rén cí) — benevolent, merciful

## Example Sentences

- 孔子提倡仁爱思想。(Kǒngzǐ tíchàng rén'ài sīxiǎng.) — Confucius advocated benevolent love.

## Notes

Core Confucian virtue of benevolence and humanity.
