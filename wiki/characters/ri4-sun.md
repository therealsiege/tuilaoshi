# 日 Sun

- **Character**: 日
- **Pinyin**: rì
- **Meaning**: sun, day, date
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 日本 (rì běn) — Japan
- 生日 (shēng rì) — birthday

## Example Sentences

- 今天是几月几日？(Jīn tiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ rì?) — What date is today?

## Notes

日 is a pictograph of the sun — a circle with a dot inside. It is used for formal dates; 号 is more colloquial.
