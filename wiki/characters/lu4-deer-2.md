# 鹿 Deer

- **Character**: 鹿
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: deer
- **Radical**: 鹿 (deer)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 梅花鹿 (méi huā lù) — sika deer
- 鹿茸 (lù róng) — deer antler velvet

## Example Sentences

- 森林里有一只鹿。(Sēnlín lǐ yǒu yī zhī lù.) — There is a deer in the forest.

## Notes

This character is itself a radical. Symbol of longevity and prosperity.
