# 签 Sign, autograph

- **Character**: 签
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: sign, autograph
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 签字 (qiān zì) — sign, autograph
- 签约 (qiān yuē) — sign a contract

## Example Sentences

- 请在这里签字。(Qǐng zài zhè lǐ qiān zì.) — Please sign here.

## Notes

Essential for contracts. 签署 is formal signing, 签订 means conclude/sign.
