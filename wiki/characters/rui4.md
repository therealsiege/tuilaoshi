# 锐 Sharp, keen, acute

- **Character**: 锐
- **Pinyin**: ruì
- **Meaning**: sharp, keen, acute
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锐角 (ruì jiǎo) — acute angle
- 尖锐 (jiān ruì) — sharp, acute

## Example Sentences

- 锐角小于九十度。(Ruì jiǎo xiǎo yú jiǔ shí dù.) — An acute angle is less than ninety degrees.
