# 协 协作, unite, assist

- **Character**: 协
- **Pinyin**: xié
- **Meaning**: 协作, unite, assist
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 协议 (xié yì) — agreement, protocol
- 协商 (xié shāng) — negotiate, consult

## Example Sentences

- 双方达成了协议。(Shuāng fāng dá chéng le xié yì.) — Both parties reached an agreement.

## Notes

协定 is agreement/pact. 协会 means association.
