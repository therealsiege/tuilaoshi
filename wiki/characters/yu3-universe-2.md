# 宇 Universe

- **Character**: 宇
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: universe, space, eaves
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 宇宙 (yǔ zhòu) — universe
- 宇航 (yǔ háng) — space navigation

## Example Sentences

- 宇宙非常浩瀚。(Yǔ zhòu fēi cháng hào hàn.) — The universe is extremely vast.

## Notes

宇 has roof 宀 with 于 (at), space under the heavens.
