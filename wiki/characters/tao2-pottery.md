# 陶 Pottery

- **Character**: 陶
- **Pinyin**: táo
- **Meaning**: pottery, to mold, pleased
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 陶瓷 (táo cí) — pottery and porcelain, ceramics
- 陶器 (táo qì) — pottery

## Example Sentences

- 中国陶瓷闻名世界。(Zhōng guó táo cí wén míng shì jiè.) — Chinese ceramics are world-famous.

## Notes

陶 shows mound 阝 and缶 (pottery vessel), representing ceramic crafts.
