# 养 Nourish

- **Character**: 养
- **Pinyin**: yǎng
- **Meaning**: nourish, recuperate
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 休养 (xiū yǎng) — to recuperate
- 养病 (yǎng bìng) — to recuperate, convalesce
- 营养 (yíng yǎng) — nutrition

## Example Sentences

- 他在家养病。(Tā zài jiā yǎng bìng.) — He is recuperating at home.

## Notes

Nourishment and rest; important in recovery.
