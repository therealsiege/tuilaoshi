# 明 Bright, clear, understand

- **Character**: 明
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: bright, clear, understand
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 明白 (míngbai) — understand, clear
- 聪明 (cōngmíng) — intelligent, clever

## Example Sentences

- 我明白了。(Wǒ míngbai le.) — I understand.

## Notes

Common character for brightness, clarity, or understanding.
