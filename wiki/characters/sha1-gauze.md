# 纱 Gauze

- **Character**: 纱
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: gauze, yarn
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 纱布 (shā bù) — gauze bandage
- 棉纱 (mián shā) — cotton yarn

## Example Sentences

- 护士用纱布包扎了伤口。(Hùshi yòng shā bù bāozā le shāngkǒu.) — The nurse bandaged the wound with gauze.

## Notes

纱 can mean both gauze fabric and yarn/thread. Common in both medical and textile contexts.
