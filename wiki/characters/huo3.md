# 火 Fire

- **Character**: 火
- **Pinyin**: huǒ
- **Meaning**: fire
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 火车 (huǒ chē) — train
- 火锅 (huǒ guō) — hotpot

## Example Sentences

- 小心火！(Xiǎo xīn huǒ!) — Watch out for fire!

## Notes

火 is a pictograph of flames. Its radical form 灬 appears at the bottom of characters like 热 (hot) and 点 (dot/point).
