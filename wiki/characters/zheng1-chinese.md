# 筝 Zheng Zither

- **Character**: 筝
- **Pinyin**: zhēng
- **Meaning**: Chinese zither
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 古筝 (gǔ zhēng) — guzheng
- 筝曲 (zhēng qǔ) — zheng music

## Example Sentences

- 她擅长弹古筝。(Tā shàn cháng tán gǔ zhēng.) — She is skilled at playing the guzheng.

## Notes

筝 is a plucked zither with movable bridges, popular in traditional Chinese music.
