# 垣 Wall

- **Character**: 垣
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: wall, low wall
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 城垣 (chéng yuán) — city wall
- 断垣 (duàn yuán) — broken wall

## Example Sentences

- 古城的城垣已经破败。(Gǔ chéng de chéng yuán yǐ jīng pò bài.) — The ancient city's walls are already dilapidated.

## Notes

垣 has earth 土 with 亘 (to extend), surrounding wall structure.
