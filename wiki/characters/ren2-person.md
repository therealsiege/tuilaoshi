# 人 Person

- **Character**: 人
- **Pinyin**: rén
- **Meaning**: person, people
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 人们 (rén men) — people
- 工人 (gōng rén) — worker

## Example Sentences

- 那个人是谁？(Nà ge rén shì shéi?) — Who is that person?

## Notes

人 is a pictograph of a person standing, viewed from the side.
