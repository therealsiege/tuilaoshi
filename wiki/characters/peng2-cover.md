# 篷 Awning

- **Character**: 篷
- **Pinyin**: péng
- **Meaning**: awning, sail, covering
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 帆篷 (fān péng) — sail
- 篷布 (péng bù) — tarpaulin

## Example Sentences

- 船上有一张大帆篷。(Chuán shàng yǒu yì zhāng dà fān péng.) — There is a large sail on the boat.

## Notes

篷 has bamboo 竹 with 逢 (to meet), bamboo mat covering.
