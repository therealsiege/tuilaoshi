# 队 Team

- **Character**: 队
- **Pinyin**: duì
- **Meaning**: team, group, line
- **Radical**: 阝(mound)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 队伍 (duì wǔ) — troops, ranks
- 球队 (qiú duì) — sports team

## Example Sentences

- 我们队赢了比赛。(Wǒ men duì yíng le bǐ sài.) — Our team won the match.

## Notes

队 is simplified from 隊. Used for military units, sports teams, and groups.
