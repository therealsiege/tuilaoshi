# 慷 Generous, fervent

- **Character**: 慷
- **Pinyin**: kāng
- **Meaning**: generous, fervent
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 慷慨 (kāng kǎi) — generous, vehement
- 慷慨激昂 (kāng kǎi jī áng) — impassioned

## Example Sentences

- 他慷慨解囊帮助贫困学生。(Tā kāng kǎi jiě náng bāng zhù pín kùn xué shēng.) — He generously donated to help impoverished students.

## Notes

Almost always used in the compound 慷慨.
