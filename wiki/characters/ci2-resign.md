# 辞 To Resign

- **Character**: 辞
- **Pinyin**: cí
- **Meaning**: to resign, word, diction
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 辞职 (cí zhí) — to resign
- 辞典 (cí diǎn) — dictionary

## Example Sentences

- 他决定辞职。(Tā jué dìng cí zhí.) — He decided to resign.

## Notes

辞 simplified from 辭. Originally referred to legal speech.
