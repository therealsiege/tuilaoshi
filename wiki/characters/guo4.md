# 过 To Pass

- **Character**: 过
- **Pinyin**: guò
- **Meaning**: to pass, to cross; experiential particle
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 过去 (guò qù) — the past
- 不过 (bú guò) — however

## Example Sentences

- 你去过中国吗？(Nǐ qù guò zhōng guó ma?) — Have you been to China?

## Notes

过 after a verb indicates experience: 吃过 (have eaten before), 去过 (have been to). It differs from 了 which marks completion.
