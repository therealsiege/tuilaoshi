# 辅 Assist, supplement, auxiliary

- **Character**: 辅
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: assist, supplement, auxiliary
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 辅助 (fǔ zhù) — assist, auxiliary
- 辅导 (fǔ dǎo) — tutor, coach

## Example Sentences

- 他需要辅助治疗。(Tā xū yào fǔ zhù zhì liáo.) — He needs auxiliary treatment.
