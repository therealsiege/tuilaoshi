# 岩 Rock

- **Character**: 岩
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: rock, cliff, crag
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 岩石 (yán shí) — rock
- 火山岩 (huǒ shān yán) — volcanic rock

## Example Sentences

- 这里的岩石很坚硬。(Zhè lǐ de yán shí hěn jiān yìng.) — The rocks here are very hard.

## Notes

岩 means rock or cliff, especially in geological context. Used in岩石 (rock), 悬崖峭壁 contexts.
