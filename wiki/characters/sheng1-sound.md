# 声 Sound

- **Character**: 声
- **Pinyin**: shēng
- **Meaning**: sound, voice
- **Radical**: 士 (scholar)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 声音 (shēng yīn) — sound, voice
- 相声 (xiàng shēng) — cross talk

## Example Sentences

- 他的声音很好听。(Tā de shēng yīn hěn hǎo tīng.) — His voice is very pleasant.

## Notes

声 means sound or voice; fundamental character in acoustic and performance terms.
