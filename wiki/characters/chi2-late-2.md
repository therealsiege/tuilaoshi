# 迟 Late, delay

- **Character**: 迟
- **Pinyin**: chí
- **Meaning**: late, delay
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 延迟 (yán chí) — delay, latency
- 迟到 (chí dào) — late

## Example Sentences

- 信号传输有延迟。(Xìn hào chuán shū yǒu yán chí.) — The signal transmission has latency.

## Notes

Used in 延迟 (latency), 迟到 (late), performance issues
