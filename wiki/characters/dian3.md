# 点 Point

- **Character**: 点
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: dot, point, o'clock, a little
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire dots)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 一点 (yī diǎn) — a little
- 几点 (jǐ diǎn) — what time

## Example Sentences

- 现在几点了？(Xiàn zài jǐ diǎn le?) — What time is it now?

## Notes

点 is used for telling time: 三点 (3 o'clock). 一点 means 'a little bit.'
