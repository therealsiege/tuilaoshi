# 蝎 Scorpion

- **Character**: 蝎
- **Pinyin**: xiē
- **Meaning**: scorpion
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蝎子 (xiē zi) — scorpion
- 毒蝎 (dú xiē) — venomous scorpion

## Example Sentences

- 沙漠里有蝎子。(Shāmò lǐ yǒu xiēzi.) — There are scorpions in the desert.

## Notes

Venomous arachnid. Insect radical for arthropod.
