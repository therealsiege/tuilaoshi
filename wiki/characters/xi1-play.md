# 嬉 Play, frolic, amuse

- **Character**: 嬉
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: play, frolic, amuse
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 嬉戏 (xī xì) — frolic, play
- 嬉笑 (xī xiào) — laugh and play

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在公园里嬉戏。(Hái zi men zài gōng yuán lǐ xī xì.) — Children are frolicking in the park.

## Notes

Playful activity and amusement
