# 瞳 Pupil of the eye

- **Character**: 瞳
- **Pinyin**: tóng
- **Meaning**: pupil of the eye
- **Radical**: 目 (eye)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 瞳孔 (tóng kǒng) — pupil (of eye)
- 目不转瞳 (mù bù zhuǎn tóng) — to gaze fixedly

## Example Sentences

- 医生检查了他的瞳孔。(Yīshēng jiǎnchále tā de tóngkǒng.) — The doctor examined his pupils.

## Notes

Anatomical term for the pupil of the eye.
