# 锹 Spade, shovel

- **Character**: 锹
- **Pinyin**: qiāo
- **Meaning**: spade, shovel
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 铁锹 (tiě qiāo) — spade, shovel
- 锹子 (qiāo zi) — spade

## Example Sentences

- 他拿着铁锹挖土。(Tā ná zhe tiě qiāo wā tǔ.) — He digs with a spade.

## Notes

Digging tool with flat blade
