# 宪 Constitution, 宪法 Constitution

- **Character**: 宪
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: constitution, 宪法 constitution
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 宪法 (xiàn fǎ) — constitution
- 违宪 (wéi xiàn) — unconstitutional
- 立宪 (lì xiàn) — establish a constitution

## Example Sentences

- 所有公民都应该遵守宪法。(Suǒyǒu gōngmín dōu yīnggāi zūnshǒu xiànfǎ.) — All citizens should abide by the constitution.

## Notes

Primarily used in legal and political contexts. 宪法 (constitution) is the fundamental law of a nation.
