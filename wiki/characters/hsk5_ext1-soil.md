# 壤 Soil, Earth, 土壤 Soil

- **Character**: 壤
- **Pinyin**: rǎng
- **Meaning**: soil, earth, 土壤 soil
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 土壤 (tǔ rǎng) — soil
- 壤土 (rǎng tǔ) — loam
- 沃壤 (wò rǎng) — fertile soil

## Example Sentences

- 这里的土壤很肥沃。(Zhèlǐ de tǔrǎng hěn féiwò.) — The soil here is very fertile.

## Notes

Technical term for soil and earth. 土壤 is standard term in agriculture and environmental science for describing soil quality.
