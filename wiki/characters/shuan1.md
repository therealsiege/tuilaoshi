# 栓 Plug, stopper, bolt

- **Character**: 栓
- **Pinyin**: shuān
- **Meaning**: plug, stopper, bolt
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 栓塞 (shuān sè) — embolism
- 消火栓 (xiāo huǒ shuān) — fire hydrant

## Example Sentences

- 医生诊断他患有脑血栓。(Yī shēng zhěn duàn tā huàn yǒu nǎo xuè shuān.) — The doctor diagnosed him with a cerebral thrombosis.

## Notes

Means plug, bolt, or stopper. 血栓 (thrombosis) is common in medical contexts.
