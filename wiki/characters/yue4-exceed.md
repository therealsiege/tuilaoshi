# 越 To Exceed

- **Character**: 越
- **Pinyin**: yuè
- **Meaning**: to exceed, Yue (state)
- **Radical**: 走 (run)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 越剧 (yuè jù) — Yue opera
- 超越 (chāo yuè) — to surpass

## Example Sentences

- 越剧是浙江的地方戏。(Yuè jù shì zhè jiāng de dì fāng xì.) — Yue opera is a local opera from Zhejiang.

## Notes

越 means to exceed or Yue state; 越剧 is a melodious opera from Zhejiang.
