# 煤 Coal, 煤炭 Coal

- **Character**: 煤
- **Pinyin**: méi
- **Meaning**: coal, 煤炭 coal
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 煤炭 (méi tàn) — coal
- 煤矿 (méi kuàng) — coal mine
- 煤气 (méi qì) — coal gas

## Example Sentences

- 煤炭是重要的能源。(Méitàn shì zhòngyào de néngyuán.) — Coal is an important energy source.

## Notes

Energy and industrial term for coal. 煤炭 (coal) is major energy resource, though usage declining with environmental concerns.
