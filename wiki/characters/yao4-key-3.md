# 钥 Key

- **Character**: 钥
- **Pinyin**: yào
- **Meaning**: key
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 钥匙 (yào shi) — key
- 密钥 (mì yào) — encryption key

## Example Sentences

- 密钥很重要。(Mì yào hěn zhòng yào.) — The encryption key is important.

## Notes

Used in 钥匙 (key), 密钥 (encryption key), 公钥 (public key)
