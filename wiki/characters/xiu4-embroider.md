# 绣 To Embroider

- **Character**: 绣
- **Pinyin**: xiù
- **Meaning**: to embroider
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 刺绣 (cì xiù) — embroidery
- 绣花 (xiù huā) — to embroider flowers

## Example Sentences

- 这块布上绣着花。(Zhè kuài bù shàng xiù zhe huā.) — Flowers are embroidered on this cloth.

## Notes

绣 combines silk 糹 with 秀 (elegant), representing the elegant decoration created by embroidery.
