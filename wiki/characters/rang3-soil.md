# 壤 Soil, earth, land

- **Character**: 壤
- **Pinyin**: rǎng
- **Meaning**: soil, earth, land
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 土壤 (tǔ rǎng) — soil
- 壤土 (rǎng tǔ) — loam

## Example Sentences

- 这里的土壤很肥沃。(Zhè lǐ de tǔ rǎng hěn féi wò.) — The soil here is very fertile.

## Notes

Specifically refers to soil quality and agricultural land
