# 焦 Anxious

- **Character**: 焦
- **Pinyin**: jiāo
- **Meaning**: anxious, burnt, focal
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 焦虑 (jiāo lǜ) — anxiety
- 焦点 (jiāo diǎn) — focus point

## Example Sentences

- 他最近很焦虑。(Tā zuì jìn hěn jiāo lǜ.) — He has been very anxious recently.

## Notes

焦 depicts birds over fire, suggesting burning anxiety.
