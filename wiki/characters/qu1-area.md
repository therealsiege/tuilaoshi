# 区 District

- **Character**: 区
- **Pinyin**: qū
- **Meaning**: area, district, region
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 地区 (dì qū) — region, area
- 小区 (xiǎo qū) — residential area

## Example Sentences

- 这个地区发展很快。(Zhè ge dì qū fā zhǎn hěn kuài.) — This region is developing rapidly.

## Notes

区 is simplified from 區. Used in administrative divisions and general spatial areas.
