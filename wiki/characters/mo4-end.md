# 末 End

- **Character**: 末
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: end, final, tip
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 周末 (zhōu mò) — weekend
- 年末 (nián mò) — end of year

## Example Sentences

- 周末你有空吗？(Zhōu mò nǐ yǒu kòng ma?) — Are you free this weekend?

## Notes

末 means end or final. 末尾 = end, tail. 期末 = end of term.
