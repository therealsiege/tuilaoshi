# 疟 Malaria

- **Character**: 疟
- **Pinyin**: nüè
- **Meaning**: malaria
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 疟疾 (nüè ji) — malaria
- 打摆子 (dǎ bǎi zi) — to have malaria

## Example Sentences

- 蚊子传播疟疾。(Wén zi chuán bō nüè ji.) — Mosquitoes spread malaria.

## Notes

Parasitic disease; causes fever cycles.
