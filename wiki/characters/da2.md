# 达 To reach, achieve, 達 (traditional)

- **Character**: 达
- **Pinyin**: dá
- **Meaning**: to reach, achieve, 達 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 到达 (dào dá) — to arrive, reach
- 表达 (biǎo dá) — to express

## Example Sentences

- 我们终于到达了目的地。(Wǒmen zhōngyú dàodále mùdìdì.) — We finally reached our destination.

## Notes

To reach, achieve, or express.
