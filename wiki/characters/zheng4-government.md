# 政 Government

- **Character**: 政
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: government, political affairs
- **Radical**: 攵 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 政府 (zhèng fǔ) — government
- 政治 (zhèng zhì) — politics

## Example Sentences

- 政府发布了新的政策。(Zhèng fǔ fā bù le xīn de zhèng cè.) — The government issued new policies.

## Notes

政 combines 正 (correct) and 攵 (to tap/strike), representing correct governance or administration.
