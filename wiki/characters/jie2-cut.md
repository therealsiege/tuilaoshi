# 截 To cut off, to intercept, section

- **Character**: 截
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: to cut off, to intercept, section
- **Radical**: 戈(spear)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 截断 (jié duàn) — to cut off, to sever
- 一截 (yī jié) — a section, a segment

## Example Sentences

- 这条路被洪水截断了。(Zhè tiáo lù bèi hóngshuǐ jiéduàn le.) — This road was cut off by the flood.

## Notes

Used for cutting into sections or intercepting.
