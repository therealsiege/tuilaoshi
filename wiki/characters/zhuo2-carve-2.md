# 琢 To Carve

- **Character**: 琢
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: to carve jade, to polish
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琢磨 (zhuó mó) — to polish, to ponder
- 雕琢 (diāo zhuó) — to carve

## Example Sentences

- 需要仔细琢磨这个问题。(Xū yào zǐ xì zhuó mó zhè ge wèn tí.) — This problem needs careful consideration.

## Notes

琢 has jade 玉 with 豖 (pig), carving and polishing jade or stone.
