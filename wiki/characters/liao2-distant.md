# 辽 Distant, far, vast

- **Character**: 辽
- **Pinyin**: liáo
- **Meaning**: distant, far, vast
- **Radical**: 辶(walk)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 辽阔 (liáo kuò) — vast, extensive
- 辽远 (liáo yuǎn) — distant, faraway

## Example Sentences

- 草原辽阔无边。(Cǎoyuán liáokuò wú biān.) — The grassland is boundlessly vast.

## Notes

Emphasizes vast distance or expanse.
