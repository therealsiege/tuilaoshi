# 但 But

- **Character**: 但
- **Pinyin**: dàn
- **Meaning**: but, however
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 但是 (dàn shì) — but
- 不但 (bú dàn) — not only

## Example Sentences

- 他很聪明，但是很懒。(Tā hěn cōng míng, dàn shì hěn lǎn.) — He is smart, but lazy.

## Notes

但 and 但是 both mean 'but.' 不但...而且... means 'not only...but also...'
