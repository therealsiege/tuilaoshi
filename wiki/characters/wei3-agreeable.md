# 娓 Agreeable, compliant

- **Character**: 娓
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: agreeable, compliant
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 娓娓 (wěiwěi) — tirelessly
- 娓娓动听 (wěiwěi dòngtīng) — fascinating, engaging (of speech)

## Example Sentences

- 他娓娓道来。(Tā wěiwěi dàolái.) — He spoke eloquently.

## Notes

Literary term always used in compound 娓娓 for engaging speech.
