# 催 Urge, hasten, press

- **Character**: 催
- **Pinyin**: cuī
- **Meaning**: urge, hasten, press
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 催促 (cuīcù) — urge, press
- 催款 (cuīkuǎn) — press for payment

## Example Sentences

- 银行催促还款。(Yínháng cuīcù huánkuǎn.) — The bank is pressing for repayment.

## Notes

Common for urging or hastening action.
