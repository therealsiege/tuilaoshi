# 悟 Realize

- **Character**: 悟
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: to comprehend, to realize, enlightenment
- **Radical**: 忄 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恍然大悟 (huǎng rán dà wù) — suddenly realize
- 觉悟 (jué wù) — awareness, consciousness

## Example Sentences

- 经过反思，他终于有所觉悟。(Jīngguò fǎnsī, tā zhōngyú yǒu suǒ juéwù.) — After reflection, he finally gained awareness.

## Notes

Used in 恍然大悟: to comprehend deeply; 悟 is also the name character of Sun Wukong (孙悟空).
