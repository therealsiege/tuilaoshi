# 躁 Manic

- **Character**: 躁
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: restless, manic
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 烦躁 (fán zào) — irritable, restless
- 急躁 (jí zào) — impatient, impetuous
- 狂躁 (kuáng zào) — manic

## Example Sentences

- 双相情感障碍包括躁狂和抑郁。(Shuāng xiàng qíng gǎn zhàng ài bāo kuò zào kuáng hé yì yù.) — Bipolar disorder includes mania and depression.

## Notes

Agitated state; pole of bipolar disorder.
