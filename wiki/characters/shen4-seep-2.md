# 渗 To seep, to ooze, to permeate

- **Character**: 渗
- **Pinyin**: shèn
- **Meaning**: to seep, to ooze, to permeate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渗透 (shèn tòu) — to permeate, to infiltrate
- 渗漏 (shèn lòu) — to seep, to leak

## Example Sentences

- 水慢慢渗入土壤。(Shuǐ mànmàn shènrù tǔrǎng.) — Water gradually seeps into the soil.

## Notes

Describes gradual penetration, especially of liquids.
