# 雪 Snow

- **Character**: 雪
- **Pinyin**: xuě
- **Meaning**: snow
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 下雪 (xià xuě) — to snow
- 雪人 (xuě rén) — snowman

## Example Sentences

- 今天下雪了。(Jīn tiān xià xuě le.) — It snowed today.

## Notes

雪 combines 雨 (rain) and 彐. The rain radical appears in all precipitation-related characters.
