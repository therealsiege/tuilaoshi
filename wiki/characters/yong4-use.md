# 用 To Use

- **Character**: 用
- **Pinyin**: yòng
- **Meaning**: to use, with, usefulness
- **Radical**: 用 (use)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 有用 (yǒu yòng) — useful
- 不用 (bú yòng) — no need

## Example Sentences

- 不用谢。(Bú yòng xiè.) — You're welcome. (No need to thank.)

## Notes

不用 is a common way to say 'no need' or 'you're welcome.' 用 can also mean 'with' (an instrument).
