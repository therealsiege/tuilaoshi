# 悟 Comprehend, realize

- **Character**: 悟
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: comprehend, realize
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 领悟 (lǐngwù) — comprehend, grasp
- 觉悟 (juéwù) — awareness, consciousness

## Example Sentences

- 他终于领悟了这个道理。(Tā zhōngyú lǐngwù le zhège dàolǐ.) — He finally comprehended this principle.

## Notes

Implies sudden understanding or enlightenment, often in philosophical contexts.
