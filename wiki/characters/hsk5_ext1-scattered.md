# 散 Scattered, Loose, 散步 Take A Walk

- **Character**: 散
- **Pinyin**: sǎn
- **Meaning**: scattered, loose, 散步 take a walk
- **Radical**: 攵 (strike)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 散步 (sàn bù) — take a walk
- 分散 (fēn sàn) — disperse, scatter
- 松散 (sōng sǎn) — loose, slack

## Example Sentences

- 晚饭后我喜欢散步。(Wǎnfàn hòu wǒ xǐhuān sànbù.) — I like to take a walk after dinner.

## Notes

Multiple pronunciations: sàn (walk, disperse), sǎn (loose, scattered). 散步 (take a walk) is very common daily activity.
