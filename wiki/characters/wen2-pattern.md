# 纹 Pattern

- **Character**: 纹
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: lines, veins, grain
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 花纹 (huā wén) — decorative pattern
- 条纹 (tiáo wén) — stripe

## Example Sentences

- 这块布有美丽的花纹。(Zhè kuài bù yǒu měi lì de huā wén.) — This cloth has a beautiful pattern.

## Notes

纹 simplified from 紋. Silk 纟 with pattern, decorative lines.
