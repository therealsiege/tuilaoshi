# 拦 To Intercept

- **Character**: 拦
- **Pinyin**: lán
- **Meaning**: to block, to stop, to拦截
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拦住 (lán zhù) — to block
- 拦截 (lán jié) — to intercept

## Example Sentences

- 警察拦住了那辆车。(Jǐng chá lán zhù le nà liàng chē.) — The police stopped that car.

## Notes

拦 is simplified from 攔. Represents blocking or intercepting movement.
