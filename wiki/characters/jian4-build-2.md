# 建 Build, construct

- **Character**: 建
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: build, construct
- **Radical**: 廴 (long stride)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 建设 (jiàn shè) — build, construct
- 建筑 (jiàn zhù) — building, architecture

## Example Sentences

- 这座大楼正在建设中。(Zhè zuò dà lóu zhèng zài jiàn shè zhōng.) — This building is under construction.

## Notes

建造 means construct. 基建 is infrastructure. Core real estate term.
