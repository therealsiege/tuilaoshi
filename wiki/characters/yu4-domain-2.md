# 域 Domain, area

- **Character**: 域
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: domain, area
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 域名 (yù míng) — domain name
- 区域 (qū yù) — region, area

## Example Sentences

- 请输入域名。(Qǐng shū rù yù míng.) — Please enter the domain name.

## Notes

Used in 域名 (domain name), 领域 (field), internet terminology
