# 讲 To Speak

- **Character**: 讲
- **Pinyin**: jiǎng
- **Meaning**: to speak, to explain, to tell
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 讲话 (jiǎng huà) — to speak
- 讲故事 (jiǎng gù shì) — to tell a story

## Example Sentences

- 老师在讲课。(Lǎo shī zài jiǎng kè.) — The teacher is giving a lecture.

## Notes

讲 is more formal than 说. 讲课 = give a lecture, 讲故事 = tell a story, 讲道理 = be reasonable.
