# 种 Kind

- **Character**: 种
- **Pinyin**: zhǒng
- **Meaning**: kind, type, seed
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 种子 (zhǒng zi) — seed (player)
- 种类 (zhǒng lèi) — type, kind

## Example Sentences

- 他是这次比赛的头号种子。(Tā shì zhè cì bǐsài de tóu hào zhǒngzi.) — He is the number one seed in this competition.
