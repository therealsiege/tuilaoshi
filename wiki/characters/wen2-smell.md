# 闻 Smell

- **Character**: 闻
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: smell, hear
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 新闻 (xīnwén) — news
- 闻到 (wén dào) — smell

## Example Sentences

- 我闻到花香了。(Wǒ wén dào huāxiāng le.) — I smell the fragrance of flowers.

## Notes

Dual meaning: smell (wén) and hear. Also means news (新闻). Versatile sensory verb.
