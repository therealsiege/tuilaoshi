# 佑 Protect, bless, assist

- **Character**: 佑
- **Pinyin**: yòu
- **Meaning**: protect, bless, assist
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 保佑 (bǎo yòu) — bless and protect
- 庇佑 (bì yòu) — bless

## Example Sentences

- 上天保佑，平安无事。(Shàng tiān bǎo yòu, píng ān wú shì.) — Heaven bless, all is safe.

## Notes

Divine blessing and protection
