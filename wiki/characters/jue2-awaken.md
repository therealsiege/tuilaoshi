# 觉 To Awaken

- **Character**: 觉
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: to awaken, to perceive
- **Radical**: 见 (see)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 觉悟 (jué wù) — enlightenment
- 自觉 (zì jué) — self-aware

## Example Sentences

- 他的思想觉悟很高。(Tā de sī xiǎng jué wù hěn gāo.) — His ideological awareness is high.

## Notes

觉 simplified from 覺. See 见 with clear perception, to awaken.
