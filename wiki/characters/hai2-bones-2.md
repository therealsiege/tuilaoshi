# 骸 Bones, skeleton

- **Character**: 骸
- **Pinyin**: hái
- **Meaning**: bones, skeleton
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 骸骨 (hái gǔ) — skeleton, remains
- 残骸 (cán hái) — remains, wreckage

## Example Sentences

- 考古学家发现了古代的骸骨。(Kǎogǔxuéjiā fāxiànle gǔdài de háigǔ.) — Archaeologists discovered ancient skeletal remains.

## Notes

Formal/literary term for bones, especially skeletal remains.
