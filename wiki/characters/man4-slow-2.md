# 慢 Slow, arrogant

- **Character**: 慢
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: slow, arrogant
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 慢慢 (mànmàn) — slowly
- 傲慢 (àomàn) — arrogant, haughty

## Example Sentences

- 慢慢走。(Mànmàn zǒu.) — Walk slowly.

## Notes

Common character for slowness or arrogance depending on context.
