# 拟 To Draft

- **Character**: 拟
- **Pinyin**: nǐ
- **Meaning**: to draft, to imitate, to propose
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 拟定 (nǐ dìng) — to draft
- 比拟 (bǐ nǐ) — to compare

## Example Sentences

- 我们正在拟定计划。(Wǒ men zhèng zài nǐ dìng jì huà.) — We are drafting a plan.

## Notes

拟 is simplified from 擬. Represents drafting or formulating proposals.
