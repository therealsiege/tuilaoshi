# 阀 Valve

- **Character**: 阀
- **Pinyin**: fá
- **Meaning**: valve, powerful family
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 阀门 (fá mén) — valve
- 水阀 (shuǐ fá) — water valve

## Example Sentences

- 工人关闭了阀门。(Gōng rén guān bì le fá mén.) — The worker closed the valve.

## Notes

阀 simplified from 閥. Gate 门 with control, regulating flow.
