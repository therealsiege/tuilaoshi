# 烦 Annoyed

- **Character**: 烦
- **Pinyin**: fán
- **Meaning**: annoyed, vexed, to bother
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烦恼 (fán nǎo) — vexed
- 麻烦 (má fán) — trouble

## Example Sentences

- 别烦我。(Bié fán wǒ.) — Don't bother me.

## Notes

烦 simplified from 煩. Shows fire 火 with head, representing a burning irritation in the head.
