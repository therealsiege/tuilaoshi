# 犯 Commit (crime), violate, criminal

- **Character**: 犯
- **Pinyin**: fàn
- **Meaning**: commit (crime), violate, criminal
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 犯罪 (fàn zuì) — to commit a crime
- 罪犯 (zuì fàn) — criminal, offender

## Example Sentences

- 他犯了严重的错误。(Tā fàn le yán zhòng de cuò wù.) — He committed a serious error.

## Notes

Used for violations and crimes. 犯法 means to break the law. 侵犯 means to infringe or violate.
