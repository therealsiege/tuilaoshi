# 官 Official

- **Character**: 官
- **Pinyin**: guān
- **Meaning**: official, government officer
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 官员 (guān yuán) — government official
- 法官 (fǎ guān) — judge

## Example Sentences

- 政府官员参加了会议。(Zhèng fǔ guān yuán cān jiā le huì yì.) — Government officials attended the meeting.

## Notes

官 depicts authority structures under a roof, representing government officials.
