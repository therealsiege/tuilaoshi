# 戏 Drama

- **Character**: 戏
- **Pinyin**: xì
- **Meaning**: play, drama, opera, to play
- **Radical**: 戈 (halberd)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 京剧 (jīng jù) — Peking opera
- 游戏 (yóu xì) — game

## Example Sentences

- 我们去看戏吧。(Wǒ men qù kàn xì ba.) — Let's go watch a play.

## Notes

戏 means drama, play, or opera. Used in看戏 (watch opera), 游戏 (game), 京戏 (Peking opera).
