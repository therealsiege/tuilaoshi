# 蜴 Lizard

- **Character**: 蜴
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: lizard (in 蜥蜴)
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 蜥蜴 (xī yì) — lizard

## Example Sentences

- 蜥蜴能断尾求生。(Xī yì néng duàn wěi qiú shēng.) — Lizards can shed their tails to survive.

## Notes

蜴 has insect 虫 with 易 (easy/change), the shape-changing reptile.
