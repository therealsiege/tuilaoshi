# 渡 Cross, ferry, transit

- **Character**: 渡
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: cross, ferry, transit
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 渡口 (dù kǒu) — ferry crossing
- 渡船 (dù chuán) — ferry boat

## Example Sentences

- 他们乘坐渡船过江。(Tā men chéng zuò dù chuán guò jiāng.) — They crossed the river by ferry.

## Notes

Also used metaphorically for passing through difficult times.
