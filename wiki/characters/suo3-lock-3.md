# 锁 Lock

- **Character**: 锁
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: lock, to lock, to锁定
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 锁门 (suǒ mén) — to lock the door
- 封锁 (fēng suǒ) — blockade

## Example Sentences

- 别忘了锁门。(Bié wàng le suǒ mén.) — Don't forget to lock the door.

## Notes

锁 is simplified from 鎖. The metal radical indicates the metal lock mechanism.
