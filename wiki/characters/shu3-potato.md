# 薯 Potato, yam

- **Character**: 薯
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: potato, yam
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 土豆 (tǔ dòu) — potato
- 红薯 (hóng shǔ) — sweet potato

## Example Sentences

- 烤红薯很甜。(Kǎo hóngshǔ hěn tián.) — Roasted sweet potatoes are very sweet.

## Notes

红薯 (sweet potato) and 马铃薯 (potato) are common. Grass radical for plants.
