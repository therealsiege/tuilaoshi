# 佣 Commission, hire, servant

- **Character**: 佣
- **Pinyin**: yōng
- **Meaning**: commission, hire, servant
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 佣金 (yōng jīn) — commission
- 女佣 (nǚ yōng) — maid, female servant

## Example Sentences

- 销售人员可以获得佣金。(Xiāo shòu rén yuán kě yǐ huò dé yōng jīn.) — Sales personnel can receive commissions.

## Notes

Means commission or hired servant. 佣工 means hired laborer. Common in business contexts.
