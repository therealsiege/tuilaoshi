# 罪 Crime

- **Character**: 罪
- **Pinyin**: zuì
- **Meaning**: crime, guilt, sin
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 犯罪 (fàn zuì) — to commit a crime
- 罪犯 (zuì fàn) — criminal

## Example Sentences

- 犯罪会受到惩罚。(Fàn zuì huì shòu dào chéng fá.) — Committing crimes will be punished.

## Notes

罪 originally 罒 (net) catching 非 (wrong), meaning caught in wrongdoing.
