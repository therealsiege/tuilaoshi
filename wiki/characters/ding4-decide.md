# 定 To Decide

- **Character**: 定
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: to decide, to fix, definite
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 一定 (yī dìng) — definitely
- 决定 (jué dìng) — to decide

## Example Sentences

- 你一定要来！(Nǐ yī dìng yào lái!) — You must come!

## Notes

一定 means 'definitely/must.' 决定 means 'to decide.' 不一定 means 'not necessarily.'
