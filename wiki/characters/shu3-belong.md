# 属 To Belong To

- **Character**: 属
- **Pinyin**: shǔ
- **Meaning**: to belong to, category
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 属于 (shǔ yú) — to belong to
- 金属 (jīn shǔ) — metal

## Example Sentences

- 这本书属于我。(Zhè běn shū shǔ yú wǒ.) — This book belongs to me.

## Notes

属 simplified from 屬. Represents belonging to a category or being attached to something.
