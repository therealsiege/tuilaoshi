# 能 Can

- **Character**: 能
- **Pinyin**: néng
- **Meaning**: can, to be able to
- **Radical**: 厶 (private)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 能力 (néng lì) — ability
- 可能 (kě néng) — possible

## Example Sentences

- 你能帮我吗？(Nǐ néng bāng wǒ ma?) — Can you help me?

## Notes

能 indicates innate ability or circumstantial possibility, while 会 indicates learned skill.
