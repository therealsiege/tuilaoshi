# 卵 Egg, ovum, spawn

- **Character**: 卵
- **Pinyin**: luǎn
- **Meaning**: egg, ovum, spawn
- **Radical**: 卵 (egg)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 卵子 (luǎn zǐ) — ovum, egg cell
- 产卵 (chǎn luǎn) — lay eggs, spawn

## Example Sentences

- 鱼类在水中产卵。(Yú lèi zài shuǐ zhōng chǎn luǎn.) — Fish lay eggs in water.
