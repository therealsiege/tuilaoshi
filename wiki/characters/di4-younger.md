# 弟 Younger Brother

- **Character**: 弟
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: younger brother
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 弟弟 (dì di) — younger brother
- 兄弟 (xiōng dì) — brothers

## Example Sentences

- 我弟弟十岁。(Wǒ dì di shí suì.) — My younger brother is ten years old.

## Notes

弟 reduplicates to 弟弟. 兄弟 means brothers or close male friends.
