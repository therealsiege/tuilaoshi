# 蛙 Frog

- **Character**: 蛙
- **Pinyin**: wā
- **Meaning**: frog
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 井底之蛙 (jǐng dǐ zhī wā) — narrow-minded person
- 青蛙 (qīng wā) — frog

## Example Sentences

- 池塘里有很多青蛙。(Chítáng lǐ yǒu hěn duō qīngwā.) — There are many frogs in the pond.

## Notes

Used in 井底之蛙: the frog with a limited worldview; uses the insect radical 虫.
