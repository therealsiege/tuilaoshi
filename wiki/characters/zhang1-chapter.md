# 章 Chapter

- **Character**: 章
- **Pinyin**: zhāng
- **Meaning**: chapter, badge, seal, rules
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 文章 (wén zhāng) — article, essay
- 规章 (guī zhāng) — rules, regulations

## Example Sentences

- 这篇文章写得很好。(Zhè piān wén zhāng xiě de hěn hǎo.) — This article is written very well.

## Notes

章 combines music (音) and marking (十), representing sections or markings.
