# 荡 To Swing

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: to swing, to sway, to wash away
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 摇荡 (yáo dàng) — to sway
- 荡秋千 (dàng qiū qiān) — to swing on a swing

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在荡秋千。(Hái zi men zài dàng qiū qiān.) — The children are swinging.

## Notes

荡 simplified from 蕩. Grass radical 艹 with水 elements, representing sweeping or swaying motion.
