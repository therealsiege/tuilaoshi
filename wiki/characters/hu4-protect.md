# 护 To protect, guard, 護 (traditional)

- **Character**: 护
- **Pinyin**: hù
- **Meaning**: to protect, guard, 護 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 保护 (bǎo hù) — to protect
- 护士 (hù shi) — nurse

## Example Sentences

- 护士正在照顾病人。(Hùshi zhèngzài zhàogù bìngrén.) — The nurse is taking care of the patient.

## Notes

Protection, care, and nursing.
