# 谅 Forgive, understand, presume

- **Character**: 谅
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: forgive, understand, presume
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谅解 (liàng jiě) — to understand, forgive
- 原谅 (yuán liàng) — to forgive

## Example Sentences

- 请原谅我的过失。(Qǐng yuán liàng wǒ de guò shī.) — Please forgive my mistakes.

## Notes

Used for understanding and forgiveness. 体谅 means to make allowances for. 见谅 means to forgive.
