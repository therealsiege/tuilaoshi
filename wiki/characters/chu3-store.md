# 储 To store, to save, to reserve

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: to store, to save, to reserve
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 储存 (chǔ cún) — to store, to deposit
- 储蓄 (chǔ xù) — to save, savings

## Example Sentences

- 银行里储存着大量现金。(Yínháng lǐ chǔcún zhe dàliàng xiànjīn.) — The bank stores a large amount of cash.

## Notes

Formal term for storing or saving, often financial.
