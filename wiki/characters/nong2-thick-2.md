# 浓 Thick, dense, concentrated

- **Character**: 浓
- **Pinyin**: nóng
- **Meaning**: thick, dense, concentrated
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 浓厚 (nóng hòu) — thick, dense, strong
- 浓度 (nóng dù) — concentration, density

## Example Sentences

- 房间里弥漫着浓浓的咖啡香。(Fáng jiān lǐ mí màn zhe nóng nóng de kā fēi xiāng.) — The room is filled with a rich coffee aroma.

## Notes

Describes thickness, density, or intensity.
