# 层 Layer, story, stratum

- **Character**: 层
- **Pinyin**: céng
- **Meaning**: layer, story, stratum
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 地层 (dì céng) — stratum, geological layer
- 大气层 (dà qì céng) — atmosphere

## Example Sentences

- 大气层保护地球免受太阳辐射。(Dà qì céng bǎo hù dì qiú miǎn shòu tài yáng fú shè.) — The atmosphere protects Earth from solar radiation.

## Notes

Used for layers in geology and atmosphere
