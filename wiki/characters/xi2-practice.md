# 习 To Practice

- **Character**: 习
- **Pinyin**: xí
- **Meaning**: to practice, to study
- **Radical**: 习 (practice)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 学习 (xué xí) — to study
- 练习 (liàn xí) — to practice

## Example Sentences

- 学习需要不断练习。(Xué xí xū yào bú duàn liàn xí.) — Study requires constant practice.

## Notes

习 simplified from 習. Shows a bird repeatedly trying to fly, representing practice and repetition.
