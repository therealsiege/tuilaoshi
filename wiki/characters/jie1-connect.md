# 接 To Connect

- **Character**: 接
- **Pinyin**: jiē
- **Meaning**: to connect, to receive
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 接口 (jiē kǒu) — interface, port
- 连接 (lián jiē) — to connect

## Example Sentences

- 这个接口不兼容。(Zhè ge jiē kǒu bù jiān róng.) — This interface is not compatible.

## Notes

接 suggests hands joining things together, fundamental to connection terminology.
