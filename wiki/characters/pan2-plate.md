# 盘 Plate, disk, coil

- **Character**: 盘
- **Pinyin**: pán
- **Meaning**: plate, disk, coil
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 磁盘 (cí pán) — disk, disc
- 盘旋 (pán xuán) — spiral, circle

## Example Sentences

- 硬盘存储了大量数据。(Yìng pán cún chǔ le dà liàng shù jù.) — The hard disk stores a large amount of data.

## Notes

Circular or flat components
