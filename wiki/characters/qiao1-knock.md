# 敲 To Knock

- **Character**: 敲
- **Pinyin**: qiāo
- **Meaning**: to knock, to tap
- **Radical**: 攴 (tap)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 敲门 (qiāo mén) — to knock on door
- 敲打 (qiāo dǎ) — to beat, to knock

## Example Sentences

- 有人在敲门。(Yǒu rén zài qiāo mén.) — Someone is knocking on the door.

## Notes

敲 combines 高 (high) and 攴 (strike), representing striking or tapping.
