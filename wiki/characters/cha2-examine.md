# 察 To Examine

- **Character**: 察
- **Pinyin**: chá
- **Meaning**: to examine, to observe, to inspect
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 观察 (guān chá) — to observe
- 考察 (kǎo chá) — to inspect

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要仔细观察。(Wǒ men xū yào zǐ xì guān chá.) — We need to observe carefully.

## Notes

察 shows roof 宀 over sacrificial altar 祭, representing careful ritual examination, hence to observe closely.
