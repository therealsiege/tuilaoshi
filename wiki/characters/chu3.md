# 储 To Store

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: to store, to save
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 储存 (chǔ cún) — to store
- 储蓄 (chǔ xù) — to save (money)

## Example Sentences

- 我每月都会储蓄一些钱。(Wǒ měi yuè dōu huì chǔ xù yī xiē qián.) — I save some money every month.

## Notes

储 simplified from 儲. Person 亻 representing accumulation for future use.
