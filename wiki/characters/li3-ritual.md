# 礼 Ritual, propriety, ceremony

- **Character**: 礼
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: ritual, propriety, ceremony
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 礼貌 (lǐ mào) — courtesy, manners
- 礼仪 (lǐ yí) — etiquette, protocol

## Example Sentences

- 古代社会非常重视礼制。(Gǔdài shèhuì fēicháng zhòngshì lǐ zhì.) — Ancient society placed great emphasis on ritual systems.

## Notes

Refers to proper conduct, rituals, and social norms. Essential concept in Confucian philosophy governing social relationships.
