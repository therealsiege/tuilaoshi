# 懒 Lazy, 懒惰 Lazy

- **Character**: 懒
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: lazy, 懒惰 lazy
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 懒惰 (lǎn duò) — lazy
- 偷懒 (tōu lǎn) — loaf, goof off
- 懒散 (lǎn sǎn) — lazy, sluggish

## Example Sentences

- 别偷懒，快点干活。(Bié tōulǎn, kuài diǎn gànhuó.) — Don't slack off, get to work quickly.

## Notes

Common colloquial term for laziness. 偷懒 (slack off) is everyday expression. 懒得 means 'too lazy to' or 'can't be bothered'.
