# 委 Committee

- **Character**: 委
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: to entrust, committee, indirect
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 委员 (wěi yuán) — committee member
- 委托 (wěi tuō) — to entrust

## Example Sentences

- 他是委员会成员。(Tā shì wěi yuán huì chéng yuán.) — He is a committee member.

## Notes

委 combines grain/plant and woman, suggesting delegation or yielding.
