# 级 Level

- **Character**: 级
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: level, grade, step
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 三级跳 (sān jí tiào) — triple jump
- 级别 (jí bié) — level, rank

## Example Sentences

- 这是国家级比赛。(Zhè shì guójiā jí bǐsài.) — This is a national-level competition.

## Notes

Level and grade; competition tiers
