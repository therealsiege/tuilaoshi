# 快 Fast

- **Character**: 快
- **Pinyin**: kuài
- **Meaning**: fast, quick, happy
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 快乐 (kuài lè) — happy
- 快板 (kuài bǎn) — clapper talk

## Example Sentences

- 快板是一种说唱艺术。(Kuài bǎn shì yī zhǒng shuō chàng yì shù.) — Kuaiban is a form of narrative rap art.

## Notes

快 means fast or happy; 快板 is a rhythmic storytelling with clappers.
