# 估 To Estimate

- **Character**: 估
- **Pinyin**: gū
- **Meaning**: to estimate, to appraise
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 估计 (gū jì) — to estimate
- 评估 (píng gū) — to assess

## Example Sentences

- 我估计他不会来了。(Wǒ gū jì tā bú huì lái le.) — I estimate he won't come.

## Notes

估 shows person 亻 and 古 (ancient/old), representing a person's judgment based on past experience.
