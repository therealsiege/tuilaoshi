# 鳃 Gill

- **Character**: 鳃
- **Pinyin**: sāi
- **Meaning**: gill (of a fish)
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 鱼鳃 (yú sāi) — fish gill
- 鳃盖 (sāi gài) — gill cover

## Example Sentences

- 鱼通过鳃呼吸。(Yú tōng guò sāi hū xī.) — Fish breathe through gills.

## Notes

鳃 simplified from 鰓. Fish 鱼 with 思 (thought), the breathing organ.
