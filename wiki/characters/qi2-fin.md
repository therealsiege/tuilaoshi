# 鳍 Fin

- **Character**: 鳍
- **Pinyin**: qí
- **Meaning**: fin (of a fish)
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 鱼鳍 (yú qí) — fish fin
- 背鳍 (bèi qí) — dorsal fin

## Example Sentences

- 鲨鱼的背鳍露出水面。(Shā yú de bèi qí lù chū shuǐ miàn.) — The shark's dorsal fin poked above the surface.

## Notes

鳍 simplified from 鰭. Fish 鱼 with 耆 (old/senior).
