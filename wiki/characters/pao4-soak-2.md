# 泡 Soak

- **Character**: 泡
- **Pinyin**: pào
- **Meaning**: soak, bubble
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 泡茶 (pào chá) — to make tea
- 浸泡 (jìn pào) — to soak, immerse
- 水泡 (shuǐ pào) — blister, bubble

## Example Sentences

- 中药需要泡水后服用。(Zhōng yào xū yào pào shuǐ hòu fú yòng.) — Chinese herbs need to be soaked in water before taking.

## Notes

Soaking preparation; herbs or medication.
