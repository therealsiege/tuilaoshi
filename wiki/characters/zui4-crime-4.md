# 罪 Crime, guilt, sin

- **Character**: 罪
- **Pinyin**: zuì
- **Meaning**: crime, guilt, sin
- **Radical**: 罒 (net)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 犯罪 (fànzuì) — commit a crime
- 定罪 (dìngzuì) — convict

## Example Sentences

- 他犯了严重的罪。(Tā fàn le yánzhòng de zuì.) — He committed a serious crime.

## Notes

Legal term for crime, guilt, and sin.
