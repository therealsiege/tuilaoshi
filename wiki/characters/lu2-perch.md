# 鲈 Perch, bass

- **Character**: 鲈
- **Pinyin**: lú
- **Meaning**: perch, bass
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鲈鱼 (lú yú) — perch, sea bass
- 清蒸鲈鱼 (qīng zhēng lú yú) — steamed sea bass

## Example Sentences

- 清蒸鲈鱼很美味。(Qīngzhēng lúyú hěn měiwèi.) — Steamed sea bass is delicious.

## Notes

Popular fish for steaming. Fish radical indicates type.
