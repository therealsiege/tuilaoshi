# 髓 Marrow

- **Character**: 髓
- **Pinyin**: suǐ
- **Meaning**: marrow
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 骨髓 (gǔ suǐ) — bone marrow
- 脊髓 (jǐ suǐ) — spinal cord
- 髓鞘 (suǐ qiào) — myelin sheath

## Example Sentences

- 他需要骨髓移植。(Tā xū yào gǔ suǐ yí zhí.) — He needs a bone marrow transplant.

## Notes

Produces blood cells; critical in hematology and oncology.
