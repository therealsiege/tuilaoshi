# 膜 Membrane

- **Character**: 膜
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: membrane, film
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 薄膜 (báo mó) — membrane, thin film
- 细胞膜 (xì bāo mó) — cell membrane

## Example Sentences

- 细胞膜控制物质进出。(Xì bāo mó kòng zhì wù zhì jìn chū.) — The cell membrane controls the passage of substances.

## Notes

膜 uses meat radical 月, referring to biological membranes or thin protective layers.
