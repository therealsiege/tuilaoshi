# 头 Head

- **Character**: 头
- **Pinyin**: tóu
- **Meaning**: head, top, first
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 头发 (tóu fà) — hair
- 石头 (shí tou) — stone

## Example Sentences

- 我头疼。(Wǒ tóu téng.) — I have a headache.

## Notes

头 is simplified from 頭. As a suffix (neutral tone) it forms nouns: 石头 (stone), 木头 (wood).
