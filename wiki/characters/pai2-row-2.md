# 排 Row

- **Character**: 排
- **Pinyin**: pái
- **Meaning**: row, to arrange
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 排列 (pái liè) — to arrange
- 排律 (pái lǜ) — extended regulated verse

## Example Sentences

- 排律是超过八句的律诗。(Pái lǜ shì chāo guò bā jù de lǜ shī.) — Pailu is regulated verse exceeding eight lines.

## Notes

排 means row or to arrange; 排律 is extended regulated verse.
