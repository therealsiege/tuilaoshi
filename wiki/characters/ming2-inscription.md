# 铭 Inscription, engrave, remember

- **Character**: 铭
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: inscription, engrave, remember
- **Radical**: 钅 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铭记 (míng jì) — remember, bear in mind
- 铭文 (míng wén) — inscription

## Example Sentences

- 我将永远铭记您的恩情。(Wǒ jiāng yǒng yuǎn míng jì nín de ēn qíng.) — I will forever remember your kindness.

## Notes

Inscriptions on metal or stone, also means to remember deeply
