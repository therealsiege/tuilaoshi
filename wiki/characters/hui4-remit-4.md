# 汇 Remit, converge

- **Character**: 汇
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: remit, converge
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汇款 (huì kuǎn) — remit money
- 外汇 (wài huì) — foreign exchange

## Example Sentences

- 我给家里汇了一千块钱。(Wǒ gěi jiā lǐ huì le yī qiān kuài qián.) — I remitted a thousand yuan to my family.

## Notes

汇率 is exchange rate. 汇总 means summarize/consolidate.
