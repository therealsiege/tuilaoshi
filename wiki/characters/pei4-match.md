# 配 To match, to pair, to coordinate, spouse

- **Character**: 配
- **Pinyin**: pèi
- **Meaning**: to match, to pair, to配合, spouse
- **Radical**: 酉(wine)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 配合 (pèi hé) — to cooperate, to coordinate
- 搭配 (dā pèi) — to match, to pair up

## Example Sentences

- 这两种颜色很配。(Zhè liǎng zhǒng yánsè hěn pèi.) — These two colors match well.

## Notes

Versatile character for matching or pairing.
