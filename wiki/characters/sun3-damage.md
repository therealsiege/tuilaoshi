# 损 To Damage

- **Character**: 损
- **Pinyin**: sǔn
- **Meaning**: to damage, to harm, to lose
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 损失 (sǔn shī) — loss
- 损坏 (sǔn huài) — to damage

## Example Sentences

- 这次事故造成了很大的损失。(Zhè cì shì gù zào chéng le hěn dà de sǔn shī.) — This accident caused great losses.

## Notes

损 indicates harm or loss, opposite of 益 (benefit). Common in discussing damage and losses.
