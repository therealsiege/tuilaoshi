# 症 Disease, illness, symptom

- **Character**: 症
- **Pinyin**: zhèng
- **Meaning**: disease, illness, symptom
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 症状 (zhèng zhuàng) — symptom
- 病症 (bìng zhèng) — disease, illness

## Example Sentences

- 发烧是感冒的常见症状。(Fā shāo shì gǎn mào de cháng jiàn zhèng zhuàng.) — Fever is a common symptom of a cold.

## Notes

Used for symptoms and medical conditions
