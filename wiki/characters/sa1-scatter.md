# 撒 To Scatter

- **Character**: 撒
- **Pinyin**: sā
- **Meaning**: to scatter, to let go
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 撒谎 (sā huǎng) — to lie
- 播撒 (bō sā) — to sow

## Example Sentences

- 不要撒谎。(Bú yào sā huǎng.) — Don't lie.

## Notes

撒 hand 扌 plus 散 (to scatter), releasing widely.
