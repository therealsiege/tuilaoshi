# 帐 Tent

- **Character**: 帐
- **Pinyin**: zhàng
- **Meaning**: tent, curtain, account
- **Radical**: 巾 (cloth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 帐篷 (zhàng péng) — tent
- 蚊帐 (wén zhàng) — mosquito net

## Example Sentences

- 他们在野外搭帐篷。(Tā men zài yě wài dā zhàng péng.) — They pitch tents in the wilderness.

## Notes

帐 simplified from 帳. Cloth 巾 plus covering, tent or curtain.
