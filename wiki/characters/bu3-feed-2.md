# 哺 Feed, nurse, suckle

- **Character**: 哺
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: feed, nurse, suckle
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哺乳 (bǔ rǔ) — breastfeed, nurse
- 哺育 (bǔ yù) — feed, nurture

## Example Sentences

- 哺乳动物用乳汁喂养后代。(Bǔ rǔ dòng wù yòng rǔ zhī wèi yǎng hòu dài.) — Mammals feed their offspring with milk.
