# 适 Suitable, comfortable, just

- **Character**: 适
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: suitable, comfortable, just
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 适合 (shì hé) — suitable
- 舒适 (shū shì) — comfortable

## Example Sentences

- 这个环境很舒适。(Zhè gè huán jìng hěn shū shì.) — This environment is very comfortable.
