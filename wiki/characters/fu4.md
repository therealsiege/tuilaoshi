# 富 Rich

- **Character**: 富
- **Pinyin**: fù
- **Meaning**: rich, wealthy, abundant
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 富裕 (fù yù) — prosperous
- 丰富 (fēng fù) — rich, abundant

## Example Sentences

- 这个国家很富裕。(Zhè gè guó jiā hěn fù yù.) — This country is very prosperous.

## Notes

富 shows a roof 宀 with fields 田 beneath, representing a household with abundant land and wealth.
