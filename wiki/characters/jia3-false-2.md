# 假 False

- **Character**: 假
- **Pinyin**: jiǎ
- **Meaning**: false, fake, to borrow
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 狐假虎威 (hú jiǎ hǔ wēi) — bully using another's power
- 假装 (jiǎ zhuāng) — to pretend

## Example Sentences

- 这个消息是假的。(Zhège xiāoxi shì jiǎ de.) — This news is fake.

## Notes

In 狐假虎威, 假 means to borrow/exploit; also read as jià meaning vacation.
