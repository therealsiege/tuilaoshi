# 织 Weave

- **Character**: 织
- **Pinyin**: zhī
- **Meaning**: weave, knit
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 编织 (biān zhī) — weave, knit
- 组织 (zǔ zhī) — organize, organization

## Example Sentences

- 奶奶正在织毛衣。(Nǎinai zhèngzài zhī máoyī.) — Grandma is knitting a sweater.

## Notes

织 means to weave or knit. Can refer to making fabric or the metaphorical 'weaving' of organizing things.
