# 悠 Long, Leisurely, 悠久 Long

- **Character**: 悠
- **Pinyin**: yōu
- **Meaning**: long, leisurely, 悠久 long
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 悠久 (yōu jiǔ) — long, age-old
- 悠闲 (yōu xián) — leisurely, carefree
- 悠扬 (yōu yáng) — melodious

## Example Sentences

- 中国有悠久的历史。(Zhōngguó yǒu yōujiǔ de lìshǐ.) — China has a long history.

## Notes

Literary term for long duration (悠久) and leisurely pace (悠闲). 悠扬 describes flowing, melodious sounds.
