# 销 Sell, cancel, melt

- **Character**: 销
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: sell, cancel, melt
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 销售 (xiāoshòu) — sell, market
- 畅销 (chàngxiāo) — sell well, be in great demand

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很畅销。(Zhè běn shū hěn chàngxiāo.) — This book sells very well.

## Notes

Used for selling and marketing; also means to cancel or melt.
