# 悯 To Pity

- **Character**: 悯
- **Pinyin**: mǐn
- **Meaning**: to pity, to sympathize
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 怜悯 (lián mǐn) — to pity
- 同情悯 (tóng qíng mǐn) — compassion

## Example Sentences

- 他怜悯地看着乞丐。(Tā lián mǐn de kàn zhe qǐ gài.) — He looked at the beggar with pity.

## Notes

悯 simplified from 憫. Heart 忄 with 闵 (pity), feeling compassion.
