# 锁 Lock

- **Character**: 锁
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: lock, to lock
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 门锁 (mén suǒ) — door lock
- 解锁 (jiě suǒ) — to unlock

## Example Sentences

- 请锁门。(Qǐng suǒ mén.) — Please lock the door.

## Notes

锁 with metal radical for lock, now used for both physical and digital locking mechanisms.
