# 助 Help, assist

- **Character**: 助
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: help, assist
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 帮助 (bāngzhù) — help, assist
- 资助 (zīzhù) — subsidize, support financially

## Example Sentences

- 基金会资助贫困学生。(Jījīnhuì zīzhù pínkùn xuésheng.) — The foundation financially supports poor students.

## Notes

Common character for help and assistance.
