# 卒 Soldier, pawn, die

- **Character**: 卒
- **Pinyin**: zú
- **Meaning**: soldier, pawn, die
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 士卒 (shì zú) — soldiers
- 小卒 (xiǎo zú) — pawn, foot soldier

## Example Sentences

- 兵来将挡，水来土掩。(Bīng lái jiàng dǎng, shuǐ lái tǔ yǎn.) — Meet soldiers with generals, meet water with earth - have a strategy for everything.

## Notes

Common soldier or pawn. Also means to die or finish. In Chinese chess, the pawn piece. Reflects the expendable nature of foot soldiers.
