# 冰 Ice

- **Character**: 冰
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: ice, to freeze
- **Radical**: 冫(ice)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 冰箱 (bīng xiāng) — refrigerator
- 冰水 (bīng shuǐ) — ice water

## Example Sentences

- 水变成冰了。(Shuǐ biàn chéng bīng le.) — The water has turned to ice.

## Notes

冰 with ice radical 冫. 冰箱 = refrigerator (ice box). 冰淇淋 = ice cream.
