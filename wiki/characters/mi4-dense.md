# 密 Dense, close, secret

- **Character**: 密
- **Pinyin**: mì
- **Meaning**: dense, close, secret
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 密集 (mì jí) — dense, concentrated
- 茂密 (mào mì) — dense, thick

## Example Sentences

- 森林茂密难以穿行。(Sēn lín mào mì nán yǐ chuān xíng.) — The forest is so dense it's hard to pass through.

## Notes

High concentration and thickness
