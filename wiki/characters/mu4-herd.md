# 牧 Herd, pasture, tend livestock

- **Character**: 牧
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: herd, pasture, tend livestock
- **Radical**: 牛 (cow)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 牧场 (mù chǎng) — pasture, ranch
- 游牧 (yóu mù) — nomadic

## Example Sentences

- 牧民在草原上放牧羊群。(Mù mín zài cǎo yuán shàng fàng mù yáng qún.) — Herders graze their flocks on the grassland.

## Notes

Related to herding and pastoral life. 牧师 means pastor or minister in Christianity.
