# 子 Child

- **Character**: 子
- **Pinyin**: zǐ
- **Meaning**: child, seed, suffix
- **Radical**: 子 (child)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 孩子 (hái zi) — child
- 儿子 (ér zi) — son

## Example Sentences

- 这个孩子很聪明。(Zhè ge hái zi hěn cōng míng.) — This child is very smart.

## Notes

子 is a pictograph of a baby. As a suffix (neutral tone zi) it forms many nouns: 桌子 (table), 椅子 (chair).
