# 说 To Speak/Say

- **Character**: 说
- **Pinyin**: shuō
- **Meaning**: to speak, to say
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 说话 (shuōhuà) — to speak, to talk
- 小说 (xiǎoshuō) — novel, fiction
- 说明 (shuōmíng) — to explain, explanation
- 听说 (tīngshuō) — to hear it said, reportedly
- 说服 (shuōfú) — to persuade, to convince

## Example Sentences

- 你说什么？(Nǐ shuō shénme?) — What did you say?
- 他说他明天来。(Tā shuō tā míngtiān lái.) — He said he will come tomorrow.
- 请你慢慢说。(Qǐng nǐ mànman shuō.) — Please speak slowly.

## Notes

说 is the simplified form of 說. The left component 讠 (speech radical) signals the meaning domain, while the right side 兑 (duì) provides a phonetic hint. This is one of the most essential communication verbs in Chinese, appearing in countless everyday expressions.
