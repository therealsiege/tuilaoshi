# 评 评价, comment, evaluate

- **Character**: 评
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: 评价, comment, evaluate
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 评价 (píng jià) — 评价, assessment
- 批评 (pī píng) — criticize

## Example Sentences

- 对员工进行绩效评价。(Duì yuán gōng jìn xíng jì xiào píng jià.) — Conduct performance evaluations on employees.

## Notes

评估 means assess. 评论 means comment/review.
