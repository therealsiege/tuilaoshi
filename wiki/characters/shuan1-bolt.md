# 栓 Bolt

- **Character**: 栓
- **Pinyin**: shuān
- **Meaning**: bolt, plug
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 消防栓 (xiāo fáng shuān) — fire hydrant
- 螺栓 (luó shuān) — bolt

## Example Sentences

- 拧紧这个螺栓。(Níng jǐn zhè ge luó shuān.) — Tighten this bolt.

## Notes

栓 with wood radical originally meant wooden pin, now for bolts and plugs.
