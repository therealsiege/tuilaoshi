# 猫 Cat

- **Character**: 猫
- **Pinyin**: māo
- **Meaning**: cat
- **Radical**: 犭(animal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 小猫 (xiǎo māo) — kitten
- 猫咪 (māo mī) — kitty

## Example Sentences

- 我有一只猫。(Wǒ yǒu yī zhī māo.) — I have a cat.

## Notes

猫 uses the animal radical 犭. The measure word for cats is 只 (zhī).
