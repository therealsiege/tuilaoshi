# 雷 Thunder

- **Character**: 雷
- **Pinyin**: léi
- **Meaning**: thunder, mine (explosive)
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 雷雨 (léi yǔ) — thunderstorm
- 打雷 (dǎ léi) — to thunder

## Example Sentences

- 外面在打雷。(Wài miàn zài dǎ léi.) — There's thunder outside.

## Notes

雷 combines rain (雨) with fields (田), representing thunder during rainstorms over farmland. Verb form uses 打雷 (literally 'strike thunder').
