# 翠 Emerald

- **Character**: 翠
- **Pinyin**: cuì
- **Meaning**: emerald green, kingfisher
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 翡翠 (fěi cuì) — jade, emerald
- 青翠 (qīng cuì) — verdant

## Example Sentences

- 山上青翠欲滴。(Shān shàng qīng cuì yù dī.) — The mountain is verdantly green.

## Notes

翠 describes the brilliant green color of kingfisher feathers, used for jade and lush vegetation.
