# 坯 Blank, billet

- **Character**: 坯
- **Pinyin**: pī
- **Meaning**: blank, billet
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 坯料 (pī liào) — blank material
- 毛坯 (máo pī) — rough blank

## Example Sentences

- 毛坯需要加工。(Máo pī xū yào jiā gōng.) — The rough blank needs processing.

## Notes

Used in 坯料 (blank), 毛坯 (rough blank), manufacturing
