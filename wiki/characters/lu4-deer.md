# 鹿 Deer

- **Character**: 鹿
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: deer
- **Radical**: 鹿 (deer)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 麋鹿 (mí lù) — elk
- 梅花鹿 (méi huā lù) — sika deer

## Example Sentences

- 森林里有野鹿。(Sēn lín lǐ yǒu yě lù.) — There are wild deer in the forest.

## Notes

鹿 is a pictograph showing a deer with antlers, body, and legs.
