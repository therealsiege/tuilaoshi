# 胜 Victory

- **Character**: 胜
- **Pinyin**: shèng
- **Meaning**: victory, to win
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 胜利 (shèng lì) — victory, to win
- 战胜 (zhàn shèng) — to defeat, to overcome

## Example Sentences

- 最后我们队取得了胜利。(Zuìhòu wǒmen duì qǔdé le shènglì.) — In the end our team achieved victory.

## Notes

More formal than 赢; used in victory, triumph, and winning contexts
