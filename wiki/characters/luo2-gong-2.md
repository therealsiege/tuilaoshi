# 锣 Gong

- **Character**: 锣
- **Pinyin**: luó
- **Meaning**: gong
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 敲锣 (qiāo luó) — beat a gong
- 锣鼓 (luó gǔ) — gongs and drums

## Example Sentences

- 锣鼓声响起，表演开始了。(Luógǔ shēng xiǎng qǐ, biǎoyǎn kāishǐ le.) — The gongs and drums sounded, and the performance began.

## Notes

锣 is a gong, a metal percussion instrument common in Chinese music and opera.
