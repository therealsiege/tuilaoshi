# 捧 To Hold In Both Hands

- **Character**: 捧
- **Pinyin**: pěng
- **Meaning**: to hold in both hands
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 捧着 (pěng zhe) — holding with hands
- 吹捧 (chuī pěng) — to flatter

## Example Sentences

- 她捧着一束花。(Tā pěng zhe yí shù huā.) — She holds a bouquet of flowers.

## Notes

捧 combines hand 扌 and 奉 (offer), representing holding respectfully with both hands.
