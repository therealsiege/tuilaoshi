# 箭 Arrow

- **Character**: 箭
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: arrow
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 弓箭 (gōng jiàn) — bow and arrow
- 火箭 (huǒ jiàn) — rocket

## Example Sentences

- 他会射箭。(Tā huì shè jiàn.) — He can shoot arrows.

## Notes

箭 uses bamboo radical because arrows were traditionally made from bamboo. 火箭 (rocket) literally means 'fire arrow'.
