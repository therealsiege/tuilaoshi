# 慨 Indignant

- **Character**: 慨
- **Pinyin**: kǎi
- **Meaning**: indignant, generous
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 感慨 (gǎn kǎi) — to sigh with emotion
- 慷慨 (kāng kǎi) — generous

## Example Sentences

- 他感慨万千。(Tā gǎn kǎi wàn qiān.) — He was filled with deep emotion.

## Notes

慨 has heart 忄 with 既 (already), heart stirred by reflection.
