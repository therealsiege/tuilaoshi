# 长 Long

- **Character**: 长
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: long, length; to grow (zhǎng)
- **Radical**: 长 (long)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 长城 (cháng chéng) — Great Wall
- 长大 (zhǎng dà) — to grow up

## Example Sentences

- 这条路很长。(Zhè tiáo lù hěn cháng.) — This road is very long.

## Notes

长 has two readings: cháng (long) and zhǎng (to grow, chief/head). 长大 (grow up) uses zhǎng.
