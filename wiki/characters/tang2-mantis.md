# 螳 Mantis

- **Character**: 螳
- **Pinyin**: táng
- **Meaning**: mantis
- **Radical**: 虫 (insect)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 螳螂 (táng láng) — mantis
- 螳臂当车 (táng bì dāng chē) — mantis trying to stop a chariot

## Example Sentences

- 螳螂捕蝉黄雀在后。(Táng láng bǔ chán huáng què zài hòu.) — The mantis stalks the cicada, unaware of the oriole behind.

## Notes

Used in idioms. Always paired with 螂. Insect radical.
