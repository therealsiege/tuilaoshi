# 婀 Graceful, lithe

- **Character**: 婀
- **Pinyin**: ē
- **Meaning**: graceful, lithe
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 婀娜 (ē nuó) — graceful, lithe
- 婀娜多姿 (ē nuó duō zī) — gracefully poised

## Example Sentences

- 柳树在风中婀娜多姿。(Liǔ shù zài fēng zhōng ē nuó duō zī.) — The willow trees sway gracefully in the wind.

## Notes

Always used in the compound 婀娜.
