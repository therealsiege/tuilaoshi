# 苦 Bitter

- **Character**: 苦
- **Pinyin**: kǔ
- **Meaning**: bitter, suffering, hard
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 痛苦 (tòng kǔ) — suffering
- 辛苦 (xīn kǔ) — hard work

## Example Sentences

- 学习很辛苦。(Xué xí hěn xīn kǔ.) — Studying is hard work.

## Notes

苦 shows grass 艹 over old 古, representing the bitter taste of aged wild plants, extended to suffering and hardship.
