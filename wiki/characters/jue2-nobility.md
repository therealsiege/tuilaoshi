# 爵 Nobility rank, peerage

- **Character**: 爵
- **Pinyin**: jué
- **Meaning**: nobility rank, peerage
- **Radical**: 爪 (claw)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 爵位 (jué wèi) — noble title, peerage
- 封爵 (fēng jué) — confer a title of nobility

## Example Sentences

- 他因战功卓著而被封爵。(Tā yīn zhàn gōng zhuózhù ér bèi fēng jué.) — He was granted a noble title for his outstanding military achievements.

## Notes

Refers to ranks of nobility. Also means an ancient bronze wine vessel used in ceremonies, symbol of status.
