# 有 To Have/There Is

- **Character**: 有
- **Pinyin**: yǒu
- **Meaning**: to have, to possess; there is, there are
- **Radical**: 月 (moon/flesh)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 没有 (méiyǒu) — don't have, there isn't
- 有名 (yǒumíng) — famous
- 有意思 (yǒu yìsi) — interesting
- 所有 (suǒyǒu) — all, every
- 有时候 (yǒu shíhòu) — sometimes

## Example Sentences

- 我有两个孩子。(Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè háizi.) — I have two children.
- 桌子上有一本书。(Zhuōzi shàng yǒu yì běn shū.) — There is a book on the table.
- 你有没有时间？(Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu shíjiān?) — Do you have time?

## Notes

有 is negated with 没 (méi), never with 不 (bù) — this is a firm rule. The character originally depicted a hand (右) holding meat (月/肉), meaning "to possess." The affirmative-negative question form 有没有 is extremely common in daily speech. 有 also expresses existence ("there is/are") when the subject is a location.
