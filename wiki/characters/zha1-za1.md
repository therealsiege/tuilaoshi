# 扎 Inject

- **Character**: 扎
- **Pinyin**: zhā/zā
- **Meaning**: prick, tie
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 扎针 (zhā zhēn) — to give injection, acupuncture
- 包扎 (bāo zā) — to bind up, bandage
- 扎实 (zhā shi) — solid, sturdy

## Example Sentences

- 护士给他扎针输液。(Hù shi gěi tā zhā zhēn shū yè.) — The nurse gave him an IV injection.

## Notes

To pierce or tie; used for injections and bandaging.
