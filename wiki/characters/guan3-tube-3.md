# 管 Tube, pipe, manage

- **Character**: 管
- **Pinyin**: guǎn
- **Meaning**: tube, pipe, manage
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 管理 (guǎn lǐ) — manage
- 管道 (guǎn dào) — pipeline

## Example Sentences

- 数据通过管道传输。(Shù jù tōng guò guǎn dào chuán shū.) — Data is transmitted through the pipeline.

## Notes

Used in 管理 (manage), 管道 (pipeline), 电子管 (vacuum tube)
