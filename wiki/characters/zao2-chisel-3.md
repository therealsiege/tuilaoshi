# 凿 Chisel

- **Character**: 凿
- **Pinyin**: záo
- **Meaning**: chisel, bore
- **Radical**: 凵(container)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 凿子 (záo zi) — chisel
- 开凿 (kāi záo) — cut, excavate

## Example Sentences

- 石匠用凿子在石头上雕刻。(Shíjiàng yòng záozi zài shítou shàng diāokè.) — The stonemason carved on the stone with a chisel.

## Notes

凿 means chisel or to chisel/bore. Used for working stone, wood, or creating holes.
