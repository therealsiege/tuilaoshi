# 按 Press

- **Character**: 按
- **Pinyin**: àn
- **Meaning**: press, according to
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 按摩 (àn mó) — massage
- 按压 (àn yā) — to press
- 按时 (àn shí) — on time

## Example Sentences

- 按摩可以放松肌肉。(Àn mó kě yǐ fàng sōng jī ròu.) — Massage can relax muscles.

## Notes

Pressing action; massage technique.
