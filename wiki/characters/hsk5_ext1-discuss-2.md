# 讨 Discuss, Demand, 讨论 Discuss

- **Character**: 讨
- **Pinyin**: tǎo
- **Meaning**: discuss, demand, 讨论 discuss
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 讨论 (tǎo lùn) — discuss
- 探讨 (tàn tǎo) — explore, investigate
- 研讨 (yán tǎo) — discuss, seminar

## Example Sentences

- 大家一起讨论这个问题吧。(Dàjiā yīqǐ tǎolùn zhège wèntí ba.) — Let's all discuss this problem together.

## Notes

Essential character for academic discussions. 讨论 (discuss) is fundamental in academic and professional settings.
