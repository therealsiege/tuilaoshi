# 避 To Avoid

- **Character**: 避
- **Pinyin**: bì
- **Meaning**: to avoid, to evade
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 避免 (bì miǎn) — to avoid
- 回避 (huí bì) — to evade

## Example Sentences

- 我们要避免犯同样的错误。(Wǒ men yào bì miǎn fàn tóng yàng de cuò wù.) — We must avoid making the same mistake.

## Notes

避 means to avoid or dodge. 避免 = to avoid (prevent something from happening).
