# 矫 Correct

- **Character**: 矫
- **Pinyin**: jiǎo
- **Meaning**: correct, rectify
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 矫正 (jiǎo zhèng) — to correct, rectify
- 矫形 (jiǎo xíng) — orthopedic correction
- 矫治 (jiǎo zhì) — to correct, rehabilitate

## Example Sentences

- 他需要戴牙套矫正牙齿。(Tā xū yào dài yá tào jiǎo zhèng yá chǐ.) — He needs to wear braces to correct his teeth.

## Notes

Correction of deformities; orthodontics and orthopedics.
