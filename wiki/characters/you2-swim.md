# 游 To Swim

- **Character**: 游
- **Pinyin**: yóu
- **Meaning**: to swim, to travel
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 游泳 (yóu yǒng) — to swim
- 旅游 (lǚ yóu) — to travel, tourism

## Example Sentences

- 夏天我喜欢游泳。(Xià tiān wǒ xǐ huān yóu yǒng.) — In summer I like to swim.

## Notes

游 with water radical for swimming. Also means to travel/roam: 游客 (tourist).
