# 鲤 Carp

- **Character**: 鲤
- **Pinyin**: lǐ
- **Meaning**: carp
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 鲤鱼 (lǐ yú) — carp
- 鲤鱼跳龙门 (lǐ yú tiào lóng mén) — carp jumping over dragon gate

## Example Sentences

- 池塘里有很多鲤鱼。(Chítáng lǐ yǒu hěn duō lǐyú.) — There are many carp in the pond.

## Notes

Symbol of perseverance (carp jumping dragon gate). Fish radical.
