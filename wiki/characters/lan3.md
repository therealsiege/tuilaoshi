# 揽 To monopolize, to grasp, to take on, to pull in

- **Character**: 揽
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: to monopolize, to grasp, to take on, to pull in
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揽活 (lǎn huó) — to take on work
- 招揽 (zhāo lǎn) — to attract, to solicit

## Example Sentences

- 他把所有责任都揽在自己身上。(Tā bǎ suǒyǒu zérèn dōu lǎn zài zìjǐ shēnshang.) — He took all the responsibility upon himself.

## Notes

Often used for taking on responsibilities or work.
