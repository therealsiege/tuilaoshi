# 赏 To Reward

- **Character**: 赏
- **Pinyin**: shǎng
- **Meaning**: to reward, to appreciate, to bestow
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 欣赏 (xīn shǎng) — to appreciate
- 奖赏 (jiǎng shǎng) — reward

## Example Sentences

- 我很欣赏他的作品。(Wǒ hěn xīn shǎng tā de zuò pǐn.) — I appreciate his work.

## Notes

赏 is simplified from 賞. The shell radical 贝 indicates valuable rewards.
