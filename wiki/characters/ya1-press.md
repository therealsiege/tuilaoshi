# 压 To press, to push down, to suppress, pressure

- **Character**: 压
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: to press, to push down, to压制, pressure
- **Radical**: 厂(cliff)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 压力 (yā lì) — pressure, stress
- 血压 (xuè yā) — blood pressure

## Example Sentences

- 工作压力很大。(Gōngzuò yālì hěn dà.) — Work pressure is very high.

## Notes

Common character for pressure, both physical and psychological.
