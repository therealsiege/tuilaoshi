# 差 Difference, error

- **Character**: 差
- **Pinyin**: chā
- **Meaning**: difference, error
- **Radical**: 羊 (sheep)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 偏差 (piān chā) — bias, deviation
- 误差 (wù chā) — error

## Example Sentences

- 计算误差。(Jì suàn wù chā.) — Calculate the error.

## Notes

Used in 偏差 (bias), 误差 (error), 差异 (difference)
