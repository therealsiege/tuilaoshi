# 冠 Champion

- **Character**: 冠
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: champion, crown
- **Radical**: 冖 (cover)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 冠军 (guàn jūn) — champion, championship
- 夺冠 (duó guàn) — to win championship

## Example Sentences

- 她是今年的网球冠军。(Tā shì jīnnián de wǎngqiú guànjūn.) — She is this year's tennis champion.

## Notes

Championship and first place; also means crown or hat
