# 禄 Official's salary, emolument

- **Character**: 禄
- **Pinyin**: lù
- **Meaning**: official's salary, emolument
- **Radical**: 示 (spirit)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 俸禄 (fèng lù) — official's salary
- 利禄 (lì lù) — wealth and position

## Example Sentences

- 古代官员的俸禄以粮食计算。(Gǔdài guānyuán de fènglù yǐ liángshi jìsuàn.) — In ancient times, officials' salaries were calculated in grain.

## Notes

The salary or stipend paid to government officials. Also appears in福禄寿 (happiness, prosperity, longevity).
