# 铆 Rivet

- **Character**: 铆
- **Pinyin**: mǎo
- **Meaning**: rivet
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 铆钉 (mǎo dīng) — rivet
- 铆接 (mǎo jiē) — rivet joint

## Example Sentences

- 船体的钢板是用铆钉连接的。(Chuántǐ de gāngbǎn shì yòng mǎodīng liánjiē de.) — The ship's steel plates are connected with rivets.

## Notes

铆 refers to riveting, a method of joining metal pieces. Before welding became common, riveting was essential.
