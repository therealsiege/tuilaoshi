# 憬 To Yearn

- **Character**: 憬
- **Pinyin**: jǐng
- **Meaning**: to awaken, to yearn for
- **Radical**: 忄(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 憧憬 (chōng jǐng) — to long for
- 憬然 (jǐng rán) — to be awakened

## Example Sentences

- 我们都憧憬和平。(Wǒ men dōu chōng jǐng hé píng.) — We all yearn for peace.

## Notes

憬 has heart 忄 with 景 (scenery/view), heart drawn to a vision.
