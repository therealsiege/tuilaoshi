# 奠 Establish, settle, libation

- **Character**: 奠
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: establish, settle, libation
- **Radical**: 大 (big)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 奠定 (diàndìng) — lay foundation, establish
- 奠基 (diànjī) — lay foundation stone

## Example Sentences

- 这为成功奠定了基础。(Zhè wèi chénggōng diàndìng le jīchǔ.) — This laid the foundation for success.

## Notes

Formal term for establishing foundations or making offerings.
