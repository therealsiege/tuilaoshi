# 办 Handle, do, manage

- **Character**: 办
- **Pinyin**: bàn
- **Meaning**: handle, do, manage
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 办法 (bànfǎ) — method, way
- 办理 (bànlǐ) — handle, process

## Example Sentences

- 请到窗口办理手续。(Qǐng dào chuāngkǒu bànlǐ shǒuxù.) — Please go to the window to process the procedures.

## Notes

Very common for handling and managing affairs.
