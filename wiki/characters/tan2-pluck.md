# 弹 To pluck, to play (stringed instrument), to bounce

- **Character**: 弹
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: to pluck, to play (stringed instrument), to bounce
- **Radical**: 弓(bow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 弹琴 (tán qín) — to play the piano or stringed instrument
- 弹性 (tán xìng) — elasticity, flexibility

## Example Sentences

- 她正在弹钢琴。(Tā zhèngzài tán gāngqín.) — She is playing the piano.

## Notes

Pronounced tán for playing music; dàn for bullet/pellet.
