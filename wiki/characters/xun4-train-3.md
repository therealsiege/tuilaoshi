# 训 Train

- **Character**: 训
- **Pinyin**: xùn
- **Meaning**: to train, instruct
- **Radical**: 讯 (message)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 训练 (xùn liàn) — to train, training
- 教训 (jiào xùn) — lesson, moral

## Example Sentences

- 教练正在训练队员。(Jiàoliàn zhèngzài xùnliàn duìyuán.) — The coach is training the team members.

## Notes

Training and instruction; more formal than 练
