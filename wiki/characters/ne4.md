# 讷 Slow in speech, inarticulate

- **Character**: 讷
- **Pinyin**: nè
- **Meaning**: slow in speech, inarticulate
- **Radical**: 讯 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 木讷 (mùnè) — slow-witted, inarticulate
- 讷言 (nèyán) — slow of speech

## Example Sentences

- 他性格木讷寡言。(Tā xìnggé mùnè guǎyán.) — He is inarticulate and taciturn.

## Notes

Literary term for being slow or awkward in speech.
