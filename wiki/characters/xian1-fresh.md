# 鲜 Fresh, delicious

- **Character**: 鲜
- **Pinyin**: xiān
- **Meaning**: fresh, delicious
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 新鲜 (xīn xiān) — fresh
- 鲜美 (xiān měi) — delicious, tasty

## Example Sentences

- 这鱼很鲜。(Zhè yú hěn xiān.) — This fish is very fresh.

## Notes

Fish radical + sheep = delicious! Also means vivid, bright. Second tone (xiǎn) means rare.
