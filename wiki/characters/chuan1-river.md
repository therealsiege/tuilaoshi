# 川 River

- **Character**: 川
- **Pinyin**: chuān
- **Meaning**: river, Sichuan
- **Radical**: 川 (river)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 四川 (sì chuān) — Sichuan
- 川剧 (chuān jù) — Sichuan opera

## Example Sentences

- 川剧的变脸很有名。(Chuān jù de biàn liǎn hěn yǒu míng.) — Sichuan opera's face-changing is very famous.

## Notes

川 means river or Sichuan; 川剧 features the famous face-changing technique.
