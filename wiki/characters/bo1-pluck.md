# 拨 To Pluck

- **Character**: 拨
- **Pinyin**: bō
- **Meaning**: to pluck, to dial
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 拨弦 (bō xián) — to pluck strings
- 拨动 (bō dòng) — to stir

## Example Sentences

- 她轻轻拨动琴弦。(Tā qīng qīng bō dòng qín xián.) — She gently plucked the strings.

## Notes

拨 emphasizes the plucking action, used for stringed instruments.
