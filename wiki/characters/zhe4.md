# 这 This

- **Character**: 这
- **Pinyin**: zhè
- **Meaning**: this
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 这个 (zhè ge) — this one
- 这里 (zhèlǐ) — here, this place
- 这些 (zhèxiē) — these
- 这样 (zhèyàng) — this way, like this
- 这儿 (zhèr) — here (colloquial, northern)

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么？(Zhè shì shénme?) — What is this?
- 这个多少钱？(Zhè ge duōshǎo qián?) — How much is this one?
- 这里的东西很好吃。(Zhèlǐ de dōngxī hěn hǎo chī.) — The food here is delicious.

## Notes

这 is the simplified form of 這. It pairs with 那 (nà, "that") as the basic demonstrative pair in Chinese. In casual speech, 这 is sometimes pronounced zhèi, especially before measure words. The radical 辶 (walk) appears in many characters related to movement or direction. 这 must be followed by a measure word before a noun: 这本书 (zhè běn shū), not *这书.
