# 率 Rate

- **Character**: 率
- **Pinyin**: lǜ
- **Meaning**: rate, ratio, to lead
- **Radical**: 玄 (dark)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 利率 (lì lǜ) — interest rate
- 效率 (xiào lǜ) — efficiency

## Example Sentences

- 银行提高了利率。(Yín háng tí gāo le lì lǜ.) — The bank raised the interest rate.

## Notes

率 originally meant to lead troops, extended to ratio/rate.
