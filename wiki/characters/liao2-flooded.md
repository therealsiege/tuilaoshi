# 潦 Flooded, sloppy, careless

- **Character**: 潦
- **Pinyin**: liáo
- **Meaning**: flooded, sloppy, careless
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潦草 (liáo cǎo) — sloppy, careless
- 潦倒 (liáo dǎo) — down and out

## Example Sentences

- 他的字写得很潦草。(Tā de zì xiě de hěn liáo cǎo.) — His handwriting is very sloppy.

## Notes

Flooded land or sloppy work
