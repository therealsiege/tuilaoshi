# 修 Repair

- **Character**: 修
- **Pinyin**: xiū
- **Meaning**: repair, cultivate
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 修复 (xiū fù) — to repair, restore
- 修理 (xiū lǐ) — to repair
- 修养 (xiū yǎng) — self-cultivation

## Example Sentences

- 心理创伤需要时间修复。(Xīn lǐ chuāng shāng xū yào shí jiān xiū fù.) — Psychological trauma takes time to repair.

## Notes

Restoration; healing process.
