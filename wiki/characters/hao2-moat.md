# 壕 Moat, trench, ditch

- **Character**: 壕
- **Pinyin**: háo
- **Meaning**: moat, trench, ditch
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 壕沟 (háo gōu) — trench, moat
- 战壕 (zhàn háo) — trench (warfare)

## Example Sentences

- 士兵们躲在战壕里。(Shì bīng men duǒ zài zhàn háo lǐ.) — The soldiers hid in the trenches.

## Notes

Military term for defensive ditches
