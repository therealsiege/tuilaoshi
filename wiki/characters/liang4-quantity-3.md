# 量 Quantity

- **Character**: 量
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: quantity, amount; to measure (liáng)
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 数量 (shù liàng) — quantity
- 力量 (lì liàng) — strength

## Example Sentences

- 这个工作量很大。(Zhè ge gōng zuò liàng hěn dà.) — This workload is very heavy.

## Notes

量 as liàng: quantity (数量). As liáng: to measure (量体温, take temperature).
