# 靠 To Lean On

- **Character**: 靠
- **Pinyin**: kào
- **Meaning**: to lean on, to rely on, to approach
- **Radical**: 非 (wrong)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 依靠 (yī kào) — to rely on
- 靠近 (kào jìn) — to approach

## Example Sentences

- 他靠墙站着。(Tā kào qiáng zhàn zhe.) — He stood leaning against the wall.

## Notes

靠 shows 非 (not) and 告 (announce), originally meaning to draw near or lean against for support.
