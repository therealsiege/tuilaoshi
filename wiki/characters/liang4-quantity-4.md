# 量 Quantity

- **Character**: 量
- **Pinyin**: liàng
- **Meaning**: quantity, amount
- **Radical**: 里 (village)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 数量 (shù liàng) — quantity
- 容量 (róng liàng) — capacity

## Example Sentences

- 数据量很大。(Shù jù liàng hěn dà.) — The amount of data is very large.

## Notes

量 as liàng means quantity/amount; as liáng means to measure. Essential for technical measurements.
