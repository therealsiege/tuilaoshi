# 管 Tube

- **Character**: 管
- **Pinyin**: guǎn
- **Meaning**: tube, wind instrument, manage
- **Radical**: 竹(bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 管乐 (guǎn yuè) — wind music
- 管弦乐 (guǎn xián yuè) — orchestral music

## Example Sentences

- 他在管弦乐队吹长笛。(Tā zài guǎnxián yuèduì chuī chángdí.) — He plays the flute in the orchestra.

## Notes

管 means tube or pipe, and refers to wind instruments. The bamboo radical reflects early flutes made from bamboo.
