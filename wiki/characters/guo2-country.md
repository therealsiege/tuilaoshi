# 国 Country

- **Character**: 国
- **Pinyin**: guó
- **Meaning**: country, nation
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 中国 (zhōng guó) — China
- 国家 (guó jiā) — country

## Example Sentences

- 你是哪国人？(Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?) — What nationality are you?

## Notes

国 is simplified from 國. A jade (玉) treasure inside walls (囗) = a nation.
