# 缆 Cable

- **Character**: 缆
- **Pinyin**: lǎn
- **Meaning**: cable, rope
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 电缆 (diàn lǎn) — electric cable
- 光缆 (guāng lǎn) — optical cable

## Example Sentences

- 这条光缆刚安装好。(Zhè tiáo guāng lǎn gāng ān zhuāng hǎo.) — This optical cable was just installed.

## Notes

缆 specifically refers to thick cables, crucial for infrastructure and networking.
