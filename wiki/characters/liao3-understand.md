# 了 To understand, finish, 瞭 (traditional for this sense)

- **Character**: 了
- **Pinyin**: liǎo
- **Meaning**: to understand, finish, 瞭 (traditional for this sense)
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 2

## Compounds

- 了解 (liǎo jiě) — to understand
- 明了 (míng liǎo) — clear, obvious

## Example Sentences

- 我了解情况。(Wǒ liǎojiě qíngkuàng.) — I understand the situation.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: liǎo (understand/finish), le (particle).
