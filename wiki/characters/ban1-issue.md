# 颁 To Issue

- **Character**: 颁
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: to issue, to promulgate
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 颁布 (bān bù) — to promulgate
- 颁发 (bān fā) — to award

## Example Sentences

- 校长颁发了奖状。(Xiào zhǎng bān fā le jiǎng zhuàng.) — The principal awarded the certificate.

## Notes

颁 is formal — used for officially issuing laws, awards, or certificates.
