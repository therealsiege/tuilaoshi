# 岁 Years Old

- **Character**: 岁
- **Pinyin**: suì
- **Meaning**: years old, age
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 几岁 (jǐ suì) — how old
- 岁月 (suì yuè) — years, time

## Example Sentences

- 你几岁了？(Nǐ jǐ suì le?) — How old are you?

## Notes

几岁 is used to ask the age of children. For adults, use 多大 (duō dà) instead.
