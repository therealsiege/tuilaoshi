# 簸 To Jolt

- **Character**: 簸
- **Pinyin**: bǒ
- **Meaning**: to jolt, to toss (also bò: winnow)
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 颠簸 (diān bǒ) — to bump along
- 簸箕 (bò ji) — dustpan

## Example Sentences

- 车在颠簸的路上行驶。(Chē zài diān bǒ de lù shàng xíng shǐ.) — The车 traveled on the bumpy road.

## Notes

簸 as bǒ means to jolt, as bò means to winnow. Bamboo radical 竹 for winnowing basket.
