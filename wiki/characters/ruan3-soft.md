# 软 Soft

- **Character**: 软
- **Pinyin**: ruǎn
- **Meaning**: soft, weak
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 柔软 (róu ruǎn) — soft, supple
- 软弱 (ruǎn ruò) — weak, feeble
- 软骨 (ruǎn gǔ) — cartilage

## Example Sentences

- 软骨磨损会导致关节炎。(Ruǎn gǔ mó sǔn huì dǎo zhì guān jié yán.) — Cartilage wear can lead to arthritis.

## Notes

Describes tissue consistency; can indicate weakness.
