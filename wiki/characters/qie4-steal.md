# 窃 To Steal

- **Character**: 窃
- **Pinyin**: qiè
- **Meaning**: to steal, secretly
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 偷窃 (tōu qiè) — to steal
- 窃取 (qiè qǔ) — to pilfer

## Example Sentences

- 偷窃是违法的。(Tōu qiè shì wéi fǎ de.) — Stealing is illegal.

## Notes

窃 simplified from 竊. Cave 穴 plus components suggesting secretive action.
