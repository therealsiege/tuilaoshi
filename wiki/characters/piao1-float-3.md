# 漂 To Float

- **Character**: 漂
- **Pinyin**: piāo
- **Meaning**: to float, to drift
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漂流 (piāo liú) — to drift
- 漂亮 (piào liang) — beautiful

## Example Sentences

- 树叶在河上漂流。(Shù yè zài hé shàng piāo liú.) — The leaves are drifting on the river.

## Notes

漂 water 氵 plus 票 (ticket), floating lightly.
