# 缝 Sew

- **Character**: 缝
- **Pinyin**: féng
- **Meaning**: sew, stitch
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 缝纫 (féngrèn) — sewing
- 缝补 (fèngbǔ) — mend

## Example Sentences

- 她会缝衣服。(Tā huì féng yīfu.) — She knows how to sew clothes.

## Notes

Sewing action. Silk radical indicates thread work. Two pronunciations: féng (sew) and fèng (seam, gap).
