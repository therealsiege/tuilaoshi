# 凿 Chisel, drill, certain

- **Character**: 凿
- **Pinyin**: záo
- **Meaning**: chisel, drill, certain
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 凿子 (záo zi) — chisel
- 确凿 (què záo) — conclusive, definite

## Example Sentences

- 证据确凿，无可辩驳。(Zhèng jù què záo, wú kě biàn bó.) — The evidence is conclusive and irrefutable.

## Notes
