# 索 Demand, search, rope

- **Character**: 索
- **Pinyin**: suǒ
- **Meaning**: demand, search, rope
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 索取 (suǒqǔ) — demand, ask for
- 索赔 (suǒpéi) — claim damages

## Example Sentences

- 受害者索赔损失。(Shòuhàizhě suǒpéi sǔnshī.) — The victim claimed damages for losses.

## Notes

Formal term for demanding or claiming.
