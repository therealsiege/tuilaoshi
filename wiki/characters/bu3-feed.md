# 哺 To feed, to nurse

- **Character**: 哺
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: to feed, to nurse
- **Radical**: 口(mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哺乳 (bǔ rǔ) — to breastfeed, to nurse
- 哺育 (bǔ yù) — to feed, to nurture

## Example Sentences

- 母亲在哺育婴儿。(Mǔqīn zài bǔyù yīng'ér.) — The mother is nursing the baby.

## Notes

Used for feeding or nurturing, especially infants.
