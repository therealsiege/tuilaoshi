# 助 Help

- **Character**: 助
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to help, to assist
- **Radical**: 力 (strength)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 助人为乐 (zhù rén wéi lè) — find joy in helping others
- 帮助 (bāng zhù) — to help

## Example Sentences

- 助人为乐是一种美德。(Zhù rén wéi lè shì yī zhǒng měidé.) — Finding joy in helping others is a virtue.

## Notes

Used in 助人为乐: helping others as the source of one's happiness.
