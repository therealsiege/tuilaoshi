# 值 Worth

- **Character**: 值
- **Pinyin**: zhí
- **Meaning**: value, worth, to be worth
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 价值 (jià zhí) — value, worth
- 值得 (zhí de) — to be worth

## Example Sentences

- 这本书很值得读。(Zhè běn shū hěn zhí de dú.) — This book is very worth reading.

## Notes

值 indicates worthiness or value. 值得 (worth doing) is extremely common for recommendations.
