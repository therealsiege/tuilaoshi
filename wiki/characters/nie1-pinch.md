# 捏 To pinch, to mold, to fabricate

- **Character**: 捏
- **Pinyin**: niē
- **Meaning**: to pinch, to mold, to fabricate
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 捏造 (niē zào) — to fabricate, to concoct
- 捏泥人 (niē ní rén) — to mold clay figurines

## Example Sentences

- 孩子在捏橡皮泥。(Háizi zài niē xiàngpí ní.) — The child is molding plasticine.

## Notes

Can mean physical pinching or fabricating stories.
