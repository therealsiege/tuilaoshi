# 册 Book

- **Character**: 册
- **Pinyin**: cè
- **Meaning**: book, volume, measure word
- **Radical**: 丨 (vertical stroke)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 手册 (shǒu cè) — handbook
- 画册 (huà cè) — picture album

## Example Sentences

- 这是一本手册。(Zhè shì yī běn shǒu cè.) — This is a handbook.
