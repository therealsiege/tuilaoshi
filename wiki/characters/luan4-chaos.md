# 乱 Chaos

- **Character**: 乱
- **Pinyin**: luàn
- **Meaning**: chaos, disorder
- **Radical**: 乙 (second)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 紊乱 (wěn luàn) — disorder, chaos
- 混乱 (hùn luàn) — confusion, chaos
- 慌乱 (huāng luàn) — flustered

## Example Sentences

- 思维紊乱是精神病的症状。(Sī wéi wěn luàn shì jīng shén bìng de zhèng zhuàng.) — Thought disorder is a symptom of psychosis.

## Notes

Confusion and disorder; mental state.
