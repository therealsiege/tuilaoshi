# 颁 Promulgate, Issue, 颁布 Promulgate

- **Character**: 颁
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: promulgate, issue, 颁布 promulgate
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 颁布 (bān bù) — promulgate, issue
- 颁发 (bān fā) — issue, award
- 颁奖 (bān jiǎng) — award prizes

## Example Sentences

- 政府颁布了新的交通法规。(Zhèngfǔ bānbù le xīn de jiāotōng fǎguī.) — The government promulgated new traffic regulations.

## Notes

Formal term used for official issuance of laws, regulations, certificates, or awards by authorities.
