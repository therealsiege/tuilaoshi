# 癌 Cancer

- **Character**: 癌
- **Pinyin**: ái
- **Meaning**: cancer
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 癌症 (ái zhèng) — cancer
- 肺癌 (fèi ái) — lung cancer
- 抗癌 (kàng ái) — anti-cancer

## Example Sentences

- 早期发现癌症很重要。(Zǎo qī fā xiàn ái zhèng hěn zhòng yào.) — Early detection of cancer is very important.

## Notes

Malignant tumor; major focus of modern oncology.
