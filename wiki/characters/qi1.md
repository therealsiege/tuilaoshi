# 期 Period

- **Character**: 期
- **Pinyin**: qī
- **Meaning**: period, term, to expect
- **Radical**: 月 (moon)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 星期 (xīng qī) — week
- 日期 (rì qī) — date

## Example Sentences

- 星期天我们去公园。(Xīng qī tiān wǒ men qù gōng yuán.) — We go to the park on Sunday.

## Notes

期 indicates a span or cycle of time. 星期一 through 星期日 are Monday through Sunday.
