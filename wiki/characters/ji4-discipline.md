# 纪 Discipline

- **Character**: 纪
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: discipline, record, age
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 世纪 (shì jì) — century
- 纪律 (jì lǜ) — discipline

## Example Sentences

- 我们要遵守纪律。(Wǒ men yào zūn shǒu jì lǜ.) — We must observe discipline.

## Notes

纪 simplified from 紀. Silk radical 纟 with 己 (self), originally meaning to record on silk threads (knotted cords).
