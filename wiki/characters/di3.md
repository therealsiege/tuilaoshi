# 抵 Resist, mortgage, reach

- **Character**: 抵
- **Pinyin**: dǐ
- **Meaning**: resist, mortgage, reach
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 抵抗 (dǐkàng) — resist, withstand
- 抵押 (dǐyā) — mortgage, pledge

## Example Sentences

- 他们抵抗外来侵略。(Tāmen dǐkàng wàilái qīnlüè.) — They resisted foreign invasion.

## Notes

Multiple meanings including resistance, mortgage, and arrival.
