# 条 Strip

- **Character**: 条
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: strip, item, clause, measure word
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 条件 (tiáo jiàn) — condition
- 条款 (tiáo kuǎn) — clause, article

## Example Sentences

- 这个条件我不能接受。(Zhè ge tiáo jiàn wǒ bù néng jiē shòu.) — I cannot accept this condition.

## Notes

条 is simplified from 條. Used as a measure word for long thin objects and clauses.
