# 削 To peel, to pare

- **Character**: 削
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: to peel, to pare
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 削皮 (xiāo pí) — to peel
- 削弱 (xuē ruò) — to weaken

## Example Sentences

- 请帮我削个苹果。(Qǐng bāng wǒ xiāo gè píngguǒ.) — Please help me peel an apple.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: xiāo (peel) and xuē (cut, reduce). Knife radical.
