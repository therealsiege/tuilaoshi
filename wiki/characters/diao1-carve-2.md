# 雕 Carve, engrave, sculpture

- **Character**: 雕
- **Pinyin**: diāo
- **Meaning**: carve, engrave, sculpture
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 雕刻 (diāo kè) — carve, engrave
- 雕塑 (diāo sù) — sculpture

## Example Sentences

- 这座雕塑是著名艺术家的作品。(Zhè zuò diāo sù shì zhù míng yì shù jiā de zuò pǐn.) — This sculpture is the work of a famous artist.

## Notes

Primary character for carving and sculpture.
