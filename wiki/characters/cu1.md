# 粗 Thick

- **Character**: 粗
- **Pinyin**: cū
- **Meaning**: thick, coarse, rough
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 粗心 (cū xīn) — careless
- 粗糙 (cū cāo) — rough, coarse

## Example Sentences

- 这根绳子很粗。(Zhè gēn shéng zi hěn cū.) — This rope is very thick.

## Notes

粗 uses rice radical. Opposite of 细 (thin/fine). 粗心 = careless (rough heart).
