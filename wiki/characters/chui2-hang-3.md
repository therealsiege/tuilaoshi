# 垂 Droop

- **Character**: 垂
- **Pinyin**: chuí
- **Meaning**: to hang down, to droop
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 垂头丧气 (chuí tóu sàng qì) — dejected
- 垂直 (chuí zhí) — vertical

## Example Sentences

- 他垂头丧气地走了。(Tā chuí tóu sàng qì de zǒu le.) — He walked away dejected.

## Notes

Used in 垂头丧气: to hang one's head in discouragement.
