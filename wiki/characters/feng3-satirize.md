# 讽 To Satirize

- **Character**: 讽
- **Pinyin**: fěng
- **Meaning**: to satirize, to mock
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 讽刺 (fěng cì) — to satirize, irony
- 讽喻 (fěng yù) — to satirize

## Example Sentences

- 这部小说讽刺了当时的社会。(Zhè bù xiǎo shuō fěng cì le dāng shí de shè huì.) — This novel satirized the society of that time.

## Notes

讽 uses speech radical 讠, indicating satirical or mocking speech. 讽刺 is a common term for satire.
