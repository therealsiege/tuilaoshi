# 布 Cloth, spread, announce

- **Character**: 布
- **Pinyin**: bù
- **Meaning**: cloth, spread, announce
- **Radical**: 巾 (turban)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 分布 (fēnbù) — distribution, be distributed
- 宣布 (xuānbù) — announce, declare

## Example Sentences

- 这种植物分布很广。(Zhè zhǒng zhíwù fēnbù hěn guǎng.) — This plant is widely distributed.

## Notes

Means cloth and also spreading or announcing.
