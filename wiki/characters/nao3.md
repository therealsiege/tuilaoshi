# 恼 Annoyed

- **Character**: 恼
- **Pinyin**: nǎo
- **Meaning**: annoyed, angry, to vex
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 烦恼 (fán nǎo) — vexed
- 恼火 (nǎo huǒ) — annoyed

## Example Sentences

- 他很恼火。(Tā hěn nǎo huǒ.) — He is very annoyed.

## Notes

恼 simplified from 惱. Shows heart 心 with brain component, representing mental agitation and annoyance.
