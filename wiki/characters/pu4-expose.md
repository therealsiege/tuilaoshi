# 曝 To Expose

- **Character**: 曝
- **Pinyin**: pù
- **Meaning**: to expose to sun, to reveal
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 曝光 (pù guāng) — to expose (film/scandal)
- 曝晒 (pù shài) — to expose to sunlight

## Example Sentences

- 这件事被曝光了。(Zhè jiàn shì bèi pù guāng le.) — This matter was exposed.

## Notes

曝 combines sun 日 and 暴 (violent/sudden).
