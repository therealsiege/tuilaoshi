# 髓 Marrow, pith, essence

- **Character**: 髓
- **Pinyin**: suǐ
- **Meaning**: marrow, pith, essence
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 21

## Compounds

- 骨髓 (gǔ suǐ) — bone marrow
- 脊髓 (jǐ suǐ) — spinal cord

## Example Sentences

- 骨髓负责产生血细胞。(Gǔ suǐ fù zé chǎn shēng xuè xì bāo.) — Bone marrow is responsible for producing blood cells.

## Notes

Inner tissue of bones and plants
