# 礴 Extensive, boundless

- **Character**: 礴
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: extensive, boundless
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 磅礴 (pángbó) — majestic, boundless
- 盘礴 (pánbó) — extend far and wide

## Example Sentences

- 气势磅礴的画卷。(Qìshì pángbó de huàjuàn.) — A majestic and imposing scroll painting.

## Notes

Literary term primarily used in compound 磅礴 for majesty or vastness.
