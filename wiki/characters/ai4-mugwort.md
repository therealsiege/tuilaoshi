# 艾 Mugwort

- **Character**: 艾
- **Pinyin**: ài
- **Meaning**: mugwort, to stop
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 艾草 (ài cǎo) — mugwort
- 艾灸 (ài jiǔ) — moxibustion

## Example Sentences

- 艾灸是传统中医疗法。(Ài jiǔ shì chuán tǒng zhōng yī liáo fǎ.) — Moxibustion is a traditional Chinese medical treatment.

## Notes

艾 is mugwort, used in traditional Chinese medicine for moxibustion therapy.
