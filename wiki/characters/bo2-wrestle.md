# 搏 Wrestle

- **Character**: 搏
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: to wrestle, fight
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 拼搏 (pīn bó) — to struggle, fight
- 搏斗 (bó dòu) — to fight, wrestle

## Example Sentences

- 运动员在赛场上奋力拼搏。(Yùndòngyuán zài sàichǎng shàng fènlì pīnbó.) — Athletes fight hard on the field.

## Notes

Wrestling and fighting; intense struggle
