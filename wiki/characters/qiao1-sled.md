# 橇 Sled

- **Character**: 橇
- **Pinyin**: qiāo
- **Meaning**: sled, sledge
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 雪橇 (xuě qiāo) — sled, sledge
- 滑橇 (huá qiāo) — to sled

## Example Sentences

- 雪橇是冬季运动项目。(Xuěqiāo shì dōngjì yùndòng xiàngmù.) — Sledding is a winter sport.
