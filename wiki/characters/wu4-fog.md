# 雾 Fog, mist

- **Character**: 雾
- **Pinyin**: wù
- **Meaning**: fog, mist
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 大雾 (dà wù) — heavy fog
- 雾霾 (wù mái) — smog

## Example Sentences

- 早晨有大雾,能见度很低。(Zǎo chén yǒu dà wù, néng jiàn dù hěn dī.) — There is heavy fog in the morning with very low visibility.
