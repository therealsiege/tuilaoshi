# 倒 Fall

- **Character**: 倒
- **Pinyin**: dào
- **Meaning**: to fall, pour, upside down
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倒立 (dào lì) — handstand
- 摔倒 (shuāi dǎo) — to fall down

## Example Sentences

- 她能倒立走路。(Tā néng dàolì zǒulù.) — She can walk on her hands.

## Notes

Inverted position; key for handstands and acrobatics
