# 辉 Brightness

- **Character**: 辉
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: brightness, splendor, to shine
- **Radical**: 光 (light)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 光辉 (guāng huī) — radiance
- 辉煌 (huī huáng) — splendid

## Example Sentences

- 阳光辉煌灿烂。(Yáng guāng huī huáng càn làn.) — The sunlight is splendidly brilliant.

## Notes

辉 simplified from 輝. Light 光 radiating brilliantly.
