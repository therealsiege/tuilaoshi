# 雅 Elegant, refined, graceful

- **Character**: 雅
- **Pinyin**: yǎ
- **Meaning**: elegant, refined, graceful
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 优雅 (yōu yǎ) — elegant, graceful
- 幽雅 (yōu yǎ) — serene and elegant

## Example Sentences

- 她举止优雅，谈吐不凡。(Tā jǔ zhǐ yōu yǎ, tán tǔ bù fán.) — She behaves elegantly and speaks extraordinarily.

## Notes

Important aesthetic concept in Chinese culture.
