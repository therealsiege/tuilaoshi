# 搓 To Rub

- **Character**: 搓
- **Pinyin**: cuō
- **Meaning**: to rub, to twist
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 搓手 (cuō shǒu) — to rub hands
- 搓洗 (cuō xǐ) — to rub and wash

## Example Sentences

- 他搓着手取暖。(Tā cuō zhe shǒu qǔ nuǎn.) — He rubbed his hands to warm up.

## Notes

搓 combines hand 扌 and 差 (differ), representing rubbing hands together.
