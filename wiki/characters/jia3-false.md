# 假 False, fake, borrow

- **Character**: 假
- **Pinyin**: jiǎ
- **Meaning**: false, fake, borrow
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 假如 (jiǎrú) — if, suppose
- 假释 (jiǎshì) — parole

## Example Sentences

- 罪犯获准假释。(Zuìfàn huòzhǔn jiǎshì.) — The prisoner was granted parole.

## Notes

Two pronunciations: jiǎ (false, borrow) and jià (vacation).
