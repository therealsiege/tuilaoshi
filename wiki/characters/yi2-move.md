# 移 To Move

- **Character**: 移
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: to move, to shift, to change
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 移动 (yí dòng) — to move
- 转移 (zhuǎn yí) — to transfer

## Example Sentences

- 请把桌子移到那边。(Qǐng bǎ zhuō zi yí dào nà biān.) — Please move the table over there.

## Notes

移 combines grain and movement, representing shifting or moving things. Common in 移动 (to move, mobile).
