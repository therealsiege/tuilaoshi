# 浸 To Soak

- **Character**: 浸
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to soak, to immerse
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浸泡 (jìn pào) — to soak
- 沉浸 (chén jìn) — to immerse

## Example Sentences

- 把衣服浸在水里。(Bǎ yī fu jìn zài shuǐ lǐ.) — Soak the clothes in water.

## Notes

浸 indicates gradual absorption or immersion, often implying a slow process.
