# 靡 Wasteful, decadent, not

- **Character**: 靡
- **Pinyin**: mí
- **Meaning**: wasteful, decadent, not
- **Radical**: 非 (not)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 奢靡 (shēmí) — extravagant and wasteful
- 靡靡之音 (mǐmǐzhīyīn) — decadent music

## Example Sentences

- 反对奢靡浪费之风。(Fǎnduì shēmí làngfèi zhī fēng.) — Oppose the trend of extravagance and waste.

## Notes

Literary term for wastefulness, decadence; also means 'not' in classical Chinese.
