# 迁 Move, relocate

- **Character**: 迁
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: move, relocate
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 迁移 (qiān yí) — move, migrate
- 搬迁 (bān qiān) — move, relocate

## Example Sentences

- 公司要搬迁到新址。(Gōng sī yào bān qiān dào xīn zhǐ.) — The company will relocate to a new location.

## Notes

迁址 means change address. 拆迁补偿 is demolition compensation.
