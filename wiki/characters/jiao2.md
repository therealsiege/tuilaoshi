# 嚼 To Chew

- **Character**: 嚼
- **Pinyin**: jiáo
- **Meaning**: to chew (also jué, jiào)
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 咀嚼 (jǔ jiáo) — to chew
- 细嚼慢咽 (xì jiáo màn yàn) — to chew slowly

## Example Sentences

- 吃饭要细嚼慢咽。(Chī fàn yào xì jiáo màn yàn.) — When eating, chew slowly and swallow carefully.

## Notes

嚼 shows mouth 口 with 爵 (noble rank), as jiáo/jué means to chew, as jiào in colloquial speech.
