# 千 Thousand

- **Character**: 千
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: thousand
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 一千 (yī qiān) — one thousand
- 千万 (qiān wàn) — by all means, must

## Example Sentences

- 这里有几千人。(Zhè lǐ yǒu jǐ qiān rén.) — There are several thousand people here.

## Notes

千 means 'thousand.' 千万 as an adverb means 'by all means' or 'absolutely must.'
