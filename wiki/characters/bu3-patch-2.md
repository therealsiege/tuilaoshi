# 补 Patch, supplement

- **Character**: 补
- **Pinyin**: bǔ
- **Meaning**: patch, supplement
- **Radical**: 衤 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 修补 (xiū bǔ) — repair

## Example Sentences
