# 狂 Mad

- **Character**: 狂
- **Pinyin**: kuáng
- **Meaning**: mad, crazy, wild
- **Radical**: 犭(dog)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 疯狂 (fēng kuáng) — crazy
- 狂热 (kuáng rè) — fanatical

## Example Sentences

- 球迷们疯狂地欢呼。(Qiú mí men fēng kuáng de huān hū.) — The fans cheered crazily.

## Notes

狂 dog 犭 plus 王 (king), dog acting like royalty = mad.
