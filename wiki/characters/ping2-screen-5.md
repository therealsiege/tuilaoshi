# 屏 Screen

- **Character**: 屏
- **Pinyin**: píng
- **Meaning**: screen, to shield
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 屏幕 (píng mù) — screen (display)
- 屏蔽 (píng bì) — to block, shield

## Example Sentences

- 屏幕太亮了。(Píng mù tài liàng le.) — The screen is too bright.

## Notes

屏 can be read as bǐng meaning 'to hold breath' but píng for screen is more common now.
