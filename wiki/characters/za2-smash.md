# 砸 To Smash

- **Character**: 砸
- **Pinyin**: zá
- **Meaning**: to smash, to pound
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 砸碎 (zá suì) — to smash to pieces
- 砸烂 (zá làn) — to smash

## Example Sentences

- 他不小心砸碎了花瓶。(Tā bù xiǎo xīn zá suì le huā píng.) — He accidentally smashed the vase.

## Notes

砸 shows stone 石 and杂 (miscellaneous), representing breaking something into pieces by pounding.
