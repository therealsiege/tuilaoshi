# 条 Strip, Item, 条件 Condition

- **Character**: 条
- **Pinyin**: tiáo
- **Meaning**: strip, item, 条件 condition
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 条件 (tiáo jiàn) — condition, requirement
- 条款 (tiáo kuǎn) — clause, article
- 条例 (tiáo lì) — regulation, rule

## Example Sentences

- 合同中的每一条款都很重要。(Hétong zhōng de měi yī tiáokuǎn dōu hěn zhòngyào.) — Every clause in the contract is important.

## Notes

Used as measure word for long, thin objects and for items in lists or regulations. Essential in legal and formal documents.
