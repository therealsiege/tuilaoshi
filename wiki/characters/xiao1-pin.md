# 销 Pin

- **Character**: 销
- **Pinyin**: xiāo
- **Meaning**: pin, to sell, to cancel
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 销售 (xiāo shòu) — to sell, sales
- 营销 (yíng xiāo) — marketing

## Example Sentences

- 销售额增长了。(Xiāo shòu é zēng zhǎng le.) — Sales revenue has increased.

## Notes

销 with metal radical for pin, extended to销 sales (moving goods) and cancellation.
