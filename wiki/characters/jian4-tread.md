# 践 Tread, practice, fulfill

- **Character**: 践
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: tread, practice, fulfill
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 实践 (shíjiàn) — practice, put into practice
- 践行 (jiànxíng) — carry out, practice

## Example Sentences

- 我们要践行诺言。(Wǒmen yào jiànxíng nuòyán.) — We must fulfill our promises.

## Notes

Emphasizes putting principles into action through practice.
