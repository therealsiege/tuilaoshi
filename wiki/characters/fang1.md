# 方 Direction

- **Character**: 方
- **Pinyin**: fāng
- **Meaning**: square, direction, method
- **Radical**: 方 (square)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 地方 (dì fāng) — place
- 方法 (fāng fǎ) — method

## Example Sentences

- 你有什么好方法？(Nǐ yǒu shén me hǎo fāng fǎ?) — Do you have any good methods?

## Notes

方 is a pictograph of a boat or raft — something that goes in a direction. Extended to 'square/direction/method.'
