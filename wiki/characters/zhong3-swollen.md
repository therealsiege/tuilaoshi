# 肿 Swollen

- **Character**: 肿
- **Pinyin**: zhǒng
- **Meaning**: swollen
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 肿胀 (zhǒng zhàng) — swelling
- 红肿 (hóng zhǒng) — red and swollen

## Example Sentences

- 他的脚肿了。(Tā de jiǎo zhǒng le.) — His foot is swollen.

## Notes

肿 combines meat 月 with 重 (heavy), representing flesh that has become heavy and swollen.
