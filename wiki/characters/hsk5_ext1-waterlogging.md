# 涝 Waterlogging, Flood, 水涝 Waterlogging

- **Character**: 涝
- **Pinyin**: lào
- **Meaning**: waterlogging, flood, 水涝 waterlogging
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 水涝 (shuǐ lào) — waterlogging
- 涝灾 (lào zāi) — flood disaster
- 内涝 (nèi lào) — urban flooding

## Example Sentences

- 暴雨导致城市内涝。(Bàoyǔ dǎozhì chéngshì nèilào.) — Heavy rain caused urban flooding.

## Notes

Agricultural and meteorological term for excessive water/flooding. Opposite of 旱 (drought). Common in disaster reports and agriculture.
