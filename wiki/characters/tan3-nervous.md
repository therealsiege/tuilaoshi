# 忐 Nervous

- **Character**: 忐
- **Pinyin**: tǎn
- **Meaning**: nervous, apprehensive
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 忐忑不安 (tǎn tè bù ān) — uneasy, anxious
- 忐忑 (tǎn tè) — nervous

## Example Sentences

- 考试前他忐忑不安。(Kǎoshì qián tā tǎn tè bù ān.) — He was anxious before the exam.

## Notes

Used in 忐忑不安: heart going up (忐) and down (忑); shows the heart above 上.
