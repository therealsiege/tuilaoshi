# 利 Sharp, benefit, profit

- **Character**: 利
- **Pinyin**: lì
- **Meaning**: sharp, benefit, profit
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 锋利 (fēng lì) — sharp, keen
- 利益 (lì yì) — benefit, interest

## Example Sentences

- 这把刀很锋利。(Zhè bǎ dāo hěn fēng lì.) — This knife is very sharp.

## Notes

Sharpness and advantage
