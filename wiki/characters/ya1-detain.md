# 押 To Detain

- **Character**: 押
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: to detain, to mortgage
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 押金 (yā jīn) — deposit
- 抵押 (dǐ yā) — to mortgage

## Example Sentences

- 租房要交押金。(Zū fáng yào jiāo yā jīn.) — Renting a house requires paying a deposit.

## Notes

押 has dual meanings: detaining a person or putting up collateral/deposit. Both involve temporary holding.
