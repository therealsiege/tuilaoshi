# 园 Garden

- **Character**: 园
- **Pinyin**: yuán
- **Meaning**: garden, park
- **Radical**: 囗 (enclosure)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 公园 (gōng yuán) — park
- 花园 (huā yuán) — garden

## Example Sentences

- 我们去公园散步。(Wǒ men qù gōng yuán sàn bù.) — We go for a walk in the park.

## Notes

园 with enclosure radical for enclosed garden/park, public and private green spaces.
