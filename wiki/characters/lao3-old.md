# 老 Old

- **Character**: 老
- **Pinyin**: lǎo
- **Meaning**: old, elderly, always
- **Radical**: 耂 (old)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 老师 (lǎo shī) — teacher
- 老人 (lǎo rén) — elderly person

## Example Sentences

- 王老师很好。(Wáng lǎo shī hěn hǎo.) — Teacher Wang is great.

## Notes

老 originally showed an old person with long hair and a cane. It can be a respectful prefix: 老王 (Old Wang).
