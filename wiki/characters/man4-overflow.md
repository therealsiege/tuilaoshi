# 漫 To Overflow

- **Character**: 漫
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: to overflow, vast
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漫长 (màn cháng) — very long
- 弥漫 (mí màn) — to pervade

## Example Sentences

- 烟雾弥漫整个房间。(Yān wù mí màn zhěng gè fáng jiān.) — Smoke pervaded the entire room.

## Notes

漫 suggests something spreading widely like overflowing water. Used for time, space, or pervasive conditions.
