# 点 Point

- **Character**: 点
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: point, dot, to点点点to light, a点点点little

## Example Sentences

- 他点了一支烟。(Tā diǎn le yī zhī yān.) — He lit a cigarette.
