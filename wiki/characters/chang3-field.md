# 场 Field

- **Character**: 场
- **Pinyin**: chǎng
- **Meaning**: field, place, scene; measure word for events
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 广场 (guǎng chǎng) — square, plaza
- 一场 (yī chǎng) — a (game/show)

## Example Sentences

- 我们去广场散步。(Wǒ men qù guǎng chǎng sàn bù.) — Let's take a walk at the square.

## Notes

场 as chǎng is a place (机场, 操场). As chǎng it's a measure word for events (一场电影).
