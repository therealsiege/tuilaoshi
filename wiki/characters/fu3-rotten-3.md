# 腐 Rotten

- **Character**: 腐
- **Pinyin**: fǔ
- **Meaning**: rotten, corrupt, tofu
- **Radical**: 肉 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 腐败 (fǔ bài) — corrupt
- 豆腐 (dòu fǔ) — tofu

## Example Sentences

- 他们要反对腐败。(Tā men yào fǎn duì fǔ bài.) — They must oppose corruption.

## Notes

腐 combines 府 (government office) and 肉 (meat), meat rotting in storage.
