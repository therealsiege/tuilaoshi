# 约 约定, approximately

- **Character**: 约
- **Pinyin**: yuē
- **Meaning**: 约定, approximately
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 合约 (hé yuē) — contract
- 条约 (tiáo yuē) — treaty

## Example Sentences

- 双方签订了合约。(Shuāng fāng qiān dìng le hé yuē.) — Both parties signed a contract.

## Notes

契约 means covenant. 违约 means breach of contract.
