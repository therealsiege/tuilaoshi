# 练 Practice

- **Character**: 练
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to practice, train
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 练习 (liàn xí) — to practice, exercise
- 训练 (xùn liàn) — to train

## Example Sentences

- 他每天练习两小时。(Tā měi tiān liànxí liǎng xiǎoshí.) — He practices two hours every day.

## Notes

Essential practice and training character; used across all sports
