# 贫 Poor

- **Character**: 贫
- **Pinyin**: pín
- **Meaning**: poor, lacking
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell/money)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 贫困 (pín kùn) — impoverished
- 贫穷 (pín qióng) — poor

## Example Sentences

- 政府帮助贫困地区。(Zhèng fǔ bāng zhù pín kùn dì qū.) — The government helps impoverished areas.

## Notes

贫 simplified from 貧. Shows shell/money 贝 split/divided 分, representing divided or scarce resources.
