# 哮 Wheeze

- **Character**: 哮
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: wheeze, roar
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 哮喘 (xiào chuǎn) — asthma
- 咆哮 (páo xiào) — to roar

## Example Sentences

- 哮喘发作时呼吸困难。(Xiào chuǎn fā zuò shí hū xī kùn nan.) — Breathing is difficult during an asthma attack.

## Notes

Wheezing sound; asthma characteristic.
