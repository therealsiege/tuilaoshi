# 稽 Examine, investigate

- **Character**: 稽
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: examine, investigate
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 稽查 (jīchá) — inspect, investigate
- 无稽之谈 (wújīzhītán) — unfounded statement, nonsense

## Example Sentences

- 海关正在稽查走私货物。(Hǎiguān zhèngzài jīchá zǒusī huòwù.) — Customs is investigating smuggled goods.

## Notes

Formal term used in official contexts for inspection and verification.
