# 邻 Neighbor, Adjacent, 邻居 Neighbor

- **Character**: 邻
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: neighbor, adjacent, 邻居 neighbor
- **Radical**: ⻏ (city)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 邻居 (lín jū) — neighbor
- 相邻 (xiāng lín) — adjacent
- 邻国 (lín guó) — neighboring country

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该和邻居和睦相处。(Wǒmen yīnggāi hé línjū hémù xiāngchǔ.) — We should get along harmoniously with our neighbors.

## Notes

Common in both everyday (邻居 - neighbor) and formal contexts (邻国 - neighboring country).
