# 碰 To Touch

- **Character**: 碰
- **Pinyin**: pèng
- **Meaning**: to touch, to bump into, to meet
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 碰见 (pèng jiàn) — to run into, to meet by chance
- 碰巧 (pèng qiǎo) — by chance

## Example Sentences

- 我昨天碰见了老朋友。(Wǒ zuó tiān pèng jiàn le lǎo péng yǒu.) — I ran into an old friend yesterday.

## Notes

碰 uses stone radical 石, representing the contact or collision of hard objects.
