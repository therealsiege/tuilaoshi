# 铜 Copper

- **Character**: 铜
- **Pinyin**: tóng
- **Meaning**: copper, bronze
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 铜器 (tóng qì) — bronze ware
- 青铜 (qīng tóng) — bronze

## Example Sentences

- 古代中国有很多铜器。(Gǔ dài zhōng guó yǒu hěn duō tóng qì.) — Ancient China had many bronze wares.

## Notes

铜 refers to copper or bronze. Chinese Bronze Age artifacts are world-renowned. 青铜 (bronze) literally means 'green-copper'.
