# 序 Order

- **Character**: 序
- **Pinyin**: xù
- **Meaning**: order, sequence, preface
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 程序 (chéng xù) — program
- 顺序 (shùn xù) — sequence, order

## Example Sentences

- 请按顺序排队。(Qǐng àn shùn xù pái duì.) — Please queue in order.

## Notes

序 appears in 程序 (program) and many ordering/sequencing contexts.
