# 燃 To Burn

- **Character**: 燃
- **Pinyin**: rán
- **Meaning**: to burn, to ignite, fuel
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 燃烧 (rán shāo) — to burn
- 点燃 (diǎn rán) — to ignite

## Example Sentences

- 木头在燃烧。(Mù tou zài rán shāo.) — The wood is burning.

## Notes

燃 means to burn or ignite. Used in燃烧 (burning), 燃料 (fuel), 点燃 (to ignite).
