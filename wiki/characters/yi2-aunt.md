# 姨 Aunt

- **Character**: 姨
- **Pinyin**: yí
- **Meaning**: aunt (mother's sister)
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 阿姨 (ā yí) — auntie
- 姨妈 (yí mā) — aunt (maternal)

## Example Sentences

- 阿姨好！(Ā yí hǎo!) — Hello auntie!

## Notes

姨 mother's sister. 阿姨 respectful address for any middle-aged woman, like cleaning lady.
