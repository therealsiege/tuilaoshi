# 紧 Tight

- **Character**: 紧
- **Pinyin**: jǐn
- **Meaning**: tight, urgent, tense
- **Radical**: 糸 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 紧张 (jǐn zhāng) — nervous, tense
- 要紧 (yào jǐn) — important, urgent

## Example Sentences

- 考试前我很紧张。(Kǎo shì qián wǒ hěn jǐn zhāng.) — I'm very nervous before exams.

## Notes

紧 simplified from 緊. Silk threads 糸 pulled taut, representing tightness or urgency.
