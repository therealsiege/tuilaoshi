# 问 Inquiry

- **Character**: 问
- **Pinyin**: wèn
- **Meaning**: ask
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 问诊 (wèn zhěn) — inquiry (medical history taking)
- 提问 (tí wèn) — to ask a question
- 询问 (xún wèn) — to inquire

## Example Sentences

- 医生问诊很详细。(Yī shēng wèn zhěn hěn xiáng xì.) — The doctor's inquiry was very detailed.

## Notes

Questioning; third TCM diagnostic method.
