# 佛 Buddha, Buddhism

- **Character**: 佛
- **Pinyin**: fó
- **Meaning**: Buddha, Buddhism
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 佛教 (fó jiào) — Buddhism
- 佛法 (fó fǎ) — Buddhist doctrine

## Example Sentences

- 他信仰佛教已经十年了。(Tā xìn yǎng fó jiào yǐ jīng shí nián le.) — He has believed in Buddhism for ten years.

## Notes

Transliteration of Sanskrit Buddha, central to Chinese religious life
