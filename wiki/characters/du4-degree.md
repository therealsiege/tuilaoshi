# 度 Degree

- **Character**: 度
- **Pinyin**: dù
- **Meaning**: degree, extent, to spend (time), system
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 制度 (zhì dù) — system
- 温度 (wēn dù) — temperature

## Example Sentences

- 今天的温度很高。(Jīn tiān de wēn dù hěn gāo.) — Today's temperature is very high.

## Notes

度 represents measurement or extent. Also means to pass/spend time (度假 to vacation).
