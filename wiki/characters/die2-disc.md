# 碟 Disc, disk

- **Character**: 碟
- **Pinyin**: dié
- **Meaning**: disc, disk
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 光碟 (guāng dié) — optical disc, DVD
- 磁碟 (cí dié) — magnetic disk

## Example Sentences

- 请插入光碟。(Qǐng chā rù guāng dié.) — Please insert the disc.

## Notes

Used in 光碟 (DVD), 磁碟 (floppy disk), storage media
