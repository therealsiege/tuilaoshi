# 糕 Cake, pastry

- **Character**: 糕
- **Pinyin**: gāo
- **Meaning**: cake, pastry
- **Radical**: 米 (rice)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 蛋糕 (dàn gāo) — cake
- 年糕 (nián gāo) — New Year cake

## Example Sentences

- 她做了一个生日蛋糕。(Tā zuòle yī gè shēngrì dàngāo.) — She made a birthday cake.

## Notes

Originally rice-based pastries, now includes all cakes. Rice radical shows origin.
