# 斑 Spot, speck, stripe

- **Character**: 斑
- **Pinyin**: bān
- **Meaning**: spot, speck, stripe
- **Radical**: 文 (script)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 斑点 (bān diǎn) — spot, speckle
- 斑斓 (bān lán) — multicolored, gorgeous

## Example Sentences

- 老虎身上有黑色的斑纹。(Lǎo hǔ shēn shàng yǒu hēi sè de bān wén.) — Tigers have black stripes on their bodies.

## Notes

Describes patterns of spots or stripes.
