# 瘫 Paralyzed

- **Character**: 瘫
- **Pinyin**: tān
- **Meaning**: paralyzed, to be paralyzed
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 瘫痪 (tān huàn) — paralysis
- 瘫软 (tān ruǎn) — to go limp

## Example Sentences

- 交通瘫痪了。(Jiāo tōng tān huàn le.) — Traffic was paralyzed.

## Notes

瘫 simplified from 癱. Sickness 疒 with 难 (difficult), debilitating illness.
