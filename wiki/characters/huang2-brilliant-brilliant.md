# 煌 Brilliant

- **Character**: 煌
- **Pinyin**: huáng
- **Meaning**: brilliant, shining
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 辉煌 (huī huáng) — brilliant
- 灯火辉煌 (dēng huǒ huī huáng) — brightly lit

## Example Sentences

- 城市灯火辉煌。(Chéng shì dēng huǒ huī huáng.) — The city is brightly lit.

## Notes

煌 has fire 火 with 皇 (emperor), brilliantly shining like imperial glory.
