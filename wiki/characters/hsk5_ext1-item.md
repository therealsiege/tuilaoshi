# 项 Item, Project, 项目 Project

- **Character**: 项
- **Pinyin**: xiàng
- **Meaning**: item, project, 项目 project
- **Radical**: 页 (page)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 项目 (xiàng mù) — project, item
- 款项 (kuǎn xiàng) — sum of money
- 事项 (shì xiàng) — matter, item

## Example Sentences

- 这个项目需要三个月完成。(Zhège xiàngmù xūyào sān gè yuè wánchéng.) — This project needs three months to complete.

## Notes

Very common in business and academic contexts. 项目 (project) is essential term in project management and research.
