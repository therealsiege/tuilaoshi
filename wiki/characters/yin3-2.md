# 引 Guide, lead

- **Character**: 引
- **Pinyin**: yǐn
- **Meaning**: 引导, lead
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 引擎 (yǐn qíng) — engine
- 索引 (suǒ yǐn) — index

## Example Sentences

- 搜索引擎很强大。(Sōu suǒ yǐn qíng hěn qiáng dà.) — Search engines are powerful.

Used in 引擎 (engine), 索引 (index), 引用 (reference)
