# 难 Difficult

- **Character**: 难
- **Pinyin**: nán
- **Meaning**: difficult; disaster (nàn)
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 困难 (kùn nán) — difficulty
- 灾难 (zāi nàn) — disaster

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题很难。(Zhè gè wèn tí hěn nán.) — This problem is very difficult.

## Notes

难 simplified from 難. As nán means difficult, as nàn means disaster/calamity. Shows a bird 隹 caught in a trap.
