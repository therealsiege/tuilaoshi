# 绳 Rope, cord, string

- **Character**: 绳
- **Pinyin**: shéng
- **Meaning**: rope, cord, string
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 绳子 (shéng zi) — rope, cord
- 跳绳 (tiào shéng) — to jump rope

## Example Sentences

- 他用绳子把箱子捆好了。(Tā yòng shéng zi bǎ xiāng zi kǔn hǎo le.) — He tied up the box with rope.

## Notes

General term for rope or cord. 准绳 means standard or criterion. 绳之以法 means to punish according to law.
