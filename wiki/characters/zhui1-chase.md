# 追 Chase, pursue, trace

- **Character**: 追
- **Pinyin**: zhuī
- **Meaning**: chase, pursue, trace
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 追求 (zhuīqiú) — pursue, seek
- 追捕 (zhuībǔ) — pursue and capture

## Example Sentences

- 警方正在追捕逃犯。(Jǐngfāng zhèngzài zhuībǔ táofàn.) — Police are pursuing the fugitive.

## Notes

Common for chasing and pursuing.
