# 磬 Stone Chime

- **Character**: 磬
- **Pinyin**: qìng
- **Meaning**: stone chime
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 编磬 (biān qìng) — set of stone chimes
- 磬声 (qìng shēng) — chime sound

## Example Sentences

- 磬是古代重要的打击乐器。(Qìng shì gǔ dài zhòng yào de dǎ jī yuè qì.) — The stone chime was an important ancient percussion instrument.

## Notes

磬 is an ancient Chinese percussion instrument made of stone or jade.
