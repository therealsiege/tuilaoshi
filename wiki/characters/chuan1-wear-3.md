# 穿 Wear, pierce, pass through

- **Character**: 穿
- **Pinyin**: chuān
- **Meaning**: wear, pierce, pass through
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 穿过 (chuānguò) — pass through, cross
- 穿着 (chuānzhuó) — dress, attire

## Example Sentences

- 火车穿过隧道。(Huǒchē chuānguò suìdào.) — The train passes through the tunnel.

## Notes

Common character meaning wear clothes or piercing through.
