# 荡 Swing, wash away, dissipated

- **Character**: 荡
- **Pinyin**: dàng
- **Meaning**: swing, wash away, dissipated
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荡漾 (dàng yàng) — ripple, undulate
- 浩荡 (hào dàng) — vast and mighty

## Example Sentences

- 湖面上荡漾着轻柔的涟漪。(Hú miàn shàng dàng yàng zhe qīng róu de lián yī.) — Gentle ripples undulate across the lake surface.

## Notes

Multiple meanings including movement, vastness, and dissipation.
