# 冰 Ice

- **Character**: 冰
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: ice
- **Radical**: 冫 (ice)

## Compounds

- 滑冰 (huá bīng) — ice skating
- 冰球 (bīng qiú) — ice hockey

## Example Sentences
