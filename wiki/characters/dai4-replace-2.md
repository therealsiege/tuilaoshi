# 代 Replace, represent, generation

- **Character**: 代
- **Pinyin**: dài
- **Meaning**: replace,代表, generation
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 代表 (dàibiǎo) — represent, representative
- 代理 (dàilǐ) — agent, act on behalf of

## Example Sentences

- 律师代理诉讼。(Lǜshī dàilǐ sùsòng.) — The lawyer handles the litigation as an agent.

## Notes

Very common for representing and replacing.
