# 募 To raise, to recruit, to collect

- **Character**: 募
- **Pinyin**: mù
- **Meaning**: to raise, to recruit, to collect
- **Radical**: 力(power)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 募捐 (mù juān) — to raise funds, to solicit donations
- 招募 (zhāo mù) — to recruit

## Example Sentences

- 学校正在为贫困学生募捐。(Xuéxiào zhèngzài wèi pínkùn xuésheng mùjuān.) — The school is raising funds for poor students.

## Notes

Often used for fundraising campaigns.
