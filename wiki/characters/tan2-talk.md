# 谈 Talk, discuss, chat

- **Character**: 谈
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: talk, discuss, chat
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 谈判 (tán pàn) — to negotiate
- 会谈 (huì tán) — talks, conference

## Example Sentences

- 双方正在进行和平谈判。(Shuāng fāng zhèng zài jìn xíng hé píng tán pàn.) — Both sides are conducting peace negotiations.

## Notes

Essential for negotiations. 谈话 means to talk. 访谈 means interview. 洽谈 means to discuss business.
