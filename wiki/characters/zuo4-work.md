# 作 Work

- **Character**: 作
- **Pinyin**: zuò
- **Meaning**: to do, to write, work
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 工作 (gōng zuò) — work, job
- 作业 (zuò yè) — homework

## Example Sentences

- 你做什么工作？(Nǐ zuò shén me gōng zuò?) — What do you do for work?

## Notes

作 is more formal/literary than 做. Used in 工作 (work), 作业 (homework), 作文 (essay).
