# 表 Table

- **Character**: 表
- **Pinyin**: biǎo
- **Meaning**: table, form, to express
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothes)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 表格 (biǎo gé) — form, table
- 代表 (dài biǎo) — representative

## Example Sentences

- 请填写这个表格。(Qǐng tián xiě zhè ge biǎo gé.) — Please fill out this form.

## Notes

表 means surface/outside, extended to tables and forms that display information.
