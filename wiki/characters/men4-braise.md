# 焖 To Braise

- **Character**: 焖
- **Pinyin**: mèn
- **Meaning**: to braise, to cook in a covered pot
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 焖饭 (mèn fàn) — braised rice
- 焖鸡 (mèn jī) — braised chicken

## Example Sentences

- 这道焖鸡非常好吃。(Zhè dào mèn jī fēi cháng hǎo chī.) — This braised chicken is delicious.

## Notes

焖 simplified from 燜. Fire 火 plus 闷 (stuffy/sealed), cooking sealed.
