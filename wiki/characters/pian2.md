# 骈 Parallel

- **Character**: 骈
- **Pinyin**: pián
- **Meaning**: parallel, paired
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 骈文 (pián wén) — parallel prose
- 骈体 (pián tǐ) — parallel style

## Example Sentences

- 骈文讲究对偶和声律。(Pián wén jiǎng jiū duì ǒu hé shēng lǜ.) — Parallel prose emphasizes antithesis and tonal patterns.

## Notes

骈 means parallel or paired; 骈文 is a literary style with parallel structures.
