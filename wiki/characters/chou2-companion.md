# 俦 Companion, equal

- **Character**: 俦
- **Pinyin**: chóu
- **Meaning**: companion, equal
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 无与伦比 (wúyǔlúnbǐ) — incomparable, unparalleled
- 俦侣 (chóulǚ) — companion, mate

## Example Sentences

- 他的才华无人可俦。(Tā de cáihuá wú rén kě chóu.) — His talent is unmatched.

## Notes

Literary term for companions or equals, often in negative constructions.
