# 崩 To Collapse

- **Character**: 崩
- **Pinyin**: bēng
- **Meaning**: to collapse, to fall apart
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 崩溃 (bēng kuì) — to collapse, to break down
- 山崩 (shān bēng) — landslide

## Example Sentences

- 他的心理防线完全崩溃了。(Tā de xīn lǐ fáng xiàn wán quán bēng kuì le.) — His psychological defense completely collapsed.

## Notes

崩 uses mountain radical 山, originally describing landslides. Extended to mean any kind of collapse or breakdown.
