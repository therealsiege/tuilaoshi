# 典 Standard, classic, ceremony

- **Character**: 典
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: standard, classic, ceremony
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 典范 (diǎnfàn) — model, paragon
- 经典 (jīngdiǎn) — classic, classical

## Example Sentences

- 这是经典之作。(Zhè shì jīngdiǎn zhī zuò.) — This is a classic work.

## Notes

Refers to standards, classics, or ceremonial books and practices.
