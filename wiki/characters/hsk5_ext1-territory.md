# 域 Territory, Domain, 领域 Field

- **Character**: 域
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: territory, domain, 领域 field
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 领域 (lǐng yù) — field, domain
- 区域 (qū yù) — region, area
- 地域 (dì yù) — region, territory

## Example Sentences

- 他在科研领域取得了重大突破。(Tā zài kēyán lǐngyù qǔdé le zhòngdà tūpò.) — He achieved a major breakthrough in the research field.

## Notes

Common in academic contexts for fields of study. 领域 (field/domain) is essential terminology in research and specialization.
