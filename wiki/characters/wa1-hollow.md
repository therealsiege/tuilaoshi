# 洼 Hollow, low-lying

- **Character**: 洼
- **Pinyin**: wā
- **Meaning**: hollow, low-lying
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 低洼 (dī wā) — low-lying
- 水洼 (shuǐ wā) — puddle

## Example Sentences

- 这块地势低洼，容易积水。(Zhè kuài dì shì dī wā, róng yì jī shuǐ.) — This land is low-lying and prone to water accumulation.

## Notes

Depression in terrain that collects water
