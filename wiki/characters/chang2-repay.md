# 偿 To Repay

- **Character**: 偿
- **Pinyin**: cháng
- **Meaning**: to repay, to compensate
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 赔偿 (péi cháng) — to compensate
- 补偿 (bǔ cháng) — to compensate

## Example Sentences

- 他决定补偿我的损失。(Tā jué dìng bǔ cháng wǒ de sǔn shī.) — He decided to compensate for my losses.

## Notes

偿 simplified from 償. Person 亻 with 赏 (reward), representing repaying what is owed.
