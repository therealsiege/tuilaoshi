# 杆 Rod, pole

- **Character**: 杆
- **Pinyin**: gǎn
- **Meaning**: rod, pole
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 杠杆 (gàng gǎn) — lever
- 连杆 (lián gǎn) — connecting rod

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个简单的杠杆装置。(Zhè shì yī gè jiǎn dān de gàng gǎn zhuāng zhì.) — This is a simple lever device.

## Notes

Used in 杠杆 (lever), 连杆 (connecting rod), mechanical parts
