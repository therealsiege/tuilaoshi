# 褶 Pleat, crease, fold

- **Character**: 褶
- **Pinyin**: zhě
- **Meaning**: pleat, crease, fold
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 褶皱 (zhě zhòu) — wrinkle, crease
- 褶子 (zhě zi) — pleat

## Example Sentences

- 这件衣服有很多褶皱。(Zhè jiàn yī fu yǒu hěn duō zhě zhòu.) — This garment has many wrinkles.

## Notes

Folds in fabric or skin
