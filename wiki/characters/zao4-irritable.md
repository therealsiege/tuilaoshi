# 躁 Irritable

- **Character**: 躁
- **Pinyin**: zào
- **Meaning**: irritable, restless
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 烦躁 (fán zào) — irritable
- 急躁 (jí zào) — impetuous

## Example Sentences

- 她心情很烦躁。(Tā xīn qíng hěn fán zào.) — She is feeling very irritable.

## Notes

躁 combines foot 足 and 喿 (noise), restless pacing.
