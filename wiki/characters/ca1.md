# 擦 To Wipe

- **Character**: 擦
- **Pinyin**: cā
- **Meaning**: to wipe, to rub, to scrape
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 擦拭 (cā shì) — to wipe
- 摩擦 (mó cā) — friction

## Example Sentences

- 请擦干净桌子。(Qǐng cā gān jìng zhuō zi.) — Please wipe the table clean.

## Notes

擦 shows hand 扌 with 察 (examine), representing rubbing or wiping action.
