# 板 Board

- **Character**: 板
- **Pinyin**: bǎn
- **Meaning**: board, plank, beat
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 黑板 (hēi bǎn) — blackboard
- 快板 (kuài bǎn) — clapper talk

## Example Sentences

- 演员打着板子说唱。(Yǎn yuán dǎ zhe bǎn zi shuō chàng.) — The performer narrates while beating the clappers.

## Notes

板 means board; in music refers to beats or rhythm boards.
