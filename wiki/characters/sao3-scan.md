# 扫 Scan, sweep

- **Character**: 扫
- **Pinyin**: sǎo
- **Meaning**: scan, sweep
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 扫描 (sǎo miáo) — scan
- 打扫 (dǎ sǎo) — clean

## Example Sentences

- 扫描二维码。(Sǎo miáo èr wéi mǎ.) — Scan the QR code.

## Notes

Used in 扫描 (scan), 扫描仪 (scanner), 扫码 (scan code)
