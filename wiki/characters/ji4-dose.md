# 剂 Dose, agent, preparation

- **Character**: 剂
- **Pinyin**: jì
- **Meaning**: dose, agent, preparation
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 剂量 (jì liàng) — dosage
- 药剂 (yào jì) — medicine, pharmaceutical

## Example Sentences

- 服药时要注意剂量。(Fú yào shí yào zhùyì jì liàng.) — Pay attention to the dosage when taking medicine.

## Notes

Medicinal preparation or dose. Used as a measure word for doses of medicine (一剂药 - one dose of medicine).
