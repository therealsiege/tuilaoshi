# 校 School

- **Character**: 校
- **Pinyin**: xiào
- **Meaning**: school, military officer, to proofread
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 学校 (xué xiào) — school
- 校长 (xiào zhǎng) — principal

## Example Sentences

- 我们学校很大。(Wǒ men xué xiào hěn dà.) — Our school is very big.

## Notes

校 also read as jiào meaning 'to compare' or 'to proofread'.
