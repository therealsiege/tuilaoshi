# 琴 Musical Instrument

- **Character**: 琴
- **Pinyin**: qín
- **Meaning**: musical instrument, zither, piano
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 钢琴 (gāng qín) — piano
- 小提琴 (xiǎo tí qín) — violin

## Example Sentences

- 她在弹钢琴。(Tā zài tán gāng qín.) — She is playing the piano.

## Notes

琴 means stringed instrument. Used in钢琴 (piano), 小提琴 (violin), 古琴 (guqin).
