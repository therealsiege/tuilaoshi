# 峨 Lofty (of mountain)

- **Character**: 峨
- **Pinyin**: é
- **Meaning**: lofty (of mountain)
- **Radical**: 山 (mountain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 峨眉山 (Éméi Shān) — Mount Emei
- 巍峨 (wēi'é) — lofty, towering

## Example Sentences

- 峨眉山是著名的佛教圣地。(Éméi Shān shì zhùmíng de fójiào shèngdì.) — Mount Emei is a famous Buddhist sacred site.

## Notes

Literary term specifically for lofty mountains, used in Mount Emei.
