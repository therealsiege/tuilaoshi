# 漫 Overflow, free, all over

- **Character**: 漫
- **Pinyin**: màn
- **Meaning**: overflow, free, all over
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 漫长 (màncháng) — very long, endless
- 弥漫 (mímàn) — fill the air, permeate

## Example Sentences

- 等待的时间很漫长。(Děngdài de shíjiān hěn màncháng.) — The waiting time is very long.

## Notes

Describes overflowing, spreading, or casual, free manner.
