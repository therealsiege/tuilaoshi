# 砸 To Smash

- **Character**: 砸
- **Pinyin**: zá
- **Meaning**: to smash, to pound
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 砸碎 (zá suì) — to smash to pieces
- 砸锅 (zá guō) — to fail

## Example Sentences

- 小心别砸到脚。(Xiǎo xīn bié zá dào jiǎo.) — Be careful not to smash your foot.

## Notes

砸 stone 石 plus 匝 (to encircle), heavy striking.
