# 嫉 Jealous

- **Character**: 嫉
- **Pinyin**: jí
- **Meaning**: jealous
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 嫉妒 (jídù) — jealous
- 嫉恨 (jíhèn) — hate out of jealousy

## Example Sentences

- 她嫉妒别人的成功。(Tā jídù biérén de chénggōng.) — She's jealous of others' success.

## Notes

Negative emotion. Almost always appears as 嫉妒. Woman radical has historical context. Modern usage is gender-neutral.
