# 荒 Wasteland, desolate, absurd

- **Character**: 荒
- **Pinyin**: huāng
- **Meaning**: wasteland, desolate, absurd
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 荒谬 (huāng miù) — absurd
- 荒凉 (huāng liáng) — desolate, barren

## Example Sentences

- 这片土地曾经很荒凉。(Zhè piàn tǔ dì céng jīng hěn huāng liáng.) — This land was once very desolate.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: wasteland, desolate, absurd, or neglected. 荒废 means to waste or neglect.
