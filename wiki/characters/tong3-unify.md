# 统 To Unify

- **Character**: 统
- **Pinyin**: tǒng
- **Meaning**: to unify, gather, system
- **Radical**: 糹 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 系统 (xì tǒng) — system
- 传统 (chuán tǒng) — tradition

## Example Sentences

- 这个系统很复杂。(Zhège xìtǒng hěn fùzá.) — This system is very complex.

## Notes

统 combines silk 糹 with 充 (full), suggesting bundling threads together — hence unification.
