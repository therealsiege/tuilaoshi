# 吮 To Suck

- **Character**: 吮
- **Pinyin**: shǔn
- **Meaning**: to suck
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 吮吸 (shǔn xī) — to suck

## Example Sentences

- 婴儿在吮吸母乳。(Yīng ér zài shǔn xī mǔ rǔ.) — The baby is sucking milk.

## Notes

吮 shows mouth 口 and 允 (permit), representing sucking action with the mouth.
