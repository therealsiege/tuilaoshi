# 苦 Bitter

- **Character**: 苦
- **Pinyin**: kǔ
- **Meaning**: bitter, suffering
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 辛苦 (xīn kǔ) — hard work, hardship
- 苦难 (kǔ nàn) — suffering

## Example Sentences

- 这个药很苦。(Zhè ge yào hěn kǔ.) — This medicine is very bitter.

## Notes

苦 with grass radical, many bitter herbs. Also means suffering/hardship. 辛苦了 = you've worked hard.
