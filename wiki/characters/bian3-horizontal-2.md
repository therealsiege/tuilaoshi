# 匾 Horizontal inscribed board

- **Character**: 匾
- **Pinyin**: biǎn
- **Meaning**: horizontal inscribed board
- **Radical**: 匚 (box)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 匾额 (biǎn é) — horizontal inscribed board
- 牌匾 (pái biǎn) — signboard, plaque

## Example Sentences

- 这家老店的匾额是清朝皇帝题写的。(Zhè jiā lǎo diàn de biǎn'é shì Qīng cháo huángdì tí xiě de.) — The plaque of this old shop was inscribed by a Qing Dynasty emperor.

## Notes

Horizontal boards with inscriptions hung above doorways. Often contain shop names or auspicious phrases in calligraphy.
