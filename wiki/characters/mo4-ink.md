# 墨 Ink, black

- **Character**: 墨
- **Pinyin**: mò
- **Meaning**: ink, black
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 墨水 (mò shuǐ) — ink
- 笔墨 (bǐ mò) — pen and ink, writing

## Example Sentences

- 中国书法离不开笔墨纸砚。(Zhōng guó shū fǎ lí bù kāi bǐ mò zhǐ yàn.) — Chinese calligraphy cannot do without brush, ink, paper, and inkstone.

## Notes

Essential material in traditional Chinese arts.
