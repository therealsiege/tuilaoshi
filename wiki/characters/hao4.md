# 号 Number

- **Character**: 号
- **Pinyin**: hào
- **Meaning**: number, date, size
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 号码 (hào mǎ) — number
- 几号 (jǐ hào) — what date/number

## Example Sentences

- 今天几号？(Jīn tiān jǐ hào?) — What is today's date?

## Notes

号 is the colloquial way to say the date of the month (compared to more formal 日). Also used for sizes: 大号 (large size).
