# 财 Wealth, finance

- **Character**: 财
- **Pinyin**: cái
- **Meaning**: wealth, finance
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 财产 (cái chǎn) — property, assets
- 财务 (cái wù) — finance, financial affairs

## Example Sentences

- 财务部负责公司账目。(Cái wù bù fù zé gōng sī zhàng mù.) — The finance department is responsible for company accounts.

## Notes

财政 is public finance. 理财 means financial management.
