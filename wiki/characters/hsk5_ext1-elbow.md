# 肘 Elbow, 胳膊肘 Elbow

- **Character**: 肘
- **Pinyin**: zhǒu
- **Meaning**: elbow, 胳膊肘 elbow
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 胳膊肘 (gē bo zhǒu) — elbow
- 肘部 (zhǒu bù) — elbow area
- 掣肘 (chè zhǒu) — hinder, hamper

## Example Sentences

- 他用肘部碰了我一下。(Tā yòng zhǒubù pèng le wǒ yīxià.) — He nudged me with his elbow.

## Notes

Specific body part term for elbow. 胳膊肘 is the full colloquial term. Can be used figuratively for constraints or obstacles.
