# 单 — Single / Simple

- **Character**: 单
- **Pinyin**: dān
- **Meaning**: single, alone; simple; a list, a sheet
- **Radical**: 十 (ten)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 简单 (jiǎndān) — simple, easy
- 单词 (dāncí) — vocabulary word
- 单位 (dānwèi) — work unit; unit (of measurement)
- 单独 (dāndú) — alone, by oneself
- 菜单 (càidān) — menu
- 单人 (dānrén) — single person, solo

## Example Sentences

- 这些单词你都认识吗？(Zhèxiē dāncí nǐ dōu rènshi ma?) — Do you know all these vocabulary words?
- 请给我一份菜单。(Qǐng gěi wǒ yī fèn càidān.) — Please give me a menu.
- 我想单独跟你谈谈。(Wǒ xiǎng dāndú gēn nǐ tántán.) — I'd like to talk with you alone.

## Notes

单 (simplified from 單) originally depicted a forked hunting weapon, but its modern meanings have diverged entirely from this origin. As an adjective, it means "single" or "alone" — the opposite of 双 (shuāng, double/pair). As a noun, it means "list" or "sheet," as in 菜单 (menu, literally "dish list") and 名单 (míngdān, name list). In 简单, the 单 contributes the idea of "uncomplex" — something that stands alone without layers. For language learners, 单词 (vocabulary word, literally "single word") is an essential term you will encounter in every Chinese class.
