# 汹 Turbulent, noisy

- **Character**: 汹
- **Pinyin**: xiōng
- **Meaning**: turbulent, noisy
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 汹涌 (xiōng yǒng) — turbulent, surging
- 气势汹汹 (qì shì xiōng xiōng) — menacing

## Example Sentences

- 洪水汹涌而来。(Hóng shuǐ xiōng yǒng ér lái.) — The flood waters came surging.

## Notes

Turbulent water or aggressive manner
