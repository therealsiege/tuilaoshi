# 广 Wide

- **Character**: 广
- **Pinyin**: guǎng
- **Meaning**: wide, broad, extensive
- **Radical**: 广 (shelter)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 广告 (guǎng gào) — advertisement
- 广大 (guǎng dà) — vast

## Example Sentences

- 这个广场很大。(Zhè ge guǎng chǎng hěn dà.) — This square is very big.

## Notes

广 is simplified from 廣. 广州 = Guangzhou, 广场 = plaza/square.
