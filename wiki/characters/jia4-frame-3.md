# 架 Frame

- **Character**: 架
- **Pinyin**: jià
- **Meaning**: frame, shelf, to support
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 书架 (shū jià) — bookshelf
- 架子 (jià zi) — frame

## Example Sentences

- 他在书架上找书。(Tā zài shū jià shàng zhǎo shū.) — He looks for books on the bookshelf.

## Notes

架 has wood 木 components forming a supporting structure.
