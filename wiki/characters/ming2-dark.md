# 冥 Dark

- **Character**: 冥
- **Pinyin**: míng
- **Meaning**: dark, deep, netherworld
- **Radical**: 冖 (cover)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 冥想 (míng xiǎng) — to meditate
- 幽冥 (yōu míng) — netherworld

## Example Sentences

- 他在冥想中获得平静。(Tā zài míng xiǎng zhōng huò dé píng jìng.) — He finds peace in meditation.

## Notes

冥 has cover 冖 over sun 日, deep obscurity or the underworld.
