# 抖 To Shake

- **Character**: 抖
- **Pinyin**: dǒu
- **Meaning**: to shake, to tremble
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 颤抖 (chàn dǒu) — to shiver
- 发抖 (fā dǒu) — to tremble

## Example Sentences

- 他紧张得发抖。(Tā jǐn zhāng de fā dǒu.) — He trembled with nervousness.

## Notes

抖 shows hand 扌 and斗 (dipper), representing shaking or vibrating motion.
