# 购 Purchase, buy

- **Character**: 购
- **Pinyin**: gòu
- **Meaning**: purchase, buy
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 购买 (gòumǎi) — purchase, buy
- 采购 (cǎigòu) — purchase, procurement

## Example Sentences

- 公司采购了大量设备。(Gōngsī cǎigòu le dàliàng shèbèi.) — The company purchased a large amount of equipment.

## Notes

Common character for purchasing in business and retail contexts.
