# 盐 Salt

- **Character**: 盐
- **Pinyin**: yán
- **Meaning**: salt
- **Radical**: 皿(dish)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 食盐 (shí yán) — table salt
- 盐分 (yán fèn) — salt content

## Example Sentences

- 化学中的盐是酸和碱反应的产物。(Huàxué zhōng de yán shì suān hé jiǎn fǎnyìng de chǎnwù.) — In chemistry, salts are products of acid-base reactions.

## Notes

盐 means salt, both common table salt and chemical salts formed from acids and bases.
