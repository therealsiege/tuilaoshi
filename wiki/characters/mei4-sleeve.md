# 袂 Sleeve

- **Character**: 袂
- **Pinyin**: mèi
- **Meaning**: sleeve
- **Radical**: 衣 (clothing)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 联袂 (lián mèi) — jointly, hand in hand
- 分袂 (fēn mèi) — part, separate

## Example Sentences

- 两位艺术家联袂演出。(Liǎng wèi yì shù jiā lián mèi yǎn chū.) — Two artists perform jointly.

## Notes

Classical term for sleeve, used in formal expressions
