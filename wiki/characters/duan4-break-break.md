# 断 To Break

- **Character**: 断
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: to break, to cut off, to judge
- **Radical**: 斤 (axe)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 不断 (bú duàn) — continuously
- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge

## Example Sentences

- 他不断地学习。(Tā bú duàn de xué xí.) — He studies continuously.

## Notes

断 means to break or sever. 不断 = continuously (not breaking), 中断 = interrupt.
