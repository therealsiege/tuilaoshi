# 沾 To Moisten

- **Character**: 沾
- **Pinyin**: zhān
- **Meaning**: to moisten, to touch, to stain
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 沾光 (zhān guāng) — to benefit from association
- 沾染 (zhān rǎn) — to be contaminated

## Example Sentences

- 别让衣服沾上水。(Bié ràng yī fú zhān shàng shuǐ.) — Don't let the clothes get wet.

## Notes

沾 combines water 氵 and 占 (to occupy), liquid occupying a surface.
