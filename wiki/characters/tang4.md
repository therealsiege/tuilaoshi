# 烫 Scald

- **Character**: 烫
- **Pinyin**: tàng
- **Meaning**: scald, burn
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烫伤 (tàng shāng) — scald, burn
- 滚烫 (gǔn tàng) — boiling hot
- 烫手 (tàng shǒu) — burning hot to touch

## Example Sentences

- 开水烫伤了手。(Kāi shuǐ tàng shāng le shǒu.) — Boiling water scalded my hand.

## Notes

Hot liquid burns; common household injury.
