# 拽 Pull

- **Character**: 拽
- **Pinyin**: zhuài
- **Meaning**: pull, drag
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 拽住 (zhuài zhù) — grab hold of
- 拽着 (zhuàizhe) — pulling

## Example Sentences

- 他拽着我的袖子。(Tā zhuàizhe wǒ de xiùzi.) — He's pulling my sleeve.

## Notes

Forceful pulling. Hand radical. More forceful than 拉. Common in describing physical actions.
