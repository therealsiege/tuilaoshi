# 氢 Hydrogen

- **Character**: 氢
- **Pinyin**: qīng
- **Meaning**: hydrogen
- **Radical**: 气 (gas)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 氢气 (qīng qì) — hydrogen gas
- 氢弹 (qīng dàn) — hydrogen bomb

## Example Sentences

- 氢是宇宙中最丰富的元素。(Qīng shì yǔ zhòu zhōng zuì fēng fù de yuán sù.) — Hydrogen is the most abundant element in the universe.

## Notes

Lightest chemical element, highly flammable
