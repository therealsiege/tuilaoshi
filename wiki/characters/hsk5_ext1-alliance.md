# 盟 Alliance, Oath, 联盟 Alliance

- **Character**: 盟
- **Pinyin**: méng
- **Meaning**: alliance, oath, 联盟 alliance
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 联盟 (lián méng) — alliance, league
- 同盟 (tóng méng) — alliance
- 盟友 (méng yǒu) — ally

## Example Sentences

- 这些国家组成了军事联盟。(Zhèxiē guójiā zǔchéng le jūnshì liánméng.) — These countries formed a military alliance.

## Notes

Used in political and international relations contexts for alliances and coalitions between nations or organizations.
