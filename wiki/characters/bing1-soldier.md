# 兵 Soldier

- **Character**: 兵
- **Pinyin**: bīng
- **Meaning**: soldier, military, weapon
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 士兵 (shì bīng) — soldier
- 兵器 (bīng qì) — weapon

## Example Sentences

- 他是一名士兵。(Tā shì yī míng shì bīng.) — He is a soldier.

## Notes

兵 originally meant weapon, then extended to soldier. 纸上谈兵 = armchair strategist.
