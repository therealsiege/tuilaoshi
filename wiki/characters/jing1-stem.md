# 茎 Stem, stalk

- **Character**: 茎
- **Pinyin**: jīng
- **Meaning**: stem, stalk
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 茎干 (jīng gàn) — stem, stalk
- 根茎 (gēn jīng) — rhizome

## Example Sentences

- 植物的茎支撑叶子和花朵。(Zhí wù de jīng zhī chēng yè zi hé huā duo.) — Plant stems support leaves and flowers.
