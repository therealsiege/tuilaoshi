# 潜 To hide, submerge, potential, 潛 (traditional)

- **Character**: 潜
- **Pinyin**: qián
- **Meaning**: to hide, submerge, potential, 潛 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 潜力 (qián lì) — potential
- 潜水 (qián shuǐ) — to dive

## Example Sentences

- 他很有潜力。(Tā hěn yǒu qiánlì.) — He has great potential.

## Notes

To hide/submerge or latent potential.
