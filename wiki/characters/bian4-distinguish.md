# 辨 To Distinguish

- **Character**: 辨
- **Pinyin**: biàn
- **Meaning**: to distinguish, to differentiate
- **Radical**: 辛 (bitter)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 辨别 (biàn bié) — to distinguish
- 分辨 (fēn biàn) — to differentiate

## Example Sentences

- 你能辨别真假吗？(Nǐ néng biàn bié zhēn jiǎ ma?) — Can you distinguish real from fake?

## Notes

辨 means to distinguish. Similar to 辩 (argue) and 瓣 (petal) — commonly confused.
