# 你 You

- **Character**: 你
- **Pinyin**: nǐ
- **Meaning**: you
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 你好 (nǐ hǎo) — hello
- 你们 (nǐ men) — you (plural)

## Example Sentences

- 你是哪里人？(Nǐ shì nǎ lǐ rén?) — Where are you from?

## Notes

你 is informal 'you.' The polite form is 您 (nín), used with elders and in formal situations.
