# 嘴 Mouth

- **Character**: 嘴
- **Pinyin**: zuǐ
- **Meaning**: mouth
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 嘴巴 (zuǐba) — mouth
- 张嘴 (zhāng zuǐ) — open mouth

## Example Sentences

- 请张开嘴巴。(Qǐng zhāngkāi zuǐba.) — Please open your mouth.

## Notes

Common colloquial word for mouth. The radical 口 indicates it relates to the mouth or speaking.
