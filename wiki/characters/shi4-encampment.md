# 室 Encampment

- **Character**: 室
- **Pinyin**: shì
- **Meaning**: Encampment constellation, room
- **Radical**: 宀(roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 室宿 (shì xiù) — Encampment constellation
- 室内 (shì nèi) — indoor

## Example Sentences

- 室宿象征着军队的营房。(Shì xiù xiàngzhēng zhe jūnduì de yíngfáng.) — Encampment constellation symbolizes military barracks.

## Notes

室 means room or chamber. In astronomy, the Encampment constellation, representing a sheltered dwelling.
