# 恋 To love, to feel attached to

- **Character**: 恋
- **Pinyin**: liàn
- **Meaning**: to love, to feel attached to
- **Radical**: 心(heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恋爱 (liàn ài) — to be in love, romantic love
- 留恋 (liú liàn) — to be reluctant to leave

## Example Sentences

- 他们正在恋爱。(Tāmen zhèngzài liàn'ài.) — They are in love.

## Notes

Used for romantic love or strong attachment.
