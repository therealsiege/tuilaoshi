# 判 Judge

- **Character**: 判
- **Pinyin**: pàn
- **Meaning**: to judge, decide
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 裁判 (cái pàn) — referee, judge
- 判断 (pàn duàn) — to judge

## Example Sentences

- 裁判的判决很公正。(Cáipàn de pànjué hěn gōngzhèng.) — The referee's decision is very fair.

Judging; referee decisions
