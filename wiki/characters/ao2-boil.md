# 熬 To boil, to simmer, to endure

- **Character**: 熬
- **Pinyin**: áo
- **Meaning**: to boil, to simmer, to endure
- **Radical**: 火 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 熬夜 (áo yè) — to stay up late
- 熬粥 (áo zhōu) — to cook porridge

## Example Sentences

- 熬粥要用小火慢慢煮。(Áo zhōu yào yòng xiǎo huǒ mànman zhǔ.) — To cook porridge, you need to simmer slowly on low heat.

## Notes

Implies long cooking time or enduring hardship. 熬夜 is very common.
