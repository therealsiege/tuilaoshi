# 韵 Rhyme

- **Character**: 韵
- **Pinyin**: yùn
- **Meaning**: rhyme, charm
- **Radical**: 音 (sound)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 韵味 (yùn wèi) — charm, appeal
- 押韵 (yā yùn) — to rhyme

## Example Sentences

- 这首诗很有韵味。(Zhè shǒu shī hěn yǒu yùn wèi.) — This poem has great charm.

## Notes

韵 uses sound radical 音, relating to phonetic rhyme in poetry and metaphorical charm or appeal.
