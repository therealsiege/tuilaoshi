# 舞 Dance

- **Character**: 舞
- **Pinyin**: wǔ
- **Meaning**: to dance, dance
- **Radical**: 夕 (evening)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — to dance
- 舞蹈 (wǔ dǎo) — dance

## Example Sentences

- 她跳舞跳得很好。(Tā tiào wǔ tiào de hěn hǎo.) — She dances very well.

## Notes

舞 means to dance or dance performance; also used metaphorically.
