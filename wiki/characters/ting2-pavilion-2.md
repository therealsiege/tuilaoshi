# 亭 Pavilion

- **Character**: 亭
- **Pinyin**: tíng
- **Meaning**: pavilion, kiosk
- **Radical**: 亠 (lid)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 凉亭 (liáng tíng) — pavilion
- 书亭 (shū tíng) — bookstall

## Example Sentences

- 我们在凉亭里休息。(Wǒ men zài liáng tíng lǐ xiū xī.) — We rest in the pavilion.

## Notes

亭 originally meant roadside shelter, now for decorative open pavilions.
