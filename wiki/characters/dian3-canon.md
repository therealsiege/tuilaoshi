# 典 Canon, classic, ceremony

- **Character**: 典
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: canon, classic, ceremony
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 典礼 (diǎn lǐ) — ceremony
- 经典 (jīng diǎn) — classic

## Example Sentences

- 这是一部经典著作。(Zhè shì yī bù jīng diǎn zhù zuò.) — This is a classic work.

## Notes

Represents authoritative texts and important ceremonies
