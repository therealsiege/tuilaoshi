# 澈 Clear, transparent

- **Character**: 澈
- **Pinyin**: chè
- **Meaning**: clear, transparent
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 清澈 (qīng chè) — clear, limpid
- 澄澈 (chéng chè) — clear, transparent

## Example Sentences

- 湖水清澈见底。(Hú shuǐ qīngchè jiàn dǐ.) — The lake water is crystal clear to the bottom.

## Notes

Crystal clear transparency, especially of water.
