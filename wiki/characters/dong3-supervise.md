# 董 Supervise, director

- **Character**: 董
- **Pinyin**: dǒng
- **Meaning**: supervise, director
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 董事 (dǒng shì) — director (of board)
- 董事长 (dǒng shì zhǎng) — chairman of the board

## Example Sentences

- 他是这家公司的董事。(Tā shì zhè jiā gōng sī de dǒng shì.) — He is a director of this company.

## Notes

Common in corporate titles. 董事会 (board of directors) is a key business term.
