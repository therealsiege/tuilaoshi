# 合 To Combine

- **Character**: 合
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: to combine, to suit, together
- **Radical**: 人 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 合作 (hé zuò) — cooperation
- 结合 (jié hé) — to combine

## Example Sentences

- 我们需要合作才能成功。(Wǒ men xū yào hé zuò cái néng chéng gōng.) — We need cooperation to succeed.

## Notes

合 mouth 口 closing together, uniting.
