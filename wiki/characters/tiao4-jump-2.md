# 跳 Jump

- **Character**: 跳
- **Pinyin**: tiào
- **Meaning**: jump, leap
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 跳舞 (tiào wǔ) — dance
- 跳高 (tiào gāo) — high jump

## Example Sentences

- 她喜欢跳舞。(Tā xǐhuan tiào wǔ.) — She likes to dance.

## Notes

Jumping or leaping. Foot radical. 跳舞 is dancing. Common movement and sports verb.
