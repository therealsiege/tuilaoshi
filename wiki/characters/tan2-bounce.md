# 弹 Bounce

- **Character**: 弹
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: to bounce, spring
- **Radical**: 弓 (bow)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 弹跳 (tán tiào) — to bounce, jump
- 反弹 (fǎn tán) — to rebound

## Example Sentences

- 篮球在地上弹了几下。(Lánqiú zài dì shàng tán le jǐ xià.) — The basketball bounced on the ground a few times.

## Notes

Bouncing and springing; ball movement
