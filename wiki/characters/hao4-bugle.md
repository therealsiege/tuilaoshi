# 号 Bugle

- **Character**: 号
- **Pinyin**: hào
- **Meaning**: bugle, number, mark
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 号角 (hào jiǎo) — bugle
- 军号 (jūn hào) — military bugle

## Example Sentences

- 吹号手吹响了集合号。(Chuī hào shǒu chuī xiǎng le jí hé hào.) — The bugler sounded the assembly call.

## Notes

号 as a musical instrument refers to brass horns; also means number or mark.
