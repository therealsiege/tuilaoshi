# 刻 Quarter

- **Character**: 刻
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: quarter (hour), to carve
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 一刻钟 (yī kè zhōng) — quarter hour
- 深刻 (shēn kè) — profound

## Example Sentences

- 现在三点一刻。(Xiàn zài sān diǎn yī kè.) — It's 3:15 now.

## Notes

刻 means quarter hour (15 minutes). 刻苦 = hardworking. 雕刻 = to carve.
