# 甲 Armor, shell, first

- **Character**: 甲
- **Pinyin**: jiǎ
- **Meaning**: armor, shell, first
- **Radical**: 田 (field)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 盔甲 (kuī jiǎ) — armor
- 甲等 (jiǎ děng) — first-class, grade A

## Example Sentences

- 古代士兵穿着盔甲上战场。(Gǔdài shìbīng chuānzhe kuījiǎ shàng zhànchǎng.) — Ancient soldiers wore armor to the battlefield.

## Notes

Means armor or shell. Also the first in the series of Heavenly Stems, representing first or grade A.
