# 靛 Indigo

- **Character**: 靛
- **Pinyin**: diàn
- **Meaning**: indigo
- **Radical**: 青 (blue/green)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 靛蓝 (diàn lán) — indigo blue
- 靛青 (diàn qīng) — indigo

## Example Sentences

- 这块布染成了靛蓝色。(Zhè kuài bù rǎn chéng le diàn lán sè.) — This cloth was dyed indigo blue.

## Notes

Traditional natural dye color.
