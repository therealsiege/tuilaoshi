# 别 Don't

- **Character**: 别
- **Pinyin**: bié
- **Meaning**: don't, other, to leave
- **Radical**: 刂 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 别的 (bié de) — other
- 特别 (tè bié) — especially

## Example Sentences

- 别着急！(Bié zháo jí!) — Don't worry!

## Notes

别 + verb means 'don't (do something).' 别的 means 'other.' 特别 means 'especially/special.'
