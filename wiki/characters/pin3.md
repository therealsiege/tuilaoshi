# 品 Taste, 品, 品品

- **Character**: 品
- **Pinyin**: pǐn
- **Meaning**: taste,品, 品品
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 品尝 (pǐn cháng) — taste, sample
- 品茶 (pǐn chá) — savor tea

## Example Sentences

- 他细细品味着这杯香茗。(Tā xì xì pǐn wèi zhe zhè bēi xiāng míng.) — He savored this cup of fragrant tea carefully.
