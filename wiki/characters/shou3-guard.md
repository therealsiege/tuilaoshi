# 守 To Guard

- **Character**: 守
- **Pinyin**: shǒu
- **Meaning**: to guard, to defend
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 守护 (shǒu hù) — to guard
- 遵守 (zūn shǒu) — to observe (rules)

## Example Sentences

- 我们要遵守规则。(Wǒ men yào zūn shǒu guī zé.) — We must observe the rules.

## Notes

守 shows a hand 寸 under a roof 宀, representing staying at home to guard and protect it.
