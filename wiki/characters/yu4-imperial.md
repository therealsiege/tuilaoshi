# 谕 Edict

- **Character**: 谕
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: imperial edict, to inform
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 教谕 (jiào yù) — to instruct
- 明谕 (míng yù) — clear instruction

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝下谕给大臣。(Huáng dì xià yù gěi dà chén.) — The emperor issued an edict to his ministers.

## Notes

谕 uses speech radical 讠, indicating authoritative instruction or command from superior to subordinate.
