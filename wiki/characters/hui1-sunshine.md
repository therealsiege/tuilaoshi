# 晖 Sunshine, radiance

- **Character**: 晖
- **Pinyin**: huī
- **Meaning**: sunshine, radiance
- **Radical**: 日 (sun)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 春晖 (chūn huī) — spring sunshine
- 朝晖 (zhāo huī) — morning sunshine

## Example Sentences

- 谁言寸草心，报得三春晖。(Shuí yán cùn cǎo xīn, bào dé sān chūn huī.) — How can the heart of an inch of grass repay the warmth of three spring months of sunshine?

## Notes

Poetic term for sunlight; famous from Meng Jiao's poem.
