# 典 Classic, standard, pawn

- **Character**: 典
- **Pinyin**: diǎn
- **Meaning**: classic, standard, pawn
- **Radical**: 八 (eight)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 典型 (diǎn xíng) — typical, model
- 典礼 (diǎn lǐ) — ceremony

## Example Sentences

- 这是典型的商业模式。(Zhè shì diǎn xíng de shāng yè mó shì.) — This is a typical business model.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: classic texts, standard, or pawn. 字典 means dictionary. 典当 means to pawn.
