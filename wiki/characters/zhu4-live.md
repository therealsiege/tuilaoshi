# 住 To Live

- **Character**: 住
- **Pinyin**: zhù
- **Meaning**: to live, to reside, to stop
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 住在 (zhù zài) — to live at
- 记住 (jì zhù) — to remember

## Example Sentences

- 你住在哪里？(Nǐ zhù zài nǎ lǐ?) — Where do you live?

## Notes

住 as a result complement means 'firmly, securely': 拿住 (hold firmly), 记住 (remember).
