# 琢 Carve, chisel, polish

- **Character**: 琢
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: carve, chisel, polish
- **Radical**: 玉 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 琢磨 (zhuó mó) — polish, refine
- 雕琢 (diāo zhuó) — carve and polish

## Example Sentences

- 这件玉器琢磨得非常精细。(Zhè jiàn yù qì zhuó mó de fēi cháng jīng xì.) — This jade article is polished very finely.

## Notes

Used for carving jade or refining literary works.
