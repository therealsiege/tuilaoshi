# 鸭 Duck

- **Character**: 鸭
- **Pinyin**: yā
- **Meaning**: duck
- **Radical**: 鸟 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 鸭子 (yā zi) — duck
- 烤鸭 (kǎo yā) — roast duck

## Example Sentences

- 北京烤鸭很有名。(Běijīng kǎoyā hěn yǒumíng.) — Beijing roast duck is very famous.

## Notes

Important in Chinese cuisine. 北京烤鸭 is a famous dish.
