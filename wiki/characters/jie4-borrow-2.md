# 借 To borrow, to lend

- **Character**: 借
- **Pinyin**: jiè
- **Meaning**: to borrow, to lend
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 借钱 (jiè qián) — to borrow/lend money
- 借口 (jiè kǒu) — excuse, pretext

## Example Sentences

- 我能借你的笔用一下吗？(Wǒ néng jiè nǐ de bǐ yòng yīxià ma?) — Can I borrow your pen?

## Notes

Can mean both to borrow and to lend depending on context.
