# 涤 To wash, cleanse, purge, 滌 (traditional)

- **Character**: 涤
- **Pinyin**: dí
- **Meaning**: to wash, cleanse, purge, 滌 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 洗涤 (xǐ dí) — to wash, cleanse
- 涤荡 (dí dàng) — to wash away, cleanse

## Example Sentences

- 用清水洗涤衣物。(Yòng qīngshuǐ xǐdí yīwù.) — Wash clothes with clean water.

## Notes

Formal term for washing or cleansing, often metaphorical.
