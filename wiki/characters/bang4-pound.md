# 磅 Pound

- **Character**: 磅
- **Pinyin**: bàng
- **Meaning**: pound (weight), scale
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 磅秤 (bàng chèng) — platform scale
- 英磅 (yīng bàng) — British pound

## Example Sentences

- 这个包裹有多少磅？(Zhè ge bāo guǒ yǒu duō shǎo bàng?) — How many pounds is this package?

## Notes

磅 is a transliteration for pound (weight and currency).
