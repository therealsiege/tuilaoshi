# 笛 Flute, pipe

- **Character**: 笛
- **Pinyin**: dí
- **Meaning**: flute, pipe
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 笛子 (dí zi) — bamboo flute
- 长笛 (cháng dí) — flute

## Example Sentences

- 牧童吹着笛子赶着羊群。(Mù tóng chuī zhe dí zi gǎn zhe yáng qún.) — The shepherd boy plays the flute while herding sheep.

## Notes

Generic term for flutes, including bamboo and Western flutes.
