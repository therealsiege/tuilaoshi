# 窟 Cave, cavern, hole

- **Character**: 窟
- **Pinyin**: kū
- **Meaning**: cave, cavern, hole
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 洞窟 (dòng kū) — cave, cavern
- 石窟 (shí kū) — grotto, rock cave

## Example Sentences

- 古人在石窟中留下了壁画。(Gǔ rén zài shí kū zhōng liú xià le bì huà.) — Ancient people left murals in the grottoes.
