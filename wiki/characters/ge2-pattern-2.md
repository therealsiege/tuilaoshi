# 格 Pattern

- **Character**: 格
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: pattern, standard, style
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 格律 (gé lǜ) — meter (poetry)
- 风格 (fēng gé) — style

## Example Sentences

- 律诗有严格的格律。(Lǜ shī yǒu yán gé de gé lǜ.) — Regulated verse has strict meter.

## Notes

格 means pattern, standard, or style; in 格律 refers to poetic meter.
