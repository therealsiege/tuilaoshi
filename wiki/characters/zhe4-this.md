# 这 This

- **Character**: 这
- **Pinyin**: zhè
- **Meaning**: this
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 这里 (zhè lǐ) — here
- 这个 (zhè ge) — this one

## Example Sentences

- 这是什么？(Zhè shì shénme?) — What is this?

## Notes

这 is simplified from the traditional 這. It pairs with 那 (that).
