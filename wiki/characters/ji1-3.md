# 激 Excite, activate, stimulate

- **Character**: 激
- **Pinyin**: jī
- **Meaning**: 激 激活, stimulate
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 16

## Compounds

- 激光 (jī guāng) — laser
- 激活 (jī huó) — activate

## Example Sentences

- 激光通信很先进。(Jī guāng tōng xìn hěn xiān jìn.) — Laser communication is advanced.

## Notes

Used in 激光 (laser), 激活 (activate), 激活函数 (activation function)
