# 寡 Few, widowed

- **Character**: 寡
- **Pinyin**: guǎ
- **Meaning**: few, widowed
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 寡言 (guǎyán) — taciturn, of few words
- 寡妇 (guǎfù) — widow

## Example Sentences

- 他平时沉默寡言。(Tā píngshí chénmò guǎyán.) — He is usually taciturn.

## Notes

Literary term for fewness or widowhood.
