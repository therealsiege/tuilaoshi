# 宅 Residence, house

- **Character**: 宅
- **Pinyin**: zhái
- **Meaning**: residence, house
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 住宅 (zhù zhái) — residence, housing
- 宅院 (zhái yuàn) — house with courtyard

## Example Sentences

- 这是高档住宅区。(Zhè shì gāo dàng zhù zhái qū.) — This is a high-end residential area.

## Notes

宅基地 is homestead land. 豪宅 means luxury house.
