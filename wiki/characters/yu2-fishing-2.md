# 渔 Fishing

- **Character**: 渔
- **Pinyin**: yú
- **Meaning**: fishing, fisherman
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 渔民 (yú mín) — fisherman
- 渔鼓 (yú gǔ) — fishing drum (narrative)

## Example Sentences

- 渔鼓是道教说唱艺术。(Yú gǔ shì dào jiào shuō chàng yì shù.) — Yugu is a Taoist narrative art form.

## Notes

渔 means fishing; 渔鼓 is narrative singing with a bamboo drum.
