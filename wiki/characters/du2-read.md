# 读 To Read

- **Character**: 读
- **Pinyin**: dú
- **Meaning**: to read, to study
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 读书 (dú shū) — to study, to read
- 阅读 (yuè dú) — to read

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢读书。(Wǒ xǐ huān dú shū.) — I like to read.

## Notes

读 uses the speech radical 讠. 读书 literally means 'read books' but generally means 'to study.'
