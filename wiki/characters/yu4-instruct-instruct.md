# 谕 Edict

- **Character**: 谕
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: to instruct, edict
- **Radical**: 讠(speech)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 教谕 (jiào yù) — to instruct
- 谕旨 (yù zhǐ) — imperial edict

## Example Sentences

- 皇帝颁布了圣谕。(Huáng dì bān bù le shèng yù.) — The emperor issued an edict.

## Notes

谕 simplified from 諭. Speech 讠 with 俞 (to agree), authoritative instruction.
