# 姐 Older Sister

- **Character**: 姐
- **Pinyin**: jiě
- **Meaning**: older sister
- **Radical**: 女 (woman)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 姐姐 (jiě jie) — older sister
- 大姐 (dà jiě) — eldest sister

## Example Sentences

- 她是我姐姐。(Tā shì wǒ jiě jie.) — She is my older sister.

## Notes

姐 with woman radical 女. Reduplicates to 姐姐. Can respectfully address older women.
