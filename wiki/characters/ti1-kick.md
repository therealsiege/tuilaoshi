# 踢 Kick

- **Character**: 踢
- **Pinyin**: tī
- **Meaning**: kick
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 踢球 (tī qiú) — play football, kick ball
- 踢开 (tī kāi) — kick open

## Example Sentences

- 他喜欢踢足球。(Tā xǐhuan tī zúqiú.) — He likes to play football.

## Notes

Kicking action. Foot radical. 踢球 is very common for playing soccer. Sports vocabulary.
