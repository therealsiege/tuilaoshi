# 客 Guest

- **Character**: 客
- **Pinyin**: kè
- **Meaning**: guest, customer, visitor
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 客人 (kè rén) — guest
- 顾客 (gù kè) — customer

## Example Sentences

- 家里来客人了。(Jiā lǐ lái kè rén le.) — Guests have come to the house.

## Notes

客 means guest or visitor. 请客 = to treat someone to a meal. 客气 = polite.
