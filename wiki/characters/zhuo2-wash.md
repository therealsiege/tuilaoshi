# 濯 To wash, rinse

- **Character**: 濯
- **Pinyin**: zhuó
- **Meaning**: to wash, rinse
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 洗濯 (xǐ zhuó) — to wash, launder
- 濯足 (zhuó zú) — to wash feet

## Example Sentences

- 清水濯濯，可以洗脚。(Qīngshuǐ zhuózhuó, kěyǐ xǐjiǎo.) — The clear water can wash feet.

## Notes

Literary term for washing or rinsing, especially in classical texts.
