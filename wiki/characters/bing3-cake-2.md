# 饼 Cake

- **Character**: 饼
- **Pinyin**: bǐng
- **Meaning**: cake, pancake, cookie
- **Radical**: 饣 (food)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 画饼充饥 (huà bǐng chōng jī) — feed on illusions
- 月饼 (yuè bǐng) — mooncake

## Example Sentences

- 空谈理想等于画饼充饥。(Kōngtán lǐxiǎng děngyú huà bǐng chōng jī.) — Empty talk about ideals is like drawing a cake to satisfy hunger.

## Notes

Used in 画饼充饥: the cake that is only drawn, not real; using the food radical 饣.
