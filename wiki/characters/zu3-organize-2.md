# 组 Group

- **Character**: 组
- **Pinyin**: zǔ
- **Meaning**: to organize, group, team
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 小组 (xiǎo zǔ) — small group, team
- 组织 (zǔ zhī) — organization

## Example Sentences

- 我们分成几个小组。(Wǒ men fēn chéng jǐ ge xiǎo zǔ.) — We divided into several small groups.

## Notes

组 is simplified from 組. The silk radical suggests weaving threads together.
