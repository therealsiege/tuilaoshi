# 订 Subscribe, book, revise

- **Character**: 订
- **Pinyin**: dìng
- **Meaning**: subscribe, book, revise
- **Radical**: 讠 (speech)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 订阅 (dìng yuè) — to subscribe
- 预订 (yù dìng) — to book, reserve

## Example Sentences

- 我订阅了几份专业期刊。(Wǒ dìng yuè le jǐ fèn zhuān yè qī kān.) — I subscribed to several professional journals.

## Notes

Multiple meanings: to订 subscribe, to book, to revise. 订正 means to make corrections.
