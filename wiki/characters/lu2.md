# 颅 Skull

- **Character**: 颅
- **Pinyin**: lú
- **Meaning**: skull
- **Radical**: 页 (head)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 头颅 (tóulú) — head, skull
- 颅骨 (lúgǔ) — cranium, skull

## Example Sentences

- 医生检查了他的颅骨损伤。(Yīshēng jiǎnchá le tā de lúgǔ sǔnshāng.) — The doctor examined his skull injury.

## Notes

Medical and anatomical term for the skull or cranium.
