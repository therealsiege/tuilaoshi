# 疗 To treat, cure, 療 (traditional)

- **Character**: 疗
- **Pinyin**: liáo
- **Meaning**: to treat, cure, 療 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 疒 (sickness)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 治疗 (zhì liáo) — to treat, cure
- 疗养 (liáo yǎng) — to recuperate

## Example Sentences

- 这种病很难治疗。(Zhè zhǒng bìng hěn nán zhìliáo.) — This disease is difficult to treat.

## Notes

Medical treatment and therapy.
