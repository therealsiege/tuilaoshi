# 献 To Offer

- **Character**: 献
- **Pinyin**: xiàn
- **Meaning**: to offer, dedicate
- **Radical**: 犬 (dog)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 贡献 (gòng xiàn) — contribution
- 奉献 (fèng xiàn) — to dedicate, devote

## Example Sentences

- 他把一生都奉献给了教育事业。(Tā bǎ yīshēng dōu fèngxiàn gěi le jiàoyù shìyè.) — He dedicated his whole life to education.

## Notes

献 originally showed offering an animal sacrifice, now means to献, present, or dedicate.
