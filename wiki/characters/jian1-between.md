# 间 Between

- **Character**: 间
- **Pinyin**: jiān
- **Meaning**: between, room, space
- **Radical**: 门 (door)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 时间 (shí jiān) — time
- 房间 (fáng jiān) — room

## Example Sentences

- 你有时间吗？(Nǐ yǒu shí jiān ma?) — Do you have time?

## Notes

间 shows the moon (日→月) through a door gap. As jiān: 'between/room.' As jiàn: 'gap/to separate.'
