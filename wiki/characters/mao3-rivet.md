# 铆 Rivet, fasten firmly

- **Character**: 铆
- **Pinyin**: mǎo
- **Meaning**: rivet, fasten firmly
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 铆钉 (mǎo dīng) — rivet
- 铆接 (mǎo jiē) — to rivet

## Example Sentences

- 工人用铆钉把钢板固定住。(Gōng rén yòng mǎo dīng bǎ gāng bǎn gù dìng zhù.) — Workers used rivets to secure the steel plates.

## Notes

Technical term for riveting. 铆足劲 means to exert all one's strength.
