# 瑟 Stringed Instrument

- **Character**: 瑟
- **Pinyin**: sè
- **Meaning**: stringed instrument, to shiver
- **Radical**: 王 (jade)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 瑟瑟 (sè sè) — rustling, shivering
- 瑟缩 (sè suō) — to cower

## Example Sentences

- 他在寒风中瑟瑟发抖。(Tā zài hán fēng zhōng sè sè fā dǒu.) — He shivered in the cold wind.

## Notes

瑟 originally depicted an ancient stringed instrument, also used for shivering sounds.
