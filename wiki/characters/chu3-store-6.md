# 储 Store, save

- **Character**: 储
- **Pinyin**: chǔ
- **Meaning**: store, save
- **Radical**: 亻 (person)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 存储 (cún chǔ) — storage
- 储存 (chǔ cún) — to store

## Example Sentences

- 数据储存在云端。(Shù jù chǔ cún zài yún duān.) — Data is stored in the cloud.

## Notes

Used in 存储 (storage), 储存 (store), essential for data management
