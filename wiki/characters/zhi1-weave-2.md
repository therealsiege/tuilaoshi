# 织 To Weave

- **Character**: 织
- **Pinyin**: zhī
- **Meaning**: to weave, to knit, textile
- **Radical**: 纟 (silk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 组织 (zǔ zhī) — organization
- 编织 (biān zhī) — to weave

## Example Sentences

- 这个组织很大。(Zhè ge zǔ zhī hěn dà.) — This organization is very large.

## Notes

织 depicts weaving silk threads, extended to knitting and织ganizations (组织).
