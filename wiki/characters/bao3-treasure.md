# 宝 Treasure

- **Character**: 宝
- **Pinyin**: bǎo
- **Meaning**: treasure, precious
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 宝贝 (bǎo bèi) — treasure, baby
- 珍宝 (zhēn bǎo) — treasure

## Example Sentences

- 这是无价之宝。(Zhè shì wú jià zhī bǎo.) — This is a priceless treasure.

## Notes

宝 is simplified from 寶. 宝贝 is also used as a term of endearment.
