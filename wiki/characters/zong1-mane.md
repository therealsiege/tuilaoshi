# 鬃 Mane

- **Character**: 鬃
- **Pinyin**: zōng
- **Meaning**: mane, bristle
- **Radical**: 髟 (long hair)
- **Stroke count**: 18

## Compounds

- 鬃毛 (zōng máo) — mane, bristles
- 马鬃 (mǎ zōng) — horse mane

## Example Sentences

- 狮子的鬃毛很威武。(Shī zi de zōng máo hěn wēi wǔ.) — The lion's mane is majestic.

## Notes

鬃 has long hair 髟 with 宗 (ancestor), long hair on neck of animals.
