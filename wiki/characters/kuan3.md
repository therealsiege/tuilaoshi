# 款 Clause

- **Character**: 款
- **Pinyin**: kuǎn
- **Meaning**: section, clause, sum of money
- **Radical**: 欠 (lack)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 付款 (fù kuǎn) — to pay
- 贷款 (dài kuǎn) — loan

## Example Sentences

- 请先付款。(Qǐng xiān fù kuǎn.) — Please pay first.

## Notes

款 represents sections of documents or sums of money.
