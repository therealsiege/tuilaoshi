# 破 Break, crack

- **Character**: 破
- **Pinyin**: pò
- **Meaning**: break, crack
- **Radical**: 石 (stone)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 破解 (pò jiě) — crack, decrypt
- 攻破 (gōng pò) — breach

## Example Sentences

- 密码被破解了。(Mì mǎ bèi pò jiě le.) — The password was cracked.

## Notes

Used in 破解 (crack), 攻破 (breach), 突破 (breakthrough)
