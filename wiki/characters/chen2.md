# 沉 To sink, to submerge, heavy

- **Character**: 沉
- **Pinyin**: chén
- **Meaning**: to sink, to submerge, heavy
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沉默 (chén mò) — silent, taciturn
- 下沉 (xià chén) — to sink, to subside

## Example Sentences

- 船慢慢沉入水中。(Chuán mànmàn chénrù shuǐ zhōng.) — The ship slowly sank into the water.

## Notes

Can mean to sink or describe heaviness/silence.
