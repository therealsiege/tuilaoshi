# 转 Turn

- **Character**: 转
- **Pinyin**: zhuǎn
- **Meaning**: to turn, revolve
- **Radical**: 车 (cart)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 旋转 (xuán zhuǎn) — to rotate, spin
- 转身 (zhuǎn shēn) — to turn around

## Example Sentences

- 她转身接住了球。(Tā zhuǎnshēn jiē zhù le qiú.) — She turned around and caught the ball.

## Notes

Turning motion; essential for pivots and rotations
