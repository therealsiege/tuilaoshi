# 浆 Thick liquid, pulp, starch

- **Character**: 浆
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: thick liquid, pulp, starch
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 岩浆 (yán jiāng) — magma, lava
- 豆浆 (dòu jiāng) — soy milk

## Example Sentences

- 岩浆从火山口喷出。(Yán jiāng cóng huǒ shān kǒu pēn chū.) — Magma erupts from the volcanic crater.

## Notes

Used for magma and thick liquids
