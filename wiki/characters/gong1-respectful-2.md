# 恭 Respectful, polite

- **Character**: 恭
- **Pinyin**: gōng
- **Meaning**: respectful, polite
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 恭敬 (gōng jìng) — respectful
- 恭喜 (gōng xǐ) — congratulations

## Example Sentences

- 恭喜你考上大学！(Gōngxǐ nǐ kǎo shàng dàxué!) — Congratulations on getting into university!

## Notes

Respectful attitude, used in greetings and congratulations.
