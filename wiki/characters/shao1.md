# 烧 To burn, to cook, to heat, fever

- **Character**: 烧
- **Pinyin**: shāo
- **Meaning**: to burn, to cook, to烧制, fever
- **Radical**: 火(fire)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 烧饭 (shāo fàn) — to cook a meal
- 发烧 (fā shāo) — to have a fever

## Example Sentences

- 他发烧了，要去看医生。(Tā fāshāo le, yào qù kàn yīshēng.) — He has a fever and needs to see a doctor.

## Notes

Can mean to burn, cook, or have a fever.
