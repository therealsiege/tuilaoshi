# 倚 To Lean On

- **Character**: 倚
- **Pinyin**: yǐ
- **Meaning**: to lean on, to rely on
- **Radical**: 亻(person)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 倚靠 (yǐ kào) — to lean on
- 倚仗 (yǐ zhàng) — to rely on

## Example Sentences

- 她倚在门边等候。(Tā yǐ zài mén biān děng hòu.) — She waited leaning by the door.

## Notes

倚 combines person 亻 and 奇 (odd), representing leaning against something.
