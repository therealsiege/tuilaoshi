# 照 To Shine

- **Character**: 照
- **Pinyin**: zhào
- **Meaning**: to shine, to illuminate, to photograph, according to
- **Radical**: 灬 (fire)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 照片 (zhào piàn) — photograph
- 按照 (àn zhào) — according to

## Example Sentences

- 我喜欢拍照片。(Wǒ xǐ huān pāi zhào piàn.) — I like taking photos.

## Notes

照 has multiple meanings from its root sense of 'shining light': to illuminate, to photograph (capturing light), and 'according to' (shedding light on).
