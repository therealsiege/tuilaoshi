# 站 To Stand

- **Character**: 站
- **Pinyin**: zhàn
- **Meaning**: to stand, station
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 站立 (zhàn lì) — to stand
- 车站 (chē zhàn) — station

## Example Sentences

- 大家站起来。(Dà jiā zhàn qǐ lái.) — Everyone stand up.

## Notes

站 shows standing person 立 occupying a position, representing maintaining an upright position.
