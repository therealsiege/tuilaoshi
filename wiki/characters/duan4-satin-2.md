# 缎 Satin

- **Character**: 缎
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: satin
- **Radical**: 纟(silk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 缎子 (duàn zi) — satin fabric
- 软缎 (ruǎn duàn) — soft satin

## Example Sentences

- 她穿着一件红色缎子做的旗袍。(Tā chuān zhe yī jiàn hóngsè duàn zi zuò de qípáo.) — She wore a red satin qipao.

## Notes

缎 refers to satin, a smooth lustrous fabric. The silk radical 纟indicates its connection to textiles.
