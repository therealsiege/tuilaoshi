# 翼 Wing, 左翼 Left Wing

- **Character**: 翼
- **Pinyin**: yì
- **Meaning**: wing, 左翼 left wing
- **Radical**: 羽 (feather)
- **Stroke count**: 17

## Compounds

- 左翼 (zuǒ yì) — left wing
- 右翼 (yòu yì) — right wing
- 机翼 (jī yì) — aircraft wing

## Example Sentences

- 飞机的机翼受损了。(Fēijī de jīyì shòusǔn le.) — The aircraft's wing was damaged.

## Notes

Used both literally for wings and figuratively in political contexts (left wing/right wing political groups).
