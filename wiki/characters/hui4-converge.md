# 汇 Converge, remit, collect

- **Character**: 汇
- **Pinyin**: huì
- **Meaning**: converge, remit, collect
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 汇集 (huìjí) — converge, gather
- 汇报 (huìbào) — report, give an account

## Example Sentences

- 河流汇集成大海。(Héliú huìjí chéng dàhǎi.) — Rivers converge into the ocean.

## Notes

Common term for convergence, collecting, or remitting money.
