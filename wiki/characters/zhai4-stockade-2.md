# 寨 Stockade

- **Character**: 寨
- **Pinyin**: zhài
- **Meaning**: stockade, mountain stronghold
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 山寨 (shān zhài) — mountain stronghold, knockoff
- 村寨 (cūn zhài) — village

## Example Sentences

- 这个山寨地势险要。(Zhè ge shān zhài dì shì xiǎn yào.) — This mountain stronghold has strategic terrain.

## Notes

寨 has roof 宀 with 塞 (to block), fortified settlement.
