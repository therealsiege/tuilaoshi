# 沙 Sand

- **Character**: 沙
- **Pinyin**: shā
- **Meaning**: sand, granulated
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 沙漠 (shā mò) — desert
- 沙滩 (shā tān) — beach

## Example Sentences

- 孩子们在沙滩上玩。(Hái zi men zài shā tān shàng wán.) — The children are playing on the beach.

## Notes

沙 means sand or granulated. Used in沙漠 (desert), 沙子 (sand), 沙发 (sofa, phonetic).
