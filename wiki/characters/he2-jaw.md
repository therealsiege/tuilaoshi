# 颌 Jaw

- **Character**: 颌
- **Pinyin**: hé
- **Meaning**: jaw
- **Radical**: 页 (page/head)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 下颌 (xià hé) — lower jaw
- 上颌 (shàng hé) — upper jaw

## Example Sentences

- 他的下颌受伤了。(Tā de xià hé shòu shāng le.) — His lower jaw was injured.

## Notes

颌 simplified from 頜. Head 页 with 合 (to close), the closing bones of the jaw.
