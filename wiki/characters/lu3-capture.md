# 掳 Capture, take prisoner

- **Character**: 掳
- **Pinyin**: lǔ
- **Meaning**: capture, take prisoner
- **Radical**: 手 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 掳掠 (lǔlüè) — plunder, pillage
- 掳走 (lǔzǒu) — carry off, abduct

## Example Sentences

- 匪徒掳走了人质。(Fěitú lǔzǒu le rénzhì.) — The bandits abducted the hostages.

## Notes

Refers to capturing people or property by force, often in wartime context.
