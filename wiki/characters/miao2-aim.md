# 瞄 To aim, to take aim

- **Character**: 瞄
- **Pinyin**: miáo
- **Meaning**: to aim, to take aim
- **Radical**: 目(eye)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 瞄准 (miáo zhǔn) — to aim at, to target
- 瞄一眼 (miáo yī yǎn) — to glance at

## Example Sentences

- 猎人瞄准了目标。(Lièrén miáozhǔn le mùbiāo.) — The hunter aimed at the target.

## Notes

Used for aiming or quick glances.
