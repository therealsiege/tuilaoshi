# 屡 Repeatedly, Time And Again, 屡次 Repeatedly

- **Character**: 屡
- **Pinyin**: lǚ
- **Meaning**: repeatedly, time and again, 屡次 repeatedly
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 屡次 (lǚ cì) — repeatedly, many times
- 屡屡 (lǚ lǚ) — again and again
- 屡见不鲜 (lǚ jiàn bù xiān) — common occurrence

## Example Sentences

- 他屡次获奖。(Tā lǚ cì huòjiǎng.) — He has won awards repeatedly.

## Notes

Formal adverb for repeated occurrence. 屡次/屡屡 emphasize frequency. 屡见不鲜 is idiom meaning 'nothing new/common'.
