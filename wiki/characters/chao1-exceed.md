# 超 Super

- **Character**: 超
- **Pinyin**: chāo
- **Meaning**: to exceed, super-, ultra-
- **Radical**: 走 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 超市 (chāo shì) — supermarket
- 超过 (chāo guò) — to exceed

## Example Sentences

- 我去超市买东西。(Wǒ qù chāo shì mǎi dōng xī.) — I'm going to the supermarket to buy things.

## Notes

超 means exceeding or surpassing. Used as prefix for 'super-': 超人 (superman), 超级 (super).
