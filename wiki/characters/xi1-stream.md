# 溪 Stream, creek, brook

- **Character**: 溪
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: stream, creek, brook
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 小溪 (xiǎo xī) — brook, stream
- 溪水 (xī shuǐ) — stream water

## Example Sentences

- 小溪潺潺流过村庄。(Xiǎo xī chán chán liú guò cūn zhuāng.) — The brook babbles through the village.

## Notes

Small natural watercourse
