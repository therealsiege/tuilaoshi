# 吗 Question Particle

- **Character**: 吗
- **Pinyin**: ma
- **Meaning**: question particle
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 是吗 (shì ma) — is that so?
- 好吗 (hǎo ma) — okay?

## Example Sentences

- 你是学生吗？(Nǐ shì xué shēng ma?) — Are you a student?

## Notes

吗 turns a statement into a yes/no question. Simply add 吗 to the end of any declarative sentence.
