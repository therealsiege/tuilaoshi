# 灌 To Irrigate

- **Character**: 灌
- **Pinyin**: guàn
- **Meaning**: to pour, to irrigate, to fill
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 灌溉 (guàn gài) — to irrigate
- 灌水 (guàn shuǐ) — to fill with water

## Example Sentences

- 农民在灌溉农田。(Nóng mín zài guàn gài nóng tián.) — The farmers are irrigating the fields.

## Notes

灌 means to pour or irrigate. Used for watering crops (灌溉), filling containers, and灌ing liquids.
