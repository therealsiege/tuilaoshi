# 究 Investigate, Study, 研究 Research

- **Character**: 究
- **Pinyin**: jiū
- **Meaning**: investigate, study, 研究 research
- **Radical**: 穴 (cave)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 研究 (yán jiū) — research, study
- 追究 (zhuī jiū) — investigate, look into
- 探究 (tàn jiū) — explore, probe into

## Example Sentences

- 他在研究中国历史。(Tā zài yánjiū Zhōngguó lìshǐ.) — He is researching Chinese history.

## Notes

Most commonly appears in 研究 (research), one of the most important academic terms. Implies deep, thorough investigation.
