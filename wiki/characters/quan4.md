# 劝 To advise, persuade, 勸 (traditional)

- **Character**: 劝
- **Pinyin**: quàn
- **Meaning**: to advise, persuade, 勸 (traditional)
- **Radical**: 力 (power)
- **Stroke count**: 4

## Compounds

- 劝说 (quàn shuō) — to persuade, advise
- 劝告 (quàn gào) — to advise, exhort

## Example Sentences

- 我劝他别去。(Wǒ quàn tā bié qù.) — I advised him not to go.

## Notes

To persuade or advise someone toward better action.
