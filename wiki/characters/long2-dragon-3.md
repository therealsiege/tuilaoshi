# 龙 Dragon

- **Character**: 龙
- **Pinyin**: lóng
- **Meaning**: dragon
- **Radical**: 龙 (dragon)
- **Stroke count**: 5

## Compounds

- 龙飞凤舞 (lóng fēi fèng wǔ) — lively, flamboyant
- 恐龙 (kǒng lóng) — dinosaur

## Example Sentences

- 他的书法龙飞凤舞。(Tā de shūfǎ lóng fēi fèng wǔ.) — His calligraphy is bold and lively.

## Notes

Used in 龙飞凤舞: symbol of power and majesty in Chinese culture.
