# 登 Climb

- **Character**: 登
- **Pinyin**: dēng
- **Meaning**: to climb, ascend
- **Radical**: 癶 (footsteps)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 攀登 (pān dēng) — to climb, scale
- 登山 (dēng shān) — mountain climbing

## Example Sentences

- 他们计划登上珠穆朗玛峰。(Tāmen jìhuà dēng shàng Zhūmùlǎngmǎ fēng.) — They plan to climb Mount Everest.

## Notes

Ascending; climbing mountains
