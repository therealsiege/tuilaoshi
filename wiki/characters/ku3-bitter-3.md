# 苦 Bitter

- **Character**: 苦
- **Pinyin**: kǔ
- **Meaning**: bitter
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 苦瓜 (kǔ guā) — bitter melon
- 辛苦 (xīn kǔ) — hard, toilsome

## Example Sentences

- 良药苦口。(Liáng yào kǔ kǒu.) — Good medicine tastes bitter.

## Notes

One of the five basic tastes. Grass radical from bitter herbs.
