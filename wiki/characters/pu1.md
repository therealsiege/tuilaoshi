# 铺 To spread, to lay, to pave

- **Character**: 铺
- **Pinyin**: pū
- **Meaning**: to spread, to lay, to pave
- **Radical**: 钅(metal)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 铺路 (pū lù) — to pave a road
- 铺床 (pū chuáng) — to make a bed

## Example Sentences

- 工人们在铺地砖。(Gōngrénmen zài pū dìzhuān.) — The workers are laying floor tiles.

## Notes

Pronounced pū for spreading; pù for shop/store.
