# 蒂 Stem

- **Character**: 蒂
- **Pinyin**: dì
- **Meaning**: stem, base, root
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 根蒂 (gēn dì) — root
- 瓜蒂 (guā dì) — melon stem

## Example Sentences

- 这个问题要从根蒂上解决。(Zhè ge wèn tí yào cóng gēn dì shàng jiě jué.) — This problem must be solved from the root.

## Notes

蒂 grass 艹 plus 帝 (emperor), stem connecting fruit to plant.
