# 曲 Tune, song, melody

- **Character**: 曲
- **Pinyin**: qǔ
- **Meaning**: tune, song, melody
- **Radical**: 曰(say)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 歌曲 (gē qǔ) — song
- 乐曲 (yuè qǔ) — musical composition

## Example Sentences

- 这首歌曲很动听。(Zhè shǒu gēqǔ hěn dòngtīng.) — This song is very pleasant to listen to.

## Notes

Pronounced qǔ for song/melody; qū for bent/curved.
