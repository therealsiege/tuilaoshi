# 将 General

- **Character**: 将
- **Pinyin**: jiāng
- **Meaning**: will, general, to take
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 将来 (jiāng lái) — future
- 将军 (jiāng jūn) — general

## Example Sentences

- 他将来想当医生。(Tā jiāng lái xiǎng dāng yī shēng.) — He wants to be a doctor in the future.

## Notes

将 is simplified from 將. Also read as jiàng (general) or qiāng (to support).
