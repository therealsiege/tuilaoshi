# 鳞 Scale

- **Character**: 鳞
- **Pinyin**: lín
- **Meaning**: scale (of fish/reptile)
- **Radical**: 鱼 (fish)
- **Stroke count**: 20

## Compounds

- 鱼鳞 (yú lín) — fish scales
- 鳞片 (lín piàn) — scale

## Example Sentences

- 鱼身上有很多鳞片。(Yú shēn shàng yǒu hěn duō lín piàn.) — Fish have many scales on their bodies.

## Notes

鳞 simplified from 鱗. Fish 鱼 with 粦 (phosphorus), shining scales.
