# 锻 Forge, hammer, temper

- **Character**: 锻
- **Pinyin**: duàn
- **Meaning**: forge, hammer, temper
- **Radical**: 金 (metal)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 锻炼 (duàn liàn) — exercise, temper
- 锻造 (duàn zào) — forge, hammer

## Example Sentences

- 铁匠正在锻造一把宝剑。(Tiě jiàng zhèng zài duàn zào yī bǎ bǎo jiàn.) — The blacksmith is forging a sword.

## Notes

Used for forging metal through hammering.
