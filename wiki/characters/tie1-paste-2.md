# 贴 Paste, stick

- **Character**: 贴
- **Pinyin**: tiē
- **Meaning**: paste, stick
- **Radical**: 贝 (shell)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 粘贴 (zhān tiē) — paste
- 贴图 (tiē tú) — texture map, post image

## Example Sentences

- 请把链接贴在这里。(Qǐng bǎ liàn jiē tiē zài zhè lǐ.) — Please paste the link here.

## Notes

Used in 粘贴 (paste), 贴图 (texture), common in UI operations
