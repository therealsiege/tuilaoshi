# 溅 To Splash

- **Character**: 溅
- **Pinyin**: jiàn
- **Meaning**: to splash, to spatter
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 飞溅 (fēi jiàn) — to splash
- 溅起 (jiàn qǐ) — to splash up

## Example Sentences

- 水花四溅。(Shuǐ huā sì jiàn.) — Water splashes everywhere.

## Notes

溅 simplified from 濺. Water 氵 scattering, splashing droplets.
