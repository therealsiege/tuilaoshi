# 签 To Sign

- **Character**: 签
- **Pinyin**: qiān
- **Meaning**: to sign, bamboo slip
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 签名 (qiān míng) — signature
- 签证 (qiān zhèng) — visa

## Example Sentences

- 请在这里签名。(Qǐng zài zhè lǐ qiān míng.) — Please sign here.

## Notes

签 is simplified from 簽. Originally bamboo slips for records, now means to sign.
