# 捷 Quick

- **Character**: 捷
- **Pinyin**: jié
- **Meaning**: quick, agile, victory
- **Radical**: 扌 (hand)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 敏捷 (mǐn jié) — agile, nimble
- 捷径 (jié jìng) — shortcut

## Example Sentences

- 他的反应很敏捷。(Tā de fǎnyìng hěn mǐnjié.) — His reactions are very quick.

## Notes

Quickness and victory; used for agility and success
