# 魄 Soul

- **Character**: 魄
- **Pinyin**: pò
- **Meaning**: soul, spirit
- **Radical**: 鬼 (ghost)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 气魄 (qì pò) — boldness, imposing manner
- 魂魄 (hún pò) — soul

## Example Sentences

- 这座建筑很有气魄。(Zhè zuò jiàn zhù hěn yǒu qì pò.) — This building has an imposing manner.

## Notes

魄 is the earthly soul (as opposed to 魂, the heavenly soul). 气魄 means vigor or boldness.
