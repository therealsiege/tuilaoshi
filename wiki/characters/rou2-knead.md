# 揉 To knead, to rub, to massage

- **Character**: 揉
- **Pinyin**: róu
- **Meaning**: to knead, to rub, to massage
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 揉面 (róu miàn) — to knead dough
- 揉眼睛 (róu yǎnjing) — to rub one's eyes

## Example Sentences

- 她在揉面团做包子。(Tā zài róu miàntuán zuò bāozi.) — She is kneading dough to make steamed buns.

## Notes

Used for kneading, rubbing, or massaging actions.
