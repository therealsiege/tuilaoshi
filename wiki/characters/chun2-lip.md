# 唇 Lip

- **Character**: 唇
- **Pinyin**: chún
- **Meaning**: lip
- **Radical**: 口 (mouth)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 嘴唇 (zuǐ chún) — lips
- 唇裂 (chún liè) — cleft lip
- 唇炎 (chún yán) — cheilitis

## Example Sentences

- 他嘴唇干裂。(Tā zuǐ chún gān liè.) — His lips are dry and cracked.

## Notes

Part of oral cavity; examined in physical assessments.
