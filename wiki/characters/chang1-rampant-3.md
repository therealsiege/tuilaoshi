# 猖 Rampant

- **Character**: 猖
- **Pinyin**: chāng
- **Meaning**: rampant, furious, savage
- **Radical**: 犭(animal)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 猖狂 (chāng kuáng) — savage, rampant
- 猖獗 (chāng jué) — rampant

## Example Sentences

- 犯罪活动十分猖獗。(Fàn zuì huó dòng shí fēn chāng jué.) — Criminal activity is rampant.

## Notes

猖 means rampant or wild. Always used in negative contexts.
