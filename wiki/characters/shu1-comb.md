# 梳 Comb, sort out

- **Character**: 梳
- **Pinyin**: shū
- **Meaning**: comb, sort out
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 11

## Compounds

- 梳理 (shūlǐ) — comb, sort out
- 梳子 (shūzi) — comb

## Example Sentences

- 需要梳理一下思路。(Xūyào shūlǐ yīxià sīlù.) — Need to sort out the思路.

## Notes

Literally means comb; figuratively means to sort out or organize.
