# 履 Tread,履行, shoe

- **Character**: 履
- **Pinyin**: lǚ
- **Meaning**: tread,履行, shoe
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 履行 (lǚxíng) — fulfill, perform
- 履历 (lǚlì) — resume, curriculum vitae

## Example Sentences

- 双方都要履行合同义务。(Shuāngfāng dōu yào lǚxíng hétóng yìwù.) — Both parties must fulfill contractual obligations.

## Notes

Formal term for履illing duties or obligations.
