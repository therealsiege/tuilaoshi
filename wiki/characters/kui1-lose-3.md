# 亏 Lose, deficit

- **Character**: 亏
- **Pinyin**: kuī
- **Meaning**: lose, deficit
- **Radical**: 一 (one)
- **Stroke count**: 3

## Compounds

- 亏损 (kuī sǔn) — loss, deficit
- 亏本 (kuī běn) — lose money

## Example Sentences

- 公司去年亏损了一百万。(Gōng sī qù nián kuī sǔn le yī bǎi wàn.) — The company lost one million last year.

## Notes

Opposite of 盈利. 亏空 means deficit/shortage.
