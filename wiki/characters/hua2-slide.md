# 滑 Slide

- **Character**: 滑
- **Pinyin**: huá
- **Meaning**: to slide, slippery
- **Radical**: 氵 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 滑冰 (huá bīng) — ice skating
- 滑雪 (huá xuě) — skiing

## Example Sentences

- 冬天我喜欢滑雪。(Dōngtiān wǒ xǐhuan huáxuě.) — I like skiing in winter.

## Notes

Sliding and skating; fundamental for winter sports
