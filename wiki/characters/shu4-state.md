# 述 State, narrate, follow

- **Character**: 述
- **Pinyin**: shù
- **Meaning**: state, narrate, follow
- **Radical**: 辶 (walk)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 叙述 (xùshù) — narrate, recount
- 陈述 (chénshù) — state, declare

## Example Sentences

- 他述说了事情的经过。(Tā shùshuō le shìqing de jīngguò.) — He recounted what happened.

## Notes

Used for formal narration or following traditions.
