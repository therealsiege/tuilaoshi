# 筷 Chopsticks

- **Character**: 筷
- **Pinyin**: kuài
- **Meaning**: chopsticks
- **Radical**: 竹 (bamboo)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 筷子 (kuàizi) — chopsticks
- 竹筷 (zhúkuài) — bamboo chopsticks

## Example Sentences

- 他会用筷子吃饭。(Tā huì yòng kuàizi chīfàn.) — He knows how to eat with chopsticks.

## Notes

Essential Chinese dining vocabulary. Always plural in meaning. The 竹 radical indicates bamboo material.
