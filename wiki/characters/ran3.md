# 染 To Dye

- **Character**: 染
- **Pinyin**: rǎn
- **Meaning**: to dye, infect
- **Radical**: 木 (tree)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 染色 (rǎn sè) — to dye,染色
- 感染 (gǎn rǎn) — to infect

## Example Sentences

- 她把头发染成了红色。(Tā bǎ tóufa rǎn chéng le hóngsè.) — She dyed her hair red.

## Notes

染 combines tree 木 with 九 (nine) and water, originally representing a dye tree. Extended to mean染 or infection.
