# 矩 Rectangle, square, rule

- **Character**: 矩
- **Pinyin**: jǔ
- **Meaning**: rectangle, square, rule
- **Radical**: 矢 (arrow)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 矩形 (jǔ xíng) — rectangle
- 规矩 (guī ju) — rule, regulation

## Example Sentences

- 这是一个标准的矩形。(Zhè shì yī ge biāo zhǔn de jǔ xíng.) — This is a standard rectangle.
