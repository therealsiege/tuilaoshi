# 审 Examine, investigate

- **Character**: 审
- **Pinyin**: shěn
- **Meaning**: examine, investigate
- **Radical**: 宀 (roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 审查 (shěn chá) — examine, review
- 审批 (shěn pī) — examine and approve

## Example Sentences

- 需要经理审批这个方案。(Xū yào jīng lǐ shěn pī zhè ge fāng àn.) — This proposal needs manager approval.

## Notes

Used in approval workflows. 审计 means audit.
