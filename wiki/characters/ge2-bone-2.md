# 骼 Skeleton

- **Character**: 骼
- **Pinyin**: gé
- **Meaning**: bone, skeleton
- **Radical**: 骨 (bone)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 骨骼 (gǔ gé) — skeleton
- 骸骼 (hái gé) — skeleton, bones

## Example Sentences

- 人体有206块骨骼。(Rén tǐ yǒu èr bǎi líng liù kuài gǔ gé.) — The human body has 206 bones.

## Notes

Framework of the body; studied in orthopedics and anatomy.
