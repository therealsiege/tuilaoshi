# 膊 Arm, Shoulder, 胳膊 Arm

- **Character**: 膊
- **Pinyin**: bó
- **Meaning**: arm, shoulder, 胳膊 arm
- **Radical**: 月 (meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 胳膊 (gē bo) — arm
- 赤膊 (chì bó) — bare-chested
- 光膊 (guāng bó) — bare upper body

## Example Sentences

- 他的胳膊受伤了。(Tā de gēbo shòushāng le.) — His arm is injured.

## Notes

Colloquial term for arm, almost always used in compound 胳膊. This is the everyday word for arm, more common than 手臂.
