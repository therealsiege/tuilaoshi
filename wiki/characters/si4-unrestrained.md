# 肆 Unrestrained

- **Character**: 肆
- **Pinyin**: sì
- **Meaning**: four (formal), unrestrained, shop
- **Radical**: 聿 (brush)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 放肆 (fàng sì) — wanton, presumptuous
- 肆意 (sì yì) — wantonly

## Example Sentences

- 不要太放肆了。(Bú yào tài fàng sì le.) — Don't be so presumptuous.

## Notes

肆 is the formal/financial form of 四 (four); also means unrestrained.
