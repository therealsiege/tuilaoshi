# 垂 Hang down, hand down

- **Character**: 垂
- **Pinyin**: chuí
- **Meaning**: hang down, hand down
- **Radical**: 土 (earth)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 垂直 (chuízhí) — vertical, perpendicular
- 垂范 (chuífàn) — set an example

## Example Sentences

- 柳条垂到水面。(Liǔtiáo chuí dào shuǐmiàn.) — Willow branches hang down to the water surface.

## Notes

Means hanging down physically or passing down traditions and examples.
