# 换 To Exchange

- **Character**: 换
- **Pinyin**: huàn
- **Meaning**: to exchange, to change, to swap
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 交换 (jiāo huàn) — to exchange
- 兑换 (duì huàn) — to exchange (money)

## Example Sentences

- 我想换座位。(Wǒ xiǎng huàn zuò wèi.) — I want to change seats.

## Notes

换 specifically means to exchange one thing for another, unlike 变 which is general change or 改 which is modification.
