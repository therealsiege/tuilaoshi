# 盈 Surplus, profit

- **Character**: 盈
- **Pinyin**: yíng
- **Meaning**: surplus, profit
- **Radical**: 皿 (dish)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 盈利 (yíng lì) — profit, gain
- 盈余 (yíng yú) — surplus

## Example Sentences

- 公司开始盈利了。(Gōng sī kāi shǐ yíng lì le.) — The company started making profits.

## Notes

盈亏 means profit and loss. 非盈利 means non-profit.
