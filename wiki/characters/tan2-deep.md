# 潭 Deep Pool

- **Character**: 潭
- **Pinyin**: tán
- **Meaning**: deep pool, pond
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 15

## Compounds

- 水潭 (shuǐ tán) — pool, pond
- 深潭 (shēn tán) — deep pool

## Example Sentences

- 瀑布下面有个深潭。(Pùbù xiàmiàn yǒu ge shēntán.) — There is a deep pool below the waterfall.

## Notes

潭 combines water 氵 with 覃 (deep), representing a deep body of water.
