# 雇 Hire, employ

- **Character**: 雇
- **Pinyin**: gù
- **Meaning**: hire, employ
- **Radical**: 隹 (bird)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 雇佣 (gù yōng) — to employ, hire
- 雇员 (gù yuán) — employee

## Example Sentences

- 他雇佣了一名助手。(Tā gù yōng le yī míng zhù shǒu.) — He hired an assistant.

## Notes

Common employment term. 雇主 means employer. Less formal than 聘.
