# 溪 Stream

- **Character**: 溪
- **Pinyin**: xī
- **Meaning**: stream, creek
- **Radical**: 水 (water)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 小溪 (xiǎoxī) — small stream
- 溪水 (xīshuǐ) — stream water

## Example Sentences

- 我们在小溪边玩。(Wǒmen zài xiǎoxī biān wán.) — We played by the small stream.

## Notes

Small water body. Water radical. Common in nature and landscape descriptions. Peaceful imagery.
