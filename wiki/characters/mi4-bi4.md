# 秘 Secret

- **Character**: 秘
- **Pinyin**: mì/bì
- **Meaning**: secret; constipation
- **Radical**: 禾 (grain)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 便秘 (biàn mì) — constipation
- 秘密 (mì mì) — secret
- 秘书 (mì shū) — secretary

## Example Sentences

- 多喝水可以预防便秘。(Duō hē shuǐ kě yǐ yù fáng biàn mì.) — Drinking more water can prevent constipation.

## Notes

Blocked or secret; mì for secret, bì in constipation.
