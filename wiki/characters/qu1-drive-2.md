# 驱 Drive, propel

- **Character**: 驱
- **Pinyin**: qū
- **Meaning**: drive, propel
- **Radical**: 马 (horse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 驱动 (qū dòng) — drive, driver
- 驱动器 (qū dòng qì) — disk drive

## Example Sentences

- 需要安装驱动程序。(Xū yào ān zhuāng qū dòng chéng xù.) — Need to install the driver program.

## Notes

Used in 驱动 (driver), 驱动器 (drive), essential for hardware
