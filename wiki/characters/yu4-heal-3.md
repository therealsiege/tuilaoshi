# 愈 Heal

- **Character**: 愈
- **Pinyin**: yù
- **Meaning**: heal, recover
- **Radical**: 心 (heart)
- **Stroke count**: 13

## Compounds

- 痊愈 (quán yù) — to recover completely
- 治愈 (zhì yù) — to cure
- 愈合 (yù hé) — to heal (of wound)

## Example Sentences

- 伤口已经愈合了。(Shāng kǒu yǐ jīng yù hé le.) — The wound has already healed.

## Notes

Recovery and healing; positive treatment outcome.
