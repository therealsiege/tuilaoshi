# 浸 To Soak

- **Character**: 浸
- **Pinyin**: jìn
- **Meaning**: to soak, to immerse, to infiltrate
- **Radical**: 氵(water)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 浸泡 (jìn pào) — to soak
- 浸入 (jìn rù) — to immerse

## Example Sentences

- 把衣服浸泡在水里。(Bǎ yī fu jìn pào zài shuǐ lǐ.) — Soak the clothes in water.

## Notes

浸 has water 氵 with 侵 (to invade), water gradually penetrating.
