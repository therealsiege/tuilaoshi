# 将 General, commander

- **Character**: 将
- **Pinyin**: jiàng
- **Meaning**: general, commander
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 将军 (jiāng jūn) — general
- 大将 (dà jiàng) — senior general

## Example Sentences

- 这位将军身经百战，战功卓著。(Zhè wèi jiāngjūn shēn jīng bǎi zhàn, zhàn gōng zhuózhù.) — This general has fought countless battles and achieved outstanding military exploits.

## Notes

Military general or high-ranking officer. Also pronounced jiāng as a verb meaning 'will' or in 将棋 (Japanese chess).
