# 射 Radiate, emit

- **Character**: 射
- **Pinyin**: shè
- **Meaning**: radiate, emit
- **Radical**: 寸 (inch)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 辐射 (fú shè) — radiation
- 发射 (fā shè) — transmit, emit

## Example Sentences

- 天线发射信号。(Tiān xiàn fā shè xìn hào.) — The antenna transmits signals.

## Notes

Used in 辐射 (radiation), 发射 (transmit), 射频 (RF)
