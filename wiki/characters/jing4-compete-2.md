# 竞 Compete, contest

- **Character**: 竞
- **Pinyin**: jìng
- **Meaning**: compete, contest
- **Radical**: 立 (stand)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 竞争 (jìngzhēng) — compete, competition
- 竞标 (jìngbiāo) — bid competitively

## Example Sentences

- 几家公司竞标这个项目。(Jǐ jiā gōngsī jìngbiāo zhège xiàngmù.) — Several companies bid competitively for this project.

## Notes

Used for competition and bidding.
