# 挂 To hang, to put up, to worry about

- **Character**: 挂
- **Pinyin**: guà
- **Meaning**: to hang, to put up, to worry about
- **Radical**: 扌(hand)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 挂念 (guà niàn) — to worry about, to miss
- 牵挂 (qiān guà) — to worry about, to be concerned

## Example Sentences

- 墙上挂着一幅画。(Qiáng shàng guàzhe yī fú huà.) — A painting is hanging on the wall.

## Notes

Can mean physical hanging or emotional worry.
