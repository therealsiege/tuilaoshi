# 刺 Stab

- **Character**: 刺
- **Pinyin**: cì
- **Meaning**: to stab, pierce
- **Radical**: 刀 (knife)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 冲刺 (chōng cì) — to sprint, final spurt
- 刺杀 (cì shā) — to stab

## Example Sentences

- 他在终点前全力冲刺。(Tā zài zhōngdiǎn qián quánlì chōngcì.) — He sprinted with full force before the finish line.

## Notes

Piercing motion; sprinting and thrusting
