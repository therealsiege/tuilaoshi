# 舟 Boat, vessel

- **Character**: 舟
- **Pinyin**: zhōu
- **Meaning**: boat, vessel
- **Radical**: 舟 (boat)
- **Stroke count**: 6

## Compounds

- 舟船 (zhōu chuán) — boats and ships
- 扁舟 (piān zhōu) — small boat

## Example Sentences

- 一叶扁舟在湖面上荡漾。(Yī yè piān zhōu zài hú miàn shàng dàng yàng.) — A small boat ripples across the lake surface.

## Notes

Literary term for boat; also a radical.
