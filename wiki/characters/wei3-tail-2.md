# 尾 Tail

- **Character**: 尾
- **Pinyin**: wěi
- **Meaning**: tail, end
- **Radical**: 尸 (corpse)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 虎头蛇尾 (hǔ tóu shé wěi) — start strong, finish weak
- 尾巴 (wěi ba) — tail

## Example Sentences

- 猫的尾巴很长。(Māo de wěiba hěn cháng.) — The cat's tail is very long.

## Notes

Used in 虎头蛇尾: the snake tail represents a weak ending.
