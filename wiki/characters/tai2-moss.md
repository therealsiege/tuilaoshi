# 苔 Moss

- **Character**: 苔
- **Pinyin**: tái
- **Meaning**: moss, lichen
- **Radical**: 艹 (grass)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 苔藓 (tái xiǎn) — moss
- 青苔 (qīng tái) — moss, lichen

## Example Sentences

- 石头上长满了青苔。(Shí tou shàng zhǎng mǎn le qīng tái.) — The stones are covered with moss.

## Notes

苔 has grass 艹 with 台 (platform), low-growing plant on surfaces.
