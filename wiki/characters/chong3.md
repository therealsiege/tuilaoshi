# 宠 To pamper, to spoil, to favor

- **Character**: 宠
- **Pinyin**: chǒng
- **Meaning**: to pamper, to spoil, to favor
- **Radical**: 宀(roof)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 宠爱 (chǒng ài) — to dote on, to spoil
- 宠物 (chǒng wù) — pet

## Example Sentences

- 爷爷很宠爱孙子。(Yéye hěn chǒng'ài sūnzi.) — Grandpa dotes on his grandson.

## Notes

Often implies excessive indulgence or favoritism.
