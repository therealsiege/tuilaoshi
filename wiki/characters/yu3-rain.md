# 雨 Rain

- **Character**: 雨
- **Pinyin**: yǔ
- **Meaning**: rain
- **Radical**: 雨 (rain)
- **Stroke count**: 8

## Compounds

- 下雨 (xià yǔ) — to rain
- 雨伞 (yǔ sǎn) — umbrella

## Example Sentences

- 外面在下雨。(Wài miàn zài xià yǔ.) — It's raining outside.

## Notes

雨 is a pictograph showing rain falling from clouds. It's both a character and a common radical in weather-related characters.
