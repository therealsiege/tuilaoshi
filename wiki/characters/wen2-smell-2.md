# 闻 Smell

- **Character**: 闻
- **Pinyin**: wén
- **Meaning**: smell, hear
- **Radical**: 门 (gate)
- **Stroke count**: 9

## Compounds

- 闻诊 (wén zhěn) — auscultation and olfaction (TCM)
- 新闻 (xīn wén) — news
- 听闻 (tīng wén) — to hear of

## Example Sentences

- 闻诊包括听声音和闻气味。(Wén zhěn bāo kuò tīng shēng yīn hé wén qì wèi.) — Auscultation includes listening to sounds and smelling odors.

## Notes

Listening and smelling; second TCM diagnostic method.
