# 敦 Honest, press, urgent

- **Character**: 敦
- **Pinyin**: dūn
- **Meaning**: honest, press, urgent
- **Radical**: 攵 (rap)
- **Stroke count**: 12

## Compounds

- 敦厚 (dūnhòu) — honest and sincere
- 敦促 (dūncù) — urge, press

## Example Sentences

- 他为人敦厚。(Tā wéirén dūnhòu.) — He is honest and sincere.

## Notes

Formal term for honesty or urging/pressing.
