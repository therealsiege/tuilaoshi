# 膜 Membrane

- **Character**: 膜
- **Pinyin**: mó
- **Meaning**: membrane, film
- **Radical**: 月(meat)
- **Stroke count**: 14

## Compounds

- 薄膜 (báo mó) — thin film, membrane
- 眼膜 (yǎn mó) — eye mask

## Example Sentences

- 这种材料表面有一层保护膜。(Zhè zhǒng cáiliào biǎomiàn yǒu yī céng bǎohù mó.) — This material has a protective film on its surface.

## Notes

膜 refers to a membrane or thin film. Used in biology, materials science, and coatings.
