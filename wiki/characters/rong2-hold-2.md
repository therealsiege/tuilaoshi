# 容 To hold, to contain, to tolerate, appearance

- **Character**: 容
- **Pinyin**: róng
- **Meaning**: to hold, to contain, to tolerate, appearance
- **Radical**: 宀(roof)
- **Stroke count**: 10

## Compounds

- 容易 (róng yì) — easy
- 宽容 (kuān róng) — tolerant, lenient

## Example Sentences

- 我们应该宽容待人。(Wǒmen yīnggāi kuānróng dàirén.) — We should treat people with tolerance.

## Notes

Versatile character meaning to hold, tolerate, or appearance.
