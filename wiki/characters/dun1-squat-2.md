# 蹲 To Squat

- **Character**: 蹲
- **Pinyin**: dūn
- **Meaning**: to squat, to crouch
- **Radical**: 足 (foot)
- **Stroke count**: 19

## Compounds

- 蹲下 (dūn xià) — to squat down
- 蹲坐 (dūn zuò) — to squat

## Example Sentences

- 他蹲在地上休息。(Tā dūn zài dì shàng xiū xi.) — He squatted on the ground to rest.

## Notes

蹲 combines foot 足 and 尊 (respect), representing squatting posture.
