# 杆 Pole

- **Character**: 杆
- **Pinyin**: gān
- **Meaning**: pole, stick, shaft
- **Radical**: 木 (wood)
- **Stroke count**: 7

## Compounds

- 球杆 (qiú gān) — cue stick, club (golf)
- 旗杆 (qí gān) — flagpole

## Example Sentences

- 高尔夫球杆有很多种。(Gāo'ěrfū qiúgān yǒu hěn duō zhǒng.) — There are many types of golf clubs.

## Notes

Shaft and stick for various sports equipment
