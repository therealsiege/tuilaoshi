## Description (4,000 at most)

TuiLaoShi (推老师, "the push teacher") is a Mandarin study game set on the road of Journey to the West (西游记). It is made for learners who studied Chinese once and have grown rusty: you can still read more than you can recall, and the game works that gap.

Study at camp
- Spaced review that adapts to each answer: Again, Hard, Good or Easy.
- Four kinds of card: recognition, production (type the Chinese), listening, and sentences with a missing word.
- More than 5,000 simplified characters, with HSK 1 to 6 vocabulary and grammar, each with pinyin, meaning and examples.

Read with a gloss on every word
- Short readings in simplified Chinese. Tap any word for its pinyin and meaning, mark the ones you didn't know, and turn a sentence into a card.

Shadow a line
- Hear a sentence, say it back, and see which characters were heard. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone where it can.

Walk the road
- Take the pilgrims west a step at a time. Regions open as your study grows, and the demons of the novel stand in the way.

Camp papers
- A letter, the court gazette, a travel diary, and My progress: the last seven days, what is due next, and the words that keep slipping.

Tone colours, voices and sound cues can each be turned off in Settings.

Your progress stays on your iPhone. If you also play TuiLaoShi on a Mac, sign in with Apple to sync your study between the two. There are no ads, no tracking and no purchases.
